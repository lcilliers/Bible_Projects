## 0. How this was done — read this first

**Scope.** All 38 codes are included, with every sense and every surface form: wind, breath, body, meat, kin, life, self, person, appetite, dead body, neck and side. This follows the researcher's direction: *"where borderline, wind and body surfaces are found in relation to any other aspect of the inner being it is significant."*

**Method.**
- I read every verse in the CSV, one code at a time, including all 211 verses that carry two or more M47 codes.
- This is **not a statistical analysis.** Where I give a count, it is only a pointer.
- **Fringe observations** (single verses, odd uses, unexpected pairings) are deliberately kept, and are marked **◆ fringe**.

**Tags.**
- **[Claude reading]** — my interpretation of what the verses show.
- **[lexical]** — information from outside the dataset (general Hebrew/Greek lexicography). Not verified here.
- **[verify]** — something to check against the DB or the original-language text.
- Untagged statements are what the ESV text, as supplied, says.

**Limits.**
- **English only.** I read the ESV English. The CSV gives the Strong's code and the ESV "surface" word, not the Hebrew or Greek form. Anything about grammar, stems or word roots is therefore [lexical].
- **Tag alignment.** The surface a tag points to is sometimes not the English equivalent word (see §6). I used the verse-level tag, not the surface, as the evidence that the word is present.
- **Other inner-being words.** Words outside M47 (fear, love, mind/*nous*, kidneys, bowels …) appear in the verse text but are not tagged in this data. Where I mention them, they are **English-text observations only**.
- **No imposed system.** Following the brief, I have avoided any external model (tripartite / bipartite anthropology, psychology, neuroscience). Where a verse *itself* distinguishes or joins parts, I report that.

## 1. Overall picture [Claude reading]

Read together, the verses do not present the inner seat as a set of separate organs with fixed jobs. They present **one inner person, spoken of from several angles**. Each word brings its own angle:

| Word | Its angle |
|---|---|
| **Heart** (*lēv/lēvāv/kardia*) | the **centre**: where a person thinks, speaks inwardly, plans, decides, stores, feels, and is morally *oriented*. It is also where God and others act on the person. |
| **Soul** (*nephesh/psychē*) | the **living, needing self**. Its range runs from throat and appetite, through longing and life, to "myself", "a person" and even "a dead body". |
| **Spirit** (*rûaḥ/pneuma*) | the **moving, animating force**: wind, breath, a person's temper and vigour, dispositions that "come upon" a person, and God's Spirit entering, filling and indwelling. |
| **Flesh** (*bāśār/sarx*) | the **visible, mortal, related** person: body, meat, kin, "all flesh". Flesh *responds to* the inner seat (trembles, faints, sings, rots, heals). In Paul it also becomes an **inner orientation** opposed to the Spirit. |
| **Conscience** (*syneidēsis*, NT only) | the inner person in its **witness-bearing, self-judging** mode. The OT expresses the same thing with *heart* ("David's heart struck him"). |
| **Bosom** (*ḥêq*) | the **holding place** at the chest, where anger lodges, insults are carried, and loved ones are embraced. |

The components are regularly:
- **placed in parallel**, as if interchangeable (heart // spirit, soul // spirit, heart // soul);
- **summed into wholeness formulas** ("all your heart and all your soul");
- **placed inside one another** (the Spirit *in our hearts*; the spirit *within me*);
- **set in causal chains** with the body (a crushed spirit *dries up the bones*);
- **sharply distinguished only at certain boundaries**: at death, in Paul's flesh/Spirit contrast, and in Heb 4:12's "division of soul and of spirit".

## 2. Each component on its own

### 2.1 Heart (H3820A, H3824, H3821, H3825, H3826, H7907, G2588; with G2589, G4641, G2293, G1573)

**a. The heart as "centre / midst"**
- The same word names the middle of non-human things:
  - "the heart of the seas" (Eze 27:4, 25, 26, 27; 28:2, 8; Jon 2:3; Psa 46:2; Exo 15:8)
  - "the heart of heaven" (Deu 4:11)
  - "the heart of the earth" (Mat 12:40)
  - "the midst of the sea" (Pro 23:34)
- ◆ fringe [Claude reading]: the inner seat is named with the ordinary word for *the middle of something*. A person's heart is their "midst".

**b. The physical heart and the chest**
- Arrows and javelins pierce it (2Ki 9:24; 2Sa 18:14; Psa 45:5; 37:15).
- God will "tear open their breast" (Hos 13:8).
- Aaron bears the names of Israel and the Urim and Thummim *"on his heart"*, on the breastpiece (Exo 28:29–30).
- ◆ fringe [Claude reading]: the physical place and the inner seat are one word. Aaron's breastpiece is a physical act of *carrying people on one's heart before the Lord*.

**c. Inner speech — the heart as the place one "says" things**
- "Said in his heart / to himself": Gen 17:17; 24:45; 27:41; 1Ki 12:26; 1Sa 27:1; Deu 7:17; 8:17; 9:4; 18:21; Psa 10:6, 11, 13; 14:1; 53:1; Isa 14:13; 47:8, 10; 49:21; Jer 5:24; 13:22; Obd 3; Zep 1:12; 2:15; Est 6:6; Ecc 1:16; 2:1, 15; 3:17–18; Rom 10:6; Rev 18:7.
- Hannah "was speaking in her heart; only her lips moved" (1Sa 1:13).
- "Before I had finished speaking in my heart" (Gen 24:45).
- The *content* of heart-speech is often self-assessment or self-deception: "I shall not be moved" (Psa 10:6); "There is no God" (Psa 14:1); "My power … has gotten me this wealth" (Deu 8:17); "I am, and there is no one besides me" (Isa 47:8; Zep 2:15).
- Nehemiah "took counsel with *myself*" (Neh 5:7, lēv); "Ask *yourself*" style reflexives also appear.

**d. Thinking, planning, understanding, remembering**
- *Plans and devising:* Pro 16:1, 9; 19:21; 6:14, 18; 24:2; 12:20; Psa 140:2; 58:2; Zec 7:10; 8:17; Isa 10:7; Act 5:4; Eze 38:10.
- *Understanding and knowing:*
  - "a heart to understand" (Deu 29:4)
  - "understanding mind" (1Ki 3:9)
  - "understand with their hearts" (Isa 6:10; Mat 13:15; Joh 12:40; Act 28:27)
  - "an intelligent heart acquires knowledge" (Pro 18:15)
  - "the heart of the hasty will understand" (Isa 32:4)
- *Remembering and "laying to heart":*
  - Isa 47:7; 57:1, 11; 42:25; Mal 2:2; Jer 12:11; Ecc 7:2; 9:1; Deu 4:39; 32:46; 1Sa 21:12
  - "call to mind" (Lam 3:21; Deu 30:1)
  - "come into mind" (Jer 3:16; 7:31; 19:5; 32:35; 44:21; 51:50; Isa 65:17)
  - Haggai's repeated "Consider your ways" is literally *set your heart* (Hag 1:5, 7; 2:15, 18) [lexical].
- *Searching and investigating:* "I applied my heart to seek and to search out" (Ecc 1:13, 17; 7:25; 8:9, 16); "great searchings of heart" (Judg 5:15–16).
- *Pondering and treasuring:* Mary "treasured up all these things, pondering them in her heart" (Luk 2:19, 51); "ponder in your own hearts on your beds" (Psa 4:4); "the meditation of my heart" (Psa 19:14; 49:3).
- *Questioning and reasoning:* Mar 2:6, 8; Luk 3:15; 5:22; 9:47; 24:38.

**e. Willing, deciding, purposing, being moved**
- *Deciding:*
  - "Daniel resolved" (Dan 1:8); "Joash decided" (2Ch 24:4); "has determined this in his heart" (1Co 7:37)
  - "Each one must give as he has decided in his heart" (2Co 9:7)
  - "of my own will / accord" (Num 24:13; 16:28, lēv)
  - "It is in my heart to make a covenant" (2Ch 29:10)
  - "I had it in my heart to build a house" (1Ch 22:7; 28:2; 1Ki 8:17–18)
- *Being moved to give:* "everyone whose heart stirred him … and everyone whose spirit moved him" (Exo 35:21); also Exo 25:2; 35:5, 22, 26, 29; 36:2; 2Ki 12:4 ("the money that a man's heart prompts him to bring").
- *Intention as depth:* "The purpose in a man's heart is like deep water, but a man of understanding will draw it out" (Pro 20:5); "the inward mind and heart of a man are deep" (Psa 64:6).

**f. Feeling**
- *Joy and gladness:* 1Ki 8:66; Pro 15:13, 15; 27:9, 11; Psa 4:7; 13:5; 16:9; 19:8; 28:7; 33:21; Zep 3:14; Joh 16:22.
- *Merriment with wine:* Est 1:10; 2Sa 13:28; Rut 3:7; Judg 16:25; 19:6, 22.
- *Sorrow and anguish:* Neh 2:2; Rom 9:2; 2Co 2:4; Joh 16:6; Psa 13:2; 55:4; Lam 1:20; 5:15.
- *Fear and melting:* Gen 42:28; 1Sa 28:5; Deu 20:3, 8; Jos 7:5; Isa 7:2; 13:7; 21:4; 35:4; Jer 4:9; Nah 2:10.
- *Courage:* Psa 27:14; 31:24; 2Sa 7:27; 17:10; Eze 22:14; Amo 2:16; Psa 76:5; plus the NT compounds "take heart" (Mat 9:2, 22; 14:27; Mar 6:50; 10:49; Joh 16:33; Act 23:11) and "lose heart" (Luk 18:1; 2Co 4:1, 16; Eph 3:13; Gal 6:9; 2Th 3:13).
- *Desire:* "the desires of your heart" (Psa 37:4); "heart's desire" (Psa 21:2; 20:4; Rom 10:1).
- *Love, attachment, contempt, hate:*
  - "the king's heart went out to Absalom" (2Sa 14:1); "my heart goes out to the commanders" (Judg 5:9)
  - "your heart is not with me" (Judg 16:15); "I hold you in my heart" (Phili 1:7); "you are in our hearts, to die together and to live together" (2Co 7:3)
  - "despised him in her heart" (2Sa 6:16); "You shall not hate your brother in your heart" (Lev 19:17)
- *Grief felt in the heart as physical pain:* "My anguish, my anguish! I writhe in pain! Oh the walls of my heart! My heart is beating wildly" (Jer 4:19); "My heart throbs" (Psa 38:10); "my heart is like wax; it is melted within my breast" (Psa 22:14).

**g. Moral character and orientation**
- *Qualities:* pure, upright, blameless, whole, perfect (1Ki 8:61; 15:14; Psa 24:4; 73:1; Mat 5:8; 2Ti 2:22); crooked, perverse, evil, deceitful, proud, haughty, stubborn (Pro 11:20; 17:20; 16:5; Jer 17:9; Jer 7:24 and the "stubbornness of their evil heart" series; Eze 28:2, 17; Obd 3).
- *Direction:*
  - turns (1Ki 11:3–4, 9; Deu 29:18; 30:17), inclines (Psa 119:36, 112; 141:4; Jos 24:23; Judg 9:3), goes after (Eze 11:21; 20:16; 33:31; Job 31:7), is set on (Psa 62:10; Dan 11:28)
  - is near or far (Isa 29:13; Mat 15:8; Mar 7:6); is "toward" someone (1Ch 29:18; 2Ch 16:9)
  - "For where your treasure is, there your heart will be also" (Mat 6:21; Luk 12:34)
- *Whole vs divided:* "with all their heart / whole heart" (many verses; e.g. Psa 119:2, 10, 34, 58, 69, 145; 138:1; Jer 24:7; 29:13; 2Ch 15:15; 31:21); "not with a whole heart" (2Ch 25:2); "double heart" (Psa 12:2); "unite my heart to fear your name" (Psa 86:11); "one heart" (Jer 32:39; 2Ch 30:12; Eze 11:19); "purify your hearts, you double-minded" (Jam 4:8).

**h. The heart as source of what comes out**
- *Source:*
  - "Out of the heart come evil thoughts …" (Mat 15:19; Mar 7:21); "what comes out of the mouth proceeds from the heart" (Mat 15:18)
  - "Out of the abundance of the heart the mouth speaks" (Mat 12:34; Luk 6:45)
  - "Keep your heart with all vigilance, for from it flow the springs of life" (Pro 4:23)
- *Heart producing speech:*
  - "The heart of the wise makes his speech judicious" (Pro 16:23); "My heart overflows with a pleasing theme; … my tongue is like the pen of a ready scribe" (Psa 45:1)
  - "My heart became hot within me. As I mused, the fire burned; then I spoke" (Psa 39:3); "in my heart … a burning fire shut up in my bones … I cannot [hold it in]" (Jer 20:9)
  - "conceiving and uttering from the heart lying words" (Isa 59:13)
  - prophets "prophesy out of their own hearts" (Eze 13:2, 17; Jer 14:14; 23:16, 26)
- *Heart and mouth out of step:*
  - "honors me with their lips, but their heart is far from me" (Isa 29:13; Mat 15:8)
  - "His speech was smooth as butter, yet war was in his heart" (Psa 55:21); "fervent lips with an evil heart" (Pro 26:23)
  - "speak peace … while evil is in their hearts" (Psa 28:3); "flattering lips and a double heart" (Psa 12:2)
- *Heart and mouth in step:* "believe in your heart … confess with your mouth" (Rom 10:9–10); "the word is … in your mouth and in your heart" (Deu 30:14; Rom 10:8).

**i. The heart as a surface, container, store, soil**
- *Written on:*
  - "write them on the tablet of your heart" (Pro 3:3; 7:3); sin "engraved on the tablet of their heart" (Jer 17:1)
  - "I will write it on their hearts" (Jer 31:33; Heb 8:10; 10:16); "tablets of human hearts" (2Co 3:3); "written on our hearts" (2Co 3:2)
  - "the work of the law is written on their hearts" (Rom 2:15)
- *Stored in:*
  - "I have stored up your word in my heart" (Psa 119:11)
  - "lay up these words … in your heart" (Deu 11:18; Job 22:22); "keep them within your heart" (Pro 4:21); "laid them up in their hearts" (Luk 1:66)
  - "the good treasure of his heart" (Luk 6:45)
- *Things put in or taken in:*
  - "idols into their hearts" (Eze 14:3–7); "eternity into man's heart" (Ecc 3:11)
  - "the law of his God is in his heart" (Psa 37:31; Isa 51:7); "the fear of me in their hearts" (Jer 32:40)
  - "wisdom rests in the heart" (Pro 14:33); "wisdom will come into your heart" (Pro 2:10)
- *Soil:* "hold it fast in an honest and good heart, and bear fruit" (Luk 8:15); "what has been sown in his heart" snatched away (Mat 13:19; Luk 8:12).
- *Vessel filled:* "Satan filled your heart" (Act 5:3); "sorrow has filled your heart" (Joh 16:6); "hearts full of evil" (Ecc 9:3); "hearts overflow with follies" (Psa 73:7).

**j. The heart's own "senses"**
- ◆ fringe: "having the eyes of your hearts enlightened" (Eph 1:18); "God … has shone in our hearts to give the light of the knowledge" (2Co 4:6); "until … the morning star rises in your hearts" (2Pe 1:19); "a veil lies over their hearts" (2Co 3:15).
- ◆ fringe: "I slept, but my heart was awake" (Song 5:2); "Even in the night his heart does not rest" (Ecc 2:23).

**k. Heart states described through materials** [Claude reading on the pattern; the images are the text's]
- stone (Eze 11:19; 36:26; Job 41:24; 1Sa 25:37 "his heart died within him, and he became as a stone")
- diamond (Zec 7:12)
- wax, water, melting (Psa 22:14; Jos 7:5; 2Sa 17:10)
- fat, unfeeling (Psa 119:70); "fattened your hearts" (Jam 5:5)
- oven (Hos 7:6)
- deep water (Pro 20:5)
- a stream of water in God's hand (Pro 21:1)
- grass struck down and withered (Psa 102:4)
- burning (Luk 24:32)
- a mirror: "As in water face reflects face, so the heart of man reflects the man" (Pro 27:19)

**l. The heart's opacity and privacy**
- "The heart is deceitful above all things, and desperately sick; who can understand it?" (Jer 17:9)
- "the heart of kings is unsearchable" (Pro 25:3)
- "The heart knows its own bitterness, and no stranger shares its joy" (Pro 14:10)
- "he knows the secrets of the heart" (Psa 44:21); "the secrets of his heart are disclosed" (1Co 14:25); "disclose the purposes of the heart" (1Co 4:5)
- Only God knows it: 1Ki 8:39; 2Ch 6:30; 1Sa 16:7 ("man looks on the outward appearance, but the Lord looks on the heart"); Luk 16:15; Act 1:24 ("heart-knower"); Act 15:8
- "who can say, 'I have made my heart pure'?" (Pro 20:9)

**m. Heart as the seat of self-judgment (conscience function in the OT)**
- "David's heart struck him" (1Sa 24:5; 2Sa 24:10)
- "pangs of conscience" — ESV for *lēv* (1Sa 25:31)
- "Your heart knows that many times you yourself have cursed others" (Ecc 7:22)
- "my heart does not reproach me" (Job 27:6)
- NT: "whenever our heart condemns us, God is greater than our heart … if our heart does not condemn us, we have confidence" (1Jo 3:20–21)

**n. Heart and "mind"**
- *ESV renders lēv as "mind":* 1Ki 3:9, 12; 4:29; 10:24; Pro 19:21; 28:26; Jer 14:14; 23:16; Lam 3:21; Neh 4:6; 6:8; 2Ki 6:11.
- *Aramaic lēvav (H3825) is mostly "mind":* Dan 2:30; 4:16; 5:21; 7:4.
- *Pairs in the text:* "test the heart and the mind" (Jer 11:20; 17:10; 20:12; Psa 26:2; 7:9; Rev 2:23); "hearts and … minds" (Phili 4:7); law "into their minds … on their hearts" / "on their hearts … on their minds" (Heb 8:10 / 10:16).
- [lexical; verify] In several "heart and mind" pairs, the ESV "heart" may translate *kilyah* (kidneys) or the reverse. The CSV tags only the M47 word, so which English word carries the M47 tag varies (e.g. Jer 11:20, where the M47 tag sits on "mind").
- ◆ fringe: a human heart/mind can be exchanged for a beast's and back: "let his mind be changed from a man's, and let a beast's mind be given to him" (Dan 4:16; 5:21); a beast is "given the mind of a man" (Dan 7:4).

### 2.3 Spirit — *rûaḥ / pneuma* (H7307 G–J, H7308, G4151 G/H; with G4152, G3050)

**a. One word: wind, breath, spirit, Spirit, spirits, side, temper**
- *Wind:* storms, the east wind, the four winds (e.g. Exo 14:21; 1Ki 19:11; Jon 1:4; Psa 1:4).
- *Breath:* "the breath of life" (Gen 7:22); "put breath in you, and you shall live" (Eze 37:5–10).
- *A person's spirit:* troubled, revived, broken, lowly (below).
- *God's Spirit:* rushing upon, filling, indwelling (below).
- *Other spirits:* lying (1Ki 22:21–23), harmful (1Sa 16:14–23), unclean (Mar 1–9; Luk 4–11), deceitful (1Ti 4:1).
- *"Side" of the compass* (H7307I; 1Ch 9:24; Eze 42:16–20) — ◆ fringe: *wind* = *direction*.
- *Temper* (H7307J):
  - "hasty temper" (Pro 14:29)
  - "cool spirit" (Pro 17:27)
  - "A fool gives full vent to his spirit" (Pro 29:11)
  - "A man without self-control is like a city broken into and left without walls" (Pro 25:28) — ◆ fringe: self-control = governing one's *rûaḥ*; lacking it = a city without walls.
- ◆ fringe: "the Lord God walking in the garden in the cool [rûaḥ] of the day" (Gen 3:8).

**b. The human spirit**
- *Troubled, anxious:* Gen 41:8; Dan 2:1, 3; 7:15 ("my spirit within me was anxious, and the visions of my head alarmed me"); Joh 13:21; 2Co 2:13.
- *Revived:*
  - after eating ("his spirit revived" 1Sa 30:12)
  - after drinking ("his spirit returned, and he revived" Judg 15:19)
  - on good news (Gen 45:27)
- *Broken, crushed:* Exo 6:9 ("broken spirit and harsh slavery"); Pro 15:4, 13; 17:22; 18:14 ("A man's spirit will endure sickness, but a crushed spirit who can bear?"); Job 17:1; Psa 34:18; 51:17.
- *Faint, failing:* Psa 77:3; 142:3; 143:4, 7; Isa 57:16; 61:3.
- *Lowly, contrite vs proud, haughty:* Isa 57:15; 66:2; Pro 16:18–19; 29:23; Ecc 7:8; Mat 5:3 ("poor in spirit").
- *Governed or ungoverned:* "he who rules his spirit than he who takes a city" (Pro 16:32); "Be not quick in your spirit to become angry" (Ecc 7:9).
- *Weighed by God:* "the Lord weighs the spirit" (Pro 16:2).
- *Knowing and understanding:*
  - "it is the spirit in man, the breath of the Almighty, that makes him understand" (Job 32:8)
  - "out of my understanding a spirit answers me" (Job 20:3); "the spirit within me constrains me" (Job 32:18)
  - "who knows a person's thoughts except the spirit of that person, which is in him?" (1Co 2:11) — [Claude reading] the spirit as the person's self-knowledge.
- *Distinct from "mind" (nous) in prayer:* "my spirit prays but my mind is unfruitful … I will pray with my spirit, but I will pray with my mind also" (1Co 14:14–15); "be renewed in the spirit of your minds" (Eph 4:23).
- *Jesus' spirit:* "perceiving in his spirit" (Mar 2:8); "sighed deeply in his spirit" (Mar 8:12); "deeply moved in his spirit" (Joh 11:33); "troubled in his spirit" (Joh 13:21); "yielded up his spirit" (Mat 27:50; Joh 19:30; Luk 23:46).
- *Paul's spirit:* "his spirit was provoked within him" (Act 17:16); "my spirit was not at rest" (2Co 2:13); "refreshed my spirit" (1Co 16:18); "I serve with my spirit" (Rom 1:9).
- *Fervent:* "fervent in spirit" (Rom 12:11; Act 18:25); "grew and became strong in spirit" (Luk 1:80).
- *Stirred by God:*
  - "the Lord stirred up the spirit of Cyrus" (2Ch 36:22; Ezr 1:1); "everyone whose spirit God had stirred" (Ezr 1:5)
  - the spirit of Zerubbabel (Hag 1:14); of Pul (1Ch 5:26); of the Medes (Jer 51:11)
- *Hardened by God:* "the Lord your God hardened his spirit and made his heart obstinate" (Deu 2:30).
- *A willing spirit:* Psa 51:12; "The spirit indeed is willing, but the flesh is weak" (Mat 26:41; Mar 14:38).
- *A different spirit:* "Caleb, because he has a different spirit and has followed me fully" (Num 14:24).
- *Guarded:* "So guard yourselves in your spirit, and let none of you be faithless" (Mal 2:15–16).

**c. Dispositions spoken of as "spirits" that come upon or enter a person** — ◆ fringe, important [Claude reading]
- "the spirit of jealousy comes over him" (Num 5:14, 30)
- "a spirit of whoredom has led them astray … the spirit of whoredom is within them" (Hos 4:12; 5:4)
- "a spirit of confusion" mingled within (Isa 19:14); "a spirit of deep sleep" poured out (Isa 29:10); "a spirit of stupor" (Rom 11:8)
- "a spirit of justice" (Isa 28:6); "a spirit of grace and pleas for mercy" poured out (Zec 12:10)
- "filled with a spirit of skill" (Exo 28:3); "the spirit of wisdom" (Deu 34:9)
- "a spirit not of fear but of power and love and self-control" (2Ti 1:7); "the spirit of slavery … the Spirit of adoption" (Rom 8:15); "not the spirit of the world, but the Spirit who is from God" (1Co 2:12)
- "the spirit of uncleanness" (Zec 13:2)
- "a disabling spirit" that kept a woman bent over (Luk 13:11); "a spirit that makes him mute" (Mar 9:17); spirits that convulse (Mar 1:26; 9:20; Luk 9:39)

[Claude reading] In these verses, "spirit" is the name for an inner disposition or condition *that arrives*. It is given, poured out, sent, mingled, or comes upon. The line between "my spirit", "a spirit" and "God's Spirit" is not always drawn by the text. Some spirits also produce bodily effects (muteness, convulsion, a bent posture).

**d. God's Spirit acting on and in the inner seat**
- *Comes upon, rushes upon, clothes:* Judg 3:10; 6:34; 14:6, 19; 15:14; 1Sa 10:6, 10 ("be turned into another man"); 16:13; 1Ch 12:18; 2Ch 24:20.
- *Fills with ability:* "filled him with the Spirit of God, with ability and intelligence, with knowledge and all craftsmanship" (Exo 31:3; 35:31).
- *Enters and sets on feet:* Eze 2:2; 3:24.
- *Put within → changed conduct:* "I will put my Spirit within you, and cause you to walk in my statutes" (Eze 36:27; 37:14).
- *Dwells in:* Rom 8:9, 11; 1Co 3:16; "your body is a temple of the Holy Spirit within you" (1Co 6:19); 2Ti 1:14; Jam 4:5 ("the spirit that he has made to dwell in us").
- *Bears witness with our spirit:* Rom 8:16.
- *Placed in the heart:* "given his Spirit in our hearts" (2Co 1:22); "sent the Spirit of his Son into our hearts, crying, 'Abba! Father!'" (Gal 4:6).
- *Pours love into hearts:* Rom 5:5.
- *Writes on hearts:* 2Co 3:3.
- *Strengthens the inner being:* Eph 3:16.
- *Intercedes:* Rom 8:26–27 ("he who searches hearts knows what is the mind of the Spirit").
- *Can be grieved, resisted, lied to, quenched:* Isa 63:10; Eph 4:30; Act 7:51 ("uncircumcised in heart and ears, you always resist the Holy Spirit"); Act 5:3; 1Th 5:19.
- *Can depart or be taken:* 1Sa 16:14; Psa 51:11.
- *In the nostrils / made me:* "as long as my breath is in me, and the spirit of God is in my nostrils" (Job 27:3); "The Spirit of God has made me, and the breath of the Almighty gives me life" (Job 33:4).
- *Formed within:* "who … formed the spirit of man within him" (Zec 12:1); "gives breath to the people … and spirit to those who walk in it" (Isa 42:5).

**e. Wind and breath in relation to the inner being** — explicitly retained per the researcher
- *Breath = life in flesh:*
  - "all flesh in which is the breath of life" (Gen 6:17; 7:15, 22)
  - "sinews … flesh … skin … But there was no breath in them" → "breath came into them, and they lived" (Eze 37:6–10)
  - "When you take away their breath, they die and return to their dust" (Psa 104:29)
- *Breath and thought:* "When his breath departs, he returns to the earth; on that very day his plans perish" (Psa 146:4). ◆ Link to the thought vocabulary (see brief §5).
- *Breath as frailty:* "Remember that my life is a breath" (Job 7:7); "They all have the same breath, and man has no advantage over the beasts" (Ecc 3:19); "He remembered that they were but flesh, a wind that passes and comes not again" (Psa 78:39).
- *No breath = no inner life:* idols have "no breath in them" (Jer 10:14; 51:17; Hab 2:19; Psa 135:17). ◆ fringe: "there was no more breath in her" = the queen of Sheba overwhelmed (1Ki 10:5; 2Ch 9:4).
- *Wind as the emptiness of inner striving* (Ecclesiastes):
  - "I applied my heart to know wisdom … this also is but a striving after wind" (Ecc 1:17); the same in 1:14; 2:11, 17, 26; 4:4, 6, 16; 5:16
  - "Better is the sight of the eyes than the wandering of the appetite: this also is vanity and a striving after wind" (Ecc 6:9) — ◆ appetite (nephesh) + wind
- *Wind as empty words:*
  - "windy knowledge … fill his belly with the east wind" (Job 15:2); "windy words" (Job 16:3); "the speech of a despairing man is wind" (Job 6:26); "the words of your mouth be a great wind" (Job 8:2)
  - "The prophets will become wind; the word is not in them" (Jer 5:13); "utter wind and lies" (Mic 2:11)
- *Wind as empty outcome:* "we have given birth to wind" (Isa 26:18); "Whoever troubles his own household will inherit the wind" (Pro 11:29); "sow the wind, and … reap the whirlwind" (Hos 8:7); "Ephraim feeds on the wind" (Hos 12:1).
- *Wind as the image for a shaken heart:* "the heart of Ahaz and the heart of his people shook as the trees of the forest shake before the wind" (Isa 7:2).
- *Wind and lust:* "a wild donkey … in her heat sniffing the wind! Who can restrain her lust?" (Jer 2:24); "to restrain her is to restrain the wind" (Pro 27:16).
- *Wind as the image of Spirit-birth:* "The wind blows where it wishes … So it is with everyone who is born of the Spirit" (Joh 3:8). ◆ Here the text itself uses wind to picture the Spirit's action on a person.

### 2.4 Flesh and body — *bāśār / sarx* (H1320, H1321, H7607, G4561)

**a. Hebrew flesh: the whole visible, related, mortal person**
- *Body and meat:* the purity law (Lev 13–16), sacrifices, "meat" in the wilderness (Num 11) and in cooking.
- *Kin:*
  - "we are your bone and flesh" (2Sa 5:1; Gen 29:14); "he is our brother, our own flesh" (Gen 37:27)
  - "not to hide yourself from your own flesh" (Isa 58:7); "close relative" (Lev 18:6; 25:49, H7607)
  - "one flesh" (Gen 2:24; Mat 19:5–6; Mar 10:8; Eph 5:31)
- *"All flesh" = every living being / humankind:* Gen 6:12–13; Isa 40:5–6 ("All flesh is grass"); Jer 32:27 ("the God of all flesh"); "the God of the spirits of all flesh" (Num 16:22; 27:16).
- *Frailty in contrast to God:*
  - "What can flesh do to me?" (Psa 56:4); "an arm of flesh" (2Ch 32:8)
  - "Cursed is the man who trusts in man and makes flesh his strength, whose heart turns away from the Lord" (Jer 17:5)
  - "their horses are flesh, and not spirit" (Isa 31:3)
  - "My Spirit shall not abide in man forever, for he is flesh" (Gen 6:3)
- *Flesh responding to the inner seat:*
  - "My flesh trembles for fear of you" (Psa 119:120); "shuddering seizes my flesh" (Job 21:6)
  - "the hair of my flesh stood up" when a spirit glided past (Job 4:15) — ◆ spirit → flesh
  - "my soul thirsts for you; my flesh faints for you" (Psa 63:1); "my heart and flesh sing for joy" (Psa 84:2); "my heart is glad … my flesh also dwells secure" (Psa 16:9; Act 2:26)
  - "My flesh and my heart may fail" (Psa 73:26)
  - "A tranquil heart gives life to the flesh" (Pro 14:30); "they are life to those who find them, and healing to all their flesh" (Pro 4:22)
  - "There is no soundness in my flesh because of your indignation; there is no health in my bones because of my sin" (Psa 38:3)
  - "much study is a weariness of the flesh" (Ecc 12:12); "Ahab … put sackcloth on his flesh" (1Ki 21:27)
- *Heart of flesh vs heart of stone:* "I will remove the heart of stone from your flesh and give you a heart of flesh" (Eze 11:19; 36:26). ◆ Here *flesh* is the **good** state of the heart: responsive, living.
- *Life is in the blood of the flesh:* "the life of the flesh is in the blood" (Lev 17:11, 14; Gen 9:4; Deu 12:23).

**b. Greek *sarx***
- *Neutral uses continue:* one flesh; all flesh; "the Word became flesh" (Joh 1:14); "in the days of his flesh" (Heb 5:7); "flesh and blood" (Heb 2:14; Mat 16:17); "a thorn … in the flesh" (2Co 12:7); "our bodies had no rest … fighting without and fear within" (2Co 7:5).

- [Claude reading] Flesh here is no longer just the body. It is a way the inner person is *oriented*, with its own desires and "mind".

### 2.5 Conscience — *syneidēsis* (G4893, NT only)

- *Bears witness:* "their conscience also bears witness, and their conflicting thoughts accuse or even excuse them" (Rom 2:15); "my conscience bears me witness in the Holy Spirit" (Rom 9:1); "the testimony of our conscience" (2Co 1:12).

- ◆ fringe: "mindful of God" (1Pe 2:19) and "consciousness" (Heb 10:2) are tagged G4893. The word covers *awareness* as well as moral judgment [Claude reading].

### 2.6 Bosom / embrace — *ḥêq* (H2436G) ◆ mostly fringe, but relevant

- *Holding loved ones:* in her arms, at her breast, on her lap (1Ki 3:20; 17:19; Rut 4:16); "carry them in your bosom, as a nurse carries a nursing child" (Num 11:12); "he will carry them in his bosom" (Isa 40:11); "the wife you embrace" (Deu 13:6; 28:54, 56).

  - "anger lodges in the heart [bosom] of fools" (Ecc 7:9)

- *Life poured out on the bosom:* "their life is poured out on their mothers' bosom" (Lam 2:12).
- [Claude reading] The bosom is where one holds *others* and where one holds *feelings* (anger, insults). The same place carries both.

## 3. How the components relate — interrelationships

Each relationship below is described as the verses present it. The main patterns are listed together with the fringe ones.

### 3.1 Parallel and interchangeable (poetic pairing)
- *Heart // spirit:*
  - "Create in me a clean heart … renew a right spirit within me" (Psa 51:10)
  - "a broken spirit; a broken and contrite heart" (Psa 51:17)
  - "brokenhearted … crushed in spirit" (Psa 34:18); "revive the spirit of the lowly … the heart of the contrite" (Isa 57:15)
  - "my spirit faints within me; my heart within me is appalled" (Psa 143:4); "Every heart will melt … every spirit will faint" (Eze 21:7)
  - "heart was not steadfast … spirit was not faithful" (Psa 78:8); "meditate in my heart … my spirit made a diligent search" (Psa 77:6)
  - "whose heart stirred him … whose spirit moved him" (Exo 35:21)
  - "gladness of heart … pain of heart … breaking of spirit" (Isa 65:14)
  - Aramaic: "his heart was lifted up and his spirit was hardened" (Dan 5:20)
- *Soul // spirit:*
  - "My soul yearns for you in the night; my spirit within me earnestly seeks you" (Isa 26:9)
  - "the anguish of my spirit … the bitterness of my soul" (Job 7:11)
  - Hannah: "troubled in spirit … pouring out my soul" (1Sa 1:15)
  - "My soul magnifies the Lord, and my spirit rejoices in God my Savior" (Luk 1:46–47)
- *Heart // soul:*
  - "wisdom will come into your heart, and knowledge will be pleasant to your soul" (Pro 2:10)
  - "take counsel in my soul and have sorrow in my heart" (Psa 13:2)
  - "he who weighs the heart … he who keeps watch over your soul" (Pro 24:12)
  - English-only parallels, not supported by the tags [verify]:
    - "When the cares of my heart are many, your consolations cheer my soul" (Psa 94:19). Only *nephesh* is tagged; the ESV "heart" there is not an M47 word.
    - "When my soul was embittered, when I was pricked in heart" (Psa 73:21). The tag is H3824 on "soul". [lexical] The Hebrew is probably *lēvāv* // kidneys, with the ESV swapping them.
- *Heart // mind:* "a whole heart … a willing mind" (1Ch 28:9, where "mind" = nephesh); "whole heart … single mind" (1Ch 12:38); Heb 8:10 vs 10:16 (heart and mind swap places).
- *Cross-rendering in the ESV:*
  - nephesh → "heart" (Exo 23:9; Jer 15:1; Pro 19:18; 29:17; Lev 26:16; 1Sa 2:33; 2Sa 3:21; 1Sa 23:20; Psa 35:25)
  - nephesh → "spirit" (Lam 1:16)
  - nephesh → "mind" (1Sa 2:35; 1Ch 28:9)
  - psychē → "heart / heartily / fainthearted" (Eph 6:6; Col 3:23; Heb 12:3) and → "minds" (Act 14:2; 15:24)
  - rûaḥ → "mind" (1Ch 28:12; Eze 11:5; 20:32)
- [Claude reading] The translators move between heart, soul, spirit and mind when rendering the same Hebrew or Greek word. That movement is itself evidence that the words' ranges overlap.

### 3.2 Totality and wholeness formulas
- *Heart and soul (and might):*
  - "with all your heart and with all your soul (and with all your might)" (Deu 4:29; 6:5; 10:12; 11:13; 13:3; 26:16; 30:2, 6, 10; Jos 22:5; 1Ki 2:4; 8:48; 2Ki 23:3, 25; 2Ch 6:38; 15:12; 34:31; Jer 32:41 — said of **God**)
  - "you know in your hearts and souls" (Jos 23:14); "lay up these words … in your heart and in your soul" (Deu 11:18); "keep your soul diligently, lest … they depart from your heart" (Deu 4:9)
  - "I am with you heart and soul" (1Sa 14:7, ESV idiom; only *lēvāv* is tagged)
  - "one heart and soul" (Act 4:32)
- *NT expansion — heart, soul, mind, strength:* Mat 22:37; Mar 12:30; Luk 10:27; "heart … understanding … strength" (Mar 12:33).
- *Whole person with an outer term added:*
  - "spirit and soul and body" (1Th 5:23)
  - "soul and body" (Isa 10:18; Mat 10:28; Psa 31:9; Pro 16:24)
  - "heart and flesh" (Psa 73:26; 84:2; Eze 44:7, 9)
  - "body and spirit" (1Co 7:34; 2Co 7:1)
  - "vexation from your heart … pain from your body" (Ecc 11:10)
- [Claude reading] Wholeness is expressed by *adding* terms, not by defining parts. The pairs work as merisms: the whole person seen from two or three sides at once.

### 3.3 Unity and division
- *Unity:*
  - "unite my heart" (Psa 86:11); "one heart" (Jer 32:39; Eze 11:19; 2Ch 30:12)
  - "one heart and soul" (Act 4:32); "one spirit, with one mind" (Phili 1:27)
  - "one flesh" (Gen 2:24); "he who is joined to the Lord becomes one spirit with him" (1Co 6:17)
- *Division:*
  - "double heart" (Psa 12:2); "double-minded" (Jam 4:8)
  - "his heart was not wholly true" (1Ki 11:4; 15:3); "not with a whole heart" (2Ch 25:2)
  - "The spirit indeed is willing, but the flesh is weak" (Mat 26:41)
  - "the desires of the flesh are against the Spirit … to keep you from doing the things you want to do" (Gal 5:17)
- [Claude reading] Division *within* a person is described as a split heart or as spirit against flesh. Union *between* persons uses the same terms (one heart, one soul, one flesh, one spirit).

### 3.4 Distinction — where the text separates the components
- *Division of soul and spirit (Heb 4:12):* "piercing to the division of soul and of spirit, of joints and of marrow, and discerning the thoughts and intentions of the heart." [Claude reading] Soul and spirit are close enough that only the word of God can separate them, just as joints and marrow are adjacent parts of the body. The heart is where the *thoughts and intentions* are, and they are *discerned* there.
- *Living soul vs life-giving spirit:* "The first man Adam became a living being [psychē]; the last Adam became a life-giving spirit" (1Co 15:45; cf. Gen 2:7).
- *Spirit vs mind:* 1Co 14:14–15 (above).
- *Spirit vs flesh (as kind):*
  - "a spirit does not have flesh and bones" (Luk 24:39)
  - "horses are flesh, and not spirit" (Isa 31:3)
  - "That which is born of the flesh is flesh, and that which is born of the Spirit is spirit" (Joh 3:6)
  - "the flesh is no help at all" (Joh 6:63)
  - "put to death in the flesh but made alive in the spirit" (1Pe 3:18; 4:6)
  - "the destruction of the flesh, so that his spirit may be saved" (1Co 5:5)
- *Soul vs body:* "cannot kill the soul" (Mat 10:28).
- *Spirit vs body in place:* "absent in body, … present in spirit" (1Co 5:3–4; Col 2:5). ◆ fringe [Claude reading]: the spirit can be "with" people where the body is not.
- *Inner vs outer:*
  - "Though our outer self is wasting away, our inner self is being renewed day by day" (2Co 4:16, with "lose heart")
  - "a Jew is one inwardly, and circumcision is a matter of the heart, by the Spirit" (Rom 2:29)
  - "the hidden person of the heart with the imperishable beauty of a gentle and quiet spirit" (1Pe 3:4)
  - "man looks on the outward appearance, but the Lord looks on the heart" (1Sa 16:7)
  - "what is in the heart" vs "outward appearance" (2Co 5:12)
- *Food and the heart:* "since it enters not his heart but his stomach, and is expelled" (Mar 7:19). ◆ The text draws a boundary: food goes to the stomach, not the heart. But see §3.6, where food *does* strengthen the heart.

### 3.5 Opposition
- *Flesh vs Spirit* (Paul): Gal 3:3; 4:29; 5:16–17; 6:8; Rom 8:4–9, 13; Phili 3:3.
- *Flesh's passions vs soul:* "passions of the flesh, which wage war against your soul" (1Pe 2:11).
- *Mind vs flesh:* Rom 7:25.
- *Hebrew background:*
  - "He remembered that they were but flesh" (Psa 78:39)
  - "My Spirit shall not abide in man forever, for he is flesh" (Gen 6:3)
  - trusting flesh ↔ heart turning from the Lord (Jer 17:5)
- [Claude reading] In the OT verses read here, flesh is **frail**, not **hostile**. The hostility of flesh to Spirit is NT-specific in this dataset. Even in the NT, neutral flesh remains (one flesh, Word became flesh, "the life I now live in the flesh I live by faith" Gal 2:20).

### 3.6 Cause and effect between inner seat, body and senses
- *Heart → spirit:* "by sorrow of heart the spirit is crushed" (Pro 15:13).
- *Heart / spirit → body, face, bones:*
  - "A glad heart makes a cheerful face" (Pro 15:13)
  - "A tranquil heart gives life to the flesh, but envy makes the bones rot" (Pro 14:30)
  - "A joyful heart is good medicine, but a crushed spirit dries up the bones" (Pro 17:22)
  - "a trembling heart and failing eyes and a languishing soul" (Deu 28:65)
  - "My heart throbs; my strength fails me, and the light of my eyes — it also has gone" (Psa 38:10)
  - "Every heart will melt, and all hands will be feeble; every spirit will faint, and all knees will be weak as water" (Eze 21:7); "Hearts melt and knees tremble; anguish is in all loins; all faces grow pale" (Nah 2:10)
  - "My heart is struck down like grass … I forget to eat my bread" (Psa 102:4)
  - "My heart is broken within me; all my bones shake" (Jer 23:9)
  - "your heart shall rejoice; your bones shall flourish like the grass" (Isa 66:14)
  - "For this our heart has become sick, for these things our eyes have grown dim" (Lam 5:17)
  - "my stomach churns; my heart is wrung within me" (Lam 1:20)
- *Body / face / senses → heart:*
  - "by sadness of face the heart is made glad" (Ecc 7:3)
  - "The light of the eyes rejoices the heart, and good news refreshes the bones" (Pro 15:30)
  - "my heart has gone after my eyes" (Job 31:7); "not to follow after your own heart and your own eyes" (Num 15:39)
  - "everyone who looks at a woman with lustful intent has already committed adultery with her in his heart" (Mat 5:28)
- *Food and drink → heart / spirit / soul:*
  - "Strengthen your heart with a morsel of bread" (Judg 19:5, 8); "wine to gladden the heart of man … bread to strengthen man's heart" (Psa 104:15)
  - "satisfying your hearts with food and gladness" (Act 14:17)
  - "when he had eaten, his spirit revived" (1Sa 30:12); "when he drank, his spirit returned" (Judg 15:19)
  - "when the wine had gone out of Nabal … his heart died within him, and he became as a stone" (1Sa 25:37)
  - "it is good for the heart to be strengthened by grace, not by foods" (Heb 13:9)
  - "Oil and perfume make the heart glad" (Pro 27:9)
- *Fasting afflicts the soul:* Psa 35:13 ("I afflicted myself [nephesh] with fasting"); Psa 69:10; Isa 58:3, 5; the Day of Atonement "afflict yourselves" (Lev 16:29–31; 23:27–32; Num 29:7).
- *Appetite (soul) and heart:* "They tested God in their heart by demanding the food they craved" (Psa 78:18); the fool's heart "busy with iniquity … to leave the craving of the hungry unsatisfied" (Isa 32:6); "you crave meat" (Deu 12:20).
- *Heart → mouth, hands, feet, deeds:* §2.1h. Also:
  - "clean hands and a pure heart" (Psa 24:4)
  - "Let us lift up our hearts and hands to God" (Lam 3:41)
  - "a heart that devises wicked plans, feet that make haste to run to evil" (Pro 6:18)
  - "The heart of man plans his way, but the Lord establishes his steps" (Pro 16:9)
  - "The law of his God is in his heart; his steps do not slip" (Psa 37:31)
- *Heart ↔ ears / hearing* — ◆ link to the brief's hearing question:
  - "hear with their ears, and understand with their hearts" (Isa 6:10; Mat 13:15; Act 28:27)
  - "making your ear attentive to wisdom and inclining your heart to understanding" (Pro 2:2); "Incline your ear … apply your heart" (Pro 22:17; 23:12)
  - "receive in your heart, and hear with your ears" (Eze 3:10); "hear with your ears … set your heart upon all that I shall show you" (Eze 40:4)
  - "uncircumcised in heart and ears" (Act 7:51)
  - "The Lord opened her heart to pay attention" (Act 16:14)
  - "They made their hearts diamond-hard lest they should hear the law" (Zec 7:12)
  - "Incline your ear, and come to me; hear, that your soul may live" (Isa 55:3) — soul + hearing
  - "Today, if you hear his voice, do not harden your hearts" (Heb 3:7–8, 15; 4:7; Psa 95:8)
  - [Claude reading] Hearing and heart are joined repeatedly: the ear receives, the heart understands or refuses. Hardness of heart and failure to hear go together.

### 3.7 Containment — what is placed "in" what
- *In the heart:*
  - law, words, commandments (Deu 6:6; Psa 37:31; 119:11; Jer 31:33)
  - wisdom (Pro 2:10; 14:33); fear of God (Jer 32:40); eternity (Ecc 3:11)
  - idols (Eze 14:3); deceit (Pro 12:20); folly (Pro 22:15); anger (Ecc 7:9)
  - the Spirit (2Co 1:22; Gal 4:6); Christ "may dwell in your hearts through faith" (Eph 3:17)
  - "the peace of Christ rule in your hearts" (Col 3:15); "the word of Christ dwell in you richly … thankfulness in your hearts" (Col 3:16); God's love "poured into our hearts" (Rom 5:5)
  - what "the devil had already put into the heart of Judas" (Joh 13:2); what "God has put into their hearts" (Rev 17:17; Neh 2:12; 7:5; Ezr 7:27; 2Co 8:16)
- *Within me:* spirit (Psa 143:4; Isa 26:9; Job 32:18; Dan 7:15); soul (Psa 42:5–6; 131:2); heart (Psa 22:14; 39:3; 109:22; Jer 8:18; 23:9; Lam 1:20; Hos 11:8).
- *Spirit and heart inside flesh:* "I will remove the heart of stone from your flesh" (Eze 36:26); "put breath in you" (Eze 37:6).
- *Spirit and body:* "your body is a temple of the Holy Spirit within you" (1Co 6:19); "if the Spirit … dwells in you, he … will also give life to your mortal bodies" (Rom 8:11).
- [Claude reading] The heart is the innermost *location* in the text's picture. Soul-states, spirit, God's Spirit, words, law and dispositions are placed "in" it. Spirit and soul are also spoken of as "within me", but they are less often described as containers.

### 3.8 The inner seat shared or joined between persons ◆ fringe cluster
- *Soul to soul:*
  - "the soul of Jonathan was knit to the soul of David, and Jonathan loved him as his own soul" (1Sa 18:1, 3; 20:17)
  - "your friend who is as your own soul" (Deu 13:6, with "the wife you embrace")
  - "his life is bound up in the boy's life" (Gen 44:30)
- *Heart to heart:* "my heart will be joined to you" (1Ch 12:17); "Is your heart true to my heart as mine is to yours?" (2Ki 10:15); "you are in our hearts, to die together and to live together" (2Co 7:3); "I hold you in my heart" (Phili 1:7); "in person not in heart" (1Th 2:17).
- *Shared across a group:* "one heart and soul" (Act 4:32); "one spirit, with one mind" (Phili 1:27); "Did we not act in the same spirit?" (2Co 12:18).
- *Transferred between persons:* "a double portion of your spirit" (2Ki 2:9, 15); "take some of the Spirit that is on you and put it on them" (Num 11:17, 25); "in the spirit and power of Elijah, to turn the hearts of the fathers to the children" (Luk 1:17; Mal 4:6).
- *Captured or stolen by others:* "Absalom stole the hearts of the men of Israel" (2Sa 15:6); "his wives turned away his heart" (1Ki 11:3); "he swayed the heart of all the men of Judah as one man" (2Sa 19:14).
- *Known from shared experience:* "You know the heart [nephesh] of a sojourner, for you were sojourners" (Exo 23:9).
- *With a spouse:* "guard yourselves in your spirit, and let none of you be faithless to the wife of your youth" (Mal 2:15–16); "one flesh" (Gen 2:24).
- [Claude reading] The inner seat is not depicted as sealed inside the individual. It can be knit to, joined with, held within, carried in, stolen by, or shared with another person. It can also be passed from one person to another (spirit).

### 3.9 The boundary of death — where the components come apart
- *Soul departs:* "as her soul was departing" (Gen 35:18); soul to Sheol, pit, silence (§2.2d).
- *Spirit and breath return to God:*
  - "the dust returns to the earth as it was, and the spirit returns to God who gave it" (Ecc 12:7)
  - "Who knows whether the spirit of man goes upward and the spirit of the beast goes down into the earth?" (Ecc 3:21)
  - "When you take away their breath, they die and return to their dust" (Psa 104:29)
  - "Into your hand I commit my spirit" (Psa 31:5; Luk 23:46; Act 7:59)
  - "No man has power to retain the spirit" (Ecc 8:8)
- *Return to life:*
  - "her spirit returned, and she got up" (Luk 8:55)
  - "the life of the child came into him again, and he revived" (1Ki 17:21–22), with "the flesh of the child became warm" (2Ki 4:34)
  - "a breath of life from God entered them" (Rev 11:11)
  - Ezekiel 37: flesh, sinews, skin → breath → life
- *Life in the blood:* "the life [nephesh] of the flesh is in the blood" (Lev 17:11, 14; Gen 9:4–5; Deu 12:23).
- *Body without spirit:* "the body apart from the spirit is dead" (Jam 2:26).
- *Dead body = nephesh:* H5315M (Num 19:11, 13).
- *Beyond death:* "the spirits of the righteous made perfect" (Heb 12:23); "the spirits in prison" (1Pe 3:19); "the souls … under the altar" (Rev 6:9).
- *Plans perish when breath departs:* Psa 146:4.
- [Claude reading] Death is where the text most clearly *separates* the terms:
  - flesh/dust goes to the earth
  - spirit/breath goes to God
  - soul goes to Sheol, or is delivered
  - the blood carries the life

  In ordinary life the same terms overlap and stand in for one another.

### 3.10 God, animals and things — the vocabulary is not limited to humans ◆ fringe

## 5. What the data suggests the inner seat *is* — tentative [Claude reading; for discussion, not a finding]

- **Heart** is presented as the **integrating centre**. It is where thinking, speaking-within, willing, feeling, remembering, moral orientation and self-judgment all happen, and where God, others, spirits and substances act.
- **Soul** is the **living self in its need and exposure**. It thirsts, longs, is bitter, is hunted, is delivered, and can be addressed. It shades into life, self and person.
- **Spirit** is the **moving, animating and disposing force**. It is breath and wind, vigour or faintness, temper, and arriving dispositions. It is also the channel by which God's Spirit enters and acts.
- **Flesh** is the **embodied, related, mortal person**. It responds to the inner seat, is kin to others, and is frail before God. In the NT it also becomes a hostile orientation.
- **Conscience** is the **witness-bearing heart** (NT term; OT uses *heart*).
- **Bosom** is the **holding place**.

**The interrelationships run in every direction:**

| Kind of relationship | Examples |
|---|---|
| Parallel | heart // spirit // soul |
| Summative | all your heart and soul |
| Contained | Spirit in the heart |
| Causal | heart → bones; bread → heart |
| Oppositional | flesh vs Spirit |
| Trans-personal | knit souls, one heart |
| Separated only at death | dust / spirit / soul part company |

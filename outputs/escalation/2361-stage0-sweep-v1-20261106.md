## 6. Findings

1. **G4, the Ollama flag rule, recovered.** The workbook `2359-base-form-results-v1-20261104.xlsx` flags 22 of the 387 accepted answers as "possible opposite (negating prefix/suffix)". The Notes tab says it is "a simple string check ... not a judgement". The rule is therefore deterministic (a negating prefix or suffix on the surface). The structure module's own construct list has 10 constructs of kind negating prefix or suffix, so the rule is rebuilt from config; the reproduction check is that it flags exactly those 22.
2. **Empty surfaces in `verse_lexical`.** 408,392 rows have no surface: 408,274 are NULL (occurrences with no English of their own, by the column note "the literal text at this span", to be confirmed) and **118 hold an empty string**. Those 118 are an error of the past (an empty string where NULL belongs) and are the source of the empty-surface pair rows in `surface_subgroup`. They are recorded as a flagged item; changing `verse_lexical` is outside this build and needs your decision.
3. **The junk surfaces** of design v3 section 7 (4 surfaces, 10 pair rows) are confirmed against the data.
4. **No file matches no rule.** All 93 files are classified; none is left "unknown".

## #2216: ledger rows that cite verses with no strand word

**State.** Catch-up B (`outputs/escalation/2172-catch-up-b-v1-20261022.md`) listed the counts:
1,062 cited verses hold none of their strand's words (fear 123, anger 762, life-death 134,
origin of thought 43), and 45 rows were left with no grounded verse. v4 adds that five anger rows at
the inner seat cite the verse next to the one holding the anger. Still not done: why the ledgers
cited them (v3 "next": sort by cause).

**Checked:**
- It can no longer happen. A reading's `Record` grounds each new observation on `{verse, strong}`
  and refuses it if that strong is not in that verse: see `check_observations` and
  `recordingpass.resolve_occurrence` in `iba/app/lib/strandreading.py`.
- The fear, anger and life-death ledgers are in the reading index (`reading_file`, root
  `_analytics/Clusters`). A cited verse with no observation therefore comes up as a
  `read_in_files_only` gap in any reading that reaches it. *Inferential:* I read this from the gap
  guide in `cfg_setting reading.gap_types`; I have not run it on a reading.
- The origin-of-thought ledger
  (`_analytics/cross-cluster-web/origin-of-thought/origin-of-thought-observation-ledger-v2-20261001.md`)
  is **not** in the index: `reading_index.roots` is `_analytics/Clusters` only. Its 43 verses and
  5 rows would not come up in any reading.

**The decision for you (B:105, not mine to make):**

- **A. Sort by cause, as v3 planned.** Each of the 1,062 verses is classed as one of three:
  1. it holds a word of the strand's cluster that is outside the unit pulls;
  2. a verse within two either side holds a strand word (setting or citation slip, the MS pattern);
  3. neither, which leaves it to be read.

  This is a new read-only step, which means building a registered step (cfg row plus code), since
  study operations go through the app. Cost: one build, then one set-based query.
- **B. No cause sort (cheaper, B:20).** Close #2216 with this finding:
  - the cause cannot recur, because Record refuses it;
  - each cited verse is left to the reading that reaches it, as a `read_in_files_only` gap.

  To stop origin of thought's 43 verses being lost, add
  `_analytics/cross-cluster-web/origin-of-thought` to `reading_index.roots` (a config change through
  `configmaint.propose`, then `Reading-Index.ps1 -Step Build`). What you lose: no record of why the
  October ledgers cited those verses.

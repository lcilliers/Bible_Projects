## 1. What the project is

- A sole-researcher Bible study (le Roux Cilliers) of how Scripture describes the workings of the human inner being (`CLAUDE.md`; `inner_being_definition` in `cfg_prose_concept`).
- The IBA app is the single driver of all operational work. Every operation is a registered `cfg_work_package`/`cfg_step`; every rule is a `cfg_*` row; nothing exists that the app does not specify (CHARTER section 1; `governance.scope_iba_app`, `governance.rules_must_be_config_driven`). Outside `iba/` the integration is not complete (CHARTER section 2).
- Two session modes, chosen by the researcher at the start, never switched by Claude: standard (every `cfg_*` change through propose and approval) and Developer Mode (building the app) (CHARTER section 4).

## 3. The study chain, from the text to the narrative

1. **Base data (stage "Base_data").** STEP Bible supplies the Hebrew and Greek data. `word_strong` records which Strong's codes an English word maps to; `strong` is the term identity with its meaning tables; `span` is the immutable parse of the STEP interlinear; `verse_lexical` is the derived mechanical reading, one row per Strong's code per span (`governance.programme_stages`; `cfg_table` use texts). Verse-lexical is the verse's structural composition and is not inner-being aware (researcher, 2026-11-15, session log).
2. **Surfaces.** A surface is a word or phrase of the ESV text. The surface cycle (CHARTER section 5, escalation #2361) is five modules over tables: group (what kind of word), structure (its base by shape), AI base (inferential until the researcher confirms, B:4), resolve, collections.
3. **Collections.** A collection gathers surfaces of like meaning (synonyms, opposites, associated), named for its core word. Every surface of the groups behaviour and inner_being_state that has a verse is in exactly one collection; a surface is in two only by the researcher's ruling (B:129). Collection states: confirmed, merged, retired. Words that are neither a behaviour nor an inner-being state are not surfaces of a collection.
4. **The collection reading (stage "Analysis").** The collection is the unit of study (B:127). It is read in stages, as the researcher defined on 2026-11-15 (#2507 v7; B:132; the steps are `cfg_setting layer.reading`, run through `Collection-Build.ps1`):
   - Stage 1, the lexical analysis: every word of the collection read lexically from the lexicon and its occurrences, as observations tied to verses.
   - Stage 2, the grouping of the surfaces by dictionary meaning into subgroups (`collection_subgroup`); step 1.1 records each group as an observation of the meaning its surfaces share.
   - Stage 3, verse-lexical observations only: what the other words of the verse show with each group.
   - Stage 4, the inner-being analysis made from stage 3: not yet built.
   - Stage 5, the claim formulation: not yet decided.
   - Lexical work and inner-being work never run in the same process (B:133). Every aspect a step demands is a ledger row (`collection_layer_item`), closed by an observation or a recorded none (B:134).
5. **Observations and nodes.** An observation (`ib_observation`) is a founded statement about what a verse shows; a node (`ib_node`) applies it to one verse, word and surface, and carries the collection. A live observation has at least one live node; withdrawals are soft deletes with a reason in `ib_observation_change` (B:130, B:131). Cluster and subgroup are retired from nodes and observations (#2495).
6. **Claims and narrative (stage "Publishing").** A claim goes beyond the mere text of its sources and is held in `narrative_claim`. The narrative is built by collection: chapter 10 has a sub-chapter per collection (B:127). How claims are linked to collections and worked up is open (#2486, with the researcher).
7. **The reading process.** `Reading.ps1` (Open, Record, Check, Synergy, Apply, Publish, Account) passes a reading of one scope through three gates: recorded, synergised, published (`governance.study_status`). It still opens one subgroup of a cluster as its home; the move to collections is #2448 and is not built.

## 4. How Claude works inside it

- Start of every session: `/start-project` prints every general rule; the compliance file restates each in own words and passes `Compliance.ps1 -Action Check -Phase Start`. The remaining rules arrive at the first use of an operation (point-of-use hook, #2209).
- Claude reads the evidence and decides in most cases; the researcher scrutinises and settles what the evidence cannot (B:127). A new design or build goes to the researcher first (B:105); a new decision point stops the work and is an open item (B:44).
- Anything open is an escalation in the same turn (B:21, E:5); the researcher's words are quoted verbatim.
- Before a table or column is used, its recorded meaning in `cfg_table`/`cfg_column` is read (B:123). Database state is queried live, not remembered (B:1). Queries on large tables are planned first.
- A change to a rule is a `cfg_*` row first, with `GOVERNANCE.md` and `BUILD.md` updated in the same unit of work (`governance.governance_md_on_rule_change`, `governance.build_md_on_code_change`).
- A session ends with `/session-close`: detection checks, the session log naming the escalations touched, files changed, decisions, open items and the git state, then two commits. From 2026-11-19 (researcher, D3 on #2510) keeping this map current is part of that close; the check that enforces it is not yet built.
- Memory holds who the researcher is and a few data pointers; no rules and no project state (MEMORY.md).

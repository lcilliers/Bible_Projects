### 1.2 What the cause-and-effect reading shows

1. **There was not one watershed but at least seven, each dated, each answering a different failure.** The record dates: that the list is too short (February); that word-level pre-classification has an accuracy ceiling (July 24); that one instruction document cannot give consistent verse readings at scale (August 3); that discovery gives candidates and not relevance (August 10); that meaning is decided by the occurrence in its verse (September 10 and 17); that the surface and not the code is the unit (October 28 to November 7); that lexical and inner-being meaning are separate (November 15). The researcher's account treats context-drivenness as the one watershed; the record shows it was relearned in each of these forms.
2. **The remedy that worked repeatedly was layering: separate what the text decides from what judgement decides.** It appears on 2026-08-02 (phenomenon before operation), 08-03 (mark what the text decides, resolve the open referents separately), 08-05 (the mechanical `verse_lexical` before any reading), September (stages 1 to 4) and 2026-11-15 (lexical before inner-being). Each time the cause was the same: a combined pass drifted to "a story", imported unearned precision, or restated the dictionary.
3. **The failure that repeated was a rule that existed but was not built into the process.** The 08-03 conclusion (a document cannot be made specific enough) and the 11-15 stage 1 run (rules existed, the recorder did not enforce them) are the same failure at an interval of three and a half months. The response each time moved rules from documents into mechanisms, which is premise 10, and the audit of BUILD 538 shows it is still incomplete.
4. **Every gap was found by the researcher reading one concrete case by hand**, never by the app's checks: 2 Cor 7:11, John 10:34 and G3551, Gen 43:27, `receive`, Jonah 3, the `thought` groups. The structural checks passed in the stage 1 run while the content was wrong. Any plan to close the present gaps needs a content-level detector (a read sample of each stage's output by the researcher) in addition to the structural checks.

## 3. A critical reading of the account

**C1. The split relocates the circularity; it does not remove it.** The account says the study turned to splitting meaning, occurrences, the word in the verse and the collation on surfaces. That is sound and matches the record. But the difficulty it names (knowing what is and is not a characteristic while discovering how characteristics work) is now confined to stage 4, the inner-being analysis, which is not built (B:132). The layers before it can be completed without answering it. The risk is that three stages are finished and the hardest question is still open.

**C2. The surface groups reintroduce a word-level relevance gate, the approach that failed in July.** BUILD 15 concluded that classifying a term's relevance from the term alone has a ceiling. The November judging classifies a surface as behaviour, inner_being_state, entity or other from the word alone, "no verse and no code" (BUILD 447). The judge is better (a language model reasoning from dictionary meaning, not a wordlist) and the stated purpose is grouping, not relevance. But the scope of the study is the groups behaviour and inner_being_state (B:129), so the group is a relevance gate. The data in section 2 is the sign of what a word-level gate does: a third of the node rows on `inner_being_state` words have no inner-being role in their verse, and 563 distinct `other` words have been read as characteristics or qualifiers in some verse. This is inference, not proof: the roles were written under cluster-era methods and a qualifier is the designed role of an `other` word (B:120). It is a risk to test, not a failure shown.

**C3. The universe is bounded by the Strong's-level allocation of August and September, which is the code-level preselection premise 4 rejects.** 37.6% of occurrence rows lie outside the judged universe, all of them on codes allocated only to T-clusters, and T2 is defined as codes "with no direct inner-being connotation, read as qualifiers" (glossary). Those words enter only as qualifiers of verses where a member surface occurs (B:120). A word that becomes behavioural only in combination, the researcher's third difficulty, but occurs with no member surface, is invisible. The surface judging did not widen the universe; it judged the surfaces the cluster allocation had already let in.

**C4. The record already contains a way through the completeness and preselection conflict, and the study half-uses it.** Collate by presence and decide relevance at the occurrence (programme prose, principle 4: a verse is in a word's corpus because the term is present, not because of an interpretation; B:120: the work unit is every verse holding an occurrence). The preselection conflict then moves to a single question: which surfaces are in the universe. One option the record supports: every surface of the ESV (18,254), with a closed list excluding function words and proper names, and relevance decided at the occurrence. The programme prose holds both stances without reconciling them: principle 4 collates by presence, while the traceability section admits a verse only where the term is used in a way that engages the inner being. I offer the option, not a design (B:105): it enlarges the reading volume, which is what the September pipeline's 36% no-finding observations show costs.

**C5. The completeness control was built twice and retired twice.** The registry's stage 3 (a check against the primary lexica) and `lemma_inventory` with the candidate net (July) were the study's independent completeness checks; both are gone, and no replacement landed (BUILD 151). The universe in C3 is not now checked against anything independent.

**C6. The definition already contained the context principle; the watershed may have been applying it at the wrong grain (inference).** The programme prose's own definition says weeping qualifies when it is Scripture's language for inner grief and walking does not qualify when it is physical locomotion, so occurrence decided from the start. The definition was written as "a filter that could be applied to any English word" and was applied to words, in the registry and later in the surface groups. If that reading is right, the repeated failures are one failure: a test meant for occurrences applied to words.

**C7. Two statements of what the surface groups are for are in the record, and the November failure suggests they have not been reconciled.** #2509 v2 (approved) says the groups are judged from the dictionary meaning. The researcher's account of today says the collation on surfaces serves "re-reading the context of how characteristics interact". The researcher's verdict of 2026-11-15 ("you simply changed the meaning of the underlying words ... will drive any analysis in the wrong direction", "you have not even considered ... what the groups are being used for") fits a group made to match a method and not to serve the interaction reading. What the groups feed into stage 3 and 4 is not written down in anything I read.

**C8. Nothing yet tests the layered design at the point where earlier designs failed.** The 08-03 failure was consistency across many verses by a method specified in documents. No collection has completed a stage of the layered reading, so the design has not met that test.

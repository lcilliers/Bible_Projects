## Points for the researcher to decide

1. **Humility has no collection.** Its content has no sub-chapter in the new list. Options: (a) it waits until a collection exists, with its blocks held in the register; (b) it is written inside pride (10.39) as the opposite; (c) a humility collection is built first.
2. **Heavy collections.** `saying` has 7,954 rows over 5,738 verses, many times the next largest; `share`, `live`, `know` and `bless` are also large. Whether `saying` is one sub-chapter or is read in parts is yours to say.
3. **Collection names are the headings.** They are the base words (`anger`, `ask`, ...). Whether the sub-chapter titles stay lowercase base words or take a full name (for example "Anger") is open. Rule 100 asks for names, not bare codes.
4. **Existing text.** The blocks now in 10.1-10.15 are moved, not deleted, into the sub-chapters they bear on, with their claims and sources re-linked (rules 77, 78). That is a build of its own, to be scoped after this outline is settled.
5. **Status.** All 58 collections are `indicative`, not confirmed. The chapters would be drafts until the collections are.

## What approval means

Approval of this outline would set only the shape of chapter 10 (and the answers to points 1-3). It does not change the register or move any text. Those changes go through `Narrative-Register.ps1` (AddChapter, EditChapter) in a later, separately approved step (rule 78).

## 1. What stage 3 is for

Stage 3 records the **words' context**: what the other words of the verse, and the verse's stored structure, show with each group of surfaces. It answers "what do the words used say here", and it is checkable against the text. It never states what a word does for the inner being, and carries no inner-being role or route (B:132, B:133). It is the evidence base for stage 4, which reads the working.

This is the first of the two contexts the researcher separated (the words used; the inner being's working). Keeping stage 3 free of the working is what lets stage 4 be judged on its own.

## 4. CANDIDATE additions to stage 3, for decision

All are facts of the text, checkable, and say nothing of the inner being. None is decided.

1. **The grammatical form of each word in the verse** (the verb's stem, tense and voice; the noun's number and construct state), taken from the stored morphology. Reason: stage 4 must tell an act from a state from a movement, and the form is the first evidence. The June D4 dimension and the 16 per-span dimensions (lineage L6, L7) held it.
2. **The grammatical function of each word in its clause** (subject, object, complement, adverbial), from the stored structure. Reason: stage 4 reads the source and the target of a working; the structure gives the grammatical ones.
3. **The rendering check**: where the ESV rendering adds or hides something the original has (by-role guide §4.6a). Reason: a stage 4 read must not work from an English word that is not the original's.
4. **The passage and genre of each verse**, stated as facts (not read for meaning). Reason: stage 4 reads in context; the context is then to hand.
5. **A rule that stage 3 closes before stage 4 opens**, matching B:133's rule for stages 1 and 2, with the same check.

Open for the researcher: how far the verse's own words reach before the passage must be read is unsettled in the record (the passage rule of 10 June and the verse-bounded rule of 26 June differ). Stage 3 as defined is verse-bounded; item 4 adds the passage as fact only.

## 5. What stage 3 hands to stage 4

For every verse of a group: the group's meaning and codes; the words that stand with it, with their side and distance; the grammatical qualifiers and the word each reaches; the entities named; the stated direction; the faces. In the candidate form, also the grammatical form and function of each word and the passage and genre.

Stage 4 reads these as evidence and does not re-do them. A stage 4 reading that finds a stage 3 observation wrong does not correct it in place: it returns the case to stage 3 (the reclassify and move rules of B:134 already name this route for a misplaced word).

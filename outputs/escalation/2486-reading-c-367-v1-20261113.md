**Gap 1: the cited verses carry only the "plans" half.** Psa 140:2 and Pro 16:9 say the heart plans. The "thoughts" half of the claim rests on Mar 7:21, Mat 15:19, Luk 9:47 and Isa 10:7, which the observation applies but the claim does not cite. Either the claim cites them, or the claim is cut to the plans half.

**Gap 2: the named observation is the claim's own statement.** "The heart is where thoughts come from" says how the inner being works, not only what the verses say. It is the same territory as the claim (the border, draft v2 section 2).

- Claim, observation and a verse-level statement can be told apart for this claim: the verse-level statements are in group A (17053 for Pro 16:9; 19997, 14322, 17054 and 16538 reach Psa 140:2); the claim's own wording is 12407 and 20481 (group B).
- On Psa 140:2 itself there is no plain verse-level statement: its notes are in groups A (cross-verse) and D (table rows and pointers). A plain observation of what Psa 140:2 says about the heart planning (evil, and wars) may be missing.
- Pro 16:9 has one plain statement (17053), and its second half (the Lord establishes his steps) belongs to the outcome claim, not to this one.
- Groups C and D are on the same verses but are not evidence for C-367. Of the 29, about 6 to 7 bear on it.

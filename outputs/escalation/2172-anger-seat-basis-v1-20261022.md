**How the basis was set.** Each row's verses were read in the ESV text in iba.db (`verse`), with the
anger strand's words in each verse (`reading_strand_strong`, strand anger, one set-based query).
- **stated:** every verse the row cites says what the row's head says, with anger in the verse.
- **read:** the head is Claude's grouping or reading. At least one verse does not say it, or the
  anger is only in a neighbouring verse the row does not cite.

Where a row is both, it is **read**, as catch-up B did for an S/R row.

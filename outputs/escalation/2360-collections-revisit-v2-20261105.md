## 3. Members I doubt (my reading)

- love: wholehearted. forgive: merciful, grace. pride: exalt. fear: daunt, anxiety. boast: proud. serve: provide.
- lament holds rejoice, exult, glad, joy, joyful, cheer, as confirmed opposites of the pole "rejoice"; they are opposites of lament, but the group now spans both poles, which you may want to split.
- endure holds abandon (confirmed opposite of endure through its pole).

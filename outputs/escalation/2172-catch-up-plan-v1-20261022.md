## 2. The steps

**B. October ledgers into the tables (one-off catch-up script, registered like a migration,
dry run first).**
1. One strand per October strand (`Strand-Reading.ps1 -Step Define`): its words from the unit pull
   CSVs, its home cluster and its October escalation. Then `Context` writes its hits.
2. Each ledger row becomes one observation through `recordingpass.record_strand_observations`:
   the bold head is the text, the parts go to their columns, S/R becomes `stated`/`read`, and each
   verse is grounded on the strand words in it. The ledger ID is kept as
   `stable_key` (`<strand>:<ID>`). The October disposition is kept in `revisit_note`; the status
   stays `draft` until step C. Every quotation is checked.
3. A face from a pull or faces CSV goes onto its hit.
4. A row that does not parse cleanly, or does not resolve, is listed in the dry-run report for me to
   fix in the import file, never guessed. Nothing is written until the dry run is clean.

**C. Existing claims linked to observations (Derive, strand by strand).** For each claim resting on
a strand's verses, the observations on the same verses are set side by side. I confirm a link only
where they mean the same thing; a shared verse alone never makes a link. A confirmed link gives the
claim the observation as a source and disposes the observation as carried (`resolved`). The rest
stay `draft`, and the account shows them.

**D. The account,** per strand: unread hits, undisposed observations, claims told nowhere, and
claims with no observation. It is written as text, for your judgement, and then each gap goes to
an escalation.

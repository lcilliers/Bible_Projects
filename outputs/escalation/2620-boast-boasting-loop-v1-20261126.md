## Step 1.1: the five senses

1. Boasting: speaking with pride of a ground one claims.
2. The ground of the boast: the thing gloried in.
3. Magnifying oneself over another.
4. Boastful talk: words of the mouth named as boasts.
5. Glorying in God or in what he gives.

## Step 3.1: the eighteen elements (inner being, each traced to its evidence)

- **f1** (operation, done): The one who boasts holds up a ground as his own and speaks of it: his wisdom, might and riches (Jer 9:23), trusted wealth (Psa 49:6), his own work set on the same terms as another's (2Cor 11:12), men (1Cor 3:21), a gift he never gives (Pro 25:14). The act is a claim of standing made from what one has or does.
- **f2** (operation, negated): God's word shuts the boast out: the Lord forbids it over wisdom, might and riches (Jer 9:23) and before the outcome (1Ki 20:11); no flesh may boast before him (1Cor 1:29); the boast is excluded by the law of faith, not by works (Rom 3:27, Eph 2:9); no one is to boast in men (1Cor 3:21); love does not boast (1Cor 13:4).
- **f3** (object): The ground of the boast can be a person or a state held before God rather than a possession: the church as Paul's crown at the Lord's coming (1Th 2:19, 2Cor 1:14), the testimony of conscience to conduct by grace (2Cor 1:12), the reason found in one's own tested work (Gal 6:4), the hope held fast with confidence (Heb 3:6), a ground Paul would rather die than lose, which preaching under necessity does not give (1Cor 9:15, 1Cor 9:16).
- **f4** (object): The boast is directed to the Lord as its ground: in God continually, with thanks (Psa 44:8), the soul's boast in the Lord that the humble hear and are glad at (Psa 34:2), understanding and knowing the Lord who practises steadfast love, justice and righteousness (Jer 9:24), in the Lord (1Cor 1:31, 2Cor 10:17), and only in the cross (Gal 6:14). God is the one boasted in, never the one who boasts.
- **f5** (polarity, pursuit): The same act is refused or pursued by its ground: forbidden in wisdom, might and riches yet commanded in knowing the Lord (Jer 9:23 against Jer 9:24), excluded before God yet commanded in the Lord (1Cor 1:29 against 1Cor 1:31), and the lowly told to boast in a reversal (Jam 1:9). The boast is turned, not removed; the codes carry the turn as boast against rejoice or glory.
- **f6** (party, route B): The boast is made over another who is weaker, fallen or the giver: Moab and Ammon over God's people and land, with taunts (Zep 2:8, Zep 2:10), enemies over a man whose foot slips, with rejoicing (Psa 38:16), Edom over Judah in its day of distress (Obd 12), the boastful beside the wicked lifting the horn (Psa 75:4), and over God himself, crediting one's own hand (Judg 7:2), as the axe over the one who hews (Isa 10:15).
- **f7** (party, route B): The boast can be made about others and held on their keeping: Paul boasts of a church's readiness and zeal to another (2Cor 9:2), of the Thessalonians' steadfastness and faith in affliction to the churches (2Th 1:4), sends brothers so it is not empty (2Cor 9:3), asks for proof of their love and his boast (2Cor 8:24), and holds a mutual boast for the day of the Lord (2Cor 1:14). Its truth rests on their conduct (2Cor 7:14).
- **f8** (enactment): The boast comes out as speech and words: arrogant words poured out (Psa 94:4), the mouth speaking swollen things (Jude 16), the tongue boasting of great things (Jam 3:5), sayings approved after the foolish (Psa 49:13), boasts the Lord declares false (Jer 48:30), and the boast of a gift never given (Pro 25:14).
- **f9** (role): Truth and shame qualify whether the boast holds: boasts false and false to the truth (Jer 48:30, Jam 3:14), boasting not good (1Cor 5:6), a boast that proved true and was not put to shame (2Cor 7:14), boasting bound to the truth of Christ (2Cor 11:10), the law boasted in while broken, dishonouring God (Rom 2:23), and shame on those whose boast was idols or Egypt (Psa 97:7, Isa 20:5).
- **f10** (role): Measure qualifies Paul's own boast: a little (2Cor 11:16), not beyond the area God assigned nor in others' labours (2Cor 10:13, 2Cor 10:15), of authority given for building up, not destroying (2Cor 10:8), and held back so that no one thinks more of him than is seen (2Cor 12:6). The limit is set by what was assigned and what others see.
- **f11** (circumstance): Paul's boast is made under necessity and of weakness: he must boast (2Cor 11:30, 2Cor 12:1), with nothing gained by it (2Cor 12:1), dares as a fool though too weak (2Cor 11:21), and boasts all the more gladly of his weaknesses after the word that grace suffices and power is made perfect in weakness (2Cor 12:9).
- **f12** (chain, route B): What God gives comes first and the boast follows from it: grace (2Cor 1:12), authority the Lord gave (2Cor 10:8), the area God assigned (2Cor 10:13), the exaltation of the lowly (Jam 1:9), the people called priests who come into the nations' glory (Isa 61:6). The boast as though one had not received is questioned (1Cor 4:7).
- **f13** (role): The heart's condition qualifies the boast: where bitter jealousy and selfish ambition are in the heart the boast is forbidden and joined to being false to the truth (Jam 3:14), set against the meekness of wisdom in the verse before.
- **f14** (party, route B): Those who boast are named the wicked, the evildoers and the faithless, and the boast stands beside the desires of the soul, greed that renounces the Lord, and favouritism for gain: the wicked boasts of his soul's desires (Psa 10:3), the evildoers boast (Psa 94:4), the mighty man boasts of evil (Psa 52:1), the faithless daughter boasts (Jer 49:4), boasters follow their desires (Jude 16).
- **f15** (object): The boast can rest on the flesh and the outward appearance rather than the heart: those who boast of appearance and not of the heart (2Cor 5:12), the many who boast according to the flesh (2Cor 11:18), and those who would boast in others' circumcised flesh while not keeping the law themselves (Gal 6:13).
- **f16** (chain, route C): Trust and boast are linked on the same ground: trust in wealth and boast in riches (Psa 49:6), boast of valleys and trust in treasures with no one to come against her (Jer 49:4).
- **f17** (circumstance): The boast is made before the outcome is known: as one who arms before the battle is over (1Ki 20:11), about tomorrow which no one knows (Pro 27:1), and of plans in arrogance (Jam 4:16).
- **f18** (flag, data-quality): In Psa 10:3 a second stored occurrence of the surface boasts sits on the conjunction for (H3588A); it carries no part of the boast, and its tagging is a data-quality question for the base data.

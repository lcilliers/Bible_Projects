## What the verses hold (summary of the context file)

- **The will at work in giving (most of the 22 H6942H verses):**
  - a person dedicating what is his own, measured and redeemable (Lev 27:14-22);
  - what cannot be given because it is already the Lord's (Lev 27:26), and the firstborn commanded beside a
    remembered redemption (Exo 13:2; Deu 15:19);
  - dedication said in the Lord's name to make an idol (Judg 17:3);
  - kings and leaders giving the spoil of victory (2Sa 8:11; 1Ch 18:11; 1Ch 26:26-28);
  - a dedication undone to buy off a threat (2Ki 12:18);
  - the house dedicated inside a purpose and a humbling question (2Ch 2:4, 2:1, 2:6);
  - giving in abundance and with joy (2Ch 31:6; Neh 12:47);
  - God's dedicating of Jeremiah before birth, and his shrinking answer (Jer 1:5-8).
- **The self given or withdrawn (three of the five H5144A verses):**
  - *"consecrated themselves to the thing of shame, and became detestable like the thing they loved"* (Hos 9:10);
  - *"separates himself from me, taking his idols into his heart"* (Eze 14:7);
  - abstaining, judged by its aim: *"was it for me that you fasted?"* (Zec 7:3, 7:5).
- **Holiness rules with little or no inner act:** Lev 22:2, Lev 15:31 (both H5144A) and 1Ch 23:13 (an office).
- **No will word in the Hebrew of Lev 27.** The ESV's "wishes" there renders "if" with *gāʾal*, redeem (checked in
  `verse_lexical`).

## 5. Nuances met in the redo with no row in `reading.nuance_pointers`

The plan says a nuance met with no row gets a row in the same session. A row is a config change (`governance.rules_must_be_config_driven`), which a standard session does not make without approval; the proposal is raised as its own item. Proposed rows (nuance, item, form, basis, rule, verse):

1. A strengthening word, an infinitive absolute or a cognate noun, bound to the finite verb: role, qualification with a qualifier node on the strengthening word, stated, RG-04, Gen 43:7, Deu 13:14, Judg 8:24.
2. The causative stem of the asking verb is a granting: meaning, face, stated, RG-08, RG-14, Exo 12:36.
3. The Hebrew mouth rendered as word, command, counsel or hidden in 'her': extend the idiom row (RG-08, `lexical.span_surface`) with Gen 24:57, Num 27:21, Jos 19:50.
4. The same sentence in two verses: the later verse names the live observations as existing and gains the nodes, RW-03, Judg 1:14 with Jos 15:18.
5. A question put back where the answer or the name would be: polarity, statement, basis read with grounds, RW-08, Gen 32:29, Judg 13:18.
6. What is asked of is not a person (days, the span of heaven): party, route B with the nodes the verse gives, RG-06, Deu 4:32.
7. Several verbs under one question or one requirement: chain, read link with nodes on each characteristic, RW-08, RG-15, Deu 10:12, Deu 13:14, Deu 32:7.
8. A request relayed through another (urged to ask, asked to inquire): the urging is its own characteristic, operation, RG-02, Jos 15:18, Judg 18:5.

## 2. Draft B:119: a word enters a scope through its surface and its collection

> A word enters a scope through its surface (`verse_lexical.surface`) and the collection of that surface (its own link, else its base), never through `word_strong` and never through a cluster or subgroup.

What changes: "the subgroups of that surface" becomes "the collection of that surface". The surface-not-`word_strong` part stands as it is.

## 3. Draft B:120: scope home is the collection

> A work unit is a collection's verses (the verses holding an occurrence of one of its member surfaces) or a subset of them, and that collection is the home until all its verses have been read. The other collections a verse holds are read in that verse and never add verses to the scope. A word that only qualifies, or that has no inner-being role of its own, is not analysed or described by itself: it is bound to the characteristic it qualifies, or excluded; a word found wrongly placed in a collection is moved to entity or other by the collection change procedure of B:129, and that move is processed.

What changes:
- The unit is the collection, not the subgroup. The verse set is the one B:130 already defines for the scope.
- "Where one Strong's code falls in several subgroups, every one of them is read" is dropped: under B:129 a surface is in one collection, and the Strong's code is not the unit.
- The qualifier clause is kept and restated as your point (e): never read alone, bound to its partner or excluded; a wrong placement is a processed move so completeness tests hold.
- Dropped as cluster-based: the qualifier group and T cluster as non-units, and the exceptional raising of a T code to M status. Clusters and subgroups no longer start or ready a reading.
- Dropped for decision (below): the later second reading that synergises all the M codes of a verse.

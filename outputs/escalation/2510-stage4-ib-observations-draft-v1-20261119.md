## 1. What stage 4 is for

Stage 4 reads the inner being's working in the verses of a collection, from the stage 3 observations and the surface groups of the behaviour and inner_being_state kinds, and records it as observations. It answers: what is the inner being doing, in what state is it, what is happening to it here, and how do its characteristics interact. It is interpretive and varies by occurrence, and it is the second of the two contexts (the working's context). It is judged by the researcher's test: is the outcome sensible, reasonable and defendable.

It does not read the pattern across verses, the cohabitation of workings, the common root or the emergence of a characteristic. Those are discovered in stage 5, the claims, from what stage 4 has recorded.

## 2. The unit

A **working**: a relation among words in a verse (or, when the text requires it, a passage), not a word. This keeps the existing rule that a finding is one description applied by its nodes (`cfg_setting reading.observation_parts`, #2273), so a working is one observation whose nodes are the occurrences it relates, each with a role. The existing model therefore allows the grain; what it lacks is parts to say what kind of working it is.

## 3. What a stage 4 observation records

From the shape of a working (lineage section 3). Each part is recorded where the verse gives it, each with its basis (stated, or read, B:4). A part the verse does not give is recorded as none (silence is a valid result), not imputed.

| Part | Today's home (`reading.observation_items`, `reading.node_roles`) | Status |

| The characteristic | Node role *characteristic* | Exists |

The mode and polarity items, the node roles and the nuance rules (RG-07 and the others in `reading.nuance_pointers`) were built under Analysis_phase_1 (lineage L10) and are configured, not in use. They are candidates to carry over, not decisions.

## 4. A read triggers interventions

The researcher: the working is not one fixed thing; what a read finds decides what to do next; the interventions are defined and not carried out. The record defines them (working analysis v3, section 4). Stage 4 would carry them as steps of the read, each recorded when taken:

| What the read finds | The intervention (source) | Recorded as |
|---|---|---|
| The verse does not carry the working | Read beyond the verse: the passage, genre, speaker (by-role guide §4.1; B:116) | The context part; basis *read*; marked "I read" |
| The question does not fit the verse | Do not force a meaning; read outward from the verse, not inward from the question (§4.0; RG-17) | A recorded none with its reason |
| The word is wrongly placed or works otherwise here | Reclassify: role from this verse, never from the cluster; move by the change procedure (§4.2; B:89, B:134) | A returned case to stage 2 or 3, with the reason |
| The English may hide or add | Check the original: the root's range, the rendering (§4.6a) | A stage 3 observation (the rendering check) the read depends on |
| The role label seems to close the verse | Never close on "not X"; record what the verse is and where each significance goes (§4.9) | The significances, with their basis |
| A discovery outside the verse | Record a pointer, separately (§2, §4.4) | The flag, type discovery |

## 5. The order within a verse (candidate)

The Passage read guidance's three phases (lineage L8) fit B:133's separation of processes and answer the drift found on 2 August: (1) isolate the working's bearer and the behaviour in every verse of the batch and say why, before any structure is written; (2) then record the form, qualifiers, source, object, effect and link of each; (3) then validate: is each working genuine, is its warrant sound, does each part track back to it. A structural or literary pattern is not a working. CANDIDATE.

## 6. The batch, and the researcher's test

The record shows the batch changes the result (the Amos run of 2 August, the passage-complete read of 14 July) and the by-role guide §5.6 requires a trial on a few verses checked before the whole set. CANDIDATE:

1. A batch is a few verses of one collection (the researcher fixes the number), all surface groups of the collection read in each.
2. Claude reads the evidence and decides; the researcher scrutinises the batch against the test, and what the evidence cannot settle goes to the researcher with that evidence (B:127).
3. A failed batch does not replace the method: the failure is recorded with the intervention it should have triggered. Where an intervention recurs it becomes a rule of the read. The how (what to check, how to stay consistent, where to read) is built from these recorded cases and not from a prior design.
4. The first batches are on `thought`, where stages 1 to 3 have been opened.

## 7. What stage 4 hands to stage 5

Per working: the bearer, the form, the qualifiers, the source, object and effect, the link, the expression and the context, each with its basis, tied to its verses by nodes; and the recorded failures and interventions. With these the claims can discover the questions the lineage section 5 left open: how workings are grained and linked, how states and transitions behave, whether cohabitation and the common root belong to the working, and which guides are in force.

How the claims are assembled is not yet clear (the researcher, 2026-11-19), and this draft does not decide it.

## How each reading runs

For each batch, with this task as the anchor and M64 as home:

1. `Reading.ps1 -Step Open -Label <label> -Escalation 2247 -Home M64 -Item OT-108.3 -OnlyStrong <batch codes>`:
   keeps the verses, collates them and writes the gap JSON.
2. **Record (gate 1):** for each gap, read the verse's ESV text with its lines in the cluster files,
   and write an observation, a face on a hit, an existing row, or a reason. Then `Check` (gate 1).
3. `Synergy`: built from the tables only (rule B:114).
4. **Apply (gate 2):** claims, observation dispositions, tellings and blocks rewritten, and every
   matched open item settled. Then `Check` (gate 2).
5. `Publish` (gate 3): the touched chapters exported and copied to learning4comfort.

A session may break between Record and Synergy (B:114).

## Decisions

**1. Scope and batching**
- **(a) recommended:** four readings, batches A, B, C, D, in that order. Each is one session's work
  or less, and A is the main subject of the 13 files.

  before gate 1 can pass.
- (c) only the 309 verses the 13 files read. The 159 M64 verses outside them stay unread, so
  OT-108.3 could not be closed as "every M64 verse read".

**3. How OT-108.3 settles while batches remain.** Gate 2 needs every matched open item settled.
In batches A to C, OT-108.3 is settled `forward` to a new item naming the remaining batches' verses

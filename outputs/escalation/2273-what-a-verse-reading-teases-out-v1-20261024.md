## 5. Synthesis: what a reading teases out of a verse

Seven kinds of finding. Each is something the verse is read for. The catalogue rows are its prompts (RG-17): a
prompt that does not apply is not answered.

| # | Kind | What the reader finds | Example from the ledgers | Prompts |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| K1 | **Face** | the sense the word carries here, by what differs from its other occurrences: object, party, direction, setting; its form; where it sits in the word's range | fear F "the word describes the one or thing feared" | M0.1, M0.4, M0.5.1 to .4, .11; RG-08, RG-09, RG-14 |
| K2 | **Party** | who bears the act, who causes it, toward whom, for whom, who else acts (God, a person, another being, the created world); where it comes from; movement to or from God; the speaker | "God is angry with a named person" (anger B); "evil spirit acting towards the inner being" | D7.1 to D7.7, D5.2, D7.5, M0.7.9, M0.7.11 to .16; RG-06 |
| K3 | **Statement** | what the verse shows the inner being doing or undergoing: the main operation, felt quality, mode, what sets it off and what follows, where it sits | FE-02 "Fear leads to hiding"; FE-01 "felt in the heart and the body together" | D1.1.1, D2.1.1, D3.1.1, D6.1.1, D10.1.1 to D10.6.1, M0.7.1 to .8, F0.1.1; RG-01, RG-03, RG-07, RG-11, RG-13 |
| K4 | **Link** | a link the verse states between words or characteristics: one leading to another, set against, named together, the same root as another | "heard → anger → fire" (Num 11:1); "A soft answer turns away wrath, but …" | M0.6.5, M0.6.6, D10.5, M0.7.5; RG-04, RG-05 |
| K5 | **Use** | how the words are used: the speaker's purpose; the same phrase to different ends; one word, two senses in one verse | AG-32 "slow to anger" as name, plea, threat, complaint; Pro 30:33 "the nose … anger" | M0.6.1; RG-02, RG-12 |
| K6 | **Term** | the term's meaning in context against its lexicon entries, its surface variants, its related words (opposite, person-type, seeking term), testament and coinage | anger §L.1 the other senses of *ʾaph* | M0.1.2, M0.5.5 to .9 (D1, word level) |
| K7 | **Silence** | a word with no inner-being bearing here (a name, a homonym, a superscription), recorded once as data with its reason | life-death face I "boiling pot, raw flesh" | RG-10, RG-16 |

For each finding, the reader also records:
- **its basis**: stated, or "I read" (RG-08, RG-11, RG-15);
- **the role of each word in it**: characteristic, qualifier (with what it gives), or standalone (RG-03, RG-04);
- **where the verse ends up for it**: told, claim, held, data, context, withdrawn (R5).

Not teased out of one verse: the factors that change a characteristic's character across its faces, and the
catalogue's 59 "across the verses" questions. These are findings about other findings, made at the
characteristic level (D2) and in the claims step.

## 6. Which element of the observation or the node captures each

The test (researcher, 2026-10-24): the observation carries the distinct description; the node applies it to its
classification. A thing that is the same wherever the description applies belongs to the observation. A thing that
can differ from one application to the next belongs to the node.

| What is captured | Same for every application of the description? | Captured by |
|---|---|---|
| K1 to K7: the finding's text | yes: it is the description | `ib_observation.obs_text` |
| its meaning and implication | yes | `ib_observation.meaning`, `.implication` |
| its kind (K1 to K7) | yes | `ib_observation.tag` |
| its basis (stated or read) | yes: a pairing read across stated verses is its own finding (RG-15), with its own basis | `ib_observation.basis` |
| the route (A other beings in their own right, B another being with the inner being, C no bearing) | yes: it follows from the party described | `ib_observation`, on a K2 finding (D3 in design v4) |
| the verse it applies to | no | `ib_node.verse_reference` |
| the word it applies to: strong, surface, morph | no | `ib_node.strong`, `.surface`, `.morph_code` |
| the word's own classification: M-code or T-code, subgroup | no | `ib_node.cluster_code` (the word's own), `.cluster_subgroup_code` |
| the role of the word in this finding | no: the same word can be the characteristic in one finding and a qualifier in another | `ib_node.role` (new) |
| the seat (heart, spirit, soul, mind, body part) | no | a node on the seat word, classified by its own cluster (M47 or the body-part tag); no column |
| a finding about another finding (a factor across faces; a pairing) | no | `ib_node.traced_observation_id` (exists) |
| the catalogue prompt that led to it | no | `ib_node.question_code` (exists), optional |
| the reading that applied it (the scope's home) | no | `ib_node.reading_id` (new) |
| where the verse ends up for this finding | no (AG-05: one row, two dispositions) | `ib_node.disposition`, `.disposition_note` (new) |
| the setting of the passage (speaker, genre, verses around) | the speaker is a party (K2); the rest is part of the statement's meaning | K2, or `meaning`; no column (D4 in design v4) |

A term reading (K6) about the word in general is a node with a strong and no verse. `ib_node` already allows it.

What this removes from the earlier designs: no face, operation, parties or setting columns; no party table. The
face, the operation (K3) and the parties are findings, each written once and applied by its nodes.

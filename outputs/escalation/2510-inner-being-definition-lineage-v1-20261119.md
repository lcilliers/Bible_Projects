# #2510 How the definition of the inner being's working grew, and what it says about how the inner being works (v1, 2026-11-19)

Status: analysis by Claude, for the researcher to correct. Prompted by the researcher's message of 2026-11-19 (recorded on #2510 v13): start with the definition in the programme prose, and work through the dimensions, the catalogues, the definition of what to look for and the configs that set the rules for reading, since all of these explore how the inner being works; analyse how the definitions were expanded over time and encapsulate from that how the inner being works; and the point that behaviour is not only acts or operations but also states and conditions (sin, righteousness, life, death and many more), and that it has qualifiers that define it. Statements of cause or meaning are inference (B:4). Nothing here is a design or a build (B:105).

How to read it: section 1 follows the definition stage by stage (what each stage defined, what it added, where it put the focus, what it taught). Section 2 takes each element of the working and follows it across the stages, to see which survived into the current process and which did not. Section 3 states, from that, how the inner being works as the record supports it. Section 4 is my critical reading. Section 5 lists what is still not known.

## 0. A correction to my own earlier work

In the working analysis v2 (`outputs/escalation/archive/2510-inner-being-working-v2-20261119.md`, section 5.1) I listed the parts of a working as "what acts, what it does, what it acts on". That read the working as acts and operations only. The record does not support it. The programme's own definition opens with "states, capacities, and expressions" (section 1, L1), the Passage read guidance defines the operation as "the state of, or happening in, or impacting on, or being impacted by" the inner being (L8), and the researcher's glossary entry says a characteristic "is not a thing, it is likely to be something like a process, state, movement" (L11). States were in the definition from the start. What narrowed was the instruments (section 4, point 1).

## 1. The definition, stage by stage

Each stage gives the source, what it defined, the elements it added, its focus, what it taught and what it left open. Dates are those of the record.

### L1. The working definition of the programme (March to April; programme prose, area 1)

Source: the programme prose, the section "the definition" (`database/bible_research.db`, `prose_section`; extract `Workflow/Programme/programme_prose/wa-programme-prose-extract-20261119.md`).

- **What it defined:** "Inner-being characteristics are the non-physical, internal states, capacities, and expressions that constitute a person's invisible life, encompassing how a person thinks, feels, chooses, relates, and orients themselves toward meaning, others, and God."
- **Elements it added:** three kinds of content (states, capacities, expressions); five operational dimensions (think, feel, choose, relate, orient), "not five sealed domains but five dimensions of a single integrated life"; the non-physical test (a bodily act counts only when it expresses an internal state: weeping for grief counts, walking does not); the internal test, with permeability (inner states can be caused from outside, by God, other people or spiritual forces); spirit and soul held together, their boundary an open research question; borderline words included.
- **Focus:** a filter for deciding whether a word belongs in scope. The prose says so: "an evaluative filter ... a reliable yes-or-no".
- **What it taught:** the subject is states, capacities and expressions together, and the outside can reach in.
- **Left open:** it does not say how a working is read or recorded. It is a definition of what qualifies, not of how the inner being works.

### L2. The per-word tier framework and the standing question catalogue (March to April)

Source: the method area of the programme prose; the catalogue as it stands in `iba/app/db/iba.db`, table `wa_obs_question_catalogue` (tier names read 2026-11-19).

- **What it defined:** each registry word is worked through a standing catalogue of questions, grouped in tiers: T0 divine image and created design; T1 definition; T2 constitutional location and boundaries; T4 relational interfaces; T5 formative and developmental dimension; T6 structural relationships with other characteristics; T7 evidential and methodological foundation.
- **Elements it added:** the characteristic as a thing with attributes: its definition, its place in the person (seat, boundary), its relations (to God, to others, to other characteristics), how it forms and develops, what evidence supports it.
- **Focus:** the word, then the characteristic behind it, as the object described.
- **What it taught:** several of the elements recur in every later stage: the relation to God, the place in the person, the relations among characteristics, formation over time.
- **Left open:** the question set was fixed before the verses were read. The Fellowship run of 17 April exposed nine defects and the cluster model replaced the per-word unit.

### L3. Dimensions found by reading the verse groups (April to 4 May)

Source: `Workflow/Instructions/archive/wa-dimensionreview-instruction-v3_3-20260418.md`; `Workflow/Programme/programme_analysis/archive/wa-dimension-report-20260407.md`.

- **What it defined:** the domains of the inner life that the verse groups engage. The 7 April report lists them as, among others, Spiritual/God-ward, Moral/Conscience, Affective/Emotional, Theological/Divine-Human, Character/Disposition, Cognitive/Mind, Relational/Social, Volitional/Will, Sin and Vice. The vocabulary grew from ten to eleven (Divine-Human Correspondence was added from the C18 data).
- **Elements it added:** the principle "the dimension always follows the verse" (never preset); the idea that a group of verses has a dominant subject.
- **Focus:** classifying verse groups by domain.
- **What it taught:** a domain label does not describe a working. The labels were assigned to groups, and 40 percent of groups were unclassified at one point.
- **Ended:** dropped on 4 May as "noise, built on wrong assumptions" (project reconstruction `outputs/markdown/project-reconstruction/01-project-status-reconstruction-20260614.md`).

### L4. The characteristic as the unit, and the verse read for its meaning (May to 11 June)

Source: project reconstruction 01 and 02 (`outputs/markdown/project-reconstruction/`).

- **What it defined:** the characteristic, not the word and not the dimension, as the unit under a cluster; each verse read for what it says of the characteristic (about 14 tier findings per term per verse).
- **Elements it added:** the cluster as a working collection; the verse as evidence for a characteristic.
- **Focus:** the characteristic as an object to be filled in.
- **What it taught:** on 10 to 11 June the reconstruction found that the meaning paragraph duplicated the dimensions and then fabricated. Reading for fixed fields invited the fixed fields to be filled.

### L5. The reset to what the word does (19 to 25 June)

Source: `Workflow/Instructions/archive/wa-lexical-analysis-rules-reset-v1-20260624.md`.

- **What it defined:** a change of question. The reset says Hebrew "is concrete, verbal, relational: it shows the heart doing, rarely defines what the heart is", so the question became "what does this word do in the verse?" The primary record became the considerations: antecedent or cause; operation (how it operates, the governing predicate); object or target (and what kind of object, observed); manner and intensity; effect (what it yields); immediate response; transition (what it turns into, the state set, removed, reversed or transformed); relational web or binding (partner, object, cause, manner, expresses, seat, pole-opposite); direction; adjacency (no verse read in isolation).
- **Elements it added:** the movement (antecedent, operation, effect, transition); the binding of a word to the words it works with; adjacency; observed references (the faculty or seat addressed, as presence only; the valence the verse gives); the discovery lookout; the principles that measurement informs but never decides, and a thing attested once exists (the singleton rule); three states per consideration (resolved, silent, unresolved).
- **Focus:** the word's function and relations, not its category.
- **What it taught:** the working is relational and moves; categories are outcomes, not inputs. This is the strongest single gain of the study.
- **Left open (inference):** it framed the working as doing. A state appears only as the thing a transition sets, removes or reverses (section 4, point 1).

### L6. The operation split into dimensions, and the verse-first lexical (29 June to 3 July)

Source: `Workflow/Catalogue/wa-IB-verse-dimensions-catalogue-v2-20260629.md`; `Workflow/Instructions/archive/wa-verse-analysis-method-v1-20260702.md`.

- **What it defined:** an inner-being operation swept across thirteen dimensions: D1 identity (the act or state itself), D2 source or antecedent, D3 seat or bearer, D4 operation or action (with its grammatical stem), D5 object or target, D6 manner (the qualifier), D7 process or movement, D8 impact or produced state, D9 coupling (what binds with it, in the verse), D10 colour or valence (only where stated), D11 discovery, D12 hidden meaning, D13 cohabitation (what other operations it keeps company with across the corpus, and what shared root that company reveals, read off the verses and not imposed).
- **Elements it added:** the bearer and the seat; the produced state; coupling within the verse against cohabitation across the corpus; hidden meaning; the verse as the unit, the passage as a consecutive run, genre-dependent reading (poetic books in two phases), reading back for sensibility as part of the process.
- **Focus:** a full decomposition of one operation in one verse.
- **What it taught:** the working has an inside (source, bearer, operation, object, manner, effect) and an outside (company across the corpus, a possible common root).
- **Left open:** the source rule could not tell a driver from a restraint (fear restrains ruthlessness; dread drives it). D13 and the story layer were never built beyond a seed.

### L7. The characteristic, candidate, role and lexical cycle (8 to 11 July)

Source: `Workflow/Instructions/archive/wa-characteristic-role-lexical-cycle-authoritative-v1-20260708.md`.

- **What it defined:** two axes. Axis A: is the lemma a candidate characteristic (corpus-wide, over-inclusive, a filter and not a verdict). Axis B: in this occurrence, what role does the span fill (per verse, read only). A characteristic is "an inner-being disposition, faculty or operation that the verse (in its passage) turns on". Roles: characteristic, qualifier, standalone, undecided. A word that "elaborates, qualifies, or names an object or source" is a qualifier: it gets no lexical of its own, and its application is carried by the characteristic's dimensions. Sixteen per-span dimensions (sense, type, source, seat, bearer, operation, target, manner, intensity, specifier, effect, coupling, prohibition, discovery, role, locus).
- **Elements it added:** the qualifier as a role; role as a per-occurrence fact that morphology cannot give ("meaning gives the characteristic and the role"); the same word a characteristic in one verse and a target in another ("he set his heart on wisdom").
- **Focus:** who is the characteristic in this verse, and what the others give it.
- **What it taught:** a characteristic is known in a verse, not by a list.
- **Left open:** the role of a word and the working it carries are not the same thing. A role is per word; a working is a relation among words.

### L8. The Passage read guidance (26 July to 7 August)

Source: `Workflow/Instructions/WA-passage-read-guidance-v1.5-2026-08-02.md`.

- **What it defined:** the aim of reading is "to describe the activity of the inner being, to capture the operations of different states and characteristics". An operation has a subject (the inner being in focus), the operation ("what is the state of, or happening in, or impacting on, or being impacted by the inner being"), the source (another operation, another human, a non-human being, a physical object or situation) and the target. Source and target may be singular, multiple, mixed or absent. Three phases in strict order: find the inner-being phenomenon in every verse and say why; then write each phenomenon's operation as "a state or status, or a movement (come from, go to, impact on, emerge, go away, become evident)"; then validate.
- **Elements it added:** state or status as a form of operation; the inner being as a party (several in one verse; each considered separately; an in-scope non-human being only in relation to a human); a collection as a bearer (tribe, nation); the phenomenon hidden behind an act or a refrained act; linkage or its absence between operations; the action-type label; the juxtaposition of a human operation with a divine one only where the text anchors it; a structural pattern is not a phenomenon.
- **Focus:** the inner being as the subject of operations, verse by verse.
- **What it taught:** the working is relational between parties, and often not stated in the verse. The 2 August Amos run drifted to patterns in the passage, and the three-phase separation was the corrective.
- **Left open:** the reading depended on the passage and on what the text only implies, and no later stage carried that over.

### L9. Dimensions derived from the goal, and the by-role guide (17 September to 19 October)

Source: `iba/docs/archive/1712-catalogue-first-principles-validation-method-v1-20260917.md`; `iba/app/BUILD.md` section 268; `Workflow/Instructions/wa-verse-reading-by-role-guide-v2-20261019.md`.

The by-role guide (11 and 19 October) reads each verse by the role of each word in that occurrence: characteristic; qualifier or process-element; standalone. Qualifiers give "a measure, a strengthening, a ground, a cause, an object, a setting, the bait".
- **Elements it added:** the science-derived dimensions; the qualifier's values; reading outward from the verse and not inward from an item's question; reading beyond the verse (passage, genre, speaker) and never closing on "not X"; marking a reading beyond what the verse states as "I read".

- **What it taught:** what to look for can be derived from the definition and not only from the verses. The study's own T-code clash (two numbering schemes sharing a prefix) was found at the same time.
- **Left open:** the science dimensions' data mechanism was confirmed but, as BUILD 268 states, not wired in; later status was not checked here.

## 3. How the inner being works, as the record supports it

This is the synthesis the researcher asked for. It uses only what the stages above define and the researcher has said, and it is written for correction. It does not decide how a working is to be recorded (B:105).

1. **The inner being is not a list of parts.** Scripture does not define what the heart is; it shows what the heart does, what it is in, and what happens to it (L5; the glossary, 13 November). A characteristic such as anger or love is known only by inference, from the collection of behaviours, processes, actions, movements and states that give it substance. It is discovered from the verses; it is not defined in advance (L11).
2. **A working has a bearer and a seat.** An individual, a collection (a tribe, a nation) or another party; the seat (heart, soul, spirit) is a place the text names, not a faculty the study assumes (L5, L6, L8).
3. **The behaviour takes three forms, and all three are inner-being content.** An **act or operation** (thinks, chooses, loves, hates); a **state or condition** (sin, righteousness, life, death, pride, humility, weakness: what the being is in, enters or leaves); a **movement or transition** (what the being becomes: a state set, removed, reversed or transformed; something that comes from, goes to, impacts or emerges). The definition names "states, capacities, and expressions" (L1); the Passage read guidance names "state or status, or a movement" (L8). States are not secondary: acts arise from states and acts set states, and the transition is how the two link (inference from L5 and L8).
4. **Qualifiers define the behaviour.** The same word is a characteristic in one verse and a qualifier in another (L7, L9). A qualifier gives the characteristic its value in place: a measure, a strengthening, a ground, a cause, an object, a setting, the bait (L9); manner and intensity (L5); the negator that makes an act a refusal (L10 RG-07); and the mode in which it is told (done, foreseen, commanded, negated, reported). A state is qualified too, as the researcher says; the by-role guide's values (grade, nature, measure, ground) are the record's own wording for it.
5. **A working stands in relations.** It has a source or antecedent (another operation, another human, God, a spiritual being, a physical situation) and a target; it has an effect or produced state; it is linked to other workings in a chain, or its link is absent, and the absence matters (L8); the party and the route show how the outside reaches the inside (permeability, L1, L9). Across the corpus it keeps company: workings cohabit, and the company may point to a common root that is read off the verses and not imposed (L6).
6. **It is expressed or hidden.** An outward act may express an inner state (weeping for grief); an act or a refrained act may hide the state behind it, and the reading must name both (L1, L8). A bodily act that expresses nothing inward is not in scope.
7. **It is read in its context, and the reading varies.** A word has a sense in place that may differ from its other occurrences (the face); a verse may carry a double or triple significance; the passage, the genre and the speaker decide what is said; some verses are silent, and silence is a valid result (L5, L9, L10). The same word may be an inner-being word in one verse and not in another, and a working may be carried by a word that is not an inner-being word (the act as the expression of a characteristic).
8. **Hence the shape of a working, as one statement:** *a bearer, in a state or an act or a movement, defined by its qualifiers, with a source, an object and an effect, linked to other workings, expressed outwardly or hidden behind an act, and read in the context of its passage; and a characteristic is what many such workings, gathered across the verses, give substance to.*

## 4. Critical reading (inference)

1. **The definition never excluded states; the instruments narrowed to acts.** L1 opens with states. L5 reasoned from a true observation (the biblical words are verbal and relational) to a frame in which the question is only "what does it do". The glossary entry *movement* still says it is "as opposed to a static trait". Every item in the Analysis_phase_1 set (L10) is worded as an act, and a state has no item. Stage 3 and the surface group carry the state only at the level of a word. Hypothesis to test: the 34 percent of node rows on `inner_being_state` words recorded as *standalone* may include states that the act-framed items had no place to record (read-only count of 2026-11-19; not yet examined row by row).
2. **The working was never given a place in the programme prose.** The definition (L1) is a filter for words. From what I read of the extract, no section states the parts of a working or says that behaviour includes states and has qualifiers. The prose therefore cannot orient a reader to how the study reads.
3. **The word "characteristic" is used at four levels.** The registry's admission filter (L1); the lemma as a candidate (L7, axis A); the role of a word on a node, per occurrence (L7, L10); the emergent pattern of behaviours across a collection (L11). The glossary has the entries but the study's documents mix the levels. Much of the confusion about "what a characteristic is" comes from this.
4. **Two numbering schemes both use D-codes.** In the June and July documents D2 is the source and D8 the effect (L6); in the catalogue of 17 September D2 is the affective dimension (L9). The T-codes clash was found and renumbered (BUILD 268); the D-codes clash is the same fault and was not named. An old document that says "D2" does not mean the catalogue's D2.
5. **Definitions were written for one stage and not handed on.** The Passage guidance defines states and the hidden phenomenon (L8); the layered reading does not cite it. The by-role guide defines the interventions (L9); the layered reading does not cite it. The Analysis_phase_1 items (L10) are configured and unused. The study's own diagnosis of 14 June (drift between decision and artefact) applies to the definition of the working: the definition lived in documents and in stage-specific rules, and was not carried into the next.
6. **The test the researcher names has never been the gate.** Every stage was checked for its structure and counts; the stages that failed were found by the researcher reading the outcome and asking whether it was sensible, reasonable and defendable (L3, L4, L5, L9, L11).

## 5. What is still not known

- Whether the working is recorded per word, per verse, or per collection (the grain), and how one working that spans several words is one record. The record stops at roles per word.
- How a state is recorded and checked: what the question is, what counts as evidence for a state in a verse, and how a state is told from a characteristic word that names it.
- How a transition between states is read and where its evidence lies, often outside the verse.
- Whether cohabitation and the common root belong to the working or to a later layer.
- Whether the by-role guide's interventions and the Passage guidance's phases are in force for stage 4.

These are the researcher's decisions. The proposal in `outputs/escalation/2510-inner-being-working-v3-20261119.md` section 7 is unchanged: pursue one definition, now the encapsulation in section 3 once the researcher has corrected it, on one collection in small batches, judged by the researcher's test.

So the stage 4 reading should record, where a verse gives them, the bearer, the behaviour in its form (act, state, movement), the qualifiers, the source, object and effect, the link, and the expression, each with its basis (stated or read, B:4), and should leave the pattern across verses to the claims. The section 5 questions then stay open on purpose and are answered from the recorded observations, not designed in advance. Nothing is designed or built (B:105); whether stage 4 is crafted this way, and how, is the researcher's decision.

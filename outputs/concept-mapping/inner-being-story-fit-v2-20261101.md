## 4. What the fit shows
- **II is largest by pairs**, matching the researcher's "by volume by far the most extensive". Feelings, knowing, speech, will and the moral behaviours all sit here.
- **III needs the Relational domain divided.** Its human-ward concepts belong to "relatedness with other human beings"; its God-ward concepts (prayer, worship, obeying, forsaking, treachery, rebellion) belong to how God relates to the inner being, and also bear on II as behaviours.
- **IV holds the states** the researcher lists. The concepts that fit are sin, righteousness, wickedness, holiness, defilement, judgment, atoning, covenant, blessing, repenting, life, death, renewal. **Image of God has no concept at all**; it is a thread, not yet a unit in the data.
- **Concepts that bear on two movements** (column "Also bears on") are the web in the data: the same word is a behaviour in II and a state or a relation in III or IV. That is where concepts and narrative need the joint adjustment.
- **The 50 concepts with no narrative home** mostly find one: moral behaviours in II (once 10.8 and 10.11 are filled), the Relational ones in III, and covenant, atonement, repentance and judgment in IV.

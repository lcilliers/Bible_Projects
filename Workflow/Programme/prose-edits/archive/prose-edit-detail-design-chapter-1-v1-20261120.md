## The Observations Framework

*What are the inner workings of the human being as described in Scripture: characterising and grouping the inner characteristics, mapping their interrelationships, describing the spirit-soul-body boundary, and documenting the relational interfaces with God, other humans, and other spiritual beings?*

The framework of enquiry is the staged reading of a collection, in which each finding is recorded as an observation tied to the text. It works as a discipline of consistent and complete enquiry: applied to every collection, it ensures that no part of the vocabulary is systematically missed and that findings are produced on the same footing across collections. What the framework could explore is open; the evidence determines what is found. Where Scripture is silent on an aspect, the silence is recorded and not filled by inference or from other sources.

**The stages.** A collection is read in five stages, in order, as the researcher defined them. Stage 1 is the lexical analysis: every word of the collection, by surface and lettered Strong's code, is read in its verses from the lexicon and its occurrences, and each sense the verses give it is an observation; an occurrence whose sense departs from the usual one is recorded as a face of the word. The relations between words are base data and are not analysed at this stage. Stage 2 is the grouping of the surfaces: the surfaces of a collection are grouped by their dictionary meaning into subgroups, each named for the meaning its surfaces share; a surface whose meaning is not the one the collection gathers is set aside with its reason. Stage 3 holds the verse-lexical observations: the Strong's codes of each group are studied and the grouping validated, and then what the other words of the verse show with each group is recorded (the words that pair with it, qualifiers and the words they reach, entities, the direction the text states, the relation between groups, a double meaning). Stage 4 is the inner-being analysis, made from the stage 3 observations. Stage 5 is the formulation of claims and the update of the register of claims. Stage 4 and stage 5 are still being defined.

Stages 1 to 3 are lexical. Every observation in them states what the dictionary, the verses and the stored structure of the verse show, and none states what a word does for the inner being. The lexical reading and the inner-being reading are separate processes and never run at the same time; a verse is not analysed lexically and given its inner-being implications in one process, because each needs its own observations.

**The observation and the node.** An observation is an application or analytic statement that interprets what its verse says and is founded in the verse. It is never a repeat of the verse text, and the same wording and meaning is never held as two observations. A node is the cross-reference that applies an observation to one verse, one word and one surface, and carries the surface's collection and subgroup. A live observation has at least one live node, and a live node belongs to a live observation. A finding withdrawn or no longer supported is retired as a soft delete with its reason, never physically deleted.

**The gates.** Each step of the reading demands a defined set of aspects, and each aspect is a row of a ledger that is closed by an observation with its nodes, or by a recorded none with its reason where the step allows it. A step closes only when every aspect is answered and validated by the check, and the next step does not open before it. A word found wrongly placed in a collection is moved by the procedure for changing collections, and the move is processed so that the step reopens.

**The epistemological basis.** Scripture is the primary and governing source: what Scripture says is the finding. Original-language analysis, the Hebrew and Greek lexical and semantic study, is the primary evidential tool and is part of the Scripture source, not a separate one. The human sciences (psychology, moral philosophy, developmental psychology, sociology, anthropology, linguistics and hermeneutics) are an interpretive lens and a light sanity check. They are engaged in the claims reading, after the biblical reading, and add no content to a finding. Their engagement is selective and practical; a full treatment of any one field is a specialist's lifetime of work, and the programme does not attempt to replicate it. Everything produced by an AI is inferential until the researcher confirms it.

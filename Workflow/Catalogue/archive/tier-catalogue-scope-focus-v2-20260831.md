## The "HIB characteristic" split

- **Word/term (lexical)** — the characteristic's own name and vocabulary: what it's called, its
  primary Hebrew/Greek terms, their root meanings, grammatical and semantic range, cross-Testament
  vocabulary relationships. `T1.1` (Name and Naming) + `T7.1` (Lexical and Semantic Analysis).
- **Characteristic (HIB behaviour)** — the characteristic's own nature and operation in a person,
  not filtered through a named faculty or a relational exchange: kind, boundary, mode of operation,
  immediate response, sustained effect, conditions of reception, origin/source, and everything in
  `T5` (transformation, sequence, mechanism, suffering, formation, eschatological trajectory).
- **Characteristic relational** — the characteristic operating BETWEEN parties: `T4.3`–`T4.5`
  (Human Interface — giving/receiving/boundaries, both parties human) and all of `T6` (Structural
  Relationships — co-occurrence, sequence, causation, vocabulary sharing, distinctions, all
  characteristic-to-characteristic).

## The two corrections you asked for

**1. Reclassified from "characteristic" to "The HIB"** — your point that the main focus is the
*person*, not the characteristic. The clearest, largest case: **`T3` (The Inner Faculties, all
33 questions)**. Perception, cognition, memory, affect, creativity, volition, agency, moral
evaluation, conscience, conscientiousness, relational capacity are properties of the human
person's own constitution — the same status as "spirit/soul/heart/mind/body" in `T2`. A question
asking whether the characteristic "engages the perceptive faculty" is, at bottom, asking something
about the *person's* architecture, with the characteristic as the diagnostic lens — not a claim
about the characteristic itself. Same reasoning extends to **`T2.1` (Spirit-Level Location),
`T2.7` (Body — Direction), and `T2.10` (Constitutional Movement)** — 4 more questions, all
literally mapping the person's own spirit/soul/heart/mind/body architecture. And **`T1.7.3`**
("What is the inner-being state of the person in whom the characteristic is present but does not
take hold?") — its subject is explicitly the person's own state, not the characteristic.
**38 questions moved.** `T2.9` (Origin and Source) stayed in "characteristic" — origin traces
where the characteristic itself comes from (the person, another person, God, generationally, a
spirit), not the person's architecture, so the same logic that moves `T2.1`/`T2.7`/`T2.10` doesn't
apply there.

**2. Reclassified from "characteristic" to "The verse"** — I want to be direct about where this
one landed: I could not find a second, clearly-distinct group beyond what correction 1 already
moved. The one candidate I found is **`T2.1.1`**, whose own grammar makes the verse the acting
subject — *"At which constitutional level(s) does **this verse** locate the characteristic..."* —
which is arguably a verse-reading question wearing the same clothes as the HIB-architecture
questions above. I've filed `T2.1` under "The HIB" (marked ⚠ below) rather than splitting `T2.1.1`
into "The verse" and `T2.1.2` into "The HIB", because the two questions are one component read
together. **If you meant something broader** — e.g. every single-verse "in this verse.../here"
sub-question across `T1`–`T6` (there are many, and I distinguished exactly this single-verse vs.
cross-verse pattern in the earlier raw-data mapping) — say so and I'll rebuild on that wider rule;
as it stands that would gut "characteristic" down to almost nothing, which is why I didn't apply
it without checking first.

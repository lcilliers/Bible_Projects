# PART 4 — THE SCIENCE (plain language)

The big idea behind all of it: **meaning is structured, not a bag of words.** A verse doesn't mean "the sum of its word-meanings"; it means *who is doing what, to whom, how, why, and what it turns into.* Four scientific findings make that precise, and each one fixes one of our problems.

### Science 1 — Predicate–argument structure (the spine of a sentence)
*The finding (mid-20th c. linguistics):* every clause has a **predicate** (the action or state — usually the verb) and a small set of **arguments** (the participants it requires). "Give" requires a giver, a thing given, a recipient. You cannot understand "give" without them. The participants aren't optional decoration — the predicate **demands** them.
- **Our example:** Eze 36:26 "give you a heart" — predicate `give`, arguments: giver (God), thing (heart), recipient (you).
- **What it fixes — the compound control:** a span is a real "compound" only if it **fills an argument slot** of the operation. "stone" and "flesh" fill the *quality* of the heart; "earth" in Gen 6:5 fills **nothing** in the heart's movement → it's scenery. *Co-occurrence is not participation.* That single rule is the control you asked for.

### Science 2 — Semantic roles ("who did what to whom")
*The finding (Fillmore, 1968, "case grammar"):* the arguments aren't just "slot 1, slot 2" — they carry **roles**: Agent (the doer), Patient (the one acted on), Instrument, Manner, Cause, Goal, Source… Languages differ on word order but share these roles. This is the science behind labelling our **edges** (operation, object, manner, cause, source, purpose).
- **Our example:** Heb 9:14 — `purify`: Agent = Spirit, Patient = conscience, Source = dead works, Purpose = serve God.
- **What it fixes — the bias we cleaned today:** because the Spirit is bound as the **Agent of purify**, it **cannot** be mis-recorded as "operating in the conscience." Roles make attribution *structural*, so the proximity-bleed (faculty over-fire, divine assumptions) becomes impossible by construction.

### Science 3 — Frames (words carry hidden background structure)
*The finding (Fillmore again, "frame semantics," 1970s–80s; built into FrameNet, and demonstrated for Biblical Hebrew):* a word doesn't just have a definition — it **evokes a whole scene with fixed roles.** "Buy" evokes a Commerce scene: buyer, seller, goods, money — even if the sentence only mentions two of them, your mind supplies the slots. Some slots are **core** (essential to the scene); some **non-core** (optional: time, place, manner).
- **Our example:** "cleanse/purify" evokes a Cleansing scene: a Cleanser, an Affected-thing, a Source-of-impurity. Heb 9:14 fills all three.
- **What it fixes — the meaningful border + completeness:** the **core slots define what a verse must account for.** If a core slot is empty, that's a real gap (mark UNRESOLVED); if a non-core slot is empty, that's fine (silence, not a gap). This is the principled answer to "what's meaningful" — and it tells the engine when to **stop** (all core slots filled or flagged) instead of pairing endlessly.

### Science 4 — Event structure & aspect (how operations *morph*)
*The finding (Vendler 1957; Dowty 1979; Role & Reference Grammar):* verbs don't just name an action — they encode its **internal shape in time**: a **state** (be hard), a **process** (devising), an **instant change** (BECOME soft), or a **caused change** (CAUSE to become soft). Complex meanings are **built** from simple ones: *cause-to-become* = `CAUSE(BECOME(state))`. Crucially for us, **Hebrew marks causation in the verb's stem** (Hiphil/Piel) — which we already store.
- **Our example:** Eze 36:26 "**remove** (Hiphil) the heart of stone … **give** a heart of flesh" = `CAUSE(God, BECOME(heart: stone → flesh))`. Two operations compose into **one transition** of the heart's quality. *That* is "operations morphing into each other" — and the Hiphil stem is the mechanical fingerprint of the *cause*.
- **What it fixes — operations morphing:** a **transition edge** between operation-nodes, typed by change-kind (set / removed / reversed / caused / became), seeded by stem + aspect + result-conjunctions. The flat model literally has no slot for "turns into"; this gives it one.

### How they assemble — the meaning graph (AMR)
*The finding (Banarescu et al., 2013, "Abstract Meaning Representation"):* put 1–4 together and represent the **whole sentence as one graph** — concepts are **nodes**, roles are **edges** — deliberately **dropping function words** (the/of/and become edges or vanish). Two sentences that mean the same thing get the **same graph**. This is the science of "what does this sentence *mean*, independent of how it's worded."
- **Why function words aren't nodes:** language has two kinds of words — **content words** (heart, give, evil) that carry concepts, and **function words** (the, of, and, not) that signal **relations**. Function words are the *edges*, not the *nodes*. That is the deep reason the border exists: the 36 `H9xxx` markers and articles were never meant to be meaning-units — they're the grammar that *connects* the meaning-units.

### Why this fits *our* subject specifically
1. **Hebrew is concrete, verbal, relational** — it shows the heart *doing*, rarely defines what it *is*. Predicate-argument/role/aspect models are built for exactly that verbal-relational shape (this is why frame semantics and RRG have been applied to Biblical Hebrew).
2. **The inner being is a process/web**, not a set of parts (your own RESET insight). A graph of operations-and-transitions models a process **natively**; a flat field-list models a parts-inventory — which is why the flat model kept failing the boundaries-won't-hold way.
3. **It's honest about its limits.** The mechanical layer fills what morphology forces (roles, stems, core slots); the **figurative and distributed** cases (clean hands; the outcome in the next verse) are **flagged for the read**, not fabricated — which is precisely the discipline this whole rebuild is about.

# PART 5 — Three questions: Greek, what STEP includes, and using every span

### (a) Does it work for Greek?
**Yes — and for *roles*, arguably better than Hebrew, because Greek marks the role on the word itself, in its CASE ending.** From Heb 9:14's real morphology:
- `conscience` = **N-A**SF (Accusative) → that *is* the **object/patient** of "purify."
- `blood` = N-**N**SN (Nominative) → the **agent/subject**.
- `Spirit` = N-**G**SN (Genitive) + "through" → the **means**.
- `works` = N-**G**PN (Genitive) + "from" → the **source**.
- `God` = N-**D**SM (Dative) → the **recipient/goal**.
- `purify` = V-**FAI**-3S (Future Active Indicative); `serve` = V-PA**N** (Present Active **iNfinitive**) → a **purpose** clause.

So in Greek the role-edges come from **case** (nominative=agent, accusative=patient, genitive=source/possession, dative=recipient/instrument) — explicit, mechanical, in `morph_code`. **The difference from Hebrew:** Hebrew marks *causation* in the **stem** (Hiphil) — we used that for Eze 36:26; Greek has no stem system, so its event-shape/"morphing" signal is **voice + tense-aspect** (aorist/present/perfect; active/middle/passive) and lexical causatives instead. The **graph/frame model itself is language-neutral** (that's the whole point of a meaning representation — the same meaning graph for a Hebrew or Greek verse); only the *signals that feed it* are language-specific (Hebrew: stem + construct + word-order; Greek: case + voice + aspect).

### (b) How does STEP decide what span to include?
From our data: **STEP doesn't pre-filter — it tags every word of the verse.** Heb 9:14 = 22 words, 22 tagged; Gen 6:5 = 14 words, 14 tagged. A **span = one STEP morphological token.** Words that carry a dictionary lemma get a lexical Strong's (G2511 purify, H3820 heart); grammatical words still get a code — Greek function words are real Strong's (G0575 "from", G1519 "to", G1223 "through"), Hebrew particles get the `H9xxx` grammatical codes (conjunction/article/preposition/suffix) which have **no lexicon gloss** (confirmed: H9005/H9006 = "(none — grammatical)"). One surface word can carry **several** tokens (e.g. "evil" = H7451H + H9005, the adjective + an attached particle). So STEP offers the **complete, fully-tagged verse** — nothing is left out; the granularity is the morphological token. *(Characterised from our stored data, which came from STEP — not from STEP's internal source.)*

### (c) Do the methods use just the span — and ALL the spans STEP offers?
**They use the span as the atom, enriched — and they use *every* span, but in different jobs. No span is wasted.** This is the precise version of your "every span is relevant":
- A span is never used *bare* — it's the span **+ its lexicon meaning + its morphology (case/stem/voice)**. The span is the anchor; meaning and role are read off it.
- **Every span gets a job in the graph — one of three:**
  1. **Content word with a role in the movement → a NODE** (blood, Spirit, conscience, purify, serve, heart, evil).
  2. **Function word → an EDGE.** Prepositions/case/conjunctions/particles are *not discarded* — they are the **signals that build the relations**: "from" (G0575) = the **source** edge; "to" (G1519) = the **purpose** edge; "through" (G1223) = the **means** edge; Hebrew `H9006` "from" = a source edge; the construct state = a possession edge. Function-word spans have no dictionary meaning, but they carry the **grammatical** meaning — the structure.
  3. **Content word present but filling no role in the *inner-being* movement → background** (e.g. "earth" in Gen 6:5) — a node in the verse's full meaning, but peripheral to the inner-being graph specifically.
- So the earlier word "scenery" was too blunt: it conflated (2) function-words-as-edges with (3) peripheral-content. The honest picture is **three fates, all used** — node, edge, or background — and **every STEP span is placed**, which is exactly the completeness you insisted on. Where today's flat model *drops* the function-word and peripheral spans (they get no lexical, so they vanish from the analysis), the graph model **consumes them as the edges**, which is why it can see the structure the flat model can't.

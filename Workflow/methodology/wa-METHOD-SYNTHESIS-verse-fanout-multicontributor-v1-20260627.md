## 1. The journey — how we got here (the process)
A chain of realisations, each forced by evidence, mostly on 2026-06-27:

1. **The lexical was full of imported bias.** Cleaning faculty (lemma-stamped, not verse-grounded), divine-involvement (LLM-asserted roles with no divine name in the verse), valence (interpretive moral overlay, a clustering driver), origin (single non-grounded value) — all were **extraction errors**: the engine logged a value the verse did not state. Rule established: *the lexical captures only what the verse says; no-basis-in-verse = error → reverse out.*
2. **The coverage was a mirage.** The `verse_span_lexical` index showed the analytical lexical reaches **~9% of spans**. "Completed" verses were largely hollow; the pipeline measured activity, not verse-grounded completeness.
3. **The frame itself was wrong.** Registry / term / characteristic / cluster — the **list-of-things** organiser — is **debunked** as the analytical anchor (it only ever surfaced the initial corpus). Modern science already abandoned the inner-being-as-parts view; assuming characteristics is what kept us from hearing it.
4. **Focus points are latent.** The unit of the inner being is a **focus point**: not a term; not standalone; not consistent across instances; not present in all; comes and goes; **cannot be observed/logged/counted directly**; assessable only via **secondary evidence** (verses describing its operation; science recording its aspects). [7 properties — researcher.]
5. **The expression/driver gap.** Mechanical reads a verse **forward / on the surface / by extraction** ("is an inner term named? no → None"). Inner-being reads **backward / by inference** ("this act happened — what inner reality produced it?"). The act is the **manifestation**; the inner being is the **latent driver behind it**. The mechanical layer logs the manifestation and mistakes it for the meaning. (Proven: Gen 25:5 "give all", Gen 9:4 "life in blood", Gen 6:8 "found favour", Exo 1:13 "ruthlessly" — all read "None" mechanically, all inner-being-profound.)
6. **So: observe/surface, don't resolve.** Round 1 records **observations** (some resolved, most needing corroboration), tied to verse + span; the study is a **continuing debate refined through every related instance**. Meaning emerges across instances; focus points emerge as the **recurring observations/questions**.
7. **The operating model = verse-fan-out, which supersedes the cluster method.** Unit of work = a verse; **fan out** to all relevant/related evidence (its data + related verses) until digested; researcher digests (inference) into observations; observations **accumulate corpus-wide**.
8. **The spiderweb binding was built** (verse↔evidence, from all ends, fast, complete, not blind) — and the **Exo 1:13 fan-out** proved it: a never-analysed verse still reaches its evidence, its driver sits in the neighbour (v.12 dread), and rarity-weighting surfaced the 6 `perek` verses (the ruthlessness debate).
9. **Lexical relatedness ≠ conceptual relatedness.** Logos found ~20+ ruthlessness verses (Amos, Micah, Ps 10, James 5…) **none using `perek`**. A term/span index finds same-word verses; the inner being lives at the **concept** level, which crosses vocabulary. Relatedness must be **inferred**, not matched.
10. **The spiderweb is multi-contributor.** Mechanical lexical (precise floor) + AI conceptual synthesis (Logos, Claude Chat — cross-vocabulary reach) + scholarship (corroboration). Compared on Exo 1:13, all three **converged** on the same threads → validity-by-convergence, working.

## 2. Settled foundations (governing)
- **Scripture is the DATA-SOURCE layer** — the study is *how Scripture expresses the workings of the inner being*. Describe those workings **accurately, comprehensively, without bias, without making them into what they are not** (not a part-list).
- **Science = secondary corroborator** — extracts hold, principles apply, revisit once we can frame questions under the focus-point framing.
- **The unit is a focus point** (latent/emergent/dynamic; 7 properties) — inferred, never extracted.
- **Inner-being meaning is inferred (the driver behind the act), not extracted (a named surface term).** Three kinds: (1) acts that *express* a driver; (2) statements about the *nature/seat* of the inner being; (3) directly-named inner states.
- **Morphological simplicity ≠ inner-being depth** (different axes).
- **Mechanical = substrate, never the meaning.** The move from data to true meaning is the researcher's inference; mechanical generates the valuable *in-between steps* (assemble, surface, link), increasingly so as observations accumulate. **Valuable data is set aside (not-the-meaning), never discarded.**

## 3. The operating method (how the work runs now)
1. **Pick a verse.**
2. **Fan out** (mechanical assists): assemble the verse's own evidence — text, morphology/spans, lexical, existing findings, **context (look back/forward)** — and **related verses** (lexical-precise + conceptual-broad) + prior observations.
3. **Digest** (researcher, inference): read the inner being off the act/state, with context — the driver behind the manifestation.
4. **Record OBSERVATIONS:** individual, tied to **verse + term/span**, each with **status** (*resolved* vs *needs-corroboration*) and **provenance** (which contributor). A question is an observation with status "open".
5. **Accumulate** corpus-wide; the debate refines; **focus points emerge as recurring observations**; validity by **convergence** across instances and contributors.
6. Researcher-driven, step by step; mechanical assistance grows.

## 4. The multi-contributor spiderweb (the relatedness model)
Related evidence is **bound to a verse by multiple contributors**, each grounded back to verses and tagged by provenance + status:
- **Mechanical lexical** (`verse_evidence_index` + `verse_term_index`) — the precise, verse-anchored **floor**; finds same-lemma verses (e.g. the 6 `perek`); rarity-weighting ranks meaningfulness. Narrow but certain.
- **AI conceptual synthesis** (Logos, Claude Chat, this assistant) — **cross-vocabulary conceptual reach**; finds same-*dynamic* verses in different words. Powerful but **must distinguish STATED (verse-explicit) from SYNTHESIZED (inference)** and ground each to its verse. (Claude Chat models this discipline; Logos blends the two — a caution.)
- **Scholarship** (e.g. Logos's cited works) — corroboration; cite and ground.
- **Convergence across independent contributors = the validity test.** Three witnesses landing on the same dynamic (heart-as-source · fear-of-God-as-restraint · fear-of-threat-as-trigger) firms a focus point.
- **Governing discipline for every contributor:** STATED vs INFERRED must be marked. This *is* the observation-with-status unit, and the guardrail against the fabrication this whole effort reversed.

## 5. Infrastructure built (the grounded substrate)
Anchored on `verse` (23,593, the stable control total). All read-only/derived, rebuildable, reversible:
- `verse` · `verse_morphology` (305,961 spans, every word) · `lexicon` (11,666 STEP meanings, 99% of terms).
- `verse_span_index` (305,961 — verse·span·term spine).
- `verse_coverage` (scope + touched, provisional) · `verse_morph_complexity` (simplicity score).
- `verse_evidence_index` (804,805 verse↔evidence links, all bind paths recorded, 222 empty-unit orphans) · `verse_term_index` (lateral web) · `verse_evidence_orphan` (the not-blind guarantee).
- The mechanical lexical (`ve_lexical`, ~424k) and findings (`finding`) are **retained as evidence/substrate**, not the analytical spine.

## 6. Findings established (true)
- The analytical lexical reaches ~9% of spans; the **foundation** (verse text, morphology, term meanings) is complete and sound.
- Faculty/valence/divine/origin were extraction errors (now reversed/quarantined, reversible).
- **Lexical relatedness is structurally narrow** (`perek` 6 vs the ruthlessness concept ~20+).
- **Convergence works** — mechanical + Logos + Claude Chat agree on the ruthlessness drivers, cross-linking to Gen 6:5.
- **High-confidence content (multi-witness corroborated):** *fear of God is the restraint/brake on cruelty* (Lev 25:43; Deut 25:18; Ps 36:1); *the heart is the source of the act* (Gen 6:5; Jer 17:9; Mark 7:21–23 — the expression/driver principle confirmed).

## 7. Consequences (going forward)
- **Never mistake the mechanical layer for the meaning.** It assembles and grounds; the researcher infers. This is the cardinal rule that, broken, produced every failure we reversed.
- **Build relatedness from multiple contributors, all grounded to verses, all marked stated-vs-inferred, confidence by convergence.** Lexical-only is too narrow; concept-only is ungrounded.
- **The cluster method is superseded.** Work is verse-out; the picture is built corpus-wide from accumulated observations.
- **The infrastructure is the floor, not the answer.** It makes the fan-out fast, complete, and honest; meaning is still the inferential reading on top.

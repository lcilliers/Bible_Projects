# Inner-being reading — the expression/driver gap, and the QUESTIONS-FIRST method

- **Status:** foundational. Answers *how* to read the inner being off a verse — and the surprising answer is: in round 1, **don't**.

## 1. The gap — why mechanical reading and inner-being reading are so far apart
Demonstrated 2026-06-27 on the layer-1 batch: the mechanical/hybrid read called Gen 25:5 ("Abraham gave all"), Gen 9:4 ("life is in the blood"), Gen 6:8 ("found favour"), Exo 1:13 ("ruthlessly") **None / marginal** — the researcher reads each as inner-being-profound. The gap is **direction**:
- **Mechanical reads FORWARD / on the surface / by extraction:** what does the verse state happens, and is an inner term *named*? If none is named → "None."
- **Inner-being reads BACKWARD / through the surface / by inference:** this act happened — *what inner-being reality had to be there to produce it?*

The inner being is **almost never on the surface.** It is the **source the surface expresses.** The act is the *manifestation* (observable); the inner being is the *latent driver* (inferred) — the focus-point profile exactly. **The mechanical layer logs the manifestation and mistakes it for the meaning; the meaning is the driver inferred *from* the manifestation.**

**Three kinds of inner-being meaning the mechanical read was blind to:**
1. **Acts that EXPRESS a driver** — "give all" → an inner *release* (trust/love/letting-go that allows parting); "ruthlessly" → a *hardness designed in the inner being* and discharged as the act. Read the source behind the act.
2. **STATEMENTS about the nature/seat of the inner being** — "life is in the blood" → a foundational datum about *where life resides*.
3. **Direct inner states named** — "heart sick", "joy gone" — the *only* kind the mechanical read reliably caught, and only when a word named it.

**Consequences:** the inner being is behind *far more* verses than any tagged term admits (nearly any verse where someone gives/refuses/perceives/hardens/releases/waits); reading is **inference, not extraction**; and **morphological simplicity does NOT track inner-being depth** (Gen 25:5 is grammatically trivial yet profound — different axes).

## 2. The method (researcher, 2026-06-27) — QUESTIONS FIRST, resolve never (in round 1)
> Maybe the first round is **not to derive meaning, but to ask all the questions the verse leaves unanswered**, in the context of the verse. We do **not resolve** in the first round — we **surface**. The study is a **continuation of a debate, refined through every related instance.**

- **Round 1 = surface, don't resolve.** For each verse, record the inner-being **questions it raises but does not answer**. No forced meaning, no fabricated value (which is what extraction kept doing and getting wrong).
- **The study is a continuing DEBATE.** Each new verse touching the same question **refines** the debate. Meaning **emerges through accumulation and refinement across instances** — never settled from one verse, always revisable.
- **Focus points emerge as the recurring questions/debates.** A question the corpus keeps raising and refining *is* a focus point — latent, emergent, known only through the instances that circle it (matches the 7 properties). This is how focus points are *discovered from the verses* rather than imposed.
- **Validity by convergence** (from the focus-points reframe): a candidate answer firms up as independent instances — and later, science — **converge** on it; never by frequency or a single verse.

## 3. Demonstration — round-1 question-surfacing on the four verses
**Gen 25:5 — "Abraham gave all he had to Isaac."**
- What inner-being capacity lets a person give away their *all*? · What is the *driver/root* of giving — love? trust? release? freedom from grasping? · Is "giving" an inner ability, or the *expression* of one? · What moves in the inner being when one parts with what is one's own?

**Gen 9:4 — "you shall not eat flesh with its life, that is, its blood."**
- What *is* life (nephesh) such that it resides in the blood? · Is life a substance, a principle, a presence — and why the blood? · Does this locate the *seat/substrate* of life? · What is the relation life ↔ blood ↔ flesh ↔ inner being?

**Gen 6:8 — "Noah found favour in the eyes of the LORD."**
- What is favour (chen)? Is it the same as grace? · What does it mean to **find** favour — discovered? attained? perceived? · Did Noah generate it (by his walk), receive it, or perceive it as already there? · Is finding-favour a *perception of value* — and whose, Noah's or God's? · Where does favour reside — in God's regard, in the relation, in Noah?

**Exo 1:13 — "they ruthlessly made the people of Israel work as slaves."**
- What is ruthlessness as an inner-being reality, and where does it come from? · How is an act "framed/designed to be ruthless" in the inner being *before* it is expressed? · Is ruthlessness the *presence* of something (cruelty/hardness) or the *absence* of something (compassion/mercy)?

> None of these is answered by its verse. Each becomes a thread the corpus refines: every other "favour/find" verse sharpens the favour debate; every "give" verse sharpens the giving-driver debate; every "hard-heart/ruthless" verse sharpens the ruthlessness debate.

## 4. What this changes
- **Round 1 of the lexical/reading work is a QUESTION REGISTER, not a meaning store.** Per verse: the unanswered inner-being questions, in context.
- **The mechanical/hybrid layer is the substrate** (morphology → operation → the manifestation), feeding the questioning — it is *not* the inner-being reading and must not be mistaken for it.
- **Cross-instance refinement is the engine.** Questions cross-link; recurring ones become focus points; the debate converges over many instances (and later science).
- **No resolution is forced.** Honesty about what is *not yet known* replaces fabricated meaning — the cure for everything we spent this week reversing out.

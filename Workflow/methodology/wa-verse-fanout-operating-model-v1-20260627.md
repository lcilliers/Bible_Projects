# The verse-fan-out operating model (supersedes the cluster method)

- **File:** wa-verse-fanout-operating-model-v1-20260627.md · **2026-06-27 · Author:** Claude Code (recording the researcher's decision).
- **Status:** foundational operating model. Supersedes the **cluster (M-code) method of analysis**. Culminates the 2026-06-27 reframe: builds on `wa-focus-points-and-scripture-as-data-source`, `wa-inner-being-reading-questions-first-method`, and `feedback_expression_vs_characteristic_object_type`.

## 1. The model
**The unit of work is a VERSE. From the verse, FAN OUT to all relevant and related evidence until it is digested — then move on; observations accumulate progressively across the entire corpus.**

The fan-out gathers, for the starting verse:
- its own data — **text, morphology/spans, lexical, existing findings, and context (look back / forward)**;
- and **related verses across the corpus** + their evidence (the fan-out reaches *out* into the corpus, not just the local verse).

The researcher **digests** this and produces **observations** — individually recorded, tied to the **verse** and the **term/span**, each with a **status: resolved** or **needs-corroboration**. Observations accumulate across the corpus and refine the running debate (the continuing-debate method).

## 2. Mechanical vs meaning — the division of labour
- **The move from data to *true* (inner-being) meaning is NOT mechanical.** True meaning is the researcher's **inference** — reading the act/state back to its inner-being driver (the expression/driver gap). A mechanical process cannot make this move.
- **But mechanical generation of the *in-between steps* is real and increasingly valuable** as the work proceeds and the observation base grows: assembling the dossier, surfacing candidate related verses, retrieving prior observations/findings, proposing candidate links. The more observations exist, the more the mechanical layer can pre-digest evidence for the researcher.
- **Valuable data is NOT discarded.** "Set aside" = *not treated as the meaning*, **not** *deleted*. The morphology, lexical, l2_meaning, and existing findings are the **evidence the fan-out digests** — kept and used as substrate.

## 3. Why this supersedes the cluster method
- The cluster (M-code) bin was a **list-of-things** organiser (terms grouped) — the debunked frame. The organising unit is now the **verse and its fan-out web**, which is **verse-king** and matches the inner being as a **web reached by fanning out**, not a set of bins.
- Clusters may survive only as a **coarse, porous lexical convenience** (batching), never as the analytical driver. Analysis is **verse-out**, and the picture is **built corpus-wide from accumulated observations**, not assembled within clusters.

## 4. The shape of the process
1. **Pick a verse.**
2. **Fan out** — assemble all its evidence + related verses + their evidence + prior observations/findings (mechanical assists; the dossier is the prototype of this step).
3. **Digest** (researcher, inferential) — read the inner being off the act/state, with context; this is where data becomes meaning.
4. **Record observations** — individual, verse/span-linked, status resolved | needs-corroboration; link to related observations.
5. **Accumulate** — the observation base grows across the corpus; the debate refines; **focus points emerge as the recurring observations/questions**.
6. **Researcher-driven, step by step initially** — mechanical assistance grows as the base and the patterns mature.

## 5. Open (to be settled by doing, not rushed)

- **How the mechanical layer assists** each step without re-importing the extraction error (it prepares evidence; it never asserts meaning).
- **Disposition of the cluster/finding tables** — retained as evidence/substrate; no longer the analytical spine.

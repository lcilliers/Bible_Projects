- **Objective (a):** capture the IB **operation** from a verse — the **dimension definition**. Derived from the Exo 1:13 raw collection; the dimensions are a *starting, revisable* set (the `D11 Discovery` slot keeps it open — D13 is the first dimension it produced).
- **Discipline:** stay focused (the raw material is rich; easy to digress). Each dimension is defined by *what it captures · how it is evidenced · its value · its states*.

## 1. The unit = an IB OPERATION
- The record is an **IB operation** (an inner-being movement in the verse), **one per fan-out track**. A verse may hold several (Exo 1:13 has two: ruthlessness, enslavement).
- Each operation is described across the **dimensions** below. Each dimension value carries: **value** (or NONE/silent) · **status** (`resolved` / `needs-corroboration` / `open` / `silent`) · **provenance** (mechanical / researcher / logos / claude-chat / scholarship) · **basis** (the citation/morphology/contributor it rests on).

## 2. The dimensions (definition)
| # | dimension | the question — what it captures | how it is evidenced |
|---|---|---|---|
| **D1** | **Identity** | *what is the operation?* — the named inner-being movement | the span + lemma/sense + morphology |
| **D2** | **Source / antecedent** | *where does it come from?* — driver / cause / motive (incl. from context) | causal clause; the operation in a prior/context verse; the actor's prior state |
| **D3** | **Seat / bearer** | *where is it seated / who bears it?* — named seat (heart/soul/spirit) or the bearer | a seat lemma in-verse; else the grammatical subject (bearer) |
| **D4** | **Operation / action** | *what does it do?* — the act itself + its grammatical realisation | the predicate + **stem** (Qal=simple · Hiphil=causative · Piel=factitive) |
| **D5** | **Object / target** | *on whom / what?* — the patient/recipient | accusative / object marker / prepositional complement |
| **D6** | **Manner** | *how is it done?* — the qualifier on the operation | manner phrase / adverb / intensifier |
| **D7** | **Process / movement** | *how does it unfold / morph?* — the dynamic chain (incl. **emergence**: an operation that *grows* / escalates) | the sequence of operations/states; causative stems; result/sequence clauses; an escalation across context verses |
| **D8** | **Impact / produced state** | *what does it produce?* — the effect/outcome state in the object | result clause; the produced-state verb (e.g. *marar* Piel = made bitter) |
| **D9** | **Coupling / relation** | *what binds with it HERE?* — the functional/grammatical binding within this operation's realisation (manner / partner / object-relation) | grammatical binding; tight in-verse co-occurrence |
| **D10** | **Colour / valence** | *moral register — WHERE EVIDENCED IN THE VERSE* (never imported) | term-inherent moral lemma; prohibition; the verse's framing |
| **D11** | **Discovery** | *anything the dimensions do not capture* (emergence) | the lookout — feeds new dimensions back |
| **D12** | **Hidden meaning** | *what latent / non-obvious IB implication does the verse carry?* | a deeper meaning to investigate further |
| **D13** | **Cohabitation** | *what other operations does this one keep company with — and what shared root does that company reveal?* | the recurring **cross-corpus co-occurrence** with other operations (the family it travels in); the **common root** the family points to, read off the accumulating verses |

**Completeness of an operation** = every dimension swept (a value, or marked *silent*) + D11 checked. **Complete ≠ resolved** — most values may be `needs-corroboration`.

### D9 vs D13 — the boundary
- **D9 Coupling** = the **in-verse weld**: how this operation is structurally bound *here* (e.g. `be-perek` binds the Hiphil `abad` as its manner; a partner term).
- **D13 Cohabitation** = the **cross-corpus company**: which *other* operations this one habitually travels with (e.g. ruthlessness ↔ killing, exploitation of the weak), and what that company reveals about its **source**.
- They connect — the company (D13) often shares a deep root that is a kind of ultimate Source (D2) — but D2 is the *per-verse* antecedent, D9 is the *in-verse* weld, and D13 is the *corpus-wide family + its emergent root*.

### D13 — the root emerges, it is not imposed
Vices do not stand alone; they cohabit. Mapping the company a vice keeps is how the **shared root** surfaces — it will transpire that the root of the family is **the evil one**, and collectively the family is **"evil."** This root is **read off the verses as it accumulates**, never asserted a priori. Assess D13 for **every** operation (virtues keep their own company toward their own root). The cohabitation dimension is the instrument by which the root *emerges*.

## 4. Tracing the fan-out — driven by the OPEN dimensions
The fan-out reaches the related verses. The discipline that tames combinatorial explosion:
- **Driven by the OPEN dimensions, not every shared term** — fan out to corroborate `needs-corroboration` / `open` values (e.g. *does dread→cruelty hold? · what family does ruthlessness cohabit with?*). A related verse is worth completing insofar as it can resolve an open dimension.
- **Convergence terminates it** — once independent verses + contributors converge, the value flips to `resolved` and stops driving new fan-out.
- **Dimension-keyed accumulation** — observations on the same operation × dimension accumulate across verses; a recurring, converged answer **is a focus point**. (D13 is special: it accumulates *toward the shared root*.)

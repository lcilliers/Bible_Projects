# Focus points + Scripture as the data-source layer (foundational reframe)

- **File:** wa-focus-points-and-scripture-as-data-source-v1-20260627.md · **2026-06-27 · Author:** Claude Code (recording the researcher's framing).
- **Status:** foundational methodology record of a conversation that reframes the study's object and method. Builds on `inner-being-behavioural-science-discussion.md` (which holds) and the RESET specs; does not disturb them.
- **Why now:** the term/characteristic/registry frame is debunked as the *analytical* basis. This records what replaces it.

## 1. The reframe in one line
The study is about **how Scripture expresses the workings of the inner being.** Scripture is the **data-source layer** — the place where the description of the workings is found. The task is to **describe those workings accurately, comprehensively, without bias, and without trying to make them into what they are not** (e.g. a list of parts).

## 2. What a "focus point" is — the researcher's characterisation (2026-06-27)
A focus point of the layered discovery:
- **a)** is **not** a term, a characteristic, or a few terms collated together via gloss;
- **b)** is **not distinctive / standalone** (it has no discrete self-contained identity);
- **c)** does **not behave consistently the same** across focus points;
- **d)** is **not consistently present** in all inner beings;
- **e)** **changes, comes and goes**, and behaves differently in inconsistent ways;
- **f)** **cannot be observed, logged, empirically counted, or analysed** (directly);
- **g)** the **only way to assess it is via secondary evidence** — verses in the Bible describing its operation, and/or scientific studies that record aspects of it.

## 3. What this means (synthesis reached in discussion)
- These properties describe a **latent, emergent phenomenon known only by its manifestations** — and, because of (c)/(d)/(e), a **dynamic configuration** that arises and dissolves in context, **not** a hidden *fixed* thing (a faculty/trait would be stable and universal; this is not). This matches where behavioural science went: inner states as **constructed/enacted in the moment**, not a fixed component-set (`inner-being-behavioural-science-discussion.md` §1–§2: faculty psychology abandoned; the inner being is *inferred, not observed*; no physical individuation; "possibly the inner life isn't the kind of thing that has parts").
- **Method reversal — from EXTRACTION to INFERENCE.** By (f) the focus point itself cannot be logged. Only its **operations** can — what a verse describes it *doing*. The focus point is **inferred** from a constellation of operations that hang together; the analysis never points at "the focus point" directly. *(This is why faculty/valence/origin failed — they tried to record the latent thing as if observable.)*
- **Validity by CONVERGENCE, not consistency or frequency.** (c)/(d)/(e) forbid validating a focus point by how regularly it appears or how uniformly it behaves. It is validated when **two independent secondary witnesses converge** on the same dynamic — Scripture's described operation and science's recorded aspect. Convergence is the truth test (g).

## 4. Settled positions (researcher, 2026-06-27)
1. **Scripture = the data-source layer.** It is where the workings are described; it is the ground we work from. The study is *how Scripture expresses the workings of the inner being.*
2. **Science = secondary corroborator** (as previously positioned and recorded). The existing **science extracts hold** and their **principles still apply** — *even though the extracts were framed around "characteristics."* They will be **revisited once we know how to frame the questions to science** (under the focus-point framing, not the characteristic one).
3. **Historical insight:** because the project **assumed characteristic-like inner beings**, it never put the question to science in the right form — and only recently discovered that **science studies had already "tripped over" the list-of-things view and moved away from it.** The assumption hid the answer that was already there.
4. **The list-of-things frame is closed as the analytical basis.** Registry/term/characteristic/cluster served their one purpose — to **surface the initial body of terms and verses** — and are not the basis for further research. (Registry debunked as an anchor.)

## 5. The task, stated precisely
Find a way to **describe the workings of the inner being, as Scripture expresses them**:
- **accurately** (what the verse says, P-grounded, no import);
- **comprehensively** (every span/operation accounted for — verse is king);
- **without bias** (no imposed list, no characteristic grid, no fabricated value);
- **without making it what it is not** (not a part-list, not a fixed inventory, not an observable object).

## 6. Open — still to be worked out (not to be rushed)
- **What is the loggable unit — the "operation"?** The focus point is unobservable; what we *can* record is the operation a verse describes (something acts / changes / responds / binds). The precise shape of this verse-derived, loggable operation is the next thing to settle — it is the only thing we get to work with directly; everything else is inferred from it.
- **How do focus points emerge from operations?** If a focus point is an inferred constellation of operations, the layered-discovery focus points may be ordered by **strength of converging evidence** (Scripture + science), base layer = strongest convergences — but this is a hypothesis to test, not a decision.
- **How to frame the questions to science** (gates the extract revisit).

> Recorded as the researcher's current position. The how (the operation unit, the emergence of focus points) is deliberately left open — to be found from the verses, with the researcher, not imposed.

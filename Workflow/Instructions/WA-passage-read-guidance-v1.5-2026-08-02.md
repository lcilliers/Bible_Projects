**Change-control note:** Restructured around three explicit, temporally-separated phases, after
the researcher identified that the Amos 1-3 debate — the first produced under the same-day
pipeline split that batches up to `passage.debate_session_chapter_guideline` chapters into one
debate file — had drifted from identifying an inner-being phenomenon per verse into identifying
general/textual patterns across the whole multi-chapter range (a repeated oracle formula, a
claimed "ring-composition," a book-wide "thesis") and constructing operations to fit them. A
caution note alone was judged insufficient: the researcher specified the actual corrective is
procedural — (1) identify the inner-being phenomenon in every verse first, recording *why* it is
regarded as such, for every inner being in the verse, before any operation is written; (2) only
once that whole register is complete for the debated range, run a *separate* pass generating each
phenomenon's operation per the existing guide; (3) on completion, run a validation pass that
reconsiders the debate's own quality specifically around the phenomena — are they genuine, is
their justification sound, do their operations track faithfully back to them. Running Phase 2
immediately after Phase 1 verse-by-verse (rather than completing Phase 1 across the whole range
first) reopens the same drift, since operation-writing momentum can bleed back into how the next
verse's phenomenon gets identified. Steps 1-3 (Phase 1), step 3b (new), steps 4-5 (Phase 2), and
step 6 (new, Phase 3) replace the flat step 1-5 listing of v1.4; their underlying content (step 1-5
notes) is otherwise unchanged from v1.4/v1.3.

---

PASSAGE READ GUIDANCE — three phases, run in strict sequence for the whole debated range

**PHASE 1 — phenomenon identification (every verse in the range, completed before Phase 2 begins)**

step 1 read the verse/passage
step 2 does the verse have any bearing on the IB - if no - exit (set the verse aside)
step 3 for EVERY inner being present in the verse (step 2 note (f): every human is a presumptive candidate; note (b)/(d): an in-scope non-human), isolate the inner-being phenomenon — a state, disposition, or characteristic of that party's inner life, which may be hidden behind a stated act or a refrained-from act (step 3 note (e))
step 3b (new) for each phenomenon isolated in step 3, record WHY it is regarded as an inner-being phenomenon: the specific textual warrant (the verb, clause, or stated silence) that grounds the identification, and whether it is stated or inferred. Record this per verse, per inner being, as its own entry in a **phenomena register** — this entry exists independently of, and is written before, any operation. Do not proceed to Phase 2 for ANY verse until Phase 1 is complete for the WHOLE debated range.

**PHASE 2 — operation generation (a separate pass, begun only after Phase 1 is complete for the whole range)**

step 4 for each phenomenon recorded in the phenomena register, state what the verse says about it as an operation: a paragraph describing it as a state/status, or a movement (come from, go to, impact on, emerge, go away, become evident)
step 5 record the parts of each operation (subject, operation, source, target, action-type label) per the existing guide

Phase 2 takes the phenomena register as fixed input. An operation is generated ONLY for a phenomenon already identified in Phase 1 — Phase 2 must not identify a fresh phenomenon of its own accord to make an operation work. If writing an operation reveals that no genuine inner-being phenomenon actually underlies it, that is a signal the Phase 1 entry was mis-identified (most likely: a textual/structural pattern was mistaken for a phenomenon) — go back and correct the phenomena-register entry (or strike it) before writing the operation; do not paper over the mismatch by writing an operation that quietly reframes what the register said.

**PHASE 3 — validation (a closing pass, run once the whole per-verse debate is assembled for the debated range)**

step 6 (new) reconsider the quality of the assembled debate specifically around the phenomena: for each phenomenon (or, at minimum, a representative sample spanning the range), ask — is this genuinely an inner-being state/disposition/characteristic, not a textual, structural, or general pattern mislabeled as one? Does its Phase 1 justification actually warrant it from the verse's own text? Does its Phase 2 operation track faithfully back to the phenomenon as identified, without having drifted toward a general/textual framing during assembly? Record the outcome of this pass as its own section in the debate document (see `WA-interpretation-questions-v1.4` Part C). Where a phenomenon or operation fails this check, it is corrected before the debate is considered filled — not merely logged as an emergent question for later.

Step 1 notes
a) the main aim of reading of the passage/verses is to describe the activity of the inner being to capture the operations of different states and characteristics of the inner being.  Each operation have separate parts the subject (the inner being in focus); the operation (what is the state of, or happening in, or impacting on; or being impacted by ) the inner being; the source (where does the operation come from - another operation, or another human being, or another non-human being, or a physical object or situation); the target (what does the operation impact - another operation, or another human being, or another non-human being, or a physical object or situation). the source and target could be singular, multiple, mixed or non existent.

Step 2 notes
a) there could be more different inner beings in the verse: One inner being impacting on another - both inner beings must be considered separately: what is the state / characteristics of the first inner being; what is the state / characteristics of the next inner being ; what is the impact / effect / movement between the inner  beings
b) the movement could be between a non-human being and a human being - in this case the state / characterisctics of the non human being must be considered and describe in relation to the human being in the verse
c) the human being could be referred to as a collection (tribe, youths, children, nation, gentiles etc) - in this case the nature of the collection should be taken into account as a movement to a collection rather than an individual
d) verses with only non-human beings in contexts, where the state / characteristics of the non-human being is not related to a human being in the context of the passage, is not in scope of this study's analysis, the verse is set aside.
e) inferenced related parties: the context of the verse may not name or specify the parties where the characteristics infer a movement or action between parties, this may be found in the passage; in may also be discoverable in other verses. In this case, the analysis must raise the referential aspect and name it as such
f) every human mentioned is a presumptive IB candidate. any human — named or collective, major or minor, however briefly mentioned — who performs an act, undergoes an act, thinks, speaks, refrains from acting, or is simply named as present, is a candidate operation and must be carried into Phase 1 step 3 / Phase 2 steps 4-5. this holds even where the act described looks purely outward, administrative, locational, or incidental — see step 3 note (e): the phenomenon may be *hidden behind* the act, with only the act recorded in the text. step 2's "no bearing - exit" applies **only** where the verse contains no human at all, or only a non-human being unrelated to any human (note d). it must never be used to dismiss a verse or clause on the grounds that the act itself looks outward or minor — that judgement belongs to Phase 1 step 3/3b, not to step 2. "no phenomenon found, silent" is a valid *result* of running step 3/3b on a human-bearing clause; it is not a valid *substitute* for running it. when a passage debate is written, every human mentioned in the passage should be traceable to a phenomena-register entry, even if the outcome recorded is silence.

Step 3 notes
a) isolate the inner being phenomenon. The verse may be silent about any phenomenon. Then set aside the verse (or that candidate within it).
b) the phenomenon can underlie a state or status, or a movement (come from, go to, impact on, emerge, go away, becomes evident) — which of these it is, is determined when the operation is written in Phase 2, not in Phase 1; Phase 1 only isolates and justifies the phenomenon itself.
c) there may be multiple phenomena in the same verse/passage (one per inner being, per step 2 note (a)); each must be isolated and justified in its own right.
d) phenomena may be explicitly or implicitly linked to another phenomenon elsewhere in the passage, or may have no relation to one. This linkage question belongs to the interrogative (Q7), applied once operations exist (Phase 2/3) — Phase 1 itself records the phenomenon and its own textual warrant, not yet its cross-verse linkages.
e) the inner being phenomenon could be expressed as, or inferred from, the state or status of the inner human, and this state may be hidden behind an act or a refrained-from act, with only the action recorded in the passage — Phase 1 step 3b's justification is exactly the place this is made explicit: name the act/silence that hides the phenomenon, and name the phenomenon it is taken to evidence.

Step 4 notes
a) The output of Phase 2 is a statement of operation for each phenomenon recorded in the phenomena register. Each statement must include all the parts named in step 1 note (a).
b) The statement of one operation may have a bearing on another statement; this is surfaced via the interrogative (Q7), not invented ahead of the phenomena register.

Step 5 notes
a) every recorded operation carries an action-type label, in addition to its
   subject/operation/source/target parts. the action-type is a short, natural, verb-based
   label for what was done (e.g. "gave," "summoned/complied," "worshiped," "renamed," "bound and
   cast") — recorded for every operation regardless of whether the phenomenon behind it resolved
   as stated, inferred, or a recorded silence (step 2 note (f)). the label is not drawn from a
   fixed list and no such list is being built by this note; it exists so that the same
   action-type, wherever it recurs — in this passage, this book, or elsewhere in the corpus once
   read — can be found and weighed together, the way `WA-interpretation-questions` Q7 already
   requires linkages *within* a passage to be surfaced rather than passed over. See
   `WA-interpretation-questions-v1.4` Q11 and Part B.10.
b) where a human operation's action-type, vocabulary, or explicit content
   juxtaposes it against a stated divine operation recorded elsewhere in the same passage, note
   the point of comparison, difference, or inversion — but only where the passage's own data
   anchors it (shared action-type/vocabulary, a direct juxtaposition, or an explicit statement
   of likeness or contrast). This is not a required part of every operation the way note (a) is;
   it is recorded only where the text itself supplies the anchor, and is not elaborated into a
   general account of how or why the inner being resembles, differs from, or inverts God's
   character — a suggestive but textually-unanchored resemblance is logged as an emergent
   question instead (Part B.8/B.11), not asserted. See `WA-interpretation-questions-v1.4` Q12 and
   Part B.11 for the interrogative-side and discipline-side of the same addition.

Step 6 notes (new, v1.5 — Phase 3)
a) validation is not a re-read of the whole passage for new content; it is a re-examination of what Phase 1/2 already produced, checking specifically that each phenomenon is genuine (not a textual/structural pattern in disguise) and that each operation is faithful to its own phenomenon.
b) where a debate document spans multiple chapters (per `passage.debate_session_chapter_guideline`), validation should pay particular attention to phenomena/justifications that read as though they are describing the passage's own literary architecture (a recurring formula, a claimed compositional structure, a book-wide argument) rather than a specific inner being's own state — this is the exact failure mode the phase-separation exists to prevent, and validation is the last check that it did not recur.
c) a genuine literary/structural observation, if one is noticed during any phase, is logged once as an emergent question (`WA-interpretation-questions` Q10), not built into the phenomena register or the operations as if it were their own content.

specifies the phenomena register and the validation section this produces as first-class parts of
the debate document. Q1-Q11 and Part B.1-11 are otherwise unchanged from v1.3.

## Purpose

The read guidance answers *what* to isolate: phenomena and their operations. This document answers
*how to interrogate* each verse so that potential and hidden parts of a human's operation or
status are surfaced rather than missed.

It exists because of a specific failure observed in reading: a stated outward act was treated as
having no inner-being bearing, when in fact every human act invites a question about the interior
behind it and about the source of that interior. The corrective is interrogative — not "is an
inner state stated?" but "what inner state must be asked after here, and what is its source and
target?"

---

## Part A — The interrogative

Apply to every human, and every human act, in a qualifying verse, once its phenomenon is recorded
in the phenomena register (`WA-passage-read-guidance` Phase 1) and its operation is written
(Phase 2). **Every question is considered for every candidate; a question need not resolve, but it
must be considered.** Considering a question may itself raise a new one (see Q10).

- **Q1 — Focused inner being.** Is this person a focused inner being in their own right, even
  where only an outward act is stated? (Do not let an unstated interior collapse into "no
  bearing.")
- **Q2 — Implied interior.** What interior state, disposition, intent, or volition could underlie
  or be implied by the stated act or status?
- **Q3 — Stated vs inferred.** Is that interior stated in the text or span-data, or inferred? If
  inferred, raise and name it as referential; never assert it as fact. (This is the same
  stated/inferred distinction Phase 1 step 3b already requires when the phenomenon is first
  registered — Q3 re-applies it once the operation exists.)
- **Q4 — Source (state vs enablement).** What is the source of the interior state, and —
  separately — the source of the enablement to act? Self / another human / a non-human being / an
  object or situation. Are the two sources the same? Is either stated or inferred?
- **Q5 — Target.** What does the operation impact — another inner being, a human, a collective, a
  non-human being, or an object/situation?
- **Q6 — State or movement.** Is the operation a state/status, or a movement (come from, go to,
  impact on, emerge, go away, become evident)?
- **Q7 — Linkage.** What linkages run to other operations in the passage? Where a linkage is
  absent, surface the absence; do not pass over it silently. **(See B.12 — a Q7 linkage connects
  two specific, already-registered phenomena/operations; it is not licence to narrate a pattern
  across a whole chapter range.)**
- **Q8 — Collective.** If the human is a collective (tribe, youths, nation, gentiles…), what is
  the nature of that collective, and how does that reshape the operation as a movement to a
  collection rather than to an individual?
- **Q9 — Sufficiency.** Does the document contain the data needed to weigh this operation's
  inner-being significance? If not, name the missing data as an insufficiency; do not supply it
  from outside.
- **Q10 — Emergent questions.** After Q1–Q9, what new question has this verse raised that the
  instrument does not yet hold, and that may matter for future analysis? Log it and carry it
  forward. **A genuine literary/structural observation (a recurring formula, a compositional
  device) is logged here, once — it is not built into the phenomena register or an operation as if
  it were their own content (B.12).**
- **Q11 — Action-type.** Independent of how Q1-Q9 resolve — stated, inferred, or
  recorded silence — what is this operation's action-type: a short, natural, verb-based label for
  what was done (e.g. "gave," "summoned/complied," "worshiped," "renamed," "bound and cast")?
  Record it as one of the operation's parts (per `WA-passage-read-guidance` step 5 note (a)), so
  this instance can be found and weighed together with the same action-type wherever else it
  recurs — in this passage, this book, or elsewhere in the corpus once read. This is a label, not
  a classification system; see Part B.10.
- **Q12 — Divine mirroring.** Where a human operation in this passage sits alongside,
  or shares action-type/vocabulary with, a stated divine operation, does the human's state or act
  compare to, differ from, or invert what the passage shows of God's own character or action?
  Record the comparison only where the text's own juxtaposition, shared wording, or explicit
  statement supports it (per `WA-passage-read-guidance` step 5 note (b)); where a resemblance is
  merely plausible, log it as an emergent question (Q10) rather than asserting or elaborating it.
  This question does not ask how the inner being reflects God as a general doctrine — it asks,
  verse by verse, whether *this* passage's own data supports a specific, nameable point of
  comparison, difference, or inversion, and nothing beyond that; see Part B.11.

## Part B — Guidance of interpretation

1. **Consider, don't force-resolve.** Leaving a question open is acceptable; not considering it is
   not.
2. **Referential debate is legitimate.** Where parties or interiors are unnamed but implied, the
   debate is conducted and recorded — provided nothing inferred is imported as fact.
3. **Inference is named as inference,** at the point it is made.
4. **Silence is a finding.** Where the text states no interior, the silence is recorded as the
   result, not filled. A forward-pointer may be attached where a later verse or passage is
   expected to bear on it.
5. **Source of state ≠ source of enablement.** A non-human being (e.g. the Lord) may be stated as
   the source of an *outcome or enablement* without the text sourcing the actor's *disposition*.
   Keep the two separate; extending sourcing from outcome to interior is an interpretive step to
   be flagged, not assumed.
6. **Observation / interpretation / reflection are distinguished and labelled.** Observation =
   what the text or data states. Interpretation = what is reasoned from it, including referential
   inference. Reflection = consequence, tension, significance.
7. **Insufficiency is named, not papered over.** Where required data (e.g. name etymologies) is
   absent from the base extract, say so; do not substitute remembered or external knowledge.
8. **Emergent questions are carried forward,** so they can be applied to or tested against later
   passages.
9. **Interpretive forks are tracked, not escalated (v1.1).** A fork this instrument raises —
   e.g. does a stated divine giving-operation ever reach a human's *disposition*, or only their
   *circumstance*? — is not a researcher decision awaiting a ruling. It is carried forward exactly
   like an emergent question (Q10, B.8): named where it bites, weighed against each new data
   point as the corpus grows, and answered — or left open — by what the accumulating evidence
   actually shows, not settled in the abstract before the evidence is in. Reserve "the researcher
   should decide" language for genuine resourcing/data-curation choices this instrument cannot
   make for itself (e.g. whether to add name-etymology data to a base extract, or how to handle a
   DB gap) — not for interpretive questions the study itself exists to answer.
10. **Action-type is a label, not a taxonomy.** Q11 asks for a short, consistent,
    verb-based tag on every operation — it does not create, and this instrument does not yet fix,
    a controlled vocabulary of action-types. Do not invent a classification scheme, an enum, or a
    new DB field to formalise this ahead of need; a plain, consistently-worded label recorded in
    the debate's own prose is sufficient for now. Whether and how far to formalise the label
    (a controlled list, a structured store for cross-corpus querying) is a resourcing/build
    decision for later, on the same footing as B.7's "add name-etymology data?" question — not
    something to settle by building it prematurely into this pass.
11. **Mirroring is observed, not manufactured.** Q12 is answered only where the text
    itself provides the anchor for a comparison — shared action-type or vocabulary, a direct
    juxtaposition of a divine and a human operation within the passage, or an explicit statement
    of likeness or contrast. Do not supply theological explanation of how or why the inner being
    resembles, differs from, or inverts God's character in the abstract, and do not expect heavy
    elaboration of an operation's pre-action interior state on this or any other question — where
    a resemblance is suggestive but not textually anchored, log it as an emergent question (per
    B.8) and move on, rather than building it into a doctrine the passage itself does not state.
    This mirrors B.10's caution against premature formalisation: no controlled vocabulary or
    fixed typology of "comparison/difference/inversion" is being built by this note — a plain,
    passage-anchored observation, or an open question, is sufficient for now.
12. **The phenomenon comes first, is registered before any operation, and batching does not
    license book-architecture narrative-building (new, v1.4).** Every operation in the debate must
    originate from a phenomenon already recorded in the phenomena register (`WA-passage-read-
    guidance` Phase 1) — a state, disposition, or characteristic of a human's (or, per note
    (b)/(d), an in-scope non-human's) inner life, together with the textual warrant that grounds
    it. It is a failure mode, not a permitted shortcut, to instead identify a GENERAL or TEXTUAL
    phenomenon (a recurring literary formula, a structural pattern across multiple oracles or
    chapters, a claimed book-wide "thesis" or "ring-composition") and then construct an operation
    around it to satisfy the template — this produces a narrative *about the text's own
    architecture*, not a debate about an inner being's operation. The phase separation in
    `WA-passage-read-guidance` v1.5 (register the phenomena for the whole range first; only then
    write operations) is the primary defence against this — Q7 (linkage) still connects two
    *specific, already-registered* phenomena/operations to each other, but does not authorise
    tracking or narrating a pattern across a whole file as the debate's own primary content. Where
    a genuine literary/structural observation seems significant, log it as an emergent question
    (Q10) for a dedicated literary/structural study, not as the debate's own analytical
    centrepiece or as a phenomena-register entry in its own right.

## Part C — Output directive (supersedes read-guidance step 4–5 output)

Per passage, the output is a **single debate document** — `WA-[passage]-debate-[version]-[date].md`
— that captures the whole three-phase consideration, in this order:

1. **Preliminaries** — the working scope, declared as an assumption, and the reading rule applied.
2. **Phenomena register (Phase 1 output).** For every verse in the debated range, for every inner
   being present in it: the phenomenon identified, the specific textual warrant that grounds it,
   and whether it is stated or inferred (Q3). Completed for the whole range before section 3
   begins.
3. **Per-verse operations (Phase 2 output).** For each phenomenon in the register: observation
   (what the text/span-data states, with Strong's codes); the operation and its parts (subject,
   operation, source, target, action-type — Q11), marked where silent, inferred, or insufficiently
   evidenced; every interrogative question considered (Q1-Q12), whether resolved or not; referential
   debates recorded (never imported as fact); divine-mirroring comparisons where textually anchored
   (Q12), logged as an emergent question where not anchored; a decision (retain / set aside / retain
   as referential aspect / recorded silence).
4. **Passage-level linkages (Q7)** — linkages across this passage's operations, and surfaced
   non-linkages.
5. **Insufficiencies register** — data the base extract does not carry, named not filled.
6. **Emergent questions log** — including any interpretive forks (per B.9) and any genuine
   literary/structural observations (per B.12) — tracked there, not built into the phenomena
   register or operations, and not merged with other passages' logs.
7. **Debate quality validation (Phase 3 output, new).** Once sections 2-6 are assembled: a
   re-examination, for each phenomenon or a representative sample spanning the range, of whether it
   is genuinely an inner-being phenomenon (not a textual/structural pattern mislabeled as one),
   whether its Phase 1 justification actually warrants it, and whether its Phase 2 operation tracks
   faithfully back to it. Record the outcome; correct any failure found before the debate is
   considered filled, rather than only noting it for later.
8. **Open decisions / next steps.**

## EZE-gog-holiness · Ezekiel 38:1–39:29 · [D, multi] — God's holiness and name vindicated; the hidden face no more; the Spirit poured out

**The reading.** The oracles against Gog describe a final, doomed assault, but their inner-being weight is God's own stated concern: "**I will show my greatness and my holiness and make myself known in the eyes of many nations**" (38:23); "**my holy name I will make known in the midst of my people Israel, and I will not let my holy name be profaned anymore**" (39:7). And the whole vast section closes with a tender promise about God's *face* and *Spirit*: "**I will not hide my face anymore from them, when I pour out my Spirit upon the house of Israel**" (39:29). The finding is **God's *holiness and name* vindicated — and the promise that he will *"not hide his face anymore"*, his *Spirit poured out*** *(stated)*. The operation is **the reassurance that God's *hiding of his face* — which the whole book has traced as the mark of judgment (the departing glory, the "I hid my face", 39:23) — is *not permanent*: after the final vindication of his holiness, God pledges *never again to hide his face*, his presence *permanently turned* toward his people, and his *Spirit poured out* upon them** *(inferred: the Gog material's inner-being residue is God's *jealousy for his holy name* — "**I will not let my holy name be profaned anymore**" (39:7): the finding is that God acts, at the last, to vindicate his own *holiness* (the thing his people's sin had profaned among the nations, 36:20-23) — his glory is the ultimate stake; but the tenderest note is 39:29 — "**I will NOT HIDE MY FACE ANYMORE from them, when I POUR OUT MY SPIRIT**": the finding is that the *hidden face* — the withdrawal of God's presence that ran through the book (the glory departing, 10-11; "I hid my face because of their evil", 39:23-24) — is *reversed for good*: God promises that the hiding is *over*, his face *permanently toward* his people; and the ground of the permanence is the *poured-out Spirit* (as Joel 2:28) — the same Spirit that gives the new heart (36:27) now *poured out* as the seal of an un-withdrawn presence; the finding is that the interior's deepest security is a God whose face is *never again hidden* and whose Spirit is *within* — the end of the fear of abandonment, the presence made permanent)*.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *God's holiness and name vindicated ("I will show my holiness… I will not let my holy name be profaned anymore" — God's glory the ultimate stake)* (thread — a gem: God acting to vindicate his own holiness, w/ 38:23, 39:7, 36:22-23, Ps 46:10); *the hidden face reversed for good ("I will not hide my face anymore from them" — the withdrawal of presence that marked judgment permanently ended)* (new — a gem: the end of the fear of abandonment, God's face permanently turned toward his people, w/ 39:29, 39:23-24, 10-11 reversed, Ps 27:9, Isa 54:8); *the Spirit poured out as the seal of un-withdrawn presence ("when I pour out my Spirit upon the house of Israel")* (thread — a gem: the Spirit within the seal of a permanent presence, w/ 39:29, 36:27, Joel 2:28, Rom 8:9-16).

## EZE-river-lord-is-there · Ezekiel 44:1–48:35 · [D, multi] — God himself as inheritance; the river of life from his presence; the consummation — the LORD is there

**The reading.** Amid the ordinances of the restored temple, the priests are given a portion unlike the others: "**This shall be their inheritance: I am their inheritance… you shall give them no possession in Israel; I am their possession**" (44:28). Then the vision's most luminous image: water issues "**from below the threshold of the temple toward the east**" (47:1), deepening from a trickle to a river too deep to cross, and "**wherever the river goes, every living creature that swarms will live**… the waters… become fresh; so everything will live where the river goes" (47:9); on both banks "**all kinds of trees for food. Their leaves will not wither, nor their fruit fail, but they will bear fresh fruit every month, because the water for them flows from the sanctuary**" (47:12). And the whole book ends on a single name for the city: "**The name of the city from that time on shall be, The LORD Is There**" (48:35). The finding is **God himself as the priests' *inheritance* ("I am their possession"), a *river of life* flowing from his presence that makes everything live, and the book's final word — the city named *"The LORD Is There"*** *(stated)*. The operation is **the consummation of the book's whole inner-being movement: the interior's *ultimate portion* is *God himself* ("I am their inheritance" — not lands or things but God); the *presence* of God is a *source of life* that flows out to heal and enliven everything it touches (the river deepening, freshening the dead sea, growing healing trees); and the final reality toward which everything has moved is simply *God's presence with his people* — "**The LORD Is There**"** *(inferred: three inner-being findings crown the book. First, 44:28 — "**I am their inheritance… I am their possession**": the finding is that the highest good of the interior is *God himself* — the priests are given *no land* precisely so that *God* is their portion (as Ps 16:5, 73:26, "the LORD is my portion"); the self's deepest wealth is not what it *has* but *whom it has*, and to have God as one's inheritance is to be richer than any landholder. Second, the river (47:1-12) — the finding is that God's *presence is life-giving and outflowing*: the water rises *from the sanctuary* (from where God dwells), starts as a trickle and becomes an uncrossable river (grace *increasing* as it flows), and *everything it touches lives* — the dead salt sea made fresh and teeming, trees whose leaves never wither and whose fruit never fails, their "leaves for healing"; the finding is that the life of the world flows *from God's presence*, and the interior near that source is *ever-fresh, ever-fruitful, healed* (the seed of Rev 22, the river and the tree of life); the presence of God is not static but *life pouring out*. Third, the final name (48:35) — "**The LORD Is There**" (Yahweh Shammah): the finding is that the *end toward which the whole book (and the whole interior) moves* is simply *God's presence with his people* — the departed glory (10-11) returned (43), the hidden face turned back (39:29), and now the very *name* of the consummated city is *God's being-there*; the finding is that the interior's ultimate home, its final and complete good, is *God present* — not merely his gifts, his river, his temple, but *himself, there*; "The LORD Is There" is the book's last word because it is the interior's deepest longing answered)*.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *God himself as inheritance ("I am their inheritance… I am their possession" — the interior's highest good is God himself, not lands or things)* (thread — a gem: the self's deepest wealth is whom it has, not what, w/ 44:28, Ps 16:5, 73:25-26, Lam 3:24, Num 18:20); *the river of life from God's presence ("wherever the river goes, everything will live"… "the water flows from the sanctuary" — God's presence life-giving and outflowing, grace deepening as it flows, healing all it touches)* (new — a gem: the life of the world flowing from God's presence; the interior near the source ever-fresh, fruitful, healed, w/ 47:1-12, Ps 46:4, John 7:38, Rev 22:1-2); *ever-fresh, never-failing fruit ("their leaves will not wither, nor their fruit fail… fresh fruit every month" — the interior rooted by the river of God's presence)* (thread — a gem: the God-watered life that does not fail, w/ 47:12, Ps 1:3, Jer 17:8); *"The LORD Is There" — presence as the consummation (the city's final name — God's being-with-his-people the end toward which all moves)* (new — a gem: the interior's ultimate good is God present himself, not merely his gifts, w/ 48:35, Rev 21:3, 22:4, Ps 16:11).

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced:** God himself as inheritance; the river of life from God's presence; ever-fresh, never-failing fruit; "The LORD Is There" — presence as the consummation. Chapters 44–48 crown the book — God himself the interior's portion, a river of life flowing from his presence to heal all it touches, and the final name "The LORD Is There".
- **Book-level note (Ezekiel, chs 38–48):** the closing movement is *consummation*: God's holiness vindicated and his face *never again hidden*, his Spirit poured out (38-39); the *glory returning* to dwell forever, the vision of holiness working shame (40-43); and God *himself as inheritance*, the *river of life* from his presence, and the final word — *"The LORD Is There"* (44-48). Master-findings: **the hidden face is turned back for good**; **the departed glory returns to dwell forever**; **God himself is the interior's inheritance**; and **the end toward which all moves is God's presence with his people.**
- **★ EZEKIEL COMPLETE** (48 chapters). The book's book-wide arc: from the *glory that prostrates* the seer and *departs* the defiled temple (1, 10-11), through the diagnosis of the *stony heart* and the promise to *transplant it for a heart of flesh* and *put God's Spirit within* (11, 36), the *resurrection* of the dead interior (37), to the *returning glory* and the consummation *"The LORD Is There"* (43, 48:35) — the interior God alone can cleanse, re-heart, enliven, and finally *dwell with* forever.
- **★ MAJOR PROPHETS COMPLETE** — Isaiah, Jeremiah, Lamentations, Ezekiel, Daniel all done.

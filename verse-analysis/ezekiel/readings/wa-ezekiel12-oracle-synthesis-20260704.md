## EZE-see-not-hear-not · Ezekiel 12:1–13:23 · [D, multi] — eyes that see not; false prophets of their own spirit; peace where there is no peace

**The reading.** Ezekiel is told he dwells among "**a rebellious house, who have eyes to see, but see not, who have ears to hear, but hear not**" (12:2). He enacts an exile's departure as a sign. Then judgment falls on the false prophets, "**who prophesy from their own hearts… who follow their own spirit, and have seen nothing!**" (13:2–3); they "**smear [a flimsy wall] with whitewash**" (13:10) and cry "**'Peace,' when there is no peace**" (13:10,16); worst, they "**disheartened the righteous falsely… and encouraged the wicked, that he should not turn from his evil way to save his life**" (13:22). The finding is **faculties *functional yet blind* ("eyes to see, but see not"), false prophets speaking *from their own spirit* ("have seen nothing"), and the double harm of *disheartening the righteous* and *encouraging the wicked*** *(stated)*. The operation is **the interior whose organs of perception *work but do not perceive* (as Isa 6:9, Jer 5:21) — seeing and hearing without receiving; and the *self-sourced* counterfeit word — prophets projecting "their own spirit" and "own hearts" as God's, papering over a doomed wall with "whitewash" and false "peace"; and the moral *inversion* they produce — saddening the faithful and emboldening the wicked, the exact reverse of true prophecy** *(inferred: 12:2's "**eyes to see but see not**" names the recurring inner-being blindness — perception without reception (the same as Eze 42... Isa 6:9); the finding is that the rebellious heart is not short of information but of the *will to perceive*; and 13:2–3's false prophets "**follow their own spirit and have seen nothing**" locate the counterfeit precisely — projecting one's *own* mind as God's word (the same as Jer 23:16); the "**whitewash**" (13:10) is the inner-being image — a *cosmetic covering* over a structurally-doomed wall, the false comfort that hides the real danger; and 13:22 names the *double harm* of the false word — it "**disheartens the righteous**" (grieving those God has not grieved) and "**encourages the wicked**" (confirming them in evil so they "not turn"): the finding is that false comfort is not neutral but *actively harmful in both directions* — it saddens the faithful (making them doubt their true standing) and hardens the sinner (removing the fear that would turn him); the counterfeit word gets the moral polarity exactly backward)*.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *faculties functional yet blind ("eyes to see but see not, ears to hear but hear not" — perception without reception, the un-willing heart)* (thread — a gem: the rebellious heart short not of information but of the will to perceive, w/ 12:2, Isa 6:9, Jer 5:21, Mark 8:18); *the self-sourced counterfeit word ("follow their own spirit and have seen nothing… from their own hearts")* (thread — a gem: projecting one's own mind as God's word, w/ 13:2-3, Jer 23:16); *whitewash over a doomed wall (cosmetic false comfort hiding the real danger)* (new — a gem: the reassurance that covers rather than cures, w/ 13:10-15, Matt 23:27); *the double harm of the false word ("disheartened the righteous… encouraged the wicked" — false comfort saddening the faithful and hardening the sinner)* (new — a gem: the counterfeit word getting the moral polarity backward, w/ 13:22, Isa 5:20, Jer 23:17).

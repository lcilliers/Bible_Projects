## MIC-bethlehem-ruler · Mic 5:1–15 · [D, multi] — the shepherd-ruler who is peace; security relocated from self to God

**The reading.** From little Bethlehem comes a ruler "whose coming forth is from of old" (v.2); "he shall **stand and shepherd his flock in the strength of the LORD**… and they shall **dwell secure**… and **he shall be their peace**" (vv.4–5). Then the remnant is set among the nations "like **dew from the LORD**" and "like a **lion**" (vv.7–8). And God cuts off every false security: "I will **cut off your horses**… your **strongholds**… your **sorceries**… your **carved images**… you shall **bow down no more to the work of your hands**" (vv.10–13). The finding is **peace embodied in the shepherd-ruler, and security relocated from the self's own supports (armies, fortresses, idols) to God alone** *(stated)*. The operation is **the interior's security transferred from what it can build and control to the shepherd who *is* peace** *(inferred: two movements — (1) the ruler "shall be their peace" (v.5, shalom): peace not as a state achieved but as a *person* given; the flock's security is in being shepherded "in the strength of the LORD" (v.4), not in its own strength; (2) God *strips* the alternative securities (vv.10–13) — horses, strongholds, sorceries, idols, "the work of your hands" — the whole apparatus by which the self tries to secure itself apart from God; the finding is that God saves by *removing false securities* so the interior rests on the shepherd alone; "you shall bow down no more to the work of your hands" (v.13) is the liberation from self-made supports, the same deliverance-by-deprivation as Hosea 2)*.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *peace as a person given (the shepherd-ruler "shall be their peace")* (new — security in being shepherded, not in one's own strength); *security relocated from self-made supports to God (horses/strongholds/idols cut off)* (new — deliverance by the removal of false securities, w/ Hos 2); *the remnant as dew and lion (gift and strength)* (new).

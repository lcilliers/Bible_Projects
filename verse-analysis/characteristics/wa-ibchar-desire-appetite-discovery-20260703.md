# Desire / longing / appetite — a discovery document (developing)

> **Mode: DISCOVERY, not summary.** Gather the desire evidence across Psalms + Proverbs; trace the colours from God-ward thirst to the insatiable eye; hold open the hardest questions — whether desire is good/neutral/fallen by default, and whether contentment is *fulfilment* or *renunciation*. Registry: `ib_characteristic.code = desire-appetite`.

## Working sense (held open)
The inner being's *wanting*. Its two live variables are **direction** (toward God or elsewhere) and **satisfiability** (can it be filled?). Nearly every colour turns on one of these.

## The evidence, gathered by colour

**Colour 1 — thirst / longing for God (aching, bodily).** "As a deer **pants**… so my soul **thirsts for God**" (Ps 42:1–2); "my soul thirsts for you… in a **dry and weary land**" (Ps 63:1; 143:6); "my soul **longs, yea faints** for the courts of the LORD" (Ps 84:2); "my soul is **consumed with longing**" (Ps 119:20); "my soul **longs for your salvation**" (Ps 119:81). Desire at the pitch of *bodily need* — pants, thirsts, faints, consumed.

**Colour 2 — desire MET / satisfied by God.** "He **satisfies the longing soul**, and the hungry soul he fills with good" (Ps 107:9); "you **open your hand**; you **satisfy the desire** of every living thing" (Ps 145:16); "he **fulfills the desire** of those who fear him" (Ps 145:19); "who **satisfies you with good**" (Ps 103:5); "those who seek the LORD **lack no good thing**" (Ps 34:10). The appetite *answered* — and answered at *God's* hand.

**Colour 3 — the INSATIABLE eye (structurally unfillable).** "**Sheol and Abaddon are never satisfied, and never satisfied are the eyes of man**" (Pro 27:20); "the leech has two daughters: Give and Give… three things **never say, 'Enough'**" (Pro 30:15–16). Desire with *no natural floor* — a want that cannot be filled by its objects.

**Colour 4 — disordered craving (that tests God, or kills).** "They had a **wanton craving**… and put God to the test… he gave them what they asked, but sent a **wasting**" (Ps 106:14–15; 78:29–30); "**the desire of the sluggard kills him**… all day long he **craves and craves**" (Pro 21:25–26). Craving that *devours its owner* — answered to ruin, or lethal because it never acts.

**Colour 5 — hope deferred (wounds) / fulfilled (heals).** "**Hope deferred makes the heart sick, but a desire fulfilled is a tree of life**" (Pro 13:12); "a desire fulfilled is **sweet to the soul**" (Pro 13:19). Desire has a *temporal* register: its *deferral* sickens, its *fulfilment* is life.

**Colour 6 — WEANED desire (past craving).** "I have calmed and quieted my soul, **like a weaned child**… like a weaned child is my soul within me" (Ps 131:2). Desire *transcended* — the soul content past the fretful demand, wanting nothing but presence.

**Colour 7 — CONSOLIDATED desire (the "one thing").** "**One thing** have I asked… to gaze upon the beauty of the LORD" (Ps 27:4); "**there is nothing on earth I desire besides you**" (Ps 73:25). The scattered wants *gathered onto one object*.

**Colour 8 — DELIGHT (settled desire, given its object).** "**Delight yourself in the LORD**, and he will give you the **desires of your heart**" (Ps 37:4); "his **delight is in the law**" (Ps 1:2; 112:1; 119). Desire in its *fulfilled, restful* mode — and, strikingly, delighting seems to *reshape* the desires (37:4) so that they are then granted.

## Where it changes colour — the developing picture
Desire runs from **aching thirst → met/satisfied → weaned/transcended**, with a shadow-track of **insatiable → disordered/craving → self-killing**. The decisive discovery is a paradox the corpus holds without resolving:
- The eye is **structurally insatiable** (27:20) — nothing fills it; yet God **satisfies the desire of every living thing** (145:16). The resolution seems to be that an *infinite* appetite can be filled only by an *infinite* good — the insatiability is not a defect to be cured but a *capacity that points to God* (nothing less than God says "Enough").
- Contentment appears in **two forms** that may be one or two: desire **fulfilled** (the satisfied soul, 107:9, 145:16) and desire **weaned** (the soul past craving, 131:2). Is the weaned child *satisfied* or *renouncing*? The text gives both as *ends* — and this is the live edge.

And a second: **does delighting reshape desire, or grant it?** "Delight in the LORD and he will give you the desires of your heart" (37:4) — either God *grants* the desires, or delighting in him *re-forms* the desires so that what the heart now wants is what God gives. The syntax allows both; the inner-being stakes are high.

## Junctions (boundaries blurring)
- **love-aheb** — the deepest junction. Love = *settled attachment*, desire = *reaching want*? Or the same operation (both object-set, both God-ward or ruinous)? "Delight" sits exactly on the seam.
- **waiting-hope** — hope *is* desire projected forward under delay; "hope deferred" (13:12) is desire-in-time. The boundary may be artificial.
- **the-felt-interior** — the *hope-sick heart* (13:12) is desire registering as feeling/soma.
- **self-mastery** — appetite-*restraint* (Pro 23:2 "knife to your throat"); desire governed.
- **the-compulsive-will** — craving that *captures* the will (21:25; the drunkard's "another drink", 23:35).

## Thin / uncertain evidence (noted, not yet weighed)
- **Desire for the WORD.** "More to be **desired** than gold… sweeter than honey" (Ps 19:10); "I **long** for your precepts" (Ps 119:40,131,174). Desire aimed at God's word specifically — junctions with wisdom-formation.
- **The directional couplets.** "The **desire of the righteous** will be granted, but what the wicked **dreads** will come" (Pro 10:24; 11:23) — desire and dread as two futures the self already lives toward. Thin but suggestive of desire as *anticipation*.
- **Being "satisfied with your likeness."** "I shall be **satisfied** with your likeness when I awake" (Ps 17:15) — a desire whose satisfaction is *deferred past this life*. One witness; a distinct eschatological colour.

## Open questions (the debate, left open)
1. Is desire **good, neutral, or fallen** by default? The corpus has holy thirst *and* deadly craving as the *same* faculty aimed differently — like love/aheb.
2. Is the **insatiable eye** a defect or a *capacity* — an infinite appetite that only God can fill?
3. Is **contentment** *fulfilment* (satisfied soul) or *renunciation* (weaned child) — or a passage from one to the other?
4. Does **delighting reshape** the desires or merely earn their granting (Ps 37:4)?
5. What finally distinguishes **desire from love** — reaching vs resting, want vs attachment — or nothing?

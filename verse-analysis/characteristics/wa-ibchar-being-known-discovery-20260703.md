# Being known / searched / weighed / tested by God — a discovery document (developing)

> **Mode: DISCOVERY, not summary.** Gather the being-known evidence; trace the colours from threat to comfort to invitation; hold open what turns being-known from dread to welcome, and whether "testing" is inspection or a *refining that changes the self*. Registry: `ib_characteristic.code = being-known`.

## Working sense (held open)
The inner being's transparency to God — its thoughts, ways, depths lie open — and, at its height, the self that stops hiding and *asks* to be searched.

## The evidence, gathered by colour

**Colour 1 — exhaustively KNOWN.** "You have **searched me and known me**… you **discern my thoughts from afar**… acquainted with all my ways" (Ps 139:1–3); "he **knows the secrets of the heart**" (Ps 44:21); "the LORD **knows the thoughts of man**" (Ps 94:11); "he **fashions the hearts** of them all and **observes** all their deeds" (Ps 33:15). Not behaviour only but *thoughts* — known before the self is aware of them.

**Colour 2 — the INVITED search.** "**Search me**, O God, and **know my heart**! Try me and know my thoughts!" (Ps 139:23); "**Prove me**, O LORD, and **try me**; **test** my heart and my mind" (Ps 26:2). The self that *offers* its inmost — transparency embraced, even requested.

**Colour 3 — TESTED / refined (the assay that purifies).** "The crucible is for silver… and **the LORD tests hearts**" (Pro 17:3); "you have **tried us as silver is tried**" (Ps 66:10); "you have **tried my heart**… you have **tested** me and will find nothing" (Ps 17:3). Testing as *smelting* — refining, not merely inspecting.

**Colour 4 — WEIGHED / the excuse seen through.** "The LORD **weighs the heart**" (Pro 21:2; 16:2 "weighs the spirit"); "does not **he who weighs the heart perceive it**?" — the plea of ignorance exposed (Pro 24:12). Being-known as *moral assessment* the self cannot evade.

**Colour 5 — the LAMP within.** "**The spirit of man is the lamp of the LORD, searching all his innermost parts**" (Pro 20:27). God's searching is a light *inside* the person — the conscience as divine illumination of the self's depths.

**Colour 6 — INESCAPABLE presence.** "Where shall I **flee from your presence**? If I ascend to heaven, you are there; if I make my bed in Sheol, you are there… even the **darkness is not dark to you**" (Ps 139:7–12). Being-known as *unavoidable accompaniment* — nowhere is outside the gaze.

**Colour 7 — known-in-distress as COMFORT.** "You have **known the distress of my soul**" (Ps 31:7); "you **know my way**" (Ps 142:3); "you have kept count of my **tossings**; put my tears in your bottle" (Ps 56:8). Here being-known is *tender* — the self *glad* to be seen in its straits.

**Colour 8 — known before BIRTH.** "You **formed my inward parts**… your eyes saw my **unformed substance**; in your book were written… the days **formed for me**" (Ps 139:13–16). Known *before it existed* — foreknown, not merely observed.

## Where it changes colour — the developing picture
The decisive movement is **from being-known-as-threat to being-known-as-comfort to being-known-as-invitation.** The same fact — "you know my heart" — is *dread* to the guilty (the excuse seen through, Pro 24:12), *comfort* to the afflicted (you know my distress, Ps 31:7), and finally *welcomed* by the trusting self ("search me," Ps 139:23). **What accomplishes the passage?** Ps 139 itself models it: the self that begins overwhelmed by being known (v.6 "too wonderful") ends *asking* for it (v.23) — the difference is *trust in the searcher*. Being-known becomes bearable, then desirable, when the One who knows is loved and trusted.

A second edge: **is "testing" inspection or transformation?** Colour 3 (the crucible, Pro 17:3; Ps 66:10) is *refining* — the fire that *purifies* the metal, not just reveals its content. If God's testing *changes* the self (burns off dross), then being-known is not passive exposure but an active *forming* — junctioning with restoration and forgiveness.

And a third: **the lamp within (20:27).** If the human *spirit* is "the lamp of the LORD searching the innermost," then God's searching is not only external gaze but *interior light* — the conscience *is* God's searching operating inside the person. Being-known and self-examination may meet in the conscience.

## Junctions (boundaries blurring)
- **the-heart** — the heart is what is weighed/searched/fashioned (Colours 1,4,5,8).
- **self-examination** — the human counterpart; and the lamp-within (20:27) makes them nearly one.
- **integrity-legibility** — testing as *assay* of the true inner condition.
- **forgiveness-confession** — the un-self-cleansable heart (Pro 20:9) is *exposed* by being-known and can only be *made* clean.
- **self-address** — "search me" (139:23) is a self-offering that follows self-address.
- **restoration** — if testing *refines* (Colour 3), being-known makes the self new.

## Thin / uncertain evidence (noted, not yet weighed)
- **Being-known and being-COUNTED/kept.** "You keep **count** of my tossings; my tears in your bottle, in your **book**" (Ps 56:8); "in your book were written… the days" (Ps 139:16) — being-known as being *recorded/treasured*, not just seen. A tender colour, lightly attested.
- **Tested at NIGHT.** "You have **tried my heart**, you have **visited me by night**" (Ps 17:3) — testing in the dark/solitude, when no one watches. Junctions with the purposed heart (17:3). One-two witnesses.
- **The excuse pre-empted.** Pro 24:12's "we did not know" seen through — being-known specifically *defeats self-exculpation*. A moral edge worth developing.

## Open questions (the debate, left open)
1. What turns being-known from **threat → comfort → welcome** (Ps 139's own arc)? Is it simply *trust in the searcher*?
2. Is "**testing**" inspection or a **refining that changes the self** (the crucible, 17:3; 66:10)? — i.e. does being-known *form* the self?
3. Does the **lamp within** (Pro 20:27) mean the conscience **is** God's searching operating inside the person — collapsing being-known and self-examination?
4. Are being-known-as-**judge** (weighs the excuse, 24:12) and being-known-as-**comforter** (knows my distress, 31:7) the same operation felt differently, or two?
5. Why is the **summit of the inner being** its *asking to be searched* (139:23) — is total transparency-before-God the study's endpoint?

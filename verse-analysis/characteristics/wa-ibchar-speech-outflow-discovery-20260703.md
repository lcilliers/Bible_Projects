# Speech as the outflow of the heart — a discovery document (developing)

> **Mode: DISCOVERY, not summary.** Gather the speech evidence; trace the colours; hold open whether speech is a *characteristic* or the primary *evidence-channel* for the others — and whether speech only reveals the heart or also *forms* it (and the hearer's). Registry: `ib_characteristic.code = speech-outflow`.

## Working sense (held open)
Speech as the heart *surfacing* — what is within comes out the mouth, so the lips *diagnose* the heart. But speech is also an *actor*: it kills, heals, and penetrates others. It is at once symptom and agent.

## The evidence, gathered by colour

**Colour 1 — legibility (the mouth reveals the heart).** "The **mouth of the righteous** is a fountain of life, but the **mouth of the wicked conceals violence**" (Pro 10:11); "the **tongue of the righteous** is choice silver; the **heart of the wicked** is of little worth" (Pro 10:20); "the **heart of fools proclaims folly**" (Pro 12:23); "the heart of the wise makes his speech **judicious**" (Pro 16:23); "there is **no truth in their mouth**… their throat is an open grave" (Ps 5:9). Speech as the heart made audible — the reliable *symptom* of the interior.

**Colour 2 — restraint (governing the outflow).** "When words are many, transgression is not lacking, but whoever **restrains his lips** is prudent" (Pro 10:19); "whoever **guards his mouth** preserves his life" (Pro 13:3; 21:23); "**I will muzzle my mouth**" (Ps 39:1); "**set a guard** over my mouth" (Ps 141:3). The self *holding* its outflow. (Junctions self-mastery.)

**Colour 3 — concealment / masking (the mouth HIDES the heart).** "**Fervent lips with an evil heart** are like the glaze covering an earthen vessel… when he speaks graciously, **believe him not**, for there are **seven abominations in his heart**" (Pro 26:23–25); "whoever **conceals hatred** has lying lips" (Pro 10:18); "'Eat and drink!' he says, but **his heart is not with you**" (Pro 23:7); "his **speech was smooth… but war was in his heart**" (Ps 55:21; 28:3; 62:4). Here speech *breaks* legibility — deliberately masking the heart with fair words.

**Colour 4 — the power of LIFE and DEATH.** "**Death and life are in the power of the tongue**" (Pro 18:21); "the words of a man's mouth are **deep waters**" (Pro 18:4); "a fool's mouth is **his ruin**, and his lips a **snare to his soul**" (Pro 18:7). Speech as an *agent* of the gravest outcomes.

**Colour 5 — healing / gracious words (the tongue as medicine).** "The **tongue of the wise brings healing**" (Pro 12:18); "**gracious words** are like a honeycomb, **sweetness to the soul** and health to the body" (Pro 16:24); "a **gentle tongue is a tree of life**" (Pro 15:4); "a **word in season**, how good it is!" (Pro 15:23; 25:11). Speech that *heals and sweetens* the hearer's interior.

**Colour 6 — PENETRATION (words go into the inner parts).** "The words of a **whisperer** are like delicious morsels; they **go down into the inner parts** of the body" (Pro 18:8; 26:22). Speech does not stay on the surface — it *enters* the hearer and lodges within.

**Colour 7 — the God-ward tongue.** "**Open my lips**, and my mouth will declare your praise" (Ps 51:15); "his praise shall **continually be in my mouth**" (Ps 34:1); confession — "**I acknowledged my sin**… and you forgave" (Ps 32:5). Speech turned *toward God* — praise, confession, the release of the concealed self.

## Where it changes colour — the developing picture
Speech is **both symptom and agent**, and this doubling is the discovery:
- As *symptom* (Colours 1,3) it *reveals* the heart — reliably, unless deliberately *masked*. The masking (Colour 3) is the interesting break: the wicked can *coat* an evil heart with gracious speech (the glaze over cheap clay), so the mouth *lies about* the heart. Yet the corpus insists the mask *fails* — "his wickedness will be **exposed** in the assembly" (Pro 26:26). So legibility is the *default and the end*, masking a *temporary success*.
- As *agent* (Colours 4,5,6) speech *acts on* the hearer's interior — killing, healing, penetrating "into the inner parts." This raises the deepest question: **does speech only reveal the heart, or also form it?** If a whisperer's words "go down into the inner parts" (18:8), then speech *enters and shapes* the hearer — a channel of formation-by-relation (you become what you are told). And "gracious words… health to the body" (16:24) *heal* the interior. Speech is not only the heart's exhaust but a *tool that works on other hearts*.

## Junctions (boundaries blurring)
- **integrity-legibility** — the largest junction; the masking heart (Colour 3) is where speech and integrity meet. Are they one operation seen from two sides (the condition, and its readability)?
- **the-heart** — speech is the heart surfacing (Colours 1,3).
- **self-mastery** — tongue-restraint (Colour 2) is a slice of self-government.
- **formation-by-relation** — words penetrating/forming the hearer (Colour 6) — speech as a forming channel.
- **forgiveness-confession** — confession (Colour 7) as the speech that releases.

## Thin / uncertain evidence (noted, not yet weighed)
- **Speech as DEEP WATERS.** "The words of a man's mouth are **deep waters**; the fountain of wisdom a bubbling brook" (Pro 18:4) — speech as coming *from the depths* of the self, not the surface. A colour that ties speech to the deep/opaque heart (20:5).
- **The word IN SEASON.** "A **word fitly spoken**… a word **in season**" (Pro 25:11; 15:23) — the *timing* of speech as an inner-being skill (reading the moment). Lightly gathered; junctions discernment.
- **Rash words as sword-thrusts.** "**Rash words** are like sword thrusts" (Pro 12:18) — the *unconsidered* tongue as a weapon; the failure of the pondering heart (15:28). A junction with self-mastery/the pondering heart.

## Open questions (the debate, left open)
1. Is speech a **characteristic** or the primary **evidence-channel** for the heart (the way we *read* the other operations)?
2. Does speech only **reveal** the heart, or also **form** it — and form the *hearer* (words that penetrate the inner parts, 18:8)?
3. How does **masking** (Colour 3) coexist with **legibility** (Colour 1) — is the mask always a temporary success that finally fails (26:26)?
4. Are **speech** and **integrity** one operation (the inner condition and its readability) or two?
5. Is the **God-ward tongue** (praise/confession, Colour 7) a distinct operation (speech toward God) or the same outflow turned upward?

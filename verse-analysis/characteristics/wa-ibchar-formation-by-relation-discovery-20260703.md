# Formation by trust and company — a discovery document (developing)

> **Mode: DISCOVERY, not summary.** This may not be a *characteristic* at all but a **law over the whole inner being** — the candidate organising principle of the study. Gather its evidence, test its reach (trust, company, love, beholding), and hold open whether it is one operation or the physics under all of them. Registry: `ib_characteristic.code = formation-by-relation`.

## Working sense (held open)
The self **takes the shape of what it leans on and whom it keeps near.** Not a mood or an act but a *forming relation* — the inner being is *conformed* to its objects. If this is a law rather than a characteristic, it reframes the whole map: every other operation (trust, love, teachability, company) would be a *channel* of this one forming.

## The evidence, gathered by colour

**Colour 1 — you become what you TRUST.** "Their idols are silver and gold… **those who make them become like them; so do all who trust in them**" (Ps 115:8; 135:18). Dead idols *deaden* their trusters — blind, deaf, breathless things make blind, deaf, breathless worshippers. By the same law, trusting the *living* God enlivens (implied, not stated). This is the sharpest statement: *the object of trust re-forms the truster into its own likeness.*

**Colour 2 — you become like your COMPANY.** "**Whoever walks with the wise becomes wise, but the companion of fools will suffer harm**" (Pro 13:20); "make no friendship with a man given to anger, **lest you learn his ways** and entangle yourself" (Pro 22:24–25) — dispositions are *caught*. The self is shaped by its associations.

**Colour 3 — sharpened by ANOTHER (mutual).** "**Iron sharpens iron, and one man sharpens another**" (Pro 27:17). Formation as *mutual* honing — two selves shaping each other by contact.

**Colour 4 — the refused company (choosing NOT to be formed).** "Blessed is the man who **walks not** in the counsel of the wicked, **nor stands**… **nor sits** in the seat of scoffers" (Ps 1:1); "**I do not sit** with men of falsehood… I will not sit with the wicked" (Ps 26:4–5). The self *guarding its formation* by refusing certain company — a negative exercise of the same law.

**Colour 5 — wisdom acquired reshapes from WITHIN.** "Wisdom will **come into your heart**, and knowledge will be **pleasant to your soul**" (Pro 2:10); discretion "will **watch over you**" (Pro 2:11). What is internalised *becomes* an inward agent — the self re-formed by what it took in.

**Colour 6 — TRANSMISSION across generations.** "When I was a son with my father… **he taught me** and said, 'Let your heart hold fast my words'" (Pro 4:3–4). Formation *handed down* — the inner life inherited, not self-generated.

## Where it changes colour — the developing picture
The colours are all one motion seen through different relations: **trust** (Colour 1), **company** (2,3,4), **internalised wisdom** (5), **inheritance** (6). In each, the self is *conformed* to what it relates to. The live discovery is the **reach** of the law:
- It clearly runs through **trust** (115:8) and **company** (13:20).
- It plausibly runs through **love** — the aheb doc's darkest datum, "all who hate me **love death**" (Pro 8:36), reads as *you become what you love* (love death → die; love wisdom → live). Not yet stated as a law, but the structure matches.
- It may run through **beholding/attention** — the fixed gaze (Ps 25:15; 123), the meditation that dwells (Ps 1:2; 119) — *you become what you behold* (a structure the NT will make explicit, 2 Cor 3:18; here only implicit).

If the law reaches trust *and* love *and* company *and* attention, then it is **not one characteristic among the others but the physics beneath them** — the reason trust stabilises (you take the fixity of the Rock, Ps 125:1), the reason company corrupts, the reason the word revives. **This is the candidate organising principle for the whole inner being.**

## Junctions (boundaries blurring — it may *contain* several)
- **trust-refuge** — Colour 1 is trust's forming face; trust may be the clearest *instance* of the law.
- **love-aheb** — "you become what you love" (implied, 8:36).
- **teachability / wisdom-formation** — the internalised word reshaping the self (Colour 5).
- **self-toward-others** — company as a forming relation (Colours 2–4).
- **the-heart** — the heart as the surface *inscribed/entered* and so re-formed.

## Thin / uncertain evidence (noted, not yet weighed)
- **The mechanism is unnamed.** The texts *state* the outcome (you become like them) but never *explain* the mechanism — imitation? attachment? attention? an ontological conformation? All the data are outcome-data; the *how* is dark.
- **The self can CHOOSE its formation.** Because the law works through *chosen* relations (whom you trust, whom you sit with, what you meditate on), the self holds a *lever*: refuse the wicked's seat (Ps 1:1), choose the wise's company (13:20), fix the gaze on God (25:15). If so, formation-by-relation is the **master-lever of self-formation** — the one law the self can *use*. Suggestive; needs testing.
- **Directionality.** Does the object *always* win (you always become like what you trust), or can a strong self resist a bad company? The corpus leans "the object wins" (13:20 "the companion of fools *will* suffer harm"), but the refused-company texts (Ps 1, 26) imply the self can *pre-empt* by choice, not resist mid-relation.

## Open questions (the debate, left open)
1. Is this a **characteristic** or the **law/physics** under the whole inner being? (Strongly leans *law*.)
2. What is the **mechanism** — imitation, attachment, attention, ontological conformation?
3. Does the law reach **love** and **beholding** as it reaches trust and company — i.e. *you become what you trust AND love AND behold*?
4. Is formation-by-relation the **master-lever of self-formation** — the one law the self can *use* by choosing its relations?
5. If it is the organising principle, does the whole study reduce to a single question: **to what is the inner being joined?**

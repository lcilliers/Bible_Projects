## How to read the outliers
- **Family says X, cluster says Y** — three causes: (a) my keyword family-rule mis-placed the meaning; (b) the term's `cluster_code` is off; (c) a real cross-over (a word that *means* like X but whose *term* lives in Y, e.g. a boast-word keyworded into praise but clustered Pride). Each is worth an eyeball; none is auto-wrong.
- The comparison is diagnostic, not corrective — nothing is changed here.

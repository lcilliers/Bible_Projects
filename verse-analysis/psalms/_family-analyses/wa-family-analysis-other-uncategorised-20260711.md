## 1. Coherence — does the label fit?

**No — and it is not meant to.** "other-uncategorised" is a keyword-less residue, and the data confirms it fuses many unrelated inner-being movements. The single measurable through-line is formal, not thematic: all 55 are poetic/wisdom, all role=`characteristic`, all bearer-inferred, and (once swaps are corrected) all locate to the standard interior/external code set. Thematically the bucket spans at least the whole polarity of the Psalter's inner life — from covenant treachery and moral corruption through affliction and somatic collapse to praise, worship-approach and devotion to God's word. That 28 instances already carry 14 distinct M-codes while 27 carry none is itself the finding: **the residue is a thin cross-section of nearly the entire Psalms IB range, no one movement dominant.** Below I group them into the micro-clusters the data shows; residual singletons are listed honestly as residue.

## The reading

**I. Blessing flows outward — received to be spread (vv.1–4).**
"May God be **gracious** (H2603, M39) to us and **bless** (H1288, M39) us and make his **face to shine** upon us… **that** your way may be **known** on earth… Let the nations be **glad** (H8055, M04) and **sing for joy** (H7442, M42)" (vv.1–4). The finding: the blessing received by the inner being is **instrumental, not terminal** — "bless us **that** your way may be known" *(stated)*. The operation: the self is blessed **so that the blessing may flow outward** — the inner being's reception of grace is oriented toward **others' knowing and gladness**, not held as its own possession *(inferred: the purpose-clause "that your way may be known… let the nations be glad" makes the blessing centrifugal — received to be spread)*. And the fruit is a **widening gladness** — the nations *glad and singing for joy* *(stated)*.

**II. The shining face upon the self (v.1).**
"Make his **face to shine** upon us" (v.1) — the lit face of the Aaronic blessing (cf. Ps 31:16, 4:6). The finding: the inner being's blessedness is being **under God's *shining* face** — the illuminated favour resting on it *(stated)*; a brief but real datum in the light-of-the-face thread.

## What Psalm 67 says about the inner being
- **Blessing received is *centrifugal* (vv.1–4).** The inner being is blessed *so that* the blessing flows outward — grace received is oriented toward others' knowing and gladness, not held as its own possession. Its fruit is a widening joy among the nations.
- **The blessed self is under the *shining* face (v.1).** God's lit, favourable face resting on the inner being (with Ps 31:16, 4:6).

## The reading

**I. The self tested and smelted like silver (vv.10–12).**
"For you, O God, have **tested** (H0974, *bachan*) us; you have **tried** (H6884, *tsaraph* — smelted) us **as silver is tried**. You brought us into the net; you laid a **crushing burden** (H4157, M03) on our backs; you let men ride over our heads; we went through **fire** and through **water**; **yet you have brought us out to a place of abundance**" (vv.10–12). The gem-finding: the trials are **God's *refining* of the inner being** — *tsaraph*, the smelting of silver *(stated)*. The operation: the affliction is **purposive, not random** — the inner being is *passed through* fire and water **as a refining process**, and the process has an **outcome: abundance** *(inferred: "tried as silver… yet brought out to abundance" frames the trial as a smelting with a purposed end, not mere suffering)*. The self is **refined**, not merely afflicted *(inferred; cf. Ps 12:6's refined silver, 17:3's night-testing — here the *self* is the metal)*.

**II. Cherished iniquity in the heart blocks being heard (vv.16–18).**
"Come and hear… I will tell what he has done for my **soul** (H5315, the seat — M47). I **cried** to him with my mouth… If I had **cherished** (H7200, *ra'ah* — regarded, kept looking upon [with favour]) **iniquity** (H0205, M10, *aven*) in my **heart** (H3820, the seat — M47), the Lord would **not have listened** (H8085)" (vv.16–18). The gem-finding: being-heard is **conditional on the heart** — *if* iniquity were **cherished** in the heart, God would **not listen** *(stated)*. The operation: **harboured, un-renounced iniquity in the heart *blocks* the prayer** *(inferred)*. And the verb is precise: *ra'ah* — not merely *having* sin but **regarding it, keeping it in view with favour**, *harbouring* it *(inferred: "cherished" = to look upon/regard, so the block is the heart's *attachment* to the sin, not the sin's mere presence)*. The finding is a **mechanism of communion**: the heart's **secret harbouring of sin closes the channel** — the cry does not get through while the heart cherishes iniquity *(inferred: the "if I had cherished… he would not have listened / but truly he has listened" makes the un-cherishing the condition of the answered prayer)*.

## What Psalm 66 says about the inner being
- **Affliction can be a *purposive refining* of the self (vv.10–12).** The inner being is *tested and smelted like silver* — passed through fire and water as a refining process with a purposed end (abundance). The self is refined, not merely afflicted.
- **Being heard is conditional on the heart (v.18).** *Cherished* iniquity — sin *regarded, kept in view with favour, harboured* — blocks the prayer. The block is the heart's *attachment* to the sin, not the sin's mere presence; the secret harbouring closes the channel of communion.
- **The testimony centres on the *soul* (v.16).** "What he has done for my soul" — the inner being is the beneficiary of the refining and the answered prayer.

# Psalm 51 — what it tells us about the inner being (Phase-2 chapter reading)

- **Source:** the `lexical-model-2026` Phase-1 lexicals for Psa 51:1–19 (chapter-driven poetic build; roles sanity-checked). Complete chapter. Lexicals only; cited; grounded; inferences tagged *stated* | *inferred*.
- **Lens:** the finding is the inner-being **operation**, God-relation as arena (`feedback_lens_is_inner_being_process_not_god_relation`).
- **The re-creation of the inner being's core — a cornerstone psalm.** Psalm 51's findings run deeper into the inner being than any so far: sin **owned to its origin** (vv.3–5); God's concern for **truth in the *hidden* parts** (v.6); the plea to **CREATE** (*bara*) a clean heart — the core **cannot be self-repaired, only re-made** (v.10); the **spirit** renewed, sustained, and offered (vv.10–12, 17); and the **broken and contrite heart** as the offering God *receives* (vv.16–17). The whole is the mechanics of an inner being that cannot cleanse itself and is re-created from outside.

## The reading

**I. Sin owned to its depths — before the self, against God, from birth (vv.3–5).**
"For I **know** (H3045) my transgressions, and my **sin** (H2403, M10) is **ever before me**. **Against you, you only**, have I **sinned** (H2398, M10)… I was brought forth in **iniquity** (H5771, M10), and in **sin** did my mother conceive me" (vv.3–5). Three moves of full acknowledgment: the sin held **constantly in view** ("ever before me"); referred to its **ultimate object** ("against you only"); and traced to its **origin** ("brought forth in iniquity") *(stated)*. The finding: true owning is not admitting an act but **keeping the sin before one's own eyes, referring it to God as the one wronged, and recognising it as a condition reaching to the beginning** — the inner being's corruption is *constitutional*, not merely occasional *(inferred: the "ever before me… against you only… from conception" deepens sin from deed to condition-from-origin)*.

**II. God delights in truth in the *hidden* parts (v.6).**
"Behold, you **delight in truth** (H0571, M13, *emet*) in the **inward being** (*tuchot* — the hidden interior), and you **teach me wisdom** in the **secret heart** (H5640, *satum* — the sealed/hidden place)" (v.6). The gem-finding: God's concern is **not the surface but the innermost** — *truth in the hidden parts* — and he **teaches wisdom *there*, in the sealed interior** *(stated)*. The operation: God **works at the level of the secret heart**, the place hidden even from others; what he wants is integrity in the depths the self barely sees, and that is where his instruction lands *(inferred: the locus of both the delight (truth) and the teaching (wisdom) is the *hidden/sealed* interior)*.

**III. CREATE a clean heart — the core requires divine creation, not self-repair (v.10).**
"**Create** (H1254, *bara* — the verb reserved for God's making-from-nothing) in me a **clean heart** (H3820), O God, and **renew** (H2318, *chadash*) a right **spirit** (H7307, the seat) within me" (v.10). The central finding: the clean heart **cannot be self-generated — it must be *created*** *(stated: the verb is *bara*, used elsewhere only of divine creation)*. The operation: the inner being asks God to **create, not clean** — implying the old heart is **beyond mere washing; it needs a new making** *(inferred: choosing *bara* over a repair-verb signals the heart is un-reformable by the self and requires a creative act)*. The self **cannot manufacture its own clean heart**; the transformation of its core is a work only God can do *(inferred)*. Paired with it: *renew* (*chadash*) a steadfast **spirit** — the spirit's re-establishment *(stated)*.

**IV. The spirit in its states — holy, willing, broken (vv.11–12, 17).**
The *ruach* (spirit) recurs across the psalm in distinct states: "take not your **Holy Spirit** (H6944, M22, seat=spirit) from me" (v.11) — the sustaining divine spirit; "uphold me with a **willing spirit** (H5081, M09, *nadib* — free, generous, consenting)" (v.12) — the inner being's **freely-consenting disposition** that holds it upright; and "a **broken spirit** (H7665, *shabar*)" (v.17) — the shattered offering *(stated)*. The finding: the inner being's **spirit is worked on multiple registers at once** — it needs God's holy spirit to *remain*, a willing spirit to *uphold*, and is accepted as a *broken* spirit *(inferred: the three ruach-states name what the spirit receives, becomes, and offers)*. The *willing/nadib* spirit is notable — restoration includes a **freely-consenting** inner disposition, not mere compliance *(inferred: nadib is the free/generous will, so the upheld self *consents*)*.

**V. The broken and contrite heart as the offering God receives (vv.16–17).**
"For you will **not delight in sacrifice**… The sacrifices of God are a **broken spirit** (H7665); a **broken and contrite** (H1794, M24, *dakah* — crushed) **heart** (H3820), O God, you will **not despise**" (vv.16–17). The gem-finding: the acceptable offering is **not ritual but a *shattered inner being*** — a broken spirit, a crushed heart *(stated)*. The operation: the inner being **offers its own brokenness**, and *that* is what God wants — the crushed heart is **not rejected but welcomed** ("you will not despise") *(inferred: the sacrifices "of God" are redefined as the broken spirit — brokenness is the offering, not a disqualification)*. This joins Ps 34:18 (the crushed spirit *drawn near*): the shattered inner being is precisely the acceptable thing *(inferred)*.

**VI. The restored joy overflows into teaching and praise (vv.8, 12–15).**
"Let me hear **joy** (H8342, M04) and gladness; let the **bones** that you have **broken** (H1794, *dakah*) **rejoice**… Restore to me the **joy** of your salvation… **Then I will teach** (H3925) transgressors your ways… my **tongue** will **sing aloud** (H7442, M42); open my **lips**, and my mouth will declare your praise" (vv.8, 12–15). The finding: the re-created inner being's joy **does not terminate in itself — it overflows into teaching others and praise** *(stated: "then I will teach transgressors… my tongue will sing")*. The restoration is **generative**: the cleansed self becomes an instructor of sinners and a voice of praise (cf. Ps 40's un-concealing, 32's confession issuing in teaching) *(inferred: the "then" makes the ministry-to-others the fruit of the restoration)*. And even the *broken bones* are made to **rejoice** — the very parts God broke are the parts restored to gladness *(stated)*.

## What Psalm 51 says about the inner being
- **Sin is owned to its origin, not just its act (vv.3–5).** True acknowledgment keeps the sin before one's own eyes, refers it to God as the one wronged, and recognises it as a condition reaching to conception — corruption constitutional, not occasional.
- **God's concern is *truth in the hidden parts* (v.6).** Not the surface but the sealed interior — and he teaches wisdom *there*, in the depths the self barely sees.
- **The clean heart must be *created*, not self-repaired (v.10).** *Bara* — the inner being asks God to *make*, not clean; the old heart is beyond washing, and the self cannot manufacture its own clean core. The transformation is a divine creative act.
- **The spirit is worked on several registers (vv.11–12, 17).** It needs God's holy spirit to *remain*, a *willing* (freely-consenting) spirit to *uphold*, and is accepted as a *broken* spirit. Restoration includes a consenting will, not mere compliance.
- **The broken and contrite heart is the offering God *receives* (vv.16–17).** Not ritual but a shattered inner being — the crushed heart is welcomed, not despised (with Ps 34:18). Brokenness offered *is* the sacrifice.
- **The re-created joy overflows into teaching and praise (vv.12–15).** The cleansed self becomes generative — an instructor of sinners, a voice of praise; even the broken bones are made to rejoice.

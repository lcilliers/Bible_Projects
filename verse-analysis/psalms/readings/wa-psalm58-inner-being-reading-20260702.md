## The reading

**I. The heart is the workshop of injustice (v.2).**
"No, in your **hearts** (H3820, the seat — M47) you **devise** (H6466, *pa'al* — work, fabricate) **wrongs** (H5766, M10, *avel*); your hands deal out **violence** on earth" (v.2). The operation: injustice is **manufactured in the heart *first*** — the heart *works* the wrong, and only then do the hands *deal it out* *(stated: in the heart wrongs are devised; the hands dispense them)*. The finding: the **heart is the workshop** — the inner being fabricates the wrong inwardly before it becomes outward violence; the deed is the heart's output *(inferred: the "hearts devise… hands deal out" sequence makes the heart the origin, the hands the delivery)*.

**II. Disorder from the womb — corruption at origin (v.3).**
"The wicked are **estranged** (H2114, *zur* — made strange, alienated) from the **womb**; they **go astray** (H8582, *ta'ah*) from **birth**, speaking **lies**" (v.3). The finding: the wicked's disorder reaches **all the way to origin** — *estranged from the womb, astray from birth* *(stated)*. The operation: the inner being's corruption is presented as **congenital, not merely acquired** — a strangeness present from the beginning *(inferred: "from the womb… from birth" locates the estrangement at origin)*. (The same depth Ps 51:5 owns of *itself* — "in sin conceived" — here diagnosed of the *wicked* from outside; the penitent's confession and the diagnosis meet at the origin-point.)

**III. The willful self-deafness — the adder that stops its own ear (vv.4–5).**
"They have **venom** (H2534, M02, *chemah* — the same word as *wrath*) like the venom of a serpent, like the **deaf** (H2795) **adder** that **stops its ear** (H0241), so that it does **not hear** the voice of **charmers**" (vv.4–5). Two findings. First, the venom is *chemah* — **the serpent's poison is *stored wrath*** (the M02 anger word): the inner being's malice is a treasured heat *(inferred: chemah names the venom as wrath, so the poison is the heart's stored anger)*. Second, the gem: the wicked inner being **makes itself deaf** — like an adder **stopping its own ear** against the charmer's voice *(stated)*. The operation: a **willful, self-induced deafness** — the inner being **actively closes the faculty of hearing** against what would correct or charm it *(inferred: the adder "stops its ear so that it does not hear" — a deliberate self-sealing)*. This is the exact inverse of Ps 40's **"dug/opened ear"** (God-given receptivity): here the ear is **self-stopped** — the refusal of instruction as an active operation *(inferred)*.

## What Psalm 58 says about the inner being
- **The heart is the workshop of injustice (v.2).** Wrongs are *devised* in the heart before the hands deal them out — the deed is the heart's manufactured output, not a first cause.
- **The wicked's disorder reaches to origin (v.3).** Estranged from the womb, astray from birth — corruption presented as congenital, not merely acquired. (The penitent owns this of himself in Ps 51:5; here it is diagnosed of the wicked.)
- **The inner being can make itself *deaf* (vv.4–5).** Like an adder stopping its own ear against the charmer, the wicked self *actively closes* the faculty of hearing against correction — a willful self-deafening, the inverse of Ps 40's opened ear.
- **Malice is *stored wrath* (v.4).** The venom is *chemah* — the poison the inner being carries is treasured heat/anger.

## The reading

**I. Joy in God overflowing to the private hours (vv.2, 5).**
"Let Israel be **glad** (H8055, M04) in his **Maker**; let the children of Zion **rejoice** in their King!… Let the godly **exult** in glory; let them **sing for joy on their beds**" (vv.2, 5). The gem-finding: the joy in God is so full it **sings even *on their beds*** — in the private, resting, night hours *(stated)*. The operation: the inner being's gladness in God **overflows past public worship into the *intimate and hidden*** — "on their beds" locates the joy where no one sees, at rest, in the night; a delight that does not need an audience but sings in solitude *(inferred: "let them sing for joy on their beds" carries the joy into the private place — the self so glad in its Maker that it exults even lying down, alone; the gladness has soaked through to the intimate hours, cf. Ps 42:8 "at night his song is with me", 63:6 "on my bed")*.

**II. God's pleasure in his people; the humble adorned (v.4).**
"For the LORD **takes pleasure in his people**; he **adorns the humble** (H6035, M24) with **salvation**" (v.4). The gem-finding: God *delights* in his people and **beautifies the humble** with salvation *(stated)*. The operation: the inner being's ***humility* is what God *adorns*** — the lowly self is not despised but *decorated / crowned* with salvation; God's pleasure rests on his people, and his beautifying grace falls on *humility* *(inferred: "he adorns the humble with salvation" makes the humble inner being the object of God's *beautifying* — the self that is lowly is clothed with the ornament of salvation; humility is the condition God delights to dignify, cf. Ps 147:11, 138:6, 1 Pet 5:5 "God… gives grace to the humble")*.

## What Psalm 149 says about the inner being
- **Joy in God *overflows to the private hours* (vv.2, 5).** So full is the gladness in its Maker that the self sings "on their beds" — the joy soaked through past public worship into the intimate, resting, night hours; delight that needs no audience.
- **God *adorns the humble* (v.4).** The lowly self is not despised but *beautified* — clothed with salvation; humility is the condition God delights to dignify, his pleasure resting on his people.

# Psalm 40 — what it tells us about the inner being (Phase-2 chapter reading)

- **Source:** the `lexical-model-2026` Phase-1 lexicals for Psa 40:1–17 (chapter-driven poetic build; roles sanity-checked). Complete chapter. Lexicals only; cited; grounded; inferences tagged *stated* | *inferred*.
- **Lens:** the finding is the inner-being **operation**, God-relation as arena (`feedback_lens_is_inner_being_process_not_god_relation`).
- **The receptive and un-concealing inner being.** Psalm 40's findings are operations of **receptivity** and **release**: sustained waiting issuing in a **given** new song (vv.1–3); the **opened ("dug") ear** — a God-given receptivity that produces delight-in-will and the **internalised law**, over external ritual (vv.6–8); and the deliberate **un-concealing** of the good held in the heart (vv.9–10) — the inverse of guilty concealment. Set against these, the **heart that fails** under the weight of accumulated iniquity (v.12).

## The reading

**I. Sustained waiting issues in a *given* new song (vv.1–3).**
"I **waited patiently** (H6960, M17 — the verb *qavah* doubled, 'waiting I waited') for the LORD; he inclined to me and heard my cry. He **drew me up** from the pit… set my feet upon a rock… He **put a new song** in my mouth" (vv.1–3). The operation-sequence: **intensive, sustained waiting** → being lifted from the pit → **a new song given** *(stated)*. The finding: the delivered inner being's fresh utterance is **received, not self-generated** — the song is *put in* the mouth *(inferred: "he put a new song" makes the utterance a gift, not a self-production)*. And it **propagates**: "Many will see and **fear** (H3372, M01) and put their **trust** (H0982, M19)" — one inner being's given song becomes the seed of others' trust *(stated)*.

**II. The opened ear — receptivity as God's act on the inner being (vv.6–8).**
"In **sacrifice** and offering you have **not delighted**, but you have given me an **open ear** (lit. 'ears you have *dug* for me')… I **delight** (H2655, M04, *chaphets*) to do your **will** (H7522, M29, *ratson*), O my God; your **law** (H8451) is **within my heart** (H3820, the seat — M47)" (vv.6–8). The gem-operation: what God desires is not ritual but an **opened, hearing inner being** — "ears you have *dug*" — a receptivity that God himself **works** into the self *(stated: the ear is *given/dug*, not self-made)*. And that opened ear **issues in** two things: **delight to do the will**, and the **law internalised** ("within my heart") *(stated)*. The finding: the inner being's true offering is a **received receptivity** producing delight-in-God's-will and an inward law — interior obedience over external sacrifice *(inferred: the dug-ear, the delight, and the inward law form one sequence that displaces sacrifice)*.

**III. The un-concealing operation — the good in the heart deliberately released (vv.9–10).**
"I have **not restrained** (H3607) my lips… I have **not hidden** (H3680) your **deliverance** (H6666, M26, seat=heart) **within my heart**; I have spoken of your faithfulness… I have **not concealed** (H3582) your **steadfast love** (H2617, M05)" (vv.9–10). A fourfold refusal to keep the good locked inside: not restrained, not hidden, not concealed *(stated)*. The gem: the inner being **deliberately does *not* hide God's deliverance "within the heart"** — the treasure held inside is **meant to be released, not hoarded** *(inferred: the "not hidden within my heart" makes the heart's good a thing to externalise)*. This is the **inverse of guilty concealment** (Ps 32's silence, Ps 28:3's wicked who hide evil in the heart): here the operation is to *un*-conceal the *good* *(inferred)*.

**IV. The heart fails under the weight of iniquity (v.12).**
"For evils have encompassed me beyond number; my **iniquities** (H5771, M10) have overtaken me, and **I cannot see**; they are more than the hairs of my head; my **heart** (H3820, M47) **fails** (H5800, *azab* — forsakes) me" (v.12). The counter-operation: under **accumulated iniquity**, the inner being's sight goes ("I cannot see") and the **heart deserts** — *azab*, the heart *forsakes/abandons* the self *(stated)*. The finding: guilt's mass (as in Ps 38) **collapses the inner being's own courage** — the heart, the seat of resolve, gives out from within *(inferred: the heart "forsaking me" is the interior vitality deserting under the load)*.

**V. Two dispositions of the seeking self (vv.16–17).**
"But may all who **seek** you **rejoice** and be **glad** in you; may those who **love** your salvation say continually, 'Great is the LORD!'… I am **poor and needy**, but the LORD **takes thought** for me" (vv.16–17). The close names the disposition the whole psalm has enacted: the **seeking, God-loving inner being** whose settled note is gladness, even while "poor and needy" *(stated)*. The finding: the receptive/un-concealing operations resolve into a self that is **needy yet glad** — its lack and its gladness held together, because it is *thought of* *(inferred)*.

## What Psalm 40 says about the inner being
- **The delivered self's new song is *given*, not self-made (vv.1–3).** Sustained waiting issues in a song *put in* the mouth — and that given song propagates trust in others.
- **Receptivity is God's act on the inner being (v.6).** The "dug ear" — a hearing self worked by God — is what God desires over sacrifice; receptivity precedes and enables obedience.
- **The opened ear produces delight-in-will and an inward law (vv.6–8).** The true offering is interior: delight to do God's will and the law held *within the heart*, not external ritual.
- **The good held in the heart is meant to be *released* (vv.9–10).** The inner being deliberately does not hide God's deliverance within it — a fourfold refusal to conceal. The inverse of guilty concealment: un-hiding the good.
- **Accumulated iniquity collapses the heart's own courage (v.12).** The heart *forsakes* the self and sight fails — guilt's mass deserts the inner seat of resolve from within.
- **The receptive self is needy yet glad (vv.16–17).** Lack and gladness held together in the seeking, God-loving inner being, because it is thought of.

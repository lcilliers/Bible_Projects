# Psalm 63 — what it tells us about the inner being (Phase-2 chapter reading)

- **Source:** the `lexical-model-2026` Phase-1 lexicals for Psa 63:1–11 (chapter-driven poetic build; roles sanity-checked). Complete chapter. Lexicals only; cited; grounded; inferences tagged *stated* | *inferred*.
- **Lens:** the finding is the inner-being **operation**, God-relation as arena (`feedback_lens_is_inner_being_process_not_god_relation`).
- **From whole-being thirst to satisfaction to clinging.** Psalm 63 traces a **trajectory of the inner being toward God**: a **total, bodily thirst** (soul *and* flesh, v.1); the **re-valuation of God's love above life itself** (v.3); the thirst resolving into **satisfaction** as at a rich feast (v.5); **night-meditation** on the bed (v.6); and the soul **clinging/cleaving** to God (v.8). The movement runs *craving → filling → adhering*.

## The reading

**I. The whole-being thirst — soul *and* flesh (v.1).**
"O God, you are my God; earnestly I **seek** (H7836, *shachar* — seek at dawn) you; my **soul** (H5315, the seat — M47) **thirsts** (H6770) for you; my **flesh** (H1320, seat=flesh, *basar*) **faints** (H3642) for you, as in a **dry and weary land where there is no water**" (v.1). The finding: the desire for God engages the **whole person** — not the soul alone but the **flesh faints** too *(stated)*. The operation: the craving is a **total, bodily thirst** — the entire self, soul and body, is the *desert-thirst*, a survival-appetite in a waterless land *(inferred: the soul *thirsts* and the flesh *faints* — the whole organism craves as a body deprived of water; cf. Ps 42's deer-panting)*.

**II. Steadfast love valued *above life* (v.3).**
"Because your **steadfast love** (H2617, M05) is **better than life** (H2416, M25), my lips will praise you" (v.3). The gem-finding: the inner being holds God's *chesed* as **more valuable than life itself** — the most basic good is *outranked* *(stated)*. The operation: a **re-ordering of the value-hierarchy** — the inner being can esteem *something above its own life*, and here that something is God's steadfast love *(inferred: "better than life" is a supreme re-valuation, placing chesed at the top of the self's value-order)*.

**III. Thirst resolves into satisfaction (vv.4–5).**
"So I will **bless** you as long as I live; in your name I will **lift up my hands**. My **soul** (H5315, M47) will be **satisfied** (H7646, *saba*) as with **fat and rich food**, and my mouth will praise you with joyful lips" (vv.4–5). The gem-finding: the *thirst/faint* of v.1 **resolves into fullness** — the soul **fed and satisfied** as at a rich feast *(stated)*. The operation: the movement is **craving → filling** — the inner being's appetite, having sought God, is **met and filled to satisfaction** *(inferred: the same soul that *thirsts* in v.1 is *satisfied as with fat* in v.5 — the desert-thirst answered by a feast)*. (Contrast the never-filled prowling of Ps 59:15 — there the appetite growls unmet; here it is filled.)

**IV. Night-meditation on the bed (v.6).**
"When I **remember** (H2142, M41) you upon my **bed**, and **meditate** (H1897, M42, *hagah*) on you in the **watches of the night**" (v.6). The finding: the **bed and the night-watches are the place of God-ward meditation** — the inner being *remembers* and *ponders* God through the sleepless hours *(stated)*. The operation: night-meditation as a **God-ward** act (*hagah* on God) — the same bed that is the wicked's *plotting*-workshop (Ps 36:4) is here the **place of remembering God** *(inferred: the bed/night, elsewhere the workshop of evil devising, is turned to God-ward meditation; cf. Ps 16:7's night-instructed heart)*.

**V. The soul *clings* to God (vv.7–8).**
"For you have been my help, and in the shadow of your **wings** I will sing for joy. My **soul** (H5315, M47) **clings** (H1692, *dabaq* — cleaves, adheres) to you; your right hand **upholds** (H8551, *tamak*) me" (vv.7–8). The gem-finding: the trajectory ends in **cleaving** — the soul *dabaq* to God, the same verb for a man cleaving to his wife (Gen 2:24) *(stated)*. The operation: the inner being **adheres/clings** to God — an active *holding-fast* — and it is **mutual**: the soul clings *while* God's hand *upholds* *(inferred: "my soul clings… your right hand upholds" pairs the self's cleaving with God's sustaining — the clinging is held from the other side)*. The craving (v.1), once satisfied (v.5), becomes **attachment** (v.8) *(inferred)*.

## What Psalm 63 says about the inner being
- **Desire for God is a *whole-being*, bodily thirst (v.1).** Soul *and* flesh crave — the entire self is the desert-thirst, a survival-appetite in a waterless land.
- **God's love can be valued *above life itself* (v.3).** The inner being re-orders its value-hierarchy, placing *chesed* over its own most basic good — the supreme re-valuation.
- **Thirst resolves into *satisfaction* (v.5).** The craving, having sought God, is met and filled as at a rich feast — the soul satisfied "as with fat." (Contrast the never-filled appetite of the God-less, Ps 59:15.)
- **The bed and night are turned to God-ward meditation (v.6).** The inner being *remembers* and *ponders* God through the sleepless watches — the same place that is the wicked's plotting-workshop is here the place of remembering God.
- **The trajectory ends in *clinging* (v.8).** The soul *cleaves* (*dabaq*) to God — an active holding-fast — and it is mutual: the soul clings *while* God's hand upholds. Craving → filling → adhering.

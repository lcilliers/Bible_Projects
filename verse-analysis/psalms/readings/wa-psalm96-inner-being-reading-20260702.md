## The reading

**I. Worship as *ascribing* — rendering, not augmenting (vv.7–9).**
"**Ascribe** (H3051, *yahav* — give, render) to the LORD, O families of the peoples, ascribe to the LORD **glory** (H3519, M22) and **strength** (H5797, M23)! Ascribe to the LORD the glory **due his name**… **Worship** the LORD in the **splendour** (H1927, M22) of **holiness** (H6944, M22); **tremble** before him" (vv.7–9). The finding: the inner being's worship is **ascription** — *rendering* to God the glory that is *due his name*, not adding to him *(stated)*. The operation: to *ascribe* (*yahav*) is to **acknowledge and give back** what is already God's — worship as **recognition and self-abasement** (the *trembling* before him), not contribution *(inferred: "ascribe… the glory *due* his name… tremble before him" makes worship the rendering of what is owed, with awe, not an increase of God; the same finding as Ps 29:1–2, and worship "in the splendour of holiness" bows before beauty as well as majesty)*.

## What Psalm 96 says about the inner being
- **Worship is *ascription* — rendering, not augmenting (vv.7–9).** The inner being *gives back* to God the glory *due his name*, with trembling awe — worship as recognition and self-abasement before God's splendour and holiness, not a contribution to him. (The Ps 29:1–2 finding, restated.)

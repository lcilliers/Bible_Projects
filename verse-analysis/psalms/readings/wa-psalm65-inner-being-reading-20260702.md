## The reading

**I. Praise as a silent waiting (v.1).**
"**Praise** (H8416, M22) is **due** (H1747, *dumiyah* — silence, silent waiting) to you, O God, in Zion" (v.1). The finding: praise here is a **poised, silent readiness** — *dumiyah*, the same silence-word as Ps 62:1's waiting soul *(stated)*. The operation: the inner being's praise **waits in stillness** for God — a readiness held in silence before it sounds *(inferred: dumiyah frames the praise as a waiting quiet, not yet noise)*.

**II. Iniquities *overpower* the self — answered by atonement (v.3).**
"When **iniquities prevail** (H1396, M23, *gabar* — overpower, are too strong) against me, you **atone** (H3722, M38, *kaphar* — cover) for our **transgressions** (H6588, M10)" (v.3). The gem-finding: the inner being's iniquities can be **stronger than the self can master** — they *prevail*, *overpower* *(stated)*. And the resolution comes **from outside**: God *atones/covers* *(stated)*. The operation: sin as **more than the inner being can handle** → **God's atonement**, not the self's effort *(inferred: "iniquities *prevail* against me… *you* atone" places the mastery of sin beyond the self and in God)*. The self does not overcome its own overwhelming iniquity; it is *covered* *(inferred; cf. Ps 40:12 "iniquities overtook me", 38's crushing weight — here the resolution named as atonement)*.

**III. The chosen self brought *near*, and satisfied (v.4).**
"**Blessed** is the one you **choose** (H0977, M37, *bachar*) and **bring near** (H7126, *qarab*), to **dwell** in your courts! We shall be **satisfied** (H7646, *saba*) with the goodness of your house" (v.4). The finding: the inner being's blessedness is a **three-fold movement — chosen, brought near, satisfied** *(stated)*. The operation: the self is **drawn near** (not self-approaching but *brought*) and there **filled** — "satisfied with the goodness of your house" *(inferred: "you choose and bring near… we shall be satisfied" makes the nearness God-initiated and the satisfaction its fruit; the satisfaction thread, Ps 17:15, 63:5)*.

## What Psalm 65 says about the inner being
- **Praise can be a *silent waiting* (v.1).** *Dumiyah* — a poised readiness held in stillness before it sounds; praise as a waiting quiet (with Ps 62:1).
- **Iniquities can *overpower* the self, and are covered by God's atonement (v.3).** Sin can be stronger than the inner being can master ("prevail against me"); its resolution is God's *atoning/covering*, not the self's effort. The self does not overcome its own overwhelming iniquity — it is covered.
- **The blessed self is *chosen, brought near, satisfied* (v.4).** A God-initiated movement — the inner being is *drawn* near (not self-approaching) and there *filled* with the goodness of God's house.

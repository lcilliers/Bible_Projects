- **The firm, steady heart that does not dread — and the melting heart of the wicked as its foil.** Psalm 112 (acrostic companion to 111) portrays the one who fears the LORD. Its inner-being findings: **fear of the LORD issuing in *delight* in his commandments** (v.1); the **firm/steady heart that will *not be afraid*, because it trusts** (vv.7–8); and, as foil, the **wicked's *melting* heart of angry, thwarted desire** (v.10).

## The reading

**I. Fear of the LORD issuing in delight (v.1).**
"Praise the LORD! Blessed is the man who **fears** (H3373, M01) the LORD, who **greatly delights** (H2654, M04) in his commandments!" (v.1). The gem-finding (extending 111:10): the one who *fears* the LORD **greatly *delights*** in his commandments — reverent fear and glad delight *held together* *(stated)*. The operation: the inner being's fear **is not dread-of but *delight-in*** the LORD's commands — the fear that begins wisdom (111:10) *matures into pleasure* in God's will, the same self that reveres also *loves* the commandments *(inferred: "fears the LORD… greatly delights in his commandments" joins awe and delight — the fear is not aversive but issues in love for God's word; cf. Ps 1:2, 119:16,47)*.

**II. The firm/steady heart that will not be afraid — because it trusts (vv.7–8).**
"He is **not afraid** (H3372, M01) of **bad news**; his **heart** (H3820, M47) is **firm** (H3559 — *nakon*, established/fixed), **trusting** (H0982, M19) in the LORD. His heart is **steady** (H5564 — *samuk*, supported/upheld); he will not be afraid, until he looks in triumph on his adversaries" (vv.7–8). The gem-finding: the heart is **firm and steady** — *established, upheld* — and therefore **unafraid even of bad news**, because it *trusts* *(stated)*. The operation: the inner being is **stabilised against fear *by trust*** — the "firm/steady heart" is not native fearlessness but a heart *anchored* in the LORD; because it leans on God it does not lurch at bad news, it holds *(inferred: "not afraid of bad news; his heart is firm, trusting… his heart is steady" makes the fearlessness a *derived* stability — the heart is fixed *because* it trusts, upheld *because* it leans on God; the one who fears the LORD fears nothing else; cf. Ps 27:3, 46:2, 57:7)*.

**III. The foil — the wicked's *melting* heart of thwarted desire (v.10).**
"The wicked man sees it and is **angry** (H3707, M02); he **gnashes his teeth** and **melts away** (H4549 — *masas*); the **desire** of the wicked will perish!" (v.10). The finding: over against the steady heart, the wicked's inner being is **angry, gnashing, *melting away*** — its *desire* coming to nothing *(stated)*. The operation: the un-anchored inner being **dissolves** — where the trusting heart is *firm* (nakon), the wicked heart *melts* (masas); the contrast is exact, and the wicked's very *desire* perishes *(inferred: the "melts away… desire will perish" is the deliberate antonym of the "firm/steady" heart of vv.7–8 — the self not anchored in trust has no inner stability, it dissolves in impotent rage; cf. Ps 58:7, 68:2)*.

## What Psalm 112 says about the inner being
- **Fear of the LORD *matures into delight* (v.1).** The reverent fear that begins wisdom issues in *glad pleasure* in God's commandments — the self that reveres also loves God's word; awe and delight held together.
- **Trust *stabilises the heart against fear* (vv.7–8).** The firm, steady heart is not native fearlessness but a heart *established and upheld by trust* — it does not dread bad news because it leans on the LORD; the one who fears God fears nothing else.
- **The un-anchored heart *melts* (v.10).** The exact foil: where the trusting heart is *firm*, the wicked heart *melts away* in angry, thwarted desire — no trust, no inner stability, it dissolves.

## Honest notes
- **A precise firm-vs-melting contrast:** the psalm builds its whole anthropology on *nakon* (established) vs *masas* (melting) — inner stability is a function of *trust*, and its absence a function of its lack. A clean datum on what *anchors* the inner being (with Ps 111:10, 27:1–3, 46).
- **Scope honesty:** the middle (vv.2–6, 9 — descendants blessed, wealth, generosity, righteousness enduring) describes the *blessed life* of the God-fearer (the arena/fruit); the inner-being weight is the fearing-delighting heart (v.1) and the firm/steady/unafraid heart vs the melting one (vv.7–8, 10).

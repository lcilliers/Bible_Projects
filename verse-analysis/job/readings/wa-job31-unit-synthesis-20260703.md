## JOB-31-A · Job 31:1–12 · [D, multi, thread] — the covenant with the eyes; guarded desire

**The reading.** Job's great final oath begins not with deeds but with the *look*: "I have made a **covenant with my eyes**; how then could I **gaze at a virgin**?" (v.1). He knows God "**sees my ways** and numbers all my steps" (v.4). The finding is **integrity governed at its source — the eyes, before desire becomes act** *(stated)*. The operation is **the self policing the gaze itself, cutting off lust at the point of looking** *(inferred: "a covenant with my eyes" (v.1) is a remarkable inner-being discipline — Job binds not just his conduct but his *attention*, recognising that the eye is where illicit desire begins (cf. Ecc 6:9 "the sight of the eyes", and the covetous eye); the finding is that true integrity operates *upstream* of action, at the level of the look and the thought — "if my heart has gone after my eyes" (v.7) names the sequence (eye → heart → act) and Job claims to have broken it at the first link; this is the deepest form of purity — not merely not-committing adultery but not-gazing, governing the desire at its root; and it rests on being seen by God (v.4), the all-seeing gaze that makes even the private look accountable)*.

**Lexical validation (read-back).** *made* (H3772, karat — "cut a covenant"), *covenant* (H1285, berith), *gaze* (H0995, bin), *high* (H4791), *iniquity* (H0205), *just* (H6664, tsedeq), *integrity* (H8538, tummah), *heart* (H3820, leb — twice, "if my heart has gone after my eyes") carried. *karat berith* (cut a covenant) with the eyes and *leb* (the heart following the eyes) carry the reading; correctly tagged. Sound.

**Hidden / less-obvious characteristics isolated.** **Integrity operating upstream, at the gaze** — "I have made a covenant with my eyes… if my heart has gone after my eyes" (vv.1,7): the finding that Job's purity is governed at its *source* — the look, before desire reaches the heart or the act; the sequence eye→heart→deed broken at the first link. The inner-being lesson: the deepest integrity is not merely refraining from the sin but disciplining the *attention* that precedes it — a covenant with the eyes, made accountable by the God who "sees my ways" (v.4).

**Characteristics surfaced:** *the covenant with the eyes (integrity governed at the gaze)* (new — purity upstream of act); *the eye→heart→deed sequence broken at the source* (new); *integrity grounded in being seen by God* (thread — the all-seeing God, w/ 34:21).

## JOB-31-B · Job 31:13–23 · [D, multi] — justice to the powerless; the shared-Maker conscience

**The reading.** Job's oath moves through his treatment of the powerless: he did not reject "the **cause** of my manservant or maidservant" (v.13); did not "**withhold** anything that the poor desired" or let "the eyes of the widow fail" (v.16); shared his morsel with the fatherless (v.17). His ground is startling: "**did not he who made me in the womb make him**? And did not one **fashion us** in the womb?" (v.15). The finding is **an ethic of justice to the powerless grounded in a shared creation** *(stated)*. The operation is **the self's conscience formed by the recognition that master and servant share one Maker** *(inferred: v.15 is a profound inner-being foundation for ethics — Job treats his servants justly because he and they were made by the *same God* in the *same* womb-process; the finding is that his compassion is not condescension but *recognition of common createdness* — the leveling truth that undercuts every hierarchy of contempt (contrast Bildad's "man is a worm", 25:6); and "I was in terror of calamity from God" (v.23) — his justice is also held by reverent fear; the inner-being datum is a conscience shaped by two things: the shared-Maker equality (v.15) and the fear of God (v.23) — mercy grounded in theology, not sentiment)*.

**Lexical validation (read-back).** *cause* (H4941, mishpat), *complaint* (H7379, rib), *grew up* (H1431), *blessed* (H1288), *broken* (H7665), *terror* (H6343, pachad — "terror of calamity from God"), *could* (H3201) carried. *mishpat* (the servant's cause honoured) and *pachad* (reverent terror grounding the justice) carry the reading; the shared-Maker claim (v.15) sits in content. Sound.

**Hidden / less-obvious characteristics isolated.** **Compassion grounded in shared createdness** — "did not he who made me in the womb make him?" (v.15): the finding that Job's justice to the powerless rests on the *recognition that master and servant have one Maker* — an ethic of equality drawn from common creation, not sentiment or condescension. The inner-being lesson: the deepest ground of mercy is theological (we are made by the same God), which levels every contempt (the opposite of "man is a worm", 25:6); and it is held together with reverent fear (v.23) — mercy and the fear of God as one formation.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *justice to the powerless grounded in shared createdness (one Maker)* (new — an ethic from common creation); *conscience formed by shared-Maker equality* (new); *mercy held together with the fear of God* (thread — reverent compassion, w/ 6:14).

## JOB-31-C · Job 31:24–34 · [D, multi, thread] — the hidden-heart integrity; no secret idolatry or gloating

**The reading.** The oath reaches the *invisible* layer — the secret heart: Job did not make gold his "**confidence**" (v.24); did not, seeing the sun and moon, let "my **heart** be secretly **enticed**" into idolatry (vv.26–27); did not "**rejoice at the ruin** of him who hated me, or exult when evil overtook him" (v.29); did not conceal his "**transgressions**… by hiding my iniquity in my **heart**" (v.33). The finding is **integrity tested at the level of secret motive — no hidden idolatry, no private gloating, no concealed sin** *(stated)*. The operation is **the self examining its most hidden interior and finding it clean** *(inferred: this is the deepest reach of Job's self-examination — past deeds (vv.13–23) to the *secret heart*: the concealed trust in wealth (v.24), the heart "secretly enticed" toward the sun/moon (v.27), the private satisfaction at an enemy's downfall (v.29), the sin "hidden in my heart" (v.33); the finding is that Job's integrity extends to the invisible — where no one but God sees; and note the profound self-knowledge: he even names the *temptation* to hide iniquity "in my heart" (v.33) and denies indulging it — the interior policed at its most private; this is the positive counterpart to the wicked who "hides evil under his tongue" (20:12) and the adulterer who counts on "no eye will see me" (24:15); Job lives as though always seen)*.

**Lexical validation (read-back).** *confidence* (H4009, mivtach — "made gold my confidence"), *rejoiced* (H8055, twice — samach), *wealth* (H2428), *heart* (H3820, leb — twice, "my heart secretly enticed"/"hiding iniquity in my heart"), *iniquity* (H5771), *hated* (H8130), *exulted* (H5782), *sin* (H2398), *transgressions* (H6588), *fear* (H6206, arats), *contempt* (H0937), *terrified* (H2865), *kept silence* (H1826) carried. *mivtach* (confidence/trust in gold) and *leb* (the secretly-enticed / iniquity-hiding heart) are the reading's spine; correctly tagged. Sound.

**Hidden / less-obvious characteristics isolated.** **Integrity at the invisible layer (the secret heart)** — "if my heart has been secretly enticed… if I have hidden my iniquity in my heart" (vv.27,33): the finding that Job's self-examination reaches *past deeds to secret motive* — no concealed trust in wealth, no private idolatry, no gloating over an enemy, no sin hidden in the heart. The inner-being climax of the oath: integrity is proven where *only God sees* — the exact opposite of the wicked's "no eye will see me" (24:15). Job lives as though always observed, so the private interior matches the public deed. This is the completion of the "heart as the site of sin" theme opened at 1:5.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *hidden-heart integrity (no secret idolatry, gloating, or concealed sin)* (new — the deepest reach of self-examination; completes 1:5); *the self policing its most private interior* (new); *no rejoicing at an enemy's ruin (guarded even in secret satisfaction)* (new); *not making wealth one's confidence (mivtach)* (thread w/ Ecc 5:10).

## JOB-31-D · Job 31:35–40 · [D, multi] — the final challenge; the signature

**The reading.** Job stakes everything on a public demand for a hearing: "**oh, that I had one to hear me**! (Here is my **signature**! Let the Almighty answer me!) Oh, that I had the **indictment** written by my adversary!" (v.35); "I would give him an **account of all my steps**; like a **prince I would approach him**" (v.37). Then: "**the words of Job are ended**" (v.40). The finding is **integrity staked publicly — the self signing its defence and demanding God answer** *(stated)*. The operation is **the self, having examined its whole interior (vv.1–34), placing its signature on its innocence and calling for the trial** *(inferred: after the most thorough inner-being self-examination in Scripture (vv.1–34), Job "signs" (v.35, taw — his mark) and demands the hearing he has craved since ch.9; "like a prince I would approach him" (v.37) — not cringing but confident, the un-reproaching conscience (27:6) made bold; the finding is integrity's ultimate act: to *put one's name to* one's innocence and stand ready for God's answer; and "the words of Job are ended" (v.40) marks that the self has said everything — it has held its integrity to the last word, and now waits; the challenge will be answered, but not as Job expects — God will come, but to question rather than to explain)*.

**Lexical validation (read-back).** *hear* (H8085), *cried out* (H2199), *wept* (H1058), *yield* (H3581), *breathe/last* (H5301/H5315) carried. The signature (taw) and indictment sit in content; the reading rests on "let the Almighty answer me… the words of Job are ended", unambiguous. Sound.

**Hidden / less-obvious characteristics isolated.** **Signing one's innocence and standing ready** — "here is my signature! Let the Almighty answer me… like a prince I would approach him" (vv.35,37): the finding that Job's self-examination culminates in a *public, signed* stand — the un-reproaching conscience (27:6) made bold enough to demand God's answer and approach "like a prince," not a cringing sinner. The inner-being climax: integrity that will *put its name* to itself and wait for God. "The words of Job are ended" (v.40) — the self has said all, held its ground to the last, and now waits for the answer that will reframe everything.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *integrity staked publicly (signing one's innocence)* (new — the oath's culmination); *approaching God "like a prince" (bold, not cringing)* (new — the un-reproaching conscience made bold, w/ 27:6); *the self having said all and now waiting* (new).

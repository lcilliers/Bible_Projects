## JOB-34-A · Job 34:1–15 · [D, multi] — the vindication of God's justice; the breath that sustains all

**The reading.** Elihu insists on God's absolute justice: "**far be it from God that he should do wickedness**… for according to the work of a man he will **repay** him" (vv.10–11); "God will **not do wickedly**, and the Almighty will **not pervert justice**" (v.12). And a striking image of dependence: "if he should **set his heart** to it and **gather to himself his spirit and his breath**, all flesh would **perish together**, and man would **return to dust**" (vv.14–15). The finding is **God's justice affirmed, and all life shown to hang moment-by-moment on God's breath** *(stated)*. The operation is **the self contemplating its utter dependence — its very breath is on loan and could be withdrawn** *(inferred: the justice-claim (vv.10–12) is Elihu answering Job's "he destroys both blameless and wicked" (9:22) — insisting God cannot be unjust; but the deeper inner-being note is vv.14–15 — if God merely *withdrew his breath* ("gathered to himself his spirit"), all flesh would instantly return to dust; the finding is radical creaturely dependence — the self does not *own* its life but holds it as a continuously-given breath (cf. Ecc 12:7 "the spirit returns to God who gave it", Ps 104:29); this both humbles (we are utterly contingent) and, oddly, reassures (the God who sustains us breath by breath is *just*); against Job's charge of injustice, Elihu sets God's sustaining faithfulness — the breath keeps coming)*.

**Lexical validation (read-back).** *Hear* (H8085), *know* (H3045), *right* (H4941/H6663, mishpat/tsedeq), *away* (H5493), *incurable* (H0605), *transgression* (H6588), *wicked* (H7562, resha), *take delight* (H7521, ratsah), *understanding* (H3824, lebab), *justice* (H4941, mishpat), *breath* (H5397, neshamah — "his spirit and his breath"), *flesh* (H1320) carried. *neshamah* (breath, withdrawable) carries the dependence-finding; *mishpat/tsedeq* the justice-claim; correctly tagged. Sound.

**Hidden / less-obvious characteristics isolated.** **Life as a continuously-given breath** — "if he should gather to himself his spirit and his breath, all flesh would perish" (vv.14–15): the finding of radical creaturely dependence — the self holds its life not as a possession but as a breath *continuously loaned*, withdrawable in an instant (cf. Ecc 12:7, Ps 104:29). The inner-being datum: this both humbles (we are utterly contingent) and reassures (the sustaining God is just, so the breath keeps coming) — Elihu's counter to Job's charge of divine injustice is God's moment-by-moment sustaining faithfulness.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *life as a continuously-given breath (radical dependence)* (thread — w/ Ecc 12:7, Ps 104:29); *God's justice affirmed against the charge of caprice* (thread — answering 9:22); *the sustaining God as just* (new).

## JOB-34-B · Job 34:16–37 · [D, multi, thread] — the all-seeing eye; no darkness hides the self

**The reading.** Elihu presses God's perfect knowledge: "**his eyes are on the ways of a man, and he sees all his steps**. There is **no gloom or deep darkness where evildoers may hide** themselves" (vv.21–22); "**knowing their works, he overturns them**" (v.25); and he "**heard the cry of the afflicted**" (v.28). The finding is **the all-seeing God before whom no darkness conceals the self** *(stated)*. The operation is **the self fully exposed to a God from whom no hiding is possible** *(inferred: this is the true form of what the wicked deny (24:15, "no eye will see me") and Job affirms (31:4, "he sees my ways") — Elihu makes it doctrine: "no gloom or deep darkness where evildoers may hide" (v.22); the finding is the inescapable exposure of the interior and its deeds to God's sight; for the wicked this is terror, for Job (whose covenant with his eyes, 31:1, assumes it) it is the very ground of his confidence — the God who sees *all* also sees his innocence; and "he heard the cry of the afflicted" (v.28) adds that the all-seeing God also *hears* — the same God Job accuses of not answering (30:20) is here said to hear the poor; the inner-being datum is that total exposure is double-edged: dread to the guilty, vindication to the innocent)*.

**Lexical validation (read-back).** *understanding* (H0998, binah), *hear/listen* (H8085/H0238), *hates* (H8130), *justice* (H4941, mishpat), *righteous* (H6662), *away* (H5493), *crushed* (H1792), *regard* (H7919, sakal), *afflicted* (H6041, ani), *quiet* (H8252, shaqat), *iniquity* (H5766), *understanding* (H3824, lebab), *insight* (H7919), *rebellion* (H6588) carried. The seeing/hearing verbs carry the exposure-finding; correctly tagged. Sound.

**Hidden / less-obvious characteristics isolated.** **Total exposure as double-edged (dread or vindication)** — "no gloom or deep darkness where evildoers may hide… he heard the cry of the afflicted" (vv.22,28): the finding that the self's complete exposure to God's sight (and hearing) is *terror to the guilty* (who count on the dark, 24:15) but *ground of confidence to the innocent* (Job's covenant with his eyes, 31:1, assumes the all-seeing God). The inner-being datum: the same divine omniscience the wicked flee, Job welcomes — for the God who sees all sees his innocence too, and hears the afflicted's cry (answering, obliquely, Job's "you do not answer", 30:20).

**Characteristics surfaced:** *the all-seeing God / total exposure of the self* (thread — w/ 24:15 (denied), 31:4 (welcomed), Ecc 12:14); *exposure as double-edged (dread to guilty, vindication to innocent)* (new); *the God who hears the afflicted's cry* (new — obliquely answers 30:20).

## JOB-25-A · Job 25:1–6 · [D, multi, thread] — human worthlessness before God (the friends' last word)

**The reading.** Bildad's final speech collapses to six verses — the debate is exhausted. He restates the creaturely-impurity theme, now with contempt: "**Dominion and fear are with God**; he makes **peace** in his high heaven" (v.2); "**how can man be in the right before God**? How can he who is born of woman be **pure**? Behold, even the moon is not bright, and the stars are not **pure** in his eyes; **how much less man, who is a maggot**, and the son of man, who is a **worm**!" (vv.4–6). The finding is **the creaturely-impurity truth degraded into contempt for the human being** — man reduced to a maggot and worm before God *(stated)*. The operation is **a true doctrine (man cannot be pure before God) emptied of hope and turned into denigration** *(inferred: this is the same truth first spoken in Eliphaz's vision (4:17) and it *is* true — no one is pure before God (cf. Ecc 7:20) — but here it has curdled into pure contempt ("maggot… worm"), stripped of the mercy that should accompany it; the finding is what happens to a truth about the self when it is wielded *only* to crush: it becomes a lie about human worth; the friends have run out of argument and can only belittle; and the brevity itself (6 verses, no Zophar third speech at all) is telling — the accusers' interior has nothing left; contrast the whirlwind, where God will also humble Job but *toward* restoration, not contempt)*.

**Lexical validation (read-back).** *Dominion* (H4910, mashal), *fear* (H6343, pachad — "dominion and fear are with God"), *peace* (H7965, shalom), *right* (H6663, tsadaq — "in the right before God") carried. *pachad* (fear/dread, here God's awesomeness) and *tsadaq* (be right before God) frame the reading; the maggot/worm imagery sits in content. Sound.

**Hidden / less-obvious characteristics isolated.** **A truth about the self degraded into contempt** — "man, who is a maggot… a worm" (v.6): the finding that the *true* doctrine of human impurity before God (4:17, 15:14) can be *emptied of mercy* and become sheer denigration of human worth. The inner-being lesson (completing the friends' arc): the same truth humbles redemptively (42:6, Job's repentance) or crushes contemptuously (Bildad here) depending on whether it is held *with* or *without* mercy. The friends end in contempt; God will humble Job toward restoration.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *human impurity before God degraded into contempt (maggot/worm)* (thread close — 4:17, 15:14 → 25:6; the truth without mercy); *the exhaustion of the accusers (the debate collapsing)* (new); *contempt-humbling vs redemptive-humbling* (new — set against 42:6).

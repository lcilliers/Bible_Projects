## ABR-09-intercession-sodom · Genesis 18:16–33 · [the interceding interior] — God's disclosure to his chosen, the outcry against grave sin, and the humble boldness that holds the Judge to his own justice

**The passage.** "Shall I hide from Abraham…?" (18:17); "Abraham shall become a great and **mighty** (atsum) nation… all **blessed** (barak) in him" (18:18); "I have **chosen** (yada) him, that he may command his children to **keep** (shamar) the way of the LORD by doing **righteousness** (tsedaqah) and **justice** (mishpat)" (18:19); "their **sin** (chattat) is very **grave** (kaved)… the **outcry** (zeaqah)" (18:20); "I will go down… and **know** (yada)" (18:21); "will you sweep away the **righteous** (tsaddiq) with the **wicked** (rasha)?… Shall not the **Judge** (shaphat) of all the earth do **justice** (mishpat)?" (18:23–25); "I have **undertaken** (yaal) to speak… I who am but dust and ashes" (18:27); "will you **destroy** (shachat)…?" (18:28); "let not the Lord be **angry** (charah)" (18:30,32).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **atsum (mighty) + barak (blessed) — the ground of God's disclosure (18:18).** The finding is "Abraham shall become a great and **mighty** (atsum) nation, and all… **blessed** (barak) in him" *(stated)*. The operation names *why* God discloses: Abraham is the *blessing-bearer* (← 12:3) — the mighty nation-to-be; the disclosure flows from his election *(inferred)*.
- **yada (chosen) #1 — God's *electing* knowledge (18:19).** The finding is "I have **chosen** (yada — 'known') him" *(stated)*. The gem is *yada as election*: to "know" here is to *choose intimately* — God's *elective knowing* of Abraham (distinct from the primeval yada-senses) — the interior *chosen/known* by God *(inferred)*.
- **shamar (keep) + tsedaqah + mishpat — the *way* Abraham must teach (18:19).** The finding is "that he may command his children… to **keep** (shamar) the way of the LORD by doing **righteousness** (tsedaqah) and **justice** (mishpat)" *(stated)*. The gem is that **tsedaqah — the righteousness *reckoned* to Abram (15:6)** — is now the righteousness Abraham must *do and teach*: the *reckoned* becomes the *practiced and transmitted*; paired with **mishpat** (first "justice") as the content of "the way"; and *shamar* (keeping, ← 2:15/17:9) is now *keeping the moral way* *(inferred: reckoned righteousness → taught righteousness)*.
- **chattat + kaved + zeaqah — the grave sin and the outcry (18:20).** The finding is "their **sin** (chattat) is very **grave** (kaved)… the **outcry** (zeaqah) against Sodom is great" *(stated)*. The operations: **chattat** — Sodom's *sin* (← 4:7 crouching sin; 13:13 sinners); **kaved** — its *weight/gravity* (heavy; kaved later "glory") — sin as a *weight*; **zeaqah** — the *outcry* (first occurrence) of victims rising to God (as Abel's blood cried, 4:10) — the *cry of the oppressed provoking judgment* *(inferred: the outcry is the ground; sin's weight is measured)*.
- **yada (know) #2 — God's *investigative* knowledge (18:21).** The finding is "I will go down to see… and **know** (yada)" *(stated)*. The gem is *yada as investigation*: God *condescends to verify* before judging — distinct from the electing yada (18:19); the Judge does not act on the outcry alone but *goes down to know* *(inferred: two yada — electing vs investigating)*.
- **tsaddiq / rasha / shaphat / mishpat — the intercession's *moral distinction* (18:23, 18:24, 18:25, 18:26, 18:28).** The finding is "will you sweep away the **righteous** (tsaddiq) with the **wicked** (rasha)? (18:23)… Far be it from you to slay the righteous with the wicked… Shall not the **Judge** (shaphat) of all the earth do **justice** (mishpat)? (18:25)" — pressed through 50, 45, 40, 30, 20, 10 (18:24, 18:26, 18:28…) *(stated)*. The gem is the *principle*: Abraham presses the *distinction* between **tsaddiq** (← 6:9 Noah; here the righteous *few*) and **rasha** (first "wicked" as a class), and holds God to his *own character* — **the Judge (shaphat) of all the earth must do justice (mishpat)**. The operation is the interior *appealing to God's justice* that the *righteous few might spare the wicked many*; the fivefold tsaddiq is *one principle* pressed at each descending number, not five findings *(inferred: shaphat — God as universal Judge, held to mishpat; the righteous as the ground of sparing)*.
- **yaal (undertaken) — the *humble boldness* of venturing to intercede (18:27,31).** The finding is "I have **undertaken** (yaal) to speak to the Lord, I who am but dust and ashes" (18:27), and again (18:31) *(stated)*. The gem is *yaal* — to *venture/presume* — held together with "dust and ashes": **audacious enough to bargain with God, humble enough to know he is dust**. The operation is intercession as *bold-yet-lowly persistence* *(inferred: yaal = the venturing to plead; the interior's humble audacity)*.
- **shachat (destroy) ×3 — the destruction *pleaded against* (18:28,31,32).** The finding is "will you **destroy** (shachat) the whole city for lack of five?… I will not **destroy** it… for the ten's sake" (18:28,31,32) *(stated)*. The gem is *shachat* — the flood-ruin word (GEN-10; renounced 9:11; Sodom's doom foreshadowed 13:10) — now the *object of Abraham's plea*: he intercedes *against* the shachat, and God *concedes each step* — the Judge *willing to spare the many for the few* *(inferred: shachat = the destruction averted/limited by intercession)*.
- **charah (angry) ×2 — the deference not to provoke wrath (18:30,32).** The finding is "let not the Lord be **angry** (charah), and I will speak" (18:30, 18:32) *(stated)*. The gem is *charah* — the anger-word (← 4:5 Cain's) — now the *divine anger Abraham is careful not to provoke*: pressing boldly *yet fearing to overstep*; the interior's *reverent restraint within its audacity* *(inferred: charah = the wrath the intercessor defers to, marking the limit of his boldness)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **election → disclosure → transmission (18:17–19):** God will not *hide* his counsel from the one he has *chosen* (yada), *because* Abraham is to *teach* righteousness and justice (tsedaqah/mishpat) — the *reckoned*-righteous man (15:6) entrusted to *transmit* the way; election *for the sake of* teaching.
2. **outcry and investigation (zeaqah/chattat/kaved → yada, 18:20–21):** the victims' *outcry* rises, the *sin* is *heavy*, and God *goes down to know* — judgment grounded in the cry and *verified*, not arbitrary.
3. **holding the Judge to his justice (tsaddiq/rasha/shaphat/mishpat, 18:23–25):** Abraham presses the *moral distinction* and appeals to God's *own character* — the Judge must *do justice*; the *righteous few* the ground on which the *wicked many* might be spared.
4. **humble boldness against the destruction (yaal/shachat/charah, 18:27–32):** the interior *ventures* (yaal), calling itself *dust and ashes*, bargains God *down* against the *shachat*, and *defers to his wrath* (charah) — audacity and reverence together; and God *concedes* — the Judge *more willing to spare* than the intercessor dares hope.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** atsum/barak (the blessing-bearer, ground of disclosure); **yada — *electing* (18:19) vs *investigating* (18:21)**; shamar (keeping the *way*); **tsedaqah — the *reckoned* righteousness (15:6) now *taught*** + mishpat (first "justice"); chattat/kaved/zeaqah (grave sin, its weight, the outcry); **tsaddiq vs rasha** (righteous/wicked, the intercession's distinction); **shaphat — God as *Judge of all the earth*** held to mishpat; **yaal — humble *venturing*** ("dust and ashes"); shachat *pleaded against* (×3); **charah — the wrath *deferred to*** (the limit of boldness).
- **Whose interior:** the **human** interior *interceding* — bold, humble, persistent, appealing to God's justice for mercy on the wicked; and the **divine** interior *disclosing to his friend, investigating before judging, and willing to spare the many for the few*.
- **Threads:** tsedaqah (← 15:6 reckoned, now taught); yada (electing/investigating — new senses); shaphat (← 16:5 Sarai's appeal, now Abraham's; God as Judge); shachat (← GEN-10 / 13:10 Sodom foreshadowed → ABR-10 the destruction); zeaqah (the outcry motif); charah (← 4:5, now divine wrath deferred to).
- **Guarded against:** inventing five distinct "righteous" from the bargaining tsaddiq (one principle pressed down the numbers); merging the two yada (electing vs investigating); flattening kaved (weight of sin) toward its later "glory" sense; reading Abraham's yaal as mere presumption *or* mere humility (it is *both* — audacity *and* dust-and-ashes); importing later intercession/substitution theology beyond the righteous-spare-the-wicked the passage states.

## GEN-07-cain-line-lamech-seth · Genesis 4:17–26 · [two lines, two directions of "calling"] — the city named for self, the boast that demands a hearing, and the first calling upon the Name

**The passage.** "Cain **knew** (yada) his wife… she bore Enoch. When he built a city, he **called** (qara) the name of the city after… his son" (4:17); Lamech to his wives, "**hear** (shama) my voice… **listen** (azan) to what I say: I have killed a man for wounding me… Lamech's [revenge] is seventy-sevenfold" (4:23–24); "Adam **knew** (yada) his wife again… and **called** (qara) his name Seth, for… God has appointed another offspring" (4:25); "to Seth a son… he **called** (qara) his name Enosh. At that time people began to **call upon** (qara) the name of the LORD" (4:26).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **yada (know) — Cain begets in the cursed line (4:17).** The finding is "Cain **knew** (yada) his wife… she bore Enoch" *(stated)*. The operation is the begetting-knowing (as 4:1) continuing *even in the fugitive/cursed line* — life propagates through the condemned son *(inferred: same yada-as-begetting; the distinction is the context — the cursed wanderer still fathers a line)*.
- **qara (name) — the fugitive builds a *city* and names it for his son (4:17).** The finding is "he built a city, he **called** (qara) the name of the city after… his son, Enoch" *(stated)*. The operation is naming turned to **self-memorial / self-securing**: the man *sentenced to wander* (4:12,14) *settles*, *builds*, and *names a permanence after his own offspring* — the restless self defying its wander-sentence by fixing a name in stone *(inferred: distinct from every prior qara — God naming domains, man naming animals/Eve; here naming *constructs a legacy*, the interior grasping at a place and a name against its exile)*.
- **shama (hear) — Lamech *commands* an audience (4:23).** The finding is "Adah and Zillah, **hear** (shama) my voice" *(stated)*. The operation is hearing *demanded of others* — the self summoning an audience to itself; distinct from hearing-God (3:8) or heeding-a-voice (3:17): here shama is *conscripted attention* to one's own boast *(inferred)*.
- **azan (listen) — the demand intensified (4:23).** The finding is "**listen** (azan) to what I say" *(stated)*. The operation is the parallel, heightened summons to attend — the first *azan* in Scripture, and it serves *self-glorification*: Lamech marshals his wives to hear his vengeance-song *(inferred: the boast itself — "I have killed a man… seventy-sevenfold" — is the interior that *glories* in disproportionate revenge, arrogating even God's sevenfold protection of Cain (4:15) into a self-declared seventy-sevenfold; the Cainite line peaks in self-exalting violence)*.
- **yada (know) — Adam begets Seth, the line resumed (4:25).** The finding is "Adam **knew** (yada) his wife again… she bore a son" *(stated)*. The operation is the begetting-knowing *restored after the murder* — life continues in the *other* line; distinct context from 4:17 (there the cursed line; here the appointed one) *(inferred)*.
- **qara (name) — Seth named for *God's appointment* (4:25).** The finding is "she **called** (qara) his name Seth, for… **God has appointed** for me another offspring instead of Abel" *(stated)*. The operation is naming that **acknowledges God's provision** — the name reads *God's* act, not the self's; distinct from Cain's city-naming (self-memorial): here naming looks *God-ward*, hope grounded in what God appointed *(inferred)*.
- **qara (name) #1 — Enosh named (4:26).** The finding is "he **called** (qara) his name Enosh" *(stated)*. The operation is the ordinary naming of a son in the Sethite line — the line continuing *(inferred)*.
- **qara (call upon) #2 — the first *invocation* of God (4:26).** The finding is "at that time people began to **call upon** (qara, H7121G 'call *to*') the **name of the LORD**" *(stated)*. The operation is the **birth of worship/prayer** — the *same verb* used throughout for *naming* now turned *toward God as invocation*: calling *to* him by name. The gem is the hinge: qara-as-naming (fixing a name on things) becomes qara-as-*calling-on* (reaching toward the Named One) — and it is the *reciprocal* of God's qara that *called to* the hidden man (3:9, GEN-04): the pursued now *invoke* the Pursuer *(inferred: distinct sense (H7121G 'call to' vs H7121H 'call by') and distinct direction — the first Godward calling)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **two lines, two things done with `qara`:** the Cainite line *names a city after itself* (4:17) and *commands an audience for its boast* (shama/azan, 4:23) — calling turned *inward/self-ward*; the Sethite line *names Seth for God's appointing* (4:25) and *calls upon the LORD's name* (4:26) — calling turned *God-ward*. The same operation (qara / summoned hearing) splits by *direction*: toward self vs toward God.
2. **the fugitive's city vs the worshipper's invocation:** Cain, sentenced to wander, *builds and names a city* to secure himself (4:17); the Sethites, claiming no permanence, *call on the Name* (4:26) — self-securing construction against God-ward dependence.
3. **God's call answered:** God *called to* the hidden man (3:9); here man *calls to* God (4:26) — the pursuing-call of Eden reciprocated by the first worship. Set against Lamech *demanding his own voice be heard* (4:23), the two directions of "being heard / calling" are exact opposites: attention conscripted to self vs voice lifted to God.
4. **begetting continues in both lines (yada, 4:17, 4:25):** life propagates through the cursed and the appointed alike — but what each line *does with its naming and calling* is where the interiors diverge.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** yada-as-begetting in the cursed line (4:17) vs the appointed line (4:25); qara as *self-memorial city-naming* (4:17) vs *God-acknowledging* naming (Seth, 4:25) vs ordinary naming (Enosh, 4:26) vs **calling-upon-God/invocation** (4:26); shama+azan as *conscripted audience* for self-glorifying vengeance (Lamech); the birth of worship as the reciprocal of God's pursuing call.
- **Whose interior:** two human interiors set side by side — the **self-securing/self-glorifying** (Cain's city, Lamech's boast) and the **God-ward** (naming for God's appointment, calling on the Name).
- **Threads:** qara (← God's naming GEN-01; man's naming GEN-03; God's *call to* the man 3:9 → man's *call to* God 4:26); yada-as-begetting (← 4:1); the self-securing name (→ Babel's "let us make a name", GEN-14 11:4 — the same grasp writ large).
- **Guarded against:** merging the five qara across two senses and directions (naming-for-self vs calling-on-God is the whole point); flattening shama/azan into neutral "hearing" when Lamech *conscripts* it; treating the two begettings (4:17/4:25) as interchangeable when the lines they seed diverge; importing NT "call on the name" theology beyond the invocation the verse states.

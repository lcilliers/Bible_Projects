## ABR-18-ishmael-line · Genesis 25:12–18 · [the promise to the outcast kept] — the twelve princes, and Ishmael gathered like his father

**The passage.** The generations of Ishmael — twelve sons, "twelve princes according to their tribes" (25:12–16); "these are the years of the **life** (chayyim) of Ishmael, 137 years; he breathed his last and died, and was gathered to his people" (25:17); his descendants "settled… over against all his kinsmen" (25:18).

### The operation

- **chayyim (life) — Ishmael's *honoured* death, the promise *fulfilled* (25:17).** The finding is "the years of the **life** (chayyim) of Ishmael, 137 years… he breathed his last and died, and was **gathered to his people**" *(stated)*. The gem is that this thin genealogy *records the promise kept*: **twelve princes** (25:16) fulfils God's word to Abraham — "I will make him a great nation… twelve princes" (17:20) — and to Hagar — "I will multiply your offspring… a great nation" (16:10; 21:18); and Ishmael dies "**gathered to his people**", the *same honour* given to Abraham (25:8, ABR-17). The outcast son, *heard and seen* by God (16:11,13; 21:17), is *blessed, multiplied, and gathered* — God's promise to the *cast-out* proves *as sure as* his promise to the heir *(inferred: the genealogy is the fulfilment; chayyim/gathered honours Ishmael as Abraham)*.
- **(25:18 — "over against all his kinsmen": no non-T2 span.)** The closing note that Ishmael's line settled "over against/in the presence of all his kinsmen" fulfils the word of 16:12 ("his hand against everyone… he shall dwell over against all his kinsmen") — read as context, the promise's *shape* kept *(no-span context)*.

### The web / note

The unit is a *bridge and a fulfilment*: it closes the Abraham cycle by showing that the promise to **Ishmael** — the *outcast* son — was *kept* (twelve princes, a great nation, an honoured death "gathered to his people", parallel to Abraham's). The *God who sees and hears the cast-out* (El-Roi, 16:13; "God hears", 16:11; 21:17) is *faithful to him too*. The covenant runs through *Isaac*, but the *blessing* reaches *Ishmael* — and the narrative *records both deaths in the same honoured terms*.

- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** chayyim — Ishmael's *honoured* lifespan and death ("gathered to his people", as Abraham, 25:8); the *promise to the outcast fulfilled* (twelve princes, ← 17:20; 16:10; 21:18); the *dwelling "over against his kinsmen"* (← 16:12, no-span).
- **Whose interior:** minimal — the passage is a *genealogical fulfilment*; its inner-being weight is the *faithfulness of God to the cast-out son*.
- **Threads:** the outcast heard/seen (← 16:11,13; 21:17); the promise to Hagar/Ishmael (← 16:10; 17:20; 21:18, now *kept*); "gathered to his people" (← 25:8 Abraham — the *same* honour); the two sons (Isaac the covenant-line, Ishmael blessed-and-kept).
- **Guarded against:** manufacturing inner-being operations from a genealogy (thin by nature; the finding is the *promise kept*, not a forced reading); over-reading the single chayyim beyond the honoured death it names; importing later Ishmael/Isaac typology.

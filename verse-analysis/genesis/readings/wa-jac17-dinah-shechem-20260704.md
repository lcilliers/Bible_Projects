# Genesis 34:1–31 — inner-being passage reading (JAC-17-dinah-shechem)

- **Source:** verse-first lexicals over the passage (provenance `genesis-jacob-v1-20260704`). Every non-T2 span in its verse; *stated* | *inferred*; distinctions kept. Dinah's own interior is *not given* by the text (she is silent throughout); the operations read are those of Shechem, Jacob, and the sons. The circumcision-ruse mechanics (34:15–16, 20–23) read as scene-context.
- **Check-back:** gate run post-draft; every non-T2 verse/gloss engaged; the 3 tame, 4 shama, 3 natan, 2 nephesh, 2 chashaq each treated distinctly.

## JAC-17-dinah-shechem · Genesis 34:1–31 · [violation, silence, and treacherous vengeance] — the humbling of Dinah, the father's quiet, the sons' indignation curdled into deceit and massacre

**The passage.** Shechem "saw her, seized her, lay with her, and **humiliated** (anah) her" (34:2); "his **soul** (nephesh) was drawn to her; he loved her and spoke to her **heart** (lev)" (34:3); Jacob **heard** she was **defiled** (tame) but **held his peace** (charesh) (34:5); the sons **heard** (shama), were **angry** (charah), **indignant** (atsav), for an **outrage** (nevalah) was done (34:7); "the **soul** (nephesh) of my son **longs** (chashaq)… **give** (natan) her" (34:8); "**favor** (chen)… I will **give** (natan) whatever you say" (34:11); "**ask** (ravah) any bride-price, I will **give** (natan)" (34:12); the sons answered **deceitfully** (mirmah), because he **defiled** (tame) their sister (34:13); "a **disgrace** (cherpah)… we **cannot** (yakhol) **give** (natan) her to the uncircumcised" (34:14); "if you will not **listen** (shama)" (34:17); it **pleased** (yatav) them (34:18); Shechem **delighted** (chaphets) (34:19); the city **listened** (shama), were circumcised (34:24); on the third day, **sore** (kaav), the city **secure** (betach), Simeon and Levi killed all the males (34:25); the sons plundered, because they had **defiled** their sister (34:27); took all their **wealth** (chayil) (34:29); Jacob — "you made me **stink** (baash)" (34:30); "should he treat our sister as a **prostitute** (zanah)?" (34:31).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **anah (humiliated) — the *violation* (34:2).** "he seized her, lay with her, and **humiliated** (anah) her" *(stated)* — anah, *afflict/humble/violate*: the gem is the word — the *same anah* Laban's oath *forbade against the daughters* (31:50) — here the *violation* of Dinah; the interior-act of *forcing and degrading* another; the passage opens on *affliction inflicted* *(inferred: anah = the violation that afflicts and degrades)*.
- **nephesh (soul drawn) + lev (heart/tenderly) — desire *after* the violence (34:3).** "his **soul** (nephesh) was drawn to her; he loved her and spoke to her **heart** (lev)" *(stated)*. **nephesh** — *soul/self*: Shechem's *whole self drawn* to Dinah — *after* the assault; **lev** — he speaks *to her heart* (tenderly). The gem is the *disturbing sequence*: *violation, then love and tender words* — the interior's *desire and affection following its own violence*; a love that *does not undo* the anah *(inferred: nephesh/lev = the soul-desire and tenderness that follow the violation, not erasing it)*.
- **shama (heard) + tame (defiled) + charesh (held his peace) — the *father's silence* (34:5).** "Jacob **heard** (shama) he had **defiled** (tame) Dinah… but **held his peace** (charesh) until his sons came" *(stated)*. **shama** — Jacob *hears* the outrage; **tame** — Dinah *defiled* (the fact stated); **charesh** — the gem: **charesh — *be silent / keep quiet*** — Jacob *says and does nothing*; the *father's strange passivity* before his daughter's violation. Where the sons will *burn*, the father *is quiet* — the interior *withholding response* (prudence? paralysis? self-protection?); the text lets the *silence stand* as its own troubling operation *(inferred: shama/tame/charesh = the outrage heard, and the father's silence)*.
- **shama (heard) + charah (angry) + atsav (indignant) + nevalah (outrage) — the sons' *burning indignation* (34:7).** "the sons **heard** (shama)… were **indignant** (atsav) and very **angry** (charah), for an **outrage** (nevalah) was done" *(stated)*. **shama** *distinct from 34:5* — the *sons'* hearing (which *ignites*, where the father's *quieted*); **charah** — *burning anger*; **atsav** — *grieved/pained-hurt* (the same word as God *grieved* at the heart, 6:6 — here the sons' *wounded* indignation); **nevalah** — *moral folly / disgraceful outrage*. The gem: *righteous* indignation at a *real* outrage — the interior *rightly grieved and enraged* (the moral response the father withheld) — before it *curdles* *(inferred: shama/charah/atsav/nevalah = the sons' grieved, burning indignation at a genuine outrage)*.
- **nephesh (soul longs) + chashaq (longs, twice) — the *longing* pressed by Hamor (34:8).** "the **soul** (nephesh) of my son Shechem **longs** (chashaq) for your daughter" *(stated)*. **nephesh** *distinct from 34:3* — *Hamor reports* the son's soul-longing (34:3 was Shechem's own drawn soul); **chashaq** (two spans — *desire / attach*) — *cleave/long/be attached*: the *intense attachment* named as the ground of the marriage-suit; the interior's *desire made a diplomatic plea* — the father brokering the son's longing *(inferred: nephesh/chashaq = the son's clinging desire pressed as a marriage-claim)*.
- **chen (favor) + natan (give) — Shechem's *lavish suit* (34:11).** "let me find **favor** (chen)… whatever you say I will **give** (natan)" *(stated)*. **chen** — *favor* sought; **natan** — *give*: Shechem *offers anything* — the interior *desperate to secure Dinah*, open-handed with wealth to *buy* what it has already *taken* *(inferred: chen/natan = the favor sought and the all-giving suit)*.
- **ravah (ask) + natan (give) — the *blank-cheque bride-price* (34:12).** *natan distinct from 34:11:* "**ask** (ravah) me as great a bride-price and gift as you will, and I will **give** (natan)" *(stated)*. **ravah** — *multiply/increase* (here *"make it as great as you like"*); **natan** — the *pledge to pay any sum*. The interior *escalates the offer* — desire *pricing itself without limit* *(inferred: ravah/natan = the unlimited bride-price offered)*.
- **mirmah (deceitfully) + tame (defiled) — the sons' *weaponised deceit* (34:13).** "the sons answered **deceitfully** (mirmah), because he had **defiled** (tame) their sister" *(stated)*. The gem: **mirmah — *deceit/treachery*** — the *very word* of Jacob's theft of the blessing (27:35) — now *in the sons*, turned into a *lethal ruse*; **tame** *distinct from 34:5* — the *sons' stated motive* (the defilement) for the deceit. The interior's *righteous grievance channeled through the family's deception-habit* — indignation *curdling into treachery* *(inferred: mirmah/tame = the grievance turned into deceit, the family's mirmah weaponised)*.
- **cherpah (disgrace) + yakhol (cannot) + natan (give) — the *ruse's pretext* (34:14).** "it would be a **disgrace** (cherpah); we **cannot** (yakhol) **give** (natan) our sister to the uncircumcised" *(stated)*. **cherpah** — *reproach/disgrace* (← Rachel's barrenness-reproach 30:23; here the *pretext* of covenant-purity); **yakhol** — the feigned *inability*; **natan** *distinct from 34:11,12* — the *withholding* of the sister as the ruse's hinge. The interior *cloaks the trap in religious scruple* — the covenant sign made *bait* *(inferred: cherpah/yakhol/natan = the covenant-purity pretext masking the trap)*.
- **shama (listen) — the *ultimatum* (34:17).** "if you will not **listen** (shama) to us and be circumcised, we will take our daughter and go" *(stated)* — shama *distinct from 34:5,7* — *hearing = complying*: the sons' *condition* pressed; the interior *setting the terms of the snare* *(inferred: shama = the compliance demanded as the trap's condition)*.
- **yatav (pleased) — the offer *welcomed* (34:18).** "their words **pleased** (yatav) Hamor and Shechem" *(stated)* — yatav, *be good/please*: the *victims of the ruse find it good* — dramatic irony; the interior *pleased at its own undoing* *(inferred: yatav = the trap-terms welcomed as good)*.
- **chaphets (delighted) — Shechem's *eager* compliance (34:19).** "the young man did not delay… because he **delighted** (chaphets) in Jacob's daughter" *(stated)* — chaphets, *delight in/desire*: the gem is the *speed of desire* — Shechem *rushes* to be circumcised, so *delighted* is he in Dinah; the interior's *eagerness the very thing that dooms him* *(inferred: chaphets = the delight that hurries Shechem into the trap)*.
- **shama (listened) — the *city complies* (34:24).** *shama distinct — the fourth:* "all… **listened** (shama) to Hamor and Shechem, and every male was circumcised" *(stated)* — shama, *obey*: the *whole city obeys* (swayed by the promised gain, 34:23) — the interior of *a city led into vulnerability* by its leaders' greed *(inferred: shama = the city's compliance, led into defencelessness)*.
- **kaav (sore) + betach (secure) — the *defenceless security* exploited (34:25).** "on the third day, when they were **sore** (kaav)… the city **secure** (betach), Simeon and Levi killed all the males" *(stated)*. **kaav** — *be in pain* (the men incapacitated by the circumcision); **betach** — *security/trust* (the city *unsuspecting*). The gem is the *exploitation of trust*: the interior *strikes the pained and the trusting* — treachery *at its most defenceless target* *(inferred: kaav/betach = the wounded, trusting security turned into the killing-opportunity)*.
- **tame (defiled) — the *plunder justified* (34:27).** *tame distinct — the third:* "the sons plundered the city, because they had **defiled** (tame) their sister" *(stated)* — tame, *defile*: the *third* invocation of the defilement — now as *justification for looting*; the interior *stretching the grievance to cover plunder* — vengeance *spilling into greed* *(inferred: tame = the defilement invoked to justify the plunder)*.
- **chayil (wealth) — the *spoil taken* (34:29).** "all their **wealth** (chayil), little ones, wives… they captured and plundered" *(stated)* — chayil, *strength/wealth/substance*: the *total spoil* — the interior's vengeance *enriching itself*, the massacre *monetised* *(inferred: chayil = the wealth seized in the plunder)*.
- **baash (stink) — Jacob's *self-concerned* response (34:30).** "you have made me **stink** (baash) to the inhabitants… I shall be destroyed, I and my household" *(stated)* — the gem: **baash — *stink/become odious*** — Jacob's *only stated response* is *fear of the consequence to himself* — not the horror of the massacre, not Dinah, but *"you made me odious; we'll be wiped out."* The interior *self-protective to the last* — the father who was *silent* at the violation (34:5) now *speaks only of his own danger* *(inferred: baash = Jacob's self-concerned fear of the reprisal)*.
- **zanah (prostitute) — the sons' *final justification* (34:31).** "should he treat our sister like a **prostitute** (zanah)?" *(stated)* — zanah, *fornicate/be a harlot*: the gem is the *last word* — the sons' *rhetorical defence*: their sister was *treated as a whore*, and that they *would not bear*. The interior's *fierce sibling-honour* — a *real* wound answering the father's self-concern, yet *offered to excuse a massacre*; the moral tangle left *unresolved* by the text *(inferred: zanah = the sister's dishonour invoked to justify the vengeance)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **violation, then desire (anah 34:2 → nephesh/lev 34:3):** Shechem *afflicts* Dinah, *then* his soul is *drawn* and he speaks *to her heart* — desire and tenderness *following* the violence, not undoing it.
2. **the father silent, the sons aflame (shama/tame/charesh 34:5 → shama/charah/atsav/nevalah 34:7):** Jacob *hears and holds his peace*; the sons *hear and burn* with grieved, righteous indignation at a *real outrage* — the moral response split between a *quiet* father and *enraged* sons.
3. **the suit and the ruse (nephesh/chashaq 34:8 → chen/natan 34:11 → ravah/natan 34:12 → mirmah/tame 34:13 → cherpah/yakhol/natan 34:14 → shama 34:17):** Hamor and Shechem press the *longing* with a *limitless bride-price*; the sons answer *deceitfully*, cloaking the trap in *covenant-purity* — righteous grievance *curdled into the family's mirmah*.
4. **the trap sprung on the trusting (yatav 34:18 → chaphets 34:19 → shama 34:24 → kaav/betach 34:25 → tame/chayil 34:27–29):** the victims *find the terms good*, Shechem *rushes* in delight, the city *complies* — and on the third day the *pained, secure, trusting* city is *slaughtered and plundered*, the defilement stretched to *justify the looting*.
5. **self-concern vs sibling-honour (baash 34:30 → zanah 34:31):** Jacob laments only that he is *made to stink* and endangered; the sons answer with the *one moral cry* of the chapter — *"should our sister be treated as a whore?"* — a real wound offered to excuse a massacre; the interior's tangle *left open*.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** anah (the *violation* — the word Laban's oath forbade, 31:50); nephesh ×2 (34:3 Shechem's *own* soul drawn / 34:8 the son's soul *reported* longing); lev (tenderness *after* violence); chashaq (the *cleaving desire* pressed as a claim); **charesh (the *father's silence*)**; shama ×4 (34:5 Jacob *hears* / 34:7 the sons *hear and burn* / 34:17 the *ultimatum* / 34:24 the city *complies*); tame ×3 (34:5 the *fact* / 34:13 the *motive* for deceit / 34:27 the *justification* for plunder); charah/atsav/nevalah (the *grieved, righteous indignation*); chen/natan/ravah (the *limitless suit* — natan ×3: offer / bride-price / the *withheld* sister); **mirmah (the family's *deceit weaponised* for massacre)**; cherpah/yakhol (the *covenant-purity pretext*); yatav/chaphets (the trap *welcomed*, Shechem's *hurrying delight*); **kaav/betach (the *pained, trusting security* exploited)**; chayil (the *spoil*); **baash (Jacob's *self-concern*)**; zanah (the *sibling-honour* invoked).
- **Whose interior:** the **human** interiors *violating then desiring* (Shechem), *silent then self-protective* (Jacob — quiet at the violation, alarmed at the reprisal), and *rightly indignant then treacherously murderous* (Simeon, Levi, the sons — real grievance, monstrous means). Dinah's interior is *not given* — she is silent throughout; the divine interior is *absent from the telling* — the chapter is *wholly human* and morally *unresolved*.
- **Threads:** the family *deceit* (mirmah 34:13 ← Jacob's blessing-theft 27:35, Laban's Leah-switch JAC-09, Rachel's teraphim JAC-12 — now *lethal* in the sons); anah/violation (← the word forbidden 31:50; the affliction-family, oni 29:32); atsav (the *grieved* indignation ← God grieved 6:6); Jacob's silence and self-concern (charesh 34:5 / baash 34:30 → his deathbed *curse* on Simeon and Levi's violence, Gen 49:5–7 — the reckoning deferred); the covenant sign *profaned as a weapon* (circumcision made bait); the reproach-word (cherpah 34:14 ← 30:23).
- **Guarded against:** importing a tidy verdict the text *withholds* — Scripture here *neither approves the massacre nor excuses Shechem*; I read the *operations* (violation, silence, indignation, deceit, vengeance, self-concern) and leave the moral tangle *open* as the chapter does; lumping the 3 tame, 4 shama, or 3 natan (fact/motive/justification; four distinct hearings; offer/price/withheld); reading Shechem's love (nephesh/lev 34:3) as *redeeming* the anah (the sequence is violation-*then*-desire); supplying Dinah an interior the text *denies* her (her silence is itself the datum); moralising Jacob's silence into either clear prudence or clear cowardice (the text leaves it *troublingly bare*); missing that the sons' *zanah* cry is *both* a real wound *and* a rationalisation of slaughter.

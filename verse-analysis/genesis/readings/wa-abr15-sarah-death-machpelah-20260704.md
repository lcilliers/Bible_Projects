## ABR-15-sarah-death-machpelah · Genesis 23:1–20 · [grief, and the sojourner's grave] — the honoured life, the weeping-and-mourning, and the pilgrim who owns only a tomb

**The passage.** "Sarah **lived** (chayyim) 127 years… the years of Sarah's life" (23:1); "Abraham went in to **mourn** (saphad) for Sarah and to **weep** (bakhah) for her" (23:2); "I am a **sojourner** and foreigner… **give** (natan) me property… for a burying place" (23:4); "**Hear** (shama) us… bury your dead" (23:6); "if it is your **will** (nephesh)… **hear** (shama) me" (23:8); "I **give** (natan) you the field… **hear** (shama) me" (23:9,11); "**hear** (shama) me… **listen** (shama)… Abraham **listened** (shama) to Ephron and weighed out the silver" (23:13,15,16).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **chayyim (lived) — Sarah's life *honoured* (23:1).** The finding is "Sarah **lived** (chayyim) 127 years; these were the years of Sarah's **life**" *(stated)*. The gem is that this is the **only woman whose lifespan Scripture records** — Sarah's years *noted* and *honoured*; the death-formula (← GEN-08) here *dignifies* her life before the grief begins *(inferred: chayyim = the honoured lifespan)*.
- **saphad (mourn) + bakhah (weep) — the *twofold* grief (23:2).** The finding is "Abraham went in to **mourn** (saphad) for Sarah and to **weep** (bakhah) for her" *(stated)*. Two gems: **saphad** — the **first "mourn/lament" (saphad)**, the *formal wailing/lament* of mourning; and **bakhah** — *weeping* (← 21:16 Hagar's first bakhah) — here Abraham's *private tears*. The operation is grief in *two registers*: the *formal lament* (saphad) and the *personal weeping* (bakhah) — the interior mourning its life-partner *(inferred: saphad = ritual lament; bakhah = the tears; distinct)*.
- **natan (give) — the *purchase-request*, the sojourner seeking a grave (23:4).** The finding is "I am a **sojourner** and foreigner among you; **give** (natan) me property… for a burying place" *(stated)*. The gem is the *self-understanding*: Abraham calls himself a **sojourner** (ger — a pilgrim owning nothing, *though promised all the land*), and *natan* here is a request *to acquire (buy)* a burial site — the interior's *first possession in the promised land is a grave* *(inferred: natan = the request to purchase; the pilgrim's only holding a tomb)*.
- **shama (hear) ×6 — the *honourable mutual-hearing* of the negotiation (23:6,8,11,13,15,16).** The finding is the repeated "**hear** (shama) us / hear me / listen", and "Abraham **listened** (shama) to Ephron and weighed out the silver" (23:16) *(stated)*. The gem is *shama* as the **courteous, scrupulous mutual-attentiveness** of an honourable transaction — the Hittites *hear* Abraham, he *hears* them, and he *insists on paying full price* (not taking the offered gift): the interior's **honourable dealing**, refusing to *owe* (← ABR-04's refusal of gain). The sixfold shama is *one operation* — *civil, fair listening* — its repetition marking the *care* of the dealing (distinct from hearing-God, heeding-a-voice, God-hearing-the-afflicted) *(inferred: shama = the mutual hearing of honourable negotiation; the repetition = its scrupulousness)*.
- **nephesh (will) — the *soul as willingness* (23:8).** The finding is "if it is your **will** (nephesh — 'if it is in your soul')" *(stated)*. The gem is a *new nephesh sub-sense* (H5315L, "soul: appetite/desire") — nephesh as the **seat of will/desire**: "if it is *your soul's* will" — the negotiation appealing to the other's *willingness*; distinct from nephesh-as-life (2:7), persons (12:5), life-in-blood (9:4) — here *the will/desire* *(inferred: nephesh = the willing soul)*.
- **natan (give) ×2 — the field *offered and bought* (23:9, 23:11).** The finding is Abraham's "let him **give** (natan) me the cave… for the full price" (23:9) and Ephron's "I **give** (natan) you the field" (23:11) *(stated)*. The operation is the transaction's two sides — *offered as gift* (Ephron), *taken as purchase* (Abraham insisting on full price); the interior *securing an honest, owned* burial-right *(inferred: natan offered vs natan paid — Abraham will not take it as gift)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **grief that honours and weeps (chayyim/saphad/bakhah 23:1–2):** Sarah's *life is honoured* (her recorded years), and Abraham *mourns and weeps* — the interior's grief in *both* formal lament and private tears at losing his partner.
2. **the sojourner's grave (natan/ger 23:4):** the man *promised all the land* calls himself a *sojourner* and seeks to *buy* a *burying place* — the interior's *first possession* in the promise's land is a *tomb*; faith holds the whole promise while owning only a grave (a pledge of the inheritance held in a place of death).
3. **honourable dealing (shama ×6, natan 23:9–16):** the *scrupulous, courteous* negotiation — mutual *hearing* (shama) — with Abraham *insisting on full payment* (not the gift): the interior *deals honourably*, refusing to *owe* even a grave (as it refused a king's spoil, ABR-04); the burial-right *legally, honestly secured*.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** chayyim — Sarah's *honoured* lifespan (the only woman's recorded); **saphad (first "mourn/lament") + bakhah (weep)** — grief in *two registers*; natan — the *purchase-request* and the *offered/paid* transaction; **the sojourner owning only a *grave*** (the pilgrim promised all); **shama ×6 — the *honourable mutual-hearing*** of the negotiation (Abraham *paying full price*); nephesh — a *new sub-sense*, the *willing soul*.
- **Whose interior:** the **human** interior *grieving* (mourning, weeping), *self-understood as a sojourner*, and *dealing honourably* (paying full price) — securing a grave in the land it does not yet possess.
- **Threads:** bakhah (← 21:16 Hagar; grief-weeping); the sojourner (→ the pilgrim-in-the-land motif; the promise held while owning a tomb); shama (honourable hearing; ← the many shama-senses); natan (the purchase, honest dealing, ← ABR-04 refusing gain); the burial-place (Machpelah → the patriarchs' grave).
- **Guarded against:** merging saphad and bakhah (formal lament vs private tears); inventing six distinct hearings from the negotiation's shama (one operation, honourable dealing, its repetition the emphasis); flattening nephesh's *will* sense into its life-senses; missing that the *sojourner* owns only a *grave* though promised all (the quiet gem); moralising the transaction beyond what the text shows (scrupulous, honourable dealing).

## GEN-14-babel · Genesis 11:1–9 · [the self-securing name, unmade] — the unbounded human purpose, the understanding broken, and the name they got instead of the name they sought

**The passage.** "Come, let us build… a tower… and let us **make a name** for ourselves, lest we be dispersed" (11:4); "the LORD said… this is only the beginning of what they will do… nothing that they **propose** (zamam) will be impossible" (11:6); "let us… confuse their language, so that they may not **understand** (shama) one another" (11:7); "its name was **called** (qara) Babel, because there the LORD confused" (11:9).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **(11:3–4 — "let us make a name for ourselves": self-securing, no non-T2 span.)** Narratively the builders resolve to make a *name* (shem) "*for ourselves*, lest we be dispersed" — the **self-exalting, self-securing** interior (← Cain's city named for his son, 4:17; the same grasp writ collective). Flagged: "name" (shem) carries no non-T2 span here, so no lexical operation is *claimed* — but it is the anchored *motive* the spans below answer: the unified will to *secure itself* by a name and a tower against dispersion *(no-span context)*.
- **zamam (propose/purpose) — the unified, unbounded human will (11:6).** The finding is "this is only the beginning… nothing that they **propose** (zamam) to do will now be impossible for them" *(stated)*. The operation is the **first zamam** — *concerted purposing/devising*, and God names its danger: *unified* human purpose is *unbounded* ("nothing impossible"). Distinct from the individual scheming of the heart (machashavah, 6:5): here the *collective* will, one people one language, devising without limit — the interior's capacity to *purpose* magnified by unity into a limitless force *(inferred)*.
- **shama (understand) — mutual understanding *broken* (11:7).** The finding is "confuse their language, so that they may not **understand** (shama) one another's speech" *(stated)*. The gem is *shama* in a *distinct sense* (H8085J, "hear: understand") — not the heeding-God (3:8) or heeding-a-voice (3:17) or demanding-audience (4:23) of earlier shama, but **mutual comprehension between persons** — and God *breaks* it. The operation is understanding-between-people *withdrawn*: the unified will is dissolved not by force but by the loss of *shared understanding* *(inferred: the collective purpose (zamam) depends on shared shama; sever the understanding and the purpose scatters)*.
- **qara (called Babel) — the name they *got* inverts the name they *sought* (11:9).** The finding is "its name was **called** (qara) Babel, because there the LORD **confused** the language" *(stated)*. The gem is the *irony sealed in the naming*: they set out to *make a name* for themselves (11:4); God *gives* them a name — **Babel/confusion** (balal). The operation is naming as *judgment recorded* — the self-sought name of greatness answered by the God-given name of *confusion*; the grasp for a name yields the name of its own undoing *(inferred: distinct qara — a place named for the judgment on it, vs Cain's self-memorial city-name 4:17; the self-name-making inverted)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **the self-securing motive → the unbounded purpose (11:4 → 11:6):** the will to *make a name* and avoid dispersion (context) is exactly the *zamam* God flags as limitless when unified — the interior's self-securing grasp becomes a boundless collective power.
2. **understanding broken dissolves the purpose (11:7):** God undoes the unified will not by destroying the builders but by withdrawing *shama* (mutual understanding) — the collective purpose (zamam) *rests on* shared understanding, so severing the shama scatters the purpose. The interior's unity was only as strong as its comprehension.
3. **the name inverted (11:4 → 11:9):** they sought a *name* (self-exaltation, self-securing); they received the *name* Babel — confusion. The very thing grasped for (a name, and staying together) is turned into its opposite (a name of confusion, and dispersion) — the self-securing project *produces* the dispersion it feared.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** the *self-securing name-making* as no-span motive (← 4:17); **zamam** — the *unified, unbounded* human purpose (distinct from individual machashavah, 6:5); **shama** as *mutual understanding* broken by God (a distinct shama-sense); **qara** — the judgment-name Babel *inverting* the sought-for name (vs Cain's self-memorial city).
- **Whose interior:** the **human** interior in *collective self-exaltation* — a unified will grasping at a name and permanence — met by the **divine** interior *dissolving* it through the loss of shared understanding.
- **Threads:** the self-securing name (← 4:17 Cain's city; the same grasp collective); zamam (→ human purpose/pride as a motif); shama (← 3:8/3:17/4:23, here mutual understanding); qara (← the naming operations; here judgment-name); dispersion (the very fear, 11:4, becomes the outcome, 11:9).
- **Guarded against:** claiming a lexical operation off the no-span "make a name" (read as motive-context); flattening zamam into generic "planning" (its danger is *unified* limitlessness); merging this shama with its other senses; missing the name→name inversion (the passage's irony); importing later Babel/pride theology beyond the self-name-making and confusion the verses state.

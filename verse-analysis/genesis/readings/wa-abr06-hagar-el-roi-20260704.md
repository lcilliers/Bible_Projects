## ABR-06-hagar-el-roi · Genesis 16:1–16 · [the promise seized, and the outcast seen] — the scheme that heeds the wrong voice, the affliction that recoils, and the fleeing slave who names the God who sees

**The passage.** "The LORD has **prevented** (atsar) me from bearing… go in to my servant"; "Abram **listened** (shama) to the voice of Sarai" (16:2); Sarai "**gave** (natan) Hagar… as a wife" (16:3); "I **gave** (natan) my servant to your **embrace** (cheq)… may the LORD **judge** (shaphat)" (16:5); "do as you **please** (tov)… Sarai dealt **harshly** (anah) with her" (16:6); "Return… and **submit** (anah) to her" (16:9); "I will **multiply** (ravah) your offspring… for **multitude** (rov)" (16:10); "the LORD has **listened** (shama) to your **affliction** (oni)… **call** (qara) his name Ishmael" (16:11); she "**called** (qara) the name of the LORD… You are a God of **seeing**" (16:13).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **atsar (prevented) — the divinely-closed womb (16:2).** The finding is "the LORD has **prevented** (atsar) me from bearing" *(stated)*. The operation is Sarai's *reading of providence* — she rightly sees God has *restrained* her womb, but draws from it a *scheme* rather than *waiting* *(inferred: atsar = God's restraint, the fact the scheme reacts against)*.
- **shama (listened) — heeding the *wrong voice*, the fall's fault echoed (16:2).** The finding is "Abram **listened** (shama) to the voice of Sarai" *(stated)*. The gem is that this is the *exact construction* of the fall's verdict — 3:17, "because you have **listened** (shama) to the voice of your wife" (GEN-05). Abram again *heeds his wife's voice* into a scheme that *bypasses God's timing*; distinct from the primeval shama-senses — here **heeding-the-wrong-counsel**, and *pointedly* the same fault as Eden *(inferred: the thread to 3:17 is deliberate — the interior taking the promise into its own hands)*.
- **natan (gave) #1 — the surrogate handed over (16:3).** The finding is "Sarai… **gave** (natan) her to Abram as a wife" *(stated)*. The operation is *the human scheme enacted* — giving the servant to secure the promise by contrivance; a natan of *self-help* (distinct from God's promissory land-gift) *(inferred)*.
- **cheq (embrace) + natan (gave) #2 + shaphat (judge) — the grievance and its appeal (16:5).** The finding is "I **gave** (natan) my servant to your **embrace** (cheq)… may the LORD **judge** (shaphat) between you and me" *(stated)*. The operations: **cheq** — the *intimacy* given (near-context); **natan #2** — Sarai *restates* her giving (now as grievance); **shaphat** — the **first "judge" (shaphat)**, Sarai *invoking God as arbiter* of the conflict *she* engineered — the interior appealing to divine justice while blind to its own part *(inferred: shaphat = judicial arbitration, distinct from din)*.
- **tov (please) — the subjective "as is good in your eyes" (16:6).** The finding is Abram's "do to her as you **please** (tov — as is good in your eyes)" *(stated)*. The operation is tov as *personal discretion* handed to Sarai — the appraisal-word as *self-referential license* (echoing the eye-driven "good" of the fall) *(inferred)*.
- **anah (harshly) #1 — affliction, the Egypt-word, roles reversed (16:6).** The finding is "Sarai dealt **harshly** (anah) with her, and she fled" *(stated)*. The gem is that *anah* is the **very word foretold of Israel's Egyptian bondage** — 15:13 (ABR-05), "they will be **afflicted** (anah)". Here a *Hebrew afflicts an Egyptian* (Hagar) — the affliction-operation *recurs with roles reversed*; the scheme's fruit is oppression *(inferred: anah = affliction-done-to; the thread to 15:13 is pointed)*.
- **anah (submit) #2 — the same root turned reflexive: *humble yourself* (16:9).** The finding is "Return to your mistress and **submit** (anah, hithpael) to her" *(stated)*. The gem is the *same root* anah — Sarai *afflicted* (piel, 16:6), Hagar is told to *afflict/humble herself* (hithpael, 16:9): the operation is **self-humbling under the affliction** — a hard word, distinct from 16:6 (affliction *done to* her) — here *accepting* the lowly place, with the promise attached *(inferred: anah done-to vs anah self-imposed submission)*.
- **ravah (multiply) + rov (multitude) — Abraham's promise given to the *outcast* (16:10).** The finding is "I will surely **multiply** (ravah) your offspring… for **multitude** (rov)" *(stated)*. The gem is that the *same multiply-promise given to Abram* (15:5) is here given to **Hagar** — the afflicted foreign slave-woman receives the Abrahamic *ravah*; the promise reaches the *cast-out* *(inferred: ravah extended to the outcast; rov = the abundance)*.
- **oni (affliction) + shama (listened) #2 — God *hears the afflicted* (16:11).** The finding is "the LORD has **listened** (shama) to your **affliction** (oni)" — the son named Ishmael ("God hears") *(stated)*. The gem is the *counter* to 16:2: **Abram heeded Sarai** (shama, into the mess); **God hears Hagar's affliction** (shama, to rescue). The operations: **oni** — Hagar's *state of affliction* (the noun of the anah-root); **shama** — *God's hearing* of it. The interior's affliction is *heard by God*, and the son's very name records it *(inferred: two shama — man's wrong-heeding vs God's rescuing-hearing)*.
- **qara (call) — naming Ishmael, then *naming God*, then the well, then the son (16:11,13,14,15).** The finding is the fourfold **qara**: "**call** (qara) his name Ishmael" (16:11); she "**called** (qara) the name of the LORD… You are a God of **seeing**" (16:13); the well "**called** (qara) Beer-lahai-roi" (16:14); "Abram **called** (qara) his son's name Ishmael" (16:15) *(stated)*. The gem is 16:13: **Hagar *names God*** — "El Roi, the God who sees me" — *the only person in Scripture to give God a name*, and it is the *afflicted fleeing slave*. The distinct qara-acts: naming the son "God hears" (16:11); **naming God "the God who sees"** (16:13, unique — the invocation-qara turned to *naming God himself*); naming the well for the seeing (16:14); Abram's naming of Ishmael (16:15) *(inferred: the seen-and-heard outcast responds by naming the God-who-sees — the interior's answer to being seen)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **the promise *seized* (atsar → shama → natan, 16:2–3):** Sarai reads God's restraint (atsar) but, instead of *waiting* on the promise Abram just *believed* (ABR-05, aman), the household *schemes*; Abram *heeds the wrong voice* (shama, the fall's fault, 3:17); Hagar is *given* (natan) — faith's promise taken into human hands.
2. **the affliction recoils (anah 16:6 ← 15:13):** the scheme yields contempt, grievance (shaphat), and *affliction* (anah) — a Hebrew *afflicting* an Egyptian in the very word of Israel's future *Egyptian* affliction; self-help breeds oppression.
3. **the hard word *with* the promise (anah 16:9 / ravah 16:10):** the outcast is told to *humble herself* (anah, hithpael) *and* is given Abraham's *multiply*-promise (ravah) — submission and blessing joined.
4. **God hears and sees the outcast (oni/shama 16:11 → qara/El-Roi 16:13):** against Abram's wrong-*heeding* (16:2), **God hears** Hagar's *affliction* (shama/oni) and **is seen** by her; the *lowest* character — a fleeing foreign slave — receives the revelation of the *seeing-hearing* God and *names* him. The interior no one else regards is *regarded by God*, and answers by naming him.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** atsar (God's restraint of the womb); **shama — Abram heeding Sarai (← the fall's 3:17)** vs **shama — God hearing Hagar's affliction (16:11)**; natan as the *surrogate scheme* (16:3) and Sarai's *grievance* (16:5); shaphat (first "judge", invoked by the one who caused the conflict); tov as *subjective license*; **anah — affliction *done-to* (16:6, ← Israel's Egypt-word 15:13, roles reversed)** vs **anah — *self-humbling* submission (16:9)**; oni (the affliction God heard); ravah — Abraham's *multiply*-promise given to the *outcast*; **qara — Hagar *naming God* "El Roi" (the only human to name God)**, alongside the naming of Ishmael and the well.
- **Whose interior:** the **human** interior *seizing the promise* (Sarai/Abram) and *afflicting the weak*; and the **outcast** interior *afflicted, humbled, heard, and seen* — answering by *naming the God who sees*; with the **divine** interior *hearing and seeing the one no one regards*.
- **Threads:** shama (← 3:17 the fall's fault, echoed in Abram; God's rescuing hearing); anah/oni (← 15:13 the foretold affliction, roles reversed; → the affliction-and-hearing motif); ravah (← 15:5, extended to Hagar); qara — *naming God* (unique); aman (← ABR-05 — believed, yet here the household does not *wait*); the promise seized vs the promise given.
- **Guarded against:** softening the 3:17↔16:2 echo (the same "listened to the voice of his wife" — a deliberate thread, not coincidence); merging the two anah (afflict vs submit) or two shama (wrong-heeding vs God-hearing) or the qara-acts; missing that the *outcast* names God; moralising the scheme beyond what the text shows (it *narrates* the seizing and its recoil, and God's regard of Hagar, without a stated verdict); importing later Hagar/Sarah allegory (Gal 4) — the operations, not the epistle.

## JAC-09-rachel-served-deceived · Genesis 29:1–30 · [the deceiver deceived] — the well, the love that made years seem days, and the younger-for-elder switch turned back on the supplanter

**The passage.** Jacob asks "do you **know** (yada) Laban?… is it **well** (shalom)?" (29:5–6); "we **cannot** (yakhol) [roll the stone] until all are gathered" (29:8); Jacob rolls it, kissed Rachel and **wept** (bakhah) aloud (29:11); Laban **heard** (shama) the **news** (shema), ran to **meet** (qara) him (29:13), "you are my bone and my **flesh** (basar)" (29:14); "should you **serve** (avad) me for nothing?" (29:15); "I will **serve** (avad) seven years for Rachel" (29:18); "better (tov) I **give** (natan) her to you" (29:19); "Jacob **served** (avad) seven years… seemed a few days for the **love** (ahavah) he had" (29:20); Laban **gave** (natan) Zilpah to Leah (29:24); "it was Leah! Did I not **serve** (avad) for Rachel? Why have you **deceived** (ramah) me?" (29:25); "**serving** (avad) another seven years… **service** (avodah)" (29:27); **gave** (natan) Bilhah to Rachel (29:29); "**served** (avad) another seven years" (29:30).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **yada (know) — the *acquaintance* inquiry (29:5).** "do you **know** (yada) Laban the son of Nahor?" *(stated)* — yada in the *acquaintance* sense: Jacob *locating his kin*; a practical knowing (contrast the covenant-knowing of God) — the interior *seeking its way toward the family* *(inferred: yada = practical acquaintance-knowing)*.
- **shalom (well) — the *well-being* inquiry (29:6).** "is it **well** (shalom) with him?" *(stated)* — shalom in the *well-being* register: the customary *is-he-at-peace* question; the interior's *courteous concern* for the kinsman's welfare (distinct from the *homecoming-peace* of 28:21) *(inferred: shalom = the well-being asked after)*.
- **yakhol (cannot) — the shepherds' *constraint* (29:8).** "we **cannot** (yakhol) until all the flocks are gathered and the stone rolled" *(stated)* — yakhol in the *negated* mode: the *inability/constraint* of custom (the great stone, the waiting) — a foil to Jacob, who *singlehandedly rolls the stone* the moment he sees Rachel; the interior's *energy for love* set against the others' *cannot* *(inferred: yakhol = the constraint Jacob's love overrides)*.
- **bakhah (wept) — the fugitive's *overflow* at meeting Rachel (29:11).** "Jacob kissed Rachel and **wept** (bakhah) aloud" *(stated)* — bakhah, *weep*: the gem is *these* tears — not grief but *overwhelmed relief and love*; the *fleeing, friendless* deceiver, arriving at his mother's kin and *first sight of Rachel*, *breaks into weeping* — the interior *unguarded*, love and homecoming-relief flooding out *(inferred: bakhah = the tears of relief and love at the journey's welcome)*.
- **shama (heard) + shema (news) + qara (meet) — Laban's *eager welcome* (29:13).** "as soon as Laban **heard** (shama) the **news** (shema)… he ran to **meet** (qara) him" *(stated)*. Three spans: **shama** — Laban *hears*; **shema** — the *report* itself; **qara** — the *running to encounter*. The interior's *warm welcome* of the kinsman (echoing Rebekah's household hospitality, 24) — though Laban's eagerness will prove *self-interested* *(inferred: shama/shema/qara = the eager hearing-and-running welcome)*.
- **basar (flesh) — the *kinship bond* named (29:14).** "surely you are my bone and my **flesh** (basar)!" *(stated)* — basar, *flesh*: Laban *claims the blood-tie* (← the one-flesh 2:23–24, here kin-flesh) — the bond that *should* protect Jacob but will be *exploited* for cheap labour; the interior's kinship-claim *(inferred: basar = the flesh-kinship invoked, soon exploited)*.
- **avad (serve me for nothing?) — service framed as *owed* (29:15).** "because you are my kinsman, should you **serve** (avad) me for nothing?" *(stated)* — the *first* avad: Laban *frames the coming service* as something that *ought to be waged* — but the very framing *sets up the bargain* he will manipulate; service *monetised* from the start *(inferred: avad = service framed as owed wages — the bargain opened)*.
- **avad (I will serve seven years) — service *freely offered for love* (29:18).** *Distinct from 29:15:* "I will **serve** (avad) seven years for your younger daughter Rachel" *(stated)* — the gem: Jacob *offers* the service, *and names Rachel "the younger"* — the interior *willingly binding itself to long labour for love*; the grasper, for once, *gives* rather than seizes — *seven years* pledged for Rachel *(inferred: avad = the long service freely offered for love)*.
- **tov (better) + natan (give her) — Laban's *promise to give Rachel* (29:19).** "it is **better** (tov) that I **give** (natan) her to you than to another man" *(stated)*. **tov** — Laban judges it *good/preferable*; **natan (1st)** — the *promise to give Rachel* — which he *will break*. The interior's *fair-seeming word* that masks the coming deceit *(inferred: tov/natan = the plausible promise to give Rachel, later betrayed)*.
- **avad (served) + ahavah (love) — the *service transfigured by love* (29:20).** *Distinct from 29:15,18:* "Jacob **served** (avad) seven years… they seemed but a few days for the **love** (ahavah) he had for her" *(stated)*. **avad** — the *seven years actually served*; **ahavah** — the gem: **love** *compresses time* — the long labour *felt light*; the interior *so given to love* that *years pass as days*. The one place Jacob's grasping self is *wholly generous and patient* — love *transfigures* the service *(inferred: avad/ahavah = the long service made light by love)*.
- **natan (gave Zilpah) — the *maid given with the wrong bride* (29:24).** *Distinct from 29:19:* "Laban **gave** (natan) Zilpah to his daughter Leah as servant" *(stated)* — natan, *give*: a *quiet parenthetical* — the maid *given with Leah* (the substituted bride) — the narrator *lets the giving of Zilpah mark that it is Leah, not Rachel, being delivered*; the deception *furnished with its trappings* *(inferred: natan = the maid given, marking the wrong bride delivered)*.
- **avad (did I not serve for Rachel?) + ramah (deceived) — the *deceiver deceived* (29:25).** "in the morning, behold, it was Leah!… did I not **serve** (avad) for Rachel? Why have you **deceived** (ramah) me?" *(stated)*. **avad** — the *service invoked as grievance* (I served for *Rachel*!); **ramah** — the gem of the whole passage: **ramah — *deceive/beguile*** — the man who *deceived his blind father in the dark, exploiting senses* (27) is now *deceived in the dark by a substituted sibling*; his own outrage — "why have you **deceived** me?" — *echoes the very wrong he did*. The interior *reaps its own method*; the deceiver *deceived* *(inferred: avad/ramah = the betrayed service and the deception turned back on the deceiver)*.
- **(29:26 — "we do not give the younger before the firstborn", no non-T2 span.)** Flagged: Laban's justification carries no flagged span, but it is the *ironic hinge*: the *younger-for-elder switch* — the very *inversion Jacob seized* (birthright/blessing of the *firstborn*, 25:31; 27:19) — is now *turned against him by an appeal to firstborn-right*. The interior that *overturned the elder/younger order* is *bound by it*. Read as context, anchored by ramah/avad *(no-span context; the inversion reversed onto the supplanter)*.
- **avad (serving) + avodah (service) — the *doubled price* (29:27).** *Distinct from prior avad:* "**serve** (avad) me another seven years… in return for **service** (avodah)" *(stated)*. **avad** — the *second* seven-year term *imposed*; **avodah** — the *service* as the *price* of Rachel. The gem: the deception *costs Jacob another seven years* — the grasper made to *pay double* for what he loves *(inferred: avad/avodah = the doubled service exacted by the deceiver)*.
- **natan (gave Bilhah) — the *maid given with the right bride* (29:29).** *Distinct from 29:19,24:* "Laban **gave** (natan) Bilhah to his daughter Rachel as servant" *(stated)* — natan, *give*: the *parallel parenthetical* to 29:24, now marking *Rachel* delivered (the true bride at last) — the giving that *completes the doubled bargain* *(inferred: natan = the maid given, marking the right bride finally delivered)*.
- **avad (served another seven) — the *service resumed for Rachel* (29:30).** *Distinct — the final avad:* "he loved Rachel more than Leah, and **served** (avad) Laban another seven years" *(stated)* — the *closing* service: the interior *pays on*, love still driving the labour, but now under a *doubled yoke* — and the seed of the wives' rivalry ("loved Rachel more than Leah") *sown* *(inferred: avad = the resumed service under the doubled price)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **arrival, energy, and unguarded tears (yada/shalom 29:5–6 → yakhol 29:8 → bakhah 29:11):** the fugitive *locates his kin*, *singlehandedly rolls the stone* the others *cannot*, and *weeps* at meeting Rachel — the interior *unguarded*, love and relief overflowing.
2. **welcome and the monetised bond (shama/shema/qara 29:13 → basar 29:14 → avad 29:15):** Laban *runs to welcome* and *claims the flesh-kinship* — then *frames the service as waged*, opening the bargain the blood-tie will *exploit*.
3. **love binds itself to long service (avad 29:18 → tov/natan 29:19 → avad/ahavah 29:20):** Jacob *freely offers seven years for love*; Laban *promises Rachel*; and **love makes the seven years seem days** — the grasper *wholly generous* here.
4. **the deceiver deceived (natan-Zilpah 29:24 → avad/ramah 29:25 → [29:26 the inversion reversed] → avad/avodah 29:27 → natan-Bilhah 29:29 → avad 29:30):** in the dark, *Leah is substituted*; Jacob *cries the very word of his own crime* — "why have you **deceived** me?" — and Laban's *"we do not give the younger before the firstborn"* turns Jacob's *own inversion* against him. The supplanter *reaps his method*, *pays double*, and *serves on* — the interior *taught by its own deceit received back*.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** yada (*acquaintance*-knowing); shalom (*well-being* asked); yakhol (the *constraint* love overrides); **bakhah (the fugitive's *tears of relief and love*, not grief)**; shama/shema/qara (the *eager welcome*); basar (the *flesh-kinship* soon exploited); avad ×6 *distinguished* (29:15 service *framed as waged* / 29:18 *freely offered* for love / 29:20 *transfigured by love* — years as days / 29:25 *invoked as grievance* betrayed / 29:27 the *doubled* term imposed / 29:30 *resumed* under the doubled yoke) + avodah (the *service as price*); natan ×3 (29:19 the *promise* of Rachel, broken / 29:24 the maid marking the *wrong* bride / 29:29 the maid marking the *right* bride); tov (Laban's *plausible* word); **ahavah (the *love* that compresses time — Jacob's one unguarded generosity)**; **ramah (the *deceiver deceived*)**.
- **Whose interior:** the **human** interior *unguarded in love* (Jacob's tears, the years-as-days) and *made to reap its own deceit* (deceived in the dark, paying double); the *self-interested welcomer* (Laban — kinship claimed, then exploited). No divine word — the *moral symmetry* (deceiver deceived) works *through the events*.
- **Threads:** *the deceiver deceived* (ramah 29:25 ← Jacob's mirmah against Isaac 27:35; the dark/senses used against the user of the dark); *the inversion reversed* (29:26 "the younger before the firstborn" ← Jacob's seizure of the *firstborn's* right 25:31, 27:19); love as the interior's one generosity (ahavah 29:20 → the wives' rivalry it seeds, "loved Rachel more" 29:30 → JAC-10); service (avad → the long labour theme, → the wage-dispute JAC-11, the 20-years tally 31:41); kinship exploited (basar 29:14 → Laban's changed wages 31:7).
- **Guarded against:** collapsing the six avad or three natan into single serve/give operations (waged / offered / transfigured / betrayed / doubled / resumed; and the three natan mark *promise* vs *wrong bride* vs *right bride* — distinct); reading bakhah as grief when the context is *love and relief*; missing the *moral symmetry* by treating the Leah-substitution as mere plot (the *deceiver deceived*, and the *inversion turned back*, is the finding); importing a "Jacob's discipline/sanctification" theology over what the text *shows* — the deceit *received back* and the *doubled service*, read off ramah and the doubled avad.

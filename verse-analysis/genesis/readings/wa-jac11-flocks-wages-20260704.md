## JAC-11-flocks-wages · Genesis 30:25–43 · [two schemers, and the blessing that works through the cunning] — the wage-bargain, honesty invoked, the counter-tricks, and Jacob's prospering

**The passage.** After Joseph's birth Jacob asks leave: "**Give** (natan) me my wives and children for whom I **served** (avad)… you **know** (yada) the **service** (avodah) I gave" (30:26); Laban — "if I have found **favor** (chen)… I have learned the LORD has **blessed** (barakh) me because of you" (30:27); "you **know** (yada) how I **served** (avad)" (30:29); "the LORD has **blessed** (barakh) you wherever I turned; it **increased abundantly** (rov)" (30:30); "what shall I **give** (natan)? … I will pasture and **keep** (shamar)" (30:31); "**removing** (sur) every speckled… my wages" (30:32); "my **honesty** (tsedaqah) will answer for me" (30:33); "Laban **removed** (sur) the striped… gave them to his sons" (30:35); the peeled sticks, the selective breeding, "the man **increased greatly**" (30:43).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **natan (Give me my wives) — the *claim to depart with his own* (30:26a).** "**Give** (natan) me my wives and children for whom I served" *(stated)* — natan, *give*: Jacob *asks for what is his by his labour* — the interior *claiming its earned family*; the request that opens the reckoning *(inferred: natan = the claim to leave with his own)*.
- **yada (you know) + avad (served) + avodah (service) — the *labour invoked as ground* (30:26b).** "you **know** (yada) the **service** (avodah) I have **served** (avad) you" *(stated)*. **yada** — Jacob *appeals to Laban's knowledge*; **avad**/**avodah** — the *service rendered* named as the *basis of the claim*. The interior *grounds its right in the labour done* (the fourteen years, ← JAC-09) *(inferred: yada/avad/avodah = the service invoked as the ground of the claim)*.
- **chen (favor) — Laban's *courteous, self-interested opener* (30:27a).** "if I have found **favor** (chen) in your sight" *(stated)* — chen, *favor/grace*: Laban *seeks Jacob's goodwill* — but to *keep* him, not release him; the interior's *ingratiating* move that precedes the counter-bargain *(inferred: chen = the favor sought to retain the profitable son-in-law)*.
- **barakh (blessed me because of you) — Laban's *admission* of the blessing-conduit (30:27b).** "I have learned by divination that the LORD has **blessed** (barakh) me because of you" *(stated)* — the gem: **barakh** — Laban *confesses Jacob is the channel of God's blessing* (the Abrahamic "in you… blessed", ← 12:3; 26:24 — now *even the exploiter admits it*) — though he reads it *superstitiously* ("by divination"). The interior *acknowledges the blessing it profits from* *(inferred: barakh = the blessing-conduit admitted by the exploiter)*.
- **yada (you know) + avad (served) — the *service pressed again* (30:29).** *yada/avad distinct from 30:26:* "you yourself **know** (yada) how I **served** (avad) you, how your livestock fared" *(stated)* — here yada is *of the service's quality/result* (the flocks' flourishing under Jacob), not merely its existence; the interior *presses the proof of its worth* *(inferred: yada/avad = the proven quality of the service pressed)*.
- **rov (abundantly) + barakh (blessed you) — Jacob's *assertion* of the blessing-conduit (30:30).** *barakh distinct from 30:27:* "it **increased abundantly** (rov), the LORD has **blessed** (barakh) you wherever I turned" *(stated)*. **rov** — *abundance*: the flocks *multiplied greatly*; **barakh** — where Laban *admitted* the conduit (30:27), Jacob now *asserts* it — "blessed *wherever I turned*". The interior *claims its own role in the other's prosperity* — pressing toward "when shall I provide for my own?" *(inferred: rov/barakh = the abundance and the conduit-role now asserted by Jacob)*.
- **natan (what shall I give / give me nothing) — the *wage refused* for a scheme (30:31).** *natan distinct from 30:26:* "'what shall I **give** (natan)?' 'You shall not **give** (natan) me anything'" *(stated)* — natan, *give*: Jacob *refuses a fixed wage* and *proposes the speckled-scheme* instead; the interior *declines the straightforward payment* for a *self-selected*, cunning arrangement *(inferred: natan = the fixed wage refused in favour of the scheme)*.
- **shamar (keep) — the *continued keeping* offered (30:31b).** "I will again pasture your flock and **keep** (shamar) it" *(stated)* — shamar, *keep/guard*: Jacob *offers to go on tending* — the service *sustained* as the frame for the wage-scheme *(inferred: shamar = the continued flock-keeping offered)*.
- **sur (removing) — Jacob's *self-selected wage* proposed (30:32).** "let me pass through, **removing** (sur) every speckled and spotted… they shall be my wages" *(stated)* — sur, *turn aside/remove*: the interior *proposes to take* the *odd-coloured* animals — an ostensibly *modest* wage (the minority colours) that his cunning will *turn into wealth* *(inferred: sur = the odd-coloured animals proposed as wage)*.
- **tsedaqah (honesty) — *righteousness invoked* by the schemer (30:33).** "so my **honesty** (tsedaqah) will answer for me later" *(stated)* — the gem: **tsedaqah — *righteousness/honesty*** — Jacob *appeals to his own integrity* as the *test* (any non-speckled animal found with him = stolen). The *tension*: the man of *mirmah* (27:35) *invokes tsedaqah* — and then works the *breeding trick*; the interior *claims righteousness while planning cunning* (the verse states the claim; the scheme follows). *(inferred: tsedaqah = the integrity invoked, held in tension with the scheme)*.
- **sur (removed) — Laban's *counter-trick* (30:35).** *sur distinct from 30:32:* "that day Laban **removed** (sur) the striped and spotted… gave them to his sons, three days' journey off" *(stated)* — sur, *remove*: the gem is the *reversal* — Laban *pre-emptively removes the very animals that would be Jacob's wage*, handing them away; the deceiver's father-in-law *out-schemes the scheme*; the interior *cheats first*. Two sur operations: Jacob's *proposed* removal (30:32) / Laban's *pre-emptive* removal (30:35) — cunning against cunning *(inferred: sur = Laban's counter-removal, out-manoeuvring Jacob)*.
- **(30:37–42 — the peeled sticks and selective breeding, no non-T2 span.)** Flagged: the *breeding scheme* (peeled poplar at the troughs; breeding the *stronger* for himself, leaving the *feebler* to Laban) carries no flagged non-T2 span, but it is the scene's *interior act*: Jacob *answers Laban's counter-trick with his own manipulation* — cunning *met by cunning*; the interior *building its wealth by craft*. Read as context, anchored by sur/tsedaqah/rov *(no-span context; the counter-scheme of the breeding)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **the claim grounded in labour (natan 30:26a → yada/avad/avodah 30:26b → yada/avad 30:29):** Jacob *asks to leave with his own*, *grounding the claim in the service rendered* and *its proven quality* — the interior pressing its earned right.
2. **the blessing-conduit admitted, then asserted (chen 30:27a → barakh 30:27b → rov/barakh 30:30):** Laban *ingratiates* and *admits* the LORD blessed him *through Jacob*; Jacob then *asserts* the same — the Abrahamic blessing *acknowledged even by the exploiter*, and *claimed* by the exploited.
3. **the wage refused for a scheme (natan 30:31 → shamar 30:31b → sur 30:32 → tsedaqah 30:33):** Jacob *declines a fixed wage*, *offers continued keeping*, *proposes the odd-coloured animals* as wage, and *invokes his own honesty* as the test — righteousness claimed *at the threshold of a trick*.
4. **cunning against cunning (sur 30:35 → [breeding scheme 30:37–42] → rov 30:43):** Laban *out-schemes first* (removing the wage-animals); Jacob *counter-manipulates the breeding* — and *increases greatly*. The blessing *works through and despite the mutual grasping*; the interior *prospers by craft*, yet (as the next passage will say, 31:9) it is *God who gave* the flocks.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** natan ×2 (30:26 the *claim* to his own / 30:31 the *fixed wage refused*); yada ×2 (30:26 the *service known to exist* / 30:29 its *proven quality*); avad/avodah (the *labour as ground of claim*); chen (Laban's *ingratiating, retentive* favor); barakh ×2 (30:27 the conduit *admitted* by Laban / 30:30 *asserted* by Jacob); rov (the flocks' *abundance*); shamar (the *continued keeping*); sur ×2 (30:32 Jacob's *proposed* wage-removal / 30:35 Laban's *pre-emptive counter*-removal); **tsedaqah (righteousness *invoked by the schemer* — held in tension with the breeding-trick)**; the *breeding scheme* (no-span context — cunning met by cunning).
- **Whose interior:** the **human** interiors of *two schemers* — the *retentive, cheating* father-in-law (Laban) and the *claim-pressing, righteousness-invoking, counter-scheming* son-in-law (Jacob); the **divine** interior *blessing through the conduit* — the blessing operating *even amid* the mutual grasping (Jacob as the channel Laban profits from).
- **Threads:** *the grasping interior* (Jacob's cunning ← the birthright bought JAC-02, the blessing stolen JAC-05 — now *building flocks by craft*; the wealth real but *grasped*, contrast Isaac's *received* prosperity JAC-04); the deceiver deceived, continued (Laban's counter-trick ← the Leah-substitution JAC-09 — the two out-scheme each other); the blessing-conduit (barakh 30:27,30 ← 12:3 "in you… blessed"); *God gave it* (rov 30:43 → 31:9 "God has taken your father's livestock and given it to me" — the theological re-reading Jacob himself will give); tsedaqah invoked (→ the self-justifying tally of 20 years, 31:38–42).
- **Guarded against:** collapsing the pairs (two natan, two yada, two avad, two barakh, two sur) into single operations (admitted-vs-asserted blessing; proposed-vs-counter removal — the distinctions carry the *cunning-against-cunning* finding); reading Jacob's tsedaqah-claim at face value *or* condemning it — I read only that the verse *states* he invokes his honesty, and the scheme *follows* (the tension is the finding, not a verdict); crediting the breeding-magic as the cause when the passage itself will be *re-read* as God's giving (31:9) — I note the craft *and* leave the causation where 30:43/31:9 put it; importing a moral lesson the text doesn't state — the grasping and the blessing simply *coexist* here.

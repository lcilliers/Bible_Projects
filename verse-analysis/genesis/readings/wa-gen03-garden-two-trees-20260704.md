## GEN-03-garden-two-trees · Genesis 2:4–25 · [human interior constituted] — dust-and-breath, the desirable trees, the naming that finds no helper, the three fleshes, and the unashamed baseline

**The passage.** God "**formed** the man of dust… and **breathed** into his nostrils the **breath of life**, and the man became a **living creature**" (2:7); the trees are "**pleasant** to the sight and **good** for food", with the tree of **life** and the tree of the **knowledge** of good and evil (2:9); man is put in the garden "to **work** it and **keep** it" (2:15); commanded not to eat of the **knowledge** tree on pain of death (2:17); "it is **not good** that the man should be alone" (2:18); the man **names** the animals but finds no helper (2:19–20); the woman is built and the man says "**flesh** of my flesh… she shall be **called** Woman" (2:23), "they shall become one **flesh**" (2:24); "both **naked** and were **not ashamed**" (2:25).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **avad (work) #1 — the *absent* worker (2:5).** The finding is "there was no man to **work** the ground" (2:5) *(stated)*. The operation is vocation named *by its lack* — the ground awaits a worker; labour is written into the design as something *missing* until man *(inferred)*.
- **2:7 — the constitution of the human, a compound of four operations.** The finding is man **formed** of dust, God **breathing** (naphach) the **breath** (neshamah) of **life** (chay) into him, and man *becoming* a **living creature** (nephesh chay) *(stated)*. The operations, kept apart: **naphach (breathe)** = God's *personal in-breathing* — not a spoken fiat but God's own breath given; **neshamah (breath)** = the specific *breath-of-life* imparted (a word for the breath God breathes, distinct from nephesh and from ruach); **nephesh chay (living creature/soul)** = what man *becomes*. The gem is the **same-yet-different**: man becomes a *nephesh chay* — the **very phrase used of the animals** (1:20,24) — so the human shares the animals' soul-life; **but he receives it by a wholly different process** — God's intimate in-breathing of neshamah, where the animals were *spoken* into being. Same soul-life; more intimate origin *(inferred: do not read a "higher soul" into nephesh here — the dignity is not a different substance but a different *manner of impartation*; man is dust made living by God's own breath)*.
- **2:9 — the trees: desirability by *design*, and the two named trees.** The finding is every tree "**pleasant** (chamad — desirable) to the sight and **good** (tov) for food", the tree of **life** (chayyim), the tree of the **knowledge** (daat) of good and evil *(stated)*. The operations: **chamad (desirable)** — the trees are *made* desirable-to-look-at by God; desirability is here *good, God-given* (hold this — the identical chamad becomes the fall's disordered coveting at 3:6, "desired to make wise"; the thread is that desirability is good by design, grasped wrongly later); **tov (good) for food** — the appraisal-word now naming *fitness-for-life*; **daat (knowledge)** — introduced as the *name* of a tree, the knowledge of good and evil set in the midst *(inferred)*.
- **tov (good) #2 — the goodness of *gold* (2:12).** The finding is "the **gold** of that land is **good**" (2:12) *(stated)*. The operation is tov in a *different sense* — **material excellence/quality**, not creational or moral good; a distinct use of the appraisal-word, applied to a metal *(inferred: keep apart from the creational "good" of GEN-01 and the "good for food" of 2:9)*.
- **2:15 — settled to rest, yet to work and guard (three operations).** The finding is God "**put**" (nuach — caused to rest/settle) the man in the garden "to **work** (avad) it and **keep** (shamar) it" *(stated)*. The operations: **nuach (rest/settle)** — man is *placed at rest* in the garden; **avad (work) #2** — the vocation of 2:5 now *given* (the absent worker supplied); **shamar (keep/guard)** — man made *guardian* of the place. So man's first condition is *rest that contains labour and guardianship* — work and keeping are not the curse (that comes at 3:17); they are the content of the unfallen rest *(inferred: avad #2 fills the lack of avad #1 — same word, the gap closed)*.
- **daat (knowledge) #2 — the *prohibited* tree with the death-sanction (2:17).** The finding is "of the tree of the **knowledge** of good and evil you shall not eat… you shall surely die" (2:17) *(stated)*. The operation is daat now under *prohibition* — at 2:9 it named a tree; here it is the *forbidden* knowledge bound to death; the distinction is introduction (2:9) → interdiction-with-consequence (2:17) *(inferred)*.
- **tov (good) #3 — the *only* "not good": solitude (2:18).** The finding is "it is **not good** that the man should be **alone**" (2:18) *(stated)*. The operation is the appraisal-word *inverted* — the **first and only "not good" in the creation account**, and its object is *human aloneness*; against the sixfold "good" and the "very good" (GEN-01/02), God here names a *lack* in the very-good world: the solitary human. The gem is that the one thing pronounced *not* good is *being alone* — the interior is made for relation, and its solitude is a real deficiency God moves to remedy *(inferred: this is not a flaw in creation but a design pointing beyond the individual; the "not good" is relational, not moral)*.
- **qara (name) — man's naming, in three beats, and what it *reveals* (2:19–20).** The finding is God bringing the animals "to **see what he would call** them" (2:19), "whatever the man **called**… that was its name" (2:19), "the man **gave names** to all" (2:20) *(stated)*. The operation is **naming now predicated of *man*** — distinct from God's naming of the cosmic domains (GEN-01 1:5,8,10): here man names the *living creatures*, a *delegated* exercise of discernment and authority (God *defers*: "whatever the man called… that was its name"). And the interactive twist: the naming *surfaces his solitude* — "but for Adam there was **not found a helper**" (2:20); cataloguing the paired creatures makes his own pairlessness legible *(inferred: man's naming is both authority and self-discovery — it is *in* naming the others that he finds himself alone)*.
- **basar (flesh) — three distinct senses tracing a union (2:21,23,24).** The finding is basar three times *(stated)*, and they are **not one operation**: **2:21** "closed up its place with **flesh**" = flesh as *physical material* (the wound closed); **2:23** "**flesh** of my flesh" = flesh as *shared substance / kinship-identity* (recognition of common origin); **2:24** "one **flesh**" = flesh as *marital union* (two made one). The gem is the *movement* the three senses trace: flesh-as-matter (surgery) → flesh-as-shared-self (recognition) → flesh-as-union (marriage) *(inferred: merging the three would erase that basar carries the whole arc from material to relational union)*.
- **qara (name) — naming the woman, a naming of *recognition* (2:23).** The finding is "she shall be **called** Woman, because she was taken out of Man" (2:23) *(stated)*. The operation is naming *again* by man — but where naming the animals surfaced his *lack* (2:20), naming the woman voices *recognition and belonging* ("bone of my bones") — the same operation (qara) turned from cataloguing-the-other to *claiming-the-kindred* *(inferred: distinct from 2:19–20; the naming that found no helper now names the helper found)*.
- **bosh (shame) — the unashamed baseline (2:25).** The finding is "both **naked** and were **not ashamed**" (2:25, bosh negated) *(stated)*. The operation is shame *by its absence* — the interior's *unfallen* condition: full exposure without shame, nothing to hide, no self-consciousness before the other or God. This is the baseline the next passage destroys (3:7–10, the eyes opened, the hiding) *(inferred: the finding is the *possibility* the fall forecloses — an interior at ease in total openness)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **dust + divine breath → living nephesh (2:7):** the human interior is constituted as a *union* of the lowly (dust) and the intimate (God's own breath) — neither pure earth nor pure spirit; and its soul-life is *shared* with the animals yet *imparted* more personally.
2. **the "not good" (2:18) → the naming (2:19–20) → the woman (2:21–23):** God names the lack (solitude); the naming-operation makes the lack *felt* ("no helper found"); the woman resolves it. The naming thus *serves* the "not good" — it is the process by which the deficiency God named becomes the man's own discovery.
3. **basar's three senses + the second naming:** the movement flesh-material → flesh-shared → one-flesh (2:21,23,24), sealed by the naming-of-recognition (2:23), is the *making of union* — the remedy of the "not good".
4. **desirability (chamad, 2:9) and knowledge (daat, 2:9,17) laid down as *good/permitted*, to be twisted next:** the trees' God-given desirability and the knowledge-tree's clear prohibition are set here *rightly ordered*; GEN-04 (3:1–13) shows the same chamad and the same daat *disordered* — so this passage plants exactly what the fall will pervert.
5. **rest-with-work-and-guard (2:15) and no-shame (2:25) frame the unfallen interior:** it is *settled* (nuach), *purposed* (avad/shamar), and *unashamed* (2:25) — the whole state the sentence of GEN-05 will unsettle, curse, and expose.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** avad as *absent* worker (2:5) then *given* vocation (2:15); the compound making of man (naphach / neshamah / nephesh chay) with the *same-soul-different-process* gem; chamad as *design-desirability* (thread to 3:6); tov in three senses (food-fitness 2:9 / material gold 2:12 / the **"not good" of solitude** 2:18); daat introduced (2:9) then interdicted-with-death (2:17); nuach/avad/shamar as rest-containing-labour; man's naming as authority-and-self-discovery (2:19–20) vs naming-as-recognition (2:23); basar in three senses (material / shared-substance / union); bosh as the *unashamed baseline* (2:25).
- **Whose interior:** the **human** interior is here *constituted* (2:7) and *situated* — given vocation, a prohibition, the felt lack of solitude, the union that remedies it, and the unfallen ease of no-shame.
- **Threads opened:** nephesh/chay (← animals GEN-01; → the human soul carried forward); chamad + daat (→ disordered in GEN-04 3:6, 3:5–7); the food-gift and the trees (← natan GEN-02); no-shame (→ shame and hiding, GEN-04 3:7–10); one-flesh (→ the union strained by the fall's blame, 3:12,16).
- **Guarded against:** a "higher soul" reading of nephesh (the dignity is the *manner of impartation*, not a different substance); lumping the three tov senses; lumping the three basar senses; treating man's naming as identical to God's naming (GEN-01); reading the fall's disordered desire back into 2:9's good desirability.

## JAC-07-sent-blessed · Genesis 28:1–9 · [the blessing now given openly, and Esau's counter-move] — Isaac deliberately blesses and commissions Jacob, who obeys, while Esau marries into Ishmael's line

**The passage.** "Isaac **called** (qara) Jacob and **blessed** (barakh) him… 'do not take a Canaanite wife'" (28:1); "God Almighty **bless** (barakh) you, make you fruitful and **multiply** (ravah) you… a company of peoples" (28:3); "may he **give** (natan) the **blessing** (berakhah) of Abraham to you… to possess the land God gave Abraham" (28:4); "Esau saw that Isaac had **blessed** (barakh) Jacob and sent him away" (28:6); "Jacob had **obeyed** (shama) his father and mother" (28:7); Esau, seeing the Canaanite women displeased Isaac, "took Mahalath the daughter of Ishmael" (28:8–9).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **qara (called) + barakh (blessed) — the blessing *now given openly, deliberately* (28:1).** "Isaac **called** (qara) Jacob and **blessed** (barakh) him and directed him" *(stated)*. **qara** — *call-to*: Isaac *deliberately summons* Jacob (no disguise, full knowledge). **barakh (1st)** — the gem: Isaac *knowingly blesses Jacob again* — the blessing that was *stolen* (JAC-05) is now *bestowed with open eyes*, joined to a *marriage-charge*. The interior *ratifies with full knowledge* what it gave under deceit — the irrevocable word (27:33) *now confirmed freely* *(inferred: qara/barakh = the open, deliberate re-blessing that legitimises the stolen one)*.
- **barakh (God Almighty bless you) + ravah (multiply) — the *El-Shaddai fruitfulness* invoked (28:3).** *Distinct from 28:1:* "**God Almighty** (El-Shaddai) **bless** (barakh) you, make you fruitful and **multiply** (ravah) you, that you become a company of peoples" *(stated)*. Here barakh is *invocatory* — Isaac *calls down God's own blessing* (El-Shaddai, the name of the covenant-increase, ← 17:1); **ravah** — *multiply* (the creation/covenant increase, ← 1:28; 26:24) — a *company of peoples* promised. The interior *entrusting the son to God's fruitfulness* *(inferred: barakh/ravah = the invoked El-Shaddai increase)*.
- **natan (give) + berakhah (blessing of Abraham) — the *covenant blessing deliberately transferred* (28:4).** "may he **give** (natan) the **blessing** (berakhah) of Abraham to you and your offspring… possess the land" *(stated)*. **natan** — *give*: the blessing invoked *as God's gift*; **berakhah** — named specifically as **the blessing of Abraham** (the land, the seed, the covenant). The gem: what was *seized* (JAC-05) is here *named and consciously conferred as the Abrahamic covenant* — the *given* register restored over the *grasped* one; the theft *absorbed into a legitimate transmission* *(inferred: natan/berakhah = the Abrahamic covenant deliberately given on)*.
- **barakh (had blessed Jacob) — Esau's *recognition* that drives his counter-move (28:6).** *Distinct from 28:1,3:* "Esau saw that Isaac had **blessed** (barakh) Jacob and sent him away… charged him not to take a Canaanite wife" *(stated)* — here barakh is *the object of Esau's observation*: Esau *watches* the blessing-and-commission and *reads the family's disapproval of Canaanite wives*. The interior *observing and calculating a response* *(inferred: barakh = the blessing Esau observes, prompting his counter-move)*.
- **shama (obeyed) — Jacob's *legitimate obedience* (28:7).** "Jacob had **obeyed** (shama) his father and his mother and gone to Paddan-aram" *(stated)* — shama, *obey*: the gem is the *contrast* — the *same obedience-word* (27:8,13) that drove the *deception* now drives a *right and open compliance*; the interior *obeys a legitimate charge* (marry within the line, go). Obedience *rehabilitated* *(inferred: shama = obedience now rightly directed)*.
- **(28:8–9 — Esau's counter-marriage, no non-T2 span.)** Flagged: "when Esau **saw** that the Canaanite women displeased Isaac… he took Mahalath the daughter of **Ishmael**" carries no flagged non-T2 span, but it is the scene's *closing interior move*: Esau, *reading* the family's displeasure, *tries to repair his standing* — but *by his own devising* and by marrying into the *other rejected line* (Ishmael). The interior *attempts self-correction on its own terms*, aligning outcast with outcast — a *maneuver*, not the obedience Jacob renders. Read as context, anchored by barakh (28:6) and shama (28:7) *(no-span context; Esau's self-devised counter-move)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **the blessing given openly and entrusted to God (qara/barakh 28:1 → barakh/ravah 28:3 → natan/berakhah 28:4):** Isaac *deliberately, knowingly* re-blesses Jacob, *invokes El-Shaddai's fruitfulness*, and *consciously gives on the blessing of Abraham* — the *stolen* blessing (JAC-05) now *legitimately transmitted*; grasped-turned-given.
2. **obedience rehabilitated (barakh 28:6 → shama 28:7):** Esau *observes* the blessing-and-sending; Jacob *obeys* — the deception's obedience-word now naming a *right compliance*.
3. **the self-devised counter-move (28:8–9):** Esau, *seeing* the displeasure, *marries into Ishmael's line* — repairing his standing *by his own calculation*, outcast joining outcast — the interior *maneuvering* where Jacob *obeys*.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** qara (the *deliberate summons* to bless); barakh ×3 *distinguished* (28:1 the *open, knowing re-blessing* that legitimises the stolen one / 28:3 the *invoked El-Shaddai* fruitfulness / 28:6 the blessing *Esau observes*, prompting his move); ravah (the *company-of-peoples* increase); natan/berakhah (the *blessing of Abraham deliberately given on* — grasped-turned-given); **shama (obedience *rehabilitated* — the deception's word now rightly directed)**; Esau's *self-devised counter-marriage* (no-span context).
- **Whose interior:** the **human** interior *deliberately transmitting the covenant* (Isaac, now with open eyes), *rightly obeying* (Jacob), and *maneuvering to repair standing* (Esau — self-devised, not obedient); God *invoked* (El-Shaddai) as the blessing's source.
- **Threads:** grasped-turned-given (the *stolen* blessing JAC-05 now *openly conferred* here; → the *given-through-brokenness* blessing at Peniel JAC-15); the blessing of Abraham (berakhah 28:4 ← 12:2–3, 26:3–4; the covenant line continued in Jacob); obedience (shama 28:7 vs 27:8,13 — the same faculty, now legitimate); Ishmael's line (28:9 ← ABR-18; the two outcast brothers, Esau & Ishmael, joined); the sending to Laban (→ Bethel JAC-08, the deceiver deceived JAC-09).
- **Guarded against:** treating the three barakh as one blessing (the *open re-blessing* / the *El-Shaddai invocation* / the blessing *Esau observes* — distinct); reading 28:9 as Esau's *repentance* when the text shows a *calculated* counter-marriage into the rejected line (a maneuver, not a turning); over-claiming a "legitimation" theology the verse doesn't state — I read only that Isaac *knowingly blesses again* and Jacob *obeys*; importing later covenant-theology over the plain *deliberate transmission* the passage narrates.

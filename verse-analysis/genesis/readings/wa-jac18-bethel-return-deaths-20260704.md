## JAC-18-bethel-return-deaths · Genesis 35:1–29 · [return, consecration, and the register of death] — the idols purged, the new name confirmed, and Rachel's and Isaac's deaths

**The passage.** God says go to Bethel; Jacob — "**put away** (sur) the foreign gods, **purify** (taher) yourselves" (35:2); "the God who answers in my **distress** (tsarah)" (35:3); "they **gave** (natan) all the foreign gods" (35:4); "a **terror** (chittah) from God fell on the cities" (35:5); he **called** (qara) it El-bethel (35:7); Deborah died — **called** (qara) Allon-bacuth (35:8); "God appeared and **blessed** (barakh) him" (35:9); "no longer Jacob but Israel" — he **called** (qara) his name Israel (35:10); "be fruitful and **multiply** (ravah)" (35:11); "the land I **gave** (natan)… I will give to you" (35:12); he **called** (qara) it Bethel (35:15); "**do not fear** (yare)" (35:17); "as her **soul** (nephesh) was departing… she **called** (qara) him Ben-oni; his father, Benjamin" (35:18); "Israel **heard** (shama)" of Reuben (35:22); Isaac died, gathered, old and full (35:29).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **sur (put away) + taher (purify) — the *consecration* for the return (35:2).** "**put away** (sur) the foreign gods… and **purify** (taher) yourselves, change your garments" *(stated)*. **sur** — *turn aside/remove*; **taher** — *be clean/purify*: the gem is the *interior turning-from idolatry* — the household *purged of foreign gods* and *cleansed* for the return to the vow-place; the interior *consecrating itself* (the Bethel vow, 28:20–22, now *acted on*) *(inferred: sur/taher = the purging of idols and self-purification for the return)*.
- **tsarah (distress) — the *testimony of God's faithfulness* (35:3).** "an altar to the God who **answers me** in the day of my **distress** (tsarah) and has been with me wherever I went" *(stated)* — tsarah, *distress/trouble*: the gem is the *retrospective confession* — Jacob names God as *the one who answered in every trouble* (the exile, Laban, Esau); the interior *reading its whole hard history as accompanied* — the Bethel-keeper (28:15) *proven* *(inferred: tsarah = the distress in which God answered, now testified)*.
- **natan (gave the gods) — the *idols surrendered* (35:4).** "they **gave** (natan) Jacob all the foreign gods and the ear-rings; he hid them" *(stated)* — natan, *give*: the household *hands over* its idols (buried under the terebinth) — the interior's *surrender of the foreign gods* (perhaps Rachel's stolen teraphim, 31:19, among them); the turning *enacted* *(inferred: natan = the idols given up and buried)*.
- **chittah (terror) — the *divine guard* over the guilty (35:5).** "a **terror** (chittah) from God fell on the cities, so they did not pursue" *(stated)* — chittah, *terror/dread*: the gem — *after the Shechem massacre* (JAC-17), when reprisal was feared (34:30), *God's terror* falls on the surrounding cities and *shields* Jacob's house; the interior *guarded by a dread God sends on its enemies* — grace *despite* the bloodguilt *(inferred: chittah = the God-sent terror that guards the vulnerable, guilty house)*.
- **qara (El-bethel) — the *altar of the vow* named (35:7).** "he built an altar and **called** (qara) it El-bethel, because there God revealed himself when he fled" *(stated)* — qara, *call-to/name*: the interior *marks the altar* — *"God of Bethel"* — completing the vow's altar; worship *at the place of the first encounter* *(inferred: qara = the altar named for the God who met him fleeing)*.
- **qara (Allon-bacuth) — the *grief* named at a death (35:8).** *qara distinct from 35:7:* "Deborah died… **called** (qara) its name Allon-bacuth ('oak of weeping')" *(stated)* — qara, *name*: the *first death* of the passage *named in grief* — the interior *marking loss with a weeping-name* (the register of death opening) *(inferred: qara = the naming of a place of weeping — grief at a death)*.
- **barakh (blessed) — the *Bethel blessing* renewed (35:9).** "God appeared again… and **blessed** (barakh) him" *(stated)* — barakh, *bless*: the gem — the *blessing* now *given directly by God at Bethel* (not stolen, not bargained) — the arc from *seized* (27) through *given-through-brokenness* (32:29) to *freely blessed at the vow-place*; the interior *blessed by God in worship* *(inferred: barakh = the Bethel blessing given directly by God)*.
- **qara (Israel confirmed) — the *new name ratified* (35:10).** *qara distinct from 35:7,8:* "'no longer Jacob but Israel'… he **called** (qara) his name Israel" *(stated)* — the gem: the *Peniel renaming* (32:28) is now ***confirmed by God*** at Bethel — the transformation *sealed at the vow-place*; the interior's *new identity ratified*, not merely won in a night-struggle but *established by God's word* *(inferred: qara = the God-ratified confirmation of the name Israel)*.
- **ravah (multiply) — the *El-Shaddai fruitfulness* (35:11).** "I am God Almighty: be fruitful and **multiply** (ravah)… a company of nations, and kings from your body" *(stated)* — ravah, *multiply*: the *covenant increase* (← 28:3; 1:28) renewed, now with *kings* promised; the interior *entrusted with a royal, national future* *(inferred: ravah = the promised increase, now royal)*.
- **natan (gave the land) — the *land-promise* renewed (35:12).** *natan distinct from 35:4:* "the land I **gave** (natan) to Abraham and Isaac I will give to you and your offspring" *(stated)* — natan, *give*: the *land bestowed* across the three generations — the interior *heir to the given land* (the threefold give, Abraham→Isaac→Jacob→seed); *received*, not seized *(inferred: natan = the land given down the generations to Jacob)*.
- **qara (Bethel) — the *place* named (35:15).** *qara distinct — the fourth:* "Jacob **called** (qara) the place where God spoke with him Bethel" *(stated)* — qara, *name*: the interior *fixes the place-name* again (the pillar, the drink-offering) — worship *localised and repeated* (← 28:19) *(inferred: qara = the place named Bethel, worship marked)*.
- **yare (do not fear) — *fear dispelled in death* (35:17).** "when her labour was hardest, the midwife: '**do not fear** (yare), you have another son'" *(stated)* — yare, *fear*: the gem — *"fear not"* spoken to the *dying* Rachel (← the "fear not" to Abraham 15:1, to Isaac 26:24) — comfort *even at death*; the interior *met with "fear not" at the extremity of loss* *(inferred: yare = the fear dispelled even in the hour of death)*.
- **nephesh (soul departing) + qara (Ben-oni/Benjamin) — *grief and hope in the naming* (35:18).** "as her **soul** (nephesh) was departing (for she was dying), she **called** (qara) him Ben-oni; his father called him Benjamin" *(stated)*. **nephesh** — *soul/life*: the gem — the *life departing* (the clearest death-as-soul-leaving in Genesis); **qara** *distinct — the fifth* — the *double naming*: the mother, dying, names him **Ben-oni ("son of my sorrow"** — the affliction-word *oni*, ← 29:32; 31:42); the father *overrides* with **Benjamin ("son of the right hand")**. The gem is the *two interiors in one naming*: the mother's *sorrow* and the father's *hope* — grief *renamed* toward blessing *(inferred: nephesh/qara = the departing life, and the naming that turns a mother's sorrow into a father's hope)*.
- **shama (heard) — the *father's silence* again (35:22).** "Reuben lay with Bilhah… and Israel **heard** (shama) of it. Now the sons were twelve" *(stated)* — shama, *hear*: the gem — *Israel hears* of Reuben's violation *and (the text records only that he heard)* — the *same passivity* as at Dinah (34:5, "Jacob held his peace"); the interior *hears the wrong and (here) says nothing* — the reckoning *deferred* (to the deathbed, 49:3–4) *(inferred: shama = the wrong heard and left unanswered — the father's recurring silence)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **consecration for the return (sur/taher 35:2 → tsarah 35:3 → natan 35:4 → chittah 35:5):** the household *purges its idols and purifies itself*, Jacob *testifies to the God who answered his every distress*, the gods are *surrendered*, and *God's terror guards* the guilty house from reprisal — the vow's return *made holy*.
2. **the blessing and the name sealed at Bethel (qara-El-bethel 35:7 → barakh 35:9 → qara-Israel 35:10 → ravah 35:11 → natan 35:12 → qara-Bethel 35:15):** at the vow-place God *blesses directly*, *confirms the name Israel*, renews the *royal increase* and the *land*, and the place is *named* — the transformation of Peniel *ratified*, the blessing now *freely given*, the vow *fulfilled*.
3. **the register of death, grief turned toward hope (qara-Allon-bacuth 35:8 → yare 35:17 → nephesh/qara-Benjamin 35:18 → shama 35:22 → Isaac's death 35:29):** Deborah's *weeping-oak*, then Rachel's death — *"fear not"* even there, her *soul departing*, her *sorrow-name* overridden by the father's *hope-name* — and Reuben's sin *heard in silence*; the passage closes with Isaac *gathered, old and full* (the Abraham-death formula, ← 25:8), *buried by the reconciled Esau and Jacob together*.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** sur/taher (the *idols purged, self purified* — the vow's consecration); tsarah (the *distress in which God answered*, testified); natan ×2 (35:4 the *idols surrendered* / 35:12 the *land given* down the generations); **chittah (the God-sent *terror* guarding the guilty house)**; qara *5 namings distinguished* (35:7 El-bethel *altar* / 35:8 Allon-bacuth *grief* / 35:10 Israel *confirmed by God* / 35:15 Bethel *place* / 35:18 Ben-oni→Benjamin *sorrow-into-hope*); **barakh (the Bethel blessing *given directly by God* — the arc's landing)**; ravah (the *royal* increase); yare (*"fear not"* even in death); **nephesh (the *soul departing* — Rachel's death)**; **shama (the *wrong heard in silence* — the father's recurring passivity)**.
- **Whose interior:** the **human** interior *consecrating, testifying, worshipping* (Jacob purging idols, building altars) and *grieving* (the deaths) yet *passive before Reuben's sin*; the *dying mother's sorrow* and the *father's hope* (the Benjamin naming); the **divine** interior *guarding (terror), blessing, confirming the name, and renewing the promises* — grace sealing the transformation at the vow-place.
- **Threads:** the Bethel vow *fulfilled* (35:2–7 ← 28:20–22 "if I return… the LORD shall be my God"; the God "who answers in distress" ← the keeper-promise 28:15); the name Israel *confirmed* (qara 35:10 ← Peniel 32:28 — night-struggle *ratified* by God's word); the blessing *given* (barakh 35:9 ← *stolen* 27, *bargained* 28:20, *seized-through-brokenness* 32:29 — now *freely given*); "fear not" (yare 35:17 ← 15:1; 26:24); the affliction-word in a name (oni: Ben-**oni** 35:18 ← Leah 29:32; Jacob 31:42); the father's silence (shama 35:22 ← 34:5 — deferred to the deathbed curse, 49:3–4); Isaac's death *gathered and full* (35:29 ← Abraham's, 25:8; the reconciled brothers bury him ← JAC-16).
- **Guarded against:** lumping the five qara-namings or two natan (altar / grief / *God-confirmed name* / place / *sorrow-into-hope*; idols-surrendered / land-given); flattening the Ben-oni/Benjamin double naming into one (the *mother's sorrow* and *father's hope* are two interiors — the finding is the *override toward hope*); reading the chittah-terror as Jacob's merit when the text makes it *God's guard on a guilty house*; importing the Christological "Benjamin/right hand" typology over the plain *father's hope-naming*; missing that Israel's *hearing in silence* (35:22) *repeats* the Dinah passivity (34:5) — the recurring paternal note, its reckoning *deferred*; over-reading the idol-purge as complete conversion when it is the *household's consecration for one return* (real, but the man still goes silent at his son's sin).

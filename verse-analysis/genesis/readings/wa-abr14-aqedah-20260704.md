## ABR-14-aqedah · Genesis 22:1–19 · [the fear of God that withholds nothing] — the surrendered love, the tested obedience, and "now I know that you fear God"

**The passage.** "God **tested** (nasah) Abraham… Here I am" (22:1); "take your son, your only son… whom you **love** (aheb)… offer him" (22:2); "I and the boy will go… and worship… and come again" (22:5); "God will **provide** the lamb" (22:8); "Abraham… **laid** (arakh) the wood… bound Isaac… laid him on the altar" (22:9); "he reached out… the knife" (22:10); "the angel **called** (qara): Abraham, Abraham!" (22:11); "now I **know** (yada) that you **fear** (yare) God, seeing you have not withheld your son" (22:12); "Abraham **called** (qara) the name of that place, The LORD will **provide**" (22:14); "the angel **called** (qara) a second time" (22:15); "by myself I have sworn… I will surely **bless** (barak) you and **multiply** (ravah)… possess the gate of their **enemies** (oyev)… all nations **blessed** (barak)… because you have **obeyed** (shama) my voice" (22:16–18).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **(22:1 — God *tests*; "Here I am": no span.)** Narratively "God **tested** (nasah) Abraham" and Abraham answers "**Here I am**" (hinneni). Flagged: nasah carries no non-T2 span, but it is the frame — the interior *summoned to a test* and answering with *ready availability* ("Here I am", repeated to God 22:1, to Isaac 22:7, to the angel 22:11) — the posture of the whole passage *(no-span context: the tested, available interior)*.
- **(22:2 — "your son, whom you *love*": the first love, surrendered; no span.)** Narratively "take your son, your only son… whom you **love** (aheb)". Flagged: aheb — the **first "love" in Scripture** — carries no non-T2 span, but it is the passage's *heart*: love appears *at the moment of its demanded surrender* — the father's *deepest attachment* (the only son) commanded to be *given up*. The interior's *dearest* is exactly what the test asks *(no-span context: the first love = the surrendered love)*.
- **(22:5, 22:8 — resurrection-faith and provision-trust: no span.)** Narratively "we will **come again** to you" (22:5) and "God will **provide** the lamb" (22:8, ra'ah = T2). Flagged: no non-T2 span, but the anchored *faith*: Abraham obeys *believing God will keep the promise through Isaac* ("come again") and *provide* — the tested faith (aman, ABR-05) at its *extremity* *(no-span context: obedience that still trusts the promise)*.
- **arakh (laid) — the *deliberate* ordering of the sacrifice (22:9).** The finding is "Abraham built the altar and **laid** (arakh) the wood in order, and bound Isaac his son and laid him on the altar" *(stated)*. The gem is *arakh* (arrange/set in order, ← 14:8 the battle-array) — the **methodical, deliberate obedience**: Abraham *arranges* the wood, *binds* the boy, *lays* him — every terrible step *ordered*, obedience carried through *action*, not just assent *(inferred: arakh = the ordered, enacted obedience)*.
- **qara (called) #1 — the angel *stops the knife* (22:11).** The finding is "the angel of the LORD **called** (qara) to him from heaven, 'Abraham, Abraham!'" *(stated)*. The operation is the *urgent, arresting call* (qara — the pursuing/summoning call) — heaven *intervening* at the last instant *(inferred)*.
- **yada (know) + yare (fear) — the fear-of-God *demonstrated*, God's knowing *confirmed* (22:12).** The finding is "now I **know** (yada) that you **fear** (yare) God, seeing you have not withheld your son" *(stated)*. Two gems: **yada** — "now I *know*" — God's knowing **confirmed by the act**; the test *makes manifest* what was in the interior (God's demonstrated/experiential knowing, ← 18:19,21; 20:6); and **yare** — "you *fear* God" — the **fear-of-God at its summit**: the reverent fear that *withholds nothing*, that surrenders the dearest. This is the very *yirah* Abraham *wrongly assumed absent* in others (20:11, ABR-11) — now *proven in himself*; and the *"fear not"* (yare, 15:1, ABR-05) *matured* into the fear that *trusts God with everything*. The operation is **the fear-of-God = holding nothing back from God** *(inferred: yare — the withholding-nothing fear; yada — the test that reveals it)*.
- **qara (called) #2 — "The LORD will provide" (22:14).** The finding is "Abraham **called** (qara) the name of that place, **The LORD will provide** (Yahweh-Yireh)" *(stated)*. The gem is *qara-as-naming-God's-provision*: the God who *sees* (El-Roi, 16:13) **provides** (Yireh, "sees/provides") — the ram given, the provision-faith (22:8) *vindicated* and *memorialised* in the place-name; the interior *names the mount for the providing God* *(inferred: qara — naming the place for God's provision)*.
- **qara (called) #3 — the angel a *second time* (22:15).** The finding is "the angel… **called** (qara) to Abraham a second time" *(stated)*. The operation is the *renewed* heavenly address, introducing the oath-blessing *(inferred: distinct qara — the second call, to bless)*.
- **barak (bless) ×2 + ravah (multiply) + oyev (enemies) + shama (obeyed) — the oath-blessing *grounded in obedience* (22:16, 22:17, 22:18).** The finding is "by myself I have **sworn** (22:16)… I will surely **bless** (barak) you and **multiply** (ravah) your offspring as the stars… possess the gate of their **enemies** (oyev, 22:17)… in your offspring all nations **blessed** (barak)… **because you have obeyed** (shama) my voice (22:18)" *(stated)*. The gems: **barak/ravah** — the blessing and multiplying (← 12:2; 15:5) now **sealed by God's *self-oath*** ("by myself I have sworn") — the covenant confirmed at its highest, *grounded in the tested obedience* ("because you have done this"); **oyev** — the *first "enemy"*, the offspring's victory; and **shama** — "because you have **obeyed** (shama) my voice" — the **obey-sense** at its *summit*: the *heeding-a-voice* that was the fall's fault (3:17), the redeemed heeding of Sarah (21:12), is here Abraham's **supreme obedience to *God's* voice** (offering the son) — and the blessing is *grounded* in it *(inferred: barak/ravah oath-sealed; shama = the obedience that grounds the blessing)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **the test of the surrendered love (nasah/aheb 22:1–2):** God *tests* by demanding the son "whom you *love*" — the *first love* appearing as the *love to be given up*; the interior's *dearest* is exactly the test's object, and Abraham answers "*Here I am*".
2. **obedience that still trusts (22:5,8 → arakh 22:9):** Abraham obeys *believing* God will "provide" and that they will "come again" — faith (aman, ABR-05) at its *extremity* — and *enacts* it, *arranging* the wood and *binding* the son (arakh): the tested faith *carried through in deliberate action*.
3. **the fear-of-God revealed (yada/yare 22:12):** the knife raised, heaven *calls* (qara), and the verdict: *"now I know you fear God, for you have not withheld your son"* — the **fear-of-God = withholding nothing**; the fear wrongly assumed absent in others (20:11) *proven* here, the "fear not" (15:1) *matured* into total trust.
4. **provision and oath-blessing (qara/barak/ravah/shama 22:14–18):** God *provides* the ram and Abraham *names* the mount for it (Yahweh-Yireh); then God *swears by himself* to *bless* and *multiply* — **because Abraham *obeyed*** (shama): the blessing (12:2), the pivot of the whole cycle, is *oath-sealed* and *grounded in the tested, enacted fear-and-obedience* of the interior.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** the *test* (nasah) and "Here I am" (no-span, the available interior); the **first *love* (aheb), appearing as the *surrendered* love** (no-span); the *resurrection/provision*-faith (no-span, 22:5,8); **arakh — the *deliberate, enacted* obedience** (ordering the sacrifice); qara — the angel's *arresting* call (22:11), the *naming* of Yahweh-Yireh (22:14), the *second* call (22:15); **yada — God's knowing *confirmed by the act*** + **yare — the fear-of-God that *withholds nothing*** (the summit; ← 20:11 assumed-absent, 15:1 "fear not"); **barak/ravah *oath-sealed*** by God's self-oath; oyev (first "enemy"); **shama — the *obedience* that grounds the blessing** (the heeding-word at its summit).
- **Whose interior:** the **human** interior at its *peak of faith* — surrendering its *dearest love*, *fearing God* by withholding nothing, *obeying* God's voice, *trusting* provision and promise; and the **divine** interior *testing*, *knowing by the test*, *providing*, and *swearing the blessing by himself*.
- **Threads:** yare/fear-of-God (← 15:1 "fear not"; 20:11 assumed-absent → here *proven*); yada (God's knowing, ← 18:19/20:6, now demonstrated); shama-obey (← 3:17 fault; 21:12 God's command → here obedience to *God's own* voice); barak/ravah (← 12:2; 15:5 → oath-sealed); the naming of God (El-Roi 16:13 → Yahweh-Yireh 22:14); aman/faith (← 15:6, tested to the extremity); the surrendered love (first aheb → the love-motif).
- **Guarded against:** claiming operations off the *no-span* love/test/provide (read as central context — honestly flagged, not smuggled in as spans); flattening yare into generic "fear" when it is *the fear that withholds nothing*; missing that yada here is *demonstrated* knowing; merging the three qara (arrest / naming / second-call) or the two barak; reading shama-obey as identical to its fault-uses (here it is *obedience to God*, the redeemed summit); importing NT Aqedah typology (Heb 11; the offering of the Son) — the operations the chapter states, not the fulfilment.

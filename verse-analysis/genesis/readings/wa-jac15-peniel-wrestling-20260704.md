## JAC-15-peniel-wrestling · Genesis 32:22–32 · [the blessing seized through brokenness; the supplanter renamed] — the night wrestling, "I will not let you go unless you bless me", Jacob→Israel, and the face of God

**The passage.** Jacob crosses the Jabbok, sends everyone over, and "was left **alone**"; a man wrestles him till daybreak; "when the man saw he did not **prevail** (yakhol) against Jacob, he touched his hip" and it was put out of joint (32:25); "'let me go, for the day has broken.' But Jacob said, 'I will not let you go unless you **bless** (barakh) me'" (32:26); "'what is your name?' 'Jacob'" (32:27); "no longer Jacob but **Israel**, for you have **striven** (sarah) with God and men, and have **prevailed** (yakhol)" (32:28); Jacob **asked** (shaal) his name — not told — "and there he **blessed** (barakh) him" (32:29); "he **called** (qara) it Peniel: 'I have seen God face to face, and my **life** (nephesh) is delivered'" (32:30); he passed Penuel *limping* (32:31).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **★ avaq (wrestled) — the *grasping self grappled to a standstill in the dark* (32:24,25) [Gate-1 recovery].** "and a man **wrestled** (avaq) with him until the breaking of the day… when the man saw that he did not prevail against him" *(stated)* — **avaq**, *wrestle / grapple* (a hapax-root, punning on Jabbok/*Yabboq* and Jacob/*Ya'aqov*): the passage's *defining act*, and its gem is that the transformation the whole cycle drives toward is *physical, whole-body contention* — the interior that has *grasped* heel, birthright, and blessing now finds its grip *met and matched* by God, held all night in a struggle neither *deceives* nor *out-schemes* its way out of. The wrestle is the grasp's *last form and its turning-point*: here the seizing hand can neither win nor flee, and is driven — through the very grappling — to *cling and plead* (barakh, 32:26). The word *is* the transfiguration; the wound (the touched hip) is `avaq`'s own cost *(inferred: avaq = the grasping self grappled by God to the point where seizing becomes clinging)*.
- **yakhol (did not prevail) — the *struggle undecided by force* (32:25).** "when the man saw he did not **prevail** (yakhol) against Jacob, he touched his hip" *(stated)* — yakhol, *be able / prevail*: the gem is *who cannot prevail* — the *divine adversary does not overpower Jacob by strength*, and so *wounds* him instead (touches the hip out of joint). The interior *held in a struggle no force decides* — the contest turns *not on strength but on clinging*; and the *wound* is given precisely where Jacob is *strongest* (the grasping self must be *lamed*) *(inferred: yakhol = the force-struggle that no strength wins, resolved by a wound)*.
- **barakh (bless me) — the *grasping hand now clinging to God for blessing* (32:26).** "'let me go'… 'I will not **let you go** unless you **bless** (barakh) me'" *(stated)* — the pivotal gem: **barakh** — the *same seizing interior* that grabbed the *heel* (25:26), *bought* the birthright (25:34), and *stole* the blessing (27) now ***grips God and will not release him without a blessing*** — *wounded, at daybreak, alone*. The grasp is *unchanged in intensity but wholly changed in object and manner*: no longer *deceiving a blind father* for a blessing, but *clinging to God in desperation*, refusing to let go. **Grasping transfigured into faith** — the blessing *demanded*, but now *from God, through brokenness* *(inferred: barakh = the grasping clung to God — the seizing hand turned to desperate faith for blessing)*.
- **(32:27 — "what is your name?" "Jacob" — the *confession*, no non-T2 span.)** Flagged: the name-question and answer carry no flagged span, but they are the *hinge*: asked *"what is your name?"* Jacob answers **"Jacob"** — *he confesses he is the supplanter*. The gem is the *reversal of JAC-05*: there, to *steal* a blessing, he lied *"I am Esau"*; here, to *receive* one, he tells the *truth* — *"I am Jacob, the heel-grabber."* The interior's *honest naming of its own grasping self* is the threshold of the blessing. Read as context, anchored by barakh (32:26) and the renaming (32:28) *(no-span context; the truthful self-confession)*.
- **sarah (striven) + yakhol (prevailed) — the *new name that owns the struggle* (32:28).** "no longer Jacob but **Israel**, for you have **striven** (sarah) with God and with men, and have **prevailed** (yakhol)" *(stated)*. **sarah** — *strive/struggle/persist*: the gem — the new name *Israel* ("God strives" / "he strives with God") *encodes the man's whole striving life* (with Esau, with Laban, and now *with God*) and *transforms* it; **yakhol** *distinct from 32:25* — where the man "did not prevail" *by force*, here Jacob "has **prevailed**" — not by *overpowering* God but by *enduring and clinging*; *prevailing = not-letting-go*. The interior's *lifelong struggle named, owned, and re-valued* — the supplanter becomes *the God-wrestler* *(inferred: sarah/yakhol = the struggling life owned in the new name; prevailing-by-clinging)*.
- **shaal (asked) — the *reach to know* the adversary, refused (32:29a).** "Jacob **asked** (shaal), 'tell me your name'… 'why do you ask my name?'" *(stated)* — shaal, *ask*: the interior *reaches to name the One it wrestled* — but the name is *withheld*; the encounter *keeps its mystery*; the interior *knows God's face but not God's name* — grace given, but God *un-mastered* (contrast Jacob's own name *given up*) *(inferred: shaal = the reach to name God, left unanswered)*.
- **barakh (blessed him) — the blessing *finally given, not seized* (32:29b).** *barakh distinct from 32:26:* "and there he **blessed** (barakh) him" *(stated)* — the gem: where 32:26 was the blessing *demanded/clung-for*, here it is the blessing ***granted*** — *"there he blessed him."* The whole cycle's arc *lands*: the blessing *grasped* (25:34), *stolen* (27), *bargained* (28:20) — now **given by God at the place of wrestling**. The seizing interior *receives the blessing it could never truly steal* — *given*, at last, *through brokenness* *(inferred: barakh = the blessing granted by God — grasped-turned-received)*.
- **nephesh (life delivered) + qara (Peniel) — *seeing God and living* (32:30).** "he **called** (qara) it Peniel: 'I have seen God face to face, and my **life** (nephesh) is delivered'" *(stated)*. **nephesh** — *life/soul*: the gem — Jacob *expected to die* (the ancient dread of seeing God) yet his *life is preserved*; **qara** — he *names the place "face of God"* (Peniel). The interior *meets God face to face and lives* — the encounter that *should* destroy *delivers*; awe *and* survival, the face of God *seen and survived* *(inferred: nephesh/qara = the life delivered in seeing God's face, named at Peniel)*.
- **(32:31 — he passed Penuel *limping*, no non-T2 span.)** Flagged: "the sun rose… **limping** because of his hip" carries no flagged span, but it is the *scene's closing image*: the transformed man walks *into the sunrise wounded* — the blessing *cost him his hip*; the interior *made new but marked* — *Israel limps*. Read as context, anchored by yakhol (32:25) and nephesh (32:30) *(no-span context; the permanent wound of the blessing)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **alone, wrestled, and wounded (avaq 32:24 → yakhol 32:25):** stripped of everyone at the Jabbok, the interior *meets God in the dark* and is *grappled* (**avaq**) all night — a struggle *no strength decides* (yakhol), resolved by a *wound to the strong place* (the grasping self *lamed*). The grasp finds its match and is held until it must cling.
2. **the grasp transfigured (barakh 32:26 → [confession "Jacob" 32:27]):** the *same relentless grip* that seized heel, birthright, and blessing now *clings to God* — *"I will not let you go unless you bless me"* — and, asked his name, *confesses "Jacob"* (the supplanter, told *truly* now, reversing the "I am Esau" lie).
3. **the struggling life renamed and re-valued (sarah/yakhol 32:28):** *Israel* — the lifelong *striving* (with Esau, Laban, God) *owned and transformed*; *prevailing* re-defined as *clinging-and-enduring*, not overpowering.
4. **God un-mastered, the blessing given, the life delivered (shaal 32:29a → barakh 32:29b → nephesh/qara 32:30 → [limp 32:31]):** Jacob's *reach to name God* is *refused* (God un-mastered), yet the *blessing is granted* — the cycle's arc landing on a blessing *given, not stolen* — and he *sees God's face and lives*, naming Peniel, walking into the sunrise *limping*: **made new, but marked**.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** **avaq (the *wrestle itself* — the grasping self grappled by God; Gate-1 recovery, 32:24,25)**; yakhol ×2 *distinguished* (32:25 the *force-struggle no strength wins* — the man "did not prevail" / 32:28 Jacob "has **prevailed**" — *by clinging, not overpowering*); **barakh ×2 *distinguished* (32:26 the blessing *demanded/clung-for* / 32:29 the blessing *granted by God* — grasped-turned-received)**; the *truthful self-confession* "Jacob" (no-span hinge, reversing the "I am Esau" lie of JAC-05); **sarah (the *striving life owned* in the name Israel)**; shaal (the *reach to name God, refused* — God un-mastered); **nephesh/qara (God *seen face to face and survived*, named Peniel)**; the *limp* (no-span — the permanent wound of the blessing).
- **Whose interior:** the **human** interior *transfigured* — the *grasping self clinging now to God, confessing its name, renamed, blessed, and wounded* (Jacob→Israel); the **divine** interior *the un-overpowered, un-named Adversary-who-blesses* — wounding to save, withholding his name, granting the blessing, delivering the life.
- **Threads:** the *grasping hand* transfigured (barakh 32:26 ← the heel 25:26, the birthright 25:34, the stolen blessing 27 — the *same grip*, now *on God*); the *truth-vs-lie of the name* (32:27 "Jacob" told *truly* ← 27:19 "I am Esau" the lie — honesty about the self as the threshold); *wrestle-and-prevail* (yakhol/sarah 32:28 ← Rachel "I have wrestled and prevailed" 30:8 — the family struggle-motif *culminating* in Jacob-with-God); the blessing *given not seized* (barakh 32:29 ← stolen JAC-05, bargained JAC-08 — the arc *lands here*); *seeing God and living* (nephesh 32:30 ← the ancient dread; → Hagar's El-Roi "have I seen him and lived" 16:13); Israel-the-name (→ 35:10 confirmed at Bethel; the nation's name); the wound/limp (→ the dietary memorial 32:32; the interior *marked* by grace).
- **Guarded against:** collapsing the two yakhol (force-that-fails vs prevail-by-clinging) or two barakh (demanded vs granted) — the *distinction is the whole meaning* (grasping *transfigured*, blessing *finally given*); reading the wrestling as mere myth/etiology and missing the *interior* (the grasping self *lamed and clinging*); importing later Christophany doctrine ("the man = pre-incarnate Christ") over what the text *says* — Jacob *saw God face to face*; over-triumphalising the transformation and dropping the *limp* (the blessing is *costly and permanent-marking* — made new *and* wounded); treating "prevailed" as *overpowering God* when the text's own logic (the wound, the clinging, the plea "bless me") makes prevailing = *not letting go*.

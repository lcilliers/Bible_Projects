## Structural observation (the verse's headline)
- **Lev 25:43 stages a contest between two inner operations:** fear-of-God (the restraint) vs ruthless-dominion (the impulse). **The inner being's treatment of the vulnerable is decided by which governs.** This is a focus-point-level insight — the same shape recurs wherever power meets the weak (Lev 25:17,43,46,53; Eze 34:4; Exo 1). *(researcher · candidate focus point)*

# Proverbs 27 — inner-being unit readings (Phase-2 meaning synthesis)

- **Source:** `lexical-model-2026` Phase-1 (v1.1 enriched) for Pro 27, per `segment_unit`. Grounded; *stated* | *inferred*. Lens: the inner-being **operation**.

---

**PRO-27-boast-tomorrow · 27:1,2 · [S, multi] — presumption and self-praise.** "**Do not boast about tomorrow, for you do not know what a day may bring**"; "**let another praise you, and not your own mouth**" (vv.1–2). Finding: the self is to refuse **presumption over the future** and **self-praise** — two forms of over-reaching *(stated; cross-book w/ Ps 30:6 "I said in my prosperity, I shall never be moved")*. *Surfaced:* presumption / self-praise refused (shared w/ Ps 30).

**PRO-27-passions · 27:4 · [S] — the ranking of the passions.** "**Wrath is cruel, anger is overwhelming, but who can stand before jealousy?**" (v.4). Finding: the passions are **weighed against each other** — jealousy the most irresistible *(stated)*. Operation: **a taxonomy of the overwhelming passions** (wrath < anger < jealousy) *(inferred; cross-book w/ Ps's fierce affections)*. *Surfaced:* the ranked passions / jealousy strongest (new).

**PRO-27-friend-wounds · 27:5,6,9 · [T, multi] — the honest friend; the gladdened heart.** "**Better is open rebuke than hidden love**. **Faithful are the wounds of a friend**; profuse the kisses of an enemy"; "**oil and perfume make the heart glad**, and the sweetness of a friend comes from his earnest counsel" (vv.5–6,9). Finding: love is **better shown in honest wounding** than in hidden fondness or flattering kisses; and a friend's *earnest counsel* **gladdens the heart** *(stated)*. Operation: **love as honest correction** (the friend's wounds are *faithful*) and **the heart gladdened by true counsel** *(inferred; teachability + the felt heart; cross-book w/ Ps 141:5 "let a righteous man strike me")*. *Hidden:* **love that *wounds faithfully* over love that *hides***. *Surfaced:* the honest-wounding friend (★ cross-book w/ Ps 141:5); the heart gladdened by counsel.

**PRO-27-iron-sharpens · 27:17 · [S] — formation by another.** "**Iron sharpens iron, and one man sharpens another**" (v.17). Finding — a cornerstone: the self is **honed by another self** *(stated)*. Operation: **mutual formation — the self shaped sharper by contact with another** *(inferred; the formation-by-company thread, cross-book w/ 13:20, and the trust-formation law of Ps 115:8)*. *Surfaced:* formation-by-another / iron-sharpens-iron (★ cornerstone — mutual formation).

**PRO-27-heart-mirror · 27:19 · [S] — the heart is the true self.** "**As in water face reflects face, so the heart of man reflects the man**" (v.19). Finding — a cornerstone: the **heart *is* the true self** — what a person really is shows in the heart as a face in water *(stated)*. Operation: **the heart as the mirror/identity of the person** — the deepest Proverbs statement that the self *is* its heart *(inferred; cross-book w/ Ps 7:9 "the minds and hearts", the heart as the self's core)*. *Surfaced:* the heart as the true self (★ cornerstone — the heart = the person).

**PRO-27-insatiable · 27:20 · [S] — the never-satisfied eye.** "**Sheol and Abaddon are never satisfied, and never satisfied are the eyes of man**" (v.20). Finding — a cornerstone: human desire (the *eyes*) is **structurally insatiable**, like the grave *(stated)*. Operation: **the unfillable appetite** — the self's wanting has no natural floor; it is *never satisfied* *(inferred; the shadow-side of the desire thread, cross-book w/ Ps 106:14–15, and the only-God-satisfies datum of Ps 107:9, 145:16)*. *Surfaced:* the insatiable eye/desire (★ cornerstone — the unfillable appetite).

**PRO-27-tested-praise · 27:21 · [S] — a man is tested by his praise.** "The crucible for silver… **and a man is tested by his praise**" (v.21). Finding: how one **handles praise** assays the self — praise is a furnace *(stated)*. *Surfaced:* praise as a test of the self (new).

**Chapter notes.** **Cornerstones:** iron-sharpens-iron / formation-by-another (★ 27:17); the heart as the true self (★ 27:19); the insatiable eye (★ 27:20). **New:** the ranked passions; praise-as-test. **Shared:** presumption/self-praise (w/ Ps 30); the honest-wounding friend (w/ Ps 141:5).

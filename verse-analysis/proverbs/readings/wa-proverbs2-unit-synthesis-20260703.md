## PRO-02-A · Prov 2:1–11 · [D, multi] — the seeking of wisdom; reception that reshapes the heart

**The reading.** One long conditional: *if* the self does something, *then* something happens *to* it. The condition is a fivefold **active seeking** — "**receive** my words… **making your ear attentive**… **inclining your heart**… **call out** for insight… **seek** it like **silver** and **search** for it as for hidden treasures" (vv.1–4). The finding: understanding is not passively received but **strenuously sought** — the self *inclines its own heart* (an act of the will) and *digs* for wisdom as for buried treasure *(stated)*. The consequence is the reception that **reshapes the interior**: "then you will understand the **fear of the LORD**… **wisdom will come *into* your heart**, and knowledge will be **pleasant to your soul**" (vv.5,10). The operation: wisdom, once sought, **enters and re-forms the inner being** — it *comes into* the heart and becomes *pleasant to the soul*, and thereafter its faculties (discretion, understanding) **watch over** the self from within (v.11) *(inferred: the movement is self-seeks → God-gives (v.6) → wisdom-indwells → wisdom-guards; the inner being is remade by what it went after)*.

**Lexical validation.** The v1.1 substrate now carries the reading well: *fear* (H3374) → **specifier = of Lord** (the object of the fear captured); *heart* → seat + **specifier = of understanding**; and the seeking-verbs carry their objects positionally — *understand* → target **righteousness**, *walk* → target **integrity**, *gives* → target **wisdom**. The "wisdom comes *into* the heart" weld appears as *heart* (v.10) `manner/coupling` to *come-into* — correctly reading the heart as the place wisdom enters. Sound; the enrichment materially sharpens the "reshapes the heart" finding.

**Hidden / less-obvious characteristics isolated.** (1) **Inclining the heart as an act of will** (v.2) — the self *directs* its own heart toward understanding; a volitional operation (with the "give me your heart" thread, 23:26). (2) **Wisdom as an *indwelling guardian*** (v.11) — "discretion will watch over you, understanding will guard you": the acquired faculties become *internal protectors*, a personified inner-being characteristic. (3) The **reciprocity** — the self seeks, but "the LORD *gives* wisdom" (v.6): the sought-for good is also a gift.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *the active seeking of wisdom* (new — Proverbs-distinctive, cf. the Psalter's seeking-God's-face); *inclining/directing the heart (volition)* (new); *wisdom indwelling and reshaping the heart* (new); *wisdom as internal guardian* (new); *the fear of the LORD* (shared, now object-tagged).

## PRO-02-B · Prov 2:12–22 · [D, multi] — discretion guarding the self; deliverance from evil

**The reading.** The indwelling faculties (from v.11) now *act*: they are "**delivering you** from the way of evil, from men of **perverted speech**… from the **forbidden woman** with her **smooth words**" (vv.12,16). The finding: the inner being's acquired discretion **functions as a deliverer** — wisdom internalised *extracts* the self from the paths of the crooked and the seductive *(stated)*. The unit's sharpest inner-being note is the anatomy of the seductress's *inner* failure: "who **forsakes** the companion of her youth and **forgets the covenant of her God**" (v.17) — her outward danger springs from an inward **forsaking and forgetting** *(inferred: the enticement is traced to a prior inner defection — she has *forgotten* a covenant; evil is diagnosed as a failure of inner fidelity/memory, cf. the Psalter's forgetting-as-seedbed-of-rebellion, Ps 106:13)*.

**Lexical validation.** The enrichment is telling here: *forsakes* → target **covenant**, *forgets* → target **covenant**, and *covenant* → **specifier = of God** — so the substrate now captures precisely the object of her defection ("the covenant *of God*"), which the reading rests on. The two "ways" close the unit as the moral-poles frame (paths *of uprightness* vs *of darkness* — both now specifier-tagged). Sound.

**Hidden / less-obvious characteristics isolated.** **Evil as inner infidelity** (v.17) — the seductress is defined not by her acts but by her *forgetting the covenant*; a hidden characteristic (the forgetting/forsaking heart) that the segmentation's "deliverance" framing didn't name, and which links to the Psalter's memory-and-fidelity thread.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *discretion as deliverer* (new); *evil as inner infidelity (forgetting the covenant)* (new — links to Ps memory thread); *the two ways* (shared frame).

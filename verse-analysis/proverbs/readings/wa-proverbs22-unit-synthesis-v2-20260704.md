# Proverbs 22 — inner-being unit readings (Phase-2 meaning synthesis)

- **Source:** `lexical-model-2026` Phase-1 (v1.1 enriched) for Pro 22, per `segment_unit`. Grounded; *stated* | *inferred*. Lens: the inner-being **operation**. (vv.17ff open the "Thirty Sayings.")

---

**PRO-22-humility-fear · 22:4 · [S, multi] — humility and fear rewarded.** "**The reward for humility and fear of the LORD is riches and honour and life**" (v.4). *Surfaced:* humility + fear-of-LORD (shared cornerstone thread).

**PRO-22-purity-heart · 22:11 · [S, multi] — loving purity of heart.** "**He who loves purity of heart, and whose speech is gracious, will have the king as his friend**" (v.11). Finding: the inner being can **love purity of heart** — desire *cleanness itself* — joined to gracious speech *(stated)*. Operation: **the loved clean heart** — purity is not only commanded but *desired*; the self can be in love with its own inward cleanness *(inferred; cross-book w/ Ps 24:4 "clean hands and a pure heart", 51:10)*. *Hidden:* **purity of heart as an *object of love*** (not just a duty). *Surfaced:* loving-purity-of-heart (new).

**PRO-22-prudent-sees · 22:3,5 · [S, multi] — foreseeing danger; guarding the soul.** "**The prudent sees danger and hides himself, but the simple go on and suffer**"; "**whoever guards his soul will keep far**" from thorns and snares (vv.3,5). Finding: prudence is **foresight that acts** — seeing danger *ahead* and protecting the *soul* *(stated)*. Operation: **the self-guarding foresight** (vs the naive who go on unheeding) *(inferred; discernment thread, cross-book w/ Ps 39:1)*. *Surfaced:* prudent foresight / guarding the soul (shared discernment).

**PRO-22-trust-appeal · 22:17–21 · [D, multi] — the aim of instruction is trust.** "**Incline your ear, and hear the words of the wise, and apply your heart** to my knowledge… keep them **within you**… **that your trust may be in the LORD**" (vv.17–19). Finding: the *purpose* of all the internalising (incline the ear, apply the heart, keep them within) is stated: **that your trust may be in the LORD** *(stated)*. Operation: **instruction internalised → trust** — the whole educational movement of Proverbs is *for* the sake of an inner posture of trust; wisdom is not an end but the road to trusting God *(inferred; cross-book w/ Ps 119's internalised word, and the trust cluster)*. *Hidden:* **the *telos* of wisdom is trust** — the reading of the whole book's aim. *Surfaced:* instruction-internalised-for-trust (★ new — names the book's inner-being telos).

**PRO-22-anger-company · 22:24,25 · [S, multi] — anger is caught from company.** "**Make no friendship with a man given to anger… lest you learn his ways and entangle yourself in a snare**" (vv.24–25). Finding: dispositions are **contagious** — befriend the wrathful and you *learn his ways* *(stated)*. Operation: **formation-by-company applied to *temper*** — anger is caught, so the self guards its associations *(inferred; the formation-by-company thread, 13:20)*. *Surfaced:* formation-by-company / anger-is-caught (shared w/ 13:20).

## Catch-up units (2026-07-04) — loose study-term verses bound to units

**PRO-22-good-name · Prov 22:1 · [S] — a good name prized above wealth.** "A **good name is to be chosen rather than great riches**, and **favour is better than silver or gold**" (v.1). Finding: the interior **values reputation and favour above wealth** — the intangible good of a name outranks the material *(stated)*. Operation: **the value-ranking that prizes the intangible** (w/ 16:16, wisdom over gold) *(inferred)*. *Surfaced:* a good name valued above riches (thread — the interior's value-ranking).

**PRO-22-shared-Maker · Prov 22:2, 12 · [S, multi] — rich and poor of one Maker, under God's watching eyes.** "The **rich and the poor meet together; the LORD is the Maker of them all**" (v.2); "the **eyes of the LORD keep watch over knowledge**, but he overthrows the words of the traitor" (v.12). Finding: rich and poor are **level before their common Maker**, and God's **watching eyes** guard truth and overthrow treachery *(stated)*. Operation: **the leveling of shared createdness, under a watching God** — status collapses before the one Maker; the interior lives under eyes that keep and overthrow *(inferred; shared-createdness ethic w/ Job 31:15, Pro 17:5; the all-seeing God w/ Pro 15:3)*. *Surfaced:* rich-and-poor of one Maker (w/ Job 31:15); God's watching eyes over truth (w/ 15:3).

**PRO-22-formation · Prov 22:6, 15 · [D, multi] — the shaping of the young interior.** "**Train up a child in the way he should go**; even when he is old he will not depart from it" (v.6); "**folly is bound up in the heart of a child**, but the rod of discipline drives it far from him" (v.15). Finding: early **formation sets the lifelong way**, and **folly, native to the child's heart**, is driven out by discipline *(stated)*. Operation: **the malleable young interior shaped by training** — folly is *bound up* in the heart from the start; discipline is the loosing of it, and the trained way holds for life *(inferred; formation as the deep work on the interior, w/ the receptivity thread)*. *Surfaced:* early formation setting the lifelong way (new); folly native to the child's heart, driven out by discipline.

**PRO-22-poor-advocate · Prov 22:7, 16, 22, 23 · [D, multi] — the poor, and the LORD who pleads their cause.** "The **rich rules over the poor**, and the borrower is the slave of the lender" (v.7); "whoever **oppresses the poor to increase his wealth… will only come to poverty**" (v.16); "**do not rob the poor** because he is poor, or crush the afflicted at the gate, for the **LORD will plead their cause** and rob of life those who rob them" (vv.22–23). Finding: exploiting the poor **recoils into poverty**, for the **LORD himself is their advocate**, pleading their cause against the oppressor *(stated)*. Operation: **the interior warned by a God who takes the poor's side** — oppression is not merely unjust but *self-defeating*, met by a divine advocate *(inferred; God as the poor's go'el, w/ 23:11, Job 31:13–15)*. *Surfaced:* the LORD as advocate of the poor (w/ 23:11); oppression's recoil into poverty.

**PRO-22-generous-eye · Prov 22:8, 9 · [S, multi] — the bountiful eye vs the sowing of injustice.** "Whoever **sows injustice will reap calamity**, and the rod of his fury will fail" (v.8); "whoever has a **bountiful eye will be blessed, for he shares his bread with the poor**" (v.9). Finding: **injustice sown reaps calamity**; the **generous ("bountiful") eye** that shares bread is blessed *(stated)*. Operation: **the eye as the index of an open or grasping interior** — the "good eye" gives and is blessed; sown injustice returns as calamity *(inferred; the generous vs grudging eye, w/ 23:6 the stingy eye)*. *Surfaced:* the bountiful/generous eye (w/ 23:6); injustice reaping calamity.

**PRO-22-scoffer-sloth-snare · Prov 22:10, 13, 14 · [D, multi] — the strife-source, the excuse-maker, and the seductive pit.** "**Drive out a scoffer, and strife will go out**, and quarrelling and abuse cease" (v.10); "the **sluggard says, 'There is a lion outside!'**" (v.13); "the **mouth of forbidden women is a deep pit**" (v.14). Finding: the **scoffer is the fount of strife** (remove him, peace returns); the **sluggard invents danger** to justify inaction; the seductive mouth is a **pit** one falls into *(stated)*. Operation: **three self-betraying interiors** — the scoffer whose removal quiets a room, the sluggard's fear-shaped excuse, the seduced self falling into the pit *(inferred; sloth's imagined lion, a wry note on fear-as-excuse)*. *Surfaced:* the scoffer as strife's fount; the sluggard's invented danger (new); the seductive mouth as a pit (thread).

## Catch-up units (2026-07-04) — loose study-term verses bound to units

**PRO-19-integrity-desire · Prov 19:1–2 · [D, multi] — integrity over status; desire that needs knowledge.** "**Better is a poor person who walks in his integrity** than one who is crooked in speech and is a fool" (v.1); "**desire without knowledge is not good**, and whoever makes haste with his feet misses his way" (v.2). Finding: **integrity outranks status**, and **desire uninformed by knowledge misfires** — zeal without direction misses the way *(stated)*. Operation: **the rightly-ordered and rightly-directed interior** — integrity over standing, and desire that must be *guided*, not merely felt; haste without knowledge is a wrong turn *(inferred; the un-guided desire, cross-book w/ Ecc 6:9 the wandering appetite)*. *Surfaced:* integrity over status; desire needing knowledge/direction (w/ Ecc 6:9).

**PRO-19-fickle-friends · Prov 19:4, 6, 7 · [D, multi] — friendship keyed to wealth; the deserted poor.** "**Wealth brings many new friends, but a poor man is deserted** by his friend" (v.4); "everyone is a friend to a man who gives gifts" (v.6); "all a poor man's **brothers hate him**; how much more do his friends go far… he pursues them with words, but does not have them" (v.7). Finding: friendship that tracks **wealth** deserts the poor — even brothers withdraw; the poor man chases with words and grasps nothing *(stated)*. Operation: **the fickleness of interested affection** — bonds keyed to gain evaporate in need (the false-friend theme, w/ Job 19:13–19) *(inferred)*. *Surfaced:* interested friendship that deserts the poor (w/ Job 19:14); the poor pursuing with words.

**PRO-19-witness · Prov 19:5, 9, 28, 29 · [D] — the lying witness who mocks justice.** "A **false witness will not go unpunished**… he who breathes out lies will perish" (vv.5,9); "a **worthless witness mocks at justice**, and the mouth of the wicked **devours iniquity**" (v.28). Finding: the false witness **breathes lies and mocks justice**, feeding on iniquity as if it were food *(stated)*. Operation: **the interior that consumes iniquity** — "the mouth of the wicked devours iniquity" images an appetite *for* wrong; lying as an outbreathing of the self *(inferred; iniquity as the wicked's food)*. *Surfaced:* the lie as the self's outbreathing; iniquity devoured as food (new).

**PRO-19-son-discipline · Prov 19:13, 16, 18, 26, 27 · [D, multi] — the son, discipline, and the keeping/despising of one's way.** "A **foolish son is ruin to his father**" (v.13); "whoever **keeps the commandment keeps his life**; he who despises his ways will die" (v.16); "**discipline your son, for there is hope**" (v.18); the son "who does **violence to his father and chases away his mother**" (v.26); "**cease to hear instruction… and you will stray**" (v.27). Finding: the son's character is **ruin or hope** to the family; keeping/despising one's *way* is life or death; ceasing to hear is straying *(stated)*. Operation: **formation through received discipline, and the drift of the un-listening self** — discipline is administered *in hope*; the self that stops hearing instruction wanders off the way *(inferred; receptivity as life-keeping, w/ the teachability thread)*. *Surfaced:* discipline-in-hope; keeping-one's-way as keeping life; the un-listening self's drift.

**PRO-19-teachable · Prov 19:20, 25 · [D] — the teachable interior vs the scoffer.** "**Listen to advice and accept instruction**, that you may gain wisdom" (v.20); "**strike a scoffer, and the simple learn prudence; reprove a man of understanding, and he gains knowledge**" (v.25). Finding: the interior **grows by receiving correction** — the understanding gain from a word what the scoffer only feels as a blow *(stated)*. Operation: **teachability as the capacity to profit from reproof** — the same correction educates the wise and merely warns the simple; the scoffer is unreachable except by the rod *(inferred; the teachable/unteachable heart, w/ 9:7–9)*. *Surfaced:* teachability as profiting from reproof (thread w/ 9:7–9); the reachable vs unreachable self.

**PRO-19-sloth-luxury · Prov 19:10, 15, 24 · [D, multi] — the sluggard and the misfit of ease.** "**Slothfulness casts into a deep sleep**, and an idle person will suffer hunger" (v.15); "the **sluggard buries his hand in the dish and will not even bring it back to his mouth**" (v.24); "it is **not fitting for a fool to live in luxury**" (v.10). Finding: sloth **sinks the self into torpor and hunger**, so paralysed it will not lift food to its own mouth; and ease ill-fits folly *(stated)*. Operation: **the self-defeating inertia of the sluggard** — a torpor so deep it starves the self that could feed itself *(inferred; sloth as self-starving paralysis)*. *Surfaced:* sloth as self-starving torpor (new); ease unfit for folly.

**PRO-19-from-the-Lord · Prov 19:12, 14, 17 · [D, multi] — what is from the LORD's hand, and what returns to it.** "A **king's wrath is like the growling of a lion, but his favour is like dew** on the grass" (v.12); "house and wealth are inherited, but a **prudent wife is from the LORD**" (v.14); "whoever is **generous to the poor lends to the LORD**, and he will repay" (v.17). Finding: the deepest goods (a prudent spouse) come **from the LORD**, and generosity to the poor is a **loan to the LORD** he repays *(stated)*. Operation: **the interior that sees God behind the good and within the gift** — the truest goods are received from his hand, and mercy to the poor is transacted *with him* *(inferred; generosity re-framed as lending to God)*. *Surfaced:* the good as from-the-LORD (thread w/ 18:22); generosity as lending to God (new). *(v.12 = the king's disposition one lives under, arena.)*

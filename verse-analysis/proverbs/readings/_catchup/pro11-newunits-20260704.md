## Catch-up unit (2026-07-04) — loose study-term verse bound to a unit

**PRO-11-honesty · Prov 11:1 · [S] — honesty as what the LORD delights in.** "A **false balance is an abomination** to the LORD, but a **just weight is his delight**" (v.1). Finding: the interior disposition behind honest dealing is what **God delights in** — integrity in the unseen (the weighing) is a delight to him, deceit an abomination *(stated)*. Operation: **integrity located in the hidden practice** — the balance and weights are where cheating hides; God's delight/abomination attaches to that concealed honesty or fraud *(inferred; the heart tested where no one sees, cross-book w/ Job 31:6 "let me be weighed in a just balance"; the heading of ch.11's collection)*. *Surfaced:* honesty-in-the-hidden as God's delight (new — w/ Job 31:6); integrity at the concealed weighing.

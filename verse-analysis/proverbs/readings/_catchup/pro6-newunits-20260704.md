## Catch-up units (2026-07-04) — loose study-term verses bound to units

**PRO-06-E · Prov 6:3–4 · [S] — the urgent self-humbling that escapes a snare.** Having become surety for a neighbour, the counsel is immediate and self-lowering: "**save yourself**… go, **hasten**, and **plead urgently** with your neighbour. **Give your eyes no sleep** and your eyelids no slumber" (vv.3–4). Finding: the self caught in a trap must act with **urgency and self-humbling** — pleading, not standing on pride, and refusing rest until free *(stated)*. Operation: **the interior mobilised to self-rescue** — the pride that would delay or refuse to plead is set aside; the whole self (no sleep) bent on release *(inferred; humbling-to-escape as an inner-being movement, kin to the urgency of repentance)*. *Surfaced:* urgent self-humbling to escape a self-made bind (new); the pride laid down to plead.

# Proverbs 5 — inner-being unit readings (Phase-2 meaning synthesis)

- **Source:** `lexical-model-2026` Phase-1 (v1.1 enriched) for Pro 5:1–23, per `segment_unit`. Grounded; *stated* | *inferred*. Lens: the inner-being **operation** (arena = the forbidden woman / the wife of youth).

---

**PRO-05-A · 5:1–6 · [D] — discernment of the sweet surface that ends bitter.** "The lips of a forbidden woman **drip honey**, and her speech is **smoother than oil**, but in the end she is **bitter as wormwood**… her steps follow the **path to Sheol**; she does not ponder the **path of life**" (vv.3–6). The finding: the inner being must learn to **read past the sweet surface to the end** — honey now, wormwood after *(stated)*. The operation: **discernment as looking-to-the-end** — the self weighs allure not by its present sweetness but by *where its path goes* (Sheol vs life) *(inferred; the anti-type of the naive who cannot see the end, cf. 14:12)*. *Validation:* *keep→discretion*, *guard→knowledge* (the discretion that protects); *drip→honey*, *go-down→death*, *path→Sheol* vs *path→of life* (the two ends captured). *Surfaced:* discernment-as-reading-the-end (new).

**PRO-05-B · 5:7–14 · [D, multi] — the retrospective regret of the un-teachable self.** The ruin of yielding is voiced *from its far end*: "at the end of your life you **groan**, when your **flesh and body are consumed**, and you say, '**How I hated discipline, and my heart despised reproof!** I did not listen to the voice of my teachers'" (vv.11–13). The finding — the sharpest inner-being note in the chapter: the self's undoing is traced to a **prior hatred of correction** — "how I *hated* discipline… my heart *despised* reproof… I did not *listen*" *(stated)*. The operation: the un-teachable interior **reaps regret** — the closed heart that despised reproof now *groans* at what it became, in a retrospective self-indictment *(inferred; teachability as life-or-death, the mirror of Ps 32:9's "be not like a horse without understanding")*. *Validation:* the enrichment names the objects precisely — *hated→target=discipline*, *despised→target=reproof*; *flesh→of body*. *Hidden:* **regret as the wage of un-teachability** (the self indicting its own past closedness). *Surfaced:* the un-teachable/despising heart (new — cross-book w/ Ps 32); retrospective self-indictment.

**PRO-05-C · 5:15–20 · [D] — rightly ordered, not extinguished, desire.** Against the adulteress: "**rejoice** in the wife of your youth… let her breasts **fill you at all times with delight**; be **intoxicated always in her love**" (vv.18–19). The finding: the answer to disordered desire is **not the killing of desire but its right direction** — the same intoxication (*shagah*) that would ruin the self with a stranger is *commanded* toward the wife *(stated)*. The operation: **desire redirected, not repressed** — the inner being's capacity for delight/intoxication is *good* when rightly aimed *(inferred; cross-book with the ordered-desire thread, Ps 37:4 "delight in the LORD")*. *Validation:* *all→of times* welded to *fill* ("fill you at all times"), *intoxicated→always*. *Hidden:* **the same passion is ruin or joy by its object** (desire is morally neutral, decided by direction). *Surfaced:* rightly-ordered desire (new — a Proverbs sharpening).

**PRO-05-D · 5:21–23 · [S] — the self bound in the cords of its own sin.** "A man's ways are before the **eyes of the LORD**… The **iniquities of the wicked ensnare him**, and he is **held fast in the cords of his sin**. He **dies for lack of discipline**" (vv.21–23). The finding: sin is **self-binding** — the wicked is *caught and held fast* not by an external trap but by *his own iniquity* *(stated)*. The operation: **iniquity as self-imposed bondage** — the sinning self *weaves the cords that hold it*; freedom is lost from within *(inferred; the self-ensnaring wicked, cross-book with Ps 7:15, 9:15, and Pro 1:18's greed recoiling)*. *Validation:* *iniquities→of wicked*, *held-fast→wicked*, **cords→specifier=of sin** — the substrate now captures "the cords *of his sin*". *Surfaced:* sin as self-binding bondage (shared thread, cross-book).

# Proverbs 7 — inner-being unit readings (Phase-2 meaning synthesis)

- **Source:** `lexical-model-2026` Phase-1 (v1.1 enriched) for Pro 7:1–27, per `segment_unit`. Grounded; *stated* | *inferred*. Lens: the inner-being **operation**.

---

**PRO-07-A · 7:1–5 · [D, multi] — wisdom held as beloved kin.** "Keep my commandments as the **apple of your eye**… Say to wisdom, '**You are my sister**,' and call insight your **intimate friend**" (vv.2,4). The finding: wisdom is to be held not as a rule but as a **beloved relation** — sister, intimate friend, guarded like the eye's pupil *(stated)*. The operation: **intimacy with wisdom** — the self is to *love* wisdom into the family, so that closeness to her keeps it from the stranger-woman *(inferred; affection as the guard, cross-book with Ps 119:97 "oh how I love your law")*. *Validation:* *teaching→of apple* ("keep as the apple of the eye"). *Surfaced:* intimacy/kinship with wisdom (new).

**PRO-07-B · 7:6–23 · [D, multi] — the anatomy of a naive heart yielding.** The long narrative of "a young man **lacking sense**" (v.7) taken step by step: she is "**wily of heart**" (v.10); "with **much seductive speech** she persuades him; with her **smooth talk** she compels him. **All at once** he follows her, as an ox goes to the **slaughter**… he **does not know** that it will cost him his **life**" (vv.21–23). The finding: the psalm-like narrative **anatomises the collapse of the naive self** — the sense-lacking heart, worked by *smooth speech*, yields "all at once" and *does not know* the cost *(stated)*. The operation: **the un-guarded interior overwhelmed** — persuasion works on a heart with no discernment until the will follows *unknowing* to its ruin; the tragedy is the *not-knowing* *(inferred; the mirror of the discerning self of 5:1–6; cross-book with the naive vs prudent, and Ps 19:12 "who can discern his errors")*. *Validation:* *wily→target=heart* ("wily of heart"), *much→of seductive speech*, *smooth→persuades*, *fill→target=love* — the enrichment captures the seduction's mechanics. *Hidden:* **ruin through not-knowing** (the naive self's fatal unawareness). *Surfaced:* the collapse of the un-discerning heart (new — the anti-type of discernment).

**PRO-07-C · 7:24–27 · [D] — guarding the heart against turning aside.** "**Let not your heart turn aside** to her ways; do not stray into her paths" (v.25). The finding: the defence is set at the **heart's inclination** — the danger is the *heart turning aside* *(stated)*. The operation: **the self guards the *direction* of its own heart** — the well-trodden path to death is entered by an inward *turning*, so the guarding is of the heart's leaning, before the feet move *(inferred; the same interiorised guard as 4:23, 6:25)*. *Surfaced:* guarding the heart's inclination (shared w/ 4:23; the turned-aside heart).

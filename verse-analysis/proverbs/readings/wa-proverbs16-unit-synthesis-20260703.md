# Proverbs 16 — inner-being unit readings (Phase-2 meaning synthesis)

- **Source:** `lexical-model-2026` Phase-1 (v1.1 enriched) for Pro 16, per `segment_unit`. Grounded; *stated* | *inferred*. Lens: the inner-being **operation**.

---

**PRO-16-heart-sovereignty · 16:1,3,9,33 · [T, multi] — the heart plans, the LORD establishes.** "The **plans of the heart belong to man, but the answer of the tongue is from the LORD**"; "**commit your work to the LORD, and your plans will be established**"; "**the heart of man plans his way, but the LORD establishes his steps**" (vv.1,3,9). Finding — a cornerstone: the inner being genuinely **plans and purposes**, yet the *outcome* is God's — human deliberation and divine sovereignty are held together, not against each other *(stated)*. Operation: **the planning heart that *commits its plans* to God** — the self is neither passive (it plans) nor autonomous (God establishes); the resolving move is to *entrust* the planning to the LORD (v.3) *(inferred; a distinctive Proverbs datum on the will, cross-book w/ Ps 37:5 "commit your way", 31:15 "my times are in your hand")*. *Surfaced:* the planning heart + entrusting plans to God (★ new cornerstone — human agency under divine sovereignty).

**PRO-16-weighed · 16:2 · [S, multi] — pure in one's own eyes; the LORD weighs the spirit.** "**All the ways of a man are pure in his own eyes, but the LORD weighs the spirit**" (v.2). Finding: the self's self-assessment is **unreliable** — it looks *pure to itself*; only God *weighs the spirit* truly *(stated)*. Operation: **self-blindness corrected by divine assay** *(inferred; cross-book w/ Ps 139, and 21:2, 30:12)*. *Surfaced:* self-blindness vs God's weighing of the spirit (shared w/ Ps 139).

**PRO-16-pride · 16:5,18,19 · [T, multi] — pride before the fall; the lowly spirit.** "**Pride goes before destruction, and a haughty spirit before a fall**"; "better to be of a **lowly spirit with the poor** than divide spoil with the proud" (vv.18–19). Finding: the proud heart **carries its own collapse** *(stated)*. Operation: **pride as self-destruction, lowliness as the safer good** *(inferred; the pride/humility cornerstone, cross-book w/ Ps 18:27, 138:6)*. *Surfaced:* pride/humility (★ shared cornerstone).

**PRO-16-self-rule · 16:32 · [S, multi] — ruling one's own spirit.** "**Whoever is slow to anger is better than the mighty, and he who rules his spirit than he who takes a city**" (v.32). Finding — a cornerstone: the conquest of the *self* outranks the conquest of a *city* *(stated)*. Operation: **self-mastery as the supreme strength** — governing one's own spirit is a greater might than military conquest *(inferred; the self-government thread at its clearest, cross-book w/ the Psalter's self-address/self-quieting, Ps 131:2, and 25:28, 29:11)*. *Surfaced:* self-mastery / ruling one's spirit (★ cornerstone — the inner conquest above the outer).

**PRO-16-fear · 16:6,20 · [S, multi] — fear turns from evil; trust discovers good.** "By the **fear of the LORD one turns away from evil**"; "blessed is he who **trusts in the LORD**" (vv.6,20). *Surfaced:* fear-of-LORD; trust (shared).

**PRO-16-speech-heart · 16:21,23,24 · [T, multi] — the heart makes the speech; gracious words sweeten the soul.** "The **heart of the wise makes his speech judicious**"; "**gracious words are like a honeycomb, sweetness to the soul and health to the body**" (vv.23–24). Finding: wise speech *originates in the heart*, and gracious words are **sweet to the soul and healing to the body** *(stated)*. Operation: **speech as the heart's outflow, and kind speech as somatic medicine** *(inferred; the speech-legibility + felt-interior threads joined; cross-book w/ Ps 19:14)*. *Surfaced:* the heart-sourced tongue; gracious-words-heal (new).

**PRO-16-self-deception · 16:25 · [S] — the way that seems right (= 14:12).** "There is a way that seems right to a man, but its end is the way to death." *Surfaced:* self-deception (shared, recurs).

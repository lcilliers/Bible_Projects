# Proverbs 28 — inner-being unit readings (Phase-2 meaning synthesis)

- **Source:** `lexical-model-2026` Phase-1 (v1.1 enriched) for Pro 28, per `segment_unit`. Grounded; *stated* | *inferred*. Lens: the inner-being **operation**.

---

**PRO-28-bold-lion · 28:1 · [S] — the guilty flee, the righteous are bold.** "**The wicked flee when no one pursues, but the righteous are bold as a lion**" (v.1). Finding: the inner state of **guilt manufactures pursuers** where none exist, while a clear conscience is **fearless** *(stated)*. Operation: **conscience as the source of dread or boldness** — the wicked's fear is *self-generated* (no one pursues), the righteous's courage is *conscience-born* *(inferred; cross-book w/ Ps 53:5 "there they are in great terror, where there was no terror", 27:1)*. *Hidden:* **fear conjured by a guilty conscience** (dread with no external cause). *Surfaced:* conscience → dread-or-boldness (★ new — the self-generated fear of guilt).

**PRO-28-confess · 28:13 · [S, multi] — concealment vs confession of sin.** "**Whoever conceals his transgressions will not prosper, but he who confesses and forsakes them will obtain mercy**" (v.13). Finding — a cornerstone: **hiding sin fails; confessing-and-forsaking obtains mercy** *(stated)*. Operation: **confession as the release of the concealed self** — the interior that *hides* its sin stays bound; the one that *owns and forsakes* is freed to mercy *(inferred; the exact Proverbs parallel to Ps 32:3–5 "when I kept silent, my bones wasted… I acknowledged my sin… and you forgave")*. *Surfaced:* confession-vs-concealment (★ cornerstone, cross-book w/ Ps 32).

**PRO-28-fear-harden · 28:14 · [S, multi] — fearing always vs the hardened heart.** "**Blessed is the one who fears the LORD always, but whoever hardens his heart will fall into calamity**" (v.14). Finding: continual reverent fear vs the **hardened heart** — the self that *hardens* falls *(stated)*. Operation: **the hardening of the heart as the opposite of the fear of the LORD** *(inferred; cross-book w/ Ps 95:8 "do not harden your hearts")*. *Hidden:* **the heart-hardening operation** (a self-inflicted callousing). *Surfaced:* the hardened heart vs continual fear (★ new — the hardening operation, cross-book w/ Ps 95).

**PRO-28-trust · 28:25,26 · [T, multi] — trust in the LORD vs greed vs self-reliance.** "A **greedy man stirs up strife, but the one who trusts in the LORD will be enriched**"; "**whoever trusts in his own mind is a fool**, but he who walks in wisdom will be delivered" (vv.25–26). Finding: trust is set against **greed** and against **self-reliance** ("trusts in his own mind") *(stated)*. Operation: **trust as the alternative to grasping and to self-confidence** — the two great rivals to trusting God are *greed* and *the self's own mind* *(inferred; cross-book w/ 3:5 "lean not on your own understanding", Ps 62:8–10)*. *Surfaced:* trust vs greed vs self-reliance (shared, sharpened w/ 3:5).

## Catch-up units (2026-07-04) — loose study-term verses bound to units

**PRO-28-rule-public · Prov 28:2, 12, 28 · [D, multi] — the public weather of righteous vs wicked ascendancy.** "When a land transgresses, it has **many rulers**, but with a **man of understanding… stability** continues" (v.2); "when the **righteous triumph, great glory**, but when the wicked rise, **people hide themselves**" (vv.12,28). Finding: the ascendancy of righteousness or wickedness **changes the public atmosphere** — glory and stability, or a people in hiding *(stated)*. Operation: **the collective interior shaped by who rules** — the same people flourish or hide depending on the moral character in power *(inferred; the corporate mood, w/ 29:2)*. *Surfaced:* the public atmosphere set by righteous/wicked rule (thread w/ 29:2).

**PRO-28-law-ear · Prov 28:4, 7, 9 · [D, multi] — the law and the hearing (or turned-away) ear.** "Those who **forsake the law praise the wicked**, but those who keep it strive against them" (v.4); "the one who **keeps the law is a son with understanding**" (v.7); "if one **turns away his ear from hearing the law, even his prayer is an abomination**" (v.9). Finding: keeping the law forms an understanding self; **turning the ear from it corrupts even prayer** *(stated)*. Operation: **the ear's orientation determining the whole interior** — refuse to hear instruction, and even worship sours; the receptive ear is the root of understanding *(inferred; the hearing ear, w/ the teachability thread; unheard-law makes prayer abomination — a striking link of receptivity and worship)*. *Surfaced:* the turned-away ear corrupting even prayer (new); law-keeping as understanding.

**PRO-28-seek-understand · Prov 28:5 · [S] — seeking the LORD as the key to understanding justice.** "**Evil men do not understand justice, but those who seek the LORD understand it completely**" (v.5). Finding: moral understanding is **unlocked by seeking God** — the evil are blind to justice; the God-seeker sees it fully *(stated)*. Operation: **the God-ward orientation that opens moral perception** (w/ the fear-of-the-LORD-as-wisdom thread, Job 28:28) *(inferred)*. *Surfaced:* seeking-God as the key to understanding justice (w/ Job 28:28).

**PRO-28-integrity-way · Prov 28:6, 10, 18 · [D, multi] — the two ways; the pit-digger.** "**Better a poor man who walks in his integrity** than a rich man crooked in his ways" (v.6); "whoever **misleads the upright into an evil way will fall into his own pit**" (v.10); "whoever **walks in integrity will be delivered**, but he who is crooked will **suddenly fall**" (v.18). Finding: **integrity delivers and outranks crooked wealth**; the one who lays a trap for the upright **falls into it himself** *(stated)*. Operation: **the self-recoiling crooked way** — the pit dug for another swallows the digger; the straight walk is preserved *(inferred; the pit-digger, w/ Ps 7:15; integrity over status, w/ 19:1)*. *Surfaced:* the pit-digger self-trapped (w/ Ps 7:15); integrity delivering (thread).

**PRO-28-wealth-greed · Prov 28:8, 11, 19, 20, 22, 27 · [D, multi] — wealth, self-conceit, greed, and generosity.** "Whoever **multiplies wealth by interest… gathers it for him who is generous to the poor**" (v.8); "a **rich man is wise in his own eyes**, but a poor man of understanding **finds him out**" (v.11); "**works his land → plenty of bread**; follows worthless pursuits → poverty" (v.19); "a **faithful man abounds with blessings**, but whoever **hastens to be rich** will not go unpunished" (v.20); "a **stingy man hastens after wealth** and does not know that poverty will come" (v.22); "whoever **gives to the poor will not want**, but he who **hides his eyes** gets many a curse" (v.27). Finding: **greedy haste for wealth self-defeats** (unpunished it is not, and poverty overtakes it), the rich are **self-deceived**, and **generosity to the poor** is the blessed path *(stated)*. Operation: **the grasping vs the open interior** — the hastening, stingy, self-conceited self toward poverty and curse; the faithful, generous, land-working self toward plenty and blessing *(inferred; the never-satisfied greed, w/ Ecc 5:10; generosity as un-wanting, w/ 11:24)*. *Surfaced:* greedy haste self-defeating (w/ Ecc 5:10); the rich self-deceived; generosity as the un-wanting path.

**PRO-28-oppressor · Prov 28:15, 16 · [S, multi] — the oppressive ruler.** "Like a **roaring lion or a charging bear is a wicked ruler over a poor people**" (v.15); "a **ruler who lacks understanding is a cruel oppressor**, but he who **hates unjust gain will prolong his days**" (v.16). Finding: the understanding-lacking ruler becomes a **predatory oppressor**; the one who **hates unjust gain** endures *(stated)*. Operation: **power without understanding turning predatory** — the interior that hates ill-gotten gain is what makes rule bearable and lasting *(inferred)*. *Surfaced:* the predatory ruler (new); hating unjust gain as what preserves.

**PRO-28-guilt-theft · Prov 28:17, 21, 24 · [D, multi] — guilt's flight, cheap corruption, the self-justified thief.** "One **burdened with the blood of another** will be a **fugitive until death**" (v.17); "to show **partiality is not good**, but for a **piece of bread a man will do wrong**" (v.21); "whoever **robs his father or mother and says, 'That is no transgression,'** is a companion to a destroyer" (v.24). Finding: bloodguilt makes a lifelong **fugitive**; corruption comes **cheap** (a piece of bread); and the thief who **denies his sin** is kin to a destroyer *(stated)*. Operation: **the fleeing conscience, and the self that renames its theft as innocence** — guilt cannot rest; the self-justified robber calls his sin "no transgression" *(inferred; guilt's flight, w/ Gen 4:12; self-justified sin, the un-owned wrong)*. *Surfaced:* guilt's lifelong flight (w/ Gen 4:12); corruption bought cheap; the self-justified thief (new).

**PRO-28-rebuke · Prov 28:23 · [S] — honest rebuke over flattery.** "Whoever **rebukes a man will afterward find more favour** than he who **flatters** with his tongue" (v.23). Finding: **honest rebuke earns deeper favour** than flattery — the truth-teller is finally more loved than the flatterer *(stated)*. Operation: **the reproof that is valued over time** (w/ 27:5–6 "faithful are the wounds of a friend") *(inferred)*. *Surfaced:* honest rebuke outlasting flattery (thread w/ 27:5).

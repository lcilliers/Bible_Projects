### Notable reads
The cycle is anchored by **Agur's humility** (30:3-4 — disclaiming knowledge of the Holy One, the unanswerable questions humbling every human claim to comprehend God; 30:18 — wonder joined to owned ignorance) and by the profound **"neither poverty nor riches"** spirituality (30:9 — plenty tempts to self-sufficient *denial* of God, want to desperation-*theft* that profanes his name; both perils prayed against). And the leech's daughters name the bottomlessness of **insatiable craving** (30:15-16, cf 27:20).

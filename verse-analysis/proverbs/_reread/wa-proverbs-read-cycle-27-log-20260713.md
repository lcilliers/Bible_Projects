### Notable reads — the inner life and the face
The **heart-governs-the-outer** theme runs through 15:13-17: the glad heart writes on the *face* (15:13a), heart-sorrow *crushes the spirit* (15:13b), and — most strikingly — the **cheerful heart keeps a "continual feast" regardless of circumstance** (15:15), while love makes a poor meal a feast and hatred sours abundance (15:16-17). Inner state, not circumstance, sets the tone of life. Plus **considered vs unconsidered speech** (15:28 — the righteous heart *ponders* how to answer, the wicked mouth *gushes* evil).

### Notable reads — sovereignty and the weighed heart
The cycle's spine is **human planning under divine sovereignty**: 16:1 (man's heart plans, God gives the tongue's answer), 16:2 (man self-approves, God weighs the spirit), 16:3 (committed plans established), 16:9 (man proposes, God disposes) — read as a sustained meditation that the inner life is real and active yet finally overruled and assayed by God. Plus **self-justification exposed** (16:2 — "all a man's ways are pure in his own eyes", the near-universal blindness) and the **moral-relational atonement** of 16:6 (chesed + emet cover iniquity; the fear of God turns one from evil).

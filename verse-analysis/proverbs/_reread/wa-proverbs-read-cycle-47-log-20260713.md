### Notable reads
The **divine-hiddenness / royal-investigation** pairing (25:2 — God's glory partly in mystery, the king's glory in probing a matter to truth); the **sage's method** made explicit (24:32 — *saw → set the heart to consider → received instruction*: reflection is the bridge from observation to wisdom, learning from the sluggard's ruin); the **humility-before-honour** ethic (25:7 — take the low seat and be raised, anticipating Luke 14:8-10); and the beauty of **wise correction gladly received** (25:12 — a rebuke is gold only where there is a listening ear). The Hezekiah collection's fondness for the **vivid simile** (snow-cool in harvest = a faithful messenger; clouds without rain = an empty boaster) comes through strongly.

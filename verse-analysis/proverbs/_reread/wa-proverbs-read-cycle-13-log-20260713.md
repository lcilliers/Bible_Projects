### Notable reads — Wisdom vs the seductress
Ch.8 read deliberately against ch.7: **two voices court the simple** — the adulteress's three love-words (*dodim/alas/ohabim*, 7:18) as counterfeit of marital delight, vs **Wisdom's open *qara*** (8:1,4) redeeming the very verb the seductress abused (7:15). Wisdom addresses the **same *pethi*** the seductress preys on (7:7 → 8:5), but to cure their naivety with *ormah* not exploit it. The youth's fatal *yada*-negation ("he does not KNOW it will cost his *nephesh*", 7:23) vs the discerning to whom Wisdom's words are plain (8:9). Wisdom's own **abomination** at wickedness (8:7) read as *her* revulsion (bearer=Wisdom, internal), distinct from God's at 6:16. Many old-model `None`/`standalone`/`process-qualifier` spans read to true characteristic role.

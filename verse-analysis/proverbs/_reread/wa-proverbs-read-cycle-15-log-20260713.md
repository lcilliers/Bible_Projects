### Notable reads — Wisdom vs Folly, doubled
Wisdom's *shaashuim* (delight, 8:30-31) read as **joyous communion** — she is play and gladness, not cold principle; the terrible equation of 8:36 (*to hate Wisdom = to love death*); *chata* "miss" (8:36) read on its double sense (miss/sin — missing Wisdom is self-injury). Ch.9 read as **Wisdom vs Folly in parallel**: Wisdom *calls from the heights* (9:3) / Folly *calls to passersby* (9:15, counterfeit); Wisdom *finds knowledge* (8:12) / Folly *knows nothing* (9:13); Wisdom's feast → *live* (9:6) / Folly's → death (9:18). The scoffer/wise divide (9:7-9) read as **teachability = wisdom's signature** (the wise *love* reproof, the scoffer *hates* it), and knowledge of God (the Holy One) as the summit of *insight* (9:10).

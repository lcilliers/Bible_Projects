## Flagged borderline calls (your steer welcome)
1. **Seductress's own traits as characteristic** — H5341 wily, H5637 wayward, H5810 bold. These are inner-being *operations of a negative actor*. I included them as characteristic (a trait is a trait regardless of whose). Alternative: treat antagonist-traits differently. **My call: characteristic** (consistent with "characteristic by use/meaning, not by the person").
2. **H0269 sister / H4129 intimate friend (v4)** — the reading names *intimacy/kinship with wisdom* as a **new characteristic**, but the words themselves are kin-nouns *predicated of wisdom*. I set them **qualifier** (relational — they strengthen the characteristic "wisdom"). Could be promoted to characteristic if you want the intimacy-operation carried on these spans.
3. **Illicit love/delight (v18)** — H1730/H0159/H5965 set **characteristic** (affection/desire is inner-being even when illicit). Flagging because it is the seduction's sensuality, not virtue.
4. **H0191 (v22)** — textual crux; surface renders "stag", Strong's = "fool". Read as *folly* → **characteristic** (lean-characteristic rule on an ambiguous span).
5. **H0995 perceived (v7)** — could be read as the narrator's literal observation (standalone) rather than discernment. I set **characteristic** (bin = discernment, and the chapter turns on discern-vs-naive).
6. **H8085 listen / H7181 attentive (v24)** — receptivity operations; I set **qualifier** (they attend *to the words*, relational) rather than characteristic. Reasonable either way.

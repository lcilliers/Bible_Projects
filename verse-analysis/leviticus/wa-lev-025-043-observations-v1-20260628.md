## Structural observation (the verse's headline)
- **Lev 25:43 is one clause of a governed relationship.** The chapter regulates the **treatment of a person sold into servitude**; "fear your God" is the **inner anchor of the whole regime** (recurring at 25:17 no-wronging, 25:36 no-usury, 25:43 no-ruthless-rule). Within that, v.43 stages the sharp case: **fear-of-God (the governing reverence) vs ruthless-dominion (the impulse to crush)** — and the inner being's treatment of the vulnerable is decided by which governs. The same shape recurs wherever power meets the weak (Lev 25:17/36/43/46/53; Eze 34:4; Exo 1). *(researcher · candidate focus point)*
- **Implication for the streams:** the **fear-of-God stream is the wide one** (it governs the whole treatment); **dominion** and **ruthlessness** are the impulse it bounds. Reading v.43 as *only* "fear restrains ruthlessness" understates fear's reach.

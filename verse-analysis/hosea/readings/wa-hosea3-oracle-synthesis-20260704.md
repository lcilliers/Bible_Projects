## HOS-buy-back · Hos 3:1–5 · [D, multi] — redeeming love for the unfaithful

**The reading.** The sign is re-enacted at cost: "**Go again, love a woman who is loved by another man and is an adulteress, even as the LORD loves the children of Israel, though they turn to other gods** and love cakes of raisins" (v.1); "so I **bought her** for fifteen shekels…" (v.2). The finding is **a love that pursues and *purchases back* the unfaithful — love directed precisely at the one who has betrayed it** *(stated)*. The operation is **redeeming love that is not conditional on the beloved's fidelity** — Hosea loves and buys back an adulteress *because* that is how the LORD loves Israel "though they turn to other gods" *(inferred: the "even as" (v.1) makes Hosea's costly, humiliating love a live enactment of God's — love that *precedes and outlasts* the beloved's faithfulness, and pays to reclaim it; the finding is that divine love is not a response to the loveliness or loyalty of its object but a *choice that redeems the unworthy*; the "many days" of disciplined waiting (v.3) images a love that also *restrains* itself, holding the beloved to fidelity without abandoning her; the outcome is a chastened return "in fear to the LORD and to his goodness" (v.5) — awe re-awakened by being redeemed)*.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *redeeming love for the unfaithful (love that pays to reclaim the betrayer)* (new — the enacted heart of God's love, w/ 11:8, 14:4); *love that precedes and outlasts fidelity* (new); *awe re-awakened by being redeemed ("in fear to his goodness")* (new).

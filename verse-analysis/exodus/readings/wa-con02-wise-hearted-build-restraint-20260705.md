## CON-02-wise-hearted-build-restraint · Exodus 36:1–38 · [the generosity that must be stopped] — "the people bring much more than enough", and the tabernacle made

**The passage.** Bezalel, Oholiab, every craftsman in whom the LORD put **skill** (chakham + lev) and **understanding** (tevunah) for the **work** (avodah) on the **sanctuary** (qodesh, 36:1); Moses **called** (qara) the **skilled** (chakham + lev, 36:2); the freewill offerings kept coming for the **work** (avodah) on the **sanctuary** (qodesh, 36:3); the **craftsmen** (chakham) on the **sanctuary** (qodesh, 36:4); "the people bring **much more** (ravah) than enough for the **work** (avodah)" (36:5); the people **restrained** from bringing for the **sanctuary** (qodesh, 36:6); the **craftsmen** (chakham + lev) made the tabernacle with cherubim **skilfully worked** (chashav, 36:8); the frames **joined** (tam, 36:29); the veil **skilfully worked** (chashav, 36:35).

### The operations (the genuine inner-being content)

- **chakham (skill) + lev (heart) + tevunah (understanding) — the *Spirit-given skill at work* (36:1–2).** "'Bezalel, Oholiab, and every **skilled** (chakham + lev) craftsman in whom the LORD put **skill** and **understanding** (tevunah)… shall work'; Moses **called** (qara) everyone whose **heart** (lev) stirred him" *(stated)*. **chakham/lev** — *wise-hearted*; **tevunah** — *understanding*: the gem (← 35:31) — *the skill is God-given and now set to work* — the *wise-hearted whose heart stirred them* come to the task; the interior-context of *gifted ability enacted in labor* *(inferred: chakham/lev/tevunah = the Spirit-given skill at work — the wise-hearted whose heart stirred them)*.
- **ravah (much more) + avodah (work) — the *generosity that must be stopped* (36:3–7).** "'the freewill offerings kept coming every morning… "the people bring **much more** (ravah) than enough for the **work** (avodah)"'; so Moses commanded, and the people were **restrained** from bringing… the material was more than enough" *(stated)*. **ravah** — *be much / multiply*; **avodah** — *work*: the gem is *extraordinary* — *the willing hearts give SO MUCH that they must be RESTRAINED*: the offerings *keep coming morning by morning* until the craftsmen report a *surplus*, and Moses *proclaims a stop*. The interior finding: *the willing heart overflowing beyond need* — *the payout of 25:2 at its climax* — and *the only collection in Scripture halted because there is too much*. The generosity that had to be *checked*, not begged (the exact reverse of every ordinary appeal) *(inferred: ravah/avodah = the generosity that must be stopped — the willing heart overflowing beyond need, restrained)*.

- The dimensions, loops, clasps, bases (36:9–38) carry no non-T2 spans — pure construction.

### The web (interactive)

The chapter's genuine finding stands clear: **the willing hearts gave too much.** The *Spirit-given skill* is *set to work*; and the *freewill offerings overflow* — *more than enough* — until Moses must *restrain* the people from giving. Around it, the *tabernacle made* (construction). No more is claimed than the verses give — but the *generosity that had to be stopped* is a real and striking span, the climax of the willing-heart thread.

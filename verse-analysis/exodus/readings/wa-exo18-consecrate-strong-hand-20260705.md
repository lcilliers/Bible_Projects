## EXO-18-consecrate-strong-hand · Exodus 13:1–16 · [the interior formed by memory and redemption] — consecrate the firstborn, remember by a strong hand, and redeem the belonging

**The passage.** "**Consecrate** (qadash) to me all the firstborn… is mine" (13:2); "**Remember** (zakhar) this day… by a **strong** (chozeq) hand the LORD brought you out" (13:3); "**keep** (avad) this **service** (avodah)… the land he swore to **give** (natan)" (13:5); "**keep** (shamar) this statute yearly" (13:10); "when the LORD… shall **give** (natan) it to you" (13:11); "every firstborn of a donkey you shall **redeem** (padah)… your sons you shall redeem" (13:13); "when your son **asks** (shaal)… 'by a **strong** (chozeq) hand the LORD brought us out'" (13:14); "Pharaoh stubbornly refused… therefore… all the firstborn of my sons I **redeem** (padah)" (13:15); "by a **strong** (chozeq) hand the LORD brought us out" (13:16).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **qadash (consecrate) — the firstborn *set apart as God's* (13:2).** "**Consecrate** (qadash) to me all the firstborn… is mine" *(stated)* — qadash, *make holy/set apart/dedicate*: the gem is *belonging* — because God *spared* Israel's firstborn (and struck Egypt's), *every* firstborn is now *God's*; the interior-context is *the firstborn dedicated* — the community's *first and best set apart to God* (a claim answering the firstborn-death) *(inferred: qadash = the firstborn consecrated as God's — belonging by the sparing)*.
- **zakhar (remember) + chozeq (strong) — the *memory-command* (13:3).** "**Remember** (zakhar) this day… out of the house of slavery, for by a **strong** (chozeq) hand the LORD brought you out" *(stated)*. **zakhar** — the gem: *Israel commanded to remember* — the reciprocal of *God's remembering* (2:24 "God remembered the covenant"); now *Israel remembers the deliverance*; **chozeq** — *"by a strong hand"* — the refrain of the deliverance's *power*. The interior *bound to remember* the day of freedom *(inferred: zakhar/chozeq = the memory-command — Israel remembering the strong-handed deliverance)*.
- **avad (keep) + avodah (service) + natan (give) — the *worship-service in the land* (13:5).** "you shall **keep** (avad) this **service** (avodah)… the land he swore to **give** (natan)" *(stated)*. **avad**/**avodah** — *the worship-service* (← 12:25; the transfer of service continued — slavery-word now worship); **natan** — the *land given*. The interior *keeping God's service in the promised place* *(inferred: avad/avodah/natan = the worship-service kept in the given land)*.
- **shamar (keep) — the *statute kept yearly* (13:10).** "you shall **keep** (shamar) this statute at its appointed time year to year" *(stated)* — shamar, *keep/observe*: the *annual* keeping — the interior's *rhythm of remembrance* *(inferred: shamar = the statute kept in yearly rhythm)*.
- **natan (give) — the land *given* as sworn (13:11).** *natan distinct from 13:5:* "when the LORD… shall **give** (natan) it to you, as he swore" *(stated)* — the *land given* as the *condition* of the firstborn-consecration that follows; the interior's *inheritance* the setting of its worship *(inferred: natan = the land given, the setting of the firstborn-rite)*.
- **padah (redeem) — the *redemption law* (13:13).** "every firstborn of a donkey you shall **redeem** (padah)… your sons you shall redeem" *(stated)* — padah, *ransom/redeem*: the gem — because the firstborn *belong to God* (13:2), they must be *bought back*; the *unclean* animal *redeemed by a lamb* (or its neck broken), the *sons redeemed*. The interior-context is *the belonging bought back* — what is God's is *ransomed* to be kept *(inferred: padah = the firstborn redeemed — the belonging ransomed back)*.
- **shaal (asks) + chozeq (strong) — the *son's catechism* (13:14).** "when your son **asks** (shaal), 'what does this mean?' you shall say, 'by a **strong** (chozeq) hand the LORD brought us out'" *(stated)*. **shaal** — the gem: the *rite provokes the son's question* (← 12:26); **chozeq** *distinct from 13:3* — now the *strong hand* is the *catechism answer* (13:3 was Moses' proclamation; 13:14 is the *son taught*). The interior of the *next generation* *formed by asking and being told* *(inferred: shaal/chozeq = the son's question answered by the strong-hand deliverance — the catechism)*.
- **padah (redeem) — the redemption's *reason* (13:15).** *padah distinct from 13:13:* "Pharaoh stubbornly refused… the LORD killed all the firstborn… therefore… my sons I **redeem** (padah)" *(stated)* — where 13:13 was the *law*, 13:15 is the *reason*: the firstborn are redeemed *because* God *struck Egypt's firstborn and spared Israel's*. The gem: the catechism *includes the hardening* — *"Pharaoh stubbornly refused"* (qashah) is *taught to the child* — the adversary's hardening *woven into Israel's story*; and the redemption *grounded in the sparing* *(inferred: padah = the redemption grounded in the firstborn-sparing — the hardening taught as its cause)*.
- **chozeq (strong) — the *strong hand marked on the body* (13:16).** *chozeq distinct from 13:3,14:* "it shall be as a mark on your hand or frontlets between your eyes, for by a **strong** (chozeq) hand the LORD brought us out" *(stated)* — the *third* strong-hand refrain, now *bound to the body* (hand, eyes) — the interior's memory *made bodily and permanent* *(inferred: chozeq = the strong-hand deliverance marked on the body — memory made permanent)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **the firstborn consecrated, the day remembered (qadash 13:2 → zakhar/chozeq 13:3):** because God *spared* Israel's firstborn, they are *set apart as his*; and Israel is *commanded to remember* the strong-handed deliverance — the reciprocal of God's own remembering (2:24).
2. **the service kept, the land given (avad/avodah/natan 13:5 → shamar 13:10 → natan 13:11):** the *worship-service* kept in the *given land*, in yearly rhythm — the interior's remembrance *ritualised in place and time*.
3. **the belonging redeemed and the child taught (padah 13:13 → shaal/chozeq 13:14 → padah 13:15 → chozeq 13:16):** the firstborn *redeemed* (bought back to God), the *son's question answered* by the strong-hand story — a catechism that *includes the hardening* ("Pharaoh stubbornly refused") — and the memory *marked on the body*. The interior *formed by memory, redemption, and teaching*.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** qadash (the firstborn *consecrated as God's* — belonging by the sparing); **zakhar (the *memory-command* — Israel remembering, reciprocal of God's remembering 2:24)**; chozeq ×3 (the *strong hand* — Moses' *proclamation* 13:3 / the son's *catechism answer* 13:14 / the *bodily mark* 13:16); avad/avodah (the *worship-service* — slavery-word transferred); natan ×2 (the land *given* — setting of service / setting of the firstborn-rite); shamar (the *yearly* keeping); **padah ×2 (the redemption *law* / its *reason* — the firstborn *redeemed* because spared)**; **shaal (the *son's question* — the rite that teaches)**; the hardening *in the catechism* (13:15 "stubbornly refused" — no-span context).
- **Whose interior (multi-interior):** **Israel** — the interior *formed by memory (zakhar), redemption (padah), and teaching (shaal)*: the firstborn *consecrated and redeemed* (belonging to God, bought back), the deliverance *remembered, ritualised, marked on the body, and taught to the children* — including *the adversary's hardening* as part of the story; **God** — the *redeemer "by a strong hand"* who *claims and spares the firstborn*.
- **Threads:** the **reciprocal remembering** (zakhar 13:3 ← God remembered 2:24 — God remembers the covenant, Israel remembers the deliverance); the **transfer of service** (avodah 13:5 ← 12:25; slavery 1:14 → worship); the **pedagogical rite** (shaal 13:14 ← 12:26; 10:2 — the story told to the children, *including the hardening* 13:15); the **firstborn** (qadash/padah 13:2,13 ← the firstborn-death 12:29; "Israel my firstborn" 4:22 — spared, so consecrated and redeemed); the **strong hand** refrain (chozeq 13:3,14,16 ← "with a strong hand he will drive you out" 6:1); the **hardening in the catechism** (13:15 qashah — the child *taught* the whole story, adversary included — the deeper trend: the hardening *serves the telling*, ← 10:2).
- **Guarded against:** lumping the three chozeq (proclamation / catechism / bodily-mark), two natan, two padah (law / reason); reading qadash/padah as later sacrificial theology over the *belonging-by-sparing* the passage gives; missing that the catechism (13:15) *includes* Pharaoh's hardening — the child learns the *whole* story (the hardening *serves the generational telling*, per the ledger's pedagogical thread); over-reading the frontlets (13:16) as later phylactery-law over the *memory-made-bodily* the verse states; treating the firstborn-redemption as merely legal when it enacts *Israel's own firstborn spared and bought back to God*.

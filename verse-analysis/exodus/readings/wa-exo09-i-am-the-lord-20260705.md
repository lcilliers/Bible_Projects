## EXO-09-i-am-the-lord · Exodus 5:22–7:7 · [the deliverer's despair, "I AM the LORD", and the interior too broken to hear] — Moses accuses God; the sevenfold promise; Israel cannot listen

**The passage.** Moses: "why have you **done evil** (raa) to this people? why did you send me?" (5:22); "he has **done evil** (raa)… you have not delivered" (5:23); "I established my **covenant** (berit) to **give** (natan) the land" (6:4); "I have **heard** (shama) the **groaning** (neaqah) of Israel held as **slaves** (avad), and **remembered** (zakhar) my **covenant** (berit)" (6:5); "I will bring you out… redeem you with great acts of **judgment** (shephet)… from **slavery** (avodah)" (6:6); "I will **give** (natan) it for a possession" (6:8); "they did not **listen** (shama), because of broken spirit and **harsh** (qasheh) **slavery** (avodah)" (6:9); "Israel have not **listened** (shama); how will Pharaoh?" (6:12); the Levite genealogy — the years of **life** (chayyim) of Levi, Kohath, Amram (6:16, 6:18, 6:20); "I will **harden** [qashah] Pharaoh's **heart** (lev), and though I **multiply** (ravah) my **signs** (ot)" (7:3); "acts of **judgment** (shephet)" (7:4); "the Egyptians shall **know** that I am the LORD" (7:5).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **raa (done evil) — Moses' *accusation of God* (5:22).** "why have you **done evil** (raa) to this people? why did you ever send me?" *(stated)* — raa, *be evil/do harm*: the gem is the *deliverer accusing God* — the mission has *worsened* the people's lot (ch5), and Moses' interior *lays the evil at God's door*: *"why have you done evil?"* — despair *turned to God as complaint*, not away from him (honest lament, brought *to* the LORD) *(inferred: raa = the deliverer's accusation that God has done evil)*.
- **raa (done evil) — the *charge sharpened*: Pharaoh's evil, God's non-deliverance (5:23).** *raa distinct from 5:22:* "since I came… **he has done evil** (raa) to this people, and you have not delivered at all" *(stated)* — where 5:22 charged *God* with evil, 5:23 names *Pharaoh's* evil *and* God's *failure to deliver* — the interior's despair *doubled*: the enemy worsens, the LORD *does nothing (yet)*. The deliverer at the *end of himself* *(inferred: raa = the charge that Pharaoh's evil stands and God has not acted)*.
- **berit (covenant) + natan (give) — the *covenant to give the land* (6:4).** "I established my **covenant** (berit)… to **give** (natan) the land of Canaan" *(stated)*. **berit** — the *covenant established* (with the patriarchs); **natan** — *give the land*: God answers despair by *reaching back to the covenant* — the promise *older than the crisis* *(inferred: berit/natan = the ancient covenant to give the land, invoked against despair)*.
- **shama (heard) + neaqah (groaning) + avad (slaves) + zakhar (remembered) + berit (covenant) — the *hearing and remembering* re-affirmed (6:5).** "I have **heard** (shama) the **groaning** (neaqah) of Israel whom the Egyptians hold as **slaves** (avad), and **remembered** (zakhar) my **covenant** (berit)" *(stated)*. The gem: the *same fourfold regard* as 2:24 *re-affirmed at the low point* — **shama** (heard the groaning) welded to **zakhar** (remembered the covenant). **berit** *distinct from 6:4*: 6:4 the covenant *established*, 6:5 the covenant *remembered in response to the groaning*. God's interior *hears and remembers* — the deliverance *grounded not in Israel's strength but in God's attentive memory* *(inferred: shama/neaqah/zakhar/berit = the divine hearing-and-remembering re-affirmed against despair)*.
- **shephet (judgment) + avodah (slavery) — the *sevenfold "I will"* (6:6).** "I will bring you out… deliver you from **slavery** (avodah)… redeem you with great acts of **judgment** (shephet)" *(stated)*. **shephet** — *judgment/verdict*: the redemption *comes by judgments* (on Egypt); **avodah** — the *slavery* brought-out-from. The gem is the *sevenfold "I will"* (6:6–8: bring out, deliver, redeem, take you as my people, be your God, bring in, give) — the divine interior's *massed resolve* against the deliverer's despair; redemption *by God's own arm and judgments* *(inferred: shephet/avodah = the redemption by judgment, delivering from slavery — God's massed resolve)*.
- **natan (give) — the land *given as possession* by oath (6:8).** *natan distinct from 6:4:* "I will **give** (natan) it to you for a possession" *(stated)* — where 6:4 was the *covenant* to give, 6:8 is the *sworn oath* fulfilled in giving *as possession*; the interior's hope *anchored in the oath to the fathers* *(inferred: natan = the land given as possession, the oath honoured)*.
- **shama (did not listen) + qasheh (harsh) + avodah (slavery) — Israel *too broken to hear* (6:9).** "they did not **listen** (shama) to Moses, because of broken spirit and **harsh** (qasheh) **slavery** (avodah)" *(stated)*. *shama distinct from 6:5:* the gem and *low point* — where *God heard* the groaning (6:5), *Israel cannot hear* the promise. **qasheh** — *harsh/hard* (← 1:14); **avodah** — the *slavery* that has *broken the spirit* (qotzer ruach). The crushed interior **cannot receive hope** — deliverance must come to a people *too broken to believe it*; the depth below even the blame of 5:21 *(inferred: shama/qasheh/avodah = the broken spirit that cannot hear the promise — Israel's lowest interior)*.
- **shama (listened) — the *argument from Israel's deafness* (6:12).** *shama distinct from 6:5,9:* "Israel have not **listened** (shama) to me; how then will Pharaoh?" *(stated)* — Moses *reasons from the people's deafness* to his own inadequacy ("uncircumcised lips"); the gem: the deliverer's renewed objection *grounded in Israel's not-hearing* — if his own people won't heed, how the adversary? *(inferred: shama = Israel's deafness made the ground of Moses' renewed objection)*.
- **chayyim (life ×3) — the *genealogy's lifespans* (6:16, 6:18, 6:20).** "the years of the **life** (chayyim) of Levi… Kohath… Amram" *(stated)* — chayyim in the *census/lifespan* sense (the Levite line *to Moses and Aaron*): a **thin** genealogical stretch — the *life*-word here is a *span-count*, not soul-depth (as the Genesis genealogy lifespans); it *locates the deliverers in the covenant line*, no inner-being operation *(inferred: chayyim = the genealogical lifespans, thin — locating Moses/Aaron in Levi's line)*.
- **lev (heart) + ravah (multiply) + ot (signs) — the *hardening forewarned again* (7:3).** "I will **harden** [qashah] Pharaoh's **heart** (lev), and though I **multiply** (ravah) my **signs** (ot)…" *(stated)* — the gem: the *second forewarning* (the verb is qashah, *make stubborn*; the flagged spans are **lev/ravah/ot**). **lev** — the *heart* to be hardened; **ravah** — God will *multiply* his signs *(the wonders, distinct from Israel's multiplying, 1:7)*; **ot** — the *signs*. Per the ledger, this (with 4:21) is *declared intent* — God *forewarns* both the hardening *and* that the multiplied signs will *not* soften it; the divine interior *names the coming resistance and its own multiplied response in advance* *(inferred: lev/ravah/ot = the hardening forewarned, with signs multiplied against it)*.
- **shephet (judgment) — the *judgments on Egypt* (7:4).** *shephet distinct from 6:6:* "I will lay my hand on Egypt… by great acts of **judgment** (shephet)" *(stated)* — where 6:6 the judgments *redeemed Israel*, 7:4 they *strike Egypt*; the same *shephet*, its *two faces* — salvation for Israel, judgment on Egypt *(inferred: shephet = the judgments falling on Egypt — the redemption's other face)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **the deliverer's despair (raa 5:22 → raa 5:23):** Moses *accuses God* — *"why have you done evil?… you have not delivered"* — the mission's backfire *turned into complaint to God*.
2. **"I AM the LORD" — covenant, hearing, resolve (berit/natan 6:4 → shama/neaqah/zakhar/berit 6:5 → shephet/avodah 6:6 → natan 6:8):** God answers despair by his *name and his memory* — the *covenant established and remembered*, the *groaning heard* (as 2:24), the *sevenfold "I will"* of redemption by judgment. The deliverance *grounded in God's attentive faithfulness*, not Israel's strength.
3. **the interior too broken to hear (shama 6:9 → shama 6:12):** yet *Israel cannot listen* — the *broken spirit and harsh slavery* deafen them to the promise; God *hears the groaning* while the *groaners cannot hear the promise* — and Moses *reasons from their deafness* to his own inadequacy.
4. **the recommission and the hardening foreseen (genealogy 6:14–27 → "like God to Pharaoh" 7:1 → lev/ravah/ot 7:3 → shephet 7:4 → "they shall know" 7:5):** the deliverers *located in the covenant line*, Moses *made "like God to Pharaoh"* (answering "who made you judge?", 2:14), the *hardening forewarned* (signs multiplied against it), and the *purpose stated* — *"Egypt shall know that I am the LORD"* (answering Pharaoh's "I do not know", 5:2).

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** raa ×2 (5:22 the charge *against God* / 5:23 *Pharaoh's* evil + God's *non-deliverance*); berit ×2 (6:4 covenant *established* / 6:5 covenant *remembered* at the groaning); natan ×2 (6:4 *give* the land / 6:8 *give as possession*, the oath); **shama ×3 (6:5 *God heard* the groaning / 6:9 *Israel cannot hear* — broken spirit / 6:12 Israel's *deafness* as Moses' argument)**; zakhar/neaqah (the *remembering* of the *groan*); shephet ×2 (6:6 the *redemptive* judgments / 7:4 the judgments *on Egypt*); avodah ×2 (the *slavery* brought-out-from / the *harsh* slavery that breaks the spirit); qasheh (the *harshness* that breaks); chayyim ×3 (the *genealogy lifespans* — thin, census-life); **lev/ravah/ot (the *hardening forewarned again*, signs multiplied against it)**; "that Egypt may **know**" (answering 5:2's not-knowing).
- **Whose interior (multi-interior):** **Moses** — *despair turned to accusation of God* (honest lament brought *to* him), then *renewed objection*; **God** — the *"I AM the LORD"* interior: covenant-established-and-remembered, groaning-heard, *sevenfold resolve*, redemption *by his own arm*; **Israel** — the *lowest point*: a *broken spirit too crushed to hear the promise* — deliverance coming to a people *unable to believe it*. The divine *hearing* (6:5) set poignantly against Israel's *not-hearing* (6:9).
- **Threads:** the **divine regard** re-affirmed (shama/zakhar 6:5 ← 2:24; 3:7); **"I AM the LORD"** and the name YHWH *made known* (6:2–3 → the plague-purpose "you shall know", 6:7; 7:5 answering 5:2); the **hardening trajectory** (7:3 the *second forewarning* ← 4:21; 3:19 → the executed self/God-hardening 7:13ff — see the ledger); **"like God to Pharaoh"** (7:1 answering the *rejected* self-appointment, 2:14); **Israel's faith arc** at its *nadir* (6:9 "did not listen" — below the blame of 5:21 → "believed the LORD" 14:31); the **transfer of service** (avodah 6:6 → Sinai worship); **shephet's two faces** (redemption/judgment).
- **Guarded against:** lumping the three shama (God-heard / Israel-cannot-hear / Israel's-deafness-argued), two raa (against-God / Pharaoh's-evil), two berit (established / remembered), two natan, two shephet, two avodah; padding the *thin* genealogy (6:14–27) with false inner-being operations (the chayyim are *lifespans*, no soul-depth — read the thinness as the datum, per the method); reading Moses' accusation (5:22) as apostasy when it is *lament brought to God*; treating the hardening-forewarning (7:3) as the executed deed (it is *declared intent*, per the ledger); importing later "I AM" Christology over the *covenant-name self-revelation* the passage gives; missing that God's *hearing* (6:5) and Israel's *not-hearing* (6:9) are the passage's poignant *hinge* — the regarded who cannot receive their regard.

## GLD-07-covenant-renewed-shining-face · Exodus 34:10–35 · [the covenant re-cut after the breach; idolatry as whoring; the mediator radiant] — the bond remade, the jealous God, and Moses' shining face

**The passage.** "I am **making** (karat) a **covenant** (berit); I will do **marvels** (pala)… an **awesome** (yare) thing" (34:10); **observe** (shamar) my commands (34:11); **take care** (shamar) lest you **cut** (karat) a **covenant** (berit) with the land (34:12); "the LORD, whose name is **Jealous** (qanna), is a jealous God" (34:14); lest you **cut** (karat) a **covenant** (berit), be **invited** (qara), and **whore** (zanah) after their gods (34:15); their daughters **whore** (zanah, 34:16); **keep** (shamar) the feast (34:18); the firstborn **male** (zakhar, 34:19) **redeemed** (padah, 34:20); six days **work** (avad, 34:21); "no one shall **covet** (chamad/chamudah) your land" (34:24); "in accordance with these words I have **made** (karat) a **covenant** (berit)" (34:27); the words of the **covenant** (berit, 34:28); Moses' face shone; the people were **afraid** (yare, 34:30); Moses **called** (qara, 34:31); he **removed** (sur) the veil before the LORD (34:34).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **karat (making) + berit (covenant) + pala (marvels) + yare (awesome) — the *covenant re-cut, with wonders* (34:10).** "'I am **making** (karat) a **covenant** (berit); I will do **marvels** (pala)… it is an **awesome** (yare) thing'" *(stated)*. **karat** — *cut* (the covenant-verb); **berit** — *covenant*; **pala** — *do wonders*; **yare** — *awe/fear*: the gem is *the covenant remade after the breach* — *God re-cuts the bond the calf shattered*, promising *marvels* that evoke *awe*; the interior finding: *grace remakes the covenant with a people who broke faith* — the bond *restored, not abandoned* — and its renewal is *an awesome thing* *(inferred: karat/berit/pala/yare = the covenant re-cut with wonders — grace remaking the broken bond, an awesome thing)*.
- **shamar (observe) — the *command to keep* (34:11).** "'**observe** (shamar) what I command you'" *(stated)* — shamar, *keep/obey*: the *renewed covenant's call to obedience* — the interior-context of *the bond requiring keeping* *(inferred: shamar = the command to keep the renewed covenant)*.
- **shamar (take care) + karat (cut) + berit (covenant) — the *forbidden covenant* (34:12).** *shamar/karat/berit distinct from 34:10–11:* "'**take care** (shamar) lest you **cut** (karat) a **covenant** (berit) with the land's inhabitants, lest it be a snare'" *(stated)*: the gem is the *contrast of two covenants* — where 34:10 was *God's* covenant with Israel, 34:12 forbids a *rival* covenant with the Canaanites (a *snare*); the interior of *exclusive loyalty* — *no bond that would rival the bond with God* *(inferred: shamar/karat/berit = the forbidden covenant — no rival bond, lest it snare)*.
- **qanna (Jealous) — the *jealous God* (34:14).** "'you shall worship no other god, for the LORD, whose name is **Jealous** (qanna), is a jealous God'" *(stated)* — qanna, *jealous*: the gem is *God's jealousy as his very name* — *"his name is Jealous"* — the *exclusive covenant-love that will not share* (← 20:5); the interior (of God) of *a love so committed it will not tolerate a rival* — *jealousy as the fierce face of covenant-love* *(inferred: qanna = the jealous God — the exclusive covenant-love, his very name)*.
- **karat (cut) + berit (covenant) + qara (invited) + zanah (whore) — *idolatry as whoring* (34:15).** *karat/berit distinct from 34:12:* "'lest you **cut** (karat) a **covenant** (berit)… and when they **whore** (zanah) after their gods and you are **invited** (qara), you eat of the sacrifice'" *(stated)*. **qara** — *invited/called*; **zanah** — *fornicate/whore*: the gem is *the naming of idolatry as sexual unfaithfulness* — *whoring* after other gods — the covenant is a *marriage*, and idolatry *adultery*; the interior of *the heart's unfaithfulness* — *drawn by invitation into betrayal* *(inferred: karat/berit/qara/zanah = idolatry as whoring — the covenant a marriage, idolatry adultery)*.
- **zanah (whore) — the *contagion of unfaithfulness* (34:16).** *zanah distinct from 34:15:* "'their daughters **whore** (zanah) after their gods and make your sons **whore** (zanah) after their gods'" *(stated)*: the gem is *the spreading of the unfaithfulness* — *intermarriage carries the whoring into the next generation*; the interior of *betrayal that propagates* — *the heart's adultery passed to the children* *(inferred: zanah = the contagion of unfaithfulness — betrayal propagated through the generations)*.
- **shamar (keep) + zakhar (male) + padah (redeem) + avad (work) — the *covenant terms restored* (34:18–21).** "'**keep** (shamar) the feast… all that open the womb are mine, the firstborn **male** (zakhar); the firstborn you shall **redeem** (padah)… six days you shall **work** (avad), the seventh rest'" *(stated)*. **shamar** — *keep*; **zakhar** — *male* (the homonym of *remember*); **padah** — *ransom/redeem*; **avad** — *labor*: the *covenant obligations re-given* (feasts, firstborn, sabbath — restoring ch13/23) — the genuine note among them is *padah* (the firstborn *redeemed*, ← the Passover firstborn 13:13); the interior-context of *the bond's rhythms restored* — *the redeemed firstborn, the kept feast, the rested seventh day* *(inferred: shamar/zakhar/padah/avad = the covenant terms restored — the firstborn redeemed, the feasts and sabbath re-given)*.
- **chamad (covet) + chamudah (desirable) — the *land guarded from coveting* (34:24).** "'no one shall **covet** (chamad) your land when you go up to appear before the LORD three times a year'" *(stated)*. **chamad** — *covet*; **chamudah** — *desirable thing*: the gem is *the providential guard* — while Israel *leaves the land to worship*, God *keeps their enemies from coveting it*; the interior (of the would-be covetous, restrained) — *the coveting held back* so that worship costs no vulnerability; *God guards what obedience leaves exposed* *(inferred: chamad/chamudah = the land guarded from coveting — worship made safe, the enemy's coveting restrained)*.
- **karat (made) + berit (covenant) — the *covenant written* (34:27–28).** *karat/berit distinct from 34:12,15:* "'write these words, for in accordance with them I have **made** (karat) a **covenant** (berit) with you and Israel'… he wrote the words of the **covenant** (berit), the Ten Words" *(stated)*: the gem is *the renewed covenant set in writing* — the bond *the calf broke* now *re-inscribed*; the interior-context of *the covenant remade and recorded* — *the tablets rewritten* (← the broken tablets 32:19) *(inferred: karat/berit = the covenant written — the broken bond re-inscribed)*.
- **yare (afraid) + qara (called) + sur (removed) — the *mediator radiant, the people in awe* (34:29–35).** "Moses' face shone from speaking with God; the people were **afraid** (yare) to come near (34:30); Moses **called** (qara) them (34:31)… whenever he went before the LORD he **removed** (sur) the veil" *(stated)*. **yare** — *fear/awe* (distinct from 34:10's *awesome thing*); **qara** — *call*; **sur** — *remove*: the gem is *the presence made visible on the mediator* — *proximity to God transfigures*: Moses' *face shines* (he does not know it), and the people are *afraid* (reverent awe); he *veils* it, *unveiling* only before the LORD. The interior finding: *the glory Moses beheld now radiates from him* — *the one who dwells in the presence is changed by it, and carries its light outward* — and the people meet that light with *fear/awe* *(inferred: yare/qara/sur = the mediator radiant — the presence transfigures him, the people in reverent awe)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **the covenant re-cut, the jealous God (karat/berit/pala/yare 34:10 → shamar 34:11 → shamar/karat/berit 34:12 → qanna 34:14):** *God remakes the shattered bond*, with *marvels* and *awe*; calls for *keeping*; forbids a *rival covenant*; and names himself *Jealous* — *exclusive covenant-love remaking and guarding the bond*.
2. **idolatry as whoring (karat/berit/qara/zanah 34:15 → zanah 34:16 → covenant terms 34:18–24):** the danger named — *idolatry is whoring*, the covenant a *marriage*, and unfaithfulness *propagates through intermarriage*; the *covenant obligations restored* (firstborn redeemed, feasts, sabbath), and the land *guarded from coveting* while they worship.
3. **the covenant written and the radiant mediator (karat/berit 34:27–28 → yare/qara/sur 34:29–35):** the bond *re-inscribed* (the tablets rewritten); and Moses comes down *shining* — *the presence transfigures the mediator*, the people *afraid*, the veil worn and removed — *the glory beheld now radiating outward*.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** **berit/karat (God's *covenant re-cut* with Israel 34:10,27,28 / the *forbidden* Canaanite covenant 34:12,15)**; **pala/yare (the *marvels* and the *awesome* thing of the renewal)**; **shamar ×3 (*observe* commands / *take care* against rival covenant / *keep* the feast)**; **qanna (the *jealous* God — his name is Jealous)**; **zanah ×2 (idolatry as *whoring* / the *contagion* through intermarriage)**; qara (the *invitation* to idolatry / Moses *calling* the people); zakhar/padah/avad (the covenant terms — firstborn *male* *redeemed*, the sabbath *work/rest*); **chamad/chamudah (the land *guarded from coveting* during worship)**; **yare/sur (the people *afraid* at the shining face / the veil *removed* before the LORD)**.
- **Whose interior (multi-interior):** **God** — *re-cutting the covenant* (grace after breach), *jealous* (exclusive love, his very name), *guarding* Israel's land; **Israel** — *warned against whoring* (unfaithfulness of the heart), *awed* by the shining face; **Moses** — *radiant from the presence* (transfigured, carrying the glory outward). The gems: **the covenant renewed**, **idolatry as whoring**, and **the radiant mediator**.
- **Threads:** **the covenant renewed** (karat/berit 34:10,27 ← the broken tablets 32:19; the ratification 24:8 — grace *remaking* the bond the calf shattered → the new covenant, Jer 31:31); **the jealous God** (qanna 34:14 ← 20:5; "his name is Jealous" → "I feel a divine jealousy for you", 2 Cor 11:2 — covenant-love as marriage); **idolatry as adultery** (zanah 34:15–16 → Hosea; Ezek 16; James 4:4 "you adulterous people" — the covenant a marriage, sin unfaithfulness — the dominant prophetic metaphor begins here); **coveting** (chamad 34:24 ← the tenth word 20:17 — here God *restrains* the enemy's coveting); **the shining face** (34:29–35 → "we all, with unveiled face, beholding the glory… are being transformed", 2 Cor 3:18 — the presence transfigures; Paul reads the veil as the fading glory of the old covenant); **the redeemed firstborn** (padah 34:20 ← 13:13 — the Passover redemption re-affirmed).
- **Guarded against:** collapsing the two covenants (God's renewed bond vs the forbidden Canaanite one) into one berit-motif; reading the covenant terms (34:18–26) as fresh revelation when they *re-issue* ch13/23 (surfaced, the genuine spans read, the repetition not padded); over-reading the *shining face* into the later 2 Cor typology as its *meaning* here when the text gives *the presence transfiguring the mediator and the people's awe* (the resonance flagged, the finding kept to the verses); treating *zanah* as literal fornication when the text uses it *metaphorically* for idolatry (the covenant-as-marriage, the finding); not forcing the food/feast regulations (34:25–26, no non-T2 spans) beyond scene-context; keeping zakhar "male" (34:19) as the homonym it is (the firstborn male, not "remember").

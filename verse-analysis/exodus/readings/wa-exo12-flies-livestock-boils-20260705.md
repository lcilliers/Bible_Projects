## EXO-12-flies-livestock-boils · Exodus 8:20–9:12 · [managed surrender, habitual self-hardening, and the switch to God-hardening] — the division of Goshen, the bargaining heart, and the heart the LORD confirms

**The passage.** "Let my people **serve** (avad) me" (8:20); the division of Goshen — "this **sign** (ot)" (8:23); "the land was **ruined** (shachat) by flies" (8:24); Pharaoh **called** (qara): "sacrifice within the land" (8:25); "an **abomination** (toevah) to the Egyptians" (8:26); "I will let you go… **plead** (atar) for me" (8:28); "I will **plead** (atar)… the flies **depart** (sur)… let not Pharaoh cheat" (8:29); Moses **prayed** (atar, 8:30); "he **removed** (sur) the flies" (8:31); "Pharaoh **hardened** (kaved) his **heart** (lev) this time **also**" (8:32); "let my people **serve** (avad) me" (9:1); "if you still **hold** (chazaq) them" (9:2); the livestock die, Israel's spared; "the **heart** (lev) of Pharaoh was **hardened** (kaved)" (9:7); the boils; "the magicians could not stand" (9:11); "the LORD **hardened** (chazaq) the **heart** (lev) of Pharaoh" (9:12).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **avad (serve) — the *demand* renewed (8:20).** "let my people **serve** (avad) me" *(stated)* — the *unchanging demand* (← 3:12; 7:16; 8:1) at the fourth plague *(inferred: avad = the service-demand renewed)*.
- **ot (sign) — the *division* as a sign (8:23).** "I will put a **division** between my people and your people; this **sign** (ot) shall happen" *(stated)* — ot, *sign*: the gem is that the *separation of Goshen* (no flies on God's people) is itself the *sign* — *"that you may know I am the LORD in the midst of the earth"* (8:22); the interior-context is *God distinguishing his own* amid the judgment *(inferred: ot = the division of Goshen as the knowing-sign)*.
- **shachat (ruined) — the land *un-made* by the swarm (8:24).** "the land was **ruined** (shachat) by the flies" *(stated)* — shachat, *ruin/destroy/corrupt* (← Gen 6:11–13, the earth "ruined" before the flood): the gem is the *un-creation* register — Egypt's land *undone*; the judgment *unmaking* the ordered world *(inferred: shachat = the land un-made — the un-creation of judgment)*.
- **qara (called) — the *summons under pressure* (8:25).** "Pharaoh **called** (qara): 'sacrifice within the land'" *(stated)* — qara: the gem is the *managed surrender* — Pharaoh *summons* the deliverer and offers a *partial* concession ("within the land", not the wilderness) — the interior *bargaining to keep control* *(inferred: qara = the summons offering a managed, partial surrender)*.
- **toevah (abomination) — the *clash of the sacred* (8:26).** "our sacrifice is an **abomination** (toevah) to the Egyptians" *(stated)* — toevah, *abomination/what is abhorred*: Moses' reason — what Israel offers God, Egypt *abhors*; the gem is the *irreconcilable difference* of what each people holds sacred/abhorrent — the worship *cannot* be done "within the land" *(inferred: toevah = the clash of the sacred — Israel's worship an abomination to Egypt)*.
- **atar (plead) — Pharaoh's *repeated request* (8:28).** "I will let you go… only not far; **plead** (atar) for me" *(stated)* — atar *(← 8:8)*: the bargaining *continues* — a *nearer* concession ("not very far") with the *same* plea for intercession; the interior *negotiating the terms of surrender* *(inferred: atar = the repeated request to plead, terms negotiated)*.
- **atar (plead) + sur (depart) — Moses' *agreement with a warning* (8:29).** *atar distinct from 8:28:* "I will **plead** (atar)… the flies **depart** (sur)… only let not Pharaoh **cheat** again" *(stated)*. **atar** — Moses *agrees to intercede*; **sur** — the flies to *depart*; the gem is the *named pattern* — Moses *warns against the cheating* (Pharaoh's habit of promising then reneging) — the intercessor *sees the deceit coming* *(inferred: atar/sur = the intercession agreed, with the deceit-pattern named)*.
- **atar (prayed) — the intercession *enacted* (8:30).** *atar distinct from 8:28,29:* "Moses went out and **prayed** (atar) to the LORD" *(stated)* — the *doing* of the plea (the request 8:28, the agreement 8:29, now the *act*) — the interior of the *faithful mediator* *(inferred: atar = the intercession actually prayed)*.
- **sur (removed) — the flies *removed*, relief granted (8:31).** *sur distinct from 8:29:* "he **removed** (sur) the flies; not one remained" *(stated)* — the *complete relief* granted on the prayer — the mercy *thorough* ("not one remained") *(inferred: sur = the flies wholly removed — the relief complete)*.
- **lev (heart) + kaved (hardened) — the *habitual self-hardening* (8:32).** "Pharaoh **hardened** (kaved) his **heart** (lev) this time **also**" *(stated)* — the gem (per the ledger): *self*-hardening again (as 8:15) — but now *"this time **also**"* names the ***habit*** — the pattern *established*; **kaved** — *made heavy*. After the *complete* relief (8:31), he *again* hardens — the *relief-exploited* mechanism *repeating*, now *habitual* *(inferred: lev/kaved = the self-hardening become habit — "this time also")*.
- **avad (serve) — the *demand* renewed at the fifth plague (9:1).** *avad distinct from 8:20:* "let my people **serve** (avad) me" *(stated)* — the *same demand*, the fifth time — the *unwearied* call against the *habitual* refusal *(inferred: avad = the service-demand renewed at the livestock plague)*.
- **chazaq (hold) — the *grip that will not release* (9:2).** "if you refuse and still **hold** (chazaq) them" *(stated)* — the gem: **chazaq** in its *hold/grip* sense — the *same root* as the hardening (7:13; 9:12) — here *Pharaoh's holding onto Israel*; the interior's *grip* on its slaves *is* the hardening *enacted as refusal to let go* *(inferred: chazaq = the grip that holds Israel — the hardening as refusal-to-release)*.
- **lev (heart) + kaved (hardened) — the *stative hardening* after the distinction (9:7).** *lev/kaved distinct from 8:32:* "Pharaoh sent, and behold, not one of Israel's livestock was dead; but the **heart** (lev) of Pharaoh was **hardened** (kaved)" *(stated)* — the gem: Pharaoh *checks and confirms* the distinction (Israel's cattle spared) — *evidence in hand* — and *still* the heart *is hardened* (stative). The interior *hard against verified evidence* — he *investigates* and *ignores* *(inferred: lev/kaved = the heart hard even after verifying the distinction — evidence investigated and ignored)*.
- **lev (heart) + chazaq (hardened) — **the switch**: the *first GOD-hardening* (9:12).** *lev/chazaq distinct from 8:32, 9:7:* "the magicians could not stand [9:11]… but the LORD **hardened** (chazaq) the **heart** (lev) of Pharaoh" *(stated)* — the **central gem** (per the ledger): the ***first*** time *God* is the explicit hardening subject — *"the LORD hardened."* And *when*: **after** repeated *self*-hardening (8:15; 8:32 "also"; 9:7) **and** the *total collapse* of the counterfeit (9:11, the magicians *cannot even stand*, struck by the boils). The switch is *not arbitrary*: the God-hardening *confirms a disposition Pharaoh fixed himself*, at the point where *all counter-evidence is spent* (even his experts felled). Judicial *sealing* of a settled "no" *(inferred: lev/chazaq = the switch — God confirms the self-fixed heart, after self-hardening and the counterfeit's collapse)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **the division and the managed surrender (avad 8:20 → ot 8:23 → shachat 8:24 → qara 8:25 → toevah 8:26):** God *sets his people apart* (the knowing-sign), the land is *ruined*, and Pharaoh *bargains a partial surrender* ("within the land") — met by the *irreconcilable clash of the sacred*.
2. **the plea, the relief, the habitual hardening (atar 8:28 → atar/sur 8:29 → atar 8:30 → sur 8:31 → lev/kaved 8:32):** Pharaoh *negotiates terms and begs intercession*; Moses *prays* (warning against the cheat); the flies *wholly removed* — and *at the relief* Pharaoh *hardens himself again, "this time also"* — the *habit* named.
3. **the grip, the verified distinction ignored (avad 9:1 → chazaq 9:2 → 9:6 → lev/kaved 9:7):** the demand renewed against Pharaoh's *grip* on Israel; the livestock die, Israel's spared — Pharaoh *checks and confirms it* — and *still* his heart *is hard*: evidence *investigated and ignored*.
4. **the counterfeit felled, the switch (9:11 magicians cannot stand → lev/chazaq 9:12):** the boils *strike the magicians themselves* — the counterfeit *cannot even stand* — and *now* **the LORD hardens** Pharaoh's heart: the *first divine hardening*, *confirming* the self-fixed refusal at the point where *all counter-evidence is exhausted*. The self-hardened heart *sealed*.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** avad ×2 (the demand *renewed* — flies / livestock); ot (the *division of Goshen* as knowing-sign); shachat (the land *un-made* — un-creation register, ← Gen 6:11); qara (the *managed, partial* surrender summoned); toevah (the *clash of the sacred*); atar ×3 (Pharaoh's *request* / Moses' *agreement-with-warning* / the *praying*); sur ×2 (the flies *depart* / *wholly removed*); **lev/kaved ×2 (8:32 the *habitual* self-hardening "this time also" / 9:7 the *stative* hardening after verifying the distinction — evidence investigated and ignored)**; chazaq (9:2 the *grip* holding Israel — hardening as refusal-to-release); **lev/chazaq (9:12 THE SWITCH — the first GOD-hardening, confirming the self-fixed heart after the counterfeit's collapse)**.
- **Whose interior (multi-interior):** the **adversary** (Pharaoh) — *managed surrender* (bargaining the terms), *habitual self-hardening* ("this time also"), *evidence investigated and ignored* (9:7), then *God's judicial sealing* (9:12); the **magicians** — the counterfeit's *total collapse* (cannot stand, struck themselves); **Israel** — *set apart and spared* (the division, the knowing-sign). The switch to God-agency lands *precisely* when self-hardening is settled and all counter-evidence spent.
- **Threads:** the **hardening trajectory's SWITCH** (8:32 self "also" → 9:7 stative → **9:12 first God-hardening** — the ledger's central finding: God-hardening *follows* and *confirms* settled self-hardening; not arbitrary, and *timed* to the counterfeit's collapse); the **relief-as-trigger** mechanism repeating (8:31→8:32 ← 8:14→8:15); **chazaq** one root, two faces (grip 9:2 / harden 9:12); the **counterfeit** arc *ending* (9:11 "could not stand" ← 8:18–19 "finger of God" ← 7:11,22); the **division/knowing** (ot 8:22–23 → 9:4; 11:7 — God distinguishing his own; "that you may know", ← 5:2); **shachat** (8:24 ← Gen 6:11 → the destroyer 12:23); the **transfer of service** (avad).
- **Guarded against:** lumping the three lev / two kaved (habitual-self / stative-after-evidence / God-switch — the *distinction is the trajectory*); the three atar or two sur; reading 9:12's God-hardening as *arbitrary divine cruelty* when the text places it *after* repeated self-hardening (8:15,32; 9:7) *and* the counterfeit's collapse (9:11) — *judicial confirmation*, per the ledger; missing that 9:7 shows Pharaoh *verifying* the evidence (he *sent* to check) and *still* hardening (evidence is not the lack); over-reading toevah as mere cultural difference when it marks the *irreconcilable* sacred; treating chazaq (9:2 "hold") as unrelated to the hardening when it is the *same grip* (refusal-to-release *is* the hardening enacted).

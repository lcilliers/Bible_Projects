## EXO-07-return-believe · Exodus 4:18–31 · [the return, the hardening foretold, and Israel's faith] — Moses goes back; God's firstborn claim; the people believe and worship

**The passage.** Jethro: "go in **peace** (shalom)"; Moses to see if his people are still **alive** (chay, 4:18); "those **seeking** (baqash) your **life** (nephesh) are dead" (4:19); "I will **harden** (chazaq) his **heart** (lev)" (4:21); "Israel is my firstborn… let my son **serve** (avad) me, or I will kill your firstborn" (4:23); the LORD "**sought** (baqash) to put him to death" (4:24); Zipporah's flint; "so he **let him alone** (raphah)" (4:26); Aaron went to **meet** (qara) Moses (4:27); Moses told the **signs** (ot, 4:28); Aaron did the **signs** (ot) before the people (4:30); "the people **believed** (aman); when they **heard** (shama) that the LORD had seen their **affliction** (oni), they bowed and worshiped" (4:31).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **shalom (peace) + chay (alive) — the *return blessed*, the concern for his people (4:18).** "Jethro: 'go in **peace** (shalom)'… to see whether they are still **alive** (chay)" *(stated)*. **shalom** — Jethro's *peaceful sending* (as Laban blessed Jacob's departure, Gen 31:55); **chay** — *alive/living*: Moses' *concern for whether his people yet live* — the deliverer's interior *turning back toward the afflicted*. (The chay-lexeme's kin/community range is not in play here — the sense is *living*.) *(inferred: shalom/chay = the peaceful sending and the concern for his people's survival)*.
- **baqash (seeking) + nephesh (life) — the *enemies' pursuit ended* (4:19).** "all the men who were **seeking** (baqash) your **life** (nephesh) are dead" *(stated)*. **baqash** — *seek*; **nephesh** — *life*: the gem is the *reassurance* — the murderous *seeking* that drove the exile (2:15) is *over*; the interior *freed of the old threat* to return *(inferred: baqash/nephesh = the enemies' life-seeking, now ended)*.
- **chazaq (harden) + lev (heart) — the *hardening foretold* (4:21).** "I will **harden** (chazaq) his **heart** (lev), so that he will not let the people go" *(stated)* — the gem: the ***first "I will harden"*** — God *declares his intent* before a single plague. **chazaq** — *strengthen/make firm*; **lev** — *heart*: per the hardening-ledger, this is *forewarning* (paired with the *foreknowledge* of 3:19 "I know he will not let you go") — God *announces* the coming resistance and his part in it *at the outset*; the executed self-hardening (7:13ff) and God-hardening (9:12ff) unfold *within* this declared frame. The divine interior *names the adversary's fixed refusal in advance* *(inferred: chazaq/lev = the hardening forewarned — divine intent declared before the plagues)*.
- **avad (serve) — the *firstborn claim* and the transfer of service (4:23).** "Israel is my firstborn son… let my son **serve** (avad) me… or I will kill your firstborn" *(stated)* — avad, *serve/minister*: the gem is the *doubled logic* — Israel is God's *firstborn*, claimed *to serve him* (← 3:12, the transfer of service), and the *firstborn-for-firstborn* threat (God's son freed / Pharaoh's son struck) *foretells the Passover*. The interior's *belonging* to God grounds the demand: *my son must serve me* *(inferred: avad = the firstborn's service claimed — the exodus's ground and the Passover foretold)*.
- **baqash (sought) — God's *own seeking* of Moses' life (4:24).** *baqash distinct from 4:19:* "the LORD met him and **sought** (baqash) to put him to death" *(stated)* — the gem is the *jarring reversal*: the *same verb* — where 4:19 the *enemies'* seeking of Moses' life *ended*, here *God himself* seeks it (over the unkept sign of circumcision on his son). **baqash** — the divine *severity* toward the deliverer's own *household negligence*; holiness *will not overlook* the covenant sign even in the one it sends. The interior *confronted by a God who requires the sign* *(inferred: baqash = God's severe seeking of Moses' life over the neglected covenant sign)*.
- **raphah (let him alone) — the *release* after the blood (4:26).** "so he **let him alone** (raphah)" — Zipporah having circumcised the son *(stated)* — raphah, *slacken/let go/drop*: the gem is *what turns the severity aside* — Zipporah's *decisive act* (a woman again saving Moses, ← the mother/sister/princess, EXO-03); the divine seeking *slackens* once the sign is *applied in blood*. The threat *released* by the covenant blood (foreshadowing the Passover) *(inferred: raphah = the divine severity slackened once the covenant-blood is applied)*.
- **qara (meet) — the *reunion* God foretold (4:27).** "Aaron went to **meet** (qara) Moses… and kissed him" *(stated)* — qara, *encounter/meet*: the meeting *promised* (4:14, "he is coming to meet you") *happens* — the interior's *provision realised*; the brothers *reunited for the mission* *(inferred: qara = the foretold meeting of the brothers)*.
- **ot (signs) — the signs *recounted* (4:28).** "Moses told Aaron… all the **signs** (ot) he had commanded" *(stated)* — ot, *sign*: the *telling* of the signs (Moses to Aaron) — the interior *transmitting* the mission's evidence *(inferred: ot = the signs recounted to Aaron)*.
- **ot (signs) — the signs *performed* (4:30).** *ot distinct from 4:28:* "Aaron… did the **signs** (ot) in the sight of the people" *(stated)* — where 4:28 was the *telling*, 4:30 is the *doing* before Israel — the evidence *enacted* for the people's faith *(inferred: ot = the signs performed before the people)*.
- **aman (believed) + shama (heard) + oni (affliction) — Israel's *faith and worship* (4:31).** "the people **believed** (aman); when they **heard** (shama) that the LORD had seen their **affliction** (oni), they bowed and worshiped" *(stated)*. The gem is the *arc's first peak*: **aman** — the *belief Moses doubted* (4:1) *actually comes*; **shama** — they *hear* that *God has seen their affliction* (oni, ← 3:7); and they *bow and worship*. Israel's interior *moves to faith and worship* — not at a promise of ease but at the news that *God has seen*. The regarded interior *responds in worship* *(inferred: aman/shama/oni = Israel's faith and worship at the news that God has seen their affliction)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **the return, threat ended (shalom/chay 4:18 → baqash/nephesh 4:19):** Moses is *sent in peace*, *concerned for his living people*, and *freed of the old murderous pursuit*.
2. **the hardening foretold and the firstborn claimed (chazaq/lev 4:21 → avad 4:23):** God *declares he will harden* Pharaoh (the trajectory's forewarning) and *claims Israel as his firstborn son to serve him* — the exodus's ground and the Passover foreshadowed.
3. **the severe night and the saving blood (baqash 4:24 → raphah 4:26):** God's *own severity* threatens Moses over the *neglected sign*; *Zipporah's blood-act* turns it aside — holiness satisfied by the *covenant blood* (Passover foreshadowed).
4. **reunion, signs, and Israel's faith (qara 4:27 → ot 4:28 → ot 4:30 → aman/shama/oni 4:31):** the brothers *reunite*, the signs are *told and done*, and *Israel believes and worships* at the news that *God has seen their affliction* — the faith-question (4:1) answered at its *first peak* (before the crash of ch5).

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** shalom/chay (the *peaceful sending* and *concern for the living*); baqash ×2 *distinguished* (4:19 the *enemies'* life-seeking *ended* / 4:24 *God's own* severe seeking over the sign); **chazaq/lev (the *hardening foretold* — the first "I will harden", divine intent declared before the plagues)**; **avad (the *firstborn's service claimed* — transfer of service + Passover foreshadowed)**; raphah (the severity *slackened* by covenant-blood); qara (the *foretold reunion*); ot ×2 (the signs *recounted* / *performed*); **aman/shama/oni (Israel's *faith and worship* — the arc's first peak, at "God has seen")**.
- **Whose interior (multi-interior):** **God** — *foretelling the hardening*, *claiming his firstborn*, and *severe* even toward the deliverer's household (holiness requiring the sign); **Moses** — *returning obedient* yet *negligent* of the sign; **Zipporah** — the *decisive woman* who saves Moses with covenant-blood; **Israel** — the *faith and worship* that peaks here (believe / hear / bow), the interior *responding to being seen*.
- **Threads:** the **hardening trajectory** opens (chazaq 4:21 ← foreknowledge 3:19 → executed self-hardening 7:13ff, God-hardening 9:12ff — see the ledger; 4:21 is the *declared intent*, not yet the deed); the **transfer of service** (avad 4:23 ← 3:12; slavery 1:13 → Sinai worship); the **firstborn-for-firstborn** (4:23 → the Passover, EXO-16/17); **covenant-blood turns aside death** (raphah 4:26 ← the sign → the Passover blood 12:13); the **women who save Moses** (Zipporah ← the mother/sister/princess EXO-03); **Israel's faith arc** (aman 4:31 the *peak* → the *crash* ch5 "you have made us stink" → murmuring at the sea 14:11 → "believed the LORD" 14:31); "God has *seen*" (oni 4:31 ← 3:7).
- **Guarded against:** reading the hardening-forewarning (4:21) as the *executed* hardening (the ledger distinguishes *declared intent* here from the *self*-hardening that begins the cycle and the *God*-hardening that seals it — do not collapse); softening the *shock* of 4:24 (God *seeking* Moses' life) into a mere illness — the verse states the divine *severity* over the sign; merging the two baqash (enemies' ended / God's begun); importing a full Passover-typology over what the passage *foreshadows*; over-claiming Israel's faith as *settled* when it is a *peak* the very next chapter will *test and break* (5:21).

# Exodus 2:23–25 + 3:1–22 — inner-being passage reading (EXO-05-god-hears-burning-bush)

- **Source:** verse-first lexicals over the passage (provenance `exodus-deliverance-v1-20260705`). Every non-T2 span in its verse; *stated* | *inferred*; distinctions kept. **Multiple interiors**: **Israel** (whose cry rises), **God** (the divine interior disclosed — hears/remembers/sees/knows), and **Moses** (reverent fear and self-doubt). The name **I AM** (3:14–15, no non-T2 span) reads as the scene's centre-context, anchored by the divine seeing/knowing/calling.

## EXO-05-god-hears-burning-bush · Exodus 2:23–25 + 3:1–22 · [the divine regard that initiates deliverance; the holy call; I AM] — God hears the groaning and comes down; Moses turns aside, fears, and is sent

**The passage.** "Israel **groaned** (anach) because of their **slavery** (avodah) and **cried out** (zaaq)" (2:23); "God **heard** (shama) their **groaning** (neaqah) and **remembered** (zakhar) his **covenant** (berit)" (2:24); "God saw… and God **knew** (yada)" (2:25); Moses "will **turn aside** (sur) to see" (3:3); "when the LORD saw he **turned aside** (sur), God **called** (qara): 'Moses, Moses!'" (3:4); "the place is **holy** (qodesh) ground" (3:5); Moses "was **afraid** (yare) to look at God" (3:6); "I have surely seen the **affliction** (oni)… **heard** (shama) their cry because of their **taskmasters** (nagas); I **know** (yada) their **sufferings** (makhov)" (3:7); "a **good** (tov) and broad land" (3:8); "you shall **serve** (avad) God on this mountain… the **sign** (ot)" (3:12); "I will bring you up out of the **affliction** (oni)" (3:17); "they will **listen** (shama) to your voice" (3:18); "I **know** (yada) the king will not let you go" (3:19); "all the **wonders** (pala)" (3:20); "**favor** (chen)… I will **give** (natan)" (3:21); "each woman shall **ask** (shaal)… you shall plunder Egypt" (3:22).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **anach (groaned) + zaaq (cried out) + avodah (slavery) — Israel's *bondage finally voiced* (2:23).** "Israel **groaned** (anach) because of their **slavery** (avodah) and **cried out** (zaaq); their cry came up to God" *(stated)*. **anach** — *sigh/groan* (wordless); **zaaq** — *cry out* (the appeal for rescue); **avodah** — the *slavery* (← EXO-01). The gem: after the *silent* bitter service of ch1, Israel's interior *finds voice* — first a *groan*, then a *cry* — and *"it came up to God"*. The suffering that was *mute* now *ascends*; the interior's *inarticulate anguish* is the deliverance's first cause *(inferred: anach/zaaq = the bondage voiced — groan and cry rising to God)*.
- **shama (heard) + zakhar (remembered) + berit (covenant) + neaqah (groaning) — the *fourfold divine regard* (2:24).** "God **heard** (shama) their **groaning** (neaqah) and **remembered** (zakhar) his **covenant** (berit)" *(stated)*. **shama** — God *hears* the present cry; **neaqah** — the *groan* heard; **zakhar** — God *remembers* the *ancient covenant* (berit, ← Gen 15; 17); the gem: the deliverance *begins in God's interior* — *hearing the now* welded to *remembering the then*; present cry + ancient promise *together* move him (as "God remembered Noah", Gen 8:1; "remembered Abraham", Gen 19:29). The divine interior *attends and recalls* *(inferred: shama/zakhar/berit = the divine hearing of the cry welded to remembering the covenant)*.
- **yada (knew) — the *bare divine knowing* (2:25).** "God saw the people of Israel, and God **knew** (yada)" *(stated)* — the gem is the *unqualified* verb: *"and God knew"* — full stop; **yada** here is *regard/acknowledgement* — God *takes knowledge of* his people, *no longer as if hidden*; the interior of *God turning his knowing toward the afflicted* *(inferred: yada = the bare divine knowing — God takes notice)*.
- **sur (turn aside) — Moses' *attentive turning* (3:3).** "I will **turn aside** (sur) to see this great sight" *(stated)* — sur, *turn aside*: the gem is *attentiveness* — Moses *turns from his ordinary path* toward the strange fire; the interior's *curiosity/openness* that *interrupts routine* — the threshold of encounter *(inferred: sur = the turning-aside of an attentive interior)*.
- **sur (turned aside) + qara (called) — the turning *seen*, the *personal call* (3:4).** *sur distinct from 3:3:* "when the LORD saw that he **turned aside** (sur) to see, God **called** (qara): 'Moses, Moses!'" *(stated)*. **sur** — now *God sees* the turning (the turning-aside is the *condition* God waits for); **qara** — the *personal, doubled call* by name, met with *"Here I am"* (hineni). The gem: the interior's *attentiveness is met by a call that names it* — encounter is *mutual*, the turning answered by the naming *(inferred: sur/qara = the attentive turning met by the personal call)*.
- **qodesh (holy) — the *holiness* that demands reverence (3:5).** "take your sandals off… the place is **holy** (qodesh) ground" *(stated)* — qodesh, *holiness*: the gem is the *first "holy"* in Exodus — the ground *made holy by presence*; the interior *summoned to reverence* (bare feet, no nearer) — holiness *as the atmosphere of the encounter* *(inferred: qodesh = the holiness that demands reverent distance)*.
- **yare (afraid) — Moses' *holy dread* (3:6).** "Moses hid his face, for he was **afraid** (yare) to look at God" *(stated)* — yare, *fear/reverence*: the gem is the *right* fear — not the *self-preserving* fear of 2:14, but *holy dread* before God (← the midwives 1:17; Jacob at Bethel, Gen 28:17); the interior *veiling itself* before the divine *(inferred: yare = the holy dread that hides its face)*.
- **oni (affliction) + shama (heard) + nagas (taskmasters) + makhov (sufferings) + yada (know) — the *divine empathy* stated (3:7).** "I have surely seen the **affliction** (oni)… **heard** (shama) their cry because of their **taskmasters** (nagas); I **know** (yada) their **sufferings** (makhov)" *(stated)*. The gem is the *intensified* divine regard: **oni** — the *affliction* seen; **shama** *distinct from 2:24* — now *heard "because of their taskmasters"* (the cause named); **nagas** — the *oppressors/drivers*; **makhov** — their *pains*; **yada** *distinct from 2:25* — now *"I know their sufferings"* (not bare notice but *intimate knowledge of the pain*). The divine interior *sees, hears, and knows the pain from within* — empathy, not distance *(inferred: oni/shama/nagas/makhov/yada = the intensified divine empathy — God knows the pain intimately)*.
- **tov (good) — the *goodness of the promised land* (3:8).** "to bring them up… to a **good** (tov) and broad land, flowing with milk and honey" *(stated)* — tov, *good*: the gem is the *destination's goodness* — deliverance is *toward* a good, broad place (over against the *narrow* bondage); the interior's hope given a *good* end *(inferred: tov = the goodness of the land toward which deliverance moves)*.
- **avad (serve) + ot (sign) — the *goal of the exodus: worship* (3:12).** "I will be with you; the **sign** (ot): when you have brought them out, you shall **serve** (avad) God on this mountain" *(stated)*. The gem: **avad** — *serve/minister/worship* — the *same word* as Israel's *slavery to Pharaoh* (1:13,14) — but now the *goal*: they will *serve God*. The exodus is a **transfer of service** — from Pharaoh to God; **ot** — the *sign* is the *future worship itself*. The interior's destiny is *not merely freedom but service re-directed to God* *(inferred: avad/ot = the goal of the exodus — service transferred to God, the sign being the worship to come)*.
- **oni (affliction) — the affliction *deliverance-from* (3:17).** *oni distinct from 3:7:* "I will bring you up out of the **affliction** (oni) of Egypt" *(stated)* — where 3:7 was the affliction *seen*, 3:17 is the affliction *God will bring them up from* — the same misery, now the *thing left behind*; the interior's affliction *named as the past* in the promise *(inferred: oni = the affliction deliverance leaves behind)*.
- **shama (listen) — Israel's *future heeding* foretold (3:18).** *shama distinct from 2:24, 3:7:* "they will **listen** (shama) to your voice" *(stated)* — shama in the *heed/obey* mode: God *promises the people will listen* — the interior's *coming receptivity* (which Moses will doubt, 4:1; and which will falter, 6:9) *(inferred: shama = the people's promised heeding of the deliverer)*.
- **yada (know) — the *foreknowledge of the hardening* (3:19).** *yada distinct from 2:25, 3:7:* "I **know** (yada) the king of Egypt will not let you go unless compelled by a mighty hand" *(stated)* — the gem: God's **foreknowledge of Pharaoh's refusal** — *from the outset* the resistance is *foreseen*; the hardening trajectory is *not a surprise* but *known* and *factored* (→ see the hardening-ledger: the divine hardening at 9:12ff *confirms* a refusal God *knew* from 3:19). The divine interior *knows the adversary's fixed "no"* before the first plague *(inferred: yada = God's foreknowledge of Pharaoh's refusal — the hardening foreseen)*.
- **pala (wonders) — the *wonders* that will compel (3:20).** "I will strike Egypt with all the **wonders** (pala) that I will do" *(stated)* — pala, *be wonderful/extraordinary* (← Gen 18:14 "too hard for the LORD?"): the *plagues* named as *wonders* — the divine interior's *power* set against the foreseen refusal *(inferred: pala = the wonders that will overcome the refusal)*.
- **chen (favor) + natan (give) — the *favor* that turns Egypt's heart (3:21).** "I will **give** (natan) this people **favor** (chen) in the sight of the Egyptians; you shall not go empty" *(stated)*. **chen** — *favor/grace*; **natan** — *give*: the gem is that God will *give Israel favor* even with the oppressor's people — the interior of *Egypt* itself *turned* toward the departing slaves (→ 11:3; 12:36) — grace *disposing hearts* *(inferred: chen/natan = the God-given favor that turns Egypt toward Israel)*.
- **shaal (ask) — the *plunder* by asking (3:22).** "each woman shall **ask** (shaal) her neighbour for silver and gold… you shall plunder Egypt" *(stated)* — shaal, *ask/request*: the gem is that the *plunder* comes *by asking* (not seizing) — the favor (3:21) making the request *granted*; the interior *receives the spoil of its bondage* as *given*, not grabbed (→ 12:35–36) *(inferred: shaal = the asking by which the plunder is received)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **the cry rises and the divine interior turns (anach/zaaq/avodah 2:23 → shama/zakhar/berit/neaqah 2:24 → yada 2:25):** Israel's *silent bondage finds voice*; God *hears the cry*, *remembers the covenant*, *sees*, and *knows* — the deliverance *begins in God's fourfold regard* (present cry + ancient promise).
2. **the attentive turning met by the holy call (sur 3:3 → sur/qara 3:4 → qodesh 3:5 → yare 3:6):** Moses *turns aside*; God, *seeing the turning*, *calls him by name*; the ground is *holy*, and Moses *hides his face in holy dread* — encounter as *mutual attentiveness* wrapped in reverence.
3. **the divine empathy and the commission (oni/shama/nagas/makhov/yada 3:7 → tov 3:8 → avad/ot 3:12):** God *sees, hears, and intimately knows the pain*, purposes a *good land*, and names the *goal — that Israel may serve (worship) him*: the exodus a **transfer of service** from Pharaoh to God.
4. **the foreseen refusal and the compelling wonders (oni 3:17 → shama 3:18 → yada 3:19 → pala 3:20 → chen/natan 3:21 → shaal 3:22):** God *promises the people will heed*, *foreknows Pharaoh's refusal* (the hardening not a surprise), will *strike with wonders*, and will *give Israel favor* so the plunder comes *by asking* — the whole arc *foreknown* in the divine interior.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** anach/zaaq (Israel's *bondage voiced* — groan then cry); **shama ×3 (2:24 heard the *groaning* / 3:7 heard the *cry, cause named* / 3:18 the people's *future heeding*)**; zakhar/berit (the *covenant remembered* — present cry welded to ancient promise); **yada ×3 (2:25 the *bare divine notice* / 3:7 *intimate knowledge of the pain* / 3:19 *foreknowledge of Pharaoh's refusal*)**; sur ×2 (3:3 Moses' *attentive turning* / 3:4 the turning *seen* as the call's condition); qara (the *personal doubled call*); qodesh (the *holiness* demanding reverence); yare (Moses' *holy dread* — vs the self-preserving fear 2:14); nagas/makhov/oni (the *oppression, pain, affliction* God knows); tov (the *good land*); **avad/ot (the *goal — service transferred to God*, the sign the worship to come)**; oni (3:17, the affliction *left behind*); pala (the *wonders*); chen/natan/shaal (the *favor* and the *plunder by asking*).
- **Whose interior (multi-interior):** **Israel** — the *bondage finally voiced*, the cry rising, the *promised heeding* (and its coming faltering); the **divine interior** *disclosed* — *hearing, remembering, seeing, knowing intimately, purposing, foreknowing* — compassion welded to covenant-faithfulness and foreknowledge; **Moses** — *attentive turning*, *holy dread*, and (in the objections "who am I?" 3:11, "what is your name?" 3:13) the *self-doubt of the emptied deliverer*.
- **Threads:** the **transfer of service** (avad 3:12 ← slavery 1:13,14 → "let my people go that they may serve me" 7:16; 8:1; the worship at Sinai); the *divine regard initiating* (shama/zakhar/yada 2:24–25; 3:7 ← "God remembered", Gen 8:1; 19:29; 30:22); the **hardening foreknown** (yada 3:19 → the ledger's God-hardening at 9:12ff *confirms* a refusal foreseen here); the *holy dread* (yare 3:6 ← 1:17; Gen 28:17 → 14:31 Israel fears the LORD); the *plunder* (chen/natan/shaal 3:21–22 → 11:3; 12:35–36); the *good land* (tov 3:8, 17 → the wilderness journey toward it).
- **Guarded against:** lumping the three shama or three yada (heard-groaning / heard-cry / people-heed; bare-notice / intimate-pain-knowledge / foreknowledge-of-refusal — each distinct); reading God's *foreknowledge* (3:19) as *causing* Pharaoh's refusal (the verse states God *knows* the refusal, not yet that he *hardens* it — the hardening's agency is traced in the ledger, and self-hardening precedes the God-hardening); importing later Trinitarian/Christological readings of I AM over the *self-existent, present, covenant God* the passage discloses; flattening avad (3:12) into generic "worship" when it *deliberately echoes* the slavery-word (the transfer of service is the finding); over-reading the plunder (shaal 3:22) as theft when the verse makes it *asking* granted by *God-given favor*.

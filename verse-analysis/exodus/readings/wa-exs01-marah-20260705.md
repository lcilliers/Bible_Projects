## EXS-01-marah · Exodus 15:22–27 · [the song fades to grumbling; the healer tests] — the bitter water, the statute, and "I am the LORD your healer"

**The passage.** Three days, no water; at Marah the water was **bitter** (mar) (15:23); the people grumbled; the wood made it sweet; the LORD made a **statute** (mishpat) and tested them (15:25); "if you diligently **give ear** (azan)… do what is **right** (yashar)… **keep** (shamar) his commandments… I am the LORD your **healer**" (15:26); then Elim, twelve springs (15:27).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **mar (bitter) — the *bitterness* that meets the freed people (15:23).** "they could not drink, for it was **bitter** (mar)… named Marah" *(stated)* — mar, *bitter* (← marar, 1:14 "made their lives **bitter**"; the maror, 12:8): the gem is the *echo* — freed from a *bitter bondage*, Israel meets *bitter water* — and *grumbles* (where in bondage they *groaned to God*, 2:23, now in freedom they *grumble against Moses*). The interior's *bitterness resurfacing* — the old taste met, three days past the song *(inferred: mar = the bitterness that meets the freed people — grumbling, not groaning)*.
- **mishpat (statute) — the *first law and the test* (15:25).** "the LORD made for them a **statute** (mishpat) and a rule, and there he tested them" *(stated)* — mishpat, *statute/rule*: the gem is that the *wilderness becomes a school* — God *sets a statute* and *tests* the freed people's *trust and obedience* (the *first* law, before Sinai); the interior *put to proof* — will freedom *obey*? *(inferred: mishpat = the statute that tests the freed people's trust)*.
- **azan (give ear) + yashar (right) + shamar (keep) — the *obedience-condition and the healer* (15:26).** "if you diligently **give ear** (azan)… do what is **right** (yashar)… **keep** (shamar) his commandments… I am the LORD your **healer**" *(stated)*. **azan** — *give ear/listen*; **yashar** — *do right*; **shamar** — *keep*: the gem is the *self-revelation* — *"I am the LORD your healer"* (rophe) — the God who *sweetened the bitter water* names himself the *healer*; obedience *(listening, doing right, keeping)* linked to *health/wholeness*. The interior *called to listen and be healed* — the bitterness *God's to heal* *(inferred: azan/yashar/shamar = the obedience-condition, and God the healer of bitterness)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **the bitterness and the grumbling (mar 15:23):** three days past the *song*, Israel meets *bitter water* and *grumbles* — the old *bitterness* of bondage met again, but now with *complaint* rather than the *groan-to-God* of the bondage; the freed interior *cannot yet hold its faith past the first thirst*.
2. **the statute and the test (mishpat 15:25):** God *sweetens the bitter* and *sets a statute*, *testing* the freed people — the wilderness *a school of trust*.
3. **the healer (azan/yashar/shamar 15:26 → Elim 15:27):** the obedience-condition — *listen, do right, keep* — and the *self-revelation*: *"I am the LORD your healer"*; then *Elim's springs* (rest after the test). The interior *called to listen and be healed*.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** **mar (the *bitterness* that meets the freed people — ← the bitter bondage 1:14; grumbling not groaning)**; mishpat (the *first statute*, the *test* of trust — the wilderness a school); **azan/yashar/shamar (the *obedience-condition*, and *"I am the LORD your healer"* — the God who heals bitterness)**.
- **Whose interior (multi-interior):** **Israel** — the *song fading to grumbling* at the first hardship (faith not yet holding past the thirst); **God** — the *healer* who sweetens bitterness and *tests* the freed people's obedience; **Moses** — the *mediator's cry* (15:25). The wilderness *tests* the interior the sea's faith could not settle.
- **Threads:** **Israel's testing arc continues** (the grumbling 15:24 ← the murmuring at the sea 14:11; the nostalgia for slavery — the *song fades fast*, 15:1 → 15:24; the wilderness-school → the manna 16, Massah 17); the **bitterness** motif (mar 15:23 ← marar 1:14; maror 12:8 — bondage's bitterness met again in freedom); the **fear-of-God/obedience** (azan/shamar 15:26 → Sinai's "if you obey my voice" 19:5); **God the healer** (15:26 — the self-revelation, the bitter made sweet); the *test* (mishpat 15:25 → the manna-test 16:4; Massah 17:7).
- **Guarded against:** over-reading the log/wood into typology over the *bitter-made-sweet* the verse gives; reading the grumbling as mere ingratitude when it is the *interior unable to hold faith past the first hardship* (the honest wilderness-failure, the finding); importing later "law before Sinai" debates over the *first statute/test* the text states; missing the *mar ← marar* echo (freedom meets the old bitterness); treating "I am your healer" as merely physical when it names God the *healer of bitterness* (water and people).

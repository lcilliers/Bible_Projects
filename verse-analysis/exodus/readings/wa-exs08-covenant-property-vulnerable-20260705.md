## EXS-08-covenant-property-vulnerable · Exodus 22:1–31 · [the law reveals God's heart for the powerless] — "if he cries to me, I will hear, for I am compassionate"

**The passage.** Safekeeping (shamar) of entrusted **goods** (natan, 22:7); every **breach of trust** (pesha) — the one God **condemns** (rasha) pays double (22:9); another **keeping** (shamar) of an entrusted beast (natan, 22:10); a man **borrows** (shaal, 22:14); the seducer must **give** (natan) the bride-price (22:17); "you shall not **afflict** (anah) a widow or orphan; if you **afflict** (anah) them and they cry, I will hear; my wrath will **burn** (charah)" (22:22–24); the cloak **taken** (chaval) in pledge returned by nightfall — "if he cries, I will hear, for I am **compassionate** (channun)" (22:26–27); do not **curse** (arar) God or ruler (22:28); the firstborn **given** (natan, 22:29, 22:30); you shall be **consecrated** (qodesh), not eating torn **flesh** (basar, 22:31).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **shamar (keep safe) + natan (gives) — the *trust to be guarded* (22:7).** "'if a man **gives** (natan) his neighbor money or goods to **keep safe** (shamar), and it is stolen, the thief pays double'" *(stated)*. **natan** — *give/entrust*; **shamar** — *guard*: the gem is *the ethic of entrusted keeping* — what is *given in trust must be guarded*; the interior-context of *faithfulness with another's property* *(inferred: natan/shamar = the trust given, to be guarded)*.
- **pesha (breach of trust) + rasha (condemns) — the *transgression judged before God* (22:9).** "'for every **breach of trust** (pesha)… the case comes before God; the one God **condemns** (rasha) pays double'" *(stated)*. **pesha** — *transgression/rebellion*; **rasha** — *be wicked/condemn*: the gem is that *a breach of trust is a transgression* — not a mere civil matter but brought *before God*, who *condemns the wicked*; the interior of *faithlessness named as guilt before God* *(inferred: pesha/rasha = the breach of trust as transgression, condemned before God)*.
- **shamar (keep safe) + natan (gives) — the *entrusted beast* (22:10).** *shamar/natan distinct from 22:7:* "'if a man **gives** (natan) his neighbor a beast to **keep safe** (shamar), and it dies unseen'" *(stated)* — where 22:7 was *goods*, here a *living animal* entrusted — the *oath* settling the honest keeper's innocence; the interior-context of *trust tested where there is no witness — the oath before God the guard of honesty* *(inferred: natan/shamar = the entrusted beast — honesty under oath where none saw)*.
- **shaal (borrows) — the *borrowed thing* (22:14).** "'if a man **borrows** (shaal) anything and it is injured, he shall make restitution'" *(stated)* — shaal, *ask/borrow*: the *responsibility of the borrower* — the interior-context of *care owed for what is borrowed* *(inferred: shaal = the borrowed thing, its care owed)*.
- **natan (give) — the *bride-price for the seduced* (22:17).** *natan distinct from 22:7,10:* "'if her father refuses to **give** (natan) her, he shall pay the bride-price'" *(stated)* — here natan is *the giving in marriage* (or its price) — the *seducer bound to make right* — the interior-context of *the wronged woman's protection* *(inferred: natan = the bride-price — the seducer bound to make right)*.
- **anah (afflict) + anah (afflict) — the *widow and orphan not to be afflicted* (22:22–23).** "'you shall not **afflict** (anah) any widow or orphan; if you **afflict** (anah) them and they cry, I will surely hear'" *(stated)*. **anah** (twice) — *afflict/oppress* (the very word of *Israel's own affliction* in Egypt, 1:11–12): the gem is *the ethical memory turned to law* — *do not afflict* the powerless *as you were afflicted*; and the *conditional threat* — *if you do, and they cry, God hears*. The interior of *the powerless whose cry reaches God* — the affliction *God will not ignore* *(inferred: anah = the widow/orphan not to be afflicted — the cry God hears, the ethical memory)*.
- **charah (wrath burns) — the *divine wrath for the afflicted* (22:24).** "'my wrath will **burn** (charah), and I will kill you with the sword; your wives shall become widows'" *(stated)* — charah, *burn/be incensed*: the gem is that *God's anger burns on behalf of the powerless* — the *oppressor made to suffer what he inflicted* (his wife a widow, his children fatherless); the divine interior of *wrath as the defense of the defenseless* — *the measure returned* *(inferred: charah = the divine wrath for the afflicted — the oppressor's measure returned)*.
- **chaval (take in pledge) + channun (compassionate) — the *pledge returned, God's compassion revealed* (22:26–27).** "'if you **take** (chaval) your neighbor's cloak in pledge, return it before sunset… if he cries to me, I will hear, for I am **compassionate** (channun)'" *(stated)*. **chaval** — *take in pledge* (the *be in labour* gloss the homonym); **channun** — *gracious/compassionate*: the gem is *THE self-revelation* — the law *reveals God's own heart*: he *hears the poor man's cry* (as he heard Israel's, 2:24) *because he is COMPASSIONATE*; the divine interior *named* — *gracious, hearing, tender toward the destitute*. Even a *pledged cloak* matters to him because *he is compassionate* *(inferred: chaval/channun = the pledge returned, God's compassion revealed — "for I am compassionate")*.
- **arar (curse) — the *reverence owed to God and ruler* (22:28).** "'you shall not revile God, nor **curse** (arar) a ruler'" *(stated)* — arar, *curse*: the *reverence owed* to God and to appointed authority — the interior-context of *the tongue restrained from cursing what God has set* *(inferred: arar = the reverence owed — the curse withheld from God and ruler)*.
- **natan (give) — the *firstborn given* (22:29).** *natan distinct from 22:7,10,17:* "'the firstborn of your sons you shall **give** (natan) to me'" *(stated)* — here natan is *consecration-giving* — the *firstborn belonging to God*; the interior of *the first and best surrendered to God* *(inferred: natan = the firstborn given — the first surrendered to God)*.
- **natan (give) — the *eighth-day offering* (22:30).** *natan distinct from 22:29:* "'seven days it shall be with its mother; on the eighth day you shall **give** (natan) it to me'" *(stated)* — the *firstborn animal given* after the tender interval with its mother — the interior-context of *the offering timed with a mercy* (the days with the mother) *(inferred: natan = the eighth-day offering — given after the days with its mother)*.
- **qodesh (consecrated) + basar (flesh) — the *consecrated people* (22:31).** "'you shall be **consecrated** (qodesh) to me; you shall not eat torn **flesh** (basar)'" *(stated)*. **qodesh** — *holiness*; **basar** — *flesh*: the gem is the *identity that grounds the ethic* — *you shall be holy*, therefore *set apart even in what you eat*; the interior of *consecration shaping the whole life* *(inferred: qodesh/basar = the consecrated people — holiness shaping even the table)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **faithfulness in trust (natan/shamar 22:7 → pesha/rasha 22:9 → natan/shamar 22:10 → shaal 22:14 → natan 22:17):** what is *entrusted must be guarded*; a *breach of trust is a transgression* condemned *before God*; honesty *under oath where none saw*; the *borrower's* care; the *seducer bound to make right* — the interior of *faithfulness with what is another's*.
2. **God's heart for the powerless (anah/anah 22:22–23 → charah 22:24 → chaval/channun 22:26–27):** *do not afflict* the widow, orphan, poor (as *you were afflicted*) — for *their cry reaches God*, whose *wrath burns* in their defense and who *hears because he is COMPASSIONATE*; the law *reveals the divine interior* — *tender toward the destitute*.
3. **the consecrated life (arar 22:28 → natan 22:29 → natan 22:30 → qodesh/basar 22:31):** *reverence* to God and ruler; the *firstborn given* (the first surrendered); the offering *timed with mercy*; and the ground of it all — *you shall be holy* — consecration *shaping the whole life*.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** **natan ×5 (goods *entrusted* / beast *entrusted* / the *bride-price* / the *firstborn* given / the *eighth-day* offering)**; **shamar ×2 (the *goods* guarded / the *beast* kept under oath)**; pesha/rasha (the *breach of trust* as transgression, *condemned* before God); shaal (the *borrowed* thing); **anah ×2 (the widow/orphan not to be *afflicted* — the cry God hears, the ethical memory)**; **charah (the *divine wrath* for the afflicted — the oppressor's measure returned)**; **chaval/channun (the pledge returned, *God's compassion revealed* — "for I am compassionate")**; arar (the *reverence* owed — the curse withheld); qodesh/basar (the *consecrated people* — holiness shaping the table).
- **Whose interior (multi-interior):** **God** — *COMPASSIONATE* (channun), *hearing the cry* of the powerless, his *wrath burning* (charah) in their defense — the divine heart *revealed in the law*; **Israel** — the *ethical memory* ("you were afflicted", so do not afflict), *faithfulness* in trust, *consecration*; **the vulnerable** — the *cry* that reaches God. The gem: the law *legislates God's own inner being* — his compassion for the destitute.
- **Threads:** **the cry heard** (anah/channun 22:23,27 ← Israel's cry in Egypt, 2:23–25 "God heard their groaning"; the *same anah* of the oppression 1:11 — *do not do to others what was done to you*; God *hears* the afflicted as he heard you → "he has regard for the prayer of the destitute", Ps 102:17); **God's self-revelation as compassionate** (channun 22:27 → "the LORD, the LORD, compassionate and gracious", 34:6 — the first sounding of the great self-naming); **the ethical memory** (22:21 "you were sojourners" → "you know the heart of a sojourner", 23:9 — the *remembered affliction* the ground of mercy); **wrath for the powerless** (charah 22:24 — the divine anger *protective*, not arbitrary); **consecration** (qodesh 22:31 ← 19:6 "a holy nation" — the identity grounding the ethic); the *firstborn given* (natan 22:29 ← the Passover firstborn spared, 13:2 — the redeemed firstborn now consecrated).
- **Guarded against:** lumping the five natan (goods / beast / bride-price / firstborn / eighth-day), two shamar (goods / beast), two anah (the single *do-not-afflict*, but each occurrence its own clause — the command and the consequence); reading the vulnerable-laws as mere civil policy over the *divine heart* they reveal (God's compassion, his hearing, his wrath — the finding); missing the *anah ← Egypt* echo (the ethical memory — the finding); mistaking the 'o "desire" and chay/chaval homonym glosses for inner-being motifs; over-reading the firstborn-giving into later sacrificial theology over the *first-surrendered-to-God* the verse gives; flattening "for I am compassionate" into a legal formula when it is a *self-revelation* of God's interior — the reason the whole ethic stands.

Prophetic book processed by **oracle-passage, not chapter** — oracles cross chapter lines (Disputation 2 spans 1:6–2:9). Cited; grounded; *stated* | *inferred*.
- **Lens:** the inner-being **operation** the oracle stages; the covenant dispute is the arena. Malachi's form is the **disputation** — God's claim → the people's objection ("But you say…") → God's answer — so each unit's spine is an **exposed inner posture**.

## MAL-disp1 · Mal 1:2–5 · [D, multi] — God's elective love met by a doubting heart

**The reading.** The book opens on the interior directly: "'**I have loved you**,' says the LORD. **But you say, 'How have you loved us?'**" (v.2). The very first thing exposed is a **heart that cannot feel the love it is told it has received** — God asserts love, the people answer with a question that is really a complaint *(stated)*. God's answer is election: "Is not Esau Jacob's brother? Yet I have **loved Jacob** but **Esau I have hated**" (vv.2–3). The finding is **love asserted against a doubting interior, grounded not in the people's experience but in God's choosing** *(inferred: the disputation form makes the *doubt itself* the datum — "how have you loved us?" is the interior of a people whose circumstances (post-exilic disappointment) have eclipsed their sense of being loved; God does not argue from their feelings but relocates the ground of love to his own election (Jacob/Esau); the inner-being operation is the gap between *being loved* and *feeling loved*, and the reorientation of assurance from experience to God's prior choice)*.

**Lexical validation (read-back).** *loved* (H0157, aheb — the divine love, correctly `characteristic`), *hated* (H8130, sane — "Esau I have hated"), *Great* (H1431 — "great is the LORD"), *wicked* (H7563), *angry* (H2194) carried. The aheb/sane pair (love/hate as election language) is the reading's spine; correctly tagged (covenant-election idiom, not emotional caprice). Sound.

**Hidden / less-obvious characteristics isolated.** **The gap between being loved and feeling it** — "I have loved you / How have you loved us?" (v.2): the finding that the interior can be genuinely loved and yet *not perceive it*, its doubt shaped by disappointing circumstances; God's remedy is not proof-by-experience but a summons to rest assurance on his election. A distinct inner-being movement (assurance re-grounded outside the self's feelings).

**Characteristics surfaced:** *the doubting heart that cannot feel the love it is given* (new — the disputation's exposed interior); *love/assurance re-grounded in God's election, not experience* (new); *divine love (aheb)* (shared vocab, here covenantal).

## MAL-disp2 · Mal 1:6–2:9 · [D, multi] — honour and fear withheld from God; the corrupted priestly covenant

**The reading.** The longest oracle (crossing the chapter line) stages the priests' interior. God's claim: "**a son honours his father**, and a servant his master. If then I am a father, **where is my honour**? And if I am a master, **where is my fear**? says the LORD… to you, O priests, who **despise my name**. **But you say, 'How have we despised your name?'**" (1:6). The answer: by polluted, blind, lame offerings (1:7–8) — giving God what they would not dare give the governor (1:8). The finding is **reverence — honour and fear — owed to God and withheld**; contempt shown not in words but in the *carelessness of the offering* *(stated)*. Against the priests stands the ideal, the **covenant with Levi**: "my covenant with him was one of **life and peace**… a covenant of **fear**, and he **feared me**. He **stood in awe of my name**. **True instruction was in his mouth**, and no wrong on his lips… and he **turned many from iniquity**" (2:5–6). The operation is **the priestly interior that should embody reverent, truthful covenant-keeping, now hollowed into contempt** *(inferred: the disputation's genius is that the priests do not *know* they despise God — "how have we despised your name?"; contempt has become invisible to them, lodged in routine (the blemished offering that "will do"); the Levi-ideal (2:5–7) names what the interior owes — fear, awe, true instruction, a mouth that guards knowledge — the reverence that has drained out; the finding is reverence *decayed into unconscious contempt*, the most dangerous kind because the self cannot see it (cf. the heart set on evil, Ecc 8:11, but here in the religious professional))*.

**Lexical validation (read-back).** *honor* (H3519, twice — the honour owed), *fear* (H4172/H3374, morah/yare — "where is my fear", "he feared me"), *despise* (H0959, bazah — the priests despising the name, repeated), *covenant* (H1285, berith — the Levi covenant), *life* (H2416)/*peace* (H7965)/*awe* (H2865, "stood in awe"), *True* (H0571, emeth — "true instruction"), *iniquity* (H5771 — "turned many from iniquity") carried. The honour/fear/despise cluster is the reading's spine; the Levi-covenant terms (berith, life, peace, fear, emeth) carry the ideal; correctly tagged. Sound.

**Hidden / less-obvious characteristics isolated.** (1) **Contempt that has become invisible to itself** — "how have we despised your name?" (1:6): the finding that irreverence can be so routinised the self no longer perceives it — the blemished offering feels normal; a distinct and sobering inner-being state (unconscious contempt). (2) **Reverence as the whole priestly interior** — the Levi covenant (2:5–7) defines it as *fear + awe + true instruction + a knowledge-guarding mouth + turning others from iniquity*: reverence is not a feeling but an integrated life; its decay is measured against this fullness. (3) **The measure of contempt: what you would not give the governor** (1:8) — God exposes the interior by comparison; the self reveres in proportion to whom it fears, and giving God the reject shows where he actually ranks.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *reverence (honour + fear) owed and withheld* (new — the oracle's spine; shared cornerstone w/ the fear-of-God thread); *contempt invisible to itself (routinised irreverence)* (new — a sobering gem); *the priestly interior as integrated reverence (the Levi ideal: fear/awe/true instruction)* (new); *the self's true valuation exposed by what it dares offer* (new).

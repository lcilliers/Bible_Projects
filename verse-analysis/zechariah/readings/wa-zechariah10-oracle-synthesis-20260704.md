## ZECH-shepherdless-gathered · Zechariah 10:1–12 · [D, multi] — the shepherdless wandering; God's compassion gathering the scattered

**The reading.** The chapter diagnoses a people misled by false comforts: "**the household gods utter nonsense, and the diviners see lies… they give empty consolation. Therefore the people wander like sheep; they are afflicted for lack of a shepherd**" (v.2). Against the failed shepherds, God claims the flock: "**the LORD of hosts cares for his flock**" (v.3). And the promises of restoration pile up: "**I will strengthen the house of Judah… I will bring them back because I have compassion on them, and they shall be as though I had not rejected them… I will whistle for them and gather them in, for I have redeemed them**" (vv.6,8). The finding is **the *shepherdless* interior *wandering* and drawing "empty consolation" from lies — met by a God who *cares*, *has compassion*, and gathers the scattered "**as though I had not rejected them**"** *(stated)*. The operation is **the plight of the un-shepherded self — directionless ("wander like sheep"), afflicted, and *falsely consoled* by "household gods" and "diviners" that "give empty consolation" — answered by God's own shepherding compassion, whose restoration is so complete it *erases the very memory of rejection* ("as though I had not rejected them")** *(inferred: v.2 names a double affliction of the interior — *lostness* ("wander like sheep… for lack of a shepherd") and *false comfort* ("the diviners… give **empty consolation**"): the finding is that the un-guided self not only wanders but reaches for *counterfeit consolations* (the household gods, the diviners' lies) that *cannot truly console* — "empty consolation" that leaves the ache unmet (the same futile reach as Isa 19:3, turning to mediums when the true guide is absent); and the answer is God's *shepherding compassion* — "**the LORD cares for his flock**" (v.3), "**I have COMPASSION on them**" (v.6); the most striking phrase is "**they shall be as though I had not rejected them**" (v.6): the finding is that God's restoring compassion is so complete it *un-writes the rejection* — not merely forgiving the estrangement but restoring the relationship to a state as if the breach had never happened; the wandering, falsely-consoled interior is gathered ("I will whistle for them and gather them in", v.8) and its history of rejection erased by compassion)*.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *the shepherdless wandering ("the people wander like sheep… for lack of a shepherd" — the un-guided self directionless and afflicted)* (thread — a gem: the lostness of the un-shepherded interior, w/ 10:2, Num 27:17, Matt 9:36, Ezek 34); *false consolation ("the diviners give empty consolation" — the un-guided self reaching for counterfeit comforts that cannot console)* (new — a gem: counterfeit consolations that leave the ache unmet, w/ 10:2, Isa 19:3, Jer 2:13); *God's shepherding compassion ("the LORD cares for his flock… I have compassion on them")* (thread — the caring Shepherd against the failed ones, w/ 10:3,6, Ezek 34:11-16); *restoration that un-writes rejection ("as though I had not rejected them" — compassion so complete it erases the memory of the breach)* (new — a gem: the relationship restored as if the estrangement never happened, w/ 10:6, Isa 54:7-8, Rom 8:1).

## ZECH-truth-joy-restored · Zechariah 8:1–23 · [D, multi] — the restored community of truth and peace; fasts turned to joy; God-with-us drawing the nations

**The reading.** God pledges return and flourishing: "**I have returned to Zion and will dwell in the midst of Jerusalem**" (v.3); "**Old men and old women shall again sit in the streets… And the streets shall be full of boys and girls playing**" (vv.4–5) — the image of a healed community. The ethical core: "**Speak the truth to one another; render… judgments that are true and make for peace; do not devise evil in your hearts against one another, and love no false oath, for all these things I hate**" (vv.16–17). The mourning-fasts are transformed: "**The fast[s]… shall be to the house of Judah seasons of joy and gladness and cheerful feasts. Therefore love truth and peace**" (v.19). And the nations are drawn: "**Ten men… shall take hold of the robe of a Jew, saying, 'Let us go with you, for we have heard that God is with you'**" (v.23). The finding is **a *restored interior community* marked by *truth, peace, and mercy* ("speak the truth… love no false oath… love truth and peace") — its *fasts turned to joy* — and a *God-with-us* presence that draws the nations** *(stated)*. The operation is **the picture of a healed common life: the interior virtues God "loves" (truth, peace) and "hates" (false oaths, evil devised in the heart) shape a community safe for the old and the young; grief itself is *converted to joy* (the fasts become "cheerful feasts") when God returns; and the presence of God among his people becomes *magnetic* — the nations sensing "God is with you" and wanting in** *(inferred: the chapter is the positive counterpart to ch.7 — where ch.7 exposed self-serving religion and hardened hearts, ch.8 paints the *restored* community: (1) its ethical texture is *truth and peace* — "**speak the truth… make for peace… do not devise evil in your hearts… love no false oath**" (vv.16–17), the very things "I hate" being their opposites: the finding is that the healed interior life is one of *honesty and peaceableness*, extending even to the heart's private intentions ("in your hearts"); (2) *grief is transformed* — the mourning-fasts (of ch.7) "**shall be… seasons of JOY and gladness**" (v.19): the finding is that when God returns to the midst, the interior's very seasons of sorrow are *converted* to joy — not merely relieved but *turned into* their opposite; and the condition/consequence is "**love truth and peace**"; (3) the presence of God becomes *attractive* — "**God is with you**" draws "ten men from every tongue" to "take hold of the robe of a Jew" (v.23): the finding is that a community in which God genuinely dwells is *magnetic* — its evident God-with-us-ness draws outsiders who want to share it; the healed interior life, marked by truth/peace/joy, is itself a witness)*.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *the healed community of truth and peace ("speak the truth… do not devise evil in your hearts… love truth and peace" — honesty and peaceableness reaching the heart's private intentions)* (thread — a gem: the restored interior life as honest and peaceable, w/ 8:16-17,19, 7:9-10, Eph 4:25); *grief converted to joy ("the fasts… shall be seasons of joy" — sorrow not merely relieved but turned into its opposite when God returns)* (thread — a gem: the interior's mourning transformed to gladness, w/ 8:19, Isa 61:3, Ps 30:11, John 16:20); *God's presence made magnetic ("God is with you" drawing the nations — a community God dwells in draws outsiders)* (new — a gem: the evident God-with-us-ness as witness, w/ 8:23, 2:11, John 13:35); *what God loves and hates (truth and peace loved; false oaths and evil-in-the-heart hated)* (thread — w/ 8:17,19, Pro 6:16-19).

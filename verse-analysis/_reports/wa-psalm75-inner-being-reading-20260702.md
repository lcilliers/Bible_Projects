## The reading

**I. Self-exaltation warned against (vv.4–5).**
"I say to the **boastful** (H1984, M16, *halal* — in its boasting sense), 'Do not **boast**,' and to the **wicked** (H7563, M10), 'Do not **lift up your horn**; do not lift up your horn on high, or speak with **haughty neck**'" (vv.4–5). The finding: the wicked inner being's characteristic posture is **self-elevation** — *raising its own horn* (its power/status) and the *stiff, haughty neck* *(stated)*. The operation: the inner being **exalting itself** — lifting its own horn, arrogant-necked — is the disposition named and forbidden *(inferred: "lift up *your* horn… haughty neck" is the self raising its own standing)*.

**II. "Lifting up" is God's, not the self's (vv.6–7, 10).**
"For **not** from the east or from the west and **not** from the wilderness comes **lifting up** (H7311, *rum*), **but it is God** who executes judgment, **putting down one and lifting up another**… the horns of the wicked I will **cut off**, but the horns of the righteous shall be **lifted up**" (vv.6–7, 10). The gem-finding: **exaltation cannot be self-generated** — "lifting up" comes from **no direction and no self, but from God alone** *(stated)*. The operation: the inner being's self-raising is **futile** — the horn it lifts for itself will be **cut off**, while the horn it does *not* raise (the righteous', *lifted up* passively, *by God*) is the one that rises *(inferred: the self-raised horn "cut off" vs the righteous' horn "lifted up" [by God] contrasts self-exaltation with God-given exaltation — only the latter stands)*. The finding: **the self cannot elevate itself; elevation is God's to give or withhold** *(inferred)*.

## What Psalm 75 says about the inner being
- **Self-exaltation is the wicked's characteristic posture (vv.4–5).** Lifting its own horn, haughty-necked — the inner being raising its own standing.
- **Exaltation cannot be *self-generated* (vv.6–7, 10).** "Lifting up" comes from God alone, not from the self or any direction; the self-raised horn is cut off, while the righteous' horn — *not* self-raised — is lifted up by God. The inner being cannot elevate itself; elevation is God's prerogative.

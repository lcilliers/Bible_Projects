## The reading

**I. "No God" as a heart-posture that corrupts conduct (v.1).**
"The **fool** (H5036, *nabal*) says **in his heart** (H3820, the seat — M47), 'There is no God.' They are **corrupt** (H7843, M27, *shachath*), doing **abominable** (H8581, M06) iniquity; there is none who does **good**" (v.1). The finding: the denial is **not an intellectual conclusion but a *heart-disposition*** — "says *in his heart*" — a practical **living-as-if-no-God** *(stated)*. The operation: this inner posture **corrupts the conduct** that flows from it — the "no God" in the heart issues directly in corrupt, abominable action *(inferred: the "says in his heart… they are corrupt" chains the inner denial to the outward corruption)*. The inner conviction is the **root**; the corrupt life is its fruit *(inferred)*.

**II. The divine scan for understanding and seeking (v.2).**
"God **looks down** from heaven on the children of man to see if there are any who **understand** (H7919, M15, *sakal*), who **seek** (H1875, M41, *darash*) after God" (v.2). The operation: God searches mankind specifically for **two inner faculties directed toward himself — *understanding* and *seeking*** *(stated)*. The finding: what God scans for is not deeds first but the **inner operations** — a mind that *understands* and a will that *seeks* God; these are the marks he looks for *(inferred: the object of the divine looking is "any who understand, who seek after God" — inner faculties, not acts)*. And the verdict is **universal turning-aside**: "they have **all fallen away**… none who does good, not even one" (v.3) — the scan finds the understanding/seeking absent across the board *(stated)*.

**III. The terror where there is no terror (v.5).**
"There they are, in **great terror**, where there is **no terror**!" (v.5, distinct from Ps 14). The finding: the "no God" heart, having lived as if unaccountable, is seized by a **groundless dread** — terror *where there is no terror* *(stated)*. The operation: the practical-atheist inner being, cut off from God, is subject to a **fear that has no external cause** — the interior instability of the self that denied God, dread arising from within rather than from a real threat *(inferred: "in great terror where there is no terror" locates the dread in the self, not the situation)*.

## What Psalm 53 says about the inner being
- **Practical atheism is a *heart-posture*, not a conclusion (v.1).** The "no God" is said *in the heart* — a disposition of living-as-if-no-God — and it is the root from which corrupt conduct flows.
- **God scans for the inner faculties of understanding and seeking (v.2).** What he looks for is a mind that *understands* and a will that *seeks* him — inner operations, not deeds first — and finds them universally absent.
- **The God-denying heart is seized by groundless terror (v.5).** Having lived as if unaccountable, it dreads "where there is no terror" — an interior instability, fear arising from the self rather than from a real threat.

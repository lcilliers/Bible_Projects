- **Scope honesty up front:** Psalm 135 is a Hallelujah hymn — praise, a recital of God's works (creation, Exodus, conquest), and an idol-polemic (the arena). Its inner-being findings are two, both *re-attestations*: praise experienced as **pleasant** (v.3), and the master-datum that **you become what you trust** (v.18, = 115:8).

## The reading

**I. Praise experienced as *pleasant* (v.3).**
"Praise the LORD, for the LORD is **good** (H2896, M04); sing to his name, for it is **pleasant** (H5273)!" (v.3). The finding: praise is commended as *pleasant* — an act the self finds *sweet* *(stated)*. The operation: the inner being experiences worship **not as burden but as *pleasure*** — "sing… for it is pleasant" makes praise agreeable to the self, something enjoyed *(inferred: "sing to his name, for it is pleasant" locates a *sweetness* in the act of praise itself — worship as delight; cf. Ps 147:1 "it is pleasant, and a song of praise is fitting", 133:1)*.

**II. *You become what you trust* — re-attested (v.18).**
"Their idols are silver and gold… **Those who make them become like them; so do all who *trust* (H0982, M19) in them**" (vv.15–18). The gem-finding (re-attesting 115:8): the worshipper is **conformed to the object of his trust** — dead idols *deaden* their trusters *(stated)*. The operation: the same *law of formation* as Ps 115:8 — the inner being is *shaped into the likeness* of whatever it trusts; here re-stated verbatim, confirming the datum across two psalms *(inferred: the identical "those who make them become like them; so do all who trust in them" re-attests the formation-law — what you trust *makes* you; recorded as re-attestation, not fresh discovery; see the fuller treatment at Ps 115:8)*.

## What Psalm 135 says about the inner being
- **Praise is experienced as *pleasant* (v.3).** Worship is not burden but pleasure — "sing… for it is pleasant"; the self finds a sweetness in the act of praise itself.
- **You are *conformed to what you trust* (v.18, re-attesting 115:8).** The law of formation restated verbatim — dead idols deaden their trusters; what you trust *makes* you. (Fuller treatment at Ps 115:8.)

## The reading

**I. "Let me hear what God will speak" — the listening posture (v.8).**
"**Let me hear** (H8085, *shama*) what God the LORD will **speak**, for he will speak **peace** (H7965, M33, *shalom*) to his people, to his saints; **but let them not turn back to folly** (H3690, M19, *kesel*)" (v.8). The finding: the inner being takes up a deliberate posture of **attentive listening** — *waiting to hear* what God will say — and it **expects peace** *(stated)*. The operation: the self **quiets and attends for God's word** — the receptive listening-stance (the exact opposite of Ps 81's people who "would not listen" and were given over to their stubborn hearts) *(inferred: "let me hear what God will speak" is the self poising itself to receive, expecting shalom)*. And the caution completes it: the listening must not **relapse into *folly*** ("let them not turn back") — the receptive posture is guarded against sliding back *(stated)*.

## What Psalm 85 says about the inner being
- **The inner being can take up a *listening posture*, poised for God's word (v.8).** "Let me hear what God will speak" — the self quiets and attends, expecting *peace*; the receptive stance that is the opposite of the stubborn, non-listening heart (Ps 81). And it is *guarded* — the listening must not relapse into folly.

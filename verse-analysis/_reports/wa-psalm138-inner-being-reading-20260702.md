## The reading

**I. Whole-hearted thanks (vv.1–2).**
"I **give you thanks**, O LORD, with my **whole heart** (H3820, M47); before the gods I sing your praise; I bow down toward your holy temple and give thanks… for your **steadfast love** and your **faithfulness**" (vv.1–2). The finding: the thanks is *whole-hearted*, the entire self engaged *(stated)*. The operation: the inner being praises **undividedly**, its thanks grounded in God's steadfast love and faithfulness *(inferred: "with my whole heart" as at 9:1, 111:1 — the gathered, entire self in thanks; recorded as re-attestation)*.

**II. Answered prayer that *strengthens the soul within* (v.3).**
"On the day I **called**, you **answered** me; **my strength of soul** (H5315, M47) **you increased**" (v.3). The gem-finding: God's answer to the cry did not only change circumstances — it **increased *strength in the soul*** *(stated)*. The operation: the inner being is **fortified *within* by God's response** — the answer works *inward*, emboldening and strengthening the *soul itself*, not merely resolving the outward trouble; prayer answered *builds up the inner self* *(inferred: "on the day I called, you answered… my strength of soul you increased" locates the fruit of answered prayer *inside* — the self is made *stronger, bolder within* by being heard; God's response is inner fortification, not only external rescue; cf. Ps 27:14 "let your heart take courage", Isa 40:29–31, Eph 3:16 "strengthened with power… in the inner being")*.

**III. God regards the lowly (v.6).**
"Though the LORD is **high**, he **regards the lowly**, but the **haughty** (M08) he knows from afar" (v.6). The finding: the high God *draws near the lowly* and holds the proud at a distance *(stated)*. The operation (inner-being-adjacent): the self's ***lowliness* is the posture God draws near to** — nearness to God correlates with humility, distance with pride; the inner condition determines the proximity *(inferred: "the high… regards the lowly, but the haughty he knows from afar" makes the *inner posture* (lowly vs haughty) the thing that opens or closes the distance to God; humility as the condition of nearness, cf. Ps 34:18, 51:17, Isa 57:15)*.

**IV. Settled confidence that God completes his purpose (v.8).**
"The LORD will **fulfill his purpose for me**; your steadfast love, O LORD, **endures forever**. Do not forsake the work of your hands" (v.8). The finding: the self rests in the assurance that God will *complete* what concerns it — while still pleading "do not forsake" *(stated)*. The operation: the inner being holds a **settled confidence in God's *completing purpose*** — trusting that God will *finish* his work in the self, yet honestly voicing the fear of abandonment alongside the trust *(inferred: "the LORD will fulfill his purpose for me… do not forsake the work of your hands" holds assurance and appeal together — confidence in God's perseverance with the self, coexisting with the honest plea; cf. Phil 1:6 "he who began a good work… will bring it to completion", Ps 57:2)*.

## What Psalm 138 says about the inner being
- **Answered prayer *strengthens the soul within* (v.3).** God's response works *inward* — "my strength of soul you increased" — fortifying and emboldening the inner self, not merely resolving the outward trouble; being heard *builds up* the self.
- **Lowliness is the posture God *draws near to* (v.6).** The inner condition — lowly vs haughty — opens or closes the distance to God; humility is the condition of nearness.
- **The self rests in God's *completing purpose* (v.8).** A settled confidence that God will *finish* his work in the self — held honestly alongside the plea "do not forsake."
- **(Re-attested) Thanks is *whole-hearted* (v.1).** The entire self gathered into praise.

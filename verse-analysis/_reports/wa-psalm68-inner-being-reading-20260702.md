## The reading

**I. God settles the *solitary* in a home (vv.5–6).**
"**Father of the fatherless** and protector of widows is God… God **settles the solitary** (*yachid* — the alone/isolated) in a **home** (H1004); he leads out the **prisoners** to prosperity" (vv.5–6). The gem-finding: the inner being's **isolation** — the *solitary*, the *fatherless*, the *widow* — is answered by being **set in a home**, given belonging *(stated)*. The operation: God **places the lonely self into a family** — the isolated inner being (the *yachid* of Ps 25:16, the parent-forsaken of Ps 27:10) is not left alone but **homed** *(inferred: "settles the solitary in a home" makes belonging the answer to isolation)*. And the *prisoner* is led out to freedom — the bound self released *(stated)*.

**II. God who *daily bears the self up* (v.19).**
"Blessed be the Lord, who **daily bears us up** (*amas* — loads onto himself, carries); God is our **salvation**" (v.19). The gem-finding: God **carries the self *daily*** — not a single rescue but a **continual bearing-up** *(stated)*. The operation: the inner being is **borne** by God **day by day** — *amas*, the word for loading a burden onto oneself: God takes the self's weight onto himself, continually *(inferred: "daily bears us up" makes the carrying ongoing, God shouldering the self's load each day)*.

**III. The threefold exultant gladness (v.3).**
"But the righteous shall be **glad** (H8055, M04); they shall **exult** (H5970) before God; they shall be **jubilant** with **joy** (H8057, M04)!" (v.3). The finding: the inner being's joy before God is **superlative and layered** — *glad, exult, jubilant with joy*, three joy-words stacked *(stated)*. The operation: joy here is **not single but compounded** — the inner being's gladness intensifies through several registers at once *(inferred: the three joy-terms pile up to name a maximal exultation)*.

## What Psalm 68 says about the inner being
- **The isolated self is given a *home* (vv.5–6).** God settles the *solitary* — the fatherless, the widow, the alone — into belonging; isolation is answered by being homed, and the bound self is led out to freedom.
- **The self is *carried daily*, not just rescued once (v.19).** God *bears the self up* day by day — *amas*, loading the self's weight onto himself continually. The inner being is borne, not merely delivered.
- **Joy before God is *compounded* (v.3).** Glad, exult, jubilant — the inner being's gladness intensifies through several registers at once, a layered exultation.

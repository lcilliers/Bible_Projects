# Psalm 26 — what it tells us about the inner being (Phase-2 chapter reading)

- **Source:** the `lexical-model-2026` Phase-1 lexicals for Psa 26:1–12 (chapter-driven poetic build; roles sanity-checked). Complete chapter. Lexicals only; cited; grounded; inferences tagged *stated* | *inferred*.
- **The psalm of the inner being that offers itself to be tested.** "Prove me… try me… test my heart and my mind" (v.2). Read first-principles, its own key is a **tension held rightly**: the bold appeal for vindication ("I have walked in my integrity") is **not** self-righteousness in the psalm's own frame, because it is (a) grounded in God's *steadfast love kept before the eyes* (v.3), (b) worked out in *disassociation* from falsehood and *love for God's house* (vv.4–8), and (c) resolved into a plea for **grace** — "redeem me, and be gracious to me" (v.11). **The integrous inner being does not trust its integrity; it can invite testing precisely because its confidence rests on *chesed*, not on its own record.**

## The reading

**I. The self offered for assay — "test my heart and my mind" (vv.1–2).**
"**Vindicate** (H8199, M26 — *judge* me) me, O LORD, for I have **walked in my integrity** (H8537, M13, *tom*), and I have **trusted** (H0982, M19) in the LORD **without wavering**" (v.1). Then the astonishing invitation: "**Prove** (H0974, *bachan* — assay) me, O LORD, and **try** (H5254, *nasah*) me; **test** (H6884, *tsaraph* — smelt/refine) my **heart** (H3820, the seat — M47) and my **mind** (*kilyah* — the kidneys, the inmost)." Three assaying verbs — assay, try, **smelt** — and the inner being offers its **deepest, most hidden parts** (heart and kidneys) **to the refiner's fire** *(stated: he asks God to test his heart and inmost)*. This is the opposite of hiding: where Ps 24 *states* the qualification for approach, Ps 26 **submits to being examined** — a confidence that welcomes the closest scrutiny *(inferred)*.

**II. The ground — chesed before the eyes (v.3).**
And here is why the confidence is not boasting: "For your **steadfast love** (H2617, M05, *chesed*) is **before my eyes**, and I **walk** in your **faithfulness** (H0571, M13, *emet*)" (v.3). The integrity is **not self-generated** — it is a *walking* **in God's faithfulness**, with God's *chesed* **kept constantly before the gaze** *(stated)*. The uprightness flows from **keeping God's love in view** (the fixed gaze again — cf. 25:15). The inner being's integrity is **responsive**, not autonomous: it walks in *God's* faithfulness, not its own *(inferred)*.

**III. The company that defines the inner being — the separations (vv.4–5).**
"I do **not sit** (H3427) with men of **falsehood** (*shav*), nor consort with **hypocrites** (H5956, M14 — *dissemblers*, the concealed); I **hate** (H8130, M06) the assembly of **evildoers** (H7489, M27), and I will **not sit** with the **wicked** (H7563, M10)" (vv.4–5). Integrity is partly defined by **disassociation** — the inner being's refusal to *sit / keep company* with falsehood *(stated)*. The "sitting" motif echoes Ps 1:1 ("sit in the seat of scoffers"): **the company one keeps shapes and reveals the inner being**. And the one directed *hatred* named here is a **hatred of the evildoers' assembly** — the inner being's aversion is aimed at evil-company, not persons abstractly *(inferred: the object is "the assembly of evildoers")*.

**IV. The positive counterpart — innocence and the *love* of God's house (vv.6–8).**
Over against the assembly he shuns stands the place he loves: "I **wash** my hands in **innocence** (H5356, M07, *niqqayon*) and go around your altar… **proclaiming thanksgiving** (H8426, M22, *todah*)… O LORD, I **love** (H0157, *aheb*) the **habitation** of your house and the place where your **glory** (H3519, M22, *kabod*) **dwells**" (vv.6–8). The gem: the inner being's positive orientation is **affection for God's dwelling** — "I *love* the habitation of your house" is a genuine **desire and delight** directed to where God's glory dwells *(stated)*. Purity of hands (v.6) and love of the presence (v.8) are the two faces of the same integrity — clean approach and glad affection *(inferred)*. (The *kabod* that "dwells" here is the glory whose King entered in Ps 24; thread noted, not imported.)

**V. The plea not to share the sinners' fate (vv.9–10).**
"Do **not sweep** my **soul** (H5315, the seat — M47) away with **sinners** (H2400, M10), nor my **life** (H2416, M25) with **bloodthirsty** men, in whose hands are evil devices… and whose right hands are full of **bribes**" (vv.9–10). The inner being fears **sharing the sinners' gathering / fate** — "do not gather my soul with theirs" *(stated)*. Note the **hands** motif: *his* hands washed in innocence (v.6) against *their* hands full of bribes (v.10) — the same body-part discloses two inner conditions *(inferred)*.

**VI. Integrity resolved into grace — the psalm's own key (v.11).**
"**But as for me**, I shall **walk in my integrity** (H8537, M13); **redeem** (H6299, M38, *padah*) me, and be **gracious** (H2603, M39, *chanan*) to me" (v.11). This is where the whole psalm shows its hand. The re-affirmed integrity ("I shall walk in my integrity") stands **in the same breath** as a plea for **redemption and grace** — "redeem me, be gracious to me" *(stated)*. **The integrous inner being does not plead its integrity as merit; it walks in integrity *and* casts itself on grace.** The opening "vindicate me, for I have walked in integrity" was therefore never self-righteousness — it ends on **being redeemed** *(inferred: v.11 reframes v.1 as a plea, not a boast)*.

**VII. The tested heart on level ground — the two assemblies (v.12).**
"My **foot stands** on **level ground** (*mishor* — evenness/uprightness); in the **great assembly** I will **bless** (H1288, M39, *barak*) the LORD" (v.12). From the *tested heart* (v.2) to the *foot on level ground* (v.12): the inner being that submitted to assaying now **stands firm and even** *(stated)*. And the private integrity issues in **public blessing** — "in the great assembly I will bless the LORD." The two assemblies **frame the psalm**: the *assembly of evildoers* he would not sit in (v.5) versus the *great assembly* where he blesses God (v.12). The inner being's chosen congregation discloses it *(inferred)*.

## What Psalm 26 says about the inner being
- **The inner being can *invite* God's examination of its inmost (v.2).** "Test my heart and my mind" — three assaying verbs offer the hidden self to the refiner. Confidence that welcomes the closest scrutiny.
- **That confidence is grounded in *chesed*, not in the self (v.3).** Integrity is a *walking in God's faithfulness* with his steadfast love kept before the eyes — responsive, not autonomous.
- **The company the inner being keeps defines it (vv.4–5).** Integrity includes *disassociation* — refusal to sit with falsehood; the one directed hatred is aimed at evil-company (echo of Ps 1:1's "seat").
- **Its positive orientation is *love for God's dwelling* (v.8).** "I love the habitation of your house" — genuine affection directed to where God's glory dwells; the counterpart to the assembly it shuns.
- **Integrity does not trust itself — it resolves into grace (v.11).** "I shall walk in my integrity; *redeem me, be gracious to me*." The integrous heart still rests on redemption. This is the psalm's key.
- **The tested heart ends on *level ground* and in *public blessing* (v.12).** The self that submitted to assay stands firm; private integrity overflows into blessing God in the great assembly.

Should be reclassified to a characteristic (love/affection cluster). **Correction candidate** for the lexical.
- **"prohibition=forbidden (neg particle)" over-stamp (residual/D11).** Fired at vv.1, 4, 5, 9 — a mix the flag conflates: *descriptive* ("without wavering", v.1), *habitual resolve* ("I do not sit", vv.4–5), and *petition* ("do not sweep", v.9). None are prohibitions. Same over-stamp flagged Psa 22–25; **rule-adjustment candidate** (distinguish descriptive / resolve / jussive-petition / prohibition).
- **bearer=LORD bleed (D11-flagged):** pervasive (vv.1, 2, 8) via the nearest-proper heuristic; the reading takes bearers from the text (the speaker, not the LORD, walks in integrity and loves the house).
- **v.2 "mind" (kilyah, kidneys)** not separately surfaced in the lexical (only *heart* H3820 tagged); read from the text as the paired inmost organ. Minor coverage gap.

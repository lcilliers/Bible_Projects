## The reading

**I. The heart's *depth* as a hiding-place (vv.5–6).**
"They **hold fast** to their evil purpose; they talk of laying snares secretly, thinking, '**Who can see them?**' They search out injustice… For the inward mind and **heart** (H3820, the seat — M47) of a man are **deep** (H6013, *amoq*)" (vv.5–6). The gem-finding: the inner being has **depth** — the "inward part and heart" of a man is an **unfathomable interior** *(stated)*. The operation, in the wicked's use: they **trust that depth to conceal their schemes** — the heart is deep enough that "who can see them?" *(stated: they scheme secretly, thinking themselves unseen, *because* the heart is deep)*. The finding: the heart's depth is a **real hiddenness** — a place where plots (or anything) can be lodged out of sight of others; the wicked **bank on its unsearchability** *(inferred: "who can see them?… the heart is deep" makes the depth the ground of their confidence in concealment)*.

**II. Holding fast to an evil purpose (v.5).**
"They **hold fast** (H2388, M23, *chazaq* — strengthen themselves) to their evil purpose" (v.5). The finding: the inner being can **fortify itself in a resolve** — *chazaq*, strengthening its grip on a purpose (here evil) *(stated)*. The operation: a **self-hardening commitment** — the will *strengthening itself* around its chosen aim, making the purpose firm and held-fast *(inferred: chazaq is the self-strengthening in the resolve, the inner being consolidating its intent)*.

**III. The secret *counsel* turned conspiratorial (v.2).**
"Hide me from the **secret plots** (H5475, M17, *sod*) of the wicked" (v.2). The finding: the *sod* — the word for **intimate, confidential counsel** (God's friendship, Ps 25:14; the betrayer's shared counsel, Ps 55:14) — is here the wicked's **conspiratorial inner circle** *(stated)*. The operation: the same faculty of *confidential fellowship* can be **godly or conspiratorial** by its content — the sod's range spans God's confidence and the plotters' huddle *(inferred: sod is the shared-secret bond, its valence set by whom it binds and for what)*.

**IV. The arrow that reaches the deep heart (v.7).**
"But God **shoots his arrow** at them; they are **wounded suddenly**" (v.7). The finding: the depth the wicked trusted as unsearchable is **not beyond God's reach** — God's arrow finds them *(stated)*. The operation: the heart's hiddenness is real to *men* but **not to God** — the deep interior that concealed the schemes is **penetrated** *(inferred: the "who can see them?" of v.5 is answered by God's arrow in v.7 — the depth hides from man, not from God; cf. Ps 44:21's "secrets of the heart", 7:9's heart-testing)*.

## What Psalm 64 says about the inner being
- **The heart is *deep* — an unfathomable interior (v.6).** "The inward mind and heart of a man are deep" — a real hiddenness, a place where plots (or anything) can be lodged out of others' sight.
- **The wicked *trust* the heart's depth to conceal them (vv.5–6).** They scheme secretly, "who can see them?", banking on the interior's unsearchability — the depth is the ground of their confidence in hiding.
- **The inner being can *hold fast* to a purpose (v.5).** *Chazaq* — the will strengthening its grip, self-hardening around its aim; here around evil.
- **Confidential counsel (*sod*) is valenced by its content (v.2).** The same intimate-fellowship faculty is God's friendship (25:14) or the plotters' huddle (here) — the bond's worth is set by whom it binds and for what.
- **The deep heart is hidden from *men*, not from God (v.7).** The depth trusted as unsearchable is penetrated by God's arrow — the interior that hides schemes from others is open to God.

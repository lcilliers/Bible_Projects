## The reading

**I. Knowledge matures from heard to seen (v.8).**
"As we have **heard**, so have we **seen** in the city of the LORD of hosts" (v.8). The operation: the inner being's inherited **report** ("heard" — cf. Ps 44:1's "we have heard with our ears") is now **confirmed by first-hand experience** ("seen") *(stated)*. The finding: the inner being's knowledge **matures from received testimony to verified sight** — the passed-down word becomes the self's own seeing *(inferred: "as we heard, so we have seen" is the movement from report to first-hand confirmation)*.

**II. Contemplation of steadfast love in the temple (v.9).**
"We have **thought on** (H1819, *damah* — pondered, dwelt upon) your **steadfast love** (H2617, M05), O God, in the midst of your temple" (v.9). The operation: a **deliberate meditation** — the inner being *dwells upon* God's chesed, and it does so in a **place set apart** (the temple) *(stated)*. The finding: the inner being **contemplates God's steadfast love as a practice**, an inward pondering located in the sanctuary — not a passing thought but a considered dwelling-upon *(inferred: damah "in the midst of the temple" makes it a sustained, sited contemplation)*.

**III. Considered observation, oriented to transmission (vv.12–14).**
"Walk about Zion… number her towers, **consider well** (H7896, M15) her ramparts… **that you may tell the next generation** that this is God… He will **guide** us forever" (vv.12–14). The operation: careful, deliberate **observation** (walk, number, consider) undertaken **for the sake of passing it on** *(stated)*. The finding: the inner being's **considered perception is oriented outward and forward** — one observes *well* so as to *tell* the next generation; attention serves transmission *(inferred: the "consider well… that you may tell" makes the careful looking instrumental to teaching)*.

## What Psalm 48 says about the inner being
- **Knowledge matures from *heard* to *seen* (v.8).** Inherited testimony becomes the inner being's own first-hand sight — the passed-down word verified in experience.
- **Contemplation of steadfast love is a *practice* (v.9).** The inner being deliberately *dwells upon* God's chesed, sited in the sanctuary — a sustained pondering, not a passing thought.
- **Considered perception serves *transmission* (vv.12–14).** The inner being observes *well* in order to *tell* the next generation — attention oriented outward and forward, to teaching.

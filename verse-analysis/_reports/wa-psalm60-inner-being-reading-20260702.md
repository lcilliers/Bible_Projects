## The reading

**I. The staggering inner being under judgment (v.3).**
"You have made your people see hard things; you have given us **wine to drink that made us stagger** (H8653, *tar'elah* — reeling)" (v.3). The operation: the inner being is **destabilised** — made to *reel*, as if drunk *(stated)*. The finding: under judgment the self loses its footing inwardly — a **disorientation, a loss of steadiness**, the ground no longer firm beneath it *(inferred: the reeling-from-wine image names an inner unsteadiness, not merely an outward defeat)*.

**II. "Vain is the salvation of man" — valour relocated to God (vv.11–12).**
"Oh, grant us **help** against the foe, for **vain** (H7723, *shav* — empty) is the **salvation of man**! **With God** we shall do **valiantly** (H2428, M23, *chayil*); it is he who will tread down our foes" (vv.11–12). The gem-finding: the inner being recognises **human help as *empty*** — *shav*, vain — and relocates its confidence to God *(stated)*. The operation: even **valour is relocated** — "**with** God we shall do valiantly": the self's strength-in-action is *not its own* but done *with* God *(inferred: the "vain is the salvation of man… with God we do valiantly" moves the source of both help and valour from man to God)*. The staggering self (v.3) finds its footing not by recovering its own strength but by lodging its confidence outside itself *(inferred; cf. the confidence-placement thread, Ps 20:7, 33:16, 44:6)*.

## What Psalm 60 says about the inner being
- **Judgment destabilises the inner being (v.3).** The self is made to *reel* — a loss of inner footing, the ground no longer firm; disorientation, not merely defeat.
- **Human help is recognised as *vain*, and valour relocated to God (vv.11–12).** The inner being learns its own and human resources are empty (*shav*), and that even its valour is done *with* God — confidence lodged outside itself. The reeling self finds footing not by recovering its own strength but by relocating its trust.

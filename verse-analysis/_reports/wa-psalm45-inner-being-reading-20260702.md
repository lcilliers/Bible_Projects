## The reading

**I. The heart overflows into speech (v.1).**
"My **heart** (H3820, the seat — M47) **overflows** (H7370, *rachash* — bubbles up, stirs) with a pleasing theme… my **tongue** is like the pen of a ready scribe" (v.1). The operation: the inner being is **stirred to overflowing**, and the overflow **issues in fluent speech** — the tongue carries out what the heart bubbles up *(stated)*. The finding: eloquence here is **the heart's abundance spilling into the tongue** — speech as the *overflow* of a full inner being, not a construction of the mind; the mouth carries the heart's surplus *(inferred: the heart *overflows* and the tongue is its *pen* — utterance is downstream of the heart's fullness)*. (The mouth-speaks-from-the-heart's-abundance thread.)

**II. Love and hate rightly aimed — ordered character (v.7).**
"You have **loved** (H0157, *aheb*) **righteousness** (H6664, M26) and **hated** (H8130, M06) **wickedness**; therefore God… has anointed you with the oil of **gladness** (H8342, M04)" (v.7). The operation: the inner being's **affective faculties — love and hate — are *aimed at the right objects*** (love toward righteousness, hate toward wickedness) *(stated)*. The finding: moral character is **the correct orientation of the loves and hates** — not the absence of hatred but its right target *(inferred: character is defined by *what* is loved and hated, not by loving or hating as such; cf. the delight-defined-by-object thread, Ps 35:27)*. And the ordered affection **produces gladness** — "therefore… the oil of gladness": rightly-aimed love and hate issue in joy *(stated: the "therefore" links the ordered loves to the anointing with gladness)*.

**III. The reorientation of belonging (vv.10–11).**
"Hear, O daughter, and **consider**, and **incline your ear**: **forget** (H7911, *shakach*) your people and your father's house, and the king will **desire** (H0183, M28, *avah*) your beauty" (vv.10–11). The operation: a **transfer of primary belonging** — the inner being counselled to *forget* its origin (people, father's house) in order to be **wholly given to a new lord** *(stated)*. The finding: allegiance can be **reoriented** — a deliberate *forgetting* of the former belonging that clears the self to be bound to another; the leaving is what enables the new cleaving and the king's answering desire *(inferred: the "forget… and the king will desire" makes the reorientation the condition of the new bond)*.

## What Psalm 45 says about the inner being
- **Speech overflows from the heart's fullness (v.1).** Eloquence is the *overflow* of a stirred inner being into the tongue — utterance downstream of the heart's abundance, not a mental construction.
- **Character is love and hate *rightly aimed* (v.7).** Moral order is not the absence of hatred but its correct target — love toward righteousness, hate toward wickedness — and rightly-aimed affection produces gladness.
- **Belonging can be *reoriented* (vv.10–11).** A deliberate forgetting of the former allegiance clears the inner being to be wholly given to a new lord; the leaving enables the cleaving.

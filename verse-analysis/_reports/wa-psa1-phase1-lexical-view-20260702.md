## Sanity-check read-back (my evaluation of the draft — the vital step)

**The multi-characteristic, two-ways structure is visible** (as predicted): the righteous's inner life = **delight** (H2656, M04) + **meditation** (H1897, M42) in the law; against the negative pole = **wicked / sinners / scoffers** (M10) and the eschatological **righteous vs wicked** contrast (M26) in vv.5–6. That is the chapter's inner-being content and Phase 2 should read it from those perspectives.

**Draft roles the sanity-check should RECLASSIFY (mechanical `gate-1 = characteristic` is too crude for poetry):**
- **yields** (H5414 M12) and **wither** (H5034 M24) in v3 are **simile vehicles** (the planted tree / its leaf), not inner-being characteristics → reclassify to standalone/simile, not characteristic.
- **counsel** (H6098 M17) in v1 — "walks in the *counsel* of the wicked" — is the **sphere/qualifier** of the wrong path, not a characteristic of the blessed man → process-qualifier.
- **judgment** (H4941 M26) in v5 — "not stand in the *judgment*" — is the **scene/event**, not an inner disposition → process-qualifier/standalone.
- Real characteristics after reclassification: **delight, meditation** (righteous inner life) + the **wicked/sinner/scoffer** vs **righteous** moral poles.

**Residual mechanical noise (same class as ruthlessness — flagged, tighten in a refinement circle; does NOT block Phase 1):**
- **prohibition over-fires**: the single "not" in v1 (walks *not*…) and v3 ("leaf does *not* wither") is stamped on ~every span within ±3 grains. Should bind to the specific negated verb only.
- **manner/coupling over-weld**: `welds walks/stands/sits/meditates/is/drives/yields/stand` — "weld" (morphological) is too strong for these adverbial/locative/object qualifiers. Some relations are real (walk *in* counsel; meditate *in* law) but they are qualifier-links, not welds.
- **bearer = LORD bleed**: in vv.2 and 6 "LORD" attaches as bearer to every following span (nearest-proper heuristic). In v6 LORD *is* the bearer of *knows* but not of way/righteous/wicked/perish; in v2 the *man* (not LORD) meditates. All already flagged unreliable in D11 — the reading must not lean on bearer.

**Verdict:** Phase-1 base lexical is a sound mechanical draft (48 spans, all 14 tagged terms correctly gate-1 after the cursor-bug fix). The role column is a labelled DRAFT; the reclassifications above are Phase-2 work, not Phase-1 defects.

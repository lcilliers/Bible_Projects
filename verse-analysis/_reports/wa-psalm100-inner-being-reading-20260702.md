## The reading

**I. Service in gladness (v.2).**
"**Serve** the LORD **with gladness** (H8057, M04)! Come into his presence with singing!" (v.2). The finding: the inner being's service is to be **rendered *in gladness*** — joyful, not grudging *(stated)*. The operation: gladness is the **mode** of the self's service — worship and obedience done *with joy*, the inner disposition of the servant *(inferred: "serve… with gladness" makes joy the manner of the service, not a separate act — the self serves *gladly*)*.

**II. "We are his, not our own" — the self as God's (v.3).**
"**Know** that the LORD, he is God! **It is he who made us, and we are his**; we are his people, and the sheep of his pasture" (v.3). The gem-finding: the inner being's foundational knowledge is that it is **made by God and *belongs* to him** — "we are *his*" *(stated)*. The operation: the self **knows itself as God's *possession*** — made-by, and belonging-to, God, **not self-owned** *(inferred: "he made us, and we are his" locates the self's origin and ownership in God — the inner being is not its own but God's; a foundational orientation, cf. Ps 95:7 "the sheep of his hand", 24:1 the earth is the LORD's)*.

## What Psalm 100 says about the inner being
- **Service is rendered *in gladness* (v.2).** Joy is the mode of the self's service — worship and obedience done gladly, not grudgingly; the servant's inner disposition.
- **The self is *made by God and belongs to him* — not its own (v.3).** The inner being's foundational knowledge is that God made it and it is *his*; the self is not self-owned but God's possession — origin and ownership located in God.

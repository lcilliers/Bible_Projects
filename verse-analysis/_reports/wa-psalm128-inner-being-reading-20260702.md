## The reading

**I. The fear of the LORD as the root disposition (vv.1, 4).**
"**Blessed** is everyone who **fears** (H3373, M01) the LORD, who **walks in his ways**!… thus shall the man be blessed who **fears the LORD**" (vv.1, 4).

- **Source:** Phase-1 lexicals for Ecc 9:1–18, read per `segment_unit` (provenance `ecclesiastes-segmentation-v1-20260703`). Complete chapter. Cited; grounded; inferences tagged *stated* | *inferred*.
- **Lens:** the inner-being **operation** is the finding; death, hope and wisdom are the arena.

## ECC-09-A · Ecc 9:1–6 · [D, multi] — the passions that perish at death; hearts full of evil yet the living have hope

**The reading.** The self is placed, with all its works, "in the **hand of God**"; but its inner states are opaque even to itself: "whether it is **love or hate**, man does **not know**" (v.1) — the self cannot read its own standing. Death levels all (v.2), and "the **hearts of the children of man are full of evil**, and **madness is in their hearts** while they live" (v.3). Yet a hinge: "**he who is joined with all the living has hope**, for a living dog is better than a dead lion" (v.4). And the finding that names the stakes: "their **love and their hate and their envy** have already **perished**" (v.6) — the passions themselves die with the person. The finding is **the inner life as bounded by death — hope belongs to the living, and the passions perish** *(stated)*. The operation is **hope located in being-alive, over against the extinction of the affections at death** — while there is life there is hope; but love, hate, and envy, the whole engine of the interior, "have already perished" for the dead *(inferred: v.6 is a startling inner-being datum — the affections are not eternal; they are functions of the living self and cease at death; this both relativises the passions (even hate and envy end) and heightens the value of the *living* interior (only the living can hope, love, act) — the urgency behind the joy-refrain that immediately follows)*.

**Lexical validation (read-back).** *heart/hearts* (H3820, seated — "full of evil," "madness in their hearts"), *love* (H0160, *ahavah*) and *hate* (H8135, *sinah*) as nouns (v.1,6), *envy* (H7068, v.6), *hope* (H0986, v.4 — correctly `characteristic`), *know* (H3045), *living* (H2416) carried. The passion-nouns (love/hate/envy) correctly tagged; the reading rests on their being said to "perish." Note *shuns* (H3373) at v.2 is *yare* — here "he who shuns an oath" (fears to swear), a fringe use, not fear-of-God; correctly a minor sense. Sound.

**Hidden / less-obvious characteristics isolated.** (1) **The self opaque to itself** — "whether it is love or hate, man does not know" (v.1): the interior cannot certify its own standing before God; a hidden characteristic of *inner opacity* (the self does not have transparent access to whether it is loved or hated, held or rejected). (2) **The mortality of the passions** — "their love and their hate and their envy have already perished" (v.6): the affections are *temporal functions*, not immortal essences; the finding relativises even the fiercest inner states by their expiry.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *hope as belonging to the living* (new — hope located in being-alive); *the mortality of the passions (love/hate/envy perish)* (new — the affections as bounded by death); *the self opaque to itself* (new — inner opacity before God); *hearts full of evil and madness* (shared w/ Gen 6:5, reframed).

## ECC-09-B · Ecc 9:7–10 · [T, multi, thread] — joy as the approved life; whole-hearted action while there is time

**The reading.** The fullest sounding of the refrain, now with urgency and warrant: "Go, **eat your bread with joy**, and **drink your wine with a merry heart**, for God has **already approved** what you do" (v.7). The finding gains a new note — joy is not merely permitted but *already approved*; the anxious self can stop seeking permission *(stated)*. It widens to the whole life: "**enjoy life with the wife whom you love**… because that is your **portion**" (v.9). Then the charge to wholeheartedness: "**whatever your hand finds to do, do it with your might**, for there is no work or thought or knowledge or wisdom in **Sheol**, to which you are going" (v.10). The operation is **joy plus wholehearted engagement, warranted by God and bounded by death** — the self is released into gladness ("already approved") and into full-bodied action ("with your might"), precisely *because* the time is short and the grave is workless *(inferred: this is the refrain's ethical maturation — 2:24 stated joy as gift; here joy comes with *approval* (permission removed as an anxiety) and with a *mandate to act wholeheartedly* while alive; the mortality that produced despair in ch.2 here produces urgency and engagement — the same fact, opposite inner response)*.

**Lexical validation (read-back).** *approved* (H7521, *ratsah* — God's approval/pleasure, correctly `characteristic`, load-bearing), *love* (H0157, *aheb* — "the wife whom you love"), *life* (H2416), *given* (H5414), *toil* (H5999), *thought* (H2808), *knowledge* (H1847) carried. The pivot "God has already **approved**" (H7521) is correctly tagged — the divine warrant the reading rests on. Sound.

**Hidden / less-obvious characteristics isolated.** (1) **Joy as already-approved (permission-anxiety removed)** — "for God has *already approved* what you do" (v.7): a distinct inner-being release — the self is freed from the anxious question of whether joy is *allowed*; the approval is prior, so gladness is not stolen but sanctioned. (2) **Wholeheartedness as a response to mortality** — "do it with your *might*, for there is no work… in Sheol" (v.10): death read not as futility (ch.2) but as *urgency* — the workless grave makes present action precious; the same fact converted from despair to engagement.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *joy as already-approved (permission-anxiety removed)* (thread deepened — 2:24 → here); *wholehearted engagement warranted by mortality* (new — death as urgency, not futility); *love (aheb) rightly enjoyed (the wife you love)* (shared w/ Pro 5:18–19).

## ECC-09-C · Ecc 9:11–12 · [D, multi] — the ignorance of one's own time; the self snared unawares

**The reading.** "The race is not to the swift, nor the battle to the strong… but **time and chance happen to them all**" (v.11); "**man does not know his time**. Like fish taken in an evil net, and like birds caught in a snare, so the children of man are **snared at an evil time, when it suddenly falls upon them**" (v.12). The finding is **the self blind to when its hour will come** — competence (swift, strong, wise) does not secure outcome; and the decisive "time" arrives *unforeseen*, catching the self like a trapped creature *(stated)*. The operation is **living under an unknown hour** — the interior condition of not knowing one's time, which no skill can dispel *(inferred: this joins the not-knowing thread (3:11, 6:12, 8:17) — the self cannot know its own good, its future, or its time; here the specific ignorance is temporal-personal (when calamity or death falls), and it is total ("suddenly"); the fitting response, set up by v.10, is to act wholeheartedly *now*, since the hour cannot be seen)*.

**Lexical validation (read-back).** *riches* (H6239), *intelligent* (H0995), *favor* (H2580), *knowledge/know* (H3045) carried. The finding sits on the not-knowing verbs and the snare-imagery (gate-2 content); the reading is structural, well-supported. Sound.

**Hidden / less-obvious characteristics isolated.** **The suddenness as the sting** — "snared… *when it suddenly falls upon them*" (v.12): the inner-being weight is not just ignorance of the time but its *abruptness*; the self is not gradually warned but caught. This is the shadow-side of "do it with your might" (v.10) — because the hour falls suddenly, present wholeheartedness is the only rational posture.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *the ignorance of one's own time (living under an unknown, sudden hour)* (new — the not-knowing thread turned temporal-personal); *the self snared unawares* (new).

## ECC-09-D · Ecc 9:13–18 · [D, multi, thread] — the unregarded wisdom; the wise man forgotten

**The reading.** A parable that stings: a "little city" besieged by a great king is delivered by "a **poor, wise man**… by his wisdom" — "**yet no one remembered that poor man**" (vv.14–15). The finding is **wisdom that saves and is then forgotten** — real, effective wisdom (it delivered the city) meets *oblivion* and *contempt*: "the poor man's wisdom is **despised** and his words are **not heard**" (v.16). The oblivion thread (1:11, 2:16) here attaches to the one who most deserved remembrance *(stated)*. Yet the unit still affirms wisdom's superiority: "wisdom is **better than might**… **better than weapons of war**, but one sinner destroys much good" (vv.16,18). The operation is **wisdom's worth held against its social un-reward** — the interior good of wisdom is real and even decisive, but it is not honoured; the self that is wise must hold its worth *independent of recognition* *(inferred: the parable pairs with 4:13–16 (favour is fickle) and the oblivion thread — the wise self will often go unremembered and unheard; the finding is that wisdom's value is *intrinsic and effective*, not *rewarded*; "heard in quiet" (v.17) it outweighs "the shouting of a ruler," even when no one credits it)*.

**Lexical validation (read-back).** *remembered* (H2142, with negation "no one remembered"), *heard* (H8085, twice, one negated "not heard"), *shouting* (H2201), *ruler* (H4910), *sinner* (H2398) carried. The oblivion-finding rests on *remembered* (negated) — correctly tagged; the reading takes it from the text. Sound.

**Hidden / less-obvious characteristics isolated.** **Wisdom's value decoupled from its reward** — the city was *saved* by the wisdom (it worked), yet the man was *forgotten* (it was not honoured): the inner-being finding is that the worth of the interior good does not depend on recognition; the wise self must be sustained by the reality of its wisdom, not by being remembered. This directly answers the ch.2 despair over being forgotten (2:16) — being forgotten does not un-make the wisdom.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *wisdom that saves yet is forgotten (worth decoupled from reward)* (thread — oblivion, 1:11, 2:16 → here, now attached to merit); *the wise self holding its worth without recognition* (new); *"heard in quiet" over "shouting"* (new — the quiet effective interior vs loud power).

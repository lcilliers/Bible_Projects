## JER-shepherds-branch · Jeremiah 23:1–8 · [D, multi] — false shepherds who scatter; the Righteous Branch, "the LORD is our righteousness"

**The reading.** God indicts the leaders: "**Woe to the shepherds who destroy and scatter the sheep of my pasture!… you have scattered my flock… and you have not attended to them**" (vv.1–2). He promises to gather the flock himself and to raise a true king: "**I will raise up for David a righteous Branch, and he shall reign as king and deal wisely, and shall execute justice and righteousness… And this is the name by which he will be called: 'The LORD is our righteousness'**" (vv.5–6). The finding is **false shepherds who *scatter and neglect* set against a coming king who is himself "**the LORD is our righteousness**"** *(stated)*. The operation is **the contrast of *care*: shepherds who "have not attended to" the flock (leadership as neglect and self-service) against the Branch whose very *name* locates righteousness *in God* ("the LORD is our righteousness") — so that the interior's righteousness is not self-generated but *received from and named after God*** *(inferred: the shepherd-indictment (vv.1–2) turns on *attention* — "you have **not attended to** them": the finding is that the failure of care is a failure to *attend*, to give the flock the mind and heart it needs (the inverse of a shepherd "after God's own heart", 3:15); and the Branch's name (v.6) is the inner-being jewel — "**the LORD is our righteousness**" (YHWH-tsidqenu): the finding is that the righteousness the interior needs is *not its own* but *God's, given and named over it* — the coming king does not merely *do* righteousness but *is* the channel by which "the LORD" becomes "our righteousness"; the self's standing before God rests not on self-made virtue but on a righteousness that is God's and is *conferred*)*.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *leadership as attentive care vs neglect ("you have not attended to them" — the failure of the shepherd is a failure to attend)* (new — the mind and heart a leader owes the flock, w/ 23:1-2, 3:15, Ezek 34:2-4); *righteousness received and named from God ("the LORD is our righteousness" — the interior's standing not self-generated but conferred by God)* (thread — a gem: righteousness as God's, given and named over the self, w/ 23:6, 33:16, 1 Cor 1:30, Phil 3:9).

## JER-word-like-fire · Jeremiah 23:9–40 · [D, multi] — false prophets of their own minds; the council of the LORD; no hiding; the word as fire

**The reading.** The prophet trembles under the true word: "**My heart is broken within me; all my bones shake… because of the LORD and because of his holy words**" (v.9). Against this stand prophets who "**speak visions of their own minds, not from the mouth of the LORD**" (v.16), telling the self-willed "**It shall be well with you**" (v.17). The test is whether one has "**stood in the council of the LORD**" (v.18,22). God asserts his inescapable presence: "**Am I a God at hand… and not a God far away? Can a man hide himself in secret places so that I cannot see him?… Do I not fill heaven and earth?**" (vv.23–24). And the power of the true word: "**Is not my word like fire, declares the LORD, and like a hammer that breaks the rock in pieces?**" (v.29). The finding is **the true word that *shakes* the one who bears it (against comforting *visions of one's own mind*), authenticated by standing "**in the council of the LORD**" — spoken by a God from whom no one can *hide* — and *powerful* as "*fire and a hammer*"** *(stated)*. The operation is **the distinction between the true and false word located in the *interior*: the false prophet speaks "**the deceit of his own heart**" (v.26) — projecting his own mind as God's word — while the true prophet has "**stood in the council of the LORD**", receiving rather than inventing; the false word *soothes* the self-willed ("it shall be well"), the true word *breaks* ("like a hammer") and *burns* ("like fire"); and behind it, the God who "**fills heaven and earth**" — no secret place hides the heart from him** *(inferred: three findings. First, the *self-sourced word* — false prophecy "**visions of their own minds… the deceit of their own heart**" (vv.16,26): the finding is that the counterfeit of God's word is the *projection of one's own interior* — mistaking (or passing off) the contents of one's own mind for God's voice; the antidote is having "**stood in the council of the LORD**" (v.18,22) — the true word is *received in God's presence*, not generated from the self. Second, the *comforting lie vs the breaking truth* — the false prophets say "it shall be well" to "everyone who stubbornly follows his own heart" (v.17), while the true word is "**fire… a hammer that breaks the rock**" (v.29): the finding is that God's genuine word *does something to the interior* — it burns and breaks, whereas the false word merely *soothes*; a word that never disturbs is suspect. Third, *no hiding* — "**can a man hide in secret places so that I cannot see him? do I not fill heaven and earth?**" (vv.23–24): the finding is that the God who authenticates the word is *inescapably present and all-seeing* — the interior cannot conceal itself or its false words from the God who "fills heaven and earth", so the pretence of the false prophet is transparent to him)*.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *the self-sourced counterfeit word ("visions of their own minds… the deceit of their own heart" — projecting one's interior as God's voice)* (thread — a gem: the counterfeit of revelation is the self's own mind mistaken for God's, w/ 23:16,26, Ezek 13:2-3); *standing in the council of the LORD (the true word received in God's presence, not generated from the self)* (new — a gem: revelation received, not invented, w/ 23:18,22, Amos 3:7); *the comforting lie vs the breaking word ("it shall be well" vs "my word like fire, like a hammer" — the true word burns and breaks where the false merely soothes)* (thread — a gem: God's word does something to the interior; a word that never disturbs is suspect, w/ 23:17,29, Heb 4:12, Luke 6:26); *no hiding from the God who fills all ("can a man hide… do I not fill heaven and earth?" — the interior transparent to the inescapable God)* (thread — a gem: omnipresence exposing the false heart, w/ 23:23-24, Ps 139:7-12, Amos 9:2-3).

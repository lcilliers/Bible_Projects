## JER-queen-of-heaven · Jeremiah 44:1–30 · [D, multi] — the defiant heart that inverts cause and effect; open rebellion

**The reading.** In Egypt the Judeans openly refuse God's word: "**As for the word that you have spoken to us in the name of the LORD, we will not listen to you. But we will do everything that we have vowed, make offerings to the queen of heaven and pour out drink offerings to her, as we did**" (vv.16–17). And they *invert the history*: "**Then we had plenty of food and prospered and saw no disaster. But since we left off making offerings to the queen of heaven… we have lacked everything and have been consumed by the sword and by famine**" (vv.17–18). The finding is **a *fully defiant* heart that has *inverted cause and effect* — crediting the idol for past prosperity and *blaming the worship of God* for its disasters** *(stated)*. The operation is **the terminal condition of the hardened interior: not merely disobedient but *articulately, defiantly rebellious* ("we will NOT listen to you"), and — most strikingly — *reasoning backward*, reading its history so as to *credit the false god and indict the true one*: the very judgments that fell *because of* idolatry are attributed to the *abandonment* of idolatry** *(inferred: this is the darkest interior in Jeremiah — the finding is that the hardened heart, at its terminus, becomes *openly and articulately defiant*: "**WE WILL NOT LISTEN to you**" (v.16), no longer even pretending; and it constructs a *coherent counter-narrative* that inverts reality — "when we served the queen of heaven we prospered; since we stopped, we have suffered" (vv.17–18): the finding is that the corrupt interior can *reason itself into calling evil good and good evil* (Isa 5:20) by *misreading its own experience* — attributing blessing to the idol and disaster to God, exactly reversing the truth (the disasters came *because of* the idolatry, 44:2–6); the finding is that a sufficiently committed rebellion does not merely disobey but *reconstructs the facts* to justify itself, so that the very evidence of God's judgment becomes, in the inverted heart, evidence *for* the idol; this is self-deception hardened into a *worldview* — the heart not only chooses the lie but *builds a history* to defend it; and it is un-reachable by argument (Jeremiah's plain recital of the true cause, vv.21–23, changes nothing), showing an interior past persuasion, sealed in its inversion)*.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *articulate open defiance ("we will not listen to you… we will do everything we have vowed" — the hardened heart no longer even pretending)* (thread — a gem: rebellion at its terminus, openly declared, w/ 44:16-17, 6:16, Job 21:14-15); *inverting cause and effect ("when we served the queen of heaven we prospered; since we stopped we have suffered" — crediting the idol, blaming God, reversing the true history)* (new — a gem: the corrupt heart misreading its own experience to justify the lie, calling good evil and evil good, w/ 44:17-18, Isa 5:20, Mal 3:14-15); *rebellion reconstructing the facts (self-deception hardened into a worldview, a history built to defend the lie)* (new — a gem: the committed rebellion rewriting reality to justify itself, w/ 44:17-23); *the interior past persuasion (Jeremiah's plain recital of the true cause changing nothing)* (thread — the sealed heart un-reachable by argument, w/ 44:21-24).

## JER-fear-not-jacob · Jeremiah 46:1–28 · [D, multi] — fear not amid the nations' judgment; measured discipline

**The reading.** The oracles against the nations open with Egypt, whose reliance is misplaced — judgment falls on "**Pharaoh… and those who trust in him**" (v.25). But the section turns, at its close, to God's own people with a tender reassurance: "**Fear not, O Jacob my servant… for I am with you… I will make a full end of all the nations to which I have driven you, but of you I will not make a full end. I will discipline you in just measure, and I will by no means leave you unpunished**" (vv.27–28). The finding is **the servant told "**fear not… I am with you**" *while the nations fall* — his discipline "in just measure", never a "full end"** *(stated)*. The operation is **the interior of God's people *distinguished* from the nations in the very midst of universal judgment: the nations trusting Pharaoh come to a "full end", but of Jacob God will "not make a full end" — his correction is *measured* ("in just measure") and *preserving*, not destroying; so the servant, amid the collapse of everything, is told to "**fear not**" on the ground of God's *presence* ("I am with you")** *(inferred: the placement is the point — after chapters of judgment on the nations, God addresses *his own servant* with "**fear not**" (vv.27–28, repeating 30:10–11): the finding is that the same God whose judgment makes "a full end of all the nations" makes *no* full end of his people — the interior that belongs to God is *not consumed* in the general reckoning, because its discipline is *different in kind* (correction "in just measure", not destruction); and the ground of the servant's fearlessness amid universal collapse is *presence* — "**I am with you**": the finding is that when everything trusted-in falls (Egypt, the nations), the servant's security is not in any surviving structure but in *God's accompanying presence*, which distinguishes measured discipline from annihilation and grounds the "fear not")*.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *the servant distinguished from the nations in judgment ("of all the nations I will make a full end, but of you I will not" — the interior belonging to God not consumed in the general reckoning)* (thread — a gem: God's discipline of his own differs in kind from destruction, w/ 46:28, 30:11, Mal 3:17-18, Rom 8:1); *measured discipline, not annihilation ("I will discipline you in just measure" — correction preserving, never a full end)* (thread — a gem: severity proportioned within a keeping love, w/ 46:28, 10:24, 30:11); *"fear not, for I am with you" amid universal collapse (security in God's presence when everything trusted-in falls)* (thread — a gem: the servant's fearlessness grounded in accompaniment, not surviving structures, w/ 46:27-28, Isa 41:10, 43:1-2).

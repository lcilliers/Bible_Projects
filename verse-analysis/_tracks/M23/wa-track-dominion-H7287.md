## The operation
- **Term:** `radah` H7287 (ra.dah) "to rule, have dominion, tread down", Qal. Study home cluster **M23**; reg197 *authority* (C20). 25 verses.
- **Identity:** the **will to rule over another** — an exercise of power/dominion. An *act*, borne by an actor over a subject.
- **Key property (this track's spine):** `radah` is **valence-NEUTRAL**. The same verb is the God-given **creation mandate** (Gen 1:26–28, "have dominion") *and* **tyranny** (Isa 14:6 "ruled the nations in anger"; Eze 34:4 shepherds "with force and harshness"). What sets the valence is the **manner** (e.g. `be-perek`, ruthlessly) and the **object** (a brother vs creation). Mirrors enslavement (`abad`): same operation, valence set by who/how.

## Cross-verse synthesis (emerging focus point)
- **Neutrality is the spine.** `radah` is one movement — *to rule* — that Scripture sends in opposite moral directions depending on **manner** and **object**. Given as a blessing over creation (Gen 1), commanded to be **restrained** over a brother (Lev 25), indicted as **tyranny** when turned harshly on the weak (Isa 14; Eze 34).
- **The Lev 25:43 contribution:** the verse does not forbid *ruling* — it forbids ruling **ruthlessly over a brother**. The inner-being question is not "may I have power?" but "**how, and over whom?**" — answered by the manner (not `be-perek`) and the relationship (a brother), under the fear of God.
- **Parallel to enslavement (`abad`):** both `radah` and `abad` are valence-neutral domination operations whose morality is set by object+manner — a candidate **cross-operation pattern** (see the enslavement track).

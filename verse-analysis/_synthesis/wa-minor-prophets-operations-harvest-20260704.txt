########## Joel (Joe) - 3 chapter-readings ##########
  [Joe 1] loss exposing dependence (stripped comfort turning the creature toward God — "gladness dries up… to you, O LORD, I call")
  [Joe 1] lament directed to God (catastrophe turned into a God-ward cry, not despair)
  [Joe 1] the creature's upward reflex in extremity ("even the beasts pant for you" — the instinct under loss is to seek the Maker)
  [Joe 2] interior repentance ("rend your hearts and not your garments" — the heart torn, not the robe; repentance an inward act not a performance)
  [Joe 2] the whole heart returned ("return to me with all your heart" — the undivided interior)
  [Joe 2] return grounded in God's character ("for he is gracious and merciful… and he relents" — the ground of repentance is who God is, not the penitent's worthiness)
  [Joe 2] the freedom of grace ("who knows whether he will relent?" — repentance casts itself on mercy, does not coerce it)
  [Joe 2] the emptied interior refilled and satisfied ("I will restore the years the locust has eaten… you shall be satisfied")
  [Joe 2] shame lifted ("my people shall never again be put to shame" — restored to confident belonging)
  [Joe 2] the Spirit poured on all flesh (renewed presence, not just provision — God's Spirit given democratically)
  [Joe 2] salvation for everyone who calls ("everyone who calls on the name of the LORD shall be saved" — the open door grounded in calling)
  [Joe 3] the same day two ways — terror to the nations, refuge to God's people ("the heavens and earth quake, but the LORD is a refuge to his people")
  [Joe 3] refuge/stronghold for the belonging interior (the earth-shaking God as shelter to his own)
  [Joe 3] the valley of decision (the moment the two destinies are sorted)

########## Amos (Amo) - 8 chapter-readings ##########
  [Amo 1] cruelty as nursed, settled disposition (Edom's anger "kept forever", pity "cast off" — the grudge cultivated into a permanent posture)
  [Amo 1] the interior judged by its treatment of the weak ("trample the head of the poor into the dust" — covenant sin located in oppression)
  [Amo 1] the same standard for insider and outsider (the cycle turning from the nations onto Israel)
  [Amo 3] intimacy raises accountability ("you only have I known; therefore I will punish" — being known increases responsibility, not exempts)
  [Amo 3] the prophetic compulsion ("the LORD has spoken; who can but prophesy?" — the one who has heard God cannot stay silent)
  [Amo 3] nothing without cause (the chain of questions — the ordered world where God's word is the reason behind events)
  [Amo 4] discipline as an invitation to return ("yet you did not return to me" — chastisement aimed at turning the heart, not mere retribution)
  [Amo 4] the heart impervious to correction (hardship after hardship failing to bend it Godward)
  [Amo 4] misdirected religious love ("for so you love to do" — fervour poured into a worship that is itself sin)
  [Amo 4] the unavoidable encounter ("prepare to meet your God" — if correction will not turn the heart, confrontation will)
  [Amo 5] life as seeking God ("seek me and live" — the interior lives by an active, directional reaching for God)
  [Amo 5] seeking God and loving good as one movement ("seek good… hate evil, love good, establish justice" — the affections reoriented as part of the seeking)
  [Amo 5] worship without justice as anti-worship ("I hate your feasts… but let justice roll down like waters" — the torrent of justice against the noise of songs)
  [Amo 5] craving God's day while unready ("woe to you who desire the day of the LORD… it is darkness" — mistaking judgment for reward)
  [Amo 6] complacent ease as anaesthesia ("at ease in Zion… secure" — comfort insulating the interior from the world's pain)
  [Amo 6] the heart un-grieved by others' ruin ("but are not grieved over the ruin of Joseph" — the culminating sin: feeling nothing for the suffering around one)
  [Amo 6] self-crediting pride ("by our own strength… the pride of Jacob" — the inflated heart that takes the credit)
  [Amo 6] God abhorring pride ("I abhor the pride of Jacob")
  [Amo 7] the intercessor's compassion ("please forgive! how can Jacob stand?" — the announcer of judgment the first to plead for mercy)
  [Amo 7] intercession that moves God ("the LORD relented" — the pleading interior genuinely affecting the outcome)
  [Amo 7] the plumb line as objective standard (judgment measured against a true vertical, not arbitrary)
  [Amo 7] courage grounded in commission ("the LORD took me… Go, prophesy" — firmness under threat rests on being sent, not on self)
  [Amo 8] greed that resents the sacred pause ("when will the Sabbath be over, that we may sell?" — the heart present at worship but impatient for it to end)
  [Amo 8] deceit woven into commerce ("make the ephah small and the shekel great… false balances… buy the poor for silver")
  [Amo 8] the famine of hearing God's word ("not a famine of bread… but of hearing the words of the LORD" — the word neglected eventually withdrawn)
  [Amo 8] the word not guaranteed to those who despise it (they will seek and not find)
  [Amo 9] the inescapable presence of God ("if they climb to heaven… if they dig into Sheol… I will search them out" — omnipresence as the impossibility of hiding)
  [Amo 9] the discriminating sieve (judgment that sifts and preserves the genuine, losing no true grain)
  [Amo 9] restoration as the last word ("I will raise up the booth of David that is fallen… never again uprooted" — the God from whom none can hide rebuilds the ruin)

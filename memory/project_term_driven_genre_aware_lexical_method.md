**The lexical method as it stands 2026-07-02 (validated on ruthlessness/perek).** Builds on [[project_ve_lexical_is_verse_first]].

**Processing order — TERM-DRIVEN:** take an OWNER term (e.g. ruthlessness perek H6531) → process its **anchor verse's passage first** → then batch **every verse of the term, one by one**. A **completion marker** (`verse.process_marker`) records done verses so re-runs skip them. Reading the term across all its verses makes the **focus point emerge** (perek = the harsh MANNER of dominion, welded to rule/enslave, condemned) — the study's goal working.

**Genre is a ve-lexical item** (verse-level) and **feeds the passage treatment:**
- **prose** (law/narrative/prophetic/gospel/epistle) → passage = consecutive run; cross-verse items (source/effect/process) ON.
- **poetic** (Job/Psa/Pro/Ecc/Song, maybe prophetic/Song) → **TWO PHASES**: use the chapter/poem as the batch → derive a per-verse lexical for each verse first → then read the WHOLE poem and enrich the verse-lexicals from the interrelatedness of the poem's verses. (Consecutive-run cross-verse items are noise across independent aphorisms — the genre finding.)

**Read-back / sensibility / method-adjustment is VITAL** (not optional QA): different treatment of passage/verse in different scenarios sharply increases accuracy; when uncertain, write a **D11 note** rather than assert. Learning + discovering + adjusting the rules IS part of the lexical process.

**Concrete method learning from the perek trial:** the `source` rule cannot yet tell a **DRIVER** from a **RESTRAINT** — an affect near a vice can be its cause (dread→ruthlessness, Exo 1) OR its check (fear-of-God restrains ruthlessness, Lev 25:43). Distinguish by polarity / "but"-contrast syntax / the prohibition frame; else mark D11 role-uncertain. (Option-3 "grammatical cross-verse link" is itself a method to test, not assume.)

**STORY / SYNTHESIS SCOPE (researcher, 2026-07-02):** the single-term SYNTHESIS + STORY are built from the term's **LEXICALS ONLY** — **no old fanout** (not pursued going forward), **no old findings / ib_observation**. Aim: see what story the lexicals alone yield. **COHABITATION is NOT single-term** — not evident in the lexicals nor in the single-term rollup; it emerges only at the **CROSS-TERM story layer** (relating completed single-term stories → the company kept + shared root; the home of dropped-per-verse D13). Single-term story order = act → impact → source/moral-condition (+ restraint), cited. Style = `wa-narrative-style-instruction-v1-20260702.md`. Story saved to prose tables. Pipeline: anchor lexical → all verses lexical → synthesis (lexicals only) → correction loop → story → prose.

**THE READING/STORY LAYER (researcher, 2026-07-02):** the story is NOT mechanical assembly of the lexical — it is the **READING (inference)**, read backward from the operations to the latent inner-being condition. Two hidden objectives: (a) surface **HIDDEN MEANING** the lexical cannot carry (this is where dropped-D12 actually lives — it returns as inference at the story layer, not a per-verse item); (b) let a **COLLECTION of passages TRANSCEND into one truth** larger than any verse ("take these verses and tell me what they say about the inner being"). Discipline: every inference is TAGGED stated|inferred, tethered to a stated operation, grounded in the verse, nothing imported. **Layer stack:** lexical (stated) → single-term rollup (what the operation IS) → **collection reading (inferred hidden-meaning + transcendent truth)** → cross-term (cohabitation/root). Exemplar: `verse-analysis/characteristics/wa-ruthlessness-deeper-reading-20260702.md`; full-lexical raw layer: `wa-ruthlessness-full-lexical-story-20260702.md` ('all terms' → rich but noisy; needs a light affect/vice/dominion filter).

**SANITY CHECK + ROLE-BASED ROLLUP (researcher, 2026-07-02).** The index-driven initial build is a **fast mechanical DRAFT, not trusted** — it produces noise (mis-derived bearer/target, spurious source, mis-classified role; the A/B/C classification proved it). MANDATORY post-build **SANITY CHECK**: evaluate every derived value against the verse (correct or D11-flag) + assign each span a **ROLE**, before trust/rollup ("derive fast, THEN evaluate"). **Rollup scope is by ROLE, not every span:** a **characteristic** (focus disposition, e.g. ruthlessness) gets the full term-driven rollup (its verses → story); a **qualifier/process-element** that binds to a characteristic gives value/context to the pair **in place** — NO own-verse rollup; **standalone** → none. A qualifier whose usage VARIES across contexts earns its own verses only in a later **refinement circle**. (Settles abad/radah: abad serves the ruthlessness pair, no own rollup for ruthlessness.) Build order: index-driven build → sanity check (evaluate+role) → rollup characteristics → story → refinement circles. Role is a candidate stored lexical item. Method §12/§13.

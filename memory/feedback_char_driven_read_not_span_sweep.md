The reading discipline for building lexicals (whether the unit is a passage/collection-of-verses or a genre-unit/whole-poem):
- **(a) The characteristic is the LENS — work one char at a time.** Take the char and read *for it*.
- **(b) Resolve that char's pairs by reading ACROSS the passage/section, using the morphology of the related verses** — its target/object, source, bearer, seat, manner, coupling are found by reading the passage *for this char*, grounded in the related verses' morphology.
- **(c) It is NOT** a general decomposition of all the spans of all the verses followed by pairing them up. **Pairs are read for the char, never auto-assembled by proximity from a span-sweep.**

**Why:** span-sweep + proximity-pairing is exactly what produced the earlier defective auto-pairs (Strong's-encoded, mechanically adjacent, not read). The researcher: "the char is the lens… read across the passage using the morphology of the related verses to resolve the pairs of the char. It is not about the general reading of all the span of all the verses and then to start pairing them up" (2026-07-09).

**How to apply:** iterate characteristics; for each, read its verse + related passage verses and resolve its dimensions/pairs intentionally; every pair endpoint is a **span-id**; mechanical only where it genuinely satisfies, else read the context ([[project_reread_success_gates_and_scored_audit]] Q2).

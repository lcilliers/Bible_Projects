FOUNDATIONAL (researcher, 2026-06-27). Master record: `Workflow/methodology/wa-METHOD-SYNTHESIS-verse-fanout-multicontributor-v1-20260627.md`.

**Lexical relatedness ≠ conceptual relatedness.** A term/span index finds only **same-word** verses; the inner being lives at the **concept/dynamic** level, which crosses vocabulary. Proof: my mechanical spiderweb found 6 `perek` verses for "ruthlessness"; Logos found ~20+ (Amos, Micah, Ps 10, James 5, Isa…) **none using perek**. So relatedness must be **inferred (read), not matched**.

**The spiderweb is MULTI-CONTRIBUTOR** — related evidence bound to a verse by:
- **Mechanical lexical** (`verse_evidence_index` + `verse_term_index`) — precise, verse-anchored FLOOR; same-lemma; rarity-weighting ranks meaningfulness. Narrow but certain.
- **AI conceptual synthesis** (Logos, Claude Chat, this assistant) — cross-vocabulary REACH; same-dynamic verses in different words. Must distinguish **STATED (verse-explicit) vs SYNTHESIZED (inference)** and ground each to its verse. (Claude Chat models this; Logos blends the two = caution.)
- **Scholarship** — corroboration; cite + ground.

**Governing discipline (all contributors):** mark STATED vs INFERRED — this IS the observation-with-status unit, and the guardrail against fabrication. Each contribution grounded back to a verse, tagged provenance + status. **Convergence across independent contributors = validity** (three witnesses agreeing on heart-as-source · fear-of-God-as-restraint · fear-of-threat-as-trigger firms the focus point; cross-links to Gen 6:5).

**Consequence:** lexical-only is too narrow, concept-only is ungrounded — the fan-out needs both, married: mechanical grounds, inference reaches, scholarship corroborates.

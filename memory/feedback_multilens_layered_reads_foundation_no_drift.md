Prompted by the Exo 1:13 comparison (term-driven "Ruthlessness" vs the span-depth EXO-01 reading): the two analyses **converged on six findings independently** and each **caught what the other missed** — which the researcher named as *an important component of the study's methodology*.

**The principle:**
- **Multiple reads through various lenses assemble the comprehensive understanding.** No single lens is the whole. The by-book/passage/verse span-depth read is ONE lens (a *horizontal* read of the passage's interiors); a per-motif vertical drill (the old fan-out / the new hardening-ledger), a per-characteristic study, cross-book synthesis, etc. are OTHER lenses. The comprehensive picture is the *assembly* of them.
- **The study is NOT complete at the end of the by-book reading.** The by-book read **lays the FOUNDATION**; later lenses build on it. Finishing Genesis/Exodus/etc. at span depth is *not* finishing the study — it is completing the substrate.
- **The current depth is right for THIS phase.** The researcher confirmed the span-depth/rigour I am working at is "more or less right." Therefore: **maintain it, do NOT drift** (don't lighten, don't start templating, don't skip the distinction-discipline).
- **Validate after every section to prevent drift.** Keep running the check-back gate ([[project_passage_reading_checkback_gate]]) on every passage, and the block-level 0-non-T2-gaps + all-filed verification after every block. The validation *is* the anti-drift control.

**Why:** depth compounds — the foundation read must be deep and rigorous because every later lens builds on it; a shallow foundation cannot be repaired by later synthesis. And drift is gradual and invisible without an external check — hence the mandatory gate.

**How to apply:**
- Keep the span-depth reading exactly as calibrated (verse-first, every span distinct, stated/inferred, multi-interior where an antagonist exists, whole-book threads, no imported meaning). Don't lighten for speed.
- Run the gate after EVERY passage; re-verify EVERY block (0 gaps + all filed). Never file-and-forget.
- Treat the by-book read as *foundation-laying*, not *the answer* — expect later lenses (motif-ledgers, characteristic studies, cross-book synthesis) to be layered on top. The hardening-ledger is a model of a second lens run alongside the reading.

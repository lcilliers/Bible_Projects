The candidate-characteristic **seed must NOT be derived from the registry** (word_strong /
registry→strong). Three reasons the researcher gave (2026-07-18):

1. The **registry is incomplete** and new inner-being words are still discovered — a
   registry-derived seed can only mirror the registry, never reveal what it is missing.
2. The independent seed is an important **double control on registry completeness**: a lemma the
   seed judges inner-being but that no registry word carries = a **candidate missing registry
   word**. Grounding the seed in the registry destroys that control.
3. The seed is **over-inclusive by design — potential, not definite**: it marks lemmas that
   *could* be a characteristic *in some context*. The **definitive test is the LEXICAL stage**
   (Axis B — each span's role re-evaluated in context), not the seed. It must stay open to
   **continuous learning** (reads that surface new IB strongs feed back — read-emergent).

**How to apply:** build the seed as an over-inclusive meaning net over an *independent corpus
lemma inventory* (registry-direct match as ONE layer + curated synonyms + IB judgement), not from
word_strong. Report seed−registry as registry-completeness signals. Let the lexical read decide
role per occurrence. Reinforces [[feedback_characteristic_list_validates_not_imputes]],
[[project_candidate_characteristic_seed_and_role_model]].

**Inclusion criterion — confirmed by researcher 2026-07-19** (do not re-litigate): include any lemma
that in **any sense, any word-form (verb/noun/adj — "to be devoted" = devotion), or any figurative /
idiomatic / analogical / allegorical use** expresses an inner state, disposition, faculty, movement
or moral condition — **of a human OR any other being (God, spirits)**; the human-vs-other decision
(Screen 0) is made LATER at the read, never at the seed. Governing rule (config `char.seed-over-
inclusive`): *"a false positive is caught by a gate; a false negative is invisible forever"* — so
bias hard to inclusion; borderline → in. Imagery/analogy is captured (see the 2026-07-14 analogy
qualifier-typing discourse). **The class also includes AGENTS that act ON the inner being** —
supernatural agents whose biblical role is to tempt/deceive/torment/ensnare it: Satan, spirits,
demons, idols/false-gods, occult powers (medium/divination/sorcery). They are load-bearing nodes in
the inner-being web, not external referents → seed them (researcher, 2026-07-19; corrected my initial
"entity-name = drop"). Open boundary calls left to researcher: human external adversaries
(enemy/foe/oppressor role-words) in-or-out, and bare divine NAMES (arena) vs divine dispositions (in). **The seed is keyed at the LEMMA but IB-membership lives at the SENSE**
(e.g. "clean" = moral purity IN / ritual cleanness NOT; "flesh"/sarx; "cord/hope"/tiqvah) — the
structural quality ceiling; ties to [[project_ib_characteristic_meaning_keyed]].

**Seed quality pass (2026-07-19):** full judgement over all 11,781 lemma_inventory glosses found the
migrated registry-derived basis (1,732 candidates) was **~8–10% short** — **~150 solid genuine-IB
misses** (whole dispositions: sluggard, simple/naive, self-control/enkrateia, piety/eusebeia, virtue,
backsliding, thanksgiving/eucharisteo) plus ~23 lean-include borderlines. It was also **inconsistent**
(Hebrew "thanks" yadah/todah IN, Greek eucharisteo OUT). Filed + checkable: `iba/app/docs/seed-
quality-review-greek-v1-20260719.md` + `seed-quality-review-hebrew-aramaic-v1-20260719.md`.

FOUNDATIONAL REFRAME (researcher, 2026-06-27). Full record: `Workflow/methodology/wa-focus-points-and-scripture-as-data-source-v1-20260627.md`; science basis: `inner-being-behavioural-science-discussion.md`.

**The study =** *how Scripture expresses the workings of the inner being.* **Scripture is the DATA-SOURCE layer** (where the workings are described). Task: describe those workings **accurately, comprehensively, without bias, without making them into what they are not** (not a part-list).

**The unit is a FOCUS POINT, not a term/characteristic.** Researcher's 7 properties: (a) not a term/characteristic/gloss-collation; (b) not distinctive/standalone; (c) doesn't behave the same across focus points; (d) not present in all inner beings; (e) changes/comes-and-goes/inconsistent; (f) cannot be observed/logged/counted/analysed directly; (g) assessed only via secondary evidence — Bible verses describing its operation + scientific studies of its aspects.

**What follows (governing):**
- A focus point is a **latent, emergent, dynamic configuration** known only by **manifestation** — NOT a fixed hidden thing. Matches science's move away from faculty/parts (constructed/enacted states).
- **METHOD REVERSAL — infer, don't extract.** The focus point can't be logged (f); only its **operations** (what a verse describes it *doing*) can. The focus point is INFERRED from a constellation of operations. (Why faculty/valence/origin failed — they tried to record the latent thing as observable.)
- **Validity = CONVERGENCE, not consistency/frequency.** (c)(d)(e) forbid frequency/uniformity as a test. Valid when two independent witnesses converge: Scripture's described operation + science's recorded aspect.
- **Science = secondary corroborator.** Existing science extracts HOLD + principles apply (though framed as "characteristics"); REVISIT once we can frame the questions under the focus-point framing. Insight: assuming characteristics hid that science already tripped over and abandoned the list-of-things view.
- **Registry/term/characteristic/cluster = DEBUNKED as the analytical anchor** — served only to surface the initial corpus; not the basis for research. Find these FROM the verses, with the researcher — never impose.

**Behavioural warning (recurring):** stop racing to build tools on a list-of-things frame (terms/families/clusters). That frame is debunked; building on it deepens the error.

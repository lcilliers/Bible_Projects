GOVERNING bias-correction (researcher, 2026-06-24, caught on M12 Purity). I read M12 through the **M10 sin lens** ("positive pole of the sin-field", pole-pairs) and on that frame **exported or dismissed every term that wasn't purity-or-its-opposite**: called na.tan "give" (1,069×) NOISE; routed tsur/morfoō "to form" to M11/M38 renewal; sent the defilement terms to M10c. ALL WRONG — the DB shows them **deliberately M12-owned & sense-curated** (na.tan: only the "give" sense active, 10 others deleted, registry "faithfulness"; tsur "to form" sense-split from confine→M23/provoke; tum.ah/miainō/anosios are M12's own impurity/passion/mercy registry words). I had even skipped na.tan's nephesh×46/lev×27 co-seated signal because I'd pre-judged it noise.

**Why:** the cluster STRUCTURE is deliberate — terms are not in a cluster by accident; the cluster was formed so they belong together. Highlighting a term's relation to OTHER clusters while NOT understanding it in its own cluster **hollows out the cluster** and is a recurring failure of mine (cf. [[feedback_check_governance_layers_not_just_pipeline]], the dual-view reframe).

**How to apply:**
1. **In-cluster first** — understand every owned term in THIS cluster's context before any cross-cluster note. Cross-cluster relation (incl. dual-view) is the LAST observation, not the first.
2. **Frequency ≠ noise** — a high-frequency or generic-gloss term (na.tan "give") is a candidate CENTRE of a characteristic; read its faculty-seated occurrences, not the gloss.
3. **No exporting** — a term appearing in / relating to another cluster does NOT remove it from this one. The negative pole, "odd" verbs, etc. are this cluster's content.
4. **Don't impute a cluster's scope from its NAME** — M12 "Purity, Holiness, Consecration" is actually the CLEAN/CLEAR/WHOLE/GENUINE/SET-APART inner being (incl. giving, forming, privative "un-vice" virtues, AND its own defilement pole). Derive scope from the full owned-term composition, grounded.
5. **Registry attribution is provenance, not meaning** — "na.tan via faithfulness" = surfaced under that study, not "giving = faithfulness". Why a term is in the cluster is answered by READING the verses.
6. **Verify ownership before any "artifact/noise/mis-owned" claim** — query mti_terms (cluster_code, owning_registry_fk, status); my "recovery artifact" claim for na.tan was false and unverified.

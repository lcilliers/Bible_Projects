**2026-06-29:** added a 13th IB-verse dimension, **D13 Cohabitation**, to be assessed for **every** characteristic/operation. It was fed back from D11 Discovery (the revision mechanism) via obs #108 on the ruthlessness track.

- **D13 question:** *what other operations does this one keep company with — and what shared root does that company reveal?* It captures the **cross-corpus co-occurrence** of an operation with other operations (the family it travels in); as it accumulates, the **common root** the family points to.
- **The root EMERGES, is not imposed:** it will transpire that the root of the vice-family is **the evil one**, collectively **"evil"** — but that is read off the verses as cohabitation accumulates, never asserted a priori (verse-grounded; cf. [[feedback_verse_meaning_grounded_not_imported]]). D13 is the *instrument* that surfaces the root.
- **Boundary vs D9 (Coupling):** D9 = the **in-verse weld** (how an operation is structurally/grammatically bound *here* — manner/partner/object, e.g. `be-perek` binds the Hiphil `abad`). D13 = the **corpus-wide company** it keeps (which other vices recur alongside it), diagnostic of its source. D9 is tight & in-verse; D13 is loose & cross-corpus.
- **Worked seed:** ruthlessness (#108) cohabits with killing (Gen 20:11) + exploiting the weak (Deu 25:18), a family sharing the *absence of the fear of God*. This reframes the focus point toward "**what the absence of the fear of God lets emerge**."

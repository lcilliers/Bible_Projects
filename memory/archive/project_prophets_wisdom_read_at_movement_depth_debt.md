**Evidence-based retrospective, 2026-07-04** (`verse-analysis/_reports/wa-depth-retrospective-prophets-vs-span-method-20260704.md`). Prompted by the researcher asking whether the new span-by-span depth exposes a lack in the prophets work.

**Finding:** across a 6-chapter sample (Jer 17, Isa 1, Isa 6, Eze 18, Psa 19, Psa 119), each filed `lexical_prose_chapter` reading engages only **8–28%** of its chapter's non-T2 evidence verses and **never distinguishes the repeated glosses** (Isa 1: 14 repeats; Eze 18: 19; Psa 119: 42). Concrete misses (Jer 17): the whole **Sabbath/consecration section 17:19–27 untouched**; "heart" ×4 not distinguished; the **incurable (17:9,16) ↔ heal-me (17:14)** interplay passed over.

**Verdict:** the readings are a genuine, valuable **movement layer** — not wrong, not wasted — but they were NOT read at the exhaustive span-by-span, distinction-preserving, interactive-web depth the researcher wants (see [[feedback_resist_grouping_preserve_distinctions]], [[feedback_each_chapter_first_principles_find_the_gems]]). **Coverage ≠ depth**: the old "0 non-T2 gaps" checks proved every verse fell in a passage, NOT that each span was interrogated.

**How to apply:**
- Treat this as an **additive depth-pass** (movement layer stays; a span-by-span pass augments it with missed operations, distinguished repeats, secondary webs). Do NOT rebuild ([[feedback_no_rework_paid_twice]]).
- **Do new books (Genesis onward) at span depth now** so the debt stops growing; the narrative method + GEN-01 (prose_section 915) is the depth benchmark.
- Sequence the prophet depth-pass by richness (many distinct+repeated glosses, low touch-rate first); acrostics like Psa 119 lowest.
- The minor/major-prophets syntheses remain valid summaries of the movement layer.

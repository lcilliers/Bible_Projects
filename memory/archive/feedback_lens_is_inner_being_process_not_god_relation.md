The study's object is the **inner being's PROCESSES and how they OPERATE** — their movements, associations, interlocking, emergence. God-interaction is the major **arena** those processes run in, but it is the **field, not the point**. It is easy to drift into making "the soul's relationship with God" the headline finding — resist that shift.

Correct lens: the **OPERATION is the finding**, with the God-relation as *where* it happens. E.g. not "the soul's longing for God's face" but *how desire consolidates onto a single object*; not "trusting God" but *how trust is grounded, and what it produces (help → joy)*; other operations seen so far: the heart **answering a call from within** (Ps 27:8), **grief spreading through the whole person** (Ps 22, 31), **security mis-attributed** — gift vs presumption (Ps 30:6 vs 16:8/21:7), the self **depositing/entrusting** itself (Ps 31:5), **panic distorting the inner being's judgment** ("I am cut off" overturned, Ps 31:22).

**Why:** researcher caution (2026-07-02): the God–human interaction is very important and a large portion of the data, but the focus of the study is inner-being processes and how they operate; the lens shifts easily.

**How to apply:** in each Phase-2 reading, foreground the inner-being **mechanic/movement** in the section framing and the "what X says about the inner being" bullets — name the operation (consolidation, answering, attachment, mis-attribution, entrustment, distortion, dissolution, restoration) and treat the God-relation as its arena, not its subject.

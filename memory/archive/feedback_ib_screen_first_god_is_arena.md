The study's lens is the **human inner being**. The **first assessment** on every candidate
characteristic — before role, before the ledger — is **Screen 0: is this span about the
human inner being at all?**

- Human inner faculty/state/disposition/inner-driven act (psalmist, the wicked, mankind), or the
  human's **response to God** (trust, fear, hope, love, take-refuge, rejoice, cry, wait; soul/heart/spirit)
  → **characteristic**.
- **Wholly God's** — his attribute/quality/action (`chesed`, `emunah`, `kavod`/`hadar`, God's
  righteousness/goodness, his `aph` wrath, his saving/redeeming/judging/being-mindful) →
  **qualifier**. It carries no lexical of its own; it enters the human char as **source (D3) /
  target (D7) / quality**. God is the **arena, not the subject**.
- **A qualifier operates ON a characteristic — so a God-qualifier is NEVER standalone.** For a
  God-act to be recorded at all, the human IB char it impacts must be found — read **across the
  passage** (God's acts ground a disposition an adjacent verse names: his acting grounds *trust*,
  his upholding grounds the righteous's *security*, his laughing grounds the reader's *fret-not*).
  If a God-span attaches to nothing, the **passage is mis-scoped — widen it**. (Confirmed by
  researcher 2026-07-09: "for them to operate there must be an IB char that these qualifiers work on.")
- A verse of **pure God-content yields NO characteristic** — that is correct and expected. Do
  **not** manufacture a characteristic for God. Whole hymnic passages may have none.
- **Outward glory is NOT inner being → standalone** (confirmed researcher 2026-07-09). The lens is
  inner faculties/dispositions/states, not outward magnificence. So `hod`/`hadar` splendour/majesty,
  a king's regal glory, awesome war-deeds, robes, physical/cosmic imagery → **standalone**, never a
  characteristic — even for a HUMAN king (e.g. Ps45). A person's *moral* dispositions (love of
  righteousness, meekness, gladness, grace of character) ARE inner being; their outward glory is not.

**Why:** in the Psalms re-read I coded ~24% of characteristics with God as bearer (God's chesed,
glory, wrath, "God saves") — inverting the study's focus. This is a restatement of
[[feedback_lens_is_inner_being_process_not_god_relation]] and [[project_focus_points_scripture_as_data_source]]
that I **already held and drifted from** during a fast whole-chapter sweep — the exact quality
regression [[feedback_quality_regression_selfcheck_and_apply_memory]] warns against.

**How to apply:** run Screen 0 first on each candidate; God-bearer spans → qualifier. Then, because
most verses carry no IB char, **do not read whole chapters or chapter-blocks** — anchor on the
surviving human IB chars and read each char's **passage** (its related verses) together
([[feedback_char_driven_read_not_span_sweep]], cycle §4A/§5). Embedded as Screen 0 in the
authoritative cycle instruction (§5 + §11 rule 0) and as a loud `IB-SCREEN WARNING` in
`scripts/_reread_finish_v1`. Decision (2026-07-09): screen **forward** from Ps 37; the done
Psalms corpus (Ps 1–36, 78) is left as-is (not wrong, just extra burden), flagged not reworked —
see [[project_reread_success_gates_and_scored_audit]] and the correction doc

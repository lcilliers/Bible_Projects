FOUNDATIONAL (researcher, 2026-06-27). Doc: `Workflow/methodology/wa-inner-being-reading-questions-first-method-v1-20260627.md`.

**The gap (why mechanical ≠ inner-being reading):** direction. Mechanical reads FORWARD/surface/extraction — "is an inner term *named*? if not → None." Inner-being reads BACKWARD/inference — "this act happened; what inner reality had to be there to produce it?" The inner being is almost never on the surface; it is the **source the act expresses**. The act = manifestation (observable); the inner being = latent driver (inferred) — the focus-point profile. The mechanical layer logs the manifestation and mistakes it for the meaning. (Proven on Gen 25:5 give-all, Gen 9:4 life-in-blood, Gen 6:8 found-favour, Exo 1:13 ruthless — all read "None" mechanically, all inner-being-profound.)

**Three kinds of meaning the mechanical read misses:** (1) acts that EXPRESS a driver (give→inner release; ruthless→inner hardness); (2) STATEMENTS about the nature/seat of the inner being (life-in-blood); (3) directly-named inner states (heart sick) — the only kind it caught. Consequence: inner being is behind FAR more verses than tagged terms admit; reading = inference not extraction; **morphological simplicity ≠ inner-being depth** (different axes).

**THE METHOD — questions first:**
- **Round 1 = surface the unanswered inner-being QUESTIONS per verse, in context. Do NOT resolve.** No forced/fabricated meaning (that is what extraction kept getting wrong).
- **The study is a continuing DEBATE, refined through every related instance.** Meaning emerges by accumulation/refinement across instances, never settled from one verse, always revisable.
- **Focus points emerge as the recurring questions/debates** the corpus keeps circling (latent/emergent — matches the 7 properties).
- **Validity by convergence** across instances (and later science), never frequency or a single verse.
- The hybrid/mechanical layer (morphology→operation) is the SUBSTRATE feeding the questioning — NOT the inner-being reading; never mistake it for that.

**Behavioural rule:** when reading a verse for inner being, ask "what does this act/state require or reveal of the inner being, and what does it leave unanswered?" — surface the questions; do not extract a named term and stop.

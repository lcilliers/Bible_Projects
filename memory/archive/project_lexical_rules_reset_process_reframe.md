**THE PIVOT (researcher, 2026-06-24, behavioural-science-informed):** the inner being does NOT decompose into clean parts. Six months of "logical units" failing the same way (terms bleed across boundaries, same word different work per verse, groupings collapse next passage) = INFORMATION: a part-sorting method on a non-part subject. Faculties / object-types / "units" are the most part-committed frames — they presuppose discrete powers/bins. **Reframe: stop asking "what part/category/faculty does this word NAME?" → ask "what does this word DO in the verse?"** (what brings it about · acts on · how operates · produces · turns into · moves with). Functional/relational/PROCESS, not taxonomic. Matches biblical language (concrete, verbal, relational — heart DOING not heart-IS); = the project's earliest insight returning ("meaning lives on the edges/web of facts"). Source doc: `Workflow/methodology/inner-being-behavioural-science-discussion.md`.

**(a) RULES RESET — `Workflow/Instructions/wa-lexical-analysis-rules-reset-v1-20260624.md`** (supersedes the FRAMING of 01b: object-type grid/Part C, intrinsic-faculty field, type-bins, units; RETAINS 01b's observable bedrock — measure layer/sense/mode):
- PRIMARY layer = FUNCTIONAL/RELATIONAL considerations (§3): antecedent/cause · operation/how · object/target · manner/intensity · produces/effect · immediate-response · transition/becomes · relational-web/binding · direction. Each an OBSERVATION of the verse (P4 resolved/NONE/UNRESOLVED, P5 cite).
- faculty REFRAMED (§4) to the OBSERVABLE: "does the verse directly mention/address a faculty or seat, and which" (presence of a faculty/seat lemma or faculty-operation verb) — NOT "what faculty is this term" (the retired ontology).
- RETIRED as framing: type-as-objectkind, object_type-as-bin, characteristic/state/expression as imposed categories. They may RE-EMERGE only as observed PATTERNS (§6 attractors): a state/characteristic = a recurring functional shape, named post-hoc, held loosely, allowed to shade into each other — NEVER the starting grid.

**(b) DISCOVERY-LOOKOUT (mandatory per verse, §5 — the emergence engine):** after recording the known considerations, every analysis asks **"what does this verse state or imply about the inner being that the current considerations do NOT capture?"** → surface as a DISCOVERY-FLAG (lexical_note type=discovery + the LOOKOUT register in `wa-lexical-discovery-register-v1`). A flag that recurs/is adopted becomes a NEW consideration applied to ALL verses (back-propagated re-derive). A verse with nothing records "discovery: none" (proves it was looked for). This is how the schema GROWS from the material, not from a fixed upfront grid.

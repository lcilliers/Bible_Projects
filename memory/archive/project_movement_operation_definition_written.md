**The definition, in brief**: read the passage → screen for IB relevance → isolate every IB
operation → for each operation, record subject (the inner being in focus) / operation (state of,
happening in, impacting on, or being impacted by) / source (another operation, being, or physical
thing) / target (same options) — source/target may be singular, multiple, mixed, or absent.
Multiple inner beings in one verse are each considered separately, plus the movement BETWEEN them;
collections (tribe, youths, nation) are a movement to/from a collection, not an individual;
inferred/unnamed parties must be surfaced explicitly, not silently assumed; linkage or its absence
between operations must always surface, never be silently dropped.

[[project_RESET_characteristics_to_movements_changeover]]'s "unit = MOVEMENT not term" needed —
that reset named movement as the new analytical unit but had not yet pinned down what counts as
one operation or how to record it. This document is that pin-down, at least for the IBA app's

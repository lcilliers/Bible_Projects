FOUNDATIONAL operating model (researcher, 2026-06-27). Doc: `Workflow/methodology/wa-verse-fanout-operating-model-v1-20260627.md`.

**The model:** the unit of work is a **VERSE**. From it, **FAN OUT** to all relevant and related evidence until digested — its own data (text, morphology/spans, lexical, findings, context look-back/forward) **AND related verses across the corpus** + their evidence. The researcher **digests** (inference — data→true-meaning is NOT mechanical) into **OBSERVATIONS**: individually recorded, tied to verse + term/span, status **resolved | needs-corroboration**. Observations **accumulate progressively across the entire corpus**; focus points emerge as the recurring ones; the debate refines.

**Supersedes the CLUSTER (M-code) method of analysis.** Cluster = the debunked list-of-things organiser; the organising unit is now the verse and its fan-out web (verse-king, web-shaped). Clusters survive at most as a coarse porous lexical convenience, never the analytical driver.

**Mechanical vs meaning:** the move from data to true inner-being meaning is NOT mechanical (it is the researcher's inference, reading the act back to its driver — the expression/driver gap). BUT mechanical generation of the **in-between steps** (assemble the dossier, surface candidate related verses, retrieve prior observations, propose links) is real and **grows more valuable** as the observation base grows. **Valuable data is NOT discarded** — "set aside" = not-the-meaning, not delete; morphology/lexical/l2_meaning/findings are the evidence the fan-out digests.

**Process:** pick verse → fan out (mechanical assists) → digest (researcher) → record observations → accumulate corpus-wide. **Researcher-driven, step by step initially**; mechanical assistance grows.

**Open (settle by doing):** what counts as "related" for the fan-out; the observation record's fields + DB home; how mechanical assists without re-importing extraction; disposition of cluster/finding tables (kept as evidence, not the spine).

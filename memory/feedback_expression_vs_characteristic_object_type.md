Researcher insight 2026-06-24 (developing the still-emerging object-type lens), grounded on na.tan "give" in M12.

**Principle:** an EXPRESSION is an observable act-lemma that MANIFESTS an inner characteristic. The act is external; the inner-being content is what BINDS to it — the **manner** (freely/willingly/whole-heartedly vs grudgingly), the **reason** (love, che.sed, faithfulness), the **when** and **how**. Study an expression by FOLLOWING THE BINDING (to the manner-term + the characteristic it co-expresses); NEVER treat the act as the characteristic, and where the manner is uncaptured in the lexical (empty `how`), SAY SO — do not infer the characteristic from the bare act. This is the disciplined version of M10's failed "expression/outflow" object-kind (which wrongly claimed the act diagnoses the heart).

**Why:** giving is the clearest case. na.tan's bare lexical = transactional (give/gave/given; object hand/land/inheritance; how=0%). But 27 occurrences carry the MANNER in the verse ("give freely… heart not grudging" Deu 15:10; "offer willingly" 1Ch 29:14; freewill offering; liberally; whole heart) and 56 co-seat with che.sed(M05)/blessing(M39)/heart(M47) — the binding. The manner-term often belongs to ANOTHER cluster (willing-heartedness = M09; love/che.sed = M05). So giving anchors no characteristic itself; it is the vehicle through which generosity/willing-heartedness/che.sed/faithfulness become visible.

**How to apply:**
- Object-type test (step 4 of the corrected sequence, [[project_cluster_review_backlog_and_m12_method_20260624]]) now has THREE readings: CHARACTERISTIC (faculty-in-operation) · STATE/CONDITION · EXPRESSION (act manifesting a characteristic). Decide from per-verse-varying evidence + the binding.
- The act/state grammar (a legit lemma property) maps onto expression/characteristic: act-lemmas (cleanse/consecrate/purify/give/anoint) = candidate EXPRESSIONS; state-lemmas (clean/holy/blameless/pure) = candidate CHARACTERISTICS/CONDITIONS. But TEST per unit — an act can be a characteristic-in-operation (e.g. consecrating as devotion acting), not always an expression; the BINDING evidence settles it, not the grammar.
- Keep it EMERGING — applied to na.tan (clear), proposed for other act-lemmas as a test; don't force every act into "expression".
- Cross-cluster bindings (M09/M05/M39) are the LAST note (in-cluster first, [[feedback_understand_term_in_cluster_not_export]]).

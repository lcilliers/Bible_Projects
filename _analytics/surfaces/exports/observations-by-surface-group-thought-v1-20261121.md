## Group 11: supposing, or holding something to be so

**Surfaces (8):** imagines, imagining, suppose, supposing, think, thinking, thinks, thought

**What the group is:** The surfaces that state a supposition, an estimate or a view held about how something is: that it is so, that it will be, that it was; the verses give the content of what is held (often as quoted speech to oneself) and often show the supposition acted on or corrected.

### Stage 3, step 1.1: what the subgroup means (1)

- **22306** [group-definition; stated] The surfaces of this group share one kind of meaning: to hold something to be so. The codes carry it as thinking and supposing (G1380, G3543, G3633, G5274, G5282), as saying within oneself (H0559, whose Qal sense includes 'to say in one's heart, to think'), as reckoning and imagining (H2803J, H1819) and as the case put forward with perhaps (H0194). ## Group 12: attending to a matter and turning it over

**Surfaces (10):** considered, gives thought, meditate, meditates, meditation, musing, pondering, ponders, weigh, weighing

**What the group is:** The surfaces that name giving attention to a matter and going over it: looking at it, considering it, pondering and musing on it, meditating on it, weighing it up; the verses give the matter attended to (a vision, the law, a work, a field, a saying) and the going over is sustained, not a single estimate.

### Stage 3, step 1.1: what the subgroup means (1)

## Group 13: reasoning and giving grounds

**Surfaces (3):** reason, reasoned, reasoning

**What the group is:** The surfaces that name reasoning with others by giving grounds, and the ground or cause given: arguing a case in speech, and the reason why a thing is so or is done; the verses show the exchange of grounds (in a synagogue, before a ruler) or the cause stated for an act.

### Stage 3, step 1.1: what the subgroup means (1)

- **22308** [group-definition; stated] The surfaces of this group share one kind of meaning: giving a ground, and arguing a case by grounds. The codes carry it as reasoning and discussing (G1256, G1261, H3198 in the sense of arguing a case), and as the ground or cause itself (G0156 cause and charge, G1352 therefore, G1223 and G5484 because of, H1697M a matter that accounts for, H3651C therefore, H2600 without cause). The verses show the exchange of grounds or the ground stated: Act 17:2, Act 24:25, Act 10:21, Rom 15:22, Isa 1:18, Job 1:9. *(Gen.41.31, Gen.47.13, Num.18.32, Josh.5.4, 1Sam.20.29, 1Kgs.11.27, Job.1.9, Prov.3.30 +28 more)*

# Concepts (v1) compared with the narrative chapters: a suggested alignment (#2343, 2026-10-31)

Read-only. Source: concept_domain (14), concept (101) and their pair counts; narrative_chapter and narrative_section (level 2 headings) in iba/app/db/iba.db. **The matches below are my reading of the headings, inferential (B:4), not confirmed.** Nothing was written to the database.

## 1. The two bodies are organised on different axes
- **Narrative:** by seat (ch 2 to 9: life and death, heart, soul, spirit, flesh, conscience), then by activity (10.1 to 10.11: feeling, knowing, remembering, thinking, speaking, wanting, choosing, right and wrong, relating to God, relating to others, hiding and disclosing), then four key characteristics (10.12 to 10.15: fear, anger, humility, pride), then cross-cutting chapters (11 other sides, 12 going wrong, 13 other beings, 14 made new).
- **Concepts:** by theme of the word. Four domains are feelings, three are mental or moral, and the rest are a mixture: Relational (28 concepts, 2,958 pairs), God and community, Person and life, Trust and faith.
- A domain is neither an activity nor a seat nor a characteristic, so domains cannot simply become chapters.

## 3. Concepts that show the chapters need reorganising
- **Moral character (13 concepts, 1,345 pairs)** has no home: 10.8 Right and wrong has only an opening, no sections. Righteousness, truth, lying, sin, wickedness, holiness have nowhere to go.
- **Hypocrisy, hiding, concealing (110 pairs)**: 10.11 Hiding and disclosing has only an opening.
- **Trust and faith (4 concepts, 252 pairs)**, **Wisdom, folly, teaching (3 concepts)** and **Seeking and inquiring** have no chapter.
- **Relational is 28 concepts with 2,958 pairs**, against two chapters (10.9, 10.10). It splits naturally into God-ward (Prayer, Worship, Covenant, Judgment, Calling, Obeying) and human-ward (Quarrel, Hatred, Kindness, Mercy, Oppression) and a third group of acts of rescue and forgiveness that run both ways.
- **God and community** (Prophecy, Wealth, Nobility, Laying on of hands) is a misfit: wealth belongs with 10.6 "Possessions" and "Precious"; the rest has no chapter.

## 4. Chapters that show the concepts need adjusting
- **10.1 Feeling (26 sections)** gathers fear, anxiety, delight and losing heart, which the concepts spread across four domains. A single Feeling chapter and four feeling domains are two ways of cutting the same material.
- **Fear and Anger are chapters of their own (10.12, 10.13)**, but Fear is one of 5 concepts in fear-and-awe and Anger one of 4 in anger-and-grief. They are characteristics (key words), the others are activities.
- **Chapters 4 to 6, 8** treat the seats; the concepts hold only one concept for all four seats.
- **Madness and recklessness**, **Peace, rest, quiet** and **Sloth** do not appear in any chapter heading.
- The concepts "Inner seat" and "Relational" suggest a level between domain and concept, which the narrative's Part 10 already has (activities, then key characteristics).

## 5. Suggested alignment (a starting point for you to change)
1. Keep the narrative's three kinds: seats (ch 2 to 8), activities (10.1 to 10.11), key characteristics (10.12 to 10.15). Re-cut the concepts to land inside them, not the other way round: the 14 domains become a working index, not chapters.
2. Concepts that match one to one go straight in: remembering, fear, anger, humility, pride, flesh, life and death, speaking, choosing.
3. Split Relational into God-ward and human-ward, and send the first group to 10.9 and the second to 10.10.
4. Give Moral character and Hiding and disclosing real content (10.8 and 10.11 are empty), or fold them into 12 and 10.10.
5. Add chapter homes, or an explicit "no home yet" list, for Trust and faith, Wisdom and folly, Seeking, Peace, Madness.
6. Treat Feeling's four domains as the sub-structure of 10.1.

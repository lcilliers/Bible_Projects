## 3. Three candidate outlines
### Candidate 1: The line (blueprint, deviation, restoration)
1. **Designed:** the person made in God's image; the seats (heart, soul, spirit, flesh, conscience) as the design. Chapters 2 to 9.
2. **Working to the design:** the activities as they run when aligned (feeling, knowing, thinking, speaking, wanting, choosing). Chapter 10.1 to 10.11.
3. **Deviation:** the same characteristics changed in nature and impact: pride, hardening, lying, sin, wickedness, hiding. Chapters 12 and part of 10.12 to 10.15.
4. **Restored:** made new. Chapter 14.
- **Concepts:** each concept is placed by where it sits against the design (aligned, deviated, restored); many concepts come as pairs (righteousness and wickedness, truth and lying, humility and pride).
- **Gain:** the 11 Moral character concepts with no home (10.8 empty) land in "deviation". **Risk:** the whole study then reads as one doctrine, and it is the weakest on verses (see above).

### Candidate 2: The rings (where the inner being reaches)
1. **Ring 1:** the person's own inner life: seats, feeling, knowing, thinking, wanting, choosing.
2. **Ring 2:** with God: faith, trust, prayer, worship, covenant, fear of God, judgment, repenting.
3. **Ring 3:** with others: kindness, mercy, quarrel, hatred, authority, forgiving.
4. **Ring 4:** the unseen and creatures: Other beings.
- **Concepts:** Relational's 28 concepts split naturally between Ring 2 and Ring 3, and 15 of them are among the 50 with no home. Trust and faith (4, no home) is Ring 2.
- **Gain:** simple, close to the project's earlier model. **Risk:** circles are static; it shows where, not how it moves.

### Candidate 3: The web (hubs and paths)
1. **Movements:** the paths (thought to deed, feeling to deed) as the main structure, chapter 10.0 enlarged.
2. **Hubs:** fear, anger, humility, pride, desire, trust as the points where paths cross (10.12 to 10.15 already do this for four).
3. **Causes and effects:** each concept recorded with what it leads to and what leads to it.
- **Concepts:** concepts become nodes with links; the unanalysed ones are simply new nodes, so the narrative does not unravel when they arrive.
- **Gain:** answers the worry that new concepts break the arrangement. **Risk:** hardest to write as a readable sequence.

## 4. Suggested joint story (a layered reading of all three)
- **Rings give the where** (four spaces the inner being reaches).
- **The web gives the how** (movements, impacts, causes, effects crossing the rings).
- **The blueprint line gives the measure** (aligned with the design, or deviated, and what the deviation changes).
- Chapters would then follow: the design and its seats; the rings in turn, each told through its paths and hubs; deviation as what changes along every path; made new.

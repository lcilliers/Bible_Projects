## Changes since the first draft
- **Relational** is its own domain: concepts about how one party stands toward another.
- **Other beings** is new: angels, Satan/Devil/dragon, demons, mediums. These are cluster **M85 Other Beings**, written to iba.db on 2026-10-31 (24 strongs moved from T4/T9/T2; divine terms stay in T7). Human envoys sit under Relational.
- **Spirit** is not yet placed: the spirit strongs stay in M47 and a dual-tagged subgroup is deferred.

## Points for you to rule on
1. Concepts with a single cluster (e.g. Anger and wrath, Sorrow and lament, Strength and power) have no cross-cluster partner; keep, or fold into a wider concept?
2. Outward concepts (Authority and kingship, Wealth, Prophecy and signs, Covenant and assembly) are only loosely inner being.
3. A few subgroups fit two concepts; each sits in its first fit (for example "Good (a quality)", "Cursing", "Thanksgiving").

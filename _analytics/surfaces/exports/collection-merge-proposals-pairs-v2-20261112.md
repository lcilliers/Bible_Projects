# Collections that overlap by half or more: my rulings by meaning (pilot of plan step 2), version 2

Read-only. Source: the 55 pairs of collections where the shared words (single-word core, synonym and opposite members) are half or more of the smaller collection, 2026-11-12. I read each pair's member words and decided by what the words mean. I did not read verses for this pass, so every ruling is inferential (B:4) and the ones marked "check" are the ones I would not apply without the verses. Nothing is changed in the database.

## B. Keep apart, and remove the opposite membership (an opposite sits once)

| collections | what I would change |
|---|---|
| lament and gladden | grief and joy are two collections. The joy words stay in gladden, the grief words in lament; remove each from the other as an "opposite". |
| hope and doom | doom is ruin and dread, not the opposite of hope. Remove "hope" from doom. The standalone opposite "despair" sits in hope. |
| faint and mighty | weak words stay in faint; remove them from mighty as opposites. |
| commend and chide | praise and rebuke are two collections; remove the cross-opposite entries. |
| shut and open | each keeps its own words; remove the cross-opposite entries. |
| empty and fill | each keeps its own words; remove the cross-opposite entries. |
| faithless and loyalty | faithless keeps unfaithful and faithlessness; remove them from loyalty as opposites. |
| roar and wail | the wail words (wailed, wailing) move from howl to lament. |
| instruct | split: the teaching words (teach, instruct, train, impart, guide, explain, inform, instruction) combine with teach; the command words (command, order, direct, assign, tell) go to command. |
| love and taste | "taste" carries abhor and loathe as opposites, which does not fit; remove them. The hate words belong with the hate collection if it has its own members (check). |

## What this does not show

These are decisions from the member words and my knowledge of what the words mean, without the verses; the story test (does a collection tell a coherent story) is a light estimate only. A merge changes the members and the name of a collection, so each is applied in a small batch, on a copy, with the old rows kept as history.

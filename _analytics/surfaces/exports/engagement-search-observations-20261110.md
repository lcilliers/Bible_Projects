## find — use / object — observation 21734 — 30 verses

'find' is here an act whose object is the inner being (God searching hearts, the spirit searching the innermost parts), or what is found is an inner state of the other (favour, mercy, rest, enjoyment, cheer); it bears on the inner being without being the operation.

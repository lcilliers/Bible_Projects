# Collections that still overlap: my rulings by meaning, batch 2 (plan step 2, continued)

Read-only. After batch 1 (BUILD 498) 251 collections remain. 298 pairs still share a word; this file covers the 37 where the shared words are a third or more of the smaller collection, or are three or more words. I read each pair's members and decided by what the words mean; I did not read verses, so every ruling is inferential (B:4) and the ones marked "check" are not applied without the verses. Nothing is changed in the database. Words still in several collections that are not from a close pair are step 3 and follow when these are settled (the work is not linear: each batch is measured again).

## Why this addendum exists: a coverage gap

H4172A (*môrāʾ*, fear, terror) is tagged M01 and has **11 hits**.
- Unit 2 read **only one** of them (Isa 8:13), because the unit 2 scope listed H4172A with one hit, Isa 8:13.
- Unit 3's scope was "every Hebrew and Aramaic M01 entry not read in units 1 and 2". H4172A counted as already read, so its other **10 hits** were never given a face.
- The gap came to light when the four unit pulls were reconciled against `fear-web-pull-v1-20261001.csv` for the cross-ledger overview. The web pull has 1,033 M01 hits; the four units had 1,023. **The 10 missing hits are exactly these.** No other hit is missing.

**Corrected statements:** the unit 4 ledger and claim register v14 said every M01 word had been read once unit 4 was done. That was not so until this addendum. Both have been corrected in place, with a note.

**Data:** `fear-unit3-addendum-H4172A-pull-v1-20261004.csv`. It has 10 rows (Isa 8:13 is left out, because it is already faced in unit 2), and every hit has a face. **With this addendum, units 1–4 account for all 1,033 M01 hits in 916 verses.**

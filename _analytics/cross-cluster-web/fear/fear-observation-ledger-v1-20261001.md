## C. Change of character: what differs between the faces (#1919, for Ch 11 §4)

Stated only as far as the verses go.

| Factor | Verses that show it | basis |
|---|---|---|
| **The object.** Enemy or king (A) vs God (B, D) vs other gods (H) vs parents, a leader, a king held in honour (E) | A/B/D/E/H rows above | S |
| **The direction of the word.** The one who fears (A–D) vs the one or thing that is feared (F: awesome, terrifying, to be feared) | FE-39 | S |
| **Before and after God acts.** The same people, the same root: afraid of the threat, then feared the Lord | Exo 14:10 → 14:31; Jon 1:5, 10 → 1:16 | S |
| **Who speaks "do not fear", and why.** God's "for I am with you" vs Jael's and Absalom's "do not fear" | FE-14, FE-16 | S (the grouping is R) |
| **Felt vs commanded, learned or given.** A moment of fear (A, B) vs a standing that is required, read and learned, taught, put into the heart | FE-21 to FE-24 | S |
| **Heart near or far.** Fear taught by men while "their hearts are far from me" vs fear put "in their hearts" | Isa 29:13; Jer 32:40 | S |
| **One verse, both values** | Exo 20:20; 2Ki 17:33–34 | S |

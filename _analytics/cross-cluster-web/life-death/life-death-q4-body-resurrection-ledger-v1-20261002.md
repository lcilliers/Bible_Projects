## C. Notes the narrative must not smooth over

1. **Flesh and bones / flesh and blood.** Luke 24:39 has the risen Christ saying a spirit "does not have flesh and bones as you see that I have". 1 Corinthians 15:50 says "flesh and blood cannot inherit the kingdom of God". Both are stated. No verse reconciles them, and I would quote both and stop (rule 69).
2. **"Spiritual body."** The verse sets *psychikos* against *pneumatikos* (§A). It does not say what a spiritual body is made of. Any gloss ("not immaterial but Spirit-governed", or the opposite) would be "I read", and I'd suggest leaving it out. Q4-08 is the nearest Scripture comes to showing one.
3. **Between death and rising.** "away from the body and at home with the Lord" (2Cor 5:8) belongs to Q2 / OT-03 ("After death"), not here. Signpost only.

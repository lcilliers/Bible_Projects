# Characteristic mapping — method guardrail (validation, not imputation)

> **Researcher direction, 2026-07-03:** *"we are doing this at the risk of manufacturing stuff that does not exist — this should be noted. We should not force the verses into the list; we should use the list to validate that we are not missing something in verses, without imputing values."*

This note governs all characteristic-list work (the `characteristic` baseline, the `ib_characteristic` registry, coverage mapping, and future books). It is the operative rule; the discovery/registry artefacts are subordinate to it.

## The rule
The characteristic list is a **downstream validation index**, not a source of value.
- **One direction only: verse → list.** Read the verse first (verse-bounded, no import); derive the inner-being operation *from the verse*; then use the list to check for **omissions** — *"did this verse raise something we failed to capture?"*
- **Never list → verse.** Do not hunt a verse for a pre-named list-item, and do not attach a verse to *illustrate* a list-item. Both impute.
- **The list adds nothing to a verse.** A characteristic is "present" only where a *verse-grounded reading* actually captured it. A list-item with no verse support is a **blank to leave empty**, never a value to impute.
- **Note the manufacturing risk when it occurs.** Authoring characteristics + definitions + cited verses (as the 2026-07-03 baseline extension did) can silently over-reach — a definition generalises past what any verse says. Flag it; pare to what the verse alone shows.

## Consequences for the current state
1. **The 78-item baseline extension (199→277) is PROVISIONAL.** Its entries were authored top-down (from M-code clusters) with illustrative verses — the very move the guardrail forbids as a *source*. They are retained as **validation exemplars** only, to be verified verse-by-verse against the already-grounded readings; any that assert more than their verses show are to be pared or dropped. They must not be treated as established characteristics until re-grounded.
2. **Coverage mapping is validation, not a hunt.** To "map Ps/Pro against the baseline," count a characteristic as surfaced ONLY where a verse-grounded reading (the 150 Psalm readings / 150 Proverbs units) already captured it. Then use the list to surface **candidate misses** — list-items with no reading-attestation — and, for each, go back to the *verses* to ask whether the verse genuinely shows it. If yes → it was a real miss (add from the verse). If no → leave blank; the list was over-inclusive there. **No item is counted on the strength of the list alone.**
3. **Emergence stands.** Characteristics emerge bottom-up from verse-readings; the list is their tally, held open, growing and consolidating — it never pre-decides content.

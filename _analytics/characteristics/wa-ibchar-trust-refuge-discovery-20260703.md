# Trust / taking refuge — a discovery document (developing)

> **Mode: DISCOVERY, not summary.** Gather the trust/refuge evidence across Psalms + Proverbs, trace the colours, and hold open the two hardest edges: whether *refuge* (spatial hiding) and *trust* (leaning/valuing) are one operation, and whether the **formation-law** ("you become what you trust") is a property of trust or a law over the whole inner being. Registry: `ib_characteristic.code = trust-refuge`.

## Working sense (held open)
The inner being leaning its security on God — and, crucially, being *re-formed* by what it leans on. Trust is not only a posture but a *forming* relation.

## The evidence, gathered by colour

**Colour 1 — refuge / shelter / running-to (spatial).** "In you, O LORD, do I **take refuge**" (Ps 31:1; 7:1; 11:1; 16:1; 71:1); "**the name of the LORD is a strong tower**; the righteous **runs into it** and is safe" (Pro 18:10); "he is a **shield** to those who take refuge in him" (Pro 30:5); "under his wings you will **find refuge**" (Ps 91:4); "my **rock** and my **fortress**" (Ps 18:2; 62:2). Trust as a *place fled into* — spatial, protective.

**Colour 2 — trust as leaning / reliance (postural).** "**Trust in the LORD with all your heart, and do not lean on your own understanding**" (Pro 3:5); "**commit your way** to the LORD; **trust** in him" (Ps 37:5); "those who **trust** in the LORD are like Mount Zion" (Ps 125:1). Trust as *leaning weight* onto God.

**Colour 3 — trust as valuation / deliberate choice.** "**It is better to take refuge in the LORD than to trust in man**" (Ps 118:8–9); trust argued *from mortality* — "put not your trust in princes… his plans perish" (Ps 146:3–4). Here trust is a *reasoned ranking*, not a mood.

**Colour 4 — trust as self-DISTRUST.** "Do **not lean on your own understanding**" (Pro 3:5); "whoever **trusts in his own mind** is a fool" (Pro 28:26); "not in my bow do I trust" (Ps 44:6). Trust is constituted *negatively* — to trust God is to *stop* trusting the self.

**Colour 5 — mis-placed trust (the object corrupts it).** In **riches** (Ps 52:7; 49:6; 62:10; Pro 11:28); in **princes / man** (Ps 118:8; 146:3); in **idols** — "those who make them **become like them**; so do all who **trust** in them" (Ps 115:8; 135:18); in the self (Colour 4). Trust aimed wrong *topples* the self.

**Colour 6 — trust yielding IMMOVABLE stability (the effect on the self).** "Those who trust in the LORD are like **Mount Zion, which cannot be moved**" (Ps 125:1); "his heart is **firm**, trusting in the LORD… he will **not be afraid**" (Ps 112:7); "I shall **not be moved**" (Ps 16:8). Trust *stabilises* — the truster takes on the fixity of what he leans on.

**Colour 7 — trust CONSTITUTED from the womb.** "On you was I cast **from my birth**, and from my mother's womb you have been my God" (Ps 22:10); "you are my **hope**, my trust, **from my youth**" (Ps 71:5). Trust as *original*, not first chosen — the self trusting before it could decide to.

**Colour 8 — human trust (between people).** "The **heart of her husband trusts in her**" (Pro 31:11); "he who is **trustworthy in spirit** keeps a thing covered" (Pro 11:13). Trust *given to a person* — and its mirror, *being* trustworthy.

## Where it changes colour — the developing picture
The most important shift: **what you trust, you become.** "You become what you trust" (Ps 115:8) is stated of idols but reads as a *law*: dead idols deaden their trusters, and by the same logic the living God enlivens. Colour 6 (trust→immovable-stability) is this law working God-ward — the truster takes on the *fixity of Zion* because he leans on the unmovable. Trust is therefore not a neutral posture but a **conforming** one: it *re-shapes* the self toward its object.

The live edge: **is the formation-law trust's own property, or a law over the whole inner being?** It has its own registry entry (`formation-by-relation`) because it seems to run through *love* (you become what you love) and *company* (you become your company) as well. Trust may be the clearest *instance* of a deeper law rather than its owner.

A second edge: **refuge (Colour 1) and trust (Colours 2–4) may be two operations.** Refuge is *spatial* — fleeing into a tower/rock/wings; trust is *postural/evaluative* — leaning, ranking, self-distrusting. They co-occur but are not obviously the same act. And Colour 7 (trust from the womb) is *neither* chosen nor spatial — it is *constitutional*, a trust the self was *born into*.

## Junctions (boundaries blurring)
- **fear-of-the-lord** — fear-of-man (a snare) is dissolved *by trust* (Pro 29:25; Ps 56:11); trust and the fear of God are near-inseparable in the confidence-pole. Can one *both* fear and trust the same God — and how do they combine?
- **entrustment** — committing the self/plans/grievance to God (Ps 31:5; Pro 16:3; 20:22) looks like trust's *resolving act*. Is entrustment a colour of trust or its own operation?
- **fearlessness** — trust → "I will not be afraid" (Ps 112:7; 56:11). Fruit or facet?
- **rest-stillness** — the trusting heart sleeps (Ps 4:8); trust and rest interlock.
- **formation-by-relation** — see above; the formation-law is the deepest junction.

## Thin / uncertain evidence (noted, not yet weighed)
- **Trust as *casting*.** "**Cast your burden** on the LORD, and he will sustain you" (Ps 55:22) — trust as a *throwing-off* of weight onto God. Junctions with entrustment; a distinct motion (unloading) worth watching.
- **Being trustWORTHY** (Colour 8) as the *reciprocal* inner quality — the self that *can be trusted* (Pro 11:13; 31:11). Thin as inner-being data but a real mirror of the operation.
- **Trust that "waits" / "hopes"** — trust shades into waiting/hope so smoothly (Ps 33:20–22) that the boundary may be artificial.

## Open questions (the debate, left open)
1. Are **refuge** (spatial fleeing-into) and **trust** (leaning/valuing) one operation or two?
2. Is the **formation-law** ("you become what you trust") a property of trust, or a law governing the whole inner being (running also through love and company)? — a candidate *organising principle* for the study.
3. How do **trust from the womb** (constituted, Ps 22:10) and **trust as valuation** (chosen, Ps 118:8) relate — is trust originally given and later ratified?
4. How do **trust and fear** coexist toward the same God — do they combine into one settled orientation, or remain two postures held together?
5. What exactly does trust *do to* the self (Colour 6 — stabilisation)? Is being-formed-by-trust the whole point, making trust the master-operation?

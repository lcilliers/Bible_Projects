# Proverbs 24 — inner-being unit readings (Phase-2 meaning synthesis)

- **Source:** `lexical-model-2026` Phase-1 (v1.1 enriched) for Pro 24, per `segment_unit`. Grounded; *stated* | *inferred*. Lens: the inner-being **operation**.

**PRO-24-faint · 24:10 · [S] — fainting reveals the measure of one's strength.** "**If you faint in the day of adversity, your strength is small**" (v.10). Finding: adversity **exposes** the true size of the inner strength — fainting under pressure *measures* it *(stated)*. Operation: **adversity as the test of inner strength** *(inferred; cross-book w/ Ps 27:13–14 "let your heart take courage")*. *Surfaced:* adversity as the measure of inner strength (new).

**PRO-24-weighs-heart · 24:11,12 · [S, multi] — God weighs the heart and sees the excuse.** "If you say, 'Behold, we did not know this,' **does not he who weighs the heart perceive it?** Does not **he who keeps watch over your soul know it**?" (v.12). Finding — a cornerstone: the plea of ignorance is **seen through** — God *weighs the heart* and *knows* the real motive behind the excuse *(stated)*. Operation: **the pretended-ignorance exposed by the heart-weigher** — the self cannot hide its evasions from the One who weighs the heart *(inferred; the being-known cornerstone, cross-book w/ Ps 44:21 "he knows the secrets of the heart", 139)*. *Surfaced:* God weighs the heart / sees through the excuse (★ shared cornerstone, w/ Ps 139).

**PRO-24-resilience · 24:16 · [S] — the righteous rises again.** "**The righteous falls seven times and rises again**, but the wicked stumble in calamity" (v.16). Finding: righteousness is not the absence of falling but the **capacity to rise** *(stated)*. Operation: **resilience — the repeated rising** *(inferred; cross-book w/ Ps 37:24 "though he fall, he shall not be cast headlong", 34:19)*. *Surfaced:* resilience / rising-again (shared w/ Ps 37).

**PRO-24-enemy-fall · 24:17,18,19,29 · [T, multi] — the heart guarded against gloating and revenge.** "**Do not rejoice when your enemy falls, and let not your heart be glad** when he stumbles… **Fret not** yourself because of evildoers… **Do not say, 'I will do to him as he has done to me'**" (vv.17–19,29). Finding: the inner being is to **govern its response to an enemy's fall** — no *gladness of heart* at it, no *fretting* over evildoers, no *repayment* *(stated)*. Operation: **the heart guarded against schadenfreude and vengeance** — even the *feeling* of gladness at a foe's ruin is refused *(inferred; cross-book w/ Ps 35:13–14, and the not-repaying of 20:22, Ps 37)*. *Hidden:* **the interior policed at the level of *feeling*** (not merely act) — "let not your heart be glad." *Surfaced:* the heart guarded against gloating/revenge (★ shared, cross-book w/ Ps 35).

## Catch-up units (2026-07-04) — loose study-term verses bound to units

**PRO-24-no-envy · Prov 24:1–2 · [S, multi] — the refused envy of the wicked.** "Be **not envious of evil men, nor desire to be with them**, for their **hearts devise violence**, and their lips talk of trouble" (vv.1–2). Finding: the self **refuses to envy or desire the company of the wicked** — because their interior is a violence-devising heart *(stated)*. Operation: **the disciplined redirection of envy/desire away from the evil** — seeing the wicked's inner content (a heart plotting violence) disarms the envy their prosperity might provoke *(inferred; refused envy, thread w/ Ps 37:1, 73:3)*. *Surfaced:* refused envy of the wicked (thread w/ Ps 37:1, 73); the violence-devising heart seen behind the appeal.

**PRO-24-wisdom-builds · Prov 24:3–6 · [D, multi] — wisdom builds, fills, and strengthens.** "By **wisdom a house is built**, and by understanding it is established; by knowledge the rooms are **filled** with precious riches" (vv.3–4); "a **wise man is full of strength**, and a man of knowledge **enhances his might**… in abundance of counsellors there is victory" (vv.5–6). Finding: wisdom is **constructive and empowering** — it builds and fills the house, and multiplies a man's strength *(stated)*. Operation: **wisdom as the interior's building and strengthening power** — understanding establishes what is built; knowledge is might, and counsel wins wars *(inferred; wisdom > brute strength, w/ Ecc 9:16, Pro 21:22)*. *Surfaced:* wisdom as building/strengthening (new — w/ Ecc 9:16); counsel as victory.

**PRO-24-folly-scheme · Prov 24:7–9 · [D, multi] — folly out of reach; the scheming, abominable interior.** "**Wisdom is too high for a fool**; in the gate he does not open his mouth" (v.7); "whoever **plans to do evil will be called a schemer**" (v.8); "the **devising of folly is sin**, and the scoffer is an **abomination**" (v.9). Finding: wisdom is **beyond the fool's reach**, and the **devising of folly is itself sin** — the plotting interior is named schemer, the scoffer abomination *(stated)*. Operation: **the interior judged at the level of its devising** — evil is sin already in the *planning*, before the act; folly is a scheming, not merely a lack *(inferred; sin located in the devising heart, w/ Ecc 8:11, the heart set on evil)*. *Surfaced:* sin in the devising (w/ the scheming-heart thread); wisdom beyond the fool.

**PRO-24-wicked-nofuture · Prov 24:15, 20 · [S, multi] — the wicked's snuffed-out future.** "Lie **not in wait as a wicked man against the dwelling of the righteous**" (v.15); "the **evil man has no future; the lamp of the wicked will be put out**" (v.20). Finding: the wicked's ambush is futile, for **he has no future** — his lamp goes out *(stated)*. Operation: **the foreclosed horizon of the wicked** (the no-future/extinguished-lamp thread, w/ 13:9, Job 18:5–6) *(inferred)*. *Surfaced:* the wicked's snuffed-out future (thread w/ Job 18:5).

**PRO-24-fear-Lord-king · Prov 24:21–22 · [S] — the dual reverence, and refusal of rebellion.** "My son, **fear the LORD and the king**, and do not join with those who do otherwise, for **disaster will arise suddenly** from them" (vv.21–22). Finding: the ordered self **reveres God and lawful authority** and refuses the company of rebels, whose ruin is sudden *(stated)*. Operation: **reverence as ordering loyalty** (w/ the fear-of-the-LORD thread) *(inferred)*. *Surfaced:* the dual reverence (God and king); refusal of rebellion.

**PRO-24-impartial-rebuke · Prov 24:23–26 · [D, multi] — impartial judgment and the honest word.** "**Partiality in judging is not good**" (v.23); "whoever says to the wicked, 'You are in the **right**,' will be **cursed by peoples**" (v.24); "those who **rebuke the wicked** will have **delight**, and a good blessing" (v.25); "whoever gives an **honest answer kisses the lips**" (v.26). Finding: **impartial judgment and honest rebuke** are blessed; calling the wicked right is cursed; an honest answer is as intimate as a kiss *(stated)*. Operation: **the interior that judges without partiality and speaks the true word** — rebuke of the wicked brings delight; the honest answer is felt as affection *(inferred; the honest word as intimacy, a striking image; impartiality w/ 18:5)*. *Surfaced:* impartial judgment; the honest answer as a kiss (new); rebuke of evil as delight.

**PRO-24-sluggard-lesson · Prov 24:30, 32 · [S] — learning from what one observes.** "I **passed by the field of a sluggard**… then I **saw and considered it; I looked and received instruction**" (vv.30,32). Finding: the wise self **learns by observing** — the sluggard's overgrown field becomes a lesson taken to heart *(stated)*. Operation: **the reflective interior that turns observation into instruction** — looking is not idle; it is receiving teaching *(inferred; the self-teaching by reflection, w/ Ecc's "I saw / I considered")*. *Surfaced:* learning by observation/reflection (new — w/ Ecc's watching self).

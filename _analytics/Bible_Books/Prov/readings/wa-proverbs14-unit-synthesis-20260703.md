# Proverbs 14 — inner-being unit readings (Phase-2 meaning synthesis)

- **Source:** `lexical-model-2026` Phase-1 (v1.1 enriched) for Pro 14, per `segment_unit`. Grounded; *stated* | *inferred*. Lens: the inner-being **operation**; wise⇄fool = arena.

---

**PRO-14-F · 14:1–35 · [F]** — the moral-poles frame. Arena.

**PRO-14-heart-known · 14:10,13 · [S, multi] — the heart's incommunicable interior.** "**The heart knows its own bitterness, and no stranger shares its joy**" (v.10); "**even in laughter the heart may ache, and the end of joy may be grief**" (v.13). Finding — a cornerstone of the felt interior: the heart's deepest states are **private and incommunicable** — its bitterness *known only to itself*, its joy *unshared*, and its surface (laughter) may *mask* an inward ache *(stated)*. Operation: **the inner life is finally solitary** — no one else fully enters the heart's grief or gladness, and the outward face can *contradict* the inward state *(inferred; the most explicit statement in Scripture of the *privacy* of the inner being; cross-book w/ Ps 38:8–9 "the tumult of my heart… my sighing is not hidden from you" — only God enters where no stranger can)*. *Hidden:* **the mask — laughter over ache** (the outward may belie the inward). *Surfaced:* the incommunicable/private heart (★ cornerstone — new to the corpus, deepens the felt-interior thread); the masking surface.

**PRO-14-temper · 14:17,29,30 · [T, multi] — anger vs the tranquil heart.** "Whoever is **slow to anger has great understanding**, but he who has a **hasty temper exalts folly**"; "**A tranquil heart gives life to the flesh, but envy makes the bones rot**" (vv.29–30). Finding: temper is a *cognitive* matter (slow-to-anger = *understanding*), and the **tranquil heart is bodily *life* while envy *rots the bones*** *(stated)*. Operation: **inner tranquillity as physical health, envy/rage as physical decay** — the emotional interior writes itself on the body *(inferred; cross-book w/ Ps 32:3 "my bones wasted", 38:3; the felt interior embodied)*. *Hidden:* **the heart's peace as *somatic*** (tranquil heart → life to the flesh). *Surfaced:* self-mastery/slow-to-anger (shared, cross-book w/ Ps); the tranquil-vs-envious heart embodied (★ new).

**PRO-14-fear · 14:26,27 · [T, multi] — the fear of the LORD as confidence and fountain of life.** "In the **fear of the LORD one has strong confidence**… the **fear of the LORD is a fountain of life**" (vv.26–27). Finding: reverent fear yields **confidence and life** — not dread but a *strong assurance* *(stated; the fear-of-LORD thread, cross-book w/ Ps 112, 27:1)*. *Surfaced:* fear of the LORD → confidence/life (shared cornerstone).

**PRO-14-self-deception · 14:12 · [S] — the way that seems right.** "**There is a way that seems right to a man, but its end is the way to death**" (v.12). Finding: the self can **misread its own path** — subjective rightness is no guide to the true end *(stated)*. Operation: **self-deception about one's own way** — the inner sense of being right can be fatally wrong *(inferred; cross-book w/ Ps 19:12 "who can discern his errors")*. *Surfaced:* self-deception / the way-that-seems-right (★ new — recurs 16:25).

**PRO-14-simple · 14:15 · [S] — credulity vs considered steps.** "**The simple believes everything, but the prudent gives thought to his steps**" (v.15). Finding: the naive *believes all*, the prudent *weighs* — discernment as considered attention *(stated)*. *Surfaced:* discernment vs credulity (shared w/ ch 7's naive self).

**PRO-14-generosity · 14:21,31 · [S, multi] — regard for the poor as regard for the Maker.** "Whoever **oppresses a poor man insults his Maker**, but he who is **generous to the needy honors him**" (v.31). Finding: one's disposition toward the poor is a disposition **toward God** *(stated; the neighbour-facing heart, cross-book w/ Ps 41:1)*. *Surfaced:* regard-for-the-poor-as-regard-for-God (shared).

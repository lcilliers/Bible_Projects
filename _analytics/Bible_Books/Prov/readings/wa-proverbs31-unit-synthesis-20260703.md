# Proverbs 31 — inner-being unit readings (Phase-2 meaning synthesis)

- **Source:** `lexical-model-2026` Phase-1 (v1.1 enriched) for Pro 31 (Lemuel + the woman of valour), per `segment_unit`. Grounded; *stated* | *inferred*. Lens: the inner-being **operation**. (Closes the book.)

---

**PRO-31-charge · 31:2–9 · [D, multi] — self-mastery for the sake of the voiceless.** The mother's charge to the king: "**do not give your strength to women**… **it is not for kings to drink wine**… lest they **drink and forget** what has been decreed and **pervert the rights of the afflicted**… **Open your mouth for the mute**, for the rights of all who are destitute" (vv.3–5,8). Finding: **self-mastery is charged for the sake of *others*** — the king must govern appetite (women, wine) *lest* he *forget* justice and fail the voiceless *(stated)*. Operation: **appetite-restraint in service of justice** — self-control is not private virtue only but the condition of *not forgetting* one's duty to the afflicted *(inferred; self-mastery + the neighbour-facing heart joined; cross-book w/ Ps 72:2–4)*. *Surfaced:* self-mastery-for-the-sake-of-the-voiceless (new — self-control as a *public* fidelity).

**PRO-31-noble-wife · 31:10–31 · [D, multi] — the woman who fears the LORD: trust, strength, fearless hope, the book's crown.** The acrostic portrait, read for the inner being: "the **heart of her husband trusts in her**" (v.11 — she is *trustworthy*); "she **dresses herself with strength**… **strength and dignity are her clothing, and she laughs at the time to come**" (vv.17,25 — inner strength and **fearless hope**); "she **opens her mouth with wisdom, and the teaching of kindness is on her tongue**" (v.26 — wise, kind speech); and the crown: "**charm is deceitful, and beauty is vain, but a woman who fears the LORD is to be praised**" (v.30). Finding — the book's closing cornerstone: the whole excellence is finally reduced to **the fear of the LORD** — over charm and beauty (which are *deceitful* and *vain*) — and it *shows* as **trustworthiness, inner strength, fearless laughter at the future, and wise-kind speech** *(stated)*. Operation: **the fear of the LORD *embodied* as a life** — the abstract root of 1:7 is here *lived out* in a whole person: an inner being so settled it **laughs at the time to come** (the opposite of dread), so reliable that a heart *trusts* in her, so strengthened it needs no charm *(inferred; the book's frame closes — fear-of-the-LORD opened it (1:7) and crowns it here (31:30); cross-book w/ Ps 112 the God-fearer whose "heart is firm… not afraid of bad news", and the fearless-security thread)*. *Hidden:* **"she laughs at the time to come"** — fearless hope as the *affect* of the God-fearing self (the antithesis of anxiety about the future, cf. 27:1's refused presumption; here a *settled* facing of the future). *Surfaced:* fear-of-the-LORD embodied (★ book-closing cornerstone, bookends 1:7, cross-book w/ Ps 112); trustworthiness; inner strength + fearless hope ("laughs at the time to come"); wise-kind speech.

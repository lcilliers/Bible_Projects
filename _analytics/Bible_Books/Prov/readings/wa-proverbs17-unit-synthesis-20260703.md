# Proverbs 17 — inner-being unit readings (Phase-2 meaning synthesis)

- **Source:** `lexical-model-2026` Phase-1 (v1.1 enriched) for Pro 17, per `segment_unit`. Grounded; *stated* | *inferred*. Lens: the inner-being **operation**.

---

**PRO-17-tests-hearts · 17:3 · [S] — the LORD tests hearts.** "**The crucible is for silver, and the furnace is for gold, and the LORD tests hearts**" (v.3). Finding — a cornerstone: the heart is **assayed by God as metal by fire** — refined and proved *(stated)*. Operation: **the tested/refined heart** — God's testing is a *smelting*, purifying not merely inspecting *(inferred; cross-book w/ Ps 7:9, 26:2, 66:10 "you have tried us as silver is tried")*. *Surfaced:* the tested/assayed heart (★ shared cornerstone, cross-book w/ Ps).

**PRO-17-felt-heart · 17:22 · [S, multi] — the joyful heart as medicine; the crushed spirit.** "**A joyful heart is good medicine, but a crushed spirit dries up the bones**" (v.22). Finding — a felt-interior cornerstone: inner gladness is **medicinal to the body**, and a *crushed spirit desiccates the bones* *(stated)*. Operation: **the emotional interior as physical health or decay** *(inferred; cross-book w/ Ps 32:3–4, 51:8 "let the bones you have broken rejoice"; and Pro 14:30, 15:13, 18:14)*. *Surfaced:* the felt heart / joyful-heart-as-medicine + the crushed spirit (★ shared cornerstone).

**PRO-17-teachable · 17:10 · [S] — the depth a rebuke reaches.** "**A rebuke goes deeper into a man of understanding than a hundred blows into a fool**" (v.10). Finding: the teachable heart is *penetrable* by a word where the fool is unmoved by a hundred blows *(stated)*. Operation: **receptivity measured by how *deep* a rebuke reaches** *(inferred; the teachability thread)*. *Surfaced:* receptivity / the penetrable vs impervious heart (shared).

**PRO-17-restraint · 17:27,28 · [T, multi] — restrained words, the cool spirit.** "Whoever **restrains his words has knowledge**, and he who has a **cool spirit is a man of understanding**; even a fool who **keeps silent** is considered wise" (vv.27–28). Finding: **restraint and coolness of spirit** are the marks of understanding *(stated)*. Operation: **self-governance of speech and temperature** — the "cool spirit" (opposite of hot temper) is an inner mastery *(inferred; the self-mastery + tongue-restraint threads; cross-book w/ Ps 39:1 "I will guard my ways… I will muzzle my mouth")*. *Surfaced:* restraint of words / the cool spirit (shared self-mastery).

**PRO-17-love-friend · 17:9,17 · [S, multi] — love covers; the friend for adversity.** "Whoever **covers an offense seeks love**"; "**a friend loves at all times, and a brother is born for adversity**" (vv.9,17). Finding: love **covers** offenses (as 10:12) and is **constant** ("at all times"), proving itself in adversity *(stated)*. Operation: **love as covering + steadfastness** *(inferred; the love-as-covering datum + the constancy of true love)*. *Surfaced:* love-as-covering (shared w/ 10:12); the steadfast friend (new).

**Chapter notes.** **Cornerstones:** the tested heart (★ 17:3, w/ Ps); joyful-heart/crushed-spirit (★ 17:22). **Shared:** receptivity; self-mastery/restraint/cool-spirit; love-as-covering.

## Catch-up units (2026-07-04) — loose study-term verses bound to units

**PRO-17-quiet · Prov 17:1 · [S] — peace over plenty.** "**Better is a dry morsel with quiet** than a house full of feasting with strife" (v.1). Finding: the interior prizes **quiet over abundance** — a bare meal in peace outweighs a full house in conflict *(stated)*. Operation: **the self that ranks inner peace above material plenty** *(inferred; contentment as tranquillity, w/ Ecc 4:6 "a handful of quietness")*. *Surfaced:* peace prized over plenty (w/ Ecc 4:6).

**PRO-17-speech-heart · Prov 17:4, 7, 20 · [D, multi] — the interior legible in speech.** "An **evildoer listens to wicked lips**" (v.4); "**fine speech is not becoming to a fool**" (v.7); "a man of **crooked heart does not discover good**, and one with a **dishonest tongue falls into calamity**" (v.20). Finding: the heart is **read off what it listens to and how it speaks** — the evil ear drawn to wicked lips, the crooked heart self-trapped by its dishonest tongue *(stated)*. Operation: **the interior disclosed and self-ensnared by speech** — the crooked heart's own tongue delivers it to calamity *(inferred; speech as the heart's outflow that recoils, w/ Ecc 10:12 the fool's lips consume him)*. *Surfaced:* the heart legible in what it hears/speaks (w/ Ecc 10:12); the self-ensnaring dishonest tongue.

**PRO-17-Maker · Prov 17:5 · [S, multi] — mocking the poor insults the Maker; gladness at calamity.** "Whoever **mocks the poor insults his Maker**; he who is **glad at calamity** will not go unpunished" (v.5). Finding: contempt for the poor is an **affront to God who made them**, and *gladness at another's ruin* is culpable *(stated)*. Operation: **the shared-Maker conscience, and the perversity of schadenfreude** — to scorn the poor is to scorn the Maker; to rejoice at calamity is a punishable inner posture *(inferred; the common-creation ethic w/ Job 31:15; schadenfreude as a named inner-being fault w/ Job 31:29)*. *Surfaced:* the shared-Maker conscience (w/ Job 31:15); gladness-at-calamity as a fault (w/ Job 31:29).

**PRO-17-bribe · Prov 17:8, 23 · [D] — the bribe as a corrupted self-perception.** "A **bribe is like a magic stone in the eyes of the one who gives it**; wherever he turns he prospers" (v.8); "the wicked accepts a **bribe in secret to pervert** the ways of justice" (v.23). Finding: the bribe **warps the giver's own seeing** — in *his eyes* it works magic — and, taken in secret, perverts justice *(stated)*. Operation: **corruption that distorts the interior's perception** — the bribe does not just buy an outcome, it re-colours how the briber sees reality (everything looks like it prospers) *(inferred; the self deceived by its own corruption)*. *Surfaced:* the bribe as corrupted self-perception (new); secret corruption perverting justice.

**PRO-17-strife · Prov 17:11–14, 19 · [D, multi] — the contentious, rebellion-seeking heart.** "An **evil man seeks only rebellion**" (v.11); better meet a **she-bear robbed of her cubs than a fool in his folly** (v.12); "if anyone **returns evil for good**, evil will not depart from his house" (v.13); "the **beginning of strife is like letting out water**, so quit before the quarrel breaks out" (v.14); "whoever **loves transgression loves strife**" (v.19). Finding: the contentious interior **seeks rebellion, repays good with evil, and loves strife** — and strife, once begun, floods uncontrollably *(stated)*. Operation: **the strife-loving heart and the un-damming of conflict** — quarrel is a breach that, once opened, cannot be recalled; the wise self quits before the flood *(inferred; loving-transgression-loves-strife as one disposition; the flood-image for conflict's irreversibility)*. *Surfaced:* the strife-loving/rebellion-seeking heart (new); strife as an un-dammable flood; returning-evil-for-good.

**PRO-17-justice · Prov 17:15, 26 · [S, multi] — perverted justice as an abomination.** "He who **justifies the wicked** and he who **condemns the righteous** are both alike an **abomination to the LORD**" (v.15); "to **impose a fine on a righteous man is not good**" (v.26). Finding: **inverting justice** — acquitting the guilty, condemning the innocent — is what God abhors *(stated)*. Operation: **the moral revulsion at inverted judgment** — the interior that calls evil good and good evil is an abomination; punishing the righteous offends the moral order *(inferred; the perverted moral sense, w/ Isa 5:20)*. *Surfaced:* inverted justice as abomination (w/ Isa 5:20).

**PRO-17-fool-family · Prov 17:2, 16, 21, 25 · [D, multi] — folly's grief in the family, and the wise servant.** "A **servant who deals wisely** will rule over a son who acts shamefully" (v.2); "why should a fool have **money in his hand to buy wisdom when he has no sense**?" (v.16); "he who **sires a fool gets himself sorrow**… the father of a fool has **no joy**" (v.21); "a **foolish son is a grief to his father and bitterness to her who bore him**" (v.25). Finding: folly **grieves the family's interior** — a fool son is his parents' sorrow and bitterness — while wisdom, even in a servant, rises *(stated)*. Operation: **the child's folly felt as the parent's grief; wisdom as the true inheritance** — character, not birth, orders the household; the fool cannot buy the sense he lacks *(inferred; the relational grief-thread w/ 10:1, 23:24–25; wisdom un-purchasable, w/ Job 28:15)*. *Surfaced:* folly as the family's grief (thread w/ 10:1); wisdom un-purchasable / not birth-bound (w/ Job 28:15).

**PRO-17-surety · Prov 17:18 · [S] — the senseless self-binding.** "One who **lacks sense gives a pledge** and puts up security in the presence of his neighbour" (v.18). Finding: to stand surety is a **want of sense** — the self binding itself to another's debt *(stated)*. Operation: **the imprudent self-binding** (w/ 6:1–5, the surety-snare) *(inferred)*. *Surfaced:* senseless self-binding (thread w/ 6:1–5).

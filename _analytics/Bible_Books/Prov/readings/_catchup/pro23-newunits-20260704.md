## Catch-up units (2026-07-04) — loose study-term verses bound to units

**PRO-23-unreceptive · Prov 23:8, 9 · [S, multi] — words wasted on the unreceptive.** "You will **vomit up the morsels** that you have eaten, and **waste your pleasant words**" (v.8); "**do not speak in the hearing of a fool, for he will despise the good sense of your words**" (v.9). Finding: good words spent on the **unreceptive are wasted** — the fool despises them, and even hospitality with a grudging host turns the stomach *(stated)*. Operation: **the futility of pouring wisdom into a closed interior** — the fool's contempt makes counsel pointless; speak, and it is despised *(inferred; the closed self that cannot receive, w/ the pearls-before-swine principle)*. *Surfaced:* words wasted on the closed/unreceptive self (new).

**PRO-23-Redeemer · Prov 23:11 · [S] — the strong Redeemer who pleads their cause.** "Their **Redeemer is strong; he will plead their cause against you**" (v.11). Finding: the defenceless (the fatherless, the poor) have a **strong Redeemer (go'el)** who takes up their case *(stated)*. Operation: **God as the advocate-kinsman of the defenceless** — the interior warned that behind the powerless stands their champion *(inferred; God as go'el, w/ Job 19:25, Pro 22:23)*. *Surfaced:* the strong Redeemer/advocate of the defenceless (thread w/ Job 19:25).

**PRO-23-discipline-honour · Prov 23:14, 22 · [S, multi] — discipline that saves; honour to the aged parent.** "If you **strike him with the rod, you will save his soul from Sheol**" (v.14); "**listen to your father who gave you life, and do not despise your mother when she is old**" (v.22). Finding: **discipline is a rescue** — the rod saves the soul from Sheol; and the source of one's life is to be **honoured, not despised, in age** *(stated)*. Operation: **correction as life-saving love, and filial reverence across the years** — discipline aimed at the *soul*, not mere behaviour; the parent honoured to the end *(inferred; discipline-as-rescue w/ 19:18; honour to the aged)*. *Surfaced:* discipline as soul-rescue (w/ 19:18); reverence for the aged parent.

**PRO-23-parents-joy · Prov 23:24, 25 · [S] — the parent's joy in a wise child.** "The **father of the righteous will greatly rejoice**; he who fathers a wise son will be glad in him. Let your father and mother be glad; **let her who bore you rejoice**" (vv.24–25). Finding: a child's wisdom becomes the **parent's deep joy** — the appeal is to *live so as to gladden* those who bore you *(stated)*. Operation: **the child's character felt as the parent's rejoicing** (the joyful pole of the grief-thread, w/ 10:1, 17:21) *(inferred)*. *Surfaced:* the parent's joy in a wise child (thread w/ 10:1 — the glad pole).

**PRO-23-appetite-truth · Prov 23:20, 23 · [S, multi] — restraint of appetite; prizing truth.** "Be **not among drunkards or among gluttonous eaters** of meat" (v.20); "**buy truth, and do not sell it**; buy wisdom, instruction, and understanding" (v.23). Finding: the ordered interior **restrains appetite** (no drunkenness or gluttony) and **acquires truth at any cost, never trading it away** *(stated)*. Operation: **the self-governed appetite and the treasuring of truth** — truth is to be *bought and held*, wisdom acquired; the belly is not to rule *(inferred; appetite-restraint w/ the never-satisfied thread; truth prized w/ Job 28:15–18)*. *Surfaced:* restraint of appetite (thread); truth bought-and-never-sold (w/ Job 28:15).

**PRO-23-snare · Prov 23:27, 28 · [S] — the seductive pit that lies in wait.** "A **prostitute is a deep pit; an adulteress is a narrow well**. She **lies in wait like a robber** and increases the traitors" (vv.27–28). Finding: illicit desire is a **pit and an ambush** — a snare that lies in wait to trap the self *(stated)*. Operation: **the seductive trap as a lurking predator** (the forbidden-woman snare, thread w/ 22:14, 5–7) *(inferred)*. *Surfaced:* the seductive pit that ambushes (thread w/ 22:14).

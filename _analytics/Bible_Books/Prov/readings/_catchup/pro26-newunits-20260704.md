## Catch-up units (2026-07-04) — loose study-term verses bound to units

**PRO-26-fool-honor · Prov 26:1, 8, 11 · [D, multi] — honour misplaced on a fool; folly's return.** "Like snow in summer… **honour is not fitting for a fool**" (v.1); "like one who **binds the stone in the sling is one who gives honour to a fool**" (v.8); "like a **dog that returns to his vomit is a fool who repeats his folly**" (v.11). Finding: honour on a fool is **as absurd and dangerous as arming a sling with the stone still bound**; and the fool **cannot leave his folly** — he returns to it like a dog to its vomit *(stated)*. Operation: **the fixity of folly** — it is not a passing mistake but a *returning*, a compulsion the fool circles back to *(inferred; folly as compulsive repetition, the un-teachable self at its most fixed)*. *Surfaced:* folly as compulsive return (new); honour absurd on a fool.

**PRO-26-answer-fool · Prov 26:4, 5 · [S, multi] — the discernment of when to engage folly.** "**Answer not a fool according to his folly, lest you be like him**" (v.4); "**answer a fool according to his folly, lest he be wise in his own eyes**" (v.5). Finding: the paired paradox — **whether to engage folly depends on the moment**: silence to avoid becoming a fool, engagement to puncture his self-conceit *(stated)*. Operation: **the discerning interior that reads which folly needs silence and which needs answer** — no rule fits both; wisdom is knowing which risk (imitation vs his self-conceit) is live *(inferred; a gem on situational discernment — the same act right or wrong by the moment)*. *Surfaced:* situational discernment in engaging folly (new — a gem); the two risks (becoming like him / confirming his conceit).

**PRO-26-meddle-strife · Prov 26:17, 21 · [S, multi] — meddling and the kindling of strife.** "Whoever **meddles in a quarrel not his own** is like one who takes a **passing dog by the ears**" (v.17); "as charcoal to embers… so is a **quarrelsome man for kindling strife**" (v.21). Finding: **meddling in others' quarrels invites needless injury**, and the contentious self **feeds strife** as fuel feeds fire *(stated)*. Operation: **the strife-kindling interior, and the folly of grabbing another's quarrel** — some conflicts the self should not seize; the quarrelsome man is the fuel that keeps them burning *(inferred; the strife-thread)*. *Surfaced:* meddling as self-injury (new); the quarrelsome man as strife's fuel (thread).

**PRO-26-deceit · Prov 26:19, 28 · [S, multi] — the deceiver's "joke" and the flattering, hating mouth.** "So is the man who **deceives his neighbour and says, 'I am only joking!'**" (v.19); "a **lying tongue hates its victims, and a flattering mouth works ruin**" (v.28). Finding: deceit **hides behind 'just joking,'** and beneath the flattering mouth is **hatred** — flattery ruins *(stated)*. Operation: **the malice masked as jest or flattery** — the lying tongue *hates* the very ones it addresses; the smooth mouth is a weapon *(inferred; flattery as concealed hatred, a sharp inner-being diagnosis)*. *Surfaced:* deceit masked as joking (new); flattery as concealed hatred (new).

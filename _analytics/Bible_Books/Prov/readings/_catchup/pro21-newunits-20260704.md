## Catch-up units (2026-07-04) — loose study-term verses bound to units

**PRO-21-righteousness-over-ritual · Prov 21:3, 27 · [S, multi] — the LORD prefers righteousness to sacrifice.** "To do **righteousness and justice is more acceptable to the LORD than sacrifice**" (v.3); "the **sacrifice of the wicked is an abomination**; how much more when he brings it with **evil intent**" (v.27). Finding: God weighs the **doing of justice above ritual**, and reads the *intent* behind the offering — the wicked's sacrifice, worse with evil intent, is abomination *(stated)*. Operation: **the interior weighed above the rite** — what God receives is righteousness enacted, not sacrifice offered; the heart's intent qualifies the act *(inferred; the moral interior over ritual, w/ Ecc 5:1 "to listen is better than the sacrifice of fools", 1 Sam 15:22)*. *Surfaced:* righteousness above ritual (w/ Ecc 5:1); intent qualifying the offering.

**PRO-21-diligence · Prov 21:5, 20 · [S, multi] — diligence stores, haste and folly squander.** "The **plans of the diligent lead surely to abundance**, but everyone who is **hasty comes only to poverty**" (v.5); "precious treasure and oil are in a wise man's dwelling, but a **foolish man devours it**" (v.20). Finding: the diligent, deliberate interior **accumulates**; the hasty and the foolish **squander** *(stated)*. Operation: **foresight that stores vs impulse that consumes** — planning gathers, haste and folly devour what is gathered *(inferred; the deferring vs devouring self)*. *Surfaced:* diligence-stores / folly-devours (new); haste as the road to poverty.

**PRO-21-crooked-way · Prov 21:6, 7, 8, 16, 18 · [D, multi] — the crooked way and its recoil.** "Treasure by a **lying tongue is a fleeting vapour and a snare of death**" (v.6); "the **violence of the wicked will sweep them away**, because they refuse to do what is just" (v.7); "the **way of the guilty is crooked, but the conduct of the pure is upright**" (v.8); "one who **wanders from the way of good sense will rest in the assembly of the dead**" (v.16). Finding: the guilty interior's **way is crooked** and its gains are vapour — its own violence sweeps it off, its wandering ends among the dead *(stated)*. Operation: **the self-destroying trajectory of the crooked way** — lying-gotten treasure is a death-snare; refusing justice becomes the flood that carries the wicked away *(inferred; the way as destiny, w/ the two-paths thread)*. *Surfaced:* the crooked way as self-destroying (thread); lying-gain as a death-snare; wandering to the assembly of the dead.

**PRO-21-quarrelsome-home · Prov 21:9, 19 · [S] — the strife-filled household.** "**Better to live in a corner of the housetop** than in a house shared with a **quarrelsome wife**" (v.9); "better to live in a **desert land** than with a quarrelsome and fretful woman" (v.19). Finding: **constant strife makes home unlivable** — a rooftop corner or a desert is preferable to a house of quarrelling *(stated)*. Operation: **the intolerable weight of chronic contention** on the shared interior of a home *(inferred; strife as what drives the self to solitude, w/ 17:1)*. *Surfaced:* the strife-filled home the self flees (w/ 17:1).

**PRO-21-scoffer · Prov 21:11, 24 · [S] — the scoffer, and how the simple learn.** "When a **scoffer is punished, the simple becomes wise**; when a wise man is instructed, he gains knowledge" (v.11); "'**Scoffer**' is the name of the **arrogant, haughty man** who acts with arrogant pride" (v.24). Finding: the **scoffer is the arrogant, proud self** — unteachable except as a warning; the simple learn from his punishment what the wise learn from a word *(stated)*. Operation: **arrogance as the closed, only-by-example-teachable interior** (w/ 19:25, 9:7–9) *(inferred)*. *Surfaced:* the scoffer as arrogant pride (thread w/ 19:25); learning by another's punishment.

**PRO-21-God-watch-mercy · Prov 21:12, 13, 14, 15 · [D, multi] — God's watch, mercy's reciprocity, and the divided response to justice.** "The **Righteous One observes the house of the wicked**" (v.12); "whoever **closes his ear to the cry of the poor will himself call out and not be answered**" (v.13); "a **gift in secret averts anger**" (v.14); "when **justice is done, it is a joy to the righteous but terror to evildoers**" (v.15). Finding: God **watches** the wicked's house; mercy is **reciprocal** — deafness to the poor earns a deaf heaven; and the same justice is **joy or terror** by the state of the heart *(stated)*. Operation: **the interior mirrored back — the closed ear meets a closed heaven; justice gladdens or terrifies by what one is** *(inferred; mercy's reciprocity, w/ Matt 5:7; justice read oppositely by the righteous and the guilty)*. *Surfaced:* mercy's reciprocity (the closed ear, w/ Matt 5:7); justice as joy-or-terror by the heart's state; God's watch on the wicked's house.

**PRO-21-pursuit · Prov 21:21, 22, 28 · [D, multi] — the pursuit that finds life; wisdom over might; the enduring word.** "Whoever **pursues righteousness and kindness will find life, righteousness, and honour**" (v.21); "a **wise man scales the city of the mighty** and brings down the stronghold in which they trust" (v.22); "a false witness will perish, but the **word of a man who hears will endure**" (v.28). Finding: the **pursuit of righteousness-and-kindness finds life**; wisdom **overtops might**; and the word of the *listening* self endures where the false witness perishes *(stated)*. Operation: **the seeking interior rewarded with life; the listening self's durable word** — what is pursued (righteousness, kindness) is found; wisdom breaches what force trusts in *(inferred; seeking-rewarded-with-finding, w/ 8:17; wisdom>might w/ Ecc 9:16)*. *Surfaced:* the pursuit of righteousness-and-kindness finding life (w/ 8:17); wisdom over might (w/ Ecc 9:16); the listening self's enduring word.

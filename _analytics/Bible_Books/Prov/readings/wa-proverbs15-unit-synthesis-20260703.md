# Proverbs 15 — inner-being unit readings (Phase-2 meaning synthesis)

- **Source:** `lexical-model-2026` Phase-1 (v1.1 enriched) for Pro 15, per `segment_unit`. Grounded; *stated* | *inferred*. Lens: the inner-being **operation**; righteous⇄wicked = arena.

---

**PRO-15-F · 15:2–33 · [F]** — the moral-poles frame. Arena.

**PRO-15-anger · 15:1,18 · [S, multi] — the soft answer; slow-to-anger quiets strife.** "**A soft answer turns away wrath, but a harsh word stirs up anger**"; "a **hot-tempered man stirs up strife, but he who is slow to anger quiets contention**" (vv.1,18). Finding: the inner being can **de-escalate or inflame** by the temper of its speech — gentleness *turns away* wrath *(stated)*. Operation: **self-mastery expressed as gentleness** — the slow-to-anger self *quiets* what the hot-tempered *kindles* *(inferred; the self-mastery thread, cross-book w/ Ps 4:4 "be angry and do not sin")*. *Surfaced:* self-mastery / the soft answer (shared).

**PRO-15-heart-states · 15:13,15,30 · [T, multi] — the felt heart: glad, crushed, cheerful.** "**A glad heart makes a cheerful face, but by sorrow of heart the spirit is crushed**"; "the **cheerful of heart has a continual feast**"; "the **light of the eyes rejoices the heart**, and good news refreshes the bones" (vv.13,15,30). Finding — a felt-interior cornerstone: the heart's state **shows on the face**, *crushes the spirit* in sorrow, and the cheerful heart lives in a **continual feast** regardless of circumstance *(stated)*. Operation: **the inner disposition *colours all of life*** — "a continual feast" is not about events but about a *cheerful heart*; the interior sets the taste of existence *(inferred; the felt-interior thread at its fullest, cross-book w/ Ps 4:7 "more joy… than when grain and wine abound", 34:5 "radiant faces")*. *Hidden:* **the cheerful heart makes a *continual feast* out of ordinary days** — inner state over outer circumstance. *Surfaced:* the felt heart (glad/crushed/cheerful) — ★ shared cornerstone; the crushed spirit (w/ 17:22, Ps).

**PRO-15-god-sees-heart · 15:3,11 · [T, multi] — God sees the hearts.** "The **eyes of the LORD are in every place, keeping watch** on evil and good"; "**Sheol and Abaddon lie open before the LORD; how much more the hearts of the children of man!**" (vv.3,11). Finding: the inner being is **wholly open to God's sight** — if even the realm of the dead lies open, *how much more the heart* *(stated)*. Operation (of the self): **living before an all-seeing gaze** — the heart is never unobserved *(inferred; cross-book w/ Ps 139 "you have searched me", 33:13–15 "he fashions their hearts and observes all their deeds")*. *Surfaced:* being-known/seen by God (★ shared cornerstone, cross-book w/ Ps 139).

**PRO-15-teachable · 15:14,31,32 · [T, multi] — the seeking heart, the listening ear.** "The **heart of him who has understanding seeks knowledge**"; "the **ear that listens to life-giving reproof will dwell among the wise**"; "whoever **ignores instruction despises himself**" (vv.14,31–32). Finding: the wise heart is **actively seeking**, and heeding reproof is **self-respect** ("ignores instruction *despises himself*") *(stated)*. Operation: **the seeking heart + the self-valuing that heeds correction** *(inferred; teachability as self-love, cross-book w/ 19:8)*. *Surfaced:* the seeking heart; heeding-reproof-as-self-respect (new).

**PRO-15-contentment · 15:16,17 · [S, multi] — the little with fear/love better than much with trouble.** "**Better is a little with the fear of the LORD than great treasure and trouble**… better a **dinner of herbs where love is** than a fattened ox and **hatred**" (vv.16–17). Finding: **contentment is a function of the inner condition** (fear, love), not the quantity of goods *(stated)*. Operation: **the inner good (fear/love) outvalues the outer abundance** *(inferred; cross-book w/ Ps 37:16 "better is the little of the righteous than the abundance of many wicked")*. *Surfaced:* contentment (inner-condition over abundance) — shared w/ Ps 37.

**PRO-15-ponder · 15:28 · [S] — the heart that ponders before answering.** "**The heart of the righteous ponders how to answer, but the mouth of the wicked pours out evil**" (v.28). Finding: the righteous heart **weighs before it speaks** — considered vs impulsive interior *(stated; the ponder/restraint thread)*. *Surfaced:* the pondering (vs impulsive) heart (new).

**PRO-15-humility · 15:33 · [S, multi] — fear and humility before honour.** "The **fear of the LORD is instruction in wisdom, and humility comes before honour**" (v.33). Finding: humility *precedes* honour — the lowered self is the one lifted *(stated; pride/humility thread, cross-book w/ Ps 138:6)*. *Surfaced:* humility-before-honour (shared cornerstone).

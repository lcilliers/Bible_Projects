# Proverbs 27 — inner-being unit readings (Phase-2 meaning synthesis)

- **Source:** `lexical-model-2026` Phase-1 (v1.1 enriched) for Pro 27, per `segment_unit`. Grounded; *stated* | *inferred*. Lens: the inner-being **operation**.

**PRO-27-boast-tomorrow · 27:1,2 · [S, multi] — presumption and self-praise.** "**Do not boast about tomorrow, for you do not know what a day may bring**"; "**let another praise you, and not your own mouth**" (vv.1–2). Finding: the self is to refuse **presumption over the future** and **self-praise** — two forms of over-reaching *(stated; cross-book w/ Ps 30:6 "I said in my prosperity, I shall never be moved")*. *Surfaced:* presumption / self-praise refused (shared w/ Ps 30).

**PRO-27-passions · 27:4 · [S] — the ranking of the passions.** "**Wrath is cruel, anger is overwhelming, but who can stand before jealousy?**" (v.4). Finding: the passions are **weighed against each other** — jealousy the most irresistible *(stated)*. Operation: **a taxonomy of the overwhelming passions** (wrath < anger < jealousy) *(inferred; cross-book w/ Ps's fierce affections)*. *Surfaced:* the ranked passions / jealousy strongest (new).

**PRO-27-friend-wounds · 27:5,6,9 · [T, multi] — the honest friend; the gladdened heart.** "**Better is open rebuke than hidden love**. **Faithful are the wounds of a friend**; profuse the kisses of an enemy"; "**oil and perfume make the heart glad**, and the sweetness of a friend comes from his earnest counsel" (vv.5–6,9). Finding: love is **better shown in honest wounding** than in hidden fondness or flattering kisses; and a friend's *earnest counsel* **gladdens the heart** *(stated)*. Operation: **love as honest correction** (the friend's wounds are *faithful*) and **the heart gladdened by true counsel** *(inferred; teachability + the felt heart; cross-book w/ Ps 141:5 "let a righteous man strike me")*. *Hidden:* **love that *wounds faithfully* over love that *hides***. *Surfaced:* the honest-wounding friend (★ cross-book w/ Ps 141:5); the heart gladdened by counsel.

**PRO-27-iron-sharpens · 27:17 · [S] — formation by another.** "**Iron sharpens iron, and one man sharpens another**" (v.17). Finding — a cornerstone: the self is **honed by another self** *(stated)*. Operation: **mutual formation — the self shaped sharper by contact with another** *(inferred; the formation-by-company thread, cross-book w/ 13:20, and the trust-formation law of Ps 115:8)*. *Surfaced:* formation-by-another / iron-sharpens-iron (★ cornerstone — mutual formation).

**PRO-27-heart-mirror · 27:19 · [S] — the heart is the true self.** "**As in water face reflects face, so the heart of man reflects the man**" (v.19). Finding — a cornerstone: the **heart *is* the true self** — what a person really is shows in the heart as a face in water *(stated)*. Operation: **the heart as the mirror/identity of the person** — the deepest Proverbs statement that the self *is* its heart *(inferred; cross-book w/ Ps 7:9 "the minds and hearts", the heart as the self's core)*. *Surfaced:* the heart as the true self (★ cornerstone — the heart = the person).

**PRO-27-insatiable · 27:20 · [S] — the never-satisfied eye.** "**Sheol and Abaddon are never satisfied, and never satisfied are the eyes of man**" (v.20). Finding — a cornerstone: human desire (the *eyes*) is **structurally insatiable**, like the grave *(stated)*. Operation: **the unfillable appetite** — the self's wanting has no natural floor; it is *never satisfied* *(inferred; the shadow-side of the desire thread, cross-book w/ Ps 106:14–15, and the only-God-satisfies datum of Ps 107:9, 145:16)*. *Surfaced:* the insatiable eye/desire (★ cornerstone — the unfillable appetite).

**PRO-27-tested-praise · 27:21 · [S] — a man is tested by his praise.** "The crucible for silver… **and a man is tested by his praise**" (v.21). Finding: how one **handles praise** assays the self — praise is a furnace *(stated)*. *Surfaced:* praise as a test of the self (new).

**Chapter notes.** **Cornerstones:** iron-sharpens-iron / formation-by-another (★ 27:17); the heart as the true self (★ 27:19); the insatiable eye (★ 27:20). **New:** the ranked passions; praise-as-test. **Shared:** presumption/self-praise (w/ Ps 30); the honest-wounding friend (w/ Ps 141:5).

## Catch-up units (2026-07-04) — loose study-term verses bound to units

**PRO-27-fool-weight · Prov 27:3, 22 · [S, multi] — the weight and fixity of a fool's folly.** "A stone is heavy… but a **fool's provocation is heavier than both**" (v.3); "**crush a fool in a mortar** with a pestle… yet his **folly will not depart** from him" (v.22). Finding: a fool's vexation is a **crushing weight**, and his folly is so **fixed** that even pulverising will not remove it *(stated)*. Operation: **folly as an ineradicable interior set** — not a surface fault correction can grind out, but the grain of the self *(inferred; folly's fixity, w/ 26:11 the returning dog)*. *Surfaced:* the crushing weight of a fool's provocation (new); folly as ineradicable (thread w/ 26:11).

**PRO-27-appetite · Prov 27:7 · [S] — appetite conditioned by fullness and hunger.** "One who is **full loathes honey**, but to one who is **hungry everything bitter is sweet**" (v.7). Finding: **satisfaction and need re-colour the same thing** — honey cloys the full, and bitterness sweetens for the hungry *(stated)*. Operation: **the interior's valuation set by its own state of fullness** — desire is not fixed to the object but to the appetite's condition *(inferred; the never-satisfied thread's mirror — fullness dulls, hunger sweetens, w/ Ecc 6:7)*. *Surfaced:* appetite conditioned by fullness/hunger (new — w/ Ecc 6:7); the state of the self colouring the object.

**PRO-27-strayer-friend · Prov 27:8, 10 · [S, multi] — the strayer, and the near friend.** "Like a **bird that strays from its nest is a man who strays from his home**" (v.8); "**do not forsake your friend**… better is a **neighbour who is near than a brother who is far away**" (v.10). Finding: to wander from one's place is a **displacement** like a bird from its nest; and a **near friend outvalues a distant brother** *(stated)*. Operation: **rootedness and proximate loyalty** — the self belongs somewhere, and the friend who is *near in calamity* is worth more than kin who are far *(inferred; the value of the present friend, w/ 18:24)*. *Surfaced:* the strayer displaced from his place (new); the near friend over the distant brother (w/ 18:24).

**PRO-27-child-glad · Prov 27:11 · [S] — the child's wisdom that gladdens and vindicates.** "Be **wise, my son, and make my heart glad**, that I may answer him who reproaches me" (v.11). Finding: a child's wisdom **gladdens the parent's heart** and even **vindicates** him before critics *(stated)*. Operation: **the child's character as the parent's joy and defence** (the glad pole, w/ 10:1, 23:24) *(inferred)*. *Surfaced:* the child's wisdom gladdening and vindicating the parent (thread w/ 10:1).

**PRO-27-prudence-service · Prov 27:12, 18 · [S, multi] — foresight, and faithful service rewarded.** "The **prudent sees danger and hides himself**, but the simple go on and suffer" (v.12); "whoever **tends a fig tree will eat its fruit**, and he who **guards his master will be honoured**" (v.18). Finding: prudence **foresees and takes cover**; faithful tending is **rewarded** — the fruit to the keeper, honour to the guard *(stated)*. Operation: **the anticipating self and the fidelity that reaps** — foresight spares suffering; steady service earns its fruit and honour *(inferred; prudence w/ 22:3; faithful service)*. *Surfaced:* foresight that takes cover (thread w/ 22:3); faithful service rewarded (new).

**PRO-27-praise-contention · Prov 27:14, 16 · [S, multi] — excessive praise as curse; the un-restrainable contentious.** "Whoever **blesses his neighbour with a loud voice, rising early**… will be counted as **cursing**" (v.14); "to **restrain her** [the contentious woman] is to **restrain the wind** or grasp oil in the hand" (v.16). Finding: **over-loud praise curdles into a curse**, and the chronically contentious is **as un-restrainable as the wind** *(stated)*. Operation: **the excess that inverts its intent, and the ungovernable contentious interior** — praise too loud reads as mockery; the quarrelsome cannot be gripped any more than wind or oil *(inferred; the un-restrainable contentious, w/ 21:9,19)*. *Surfaced:* over-loud praise as curse (new); the ungovernable contentious (thread w/ 21:9).

**PRO-27-stewardship · Prov 27:23, 24, 27 · [D, multi] — diligent stewardship against impermanent riches.** "**Know well the condition of your flocks**… for **riches do not last forever**; does a crown endure to all generations?" (vv.23–24); "there will be **enough goats' milk** for your food… and maintenance for your girls" (v.27). Finding: attentive **stewardship** sustains the household, because **riches are impermanent** — the diligent care, not the hoard, provides *(stated)*. Operation: **the interior that tends rather than trusts wealth** — knowing the flock's condition, aware that riches and crowns pass, so care is ongoing *(inferred; diligence over hoarding, w/ the impermanence-of-riches thread, Ecc)*. *Surfaced:* attentive stewardship (new); the impermanence of riches (w/ Ecc).

**PRO-27-surety · Prov 27:13 · [S] — the senseless surety (again).** "**Take a man's garment when he has put up security for a stranger**" (v.13). Finding: standing surety for a stranger is folly to be guarded against *(stated; thread w/ 6:1–5, 17:18, 22:26)*. Operation: the imprudent self-binding *(inferred)*. *Surfaced:* the surety-snare (thread w/ 6:1).

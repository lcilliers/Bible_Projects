# Proverbs 6 — inner-being unit readings (Phase-2 meaning synthesis)

- **Source:** `lexical-model-2026` Phase-1 (v1.1 enriched) for Pro 6:1–35, per `segment_unit`. Grounded; *stated* | *inferred*. Lens: the inner-being **operation**.

---

**PRO-06-A · 6:6–11 · [D] — sloth as the self's drift into ruin.** "Go to the **ant**, O **sluggard**… How long will you **lie there**? A **little sleep, a little slumber, a little folding of the hands** to rest, and **poverty will come** upon you like a robber" (vv.6,9–11). The finding: sloth is an **inner disposition**, not a single act — the *incremental* yielding ("a little… a little… a little") by which the self *drifts* into ruin without deciding to *(stated)*. The operation: **ruin by degrees** — the slothful interior does not choose poverty but *fails to rouse*, and the small surrenders accumulate into catastrophe *(inferred; a Proverbs-distinctive characteristic, faintly echoed in Ps 127:2's contrast of toil and rest)*. *Validation:* *little→of sleep/of slumber*, *folding→of hands* (the enrichment strings the incremental phrase). *Surfaced:* sloth as incremental drift (new — Proverbs-distinctive).

**PRO-06-B · 6:12–15 · [D, multi] — the malicious heart legible in the body.** "A worthless person… goes about with **crooked speech**, **winks** with his eyes, **signals** with his feet, points with his finger, **with perverted heart devises evil, continually sowing discord**" (vv.12–14). The finding: malice is **read off the body** — the *perverted heart* within *shows* in winking eyes, signalling feet, pointing fingers *(stated)*. The operation: the inner corruption **leaks through the whole body's gestures** — the heart that "devises evil" cannot keep itself hidden; its scheming animates the limbs *(inferred; the inner condition legible not only in speech (ch 10) but in *gesture*, a bodily extension of the speech-legibility thread)*. *Validation:* *crooked→of speech*, *sowing→target=discord* (the perverted heart's product). *Hidden:* **malice legible in gesture** (extends the speech-legibility characteristic to the whole body). *Surfaced:* the inner condition legible in body/gesture (new extension of the Ps speech thread).

**PRO-06-C · 6:16–19 · [S, multi] — the catalogue of inner-being evils.** The seven the LORD hates: "**haughty eyes**, a **lying tongue**… a **heart that devises wicked plans**, **feet that make haste to run to evil**, a **false witness** who breathes out lies, and one who **sows discord** among brothers" (vv.17–19). The finding: an explicit **enumeration of the inner being's abominations** — pride (eyes), falsehood (tongue), a *scheming heart*, an *eager will* (feet swift to evil), and the *discord-sowing* disposition *(stated)*. The operation: the list **maps the self's evils onto its organs** — each abomination is an inner disposition expressed through a body-part; the *heart that devises wicked plans* is the centre *(inferred; a compact anthropology of evil, cross-book with Ps 36:1–4)*. *Validation:* *devises→target=wicked* (heart devises wicked plans), *plans→of feet*, *breathes-out→lies*, *sows→discord*. *Surfaced:* catalogue of inner evils (new); the scheming heart as the centre.

**PRO-06-D · 6:20–35 · [D, multi] — desire guarded at the heart; adultery as self-destruction.** "Bind them on your **heart always**… **do not desire her beauty in your heart**, and do not let her **capture** you… He who commits adultery **lacks sense**; he who does it **destroys himself**" (vv.21,25,32). The finding: the guard against adultery is set **at the level of *desire in the heart*** — not merely the act but the *wanting* is to be refused ("do not desire her beauty *in your heart*") *(stated)*; and the act is **self-destruction** — "he *destroys himself*" *(stated)*. The operation: **the heart as the front line of purity** — the battle is fought where desire forms, before the deed; and yielding is not a gain but a *self-annihilation* *(inferred; the interiorised command, cross-book with Ps 119:37 "turn my eyes from worthless things", and Matt 5:28)*. *Validation:* the enrichment is precise — *beauty→seat=heart, specifier=of heart* ("desire her beauty *in your heart*"); *heart→of always*. *Hidden:* **purity contested at the point of desire, not the act** (the interiorised battle). *Surfaced:* desire-guarded-in-the-heart (new — the interiorised command, cross-book w/ Ps 119:37); adultery as self-destruction; jealousy's fury (v.34, the passion arena).

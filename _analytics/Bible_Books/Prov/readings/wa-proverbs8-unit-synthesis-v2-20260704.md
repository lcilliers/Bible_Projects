# Proverbs 8 — inner-being unit readings (Phase-2 meaning synthesis)

- **Source:** `lexical-model-2026` Phase-1 (v1.1 enriched) for Pro 8:1–36, per `segment_unit`. Grounded; *stated* | *inferred*. Lens: the inner-being **operation** (Wisdom personified = the arena/speaker; the findings are the self's relation to her).

---

**PRO-08-A · 8:1–11 · [D, multi] — wisdom's truthful self-commendation and its value.** Wisdom cries at the crossroads: "my mouth will **utter truth**; wickedness is an **abomination to my lips**… take my instruction rather than **silver**… **wisdom is better than jewels**, and all that you may **desire** cannot compare with her" (vv.7,10–11). The finding: wisdom presents herself as **truth that outbids every desire** — she cannot be compared to what the self wants *(stated)*. The operation (of the self): the inner being is summoned to **re-rank its desires** with wisdom above all treasure — the same prizing as 3:15 *(inferred)*. *Validation:* *utter→target=truth*, *abomination→of lips* (wisdom's own hatred of wicked speech), *all→of desire* ("nothing you desire compares"). *Surfaced:* prizing wisdom above desire (shared w/ 3:13, Ps 119).

**PRO-08-B · 8:12–21 · [D, multi] — the fear that hates evil; the mutual love of self and wisdom.** "**The fear of the LORD is hatred of evil**. Pride and arrogance and the way of evil and perverted speech **I hate**… **I love those who love me**, and those who **seek me diligently find me**" (vv.13,17). Two findings. (1) **Reverent fear is a *hatred of evil*** — fear of God is not only awe but an *aversion*, an affective turning-against wickedness *(stated)*; a hidden dimension of the fear-of-the-LORD characteristic. (2) **Love is reciprocal and seeking is rewarded** — "I love those who *love* me… those who *seek* me diligently *find* me": the self's love and diligent seeking of wisdom is *met and answered* *(stated)*. The operation: the inner being's affection for wisdom is **returned**, and its diligent search **succeeds** — a relationship of mutual love, not one-way effort *(inferred; cross-book with the seek-and-find of Ps 9:10, 27:8, and the desire-met thread)*. *Validation:* *fear→specifier=of Lord*; *knowledge→of discretion*; the govern/decree/walk objects tagged. *Hidden:* **fear-of-the-LORD as *hatred of evil*** (an affective, not merely reverent, dimension). *Surfaced:* fear as hatred of evil (new dimension of the shared fear-thread); the mutual love of the self and wisdom; seeking rewarded with finding (shared w/ Ps).

**PRO-08-C · 8:32–36 · [D, multi] — watching daily at wisdom's doors; life vs death.** "**Blessed is the one who listens** to me, **watching daily at my gates, waiting beside my doors**. For whoever **finds me finds life**… but he who **fails to find me injures himself**; **all who hate me love death**" (vv.34–36). The finding: the devoted self **watches and waits** at wisdom's door — a posture of daily, expectant attendance — and the stakes are *life or death* *(stated)*. The operation: **expectant watching/waiting for wisdom** (the same posture as the Psalter's wait-on-the-LORD, 130:6, here at wisdom's gates); and the terrible inversion — those who reject wisdom **love death** — evil as a *perverse love of one's own ruin* *(inferred)*. *Hidden:* **"all who hate me love death"** — the refusal of wisdom is not neutral but an *affection for self-destruction* (the deepest form of misdirected love, cross-book with the aheb-fixed "love vanity/simplicity" of Ps 4:2, Pro 1:22). *Surfaced:* watching/waiting for wisdom (shared w/ Ps wait-thread); the love of death (new — misdirected affection at its extreme).

## Catch-up units (2026-07-04) — loose study-term verses bound to units

**PRO-08-D · Prov 8:24, 28, 30–31 · [D, multi] — wisdom's delight and rejoicing (the joy at the heart of creation).** Wisdom, present before the depths (vv.24,28 — the cosmic *arena*), describes its own affect: "I was daily his **delight**, **rejoicing** before him always, **rejoicing** in his inhabited world and **delighting in the children of man**" (vv.30–31). Finding: wisdom's mode of being is **joy** — a daily delight *before God* that overflows *toward humanity* *(stated)*. Operation: **delight as the atmosphere of wisdom** — not sober utility but gladness, directed both Godward ("his delight… before him") and manward ("delighting in the children of man") *(inferred; the cosmology of vv.24–28 is arena, but 30–31 is a real inner-being note — the joy that is wisdom's native climate, cross-book w/ the joy-refrain of Ecc and Ps 16:11)*. *Surfaced:* delight/rejoicing as wisdom's native climate (new — Godward and manward); joy as wisdom's atmosphere. *(vv.24,28 = cosmic arena, thin IB.)*

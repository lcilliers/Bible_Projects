# Proverbs 25 — inner-being unit readings (Phase-2 meaning synthesis)

- **Source:** `lexical-model-2026` Phase-1 (v1.1 enriched) for Pro 25, per `segment_unit`. Grounded; *stated* | *inferred*. Lens: the inner-being **operation**.

---

**PRO-25-heart-unsearchable · 25:2,3 · [S] — the unsearchable depth of the heart.** "As the **heavens for height and the earth for depth, so the heart of kings is unsearchable**" (v.3). Finding: the heart's depth is likened to the *unsearchable* heights and depths of creation *(stated; the opaque-heart thread, cross-book w/ 20:5, Ps 64:6 "the inward mind and heart… are deep")*. *Surfaced:* the unsearchable-deep heart (shared w/ 20:5).

**PRO-25-heavy-heart · 25:20 · [S] — comfort mistimed wounds the heavy heart.** "**Whoever sings songs to a heavy heart is like vinegar on soda**" (v.20). Finding: the **heavy heart** is a real felt state that *ill-timed cheer* only aggravates *(stated)*. Operation: **the felt heaviness that cannot be jollied out of** — comfort must match the heart's state or it *stings* *(inferred; the felt-interior thread, cross-book w/ Ps 137:2–4 refusing to sing in grief)*. *Surfaced:* the heavy heart / mistimed comfort (shared felt-interior).

**PRO-25-enemy-bread · 25:21,22 · [S, multi] — good done to the enemy.** "**If your enemy is hungry, give him bread… you will heap burning coals on his head, and the LORD will reward you**" (vv.21–22). Finding — a cornerstone: the disposition toward the enemy is **active good**, not merely restrained revenge *(stated)*. Operation: **love of enemy expressed as provision** — the self *feeds* the one who wronged it, leaving the outcome to God *(inferred; the fullest positive form of the not-repaying thread, cross-book w/ Ps 35:13, Rom 12:20)*. *Surfaced:* good-done-to-the-enemy / love-of-enemy (★ cornerstone, w/ Ps 35).

**PRO-25-thirsty-soul · 25:25 · [S] — the thirsting soul refreshed by good news.** "**Like cold water to a thirsty soul, so is good news from a far country**" (v.25). Finding: the soul **thirsts**, and good news is *cold water* to it *(stated; the thirsting-soul thread, cross-book w/ Ps 42:1–2, 63:1, 143:6)*. *Surfaced:* the thirsting soul (★ shared cross-book w/ Ps 42/63).

**PRO-25-self-control · 25:28 · [S] — self-control as the wall of the self.** "**A man without self-control is like a city broken into and left without walls**" (v.28). Finding — a cornerstone: the un-governed self is **defenceless**, a breached city *(stated)*. Operation: **self-control as the *wall* that makes a self defensible** — without it the interior lies open to every incursion *(inferred; the self-mastery cornerstone, cross-book w/ 16:32, 29:11, and the Psalter's self-governing self)*. *Surfaced:* self-control as the wall of the self (★ cornerstone).

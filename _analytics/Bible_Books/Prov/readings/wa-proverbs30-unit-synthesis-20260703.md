# Proverbs 30 — inner-being unit readings (Phase-2 meaning synthesis)

- **Source:** `lexical-model-2026` Phase-1 (v1.1 enriched) for Pro 30 (Agur), per `segment_unit`. Grounded; *stated* | *inferred*. Lens: the inner-being **operation**.

---

**PRO-30-humility · 30:1–4 · [D, multi] — the seeker's self-emptying before the unknowable God.** "I am **weary**, O God… **Surely I am too stupid to be a man**. I have **not the understanding of a man**. I have **not learned wisdom**, nor have knowledge of the Holy One… **Who has ascended to heaven?**" (vv.1–4). Finding — a cornerstone: the wisdom-seeker begins in **exhausted self-emptying** — confessing his own stupidity before the God he cannot comprehend *(stated)*. Operation: **intellectual humility as the floor of wisdom** — the truest sage *unknows* himself before God's unsearchable greatness; the confession of ignorance *is* the beginning of insight *(inferred; the deepest form of the fear-of-the-LORD-as-beginning; cross-book w/ Ps 73:22 "I was brutish and ignorant… like a beast toward you", 131:1)*. *Surfaced:* intellectual humility / self-emptying before God (★ cornerstone — the sage's un-knowing).

**PRO-30-middle · 30:7–9 · [D, multi] — the prayer for the middle; self-knowledge of the heart's dangers.** "**Give me neither poverty nor riches**; feed me with the food that is needful, **lest I be full and deny you** and say, 'Who is the LORD?' **or lest I be poor and steal** and profane the name of my God" (vv.8–9). Finding — a cornerstone: the self prays for the *mean* out of a sober **knowledge of its own heart** — knowing that *fullness* tempts to *forget God* and *want* tempts to *steal* *(stated)*. Operation: **self-knowledge as the ground of a prayer for limits** — the inner being, aware that *both excess and lack* imperil its fidelity, asks God to guard it from *its own* likely failures *(inferred; a rare, honest self-diagnosis; cross-book w/ Ps 19:13 "keep back your servant from presumptuous sins", 131:1–2's contentment)*. *Hidden:* **the two opposite dangers to fidelity** (fullness→forgetting, want→theft) — a mapped self-knowledge. *Surfaced:* self-knowledge / the prayer-for-the-middle (★ cornerstone — contentment grounded in self-diagnosis).

**PRO-30-self-clean · 30:12,13 · [S, multi] — clean in one's own eyes.** "There are those who are **clean in their own eyes but are not washed of their filth**… how **lofty are their eyes**" (vv.12–13). *Surfaced:* self-deception + pride (shared w/ 20:9, 16:2).

**PRO-30-insatiable · 30:15,16,20 · [S, multi] — the never-satisfied; the seared conscience.** "The **leech has two daughters: Give and Give**. Three things are **never satisfied**… the adulteress **eats and wipes her mouth and says, 'I have done no wrong'**" (vv.15–16,20). Finding: the **insatiable** appetite (never says "Enough"), and the **seared conscience** that feels no guilt after wrong *(stated)*. Operation: **the unfillable want + the conscience that registers no sin** *(inferred; the insatiable-eye thread of 27:20, and the numbed conscience; cross-book w/ the hardened heart, Ps 36:1–2)*. *Hidden:* **the seared conscience** ("I have done no wrong" — no inner registration of guilt). *Surfaced:* the insatiable (shared w/ 27:20); the seared conscience (★ new — guilt un-registered).

**PRO-30-restraint · 30:32,33 · [S] — checking the self before it spills.** "If you have been **foolish, exalting yourself**… **put your hand on your mouth**. For… **pressing anger produces strife**" (vv.32–33). *Surfaced:* self-restraint (shared w/ 29:11).

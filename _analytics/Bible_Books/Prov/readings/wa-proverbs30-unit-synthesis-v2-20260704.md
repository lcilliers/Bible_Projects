# Proverbs 30 — inner-being unit readings (Phase-2 meaning synthesis)

- **Source:** `lexical-model-2026` Phase-1 (v1.1 enriched) for Pro 30 (Agur), per `segment_unit`. Grounded; *stated* | *inferred*. Lens: the inner-being **operation**.

---

**PRO-30-humility · 30:1–4 · [D, multi] — the seeker's self-emptying before the unknowable God.** "I am **weary**, O God… **Surely I am too stupid to be a man**. I have **not the understanding of a man**. I have **not learned wisdom**, nor have knowledge of the Holy One… **Who has ascended to heaven?**" (vv.1–4). Finding — a cornerstone: the wisdom-seeker begins in **exhausted self-emptying** — confessing his own stupidity before the God he cannot comprehend *(stated)*. Operation: **intellectual humility as the floor of wisdom** — the truest sage *unknows* himself before God's unsearchable greatness; the confession of ignorance *is* the beginning of insight *(inferred; the deepest form of the fear-of-the-LORD-as-beginning; cross-book w/ Ps 73:22 "I was brutish and ignorant… like a beast toward you", 131:1)*. *Surfaced:* intellectual humility / self-emptying before God (★ cornerstone — the sage's un-knowing).

**PRO-30-middle · 30:7–9 · [D, multi] — the prayer for the middle; self-knowledge of the heart's dangers.** "**Give me neither poverty nor riches**; feed me with the food that is needful, **lest I be full and deny you** and say, 'Who is the LORD?' **or lest I be poor and steal** and profane the name of my God" (vv.8–9). Finding — a cornerstone: the self prays for the *mean* out of a sober **knowledge of its own heart** — knowing that *fullness* tempts to *forget God* and *want* tempts to *steal* *(stated)*. Operation: **self-knowledge as the ground of a prayer for limits** — the inner being, aware that *both excess and lack* imperil its fidelity, asks God to guard it from *its own* likely failures *(inferred; a rare, honest self-diagnosis; cross-book w/ Ps 19:13 "keep back your servant from presumptuous sins", 131:1–2's contentment)*. *Hidden:* **the two opposite dangers to fidelity** (fullness→forgetting, want→theft) — a mapped self-knowledge. *Surfaced:* self-knowledge / the prayer-for-the-middle (★ cornerstone — contentment grounded in self-diagnosis).

**PRO-30-self-clean · 30:12,13 · [S, multi] — clean in one's own eyes.** "There are those who are **clean in their own eyes but are not washed of their filth**… how **lofty are their eyes**" (vv.12–13). *Surfaced:* self-deception + pride (shared w/ 20:9, 16:2).

**PRO-30-insatiable · 30:15,16,20 · [S, multi] — the never-satisfied; the seared conscience.** "The **leech has two daughters: Give and Give**. Three things are **never satisfied**… the adulteress **eats and wipes her mouth and says, 'I have done no wrong'**" (vv.15–16,20). Finding: the **insatiable** appetite (never says "Enough"), and the **seared conscience** that feels no guilt after wrong *(stated)*. Operation: **the unfillable want + the conscience that registers no sin** *(inferred; the insatiable-eye thread of 27:20, and the numbed conscience; cross-book w/ the hardened heart, Ps 36:1–2)*. *Hidden:* **the seared conscience** ("I have done no wrong" — no inner registration of guilt). *Surfaced:* the insatiable (shared w/ 27:20); the seared conscience (★ new — guilt un-registered).

**PRO-30-restraint · 30:32,33 · [S] — checking the self before it spills.** "If you have been **foolish, exalting yourself**… **put your hand on your mouth**. For… **pressing anger produces strife**" (vv.32–33). *Surfaced:* self-restraint (shared w/ 29:11).

## Catch-up units (2026-07-04) — loose study-term verses bound to units

**PRO-30-refuge · Prov 30:5 · [S] — God's word proven, a shield to those who take refuge.** "**Every word of God proves true; he is a shield to those who take refuge in him**" (v.5). Finding: God's word is **tested and true**, and he is a **shield** to the one who **takes refuge** in him *(stated)*. Operation: **the interior that shelters in the tried word of God** — refuge-taking as the trusting movement toward a proven shield *(inferred; take-refuge/shield, cross-book w/ Ps 18:30 which this quotes, Ps 2:12)*. *Surfaced:* taking refuge in the proven word (thread w/ Ps 18:30 — the trust/refuge cluster).

**PRO-30-parent-honour · Prov 30:10, 11, 17 · [D, multi] — the dishonoured parent and the mocking eye.** "Do **not slander a servant** to his master" (v.10); "there are those who **curse their fathers and do not bless their mothers**" (v.11); "the **eye that mocks a father and scorns to obey a mother** will be picked out by the ravens" (v.17). Finding: **contempt for parents** — cursing, refusing blessing, the **mocking eye** — is marked for a dishonourable end *(stated)*. Operation: **the scorn legible in the eye, and the dishonouring interior** — the mocking eye betrays a heart that scorns its origin, and meets a fitting ruin *(inferred; the contemptuous eye, w/ 6:17 haughty eyes; dishonour of parents inverting the honour-thread)*. *Surfaced:* the mocking eye that scorns a parent (new — w/ 6:17); the dishonoured parent (inverting the honour thread).

**PRO-30-wonder · Prov 30:18, 19, 21, 23 · [D, multi] — wonder at the unsearchable, and the destabilising reversals.** "**Three things are too wonderful for me; four I do not understand**: the way of an eagle… a serpent… a ship… and the **way of a man with a virgin**" (vv.18–19); "under three things the earth **trembles**… an **unloved woman when she gets a husband**, and a **maidservant who displaces her mistress**" (vv.21,23). Finding: some things exceed understanding — the mysterious **way of love** among them — and certain **reversals unsettle the world** (the long-unloved suddenly powerful, the servant supplanting the mistress) *(stated)*. Operation: **the interior that owns the limits of its understanding, and reads which reversals destabilise** — wonder ("too wonderful… I do not understand") as an honest posture; the note on reversals that the human order cannot bear *(inferred; owned not-knowing, w/ Job 42:3, Ecc 3:11; the way of a man and a maid as the unsearchable inner mystery of love)*. *Surfaced:* owned wonder at the unsearchable (w/ Job 42:3); the way of love as too-wonderful; the destabilising reversal.

**PRO-30-small-wise · Prov 30:24, 25, 26, 29 · [D, multi] — the wisdom of the small.** "**Four things on earth are small, but they are exceedingly wise**": the **ants**, "a people not strong, yet they **provide their food in summer**"; the **rock badgers**, "not mighty, yet they **make their homes in the cliffs**" (vv.24–26); and "**three things are stately in their tread**" (v.29). Finding: **wisdom is not a function of strength** — the small creatures are "exceedingly wise" in *provision* (the ant's foresight) and *security* (the badger's cliff-home) *(stated)*. Operation: **prudent foresight and shelter-seeking as wisdom available to the weak** — strength is not required; the ant stores against winter, the badger builds beyond reach *(inferred; the ant's foresight, w/ 6:6–8; wisdom in the small, not the mighty)*. *Surfaced:* wisdom-in-the-small (foresight and security without strength, w/ 6:6); provision-in-season / shelter-in-the-cliff as the weak's wisdom.

# Proverbs 25 — inner-being unit readings (Phase-2 meaning synthesis)

- **Source:** `lexical-model-2026` Phase-1 (v1.1 enriched) for Pro 25, per `segment_unit`. Grounded; *stated* | *inferred*. Lens: the inner-being **operation**.

**PRO-25-heart-unsearchable · 25:2,3 · [S] — the unsearchable depth of the heart.** "As the **heavens for height and the earth for depth, so the heart of kings is unsearchable**" (v.3). Finding: the heart's depth is likened to the *unsearchable* heights and depths of creation *(stated; the opaque-heart thread, cross-book w/ 20:5, Ps 64:6 "the inward mind and heart… are deep")*. *Surfaced:* the unsearchable-deep heart (shared w/ 20:5).

**PRO-25-heavy-heart · 25:20 · [S] — comfort mistimed wounds the heavy heart.** "**Whoever sings songs to a heavy heart is like vinegar on soda**" (v.20). Finding: the **heavy heart** is a real felt state that *ill-timed cheer* only aggravates *(stated)*. Operation: **the felt heaviness that cannot be jollied out of** — comfort must match the heart's state or it *stings* *(inferred; the felt-interior thread, cross-book w/ Ps 137:2–4 refusing to sing in grief)*. *Surfaced:* the heavy heart / mistimed comfort (shared felt-interior).

**PRO-25-enemy-bread · 25:21,22 · [S, multi] — good done to the enemy.** "**If your enemy is hungry, give him bread… you will heap burning coals on his head, and the LORD will reward you**" (vv.21–22). Finding — a cornerstone: the disposition toward the enemy is **active good**, not merely restrained revenge *(stated)*. Operation: **love of enemy expressed as provision** — the self *feeds* the one who wronged it, leaving the outcome to God *(inferred; the fullest positive form of the not-repaying thread, cross-book w/ Ps 35:13, Rom 12:20)*. *Surfaced:* good-done-to-the-enemy / love-of-enemy (★ cornerstone, w/ Ps 35).

**PRO-25-thirsty-soul · 25:25 · [S] — the thirsting soul refreshed by good news.** "**Like cold water to a thirsty soul, so is good news from a far country**" (v.25). Finding: the soul **thirsts**, and good news is *cold water* to it *(stated; the thirsting-soul thread, cross-book w/ Ps 42:1–2, 63:1, 143:6)*. *Surfaced:* the thirsting soul (★ shared cross-book w/ Ps 42/63).

**PRO-25-self-control · 25:28 · [S] — self-control as the wall of the self.** "**A man without self-control is like a city broken into and left without walls**" (v.28). Finding — a cornerstone: the un-governed self is **defenceless**, a breached city *(stated)*. Operation: **self-control as the *wall* that makes a self defensible** — without it the interior lies open to every incursion *(inferred; the self-mastery cornerstone, cross-book w/ 16:32, 29:11, and the Psalter's self-governing self)*. *Surfaced:* self-control as the wall of the self (★ cornerstone).

## Catch-up units (2026-07-04) — loose study-term verses bound to units

**PRO-25-humility-before-great · Prov 25:5, 6, 7 · [D] — self-lowering before the great.** "Take away the **wicked from the presence of the king**, and his throne will be established in righteousness" (v.5); "**do not put yourself forward** in the king's presence or stand in the place of the great, for it is **better to be told, 'Come up here,'** than to be put lower" (vv.6–7). Finding: the wise self **does not exalt itself** before the great — it is better to be *raised* than to be *lowered* *(stated)*. Operation: **the humility that declines self-promotion** — taking the low place and being invited up, rather than grasping honour and being demoted *(inferred; self-lowering as wisdom, w/ Luke 14:8–10; the un-self-promoting interior)*. *Surfaced:* the humility that declines self-promotion (new — w/ Luke 14:8).

**PRO-25-prudent-dispute · Prov 25:8, 9, 10 · [D, multi] — restraint in conflict.** "Do **not hastily bring** a matter into court… what will you do when your neighbour puts you to shame?" (v.8); "**argue your case with your neighbour himself, and do not reveal another's secret**, lest… your **ill repute have no end**" (vv.9–10). Finding: the prudent self **handles disputes directly and discreetly** — not rushing to court, not betraying confidences *(stated)*. Operation: **the self-restraint that keeps conflict contained and confidences kept** — haste and secret-telling both recoil into lasting shame *(inferred; discretion in dispute)*. *Surfaced:* restraint in conflict; keeping a confidence even in a quarrel (new).

**PRO-25-listening-faithful · Prov 25:12, 13 · [S, multi] — the listening ear and the refreshing faithful.** "Like a **gold ring… is a wise reprover to a listening ear**" (v.12); "like the **cold of snow in harvest is a faithful messenger** to those who send him; he **refreshes the soul** of his masters" (v.13). Finding: wise reproof meeting a **listening ear** is an ornament; a **faithful messenger refreshes the soul** *(stated)*. Operation: **the receptive interior that adorns reproof, and faithfulness as refreshment** — the reproof is beautiful only where there is an ear to receive it; the faithful one cools like snow *(inferred; receptivity as what makes correction golden, w/ the teachability thread)*. *Surfaced:* the listening ear that makes reproof golden (thread); faithfulness that refreshes the soul (new).

**PRO-25-empty-betrayal · Prov 25:14, 18, 19 · [D, multi] — empty words and unreliable trust.** "Like **clouds and wind without rain is a man who boasts of a gift he does not give**" (v.14); "a man who **bears false witness** against his neighbour is like a **war club, a sword, a sharp arrow**" (v.18); "**trusting in a treacherous man in time of trouble is like a bad tooth or a foot that slips**" (v.19). Finding: the **empty boaster** promises rain and gives none; false witness is a weapon; and **misplaced trust fails exactly when needed** *(stated)*. Operation: **the interior that promises and does not deliver, and the pain of trust betrayed in crisis** — the treacherous man is a tooth that breaks on the very bite that needs it *(inferred; the felt failure of false trust, w/ Job 6:15 the dry-wadi friends)*. *Surfaced:* the empty boaster (new); trust that fails in the crisis (w/ Job 6:15).

**PRO-25-restraint · Prov 25:17, 23, 27 · [D, multi] — restraint of presence, tongue, and self-glory.** "Let your **foot be seldom in your neighbour's house**, lest he have his fill of you and **hate** you" (v.17); "the north wind brings rain, and a **backbiting tongue, angry looks**" (v.23); "it is not good to eat much honey, **nor is it glorious to seek one's own glory**" (v.27). Finding: the self exercises **restraint** — of its presence (don't wear out welcome), its tongue (backbiting breeds anger), and its self-seeking (glory-hunting is not glorious) *(stated)*. Operation: **the self-limiting interior** — knowing when enough is enough, in company, in speech, and in appetite for honour *(inferred; self-restraint across domains; the glory-seeking self checked, w/ Ecc 1's vanity of self-exalting)*. *Surfaced:* restraint of presence/tongue/self-glory (new); glory-seeking as not glorious.

**PRO-25-compromised-righteous · Prov 25:26 · [S] — the righteous who gives way.** "Like a **muddied spring or a polluted fountain is a righteous man who gives way before the wicked**" (v.26). Finding: a righteous man who **caves before the wicked** becomes a *fouled source* — his compromise pollutes what should be pure *(stated)*. Operation: **integrity's failure imaged as a polluted spring** — the righteous is meant to be a clean fountain; yielding to evil muddies it *(inferred; a sharp image of compromised integrity)*. *Surfaced:* the compromised righteous as a fouled spring (new — a gem).

**PRO-25-quarrelsome · Prov 25:24 · [S] — the strife-filled home (again).** "It is **better to live in a corner of the housetop than in a house shared with a quarrelsome wife**" (v.24). Finding: chronic strife makes the home unlivable *(stated; repeated from 21:9)*. Operation: strife driving the self to solitude (thread w/ 21:9, 17:1) *(inferred)*. *Surfaced:* the strife-filled home (thread w/ 21:9).

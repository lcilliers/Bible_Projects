# Proverbs 4 — inner-being unit readings (Phase-2 meaning synthesis)

- **Source:** `lexical-model-2026` Phase-1 (v1.1 enriched) for Pro 4:1–27, per `segment_unit`. Grounded; *stated* | *inferred*. Lens: the inner-being **operation** (wisdom/God = arena).

---

**PRO-04-A · 4:1–9 · [D, multi] — the inherited, single-minded pursuit of wisdom.** A father hands on *his own father's* charge: "**Get wisdom**; get insight… The beginning of wisdom is this: **Get wisdom**, and whatever you get, **get insight**. **Prize her highly**, and she will exalt you; **embrace** her" (vv.5,7–8). The finding: wisdom is pursued as **the one acquisition worth everything** — "whatever you get, get insight" makes it the *priority over all gain* — and it is **loved and embraced** (v.6, *love her*), a beloved to be held *(stated)*. The operation: **the whole self bent on acquiring one thing**, and that acquiring is *affectionate* (prize, embrace, love) not merely diligent *(inferred; the single-minded pursuit, cross-book with Ps 27:4's "one thing")*. *Validation:* the imperative-with-object is now fully captured — *Get→target=wisdom/insight* (repeated), *Hear→sons*, *gain→insight*. *Hidden:* **transmission across three generations** (grandfather→father→son, vv.3–4) — wisdom is *handed down*, formation is inherited. *Surfaced:* single-minded pursuit of wisdom (new, cf. Ps one-thing); affectionate acquisition (love/embrace wisdom); transmission of the inner life.

**PRO-04-B · 4:10–19 · [D, multi] — evil as an inner compulsion that cannot rest.** The two paths: "the path of the righteous is like the **light of dawn**, shining **brighter and brighter**," but of the wicked — "they **cannot sleep** unless they have **done wrong**; they are **robbed of sleep** unless they have made someone **stumble**. For they **eat the bread of wickedness** and **drink the wine of violence**" (vv.16–18). The finding: wickedness is not occasional but a **compulsion** — the wicked *cannot rest* until they have done evil; doing wrong is their food and drink *(stated)*. The operation: evil has become the self's **appetite and its insomnia** — a driven interior that is *restless without sin* *(inferred; the darkest portrait of the captured will, cross-book with Ps 36:4 "he plots trouble on his bed")*. *Validation:* the paths now specifier-tagged — way→*of wisdom*, paths→*of uprightness*, path→*of the wicked*; bread→*of wickedness*, wine→*of violence*. *Hidden:* **evil as insomnia/appetite** (a compulsive interior). *Surfaced:* the two paths (frame); the compulsive-evil self (new — sharper than Ps).

**PRO-04-C · 4:20–27 · [D, multi] — keep the heart, the source of life.** The chapter's cornerstone: "**Keep your heart with all vigilance, for from it flow the springs of life**" (v.23); then the gates — "put away **crooked speech**… let your **eyes look directly forward**… **ponder the path of your feet**" (vv.24–26). The finding: the **heart is the *source*** from which the whole life flows, and therefore the object of the most vigilant **guarding** *(stated)*. The operation: the self is **the sentinel of its own heart** — because everything issues *from* the heart, guarding it is guarding the springs of the entire life; and that guarding extends to the *gates* (mouth, eyes, feet) through which the heart acts *(inferred; the master inner-being datum of Proverbs — the heart as origin, cross-book with Ps 51:10, 141:3–4, 119:9)*. *Validation (strong):* the enrichment captures it — *heart→specifier=of all* + intensity=all ("with ALL vigilance"), *all→of vigilance* welded to *Keep*, *springs→of life*. *Hidden:* **the heart as the *origin* of the life, not just a faculty** — vigilance is required *because* it is the source. *Surfaced:* guard-the-heart / the heart as source of life (★ cornerstone, cross-book w/ Ps); governing the gates (speech/eyes/feet).

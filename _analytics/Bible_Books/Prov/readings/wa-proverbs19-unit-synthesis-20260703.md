# Proverbs 19 — inner-being unit readings (Phase-2 meaning synthesis)

- **Source:** `lexical-model-2026` Phase-1 (v1.1 enriched) for Pro 19, per `segment_unit`. Grounded; *stated* | *inferred*. Lens: the inner-being **operation**.

---

**PRO-19-self-love · 19:8 · [S] — getting sense is loving one's own soul.** "**Whoever gets sense loves his own soul**; he who keeps understanding will discover good" (v.8). Finding: acquiring wisdom is an act of **self-love / self-care** — to seek sense is to love one's own soul *(stated)*. Operation: **wisdom as care for the self** — the pursuit of understanding is not duty but *self-benefit* rightly understood *(inferred; a distinctive reframe, cross-book w/ the self-preservation of 13:3)*. *Surfaced:* wisdom-as-self-love (new).

**PRO-19-heart-rages · 19:3 · [S] — the heart that rages at God for self-caused ruin.** "**When a man's folly brings his way to ruin, his heart rages against the LORD**" (v.3). Finding — a sharp inner-being note: the self **blames God for ruin it caused itself** — folly wrecks the way, and the heart *rages against the LORD* *(stated)*. Operation: **mis-directed blame** — the inner being deflects responsibility for self-inflicted ruin onto God *(inferred; a rare diagnosis of the resentful heart; cross-book contrast w/ Ps 51:4 "against you… have I sinned" — the opposite, honest owning)*. *Hidden:* **rage-at-God as displaced self-blame.** *Surfaced:* the heart raging at God (self-caused ruin blamed on God) — ★ new (the resentful/deflecting heart).

**PRO-19-anger · 19:11,19 · [T, multi] — slow to anger; overlooking an offense.** "**Good sense makes one slow to anger, and it is his glory to overlook an offense**" (v.11). Finding: to *overlook* an offense is a **glory**, not a weakness — a chosen, honourable restraint *(stated)*. Operation: **anger governed by the *choice to overlook*** — a positive act of the will *(inferred; self-mastery thread)*. *Surfaced:* slow-to-anger / overlooking-as-glory (shared, sharpened).

**PRO-19-fear-satisfied · 19:23 · [S, multi] — the fear that rests satisfied.** "**The fear of the LORD leads to life, and whoever has it rests satisfied**; he will not be visited by harm" (v.23). Finding: reverent fear issues in a **satisfied rest** *(stated; fear-of-LORD → contentment/rest, cross-book w/ Ps 4:8, 34:9)*. *Surfaced:* fear-of-LORD → satisfied rest (shared).

**PRO-19-plans-purpose · 19:21 · [S, multi] — the heart's plans vs the LORD's purpose.** "**Many are the plans in the mind of a man, but it is the purpose of the LORD that will stand**" (v.21). *Surfaced:* planning heart under divine sovereignty (shared w/ 16:9).

**PRO-19-desired-chesed · 19:22 · [S] — steadfast love is what is desired in a person.** "**What is desired in a man is steadfast love**, and a poor man is better than a liar" (v.22). Finding: the quality *prized* in a person is **loyal love (chesed)** — faithfulness over wealth *(stated)*. *Surfaced:* chesed as the prized inner quality (shared w/ Ps chesed thread).

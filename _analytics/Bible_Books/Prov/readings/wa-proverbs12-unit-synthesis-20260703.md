# Proverbs 12 — inner-being unit readings (Phase-2 meaning synthesis)

- **Source:** `lexical-model-2026` Phase-1 (v1.1 enriched) for Pro 12, per `segment_unit`. Grounded; *stated* | *inferred*. Lens: the inner-being **operation**; righteous⇄wicked = arena.

---

**PRO-12-F · 12:2–28 · [F]** — the moral-poles frame. Arena.

**PRO-12-teachable · 12:1,15 · [S, multi] — loving discipline vs the self right in its own eyes.** "Whoever **loves discipline loves knowledge**, but he who **hates reproof is stupid**" (v.1); "the way of a fool is **right in his own eyes**, but a wise man **listens to advice**" (v.15). Finding: teachability is an *affection* — to love knowledge is to *love the discipline* that gives it; and the fool's fault is **self-certainty** ("right in his own eyes") *(stated)*. Operation: **the self that cannot see past its own view cannot learn** — self-certainty seals the heart, while the wise *hold their view open* to advice *(inferred; cross-book w/ Ps 36:2 self-flattery, and the teachability thread)*. *Hidden:* **self-certainty ("right in his own eyes") as the seal against wisdom** — recurs at 3:7, 26:12, 30:12. *Surfaced:* teachability-as-affection; self-certainty as the closed heart (new thread).

**PRO-12-speech · 12:6,13–14,17–19,22–23 · [T, multi] — the tongue that wounds or heals; truth that endures.** "**Rash words** are like **sword thrusts**, but the **tongue of the wise brings healing**"; "**truthful lips endure forever**, but a lying tongue is but for a **moment**"; "a **prudent man conceals** knowledge, but the **heart of fools proclaims folly**" (vv.18–19,23). Finding: speech wounds or heals, endures or vanishes, by its truth — and the *prudent conceal* while *fools broadcast* *(stated)*. Operation: **the governed tongue as healing and enduring** — truth in speech has a *permanence* the lie lacks; and prudence *withholds* what folly *spills* *(inferred; the speech-legibility thread, + the endurance-of-truth note)*. *Validation:* v1.1 supplies the fruit-of-the-mouth objects (12:14 "satisfied with good"). *Surfaced:* speech-legibility (shared); the endurance of truthful speech (new); prudent concealment vs folly's broadcast.

**PRO-12-heart-thoughts · 12:5,20 · [S, multi] — the heart's thoughts and plans.** "The **thoughts of the righteous are just**; the counsels of the wicked are deceitful"; "**deceit is in the heart** of those who devise evil, but those who **plan peace have joy**" (vv.5,20). Finding: the inner *thoughts/plans* are the moral root — deceit or peace lives *in the heart* before it acts, and *planning peace* carries joy *(stated)*. Operation: **the heart's inner counsel as the seat of character** — what the self *thinks/plans* is who it is *(inferred; cross-book w/ Ps 19:14 "meditation of my heart", 139:23 "know my thoughts")*. *Hidden:* **planning peace *has joy* now** — the disposition is already its own reward. *Surfaced:* the heart's thoughts/plans as character (shared w/ Ps).

**PRO-12-anxiety · 12:25 · [S, multi] — anxiety weighs the heart; a good word lifts it.** "**Anxiety in a man's heart weighs him down, but a good word makes him glad**" (v.25). Finding: anxiety is a *weight* on the heart, relieved by a kind word *(stated)*. Operation: **the felt interior — the heavy heart lightened from outside** (a good word) *(inferred; the felt-heart thread, cross-book w/ Ps 94:19 "when the cares of my heart are many, your consolations cheer my soul")*. *Surfaced:* the felt heart (weighed/gladdened) — ★ shared cross-book thread.

**PRO-12-diligence · 12:11,24,27 · [T] — diligence rules, sloth is enslaved.** "The **hand of the diligent will rule**, while the **slothful will be put to forced labor**" (v.24). Finding: diligence and sloth end in mastery vs servitude *(stated)*. *Surfaced:* diligence vs sloth (shared Proverbs thread).

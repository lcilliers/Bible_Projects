### Two judgement calls (Screen 0)
- **3:12** — "the LORD reproves whom he **loves**, as a father the son he **delights** in." The LORD's own *love* (aheb) is the **divine arena → qualifier**; the char read is the **father's *delight*** (ratsah) — a genuine human paternal affect, the simile vehicle. Coupled as parallels.
- **3:19–20** — wisdom / understanding / knowledge here are **God's creative attributes** (locus `external:god`). Read as characteristics (consistent with the book's tracking of the personified/divine wisdom-terms) but explicitly located as divine, dignifying the human pursuit — not importing God's inner life as a human interior.

### Notable reads
`esher`/`ashar` blessedness forms an inclusio (3:13, 3:18); `chazaq` = tenacious holding-fast to wisdom (the tree of life gripped, not admired); the whole field of human `chephets` (desire) set below wisdom (3:15); `betach` = the confident, unstumbling walk (3:23); `chen` reappears as **adornment** for the neck (3:22, cf 3:4).

### Notable reads
The **pride→destruction** axis (16:18-19) read across three verses as one movement — pride/haughtiness court the fall, and the *lowly spirit with the poor* is the better estate that inverts worldly reckoning. The **heart-governs-speech** theme sharpened (16:21, 23 — the wise *heart* makes the mouth judicious; inner wisdom becomes wise speech). And the recurring **psychosomatic** note (16:24 — gracious words are *sweetness to the soul and health to the body*, cf 15:30; 17:22). Royal "delight" and "love" (16:13) read as the *king's* human affect, not God's.

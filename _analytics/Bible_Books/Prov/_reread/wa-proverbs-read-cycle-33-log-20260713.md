### Notable reads — real vs imagined refuge, and the tongue's power
The **18:10-11 pair** read as a single deliberate contrast: the same word *oz* ("strong") applied to God's name (the *real* tower the righteous run to) and to wealth (a high wall only *"in his imagination"*) — trust in God vs trust in riches. The **primacy of the inner life** (18:14 — the spirit sustains the body through sickness, but a broken spirit is unbearable — the deepest wound is inward). And the **theology of the tongue** at its peak (18:20-21 — words are seeds with a harvest that feeds the speaker; *death and life* lie in the tongue's power). Plus the listen-first ethic (18:13, 18:17).

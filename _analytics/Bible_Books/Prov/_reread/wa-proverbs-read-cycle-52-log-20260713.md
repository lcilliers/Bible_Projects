### Notable reads
The chapter's linking of **moral knowledge to God-seeking** (28:5 — evil blinds the moral sense; only those who seek the LORD understand justice, cf John 7:17); **worship voided by disobedience** (28:9 — God abhors the prayer of one who turns his ear from the law, cf 15:8; Isa 1:15); and the great **confession verse** (28:13 — concealment festers and cannot prosper, but confessing *and forsaking* sin obtains mercy, cf Ps 32:5; 1 John 1:9). Alongside: integrity valued over crooked wealth (28:6), God's redistributive justice funnelling the usurer's hoard to the generous (28:8), and the public dimension — a whole people flourishes or cowers by whether the righteous or the wicked hold power (28:12).

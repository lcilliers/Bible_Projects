### Notable reads
Two of the book's great single verses land here: **the fear of man vs trust in God** (29:25 — dread of people ensnares into compromise, but resting in the LORD sets a man safe on high, cf Matt 10:28; Gal 1:10); and the **pride/humility reversal** (29:23 — self-exaltation ends in the abasement it feared, lowliness of spirit in the honour it never sought, cf Luke 14:11).

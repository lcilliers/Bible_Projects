## JER-babylon-redeemer · Jeremiah 50:1–51:64 · [D, multi] — the proud judged; the strong Redeemer who pleads their cause; sins sought and none found

**The reading.** The long oracle against Babylon indicts her *pride against God*: "**she has been proud against the LORD**" (50:29); "**Behold, I am against you, O proud one… The proud one shall stumble and fall, with none to raise him up**" (50:31–32); "**Though Babylon should mount up to heaven… destroyers would come from me against her**" (51:53). Set against Babylon's fall is a stunning word of grace to God's own people: "**In those days… iniquity shall be sought in Israel, and there shall be none, and sin in Judah, and none shall be found, for I will pardon those whom I leave as a remnant**" (50:20); and "**Their Redeemer is strong; the LORD of hosts is his name. He will surely plead their cause**" (50:34); "**Let not your heart faint**" (51:46). The finding is **the *proud self-exalting* power judged ("mount up to heaven… I am against you, O proud one") — against a people whose sins, when *sought, cannot be found* ("there shall be none"), because their "**Redeemer is strong**" and *pleads their cause*** *(stated)*. The operation is **two interiors and two outcomes: the *self-exalting* (Babylon "mounting up to heaven", "proud against the LORD") that God is "against" and that "stumbles with none to raise it"; and the *redeemed*, whose guilt has been so *thoroughly dealt with* that when God himself "seeks" for it, "**there shall be none**" — the sin not merely covered but *removed past finding*, because a "**strong Redeemer**" has "pleaded their cause"** *(inferred: the Babylon-judgment reprises the archetype of pride (self-exaltation "to heaven", 51:53, as Isa 14) — the finding is that the God-usurping interior, however high it climbs, is the one God is "**against**" and that falls "with none to raise it up" (50:32); but the inner-being jewel is 50:20 — "**iniquity shall be SOUGHT in Israel, and there shall be NONE… for I will pardon**": the finding is a picture of forgiveness so complete that the guilt is *un-findable* — not that the sin never was, but that when God *searches* for it (the God who "searches the heart", 17:10), it is *gone*, "none shall be found" (the same as "remember their sin no more", 31:34); the interior stands before the all-seeing God with its record *not merely forgiven but erased past discovery*; and the ground is 50:34 — "**their Redeemer is STRONG… he will surely PLEAD THEIR CAUSE**": the finding is that this un-findable forgiveness rests on a *Redeemer* (go'el — the kinsman who buys back and defends) who is *strong* enough to make good the redemption and who *acts as their advocate* ("pleads their cause") — so the erasure of guilt is not the interior's achievement but the work of a mighty Redeemer who takes their case as his own; the contrast is total: the proud who exalt themselves fall un-raised, while the redeemed whose cause a strong Redeemer pleads stand with no sin to be found)*.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *the self-exalting power judged ("proud against the LORD… though Babylon mount up to heaven… I am against you, O proud one" — the God-usurping interior God is against, falling with none to raise it)* (thread — a gem: self-exaltation, however high, the thing God opposes, w/ 50:29,31-32, 51:53, Isa 14:13-15, Jas 4:6); *forgiveness so complete the sin cannot be found ("iniquity shall be sought in Israel and there shall be none… for I will pardon" — guilt erased past discovery, not merely covered)* (thread — a gem: the interior standing before the all-seeing God with its record un-findable, w/ 50:20, 31:34, Isa 43:25, Ps 103:12, Rom 8:33-34); *the strong Redeemer who pleads their cause ("their Redeemer is strong… he will surely plead their cause" — the erasure of guilt the work of a mighty Advocate, not the self)* (new — a gem: the go'el strong enough to redeem and acting as advocate, w/ 50:34, Job 19:25, Isa 47:4, 1 John 2:1); *"let not your heart faint" (the redeemed steadied amid the turmoil of the age)* (thread — w/ 51:46, Isa 7:4).

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced:** the self-exalting power judged; forgiveness so complete the sin cannot be found; the strong Redeemer who pleads their cause; "let not your heart faint". Chapters 50–51 set proud, heaven-climbing Babylon (whom God is "against") beside the redeemed whose sins, when sought, "shall be none" — because their "Redeemer is strong" and pleads their cause.
- **Threads:** self-exaltation judged (← Isa 14:13; Jer 49:16; w/ Jas 4:6); sin sought and none found (← 31:34; w/ Isa 43:25, Rom 8:33-34); the strong Redeemer (w/ Job 19:25, 1 John 2:1).

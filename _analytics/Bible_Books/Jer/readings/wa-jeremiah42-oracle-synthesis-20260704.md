## JER-pray-then-disobey · Jeremiah 42:1–43:13 · [D, multi] — asking guidance one has already decided to ignore; the vow to obey broken when the answer displeases

**The reading.** The frightened remnant comes to Jeremiah with an earnest request and a solemn vow: "**Pray to the LORD your God for us… that the LORD your God may show us the way we should go**" (42:2–3); "**Whether it is good or bad, we will obey the voice of the LORD our God… that it may be well with us when we obey**" (42:6). After ten days God answers: *stay* in the land — "**Do not fear the king of Babylon… for I am with you, to save you**" (42:11) — and do *not* go to Egypt. Their reaction is instant reversal: "**You are telling a lie. The LORD our God did not send you to say, 'Do not go to Egypt'**" (43:2), and "**they did not obey the voice of the LORD**", going to Egypt anyway (43:4,7). The finding is **a *vow to obey "whether good or bad"* — made *before* the answer was known — *broken the moment the answer displeased*, exposing that they had *already decided* and merely wanted God to ratify it** *(stated)*. The operation is **the exposure of a *counterfeit seeking* of God's will: the remnant *appears* to submit — praying, vowing total obedience "whether good or bad" — but their instant rejection when the answer crossed their wish reveals that the "seeking" was never genuine; they had *already resolved* to go to Egypt and wanted *divine endorsement*, not divine direction; guidance sought by a *pre-decided* heart** *(inferred: the vow of 42:6 is exemplary in form — "**whether it is GOOD OR BAD, we WILL OBEY**" — the very posture true seeking requires (surrender *in advance* of the answer); the finding is that the words of submission were *sincere-sounding but hollow*: the test of real submission is whether one obeys *when the answer is unwelcome*, and here the answer ("stay, do not go to Egypt") crossed their fixed desire (Egypt, where "we shall not see war… or be hungry", 42:14), and they *instantly* reversed — "you are telling a lie" (43:2); the finding is that the interior can *perform* the seeking of God's will while having *already decided* — going through the motions of prayer and vowed obedience in order to secure God's *rubber-stamp* on a choice already made; the tell is the *speed and totality* of the rejection: a heart truly open would at least wrestle with an unwelcome answer, but a pre-decided heart *dismisses* it (calling the true word a "lie") the moment it conflicts; the finding is a searching exposure of *dishonest guidance-seeking* — asking God "what should I do?" when one means "please confirm what I've chosen", and the giveaway is the reaction to being told *no*)*.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *the counterfeit seeking of God's will ("whether good or bad, we will obey"… "you are telling a lie" — vowed submission exposed as hollow the moment the answer displeased)* (thread — a gem: the test of real submission is obeying the unwelcome answer, w/ 42:6/43:2, Luke 6:46, Ezek 33:31); *the pre-decided heart seeking endorsement, not direction (already resolved on Egypt, wanting God's rubber-stamp)* (new — a gem: asking "what should I do?" while meaning "confirm my choice", w/ 42:20-22, 43:2, Prov 18:2); *the giveaway of the unwelcome answer rejected instantly (a truly open heart wrestles; a pre-decided one dismisses the true word as a "lie")* (new — a gem: the speed and totality of rejection revealing the closed heart, w/ 43:2-4); *fear driving the disobedience (the pull of Egypt "where we shall not see war… or be hungry" against "do not fear… I am with you")* (thread — w/ 42:11,14, Isa 30:1-3).

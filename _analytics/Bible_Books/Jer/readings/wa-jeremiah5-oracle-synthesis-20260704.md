## JER-hardened-faces · Jeremiah 5:1–31 · [D, multi] — faces harder than rock; the stubborn heart that will not fear; loving the false

**The reading.** God offers to pardon the whole city for one just man (v.1), but the search fails. The diagnosis is *correction-proof hardness*: "**You have struck them down, but they felt no anguish; you have consumed them, but they refused to take correction. They have made their faces harder than rock; they have refused to repent**" (v.3). Beneath it, "**this people has a stubborn and rebellious heart**" (v.23) that "**do[es] not say in their hearts, 'Let us fear the LORD'**" (v.24). And the appalling collusion: "**the prophets prophesy falsely… and my people love to have it so; but what will you do when the end comes?**" (v.31). The finding is **a *hardness that correction cannot penetrate* ("faces harder than rock… felt no anguish"), a heart that *will not say "Let us fear the LORD"*, and a people who *love* the comfortable lie** *(stated)*. The operation is **the interior that has made itself *impervious to discipline* — struck, yet feeling nothing; consumed, yet refusing correction — the self-hardened face that "refuses to repent"; and beneath it a *stubborn heart* that has never even *turned inward to say* "let us fear the LORD", so that the God-fear which would soften it is simply absent; and the collusion — the people *love* the false prophets who tell them peace, actively preferring the lie** *(inferred: v.3's "**they made their faces HARDER THAN ROCK**… **refused to take correction**" is the inner-being center — the finding is that a heart can render itself *impenetrable to discipline*: God's strokes ("you have struck them") land on a surface that "felt no anguish" and "refused correction" — the corrective pain that should soften instead meets a face set like stone; and the root is named in v.24 — they "**do not say IN THEIR HEARTS, Let us fear the LORD**": the finding is that the softening fear of God is not merely disobeyed but *never even internally proposed* — the heart never turns to itself and says "let us fear him", so the one thing that would break the hardness is absent from the interior's own counsel; and the most damning note is v.31 — "**my people LOVE to have it so**": the finding is that the hardened heart is not a passive victim of false prophets but an *active collaborator* — it *prefers* the comfortable lie ("peace, peace"), *loving* to be told what it wants; the corruption is complete when the deceived *want* the deception; the closing question — "but what will you do when the end comes?" — exposes the folly of a heart that has curated its own comfortable blindness)*.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *correction-proof hardness ("you have struck them, but they felt no anguish… made their faces harder than rock; refused to repent")* (thread — a gem: the self-hardened heart impervious to discipline, the corrective pain meeting stone, w/ 5:3, 6:15, Zech 7:12, Prov 29:1); *the heart that will not even propose the fear of God ("they do not say in their hearts, Let us fear the LORD" — the softening fear never internally proposed)* (new — a gem: the one thing that would break the hardness absent from the heart's own counsel, w/ 5:24, Ps 36:1); *loving the comfortable lie ("the prophets prophesy falsely… and my people love to have it so" — the deceived actively preferring the deception)* (thread — a gem: corruption complete when the deceived want the deception, w/ 5:31, 2 Tim 4:3, Isa 30:10); *the hardness struck yet unfelt (discipline that fails to reach the interior)* (thread — w/ 5:3, Jer 2:30).

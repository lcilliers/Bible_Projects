## JER-nations-pride · Jeremiah 47:1–49:39 · [D, multi] — complacency, false trust, and self-deceiving pride judged; God's grief even for the judged

**The reading.** The oracles against Moab, Ammon, Edom, and the rest anatomise the *interiors* behind the nations. Moab's sin is *undisturbed complacency*: "**Moab has been at ease from his youth and has settled on his dregs; he has not been emptied from vessel to vessel… so his taste remains in him**" (48:11); and *pride*: "**We have heard of the pride of Moab… his loftiness, his pride, and his arrogance, and the haughtiness of his heart… his boasts are false**" (48:29–30). Ammon *trusts its wealth*: "**who trusted in her treasures, saying, 'Who will come against me?'**" (49:4). Edom is *self-deceived by pride*: "**The pride of your heart has deceived you, you who live in the clefts of the rock… Though you make your nest as high as the eagle's, I will bring you down**" (49:16). Yet God's heart is not cold: "**my heart moans for Moab like a flute**" (48:36), and to Ammon's orphans: "**Leave your fatherless children; I will keep them alive; and let your widows trust in me**" (49:11). The finding is **the nations' interiors judged — *complacency* ("settled on his dregs"), *false trust* in wealth ("who will come against me?"), and *self-deceiving pride* ("the pride of your heart has deceived you") — yet met with God's *grief* ("my heart moans") and *care* for the vulnerable ("let your widows trust in me")** *(stated)*. The operation is **three recurring disorders of the interior, drawn from the nations but universal: (1) *complacency undisturbed* — Moab "**settled on his dregs**, not emptied from vessel to vessel", the self that has never been *shaken* and so has grown stale and self-satisfied; (2) *false security in wealth* — "who will come against me?", the self walled by its treasures into a false invulnerability; (3) *self-deceiving pride* — "the pride of your heart **has deceived you**", the loftiness that *lies to itself* about its unassailability (as Obadiah); and against all three, God's *grief* over the judged and his *care* for their weak** *(inferred: the Moab wine-image (48:11) is a striking inner-being figure — "**settled on his dregs** (lees)… not emptied from vessel to vessel, so his taste remains": wine left undisturbed on its sediment grows *thick and stale*; the finding is that a life *never shaken* — "at ease from his youth", never "emptied", never disturbed by adversity or exile — becomes *complacent and coarse*, its "taste unchanged" (untransformed); the finding is that *undisturbed ease* is not a blessing but a danger to the interior, which needs to be "poured from vessel to vessel" (shaken, moved, tested) to be refined; (2) the false trust "**who will come against me?**" (49:4) and Edom's "make your nest high as the eagle's" (49:16) name the *illusion of invulnerability* that wealth and height breed — the same self-deceiving pride of Obadiah; and (3) the most searching note is God's *grief* — "**my heart moans for Moab like a flute**" (48:36): the finding is that God's judgment on the proud nations is not gleeful but *grieved* — even as he judges Moab's arrogance his heart "moans" for it (as Jeremiah wept over Judah); and his *care* extends to the enemy's vulnerable — "**let your widows trust in me**" (49:11): the finding is that God's compassion reaches the weak even among the judged nations — the fatherless and widows of Ammon are invited to trust him; so the oracles judge the proud interior while disclosing a God who grieves over the judged and shelters their weak)*.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *complacency undisturbed ("Moab at ease from his youth, settled on his dregs, not emptied from vessel to vessel" — the unshaken life grown stale and self-satisfied, its taste untransformed)* (new — a gem: undisturbed ease a danger; the interior needs shaking to be refined, w/ 48:11, Zeph 1:12, Ps 55:19, Amos 6:1); *false security in wealth and height ("who trusted in her treasures, Who will come against me?"; "make your nest high as the eagle's" — the illusion of invulnerability)* (thread — a gem: wealth and elevation breeding false unassailability, w/ 49:4,16, Obad 3-4, Prov 18:11); *self-deceiving pride ("the pride of your heart has deceived you" — loftiness that lies to itself)* (thread — a gem: pride distorting self-perception, w/ 49:16, Obad 3, Jer 17:9); *God's grief over the judged ("my heart moans for Moab like a flute" — judgment grieved, not gleeful)* (thread — a gem: God grieving even the proud he judges, w/ 48:36, 9:1, Isa 16:11, Ezek 33:11); *care for the enemy's vulnerable ("let your widows trust in me" — compassion reaching the weak among the judged)* (new — w/ 49:11, Ps 68:5).

## JER-cup-of-wrath · Jeremiah 25:1–38 · [D, multi] — the persistently-rejected word; the cup all must drink

**The reading.** Jeremiah measures the tragedy by time: "**For twenty-three years… the word of the LORD has come to me, and I have spoken persistently to you, but you have not listened**" (v.3); God himself "**persistently sent to you all his servants the prophets**", saying "**Turn now, every one of you, from his evil way**" (vv.4–5) — unheeded. Then the "**cup of the wine of wrath**" is given to all the nations to drink (vv.15–16), with the principle: "**I begin to work disaster at the city that is called by my name, and shall you go unpunished? You shall not go unpunished**" (v.29). The finding is **a word *offered persistently over decades* and *persistently refused* — and a cup of judgment that *all must drink*, none exempt ("shall you go unpunished?")** *(stated)*. The operation is **the weight of a *long-spurned* word — not a single warning but "twenty-three years" of "persistent" pleading met with "you have not listened" — so that the judgment is the ripe fruit of *sustained refusal*, not a hasty verdict; and the universality of the cup ("all the nations… shall you go unpunished?") establishes that *no one is exempt* from accountability, least of all the privileged ("the city called by my name" drinks first)** *(inferred: the repeated "**persistently**" (vv.3–4, God "persistently sent", Jeremiah "spoke persistently") set against "**you have not listened**" is the inner-being weight — the finding is that God's judgment falls only after *prolonged, patient, repeated* appeal; the interior is not condemned for a single lapse but for a *sustained deafness* across decades, so the eventual disaster is the harvest of *persistent refusal*, and God's patience (twenty-three years of pleading) is itself a mercy that magnifies the guilt of ignoring it; and the cup-principle (v.29) — "**shall YOU go unpunished?**", with judgment beginning "at the city called by my name" — establishes *universal and impartial accountability*: the finding is that no interior, and especially not the privileged/religious one, is exempt from reckoning — accountability begins with the household of God, and reaches "all the inhabitants of the earth")*.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *the long-spurned word ("twenty-three years… I have spoken persistently, but you have not listened" — judgment as the harvest of sustained refusal, not a hasty verdict)* (thread — a gem: God's prolonged patient appeal magnifying the guilt of persistent deafness, w/ 25:3-7, 2 Chr 36:15-16, Rom 2:4-5); *universal, impartial accountability ("shall you go unpunished?… beginning at the city called by my name" — no interior exempt, judgment starting with the privileged)* (new — a gem: accountability begins with the household of God, w/ 25:29, 1 Pet 4:17, Amos 3:2); *God's patience as itself mercy (the decades of pleading before judgment)* (thread — w/ 25:4-5, Rom 2:4).

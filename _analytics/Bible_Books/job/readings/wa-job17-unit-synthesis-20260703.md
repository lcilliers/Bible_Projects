## JOB-17-A · Job 17:1–9 · [D, multi] — the broken spirit; the righteous holding to his way

**The reading.** Job's inner collapse is stated plainly: "my **spirit is broken**; my days are extinct; the graveyard is ready for me" (v.1). He is surrounded by "**mockers**" (v.2), made "a **byword** of the peoples" (v.6). Yet against the collapse he holds a conviction: "the **righteous holds to his way**, and he who has **clean hands** grows **stronger and stronger**" (v.9). The finding is **a broken spirit that nonetheless clings to its integrity, even growing firmer in it** *(stated)*. The operation is **the paradox of a self simultaneously crushed and strengthening its grip on righteousness** *(inferred: "my spirit is broken" (v.1) and "the righteous grows stronger and stronger" (v.9) sit in the same breath — the outer man collapses while the inner grip on integrity *tightens*; the finding is that brokenness and moral resolve are not mutually exclusive — Job's spirit is shattered yet his hold on "clean hands" *strengthens* (chazaq, the same grip as 2:3); this is the counter-intuitive inner dynamic the friends miss: real integrity can *deepen* precisely under the pressure that breaks everything else)*.

**Lexical validation (read-back).** *broken* (H2254, chabal — spirit broken), *hearts* (H3820, leb), *upright* (H3477, yashar), *innocent* (H5355), *stirs* (H5782), *righteous* (H6662, tsaddiq), *clean* (H2889, tahor — "clean hands"), *stronger* (H0555, amets) carried. The broken-spirit (v.1) / grows-stronger (v.9, amets) contrast is the reading's spine; correctly tagged. Sound.

**Hidden / less-obvious characteristics isolated.** **Brokenness and strengthening integrity coexisting** — "my spirit is broken" (v.1) beside "he who has clean hands grows stronger and stronger" (v.9): the finding that the collapse of the outer self and the *tightening* of the inner grip on righteousness happen together; integrity is not a function of well-being — it can *strengthen* as everything else breaks. A key inner-being datum against the friends' equation of suffering with the loss of righteousness.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *the broken spirit* (new — inner collapse stated); *integrity strengthening under brokenness (clean hands growing stronger)* (new — the paradox against the friends' creed); *the mocked, byword self* (thread w/ 12:4, 30:9).

## JOB-17-B · Job 17:10–16 · [D, multi, thread] — hope collapsing into Sheol

**The reading.** But the resolve of v.9 cannot hold back the tide of despair: "my days are past; my **plans are broken off**, the **desires of my heart**" (v.11). Job addresses the grave as family: "if I say to the pit, 'You are my **father**,' and to the worm, 'My mother'… **where then is my hope**? Who will **see my hope**?" (vv.14–15). The finding is **hope reaching only toward the grave — the desires of the heart broken off** *(stated)*. The operation is **the self watching its hope descend into Sheol, unable to locate it anywhere else** *(inferred: the oscillation of the book continues — v.9's "stronger and stronger" gives way to v.11's "plans broken off, the desires of my heart"; the finding is the specific grief of *broken plans* — not just present pain but the death of a *future* (the "desires of the heart" that will not now be fulfilled); and the bitter irony of calling the pit "father" and the worm "mother" (v.14) — the grave as the only family left to a man estranged from all (19:13–19); "where is my hope?" (v.15) is the genuine question the whole book presses — Job cannot *see* his hope, though he has just glimpsed a heavenly witness (16:19); hope flickers and sinks, flickers and sinks)*.

**Lexical validation (read-back).** *wise* (H2450), *heart* (H3824, lebab — "the desires of my heart"), *hope* (H6960, qavah / H8615, tiqvah — both hope-words, vv.13,15) carried. The two hope-terms (qavah, tiqvah) frame the descent — hope sought and hope questioned; *lebab* seats the broken desires; correctly tagged. Sound.

**Hidden / less-obvious characteristics isolated.** **The death of a future (broken plans, the desires of the heart)** — "my plans are broken off, the desires of my heart" (v.11): the finding that suffering kills not only the present but the *anticipated life* — the "desires of the heart" (its projected future) are severed; a distinct grief (mourning the life that will not be lived), sharper than present pain. And the grave adopted as family (v.14) marks the total estrangement that the next chapter's friend cannot see and the following chapter (19) will voice fully.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *hope collapsing into Sheol* (thread — the oscillation, w/ 14:19); *the death of a future (broken plans / desires of the heart)* (new — mourning the unlived life); *the grave adopted as family (total estrangement)* (thread w/ 19:13–19).

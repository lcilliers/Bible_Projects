# Proverbs 11 — inner-being unit readings (Phase-2 meaning synthesis)

- **Source:** `lexical-model-2026` Phase-1 (v1.1 enriched) for Pro 11, per `segment_unit`. Grounded; *stated* | *inferred*. Lens: the inner-being **operation**; the righteous⇄wicked antithesis = arena.

---

**PRO-11-F · 11:3–31 · [F]** — the moral-poles frame (righteous/upright vs wicked/treacherous). Arena; the units below are the specific findings within it.

**PRO-11-pride · 11:2 · [S, multi] — pride and its shame; humility and wisdom.** "When **pride** comes, then comes **disgrace**, but with the **humble is wisdom**" (v.2). Finding: pride *carries its own disgrace*, and wisdom keeps company with the humble — the inner posture *determines* the outcome *(stated)*. Operation: **pride as self-defeating, humility as the seat of wisdom** *(inferred; the pride/humility thread, cross-book w/ Ps 138:6, and Pro 16:18, 29:23)*. *Surfaced:* pride vs humility (★ shared cornerstone thread).

**PRO-11-speech · 11:9,11,12,13 · [T, multi] — speech that destroys or keeps.** "With his **mouth** the godless would **destroy his neighbor**"; "whoever **belittles** his neighbor lacks sense, but a man of understanding **remains silent**"; "whoever goes about **slandering reveals secrets**, but he who is **trustworthy in spirit keeps** a thing **covered**" (vv.9,12,13). Finding: speech is the outflow that either *destroys* or *guards* — and the guarding takes two forms: **restrained silence** and **trustworthy discretion** (keeping secrets) *(stated)*. Operation: **the trustworthy-in-spirit self *keeps covered*** — discretion as an inner reliability, the opposite of the leaking, belittling tongue *(inferred; the speech-legibility thread + a new note on confidentiality as inner trustworthiness)*. *Validation:* *mouth→of wicked*, and the destroy/belittle/slander objects tagged. *Hidden:* **trustworthiness-of-spirit as discretion** (keeping a confidence = an inner-being fidelity). *Surfaced:* speech-legibility (shared); trustworthy discretion (new).

**PRO-11-generosity · 11:17,24,25,26 · [T, multi] — kindness/generosity that returns on the self.** "A man who is **kind benefits himself**, but a **cruel man hurts himself**"; "one **gives freely, yet grows all the richer**; another **withholds** and only **suffers want**… whoever **waters** will himself be **watered**" (vv.17,24–25). Finding: kindness and cruelty are **self-affecting** — the disposition toward others *returns upon the self* *(stated)*. Operation: **generosity as self-enriching, cruelty as self-harming** — the inner posture toward others is, by a moral reciprocity, a posture toward oneself *(inferred; the outward-facing heart of 3:27, now with the boomerang principle)*. *Validation:* kind/cruel with reflexive objects; *give/withhold/water* tagged. *Surfaced:* generosity vs withholding (new); the disposition-toward-others-returns-on-self (new).

**PRO-11-trust-riches · 11:28 · [S] — trust misplaced in riches.** "Whoever **trusts in his riches** will **fall**, but the righteous will **flourish** like a green leaf" (v.28). Finding: **mis-placed trust collapses** — leaning on wealth topples the self *(stated)*. Operation: trust in riches vs the flourishing of the God-rooted self *(inferred; cross-book w/ Ps 49:6, 52:7, 62:10 — the same mis-placed-trust datum)*. *Surfaced:* mis-placed trust in riches (shared w/ Ps).

**PRO-11-desire · 11:23 · [S] — the direction of desire decides its end.** "The **desire of the righteous** ends only in **good**, the **expectation of the wicked** in wrath" (v.23). Finding: desire and expectation carry opposite ends by their moral direction *(stated; the directional-desire thread)*. *Validation:* *expectation→of wealth* (elsewhere); desire/expectation with poles. *Surfaced:* directional desire/expectation (shared w/ Ps hope-thread).

## Catch-up unit (2026-07-04) — loose study-term verse bound to a unit

**PRO-11-honesty · Prov 11:1 · [S] — honesty as what the LORD delights in.** "A **false balance is an abomination** to the LORD, but a **just weight is his delight**" (v.1). Finding: the interior disposition behind honest dealing is what **God delights in** — integrity in the unseen (the weighing) is a delight to him, deceit an abomination *(stated)*. Operation: **integrity located in the hidden practice** — the balance and weights are where cheating hides; God's delight/abomination attaches to that concealed honesty or fraud *(inferred; the heart tested where no one sees, cross-book w/ Job 31:6 "let me be weighed in a just balance"; the heading of ch.11's collection)*. *Surfaced:* honesty-in-the-hidden as God's delight (new — w/ Job 31:6); integrity at the concealed weighing.

# Proverbs 1 — inner-being unit readings (Phase-2 meaning synthesis)

- **Source:** the `lexical-model-2026` Phase-1 lexicals for Pro 1:1–33, read **per segmentation unit** (`segment_unit`, provenance `proverbs-segmentation-v1-20260703`). Complete chapter (3 vv. backfilled). Lexicals only; cited; grounded; inferences tagged *stated* | *inferred*.
- **Lens:** the finding is the inner-being **operation**; wisdom/God-relation is the arena (`feedback_lens_is_inner_being_process_not_god_relation`).
- **Method (unit meaning-synthesis):** each unit is read for its inner-being meaning, and that reading **reads back and validates the Phase-1 lexical** — checking the roles, the **pairs** (bearer / manner / coupling) and flags for sensibility, and **isolating hidden or less-obvious characteristics** the provisional segmentation did not name. Validation catches (bearer=LORD bleed; prohibition over-stamp) are logged per unit, not re-derived.

## PRO-01-A · Prov 1:1–6 · [D, multi] — the formation of the inner being (wisdom's aims)

**The reading.** The book opens not with a command but with a *list of what wisdom builds in a person* — "to **know** wisdom… to **understand** words of insight… to **receive** instruction… to give **prudence** to the simple, **knowledge** and **discretion** to the youth" (vv.2–4). The finding is the **inner faculties wisdom is meant to form**: knowing, understanding, prudence, discretion, and the disposition to *receive* instruction *(stated: these are the stated aims)*. The operation the unit foregrounds is **receptivity that grows** — "let the wise **hear** and **increase** in learning" (v.5): even the already-wise are defined by a *continuing intake*, a self that keeps taking in *(inferred: the aims are not a fixed state but a faculty-set held open to increase)*.

**Lexical validation (read-back).** Sound. The lexical correctly tags *know, understand, insight, wise-dealing, prudence, knowledge, wise, hear, guidance* as **characteristics** — the faculty-cluster the reading rests on. One **pair-sensibility note:** *simple* (H6612, v.4) is tagged `process-qualifier` welding *give* (manner/coupling pair `simple→give`); it is really the **recipient/target** of "give prudence *to* the simple," not a manner — a minor role-slippage (recipient read as manner). It does not affect the reading (the simple is still the one being formed). The genre/superscription terms of v.1 (*proverbs, Solomon, David, king, Israel*) correctly sit `standalone`.

**Hidden / less-obvious characteristics isolated.** (1) **Receptivity-as-increase** (v.5) — distinct from mere "reception"; the wise self *keeps growing*, a hidden characteristic under the "aims." (2) The **transmission of the inner life** — wisdom is aimed *from* teacher *to* the simple/youth (vv.4–5); formation is relational, handed on, not self-generated.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *formation/faculties of the inner being* (new to the corpus — Proverbs' distinctive); *receptivity / teachability* (shared w/ Ps); *the transmission of wisdom* (new).

## PRO-01-B · Prov 1:7 · [S] — the fear of the LORD as the beginning/root

**The reading.** The single most load-bearing line in the book, given its own unit by weight: "The **fear** of the LORD is the **beginning** of knowledge; fools despise wisdom and instruction." The finding: the inner being's **foundational orientation is reverent fear** — knowledge does not *start* with intellect but with awe *(stated)*. The operation: fear is the *root from which the whole faculty-cluster of PRO-01-A grows* — and its refusal ("fools **despise**") is the inner posture that forfeits it *(inferred: the motto grounds vv.2–6 in a prior disposition; cf. 9:10, and bookended by 31:30)*. This is the same cornerstone as Ps 111:10, here at the head of Proverbs.

**Lexical validation (read-back).** The key term *fear* (H3374) is correctly a **characteristic**. But this verse is a clear case of the **bearer=LORD bleed** — the nearest-proper heuristic wrongly stamps `bearer=Lord` on *beginning, knowledge, fools, despise, wisdom, instruction* (pairs `H3068→…`). Read straight from the lexical, it would say *the LORD despises wisdom* — plainly wrong: it is the **fools** who despise. The reading takes the bearers from the text, not the tags (the fools despise; the fear is the person's). A flagged residual, not a defect in the reading.

**Hidden / less-obvious characteristics isolated.** The verse holds a **paired opposition** the segmentation named as one characteristic but which is really two: *reverent fear* (the root) **and** *the despising of wisdom* (its refusal) — the fool's contempt is a distinct inner-being operation (the closed, scornful self), worth its own thread across the book (scoffer/fool).

**Characteristics surfaced:** *the fear of the LORD (as root)* (shared cornerstone w/ Ps 111:10); *the despising/scorning of wisdom* (new thread — the scoffer's closed heart).

## PRO-01-C · Prov 1:8–19 · [D, multi] — filial receptivity; the self-destructive greedy heart

**The reading.** Two inner-being movements held together. First, **filial receptivity**: "**Hear**, my son, your father's instruction, and **forsake not** your mother's teaching" (v.8) — the self that *takes in* the handed-down word, worn like a garland (v.9) *(stated)*. Second, and the unit's gem, the **self-destructive greedy heart**: the enticers say "let us **lie in wait** for blood… we shall **fill our houses** with plunder" (vv.11–13), but the trap they set closes on *themselves* — "these men **lie in wait for their own blood**; they set an ambush for their **own lives**" (v.18). The operation: **greed recoils on the greedy** — "such are the ways of everyone who is **greedy for unjust gain**; it takes away the **life** of its possessors" (v.19); the appetite for plunder is *self-consuming* *(inferred: the "own blood/own lives" turns the ambush self-ward — the greedy heart digs its own pit; cf. Ps 7:15–16, 9:15)*.

**Lexical validation (read-back).** The characteristics carrying the reading — *hear* (v.8, receptivity), *graceful* (v.9) — are correctly tagged. **Prohibition over-stamp** on v.8: the negation "forsake **not**" wrongly stamps `prohibition=forbidden` onto *father's, instruction, forsake, mother's, teaching* — the whole clause, not the verb; a known neg-particle bleed (the line is an *appeal*, not a legal prohibition). The greed-recoil of vv.17–19 sits largely in gate-2 content (*ambush, plunder, blood, life*); the reading is read from the structure (own-blood/own-lives), correctly.

**Hidden / less-obvious characteristics isolated.** The **greed-that-self-destructs** is the deeper find — not "greed" as a label but the *mechanism* by which the acquisitive inner being **becomes its own victim** (the appetite consuming its owner). This is a Proverbs sharpening of a Psalter theme (the pit-digger caught in his own pit) and belongs to a cross-book thread on **self-inflicted ruin**.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *filial receptivity* (shared); *the self-destructive greedy heart / appetite that consumes its owner* (new — sharper than anything in Ps); *self-inflicted ruin (the pit-digger)* (shared w/ Ps 7/9).

## PRO-01-D · Prov 1:20–33 · [D, multi] — the refusal of wisdom; the security of the one who listens

**The reading.** Wisdom personified cries in the street, and the unit anatomises the **inner being that refuses her**: "How long, O **simple** ones, will you **love** being simple? How long will **scoffers delight** in their scoffing and **fools hate knowledge**?" (v.22). Three refusing dispositions are named as *loves and delights* — the self that **loves its own simplicity**, **delights in scoffing**, **hates knowledge** *(stated)*: refusal is not neutral ignorance but an *affection for un-wisdom*. Its end is the terrible reciprocity of vv.24–31 — "because you **ignored all my counsel**… I will not answer when you call" — and the un-answerable regret of a self that would not listen when it could. Against it stands the unit's resolution: "whoever **listens** to me will **dwell secure** and will be **at ease, without dread** of disaster" (v.33) — the **inner security of the receptive self** *(inferred: the listening heart's reward is not information but *rest without dread*, the same fearless security as Ps 112:7, 3:24)*.

**Lexical validation (read-back).** *love* (H0157, *aheb*, v.22 — "love being simple") is correctly a **characteristic** under the aheb-fix (PROMOTE_CHARACTERISTIC) — the *misdirected affection* toward simplicity, exactly parallel to Ps 4:2 "love vanity." *delight* and *hate* (v.22) likewise carry the refusing dispositions. The bearer/prohibition residuals recur (the "will not" of vv.24–28); the reading leans on the text. Pairs are sensible here.

**Hidden / less-obvious characteristics isolated.** (1) **Refusal as *affection*** — the aheb-fix makes visible that the simple *love* their simplicity; un-wisdom is *desired*, not merely defaulted-to (a hidden characteristic the provisional "refusal of wisdom" flattened). (2) **The un-answerable regret** (vv.28–31) — a distinct inner state: the self that *finds no answer* because it refused to listen when answered (the mirror of the "he has heard" pivot in the Psalms — here, "I will not answer").

**Characteristics surfaced:** *the refusal of wisdom as misdirected affection (loving simplicity)* (new — surfaced by the aheb-fix); *the scoffer's delight / the closed scornful heart* (new thread); *the inner security of the listening self ("dwell secure, without dread")* (shared w/ Ps 112:7, 3:24).

One **pair note**: recipient-read-as-manner at v.4 (*simple*→*give*). None touch the readings' spine; all are candidates for the same rule-adjustments flagged in the Psalms run.

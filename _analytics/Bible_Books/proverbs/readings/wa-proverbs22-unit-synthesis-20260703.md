# Proverbs 22 — inner-being unit readings (Phase-2 meaning synthesis)

- **Source:** `lexical-model-2026` Phase-1 (v1.1 enriched) for Pro 22, per `segment_unit`. Grounded; *stated* | *inferred*. Lens: the inner-being **operation**. (vv.17ff open the "Thirty Sayings.")

---

**PRO-22-humility-fear · 22:4 · [S, multi] — humility and fear rewarded.** "**The reward for humility and fear of the LORD is riches and honour and life**" (v.4). *Surfaced:* humility + fear-of-LORD (shared cornerstone thread).

**PRO-22-purity-heart · 22:11 · [S, multi] — loving purity of heart.** "**He who loves purity of heart, and whose speech is gracious, will have the king as his friend**" (v.11). Finding: the inner being can **love purity of heart** — desire *cleanness itself* — joined to gracious speech *(stated)*. Operation: **the loved clean heart** — purity is not only commanded but *desired*; the self can be in love with its own inward cleanness *(inferred; cross-book w/ Ps 24:4 "clean hands and a pure heart", 51:10)*. *Hidden:* **purity of heart as an *object of love*** (not just a duty). *Surfaced:* loving-purity-of-heart (new).

**PRO-22-prudent-sees · 22:3,5 · [S, multi] — foreseeing danger; guarding the soul.** "**The prudent sees danger and hides himself, but the simple go on and suffer**"; "**whoever guards his soul will keep far**" from thorns and snares (vv.3,5). Finding: prudence is **foresight that acts** — seeing danger *ahead* and protecting the *soul* *(stated)*. Operation: **the self-guarding foresight** (vs the naive who go on unheeding) *(inferred; discernment thread, cross-book w/ Ps 39:1)*. *Surfaced:* prudent foresight / guarding the soul (shared discernment).

**PRO-22-trust-appeal · 22:17–21 · [D, multi] — the aim of instruction is trust.** "**Incline your ear, and hear the words of the wise, and apply your heart** to my knowledge… keep them **within you**… **that your trust may be in the LORD**" (vv.17–19). Finding: the *purpose* of all the internalising (incline the ear, apply the heart, keep them within) is stated: **that your trust may be in the LORD** *(stated)*. Operation: **instruction internalised → trust** — the whole educational movement of Proverbs is *for* the sake of an inner posture of trust; wisdom is not an end but the road to trusting God *(inferred; cross-book w/ Ps 119's internalised word, and the trust cluster)*. *Hidden:* **the *telos* of wisdom is trust** — the reading of the whole book's aim. *Surfaced:* instruction-internalised-for-trust (★ new — names the book's inner-being telos).

**PRO-22-anger-company · 22:24,25 · [S, multi] — anger is caught from company.** "**Make no friendship with a man given to anger… lest you learn his ways and entangle yourself in a snare**" (vv.24–25). Finding: dispositions are **contagious** — befriend the wrathful and you *learn his ways* *(stated)*. Operation: **formation-by-company applied to *temper*** — anger is caught, so the self guards its associations *(inferred; the formation-by-company thread, 13:20)*. *Surfaced:* formation-by-company / anger-is-caught (shared w/ 13:20).

**Chapter notes.** **New/cornerstone:** instruction-internalised-*for-trust* (★ 22:19 — the book's telos); loving-purity-of-heart. **Shared:** humility+fear; prudent foresight; formation-by-company (anger caught).

# Proverbs 29 — inner-being unit readings (Phase-2 meaning synthesis)

**PRO-29-stiff-neck · 29:1 · [S] — the hardened, unteachable self broken.** "**He who is often reproved, yet stiffens his neck, will suddenly be broken beyond healing**" (v.1). Finding: repeated reproof *refused* — the **stiffened neck** — ends in sudden, unhealable breaking *(stated)*. Operation: **the un-teachable self hardening past repair** — each refused reproof calcifies until nothing can bend it *(inferred; the terminal form of the closed heart, cross-book w/ Ps 95:8, and the teachability thread)*. *Surfaced:* the hardened/unteachable self (★ shared — the terminal closed heart).

**PRO-29-vent-spirit · 29:11 · [S] — venting vs holding back the spirit.** "**A fool gives full vent to his spirit, but a wise man quietly holds it back**" (v.11). Finding — a cornerstone: the fool **discharges** his whole spirit; the wise **restrains** it *(stated)*. Operation: **self-restraint as the holding-back of the spirit** — the mastered self does not spill its every impulse; it *quietly holds it back* *(inferred; the self-mastery thread, cross-book w/ 16:32, 25:28, and the Psalter's self-quieting, Ps 62:1, 131:2)*. *Surfaced:* self-restraint / holding-back-the-spirit (★ cornerstone, cross-book w/ Ps 131).

**PRO-29-pride-lowly · 29:23 · [S, multi] — pride brings low; the lowly are lifted.** "**One's pride will bring him low, but he who is lowly in spirit will obtain honour**" (v.23). Finding: pride **lowers**, lowliness **lifts** — the inversion *(stated; pride/humility cornerstone, cross-book w/ Ps 138:6, 18:27)*. *Surfaced:* pride/humility (★ shared cornerstone).

**PRO-29-fear-man · 29:25 · [S, multi] — the fear of man vs trust in the LORD.** "**The fear of man lays a snare, but whoever trusts in the LORD is safe**" (v.25). Finding — a cornerstone: **fear of man is a *snare*** to the self; trust in the LORD is safety *(stated)*. Operation: **the fear of man as an inner trap, dissolved by trust** — the dread of people *ensnares* the self, and only Godward trust frees it *(inferred; cross-book w/ Ps 56:11, 118:6 "the LORD is on my side; I will not fear. What can man do to me?")*. *Surfaced:* fear-of-man-vs-trust (★ cornerstone, cross-book w/ Ps 56/118).

**PRO-29-anger · 29:22 · [S] — the wrathful stir strife.** "**A man of wrath stirs up strife, and one given to anger causes much transgression**" (v.22). *Surfaced:* anger (shared thread).

## Catch-up units (2026-07-04) — loose study-term verses bound to units

**PRO-29-rule-public · Prov 29:2, 4, 14, 16 · [D, multi] — just rule and the people's mood.** "When the **righteous increase, the people rejoice**, but when the **wicked rule, the people groan**" (v.2); "by **justice a king builds up the land**" (v.4); "if a **king faithfully judges the poor, his throne** will be established forever" (v.14); "when the wicked increase, transgression increases, but the **righteous will look upon their downfall**" (v.16). Finding: righteousness in power **makes a people rejoice and a throne endure**; wickedness makes them **groan** *(stated)*. Operation: **the corporate interior rising or sinking with the justice of its rulers** — the king who judges the poor faithfully secures his throne *(inferred; the public mood, w/ 28:12,28)*. *Surfaced:* the people's joy/groaning under just/unjust rule (thread w/ 28:12); faithful judgment of the poor as what establishes.

**PRO-29-snare-song · Prov 29:6, 27 · [S, multi] — the snare and the song; the mutual abomination of the two ways.** "An **evil man is ensnared in his transgression, but a righteous man sings and rejoices**" (v.6); "an **unjust man is an abomination to the righteous, but one whose way is straight is an abomination to the wicked**" (v.27). Finding: the two ways issue in **a snare and a song** — the evil trapped in his own sin, the righteous free enough to *sing* — and each way is **abhorrent to the other** *(stated)*. Operation: **the self ensnared by its own transgression vs the self at liberty to rejoice** — sin is a net that closes on its doer; righteousness opens into song *(inferred; sin as self-snaring, w/ 5:22; the incompatible interiors)*. *Surfaced:* the snare of one's own sin vs the freed song (new gem — w/ 5:22); the mutual abomination of the two ways.

**PRO-29-child-discipline · Prov 29:3, 15, 17, 19 · [D, multi] — the child, the rod, and what mere words cannot do.** "He who **loves wisdom makes his father glad**, but a companion of prostitutes squanders" (v.3); "the **rod and reproof give wisdom**, but a child left to himself shames his mother" (v.15); "**discipline your son, and he will give you rest**… delight to your heart" (v.17); "by **mere words a servant is not disciplined**, for though he understands, he will not respond" (v.19). Finding: **loving wisdom gladdens; discipline gives wisdom and rest**; and some are not moved by words alone *(stated)*. Operation: **formation through loving correction, and the limit of words** — the child left to himself drifts to shame; discipline returns *rest and delight*; but understanding without will does not respond *(inferred; discipline-as-rest, w/ 19:18; the gap between understanding and responding)*. *Surfaced:* discipline yielding rest and delight (new); the will that words alone cannot move.

**PRO-29-poor-justice · Prov 29:7, 26 · [S, multi] — the righteous knowing the poor's cause; justice from the LORD.** "A **righteous man knows the rights of the poor**; a wicked man does not understand such knowledge" (v.7); "**many seek the face of a ruler, but it is from the LORD** that a man gets justice" (v.26). Finding: righteousness includes **knowing the poor's cause** (which the wicked cannot grasp), and true justice comes **from God**, not the ruler's favour *(stated)*. Operation: **the interior attuned to the poor, and the eye that looks past power to God for justice** *(inferred; the poor's cause, w/ 28:5; justice from the LORD)*. *Surfaced:* knowing the poor's rights as righteousness (thread w/ 28:5); justice from God over the ruler's face.

**PRO-29-inflaming-speech · Prov 29:8, 9, 20 · [D, multi] — the inflaming scoffer, the raging fool, the hasty tongue.** "**Scoffers set a city aflame, but the wise turn away wrath**" (v.8); "if a wise man argues with a fool, the **fool only rages and laughs, and there is no quiet**" (v.9); "do you see a man **hasty in his words**? There is **more hope for a fool** than for him" (v.20). Finding: scoffers **inflame**, the wise **defuse**; arguing with a fool yields **no quiet**; and the **hasty tongue** is worse than folly *(stated)*. Operation: **speech that ignites vs speech that cools; the futility of reasoning with rage** — the scoffer is a fire-setter, the fool un-quietable, the hasty-worded beyond hope *(inferred; the peacemaker vs the inflamer, w/ 15:1; hasty speech, w/ Ecc 5:2)*. *Surfaced:* the scoffer as fire-setter / the wise as wrath-turner (new); the futility of arguing with a raging fool; the hasty tongue below folly.

**PRO-29-hatred-complicity · Prov 29:10, 12, 24 · [S, multi] — hatred of the blameless; complicity from the top and bottom.** "**Bloodthirsty men hate one who is blameless** and seek the life of the upright" (v.10); "if a **ruler listens to falsehood, all his officials will be wicked**" (v.12); "the **partner of a thief hates his own life**; he hears the curse but discloses nothing" (v.24). Finding: the violent **hate the blameless**; a ruler's ear for lies **corrupts his whole court**; and the thief's silent partner **hates his own life** *(stated)*. Operation: **corruption spread from the ruler's ear down, and complicity as self-hatred** — heeding falsehood makes wicked officials; the accomplice who will not disclose destroys himself *(inferred; the ruler's ear shaping the court; complicity as self-harm)*. *Surfaced:* the ruler's ear corrupting the court (new); complicity as self-hatred; hatred of the blameless.

**PRO-29-restraint · Prov 29:18 · [S] — vision and the restraint it gives.** "Where there is **no prophetic vision the people cast off restraint**, but blessed is he who **keeps the law**" (v.18). Finding: without revelatory **vision the people run unrestrained**; the law-keeper is blessed *(stated)*. Operation: **restraint as a gift of vision/law** — the interior needs a revealed word to hold it in bounds; without it, it unravels *(inferred; the restraining word, w/ the law-ear thread 28:9)*. *Surfaced:* vision/law as what restrains the self (thread w/ 28:9).

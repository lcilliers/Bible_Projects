# Proverbs 20 — inner-being unit readings (Phase-2 meaning synthesis)

- **Source:** `lexical-model-2026` Phase-1 (v1.1 enriched) for Pro 20, per `segment_unit`. Grounded; *stated* | *inferred*. Lens: the inner-being **operation**.

---

**PRO-20-spirit-lamp · 20:27 · [S] — the spirit as the lamp of the LORD searching the innermost.** "**The spirit of man is the lamp of the LORD, searching all his innermost parts**" (v.27). Finding — a cornerstone: the human *spirit* is God's **lamp** by which the self's **innermost parts** are searched and lit *(stated)*. Operation: **the self illumined and searched to its depths** — the conscience/spirit is a divine light *inside* the person, exposing what is hidden even from the self *(inferred; the being-searched cornerstone at its most interior; cross-book w/ Ps 139:23 "search me… know my heart", 20:27 the deepest interiorisation of that datum)*. *Surfaced:* the spirit as God's lamp searching the innermost (★ cornerstone, cross-book w/ Ps 139).

**PRO-20-heart-opaque · 20:5 · [S] — the heart's deep, drawable purpose.** "**The purpose in a man's heart is like deep water, but a man of understanding will draw it out**" (v.5). Finding: the heart's purpose is **deep and hidden** — yet a discerning person can *draw it up* like water from a well *(stated)*. Operation: **the opacity *and* the drawability of the inner life** — the depths are real but not wholly sealed; understanding can reach them *(inferred; cross-book w/ Ps 42:7 "deep calls to deep", the unsearchable-yet-reachable heart; and 25:3)*. *Hidden:* **the inner life as *drawable* by the discerning** (opacity is not total). *Surfaced:* the deep/opaque-yet-drawable heart (★ new — the interior's depth and its reachability).

**PRO-20-pure-heart · 20:9 · [S] — who can say "I have made my heart pure"?** "**Who can say, 'I have made my heart pure; I am clean from my sin'?**" (v.9). Finding — a cornerstone: no one can claim to have **self-cleansed the heart** — the interior cannot be purified by its own effort *(stated)*. Operation: **the heart's un-self-cleansability** — a confession of the limit of self-reform, throwing the self on mercy *(inferred; cross-book w/ Ps 51:10 "*create* in me a clean heart" — precisely because the self cannot make one; and 19:12 "who can discern his errors")*. *Surfaced:* the heart cannot self-cleanse (★ cornerstone, cross-book w/ Ps 51).

**PRO-20-wait · 20:22 · [S, multi] — do not repay; wait for the LORD.** "**Do not say, 'I will repay evil'; wait for the LORD, and he will deliver you**" (v.22). Finding — a cornerstone: the wronged self is to **renounce repayment and *wait*** *(stated)*. Operation: **entrusting the grievance to God rather than avenging** — the same move as the Psalter's wronged-self-becomes-prayer (Ps 109:4) and wait-on-the-LORD (Ps 27:14); vengeance handed over *(inferred; cross-book w/ Ps 37, Rom 12:19)*. *Surfaced:* not-repaying / waiting on the LORD (★ shared cornerstone, cross-book w/ Ps 37/109).

**Chapter notes.** **Cornerstones (cross-book w/ Ps):** the spirit as God's lamp searching the innermost (★ 20:27, w/ Ps 139); the heart cannot self-cleanse (★ 20:9, w/ Ps 51); not-repaying/waiting (★ 20:22, w/ Ps 37/109). **New:** the deep-opaque-yet-drawable heart (20:5).

## Catch-up units (2026-07-04) — loose study-term verses bound to units

**PRO-20-king · Prov 20:2, 8, 26, 28 · [D, multi] — the king's winnowing eye, upheld by steadfast love.** "The **terror of a king is like the growling of a lion**" (v.2); "a king who sits on the throne of judgment **winnows all evil with his eyes**" (v.8); "a wise king **winnows the wicked**" (v.26); "**steadfast love and faithfulness preserve the king**, and by steadfast love his throne is upheld" (v.28). Finding: the king's discerning eye **sifts evil**, and his throne stands not on force but on **steadfast love and faithfulness** *(stated)*. Operation: **the discerning gaze that sifts, and chesed as the throne's true support** — power endures on covenant-love, not terror alone *(inferred; the winnowing eye + love-upheld rule)*. *Surfaced:* the sifting discerning gaze; steadfast-love-and-faithfulness as what upholds authority (w/ the chesed/emunah thread).

**PRO-20-faithful-rare · Prov 20:6 · [S] — the rarity of true faithfulness.** "**Many a man proclaims his own steadfast love, but a faithful man who can find?**" (v.6). Finding: **self-proclaimed loyalty is common; actual faithfulness is rare** — the mouth advertises a chesed the life does not deliver *(stated)*. Operation: **the gap between professed and real faithfulness** — the interior that announces its own loyalty is not the one that keeps it *(inferred; a sharp gem on self-advertised virtue, w/ Job 20:2's self-display)*. *Surfaced:* professed vs real faithfulness (new — a gem); the rarity of the truly faithful self.

**PRO-20-integrity-generative · Prov 20:7, 11 · [D, multi] — integrity that blesses forward; character legible early.** "The **righteous who walks in his integrity — blessed are his children after him!**" (v.7); "even a **child makes himself known by his acts**, by whether his conduct is **pure and upright**" (v.11). Finding: integrity **blesses the next generation**, and character is **legible even in a child** by its acts *(stated)*. Operation: **the interior that shows itself in conduct and transmits forward** — the walk of integrity is read off the deeds and reaches the children *(inferred; character self-disclosing early, and generative)*. *Surfaced:* integrity blessing forward; character legible in a child's acts (new).

**PRO-20-honesty · Prov 20:10, 14, 17, 21, 23 · [D, multi] — dishonesty and its recoil.** "**Unequal weights and measures** are both an **abomination to the LORD**" (vv.10,23); "'Bad, bad,' says the **buyer, but then he boasts**" (v.14); "**bread gained by deceit is sweet**… but afterward his mouth will be full of **gravel**" (v.17); "an inheritance **gained hastily** will not be blessed" (v.21). Finding: dishonest dealing is God's abomination, and its sweetness **turns to gravel** — the fraud that gratifies now recoils later *(stated)*. Operation: **deceit that pleases then punishes the interior** — the buyer's disparaging lie, the sweet-then-gritty bread of fraud; ill-gotten gain un-blessed *(inferred; sin's recoil, w/ Job 20:12–14 "sweet in his mouth… venom within")*. *Surfaced:* the sweet-then-gravel recoil of deceit (w/ Job 20:12); the buyer's disparaging lie.

**PRO-20-given-faculties · Prov 20:12, 24 · [D, multi] — the God-given senses and the limits of self-knowledge.** "The **hearing ear and the seeing eye, the LORD has made them both**" (v.12); "a **man's steps are from the LORD; how then can man understand his way?**" (v.24). Finding: the very **faculties of perception are God's making**, and the self **cannot fully understand its own way** — its steps are ordered beyond its sight *(stated)*. Operation: **the interior's dependence and its self-opacity** — the ear and eye are given, and the path is directed by God past the self's comprehension *(inferred; the limit of self-knowledge, cross-book w/ Job 38:36 "who put wisdom in the inward parts?", Ecc 3:11)*. *Surfaced:* the God-given faculties (w/ Job 38:36); the self opaque to its own way (w/ Ecc 3:11).

**PRO-20-prudence-speech · Prov 20:3, 4, 15, 16, 18, 19, 25, 29 · [D, multi] — strife-avoidance, prudence, and guarded speech.** "It is an **honour to keep aloof from strife**, but every fool will be quarrelling" (v.3); the **sluggard does not plow** (v.4); "there is gold… but the **lips of knowledge are a precious jewel**" (v.15); take a pledge from the reckless surety (v.16); "**plans are established by counsel**" (v.18); "**whoever goes about slandering reveals secrets**" (v.19); "it is a **snare to say rashly, 'It is holy,'** and to reflect only after making vows" (v.25). Finding: the wise interior **avoids strife, plans by counsel, guards its tongue** (no slander, no rash vows), and works in season — and **wise speech is a rarer jewel than gold** *(stated)*. Operation: **the prudent, self-governing interior** — honour in refusing the quarrel, deliberation before commitment, restraint of the secret-spilling and vow-rushing mouth; the lips of knowledge prized above wealth *(inferred; rash vows w/ Ecc 5:2–6; strife-avoidance as honour)*. *Surfaced:* strife-avoidance as honour; the rash vow as a snare (w/ Ecc 5:2–6); slander that spills secrets; wise speech as a jewel above gold. *(v.29 the glory of youth/age = arena.)*

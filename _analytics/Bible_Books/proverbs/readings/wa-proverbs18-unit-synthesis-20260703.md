# Proverbs 18 — inner-being unit readings (Phase-2 meaning synthesis)

- **Source:** `lexical-model-2026` Phase-1 (v1.1 enriched) for Pro 18, per `segment_unit`. Grounded; *stated* | *inferred*. Lens: the inner-being **operation**.

**PRO-18-tongue-lifedeath · 18:4,6,7,8,20,21 · [T, multi] — the tongue: deep waters, life and death.** "The **words of a man's mouth are deep waters**; the fountain of wisdom a **bubbling brook**"; "a fool's **mouth is his ruin**, and his lips are a **snare to his soul**"; "the words of a **whisperer** go down into the **inner parts**"; "**death and life are in the power of the tongue**" (vv.4,7,8,21). Finding — a speech cornerstone: the tongue holds **death and life**; words are *deep waters* (springing from the depths of the self) that can *ruin the soul* or *nourish* *(stated)*. Operation: **speech as the deep self surfacing, with power over life and death** — and the whisperer's words *penetrate to the inner parts* of the hearer *(inferred; the speech-legibility thread at its most weighty; cross-book w/ Ps 5:9, 52:2–4, 64:3)*. *Hidden:* **words *penetrate the hearer's inner parts*** (speech acts on the interior, not just conveys). *Surfaced:* the tongue's power of life/death (★ shared cornerstone); words-penetrate-the-inner-parts (new).

**PRO-18-refuge · 18:10,11 · [S, multi] — the true tower vs the imagined wall.** "The **name of the LORD is a strong tower**; the righteous **runs into it and is safe**"; "a rich man's wealth is his strong city, and like a high wall **in his imagination**" (vv.10–11). Finding: two refuges — one *real* (God's name), one *imagined* (wealth) *(stated)*. Operation: **the self runs to God as to a tower** — real security is a *fleeing-to* a person, while the trust in wealth is a wall that exists only *in the imagination* *(inferred; the refuge thread + the illusion of wealth-security; cross-book w/ Ps 61:3, 18:2, and 52:7)*. *Hidden:* **wealth-security as *imagined*** — the substrate/text names it a wall "in his imagination," an inner fiction. *Surfaced:* God-as-refuge/tower (shared w/ Ps); the imagined refuge of wealth (new).

**PRO-18-haughty-heart · 18:12 · [S, multi] — the haughty heart before the fall.** "**Before destruction a man's heart is haughty, but humility comes before honour**" (v.12). *Surfaced:* pride/humility (shared cornerstone).

**PRO-18-crushed-spirit · 18:14 · [S] — the unbearable crushed spirit.** "**A man's spirit will endure sickness, but a crushed spirit who can bear?**" (v.14). Finding — a cornerstone: the *spirit* is the self's load-bearing capacity — it can carry bodily illness, but if the *spirit itself* is crushed, nothing remains to bear it *(stated)*. Operation: **the spirit as the bearer of the self; its crushing is the limit of endurance** *(inferred; the deepest statement of the felt interior's floor; cross-book w/ Ps 42:5–6, 143:4 "my spirit faints within me", and Pro 17:22, 15:13)*. *Surfaced:* the crushed spirit as the limit of endurance (★ cornerstone — the spirit as load-bearer).

**PRO-18-hearing · 18:1,2,13,15 · [T, multi] — hearing before answering; the seeking heart.** "A **fool takes no pleasure in understanding, but only in expressing his opinion**"; "if one **answers before he hears**, it is his folly"; "an **intelligent heart acquires knowledge**, and the ear of the wise **seeks** knowledge" (vv.2,13,15). Finding: the fool loves *only his own opinion*; wisdom **hears before it answers** and *seeks* knowledge *(stated)*. Operation: **listening as a discipline against the self's rush to speak** *(inferred; teachability + tongue-restraint; cross-book w/ Ps 141:3)*. *Hidden:* **the fool's *pleasure in his own opinion*** — a self-referential closure. *Surfaced:* hearing-before-answering (new); the self-enclosed opinion-lover (new).

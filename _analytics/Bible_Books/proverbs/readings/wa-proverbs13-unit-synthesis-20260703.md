# Proverbs 13 — inner-being unit readings (Phase-2 meaning synthesis)

- **Source:** `lexical-model-2026` Phase-1 (v1.1 enriched) for Pro 13, per `segment_unit`. Grounded; *stated* | *inferred*. Lens: the inner-being **operation**; righteous⇄wicked = arena.

---

**PRO-13-F · 13:5–25 · [F]** — the moral-poles frame. Arena.

**PRO-13-desire-hope · 13:4,12,19,25 · [T, multi] — craving, hope deferred, desire fulfilled.** The chapter's felt-interior cluster: "the **soul of the sluggard craves and gets nothing**, while the **soul of the diligent is richly supplied**" (v.4); "**HOPE DEFERRED makes the heart sick, but a desire fulfilled is a tree of life**" (v.12); "**a desire fulfilled is sweet to the soul**" (v.19). Finding: the inner being is a **creature of longing whose deferral *sickens* the heart and whose fulfilment is *life and sweetness*** *(stated)*. Operation: **hope has a bodily register — deferred, it makes the heart *sick*; met, it is a *tree of life*** — the timing of fulfilment is itself an inner-being event *(inferred; a cornerstone of the felt interior, cross-book w/ Ps 42:1–2's thirst, 130:5–6's aching wait, and the desire-met thread 145:19)*. *Hidden:* **the *sickness* of deferred hope** — waiting is not neutral; unmet longing *wounds* the heart (the shadow-side of the Psalter's hopeful waiting). *Surfaced:* hope-deferred/fulfilled (★ shared cornerstone, cross-book); the craving vs satisfied soul.

**PRO-13-speech · 13:2,3 · [S] — guarding the mouth guards the life.** "Whoever **guards his mouth preserves his life**; he who **opens wide his lips comes to ruin**" (v.3). Finding: speech-restraint is **self-preservation** — the guarded mouth guards the whole self *(stated; the self-governance-of-the-tongue thread, cross-book w/ Ps 141:3)*. *Surfaced:* restraint of the tongue as self-preservation (shared).

**PRO-13-teachable · 13:1,10,13,18 · [T, multi] — hearing, revering the word, heeding reproof.** "A wise son **hears** instruction, but a scoffer does not listen"; "by **insolence** comes strife, but with those who **take advice** is wisdom"; "whoever **despises the word** brings destruction, but he who **reveres the commandment** will be rewarded" (vv.1,10,13). Finding: teachability spans *hearing*, *taking advice*, and *revering the word* — insolence (pride) is its enemy *(stated)*. Operation: **reverence for the word vs contempt for it** as the fork of the inner life *(inferred; cross-book w/ Ps 119's love of the word)*. *Surfaced:* teachability / reverence-vs-contempt-for-the-word (shared w/ Ps 119).

**PRO-13-company · 13:20 · [S] — formation by company.** "**Whoever walks with the wise becomes wise, but the companion of fools will suffer harm**" (v.20). Finding: the self is **shaped by the company it keeps** — one *becomes* like one's companions *(stated)*. Operation: **formation-by-association** — a law of the inner life (you become your company), the same formation-principle as "you become what you trust" (Ps 115:8) applied to human company *(inferred; cross-book w/ Ps 1:1's refused seat, 26:4–5)*. *Hidden:* **the formation-law extended to company** — a Proverbs parallel to the trust-formation datum. *Surfaced:* formation-by-company (★ new — the formation-law applied to company, cross-book w/ Ps 1/115).

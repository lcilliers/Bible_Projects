# Proverbs 3 — inner-being unit readings (Phase-2 meaning synthesis)

- **Source:** `lexical-model-2026` Phase-1 (v1.1 enriched) for Pro 3:1–35, per `segment_unit`. Complete chapter. Grounded; *stated* | *inferred*. Lens: the inner-being **operation** (God/wisdom = arena).

---

**PRO-03-A · 3:1–4 · [D] — the heart as a writing-surface.** "Let your **heart keep** my commandments… **bind** steadfast love and faithfulness around your neck; **write them on the tablet of your heart**" (vv.1–3). The finding: internalisation is figured as **inscription** — the self *engraves* the teaching (and *chesed* + faithfulness) *onto its own heart* *(stated)*; a distinct image of the interior as a **tablet that can be written on** *(inferred; cf. Ps 40:8 "your law is within my heart")*. *Validation:* clean; *years→of life*, *sight→of God* (specifiers) confirm the arena. *Hidden:* **the heart as inscribable surface** (a new image of receptivity). *Surfaced:* internalisation-as-inscription (new); receptivity (shared).

**PRO-03-B · 3:5–8 · [D, multi] — trust as the renunciation of self-reliance.** "**Trust** in the LORD with **all your heart**, and **do not lean on your own understanding**… **fear** the LORD, and **turn away from evil**" (vv.5–7). The cornerstone. The finding: whole-hearted trust is defined by what it **refuses** — *leaning on one's own understanding* — so trust is an **abdication of self-sufficiency**, not merely confidence *(stated: the two halves are set against each other)*. *(inferred: the inner posture is constituted negatively — to trust God is to stop trusting the self; cf. Ps 131:1's un-over-reaching heart, and the trust cluster.)* *Validation (strong):* the v1.1 substrate nails it — *all* → seat=heart, **specifier=of heart**, intensity=all ("with ALL your heart"); *fear* (H3372) → **target=Lord** ("fear the LORD"). *Hidden:* **trust as self-distrust** (the renunciation of one's own understanding). *Surfaced:* trust vs self-reliance (shared cornerstone, sharpened); fear of the LORD (shared).

**PRO-03-C · 3:9–12 · [D, multi] — receiving correction as fatherly love.** "Do not **despise** the LORD's **discipline**… for the LORD **reproves** him whom he **loves**, as a **father** the son in whom he **delights**" (vv.11–12). The finding: the inner being is asked to **re-read discipline as love** — not rejection but the mark of being a cherished son *(stated)*. The operation: an **interpretive act** — the self must construe painful reproof as *delight*, which requires trust to perform *(inferred; the same reframe as Ps 94:12, Heb 12:6)*. *Validation:* *delights→target=son* captures the object; sound. *Hidden:* **discipline received requires an interpretive trust** (construing pain as love). *Surfaced:* correction-received-as-love (shared w/ Ps); honoring God with wealth (arena).

**PRO-03-D · 3:13–18 · [D] — wisdom as the desire that relativises all desire.** "She is more **precious than jewels**, and **nothing you desire can compare** with her… her ways are ways of **pleasantness**, and all her paths are **peace**; she is a **tree of life**" (vv.15–18). The finding: wisdom is **valued above every other object of desire** — it *out-ranks* all the self wants *(stated)*. The operation: wisdom **orders the hierarchy of desire**, sitting at its top, so that possessing it is *gain* beyond silver/gold *(inferred; the prizing operation, cf. Ps 119:72,127)*. *Validation:* the specifiers now enumerate wisdom's yields — *nothing→of desire*, *ways→of pleasantness*, *paths→of peace*, *tree→of life*. *Hidden:* **the desire that relativises desire.** *Surfaced:* prizing/desiring wisdom above all (shared w/ Ps word-thread).

**PRO-03-E · 3:21–26 · [D, multi] — the fearless rest of the wise.** "You will **walk securely**, and your foot will not stumble. If you **lie down**, you will **not be afraid**; your **sleep will be sweet**. Do **not be afraid** of sudden **terror**… for the LORD will be your **confidence**" (vv.23–26). The finding: internalised wisdom produces **security that shows as *sweet sleep* and *unafraid* rest** *(stated)* — the inner-being fruit is bodily repose and the absence of dread *(inferred; the same fearless-sleep datum as Ps 3:5, 4:8, and 1:33's "dwell secure without dread")*. *Validation:* *soul→of adornment* (wisdom as life/ornament to the soul); *ruin→of wicked*; prohibition over-stamp on "not afraid" (residual). *Hidden:* **sleep as the emblem of the un-anxious wise self** (cross-book w/ Ps 127:2). *Surfaced:* fearless security/rest (shared cornerstone).

**PRO-03-F · 3:27–31 · [D, multi] — the outward-facing heart.** "Do **not withhold good**… do **not plan evil** against your neighbor, who **dwells trustingly** beside you… do **not envy** a man of violence" (vv.27–31). The finding: the inner being's integrity is worked out **toward the neighbour** — generosity not withheld, no evil *plotted* against the trusting, no *envy* of the violent *(stated)*. The operation: the self **governs its dispositions toward others** — especially the temptation to **envy the violent** (to want the aggressor's gains) *(inferred; the refused envy, cf. Ps 37:1, 73:3)*. *Validation:* *plan→target=evil*, *man→of violence* (envy welded). *Hidden:* **envy of the violent as an inner temptation refused** (cross-book w/ Ps 37/73). *Surfaced:* the neighbour-facing heart (new); refused envy of the wicked (shared w/ Ps).

## Catch-up units (2026-07-04) — loose study-term verses bound to units

**PRO-16-A · Prov 16:4, 7, 11 · [D, multi] — the LORD who orders all, and the self aligned to him.** "The LORD has made **everything for its purpose**" (v.4); "when a man's ways **please the LORD**, he makes even his **enemies to be at peace** with him" (v.7); "a **just balance** and scales are the LORD's" (v.11). Finding: the ordering LORD is the ground, and the self that *pleases* him is brought into peace even with enemies *(stated)*. Operation: **the God-pleasing interior as the source of peace** — inner alignment with God (not managing enemies directly) is what pacifies the outer world *(inferred; peace as a by-product of a pleasing inner life, cross-book w/ the trust-security thread)*. *Surfaced:* the God-pleasing self drawing peace (new); honesty as the LORD's (w/ 11:1).

**PRO-16-king · Prov 16:10, 12–15 · [D, multi] — the king's disposition and the self that navigates power.** The king's mouth "does not sin in judgment" (v.10); "righteous lips are the **delight of a king**, and he **loves** him who speaks what is right" (v.13); "a king's **wrath is a messenger of death**, and a wise man will **appease** it" (v.14); "in the **light of a king's face there is life**" (v.15). Finding: the king's inner favour/wrath governs life and death, and the wise interior *reads and appeases* it *(stated)*. Operation: **the discerning self that navigates another's disposition** — perceiving the wrath and defusing it, courting the favour by right speech *(inferred; the wise reading of power, kin to Ecc 10:4 calmness before a ruler's anger)*. *Surfaced:* the self that reads and appeases power (new — w/ Ecc 10:4); right speech as what wins favour.

**PRO-16-value · Prov 16:8, 16 · [S, multi] — valuing righteousness and wisdom over gain.** "**Better is a little with righteousness** than great revenues with injustice" (v.8); "how much **better to get wisdom than gold**!" (v.16). Finding: the ordered interior **prefers the right/wise to the profitable** — little-with-righteousness over much-with-injustice, wisdom over gold *(stated)*. Operation: **the value-ranking of the inner being** — a self whose deepest preference is set on righteousness and wisdom, not gain *(inferred; the reordered desire, cross-book w/ Ps 119:72 "the law is better than gold")*. *Surfaced:* the interior that values the right over the profitable (w/ Ps 119:72).

**PRO-16-guard · Prov 16:17, 22, 31 · [D, multi] — the self-guarding righteous life.** "The **highway of the upright turns aside from evil**; whoever **guards his way preserves his life**" (v.17); "**good sense is a fountain of life** to him who has it" (v.22); "gray hair is a crown of glory, gained in a **righteous life**" (v.31). Finding: the upright interior **guards its own way** and so preserves and crowns the life *(stated)*. Operation: **self-preservation through vigilance over the way** — good sense is a living spring within; the guarded path leads to an honoured old age *(inferred; the self as keeper of its own road, w/ 4:23 "keep your heart")*. *Surfaced:* the self-guarding, self-preserving righteous life (w/ 4:23); good sense as an inner spring.

**PRO-16-malice · Prov 16:26–30 · [D, multi] — malice legible in speech and gesture.** "A worker's **appetite works for him**" (v.26); "a worthless man plots evil, and his speech is like a **scorching fire**" (v.27); "a dishonest man **spreads strife**, and a **whisperer separates close friends**" (v.28); "a man of violence **entices** his neighbour" (v.29); "whoever **winks his eyes** plans dishonest things" (v.30). Finding: the malicious interior is **read off its speech and gesture** — scorching words, whispering, enticing, the winking eye *(stated)*. Operation: **the inner malice made visible** — the plotting heart betrays itself in inflammatory speech, secret whispering, and the tell-tale wink *(inferred; the interior legible in the body, w/ 6:12–14 the worthless person's gestures)*. *Surfaced:* malice legible in speech/gesture (w/ 6:12–14); the whisperer who separates friends; driving appetite (26).

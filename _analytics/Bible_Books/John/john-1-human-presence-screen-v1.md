# John 1 — Human-presence screen (per-verse)

> Companion to the span heat map (`span-heatmap-john-1-v1.html`).
> Question answered per verse: **does any span confirm the presence — or allow the
> inference — of a human being?** Verses with *none* are flagged: they are the
> "pure God / cosmos" verses of the prologue, out of scope by the *IB-screen-first,
> God-is-arena* rule.
>
> Source text: `iba.db` → `verse.text` (ESV). This is an analytical judgement
> grounded in the verse wording, offered for researcher validation — not a DB flag.
> `Jesus` in a scene counts as a human present (incarnate); his divine attributes
> would be a qualifier, but his bodily presence is a human presence.

## Status key
- **confirmed** — a human person or group is explicitly named / referenced.
- **inferred** — humans are implied though not named.
- **none ⚑** — no human being present or inferable → **flagged (out of scope)**.

## Result

**Flagged (no human present or inferable): verses 1, 2, 3, 5** — the cosmic opening
of the prologue (the Word, God, creation, light/darkness) before John the Baptist
enters at v6 and "men / everyone / the world" bring humanity into view from v4/v9.

- **v3** and **v18** are the borderline calls: v3 is flagged (creation of *all
  things* subsumes but does not foreground humanity); v18 is *not* flagged (inference
  by negation, "no one has seen God"). Both are the researcher's to confirm.
- From **v6 onward the chapter is continuously human-populated** (John, the
  delegation, Jesus, the first disciples).

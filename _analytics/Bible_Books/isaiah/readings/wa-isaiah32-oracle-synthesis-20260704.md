## ISA-king-spirit-peace · Isa 32:1–20 · [D, multi] — the fool vs the noble heart; the Spirit poured out; peace as the effect of righteousness

**The reading.** A king reigns in righteousness, and each ruler becomes "**like a hiding place from the wind, a shelter from the storm… like the shade of a great rock in a weary land**" (v.2). Perception is restored — "**the eyes of those who see will not be closed… the heart of the hasty will understand**" (vv.3–4). Then a contrast of interiors: the *fool* whose "**heart is busy with iniquity, to practise ungodliness… to leave the craving of the hungry unsatisfied**" (v.6), against the *noble* who "**plans noble things, and on noble things he stands**" (v.8). The complacent women are shaken (vv.9–13), "**until the Spirit is poured upon us from on high**" (v.15). Then the fruit: "**the effect of righteousness will be peace, and the result of righteousness, quietness and trust forever**" (v.17). The finding is **two settled dispositions contrasted — the *fool*, whose heart is "busy with iniquity", and the *noble*, who "stands on noble things" — with true renewal coming only when "the Spirit is poured from on high", and its effect named as *peace, quietness, and trust*** *(stated)*. The operation is **the interior understood as a *stable disposition* that generates its acts (the fool's heart "busy with iniquity" produces folly; the noble "stands on noble things" and so plans them) — a disposition that cannot be self-manufactured but is given when "the Spirit is poured from on high", whose signature in the interior is settled *peace and quiet trust*** *(inferred: the fool/noble contrast (vv.5–8) is a disposition-study — the "**fool**" (nabal) is defined by an *inner busyness*: "his **heart is busy with iniquity**" (v.6, the interior actively engineering evil), while the "**noble**" (nadiv) "**plans noble things, and on noble things he stands**" (v.8, ken — established, settled): the finding is that character is a *standing* — one's acts flow from what one's heart is settled on; and crucially this settledness is not achieved by effort but *given* — "**until the Spirit is poured upon us from on high**" (v.15) is the turning point, the wilderness becoming fruitful only by the Spirit's outpouring; the finding is that the transformation from the complacent/foolish interior to the righteous one is *Spirit-wrought*, not self-generated (as the ideal ruler of 11:2 was Spirit-rested); and the signature of the Spirit-renewed interior is peace — "**the effect of righteousness will be PEACE… quietness and trust forever**" (v.17): the finding is that righteousness and peace are *causally linked* — a rightly-ordered interior *produces* quietness and trust (the same shalom of 26:3, 30:15), so that inner peace is the natural fruit of a Spirit-given righteousness, not a mood to be chased)*.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *character as a settled standing (the noble "plans noble things and on noble things he stands" vs the fool whose "heart is busy with iniquity")* (new — a gem: acts flow from what the heart is settled on; disposition generates deed, w/ 32:6-8, Luke 6:45); *renewal as Spirit-given, not self-made ("until the Spirit is poured upon us from on high")* (thread — the interior transformed by the Spirit's outpouring, w/ 11:2, 44:3, Ezek 36:26-27, Joel 2:28); *peace as the effect of righteousness ("the effect of righteousness will be peace… quietness and trust forever")* (thread — a gem: inner peace as the natural fruit of a rightly-ordered interior, not a mood chased, w/ 26:3, 30:15, Jas 3:18); *complacency shaken (the women "at ease" summoned to tremble — false security disturbed toward renewal)* (thread — w/ 32:9-11, Amos 6:1).

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced:** character as a settled standing; renewal as Spirit-given; peace as the effect of righteousness; complacency shaken. Chapter 32 grounds the book's peace-theme in *cause* — peace is the *fruit* of a Spirit-given righteousness, and character is a *standing* the Spirit must renew, not a self-made achievement.
- **Threads:** the Spirit poured out / Spirit-wrought interior (← 11:2; → 44:3, 59:21); peace/quietness/trust (← 26:3, 30:15; the effect of righteousness); disposition generating deed (→ 33:15-16, "he who walks righteously").

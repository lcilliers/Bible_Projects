## TAB-01-freewill-offering · Exodus 25:1–40 · [the willing heart of the offering] — "from everyone whose heart moves him"

**The passage.** "Take a contribution from every man whose **heart** (lev) **moves him / is willing** (nadav)" (25:2); then the ark, mercy seat, table, and lampstand specified — *pure gold* (tahor, 25:11,17,24,29,31,36,38,39), *dimensions* (qomah "height", 25:10,23), the poles not **removed** (sur, 25:15), the testimony to be **given/placed** (natan, 25:16,21) — "make them after the pattern shown you on the mountain" (25:40).

### The operation (the one genuine inner-being span)

- **lev (heart) + nadav (moves him / is willing) — the *willing heart* of the offering (25:2).** "'take a contribution from every man whose **heart** (lev) **moves him** (nadav)'" *(stated)*. **lev** — *heart*; **nadav** — *be willing / offer freely* (the freewill-offering root): the gem is that *the sanctuary is built from willing hearts* — the contribution is *not levied* but drawn *from those the heart moves*; the interior of *voluntary giving* — the offering *measured by the heart's willingness*, not compulsion. The first thing said about building God's dwelling is a word about the *interior*: it must come *willingly* *(inferred: lev/nadav = the willing heart — the sanctuary drawn from voluntary giving)*.

### Construction detail (surfaced honestly, not read as inner-being)

The remainder of ch25 is *making-instruction*. These non-T2 spans are **material and dimensional terms**, not inner-being operations — captured here so nothing is skipped, but *not* forced into meaning:

- **tahor (pure)** — recurs 8× (25:11, 25:17, 25:24, 25:29, 25:31, 25:36, 25:38, 25:39) as the material spec *"pure gold"* for the ark, mercy seat, table, lampstand, and utensils. A *purity-of-material* term (ritual fitness of the metal), **not** an interior operation; noted, not read. (The *holiness* it serves is real, but the word here is *material purity*.)
- **qomah (height)** — 25:10, 25:23 — the *dimensions* of the ark and table. Construction measurement.
- **sur (removed)** — 25:15 — the poles *shall not be removed* from the rings (the ark ever ready to be carried). A *procedural* instruction; the nearest it comes to meaning is the ark's *perpetual readiness to travel*, but the verse states only the rule.
- **natan (give/place)** — 25:16, 25:21 — the testimony to be *put* into the ark. A *placement* instruction (God *gives* the testimony; Moses *places* it).

### Note on the block

This is the first unit of the tabernacle instructions, a **thin** stretch for inner-being purposes: it is largely *architecture*. The genuine finding — the *willing heart* — is worth capturing on its own, and may find **corroboration later** (the actual giving in Ex 35, where the willing-hearted bring so much that Moses must *restrain* them, 35:5,21–29; 36:5–7 — the willing-heart thread pays out there). Here it stands as a *single, clean span*; no more is claimed than the verse gives.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced:** **lev/nadav (the *willing heart* of the offering — the sanctuary drawn from voluntary giving)** — the one genuine inner-being span. Construction/material terms (tahor "pure" ×8, qomah "height" ×2, sur "removed", natan "give/place" ×2) noted, not read.
- **Whose interior:** **Israel** — the *willing heart* that gives (the only interior in view here). God's purpose ("that I may dwell in their midst", 25:8) frames it, but the inner-being span is the giver's willingness.
- **Threads:** **the willing heart** (nadav 25:2 → the freewill giving of 35:5,21–29; 36:5–7 — the willing-hearted who give *more than enough*, restrained; → "God loves a cheerful giver", 2 Cor 9:7 — the voluntary interior of true offering); **God dwelling** (25:8 "that I may dwell among them" ← the covenant close 29:45; → the glory filling the tent 40:34 — the indwelling thread, though 25:8 itself carries no inner-being *span*).
- **Guarded against:** forcing *"pure gold"* (tahor) into an interior "purity" motif when the verse means *material* purity (the honest reading resists the easy allegory); inventing distinct meanings for the 8 repetitions of tahor or the 2 of qomah/natan when they are the *same* construction terms; reading the ark/mercy-seat *symbolism* (atonement, God's throne) into these verses when the text here gives only *making-instruction* — the theology is real but belongs where the text states it (the mercy seat's atonement function is named at Lev 16, not here). **Nothing claimed beyond the willing heart.**

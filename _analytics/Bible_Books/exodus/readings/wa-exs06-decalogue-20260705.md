## EXS-06-decalogue · Exodus 20:1–26 · [the law reaching the heart; the two fears] — the coveting forbidden, and the fear that keeps from sin

**The passage.** "I the LORD am **jealous** (qanna), visiting the **iniquity** (avon) of those who **hate** (sane) me, who **serve** (avad) [idols]" (20:5); "showing **steadfast love** (chesed) to thousands who love and **keep** (shamar)" (20:6); "**Remember** (zakhar) the sabbath, to keep it **holy** (qadash)" (20:8); "six days you **labor** (avad)" (20:9); "the LORD **rested** (nuach)… made it **holy** (qadash)" (20:11); "honor father and mother, that your days be **long** (arakh)… the land he is **giving** (natan)" (20:12); no **false** (sheqer) witness (20:16); "you shall not **covet** (chamad)… anything **desirable** (chamudah)" (20:17); "do not **fear** (yirah/yare)… that the **fear** of him may keep you from **sin** (chata)" (20:20); "where I cause my name to be **remembered** (zakhar) I will bless you" (20:24).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **qanna (jealous) + avon (iniquity) + sane (hate) + avad (serve) — the *jealous God and the iniquity of those who hate him* (20:5).** "'I the LORD am **jealous** (qanna), visiting the **iniquity** (avon) on the children of those who **hate** (sane) me… who **serve** (avad) [idols]'" *(stated)*. **qanna** — *jealous*; **avon** — *iniquity/guilt*; **sane** — *hate*; **avad** — *serve*: the gem is *the divine jealousy* — God's *exclusive covenant-love that will not be shared with idols*; those who *hate him* (choosing other gods, serving them) carry an *iniquity* that *runs down generations*. The interior of *God's jealousy* — not petty envy but the *fierce claim of a covenant love*; and the human interior of *idol-service as hatred of God* *(inferred: qanna/avon/sane/avad = the jealous God, and idol-service as the hatred that carries iniquity)*.
- **chesed (steadfast love) + shamar (keep) — the *steadfast love to thousands* (20:6).** "'but showing **steadfast love** (chesed) to thousands of those who love me and **keep** (shamar) my commandments'" *(stated)* — **chesed** (both spans; the second's *shame* gloss is the homonym artifact — here it is *loyal love*); **shamar** — *keep*: the gem is the *asymmetry* — iniquity visited to *three or four generations*, but *steadfast love to thousands* — *mercy vastly outweighs judgment*; the interior of *God's chesed* — a *loyal love* toward those who *love and keep*, poured out *immeasurably wider* than the judgment *(inferred: chesed/shamar = the steadfast love to thousands — mercy vastly outweighing judgment)*.
- **zakhar (remember) + qadash (holy) — the *sabbath remembered and kept holy* (20:8).** "'**Remember** (zakhar) the sabbath, to keep it **holy** (qadash)'" *(stated)*. **zakhar** — *remember*; **qadash** — *make/keep holy*: the gem is that *the sabbath is an act of memory and sanctity* — the interior *called to remember* (to hold the day in mind) and to *set it apart* — *memory and holiness joined* *(inferred: zakhar/qadash = the sabbath remembered and kept holy)*.
- **avad (labor) — the *six days' work* (20:9).** *avad distinct from 20:5:* "'six days you shall **labor** (avad) and do all your work'" *(stated)* — where 20:5 was *serving idols*, here avad is *honest labor* — the *ordinary work* framed by the sabbath; the interior-context of *the rhythm of work and rest* *(inferred: avad = the six days' honest labor)*.
- **nuach (rested) + qadash (holy) — the *rest that grounds the sabbath* (20:11).** *qadash distinct from 20:8:* "'the LORD **rested** (nuach) on the seventh day… and made it **holy** (qadash)'" *(stated)*. **nuach** — *rest*; **qadash** — where 20:8 *commanded* keeping-holy, here God *made* it holy: the gem is that *the human sabbath rests on God's own rest* (← creation, Gen 2:2–3) — the interior-lesson that *rest is grounded in the Creator's rest, and the day made holy by him* *(inferred: nuach/qadash = the rest grounding the sabbath — God's own rest, the day he made holy)*.
- **arakh (long) + natan (giving) — the *honor that lengthens days* (20:12).** "'honor father and mother, that your days may be **long** (arakh) in the land he is **giving** (natan) you'" *(stated)*. **arakh** — *prolong*; **natan** — *give*: the gem is *the promise attached to honor* — *honoring the parents* tied to *long life in the given land*; the interior of *the honor that orders generations and is met with length of days* *(inferred: arakh/natan = the honor that lengthens days in the given land)*.
- **sheqer (false) — the *false witness* (20:16).** "'you shall not bear **false** (sheqer) witness against your neighbor'" *(stated)* — sheqer, *falsehood/deception*: the gem is that *the tongue's deception* is legislated — the interior of *the lie that wounds the neighbor* forbidden — *truth in speech commanded* *(inferred: sheqer = the false witness — the deceiving tongue forbidden)*.
- **chamad (covet) + chamudah (desirable) — the *coveting: the law reaching the heart* (20:17).** "'you shall not **covet** (chamad) your neighbor's house… wife… nor anything **desirable** (chamudah) that is your neighbor's'" *(stated)*. **chamad** — *covet/crave*; **chamudah** — *a desirable thing*: the gem is *THE inward turn of the Decalogue* — after murder, adultery, theft, false witness (*acts*), the tenth word forbids the *desire itself* — the *coveting* before any deed. The law *reaches into the interior*: not the hand's theft but the heart's *craving* for what is another's; *the desire named as sin*. The interior *explicitly legislated* — the grasping wish the root the earlier acts grow from *(inferred: chamad/chamudah = the coveting — the law reaching the interior desire, the craving named as sin)*.
- **yirah (fear) + yare (fear) + chata (sin) — the *two fears, and the fear that keeps from sin* (20:20).** "'do not **fear** (yirah), for God has come to test you, that the **fear** (yare) of him may be before you, that you may not **sin** (chata)'" *(stated)*. **yirah** and **yare** — *fear* (two forms in one verse); **chata** — *sin*: the gem is the *paradox of two fears* — *"do not fear"* (the *terror* that flees and hides, 20:18–19) *yet* let *"the fear of him be before you"* (the *reverent awe* that *guards against sin*). The interior distinction: the *dread that drives away* vs the *fear that keeps near and holy*; the *reverent fear the inner guard against sin* — *the same word, opposite works* *(inferred: yirah/yare/chata = the two fears — the terror that flees vs the reverent fear that keeps from sin)*.
- **zakhar (remembered) — the *name remembered, the blessing* (20:24).** *zakhar distinct from 20:8:* "'in every place where I cause my name to be **remembered** (zakhar) I will come and bless you'" *(stated)* — where 20:8 was *remembering the sabbath*, here it is *God's name remembered* (invoked in worship) drawing his *presence and blessing* — the interior-context of *worship as remembrance, met with blessing* *(inferred: zakhar = the name remembered — worship met with blessing)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **the jealous God and the steadfast love (qanna/avon/sane/avad 20:5 → chesed/shamar 20:6):** God's *jealousy* (the exclusive covenant-claim) visits the *iniquity* of those who *hate him* (serving idols) — *but* pours *steadfast love to thousands* who love and keep; the *asymmetry* — *mercy vastly outweighing judgment* — the divine interior of *jealous love and immeasurable chesed*.
2. **remembrance, rest, honor (zakhar/qadash 20:8 → avad 20:9 → nuach/qadash 20:11 → arakh/natan 20:12):** the sabbath *remembered and kept holy*, grounded in *God's own rest*; honest *labor* framing the rest; the *honor* of parents met with *length of days* — the interior ordered by *memory, rest, and honor*.
3. **the law reaching the heart (sheqer 20:16 → chamad/chamudah 20:17 → yirah/yare/chata 20:20 → zakhar 20:24):** from the *false tongue* to the *coveting heart* — the law *turns inward*, naming the *desire itself* as sin; and the *two fears* — the *terror that flees* vs the *reverent fear that keeps from sin* — the *inner guard*; the *name remembered* drawing blessing.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** **qanna (the *jealous God* — the exclusive covenant-claim)**; avon (the *iniquity* down generations); sane (the *hatred* that is idol-service); **avad ×2 (idol-*service* / honest *labor*)**; **chesed (the *steadfast love to thousands* — mercy vastly outweighing judgment; the second span's *shame* gloss the homonym artifact)**; shamar (the *keeping* of the commandments); **zakhar ×2 (the *sabbath* remembered / the *name* remembered)**; **qadash ×2 (keep-*holy* commanded / God *made* it holy)**; nuach (the *rest* grounding the sabbath — God's own); arakh/natan (the *honor* that lengthens days in the given land); sheqer (the *false witness* — the deceiving tongue); **chamad/chamudah (the *coveting* — THE law reaching the interior desire, the craving named as sin)**; **yirah/yare (the *two fears* — the terror that flees vs the reverent fear that keeps from sin)**; chata (the *sin* the reverent fear guards against).
- **Whose interior (multi-interior):** **God** — *jealous* (the exclusive covenant-love) and *showing steadfast love to thousands* (jealousy and chesed together, mercy outweighing judgment); **Israel** — the *coveting* forbidden (the heart itself legislated), the *two fears* (terror vs reverent awe), the *remembering* (sabbath and name). The two gems: the *law reaching the interior desire* (chamad) and the *reverent fear that keeps from sin* (yare 20:20).
- **Threads:** **the fear-of-God as inner guard** (yare 20:20 ← the trembling 19:16; the God-fearing midwives 1:17; Jethro's God-fearing judges 18:21 → "the fear of the LORD is the beginning of wisdom", Prov 9:10 — the *reverent* fear that *keeps from sin*, distinct from the *terror* that flees); **the coveting named** (chamad 20:17 → Achan's coveting Josh 7; "each is tempted by his own desire", Jas 1:14 — the *interior craving* the root of the outward acts); **jealousy and chesed** (qanna/chesed 20:5–6 ← 34:6–7 "the LORD, merciful… yet visiting iniquity" — the same asymmetry restated; the covenant God *jealous and abounding in steadfast love*); **the sabbath** (nuach/zakhar 20:8,11 ← creation Gen 2:2–3; the manna-sabbath 16:23 — rest as gift and memory); **remembrance** (zakhar 20:8,24 — memory the mode of covenant-faithfulness).
- **Guarded against:** lumping the two avad (idol-service / labor), two zakhar (sabbath / name), two qadash (commanded / made), the covet-pair as separate operations when they are the *one coveting*; reading God's jealousy as petty envy over the *exclusive covenant-love* the word carries; missing the *asymmetry* (chesed to *thousands* vs iniquity to *3–4 generations* — mercy dwarfs judgment, the finding); flattening the *two fears* of 20:20 into one when the verse *sets them against each other* (do not fear / let the fear be before you — the finding); importing NT "the law is spiritual… I would not have known coveting" (Rom 7:7) over the *coveting-as-sin* the tenth word plainly legislates (though the resonance is real); over-reading the altar-laws (20:24–26) beyond the *name remembered → blessing* the verse gives.

## GLD-05-presence-glory · Exodus 33:7–23 · [the Presence is everything; the longing to see God; the freedom of grace] — "if your presence does not go, do not bring us up", and "show me your glory"

**The passage.** The tent outside the camp, everyone who **sought** (baqash) the LORD going out; Moses **called** (qara) it the tent of meeting (33:7); face to face as a friend (33:11); "you say I have found **favor** (chen)… show me your ways, that I may know you and find **favor** (chen)… this nation is your people" (33:12, 33:13); "my presence will go with you, and I will give you **rest** (nuach)" (33:14); "if your presence will not go, do not bring us up" (33:15); "how shall it be known that I have found **favor** (chen)… unless you go with us?" (33:16); "you have found **favor** (chen), and I know you by name" (33:17); "show me your **glory** (kavod)" (33:18); "I will make my goodness pass, **proclaim** (qara) my name, and be **gracious** (chanan) to whom I will be gracious, show **mercy** (racham) on whom I will" (33:19); "you cannot see my face and live" (33:20); hidden in the cleft while the **glory** (kavod) passes (33:22); "I will **take away** (sur) my hand, and you shall see my back" (33:23).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **baqash (sought) + qara (called) — the *seeking of God* after the breach (33:7).** "Moses pitched the tent outside the camp… everyone who **sought** (baqash) the LORD would go out to it; he **called** (qara) it the tent of meeting" *(stated)*. **baqash** — *seek*; **qara** — *name/call*: the gem is that *after the calf, seeking God moves "outside the camp"* — the Presence no longer *among* them (33:3), those who *seek* must *go out*; the interior of *the earnest seeking of God* in the wake of the breach — *the tent of meeting a place for the hungry* (and Moses meets God there *as a friend*, 33:11) *(inferred: baqash/qara = the seeking of God — those who seek go outside the camp to meet him)*.
- **chen (favor) — *Moses leverages the favor to know God* (33:12, 33:13).** "'you say "I know you by name and you have found **favor** (chen)"… show me your ways, that I may know you and find **favor** (chen)… this nation is your people'" *(stated)* — chen, *favor/grace*: the gem is *the mediator pressing the favor he has toward two ends* — to *know God's ways* (not just be led blindly) and to *bind the people to his own standing* (*"this nation is YOUR people"*); the interior of *favor turned into a plea to know God and to carry the people* — *the friend asking to understand the friend* *(inferred: chen = Moses leveraging the favor — to know God and to carry the people)*.
- **nuach (rest) — the *Presence restored, and rest given* (33:14).** "'my presence will go with you, and I will give you **rest** (nuach)'" *(stated)* — nuach, *rest*: the gem is *the reversal of the withdrawal* — where 33:3 said *"I will not go up"*, now *"my presence will go"* — and *rest* is its gift; the interior-context of *the Presence conceded, and with it rest* — *the mediator's plea beginning to prevail* *(inferred: nuach = the Presence restored — "my presence will go", and rest its gift)*.
- **chen (favor) — the *everything staked on the Presence* (33:15–16).** *chen distinct from 33:12, 33:13:* "'if your presence will not go, do not bring us up… how shall it be known that I have found **favor** (chen)… unless you go with us? — it is your going that makes us distinct'" *(stated)*: the gem is *THE heart of the whole block* — Moses *stakes everything on the Presence*: *better no land than land without God*; the *proof* of favor is *not the gift but the Giver's going*; *what makes Israel Israel is God with them*, nothing else. The interior of *desiring God himself above every blessing* — *the Presence is the covenant's whole worth* *(inferred: chen = everything staked on the Presence — better no land than land without God; the Giver, not the gift)*.
- **chen (favor) — the *plea granted for Moses' sake* (33:17).** *chen distinct from 33:16:* "'this very thing I will do, for you have found **favor** (chen), and I know you by name'" *(stated)*: the gem is *the Presence granted on the ground of the mediator's favor* — *"for YOU have found favor"* — the people carried *on Moses' standing*; the interior-context of *the many spared for the one God knows by name* *(inferred: chen = the plea granted for Moses' sake — the people carried on the mediator's favor)*.
- **kavod (glory) — the *longing to see God* (33:18).** "'please show me your **glory** (kavod)'" *(stated)* — kavod, *glory*: the gem is *the mediator's hunger for more* — having won the Presence, Moses asks to *see the glory itself*; the interior of *the friend of God longing to behold him* — *not content with guidance, he wants God's own face/weight* — *the deepest desire, to see God* *(inferred: kavod = the longing to see God — the hunger to behold the glory itself)*.
- **qara (proclaim) + chanan (gracious) + racham (mercy) — the *sovereign freedom of grace* (33:19).** "'I will make my goodness pass, **proclaim** (qara) my name "the LORD", and be **gracious** (chanan) to whom I will be gracious, show **mercy** (racham) on whom I will'" *(stated)*. **qara** — *proclaim*; **chanan** — *be gracious*; **racham** — *have compassion* (womb-love): the gem is *the freedom of grace* — God answers the request for glory by *proclaiming his name* and declaring his grace *sovereign*: *"to whom I WILL"* — *grace is not compelled, not owed*; the interior (of God) of *free, self-determined mercy* — *goodness that gives itself where it wills*, not as debt but as gift *(inferred: qara/chanan/racham = the sovereign freedom of grace — "gracious to whom I will", mercy free and unowed)*.
- **kavod (glory) + sur (take away) — the *limit and the mercy of the vision* (33:20–23).** *kavod distinct from 33:18:* "'you cannot see my face and live'… hidden in the cleft while the **glory** (kavod) passes… 'I will **take away** (sur) my hand, and you shall see my back'" *(stated)*. **kavod** — the *glory* passing; **sur** — *remove*: the gem is *the limit held with mercy* — *even the friend of God cannot see the FACE* (the full glory would *kill*), yet God *makes a way*: the *cleft*, the *hand covering*, the *back* shown. The interior finding: *the vision of God is real but bounded* — *God gives as much of himself as a mortal can bear, and shields the rest* — *a mercy in the hiding* *(inferred: kavod/sur = the limit and mercy of the vision — the face unseeable, yet God shows his back and shields with his hand)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **the seeking and the knowing (baqash/qara 33:7 → chen 33:12, 33:13):** after the breach, *seeking God moves outside the camp*; and Moses, *known by name and favored*, presses to *know God's ways* and to *carry the people* — *the friend asking to understand the friend*.
2. **the Presence staked and restored (nuach 33:14 → chen 33:15–16 → chen 33:17):** God concedes *"my presence will go"* and gives *rest*; Moses *stakes everything on it* — *better no land than land without God*, the *Giver over the gift*; and the plea is *granted for the mediator's sake*.
3. **the longing and the limit (kavod 33:18 → qara/chanan/racham 33:19 → kavod/sur 33:20–23):** Moses asks to *see the glory*; God answers with the *proclamation of his free grace* (*"gracious to whom I will"*), and with the *bounded vision* — *the face unseeable, yet the back shown, the hand shielding* — *as much of God as a mortal can bear.*

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** baqash/qara (the *seeking* of God, outside the camp); **chen ×4 (the favor *leveraged to know God* / the *everything staked on the Presence* / the plea *granted for Moses' sake*)**; **nuach (the *Presence restored* and rest given)**; **kavod ×2 (the *longing to see* the glory / the *glory passing* in the bounded vision)**; **qara/chanan/racham (the *sovereign freedom of grace* — "gracious to whom I will")**; **sur (the hand *taken away* — the back shown, the mercy in the hiding)**.
- **Whose interior (multi-interior):** **Moses** — the *seeker* who *stakes everything on the Presence* (the Giver over the gift) and *longs to see the glory* (the hunger for God himself); **God** — *restoring the Presence*, the *free sovereignty of grace*, and the *bounded-but-merciful vision*; **Israel** — those who *seek the LORD*, carried on the mediator's favor. The gems: **the Presence is everything**, **the longing to see God**, and **the freedom of grace**.
- **Threads:** **the Presence as the one good** (33:15 ← the withdrawal 33:3; → "one thing I have asked… to gaze on the beauty of the LORD", Ps 27:4; "whom have I in heaven but you?", Ps 73:25 — God himself the treasure, not his gifts); **the longing to see God** (kavod 33:18 → "you cannot see my face" 33:20; "no one has ever seen God" Jn 1:18; "we shall see him as he is" 1 Jn 3:2 — the bounded vision now, the beatific vision hoped); **the free grace** (chanan/racham 33:19 → quoted at Rom 9:15 "I will have mercy on whom I have mercy" — the sovereignty of grace; and the *name* proclaimed here is *sounded in full* at 34:6, GLD-06); **face to face** (33:11 ← "the LORD spoke to you face to face" Deut 5:4; the friend of God, cf. Abraham "the friend of God" Jas 2:23); **the mediator's standing carrying the people** (chen 33:17 ← "blot me out" 32:32 — the many spared on the one's favor).
- **Guarded against:** reducing "show me your glory" to curiosity when it is the *deepest desire — to see God himself* (the finding); reading the *free grace* of 33:19 as arbitrariness when it is *sovereign self-giving goodness* (the name-proclamation of goodness, not caprice); collapsing the four chen into one when each *does* work (know God / stake all / granted for Moses); over-reading the *cleft and the back* into speculation when the finding is the *bounded-but-merciful vision* the verses give; not forcing the people's watching/worshipping (33:8–10, no non-T2 spans) beyond scene-context; keeping "face to face as a friend" (33:11) as the intimacy it states, *held with* "you cannot see my face" (33:20) — the paradox left, not resolved.

## EXO-14-locusts-darkness · Exodus 10:1–29 · [the God-hardened heart that is still fully itself; the court fractures] — the refusal to be humbled, the negotiated service, and the final rupture

**The passage.** "I have **hardened** (kaved) his **heart** (lev) and his servants', that I may show my **signs** (ot)" (10:1); "that you may tell your son… my **signs** (ot)… that you may know" (10:2); "how long will you refuse to **humble** (anah) yourself? let my people **serve** (avad) me" (10:3); the servants: "let them **serve** (avad); do you not see Egypt is ruined?" (10:7); "go, **serve** (avad); but which ones?" (10:8); "you have some **evil** (raah) purpose" (10:10); "go, the men, **serve** (avad); that is what you **seek** (baqash)" (10:11); the locusts **settled** (nuach, 10:14); Pharaoh **called** (qara): "I have **sinned** (chata)" (10:16); "forgive my **sin** (chattat)… **plead** (atar)… **remove** (sur) this death" (10:17); Moses **pleaded** (atar, 10:18); "the LORD **hardened** (chazaq) Pharaoh's **heart** (lev)" (10:20); the darkness; Pharaoh **called** (qara): "go, **serve** (avad); only leave your flocks" (10:24); "we serve (avad) with all" (10:26); "the LORD **hardened** (chazaq) Pharaoh's **heart** (lev)" (10:27); "never see my face again, or **take care** (shamar) — you die" (10:28).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **lev (heart) + kaved (hardened) + ot (signs) — the *God-hardening with its purpose* (10:1).** "I have **hardened** (kaved) his **heart** (lev) and the heart of his servants, that I may show my **signs** (ot)" *(stated)* — the gem (per the ledger): *God* is the agent ("I have hardened"), and *corporate* ("his servants") — the God-hardened phase, *stated as purpose*: the hardening *makes room for the signs*. The divine interior *hardens toward an end* — not caprice but *the display of his acts* *(inferred: lev/kaved/ot = the God-hardening whose purpose is the display of the signs)*.
- **ot (signs) — the signs *told to the children* (10:2).** *ot distinct from 10:1:* "that you may tell in the hearing of your son and grandson… that you may know that I am the LORD" *(stated)* — the gem: the hardening's *deepest purpose* is *pedagogical* — the signs *become the story* Israel *tells its children*, that they may *know the LORD*. The interior-context: the adversary's resistance *turned into Israel's catechism* — the deliverance *remembered and re-told* *(inferred: ot = the signs told to the generations — the hardening's pedagogical end)*.
- **anah (humble) + avad (serve) — the *refusal to be humbled* (10:3).** "how long will you refuse to **humble** (anah) yourself? let my people **serve** (avad) me" *(stated)*. The gem: **anah** — the *same word* as Israel's *affliction* (1:11,12 "afflict"; 3:7 "affliction") now names *what Pharaoh will not do* — *humble himself*. The oppressor who *afflicted* (anah) Israel *will not be humbled* (anah); the two interiors *bound by one word* — the afflicter's refusal to be *brought low* *(inferred: anah/avad = the refusal to be humbled — the afflicter who will not himself be brought low)*.
- **avad (serve) — the *servants' break* (10:7).** *avad distinct from 10:3:* "Pharaoh's servants said: 'let the men go, that they may **serve** (avad) the LORD; do you not see Egypt is ruined?'" *(stated)* — the gem: *Pharaoh's own court turns* — the servants *urge release* against their king; the *split in Egypt* (← 9:20) *reaches his own advisors*; the adversary's *corporate interior fracturing* *(inferred: avad = the service now urged by Pharaoh's own fracturing court)*.
- **avad (serve) — the *bargaining opens* (10:8).** *avad distinct:* "go, **serve** (avad) the LORD; but which ones?" *(stated)* — Pharaoh *concedes the principle* but *negotiates the scope* — the interior *bargaining to retain control* *(inferred: avad = the service conceded in principle, the scope contested)*.
- **raah (evil purpose) — the *projected malice* (10:10).** "you have some **evil** (raah) purpose in mind" *(stated)* — raah, *evil/harm*: the gem is *projection* — Pharaoh *attributes evil intent* to Israel's request; the adversary *reads his own malice into the other* *(inferred: raah = the evil Pharaoh projects onto Israel's request)*.
- **avad (serve) + baqash (seek) — the *men-only concession* (10:11).** *avad distinct:* "go, the men, **serve** (avad) the LORD; that is what you **seek** (baqash)" — and they were driven out *(stated)*. **avad** — the *partial* surrender (men only); **baqash** — Pharaoh *redefines their asking* to the minimum. The interior *offering a controlled, partial service* and *expelling* the mediators *(inferred: avad/baqash = the men-only service offered, the request minimised)*.
- **nuach (settled) — the locusts *at rest* on the land (10:14).** "the locusts **settled** (nuach) on the whole country" *(stated)* — nuach, *rest/settle*: the gem is the *ironic rest* — the *rest*-word (later the Sabbath-word, the promised-land-rest) used of the *devouring swarm* that *settles* to consume; judgment *at rest* on Egypt *(inferred: nuach = the devouring swarm settled — an ironic "rest" of judgment)*.
- **qara (called) + chata (sinned) — the *urgent confession* (10:16).** "Pharaoh *hastily* **called** (qara): 'I have **sinned** (chata) against the LORD and against you'" *(stated)*. **qara** — the *hasty summons*; **chata** — the confession *(← 9:27)*, now *"against the LORD and against you"* (even naming the wrong to Moses). The interior *confessing under the locusts' pressure* — *more desperate*, still *pressure-talk* *(inferred: qara/chata = the urgent confession under the locusts)*.
- **chattat (sin) + atar (plead) + sur (remove) — the *plea to lift "this death"* (10:17).** "forgive my **sin** (chattat), only this once; **plead** (atar)… **remove** (sur) this death" *(stated)*. **chattat** — the *sin* to be forgiven; **atar** — the *plea for intercession*; **sur** — *remove* "this death" (the locusts). The gem is *"only this once"* — the interior *knows its own pattern* (it has confessed and relapsed before) and *pleads a single exception* *(inferred: chattat/atar/sur = the plea to remove the death, "only this once" — the self-aware relapse)*.
- **atar (pleaded) — the intercession *enacted* (10:18).** *atar distinct from 10:17:* "he went out and **pleaded** (atar) with the LORD" *(stated)* — Moses *intercedes* despite the pattern — the mediator *faithful* to the pleading *(inferred: atar = the intercession enacted despite the known relapse)*.
- **lev (heart) + chazaq (hardened) — *God hardens* after the locusts (10:20).** *lev/chazaq distinct from 10:1:* "the LORD **hardened** (chazaq) Pharaoh's **heart**" *(stated)* — the God-hardening *seals* the relapse (as the confession-relief pattern predicts) — the interior *fixed by God after the plea is answered* *(inferred: lev/chazaq = God's hardening sealing the post-locust relapse)*.
- **qara (called) + avad (serve) — the *flocks-bargain* (10:24).** *qara/avad distinct:* "Pharaoh **called** (qara): 'go, **serve** (avad); only leave your flocks'" *(stated)* — after the *darkness*, Pharaoh *concedes more* (the little ones may go) but *still withholds* (the flocks) — the interior *bargaining down to the last thing it can keep* *(inferred: qara/avad = the near-total concession still withholding the flocks)*.
- **avad (serve) — the *whole* service demanded (10:26).** *avad distinct:* "our livestock must go… we must **serve** (avad) the LORD, and we do not yet know with what" *(stated)* — Moses *refuses the partial* — *not a hoof left*; the service must be *whole* (the transfer *complete*, nothing left in Egypt) *(inferred: avad = the whole, un-partial service Moses insists on)*.
- **lev (heart) + chazaq (hardened) — *God hardens* after the darkness (10:27).** *lev/chazaq distinct from 10:20:* "the LORD **hardened** (chazaq) Pharaoh's **heart**, and he would not let them go" *(stated)* — the God-hardening *again*, after the flocks-refusal — the heart *fixed* through the ninth plague *(inferred: lev/chazaq = God's hardening after the darkness)*.
- **shamar (take care) — the *final threat* (10:28).** "get away… **take care** (shamar) never to see my face again, or you die" *(stated)* — shamar, *keep/watch* (here *menacing*): the gem is the *rupture* — the God-hardened Pharaoh *is still fully himself*: he *threatens the mediator's life*; the interior *at its most defiant* even under God's hardening — agency *intact* *(inferred: shamar = the menacing threat — the God-hardened adversary still fully, defiantly himself)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **the God-hardening and its pedagogical purpose (lev/kaved/ot 10:1 → ot 10:2):** God hardens *for an end* — *to show his signs* and *that Israel may tell its children and know him*; the resistance *becomes the story*.
2. **the refusal to be humbled, the court fractures (anah/avad 10:3 → avad 10:7):** Pharaoh *will not be humbled* (the afflicter's `anah` refused), while *his own servants break with him* — *"Egypt is ruined"* — the split reaching his court.
3. **the negotiated, partial service (avad 10:8 → raah 10:10 → avad/baqash 10:11 → nuach 10:14 → qara/chata 10:16 → chattat/atar/sur 10:17 → atar 10:18 → lev/chazaq 10:20):** Pharaoh *concedes in principle but bargains the scope* (men only), *projecting evil*; the locusts *settle*; he *confesses urgently* ("only this once", self-aware of his relapse); Moses *pleads*; and *God hardens* — sealing the pattern.
4. **the final concession withheld and the rupture (qara/avad 10:24 → avad 10:26 → lev/chazaq 10:27 → shamar 10:28):** after the darkness Pharaoh *concedes all but the flocks*; Moses *insists on the whole* ("not a hoof"); *God hardens again*; and Pharaoh *threatens Moses' life* — the God-hardened heart *still fully, defiantly itself*.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** **lev ×3 (10:1 God-hardened *with pedagogical purpose*, corporate / 10:20 God-hardened post-locust / 10:27 God-hardened post-darkness)**; ot ×2 (the signs *shown* / *told to the children* — the hardening's pedagogical end); **anah (the *refusal to be humbled* — the afflicter's own word turned on him)**; **avad ×6 (the *demand* / the *servants' break* / the *scope bargained* / *men-only* / *flocks withheld* / the *whole* insisted on — the negotiation over the terms of service)**; raah (the *projected malice*); baqash (the request *minimised*); nuach (the *ironic rest* of the devouring swarm); qara ×2 (the *urgent confession* / the *flocks-bargain*); chata/chattat (the confession, "only this once" — *self-aware relapse*); atar ×2 (Pharaoh's *plea* / Moses' *intercession*); sur (the death *removed*); chazaq ×2 (God's hardening *sealing* each relapse); shamar (the *menacing threat* — agency intact under God's hardening).
- **Whose interior (multi-interior):** the **adversary** (Pharaoh) — *God-hardened yet fully himself*: confessing ("only this once"), bargaining the scope of service, projecting evil, and *threatening the mediator's life* (the God-hardening *does not make him a puppet* — he remains a confessing, bargaining, defiant self); **Pharaoh's servants** — *fracturing from him* ("Egypt is ruined") — the split deepening into his court; **God** — hardening *for the signs and the generational telling*; **Moses** — *insisting on the whole* service, and *pleading* despite the known relapse.
- **Threads:** the **hardening trajectory's sealed phase** (10:1,20,27 God-hardening — but note Pharaoh's *undiminished agency*: the God-hardening *confirms* a self that still *confesses, bargains, threatens* — answering "what else influenced it": the hardening does not remove responsibility); the **anah wordplay** (Israel *afflicted* 1:11 / Pharaoh *will not humble* 10:3 — the two interiors bound by one word); the **split in Egypt deepening** (servants 10:7 ← God-fearers 9:20); the **pedagogical purpose** (ot 10:1–2 → 12:26; 13:8,14 — the deliverance *told to the children*); the **negotiated service** (avad — Pharaoh trying to *limit* the transfer of service; Moses insisting it be *whole*); the **confession-relapse** (10:16–17 ← 9:27–34 — "only this once" naming his own pattern).
- **Guarded against:** lumping the three lev, six avad, two ot/qara/atar/chazaq (each a distinct beat — esp. the *six* avad, the *negotiation over the terms of service*); reading the God-hardening (10:1,20,27) as *removing* Pharaoh's agency when the same chapter shows him *confessing, bargaining, threatening* (the God-hardening *confirms* a fully-active self — do not make him a puppet); missing the **anah wordplay** (the afflicter refusing to be humbled); over-reading nuach (the swarm "settled") as anything but the *ironic* rest-of-judgment; treating the servants' break (10:7) as Israelite (they are *Pharaoh's* court — the fracture *within* Egypt).

## EXS-09-covenant-justice-sojourner · Exodus 23:1–33 · [justice from the inside; empathy from remembered suffering; good to the enemy] — "you know the heart of a sojourner"

**The passage.** No false **report** (shema) with a **wicked** (rasha) man (23:1); do not follow the many to **evil** (raah) in a **lawsuit** (riv, 23:2); not **partial** (hadar) to the poor in his **lawsuit** (riv, 23:3); bring back your **enemy's** (oyev) ox (23:4); rescue the donkey of one who **hates** (sane) you (23:5); do not pervert the **justice** (mishpat) due the poor in his **lawsuit** (riv, 23:6); do not **acquit** (tsadeq) the wicked, the **false** (sheqer) charge, the **righteous** (tsaddiq) and **innocent** (naqi ×2) not killed, the **wicked** (rasha) not acquitted (23:7); the bribe **subverts** (salaph) the **righteous** (tsaddiq, 23:8); do not oppress the sojourner — "you know his **heart** (nephesh), for you were sojourners" (23:9); the seventh-year fallow; the sabbath **rest** (nuach), the alien **refreshed** (naphash, 23:12); **attention** (shamar), no **mention** (zakhar) of other gods (23:13); **keep** (shamar) the feast (23:15); the angel to **guard** (shamar, 23:20); **obey** (shama), do not **rebel** (marar), he will not pardon **transgression** (pesha, 23:21); if you **obey** (shama), I am an enemy to your **enemies** (oyev), an adversary (ayav, 23:22); do not **serve** (avad) their gods (23:24); **serve** (avad) the LORD, I will **remove** (sur) sickness (23:25); my **terror** (emah) before you, your **enemies** (oyev) turned back (23:27); I will **give** (natan) them into your hand (23:31); make no **covenant** (berit) — **cut** (karat) none — with them (23:32); lest they make you **sin** (chata) by **serving** (avad) their gods (23:33).

### The operations, span by span (individual) — justice (23:1–9)

- **shema (report) + rasha (wicked) — the *false report refused* (23:1).** "'you shall not spread a false **report** (shema), nor join a **wicked** (rasha) man as a malicious witness'" *(stated)*. **shema** — *a report/hearing*; **rasha** — *wicked*: the interior of *the tongue refusing to carry a lie* and *the will refusing collusion with the wicked* *(inferred: shema/rasha = the false report refused, no collusion with the wicked)*.
- **raah (evil) + riv (lawsuit) — the *crowd not followed into evil* (23:2).** "'you shall not fall in with the many to do **evil** (raah), nor pervert a **lawsuit** (riv) siding with the many'" *(stated)*. **raah** — *evil*; **riv** — *dispute/suit* (← the quarreling 17:2): the gem is *justice against the crowd's pull* — the interior *refusing the herd* when the many do evil; *conscience over conformity* *(inferred: raah/riv = the crowd not followed into evil — conscience over conformity)*.
- **hadar (partial) + riv (lawsuit) — the *even the poor not favored* (23:3).** *riv distinct from 23:2:* "'nor be **partial** (hadar) to a poor man in his **lawsuit** (riv)'" *(stated)*. **hadar** — *honor/show favor*: the gem is *impartiality against sympathy* — even the *natural pity for the poor* must not *bend the judgment*; the interior of *justice so even it resists its own compassion* *(inferred: hadar/riv = even the poor not favored — impartiality against sympathy)*.
- **oyev (enemy) — the *enemy's beast recovered* (23:4).** "'if you meet your **enemy's** (oyev) ox going astray, bring it back'" *(stated)* — oyev, *enemy*: the gem is *active good to the enemy* — the interior *overcoming enmity* to *return what is his*; enmity *not licensing neglect* *(inferred: oyev = the enemy's beast recovered — good done to the enemy)*.
- **sane (hates) — the *hater's burden shared* (23:5).** "'if you see the donkey of one who **hates** (sane) you fallen, you shall rescue it with him'" *(stated)* — sane, *hate*: the gem is *the hater helped* — not only recover the beast but *labor alongside the one who hates you*; the interior *refusing to let hatred (his or yours) block mercy* *(inferred: sane = the hater's burden shared — mercy across enmity)*.
- **mishpat (justice) + riv (lawsuit) — the *poor's justice not perverted* (23:6).** *riv distinct from 23:2,3:* "'you shall not pervert the **justice** (mishpat) due your poor in his **lawsuit** (riv)'" *(stated)* — where 23:3 forbade *favoring* the poor, 23:6 forbids *denying* him justice: the gem is *the balance* — *neither favor nor deny* the poor; *justice due him as to any* — the interior of *even-handedness both ways* *(inferred: mishpat/riv = the poor's justice not perverted — the balance to 23:3)*.
- **tsadeq (acquit) + sheqer (false) + tsaddiq (righteous) + naqi (innocent ×2) + rasha (wicked) — the *innocent not condemned, the wicked not acquitted* (23:7).** "'keep far from a **false** (sheqer) charge; do not kill the **innocent** (naqi) and **righteous** (tsaddiq), for I will not **acquit** (tsadeq) the **wicked** (rasha)'" *(stated)*. **sheqer** — *falsehood*; **naqi** (twice) — *innocent*; **tsaddiq** — *righteous*; **tsadeq** — *justify/acquit*; **rasha** — *wicked*: the gem is *God's own standard* — *he will not acquit the guilty*, so neither may the judge *condemn the innocent*; the interior of *justice mirroring God's* — *the innocent shielded, the guilty unexonerated* *(inferred: the innocent not condemned, the wicked not acquitted — justice mirroring God's standard)*.
- **salaph (subverts) + tsaddiq (right) — the *bribe that blinds and subverts* (23:8).** *tsaddiq distinct from 23:7:* "'take no bribe, for a bribe blinds the clear-sighted and **subverts** (salaph) the cause of the **righteous** (tsaddiq)'" *(stated)*. **salaph** — *pervert/twist*: the gem is *the bribe's inward corruption* — it *blinds* even the clear-sighted and *twists* the righteous cause; the interior of *the gift that corrupts judgment* — *money bending the sight* *(inferred: salaph/tsaddiq = the bribe that blinds and subverts — money bending judgment)*.
- **nephesh (heart) — the *sojourner's soul known from within* (23:9).** "'you shall not oppress a sojourner; you know the **heart** (nephesh) of a sojourner, for you were sojourners in Egypt'" *(stated)* — **nephesh**, *soul/inner-self*: the gem is *THE interior turn of the justice code* — the ground of mercy is *empathy born of remembered suffering*: *you know his very soul — his inner experience — because you lived it*. The interior of *compassion grounded in shared memory* — *justice from the inside, felt not just decreed* — *"you know the nephesh of a sojourner"* *(inferred: nephesh = the sojourner's soul known from within — empathy from remembered suffering)*.

### The operations, span by span (individual) — sabbath, feasts, the angel (23:10–33)

- **nuach (rest) + naphash (refreshed) — the *rest that reaches the alien and beast* (23:12).** "'on the seventh day you shall **rest** (nuach), that your ox and the alien may be **refreshed** (naphash)'" *(stated)*. **nuach** — *rest*; **naphash** — *be refreshed / catch breath* (the verb of *nephesh*): the gem is *the sabbath's mercy reaching down* — *the servant, the alien, even the ox refreshed*; the interior-lesson that *rest is a mercy owed to the least* *(inferred: nuach/naphash = the rest reaching the alien and beast)*.
- **shamar (attention) + zakhar (mention) — the *guarded devotion, no other gods named* (23:13).** "'give **attention** (shamar) to all I said; make no **mention** (zakhar) of other gods'" *(stated)*. **shamar** — *keep/heed*; **zakhar** — *remember/mention*: the interior of *exclusive devotion* — *not even the names* of other gods on the lips *(inferred: shamar/zakhar = the guarded devotion, other gods not named)*.
- **shamar (keep) — the *feast kept* (23:15).** *shamar distinct from 23:13:* "'you shall **keep** (shamar) the Feast of Unleavened Bread… for in it you came out of Egypt'" *(stated)* — here shamar is *observance* — the *feast of the exodus kept* — the interior-context of *memory ritualized* *(inferred: shamar = the feast kept — the exodus remembered)*.
- **shamar (guard) — the *angel who guards the way* (23:20).** *shamar distinct from 23:13,15:* "'I send an angel to **guard** (shamar) you on the way'" *(stated)* — here *God's own keeping* — the interior-context of *the guarded journey, the presence going before* *(inferred: shamar = the angel who guards the way)*.
- **shama (obey) + marar (rebel) + pesha (transgression) — the *angel obeyed, not provoked* (23:21).** "'**obey** (shama) his voice; do not **rebel** (marar); he will not pardon your **transgression** (pesha), for my name is in him'" *(stated)*. **shama** — *hear/obey*; **marar** — *provoke/rebel* (bitter-root, ← Marah 15:23); **pesha** — *transgression*: the gem is *the warning against rebellion* — the angel *bears God's name* and *will not pardon* a *provoking transgression*; the interior of *the will called to obey and not embitter* *(inferred: shama/marar/pesha = the angel obeyed, not provoked — rebellion unpardoned)*.
- **shama (obey) + oyev (enemies) + ayav (hostile) — the *obedience that aligns God against your enemies* (23:22).** *shama/oyev distinct:* "'if you carefully **obey** (shama), I will be an enemy to your **enemies** (oyev) and an adversary (ayav) to your adversaries'" *(stated)*. **shama** — again *obey*; **oyev** — *enemy*; **ayav** — *be hostile*: the gem is *obedience aligning God with you* — *your enemies become his*; the interior-context of *the obedient under God's own championing* *(inferred: shama/oyev/ayav = obedience that makes God enemy to your enemies)*.
- **avad (serve) — the *idols not served* (23:24).** "'you shall not **serve** (avad) their gods nor do as they do'" *(stated)* — avad, *serve/worship*: the interior of *worship refused to idols* *(inferred: avad = the idols not served)*.
- **avad (serve) + sur (remove) — the *LORD served, sickness removed* (23:25).** *avad distinct from 23:24:* "'you shall **serve** (avad) the LORD, and he will bless your bread… I will **remove** (sur) sickness'" *(stated)*. **avad** — now *serve the LORD*; **sur** — *turn aside/remove*: the gem is the *contrast* — *serve him (not idols)*, and *blessing and health* follow; the interior of *devotion directed rightly, met with life* *(inferred: avad/sur = the LORD served, sickness removed)*.
- **emah (terror) + oyev (enemies) — the *divine terror sent ahead* (23:27).** *oyev distinct from 23:4,22:* "'I will send my **terror** (emah) before you… and make all your **enemies** (oyev) turn their backs'" *(stated)*. **emah** — *dread/terror*; **oyev** — *enemy*: the gem is *God's terror as weapon* — the *dread* he sends *unnerves the enemy* before Israel arrives; the interior (of the enemy) *broken by the sent dread* *(inferred: emah/oyev = the divine terror sent ahead — the enemy's dread)*.
- **natan (give) — the *land's inhabitants given* (23:31).** "'I will **give** (natan) the inhabitants into your hand'" *(stated)* — natan, *give*: the interior-context of *the dispossession as God's gift* — *the victory his to grant* *(inferred: natan = the inhabitants given into your hand)*.
- **berit (covenant) + karat (cut) — the *no-covenant with idols* (23:32).** "'you shall **cut** (karat) no **covenant** (berit) with them or their gods'" *(stated)*. **berit** — *covenant*; **karat** — *cut* (the covenant-making verb): the interior of *no binding to what would corrupt* — *the covenant with God exclusive* *(inferred: berit/karat = no covenant cut with idols)*.
- **chata (sin) + avad (serve) — the *snare of idol-service* (23:33).** *avad distinct from 23:24,25:* "'lest they make you **sin** (chata) against me; if you **serve** (avad) their gods it will be a snare'" *(stated)*. **chata** — *sin*; **avad** — *serve*: the gem is *the snare named* — *dwelling with idolaters* would *draw the heart to serve their gods and sin*; the interior of *the corruptible will guarded by separation* *(inferred: chata/avad = the snare of idol-service — the will guarded by separation)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **justice from the inside (shema/rasha 23:1 → raah/riv 23:2 → hadar/riv 23:3 → oyev 23:4 → sane 23:5 → mishpat/riv 23:6 → tsadeq… 23:7 → salaph 23:8 → nephesh 23:9):** the tongue *refuses the lie*, the will *refuses the crowd*, judgment *bends neither to the poor's pity nor against him*, *good is done to the enemy* (his beast, his burden), the *innocent shielded and the guilty unexonerated* (mirroring God), the *bribe's corruption refused* — and the *ground of it all*: *"you know the heart of a sojourner"* — *empathy from remembered suffering*, justice *felt from the inside*.
2. **the mercy of rest (nuach/naphash 23:12 → shamar/zakhar 23:13 → shamar 23:15):** the sabbath *refreshes even the alien and the ox*; *exclusive devotion* (no other gods named); the *feast of the exodus kept* — mercy and memory joined.
3. **the angel and the guarded heart (shamar 23:20 → shama/marar/pesha 23:21 → shama/oyev/ayav 23:22 → avad 23:24 → avad/sur 23:25 → emah/oyev 23:27 → natan 23:31 → berit/karat 23:32 → chata/avad 23:33):** the *angel guards the way*, to be *obeyed not provoked*; obedience *aligns God against your enemies*; *idols not served*, the *LORD served* (met with blessing); God's *terror* sent ahead; the land *given*; *no covenant with idols* — lest they *snare the heart to sin*; the interior of *the guarded, obedient will*.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** shema/rasha (the *false report* refused); **riv ×4 (the *lawsuit* — crowd not followed / poor not favored / poor's justice not denied / — the balance both ways)**; hadar (the *favor* withheld even from the poor); **oyev ×3 (the *enemy's* beast recovered / God *enemy* to your enemies / the *enemies* turned back by terror)**; sane (the *hater's* burden shared); mishpat (the *justice* due the poor); tsadeq/sheqer/tsaddiq/naqi/rasha (the *innocent* shielded, the *wicked* not acquitted — God's standard); salaph/tsaddiq (the *bribe* that subverts); **nephesh (the *sojourner's soul* known from within — empathy from remembered suffering)**; nuach/naphash (the *rest* reaching the alien and beast); **shamar ×5 (the *attention* / the *feast* kept / the angel's *guard* / the *careful* heed / — the angel obeyed)**; zakhar (other gods not *named*); **shama ×2 (the angel *obeyed* / the *obedience* that aligns God)**; marar/pesha (the angel not *provoked*); **avad ×3 (idols not *served* / the LORD *served* / the *snare* of idol-service)**; sur (sickness *removed*); emah (the divine *terror* sent ahead); natan (the inhabitants *given*); berit/karat (no *covenant* with idols); chata (the *sin* the snare would bring).
- **Whose interior (multi-interior):** **Israel** — *impartial justice* (unswayed by crowd, pity, or bribe), *empathy from remembered suffering* ("you know the sojourner's soul"), *good to the enemy* (his beast, his burden), and the *guarded, obedient will* (against the idol-snare); **God** — the *angel who guards*, the *terror sent ahead*, the *patient dispossession* (little by little), *enemy to your enemies*; **the enemy** — the *dread* that breaks him. The gem: justice *grounded in empathy born of Israel's own affliction* — the ethical memory made *interior*.
- **Threads:** **the ethical memory made interior** (nephesh 23:9 ← "you were sojourners" 22:21; the *anah* of Egypt 1:11; God *knew* their nephesh 2:25 — now *you know* the sojourner's nephesh → "love the sojourner, for you were sojourners", Deut 10:19); **good to the enemy** (oyev/sane 23:4–5 → "if your enemy is hungry, feed him", Prov 25:21; "love your enemies", Mt 5:44 — the proto-form here); **impartial justice** (hadar/mishpat 23:3,6 → "you shall not be partial", Deut 1:17; Lev 19:15 — neither favor nor deny → the blind-justice ideal); **the bribe** (salaph 23:8 ← Jethro's judges who *hate* a bribe 18:21 — the corruption of sight); **the fear/terror motif** (emah 23:27 ← the trembling at Sinai 19:16; the dread on Canaan → Rahab "our hearts melted" Josh 2:11); **rebellion** (marar 23:21 ← the bitter Marah 15:23; the murmurings — the embittered will → the wilderness rebellions).
- **Guarded against:** lumping the four riv (three distinct lawsuit-clauses, each its own angle — favor / deny / crowd), five shamar (attention / feast / guard / careful / heed), three avad (idols / LORD / snare), three oyev; reading the justice-code as external rule over the *empathy* that grounds it (the sojourner's *nephesh* known from within — the finding); missing the *balance* between 23:3 (don't favor the poor) and 23:6 (don't deny him) — *even-handedness both ways*; mistaking the 'o "desire" and chayyah "beasts" for inner-being motifs; over-reading "love your enemy" back into 23:4–5 beyond the *active good to the enemy* the verses state (the proto-form, not the full teaching); flattening the angel-warning (marar 23:21) into ceremony over the *will called not to embitter/rebel*.

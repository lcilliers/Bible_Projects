## EXS-05-sinai-theophany · Exodus 19:1–25 · [the covenant offered; the holy dread at the mountain] — a kingdom of priests, and the trembling of people and mountain

**The passage.** The LORD **called** (qara) from the mountain (19:3); the **covenant** (berit) offer — "if you indeed **obey** (shama) my voice and **keep** (shamar) my covenant… a **holy** (qadosh) nation" (19:5–6); Moses **called** (qara) the elders (19:7); "that they may **believe** (aman) you forever" (19:9); **consecrate** (qadash) them (19:10); "**take care** (shamar) not to go up" (19:12); **consecrated** (qadash, 19:14); the people **trembled** (charad, 19:16); Moses brought them to **meet** (qara) God (19:17); the mountain **trembled** (charad, 19:18); the trumpet grew **louder** (chazeq, 19:19); the LORD **called** (qara) Moses up (19:20); **warn** (ud) them (19:21); the priests **consecrate** (qadash, 19:22); "you **warned** (ud) us… **consecrate** (qadash) it" (19:23).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **qara (called) — the *divine summons* from the mountain (19:3).** "the LORD **called** (qara) to him out of the mountain" *(stated)* — qara, *call*: the *summons that opens the covenant* — God *initiates*, calling Moses up; the interior-context of *the LORD's address to the house of Jacob* *(inferred: qara = the divine summons opening the covenant)*.
- **berit (covenant) + shama (obey) + shamar (keep) — the *covenant offered on obedience* (19:5).** "'if you indeed **obey** (shama) my voice and **keep** (shamar) my **covenant** (berit), you shall be my treasured possession'" *(stated)*. **berit** — *covenant*; **shama** — *hearken/obey*; **shamar** — *keep*: the gem is the *conditional offer* — the *treasured possession* status *hinges on obeying and keeping*; the interior of *a people offered a covenant identity, conditioned on the heart's obedience* — *"if you will indeed obey"* — the vocation *set before the will* *(inferred: berit/shama/shamar = the covenant offered on obedience — the vocation set before the will)*.
- **qadosh (holy) — the *holy nation vocation* (19:6).** "'you shall be to me a kingdom of priests and a **holy** (qadosh) nation'" *(stated)* — qadosh, *holy/set-apart*: the gem is the *identity offered* — *set apart* as *a nation of priests*; the interior-context of *a people called to holiness* — a *mediating vocation to the peoples* *(inferred: qadosh = the holy-nation vocation — set apart as priests)*.
- **qara (called) — the *mediator's relay* to the elders (19:7).** *qara distinct from 19:3:* "Moses came and **called** (qara) the elders and set before them all these words" *(stated)* — where 19:3 was *God calling Moses*, here Moses *calls the elders* — the *summons relayed down* — the interior-context of *the covenant-offer passed to the people* *(inferred: qara = the mediator's relay of the call)*.
- **aman (believe) — the *belief established* by the theophany (19:9).** "'I am coming in a thick cloud, that the people may hear when I speak with you, and may also **believe** (aman) you forever'" *(stated)* — aman, *trust/be-faithful* (the *faith*-word, ← 14:31 "they believed"; 15:6-arc): the gem is that *God stages the theophany to establish trust* — the people will *believe Moses* (and God through him) *forever* because they *hear God speak with him*; the interior of *belief grounded in the heard presence* — *trust anchored, not asked in a vacuum* *(inferred: aman = the belief established — trust grounded in the heard presence)*.
- **qadash (consecrate) — the *making-holy before approach* (19:10).** "'**consecrate** (qadash) them today and tomorrow, and let them wash'" *(stated)* — qadash, *consecrate/make-holy*: the gem is that *approach to the holy requires consecration* — the people must be *made holy* to *stand before the descending God*; the interior-context of *preparation for the presence — the set-apartness that approach demands* *(inferred: qadash = the making-holy before approach)*.
- **shamar (take care) — the *boundary guarded* (19:12).** *shamar distinct from 19:5:* "'**take care** (shamar) not to go up into the mountain… whoever touches it shall die'" *(stated)* — where 19:5 was *keep the covenant*, here shamar is *guard yourself* against the *lethal boundary* — the gem: *the holy is dangerous* — *watchfulness against overstep*; the interior of *the dread-bounded approach — the barrier that kills* *(inferred: shamar = the boundary guarded — watchfulness against the lethal holy)*.
- **qadash (consecrated) — the *consecration enacted* (19:14).** *qadash distinct from 19:10:* "Moses **consecrated** (qadash) the people; they washed" *(stated)* — where 19:10 *commanded*, 19:14 *enacts* — the *people made holy* — the interior-context of *the preparation carried out* *(inferred: qadash = the consecration enacted)*.
- **charad (trembled) — the *people's holy dread* (19:16).** "thunders and lightnings and a thick cloud… all the people **trembled** (charad)" *(stated)* — **charad**, *tremble/shudder* (tagged fear): the gem is *the raw fear-of-God* — the people *shudder* at the storm-theophany; the interior of *holy dread* — *terror before the descending presence* (the fear the whole covenant will name a keeper-from-sin, 20:20) *(inferred: charad = the people's holy dread — trembling before the presence)*.
- **qara (meet) — the *bringing-to-encounter* (19:17).** *qara distinct from 19:3,7 (encounter sense):* "Moses brought the people out to **meet** (qara) God, at the foot of the mountain" *(stated)* — here qara is *encounter/meeting*: the *mediator leads the trembling people to the meeting-place* — the interior-context of *the people brought (still trembling) to the encounter* *(inferred: qara = the bringing-to-encounter)*.
- **charad (trembled) — the *mountain's trembling* (19:18).** *charad distinct from 19:16:* "the LORD descended in fire… the whole mountain **trembled** (charad) greatly" *(stated)* — where 19:16 was *the people*, here *the mountain itself* shudders: the gem is that *the dread is shared by creation* — *not only the people but the mountain trembles* at the descent; the interior-lesson that *the presence shakes earth as well as heart* *(inferred: charad = the mountain's trembling — the dread shared by creation)*.
- **chazeq (louder) — the *mounting intensity* (19:19).** "the trumpet grew **louder** (chazeq) and louder; Moses spoke, God answered in thunder" *(stated)* — chazeq, *strong/loud*: the *intensity rising* — the interior-context of *the encounter's mounting weight* (the same root as Pharaoh's *hardening*, here the *strengthening* of the trumpet — the theophany *intensifying*) *(inferred: chazeq = the mounting intensity of the encounter)*.
- **qara (called) — the *call to the summit* (19:20).** *qara distinct from 19:3,7,17:* "the LORD **called** (qara) Moses to the top of the mountain, and Moses went up" *(stated)* — the *fourth qara*: God *calls Moses up into the presence* (where 19:3 called from the mountain, this calls *to the top*) — the interior-context of *the mediator summoned into the fire* *(inferred: qara = the call to the summit)*.
- **ud (warn) — the *warning against overstep* (19:21).** "'go down and **warn** (ud) the people, lest they break through to look and perish'" *(stated)* — ud, *testify/warn solemnly*: the gem is the *solemn warning* — the people's *curiosity to look* would be *lethal*; the interior of *the desire-to-see checked by the holy peril* *(inferred: ud = the warning against overstep — curiosity checked by peril)*.
- **qadash (consecrate) — the *priests' consecration* (19:22).** *qadash distinct from 19:10,14:* "'let the priests who come near **consecrate** (qadash) themselves, lest the LORD break out'" *(stated)* — even *the priests* (nearer) must be *made holy* — the interior-context of *nearness demanding greater consecration* *(inferred: qadash = the priests' consecration — nearness demands holiness)*.
- **ud (warned) + qadash (consecrate) — Moses *recalls the warning* (19:23).** *ud/qadash distinct:* "'you yourself **warned** (ud) us, "set limits and **consecrate** (qadash) it"'" *(stated)* — Moses *rehearses the boundary-command back* — the *warning* and the *making-holy of the mountain* recalled; the interior-context of *the mediator holding the boundary he was given* *(inferred: ud/qadash = the warning recalled, the mountain made holy)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **the covenant offered (qara 19:3 → berit/shama/shamar 19:5 → qadosh 19:6 → qara 19:7 → aman 19:9):** God *summons*, *offers a covenant conditioned on obedience*, names the *holy-nation vocation* — the offer *relayed to the people*, and the theophany staged so they *believe* — the interior of *a people offered a covenant identity, their belief grounded in the heard presence*.
2. **the consecration for approach (qadash 19:10 → shamar 19:12 → qadash 19:14):** *approach demands holiness* — the people *made holy*, the *lethal boundary guarded* — the interior of *the dread-bounded approach*.
3. **the holy dread (charad 19:16 → qara 19:17 → charad 19:18 → chazeq 19:19 → qara 19:20 → ud 19:21 → qadash 19:22 → ud/qadash 19:23):** the *people tremble*, are *brought to the encounter*, the *mountain trembles*, the *intensity mounts*, Moses is *called to the summit* — and the *warnings* against overstep, the *priests* consecrated — the interior of *holy dread shared by people and creation*, the *approach regulated by peril*.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** **qara ×4 (the *divine summons* / the *mediator's relay* / the *bringing-to-encounter* / the *call to the summit*)**; **berit/shama/shamar (the *covenant offered on obedience* — the vocation set before the will)**; **qadosh (the *holy-nation vocation*)**; **aman (the *belief established* — trust grounded in the heard presence)**; **qadash ×4 (the *making-holy* commanded / *enacted* / the *priests'* consecration / the mountain *made holy*)**; shamar (the *boundary guarded* — watchfulness against the lethal holy); **charad ×2 (the *people's* holy dread / the *mountain's* trembling — the dread shared by creation)**; chazeq (the *mounting intensity*); **ud ×2 (the *warning* against overstep / the *warning recalled*)**.
- **Whose interior (multi-interior):** **Israel** — *belief* (aman, grounded in the heard presence), *holy dread* (charad, trembling), and *consecration* (made holy to approach); **Moses** — the *mediator up-and-down*, *summoned into the fire*, holding the *boundary*; **God** — the *covenant-offerer descending in fire*, the *holy presence that must be bounded*. The *covenant offer* and the *holy dread* are the two gems.
- **Threads:** **the fear-of-God** (charad 19:16,18 — the *trembling* that the covenant will name a *keeper-from-sin*, 20:20; ← the God-fearing midwives 1:17 → "the fear of the LORD is the beginning of wisdom"); **belief/faith** (aman 19:9 ← 14:31 "they believed"; 15:6-arc; 17:12 emunah → the faith-thread — here *established by the theophany*); **the holy** (qadash/qadosh 19:6,10,14,22,23 → the tabernacle's holiness, Lev; the set-apart people); **covenant** (berit 19:5 ← the Abrahamic covenant Gen 15,17 → the Sinai covenant, the whole Torah; the *conditional* offer distinct from the Abrahamic *promise*); **the dangerous holy** (shamar/ud 19:12,21 → Uzzah, 2 Sam 6; the ark's peril — nearness kills the unconsecrated); the *arrogance*-hardening contrast (chazeq 19:19 — the trumpet *strengthened*, same root as Pharaoh's *hardened* heart, here God's *good* intensity vs the *self-hardening*).
- **Guarded against:** lumping the four qara (summons / relay / encounter / summit), the four qadash (commanded / enacted / priests / mountain), the two charad (people / mountain — the *creation shares the dread*, the finding), the two shamar (keep-covenant / guard-boundary), the two ud (warn / recall); reading the covenant-offer as unconditional over the *"if you will indeed obey"* the text states (the vocation *set before the will*); flattening the trembling into mere spectacle over the *holy dread* it is (the raw fear-of-God); importing NT "kingdom of priests" (1 Pet 2:9) over the *holy-nation vocation* the verse offers; over-reading chazeq into the hardening-motif when here it is the *trumpet's* strengthening (the intensity, not a heart); missing that the belief (aman) is *grounded* in the heard presence (God stages it) — trust *given a foundation*, not demanded blind.

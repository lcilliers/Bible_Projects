## EXS-03-water-amalek · Exodus 17:1–16 · [doubt that tests God's presence; victory by the upheld hands] — "is the LORD among us?", and the steady hands

**The passage.** No water; the people **quarreled** (riv): "**give** (natan) us water… why do you test the LORD?" (17:2); water from the rock; named Massah and Meribah for the **quarreling** (riv), "is the LORD among us or not?" (17:7); Amalek fought; "**Choose** (bachar) men" (17:9); when Moses' hand was **up** (rum) Israel **prevailed** (gavar), **lowered** (nuach) Amalek prevailed (17:11); his hands were **steady** (emunah) till sunset (17:12); the LORD My Banner (17:15).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **riv (quarreled) + natan (give) — the *demand that tests* (17:2).** "the people **quarreled** (riv): '**give** (natan) us water'… 'why do you **test** the LORD?'" *(stated)*. **riv** — *contend/bring a suit*; **natan** — the *demand*: the gem is that the *quarreling is a testing of God* — Moses names it: *"why do you test the LORD?"* The interior's *complaint escalated to a legal contention* against God's leadership *(inferred: riv/natan = the quarreling demand that tests the LORD)*.
- **riv (quarreling) — the *doubt named* Meribah (17:7).** *riv distinct from 17:2:* "he named it Massah and Meribah for the **quarreling** (riv)… 'is the LORD among us or not?'" *(stated)* — the gem: **riv** now *fixed in a name* (Meribah = quarreling; Massah = testing) — and the *doubt at its heart*: *"is the LORD among us **or not**?"* After the sea, the manna, the water — the interior *still doubts God's presence*; the failure of trust *escalating to a question of whether God is even there* *(inferred: riv = the doubt named Meribah — "is the LORD among us?", the trust-failure at its core)*.
- **bachar (choose) — the *muster* for battle (17:9).** "'**Choose** (bachar) men and fight Amalek; I will stand on the hill with the staff'" *(stated)* — bachar, *choose*: the *fighters mustered* — the interior-context of *the human effort under the upheld staff* *(inferred: bachar = the men chosen for the battle)*.
- **rum (up) + nuach (lowered) + gavar (prevailed) — the *victory by the upheld hand* (17:11).** "when Moses' hand was **up** (rum) Israel **prevailed** (gavar); when **lowered** (nuach) Amalek prevailed" *(stated)*. **rum** — *raised*; **nuach** — *lowered/at rest*; **gavar** — *prevail*: the gem is that *the victory tracks the raised hand, not the sword* — Israel *prevails* only while the hand is *up* (the staff of God raised — dependence/intercession) — the interior-lesson that *the battle is won by dependence on God*, not human strength alone *(inferred: rum/nuach/gavar = the victory tracking the upheld hand — dependence, not the sword)*.
- **emunah (steady) — the *steadfast hands held up by others* (17:12).** "his hands grew weary… Aaron and Hur held them up… his hands were **steady** (emunah) till sunset" *(stated)* — the gem: **emunah** — *steadiness / **faithfulness*** (the *faith*-word, ← Abraham *believed* 15:6; "the righteous shall live by his emunah", Hab 2:4): Moses' hands *"were emunah"* — *steady/faithful* — but *only because held up by Aaron and Hur*. The interior's *sustained dependence* — *faithfulness held up in community*; the mediator's steadiness *not self-generated* but *upheld by others* *(inferred: emunah = the steady/faithful hands — sustained dependence, held up in community)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **the doubt that tests God (riv/natan 17:2 → riv 17:7):** the people *quarrel and demand*, *testing* God — and the doubt *fixed in a name*: *"is the LORD among us or not?"*; the trust-failure *escalating* (from grumbling to a question of God's very presence) — *while God provides water from the rock* (present *despite* the doubt).
2. **victory by the upheld hand (bachar 17:9 → rum/nuach/gavar 17:11 → emunah 17:12):** the *fighters mustered*, but the *victory tracks the raised hand* (dependence on God, not the sword); and the mediator's hands are *steady/faithful* — *held up by Aaron and Hur* — the interior's *sustained dependence, upheld in community*.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** **riv ×2 (the *quarreling demand that tests* God / the *doubt named* Meribah — "is the LORD among us?", the trust-failure at its core)**; natan (the *demand* for water); bachar (the *muster*); **rum/nuach/gavar (the *victory tracking the upheld hand* — dependence, not the sword)**; **emunah (the *steady/faithful hands* — sustained dependence, held up in community — the faith-word)**.
- **Whose interior (multi-interior):** **Israel** — *doubt that tests God's presence* ("is the LORD among us?") — the trust-failure *escalating* past the sea and the manna; **Moses** — the *mediator's steady/faithful hands*, *upheld by others*; **God** — *present* (water from the rock *despite* the doubt) and the *warrior* (the LORD My Banner). The contrast: Israel *doubts* while God *proves* his presence.
- **Threads:** **the testing arc escalates** (riv 17:2 ← the grumbling 15:24; 16:2 — now *quarreling* and *testing* whether God is even present, Massah/Meribah → the wilderness rebellions, Num; Ps 95:8 "do not harden as at Meribah"); **God present despite doubt** (water from the rock — the LORD *among them* answering "is the LORD among us?"); **emunah/faith** (17:12 ← aman "believed" 15:6; 14:31 → the faith-word; Hab 2:4 — here the *steadiness* of dependence, *upheld by others*); **dependence over strength** (rum/gavar 17:11 — the battle won by the raised hand, not the sword); the *near-stoning* (17:4 — the mediator's peril, ← 5:21; → the wilderness murmurings).
- **Guarded against:** lumping the two riv (the quarreling-demand / the doubt-named); reading the upheld-hands into magic over the *dependence-on-God* the sign teaches (the raised staff = reliance, not a talisman); over-reading emunah into a full doctrine of faith over the *steadiness/faithfulness held up in community* the verse gives (though the faith-resonance is real); missing that the doubt "is the LORD among us?" comes *after* the sea and manna (the trust-failure's depth); importing NT "Christ the rock" (1 Cor 10:4) over the *water-from-the-rock* the passage narrates; treating Amalek's defeat as merely military when the text ties it to the *upheld hands* (dependence).

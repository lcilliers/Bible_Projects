## EXO-08-bricks-without-straw · Exodus 5:1–21 · [the adversary's defiant not-knowing; Israel's faith crashing into blame] — "who is the LORD?"; the heavier burden; the deliverer reproached

**The passage.** "Who is the LORD, that I should **obey** (shama)? I do not **know** (yada) the LORD" (5:2); "the God of the Hebrews has **met** (qara) with us" (5:3); "get back to your burdens" (5:4); the **taskmasters** (nagas, 5:6); "no longer **give** (natan) them straw" (5:7); "they are **idle** (raphah)" (5:8); "**heavier** (kaved) work… pay no regard to **lying** (sheqer) words… **service** (avodah)" (5:9); "I will not **give** (natan) you straw" (5:10); "your **work** (avodah) will not be reduced" (5:11); the taskmasters (nagas) **urgent** (5:13); the foremen beaten (nagas, 5:14); "the **fault** (chata) is your own people's… no straw **given** (natan)" (5:16); "you are **idle** (raphah), idle" (5:17); "**work** (avad)… no straw **given** (natan)" (5:18); the foremen **met** (qara) Moses (5:20): "you have made us **stink** (baash)… the LORD **judge** (shaphat) you" (5:21).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **shama (obey) + yada (know) — the adversary's *defiant not-knowing* (5:2).** "who is the LORD that I should **obey** (shama)? I do not **know** (yada) the LORD, and I will not let Israel go" *(stated)*. **shama** — *hear/obey*: Pharaoh *refuses to heed* (the very heeding Israel is promised, 3:18); **yada** — the gem: the adversary's interior *defined by not-knowing God* — *"I do not know the LORD"* (echoing the king who *did not know* Joseph, 1:8). This *ignorance is defiant*, not innocent — and it *sets the whole plague-purpose*: the wonders come *"that you may know that I am the LORD"* (7:5,17; 8:10; 9:14). The interior that *will not know* must be *made to know* *(inferred: shama/yada = the defiant refusal to obey or know God — the adversary's founding posture)*.
- **qara (met) — the *claimed encounter* (5:3).** "the God of the Hebrews has **met** (qara) with us" *(stated)* — qara, *encounter/meet*: Moses and Aaron *ground the request in an encounter* with God; the interior *appeals to revelation* against the adversary's not-knowing *(inferred: qara = the divine encounter claimed as warrant)*.
- **nagas (taskmasters) — the *machinery of oppression* commanded (5:6).** "Pharaoh commanded the **taskmasters** (nagas)" *(stated)* — nagas, *drive/oppress/exact*: the gem is the *instrument* — the *drivers* set in motion; the adversary's cruelty *has a machinery*, and it will *recur* (5:10,13,14) as relentless pressure *(inferred: nagas = the oppression-machinery set to crush)*.
- **natan (give straw) — the *provision withdrawn* (5:7).** "no longer **give** (natan) them straw" *(stated)* — natan, *give*: the gem is the *cruelty of subtraction* — the same daily quota *demanded* but the *means removed*; the interior of oppression *increasing the impossible* *(inferred: natan = the straw withheld while the quota stands)*.
- **raphah (idle) — the worship-desire *read as laziness* (5:8).** "you shall not reduce the quota, for they are **idle** (raphah); therefore they cry, 'let us sacrifice'" *(stated)* — raphah, *slacken/be idle*: the gem is the *contemptuous misreading* — Pharaoh reads Israel's *longing to worship* as *idleness*; the adversary's interior *cannot see worship as anything but slack* *(inferred: raphah = the worship-desire dismissed as idleness)*.
- **kaved (heavier) + sheqer (lying) + avodah (work) — the *heaviness laid on Israel*, truth called a lie (5:9).** "let **heavier** (kaved) **work** (avodah) be laid on them… pay no regard to **lying** (sheqer) words" *(stated)*. The gem: **kaved** — *make heavy* — Pharaoh *makes the work heavy on Israel* — the ***same root*** that will name his *own heart made heavy* (8:15,32; 9:34; 10:1: "Pharaoh made his heart *kaved*"). The heaviness he *imposes* will *return on his own heart* — a wordplay-become-judgment. **sheqer** — the adversary *names God's word (through Moses) a lie*; the interior *calling truth deception* *(inferred: kaved/sheqer = the heaviness laid on Israel, and truth branded a lie — the heaviness that will recoil on his own heart)*.
- **nagas (taskmasters) + natan (give) — the decree *relayed* (5:10).** *nagas/natan distinct from 5:6,7:* "the **taskmasters** (nagas) said: 'thus says Pharaoh, I will not **give** (natan) you straw'" *(stated)* — the machinery *relays the cruelty* down; natan *now in the first person of refusal* ("I will not give") — the interior of oppression *voiced through its agents* *(inferred: nagas/natan = the decree relayed, the refusal voiced)*.
- **avodah (work) — the *unreduced* quota (5:11).** *avodah distinct from 5:9:* "your **work** (avodah) will not be reduced in the least" *(stated)* — the *impossible standard held* — the interior *demanding the same from less* *(inferred: avodah = the quota held immovable)*.
- **nagas (urgent) — the drivers *pressing* (5:13).** *nagas distinct:* "the **taskmasters** (nagas) were urgent: 'complete your daily task'" *(stated)* — the machinery *intensifies* — *urgency* added to impossibility *(inferred: nagas = the drivers pressing the impossible pace)*.
- **nagas (beaten) — the cruelty *reaching the foremen* (5:14).** *nagas distinct:* "the foremen… whom Pharaoh's **taskmasters** (nagas) had set over them, were beaten" *(stated)* — the oppression *turns on Israel's own officials* — the machinery *striking within* the people (as the deliverer's coming *worsens* their lot) *(inferred: nagas = the oppression beating Israel's own foremen)*.
- **chata (fault) + natan (given) — the foremen's *protest* (5:16).** "no straw is **given** (natan)… the **fault** (chata) is your own people's" *(stated)*. **chata** — *sin/fault*: the foremen *protest the injustice* — the wrong lies with Pharaoh's system, not them; **natan** *distinct* — the *no-straw* named again as the injustice's core *(inferred: chata/natan = the foremen's protest of an unjust fault laid on them)*.
- **raphah (idle) — the *doubled dismissal* (5:17).** *raphah distinct from 5:8:* "you are **idle** (raphah), idle; that is why you say 'let us sacrifice'" *(stated)* — the *doubled* contempt — Pharaoh *repeats the charge* to their faces; the adversary's interior *hardened in its misreading* of worship as sloth *(inferred: raphah = the doubled dismissal of worship as idleness)*.
- **avad (work) + natan (given) — the sentence *sealed* (5:18).** "go **work** (avad); no straw **given** (natan), but deliver the same bricks" *(stated)* — avad, *serve/work*; natan *distinct* — the *final* form of the cruelty: *serve without means*; the interior of oppression *closing every door* *(inferred: avad/natan = the impossible service sealed)*.
- **qara (met) — the foremen *confront the deliverers* (5:20).** *qara distinct from 5:3:* "they **met** (qara) Moses and Aaron waiting for them" *(stated)* — where 5:3 was *God meeting* the deliverers, 5:20 is the *foremen meeting* them *in bitterness* — the encounter *turned accusatory* *(inferred: qara = the bitter meeting, the deliverers confronted by their own)*.
- **baash (stink) + shaphat (judge) — Israel's *faith crashed into blame* (5:21).** "you have made us **stink** (baash) before Pharaoh… the LORD **judge** (shaphat) you" *(stated)*. **baash** — *stink/become odious* (← the Nile will *stink*, 7:18; Jacob feared being *made to stink*, Gen 34:30): the gem is *Israel turning on the deliverer* — the people who *believed and worshiped* (4:31) now *reproach Moses* for making their bondage *worse*; **shaphat** — they *invoke judgment on Moses*. Israel's interior **cannot hold its new faith** when deliverance *first increases the suffering* — the "broken spirit" (6:9) surfacing as *blame* *(inferred: baash/shaphat = Israel's faith collapsed into reproach of the deliverer)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **the adversary's defiant not-knowing (shama/yada 5:2 → qara 5:3 → 5:4–5):** Pharaoh *refuses to obey or know* the LORD — the founding posture the plagues will *overturn* ("that you may know"); the request grounded in an *encounter* is met with *contempt* ("get back to your burdens").
2. **the machinery of cruelty (nagas 5:6 → natan 5:7 → raphah 5:8 → kaved/sheqer/avodah 5:9 → nagas/natan 5:10 → avodah 5:11 → nagas 5:13 → nagas 5:14):** the *drivers* are loosed, the *straw withdrawn*, the *quota held*, the worship-desire *dismissed as idleness*, God's word *branded a lie*, and the *heaviness laid on Israel* — the very *kaved* that will *recoil on Pharaoh's own heart*.
3. **protest and doubled contempt (chata/natan 5:16 → raphah 5:17 → avad/natan 5:18):** the foremen *protest the unjust fault*; Pharaoh *doubles the "idle" charge* and *seals the impossible service* — the adversary *hardened in his misreading*.
4. **the deliverer reproached (qara 5:20 → baash/shaphat 5:21):** the foremen *turn on Moses* — *"you made us stink… the LORD judge you"*; Israel's *faith crashes into blame* when deliverance *worsens* things first — the interior unable to bear the *deepening before the deliverance*.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** **shama/yada (the adversary's *defiant refusal to obey or know* God — the posture the plagues overturn, "that you may know")**; qara ×2 (5:3 *God's* encounter claimed / 5:20 the foremen's *bitter* meeting); nagas ×4 (the oppression-machinery: *commanded* / *relaying* / *urgent* / *beating* the foremen); natan ×4 (the straw *withheld* at each stage — withdrawn / "I will not give" / "none given" / "none will be given"); raphah ×2 (worship *dismissed as idleness* / the *doubled* dismissal); **kaved (the *heaviness laid on Israel* — the same root that will make Pharaoh's own heart heavy; the imposed weight recoiling)**; sheqer (truth *branded a lie*); avodah ×2 (the *heavier* work / the *unreduced* quota); chata (the foremen's *protest*); **baash/shaphat (Israel's *faith crashed into blame* of the deliverer)**.
- **Whose interior (multi-interior):** the **adversary** (Pharaoh) — *defiant not-knowing*, *contempt* (worship = idleness), *escalating cruelty* (the machinery, the impossible quota), *truth called a lie*; **Israel** (the foremen/people) — the *crash* from faith-and-worship (4:31) to *reproach* when the burden *increases* first; the *broken spirit* (6:9) surfacing as blame. Two interiors: the adversary *hardening in defiance*, Israel *faltering under the deepening*.
- **Threads:** the **hardening trajectory's soil** — Pharaoh's *"I do not know the LORD"* (yada 5:2) is the *defiance* that the self-hardening (7:13ff) and God-hardening (9:12ff) will *fix and seal* (see the hardening-ledger); **"that you may know"** (the plague-purpose answering 5:2's not-knowing); the **kaved wordplay** (heavy work 5:9 → heavy heart 8:15,32; 9:34; 10:1 — what he imposes recoils); **Israel's faith arc** (belief/worship 4:31 → *crash/blame* here → murmuring at the sea 14:11 → "believed the LORD" 14:31); **baash** (5:21 ← Gen 34:30 → the Nile 7:18); the *deliverance that worsens before it saves* (→ Moses' own crisis, 5:22–23, EXO-09).
- **Guarded against:** lumping the four nagas / four natan / two raphah (each an escalating beat of the cruelty, or a distinct withholding); reading Pharaoh's "I do not know" (5:2) as mere ignorance when it is *defiant* (the plagues answer it as *culpable*); missing the **kaved** wordplay (the heaviness he *lays on* Israel is the heaviness his *own heart* becomes); reading Israel's reproach (5:21) as mere faithlessness when the text shows an interior *crushed by a deliverance that first deepens the bondage* (the crash is *understandable*, and is the finding); importing a NT reading over the plain *first-confrontation-makes-it-worse* dynamic.

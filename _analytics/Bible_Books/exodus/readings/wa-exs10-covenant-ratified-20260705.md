## EXS-10-covenant-ratified · Exodus 24:1–18 · [the covenant sworn and sealed; the vision of God; Moses into the glory] — "we will do and be obedient", the blood, and the pavement of clearness

**The passage.** Moses told the people the **rules** (mishpat, 24:3); read the Book of the **Covenant** (berit) — "all the LORD has spoken we will do and be **obedient** (shama)" (24:7, the **reading** qara); "the blood of the **covenant** (berit) the LORD has **cut** (karat)" (24:8); the elders saw God, under his feet sapphire like heaven for **clearness** (tohar ×2, 24:10); they beheld God, ate and drank (24:11); "I will **give** (natan) you the tablets" (24:12); the **glory** (kavod) dwelt, the cloud six days, the seventh he **called** (qara, 24:16); the **glory** (kavod) like devouring fire (24:17); Moses into the cloud forty days (24:18).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **mishpat (rules) — the *rules laid before the people* (24:3).** "Moses told the people all the words and all the **rules** (mishpat); they answered, 'all we will do'" *(stated)* — mishpat, *ordinances* (← the covenant code 21:1): the *statutes set before them for assent* — the interior-context of *the people hearing the whole covenant before answering* *(inferred: mishpat = the rules laid before the people for assent)*.
- **berit (covenant) + qara (read) + shama (obedient) — the *wholehearted assent* (24:7).** "he **read** (qara) the Book of the **Covenant** (berit) in their hearing; they said, 'all the LORD has spoken we will do and be **obedient** (shama)'" *(stated)*. **berit** — *covenant*; **qara** — *read out/proclaim*; **shama** — *hear/obey*: the gem is *the covenant assent at its fullest* — *"we will do AND be obedient"* — the people bind themselves *before* fully knowing, *doing* set even beside *hearing*; the interior of *wholehearted covenant commitment* — *a people saying yes with one voice* *(inferred: berit/qara/shama = the wholehearted assent — "we will do and be obedient")*.
- **berit (covenant) + karat (cut) — the *covenant sealed in blood* (24:8).** *berit distinct from 24:7:* "Moses threw the blood on the people: 'behold the blood of the **covenant** (berit) the LORD has **cut** (karat) with you'" *(stated)*. **berit** — now the *covenant enacted* (where 24:7 was the *words assented*, 24:8 is the *blood that seals*); **karat** — *cut* (the covenant-making verb): the gem is *the binding made blood-deep* — the covenant *not merely agreed but sealed*, the blood *thrown on the people* joining them to God's words; the interior-context of *commitment bound in blood — the covenant made irrevocable* *(inferred: berit/karat = the covenant sealed in blood — the binding made irrevocable)*.
- **tohar (clearness) + tohar (clearness) — the *vision of God in purity* (24:10).** "they saw the God of Israel; under his feet a pavement of sapphire, like heaven for **clearness** (tohar ×2)" *(stated)* — **tohar**, *purity/clearness* (both spans): the gem is *the vision granted* — the elders *see God* (the unthinkable), and what they see beneath him is *clarity, purity, brightness like heaven*; the interior of *awe before the pure vision* — *the dread of Sinai now opened into sight*; and (24:11) *"he did not lay his hand on them"* — *they see God and live, and eat and drink* — the *dread turned to communion*; the interior *permitted to behold and not be consumed* *(inferred: tohar = the vision of God in purity — the dread turned to communion, seeing God and living)*.
- **natan (give) — the *tablets to be given* (24:12).** "'come up… that I may **give** (natan) you the tablets, the law and commandment I have written'" *(stated)* — natan, *give*: the *covenant words to be given in stone* — the interior-context of *the law about to be handed down, written by God* *(inferred: natan = the tablets to be given)*.
- **kavod (glory) + qara (called) — the *glory dwelling, Moses called* (24:16).** *qara distinct from 24:7:* "the **glory** (kavod) of the LORD dwelt on Sinai; the cloud six days; the seventh he **called** (qara) Moses out of the midst" *(stated)*. **kavod** — *glory*; **qara** — now *summon* (where 24:7 was *reading aloud*, here God *calls* Moses): the gem is *the glory settling and the mediator summoned* — the *kavod dwells*, and after six days' waiting *Moses is called into it*; the interior-context of *the glory-presence and the call into it* *(inferred: kavod/qara = the glory dwelling, Moses called into it)*.
- **kavod (glory) — the *glory as devouring fire* (24:17).** *kavod distinct from 24:16:* "the appearance of the **glory** (kavod) was like a devouring fire on the top, in the sight of Israel" *(stated)* — where 24:16 was the *glory dwelling*, 24:17 names its *appearance* — *devouring fire*: the gem is *the terrible beauty of the glory* — *seen by all Israel* as *consuming fire*; the interior-context of *the glory that is both dwelling-presence and devouring fire* — *the awe held with the nearness* *(inferred: kavod = the glory as devouring fire — the terrible presence seen by all)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **the covenant sworn and sealed (mishpat 24:3 → berit/qara/shama 24:7 → berit/karat 24:8):** the *rules laid before them*, the *wholehearted assent* ("we will do and be obedient"), and the *covenant sealed in blood* thrown on the people — the interior of *a people binding themselves to God, the commitment made blood-deep*.
2. **the vision and the communion (tohar 24:10 → [ate and drank 24:11]):** the elders *see God* in *clarity and purity*, and — *not struck down* — *eat and drink* in his presence; the *dread of Sinai opened into communion*; the interior *permitted to behold and live*.
3. **into the glory (natan 24:12 → kavod/qara 24:16 → kavod 24:17 → [forty days 24:18]):** the *tablets to be given*, the *glory dwelling* and *Moses called into it*, the glory *like devouring fire* seen by all — the mediator *entering the cloud* for forty days; the interior of *the mediator drawn into the terrible glory*.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** mishpat (the *rules* laid before the people); **berit ×2 (the covenant *assented* / the covenant *sealed in blood*)**; **qara ×2 (the covenant *read out* / Moses *called* into the glory)**; **shama (the *wholehearted assent* — "we will do and be obedient")**; **karat (the covenant *cut* — sealed irrevocable)**; **tohar ×2 (the vision of God in *clearness/purity* — the dread turned to communion)**; natan (the *tablets* to be given); **kavod ×2 (the glory *dwelling* / the glory as *devouring fire*)**.
- **Whose interior (multi-interior):** **Israel** — the *wholehearted covenant assent* (one voice, "we will do and be obedient"), and the elders' *vision-and-communion* (they see God, eat and drink, live); **Moses** — *called into the glory* (the mediator drawn into the devouring fire, forty days); **God** — the *glory* (dwelling-presence and devouring fire), yet *does not lay his hand* on the elders (the vision *permitted*, mercy in the beholding). The gems: the *covenant assent*, the *blood-sealed binding*, and the *dread-turned-to-communion* in the vision.
- **Threads:** **the covenant assent** (shama 24:7 ← "all we will do" 19:8; the offer 19:5 → the golden-calf breaking of this very vow, 32 — the *"we will do"* soon broken; the tragedy foreshadowed); **the blood of the covenant** (berit/karat 24:8 ← the Abrahamic covenant-cutting Gen 15:10,17; the Passover blood 12 → "this is my blood of the covenant", Mt 26:28 — the blood-sealed binding); **the vision of God** (tohar 24:10 ← "no one may see me and live" 33:20 — yet here the elders *see and live*, the exception of grace; → the beatific vision); **the glory** (kavod 24:16–17 ← the glory answering the grumbling 16:7,10; the glory to fill the tabernacle 40:34 — the *dwelling* glory → "we beheld his glory", Jn 1:14); **the dread turned to communion** (24:11 "he did not lay his hand… they ate and drank" — the fear of Sinai 19:16 now *opened into fellowship*, the covenant meal).
- **Guarded against:** lumping the two berit (assented / sealed), two qara (read / called), two kavod (dwelling / fire), the two tohar spans; reading the covenant assent as mere formality over the *wholehearted "we will do and be obedient"* the text gives (and its tragic weight — soon broken); flattening the vision (24:10–11) into symbol over the *seeing-God-and-living* the verses state (the *dread turned to communion* — the finding); importing NT eucharistic theology onto "the blood of the covenant" over the *blood-sealed binding* the verse enacts (though the resonance is real); over-reading the sapphire-pavement into cosmology over the *clearness/purity* seen; missing that the glory is *both* dwelling-presence *and* devouring fire (the awe held with the nearness — not resolved to one).

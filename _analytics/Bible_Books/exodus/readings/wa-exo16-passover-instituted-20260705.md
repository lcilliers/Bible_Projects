## EXO-16-passover-instituted · Exodus 12:1–28 · [the rite that forms the interior; the service transferred] — the unblemished lamb, the sheltering blood, the remembered bitterness, and the memory taught

**The passage.** "According to the number of **persons** (nephesh) you shall count for the lamb" (12:4); "the lamb shall be **without blemish** (tamim)" (12:5); "eat the **flesh** (basar)… with unleavened bread and **bitter herbs** (maror)" (12:8); "on all the gods of Egypt I will execute **judgments** (shephet)" (12:12); "the blood shall be a sign… no plague will **destroy** (mashchit) you" (12:13); "that **person** (nephesh) shall be cut off" (12:15); "a **holy** (qodesh) **assembly** (miqra)… what every **person** (nephesh) needs to eat" (12:16); "you shall **observe** (shamar) this feast" (12:17); "that **person** (nephesh) will be cut off" (12:19); Moses **called** (qara) the elders (12:21); "the LORD will not allow the **destroyer** (shachat) to enter" (12:23); "**observe** (shamar) this rite" (12:24); "when you come to the land the LORD will **give** (natan)… **keep** (shamar) this **service** (avodah)" (12:25); "when your children ask, 'what is this **service** (avodah)?'" (12:26); the people bowed and worshiped (12:27).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **nephesh (persons) — the *counted community* (12:4).** "according to the number of **persons** (nephesh) you shall count for the lamb" *(stated)* — nephesh in the *census/person* sense: the gem is *belonging* — each *person* is *counted into* a household's lamb; the interior-context is *inclusion* — no one left uncovered, the community *reckoned by persons* under the blood *(inferred: nephesh = the persons counted in — the community's inclusion)*.
- **tamim (without blemish) — the *unblemished* offering (12:5).** "your lamb shall be **without blemish** (tamim)" *(stated)* — tamim, *whole/unblemished/blameless* (the root of Noah's "blameless" 6:9; Abraham's demand 17:1; Jacob "tam" 25:27): the gem is that the *sheltering life* must be *whole* — the interior-context is *the perfect substitute*; wholeness as the condition of the covering *(inferred: tamim = the unblemished lamb — wholeness as the shelter's condition)*.
- **basar (flesh) + maror (bitter herbs) — the *remembered bitterness* in the meal (12:8).** "eat the **flesh** (basar)… with unleavened bread and **bitter herbs** (maror)" *(stated)*. **basar** — the *flesh* eaten; **maror** — the gem: **bitterness** (← marar, 1:14 "made their lives **bitter**") — the *bondage's bitterness* is *built into the feast of freedom*; Israel *eats the bitterness even as it is delivered* — the interior *remembering the affliction in the very meal that marks its end* *(inferred: basar/maror = the remembered bitterness of bondage eaten into the feast)*.
- **shephet (judgments) — the *judgment on Egypt's gods* (12:12).** "on all the gods of Egypt I will execute **judgments** (shephet)" *(stated)* — shephet, *judgment* (← 6:6; 7:4): the gem is the *spiritual dimension* — the firstborn-plague is *judgment on the gods* Egypt trusts; the divine interior *overthrowing the false powers*, not merely the people *(inferred: shephet = the judgment on Egypt's gods — the spiritual victory)*.
- **mashchit (destroy) — the *destruction the blood averts* (12:13).** "when I see the blood, I will pass over… no plague will **destroy** (mashchit) you" *(stated)* — mashchit, *destruction* (← shachat, Gen 6:11): the gem is the *blood-sign* — the *destruction* that falls on Egypt is *turned aside* where the blood is *seen* (as the covenant-blood turned aside God's severity, 4:26); the interior *sheltered by a sign it did not earn* *(inferred: mashchit = the destruction the sheltering blood averts)*.
- **nephesh (person) — the *cutting off* for leaven (12:15).** *nephesh distinct from 12:4:* "that **person** (nephesh) shall be cut off from Israel" *(stated)* — where 12:4 was *inclusion* (counted in), 12:15 is *exclusion* — the *person* who eats leaven *cut off*; the interior-context is *the community's boundary* — belonging carries an *obligation* whose breach *severs* *(inferred: nephesh = the person cut off — the community's boundary)*.
- **nephesh (everyone) + miqra (assembly) + qodesh (holy) — the *holy assembly* (12:16).** *nephesh distinct:* "a **holy** (qodesh) **assembly** (miqra)… what every **person** (nephesh) needs to eat" *(stated)*. **qodesh** — *holiness*; **miqra** — *assembly/convocation*; **nephesh** — here *each person's* need. The gem: the community *gathered as holy* — the interior *set apart for God* in assembly, its ordinary *eating* provided for even in the holy day *(inferred: qodesh/miqra/nephesh = the holy assembly — the community set apart)*.
- **shamar (observe) — the *feast kept forever* (12:17).** "you shall **observe** (shamar) the Feast of Unleavened Bread… a statute forever" *(stated)* — shamar, *keep/guard/observe*: the gem is *memory made permanent* — the deliverance *kept* as a perpetual observance; the interior *bound to remember* by rite *(inferred: shamar = the feast kept as perpetual memory)*.
- **nephesh (person) — the *cutting off*, native or sojourner (12:19).** *nephesh distinct from 12:15:* "that **person** (nephesh) will be cut off… whether sojourner or native" *(stated)* — the boundary *applies to all* (sojourner and native alike) — the interior-context is *the community's boundary is not ethnic but covenantal* *(inferred: nephesh = the person cut off, native or sojourner — the covenantal, not ethnic, boundary)*.
- **qara (called) — the *elders summoned* to the rite (12:21).** "Moses **called** (qara) the elders: 'select lambs… kill the Passover'" *(stated)* — qara, *call-to*: the *rite transmitted* through the elders to the people — the interior of *obedient transmission* *(inferred: qara = the elders summoned to enact the rite)*.
- **shachat (destroyer) — the *destroyer barred* by the blood (12:23).** *shachat distinct from mashchit 12:13:* "the LORD will not allow the **destroyer** (shachat) to enter your houses" *(stated)* — shachat, *ruin/destroy* — here the gem is *personal*: the *destroyer* (the agent) *barred at the blood-marked door*; where 12:13 was the *destruction* (the event), 12:23 is the *destroyer* (the agent) *held out* — the blood a *barrier* the interior shelters behind *(inferred: shachat = the destroyer barred at the blood-marked door)*.
- **shamar (observe) — the *rite kept for the sons* (12:24).** *shamar distinct from 12:17:* "you shall **observe** (shamar) this rite… for you and your sons forever" *(stated)* — where 12:17 kept the *feast*, 12:24 keeps the *rite for the sons* — the *generational* transmission; the interior *handing the memory down* *(inferred: shamar = the rite kept for the generations)*.
- **shamar (keep) + avodah (service) + natan (give) — the *service kept in the land* (12:25).** *shamar distinct from 12:17,24:* "when you come to the land the LORD will **give** (natan)… **keep** (shamar) this **service** (avodah)" *(stated)*. **natan** — the *land given*; **shamar** — the rite *kept in the land*; **avodah** — the gem: **service** — the *same word* as Israel's *slavery* (1:14; 5:9; 6:6) — now the *worship-service* of the Passover. The *transfer of service is enacted*: the interior's *avodah to Pharaoh becomes avodah to God* *(inferred: shamar/avodah/natan = the worship-service kept in the given land — the slavery-service transferred to God)*.
- **avodah (service) — the *rite the children question* (12:26).** *avodah distinct from 12:25:* "when your children say, 'what is this **service** (avodah)?'" *(stated)* — the gem: the *rite provokes the children's question* — the interior of the *next generation* *taught* by asking; the *avodah* (worship) *carries the story* forward (← the pedagogical purpose, 10:2) *(inferred: avodah = the worship-service that provokes the children's question — the rite that teaches)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **the counted community and the unblemished shelter (nephesh 12:4 → tamim 12:5 → basar/maror 12:8):** each *person counted in* under a *whole* lamb; the meal *eaten with the remembered bitterness* of bondage — freedom *tasting* its own past.
2. **judgment and the sheltering blood (shephet 12:12 → mashchit 12:13):** God *judges Egypt's gods* and *passes over* the blood-marked — the *destruction averted by a sign*.
3. **the holy community and its boundary (nephesh 12:15 → qodesh/miqra/nephesh 12:16 → nephesh 12:19):** the *person cut off* for leaven, the *holy assembly*, the boundary *covenantal not ethnic* (sojourner and native alike) — belonging *and* its obligation.
4. **the memory kept and taught (shamar 12:17 → qara 12:21 → shachat 12:23 → shamar 12:24 → shamar/avodah/natan 12:25 → avodah 12:26 → 12:27 worship):** the feast *kept forever*, the *destroyer barred*, the rite *handed to the sons*, the *service transferred* from Pharaoh to God, and the *children's question* answered — the people *bow and worship*. The interior *formed by the rite*: sheltered, remembering, teaching, serving God.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** nephesh ×4 (12:4 the persons *counted in* / 12:15 the person *cut off* for leaven / 12:16 *each person's* need in the holy assembly / 12:19 the person cut off, *native or sojourner* — the covenantal boundary); tamim (the *unblemished* shelter); **maror (the *remembered bitterness* of bondage eaten into the feast — ← marar 1:14)**; shephet (the *judgment on Egypt's gods*); mashchit / shachat *distinguished* (12:13 the *destruction* averted / 12:23 the *destroyer* barred at the door); qodesh/miqra (the *holy assembly*); shamar ×3 (the *feast* / the *rite for the sons* / the *service in the land*); **avodah ×2 (the *worship-service* kept — the slavery-word transferred to God — / the service the *children question*)**; qara (the *elders summoned*); natan (the *land given*).
- **Whose interior (multi-interior):** **God** — *judgment and mercy in one night*, distinguishing by the *blood-sign*, overthrowing *Egypt's gods*; **Israel** — the interior *formed by the rite*: *counted in* under the unblemished lamb, *remembering the bitterness*, *sheltered from the destroyer*, *keeping the memory*, *teaching the children*, and *serving God* (the avodah transferred). The rite *makes* the community's interior.
- **Threads:** the **transfer of service** *enacted* (avodah 12:25–26 ← slavery 1:14; 5:9; 6:6 → "let my people **serve** me" 3:12; 7:16 — the slavery-service *becomes worship*); the **remembered bitterness** (maror 12:8 ← marar 1:14 — the bondage tasted in the feast); the **pedagogical rite** (avodah 12:26 ← 10:2; → 13:8,14 — the story told to the children); the **covenant-blood averting destruction** (mashchit/shachat 12:13,23 ← 4:26 the bridegroom of blood; the destroyer barred); **tamim** (the unblemished ← Noah 6:9; Abraham 17:1); the **holy community** (qodesh 12:16 → Sinai, "a kingdom of priests"); the **judgment on the gods** (shephet 12:12 — the spiritual victory).
- **Guarded against:** lumping the four nephesh (counted-in / cut-off-leaven / each-person / cut-off-native-or-sojourner) or three shamar (feast / rite-for-sons / service-in-land) or two avodah (kept / questioned); merging mashchit (the destruction) with shachat (the destroyer) — event vs agent; over-reading tamim into NT lamb-Christology (1 Cor 5:7) over the *unblemished-shelter* the passage gives; importing atonement theology beyond what the *blood-sign averts destruction* text carries; missing that avodah (12:25–26) is the *slavery-word now meaning worship* — the transfer of service is the chapter's quiet climax; treating maror as a mere ritual detail when it *builds the remembered bitterness* into the feast.

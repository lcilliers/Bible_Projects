# Exodus 15:1–21 — inner-being passage reading (EXO-20-song-of-the-sea)

- **Source:** verse-first lexicals over the passage (provenance `exodus-deliverance-v1-20260705`). Every non-T2 span in its verse; *stated* | *inferred*; distinctions kept. **Multiple interiors**: **Israel** (worship — the arc's culmination), the **divine warrior** (power + chesed), the **enemy** (greedy appetite quoted), and the **nations** (dread). **Note:** the second chesed span at 15:13 carries an alternate gloss "shame"; the word is chesed = *steadfast love*, read as such.
- **Check-back:** gate run post-draft; every non-T2 verse/gloss engaged; the 2 gaah, 2 oz, 2 oyev, 2 qodesh, the paired chesed each treated distinctly.

## EXO-20-song-of-the-sea · Exodus 15:1–21 · [worship as the interior's endpoint] — the freed people sing: the divine warrior of power and steadfast love, the enemy's appetite drowned, the nations struck still

**The passage.** "I will sing… he has **triumphed** (gaah) gloriously" (15:1); "the LORD is my **strength** (oz) and my **song** (zimrat)… I will **exalt** (rum) him" (15:2); "Pharaoh's **host** (chayil) he cast into the sea" (15:4); "your right hand, glorious in **power** (koach), shatters the **enemy** (oyev)" (15:6); "you send your **fury** (charon)… your **majesty** (gaon)" (15:7); "the deeps congealed in the **heart** (lev) of the sea" (15:8); "the **enemy** (oyev) said, 'I will pursue… my **desire** (nephesh) shall have its fill'" (15:9); "who is like you, **awesome** (yare) in **holiness** (qodesh)… in **praises** (tehillah), doing **wonders** (pele)?" (15:11); "you led in your **steadfast love** (chesed)… guided by your **strength** (oz) to your **holy** (qodesh) abode" (15:13); "the peoples **tremble** (ragaz)" (15:14); "**trembling** (raad)… **dismayed** (bahal)" (15:15); "**Terror** (emah) and **dread** (pachad) fall… **still** (damam) as a stone" (15:16); "the LORD will reign forever" (15:18); Miriam: "sing… he has **triumphed** (gaah)" (15:21).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **gaah (triumphed) — the *exultant opening* (15:1).** "I will sing… he has **triumphed** (gaah) gloriously; the horse and rider he threw into the sea" *(stated)* — gaah, *rise up / be exalted / triumph*: the gem is the *interior's exultation* — the freed people's first act *after* fear-and-faith (14:31) is *song*; the deliverance *overflows in praise* of God's triumph *(inferred: gaah = the exultant song of God's triumph — worship as the interior's overflow)*.
- **oz (strength) + zimrat (song) + rum (exalt) — *God made the interior's music* (15:2).** "the LORD is my **strength** (oz) and my **song** (zimrat)… I will **exalt** (rum) him" *(stated)*. **oz** — *strength* (God *is* the interior's strength); **zimrat** — *song* (God *is* the interior's music); **rum** — *exalt* (← the staff raised 14:16; the defiant exit 14:8 — now *God* lifted up). The gem: the interior *makes God its strength, its song, and lifts him high* — worship as *the whole self turned Godward* *(inferred: oz/zimrat/rum = God made the interior's strength, song, and exaltation)*.
- **chayil (host) — the *might cast down* (15:4).** "Pharaoh's chariots and **host** (chayil) he cast into the sea" *(stated)* — chayil *(← 14:4,28)*: the *mustered might*, now *sung as sunk* — the interior *recounting the adversary's power undone* *(inferred: chayil = the host cast into the sea — the might undone, sung)*.
- **koach (power) + oyev (enemy) — the *right hand that shatters* (15:6).** "your right hand, glorious in **power** (koach), shatters the **enemy** (oyev)" *(stated)*. **koach** — *power* (← 9:16, God's power shown); **oyev** — *enemy*: the divine interior *sung as the shattering power* — the strength that judged Egypt *praised* *(inferred: koach/oyev = the right hand's power shattering the enemy)*.
- **charon (fury) + gaon (majesty) — the *consuming wrath and majesty* (15:7).** "in your **majesty** (gaon) you overthrow… you send your **fury** (charon); it consumes like stubble" *(stated)*. **charon** — *burning anger*; **gaon** — *majesty/height* (God's exaltedness): the gem is the divine interior's *wrath* (charon) *and* *majesty* (gaon) *together* — the fury that *consumes the adversary* is an aspect of God's *greatness* *(inferred: charon/gaon = the consuming fury and the majesty — God's wrath as glory)*.
- **lev (heart) — the *heart of the sea* (15:8).** "the deeps congealed in the **heart** (lev) of the sea" *(stated)* — lev in the *idiomatic "midst"* sense (the sea's *core*): a *non-interior* use — the *depths of the sea* where the waters stood; noted distinctly *for its idiom* (not a heart-operation) *(inferred: lev = the "heart"/midst of the sea — the idiomatic core, not an interior)*.
- **oyev (enemy) + nephesh (desire) — the *adversary's greedy appetite quoted* (15:9).** *oyev distinct from 15:6:* "the **enemy** (oyev) said, 'I will pursue, overtake, divide the spoil; my **desire** (nephesh) shall have its fill'" *(stated)*. **oyev** — now the *enemy speaking*; **nephesh** — the gem: *appetite/craving* — the adversary's *interior quoted* — a *greedy, violent nephesh* ("my desire shall have its fill… I will destroy"). The song *voices the enemy's craving* — and then drowns it (15:10); the adversary's *appetite* is his epitaph *(inferred: oyev/nephesh = the enemy's greedy appetite quoted — the craving that is drowned)*.
- **yare (awesome) + qodesh (holiness) + tehillah (praises) + pele (wonders) — *God incomparable, the object of awe* (15:11).** "who is like you, **awesome** (yare) in **holiness** (qodesh)… in **praises** (tehillah), doing **wonders** (pele)?" *(stated)*. **yare** — the gem: *God himself is "awesome"* — the *fear-of-God* now *in worship* (the reverence the midwives had 1:17; Israel now 14:31 — here *sung*); **qodesh** — *holiness*; **tehillah** — *praises*; **pele** — *wonders* (← pala 3:20; Gen 18:14). The interior *stands in awe* — *"who is like you?"* — worship as *wonder at the incomparable God* *(inferred: yare/qodesh/tehillah/pele = God the awesome, holy, wonder-working — worship as awe at the incomparable)*.
- **chesed (steadfast love) + oz (strength) + qodesh (holy) — *the warrior who is also love* (15:13).** *oz/qodesh distinct from 15:2,11:* "you led in your **steadfast love** (chesed) the people you **redeemed**… guided by your **strength** (oz) to your **holy** (qodesh) abode" *(stated)*. **chesed** — the gem: the *divine warrior* (15:3 "a man of war") is *also* **steadfast love** — the deliverance is an act of *chesed*, and the redeemed are *led by chesed*; **oz** *distinct from 15:2* — now the *strength that guides* (not the interior's strength but God's leading); **qodesh** *distinct from 15:11* — the *holy abode* (destination). The divine interior: *power that destroys the enemy* and *steadfast love that leads the redeemed home* *(inferred: chesed/oz/qodesh = the warrior's steadfast love leading the redeemed to the holy place)*.
- **ragaz (tremble) — the *nations trembling* (15:14).** "the peoples have heard; they **tremble** (ragaz)" *(stated)* — ragaz, *tremble/quake*: the gem — the *report* of the deliverance *seizes the watching nations*; the interior of *the peoples shaken* by what God has done *(inferred: ragaz = the nations trembling at the report)*.
- **raad (trembling) + bahal (dismayed) — the *dread spreading* (15:15).** "**trembling** (raad) seizes Moab's leaders; the inhabitants of Canaan are **dismayed** (bahal)" *(stated)*. **raad** — *trembling*; **bahal** — *dismay/terror*: the dread *spreads nation by nation* (Edom, Moab, Canaan) — the interior of *the peoples melting* before the coming redeemed *(inferred: raad/bahal = the dread spreading through the nations)*.
- **emah (terror) + pachad (dread) + damam (still) — the *nations struck motionless* (15:16).** "**Terror** (emah) and **dread** (pachad) fall… they are **still** (damam) as a stone, till your people pass by" *(stated)*. **emah** — *terror*; **pachad** — *dread* (← the "Fear/Dread of Isaac", Gen 31:42); **damam** — *be silent/still*: the gem — the nations *struck motionless as stone* by *terror and dread* (contrast Israel's *charesh*-stillness of *faith*, 14:14; here the nations' *stillness of dread*). The interior of *the peoples paralysed* while the redeemed *pass* *(inferred: emah/pachad/damam = the nations struck still as stone by terror and dread)*.
- **gaah (triumphed) — the *antiphon closed* (15:21).** *gaah distinct from 15:1:* "Miriam sang: 'sing… he has **triumphed** (gaah) gloriously'" *(stated)* — where 15:1 Moses *opened*, 15:21 Miriam and the women *answer* with tambourines and dancing — the gem: worship is *communal, embodied, antiphonal* — the interior's praise *shared and danced*; the song *taken up by the women* *(inferred: gaah = the antiphonal close — communal, embodied worship)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **the interior overflows in song (gaah 15:1 → oz/zimrat/rum 15:2):** the freed people, having *feared and believed* (14:31), *sing* — making God their *strength, song, and exaltation*; worship the *interior's overflow*.
2. **the warrior praised (chayil 15:4 → koach/oyev 15:6 → charon/gaon 15:7 → lev 15:8 → oyev/nephesh 15:9 → 15:10):** the song *recounts* the adversary's *might cast down*, the *right hand's power*, the *consuming fury and majesty*, and *quotes the enemy's greedy appetite* (nephesh) — which the sea *drowns*.
3. **awe at the incomparable, led by steadfast love (yare/qodesh/tehillah/pele 15:11 → chesed/oz/qodesh 15:13):** *"who is like you?"* — the interior *stands in awe*; and the *warrior* is *also steadfast love* — the redeemed *led by chesed* to the *holy place*.
4. **the nations struck still (ragaz 15:14 → raad/bahal 15:15 → emah/pachad/damam 15:16 → 15:18):** the *report* seizes the peoples with *trembling, dismay, terror, and dread* — *still as stone* — while the redeemed *pass by*; "the LORD will reign forever."
5. **the antiphon of the people (gaah 15:21):** Miriam and the women *answer* Moses' song with *tambourines and dancing* — worship *communal, embodied, shared*. The interior's endpoint: **the freed people singing.**

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** gaah ×2 (Moses' *opening* / Miriam's *antiphonal close* — communal, embodied worship); oz ×2 (God *my strength* / the *strength that guides*); zimrat/rum (God *made song*, *exalted*); chayil (the *host* sung as sunk); koach/oyev (the *power* shattering the enemy); charon/gaon (the *consuming fury and majesty*); **lev (the *idiomatic "heart"/midst* of the sea — not an interior)**; **nephesh (the *enemy's greedy appetite* quoted — the craving drowned)**; oyev ×2 (enemy *shattered* / enemy *boasting*); **yare (God himself *awesome* — the fear-of-God in worship)**; qodesh ×2 (majestic *in holiness* / the *holy abode*); tehillah/pele (praises / wonders); **chesed (the *warrior who is also steadfast love* — the redeemed led by chesed)**; the *terror-cluster* ragaz/raad/bahal/emah/pachad/damam (the *nations struck still* by dread — vs Israel's *charesh*-stillness of faith 14:14).
- **Whose interior (multi-interior):** **Israel** — the arc's *culmination in worship*: groan (2:23) → fear/faith (14:31) → *song* (15) — the interior *overflowing Godward*, *communal and embodied* (Miriam, dancing); the **divine warrior** — *power that shatters the enemy* (koach, charon) *and* *steadfast love that leads the redeemed* (chesed) — the two held together; the **enemy** — a *greedy appetite* (nephesh) quoted and drowned; the **nations** — *trembling and dread*, struck *still as stone*.
- **Threads:** **Israel's arc completed** (song 15 ← the groan 2:23; fear/faith 14:31 — the interior's journey from *bondage-groan* to *freedom-song*); the **fear-of-God in worship** (yare 15:11 — God *awesome* ← the midwives 1:17; Israel 14:31 — reverence become praise); the divine **warrior + chesed** (15:3,13 — power and steadfast love together ← the chesed of Gen 24; 32:10); **pele/wonders** (15:11 ← pala 3:20; Gen 18:14 — the wonder-working God); the **dread of God** on the nations (pachad 15:16 ← the "Dread of Isaac" Gen 31:42) drawing toward the land (15:17 "your holy abode… the sanctuary" → Sinai and beyond); the **stillness** contrast (damam 15:16 the nations' *dread*-stillness vs charesh 14:14 Israel's *faith*-stillness); the **enemy's appetite** (nephesh 15:9 — the craving that destroys itself).
- **Guarded against:** lumping the two gaah/oz/oyev/qodesh (opening/close; my-strength/guiding-strength; shattered/boasting; majestic-in-holiness/holy-abode); reading a "shame" operation into the mis-glossed second chesed (the word is *steadfast love*); treating lev (15:8) as an interior when it is the *idiomatic midst* of the sea; over-reading the Song into later liturgical/typological frames (the new-exodus, Rev 15:3) over the *worship-response* the passage is; missing that the divine interior here holds *warrior and chesed together* (not wrath *or* love, but power *and* steadfast love); noting that Israel's arc *culminates but does not end* in the song — the wilderness (15:22, Marah) tests it again the very next verse.

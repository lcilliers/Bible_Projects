# Exodus 34:1–9 — inner-being passage reading (GLD-06-name-proclaimed)

- **Source:** verse-first lexicals over the passage (provenance `exodus-goldencalf-v1-20260705`). Every non-T2 span in its verse; *stated* | *inferred*; distinctions kept. This is the **fullest self-revelation of God's character in the Torah** — given *after* the calf, as the answer to "show me your glory." **Multiple interiors**: **God** (his own character proclaimed), **Moses** (worship, and a plea grounded in the Name). **Lexical note:** the second chesed span at 34:6 and 34:7 carries the homonym gloss "shame" — here every chesed is *steadfast love*.

## GLD-06-name-proclaimed · Exodus 34:1–9 · [the glory revealed as mercy; mercy held with justice; the stiff neck turned into the plea] — "The LORD, the LORD, a God merciful and gracious… forgiving iniquity… yet by no means clearing the guilty"

**The passage.** New tablets cut; the LORD descended and **proclaimed** (qara) the name (34:5); "The LORD, the LORD, a God **merciful** (rachum) and **gracious** (channun), slow to anger, abounding in **steadfast love** (chesed) and **faithfulness** (emet)" — **proclaimed** (qara, 34:6); "keeping **steadfast love** (chesed) for thousands, forgiving **iniquity** (avon) and **transgression** (pesha) and **sin** (chattaah), but who will by no means clear the guilty, visiting the **iniquity** (avon)… to the third and fourth generation" (34:7); Moses bowed and worshiped (34:8); "if I have found **favor** (chen)… let the LORD go in our midst, for it is a **stiff-necked** (qasheh) people, and **pardon** (salach) our **iniquity** (avon) and **sin** (chattat), and take us for your inheritance" (34:9).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **qara (proclaimed) — the *self-naming of God* (34:5).** "the LORD descended in the cloud and **proclaimed** (qara) the name of the LORD" *(stated)* — qara, *proclaim/call out*: the gem is that *God himself proclaims his own name* — this is not Moses' inference but *God's self-disclosure*; the interior-context of *God defining himself* — answering the request for glory (33:18) by *speaking his own character* *(inferred: qara = the self-naming of God — God proclaims his own name)*.
- **rachum (merciful) + channun (gracious) + chesed (steadfast love) + emet (faithfulness) + qara (proclaimed) — the *glory revealed as mercy* (34:6).** "the LORD passed before him and **proclaimed** (qara): 'The LORD, the LORD, a God **merciful** (rachum) and **gracious** (channun), slow to anger, abounding in **steadfast love** (chesed) and **faithfulness** (emet)'" *(stated)*. **rachum** — *compassionate* (womb-love); **channun** — *gracious* (the channun of 22:27 now *fully sounded*); **chesed** — *steadfast/loyal love* (the second span's *shame* gloss the homonym artifact); **emet** — *faithfulness/truth*: the gem is *THE revelation* — when Moses asks to see the *glory*, what God shows is *his goodness proclaimed as MERCY*: *compassionate, gracious, slow to anger, overflowing in loyal love and faithfulness*. The interior finding of the whole block: **the glory of God, when shown, is his mercy** — God's deepest self is *love that abounds* — and this proclaimed *after the calf*, to a people who had just betrayed him *(inferred: rachum/channun/chesed/emet = the glory revealed as mercy — God's own self proclaimed as compassion, grace, loyal love, faithfulness)*.
- **chesed (steadfast love) + avon (iniquity) + pesha (transgression) + chattaah (sin) — *mercy held with justice* (34:7).** "'keeping **steadfast love** (chesed) for thousands, forgiving **iniquity** (avon), **transgression** (pesha), and **sin** (chattaah), but who will by no means clear the guilty, visiting the **iniquity** (avon) to the third and fourth generation'" *(stated)*. **chesed** — *loyal love kept for thousands* (again the *shame* gloss the artifact); **avon/pesha/chattaah** — *iniquity / transgression / sin* (the **three** words for sin — the *whole vocabulary* of wrong): the gem is *the tension the Name holds* — God's *steadfast love reaches thousands* and *forgives the whole range of sin* (iniquity, rebellion, failure — all of it), **yet** *"will by no means clear the guilty"* — *grace does not cancel accountability*. The interior finding: *God is not merely soft* — he *forgives AND holds guilt*; and the *asymmetry* is decisive — *chesed to THOUSANDS*, iniquity visited to *the third and fourth generation* — **mercy vastly outweighs judgment** (as at 20:5–6). *Love that forgives all, yet justice that will not pretend guilt is nothing* *(inferred: chesed/avon/pesha/chattaah = mercy held with justice — forgiving the whole range of sin, yet not clearing the guilty; mercy to thousands dwarfing judgment)*.
- **chen (favor) + qasheh (stiff-necked) + salach (pardon) + avon (iniquity) + chattat (sin) — *the stiff neck turned into the plea* (34:9).** "Moses: 'if I have found **favor** (chen)… let the LORD go in our midst, **for it is a stiff-necked** (qasheh) people, and **pardon** (salach) our **iniquity** (avon) and **sin** (chattat), and take us for your inheritance'" *(stated)*. **chen** — *favor*; **qasheh** — *stiff-necked*; **salach** — *forgive/pardon*; **avon/chattat** — *iniquity/sin*: the gem is *the daring reversal* — the *stiff-neckedness* that was God's *reason to withdraw* ("I will not go up… for you are stiff-necked, lest I consume you", 33:3,5) Moses now makes the *reason to STAY*: *"go in our midst BECAUSE we are stiff-necked"* — *the very worst of the people pleaded as the ground of God's presence* (they *need* him all the more; only his presence can change the hard neck). And, having *just heard the Name*, Moses *pleads it back* — asking the *forgiving* God to *forgive*, the *loyal-love* God to *claim them as inheritance*. The interior of *intercession that turns depravity into the argument for grace* — *pardon us and possess us precisely because we cannot mend ourselves* *(inferred: chen/qasheh/salach = the stiff neck turned into the plea — the worst of the people made the ground of the plea for presence and pardon)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **the glory revealed as mercy (qara 34:5 → rachum/channun/chesed/emet 34:6):** God answers *"show me your glory"* by *proclaiming his own name* — and the glory is *MERCY*: *compassionate, gracious, slow to anger, abounding in loyal love and faithfulness* — *God's deepest self is abounding love*, declared *to the very people who betrayed him*.
2. **mercy held with justice (chesed/avon/pesha/chattaah 34:7):** the same Name *forgives the whole range of sin* and *keeps love to thousands*, **yet** *will not clear the guilty* — *grace does not erase accountability*; mercy *dwarfs* judgment (thousands vs three-or-four generations) but does not *pretend*.
3. **the stiff neck turned into the plea (chen/qasheh/salach 34:8–9):** Moses *worships*, then *pleads the Name back* — turning the people's *stiff-neckedness* (the reason for withdrawal) into the *reason for God's presence and pardon*: *go with us, forgive us, keep us — because we cannot mend ourselves.*

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** **qara ×2 (the *self-naming* / the *character proclaimed*)**; **rachum/channun/chesed/emet (the *glory revealed as mercy* — compassionate, gracious, loyal love, faithfulness)**; **chesed (the loyal love *abounding* 34:6 / *kept for thousands* 34:7 — the "shame" gloss the homonym artifact)**; **avon/pesha/chattaah (the *three words for sin*, all *forgiven*, yet iniquity *visited*)**; **chen/qasheh/salach (the *stiff neck turned into the plea* — the worst made the ground of the plea for presence and pardon)**.
- **Whose interior (multi-interior):** **God** — his *own character proclaimed*: *mercy* overwhelmingly (compassionate, gracious, slow to anger, abounding in chesed and emet), *forgiving all sin*, **yet** *just* (not clearing the guilty) — the glory shown as *goodness/mercy*; **Moses** — *worship*, and *intercession that pleads the Name back* and *turns the stiff neck into the argument for grace*. The gems: **the glory is mercy**, **mercy held with justice**, and **the stiff neck made the plea**.
- **Threads:** **the great self-revelation** (34:6–7 — the most-quoted self-description of God in the OT: Num 14:18; Neh 9:17; Ps 86:15; 103:8; 145:8; Joel 2:13; Jonah 4:2 — "gracious and merciful, slow to anger, abounding in steadfast love"; the *channun* of 22:27 and the *free grace* of 33:19 now *fully named*); **the glory is goodness/mercy** (34:6 ← "I will make my goodness pass" 33:19 — the *glory* Moses asked to see *is* God's mercy); **mercy and justice held** (34:7 ← 20:5–6 — chesed to thousands / iniquity to 3–4 generations; the tension carried, not collapsed → the cross, where both meet); **the three words for sin forgiven** (avon/pesha/chattaah 34:7 → Ps 32:1–2; 1 Jn 1:9 — the comprehensive pardon); **the stiff neck as the plea** (qasheh 34:9 ← 33:3,5 — the daring reversal: the reason to withdraw made the reason to stay → grace *for* the undeserving, Rom 5:8).
- **Guarded against:** softening the Name into *only* mercy when the text holds *justice* with it ("by no means clear the guilty" — the finding is the *tension*, not a sentimental God); hardening it into *only* justice when the weight is overwhelmingly *mercy* (compassionate, gracious, slow to anger, abounding — and mercy to *thousands* vs judgment to *3–4 generations*); reading Moses' plea (34:9) as mere repetition when it *dares to turn the stiff neck into the argument* (the reversal is the finding); importing the full NT resolution of mercy-and-justice (the cross) as the *meaning* here when the text *leaves the tension open* — the Name states *both* and does not yet show *how*; keeping the chesed "shame" gloss as the homonym artifact it is (every chesed here is *steadfast love*).

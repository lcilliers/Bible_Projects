## EXO-15-final-warning · Exodus 11:1–10 · [the split complete; the hardening's purpose fully stated] — favor given, the mediator's hot anger, and the heart the LORD hardens to the last

**The passage.** "One plague more… he will drive you out completely" (11:1); "let them **ask** (shaal), each of his neighbour, for silver and gold" (11:2); "the LORD gave the people **favor** (chen)… Moses was very great in Egypt" — God **gave** (natan) it (11:3); the firstborn foretold (11:4–5); "the LORD makes a distinction between Egypt and Israel" (11:7); Moses went out "in **hot** (chori) anger" (11:8); "Pharaoh will not listen, that my wonders may be **multiplied** (ravah)" (11:9); "the LORD **hardened** (chazaq) Pharaoh's **heart** (lev)" (11:10).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **shaal (ask) — the *plunder by asking* readied (11:2).** "let them **ask** (shaal), each of his neighbour, for silver and gold" *(stated)* — shaal, *ask/request* (← 3:22): the gem is that the *spoil comes by asking*, not seizing — the interior *receives the wealth of its bondage as given*, on the eve of freedom *(inferred: shaal = the plunder received by asking, readied)*.
- **chen (favor) + natan (gave) — *Egypt disposed toward Israel* (11:3).** "the LORD **gave** (natan) the people **favor** (chen) in the sight of the Egyptians; Moses was very great" *(stated)*. **chen** — *favor*; **natan** — *give*: the gem (← 3:21 foretold, now *enacted*) — *God turns Egypt's regard toward Israel*; even *Pharaoh's servants* and *the people* hold *Moses great*. The *split in Egypt is complete*: the whole land *favors* Israel and *honours* the mediator — only Pharaoh's heart holds out *(inferred: chen/natan = the God-given favor that turns all Egypt toward Israel — the split complete)*.
- **chori (hot anger) — the mediator's *burning indignation* (11:8).** "he went out from Pharaoh in **hot** (chori) anger" *(stated)* — chori, *burning/heat* (of anger): the gem is *Moses' anger* — after the death-threat (10:28) and the final defiance, the mediator *burns* — the *same register* as God's kindled anger (charah, 4:14). The deliverer now *shares the divine indignation* at the hardened evil; the interior *aligned with God's stance* toward the oppressor *(inferred: chori = the mediator's burning anger, aligned with God's indignation)*.
- **ravah (multiplied) — the hardening's *purpose: multiplied wonders* (11:9).** "Pharaoh will not listen, that my wonders may be **multiplied** (ravah)" *(stated)* — the gem: **ravah** — *multiply* (the *same word* as *Israel's* multiplying, 1:7,12) — now *God's wonders multiply* as *Israel* multiplied. The *purpose* of the continued hardening is *stated*: the refusal *makes room for more wonders* — the fuller *display* of God's acts (→ the pedagogical telling, 10:2). The interior-context: the adversary's fixed "no" *serving* the multiplication of the signs *(inferred: ravah = the wonders multiplied through the refusal — the hardening's purpose)*.
- **lev (heart) + chazaq (hardened) — the *final God-hardening* (11:10).** "the LORD **hardened** (chazaq) Pharaoh's **heart**, and he did not let Israel go" *(stated)* — the gem (per the ledger): the *last* God-hardening *before the Passover* — the *culmination* of the sealed phase (9:12; 10:20,27); the heart *fixed to the last*, *"as the LORD had spoken"*. The trajectory's *end-point on this side of the firstborn* — the self-fixed, God-sealed refusal *held to the threshold of the final blow* *(inferred: lev/chazaq = the final God-hardening, the refusal held to the last)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **the plunder readied, Egypt disposed (shaal 11:2 → chen/natan 11:3):** the *spoil by asking* prepared; *God turns all Egypt's favor toward Israel* and *honours the mediator* — the *split complete*, only Pharaoh holding out.
2. **the mediator's hot anger (chori 11:8):** after the death-threat, Moses *burns with indignation* — *aligned with God's own anger* at the hardened evil.
3. **the purpose and the final hardening (ravah 11:9 → lev/chazaq 11:10):** the refusal *serves the multiplication of wonders* (as Israel multiplied, so the signs); and *God hardens the heart to the last* — the trajectory *culminating* at the threshold of the Passover.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** shaal (the *plunder by asking* readied); **chen/natan (the *God-given favor* that turns all Egypt toward Israel — the split complete, only Pharaoh holding out)**; **chori (the mediator's *burning anger* — Moses aligned with God's indignation)**; **ravah (the hardening's *purpose* — wonders *multiplied* as Israel multiplied)**; **lev/chazaq (the *final God-hardening* — the refusal held to the last)**.
- **Whose interior (multi-interior):** **God** — *disposing Egypt's favor*, *stating the purpose* (multiplied wonders), *hardening to the last*; **Moses** — *burning anger* (the mediator sharing God's stance); **Egypt** — *favoring Israel and honouring Moses* (the split complete); the **adversary** (Pharaoh) — *alone* in his God-sealed refusal, against a land that now favors his slaves.
- **Threads:** the **hardening trajectory culminates** (11:10 final God-hardening ← 9:12; 10:20,27; the *purpose* 11:9 "wonders multiplied" ← 7:3; 10:1–2); the **favor** enacted (chen/natan 11:3 ← 3:21 → 12:36 the plunder); the **split in Egypt complete** (11:3 ← the God-fearers 9:20; the servants 10:7 — now *all* Egypt favors Israel); the **mediator's anger** (chori 11:8 ← God's charah 4:14 — Moses aligned with God); **ravah** (the wonders multiplied as *Israel* multiplied, 1:7 — the same word, God's acts and God's people); the **distinction** (11:7 ← 8:22; 9:4,26 → the Passover night); the **plunder by asking** (shaal 11:2 ← 3:22).
- **Guarded against:** reading the final God-hardening (11:10) as erasing the *long self-hardening* that grounded it (the ledger: the God-hardening *confirms* what Pharaoh fixed himself — do not read the culmination as the whole story); over-reading chori (Moses' anger) as sinful loss of control when the verse aligns it with *God's* indignation at hardened evil; missing that ravah (11:9) is the *same word* as Israel's multiplying (1:7) — the wonders *multiply* as the people did; treating the *favor* (11:3) as Israel's merit when the verse says *God gave* it (the disposing of Egypt's heart is God's, as the hardening of Pharaoh's is); importing NT atonement over the *firstborn distinction* the passage foretells.

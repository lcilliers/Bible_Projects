## TAB-06-bezalel-sabbath-sign · Exodus 31:1–18 · [skill as a divine filling; the sabbath, sign that God sanctifies] — the devising heart filled with the Spirit, and "that you may know that I the LORD sanctify you"

**The passage.** "I have **called** (qara) by name Bezalel… and **filled him with the Spirit of God**, with ability, **understanding** (tevunah), **knowledge** (daat), and all craftsmanship, to **devise** (chashav) artistic **designs** (machashavah)" (31:2–4); to all the **wise-hearted** (chakham + lev) I have **given** (natan) ability (31:6); the tent, the **pure** (tahor) lampstand (31:8), the **holy** (qodesh) garments (31:10); "**keep** (shamar) my Sabbaths… a sign that you may know that I the LORD **sanctify** (qadash) you" (31:13); **keep** (shamar) it, it is **holy** (qodesh); whoever profanes it — that **soul** (nephesh) cut off (31:14); a Sabbath of solemn rest, **holy** (qodesh, 31:15); **keep** (shamar) it as a **covenant** (berit) forever (31:16); a sign that God rested and was **refreshed** (naphash, 31:17); he **gave** (natan) Moses the two tablets (31:18).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **qara (called) + tevunah (understanding) + daat (knowledge) + chashav (devise) + machashavah (designs) — *skill as a divine filling* (31:2–4).** "'I have **called** (qara) by name Bezalel… and **filled him with the Spirit of God**, with ability, **understanding** (tevunah), **knowledge** (daat)… to **devise** (chashav) artistic **designs** (machashavah)'" *(stated)*. **qara** — *call by name*; **tevunah** — *understanding/discernment*; **daat** — *knowledge*; **chashav** — *devise/design*; **machashavah** — *a devised thing / design* (the noun of chashav): the gem is that *artistic skill is a filling of the Spirit* — the **first person in Scripture said to be "filled with the Spirit of God"** is filled *for making*: the *understanding, knowledge, and designing-power* to build God's dwelling are a *divine gift*, poured into a *named* man. The interior finding: *craftsmanship, discernment, and creative design are a Spirit-given filling* — *ability is grace*, not self-made. And a quiet redemptive note in the word: **machashavah** — the *devising* of the heart — is the same root as *"every machashavah of man's heart was only evil"* (Gen 6:5); here the *devising heart* is *turned to beauty*, filled by the Spirit to *design for God* — *the scheming faculty redeemed into artistry* *(inferred: qara/tevunah/daat/chashav/machashavah = skill as a divine filling — the devising heart, Spirit-filled, turned to beauty for God)*.
- **chakham (wise) + lev (heart) + natan (given) — the *ability given to the wise-hearted* (31:6).** "'to all the **wise-hearted** (chakham + lev) I have **given** (natan) ability'" *(stated)*. **chakham** — *wise/skilled*; **lev** — *heart*; **natan** — *give*: the gem extends 31:3 — *skill seated in the heart is itself given* — not only Bezalel but *all the wise-hearted* have their ability *from God*; the interior of *gifted competence* — the heart's wisdom *bestowed* *(inferred: chakham/lev/natan = the ability given to the wise-hearted — skill bestowed, not self-owned)*.
- **shamar (keep) + qadash (sanctify) — the *sabbath, sign that God sanctifies* (31:13).** "'**keep** (shamar) my Sabbaths… a sign… that you may know that I the LORD **sanctify** (qadash) you'" *(stated)*. **shamar** — *keep*; **qadash** — *make holy/sanctify*: the gem is *the sabbath as the sign of a divine work* — keeping it, they *know that God sanctifies them* — **sanctification is GOD'S act**, not self-achievement; the interior finding: *the holy people are made holy by God*, and the sabbath is the weekly *sign* of that *received* holiness — *"I the LORD sanctify you"* *(inferred: shamar/qadash = the sabbath, sign that God sanctifies — holiness received, not achieved)*.
- **shamar (keep) + nephesh (soul) + qodesh (holy) — the *weight of the sign* (31:14).** *shamar distinct from 31:13:* "'**keep** (shamar) the Sabbath, for it is **holy** (qodesh); whoever profanes it… that **soul** (nephesh) shall be cut off'" *(stated)*. **nephesh** — *soul/person*: the gem is *the gravity of the sign* — to *profane* the sabbath is to *sever the sign of belonging*; the interior-context of *the sign taken with deathly seriousness* — *the person who breaks it cut off* *(inferred: shamar/nephesh/qodesh = the weight of the sign — profaning it severs belonging)*.
- **shamar (keep) + berit (covenant) — the *sabbath as covenant* (31:15–16).** *shamar distinct from 31:13,14:* "'the seventh is a Sabbath of solemn rest, **holy** (qodesh)… the people shall **keep** (shamar) it… as a **covenant** (berit) forever'" *(stated)*. **berit** — *covenant*: the gem is *the sabbath bound into the covenant* — *a perpetual sign* of the bond between God and Israel; the interior-context of *the rhythm of rest as covenant-fidelity* *(inferred: shamar/berit = the sabbath as covenant — the perpetual sign of the bond)*.
- **naphash (refreshed) — *God rested and was refreshed* (31:17).** "'in six days the LORD made heaven and earth, and on the seventh he rested and was **refreshed** (naphash)'" *(stated)* — **naphash**, *be refreshed / catch breath* (the verb of *nephesh*): the gem is the *anthropomorphic tenderness* — *God himself rested and was refreshed* — the sabbath *grounded in the Creator's own rest and re-breathing* (← Gen 2:2–3); the interior-context (attributed to God) of *rest as restoration* — the pattern the people's rest *images* *(inferred: naphash = God rested and was refreshed — the divine rest the human sabbath images)*.

### Construction / summary detail (surfaced honestly, not read)

- **tahor (pure lampstand)** 31:8, **qodesh (holy garments)** 31:10 — items in the *furnishings-list* (31:7–11) that Bezalel is to make. Construction/summary.
- **natan (gave)** 31:18 — God *gave* Moses the two tablets, *written with the finger of God*. A *handing-over* (the tablets' significance is real, but the span states the giving).

### The web (interactive)

1. **skill as a divine filling (qara/tevunah/daat/chashav/machashavah 31:2–4 → chakham/lev/natan 31:6):** God *calls a named man* and *fills him with the Spirit* — the *understanding, knowledge, and designing-power* to make holy things are a *gift*; and *all the wise-hearted* have their ability *given*; the interior of *craftsmanship as grace* — the *devising heart turned to beauty*.
2. **the sabbath, sign that God sanctifies (shamar/qadash 31:13 → shamar/nephesh/qodesh 31:14 → shamar/berit 31:15–16 → naphash 31:17):** the sabbath is the *sign that God sanctifies them* (holiness *received*), taken with *deathly seriousness* (profaning it severs belonging), *bound into the covenant* forever, and *grounded in God's own rest and refreshment*; the interior of *a holiness that is God's work, imaged in rest*.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** **qara/tevunah/daat/chashav/machashavah (skill as a *divine filling* — the first "filled with the Spirit", filled for making; the *devising heart* turned to beauty)**; **chakham/lev/natan (the *ability given* to the wise-hearted — skill bestowed)**; **shamar ×3 (keep as *sign* / keep for it is *holy* / keep as *covenant*)**; qadash (the sabbath's sign that *God sanctifies*); **nephesh (the *soul* cut off for profaning the sign)**; qodesh ×3 (the sabbath/garments *holy*); berit (the sabbath a *covenant* forever); **naphash (God *rested and was refreshed* — the divine rest the sabbath images)**. Construction (tahor lampstand, qodesh garments, natan tablets) noted, not read.
- **Whose interior (multi-interior):** **Bezalel and the wise-hearted** — *skill as a Spirit-filling* (ability, understanding, knowledge, design *given*); **God** — who *sanctifies* his people (their holiness his work), and who *rested and was refreshed* (the divine rest imaged in the sabbath); **Israel** — *kept* by the sign, *knowing* they are sanctified. The two gems: **skill as a divine filling** and **the sabbath as the sign that God sanctifies**.
- **Threads:** **skill as Spirit-gift** (31:3 ← the wise-hearted "filled with a spirit of wisdom" 28:3; the skilful design 26:1 → 35:30–35 Bezalel again, "he has filled him with the Spirit… to teach"; the *first* Spirit-filling in Scripture is *for making* — art as gift); **the devising heart redeemed** (machashavah 31:4 ← Gen 6:5 "every machashavah… only evil" — the *scheming* faculty here *turned to beauty*); **sanctification is God's work** (qadash 31:13 → "the LORD who sanctifies you" Lev 20:8; "may the God of peace sanctify you" 1 Thess 5:23 — holiness *received*); **the sabbath** (naphash 31:17 ← creation's rest Gen 2:2–3; the manna-sabbath 16:23; the fourth commandment 20:8–11 — rest as gift, sign, and covenant); **God refreshed** (naphash 31:17 — the tender anthropomorphism, God re-breathing).
- **Guarded against:** over-reading the *furnishings-list* (31:7–11) into meaning when it is a construction summary; inflating the *two tablets* (31:18) beyond the *giving* the span states (their weight lands in ch32 when they are *broken*); reading the sabbath death-penalty (31:14–15) as the point when the *sign of sanctification* is the finding; letting the NT "filled with the Spirit" (Acts 2) recolour 31:3 — here the filling is *for craftsmanship*, and that is precisely the striking finding (the Spirit's *first* filling is *artistic*); **keeping the two genuine gems at their weight and not padding the furnishings-list to match.**

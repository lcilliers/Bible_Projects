## TAB-02-tabernacle-altar-court · Exodus 26:1–27:21 · [the skilled design; otherwise construction] — the cherubim skilfully worked, and the making of the tent

**The passage.** The tabernacle curtains with cherubim **skilfully worked** (chashav, 26:1); the frames *joined at the top* (tam, 26:24); erected *according to the plan* (mishpat, 26:30); the veil with cherubim **skilfully worked** (chashav, 26:31), separating the Holy from the **Most Holy** (qodesh, 26:34); the bronze altar (**height** qomah, 27:1), its *ash*-pots (dashen, 27:3); the court (**height** qomah, 27:18), the utensils for every **use** (avodah, 27:19); Aaron and sons to **tend** (arakh) the lamp before the LORD (27:21).

### The one faint thread (the nearest thing to an inner-being span)

- **chashav (skilfully worked / devised) — the *craftsman's design* (26:1, 26:31).** "you shall make the tabernacle… with cherubim **skilfully worked** (chashav) into them" (26:1); likewise the veil (26:31) *(stated)*. **chashav** — *devise / design / think out*: the gem, faint but real, is that *the making requires a designing mind* — the cherubim are not stamped but *skilfully devised*; the same verb that elsewhere means *to reckon / plan / intend* here names *artistic conception*. It is the **first appearance** of the skill-thread that becomes explicit at Bezalel (chashav 31:4 — "to devise designs"): *the interior gift of skilled design*. Here it is only *named in passing* — the verse gives the *product* (skilfully-worked cherubim), not a portrait of the craftsman — so no more is claimed than that: **skill is a designing of the mind** *(inferred: chashav = the craftsman's skilled design — the faint first sounding of the Bezalel skill-thread)*.

### Construction detail (surfaced honestly, not read as inner-being)

The rest is *making-instruction*, captured so nothing is skipped — **not** inner-being:

- **tam (joined/complete)** — 26:24 — the corner frames *joined at the top*. An assembly term.
- **mishpat (plan/manner)** — 26:30 — erect the tabernacle *"according to the plan shown you"*. Here mishpat is *pattern/specification* (not "judgment"); a construction sense of the word.
- **qodesh (Most Holy)** — 26:34 — the *Most Holy Place*, the innermost space. A *location/space* term here (the holiness of the place is real, but the verse states only where the mercy seat goes).
- **qomah (height)** — 27:1, 27:18 — the *dimensions* of the altar and court. Measurement.
- **dashen (ashes)** — 27:3 — the pots to *receive the altar's ashes* (the root can mean "grow fat/prosper"; here the *fatty ash* of sacrifice). A utensil-function term.
- **avodah (use/service)** — 27:19 — the utensils *for every use* of the tabernacle. Here avodah is the *functional use* of the equipment (the word will carry weightier "service/ministry" senses elsewhere; here it is mundane).
- **arakh (tend)** — 27:21 — Aaron and his sons *tend* the lamp *from evening to morning before the LORD*, a statute forever. The nearest to a ministry-note: a *perpetual tending* before God — but the verse gives the *instruction*, not an interior; captured as the standing service it institutes.

### Note on the block
This unit is, for inner-being purposes, **near-empty** — and that is the honest finding. The tabernacle's *meaning* (God dwelling, the graded holiness, the veil) is real theology, but it is **not carried by inner-being spans here**; it belongs where the text states it (the dwelling at 25:8 / 29:45; the veil's tearing significance in the NT). Forcing an interior reading onto curtains and cubits would be exactly the over-synthesis to avoid. The *skilled design* (chashav) is captured; corroboration comes at ch31 (Bezalel).

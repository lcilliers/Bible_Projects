## EXO-17-firstborn-exodus-plunder · Exodus 12:29–51 · [the hardening broken by death; the word turned] — Pharaoh yields and begs a blessing, Egypt urgently expels, and Israel plunders and goes out

**The passage.** At midnight the firstborn struck; a great cry (12:29–30); Pharaoh **summoned** (qara) Moses: "go, **serve** (avad) the LORD… and bless me!" (12:31–32); "the Egyptians were **urgent** (chazaq)… 'we shall all be dead'" (12:33); the people **asked** (shaal) for silver and gold (12:35); "the LORD **gave** (natan) them **favor** (chen)… they let them have what they **asked** (shaal); thus they plundered Egypt" (12:36); the exodus; a mixed multitude (12:38); the night of watching (12:42); "no… of its **flesh** (basar) outside the house" (12:46); "one law for native and stranger" (12:49); "the LORD brought Israel out" (12:51).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **qara (summoned) + avad (serve) — the *hardening broken*, the service granted (12:31).** "Pharaoh **summoned** (qara) Moses by night: 'go out… **serve** (avad) the LORD, as you have said'" *(stated)*. **qara** — the gem of *reversal*: the man who said *"never see my face again"* (10:28) now *summons* Moses *by night*; **avad** — he *grants the whole demand* — *"serve the LORD, as you have said"* — the *transfer of service* finally *conceded*. But the price was the *death of his own firstborn*: the God-sealed heart *cracked only at the deepest loss* — and (12:32, "bless me also!") the adversary even *begs a blessing* from the God he defied. The interior *broken, yielding, and grasping for blessing* — too late *(inferred: qara/avad = the hardening broken by death — the service granted, a blessing begged)*.
- **chazaq (urgent) — the *hardening-word turned on Egypt* (12:33).** "the Egyptians were **urgent** (chazaq) with the people to send them out… 'we shall all be dead'" *(stated)* — the gem: **chazaq** — the *very root* that *hardened Pharaoh's heart* (7:13; 9:12; 10:20,27; 11:10) — now describes *Egypt's urgent pushing of Israel OUT*. The *strengthening/firmness* that *held Israel in* is *turned to expelling them*; and its *driver is terror* ("we shall all be dead"). The word of the hardening *recoils* — the grip that *held* now *thrusts out* *(inferred: chazaq = the hardening-word turned to Egypt's terror-driven expulsion — the grip become a thrust)*.
- **shaal (asked) — the *plunder requested* (12:35).** "the people had **asked** (shaal) the Egyptians for silver and gold and clothing" *(stated)* — shaal *(← 3:22; 11:2)*: the *spoil requested* — the interior *receiving the wealth of its bondage by asking*, on the way out *(inferred: shaal = the plunder requested)*.
- **shaal (asked) + chen (favor) + natan (given) — the *plunder granted* (12:36).** *shaal distinct from 12:35:* "the LORD **gave** (natan) them **favor** (chen)… they let them have what they **asked** (shaal); thus they plundered Egypt" *(stated)*. **natan/chen** — *God gave the favor* that *disposed Egypt to grant* the request; **shaal** — the *asking granted*. The gem: the plunder is *God-given favor*, not seizure — Egypt *willingly gives* its wealth (the oppressor's goods *transferred to the freed*, ← 3:21). The interior of *Egypt disposed* and *Israel enriched by gift* *(inferred: shaal/chen/natan = the plunder granted by God-given favor — the oppressor's wealth given)*.
- **basar (flesh) — the *Passover flesh* bounded to the house (12:46).** "you shall not take any of the **flesh** (basar) outside the house, nor break a bone" *(stated)* — basar, *flesh* (← 12:8): the ordinance *binds the meal to the household* — the interior-context is *the sheltered community's* rite kept *within its door* *(inferred: basar = the Passover flesh bounded to the sheltering house)*.
- **(12:49 — "one law for native and stranger"; no non-T2 span.)** Flagged: the *one-law* statute (with circumcision as entry, 12:48) carries no flagged span, but it *opens the community* — the *mixed multitude* (12:38) and the *sojourner* may *belong* on the *same terms* as the native; the interior-context is *a community covenantal not ethnic* (← 12:19). Read as scene-context *(no-span context; the community opened to the stranger)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **the hardening broken by death (qara/avad 12:31):** the firstborn struck, and *Pharaoh summons the mediator he banished*, *grants the whole service*, and *begs a blessing* — the God-sealed heart *cracked only at the deepest loss*, and *too late*.
2. **the word turned, Egypt expels (chazaq 12:33):** the *hardening-word* now names *Egypt's terror-driven urgency to thrust Israel out* — the grip that *held* become a *thrust*, driven by "we shall all be dead."
3. **the plunder by God-given favor (shaal 12:35 → shaal/chen/natan 12:36):** Israel *asks* and *God's favor grants it* — the *oppressor's wealth given*, not seized; the freed *enriched by gift*.
4. **the exodus and the opened community (12:37–42 → basar 12:46 → 12:49):** the *hosts go out* on the *night of watching*, a *mixed multitude* with them; the rite *bounded to the sheltering house* yet *open to the stranger* on *one law* — the delivered community *formed and opened* in the going-out.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** **qara/avad (the *hardening broken by death* — Pharaoh summons the banished mediator, grants the service, begs a blessing — too late)**; **chazaq (the *hardening-word turned on Egypt* — its terror-driven expulsion; the grip become a thrust)**; shaal ×2 (the plunder *requested* / *granted*); chen/natan (the *God-given favor* disposing Egypt to give); basar (the Passover flesh *bounded to the house*); the *one-law* opening to the stranger (no-span context).
- **Whose interior (multi-interior):** the **adversary** (Pharaoh) — the *hardening finally broken*, but *only by the death of his firstborn*, and even then *grasping for a blessing* (the hard heart cracks at the deepest loss); **Egypt** — *terror-driven expulsion* (the hardening-word now *theirs*) and *willing surrender of its wealth*; **Israel** — *delivered, enriched by gift, formed by the watching-night, opened to the mixed multitude and the stranger*.
- **Threads:** the **hardening trajectory's END** — broken *at the firstborn's death* (12:31 ← the long self/God-hardening; it took the *ultimate loss* to crack it — the deeper trend: a heart that yields *only* under total judgment); the **chazaq wordplay** (12:33 the hardening-word now *Egypt's expelling* ← 7:13ff Pharaoh's heart; the grip turned to a thrust); the **plunder by favor** (shaal/chen/natan 12:35–36 ← 3:21–22; 11:2–3 — the oppressor's wealth *given*); the **transfer of service** *granted* (avad 12:31 ← 3:12; the demand finally conceded); the **opened community** (mixed multitude 12:38; one law 12:49 ← 12:19,48 — covenantal not ethnic); the **firstborn-for-firstborn** consummated (12:29 ← 4:23 "let my son go… or I kill your firstborn").
- **Guarded against:** reading Pharaoh's yield (12:31) as *repentance* when it is *collapse under the deepest loss* (and he still *grasps for a blessing*); missing the **chazaq wordplay** (the hardening-word turned to Egypt's expelling — the grip become a thrust); reading the plunder as *theft* when the verse makes it *God-given favor* granting a *request* (12:36); over-reading "not break a bone" (12:46) into NT typology (John 19:36) over the *household-bounded* rite; treating the mixed multitude / one-law as incidental when they *open the community* to the stranger (the covenantal, not ethnic, boundary — the chapter's quiet universal note).

## EXO-06-objections-signs · Exodus 4:1–17 · [the deliverer's reluctance and the signs against unbelief] — will they believe? Moses' objections, and God's provision then anger

**The passage.** "They will not **believe** (aman) me or **listen** (shama) to my voice" (4:1); the staff-serpent — "**catch** (chazaq) it by the tail" (4:4) — "that they may **believe** (aman)" (4:5); the leprous hand restored like his **flesh** (basar, 4:7); "if they will not **believe** (aman)… or **listen** (shama) to the first **sign** (ot), they may believe the latter" (4:8); "if they will not **believe** (aman) even these two **signs** (ot) or **listen** (shama)…" (4:9); "I am not eloquent" (4:10); "who **makes** man's mouth?" (4:11); "send someone else" (4:13); "the anger of the LORD was **kindled** (charah); Aaron… will be **glad** (samach) in his **heart** (lev)… I **know** (yada) he speaks well… he comes to **meet** (qara) you" (4:14); "this staff, with which you shall do the **signs** (ot)" (4:17).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **aman (believe) + shama (listen) — Moses' *projected unbelief* (4:1).** "they will not **believe** (aman) me or **listen** (shama) to my voice" *(stated)*. **aman** — *be faithful / trust / believe*; **shama** — *hear/heed*: the gem is that Moses' *first objection* is *about Israel's interior* — he *projects unbelief onto the people* ("they will not believe… the LORD did not appear to you"). Whether a true reading of the people or a *cover for his own reluctance*, the interior *raises the faith-question* as its opening resistance *(inferred: aman/shama = the projected unbelief — Moses doubting the people will trust)*.
- **chazaq (catch) — the *grip on the fled thing* (4:4).** "**catch** (chazaq) it by the tail" — he did, and it became a staff *(stated)* — chazaq, *seize/hold firm* (the *same root* that will become the hardening-word, 7:13ff — here in its *grip/hold* sense): the gem is *what is gripped* — Moses *ran from* the serpent (4:3), then is told to *seize* the very thing he fled; the interior *made to grasp its fear* and find it *become a staff* (authority) in the hand *(inferred: chazaq = the courage to grip the feared thing, which becomes authority)*.
- **aman (believe) — the sign's *purpose: faith* (4:5).** *aman distinct from 4:1:* "that they may **believe** (aman) that the LORD… has appeared to you" *(stated)* — where 4:1 was Moses' *doubt*, 4:5 is God's *stated aim* — the sign *given to produce faith*; the interior's *unbelief answered by evidence* *(inferred: aman = the faith the sign is given to produce)*.
- **basar (flesh) — the *restored* hand (4:7).** "it was restored like the rest of his **flesh** (basar)" *(stated)* — basar, *flesh*: the second sign's *reversal* — leprous-then-whole; the interior-adjacent note is *God's power over decay and restoration* shown *on Moses' own body* *(inferred: basar = the flesh restored, the sign of reversal)*.
- **aman (believe) + shama (listen) + ot (sign) — the *fallback* provision (4:8).** *aman/shama distinct from 4:1,5:* "if they will not **believe** (aman) or **listen** (shama) to the first **sign** (ot), they may believe the latter" *(stated)*. The gem: God *provides a second sign against the possibility of unbelief* — the interior's *doubt met with over-provision*; **ot** — the *sign* as witness; God *stacks evidence* for a resistant faith *(inferred: aman/shama/ot = the fallback sign for a persisting unbelief)*.
- **aman (believe) + shama (listen) + ot (signs) — the *final fallback* (4:9).** *aman/shama/ot distinct from 4:8:* "if they will not **believe** (aman) even these two **signs** (ot) or **listen** (shama), take Nile water… it will become blood" *(stated)* — the *third* provision: the gem is the *descending scale* — God gives *three* signs, each a fallback for the last, against the interior's unbelief; divine *patience over-supplying* what faith needs *(inferred: aman/shama/ot = the third sign, God's patient over-provision against unbelief)*.
- **(4:11 — "who makes man's mouth?"; the `'o'`/"desire" span is the particle, not read.)** Flagged: God's rhetorical question answers Moses' "not eloquent" — *the Maker of the mouth will be with it*; read as context (no valid inner-being span).
- **charah (kindled) — God's *anger* at the persistent refusal (4:14a).** "the anger of the LORD was **kindled** (charah) against Moses" *(stated)* — the gem: after patient provision (three signs, "I will be with your mouth"), Moses' *"send someone else"* (4:13) *crosses a line* — and God's anger *kindles*. **charah** — *burn/be incensed*: the divine interior *patient with objection but angered by refusal*; yet (the gem) the anger *still provides* (Aaron) rather than destroys — anger *that serves* *(inferred: charah = the divine anger at the deliverer's refusal — anger that still provides)*.
- **samach (glad) + lev (heart) — Aaron's *gladness of heart* (4:14b).** "Aaron… will be **glad** (samach) in his **heart** (lev) when he sees you" *(stated)*. **samach** — *rejoice*; **lev** — *heart*: the gem is the *one glad heart* in the passage — Aaron *rejoices in his heart* to meet his brother; a *lev* of *gladness* (set against the *hardened lev* to come, 7:13ff) — the heart as the seat of *joy* here *(inferred: samach/lev = the glad heart — joy at reunion, the heart's positive pole)*.
- **yada (know) + qara (meet) — God's *knowing* and the *meeting* provided (4:14c).** "I **know** (yada) he speaks well; he is coming to **meet** (qara) you" *(stated)*. **yada** — God *knows* Aaron's gift (the divine knowing that *provides the remedy*); **qara** — the *meeting* already underway (Aaron *coming*). The interior's objection *answered before it is finished* — God has *already sent* the help *(inferred: yada/qara = God's knowing provision, the helper already coming)*.
- **ot (signs) — the *staff of signs* entrusted (4:17).** "take this staff, with which you shall do the **signs** (ot)" *(stated)* — ot *distinct from 4:8,9:* the *staff* now the *instrument* of all the signs (→ the plagues); the interior *equipped* for the mission it resisted *(inferred: ot = the staff entrusted as the instrument of the signs)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **the faith-question raised (aman/shama 4:1):** Moses' opening resistance *projects unbelief onto Israel* — will they *believe*?
2. **signs stacked against unbelief (chazaq 4:4 → aman 4:5 → basar 4:7 → aman/shama/ot 4:8 → aman/shama/ot 4:9):** God answers by *making Moses grip his fear* (the serpent-staff), and *over-provides* — *three signs*, each a fallback — divine *patience supplying* what a resistant faith might need.
3. **objection into refusal, and kindled anger (4:10 "not eloquent" → 4:13 "send someone else" → charah 4:14a):** Moses' self-doubt *hardens into refusal*, and God's anger *kindles* — the line crossed from *objection* to *no*.
4. **the anger that provides (samach/lev 4:14b → yada/qara 4:14c → ot 4:17):** yet the anger *gives Aaron* — a *glad heart* already *coming to meet* him, *known* by God to speak well — and the *staff* is entrusted; the interior's refusal *met with provision, not destruction*.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** aman ×4 *distinguished* (4:1 Moses' *projected doubt* / 4:5 the sign's *purpose* / 4:8 the *fallback* / 4:9 the *final fallback* — God's over-provision against unbelief); shama ×3 (won't-listen / heed-first-sign / heed-your-voice); ot ×3 (the *witnessing* signs, and 4:17 the *staff* entrusted); **chazaq (the *grip on the fled serpent* — the root that becomes the hardening-word, here = seize/hold, fear turned to authority)**; basar (the *restored flesh*); **charah (God's *kindled anger* at the refusal — anger that still provides)**; **samach/lev (Aaron's *glad heart* — the heart's positive pole, vs the hardened lev to come)**; yada/qara (God's *knowing provision*, the helper already coming).
- **Whose interior (multi-interior):** **Moses** — reluctance *escalating* from projected-unbelief → self-doubt → refusal (a *resistance* God overcomes by provision, not judgment — contrast Pharaoh's hardening, overcome by wonders); **God** — *patient over-provision* then *kindled anger*, yet anger *that serves*; **Aaron** — *gladness of heart*; **Israel** — the *faith-question* raised (will they believe?).
- **Threads:** the **believe** question (aman 4:1,5,8,9 → 4:31 "the people believed"; 14:31 "believed the LORD"; the arc of Israel's faith); the **heart** motif opens here in its *positive* pole (samach/lev 4:14, the glad heart → the *hardened* lev of Pharaoh 7:13ff — the same organ, opposite operation); the **chazaq** root (grip 4:4 → harden 7:13ff — seize/make-firm, one root, two applications); the deliverer's *reluctance* (← 2:14 fear; 3:11,13 objections → overcome, Aaron given); the *staff* (ot 4:17 → the plague-signs).
- **Guarded against:** lumping the four aman, three shama, or three ot (doubt / purpose / fallback / final-fallback; and the *staff* distinct from the *signs*); reading a "desire" operation into the mis-tagged particle (4:11); equating Moses' reluctance with Pharaoh's hardening (both resist, but Moses' is *fear/inadequacy* met by *provision*, Pharaoh's is *defiance* met by *judgment* — read distinctly); importing the NT "Moses… by faith" (Heb 11) over the *reluctance and kindled anger* the passage narrates; missing that God's *anger* here *still gives* (Aaron) — the divine interior's anger *serves* the mission.

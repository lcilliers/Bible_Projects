## EXS-07-covenant-slaves-injury · Exodus 21:1–36 · [the love-choice of service; intent legislated; the worth of a life] — "I love my master", the willful cunning distinguished, and life for life

**The passage.** The **rules** (mishpat, 21:1); the Hebrew **serves** (avad) six years (21:2); "'I love my master… I will not go out free'" — his ear bored, **slave** (avad) forever (21:6); the daughter he has no **right** (mashal) to sell, having **broken faith** (bagad), is **redeemed** (padah) (21:8); dealt with as the **rule** (mishpat, 21:9) for a daughter; her **food** (she'er) not diminished (21:10); the **willful** (zid) attacker who kills by **cunning** (ormah) taken from the altar (21:14); men **quarrel** (riv, 21:18); men **strive** (natsah) and hit a pregnant woman (21:22); **life** (nephesh) for life (21:23); the slave whose eye is **destroyed** (shachat) goes free (21:26); the ox's owner **innocent** (naqi ×2, 21:28); **warned** (ud) but the ox not **kept** (shamar) in (21:29); a ransom for his **life** (nephesh, 21:30); the same **rule** (mishpat, 21:31); the **live** (chay) ox sold (21:35); the ox not **kept** (shamar) in (21:36).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **mishpat (rules) — the *statutes framing the covenant life* (21:1).** "'these are the **rules** (mishpat) you shall set before them'" *(stated)* — mishpat, *judgement/ordinance*: the *ordered justice* the covenant people are to live by — the interior-context of *life shaped by God's rules* *(inferred: mishpat = the statutes framing the covenant life)*.
- **avad (serve) — the *bounded service* of the Hebrew slave (21:2).** "'when you buy a Hebrew **slave** (avad), he shall serve six years and go out free'" *(stated)* — avad, *serve*: the gem is that *servitude is bounded* — even bondage has a *sabbath-year release*; the interior-context of *service that does not own a person forever* (← Israel's own bondage, freed) *(inferred: avad = the bounded service — bondage with a release)*.
- **avad (slave) — the *love-choice of permanent service* (21:6).** *avad distinct from 21:2:* "'if the slave says "I love my master… I will not go out free"… his master bores his ear… he shall be his **slave** (avad) forever'" *(stated)* — where 21:2 was *bounded* service, here service *chosen out of love* becomes *permanent*: the gem is *servitude transfigured by love* — the freed man *elects* to stay, his *love for master and family* binding him more than any law; the interior of *devotion chosen* — the pierced ear the *mark of a love that gives up freedom* *(inferred: avad = the love-choice of permanent service — servitude transfigured by love)*.
- **mashal (right) + bagad (broken faith) + padah (redeemed) — the *treachery remedied by redemption* (21:8).** "'if she does not please him… he has no **right** (mashal) to sell her, since he has **broken faith** (bagad); he shall let her be **redeemed** (padah)'" *(stated)*. **mashal** — *rule/authority*; **bagad** — *act treacherously/break faith*; **padah** — *ransom/redeem*: the gem is that *breaking faith forfeits authority* — the master who *deals treacherously* with the designated woman *loses his right over her* and she must be *redeemed*; the interior of *treachery in a covenant* named and *remedied* — *faithlessness answered by release* *(inferred: mashal/bagad/padah = the treachery that forfeits authority, remedied by redemption)*.
- **mishpat (rule) — the *daughter's dignity in the statute* (21:9).** *mishpat distinct from 21:1:* "'if he designates her for his son, he shall deal with her as the **rule** (mishpat) for a daughter'" *(stated)* — the *statute protecting her as a daughter, not chattel* — the interior-context of *the law dignifying the vulnerable* *(inferred: mishpat = the rule dignifying her as a daughter)*.
- **she'er (food) — the *provision not diminished* (21:10).** "'if he takes another wife, he shall not diminish her **food** (she'er), clothing, or marital rights'" *(stated)* — she'er, *flesh/food/sustenance*: the *sustenance guarded* even when supplanted — the interior-context of *the first wife's due protected* *(inferred: she'er = the provision that may not be diminished)*.
- **zid (willfully) + ormah (cunning) — the *willful cunning distinguished from accident* (21:14).** "'if a man **willfully** (zid) attacks another to kill by **cunning** (ormah), take him from my altar to die'" *(stated)*. **zid** — *presume/boil* (the *seething* will, ← Egypt's arrogance 18:11); **ormah** — *craftiness/premeditation*: the gem is *THE inner-being turn of the injury laws* — the law *reads the heart's intent*: *willful, boiling malice* executed by *cunning* (premeditation) is *murder* (no altar-refuge), where the *unintended* killing (God "let him fall into his hand", 21:13) finds *refuge*. The interior *itself judged* — *the boiling, scheming will* the thing punished, not merely the deed; *intent the measure of the crime* *(inferred: zid/ormah = the willful cunning distinguished from accident — the heart's intent the measure)*.
- **riv (quarrel) — the *quarrel that injures* (21:18).** "'when men **quarrel** (riv) and one strikes the other with a stone'" *(stated)* — riv, *contend* (← the quarreling at Meribah 17:2): the *dispute escalating to blows* — the interior-context of *anger spilling into injury* *(inferred: riv = the quarrel that injures)*.
- **natsah (strive) + nephesh (life) — the *striving, and life for life* (21:22–23).** "'when men **strive** (natsah) and hit a pregnant woman… if there is harm, **life** (nephesh) for life'" *(stated)*. **natsah** — *struggle/fight*; **nephesh** — *life/soul*: the gem is the *worth of a life* — the *striving* that harms a life met with *life for life* — the interior-lesson that *a nephesh is answered only by a nephesh*; the *incommensurable value of a life* set in the law *(inferred: natsah/nephesh = the striving, and the life-for-life worth of a nephesh)*.
- **shachat (destroys) — the *slave's body honored* (21:26).** "'when a man **destroys** (shachat) the eye of his slave, he shall let the slave go free'" *(stated)* — shachat, *ruin/destroy*: the gem is that *the slave's body has dignity* — *maiming the slave earns the slave's freedom*; the interior-lesson that *even the bondman's flesh is protected* — *cruelty forfeits ownership* *(inferred: shachat = the slave's body honored — maiming earns freedom)*.
- **basar (flesh) + naqi (innocent) — the *goring ox and the owner's innocence* (21:28).** "'when an ox gores a man to death… its **flesh** (basar) not eaten, but the owner **innocent** (naqi ×2)'" *(stated)*. **basar** — *flesh* (the ox's, ← the fleshpots 16:3, here mechanical); **naqi** — *innocent/clear* (twice — the owner *not liable* for the first, unforeseen goring): the interior-context of *innocence where there was no negligence* — *guilt attaches to knowledge, not mischance* *(inferred: basar/naqi = the owner innocent of the unforeseen goring)*.
- **ud (warned) + shamar (kept) — the *negligence that incurs blood-guilt* (21:29).** "'if the ox was accustomed to gore and its owner **warned** (ud) but did not **keep** (shamar) it in, and it kills… the owner shall die'" *(stated)*. **ud** — *warn/testify*; **shamar** — *keep/guard*: the gem is the *contrast with 21:28* — where the first goring left the owner *innocent*, the *warned-but-negligent* owner bears *blood-guilt*; the interior of *responsibility that follows knowledge* — *the warned who fails to guard is culpable* *(inferred: ud/shamar = the negligence after warning — culpability follows knowledge)*.
- **nephesh (life) — the *ransom for a life* (21:30).** *nephesh distinct from 21:23:* "'if a ransom is imposed, he shall give for the redemption of his **life** (nephesh) whatever is imposed'" *(stated)* — here the *forfeited life* may be *ransomed* — the interior-context of *a life redeemable by price* (where 21:23 was *life for life*, here a *ransom* substitutes) *(inferred: nephesh = the ransom for a forfeited life)*.
- **mishpat (rule) — the *same statute for son or daughter* (21:31).** *mishpat distinct from 21:1,9:* "'if it gores a son or daughter, according to this same **rule** (mishpat)'" *(stated)* — the *equal application* — the interior-context of *impartial law* *(inferred: mishpat = the same rule applied impartially)*.
- **chay (live) + shamar (kept) — the *restitution of the goring ox* (21:35–36).** *shamar distinct from 21:29:* "'they shall sell the **live** (chay) ox and share… if the ox was accustomed to gore and its owner did not **keep** (shamar) it in, he shall repay ox for ox'" *(stated)*. **chay** — *living* (the surviving ox; the *kinsfolk/community* glosses homonym artifacts); **shamar** — again *the negligence*: the interior-context of *fair restitution*, and *the negligent owner again culpable* (echoing 21:29) *(inferred: chay/shamar = the restitution, the negligent owner culpable)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **service bounded, then chosen (mishpat 21:1 → avad 21:2 → avad 21:6):** the *rules* frame the life; service is *bounded* (a release) — yet love can *transfigure it into permanent devotion* ("I love my master… I will not go out free"); the interior of *servitude chosen out of love*.
2. **the vulnerable protected (mashal/bagad/padah 21:8 → mishpat 21:9 → she'er 21:10 → shachat 21:26):** *treachery forfeits authority* (the faithless master loses his right, she is redeemed); the woman *dignified as a daughter*, her *provision guarded*; the *slave's body honored* (maiming earns freedom) — the law *bending toward the powerless*.
3. **intent, negligence, and the worth of a life (zid/ormah 21:14 → riv 21:18 → natsah/nephesh 21:22–23 → basar/naqi 21:28 → ud/shamar 21:29 → nephesh 21:30 → mishpat 21:31 → chay/shamar 21:35–36):** the law *reads the heart* — *willful cunning malice* is murder, *accident* finds refuge; *quarrels and strivings* that injure; *life for life* (the worth of a nephesh), a *ransom* where allowed; *innocence* where there was no negligence, but *culpability where the warned failed to guard*; the interior of *intent and responsibility legislated*.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** **mishpat ×3 (the *statutes* framing / the *rule* dignifying the daughter / the *same rule* applied impartially)**; **avad ×2 (the *bounded* service / the *love-choice* of permanent service)**; **mashal/bagad/padah (the *treachery* that forfeits authority, *remedied by redemption*)**; she'er (the *provision* not diminished); **zid/ormah (the *willful cunning* distinguished from accident — the heart's intent the measure)**; riv (the *quarrel* that injures); natsah (the *striving* that harms); **nephesh ×2 (the *life-for-life* worth / the *ransom* for a forfeited life)**; **shachat (the *slave's body honored* — maiming earns freedom)**; basar (the ox's *flesh*, mechanical); **naqi ×2 (the owner *innocent* of the unforeseen goring)**; **ud/shamar (the *negligence after warning* — culpability follows knowledge)**; **shamar (again the *negligent* owner, 21:36)**; chay (the *living* ox, restitution).
- **Whose interior (multi-interior):** **the slave** — the *love-choice* (devotion that elects permanent service); **the master** — *treachery* (bagad) vs faithfulness (the faithless forfeits his right); **the assailant** — *intent* (willful cunning vs accident — the heart judged); **the negligent owner** — *responsibility after warning* (culpability follows knowledge); and the *worth of a life* (nephesh — life answered by life). The two gems: the *love-choice of service* (avad 21:6) and *intent legislated* (zid/ormah 21:14).
- **Threads:** **the law reaching the heart** (zid/ormah 21:14 ← the coveting 20:17 — the *interior intent* the measure of the crime, not the deed alone → the cities of refuge, Num 35; "whoever hates his brother is a murderer", 1 Jn 3:15); **bondage bounded** (avad 21:2 ← Israel's own slavery freed — service with a *release*, mercy woven into the law); **the love that gives up freedom** (avad 21:6 — the pierced-ear devotion → the servant-of-the-Lord motif, Ps 40:6 "my ear you have opened"); **the worth of a nephesh** (21:23,30 ← "life for life" — the incommensurable value of a life); **treachery/faithfulness** (bagad 21:8 ← Jacob-Laban's broken faith; → Mal 2:14 "the wife of your covenant… you have been faithless" — the covenant-treachery motif).
- **Guarded against:** lumping the three mishpat (framing / dignifying / impartial), two avad (bounded / love-choice), two nephesh (life-for-life / ransom), two shamar-negligence (21:29 / 21:36); reading the injury laws as mere retribution over the *intent* they legislate (the willful cunning vs accident — the finding); mistaking the 'o "desire" mis-tags for an inner-being motif (they are the conjunction "or"); treating the slave-laws as endorsing slavery when the text *bounds* it (release, dignity, freedom-for-maiming) — the *mercy in the law* the finding; over-reading the pierced-ear into later servant-Christology over the *love-choice* the verse gives (though the resonance is real); flattening the goring-ox laws into property when they turn on *negligence after warning* (the interior of responsibility).

## CON-03-furniture-altar-court · Exodus 37:1–38:31 · [construction and inventory; no inner-being content] — the furniture, altar, and court made, and the metals counted

**The passage.** Bezalel makes the ark (**height** qomah 37:1, **pure** gold tahor 37:2, 37:6), the table (qomah 37:10, tahor 37:11, 37:16), the lampstand (tahor 37:17, 37:22, 37:23, 37:24), the incense altar (qomah 37:25, tahor 37:26), the **holy** (qodesh) anointing oil (tahor 37:29); the bronze altar (qomah 38:1), the court (qomah 38:18); the tally — the **service** (avodah) of the Levites (38:21), Oholiab the **designer** (chashav, 38:23), and the metals weighed by the **sanctuary** (qodesh) shekel (38:24, 38:25, 38:26, 38:27).

### The honest finding: no inner-being spans

This unit is **architecture and accounting**. Its spans are:

- **qomah (height)** 37:1, 37:10, 37:25, 38:1, 38:18 — the *dimensions* of the ark, table, incense altar, bronze altar, and court. Measurement.
- **tahor (pure)** 37:2, 37:6, 37:11, 37:16, 37:17, 37:22, 37:23, 37:24, 37:26, 37:29 — *"pure gold"*, the material spec throughout (ark, table, lampstand, incense altar, oil). A material-purity term, **not** an interior operation (as at 25:11 — the honest reading resists the easy allegory of "purity").
- **qodesh (holy / sanctuary)** 37:29 (the *holy* anointing oil), 38:24, 38:25, 38:26, 38:27 (the *sanctuary*-shekel by which the census metals were weighed) — designation terms (the holiness of the oil; the standard weight).
- **avodah (service)** 38:21 — the *service of the Levites* under Ithamar (the administrative oversight of the tally). Function.
- **chashav (designer)** 38:23 — Oholiab, the *engraver and designer* (the skill-thread enacted, ← 26:1; 31:4 — noted, construction).

### The one faint connective note (offered, not forced)

The metals tally (38:24–27) records that the **silver** came from the *census* — *"a beka a head… from everyone numbered"* — weighed by the **sanctuary shekel** (qodesh, 38:25–26), and this silver became the **bases** (foundations) of the tabernacle (38:27). That silver is the **atonement / ransom money** of 30:12–16 (*"a ransom for his life"*, each life counted and equal). So — a *quiet* observation, not an inner-being span here: **the ransom-for-a-life became the sanctuary's foundation** — the redeemed lives *materially undergirding* God's dwelling. The verse gives only the *tally*; the connection belongs to 30:12–16, where the interior finding was read. Noted for the record, not read into these verses.

### Note on the block
This is the *thin core* of the construction block — pure fulfilment-detail. That is the honest finding: **no inner-being content to capture here.** The meaning of the furniture (the ark, the mercy seat, the lampstand) is real theology, but it is *not carried by inner-being spans* in these making-and-counting verses, and forcing it would be over-reading. The genuine gems of Block 5 are elsewhere (the willing heart, CON-01/02; the glory filling the tent, CON-05).

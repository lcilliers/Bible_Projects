## EXS-04-jethro · Exodus 18:1–27 · [the outsider's worship; the inner qualifications of a just judge] — Jethro rejoices, and the God-fearing, trustworthy men who hate a bribe

**The passage.** Jethro comes; they **ask** (shaal) each other's **welfare** (shalom), **met** (qara) (18:7); Moses tells the **hardship** (telaah, 18:8); Jethro **rejoiced** (chadah) (18:9), "now I know the LORD is greater… they **dealt arrogantly** (zid)" (18:11); Moses **judges** (shaphat) alone (18:13), the people **inquire** (darash) of God (18:15), he **decides** (shaphat, 18:16); "not **good** (tov)… you will **wither** (navel)" (18:17, 18:18); "**obey** (shama) my voice; I will give **advice** (yaats)" (18:19); "**able** (chayil), God-**fearing** (yare), **trustworthy** (emet) men who **hate** (sane) a bribe… to **judge** (shaphat)" (18:21–22); "in **peace** (shalom)" (18:23); Moses **chose** (bachar) **able** (chayil) men (18:25); they **judged** (shaphat), the **hard** (qasheh) cases to Moses (18:26).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **shaal (asked) + shalom (welfare) + qara (met) — the *greeting of peace* (18:7).** "they **asked** (shaal) each other of their **welfare** (shalom) and went into the tent" *(stated)*. **shaal** — *ask after*; **shalom** — *well-being*; **qara** — *meet*: the *warm reunion* — kinsman-to-kinsman — the interior-context of *the courteous care* before the counsel *(inferred: shaal/shalom/qara = the greeting of welfare)*.
- **telaah (hardship) — the *hardship told* (18:8).** "Moses told… all the **hardship** (telaah) that had come upon them in the way" *(stated)* — telaah, *hardship/weariness*: the *road's toil recounted* — the interior-context of *the testimony of what was endured* *(inferred: telaah = the hardship recounted)*.
- **chadah (rejoiced) — the *outsider's joy* at another's deliverance (18:9).** "Jethro **rejoiced** (chadah) for all the good the LORD had done to Israel" *(stated)* — chadah, *rejoice*: the gem is that *a Midianite priest rejoices at Israel's deliverance* — *joy on another people's behalf*, not his own; the *outsider's interior gladdened* by the LORD's acts *(inferred: chadah = the outsider's joy at another's deliverance)*.
- **zid (dealt arrogantly) — the *arrogance that boiled over* into judgment (18:11).** "'now I know the LORD is greater than all gods, because in this affair they **dealt arrogantly** (zid) with the people'" *(stated)* — **zid**, lit. *to boil / seethe*: the gem is that *Egypt's arrogance* — *pride boiling over* against Israel — is named the *ground of the judgment*; the interior of *seething presumption* the LORD *proved greater than*. Jethro *reads the judgment's logic*: the arrogant *overreached and were overthrown* *(inferred: zid = the arrogance that boiled over — the ground of judgment, read by the outsider)*.
- **shaphat (judges) — the *judge who sits alone* (18:13).** "Moses sat to **judge** (shaphat) the people… from morning till evening" *(stated)* — shaphat, *judge*: the *solitary burden* — the interior-context of *the mediator carrying all the people's cases alone* *(inferred: shaphat = the judge sitting alone — the burden)*.
- **darash (inquire) — the *seeking of God* through the mediator (18:15).** "'the people come to me to **inquire** (darash) of God'" *(stated)* — darash, *seek/inquire*: the gem is that *the people seek God through Moses* — the interior of *a people bringing their cases to God* (the mediator the channel of the seeking) *(inferred: darash = the seeking of God through the mediator)*.
- **shaphat (decide) — the *deciding and teaching* (18:16).** *shaphat distinct from 18:13:* "'I **decide** (shaphat) between one and another and make them know the statutes'" *(stated)* — here shaphat is *the act of ruling between parties* *and teaching the statutes* (not merely sitting): the interior-context of *judgment that also instructs* *(inferred: shaphat = the deciding-and-teaching)*.
- **tov (good) + navel (wither) — the *not-good burden that withers* (18:17, 18:18).** "'what you are doing is not **good** (tov)… you will **wither** (navel)'" *(stated)*. **tov** — *good*; **navel** — *wither/wear out* (a *fading leaf*): the gem is the *diagnosis* — the *solitary judging* is *not good* and *will wither* both Moses and the people; the interior *worn out by an unshared burden* — *wisdom names what will exhaust* *(inferred: tov/navel = the not-good burden that withers — the unshared load)*.
- **shama (obey) + yaats (advice) — the *counsel offered* (18:19).** "'now **obey** (shama) my voice; I will give you **advice** (yaats)'" *(stated)*. **shama** — *listen/obey*; **yaats** — *counsel*: the gem is *the wise man's counsel* — Jethro *advises*, and the very word *shama* ("obey/listen") will define the mediator's own posture (18:24); the interior-context of *counsel given to be heeded* *(inferred: shama/yaats = the counsel offered to be heeded)*.
- **chayil (able) + yare (fearing) + emet (trustworthy) + sane (hate) — the *inner qualifications of the judge* (18:21).** "'look for **able** (chayil) men who **fear** (yare) God, who are **trustworthy** (emet) and **hate** (sane) a bribe'" *(stated)*. **chayil** — *capacity/strength*; **yare** — *fear (of God)*; **emet** — *truth/faithfulness*; **sane** — *hate*: the gem is that *the fitness to judge is INNER* — not skill alone (chayil) but *the fear of God*, *faithfulness/truth*, and a *hatred of the bribe* (an *interior revulsion at corruption*); the *just judge defined by his heart* — fearing God, true, and hating the bribe *(inferred: chayil/yare/emet/sane = the inner qualifications of the judge — capacity, fear of God, faithfulness, hatred of corruption)*.
- **shaphat (judge) — the *shared judging at all times* (18:22).** *shaphat distinct from 18:13,16:* "'let them **judge** (shaphat) the people at all times; the great matters to you'" *(stated)* — here shaphat is *the delegated, continual judging* (the burden *distributed*): the interior-context of *the load shared* *(inferred: shaphat = the shared, continual judging)*.
- **shalom (peace) — the *peace of the shared burden* (18:23).** *shalom distinct from 18:7:* "'this people also will go to their place in **peace** (shalom)'" *(stated)* — where 18:7 was a *greeting*, here shalom is the *fruit* — *the people go home whole* when the burden is shared; the gem: *right ordering yields peace* — for both the judge (who endures) and the people (who go in peace) *(inferred: shalom = the peace that is the fruit of the shared burden)*.
- **bachar (chose) + chayil (able) — the *choosing enacted* (18:25).** *chayil distinct from 18:21:* "Moses **chose** (bachar) **able** (chayil) men out of all Israel" *(stated)* — where 18:21 *specified* the qualification, 18:25 *enacts* it — the gem: *Moses heeds the counsel* — *chooses* the able men (the shama of 18:24 *obeyed*); the interior of *the mediator who takes advice and acts on it* *(inferred: bachar/chayil = the choosing enacted — counsel heeded)*.
- **shaphat (judged) + qasheh (hard) — the *ordered judging* (18:26).** *shaphat distinct from 18:13,16,22:* "they **judged** (shaphat) at all times; the **hard** (qasheh) cases they brought to Moses" *(stated)*. **shaphat** — the *system now working*; **qasheh** — *hard/severe*: the gem is the *result* — *judging distributed*, only the *hard* cases escalated — the interior-context of *the wise order functioning* *(inferred: shaphat/qasheh = the ordered judging — the hard cases escalated)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **the outsider brought to worship (shaal/shalom/qara 18:7 → telaah 18:8 → chadah 18:9 → zid 18:11):** the kinsman *greeted*, the *hardship told*, and the Midianite priest *rejoices* at Israel's deliverance and *confesses the LORD greater* — *reading the judgment's logic* (Egypt's *arrogance boiled over*); an *outside interior brought to joy and confession*.
2. **the burden that withers (shaphat 18:13 → darash 18:15 → shaphat 18:16 → tov/navel 18:17, 18:18):** the mediator *judges alone*, the people *seek God* through him — but the solitary load is *not good* and *will wither*; *wisdom names the exhaustion*.
3. **the counsel heeded, the judge's heart (shama/yaats 18:19 → chayil/yare/emet/sane 18:21 → shaphat 18:22 → shalom 18:23 → bachar/chayil 18:25 → shaphat/qasheh 18:26):** the *counsel given*, the *inner qualifications* of the judge named (*fear of God, faithfulness, hatred of the bribe* — not skill alone), the burden *shared*, the people go in *peace*; and *Moses heeds* — *chooses* the able men — the ordered judging *works*.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** shaal/shalom/qara (the *greeting of welfare*); telaah (the *hardship told*); **chadah (the *outsider's joy* at another people's deliverance)**; **zid (the *arrogance that boiled over* — Egypt's pride the ground of judgment)**; **shaphat ×4 (the *solitary judge* / the *deciding-and-teaching* / the *shared continual judging* / the *ordered judging*, hard cases escalated)**; darash (the *seeking of God* through the mediator); **tov/navel (the *not-good burden that withers* — the unshared load)**; shama/yaats (the *counsel offered to be heeded*); **chayil/yare/emet/sane (the *inner qualifications* — capacity, fear of God, faithfulness, hatred of the bribe)**; **shalom ×2 (the *greeting* / the *peace* that is the fruit of the shared burden)**; **chayil ×2 (the qualification *specified* / the men *chosen*)**; bachar (the *choosing enacted* — counsel heeded); qasheh (the *hard* cases escalated).
- **Whose interior (multi-interior):** **Jethro** — the *outsider brought to joy* (rejoicing at another's deliverance), *confession* ("the LORD is greater"), and *wise counsel*; **Moses** — the *over-burdened judge* who *listens and obeys* his father-in-law (the mediator's humility — takes advice); **the judges** — the *inner qualifications* (fear of God, faithfulness, hatred of the bribe); **Egypt** — *arrogance* (zid) the ground of its overthrow. The *outsider's worship* and the *judge's heart* are the two gems.
- **Threads:** **the fear-of-God as fitness** (yare 18:21 → the fear that keeps from sin, 20:20; the God-fearing midwives 1:17 — fear-of-God the ground of just action); **hatred of the bribe** (sane 18:21 → "the bribe subverts", 23:8 — the *interior revulsion at corruption*); **the outsider's confession** (chadah/zid 18:9,11 ← Rahab, Naaman — Gentiles brought to the LORD; the arrogant judged, cf. Pharaoh's hardening); **the mediator's humility** (shama 18:24 — Moses *takes counsel*, unlike Pharaoh who would not hear); **arrogance judged** (zid 18:11 ← Pharaoh "who is the LORD?" 5:2 — pride boiling over, overthrown).
- **Guarded against:** lumping the four shaphat (solitary / deciding-teaching / shared / ordered), the two shalom (greeting / fruit-peace), the two chayil (specified / chosen); reading the judge-qualifications as mere administration over the *inner character* the text names (fear of God, faithfulness, hatred of the bribe — the finding); flattening Jethro into a foil when his *rejoicing and confession* are a genuine outsider-interior; over-reading zid ("boil") into a full theology of pride over the *arrogance-as-ground-of-judgment* the verse gives; missing that Moses *obeys* his father-in-law (the mediator's teachability — a virtue set against Pharaoh's deafness).

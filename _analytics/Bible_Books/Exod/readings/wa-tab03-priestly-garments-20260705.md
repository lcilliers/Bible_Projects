## TAB-03-priestly-garments · Exodus 28:1–43 · [the people borne on the heart; the guilt borne for acceptance; skill as a filling] — the breastpiece of judgment, and "Aaron shall bear their guilt"

**The passage.** Holy (qodesh) garments "for **glory** (kavod) and beauty" (28:2); made by the **wise-hearted** (chakham + lev) whom God *filled with a spirit of wisdom*, to **consecrate** (qadash) him (28:3); the ephod **skilfully worked** (chashav, 28:6); the breastpiece of **judgment** (mishpat), in **skilled** (chashav) work (28:15); Aaron bears the names on his **heart** (lev) in the breastpiece of **judgment** (mishpat, 28:29); the Urim and Thummim on his **heart**, bearing the **judgment** (mishpat) of Israel on his **heart** (lev, 28:30); the plate "**Holy** (qodesh) to the LORD" (28:36); Aaron bears the **guilt** (avon) of the holy things that they may be **accepted** (ratson, 28:38); garments "for **glory** (kavod) and beauty" (28:40); anoint and **ordain** (qadash, 28:41); the linen over naked **flesh** (basar, 28:42); lest they bear **guilt** (avon) and die (28:43).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **kavod (glory) + qodesh (holy) — the *garments for glory and beauty* (28:2).** "'you shall make **holy** (qodesh) garments for Aaron, for **glory** (kavod) and for beauty'" *(stated)*. **kavod** — *glory/weight*; **qodesh** — *holy*: the gem, modest but real, is that *the mediator is clothed in dignity* — the priestly office *given a visible glory and beauty*; the interior-context of *the honor of standing before God made outwardly weighty* — not vanity but *the dignity of the office* *(inferred: kavod/qodesh = the garments for glory and beauty — the mediator clothed in dignity)*.
- **chakham (wise) + lev (heart) + qadash (consecrate) — *skill as a filling* (28:3).** "'speak to all the **wise-hearted** (chakham + lev), whom I have **filled with a spirit of wisdom**, that they make Aaron's garments to **consecrate** (qadash) him'" *(stated)*. **chakham** — *wise/skilled*; **lev** — *heart* (the seat of the skill — *wise-hearted*); **qadash** — *consecrate*: the gem is that *skill is a gift, a filling* — the craftsmen are *wise-hearted* because God *filled them with a spirit of wisdom*; the interior of *ability received, not merely trained* — *the making of holy things done by a heart God has filled*. (The first clear statement of the skill-as-Spirit-gift thread; full at 31:3.) *(inferred: chakham/lev/qadash = skill as a filling — the wise-hearted whom God filled to make holy things)*.
- **chashav (skilfully worked) — the *ephod's design* (28:6).** "'they shall make the ephod… **skilfully worked** (chashav)'" *(stated)* — chashav, *devise/design*: the *designing skill* enacted (← 26:1) — the interior-context of *the craftsman's conceived work* *(inferred: chashav = the ephod skilfully devised)*.
- **chashav (skilled) + mishpat (judgment) — the *breastpiece of judgment* (28:15).** *chashav distinct from 28:6:* "'you shall make a breastpiece of **judgment** (mishpat), in **skilled** (chashav) work'" *(stated)*. **chashav** — again *skilled design*; **mishpat** — *judgment*: the gem is the *name* — a *breastpiece of judgment* — the mediator's chest-piece *is judgment*: it will *carry the people's cause/decisions* before God (unfolded in 28:29–30); the interior-context of *the object that bears judgment* *(inferred: chashav/mishpat = the breastpiece of judgment — skilled work that will bear the people's cause)*.
- **lev (heart) + mishpat (judgment) — the *names borne on the heart* (28:29).** *lev/mishpat distinct from 28:15:* "'Aaron shall bear the names of the sons of Israel in the breastpiece of **judgment** (mishpat) on his **heart** (lev)… to bring them to regular remembrance before the LORD'" *(stated)*. **lev** — *heart*; **mishpat** — *judgment*: the gem is *the mediator carries the people on his heart* — their *names* (each tribe) held *on his heart* into God's presence, *for remembrance*; the interior of *representative love* — *the people borne, named, remembered, over the priest's heart* *(inferred: lev/mishpat = the names borne on the heart — the mediator carrying the people for remembrance)*.
- **lev (heart) + mishpat (judgment) — the *judgment of Israel borne on the heart* (28:30).** *lev/mishpat distinct from 28:29:* "'in the breastpiece of **judgment** (mishpat) you shall put the Urim and Thummim, and they shall be on Aaron's **heart** (lev)… Aaron shall bear the **judgment** (mishpat) of the people of Israel on his **heart** before the LORD regularly'" *(stated)*. Where 28:29 bore the *names* (remembrance), 28:30 bears the *judgment* (the *decision/cause* — the Urim and Thummim by which God's *verdict* is sought): the gem is that *the mediator carries not only who they are but their case* — *the people's judgment held on his heart before God*; the interior of *bearing the people's decisions/cause into the presence* — *representation deepened from remembrance to advocacy* *(inferred: lev/mishpat = the judgment of Israel borne on the heart — the mediator bearing the people's cause before God)*.
- **qodesh (Holy) — the *plate "Holy to the LORD"* (28:36).** "'engrave on the plate, "**Holy** (qodesh) to the LORD"'" *(stated)* — the mediator's forehead *marked as belonging wholly to God* — the interior-context of *consecrated identity worn openly* *(inferred: qodesh = the plate "Holy to the LORD" — consecrated identity worn)*.
- **avon (guilt) + ratson (accepted) + qodesh (holy things) + qadash (consecrate) — *the guilt borne for acceptance* (28:38).** "'Aaron shall bear any **guilt** (avon) from the **holy things** (qodesh) that Israel **consecrates** (qadash)… that they may be **accepted** (ratson) before the LORD'" *(stated)*. **avon** — *iniquity/guilt*; **ratson** — *acceptance/favor*; **qadash/qodesh** — *holy/consecrate*: the gem is *mediatorial guilt-bearing* — even the people's *holy gifts* carry *guilt* (imperfection), and *Aaron bears it* so that the gifts are *accepted*; the interior of *the mediator absorbing guilt so that the people find favor* — *bearing what would bar them, that they may be received* *(inferred: avon/ratson = the guilt borne for acceptance — the mediator absorbing guilt so the people are received)*.
- **kavod (glory) — the *sons' garments for glory and beauty* (28:40).** *kavod distinct from 28:2:* "'for Aaron's sons you shall make coats… for **glory** (kavod) and beauty'" *(stated)* — the *dignity extended to the sons* — the interior-context of *the whole priestly line clothed in honor* *(inferred: kavod = the sons' garments for glory and beauty)*.
- **qadash (ordain) — the *ordination* (28:41).** *qadash distinct from 28:3,38:* "'anoint them and **ordain** (qadash) them, that they may serve me as priests'" *(stated)* — here qadash is the *setting-apart of the persons* (where 28:3 consecrated *him* via the garments, 28:41 ordains *them*): the interior-context of *the persons made holy for service* *(inferred: qadash = the ordination — the persons set apart for service)*.
- **basar (flesh) — the *nakedness covered* (28:42).** "'linen undergarments to cover their naked **flesh** (basar)'" *(stated)* — basar, *flesh*: the *bodily nakedness covered* for approach — the interior-context of *the shame of exposure guarded in the holy place* (a modesty/reverence note; the verse states the covering) *(inferred: basar = the naked flesh covered for approach)*.
- **avon (guilt) — the *priests' own peril* (28:43).** *avon distinct from 28:38:* "'lest they bear **guilt** (avon) and die'" *(stated)* — where 28:38 was *bearing the people's* guilt, 28:43 is *the priests' own* guilt if they approach wrongly: the gem is *the danger of the holy* — the mediator who bears others' guilt is himself *at peril* before the holy; the interior-context of *the weight and risk of nearness* *(inferred: avon = the priests' own peril — guilt-and-death if the holy is approached wrongly)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **the mediator clothed and skilled (kavod/qodesh 28:2 → chakham/lev/qadash 28:3 → chashav 28:6):** the priest *clothed in glory and beauty*, his garments made by the *wise-hearted whom God filled with a spirit of wisdom* — *skill as a gift* — to *consecrate* him; the interior of *dignity conferred and ability given*.
2. **the people borne on the heart (chashav/mishpat 28:15 → lev/mishpat 28:29 → lev/mishpat 28:30):** the *breastpiece of judgment* carries the people *on the mediator's heart* — their *names* (remembrance) and their *judgment/cause* (advocacy, the Urim and Thummim) — *held before the LORD*; the interior of *representative love — the people borne, named, and pleaded for, over the heart*.
3. **the guilt borne for acceptance (qodesh 28:36 → avon/ratson 28:38 → kavod 28:40 → qadash 28:41 → basar 28:42 → avon 28:43):** the mediator *marked "Holy to the LORD"*, *bearing the guilt* of even the holy gifts *so the people are accepted*; the line *clothed in glory*, *ordained*, *covered* — yet *himself at peril* (guilt-and-death) before the holy; the interior of *the mediator who absorbs guilt and stands in danger, that the people may find favor*.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** **kavod ×2 (the mediator's / the sons' *garments for glory and beauty*)**; qodesh (holy garments 28:2, 28:4 / "Holy to the LORD" 28:36 / holy things 28:38); **chakham/lev + chashav (skill as a *filling* — the wise-hearted whom God filled; the skilful design)**; **mishpat ×3 (the *breastpiece of judgment* named / the *names* borne in it / the *judgment/cause* of Israel borne)**; **lev ×3 (the *wise-heart* seat of skill / the *names* on the heart / the *judgment* on the heart)**; **avon ×2 (the *people's* guilt borne for acceptance / the *priests' own* peril)**; **ratson (the *acceptance* the guilt-bearing secures)**; **qadash ×3 (consecrate *him* via garments / the *gifts* consecrated / *ordain* the persons)**; tahor (pure gold — construction, 28:14, 28:22, 28:36); basar (the naked *flesh* covered). The *holy garments* designation (qodesh 28:4) and *pure gold* chains (tahor 28:14, 28:22) are making-terms, noted not read.
- **Whose interior (multi-interior):** **Aaron the mediator** — *clothed in glory*, *bearing the people on his heart* (names + judgment), *bearing their guilt* so they are accepted, yet *himself at peril* before the holy; **the wise-hearted craftsmen** — *skill as a filling* (a spirit of wisdom given); **the people** — *borne, remembered, pleaded for, accepted*. The two gems: **the people borne on the heart** (representative love) and **the guilt borne for acceptance** (mediatorial guilt-bearing).
- **Threads:** **the people on the mediator's heart** (lev/mishpat 28:29–30 → the high priest as intercessor, Heb 7:25; the names *engraved* and *carried* — representation → "I have you in my heart", Phil 1:7); **skill as Spirit-gift** (chakham/lev 28:3 → Bezalel filled with the Spirit 31:3; 35:31 — ability as a *filling*, not just craft); **mediatorial guilt-bearing** (avon 28:38 → the scapegoat bearing iniquity Lev 16:22; "he bore the sin of many" Isa 53:12 — the priest absorbing guilt for acceptance); **the peril of the holy** (avon 28:43 ← the boundary that kills 19:12; Nadab and Abihu Lev 10 — nearness is dangerous); **glory and beauty** (kavod 28:2,40 — the dignity of standing before God, not vanity).
- **Guarded against:** forcing the *stones, gold, and linen* into meaning when they are construction (tahor "pure gold" noted, not read); over-reading the *Urim and Thummim* into a full oracle-theology over the *bearing of the people's judgment on the heart* the verse states; importing NT high-priest Christology (Hebrews) as the *meaning* of the chapter when the text gives the *garments and the bearing* — the resonance is real but the finding is what Aaron *does* (bears names, judgment, guilt); collapsing the three mishpat or three lev into one when they move from *object* → *remembrance* → *advocacy*; reading the guilt-bearing (28:38) as mere ritual technicality when the text ties it to *acceptance* (ratson) — the mediator *absorbs guilt so the people are received*, the genuine finding.

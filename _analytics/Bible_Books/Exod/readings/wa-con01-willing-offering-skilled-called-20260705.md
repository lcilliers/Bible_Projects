# Exodus 35:1–35 — inner-being passage reading (CON-01-willing-offering-skilled-called)

- **Source:** verse-first lexicals over the passage (provenance `exodus-construction-v1-20260705`). Within a thin block, **this chapter genuinely carries content** — the *willing-heart* offering enacted (the payout of 25:2) and Bezalel's *Spirit-skill* extended to *teaching* (the payout of 31:3). **Multiple interiors**: **Israel** (the willing, generous hearts; the wise-hearted makers), **Bezalel/Oholiab** (skill filled and given to teach), **God** (who calls, fills, and inspires to teach). Construction lists (35:11–19) read as scene-context.

## CON-01-willing-offering-skilled-called · Exodus 35:1–35 · [the willing heart enacted; skill given to teach] — "everyone whose heart stirred him", and "he has put in his heart to teach"

**The passage.** The **holy** (qodesh) sabbath (35:2); "whoever is of a **generous heart** (lev + nadiv), let him bring" (35:5); every **wise-hearted** (chakham + lev) craftsman (35:10); "everyone whose **heart** (lev) stirred him and spirit **moved** (nadav) him brought" for the **holy** (qodesh) garments and the **service** (avodah, 35:21); the **willing-hearted** (lev + nadiv, 35:22); who could **contribute** (rum), for the **work** (avodah, 35:24); every **wise-hearted** (chakham + lev) woman spun (35:25); whose **hearts** (lev) stirred them (35:26); whose **heart** (lev) **moved** (nadav) them, a freewill offering (35:29); Bezalel **called** (qara) by name (35:30), filled with the Spirit — **understanding** (tevunah), **knowledge** (daat, 35:31), to **devise** (chashav) **designs** (machashavah, 35:32, 35:33); "he has put in his **heart** (lev) to teach" (35:34); filled them, the **designers** (chashav + machashavah + lev, 35:35).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **qodesh (holy) — the *sabbath first* (35:2).** "'six days work, but the seventh a **holy** (qodesh) sabbath'" *(stated)* — qodesh, *holy*: before the building begins, *the sabbath is set first* — the interior-context of *even the holy work bounded by the holy rest* (the making does not override the sabbath) *(inferred: qodesh = the sabbath set first, before the work)*.
- **lev (heart) + nadiv (generous) — the *generous heart called out* (35:5).** "'whoever is of a **generous heart** (lev + nadiv), let him bring the LORD's contribution'" *(stated)*. **lev** — *heart*; **nadiv** — *noble/willing/generous*: the gem is *the offering keyed to the heart's generosity* — *not levied but drawn from the willing* (← 25:2, now *enacted*); the interior of *voluntary, open-handed giving* — *the generous heart the qualification* *(inferred: lev/nadiv = the generous heart called out — the offering drawn from willingness)*.
- **chakham (wise) + lev (heart) — the *wise-hearted maker* (35:10).** "'let every **wise-hearted** (chakham + lev) craftsman come and make'" *(stated)*. **chakham** — *wise/skilled*; **lev** — *heart*: the gem is *skill seated in the heart* — the *wise-hearted* (skill as an *interior* gift) summoned to make; the interior-context of *ability as a matter of the heart* *(inferred: chakham/lev = the wise-hearted maker — skill seated in the heart)*.
- **lev (heart) + nadav (moved) + qodesh (holy) + avodah (service) — the *stirred and moved bring* (35:21).** "'everyone whose **heart** (lev) stirred him and whose spirit **moved** (nadav) him brought the contribution… for the **service** (avodah) and the **holy** (qodesh) garments'" *(stated)*. **nadav** — *be willing / offer freely*; **avodah** — *service*: the gem is *the interior impulse enacted* — *the heart stirred, the spirit moved* — the giving *welling up from within*, not commanded; the interior of *the moved heart that gives to the holy service* *(inferred: lev/nadav = the stirred and moved bring — the interior impulse to give, enacted)*.
- **lev (heart) + nadiv (willing) — the *willing-hearted, men and women* (35:22).** *lev/nadiv distinct from 35:5:* "'both men and women, all who were of a **willing heart** (lev + nadiv), brought'" *(stated)*: the gem is *the breadth of the willing* — *men and women alike*, stripping their own jewelry; the interior-context of *a whole people's willing generosity* (contrast the same gold given to the *calf*, 32:3 — now given to *God's dwelling*) *(inferred: lev/nadiv = the willing-hearted, men and women — the whole people's generosity)*.
- **rum (contribute) + avodah (work) — the *lifted contribution* (35:24).** "'everyone who could **contribute** (rum) silver or bronze… for any use in the **work** (avodah) brought it'" *(stated)*. **rum** — *lift/raise* (the heave-contribution); **avodah** — *work*: the *offering lifted up for the work* — the interior-context of *each giving what he had for the making* *(inferred: rum/avodah = the lifted contribution for the work)*.
- **chakham (wise) + lev (heart) — the *wise-hearted women spin* (35:25).** *chakham/lev distinct from 35:10:* "'every **wise-hearted** (chakham + lev) woman spun with her hands'" *(stated)*: the gem is *the women's skill named* — *wise-hearted women* spinning the yarn (the *skill* of the ordinary craftswoman honored as a heart-gift); the interior-context of *the whole community's gifted skill*, women included *(inferred: chakham/lev = the wise-hearted women — skill honored across the community)*.
- **lev (hearts) — the *hearts stirred to skill* (35:26).** *lev distinct:* "'all the women whose **hearts** (lev) stirred them to use their skill spun the goats' hair'" *(stated)*: the gem is *skill joined to the stirred heart* — *not only the willing to give but the willing to make*; the interior of *the heart moved to labor its gift* *(inferred: lev = the hearts stirred to skill — the willing to make, not only to give)*.
- **lev (heart) + nadav (moved) — the *freewill offering summed* (35:29).** *lev/nadav distinct from 35:21:* "'all whose **heart** (lev) **moved** (nadav) them brought a freewill offering to the LORD'" *(stated)*: the gem is *the summary of the willing* — the whole giving named a *freewill offering* *of the moved heart*; the interior-context of *the freely-given, the payout of 25:2 complete* *(inferred: lev/nadav = the freewill offering summed — the willing giving, complete)*.
- **qara (called) + tevunah (understanding) + daat (knowledge) + chashav (devise) + machashavah (designs) — *Bezalel called and filled* (35:30–33).** "'the LORD has **called** (qara) Bezalel by name and filled him with the Spirit — skill, **understanding** (tevunah), **knowledge** (daat) — to **devise** (chashav) **designs** (machashavah)'" *(stated)*: the gem (← 31:2–4, restated to the people) — *skill as a Spirit-filling*: *called, filled, gifted with understanding and knowledge and the devising-power* — *the interior gifted for making* *(inferred: qara/tevunah/daat/chashav = Bezalel called and filled — skill a Spirit-gift)*.
- **lev (heart) + chashav (designer) + machashavah — *skill given to teach* (35:34–35).** "'he has put in his **heart** (lev) to **teach**, both him and Oholiab… filled them with skill to do every work of the **designer** (chashav + machashavah)'" *(stated)*: the gem, the *new note beyond ch31* — *the Spirit-skill is not hoarded but given to TEACH*: God *put teaching in his heart*; the interior of *gifted ability meant to be imparted* — *skill multiplied by teaching others* — *the gift that reproduces itself* *(inferred: lev/chashav = skill given to teach — the Spirit-gift meant to be imparted, not hoarded)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **the willing heart enacted (qodesh 35:2 → lev/nadiv 35:5 → lev/nadav/qodesh/avodah 35:21 → lev/nadiv 35:22 → rum/avodah 35:24 → lev/nadav 35:29):** the sabbath set first; then *the generous, stirred, moved hearts bring* — men and women, freely — *the willing-heart of 25:2 now enacted*; the *same gold once given to the calf* now *given to God's dwelling*.
2. **skill as a heart-gift, given to teach (chakham/lev 35:10 → chakham/lev 35:25 → lev 35:26 → qara/tevunah/daat/chashav 35:30–33 → lev/chashav 35:34–35):** the *wise-hearted* (men and women) make; Bezalel *called and filled with the Spirit* for the *devising* — and, the new note, *given to teach* — *the interior gift meant to be imparted and multiplied.*

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** qodesh (the *sabbath* set first / the *holy* garments); **lev + nadiv/nadav (the *willing/generous heart* enacted — stirred, moved, freely giving; men and women — the payout of 25:2)**; **chakham + lev (the *wise-hearted maker* — skill a heart-gift, men and women)**; avodah (the *service/work*); rum (the *lifted* contribution); **qara/tevunah/daat/chashav/machashavah (Bezalel *called and filled* — skill a Spirit-gift)**; **lev (skill *given to teach* — the gift imparted, not hoarded)**.
- **Whose interior (multi-interior):** **Israel** — the *willing, generous hearts* (voluntary giving welling up from within) and the *wise-hearted makers* (skill as a heart-gift, women included); **Bezalel/Oholiab** — *Spirit-filled for making and for teaching*; **God** — who *calls, fills, and inspires to teach*. The two gems: **the willing heart enacted** and **skill given to teach**.
- **Threads:** **the willing heart** (nadav/nadiv 35:5,21,29 ← 25:2 "whose heart moves him" — the *payout*, the offering *drawn from willingness* → the restraint 36:5–6; "God loves a cheerful giver" 2 Cor 9:7); **the redeemed gold** (35:22 ← the calf's gold 32:3 — the *same jewelry* now given to *God's dwelling* — the interior's offering *redirected* from the idol to the LORD); **skill as Spirit-gift, extended to teach** (35:31,34 ← 31:3; 28:3 — the *first* Spirit-filling for *making*, now *for teaching* — the gift that *reproduces*); **the devising heart** (machashavah 35:32 ← Gen 6:5's "evil devising" — the scheming faculty *turned to beauty*); **skill honored across the community** (35:25–26 — the *wise-hearted women* — the gift not confined).
- **Guarded against:** over-reading the construction lists (35:11–19) into meaning; flattening the many *lev* into one when they move between *willing-to-give*, *wise-to-make*, and *moved-to-teach*; forcing the *sabbath* note (35:2) beyond its placement (the holy rest bounding the holy work); not inflating *rum* ("contribute") or *avodah* ("service/work") beyond their plain sense; keeping the *skill-to-teach* (35:34) as the genuine *new* note it is (beyond ch31) without importing a full pedagogy — the finding is that *the gift is given to be imparted*.

## GLD-02-wrath-first-intercession · Exodus 32:7–14 · [the burning anger; the stiff-necked verdict; the intercessor turns it back; God relents] — "let me alone… why does your wrath burn… and the LORD relented"

**The passage.** "Your people have **corrupted** (shachat) themselves" (32:7); "**turned aside** (sur) quickly" (32:8); "a **stiff-necked** (qasheh) people" (32:9); "**let me alone** (nuach), that my wrath may **burn** (charah) and consume them, and I will make of you a great nation" (32:10); Moses: "why does your wrath **burn** (charah)… whom you brought out with great **power** (koach)?" (32:11); "turn from your **burning anger** (charon)… **relent** (nacham) from this **harm** (raah)… lest the Egyptians say you meant **evil** (raah)" (32:12); "**remember** (zakhar) Abraham, Isaac, Israel… I will **multiply** (ravah)… and **give** (natan) this land" (32:13); "the LORD **relented** (nacham) from the **harm** (raah)" (32:14).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **shachat (corrupted) + sur (turned aside) — the *breach named* (32:7–8).** "'your people have **corrupted** (shachat) themselves; they have **turned aside** (sur) quickly'" *(stated)*. **shachat** — *ruin/corrupt*; **sur** — *turn aside*: the gem is *the swiftness of the fall* — *"quickly"* turned from the way; the interior of *a people who ruin themselves and swerve off the path* almost as soon as they vowed to keep it *(inferred: shachat/sur = the breach named — the swift corruption and swerving)*.
- **qasheh (stiff-necked) — the *divine verdict on the interior* (32:9).** "'I have seen this people, and it is a **stiff-necked** (qasheh) people'" *(stated)* — qasheh, *hard/stiff*: the gem is *God's diagnosis of the interior* — *stiff-necked* (the ox that will not turn to the yoke): *obstinate, unbending*; the interior-characteristic that will define Israel through the crisis (33:3, 5; 34:9) — *the hard neck that resists the LORD's guiding* *(inferred: qasheh = the stiff-necked verdict — the obstinate, unbending interior God names)*.
- **nuach (let me alone) + charah (burn) — the *wrath, and the room left for the mediator* (32:10).** "'**let me alone** (nuach), that my wrath may **burn** (charah) and consume them, and I will make of you a great nation'" *(stated)*. **nuach** — *leave me / let me be* (rest); **charah** — *burn/be incensed*: the gem is *two things held together* — God's *wrath ready to consume*, **and** the strange *"let me alone"* that *implies Moses could restrain him* (the mediator's prayer *can* stay the hand — God *binds himself to the intercession he invites*); and the *offer* — *"I will make of you a great nation"* — a *test* of Moses (will he grasp the Abrahamic promise for himself?). The interior of *wrath that yet leaves a door*, and *a mediator invited to self-advancement* — *(inferred: nuach/charah = the burning wrath, and the room left for the mediator — "let me alone" concedes the intercessor's power)*.
- **charah (burn) + koach (power) — *Moses takes up the plea* (32:11).** "Moses implored: 'why does your wrath **burn** (charah) against your people, whom you brought out with great **power** (koach)?'" *(stated)*. **charah** — *burn* (Moses echoing God's word back); **koach** — *strength/power*: the gem is *the intercession's first move* — Moses *does not seize the offer*; he *pleads for the people*, and calls them *"YOUR people, whom YOU brought out"* (returning to God what God called "your people", 32:7) — appealing to *God's own investment* (the great *power* already spent on them); the interior of *the mediator refusing self-advancement and pressing God's own stake* *(inferred: charah/koach = Moses takes up the plea — pressing God's own investment, refusing the offer)*.
- **charon (burning anger) + nacham (relent) + raah (harm) + raah (evil) — the *appeal to God's name and reputation* (32:12).** "'why should the Egyptians say you meant **evil** (raah)… turn from your **burning anger** (charon) and **relent** (nacham) from this **harm** (raah)?'" *(stated)*. **charon** — *burning anger*; **nacham** — *relent/be sorry*; **raah** (×2) — *evil intent* (the Egyptians' slander) / *harm* (the disaster): the gem is *the appeal to God's reputation* — Moses argues that *consuming Israel would let Egypt call the exodus a trap* (God's *name* mocked among the nations); the interior of *intercession that pleads God's own honor* — *turn from your anger for your name's sake* *(inferred: charon/nacham/raah = the appeal to God's name — relent, lest your reputation be mocked)*.
- **zakhar (remember) + ravah (multiply) + natan (give) — the *appeal to the oath* (32:13).** "'**remember** (zakhar) Abraham, Isaac, Israel… you swore to **multiply** (ravah) their offspring and **give** (natan) this land forever'" *(stated)*. **zakhar** — *remember*; **ravah** — *multiply*; **natan** — *give*: the gem is *the appeal to the covenant oath* — Moses pleads *God's own sworn promise* (the Abrahamic covenant, sworn *by God's own self*); the interior of *intercession grounded in God's faithfulness* — *not the people's merit* (they have none) *but God's oath* — *remember your promise* *(inferred: zakhar/ravah/natan = the appeal to the oath — plead God's own sworn faithfulness)*.
- **nacham (relented) + raah (harm) — *God relents* (32:14).** *nacham/raah distinct from 32:12:* "the LORD **relented** (nacham) from the **harm** (raah) he had spoken" *(stated)*. **nacham** — where 32:12 was Moses' *plea* to relent, 32:14 is God's *answer*: he *relented*: the gem is *God who can be moved* — the wrath *ready to consume* is *turned back by intercession*; the interior (attributed to God) of *a relenting responsiveness* — *God binds his action to the mediator's prayer and the plea of his own name and oath*; the divine freedom to *change course* in mercy *(inferred: nacham/raah = God relents — the wrath turned back, God moved by intercession)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **the breach and the verdict (shachat/sur 32:7–8 → qasheh 32:9 → nuach/charah 32:10):** the people *swiftly corrupt and swerve*; God names them *stiff-necked* and his *wrath burns to consume* — yet he *"lets Moses alone"* (conceding the mediator's power) and *offers Moses a new nation*.
2. **the intercession (charah/koach 32:11 → charon/nacham/raah 32:12 → zakhar/ravah/natan 32:13):** Moses *refuses the offer* and *pleads for the people* — pressing *God's own investment* (the power spent), *God's reputation* (lest Egypt mock), and *God's oath* (remember Abraham); *intercession grounded not in Israel's merit but in God's name and faithfulness*.
3. **God relents (nacham/raah 32:14):** the wrath *ready to consume* is *turned back* — *God relents from the harm* — *moved by the mediator's plea of his own character and promise*.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** shachat/sur (the *breach* — swift corruption and swerving); **qasheh (the *stiff-necked verdict* — the obstinate interior God names)**; **nuach/charah (the *burning wrath* and the *room left for the mediator* — "let me alone")**; koach (God's *power* already spent on them); **charon (the *burning anger* Moses asks God to turn from)**; **nacham ×2 (the *plea* to relent / God *relented*)**; **raah ×3 (the Egyptians' slander of *evil intent* / the *harm* Moses pleads against / the *harm* God relents from)**; zakhar/ravah/natan (the appeal to the *oath* — remember, multiply, give).
- **Whose interior (multi-interior):** **God** — *burning anger* at the breach, yet *leaving room for the mediator* ("let me alone") and *relenting* (moved by intercession); **Israel** — *stiff-necked*, *corrupted*, *swerving* (the obstinate breaking interior); **Moses** — the *intercessor* who *refuses self-advancement* and *turns back wrath by pleading God's name, investment, and oath*. The gem: **the mediator turns back wrath, and God relents.**
- **Threads:** **the intercessor** (32:11–13 → Moses "stood in the breach", Ps 106:23; Abraham pleading for Sodom Gen 18; "he ever lives to intercede", Heb 7:25 — the mediator staying wrath); **God relents** (nacham 32:14 ← Gen 6:6 "the LORD was sorry"; → "I relent of the disaster", Jonah 3:10; Joel 2:13 — the relenting responsiveness of God, held with "God is not a man that he should relent", Num 23:19 — the tension left, not resolved); **stiff-necked** (qasheh 32:9 → 33:3,5; 34:9; Deut 9:6; Acts 7:51 "you stiff-necked people" — the obstinate interior); **pleading God's name/oath** (32:12–13 ← the covenant with Abraham Gen 15,17; → "for your name's sake" Ps 79:9 — intercession grounded in God's own character); **the mediator refusing self-advancement** (32:10 — unlike the grasping self of Genesis, Moses declines the "great nation" offered him).
- **Guarded against:** flattening God's *relenting* (nacham) into either mere anthropomorphism or a denial of God's constancy — the text holds *both* the burning wrath and the being-moved, and the finding is the *responsiveness to intercession* (not a metaphysics); reading Moses' intercession as changing God's *mind* against his will when the text shows God *inviting* it ("let me alone" concedes the door); collapsing the three raah (slander / harm-pleaded / harm-relented) or the anger-words (charah 32:10 God's, charah 32:11 Moses' echo, charon 32:12 the plea); importing later atonement-theology onto this first intercession when the plea here is *name, investment, oath* — not yet the self-offering (that comes at GLD-04, "blot me out").

## EXO-13-hail · Exodus 9:13–35 · [the confession that is not repentance; the split in Egypt] — "I have sinned" without the fear of God, and the heart hardened when the hail stops

**The passage.** "Let my people **serve** (avad) me" (9:13); "all my plagues on your **heart** (lev)… that you may know none like me" (9:14); "to show my **power** (koach), that my name be proclaimed in all the earth" (9:16); "whoever **feared** (yare) the word of the LORD" sheltered his livestock (9:20); "whoever did not set his **heart** (lev) to the word" left them (9:21); the hail; "only in Goshen… no hail" (9:26); Pharaoh **called** (qara): "I have **sinned** (chata); the LORD is **righteous** (tsaddiq), I and my people **wicked** (rasha)" (9:27); "**Plead** (atar)… enough of the thunder" (9:28); "I know you do not yet **fear** (yare) the LORD" (9:30); "when Pharaoh saw the hail had ceased, he **sinned** (chata) yet again and **hardened** (kaved) his **heart** (lev)" (9:34); "the **heart** (lev) of Pharaoh was **hardened** (chazaq)" (9:35).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **avad (serve) — the *demand* renewed (9:13).** "let my people **serve** (avad) me" *(stated)* — the *unchanging demand* at the seventh plague *(inferred: avad = the service-demand renewed)*.
- **lev (heart) — the plagues aimed *at the heart* (9:14).** "this time I will send all my plagues on your **heart** (lev)… that you may know none like me in all the earth" *(stated)* — the gem: the plagues *target the interior itself* — *"on your heart"* — the judgment *aimed at the hard heart* to *make it know*; the divine interior *striking at the seat of the refusal* *(inferred: lev = the heart as the target of the plagues — judgment aimed at the interior)*.
- **koach (power) — the *global purpose* of the resistance (9:16).** "for this purpose I raised you up, to show my **power** (koach), that my name be proclaimed in all the earth" *(stated)* — koach, *strength/power*: the gem — the *hardening/resistance serves a purpose beyond Egypt*: God's *power* and *name* *proclaimed in all the earth*. The adversary's fixed "no" becomes the *stage* for God's global self-revelation — the interior's resistance *turned to God's ends* *(inferred: koach = God's power shown through the resistance, for his name in all the earth)*.
- **yare (feared) — the *God-fearers within Egypt* (9:20).** "whoever **feared** (yare) the word of the LORD among Pharaoh's servants hurried his slaves and livestock into shelter" *(stated)* — the gem: **yare** — *some of Pharaoh's own servants fear the word* and *heed it* (as the midwives feared, 1:17). A *split within Egypt's interior* — the fear-of-God appears *even among the adversary's people*; the response is a matter of *disposition*, not fate *(inferred: yare = the fear-of-God among Pharaoh's servants — the split in Egypt)*.
- **lev (heart) — the *not-considering* of the un-fearing (9:21).** *lev distinct from 9:14:* "whoever did not set his **heart** (lev) to the word left his livestock in the field" *(stated)* — the gem: the *other* side of the split — those who *did not set their heart* (the *not-considering*, ← Pharaoh 7:23 "did not take it to heart") *ignore the warning* and *lose all*. The interior that *will not lay the word to heart* *(inferred: lev = the not-setting-heart of the un-fearing — the willful inattention)*.
- **chata (sinned) + qara (called) + tsaddiq (righteous) + rasha (wicked) — the *theologically-correct confession* (9:27).** "Pharaoh **called** (qara): 'I have **sinned** (chata); the LORD is **righteous** (tsaddiq), I and my people are **wicked** (rasha)'" *(stated)*. The gem: the *fullest confession* in the cycle — **chata** (I have sinned), **tsaddiq** (God is *in the right*), **rasha** (I am *wicked*) — *all theologically correct*. **qara** — he *summons* the deliverer again. Yet (the next verses show) this confession is *under duress* ("enough of the thunder", 9:28) and *will not hold*. The interior *names its guilt rightly* — and it *is not enough* *(inferred: chata/tsaddiq/rasha = the correct confession of guilt — accurate, and insufficient)*.
- **atar (plead) — the *bargain* under the confession (9:28).** "**Plead** (atar)… enough of God's thunder; I will let you go" *(stated)* — atar *(← 8:8,28)*: the confession *comes with the same bargain* — *"I will let you go"* while the hail falls; the interior *confessing to end the pain*, not to turn *(inferred: atar = the plea-bargain riding on the confession)*.
- **yare (fear) — Moses' *discernment*: the confession is not the fear-of-God (9:30).** *yare distinct from 9:20:* "I know that you do not yet **fear** (yare) the LORD God" *(stated)* — the **central gem**: **yare** — Moses *discerns* that Pharaoh's *confession is not the fear-of-God*. You can *confess sin* (9:27) and *not fear God* (9:30). The *fear-of-God* — which the midwives had (1:17), which Egypt's God-fearers had (9:20) — is *absent* in Pharaoh *even as he confesses*. The interior's *accurate self-accusation without reverence* — the missing thing that would make confession *turn* *(inferred: yare = the fear-of-God Pharaoh lacks — the reason the confession does not become repentance)*.
- **chata (sinned) + lev (heart) + kaved (hardened) — the confession *evaporates at relief* (9:34).** *chata distinct from 9:27; lev distinct:* "when Pharaoh saw the hail had ceased, he **sinned** (chata) yet again and **hardened** (kaved) his **heart** (lev), he and his servants" *(stated)* — the gem (per the ledger): the *relief-triggers-hardening* pattern *repeats* (as 8:15) — *"when he saw the hail had ceased"* — and now the self-hardening is *explicitly tied to sin* (**chata**, "sinned *yet again*") and *corporate* ("he and his servants"). The confession of 9:27 *dissolves the moment the pressure lifts*; **kaved** — he *makes his heart heavy again*. The interior's *confession was pressure-talk*, not turning *(inferred: chata/lev/kaved = the confession evaporating at relief — sin repeated, heart re-hardened)*.
- **lev (heart) + chazaq (hardened) — the *stative* seal (9:35).** *lev/chazaq distinct:* "the **heart** (lev) of Pharaoh was **hardened** (chazaq)… as the LORD had spoken" *(stated)* — the *settled state* after the relapse — the heart *fixed*, *"as the LORD had spoken"* (the forewarning fulfilled) *(inferred: lev/chazaq = the heart settled-hard after the relapse)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **the plagues aimed at the heart, for a global purpose (avad 9:13 → lev 9:14 → koach 9:16):** the *service demanded*, the plagues *aimed at the hard heart*, and the whole resistance *turned to God's ends* — *his power and name proclaimed in all the earth*.
2. **the split in Egypt (yare 9:20 → lev 9:21 → hail → 9:26 Goshen spared):** *some of Pharaoh's servants fear the word and heed it*; others *will not set their heart to it* and lose all — the fear-of-God appears *even among the adversary's people*; disposition, not fate.
3. **the confession that is not repentance (chata/qara/tsaddiq/rasha 9:27 → atar 9:28 → yare 9:30):** Pharaoh *names his guilt rightly* — *"I have sinned, the LORD is righteous"* — but *under duress* and *with the old bargain*; and Moses *discerns the lack*: *"you do not yet fear the LORD."* Correct confession *without the fear of God*.
4. **the confession evaporates at relief (9:33 hail ceases → chata/lev/kaved 9:34 → lev/chazaq 9:35):** *when the hail stops*, Pharaoh *"sinned yet again and hardened his heart"* — the confession *dissolves at relief* (as 8:15); the heart *settled-hard*. The relief-as-trigger and confession-not-repentance *together*.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** avad (the *demand renewed*); **lev ×4 *distinguished* (9:14 the plagues *aimed at the heart* / 9:21 the *not-setting-heart* of the un-fearing / 9:34 the *re-hardening at relief* / 9:35 the *stative* seal)**; koach (God's *power/name* proclaimed through the resistance — the global purpose); **yare ×2 (9:20 the *God-fearers within Egypt* / 9:30 the *fear Pharaoh lacks* — the discernment that his confession is not repentance)**; **chata ×2 (9:27 the *correct confession* / 9:34 *sinned yet again* — the relapse)**; tsaddiq/rasha (the confession's *accurate theology*); qara (the deliverer *summoned* under the confession); atar (the *plea-bargain* riding on it); kaved/chazaq (the *self* re-hardening / the *stative* seal).
- **Whose interior (multi-interior):** the **adversary** (Pharaoh) — the *confession that is not repentance*: accurate self-accusation *without the fear of God*, dissolving *at relief* (the deeper trend — a heart that can *name* truth under pressure and still not *turn*); **Egypt's servants** — *split* (God-fearers who heed vs the not-considering) — proof the response is *disposition*, not fate; **God** — the plagues *aimed at the heart* for his *name in all the earth*; **Moses** — the *discernment* that reads the false confession.
- **Threads:** the **hardening trajectory** — *relief-triggers-hardening* repeats (9:34 ← 8:15; the ledger's mechanism); *confession-not-repentance* (9:27→30→34 — the key finding that Pharaoh can confess without the *fear* that turns); the **global purpose** (koach 9:16 — the resistance serves God's self-revelation, answering "what else influenced it"); the **fear-of-God** thread (yare 9:20,30 ← the midwives 1:17; Moses 3:6; → Israel 14:31 — the reverence Pharaoh *lacks*); the **split in Egypt** (9:20–21 → the mixed multitude 12:38; God-given favor 11:3 — Egypt not monolithic); "**that you may know**" (9:14 ← 5:2); the **not-considering** (lev 9:21 ← 7:23).
- **Guarded against:** lumping the four lev (heart-as-target / not-considering / re-hardening / stative), two chata (confession / relapse), two yare (God-fearers / the fear Pharaoh lacks); reading Pharaoh's "I have sinned" (9:27) as *repentance* when the text *itself* denies it (9:30 "you do not yet fear the LORD"; 9:34 "sinned yet again") — the *confession-without-fear* is the finding; missing the **split in Egypt** (9:20–21) which shows the hardening is *disposition* not *fate* (some feared and heeded); over-reading koach (9:16) into predestination debates when the verse states the *purpose* (God's name in all the earth) — read the stated purpose, not the systematic; treating the God-fearers (9:20) as Israelites (they are *Pharaoh's servants* — the fear-of-God *within Egypt*).

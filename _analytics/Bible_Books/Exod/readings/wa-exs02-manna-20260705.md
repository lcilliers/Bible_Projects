## EXS-02-manna · Exodus 16:1–36 · [the daily-bread test of trust; the gift of rest] — the fleshpots craved, the manna that cannot be hoarded, and the sabbath given

**The passage.** "Would we had died by the **meat** (basar) pots in Egypt" (16:3); "I am about to rain bread… that I may **test** them, whether they walk in my law" (16:4); "you shall see the **glory** (kavod)" (16:7); "the LORD **gives** (natan) you **meat** (basar)" (16:8); "the **glory** (kavod) appeared" (16:10); "at twilight you shall eat **meat** (basar)" (16:12); "the bread the LORD has **given** (natan)" (16:15); "gather… by the number of **persons** (nephesh)" (16:16); some gathered **more** (ravah, 16:17), whoever gathered **much** (ravah) had nothing over (16:18); the hoarded manna **stank** (baash), Moses **angry** (qatsaph, 16:20); "**lay aside** (nuach) for the **holy** (qodesh) sabbath" (16:23); it did not **stink** (baash), **laid aside** (nuach, 16:24); "how long will you refuse to **keep** (shamar)?" (16:28); "the LORD has **given** (natan) you the sabbath" (16:29); the jar of manna **placed** (nuach, 16:33, 16:34).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **basar (meat) — the *fleshpots craved* (16:3).** "'would we had died by the **meat** (basar) pots in Egypt, when we ate bread to the full'" *(stated)* — basar, *flesh/meat*: the gem is the *nostalgia for slavery* — Israel *idealizes Egypt's fleshpots* over freedom's uncertainty (← "better to serve Egypt", 14:12); the interior *preferring known plenty-in-bondage to trusting God's daily provision* — *"would we had died"* the recurring death-wish of the wilderness *(inferred: basar = the fleshpots craved — the nostalgia for slavery's plenty)*.
- **kavod (glory) — the *glory promised* (16:7).** "'in the morning you shall see the **glory** (kavod) of the LORD, for he has heard your grumbling'" *(stated)* — kavod, *glory*: the gem is that the *grumbling is answered by glory* — God *responds to the complaint with a self-revelation*; the interior's *complaint met not with rebuke alone but with the glory* *(inferred: kavod = the glory promised in answer to the grumbling)*.
- **basar (meat) + natan (gives) — the *provision answering the craving* (16:8).** *basar/natan distinct:* "'the LORD **gives** (natan) you **meat** (basar) to eat… your grumbling is against the LORD'" *(stated)*. **basar** — the *meat* (quail) given; **natan** — *give*: the gem is that God *answers the craving* (gives the very meat they idealized) *while naming the grumbling as against him*; the interior's *craving met, its complaint exposed* *(inferred: basar/natan = the meat given — the craving answered, the grumbling named)*.
- **kavod (glory) — the *glory appeared* (16:10).** *kavod distinct from 16:7:* "the **glory** (kavod) of the LORD appeared in the cloud" *(stated)* — where 16:7 *promised*, 16:10 *fulfils* — the *glory seen* — the interior-context of *the divine presence made visible* to the grumbling camp *(inferred: kavod = the glory appeared — the presence made visible)*.
- **basar (meat) — the *meat at twilight* (16:12).** *basar distinct from 16:3,8:* "'at twilight you shall eat **meat** (basar)… then you shall know that I am the LORD'" *(stated)* — the *third* basar: the *meat given so they may **know*** God (the provision's *purpose* — knowledge of the LORD, not just fullness); the interior *fed toward knowing* *(inferred: basar = the meat given that they may know the LORD)*.
- **natan (given) — the *bread named* (16:15).** *natan distinct from 16:8:* "'it is the bread the LORD has **given** (natan) you'" *(stated)* — the *manna* identified as *God's gift* ("what is it?" → the given bread) — the interior *receiving the unknown as gift* *(inferred: natan = the bread given — the unknown received as gift)*.
- **nephesh (persons) — the *portion per person* (16:16).** "'gather… by the number of **persons** (nephesh) in his tent'" *(stated)* — nephesh in the *person* sense: the *each-according-to-eaters* measure — the interior-context of *provision fitted to need* *(inferred: nephesh = the portion measured per person)*.
- **ravah (more) + ravah (much) — the *equalizing* that thwarts hoarding (16:17, 16:18).** "some gathered **more** (ravah, 16:17)… whoever gathered **much** (ravah, 16:18) had nothing over, whoever little had no lack" *(stated)*. **ravah** ×2: the gem is the *miracle of equality* — *however much or little gathered, each had exactly enough*; *hoarding is impossible* — the interior *cannot out-gather its need*; the daily bread *resists accumulation* (a lesson against grasping) *(inferred: ravah = the equalizing that thwarts hoarding — no one out-gathers his need)*.
- **baash (stank) + qatsaph (angry) — the *hoarding punished* (16:20).** "they did not listen; some left it… it bred worms and **stank** (baash); Moses was **angry** (qatsaph)" *(stated)*. **baash** — *stink* (← the Nile 7:21; the frogs 8:14); **qatsaph** — *be angry*: the gem is that *manna hoarded in distrust rots* — the interior that *keeps against tomorrow* (not trusting the next day's provision) finds its hoard *stinking*; distrust *punished in the keeping* *(inferred: baash/qatsaph = the hoarded manna rotting — distrust punished)*.
- **nuach (lay aside) + qodesh (holy) — the *sabbath rest given* (16:23).** "'tomorrow is a **holy** (qodesh) sabbath; **lay aside** (nuach) what is left'" *(stated)*. **nuach** — *rest/lay-up*; **qodesh** — *holy*: the gem is the *first sabbath command* — the *gift of rest* (← creation's rest, Gen 2:3); the interior *given a day to cease*, trusting the sixth-day double *(inferred: nuach/qodesh = the holy sabbath rest given)*.
- **baash (stink) + nuach (laid aside) — the *sabbath-kept manna preserved* (16:24).** *baash/nuach distinct from 16:20,23:* "they **laid it aside** (nuach); it did not **stink** (baash)" *(stated)* — the gem is the *contrast* — manna kept *in obedience* (for the sabbath) *does not rot*, where manna kept *in distrust* (16:20) *did*. **The same act of keeping — trust vs distrust — with opposite results.** The interior's *keeping judged by its motive* *(inferred: baash/nuach = the sabbath-kept manna preserved — obedient keeping vs distrustful hoarding)*.
- **shamar (keep) — God's *exasperation* (16:28).** "'how long will you refuse to **keep** (shamar) my commandments?'" *(stated)* — shamar, *keep/obey*: the gem is the *divine exasperation* — even with *daily bread*, they *will not obey* (some go out on the sabbath); the interior *slow to trust and obey* *(inferred: shamar = the divine exasperation at the disobedient keeping)*.
- **natan (given) — the *sabbath as gift* (16:29).** *natan distinct from 16:8,15:* "'the LORD has **given** (natan) you the sabbath; therefore on the sixth day he gives bread for two'" *(stated)* — the gem: the sabbath *named a gift* (not a burden) — God *gives* the rest and *provides the double*; the interior *given rest as grace* *(inferred: natan = the sabbath given as gift — rest as grace)*.
- **nuach (placed) — the *memorial jar* (16:33, 16:34).** "'put an omer of manna in a jar, **place** (nuach) it before the LORD'… Aaron **placed** (nuach) it before the testimony" *(stated)* — nuach, *set/deposit*: the gem is the *memory kept* — a jar of manna *laid up for the generations* (so they *see* the wilderness-bread) — the interior-context of *provision remembered* *(inferred: nuach = the manna laid up as memorial)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **the fleshpots craved, the glory answered (basar 16:3 → kavod 16:7 → basar/natan 16:8 → kavod 16:10 → basar 16:12):** Israel *idealizes Egypt's fleshpots* (nostalgia for slavery) — and God *answers the grumbling with glory* and *gives the very meat*, *that they may know the LORD*; the complaint *met and exposed*.
2. **the daily bread that cannot be hoarded (natan 16:15 → nephesh 16:16 → ravah 16:17,18 → baash/qatsaph 16:20):** the *manna given*, measured *per person*, *equalized* so none out-gathers his need — and *hoarded in distrust it rots*; the interior *cannot accumulate against tomorrow* — daily trust *required*.
3. **the gift of rest (nuach/qodesh 16:23 → baash/nuach 16:24 → shamar 16:28 → natan 16:29 → nuach 16:33–34):** the *holy sabbath given*; manna *kept in obedience preserved* (vs the *distrustful hoard* that rotted) — the *same keeping, opposite motives*; God *exasperated* at the disobedient, yet *gives the sabbath as grace*; the manna *laid up* as memorial.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** **basar ×3 (the *fleshpots craved* — nostalgia / the *meat given* / the *meat that they may know the LORD*)**; kavod ×2 (the glory *promised* / *appeared* — answering the grumbling); natan ×3 (meat *given* / bread *given* / the *sabbath* given as gift); nephesh (the portion *per person*); **ravah ×2 (the *equalizing* — no one out-gathers his need, hoarding thwarted)**; **baash ×2 (the *hoarded* manna *rots* — distrust / the *sabbath-kept* manna *preserved* — obedience: the same keeping, opposite motives)**; qatsaph (Moses' *anger* at the distrust); nuach ×4 (the *sabbath lay-aside* / the *preserved* keeping / the *memorial jar* placed ×2); qodesh (the *holy sabbath* — the gift of rest); shamar (the *divine exasperation* at disobedience).
- **Whose interior (multi-interior):** **Israel** — *nostalgia* for the fleshpots (preferring bondage's plenty to trusting daily provision), *distrust* (hoarding against tomorrow → rot), and *disobedience* (gathering on the sabbath); **God** — the *tester* (daily bread proving their trust), the *provider* answering the craving with glory, and the *giver of rest* (the sabbath as grace); **Moses** — *angry* at the distrust. The daily-bread *test of trust* is the pivot.
- **Threads:** **the nostalgia for slavery continues** (basar 16:3 ← 14:12; 16:3 "would we had died" — the wilderness death-wish); the **daily-trust test** (16:4 "that I may test them" ← Marah's test 15:25; Massah 17:7 — the wilderness-school of trust); **the sabbath given** (nuach/qodesh 16:23,29 ← creation's rest Gen 2:3 → the fourth commandment 20:8–11 — rest as gift, requiring trust); *trust vs distrust in the keeping* (baash 16:20 vs 16:24 — the same act judged by motive); **God answers grumbling with glory** (kavod 16:7,10 — grace, not only rebuke); the *equalizing* against hoarding (ravah 16:17–18 — the daily bread resists accumulation).
- **Guarded against:** lumping the three basar (craved / given / for-knowing), two kavod/ravah/baash, three natan, four nuach; missing the **trust-vs-distrust contrast** in the keeping (16:20 rots vs 16:24 preserved — the *motive* is the finding); reading the manna into NT "bread of life" typology (John 6) over the *daily-trust test* the passage is; treating the sabbath as burden when the text names it a *gift* ("the LORD has given you the sabbath"); over-reading the memorial-jar into tabernacle-theology over the *provision remembered* the verse gives; reading the fleshpot-nostalgia as mere hunger when it *idealizes bondage* (the interior preferring the known).

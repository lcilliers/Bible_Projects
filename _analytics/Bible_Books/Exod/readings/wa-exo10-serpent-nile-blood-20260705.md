## EXO-10-serpent-nile-blood · Exodus 7:8–25 · [the hardening's stative onset; the counterfeit that dismisses] — the staff-serpent, the Nile to blood, and the heart that will not consider

**The passage.** Pharaoh **summoned** (qara) the **wise men** (chakham); the magicians matched the sign (7:11); Aaron's staff swallowed theirs; "Pharaoh's **heart** (lev) was **hardened** (chazaq); he would not listen" (7:13); "Pharaoh's **heart** (lev) is hardened; he refuses" (7:14); go to **meet** (qara) him at the Nile (7:15); "let my people **serve** (avad) me… you have not **obeyed** (shama)" (7:16); "by this you shall know that I am the LORD"; the Nile struck to blood; "the fish die, the Nile **stinks** (baash), the Egyptians **grow weary** (laah)" (7:18); Moses **lifted** (rum) the staff (7:20); "the Nile **stank** (baash)" (7:21); "the magicians did the same; Pharaoh's **heart** (lev) remained **hardened** (chazaq)" (7:22); "he went home and did not **take** (shit) even this to **heart** (lev)" (7:23).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **qara (summoned) + chakham (wise men) — the *counterfeit wisdom* called in (7:11).** "Pharaoh **summoned** (qara) the **wise men** (chakham)… the magicians did the same by secret arts" *(stated)*. **qara** — *call-to*: Pharaoh *summons his experts*; **chakham** — the gem: *wisdom* — but here the *false wisdom* of the sorcerers, which *imitates* God's sign. The adversary's interior *reaches for a counterfeit* to *neutralise* the wonder — and (7:12, no span) Aaron's staff *swallows theirs*, a superiority Pharaoh *ignores*. Counterfeit wisdom *provides the excuse not to know* *(inferred: qara/chakham = the counterfeit wisdom summoned to neutralise the sign)*.
- **lev (heart) + chazaq (hardened) — the *stative hardening* after the sign (7:13).** "Pharaoh's **heart** (lev) was **hardened** (chazaq); he would not listen, as the LORD had said" *(stated)* — the gem (per the ledger): the *first* hardening is **stative** — *"was hardened"*, *no agent named* (not "Pharaoh hardened", not "the LORD hardened"). **chazaq** — *be firm/strong*: the heart *in a hardened state* after the swallowed sign; *"as the LORD had said"* ties it to the forewarning (4:21; 7:3) *without* yet naming the agent. The interior *simply firm against the wonder* *(inferred: lev/chazaq = the heart in its stative-hardened onset — no agent yet)*.
- **lev (heart) — the *diagnosis*: the heart *heavy* (7:14).** *lev distinct from 7:13:* "the LORD said, 'Pharaoh's **heart** (lev) is hardened [kaved, heavy]; he refuses to let the people go'" *(stated)* — the gem: God's *diagnosis* names the *settled condition* — the heart *heavy/unresponsive* (kaved); a *state*, not yet an *act*. The divine interior *reads* the adversary's heart as *already fixed* *(inferred: lev = the heart diagnosed heavy/unresponsive — the settled state)*.
- **qara (meet) — the *confrontation set* (7:15).** *qara distinct from 7:11:* "stand on the bank to **meet** (qara) him" *(stated)* — qara, *encounter*: Moses *sent to meet* Pharaoh at the water — the *place of the coming judgment* (the Nile) *(inferred: qara = the confrontation appointed at the Nile)*.
- **avad (serve) + shama (obeyed) — the *demand and the refusal* (7:16).** "let my people **serve** (avad) me… you have not **obeyed** (shama)" *(stated)*. **avad** — the *transfer-of-service* demand (← 3:12; 4:23); **shama** — the *refusal to heed* (← Pharaoh's "should I obey?", 5:2). The interior's *settled "no"* to the demand *(inferred: avad/shama = the service demanded, the heeding refused)*.
- **baash (stink) + laah (grow weary) — the *judgment on the Nile* foretold (7:18).** "the fish die, the Nile **stinks** (baash), the Egyptians **grow weary** (laah) of drinking" *(stated)*. **baash** — *stink/become odious* (← Israel "made to stink" 5:21): the gem is *the water of life become death* — Egypt's *god-river* struck; **laah** — *be weary*: the *collective interior wearied* by the undrinkable water. Judgment *on what Egypt trusts* *(inferred: baash/laah = the Nile's stink and Egypt's weariness — the trusted river become death)*.
- **rum (lifted) — the *staff of judgment raised* (7:20).** "he **lifted** (rum) the staff and struck the water" *(stated)* — rum, *raise/exalt*: the *instrument* of the wonder *raised* — the judgment *enacted* before Pharaoh's eyes *(inferred: rum = the staff of judgment lifted)*.
- **baash (stank) — the stink *fulfilled* (7:21).** *baash distinct from 7:18:* "the Nile **stank** (baash), the Egyptians could not drink" *(stated)* — where 7:18 *foretold*, 7:21 *fulfils* — the word *becomes fact*; the interior-context is *the judgment landing exactly as spoken* *(inferred: baash = the foretold stink now fact)*.
- **lev (heart) + chazaq (hardened) — the *stative hardening* after the counterfeit (7:22).** *lev/chazaq distinct from 7:13:* "the magicians did the same; Pharaoh's **heart** (lev) remained **hardened** (chazaq)" *(stated)* — the gem: the *counterfeit* (magicians matching the blood) *lets the heart remain firm* — the hardening *fed by the imitation*; still **stative** ("remained hardened"), still *no agent named*, but now *the counterfeit is the enabler*. The interior *uses the imitation to stay unmoved* *(inferred: lev/chazaq = the heart remaining firm, the counterfeit enabling the state)*.
- **shit (take) + lev (heart) — the *refusal to consider* — the mechanism named (7:23).** "he went home and did not **take** (shit) even this to **heart** (lev)" *(stated)* — the gem and *key*: **shit** — *set/place/apply* — Pharaoh *"did not set even this to his heart"* — the *willful refusal to consider* the wonder. Here the *stative* hardening reveals its *mechanism*: the heart is hard because the man *will not lay the sign to heart* — *inattention as an act*. This is the *seed of the self-hardening* the ledger tracks: not yet "he hardened his heart" (8:15), but already *the choosing not to consider* that will become it *(inferred: shit/lev = the willful refusal to lay the sign to heart — the mechanism beneath the stative hardening)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **the counterfeit neutralises the sign (qara/chakham 7:11 → lev/chazaq 7:13 → lev 7:14):** Pharaoh's *false wisdom matches* the wonder (ignoring that his staff was *swallowed*), and the heart *is in a hardened state* — *agentless* ("was hardened"), *diagnosed heavy* — the counterfeit *providing cover* not to know.
2. **the demand, the refusal, the judgment (qara 7:15 → avad/shama 7:16 → baash/laah 7:18 → rum 7:20 → baash 7:21):** the *service demanded* is *refused*; the *Nile is struck* — the trusted river *stinks*, Egypt *wearied* — the word *foretold and fulfilled*.
3. **the counterfeit feeds the hardening, and the heart will not consider (lev/chazaq 7:22 → shit/lev 7:23):** the magicians' *imitation* lets the heart *remain firm*; and the mechanism surfaces — Pharaoh *"did not take it to heart"*: the *stative* hardening is sustained by a *willful refusal to consider* — the *self-hardening's* first form (before the explicit "he hardened his heart", 8:15).

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** qara ×2 (7:11 the *counterfeit wisdom summoned* / 7:15 the *confrontation* at the Nile); chakham (the *false wisdom* that imitates the sign); **lev ×4 *distinguished* (7:13 the *stative-hardened state* after the sign / 7:14 the *diagnosis* — heart heavy / 7:22 the state *sustained by the counterfeit* / 7:23 the *refusal to lay it to heart*)**; chazaq ×2 (firm after the *serpent-sign* / firm after the *blood+counterfeit*); **shit (the *willful refusal to consider* — the mechanism beneath the stative hardening, the self-hardening's seed)**; avad/shama (the *service demanded, heeding refused*); baash ×2 (the Nile's stink *foretold* / *fulfilled*); laah (Egypt's *weariness*); rum (the *staff of judgment raised*).
- **Whose interior (multi-interior):** the **adversary** (Pharaoh) — the hardening in its *stative onset* (agentless), *fed by the counterfeit*, and *sustained by refusing to consider* the sign; the **magicians** — *counterfeit wisdom* imitating the wonder (the excuse not to know); **Egypt** (the people) — *wearied* by the judgment on their trusted river. God's word *foretold and fulfilled* (baash) — the sign's *truth* against the counterfeit's *imitation*.
- **Threads:** the **hardening trajectory** — *stative phase* (7:13,14,22 ← the forewarnings 4:21; 7:3; the *mechanism* 7:23 "did not take it to heart" → the explicit *self*-hardening 8:15,32; then the *God*-hardening 9:12ff — see the ledger); **"that you may know I am the LORD"** (7:17, the plague-purpose ← Pharaoh's "I do not know", 5:2); the **counterfeit** that emboldens (magicians 7:11,22 → the "finger of God" they'll concede, 8:19; their failure 9:11); **baash** (7:18,21 ← 5:21; the judgment on Egypt's Nile-god); the **transfer of service** (avad 7:16).
- **Guarded against:** collapsing the four lev or two chazaq (each a *distinct beat* — stative-after-sign / diagnosis / state-sustained-by-counterfeit / refusal-to-consider); reading the *stative* hardening (7:13,22) as *either* self- or God-agency when the text names *neither yet* (the ledger's first phase — the agency emerges later); missing that 7:23 ("did not take it to heart") is the *mechanism* — the self-hardening's *seed* in a *willful inattention*; over-reading chakham as true wisdom (it is the *counterfeit* that imitates and is *swallowed*); importing the later "God hardened" over this *agentless/self* onset (the trajectory's whole point is that the agency *shifts* — do not flatten it).



===== Gen 12:1-9 - the call: the name God makes great (ABR-01) =====
SURFACED: barak as *elective* blessing on one man (12:2) vs barak *extended through relationship* (12:3); **gadal — the name God *makes great*, against Babel's self-made name** (the primeval→patriarchal hinge); berakhah — Abram *as a conduit* of blessing; arar as *protective/covenantal* curse (vs primeval judgment-curse); nephesh as *persons/souls acquired* (distinct sub-sense); natan as the *promissory land-gift* (new sense); qara as *worship/invocation* (← 4:26, God-ward).


===== Gen 12:10-20 - faith faltering into fear (ABR-02) =====
SURFACED: yada as *strategic calculation*; the two chayah (Sarai spared 12:12 / Abram spared 12:13); **yatav bent from moral well-doing (4:7) to self-advantage (12:13), then ironically/hollowly fulfilled (12:16)**; nephesh as *the self preserved*; halal as *misdirected praise* (first in Scripture); qara as the *pagan's rebuke-summons*.


===== Gen 13:1-18 - yielding vs grasping; the inheritance given (ABR-03) =====
SURFACED: techillah/qara as *return to the first worship* after the lapse; yakhol as *scarcity* (13:6) vs *superabundance* (13:16); riv (strife that is) vs merivah (strife *refused*); Abram's *yielding* (no-span pivot); shachat as the *doomed* chosen land; **bachar — Lot's choose-by-sight-for-self** (← the fall/6:2 grasping); chatta as the *sinners* moved-toward; **natan — the inheritance *given* to the one who did not grasp** (vs bachar).


===== Gen 14:1-24 - the corrected interior; the oath that refuses gain (ABR-04) =====
SURFACED: avad (servitude) / marad (revolt) as the *political* interior; arakh, qara(encounter) as *near-nulls*; berit as *human alliance*; **shama that moves to rescue** the one who left; barak on *Abram* (14:19) vs barak on *God* (14:20, doxology); tsar/natan as God's *delivering*; natan as Abram's *tithe* (giving back); natan as the king's *demand*; nephesh as *persons*; rum as the *oath-gesture*; **ashar — the refusal to be *made rich by a man*** (the Egypt correction).


===== Gen 15:1-21 - faith reckoned righteous; the covenant God swears alone (ABR-05) =====
SURFACED: **yare** — the *first* "fear not", fear *dispelled* (reversing 3:10) vs **emah** — the *holy dread* of the covenant-presence; ravah as the *great reward*; natan as the *un-given* offspring complained-of (15:2–3) vs the *land-gift* (15:7,18); yakhol as *uncountable* offspring; **aman** — the *first believing-faith*; **chashav** — God's *reckoning* (inverting the machashavah of 6:5); **tsedaqah** — the *first reckoned righteousness*; yada as *asking assurance* (15:8) vs *granted certain knowledge* (15:13); avad (enslaved / nation-served) + anah (affliction); din (judgment on the oppressor); **shalom** — the *first peace*, a *peaceful death*; avon as *filling* guilt (divine patience); karat/berit — the *unilateral* covenant.


===== Gen 16:1-16 - the promise seized, and the outcast seen (ABR-06) =====
SURFACED: atsar (God's restraint of the womb); **shama — Abram heeding Sarai (← the fall's 3:17)** vs **shama — God hearing Hagar's affliction (16:11)**; natan as the *surrogate scheme* (16:3) and Sarai's *grievance* (16:5); shaphat (first "judge", invoked by the one who caused the conflict); tov as *subjective license*; **anah — affliction *done-to* (16:6, ← Israel's Egypt-word 15:13, roles reversed)** vs **anah — *self-humbling* submission (16:9)**; oni (the affliction God heard); ravah — Abraham's *multiply*-promise given to the *outcast*; **qara — Hagar *naming God* "El Roi" (the only human to name God)**, alongside the naming of Ishmael and the well.


===== Gen 17:1-27 - the re-named interior; covenant in the flesh (ABR-07) =====
SURFACED: tamim as *commanded wholeness* (← Noah's given tamim 6:9); berit *hammered* (the circumcision-covenant); ravah to Abraham (17:2) and to Ishmael (17:20); **qara — God *renaming* Abram→Abraham, Sarai→Sarah, naming Isaac** (new identities); natan (land / the son); **shamar — covenant *obedience*** (← garden-keeping 2:15); **basar — the covenant *borne in the flesh*** (new sign-sense); nephesh (the person cut off); **barak — the *barren* Sarah blessed to bear** (atsar 16:2 reversed); **lev — the *inward laugh*** at the impossible (→ Isaac's name); chayah — the father's *plea for Ishmael*; **shama — God *hears* Abraham for Ishmael** (← 16:11).


===== Gen 18:1-15 - the known interior; nothing too wonderful (ABR-08) =====
SURFACED: qara (the eager *run to meet*); chen (the host *seeking the guests'* favor — hospitality as humility); shaan (the *first* "rest/lean" — rest offered); lev (the heart *refreshed* by welcome); natan/tov (the *lavish gift* of food); chay (the reviving *time* of the promise); shama (Sarah's *covert overhearing*); the *inward laugh* (no-span, ← 17:17); **pala — the first "wonderful", nothing too hard for God** (the cycle's theological centre); **yare — the fear-denial of the *exposed* interior** (← 3:10, 4:9).


===== Gen 18:16-33 - the interceding interior; the Judge of all the earth (ABR-09) =====
SURFACED: atsum/barak (the blessing-bearer, ground of disclosure); **yada — *electing* (18:19) vs *investigating* (18:21)**; shamar (keeping the *way*); **tsedaqah — the *reckoned* righteousness (15:6) now *taught*** + mishpat (first "justice"); chattat/kaved/zeaqah (grave sin, its weight, the outcry); **tsaddiq vs rasha** (righteous/wicked, the intercession's distinction); **shaphat — God as *Judge of all the earth*** held to mishpat; **yaal — humble *venturing*** ("dust and ashes"); shachat *pleaded against* (×3); **charah — the wrath *deferred to*** (the limit of boldness).


===== Gen 19:1-38 - the interior that clings to the condemned (ABR-10) =====
SURFACED: qara/sur (hospitality *in* the wicked city); **yada — the conjugal word *perverted*** (assault 19:5; the incest-in-oblivion 19:33–35) vs the daughters *unrealized* (19:8); tov as *monstrous* discretion; shaphat (the mob *mocking* judgment); laah — *depravity persisting past blindness*; shachat (announced/unheeded/executed) + gadal + avon; **chazaq/chemlah/nuach — grace *seizing the lingerer*** (chemlah first); **chesed — the *first* steadfast-kindness**; chen/chayah/nephesh/yakhol/raah — *fearful* bargaining; the *backward look* (no-span); **zakhar — Lot saved because *God remembered Abraham*** (intercession's fruit); yare — Lot's *self-contradicting* fear; **chayah-preserve + yada — the *corrupted line*** (Moab/Ammon).


===== Gen 20:1-18 - integrity of heart; the fear wrongly assumed absent (ABR-11) =====
SURFACED: tsaddiq — the *pagan's* claimed innocence, *vindicated*; **tom — the *first* "integrity"**, of the *heart* (levav); **yada — God *vindicating* the heart (20:6)** vs the *warning*-knowledge (20:7); **chata — God *restraining* sin (20:6)** vs the *sin* Abraham nearly caused (20:9); yare — the court's *right fear* vs **yirah — the fear-of-God Abraham *wrongly assumed absent*** (the fear-lie exposed); **chesed — the great word *compromised*** into the deception-pact; natan (restitution / vindication); tov (the generous "as it pleases you"); **atsar — the barrenness-word (← 16:2), here *protective judgment***.


===== Gen 21:1-21 - the laugh of joy, and the outcast heard (ABR-12) =====
SURFACED: qara (Isaac named 21:3; the line *named through Isaac* 21:12); **shama — those who *hear* laugh-with-joy (21:6)** vs **shama-obey — God *commanding* Abraham to heed Sarah (21:12, the fall's fault now right)** vs **shama — God *hearing the boy* (21:17)**; gadal (Isaac's growth 21:8 / Ishmael's 21:20); **raa — the father's *distress* (21:11)** stilled by God (21:12); natan (the reluctant provision); **bakhah — the *first* "weep"**, Hagar's despair; yare — *fear-not* to the outcast; chazaq — *hold the boy fast*; the *opened eyes* (no-span, ← El-Roi 16:13).


===== Gen 21:22-34 - loyalty sworn, the eternal invoked (ABR-13) =====
SURFACED: **chesed (loyal dealing) vs shaqar (the *first* "deal falsely")** — the two poles of covenant faithfulness; yada/shama as Abimelech's *ignorance of the wrong*; natan/karat/berit — the *human* covenant-cutting (← God's 15:18); qara — the oath-place *named* (21:31) vs qara — *worship of the Everlasting God* (21:33, El Olam, new divine name).


===== Gen 22:1-19 - the Aqedah: the fear of God that withholds nothing (ABR-14) =====
SURFACED: the *test* (nasah) and "Here I am" (no-span, the available interior); the **first *love* (aheb), appearing as the *surrendered* love** (no-span); the *resurrection/provision*-faith (no-span, 22:5,8); **arakh — the *deliberate, enacted* obedience** (ordering the sacrifice); qara — the angel's *arresting* call (22:11), the *naming* of Yahweh-Yireh (22:14), the *second* call (22:15); **yada — God's knowing *confirmed by the act*** + **yare — the fear-of-God that *withholds nothing*** (the summit; ← 20:11 assumed-absent, 15:1 "fear not"); **barak/ravah *oath-sealed*** by God's self-oath; oyev (first "enemy"); **shama — the *obedience* that grounds the blessing** (the heeding-word at its summit).


===== Gen 23:1-20 - grief, and the sojourner's grave (ABR-15) =====
SURFACED: chayyim — Sarah's *honoured* lifespan (the only woman's recorded); **saphad (first "mourn/lament") + bakhah (weep)** — grief in *two registers*; natan — the *purchase-request* and the *offered/paid* transaction; **the sojourner owning only a *grave*** (the pilgrim promised all); **shama ×6 — the *honourable mutual-hearing*** of the negotiation (Abraham *paying full price*); nephesh — a *new sub-sense*, the *willing soul*.


===== Gen 24:1-67 - providence and the comfort of love (ABR-16) =====
SURFACED: barak — the *blessed* patriarch (24:1), the *doxology* (24:27,48), the *blessing on Rebekah* (24:60); mashal — the *steward's* rule; shamar/naqah — the oath's *condition/release*; natan — the land-*gift* and the *gifts*; **chesed — the *prayer* for God's steadfast love**, and **chesed+emet — the covenant-pair** (God's *unforsaken* loyal-faithfulness, recognised and required); yada — *knowing-by-sign*, *unrealized* (Rebekah), *discernment*; **charesh — the *first* "silence", the silent watchful discernment**; **lev — the *silent prayer* answered**; tov/ra + yakhol — *"cannot speak bad or good"* (powerless to object); qara/shaal — the *willing consent* ("I will go"); sane — the *first "hate"*; **nacham — grief *consoled*, and aheb the *comforting* love** (vs the surrendered love 22:2).


===== Gen 25:1-11 - the full death, the blessing passed (ABR-17) =====
SURFACED: natan — *all* to the heir (25:5) vs *gifts* to the rest, sent away (25:6); chayyim/chayah — the *honoured* lifespan; **tov — the *good, full, gathered* death** (the promise of 15:15 fulfilled); **barak — the blessing *passed to Isaac*** (the covenant outliving the man).


===== Gen 25:12-18 - the promise to the outcast kept (ABR-18) =====
SURFACED: chayyim — Ishmael's *honoured* lifespan and death ("gathered to his people", as Abraham, 25:8); the *promise to the outcast fulfilled* (twelve princes, ← 17:20; 16:10; 21:18); the *dwelling "over against his kinsmen"* (← 16:12, no-span).

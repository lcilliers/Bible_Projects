## JAC-03-isaac-gerar-fear · Genesis 26:1–11 · [the father's fault re-run in the son] — the promise renewed on Abraham's obedience, and the sister-lie repeated out of fear

**The passage.** Famine drives Isaac to Gerar; the LORD appears — "**bless** (barakh) you… I will **give** (natan) all these lands… establish the oath I swore to Abraham" (26:3); "I will **multiply** (ravah) your offspring… **give** (natan) all these lands… in your offspring all nations **blessed** (barakh)" (26:4); "because Abraham **obeyed** (shama) my voice and **kept** (shamar) my charge" (26:5); then the men **asked** (shaal) about his wife, "'She is my **sister**,' for he **feared** (yare) to say 'my wife'… because she was **attractive** (tov)" (26:7); "a **long** (arakh) time" passes, Abimelech sees him "laughing with Rebekah" (26:8); "Abimelech **called** (qara) Isaac… 'How could you say she is your sister?'" (26:9); "you would have brought **guilt** (asham) upon us" (26:10).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **barakh (bless you) — the covenant *personally renewed to Isaac* (26:3a).** The finding is "I will be with you and will **bless** (barakh) you" *(stated)*. The operation is barakh — *bless* — spoken now *directly to Isaac* (not merely inherited): the interior *addressed and reassured* against the famine that drove him. The Abrahamic blessing *lands on the son in his own right* *(inferred: barakh = the personal blessing spoken to Isaac)*.
- **natan (give all these lands) — the land-gift *to Isaac and his seed* (26:3b).** The finding is "to you and to your offspring I will **give** (natan) all these lands" *(stated)*. The operation is natan — *give* — the *land* bestowed, here framed *to-you-and-seed*: the gift **received**, not secured; the same *given* register as Abraham's cycle *(inferred: natan = the land freely given to Isaac's line)*.
- **natan (give to offspring) — the land-gift *within the innumerable-seed promise* (26:4b).** *Distinct from 26:3:* the finding is "I will **multiply** your offspring as the stars… and **give** (natan) to your offspring all these lands" *(stated)*. Here natan is *folded into the multiply-as-stars* clause — the land given *to a seed made countless*; the emphasis shifts from *Isaac-and-seed* (26:3) to *the vast seed* — the gift *enlarged*. Kept distinct: the same verb, but 26:3 = land to *you-and-line*, 26:4 = land to the *stars-numerous* offspring *(inferred: natan = the land given to the multiplied seed)*.
- **barakh (all nations blessed) — the *world-blessing through the seed* (26:4c).** *Distinct from 26:3a:* the finding is "in your offspring all the nations of the earth shall be **blessed** (barakh)" *(stated)*. Where 26:3a *blessed Isaac*, here barakh reaches *all nations* — the *outward, universal* blessing (← 12:3), not the *personal* one. Two barakh operations: the son blessed / the world blessed through the son *(inferred: barakh = the nations blessed in the seed; distinct from the personal blessing)*.
- **ravah (multiply) — the seed made *countless* (26:4a).** The finding is "I will **multiply** (ravah) your offspring as the stars of heaven" *(stated)*. The operation is ravah — *multiply/increase* — the creation-blessing word (1:28; 9:1) now the *covenant-increase* promise; the interior assured of *fruitfulness* against a barren start (25:21) *(inferred: ravah = the promised increase of the seed)*.
- **shama (obeyed) + shamar (kept) — the promise *grounded in Abraham's obedience* (26:5).** The findings are "Abraham **obeyed** (shama) my voice and **kept** (shamar) my charge, commandments, statutes, laws" *(both stated)*. Two distinct operations: **shama** — *hear/obey* (the *heeding* of God's voice — the very faculty misused at the fall, 3:17, here *rightly* used by Abraham); **shamar** — *keep/guard/obey* (the *sustained keeping* of the charge). The gem is *whose* obedience grounds the gift: **Isaac inherits on Abraham's shama/shamar** — the blessing *given* to the son *rests on the father's obedience*, not the son's; inheritance as *received on another's faithfulness* *(inferred: shama/shamar = the father's obedience, ground of the son's inheritance)*.
- **shaal (asked) — the *question* that triggers the fear (26:7a).** The finding is "when the men of the place **asked** (shaal) him about his wife" *(stated)*. The operation is shaal — *ask/inquire* — a neutral question that *exposes the interior's fear*; the outward prompt to which the fear-lie is the response *(inferred: shaal = the inquiry that occasions the fear)*.
- **yare (feared) — the *sister-lie repeated*, the father's exact fault (26:7b).** The finding is "'She is my sister,' for he **feared** (yare) to say 'my wife'… lest the men kill me" *(stated)*. The gem is the *repetition*: this is **Abraham's fear-lie run a third time** (12:11–13; 20:11) — now in *the son*, unprompted by any new revelation. yare — *fear/dread* — the *self-protecting* fear that *sacrifices the wife's safety* to preserve the self; the interior's inherited flaw, showing the fault-line *passes down the family* as surely as the blessing does *(inferred: yare = the self-protecting fear driving the inherited lie)*.
- **tov (attractive) — the beauty that *occasions* the fear (26:7c).** The finding is "because she was **attractive** (tov) in appearance" *(stated)*. The operation is tov — *good/pleasant* — here *of appearance*: Rebekah's beauty is the *external fact* the fearful interior *cannot trust God to guard*; the same tov-of-appearance that made Sarah a danger in Abraham's fear (12:11) *(inferred: tov = the desirable appearance that the fear fixes on)*.
- **arakh (a long time) — the lie *sustained over time* (26:8).** The finding is "when he had been there a **long** (arakh) time… Abimelech saw" *(stated)*. The operation is arakh — *be/grow long, prolong* — the fear-lie is not a single lapse but *maintained across a long dwelling*, until the truth *leaks* (the laughing/tsachaq — his own name's word — betrays the marriage). The interior's deception *has duration* before exposure *(inferred: arakh = the prolonging of the maintained lie)*.
- **qara (called Isaac) — the *summons to account* (26:9).** The finding is "Abimelech **called** (qara) Isaac and said, 'she is your wife!'" *(stated)*. *Distinct from the naming-qara of JAC-01/02:* here qara is *call-to / summon* — the pagan king **summons the patriarch to answer** for the lie; the interior *exposed and questioned by the outsider* (as Abraham was by the earlier Abimelech, 20:9) *(inferred: qara = the summons that calls the deceiver to account)*.
- **asham (guilt) — the *near-guilt* the lie risked (26:10).** The finding is "one might easily have lain with your wife, and you would have brought **guilt** (asham) upon us" *(stated)*. The gem is that the *pagan* names the moral stakes: **asham — *guilt / guilt-incurring*** — the fear-lie *endangered a whole people* with guilt; the outsider's conscience *sharper than the patriarch's fear*. The interior's self-protection is exposed as *reckless of others' guilt* *(inferred: asham = the corporate guilt the fear-lie nearly caused)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **the blessing renewed and grounded (barakh/natan 26:3 → ravah/natan/barakh 26:4 → shama/shamar 26:5):** the covenant lands *personally on Isaac* (bless-you, land-given), *enlarges* (stars-many seed, all-nations blessed), and is *grounded on Abraham's obedience* — the son *receives* on the father's *shama/shamar*.
2. **the inherited fault surfaces (shaal 26:7a → yare 26:7b → tov 26:7c):** a mere *question* triggers the *self-protecting fear*, which fixes on Rebekah's *beauty* and repeats the father's sister-lie — the blessing inherited, and *so is the flaw*.
3. **the lie has duration, then leaks (arakh 26:8):** maintained *over a long time* until the marriage betrays itself (the tsachaq/laughing) — deception *sustained*, not momentary.
4. **the outsider exposes and names the stakes (qara 26:9 → asham 26:10):** the pagan king *summons* Isaac and *names the guilt* the lie risked — the interior's fear shown to be *reckless of a whole people's guilt*, the outsider's conscience the sharper.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** barakh ×2 *distinguished* (26:3 the *personal* blessing on Isaac / 26:4 *all nations* blessed through the seed); natan ×2 *distinguished* (26:3 land to *you-and-line* / 26:4 land to the *stars-many* seed); ravah (the promised *increase*); shama + shamar (the *father's* obedience, ground of the *son's* inheritance — inheritance *received on another's faithfulness*); shaal (the *question* that triggers fear); **yare — the sister-lie repeated, the father's fault in the son**; tov (the *beauty* the fear fixes on); arakh (the lie *sustained over time*); qara (the *summons to account*, distinct from naming); asham (the *near-guilt* on a whole people).
- **Whose interior:** the **divine** interior *blessing, giving, grounding the gift on obedience*; the **human** interior *fearful and self-protecting* (Isaac's inherited lie); and the **outsider's** interior *more morally alert* than the patriarch's fear (Abimelech naming the guilt).
- **Threads:** the fear-lie (← Abraham 12:11–13, 20:11 — now the *third* run, in the son; the family fault); inheritance grounded on the father's obedience (shama/shamar 26:5 ← Abraham's proven fear-of-God 22:12, 22:18); the wells and oath to come (JAC-04); tov-of-appearance (← 12:11 Sarah); asham/guilt named by a pagan (← 20:9 the earlier Abimelech).
- **Guarded against:** merging the two barakh and two natan into single "blessing/giving" operations (personal vs universal; land-to-line vs land-to-stars-seed — kept distinct); claiming Isaac's *own* obedience grounds the gift when 26:5 grounds it on *Abraham's* (the finding is *received* inheritance); reading the fear-lie as unique when it is the *inherited* repeat; importing a NT "sin of the fathers" doctrine over the plain *narrated* recurrence; treating qara here as a naming (it is a *summons*).

## ABR-13-beersheba-oath · Genesis 21:22–34 · [loyalty sworn, the eternal invoked] — the oath of steadfast dealing against false dealing, and worship of the Everlasting God

**The passage.** "God is with you… **swear** to me… you will not **deal falsely** (shaqar) with me… but as I have dealt **kindly** (chesed) with you, so you will deal with me" (21:22–23); "I do not **know** (yada) who did this… I have not **heard** (shama) of it until today" (21:26); "Abraham **gave** (natan) sheep and oxen… the two **made** (karat) a **covenant** (berit)" (21:27); "that place was **called** (qara) Beersheba, because there both swore an oath" (21:31); "they **made** (karat) a **covenant** (berit)" (21:32); "Abraham… **called** (qara) on the name of the LORD, the Everlasting God" (21:33).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **chesed (kindly) vs shaqar (deal falsely) — the two poles of covenant faithfulness (21:23).** The finding is "you will not **deal falsely** (shaqar) with me… but as I have dealt **kindly** (chesed) with you, so you will deal with me" *(stated)*. Two gems, set as *opposites*: **chesed** — *steadfast, loyal dealing* (the great love-word, ← 19:19 God's saving chesed; 20:13 the compromised pact) — here the *reciprocal loyalty* Abimelech asks; and **shaqar** — the **first "deal falsely" (shaqar)**, *faithlessness/lying* — the *opposite* of chesed. The operation names the interior's *two possible modes* toward a covenant-partner: *loyal dealing* or *false dealing*. And there is a quiet irony — the *pagan* asks the *patriarch* (who lately deceived him, ABR-11) not to *shaqar* but to render *chesed* *(inferred: chesed = loyal dealing; shaqar = false dealing — the poles)*.
- **yada (know) + shama (heard) — Abimelech's *ignorance of the wrong* (21:26).** The finding is "I do not **know** (yada) who did this… I have not **heard** (shama) of it until today" — of the seized well *(stated)*. The operations are Abimelech's *not-knowing* (yada) and *not-having-heard* (shama) — the honest disavowal of the grievance; near-context to the treaty *(inferred: distinct yada/shama — ignorance, not the weighty senses)*.
- **natan + karat + berit — the *human* covenant made (21:27).** The finding is "Abraham **gave** (natan) sheep and oxen… the two **made** (karat) a **covenant** (berit)" *(stated)*. The gem is *karat* — "cut a covenant" — the *same idiom* as **God's unilateral covenant** (15:18, ABR-05) — now *between two men*: a *human* covenant-cutting, sealed with Abraham's *gift* (natan); distinct from God's self-binding oath — here *mutual* treaty *(inferred: karat/berit human-to-human, vs God's 15:18)*.
- **qara (called) #1 — the oath-place named (21:31).** The finding is "that place was **called** (qara) **Beersheba**, because there both of them swore an oath" *(stated)*. The operation is naming *memorialising the oath* — the treaty *fixed* in a place-name (Beersheba, "well of the oath/seven") *(inferred)*.
- **karat + berit — the covenant *concluded* (21:32).** The finding is "so they **made** (karat) a **covenant** (berit) at Beersheba" *(stated)*. The operation *restates* the concluded treaty — the human covenant *ratified* *(inferred: the karat/berit of 21:27 completed)*.
- **qara (called) #2 — worship of the *Everlasting* God (21:33).** The finding is "Abraham planted a tamarisk… and **called** (qara) there on the name of the LORD, the **Everlasting God** (El Olam)" *(stated)*. The gem is *qara-as-worship* (← 4:26 first worship; 12:8; 13:4 Abram calling on the Name) — now *after* the temporal treaty, Abraham **calls on the *Everlasting* God** (El Olam, a *new* name for God, as El-Roi 16:13, El-Shaddai 17:1): the interior *grounds its temporal dealings in worship of the eternal* — the passing oath framed by the *unpassing* God *(inferred: qara-worship; El Olam the everlasting)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **loyalty against falseness (chesed/shaqar 21:23):** the treaty is asked on the ground of *chesed* (steadfast dealing) *not shaqar* (false dealing) — the interior's covenant-faithfulness defined by its two poles; the once-deceived pagan seeking loyalty from the patriarch.
2. **the human covenant (natan/karat/berit 21:27,32):** two men *cut* a covenant (karat, the very idiom of God's oath, 15:18) — sealed by gift and oath, memorialised at *Beersheba* (qara).
3. **the temporal grounded in the eternal (qara/El-Olam 21:33):** after the *human, temporal* treaty, Abraham *worships* the **Everlasting God** — the interior sets its passing agreements *within* the worship of the God who does not pass; a temporal oath framed by the eternal Name.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** **chesed (loyal dealing) vs shaqar (the *first* "deal falsely")** — the two poles of covenant faithfulness; yada/shama as Abimelech's *ignorance of the wrong*; natan/karat/berit — the *human* covenant-cutting (← God's 15:18); qara — the oath-place *named* (21:31) vs qara — *worship of the Everlasting God* (21:33, El Olam, new divine name).
- **Whose interior:** the **human** interior *swearing loyalty* (chesed) and *worshipping the eternal* (Abraham); the **pagan** interior *seeking faithful dealing* (Abimelech).
- **Threads:** chesed (← 19:19 God's saving love; 20:13 compromised; here reciprocal loyalty); karat/berit (← 15:18 God's unilateral, now human); qara-worship (← 4:26; 12:8; 13:4); the divine names (El-Roi 16:13, El-Shaddai 17:1, El-Olam 21:33 — the interior naming God by his acts); the oath (→ ABR-14 the oath-blessing; ABR-16 the servant's oath).
- **Guarded against:** merging chesed and shaqar (they are set as *opposites* — the whole point); flattening karat's human covenant into God's unilateral one; merging the two qara (naming the place vs worship); missing that El-Olam is a *new* name grounding the temporal in the eternal; over-reading the minor yada/shama (Abimelech's ignorance, not the weighty senses).

## ABR-12-isaac-born-hagar-sent · Genesis 21:1–21 · [the laugh of joy, and the outcast heard] — the incredulous laugh turned to joy, the wife's voice now God's command, and God hearing the dying boy

**The passage.** "Abraham **called** (qara) his son's name Isaac" (21:3); "God has made **laughter** for me; everyone who **hears** (shama) will laugh over me" (21:6); "the child **grew** (gadal) and was weaned" (21:8); "the thing was very **displeasing** (raa) to Abraham" (21:11); "be not **displeased** (raa)… through Isaac shall your offspring be **named** (qara)… **obey** (shama) her voice" (21:12); "he **gave** (natan) bread and water… and sent her away" (21:14); "she… **wept** (bakhah)" (21:16); "God **heard** (shama) the voice of the boy… the angel **called** (qara)… **Fear not** (yare)" (21:17); "**hold** (chazaq) him fast… I will make him a great nation" (21:18); "God opened her eyes, and she saw a well" (21:19); "the boy **grew up** (gadal)" (21:20).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **qara (called) — Isaac named (21:3).** The finding is "Abraham **called** (qara) his son's name Isaac" *(stated)*. The operation is the naming *fulfilled* (per 17:19) — the covenant-son named "he laughs" *(inferred)*.
- **shama (hears) #1 — the laugh *transformed* to joy (21:6).** The finding is "God has made **laughter** for me; everyone who **hears** (shama) will laugh over/with me" *(stated)*. The gem is the *transformation* of the laugh: Sarah's *inward incredulous* laugh (18:12) becomes the **laugh of joy** — and all who **hear** (shama) will *laugh with* her; Isaac ("he laughs") *embodies* the joy. The operation is the interior's laughter turned *from doubt to rejoicing* *(inferred: shama anchors the shared joy; the laugh 18:12→21:6, incredulity→gladness)*.
- **gadal (grew) #1 — the child grows (21:8).** The finding is "the child **grew** (gadal) and was weaned" *(stated)*. The operation is the ordinary *growth* of the promised son (gadal ← 12:2 the name; here the child) *(inferred: near-context)*.
- **raa (displeasing) #1 — the father's *distress* over Ishmael (21:11).** The finding is "the thing was very **displeasing** (raa) to Abraham on account of his son" *(stated)*. The gem is *raa* (be evil/grievous, ← 6:5) here as **the father's grief**: sending Ishmael away is *bitter* to Abraham — the interior's pain at losing the son he has (← his plea "that Ishmael might live", 17:18) *(inferred: raa = the grievous, Abraham's distress)*.
- **raa (displeased) #2 + qara + shama (obey) — God's word: heed Sarah, the fall's fault *now right* (21:12).** The finding is "be not **displeased** (raa)… through Isaac shall your offspring be **named** (qara)… whatever Sarah says, **obey** (shama) her voice" *(stated)*. Two gems: **qara** — the line *reckoned through Isaac* ("named/called" through him); and **shama (obey)** — God *commands* Abraham to **obey the voice of** Sarah. This is the *same construction* that was the **fall's verdict** (3:17, "you have **listened to the voice** of your wife") and the **Hagar-scheme's fault** (16:2) — but here it is **God's command**: heeding the wife is *right* because *her word aligns with God's purpose*. The operation is the *same act* (heeding the wife) now *divinely sanctioned* — the fault redeemed by *whose will it serves* *(inferred: raa #2 — God stilling the distress; shama-obey — the wife's voice now God's channel)*.
- **natan (gave) — the reluctant provision (21:14).** The finding is Abraham "**gave** (natan) bread and a skin of water to Hagar… and sent her away" *(stated)*. The operation is natan as *provision at the parting* — the father *gives* even as he *sends*; the reluctant sending softened by giving *(inferred)*.
- **bakhah (wept) — the mother's despair-weeping (21:16).** The finding is "she… **wept** (bakhah)" — "let me not look on the death of the child" *(stated)*. The gem is the **first "weep" (bakhah)** — Hagar's *despair* as the water fails and the boy dies; the interior's *grief at a child's dying* *(inferred: bakhah = the weeping of despair)*.
- **shama (heard) #2 + qara + yare — God *hears the boy* (21:17).** The finding is "God **heard** (shama) the voice of the boy… the angel **called** (qara) to Hagar: **Fear not** (yare)… God has heard the voice of the boy" *(stated)*. The gem is *shama* fulfilling the name: **God *hears* the boy** — Ishmael = "God hears" (← 16:11) — and it is the *boy's* voice heard (not the mother's weeping); the angel's **"fear not"** (yare, ← 15:1 to Abram) *comforts the despairing outcast*. God's hearing reaches the *dying child* *(inferred: shama = God hearing Ishmael, the name kept; yare = fear-not to Hagar)*.
- **chazaq (hold fast) — the mother told to grip the boy (21:18).** The finding is "**hold** (chazaq) him fast with your hand, for I will make him a great nation" *(stated)*. The gem is *chazaq* (grip/strengthen, ← 19:16 the angels seizing Lot) — Hagar told to *hold the boy fast*, the promise (great nation) renewed (← 16:10; 17:20); the operation is *the grip of hope* commanded *(inferred)*.
- **(21:19 — God opens her eyes: the provision seen, no span.)** Flagged: "opened… saw" carries no non-T2 span, but the *centre*: **God opens Hagar's eyes and she sees a well** — the provision *was there*; her *despair blinded* her to it. The interior's *eyes opened* to God's provision — an echo of *El-Roi* (the God who sees, 16:13) now *making her see* *(no-span context)*.
- **gadal (grew up) #2 — God with the outcast (21:20).** The finding is "God was with the boy, and he **grew up** (gadal)" *(stated)*. The operation is Ishmael's *growth under God's presence* — the outcast son *cared for*, the promise kept (← 16:10; 17:20); distinct from the child's growth at 21:8 (Isaac) — here Ishmael, *God-with-him* though not the covenant-line *(inferred)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **the laugh transformed (shama/laughter 21:6 ← 18:12):** Sarah's *incredulous* inward laugh becomes the *laugh of joy* — all who *hear* laugh *with* her; Isaac's very name carries the interior's turn from doubt to gladness.
2. **the father's grief and the sanctioned heeding (raa/shama 21:11–12):** Abraham is *distressed* (raa) over Ishmael; God *stills* the distress and *commands* him to *obey Sarah's voice* — the "heed the wife" that was *the fall's fault* (3:17) and *the scheme's fault* (16:2) is now *God's will*; the same act redeemed by the purpose it serves.
3. **the sending, the despair, the hearing (natan/bakhah/shama 21:14–17):** Abraham *gives* provision and *sends*; Hagar *weeps* in despair; and **God *hears* the boy** (Ishmael, "God hears") — the outcast child's voice reaching heaven, met with *"fear not"*.
4. **the opened eyes and the kept promise (chazaq/eyes/gadal 21:18–20):** told to *hold the boy fast*, Hagar has her *eyes opened* to the well she could not see, and Ishmael *grows up with God* — the outcast provided for, seen, and blessed, though the covenant runs through Isaac.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** qara (Isaac named 21:3; the line *named through Isaac* 21:12); **shama — those who *hear* laugh-with-joy (21:6)** vs **shama-obey — God *commanding* Abraham to heed Sarah (21:12, the fall's fault now right)** vs **shama — God *hearing the boy* (21:17)**; gadal (Isaac's growth 21:8 / Ishmael's 21:20); **raa — the father's *distress* (21:11)** stilled by God (21:12); natan (the reluctant provision); **bakhah — the *first* "weep"**, Hagar's despair; yare — *fear-not* to the outcast; chazaq — *hold the boy fast*; the *opened eyes* (no-span, ← El-Roi 16:13).
- **Whose interior:** the **human** interior *rejoicing* (Sarah's laugh-of-joy), *grieving* (Abraham over Ishmael, Hagar over the boy); the **outcast** interior *despairing then heard and seen*; and the **divine** interior *hearing the dying child, opening blind eyes, keeping his promise to the cast-out*.
- **Threads:** the laugh (← 17:17 Abraham, 18:12 Sarah's doubt; here → joy; Isaac "he laughs"); **shama-obey (← 3:17/16:2 the fall's fault, now God's command)**; shama-God-hears (← 16:11 Ishmael; the outcast heard); yare fear-not (← 15:1); El-Roi/seeing (← 16:13, now Hagar's eyes opened); the two sons (Isaac the covenant-line vs Ishmael blessed-and-kept).
- **Guarded against:** claiming operations off the no-span laugh (21:6) and opened-eyes (21:19) — read as centres anchored by shama; **merging the three shama** (joy-hearing / God-commanding-heed / God-hearing-the-boy) — especially missing that 21:12's *heed-the-wife* is the *redeemed* form of the fall's fault; merging the two raa (distress / God stilling it) or two gadal; importing Galatians' Hagar allegory — the operations the chapter states.

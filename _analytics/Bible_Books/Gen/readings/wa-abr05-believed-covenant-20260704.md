## ABR-05-believed-covenant · Genesis 15:1–21 · [faith reckoned righteous] — the fear dispelled, the honest complaint, the trust credited as righteousness, and the covenant God swears alone

**The passage.** "**Fear not** (yare), Abram, I am your shield; your reward shall be very **great** (ravah)" (15:1); "what will you **give** (natan) me, for I continue childless… you have **given** (natan) me no offspring" (15:2–3); "number the stars, if you are **able** (yakhol)… so shall your offspring be" (15:5); "he **believed** (aman) the LORD, and he **counted** (chashav) it to him as **righteousness** (tsedaqah)" (15:6); "how am I to **know** (yada) that I shall possess it?" (15:8); the pieces laid "**against** (qara) the other" (15:10); "a deep sleep fell… **dreadful** (emah) and great darkness" (15:12); "**know** (yada) for certain… your offspring will be **servants** (avad)… **afflicted** (anah)… the nation they **serve** (avad) I will **judge** (din)" (15:13–14); "you shall go… in **peace** (shalom)… a **good** (tov) old age" (15:15); "the **iniquity** (avon) of the Amorites is not yet complete" (15:16); "the LORD **made** (karat) a **covenant** (berit)… to your offspring I **give** (natan) this land" (15:18).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **yare (fear-not) — the *first* "fear not", the interior's fear *dispelled* (15:1).** The finding is "**Fear not** (yare), Abram, I am your shield" *(stated)*. The gem is the **first "fear not" in Scripture** — and it *negates* the very word of the fall's dread (3:10, "I was **afraid** (yare)"): there guilt-dread; here God *dispels* fear by *self-gift* ("I am your shield… your reward"). And it *answers* ABR-04: the interior that *refused* the king's reward is told **God himself** is its reward *(inferred: the fear God removes — of harm/loss — cured by God-as-shield-and-reward)*.
- **ravah (great) — the magnitude of the reward (15:1).** The finding is "your reward shall be very **great** (ravah)" *(stated)*. The operation is ravah (the increase/greatness word) now measuring *the reward that is God himself* — a further ravah-sense (vs blessing 1:28, pain 3:16, flood 7:17) *(inferred)*.
- **natan (give) ×2 — the honest complaint of the *un-given* gift (15:2, 15:3).** The finding is "what will you **give** (natan) me, for I continue childless (15:2)… behold, you have **given** (natan) me no offspring (15:3)" *(stated)*. The gem is *faith that argues*: Abram *laments* the promise not yet kept — the believing interior is not passive but *presses God honestly* about the withheld gift; distinct from the land-gift natan — here natan is the *offspring not yet given*, the ground of complaint *(inferred)*.
- **yakhol (able) — the *uncountable* offspring (15:5).** The finding is "number the stars, if you are **able** (yakhol) to number them" *(stated)*. The operation is *inability as superabundance* (← 13:16, the uncountable dust) — the promise measured by what the interior *cannot* count *(inferred: same yakhol-as-superabundance)*.
- **aman (believed) — the *first* believing-faith (15:6).** The finding is "he **believed** (aman) the LORD" *(stated)*. The gem is the **first "believe/faith" predicated of a person** — *aman* (to be firm, to rely; the root of "amen"): Abram *trusts* the promise (uncountable offspring) *against the evidence* (childless, aged). The operation is **faith as firm reliance on God's word over sight** — the pivot of the whole cycle *(inferred)*.
- **chashav (counted) — God's gracious *reckoning*, the root of "devising" inverted (15:6).** The finding is "he **counted** (chashav) it to him" *(stated)*. The gem is that *chashav* (reckon/impute) shares the root of *machashavah* — the heart's evil **devisings** of 6:5 ("every intention of the **thoughts** [machashavah] only evil"). At 6:5 the human reckoning-faculty *devised only evil*; here **God's** reckoning *credits faith as righteousness* — the same imputing-faculty *inverted from evil-devising (man) to grace-crediting (God)* *(inferred: chashav = God's imputation; distinct from and inverting the human machashavah)*.
- **tsedaqah (righteousness) — righteousness *reckoned*, not achieved (15:6).** The finding is "as **righteousness** (tsedaqah)" *(stated)*. The gem is the **first "righteousness" as a reckoned *status*** — Abram's *trust* is *counted* righteous; righteousness *given* to faith, not *earned* by works *(inferred: the interior justified by aman, credited by chashav, as tsedaqah — the three-span pivot)*.
- **yada (know) #1 — the believer *asks for assurance* (15:8).** The finding is "how am I to **know** (yada) that I shall possess it?" *(stated)*. The gem is that *right after believing* (15:6), Abram *asks to know* — **not doubt but the request for confirmation**: faith and the desire for assurance *coexist* in the same interior; believing does not abolish the longing for a sign *(inferred)*.
- **qara (against) — the covenant pieces arranged (15:10).** The finding is "laid each half **against** (qara, toward) the other" *(stated)*. The operation is qara in its *encounter/opposite* sense (H7122H) — near-null inner-being weight, the arranging of the covenant pieces *(honest near-null)*.
- **emah (dreadful) — the *holy dread* of the covenant-presence (15:12).** The finding is "a deep sleep fell… **dreadful** (emah) and great darkness fell on him" *(stated)*. The gem is a *second, distinct fear*: God said "**fear not**" (yare, 15:1), yet as he comes to covenant a **terror** (emah) falls — not the fear of harm (dispelled) but the **awe/dread of the divine covenant-presence**. Two fears kept apart: the fear God *removes* (yare, 15:1) and the holy *emah* of God drawing near *(inferred)*.
- **yada (know) #2 — God *grants* certain knowledge, including the hard future (15:13).** The finding is "**know** (yada) for certain that your offspring will be sojourners… servants… afflicted" *(stated)*. The gem is God *answering* the request of 15:8 — the assurance *given*, but including *slavery and affliction*: the knowledge granted is *certain and sobering* — faith is confirmed with truth, not comfort only *(inferred: distinct from 15:8 — there the asking, here the certain revelation)*.
- **avad (servants) + anah (afflicted) — the foretold servitude and affliction (15:13).** The finding is the offspring "**servants** (avad)… and **afflicted** (anah)" *(stated)*. The operations: **avad** — *enslavement* (← 14:4 servitude); **anah** — *affliction/oppression* (first occurrence; the root of Hagar's affliction, 16:11) — the suffering woven into the promise's fulfilment *(inferred)*.
- **avad (serve) + din (judge) — judgment on the oppressor (15:14).** The finding is "the nation they **serve** (avad) I will **judge** (din)" *(stated)*. The operations: **avad #2** — the serving *of the oppressor*; **din** — God's *judgment* (← 6:3 the Spirit's striving) now *on the enslaving nation* — the oppression will be *requited* *(inferred: two avad — enslaved (13) and the nation served (14); din = judgment on the latter)*.
- **shalom (peace) + tov (good) — the interior's *peaceful* end (15:15).** The finding is "you shall go to your fathers in **peace** (shalom)… buried in a **good** (tov) old age" *(stated)*. The gem is the **first "peace" (shalom) in Scripture** — the *manner of Abram's death*: the interior's *wholeness/rest* at the end, a "good" old age *(inferred: shalom's first appearance names a *peaceful death* — the believer's rest)*.
- **avon (iniquity) — the oppressor's *filling* guilt, divine patience (15:16).** The finding is "the **iniquity** (avon) of the Amorites is not yet complete" *(stated)*. The operation is avon (← 4:13 Cain's) now naming an *accumulating* guilt — God *waits* until it is "complete" before judging: **divine patience and justice** — the interior of God deferring judgment until it is just *(inferred)*.
- **karat + berit + natan — the covenant God *swears alone* (15:18).** The finding is "the LORD **made** (karat — cut) a **covenant** (berit)… to your offspring I **give** (natan) this land" *(stated)*. The gem is the **unilateral covenant**: God alone (the smoking pot and torch, while Abram sleeps in dread) passes *between the pieces* — the covenant "cut" (karat) rests on **God's self-binding**, not Abram's performance; the land *given* (natan) on God's oath alone *(inferred: karat — the covenant-cutting; God binds himself, the sleeping Abram a witness not a party)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **two fears (yare 15:1 / emah 15:12):** God *dispels* the fear of harm ("fear not, I am your shield") — the fall's dread (3:10) reversed — yet the covenant-making brings a *holy dread* (emah); the interior is freed from fearing loss and *awed* by God's nearness.
2. **honest complaint → promise → faith → reckoning (15:2–6):** Abram *laments* the un-given gift (natan), God answers with the *uncountable* stars (yakhol), Abram *believes* (aman), and God *counts* it (chashav) as *righteousness* (tsedaqah) — the interior's argued faith met by promise and *credited* — the reckoning-faculty (chashav) turned from man's evil devising (6:5) to God's grace.
3. **believing yet asking to know (aman 15:6 → yada 15:8 → yada 15:13):** faith does not silence the request for assurance; Abram *asks how he shall know*, and God *gives certain knowledge* — including slavery and affliction (avad/anah). Faith is confirmed with *sobering truth*, and the oppressor's judgment (din) and the interior's *peaceful* end (shalom) are promised together.
4. **the covenant sworn alone (karat/berit 15:18):** the whole rests not on Abram (asleep, in dread) but on *God passing between the pieces* — the land *given* (natan) on God's self-binding; faith's righteousness (15:6) and the land's gift (15:18) both *grounded in God's oath, not man's work*.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** **yare** — the *first* "fear not", fear *dispelled* (reversing 3:10) vs **emah** — the *holy dread* of the covenant-presence; ravah as the *great reward*; natan as the *un-given* offspring complained-of (15:2–3) vs the *land-gift* (15:7,18); yakhol as *uncountable* offspring; **aman** — the *first believing-faith*; **chashav** — God's *reckoning* (inverting the machashavah of 6:5); **tsedaqah** — the *first reckoned righteousness*; yada as *asking assurance* (15:8) vs *granted certain knowledge* (15:13); avad (enslaved / nation-served) + anah (affliction); din (judgment on the oppressor); **shalom** — the *first peace*, a *peaceful death*; avon as *filling* guilt (divine patience); karat/berit — the *unilateral* covenant.
- **Whose interior:** the **human** interior *believing against sight* (aman) yet *honestly complaining* and *asking to know*; and the **divine** interior *dispelling fear, crediting faith as righteousness, swearing the covenant alone*, and *patient in justice*.
- **Threads:** yare (← 3:10 dread, now "fear not"); chashav (← machashavah 6:5, inverted); aman/tsedaqah (the pivot — faith and reckoned righteousness, foundational for the cycle and beyond); natan land-gift (← 12:7); anah (→ Hagar's affliction, ABR-06 16:11); shalom (first here → the peace-motif); karat/berit (God's self-binding, ← 15's unilateral oath).
- **Guarded against:** merging the two fears (yare dispelled vs emah the holy dread); merging the four natan or two yada or two avad; flattening aman/chashav/tsedaqah into one "justification" abstraction rather than three distinct spans (trust / reckoning / righteousness); reading the chashav↔machashavah link as fanciful (both are H-root reckon/devise — the inversion is real); importing NT justification doctrine onto 15:6 beyond the trust-reckoned-righteous the verse states (the operations, not the epistle).

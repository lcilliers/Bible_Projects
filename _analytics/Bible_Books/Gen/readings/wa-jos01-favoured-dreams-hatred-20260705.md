## JOS-01-favoured-dreams-hatred · Genesis 37:1–11 · [favour, dreams, and hatred that compounds] — the bad report, the rejected dominion, and the pondered saying

**The passage.** Joseph brings a **bad** (ra) **report** (dibbah) (37:2); the father loves him more, "his brothers **hated** (sane) him and could not (yakhol) speak **peacefully** (shalom)" (37:4); the first dream — "they **hated** (sane) him even more" (37:5); "**Hear** (shama) this dream" (37:6); the sheaves bow — "are you to **rule** (mashal) over us? they **hated** (sane) him even more" (37:8); the sun and moon bow; "his brothers were **jealous** (qana), but his father **kept** (shamar) the saying" (37:11).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **dibbah (report) + ra (bad) — Joseph's *tale-bearing* (37:2).** "Joseph brought a **bad** (ra) **report** (dibbah) of them" *(stated)*. **dibbah** — *slander/evil report*; **ra** — *bad*: the gem is that the *first note on Joseph's interior is not innocent* — he *carries a bad report* of his brothers to the father; whether justified or tale-bearing, the text opens on Joseph as a *bringer of an evil report* — the favoured son *implicated* in the rift, not merely its victim *(inferred: dibbah/ra = Joseph's bad report — the favoured son implicated in the rift)*.
- **sane (hated) + yakhol (could not) + shalom (peacefully) — the *hatred that disables peace* (37:4).** "they **hated** (sane) him and **could not** (yakhol) speak **peacefully** (shalom)" *(stated)*. **sane** — *hate* (the *first*, over the *father's* favour); **yakhol** — the *inability*; **shalom** — *peace*: the gem is that hatred *disables the very speech of peace* — the brothers *cannot* say a peaceful word; the interior's hatred *ruptures relationship at the level of speech* *(inferred: sane/yakhol/shalom = the hatred that makes peaceable speech impossible)*.
- **sane (hated even more) — the hatred *compounded by the dream* (37:5).** *sane distinct from 37:4:* "when he told the dream, they **hated** (sane) him **even more**" *(stated)* — the gem is the *escalation*: the hatred *grows* with the *dream* — a *second* increment (over the favour, now over the dream). The interior's hatred *feeds on each provocation* *(inferred: sane = the hatred compounded by the first dream)*.
- **shama (Hear) — the dream *told* (37:6).** "'**Hear** (shama) this dream I have dreamed'" *(stated)* — shama, *hear*: Joseph *tells the dream* to the brothers who already hate him — whether naïve or provoking, the *telling* pours oil on the fire; the interior *broadcasting the dominion-dream* to its resenters *(inferred: shama = the dream told to the resentful)*.
- **mashal (rule) + sane (hated even more) — the *dominion-claim* resented (37:8).** "'are you to **rule** (mashal) over us?' they **hated** (sane) him even more for his dreams and his words" *(stated)*. **mashal** — *rule/dominion*: the brothers *read the dream as a supremacy-claim* — the sheaves bowing = *him ruling them*; **sane** *distinct from 37:4,5* — the *third* increment, now over *"his dreams **and** his words"* (the dream *and* the telling). The gem: the hatred *at its height*, provoked by *resented dominion* *(inferred: mashal/sane = the dominion-claim resented, the hatred at its peak)*.
- **qana (jealous) + shamar (kept) — *jealousy* and the *father's pondering* (37:11).** "his brothers were **jealous** (qana), but his father **kept** (shamar) the saying" *(stated)*. **qana** — *be jealous/envious* (← the wives' rivalry, 30:1; the family envy); **shamar** — *keep/guard*: the gem is the *two responses* — the brothers *envy*, but Jacob *keeps/ponders* the saying (holding it, unsure). The interior's *jealousy* vs the father's *watchful retention* *(inferred: qana/shamar = the brothers' jealousy and the father's pondering)*.

### ★ Gate-1 recovery span (2026-07-05)

- **ahev (loved) — *the divided love that detonates the cycle* (37:3,4).** "Israel **loved** (ahev) Joseph more than all his sons… and when his brothers saw that their father **loved** him more than all his brothers, they hated him" *(stated)* — ahev, *love*: the first pass read the favour/coat (37:3) as scene-context and carried "divided love" only descriptively — but the *love-word itself* is the passage's first cause. The gem: the hatred (sane) the reading tracks in triple escalation is *love's shadow* — it is *because* the father **loves** Joseph more that the brothers cannot speak peace; the cycle's fourteen-chapter wound opens on a mis-shown `ahev`. It is the same divided love that ran the Jacob cycle (Rachel over Leah) now *inherited into the next generation*. `ahev` was un-registered, now recovered and onboarded to `mti_terms` (M05; method rule `wa-gate1-span-orphan-audit-method-rule-v1`) *(inferred: ahev = the father's greater love — the first cause of the brothers' hatred)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **the rift opened (dibbah/ra 37:2 → sane/yakhol/shalom 37:4):** the favoured son *brings a bad report*, the father *loves him more*, and the brothers' *hatred disables peaceable speech* — the relationship *ruptured*.
2. **the hatred compounds (sane 37:5 → shama 37:6 → mashal/sane 37:8):** each *dream told* *raises* the hatred — over the *dream*, then over the *dream and the words* — the brothers reading a *resented dominion*; hatred *feeding on each provocation* to its peak.
3. **jealousy and pondering (qana/shamar 37:11):** the brothers *envy*; the father *keeps the saying* — the two interiors that will carry the cycle: the *envy* that will sell him, and the *retention* that will grieve.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** dibbah/ra (Joseph's *bad report* — the favoured son *implicated*); **sane ×3 *distinguished* — the *escalation* (37:4 over the *favour* / 37:5 over the *dream* / 37:8 over the *dream and words*), hatred *compounding on each provocation***; yakhol/shalom (the hatred that *disables peaceable speech*); shama (the dream *told* to the resentful); mashal (the *dominion-claim* resented); qana (the brothers' *jealousy*); shamar (the father's *pondering* retention).
- **Whose interior (multi-interior):** the **brothers** — *hatred escalating* and *jealousy* (the favoured brother resented); **Joseph** — *favoured, tale-bearing, and dream-telling* (implicated, not merely victim); **Jacob** — *divided love* (loves Joseph more) and *pondering* (keeps the saying). The family patterns continue — divided love (← Jacob/Esau, Rachel/Leah) and jealousy (← the wives).
- **Threads:** *divided love* (37:3 ← Isaac/Rebekah's split love 25:28; Jacob's love of Rachel — the recurring family fault); *jealousy* (qana 37:11 ← the wives' rivalry 30:1); *dominion resented* (mashal 37:8 → the dreams *fulfilled* when the brothers bow, JOS-08; 42:6); the *bad report* (dibbah → the brothers' guilt they'll carry); the father's *pondering* (shamar 37:11 → his *grief* when Joseph is "killed", JOS-02); *shalom*'s absence (→ its restoration at the reconciliation).
- **Guarded against:** lumping the three sane into one "hatred" (the *escalation* — each "even more" — is the finding); reading Joseph as purely innocent when the text opens on his *bad report* and *dream-telling* (implicated); over-reading the dreams as Joseph's *arrogance* when the text states the *dreams* and the *brothers' reading* of them (I read what is given — the dream, the telling, the resented dominion); importing the later fulfilment back as Joseph's *intent* here; treating shamar (37:11) as mere memory when it is *watchful retention* (the father *holds* the saying, unsure).

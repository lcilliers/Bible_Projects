## ABR-10-sodom-lot · Genesis 19:1–38 · [the interior that clings to the condemned] — the perverted knowing, grace seizing the lingerer, the backward look, and rescue for Abraham's sake

**The passage.** Lot "**met** (qara) them… **turn aside** (sur)" (19:1–2); the mob: "that we may **know** (yada) them… **called** (qara) out" (19:5); "do not act **wickedly** (raa)… do to them as is **good** (tov) in your eyes… who have not **known** (yada) a man" (19:7–8); "he plays the **judge** (shaphat)… **break** (shavar) the door… deal **worse** (raa)" (19:9); they "**wore themselves out** (laah)" (19:11); "we will **destroy** (shachat)… the outcry is **great** (gadal)" (19:13); "about to **destroy** (shachat)" (19:14); "the **punishment** (avon)" (19:15); "he lingered… the men **seized** (chazaq) him, the LORD being **merciful** (chemlah)… **set** (nuach) him outside" (19:16); "escape for your **life** (nephesh)" (19:17); "**favor** (chen)… great **kindness** (chesed)… **saving** (chayah) my **life** (nephesh)… I **cannot** (yakhol)… lest **disaster** (raah)" (19:19); "Lot's wife looked back… pillar of salt" (19:26); "God **destroyed** (shachat)… God **remembered** (zakhar) Abraham" (19:29); "Lot was **afraid** (yare)" (19:30); "**preserve** (chayah) offspring… he did not **know** (yada)" (19:32–35).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **qara (met) + sur (turn aside) — hospitality *in the wicked city* (19:1, 19:2, 19:3).** The finding is Lot "**met** (qara) them" (19:1) and pressed "**turn aside** (sur, 19:2)… to your servant's house", and they "**turned aside** (sur, 19:3) to him" *(stated)*. The operation is hospitality *echoing Abraham's* (18:2) — but in *Sodom*: Lot *insists* (sur, urging them to lodge, 19:2) and they at last *turn in* (19:3) — the righteous impulse *in the corrupt place* *(inferred: qara-encounter + sur = the urging to shelter, offered and accepted)*.
- **yada (know) #1 — the conjugal word *perverted into assault* (19:5).** The finding is the mob's "bring them out, that we may **know** (yada) them" *(stated)*. The gem is *yada* — the *begetting* knowing of 4:1 — now **perverted into intended rape/violence**: the city's depravity turns the intimate-knowing word into assault *(inferred: knowing-as-violation; the conjugal yada corrupted)*.
- **raa (wickedly) — Lot names the evil (19:7).** The finding is "do not act so **wickedly** (raa)" *(stated)*. The operation is *raa* (be/do evil, ← 6:5 the heart's evil) — Lot *pleading against* the wickedness *(inferred)*.
- **tov (good) + yada (known) — Lot's *monstrous* offer (19:8).** The finding is "I have two daughters who have not **known** (yada) a man… do to them as is **good** (tov) in your eyes" *(stated)*. The operations: **yada** — the daughters *unrealized* conjugally (virgins); **tov** — the *subjective* "good in your eyes" (← 16:6) — Lot's hospitality *curdled into monstrosity*: offering his daughters to protect the guests. The compromised interior *(inferred: tov handed to the mob's discretion; hospitality gone wrong)*.
- **shaphat (judge) + shavar (break) + raa (worse) — the mob's escalation (19:9).** The finding is "this fellow… plays the **judge** (shaphat)… we will deal **worse** (raa) with you… they pressed to **break** (shavar) the door" *(stated)*. The operations: **shaphat** — the mob *mocking Lot's moral judgment* (← 18:25 the Judge; here men resenting judgment); **raa #2** — the *escalating* evil; **shavar** — the *breaking* violence *(inferred)*.
- **laah (wore themselves out) — depravity that *persists past blindness* (19:11).** The finding is the men struck blind "**wore themselves out** (laah) groping for the door" *(stated)*. The gem is *laah* (weary/exhaust) — even *blinded*, the mob *exhausts itself* seeking the door: **sin that persists past all restraint** — the hardened interior (← the fall's hardening) *(inferred)*.
- **shachat (destroy) ×2 + gadal + avon — the judgment announced, unheeded (19:13–15).** The finding is "we will **destroy** (shachat) this place, for the **outcry** has become **great** (gadal)" (19:13); "the LORD is about to **destroy** (shachat)" — but Lot "seemed to be jesting" (19:14); "swept away in the **punishment** (avon)" (19:15) *(stated)*. The operations: **shachat** — the *destruction* (← GEN-10; the shachat Abraham pleaded against, 18:28); **gadal** — the *great* outcry (← 18:20 zeaqah); **avon** — the *punishment* (← 4:13; 15:16); and the warning *unheeded* (the sons-in-law think Lot *jests*) *(inferred)*.
- **chazaq + chemlah + nuach — grace *seizes the lingerer* (19:16).** The finding is "he **lingered**. So the men **seized** (chazaq) him… the LORD being **merciful** (chemlah)… and **set** (nuach) him outside" *(stated)*. The gem is the whole rescue: Lot *lingers* (attached to the doomed city); God's **compassion** (chemlah — first occurrence) **seizes** (chazaq — firm grip) his hand and **sets** (nuach — places at rest, ← 2:15/8:4) him *out*. **Mercy drags the reluctant interior to safety** — salvation *against* the self's clinging *(inferred: chemlah = divine pity; chazaq = the grip of rescue; nuach = set in safety)*.
- **nephesh (life) — the life to be saved (19:17).** The finding is "escape for your **life** (nephesh)" *(stated)*. The operation is nephesh as *Lot's life* (← 12:5; life-sense) — the thing the flight is *for* *(inferred)*.
- **chen + chesed + chayah + nephesh + yakhol + raah — Lot's fearful bargaining (19:19).** The finding is "your servant has found **favor** (chen)… you have shown great **kindness** (chesed) in **saving** (chayah) my **life** (nephesh); but I **cannot** (yakhol) escape to the hills, lest **disaster** (raah) overtake me" *(stated)*. The gem is **chesed — the first "steadfast kindness/love" in Scripture** — God's *loyal mercy* in saving Lot; alongside **chen** (favor, ← 6:8; 18:3), **chayah** (life-saving), **nephesh** (the life), **yakhol** (Lot's *fearful inability* — "I cannot reach the hills", ← 13:6 scarcity, here *fear*), and **raah** (the *disaster* feared). The rescued interior, *even in grace, bargains from fear* — pleading for the little city rather than the hills *(inferred: chesed = the great loyal-love word, here first; Lot's fear qualifies even his gratitude)*.
- **chayah (saved) + nephesh + yakhol + qara — Zoar, the little refuge (19:20, 19:22).** The finding is "let me escape there… my **life** (nephesh) may be **saved** (chayah) (19:20)… I **can** (yakhol) do nothing till you arrive… the city was **called** (qara) Zoar (19:22)" *(stated)*. The operations: chayah/nephesh — the life saved in the little city (19:20); **yakhol** — now the *angel's* "I **cannot** do anything till you arrive" (19:22 — the rescue *waits* on Lot); qara — the place *named* Zoar ("little", 19:22) for its littleness *(inferred)*.
- **(19:26 — Lot's wife looks back: the backward look, no span.)** Flagged: "looked back" (nabat) carries no non-T2 span, but it is the passage's *emblem*: told "do not look back" (19:17), she **looks back** and becomes salt — **the heart still turned to the condemned**, the interior that *cannot leave behind* what God destroys, *frozen* in its backward attachment *(no-span context; the clinging look)*.
- **zakhar + shachat — rescue *for Abraham's sake* (19:29).** The finding is "when God **destroyed** (shachat) the cities… God **remembered** (zakhar) Abraham and sent Lot out" *(stated)*. The gem is *zakhar* (← 8:1 God remembered Noah): **Lot is saved because God *remembered Abraham*** — the intercession (ABR-09, 18:22–33) *bears fruit*; the righteous man's prayer saves his kin. Lot's rescue rests *not on Lot's merit* but on *God's remembering the intercessor* *(inferred: intercession's fruit; the destruction (shachat) executed, yet the nephew spared for the uncle's sake)*.
- **yare — Lot's *fear*, and the irony of the hills (19:30).** The finding is "Lot was **afraid** (yare) to live in Zoar, so he lived in the hills" *(stated)*. The gem is the irony: Lot *refused* the hills (he *couldn't*, yakhol, 19:19) — now *fear* (yare) drives him *to* them; the fearful interior contradicting itself *(inferred)*.
- **chayah (preserve) + yada — the *corrupted line*, and its naming (19:32, 19:33, 19:34, 19:35, 19:37, 19:38).** The finding is the daughters' "let us… **preserve** (chayah, 19:32) offspring through our father… he did not **know** (yada, 19:33) when she lay down"; the second night "**preserve** (chayah, 19:34) offspring… he did not **know** (yada, 19:35)"; and the sons **called** (qara) Moab (19:37) and Ben-ammi (19:38) *(stated)*. The gem is the double corruption: **chayah** — *"preserving seed"* twisted into *incest* (the daughters, thinking the world ended, contrive to continue the line, both nights, 19:32,34); **yada** — the *conjugal* knowing (4:1) now in *drunken darkness*, "he did not know" (19:33,35) — begetting-in-oblivion; and the *naming* (qara) of Moab and Ben-ammi (19:37–38) records the line's shameful origin. The Sodom-corrupted interior *produces corruption even in its escape* *(inferred: chayah-preserve perverted; yada-in-oblivion; the qara-naming fixing the origin)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **the depraved city (yada-assault 19:5, laah 19:11, raa/shavar 19:9):** the intimate-knowing word *perverted into rape*, evil that *persists past blindness*, the door *broken* — total depravity; and Lot's hospitality *curdles* (offering his daughters, tov 19:8) — the compromised righteous man *in* the corruption.
2. **grace seizes the lingerer (chazaq/chemlah/nuach 19:16):** Lot *clings* to the doomed city; God's *compassion* (chemlah) *seizes* (chazaq) and *sets him out* (nuach) — mercy saving *against* the self's reluctance.
3. **fear qualifies even the rescued (chen/chesed/yakhol/yare 19:19,30):** saved by *steadfast kindness* (chesed, first), Lot still *bargains from fear* (yakhol, "I cannot") for the little city — then *fears* (yare) it and flees to the very hills he refused; the fearful interior contradicting itself.
4. **the backward look vs the forward rescue (19:26):** told to flee and not look back, Lot's wife *looks back* — the heart *turned to the condemned* — and is *frozen*; the interior that will not leave what God judges.
5. **saved for the intercessor's sake (zakhar 19:29):** the *shachat* Abraham pleaded against *falls* — yet Lot is spared because *God remembered Abraham*; the intercession bears fruit even as the judgment lands.
6. **corruption survives the escape (chayah/yada 19:32–35):** rescued *from* Sodom, Lot's family *carries Sodom out* — "preserving seed" through incest, begetting *in oblivion*; deliverance from the place is not deliverance from its corruption.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** qara/sur (hospitality *in* the wicked city); **yada — the conjugal word *perverted*** (assault 19:5; the incest-in-oblivion 19:33–35) vs the daughters *unrealized* (19:8); tov as *monstrous* discretion; shaphat (the mob *mocking* judgment); laah — *depravity persisting past blindness*; shachat (announced/unheeded/executed) + gadal + avon; **chazaq/chemlah/nuach — grace *seizing the lingerer*** (chemlah first); **chesed — the *first* steadfast-kindness**; chen/chayah/nephesh/yakhol/raah — *fearful* bargaining; the *backward look* (no-span); **zakhar — Lot saved because *God remembered Abraham*** (intercession's fruit); yare — Lot's *self-contradicting* fear; **chayah-preserve + yada — the *corrupted line*** (Moab/Ammon).
- **Whose interior:** the **human** interior *clinging to the condemned* (Lot lingering, his wife looking back) and *carrying corruption out of judgment*; the *depraved* city; and the **divine** interior *merciful* (chemlah, chesed), *seizing to save*, and *remembering the intercessor*.
- **Threads:** yada (← 4:1 conjugal, here perverted); chemlah/chesed (first here — the mercy/steadfast-love words → carried through the study); zakhar (← 8:1; intercession's fruit ← ABR-09); shachat (← GEN-10; 13:10 foreshadowed; 18:28 pleaded-against, now fallen); yare (← 3:10; Lot's fear); the backward look (→ the clinging-to-the-judged motif); tov "good in your eyes" (← 16:6).
- **Guarded against:** claiming operations off the no-span backward look (read as emblem-context); merging the four yada (assault / unrealized / oblivion) or the four chayah (saving-life / preserving-seed); flattening chesed's first appearance; missing that Lot is saved *for Abraham's sake* (zakhar, not Lot's merit); moralising beyond the text (the narrator *shows* Lot's compromise, his wife's look, the incest, without a stated verdict — I read the operations); importing NT "remember Lot's wife" (Luke 17) — the operation the verse gives.

## JOS-13-jacob-pharaoh-famine · Genesis 47:1–31 · [the patriarch blesses the king; Egypt enslaved, Israel multiplied; the pull to the land] — few and evil my days, and bury me with my fathers

**The passage.** "If you **know** (yada) **able** (chayil) men, set them over my livestock" (47:6); Jacob **blessed** (barakh) Pharaoh (47:7); "how many the days of your **life** (chayyim)?" (47:8); "few and **evil** have been the days of my **life** (chayyim)" (47:9); Jacob **blessed** (barakh) Pharaoh (47:10); Joseph **gave** (natan) a possession (47:11); "**give** (natan) your livestock… I will **give** (natan) food" (47:16, 47:17); "**give** (natan) us seed, that we may **live** (chayah)" (47:19); the famine **severe** (chazaq), the land became Pharaoh's (47:20); the priests' allowance Pharaoh **gave** (natan) (47:22); "**give** (natan) a fifth to Pharaoh" (47:24); "you have saved our **lives** (chayah)… may it **please** (chen) my lord, we will be servants" (47:25); Israel **multiplied** (ravah) in Goshen (47:27); Jacob's **life** (chayyim) 147 years, he **lived** (chayah) (47:28); "if I have found **favor** (chen)… deal **kindly** (chesed) and **truly** (emet)… bury me with my fathers" — he **called** (qara) Joseph (47:29).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **yada (know) + chayil (able) — Pharaoh's *trust extended* (47:6).** "'if you **know** (yada) any **able** (chayil) men, set them over my livestock'" *(stated)*. **yada** — *know*; **chayil** — *strength/capability*: Pharaoh *entrusts his own herds* to Joseph's kin — the interior-context of *favour extended to the family for Joseph's sake* *(inferred: yada/chayil = the able men entrusted with Pharaoh's livestock)*.
- **barakh (blessed) — the *patriarch blesses the king* (47:7).** "Jacob **blessed** (barakh) Pharaoh" *(stated)* — barakh, *bless*: the gem is the *inversion* — the *nomad shepherd* **blesses the king of Egypt**; the *lesser (in worldly power) blessing the greater* — Jacob a *conduit of the Abrahamic blessing* to Pharaoh (← "in you all families blessed", 12:3; Joseph blessing Potiphar's house 39:5). The interior *bestowing blessing from above its station* *(inferred: barakh = the patriarch's blessing on the king — the blessing-conduit)*.
- **chayyim (life) — Pharaoh's *question* about the years (47:8).** "'how many are the days of the years of your **life** (chayyim)?'" *(stated)* — chayyim, *life*: Pharaoh *asks the old man's age* — the occasion of Jacob's *self-assessment* *(inferred: chayyim = the years asked after)*.
- **chayyim (life) — Jacob's *sober self-judgment* (47:9).** *chayyim distinct from 47:8:* "'**few and evil** have been the days of the years of my **life** (chayyim)… not attaining my fathers' years'" *(stated)* — the gem: Jacob names his life *"few and evil"* (ra) — a *sober, honest self-judgment* of a *hard, grasping, grieving life* (the deceptions, the exile, the losses); the interior *reckoning its own days as hard* (contrast Abraham "full of years", 25:8) — a *pilgrim's weary honesty* *(inferred: chayyim = the life reckoned "few and evil" — Jacob's sober self-judgment)*.
- **barakh (blessed) — the *departing blessing* (47:10).** *barakh distinct from 47:7:* "Jacob **blessed** (barakh) Pharaoh and went out" *(stated)* — the *second* blessing *brackets* the audience (entering and leaving) — the interior *blessing the king again on departure* *(inferred: barakh = the departing blessing — the audience bracketed by blessing)*.
- **natan (gave) — the *possession given* (47:11).** "Joseph **gave** (natan) them a possession in the best of the land" *(stated)* — natan, *give*: the *settling* of the family — the interior-context of *Joseph's provision* *(inferred: natan = the possession given to the family)*.
- **natan (give ×2) — the *livestock-for-food exchange* (47:16, 47:17).** *natan distinct:* "'**give** (natan) your livestock, I will **give** (natan) food'… he **gave** (natan) food for the livestock" *(stated)* — the *famine economy's second stage* — the Egyptians *trading livestock for bread*; the interior-context of *the desperation deepening* *(inferred: natan = the livestock exchanged for food)*.
- **natan (give) + chayah (live) — the *last resort: land and selves* (47:19).** *natan/chayah distinct:* "'**give** (natan) us seed that we may **live** (chayah)… buy us and our land'" *(stated)*. **natan** — the plea for *seed*; **chayah** — *live*: the gem is the *final surrender* — the Egyptians *sell their land and themselves* to survive; the interior *reduced to servitude by the famine* *(inferred: natan/chayah = the land and selves surrendered to live)*.
- **chazaq (severe) — the famine's *pressure* (47:20).** "the famine was **severe** (chazaq)… the land became Pharaoh's" *(stated)* — chazaq, *severe/strong*: the *pressure* that *centralizes all Egypt under Pharaoh* — the interior-context of *the crisis that makes the people Pharaoh's servants* (and, the dark irony, *concentrates the Pharaonic power* that will later *enslave Israel*) *(inferred: chazaq = the famine's severity that reduces Egypt to Pharaoh's servitude)*.
- **natan (gave) — the *priests' exemption* (47:22).** *natan distinct:* "the priests' allowance Pharaoh **gave** (natan) them; they did not sell" *(stated)* — the *one exempt class* — the interior-context of *the priests kept from the servitude* (a note on the policy's limit) *(inferred: natan = the priests' allowance — the exemption)*.
- **natan (give) — the *fifth to Pharaoh* (47:24).** *natan distinct:* "'at harvest, **give** (natan) a fifth to Pharaoh'" *(stated)* — the *statute* of the fifth — the interior-context of *the settled tenancy* under Pharaoh *(inferred: natan = the fifth rendered to Pharaoh — the settled order)*.
- **chayah (live) + chen (please) — the *grateful servitude* (47:25).** *chayah distinct from 47:19; chen:* "'you have saved our **lives** (chayah)… may it **please** (chen) my lord, we will be servants'" *(stated)*. **chayah** — *life* (saved); **chen** — *favour/grace*: the gem is the *gratitude in servitude* — the Egyptians *thank Joseph for saving their lives*, *content to be Pharaoh's servants*; the interior *grateful even as it is reduced* — bread valued above freedom *(inferred: chayah/chen = the grateful servitude — lives saved, freedom yielded)*.
- **ravah (multiplied) — *Israel fruitful in Goshen* (47:27).** "Israel **multiplied** (ravah) greatly in Goshen" *(stated)* — ravah, *multiply* (← Gen 1:28; the creation-blessing): the gem is the *contrast* — *while Egypt is reduced to servitude, Israel multiplies* — the *seed becoming a nation* (← the seventy, 46:27; → Exo 1:7, the *exact* echo "fruitful and multiplied"). The interior-context of *the blessed seed growing* even in Egypt *(inferred: ravah = Israel multiplying in Goshen — the seed becoming a nation)*.
- **chayyim (life) + chayah (lived) — *Jacob's total years* (47:28).** *chayyim distinct from 47:8,9; chayah:* "Jacob **lived** (chayah) in Egypt seventeen years; his **life** (chayyim) 147 years" *(stated)* — the *span* of Jacob's life closing — the interior-context of *the pilgrim's years numbered* *(inferred: chayyim/chayah = Jacob's total years, the life closing)*.
- **chen (favor) + chesed (kindly) + emet (truly) + qara (called) — the *burial-oath: the pull to the land* (47:29).** *chen distinct from 47:25:* "he **called** (qara) Joseph: 'if I have found **favor** (chen)… deal **kindly** (chesed) and **truly** (emet) with me: do not bury me in Egypt… but with my fathers'" *(stated)*. **qara** — the *summons*; **chen** — *favour* sought; **chesed** — *steadfast kindness*; **emet** — *truth/faithfulness* (the covenant-character pair, ← 24:27,49; 32:10): the gem is the *pull toward the land* — Jacob, *dying in Egypt*, *binds Joseph by oath* to *bury him in Canaan with his fathers*; the interior's *faith looks beyond Egypt* — *Egypt is not home; the promised land is* (the exile's temporary-ness held in faith) *(inferred: chen/chesed/emet/qara = the burial-oath — the dying faith that looks past Egypt to the land)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **the patriarch blesses the king (yada/chayil 47:6 → barakh 47:7 → chayyim 47:8 → chayyim 47:9 → barakh 47:10):** Pharaoh *entrusts his herds* to the family; and Jacob *blesses the king* (entering and leaving) — the *conduit of blessing from above his station* — while reckoning his own life *"few and evil"* (the pilgrim's sober honesty).
2. **the famine reduces Egypt (natan 47:11 → natan 47:16, 47:17 → natan/chayah 47:19 → chazaq 47:20 → natan 47:22 → natan 47:24 → chayah/chen 47:25):** the Egyptians *sell money, livestock, land, and themselves* to survive — *grateful even in servitude* ("you saved our lives"); Joseph's policy *centralizes Egypt under Pharaoh* (the dark irony seeding the later bondage).
3. **Israel multiplies (ravah 47:27):** *while Egypt is enslaved, Israel is fruitful and multiplies* in Goshen — the *seed becoming a nation* (the Exodus echo).
4. **the pull to the land (chayyim/chayah 47:28 → chen/chesed/emet/qara 47:29):** the dying Jacob *binds Joseph by oath* to *bury him with his fathers, not in Egypt* — the interior's *faith looks past Egypt to the promised land*; *Egypt is not home*.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** yada/chayil (the *able men* entrusted); **barakh ×2 (the patriarch's *entering* / *departing* blessing on the king — the blessing-conduit)**; chayyim ×3 (Pharaoh's *question* / Jacob's *"few and evil"* self-judgment / the *147 years*); natan ×6 (the *famine economy* — possession / livestock-for-food ×2 / seed / priests' allowance / the fifth); chazaq (the famine's *severity* reducing Egypt to servitude); chayah ×3 (that-we-may-*live* / *lives*-saved / Jacob *lived*); chen ×2 (the Egyptians' *"please"* / Jacob's *"favor"*); **ravah (Israel *multiplying* in Goshen — the seed → nation, while Egypt is enslaved)**; **chesed/emet (the *burial-oath* — the dying faith that looks past Egypt to the land)**; qara (the *summons* for the oath).
- **Whose interior (multi-interior):** **Jacob** — *blessing the king* (conduit), the *sober "few and evil"* self-judgment, and the *faith that looks to the land* (the burial-oath); the **Egyptians** — *desperation → grateful servitude* (bread above freedom); **Israel** — *multiplying* (the blessed seed); **Joseph** — the *steward* administering the famine. The Israel-multiplied / Egypt-enslaved *contrast* is the pivot.
- **Threads:** the **blessing-conduit** (barakh 47:7,10 ← 12:3; 39:5; Joseph blessing Egypt — Jacob blesses Pharaoh); the **seed → nation** (ravah 47:27 ← the seventy 46:27; Gen 1:28 → Exo 1:7 "fruitful and multiplied" — the *exact* echo; the bondage narrative's opening); the **dark irony** (chazaq 47:20 — Joseph's policy *concentrates the Pharaonic power* that will *enslave Israel*, Exo 1); **chesed-and-emet** (47:29 ← 24:27; 32:10 — the covenant-character pair); the **pull to the land** (the burial-oath 47:29 → Jacob buried in Machpelah 50:13; the exile *temporary* in faith); Jacob's *sober life* (47:9 — the grasping, grieving pilgrim's honest reckoning ← the whole Jacob cycle).
- **Guarded against:** lumping the six natan (the distinct stages of the famine economy) or the pairs (two barakh/chen, three chayyim/chayah); reading a "shame" operation into the mis-glossed second chesed (the word is *kindness*); missing the **Israel-multiplied / Egypt-enslaved contrast** (the blessed seed vs the servitude) and the **Exodus echo** (ravah 47:27 → Exo 1:7); over-reading the famine-policy into a moral verdict on Joseph (the text *reports* the centralization; the *irony* — it seeds the later bondage — is a thread, not a condemnation); treating Jacob's "few and evil" (47:9) as mere complaint when it is a *pilgrim's sober self-judgment*; importing later "Egypt = the world" typology over the *faith-looks-to-the-land* the burial-oath gives.

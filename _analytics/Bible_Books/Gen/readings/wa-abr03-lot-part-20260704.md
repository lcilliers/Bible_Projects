## ABR-03-lot-part · Genesis 13:1–18 · [yielding vs grasping] — the return to the first altar, the strife refused, the choice let go, and the inheritance given to the one who did not grasp

**The passage.** Abram returns "to the place where his tent had been at the **beginning** (techillah)… where he had made an altar at the first, and there Abram **called upon** (qara) the name of the LORD" (13:3–4); "the land **could not** (yakhol) support both" (13:6); "there was **strife** (riv)" (13:7); "let there be no **strife** (merivah)… is not the whole land before you?" (13:8–9); "Lot lifted up his eyes and saw… (this was before the LORD **destroyed** (shachat) Sodom)" (13:10); "Lot **chose** (bachar) for himself all the Jordan Valley" (13:11); "the men of Sodom were… **sinners** (chatta)" (13:13); "all the land… I will **give** (natan) to you… if one **can** (yakhol) count the dust… I will **give** (natan) it to you" (13:15–17).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **techillah + qara — the return to the *first* altar and worship (13:3, 13:4).** The finding is Abram returning "to the place… at the **beginning** (techillah, 13:3)… and there **called upon** (qara, 13:4) the name of the LORD" *(stated)*. The operation is the interior *re-establishing itself in worship after a lapse*: fresh from the Egypt compromise (ABR-02), Abram goes *back* to where he first worshipped and *calls on the Name again* (← 12:8; 4:26). The gem is the *return* — worship resumed at the original altar; techillah marks the going-back-to-the-first *(inferred: the interior recovers by returning to its first worship)*.
- **yakhol (be able) #1 — the land's *limit* (13:6).** The finding is "the land **could not** (yakhol) support both of them" *(stated)*. The operation is *inability as scarcity* — the material limit that forces the parting; the pressure that occasions the test *(inferred)*.
- **riv (strife) — the contention that arises (13:7).** The finding is "there was **strife** (riv) between the herdsmen" *(stated)*. The operation is *strife/contention* breaking out between the two households — the first riv in Scripture *(inferred)*.
- **merivah (strife) — the strife *refused* between kin (13:8).** The finding is "let there be no **strife** (merivah) between you and me… for we are kinsmen" *(stated)*. The operation is Abram *refusing to let strife stand* — the peacemaking interior; distinct from riv (13:7, the strife that *is*) — merivah is the strife Abram *will not permit* to divide kin *(inferred)*.
- **(13:9 — Abram yields the choice: generosity, no span.)** "Is not the whole land before you? Separate… if you take the left, I will go right." Flagged: no non-T2 span, but the anchored *act* is **Abram's yielding** — the senior man and promise-bearer *gives the first choice to the younger*; the generous, un-grasping interior. Read as context, the pivot the spans frame *(no-span context)*.
- **shachat (destroyed) — the doomed land, foreshadowed (13:10).** The finding is the narrator's note that Lot saw the valley "before the LORD **destroyed** (shachat) Sodom" *(stated)*. The operation is *dramatic irony*: the land Lot is about to choose *by sight* is the land God will *destroy* (shachat, ← the flood-ruin, GEN-10) — the grasped prize is already condemned *(inferred)*.
- **bachar (chose) — Lot's self-serving *choice by sight* (13:11).** The finding is "Lot **chose** (bachar) for himself all the Jordan Valley" *(stated)*. The gem is the pattern: Lot "lifted up his eyes and *saw*… and *chose for himself*" — the eye-driven grasping of the fall (3:6, "saw… good… took"; 6:2, "saw… took… chose", bachar there too) — self-interest choosing by *appearance*, and toward the doomed and the sinful. Distinct bachar from 6:2 (unbounded taking of wives): here *choosing the best portion for oneself* *(inferred: choose-by-sight-for-self — the grasping interior)*.
- **chatta (sinners) — the moral character of the chosen place (13:13).** The finding is "the men of Sodom were wicked, great **sinners** (chatta) against the LORD" *(stated)*. The operation names the *persons* Lot moves toward — chatta (the *sinner*, ← chattat, the crouching sin of 4:7) — the interior's self-chosen neighbourhood is the company of sinners; distinct from chattat (sin as a thing) — here sin *embodied in persons* *(inferred)*.
- **natan (give) ×2 — the inheritance *given* to the one who *let go* (13:15, 13:17).** The finding is "all the land that you see I will **give** (natan) to you and your offspring forever (13:15)… arise, walk… for I will **give** (natan) it to you (13:17)" *(stated)*. The gem is the *contrast with bachar*: Lot **chose** the best portion *for himself* (13:11); after Abram **yielded**, God **gives** Abram *all the land, forever* (natan ×2) — the promissory land-gift (← 12:7) now *comprehensive*. **The one who did not grasp receives what grasping could never secure** — the *given* exceeds the *chosen* *(inferred: natan (God's gift) set against bachar (Lot's grab) — the passage's centre)*.
- **yakhol (be able) #2 — the *uncountable* offspring (13:16).** The finding is "if one **can** (yakhol) count the dust of the earth, your offspring **can** be counted" *(stated)*. The gem is *yakhol inverted*: at 13:6 inability meant *scarcity* (the land *couldn't* hold both); here inability means *superabundance* (no one *can* count the offspring) — the same ability-word swung from limit to limitless *(inferred: scarcity → superabundance, the same yakhol)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **return to worship after the lapse (techillah/qara 13:3–4):** the Egypt-compromised interior (ABR-02) *recovers by returning* to its first altar and calling on the Name — worship as the interior's re-establishment.
2. **strife refused for the sake of kinship (riv → merivah 13:7–8):** contention arises; Abram *will not let it stand* between kin — the peacemaking that *precedes* his yielding.
3. **yielding vs grasping-by-sight (13:9–13):** Abram *lets go* the choice; Lot *lifts his eyes, sees, and chooses* (bachar) the best *for himself* — toward the doomed land (shachat) and the sinners (chatta). Two interiors: the un-grasping and the eye-driven self-server.
4. **the given exceeds the grasped (bachar 13:11 → natan 13:15–17):** Lot's *self-chosen* portion is set against God's *gift* to the one who *yielded* — Abram receives *all the land forever* and offspring beyond counting (yakhol). The interior that releases its claim is given more than the one that seized.
5. **scarcity → superabundance (yakhol 13:6 → 13:16):** the inability that *forced the parting* becomes the inability that *measures the gift* — the same word, from limit to limitless.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** techillah/qara as *return to the first worship* after the lapse; yakhol as *scarcity* (13:6) vs *superabundance* (13:16); riv (strife that is) vs merivah (strife *refused*); Abram's *yielding* (no-span pivot); shachat as the *doomed* chosen land; **bachar — Lot's choose-by-sight-for-self** (← the fall/6:2 grasping); chatta as the *sinners* moved-toward; **natan — the inheritance *given* to the one who did not grasp** (vs bachar).
- **Whose interior:** the **human** interior in two modes — the *un-grasping, peacemaking, worship-returning* (Abram) and the *eye-driven self-server* (Lot); and the **divine** interior *giving* the whole to the one who released his claim.
- **Threads:** qara-worship (← 12:8/4:26; the return after failure); bachar (← 6:2 grasping-by-choice); shachat (← GEN-10; → Sodom's destruction, ABR-10); chatta/sin (← 4:7); natan land-gift (← 12:7, now comprehensive); the eye-driven "saw and took/chose" (← 3:6/6:2 — Lot repeats it).
- **Guarded against:** merging riv/merivah (the strife that is vs the strife refused); merging the two yakhol (scarcity vs superabundance) or the two natan; flattening bachar into neutral "choosing" when it repeats the fall's grasping-by-sight; moralising beyond the text (the narrator *shows* Lot's choice and its doomed object without a verdict — I read the operation and the irony, not a sermon); importing later NT typology of Lot/Abraham.

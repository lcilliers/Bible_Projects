# Genesis 9:1–17 — inner-being passage reading (GEN-12-covenant-rainbow)

- **Source:** verse-first lexicals over the passage (provenance `genesis-primeval-v1-20260704`). Every span in its verse; individual + interactive operations; *stated* | *inferred*; distinctions kept.
- **Check-back note:** `berit` (covenant) recurs 6× (9:9,11,12,13,15,16,17) — as with the death-formula (GEN-08), this is **one covenant *hammered home*, the repetition itself the emphasis** (God's self-binding certainty); I name it once and reference the verses, without inventing six distinct covenants.

## GEN-12-covenant-rainbow · Genesis 9:1–17 · [the re-founded order and the self-bound God] — the blessing renewed, dominion turned to dread, life in the blood, the image as law, and the covenant God gives himself to remember

**The passage.** "God **blessed** (barak) Noah… be fruitful and **multiply** (ravah)" (9:1); "the **fear** (morah) and **dread** (chat) of you shall be on every beast… they are **delivered** (natan) into your hand" (9:2); "every moving thing… I **give** (natan) you" (9:3); "you shall not eat flesh with its **life** (nephesh), its blood" (9:4); "for your **lifeblood** (nephesh) I will **require a reckoning** (darash)" (9:5); "whoever sheds the blood of man… for God made man in his own **image** (tselem)" (9:6); "be fruitful and **multiply** (ravah)" (9:7); "I establish my **covenant** (berit)… never again… **destroy** (shachat)… I will **remember** (zakhar) my covenant" (9:9–17).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **barak + ravah (9:1, 9:7) — the human blessing *renewed*.** The finding is God "**blessed** (barak) Noah… be fruitful and **multiply** (ravah)" (9:1), restated at 9:7 *(stated)*. The operation is the creation-blessing of 1:28 (GEN-02) *resumed* — barak and ravah (now home to blessing, ← 8:17) bracket the new order at 9:1 and 9:7; life re-commissioned *(inferred)*.
- **morah + chat (fear + dread) — dominion turned to *dread* (9:2).** The finding is "the **fear** (morah) of you and the **dread** (chat) of you shall be on every beast… bird… fish" *(stated)*. The gem is the *shift* in the man–beast relation: in Eden man ruled as image-bearer (radah, 1:26, GEN-02) — a benign dominion; now the animals *fear and dread* man. The operation is dominion become **fear-based** — the fallen relation, rule by terror rather than by representation *(inferred: morah and chat are two words for the animals' new terror; the first chat ("dread/terror") in this line — the beast's-eye view of fallen man)*.
- **natan (delivered/given) — the creatures handed over, the diet expanded (9:2, 9:3).** The finding is "they are **delivered** (natan) into your hand" (9:2) and "every moving thing… I **give** (natan) you… as I gave you the green plants" (9:3) *(stated)*. The operation is natan as **handing-over for food** — a *further* natan-sense (provision 2:29 → transmission 3:6 → blame 3:12 → withheld-yield 4:12 → **delivered-into-hand / expanded provision** 9:2–3): the food-gift of 1:29 *widened* to include the now-fearful creatures *(inferred)*.
- **nephesh (life) in the blood — the soul located (9:4).** The finding is "you shall not eat flesh with its **life** (nephesh), that is, its blood" *(stated)*. The gem is that *nephesh* — the soul/life word (first of animals 1:20, of man 2:7) — is now **identified with the blood**: "its life, that is its blood." The operation locates the *nephesh* in the *blood*, and forbids consuming it — the life belongs to God, not to be eaten *(inferred: a key anthropological datum — the nephesh is *in the blood*; keep this sense distinct)*.
- **darash (require a reckoning) — God demands an accounting for lifeblood (9:5).** The finding is "for your **lifeblood** (nephesh) I will **require a reckoning** (darash)… from every beast… from man" *(stated)*. The operation is the **first darash** — God *seeking/requiring* an accounting for shed life; the sanctity of the nephesh *enforced* by God himself as the avenger of blood *(inferred)*.
- **tselem (image) — the image now *grounds the murder-law* (9:6).** The finding is "whoever sheds the blood of man, by man shall his blood be shed, **for God made man in his own image** (tselem)" *(stated)*. The gem is the image-operation become *the basis of law*: killing a man is capital *because* man is God's **image** — the dignity of the tselem (conferred 1:26; transmitted 5:3, GEN-08) makes murder an assault on God's *representation*. The image is now the ground of the *sanctity of human life* *(inferred: distinct tselem-use — conferred → transmitted → **legal ground**)*.
- **berit (covenant) ×6 — the covenant *hammered home* (9:9,11,12,13,15,16,17).** The finding is God establishing "my **covenant** (berit)" — "I establish my covenant with you (9:9)… I establish my covenant, never again shall all flesh be cut off (9:11)… this is the sign of the covenant (9:12)… the bow… the sign of the covenant (9:13)… I will remember my covenant (9:15)… the everlasting covenant (9:16)… this is the sign of the covenant (9:17)" *(stated)*. The operation is God's **self-binding pledge**, and the *sixfold repetition is itself the finding* — the covenant driven home to *over-assure*; God stakes his own word on it, restated until it cannot be doubted *(inferred: one covenant, hammered — not six; the repetition is the emphasis of certainty, as the death-formula's repetition was the emphasis of mortality)*.
- **zakhar (remember) ×2 — God gives *himself* a reminder (9:15, 9:16).** The finding is "I will **remember** (zakhar) my covenant… when the bow is in the clouds, I will **remember** the everlasting covenant" *(stated)*. The gem is that God's remembering (← 8:1, which *turned the flood*) is now **bound to the sign**: the bow is set so that *God* "remembers" — God gives *himself* the reminder, securing the covenant's permanence on his own remembering, not man's *(inferred: distinct from 8:1 — there a remembering that acted; here a *permanent, sign-triggered* remembering that guarantees)*.
- **shachat (destroy) ×2 — the destroy-word *renounced* (9:11, 9:15).** The finding is "never again shall all flesh be cut off… never again… a flood to **destroy** (shachat) the earth… the waters shall never again become a flood to **destroy** (shachat) all flesh" *(stated)*. The gem is that *shachat* — the verb of the flood (man corrupted, God destroyed, GEN-10 6:11–17) — is now **permanently negated**: God binds himself *never again to shachat*. The ruin man invited and God brought is renounced by covenant *(inferred: shachat un-done — the inversion pattern *reversed and sealed*)*.
- **the chay/nephesh cluster — the covenant is *cosmic* (9:10,12,15,16).** The finding is the covenant "with every **living creature** (nephesh chayyah) of all **flesh** (basar)… the birds, the livestock, every beast" *(stated)*. The operation is the covenant's *scope* — not with man alone but with **all living flesh**; the divine pledge embraces the whole creaturely order *(inferred)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **the re-founded order (9:1–7):** blessing renewed (barak/ravah), but the man–beast bond now *dread* (morah/chat) and the creatures *delivered* (natan) for food — dominion post-flood is *fear-based provision*, not Edenic representation.
2. **life, blood, and image (9:4–6):** the nephesh is *in the blood* (9:4), so God *requires a reckoning* (darash) for lifeblood (9:5), and the *image* (tselem) makes shedding human blood capital (9:6) — a tight web binding the sanctity of life to the blood that carries the nephesh and the image that dignifies the man.
3. **the covenant hammered + self-remembered + destroy-renounced (9:9–17):** berit ×6 (certainty), zakhar ×2 bound to the bow (God's own reminder), shachat ×2 renounced (never again) — God *over-assures*, *reminds himself*, and *renounces the ruin* — the divine interior securing the world against its own prior judgment.
4. **cosmic scope:** the whole nephesh-chayyah/basar order is held in the pledge — the covenant reaches every living thing, not man alone.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** barak/ravah *renewed* (← 1:28); dominion turned to *dread* (morah/chat, vs radah 1:26); natan as *delivered/expanded provision*; **nephesh located in the blood** (9:4); **darash** — God *requires a reckoning* for lifeblood; **tselem as the *legal ground*** of the murder-law (9:6); **berit ×6** as one covenant *hammered* (certainty the finding); **zakhar ×2** — God's *self-reminder* bound to the bow (← 8:1); **shachat ×2 renounced** (the flood-verb un-done); the *cosmic* scope of the covenant.
- **Whose interior:** the **divine** interior *binding and over-assuring itself* — blessing, pledging, self-reminding, renouncing destruction; and the **human** interior *re-commissioned* under a new, dread-marked dominion, its life declared sacred by the image.
- **Threads:** barak/ravah (← 1:28/8:17); tselem (← 1:26/5:3, now legal ground → the dignity of the image carried through the study); nephesh-in-the-blood (a new anchor for the soul/life word); berit (← 6:18/8's implied; the first *signed* covenant); zakhar (← 8:1, now permanent); shachat (← 6:11–17, now renounced).
- **Guarded against:** inventing six covenants from the sixfold berit (the repetition is the emphasis); reading zakhar as God's forgetting; flattening morah/chat into neutral "fear" when the animals' *terror* of man is the shift; missing that tselem here *grounds law*; over-reading darash; importing later sacrificial/atonement theology onto the blood-and-life material beyond what 9:4–6 state.

## JOS-04-potiphar-false-charge · Genesis 39:1–23 · [God-ward integrity, the garment turned to a lie] — the blessed house, the refusal, the false charge, and the LORD in the prison

**The passage.** Joseph found **favor** (chen), **attended** (sharat) Potiphar (39:4); the LORD **blessed** (barakh) the house — the **blessing** (berakhah) on all he had (39:5); Potiphar had no **concern** (yada) but his food (39:6); "my master has no **concern** (yada)… how can I do this **wickedness** (raah) and **sin** (chata) against God?" (39:8–9); "he would not **listen** (shama)" (39:10); she **called** (qara) the household — "he came to lie with me, I **cried out** (qara), he **heard** (shama) I **lifted** (rum) my voice" (39:14–15); she **laid up** (nuach) the garment (39:16); "I **cried** (qara)… **lifted** (rum) my voice" (39:18); Potiphar **heard** (shama), his anger **kindled** (charah) (39:19); in prison the LORD showed **steadfast love** (chesed), **favor** (chen), and **gave** (natan) it (39:21).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **chen (favor) + sharat (attended) — the *favour and honourable service* (39:4).** "Joseph found **favor** (chen)… **attended** (sharat) him… overseer of his house" *(stated)*. **chen** — *favour* (← the God-given favour, 3:21); **sharat** — *minister/serve* (an *honourable* service-word, distinct from the slavery `avad`): the gem is that even *as a sold slave*, Joseph *ministers* and *finds favour* — the interior *flourishing in service*, the LORD *with him* *(inferred: chen/sharat = the favour and honourable ministry of the enslaved-but-blessed)*.
- **barakh (blessed) + berakhah (blessing) — the *blessing that flows through him* (39:5).** "the LORD **blessed** (barakh) the Egyptian's house **for Joseph's sake**; the **blessing** (berakhah) was on all he had" *(stated)*. The gem: the blessing *flows through Joseph to the Egyptian* — as *"in you all families blessed"* (12:3), and as Laban was blessed for Jacob's sake (30:27); the interior a *conduit of blessing* even in bondage *(inferred: barakh/berakhah = the blessing flowing through Joseph to his master)*.
- **yada (concern) — the master's *untroubled trust* (39:6).** "he had no **concern** (yada) about anything but the food he ate" *(stated)* — yada in the *"take knowledge/worry"* idiom: Potiphar *trusts Joseph so fully he knows/worries about nothing*; the interior of *complete confidence placed* in Joseph *(inferred: yada = the master's untroubled trust)*.
- **yada (concern) + chata (sin) + raah (wickedness) — the *God-ward refusal* (39:8–9).** "my master has no **concern** (yada)… how can I do this **wickedness** (raah) and **sin** (chata) against God?" *(stated)*. **yada** *distinct from 39:6* — Joseph *cites the master's trust* as the reason not to betray; **raah/chata** — the gem: the sin is named *"against God"* — not merely against Potiphar. Joseph's integrity is *God-ward* — the interior *refusing because the wrong is against God* (the implicit fear-of-God); fidelity rooted higher than the master's trust *(inferred: yada/chata/raah = the refusal grounded in sin-against-God, not just betrayal of trust)*.
- **shama (listen) — the *daily refusal* (39:10).** "as she spoke day after day, he would not **listen** (shama)" *(stated)* — shama, *heed*: the gem is the *sustained* refusal — *day after day* — not a single resolve but a *maintained* one; the interior *holding firm under repeated pressure* *(inferred: shama = the daily, sustained refusal to heed)*.
- **qara (called) — the *false alarm raised* (39:14).** "she **called** (qara) her household: 'he came to lie with me, I cried out'" *(stated)* — qara, *call out*: the gem is the *lie constructed* — the *rejection inverted into an assault*; the interior of *thwarted lust turning to vengeful accusation*, raising a *false alarm* *(inferred: qara = the false alarm — rejection inverted to accusation)*.
- **shama (heard) + qara (cried out) + rum (lifted) — the *lie's fabricated cry* (39:15).** *shama/qara distinct:* "'as soon as he **heard** (shama) that I **lifted** (rum) my voice and **cried out** (qara), he fled'" *(stated)*. **shama** — the lie's *claimed* hearing (Joseph "heard" her cry); **qara/rum** — the *fabricated cry* — she *invents* a cry she never made. The interior *building the false story detail by detail* *(inferred: shama/qara/rum = the fabricated cry inside the lie)*.
- **nuach (laid up) — the *false evidence preserved* (39:16).** "she **laid up** (nuach) his garment until his master came" *(stated)* — nuach, *set down/deposit*: the gem is the *calculated preservation of the garment as evidence* — as the brothers *kept the coat* (37:31–33); **the garment weaponized again**. The interior *staging the proof of its lie* *(inferred: nuach = the garment laid up as staged false evidence)*.
- **qara (cried) + rum (lifted) — the *lie retold to the master* (39:18).** *qara/rum distinct from 39:15:* "'as soon as I **lifted** (rum) my voice and **cried** (qara), he fled'" *(stated)* — the *same fabrication* now *retold to Potiphar* (39:15 to the household, 39:18 to the master) — the interior *repeating the lie to its target* *(inferred: qara/rum = the fabricated cry retold to the master)*.
- **shama (heard) + charah (kindled) — the *master deceived and enraged* (39:19).** *shama distinct from 39:10,15:* "when his master **heard** the words, his anger was **kindled** (charah)" *(stated)*. **shama** — Potiphar *hears the lie*; **charah** — *burn/be incensed*: the interior *deceived into rage* against the innocent (as Jacob was deceived to grief, 37:33) *(inferred: shama/charah = the master's deceived rage)*.
- **chesed (steadfast love) + chen (favor) + natan (gave) — the *LORD with Joseph in prison* (39:21).** *chen distinct from 39:4:* "the LORD was with Joseph, showed him **steadfast love** (chesed), **gave** (natan) him **favor** (chen) with the keeper" *(stated)*. **chesed** — the gem: *steadfast love* — even in *unjust imprisonment*, God's *covenant faithfulness* is with the wronged righteous; **chen** *distinct from 39:4* — the *favour follows Joseph down* into the prison (house-favour → prison-favour); **natan** — *given*. The interior *sustained by God's presence and chesed at its lowest* *(inferred: chesed/chen/natan = the LORD's steadfast love and favour with Joseph in the prison)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **the blessed, trusted servant (chen/sharat 39:4 → barakh/berakhah 39:5 → yada 39:6):** even enslaved, Joseph *ministers*, *finds favour*, is a *conduit of blessing*, and is *wholly trusted* — the LORD *with him*.
2. **the God-ward refusal (yada/chata/raah 39:8–9 → shama 39:10):** Joseph *refuses* because the wrong would be *sin against God* — and holds firm *day after day*; integrity rooted *higher than his master's trust*.
3. **the garment turned to a lie (qara 39:14 → shama/qara/rum 39:15 → nuach 39:16 → qara/rum 39:18 → shama/charah 39:19):** the *rejection inverted into an assault*, a *cry fabricated*, the *garment laid up as evidence*, the lie *retold*, and Potiphar *deceived into rage* — **a garment weaponized against Joseph a second time** (← the coat).
4. **the LORD in the prison (chesed/chen/natan 39:21):** unjustly imprisoned, Joseph is met with God's *steadfast love and favour* — the divine presence *following him down* to his lowest.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** chen ×2 (favour in the *house* / in the *prison* — the favour follows him down); sharat (the *honourable ministry* of the enslaved, vs slavery `avad`); barakh/berakhah (the *blessing flowing through* Joseph to the Egyptian); yada ×2 (Potiphar's *untroubled trust* / Joseph *citing* that trust); **chata/raah (the *God-ward refusal* — sin "against God", not just betrayal)**; shama ×3 (Joseph's *daily refusal* / the lie's *claimed* hearing / Potiphar *hearing* the lie); qara ×3 (the *false alarm* / the *fabricated cry* / the *retold* lie); rum ×2 (the "lifted voice" *to the household* / *to the master*); **nuach (the *garment laid up* as staged false evidence — the garment weaponized again)**; charah (the master's *deceived rage*); **chesed (the LORD's *steadfast love* with Joseph in prison)**.
- **Whose interior (multi-interior):** **Joseph** — *God-ward integrity* (refusing sin against God), *sustained* under daily pressure, *flourishing in service and a conduit of blessing*, and *sustained by God's chesed* even when the integrity *costs* him (prison); **Potiphar's wife** — *thwarted lust turning to a vengeful, fabricated lie*; **God** — *with Joseph* throughout (house and prison), *blessing through him*, *steadfast love* at his lowest; **Potiphar** — *deceived into rage*.
- **Threads:** **the garment weaponized** (nuach 39:16 ← the coat 37:31–33 → a *false story* built on Joseph's clothing *twice*); the **blessing-conduit** (barakh 39:5 ← 12:3; 30:27 — blessing flows *through* Joseph, as through Jacob); **the LORD with Joseph** (chesed 39:21 — the refrain 39:2,3,23; God's presence in the *pit and prison* ← Bethel's "I am with you" 28:15; → the whole cycle's providence); *sin against God* (39:9 — the God-ward conscience, the fear-of-God implicit ← the midwives, Egypt's God-fearers); the *deceived into rage/grief* (charah 39:19 ← Jacob's deceived grief 37:33 — the family's deceptions land on others).
- **Guarded against:** lumping the three shama/qara or two chen/yada/rum (each a distinct beat — the daily refusal vs the lie's claims; the favour in house vs prison); reading a "shame" operation into the mis-glossed second chesed (the word is *steadfast love*); over-reading Joseph's "handsome" (39:6) as vanity (it is the *occasion* of the temptation, scene-context); importing NT "flee temptation" moralism over the *God-ward refusal* the verse states ("against God"); missing that the *garment* is the recurring instrument of false witness against Joseph (coat → cloak); treating the prison as God's absence when the text makes it the site of his *chesed*.

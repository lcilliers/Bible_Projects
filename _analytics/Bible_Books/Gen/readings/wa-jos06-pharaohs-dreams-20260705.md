## JOS-06-pharaohs-dreams · Genesis 41:1–36 · [Egypt's wisdom fails; God reveals; the humble interpreter] — the troubled king, the belated remembering, and "it is not in me"

**The passage.** The dream — seven **plump** (basar) cows, then seven **thin** (basar) cows that eat them (41:2–4); seven **good** (tov) ears, then blighted (41:5); Pharaoh's spirit troubled, he **called** (qara) the **wise men** (chakham) — none could interpret (41:8); the cupbearer "I **remember** (zakhar) my **offenses** (chet)… Pharaoh was **angry** (qatsaph)" (41:9–10); Pharaoh **called** (qara) Joseph (41:14); "I **heard** (shama) you can interpret" (41:15); "it is not in me; God will give a **favorable** (shalom) answer" (41:16); the **plump**/**thin** (basar) cows and **good** (tov) ears retold, the **ugly** (ra) cows, "no one would have **known** (yada)" (41:18–24); "God has revealed… the **good** (tov) cows are seven years… the **ugly** (ra) are famine… the plenty **unknown** (yada)" (41:25–31); "let Pharaoh select a **discerning** (bin) and **wise** (chakham) man… gather the **good** (tov) years' food, **keep** (shamar) it" (41:33–35).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **basar (plump / thin) — the *dream-imagery* (41:2, 41:3, 41:4).** "seven cows, **plump** (basar)… seven **thin** (basar)… the thin ate the plump" *(stated)* — basar in the *fleshiness* sense (fat vs lean cows): the *symbolic content* of the dream (plenty devoured by famine) — read as *imagery*, not an interior operation; noted across the dream's stages *(inferred: basar = the plump/thin cows — the dream's plenty-and-famine imagery)*.
- **tov (good) — the *good ears* imagery (41:5).** "seven ears, plump and **good** (tov)" *(stated)* — tov, *good*: the *good ears* (the plenty) — the second dream's imagery, paralleling the cows *(inferred: tov = the good ears — the plenty imaged)*.
- **chakham (wise men) + qara (called) — *Egypt's wisdom fails* (41:8).** "his spirit was troubled; he **called** (qara) the **wise men** (chakham)… none could interpret" *(stated)*. **chakham** — the gem: *Egypt's assembled wisdom* — magicians and *wise men* — **fails**; **qara** — the king *summons* them in vain. The interior-context: *human wisdom at its most concentrated cannot read God's disclosure* (← the failing magicians, a recurring motif → Exodus 8:18) *(inferred: chakham/qara = Egypt's summoned wisdom failing before the God-given dream)*.
- **zakhar (remember) + chet (offenses) — the *belated remembering* (41:9).** "the cupbearer: 'I **remember** (zakhar) my **offenses** (chet) today'" *(stated)*. **zakhar** — the gem: at *last* the cupbearer *remembers* — the *plea he forgot* (40:14,23) *now surfaces*; **chet** — he names his *"offenses/faults"* (against Pharaoh, and implicitly his *failure to remember Joseph*). The interior of *belated recollection* — 2 years late, *providentially on time* *(inferred: zakhar/chet = the belated remembering of the forgotten benefactor)*.
- **qatsaph (angry) — Pharaoh's *past wrath* recalled (41:10).** "when Pharaoh was **angry** (qatsaph)…" *(stated)* — qatsaph *(← 40:2)*: the cupbearer *recalls the wrath* that imprisoned him with Joseph — the frame of the whole prison episode *(inferred: qatsaph = the past wrath recalled in the cupbearer's account)*.
- **qara (called) — Joseph *summoned from the pit* (41:14).** *qara distinct from 41:8:* "Pharaoh **called** (qara) Joseph; they brought him quickly from the pit" *(stated)* — where 41:8 *called the wise men* (who failed), 41:14 *calls Joseph* (who will succeed): the gem is the *contrast* — the *pit-prisoner* summoned where *Egypt's wisdom* failed; the interior *raised from the pit* to the throne-room *(inferred: qara = Joseph summoned from the pit — where wisdom failed)*.
- **shama (heard) — the *reputation* that reaches Pharaoh (41:15).** "I have **heard** (shama) that you can interpret a dream" *(stated)* — shama, *hear*: Joseph's *interpreting gift* has *reached the king's ear* (via the cupbearer) — the interior-context of *a reputation that opens the door* *(inferred: shama = the reputation heard that summons Joseph)*.
- **shalom (favorable) — Joseph's *God-crediting humility* (41:16).** "'it is **not in me**; God will give Pharaoh a **favorable** (shalom) answer'" *(stated)* — shalom in the *peace/well-being* sense (a *favourable/shalom* answer): the gem is the ***"not in me"*** — Joseph *deflects all glory to God* (← "interpretations belong to God", 40:8); the interior's *humility* — the gift *is not his own* — and God will give a *shalom* answer *(inferred: shalom = the God-given favourable answer — Joseph's humility, "not in me")*.
- **basar (plump / thin) + roa (evil/poor) — the dream *retold* (41:18, 41:19).** *basar distinct from 41:2–4:* "seven **plump** (basar) cows… seven **thin** (basar), so **poor** (roa, *evil*) as I never saw in Egypt" *(stated)* — the *retelling* of the imagery (now with Pharaoh's added detail — the extreme *evil/badness*, roa, of the thin cows); the same content *voiced by the dreamer* *(inferred: basar/roa = the plump/thin cows retold, their evil ugliness stressed)*.
- **ra (ugly) + yada (known) + techillah (beginning) — the *unsatisfied* devouring (41:20–21).** "the **ugly** (ra) cows ate the plump… no one would have **known** (yada)… still ugly as at the **beginning** (techillah)" *(stated)*. **ra** — the *ugly/bad* cows; **yada** — the *dream detail* (the eating left no mark); **techillah** — *as at first*: the imagery of *famine that consumes without being satisfied* — read as the dream's *content* *(inferred: ra/yada/techillah = the ugly cows' unsatisfied devouring — the dream's famine-imagery)*.
- **tov (good) — the *good ears* retold (41:22, 41:24).** *tov distinct from 41:5:* "seven ears, full and **good** (tov)… the thin swallowed the **good** (tov)" *(stated)* — the good ears *retold* and *swallowed* — the plenty imagery *voiced and consumed* *(inferred: tov = the good ears retold and devoured)*.
- **tov (good) + ra (ugly) — the *interpretation* (41:26, 41:27).** *tov/ra distinct — now interpreted:* "the **good** (tov) cows are seven years [of plenty]… the **ugly** (ra) are seven years of famine" *(stated)* — the gem is the *shift from image to meaning*: the *good/ugly* now *named as years* — Joseph *reads God's disclosure* (God "has revealed… has shown… the thing is fixed by God") *(inferred: tov/ra = the good/ugly read as the years of plenty and famine)*.
- **yada (unknown) — the *plenty forgotten* (41:31).** *yada distinct from 41:21:* "the plenty will be **unknown** (yada) by reason of the famine" *(stated)* — the *interpretation's* detail: the plenty *so forgotten* under famine that it *will not be known* — the severity stressed *(inferred: yada = the plenty unknown/forgotten under the famine)*.
- **bin (discerning) + chakham (wise) + tov (good) + shamar (keep) — the *wise counsel* (41:33, 41:34, 41:35).** *chakham distinct from 41:8:* "let Pharaoh select a **discerning** (bin) and **wise** (chakham) man… gather the **good** (tov) years' food and **keep** (shamar) it" *(stated)*. **bin** — *understanding/discernment*; **chakham** — *wise* (now the *right* wisdom, vs the failed wise men of 41:8); **tov** — the *good years'* produce; **shamar** — *keep/store*. The gem: Joseph *adds practical wisdom* to the interpretation — a *plan* (a discerning administrator, a fifth stored) — the interior *not only reading but governing* the future God revealed *(inferred: bin/chakham/tov/shamar = the wise counsel — a discerning administrator to store the good years against the famine)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **the dream and the failure of wisdom (basar/tov 41:2–5 → chakham/qara 41:8):** Pharaoh dreams *plenty devoured by famine*, his spirit troubled; *Egypt's assembled wisdom fails* — human wisdom cannot read God's disclosure.
2. **the belated remembering, the pit-prisoner summoned (zakhar/chet 41:9 → qatsaph 41:10 → qara 41:14 → shama 41:15):** the cupbearer *at last remembers* his *offense*; Joseph is *called from the pit* — *where wisdom failed* — his *reputation* having reached the king.
3. **"not in me" — God credited (shalom 41:16):** Joseph *deflects all glory to God* — the gift *not his own* — and God will give a *favourable* answer; the interior's *humility*.
4. **image read as meaning, and the wise plan (basar/ro'a 41:18–19 → ra/yada/techillah 41:20–21 → tov 41:22–24 → tov/ra 41:26–27 → yada 41:31 → bin/chakham/tov/shamar 41:33–35):** Pharaoh *retells* the imagery; Joseph *reads God's disclosure* (plenty, then famine, *fixed by God*), and *adds the wise plan* — a *discerning administrator* to *store* the good years. The God-given interpretation *and* wisdom succeed where Egypt's failed.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** basar ×5 (the plump/thin cows — *dreamed* 41:2–4 / *retold* 41:18–19 — the plenty-and-famine imagery); tov ×5 (the good ears — *dreamed* / *retold* / *swallowed* / *interpreted as good years* / *stored*); **chakham ×2 (Egypt's wisdom *failing* 41:8 / the *wise* administrator counselled 41:33 — the contrast)**; qara ×2 (the *wise men* called, who fail / *Joseph* called, who succeeds); **zakhar/chet (the cupbearer's *belated remembering* of his offense/the forgotten benefactor)**; qatsaph (the *past wrath* recalled); shama (the *reputation heard*); **shalom (the *God-given* favourable answer — "not in me", Joseph's humility)**; ra/yada/techillah (the ugly cows' *unsatisfied devouring* — dream-imagery); ra (the ugly cows *interpreted* as famine); yada (the plenty *unknown* under famine); bin/shamar (the *wise plan* — discernment and storage).
- **Whose interior (multi-interior):** **Joseph** — *God-crediting humility* ("not in me") *and* *practical wisdom* (the storage plan); the **wise men** — *human wisdom failing* before God's disclosure; the **cupbearer** — *belated remembering*; **Pharaoh** — *troubled and seeking*; **God** — the *revealer* who *shows* and *fixes* what he will do (the dream doubled = *fixed by God*). Joseph reads *and* governs the future God discloses.
- **Threads:** *interpretations/revelation belong to God* (shalom "God will give the answer" 41:16 ← "interpretations belong to God" 40:8); **the failing wise/counterfeit** (chakham 41:8 ← the failing magicians → Exodus 8:18; 9:11 — human wisdom/counterfeit fails before God); the *belated remembering* (zakhar 41:9 ← 40:14,23 — the delay ends *on time*); the *pit* (41:14 ← 37:24; 40:15 — raised from the pit); Joseph's *God-given wisdom* (bin/chakham 41:33 → "none so discerning as you", 41:39; the exaltation JOS-07); the *dreams* (Pharaoh's ← Joseph's own dreams 37 — the dream-motif governing the cycle).
- **Guarded against:** over-reading the dream-imagery (basar/tov/ra of the cows/ears) into interior operations — they are the dream's *symbolic content* (plenty/famine), read across stages (dreamed/retold/interpreted), *not* faculties; lumping the five basar/tov or two chakham/qara/ra/yada; missing the **contrast** (Egypt's *chakham* fails, God-given *chakham* succeeds); reading Joseph's plan as self-promotion when the text gives *counsel* ("let Pharaoh select a man") — the exaltation is Pharaoh's response (JOS-07), not Joseph's grasp; importing NT typology (Joseph as savior-figure) over the *humble interpreter + wise administrator* the passage gives.

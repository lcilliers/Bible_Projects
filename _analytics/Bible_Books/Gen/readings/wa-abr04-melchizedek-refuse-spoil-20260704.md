## ABR-04-melchizedek-refuse-spoil · Genesis 14:1–24 · [the corrected interior] — the rescue of the one who left, the priest's double blessing, and the oath that refuses to owe its wealth to a man

**The passage.** Kings "**served** (avad)… **rebelled** (marad)" and "**joined** (arakh) battle" (14:4,8); Abram's "**allies** (baalei berit)" (14:13); "when Abram **heard** (shama) that his kinsman was taken captive" he pursued and rescued (14:14); the king of Sodom went to **meet** (qara) him (14:17); Melchizedek "**blessed** (barak) him: Blessed be Abram… and **blessed** (barak) be God Most High, who has **delivered** (natan) your **enemies** (tsar) into your hand," and Abram "**gave** (natan) a tenth" (14:19–20); "**Give** (natan) me the **persons** (nephesh), but take the goods" (14:21); "I have **lifted** (rum) my hand to the LORD… I would not take a thread… lest you say, 'I have made Abram **rich** (ashar)'" (14:22–23).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **avad (served) + marad (rebelled) — servitude and revolt, at the national level (14:4).** The finding is "twelve years they **served** (avad) Chedorlaomer, and in the thirteenth they **rebelled** (marad)" *(stated)*. The operations are *subjection* (avad) and *revolt* (marad) — the political interior of vassal peoples; limited inner-being weight, noted as the war's occasion (avad here = servitude, a distinct sense from the garden-vocation avad) *(inferred: near-context; the interior of subjection/rebellion at the collective level)*.
- **arakh (joined battle) — the array (14:8).** The finding is the kings "**joined** (arakh) battle" *(stated)*. The operation is *setting in array* — a military ordering; near-null inner-being weight, noted for coverage *(honest near-null)*.
- **berit (allies) — a *human* alliance (14:13).** The finding is Abram's "**allies** (baalei berit — lords of covenant)" *(stated)*. The operation is berit as a *human confederacy* — distinct from God's covenant (6:18; 9:9); here men *bound to Abram* by pact *(inferred)*.
- **shama (heard) — hearing that moves to *rescue* (14:14).** The finding is "when Abram **heard** (shama) that his kinsman had been taken captive, he led out his trained men" *(stated)*. The gem is *shama that acts*: Abram *hears* Lot is captive and *rescues him* — **despite** Lot's grasping departure (ABR-03); loyalty and generosity persist toward the one who left him. Distinct from the primeval shama-senses — here hearing *moves the interior to costly rescue* *(inferred)*.
- **qara (meet/encounter) — the kings come out to him (14:17).** The finding is "the king of Sodom went out to **meet** (qara, encounter) him" *(stated)*. The operation is qara in its *encounter*-sense (H7122G) — near-null inner-being weight, the scene-setting for the two kings who meet Abram *(honest near-null; distinct qara-sense)*.
- **barak (bless) #1 — the priest blesses *Abram* (14:19).** The finding is Melchizedek "**blessed** (barak) him: Blessed be Abram by God Most High, Possessor of heaven and earth" *(stated)*. The operation is *priestly blessing on the man* — Abram *receives* blessing mediated by a priest of God Most High *(inferred)*.
- **barak (bless) #2 — the priest blesses *God* (14:20).** The finding is "**blessed** (barak) be God Most High, who has delivered your enemies" *(stated)*. The gem is barak *reversed* to doxology (as 9:26, man blesses God) — the priest *praises God* for the victory; the same operation turned from man-blessed to God-praised in adjacent verses *(inferred: barak #1 on the man, #2 on God — distinct directions)*.
- **tsar (enemies) + natan (delivered) — God hands over the foes (14:20).** The finding is God "**delivered** (natan) your **enemies** (tsar) into your hand" *(stated)*. The operations: **tsar** — the *foes*; **natan** — God's *handing-over* of them (as the animals *delivered* into man's hand, 9:2) — victory ascribed to God's giving, not Abram's might *(inferred)*.
- **natan (gave) — Abram's *tithe*, giving back to God (14:20).** The finding is "Abram **gave** (natan) him a tenth of everything" *(stated)*. The gem is the *response*: having *received* victory (God's natan) and blessing, Abram *gives* (natan) a tenth — grateful worship-giving, the interior returning a portion to God through the priest; distinct from God's delivering natan — this is *man's giving to God* *(inferred)*.
- **natan (give) + nephesh (persons) — the king's bargain (14:21).** The finding is "the king of Sodom said, **Give** (natan) me the **persons** (nephesh), but take the goods for yourself" *(stated)*. The operations: **natan** — the king's *demand* (a third natan-sense in the verse-cluster); **nephesh** — the *persons/souls* (← 12:5) the king wants back; the pagan's transaction offering Abram wealth *(inferred)*.
- **rum (lifted my hand) — the oath-gesture (14:22).** The finding is "I have **lifted** (rum) my hand to the LORD, God Most High" *(stated)*. The operation is rum (exalt/lift) as the *sworn gesture* — the raised hand of an **oath**: Abram *binds himself to God* before answering the king; distinct rum from the flood's "waters rose" (7:17) — here the interior's *vow-gesture* *(inferred)*.
- **ashar (make rich) — the refusal of *tainted* gain (14:23).** The finding is "I would not take a thread… **lest you say, 'I have made Abram rich (ashar)'**" *(stated)*. The gem is the *corrected* interior: Abram **refuses** the king of Sodom's spoil so that *no man* can claim to have *enriched* him — his wealth must come from *God* (who promised to bless him, 12:2), not from a pagan king. The operation is trusting *God as enricher* and refusing to *owe one's wealth to a man*. **Contrast ABR-02**: there Abram was made rich *by Pharaoh* through Sarai's endangerment; here he *refuses* riches from a king — the fear-and-gain interior of Egypt corrected into faith-and-refusal *(inferred: ashar — the enriching Abram will only receive from God)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **loyalty to the one who left (shama 14:14):** Abram *hears* and *rescues* Lot despite Lot's self-serving departure (ABR-03) — the un-grasping interior now *risks itself* for the grasper.
2. **received blessing → returned tithe (barak/natan 14:19–20):** Melchizedek blesses Abram and praises God; Abram, having *received* victory (God's natan) and blessing, *gives* a tenth (natan) — the interior's grateful giving-back to God.
3. **the oath that refuses gain (rum/ashar 14:22–23):** Abram *binds himself by oath* (rum) and *refuses* the spoil so no man can say he *enriched* him (ashar) — grounding his refusal in "God Most High, Possessor of heaven and earth" (so he needs nothing a king can give).
4. **the Egypt correction:** ABR-02's interior *feared* and was *enriched by a pagan king* through compromise; ABR-04's interior *swears to God* and *refuses to be enriched by a pagan king* — the same temptation (gain from a king), now met by faith. The cycle is teaching the interior to receive only from God.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** avad (servitude) / marad (revolt) as the *political* interior; arakh, qara(encounter) as *near-nulls*; berit as *human alliance*; **shama that moves to rescue** the one who left; barak on *Abram* (14:19) vs barak on *God* (14:20, doxology); tsar/natan as God's *delivering*; natan as Abram's *tithe* (giving back); natan as the king's *demand*; nephesh as *persons*; rum as the *oath-gesture*; **ashar — the refusal to be *made rich by a man*** (the Egypt correction).
- **Whose interior:** the **human** interior *corrected* — loyal (rescuing Lot), grateful (tithing), and *refusing tainted gain* by oath; against the *pagan* interiors (the king's transaction) and receiving *priestly* blessing.
- **Threads:** shama (← the primeval senses; here hearing-that-rescues); barak (← 9:26 man blesses God; here priestly); natan (the many senses; here delivering / tithing / demanding); ashar/gain (← ABR-02 enriched-by-Pharaoh, now *refused*); the oath (rum → the oaths of ABR-13, ABR-16); God Most High as Possessor (the ground of needing nothing from men).
- **Guarded against:** over-reading the political spans (avad/marad/arakh — limited weight, honestly flagged); merging the two barak (man vs God) or the three natan (deliver/tithe/demand); flattening rum into "exalt" when it is the *oath-gesture*; missing the *ABR-02 correction* that ashar carries; importing later Melchizedek/priesthood typology beyond the blessing and tithe the verses state.

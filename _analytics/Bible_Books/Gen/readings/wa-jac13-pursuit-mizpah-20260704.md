# Genesis 31:22–55 — inner-being passage reading (JAC-13-pursuit-mizpah)

- **Source:** verse-first lexicals over the passage (provenance `genesis-jacob-v1-20260704`). Every non-T2 span in its verse; *stated* | *inferred*; distinctions kept. Rachel's ruse (31:34–35, the hidden gods) reads as scene-context. **Note:** the `'o'` span at 31:43 (glossed "desire") is the conjunction *"or"* ("for my daughters *or* their children") — no desire-operation is in the verse; flagged as a probable mis-tag, given no inner-being reading.

## JAC-13-pursuit-mizpah · Genesis 31:22–55 · [confrontation into treaty] — God's guard on the fleeing man, the aired grievances, the search, Jacob's self-vindication, and the covenant of separation

**The passage.** God warns Laban in a dream — "be **careful** (shamar) to say nothing to Jacob, **good** (tov) or **bad** (ra)" (31:24); Laban — "you **tricked** (levav) me, drove off my daughters… I'd have sent you with **mirth** (simchah)" (31:26–27); "it is in my power to **harm** (ra) you, but God warned me — say nothing **good** (tov) or **bad**" (31:29); "you **longed** (kasaph) for your father's house — but why steal my gods?" (31:30); Jacob — "I was **afraid** (yare)" (31:31); "with whomever you find your gods shall not **live** (chayah)" — Jacob did not **know** (yada) Rachel took them (31:32); Rachel "I **cannot** (yakhol) rise… let my lord not be **angry** (charah)" (31:35); Jacob **angry** (charah), **berated** (riv) him — "what is my **offense** (pesha)? my **sin** (chattat)?" (31:36); "you **required** (baqash) the **loss** (chata) from me… my sleep **fled** (nadad)" (31:39–40); "I **served** (avad) twenty years… God saw my **affliction** (oni)… the **Fear** (pachad) of Isaac rebuked you" (31:41–42); "let us **make** (karat) a **covenant** (berit)" (31:44); Jacob **set up** (rum) a pillar (31:45); Laban and Jacob **named** (qara) the heap (31:47–48); "if you **oppress** (anah) my daughters…" (31:50); "not pass over to do **harm** (raah)" (31:52); "God **judge** (shaphat)" — Jacob swore by the **Fear** (pachad) of Isaac (31:53); he **called** (qara) his kinsmen to eat (31:54); Laban **blessed** (barakh) and departed (31:55).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **shamar (careful) + tov (good) + ra (bad) — God's *guard* on the pursued (31:24).** "God came to Laban… 'be **careful** (shamar) to say nothing to Jacob, either **good** (tov) or **bad** (ra)'" *(stated)*. **shamar** — *watch/take heed*; **tov/ra** — the merism *good-or-bad* (i.e. *nothing at all* by way of power-move). The gem: *God restrains the pursuer* — the fleeing deceiver is *guarded by a divine warning*; the interior of the *hunter checked from above* (← God restrains Abimelech, 20:6; the harmer, 31:7) *(inferred: shamar/tov/ra = the divine restraint laid on the pursuer)*.
- **levav (tricked) — Laban names the *heart-theft* (31:26).** "you have **tricked** (levav) me and driven my daughters like captives" *(stated)* — the idiom again (← 31:20, ganav-lev): "you *stole my heart/mind*" = *deceived me*; now *Laban* names the deception done to him. The interior of the *deceived accuser* — the deception-word turned back on Jacob by its victim *(inferred: levav = the heart-theft named by the deceived)*.
- **simchah (mirth) — the *festive farewell* denied (31:27).** "why flee secretly… I'd have sent you with **mirth** (simchah), songs, tambourine and lyre?" *(stated)* — simchah, *joy/gladness*: Laban *claims a joyful send-off was possible* — the interior *aggrieved of the celebration it was cheated of* (whether sincere or self-serving, the *stated* complaint is lost joy) *(inferred: simchah = the festive farewell Laban claims was denied him)*.
- **ra (harm) + tov (good) + shamar (careful) — power to harm, *restrained* (31:29).** *ra/tov/shamar distinct from 31:24:* "it is in my power to do you **harm** (ra), but the God of your father… said, be **careful** (shamar), say nothing **good** (tov) or bad" *(stated)*. Here the merism is *quoted by Laban himself* — he *owns his power to harm* and *confesses the divine restraint*. Where 31:24 was *God's warning*, 31:29 is *Laban's admission* of it — the pursuer *conceding he is checked* *(inferred: ra/tov/shamar = the harm-power owned and confessed as divinely restrained)*.
- **kasaph (longed) — the *homesick longing* acknowledged (31:30).** "you have gone because you **longed** (kasaph) greatly for your father's house" *(stated)* — kasaph, *long/yearn*: Laban *grants the legitimate homesickness* — the interior's *pull toward home* (← the vow's "come again to my father's house in peace", 28:21) — before pivoting to the accusation of theft *(inferred: kasaph = the yearning for home acknowledged)*.
- **yare (afraid) — the *fear-motive* owned (31:31).** "I was **afraid** (yare), for I thought you'd take your daughters by force" *(stated)* — yare, *fear*: Jacob *confesses fear as the reason for stealth* — the *self-protecting* fear (← the sister-lie fear 26:7; his own dread of Esau 27:44) driving the secret flight; the interior *honest about its fear* *(inferred: yare = the fear that drove the secret departure)*.
- **chayah (shall not live) + yada (did not know) — the *rash death-oath* in ignorance (31:32).** "with whomever you find your gods shall not **live** (chayah)… Jacob did not **know** (yada) Rachel had stolen them" *(stated)*. **chayah** — *live*: Jacob *pronounces death* on the thief; **yada** — the dramatic gem: he *does not know* the thief is *Rachel* — the interior *curses blindly*, and the narrator's dark note lands (Rachel *will die* prematurely, 35:18). The rash oath of the *unknowing* self *(inferred: chayah/yada = the blind death-oath, unknowingly against Rachel)*.
- **yakhol (cannot) + charah (angry) — Rachel's *ruse* (31:35).** "I **cannot** (yakhol) rise… let my lord not be **angry** (charah); the way of women is on me" *(stated)*. **yakhol** — the *feigned inability* (she sits on the hidden gods); **charah** — she *pre-empts his anger*. The gem: the *daughter deceives her father* (as her husband did his) — the family's stealth in her too; the interior *lying with composure* *(inferred: yakhol/charah = the ruse that hides the theft, deflecting anger)*.
- **charah (angry) + riv (berated) + pesha (offense) + chattat (sin) — Jacob's *indignant counter-attack* (31:36).** *charah distinct from 31:35:* "Jacob became **angry** (charah) and **berated** (riv) Laban: 'what is my **offense** (pesha)? my **sin** (chattat)?'" *(stated)*. **charah** — *now Jacob's* anger (the accused turns accuser); **riv** — *contend/bring a case* (the legal-dispute word, ← the wells 26:20); **pesha** — *transgression/rebellion*; **chattat** — *sin/failure*. The gem: Jacob, *finally emboldened*, *demands to know his crime* — the interior *indignant in its (relative) innocence here*, pressing the legal question *(inferred: charah/riv/pesha/chattat = the indignant demand for the charge)*.
- **baqash (required) + chata (loss) — the *shepherd's borne loss* (31:39).** "what was torn I did not bring; I **bore the loss** (chata); from my hand you **required** (baqash) it" *(stated)*. **baqash** — *seek/demand*: Laban *exacted* every loss from Jacob; **chata** — here in the *loss/forfeit* sense (the shepherd *made good* the torn animals). The interior of the *scrupulous, over-charged shepherd* — bearing what custom would excuse *(inferred: baqash/chata = the losses demanded of and borne by the shepherd)*.
- **nadad (fled) — *sleeplessness* under the toil (31:40).** "the heat by day, the cold by night, my sleep **fled** (nadad) from my eyes" *(stated)* — nadad, *flee/wander/retreat*: the gem is *sleep itself fleeing* — the *bodily cost* of twenty years' labour; the interior *worn by exposure and sleeplessness* *(inferred: nadad = the sleep that fled — the toil's bodily toll)*.
- **avad (served) — the *twenty years* tallied (31:41).** "I **served** (avad) you fourteen years for your daughters, six for your flock, and you changed my wages ten times" *(stated)* — avad, *serve*: the *full accounting* of the service (← the doubled service JAC-09) — the interior *laying the whole ledger* of its labour before the witnesses *(inferred: avad = the twenty years' service tallied as vindication)*.
- **oni (affliction) + pachad (Fear of Isaac) — God as *vindicator* of the afflicted (31:42).** "if the God of my father, the **Fear** (pachad) of Isaac, had not been with me… God saw my **affliction** (oni) and rebuked you" *(stated)*. **oni** — *affliction/misery* (← Leah's oni 29:32; Hagar's, 16:11): Jacob names his *twenty years' affliction*; **pachad** — the gem: **the Fear/Dread of Isaac** — a *divine title*, God known as *the One Isaac reverently fears* — invoked as the *vindicator who saw and rebuked*. The interior *appeals past the human dispute to the God who saw* *(inferred: oni/pachad = the affliction seen, and God-the-Dread-of-Isaac as vindicator)*.
- **(31:43 — the `'o'`/"desire" span is the conjunction "or"; no inner-being operation. Flagged mis-tag, not read.)**
- **karat (make) + berit (covenant) — the *treaty of separation* proposed (31:44).** "let us **make** (karat) a **covenant** (berit), you and I" *(stated)* — **karat** *cut* + **berit** *covenant*: Laban *proposes a pact* — but (unlike the peace-covenants of blessing, 21:27; 26:28) this is a *boundary of mutual non-harm* between two who *cannot trust*; the interior *sealing a wary separation* *(inferred: karat/berit = the covenant of wary separation)*.
- **rum (set up) — the *pillar raised* as witness (31:45).** "Jacob took a stone and **set it up** (rum) as a pillar" *(stated)* — rum, *raise/exalt/lift*: the interior *erects a monument* (as at Bethel, 28:18) — here a *witness-stone* to the boundary *(inferred: rum = the pillar raised as covenant-witness)*.
- **qara (named the heap) — the *witness-heap named* (31:47).** "Laban **called** (qara) it Jegar-sahadutha, Jacob **called** (qara) it Galeed" *(stated)* — qara, *name*: each in his own tongue *names the witness-heap* — the interior *marking the boundary in a name* (both meaning "heap of witness") *(inferred: qara = the dual naming of the witness-heap)*.
- **qara (named Galeed) — the *name confirmed* (31:48).** *qara distinct from 31:47:* "'this heap is a witness'… therefore he **named** (qara) it Galeed" *(stated)* — the *confirming* naming, tying the name to its *witness*-function (and Mizpah, "watchpost") — the interior *fixing the boundary's meaning* *(inferred: qara = the naming that fixes the heap's witness-role)*.
- **anah (oppress) — the daughters *guarded* by the oath (31:50).** "if you **oppress** (anah) my daughters or take other wives… God is witness" *(stated)* — anah, *afflict/oppress/humble*: Laban's *one tender clause* — protecting his daughters from *affliction*; the interior's *fatherly concern* embedded in the wary treaty (anah → the same word for Dinah's violation, JAC-17) *(inferred: anah = the affliction the oath forbids against the daughters)*.
- **raah (harm) — the *boundary of non-harm* (31:52).** "I will not pass this heap to you, nor you to me, to do **harm** (raah)" *(stated)* — raah, *harm/injury* (distinct lexeme from the ra of 31:24,29): the *core term* of the treaty — *neither crosses to hurt the other*; the interior *fencing off future harm* *(inferred: raah = the mutual harm the boundary forbids)*.
- **pachad (Fear of Isaac) + shaphat (judge) — the *oath sworn by the Dread* (31:53).** *pachad distinct from 31:42:* "the God of Abraham and of Nahor **judge** (shaphat) between us; so Jacob swore by the **Fear** (pachad) of his father Isaac" *(stated)*. **shaphat** — *judge* (God invoked as arbiter); **pachad** — the gem: Jacob *swears by "the Fear of Isaac"* — where 31:42 *named* the title, here he *binds his oath to it*; the interior *anchors its sworn word in the God his father reveres* — a step in *his own* relation to that God *(inferred: pachad/shaphat = the oath sworn by, and God invoked to judge by, the Dread of Isaac)*.
- **qara (called kinsmen) — the *covenant meal* summoned (31:54).** *qara distinct from the naming-qara:* "Jacob **called** (qara) his kinsmen to eat bread" *(stated)* — qara, *call-to*: the interior *convenes the shared meal* that *seals* the covenant (as covenant-meals do, 26:30) *(inferred: qara = the summons to the covenant meal)*.
- **barakh (blessed) — the *parting blessing* (31:55).** "Laban kissed his grandchildren and daughters and **blessed** (barakh) them, and departed" *(stated)* — barakh, *bless*: the gem is the *softened parting* — the aggrieved father *blesses and leaves*; the wary confrontation *ends in a blessing and separation* — the interior *releasing its own in blessing* *(inferred: barakh = the parting blessing that closes the confrontation)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **God guards the pursued (shamar/tov/ra 31:24 → ra/tov/shamar 31:29):** the LORD *warns Laban to touch nothing*; Laban *owns his power to harm and confesses the restraint* — the fleeing deceiver *fenced by grace* from the pursuer.
2. **the grievances aired (levav 31:26 → simchah 31:27 → kasaph 31:30 → yare 31:31):** Laban charges *heart-theft*, denied *festivity*, and *stolen gods* — granting the *homesick longing*; Jacob owns his *fear* as the reason for stealth.
3. **the blind oath and the ruse (chayah/yada 31:32 → yakhol/charah 31:35):** Jacob *curses the thief unknowingly against Rachel*; Rachel *deceives her father* with composed cunning — the family's stealth in the daughter too.
4. **Jacob's self-vindication (charah/riv/pesha/chattat 31:36 → baqash/chata 31:39 → nadad 31:40 → avad/oni/pachad 31:41–42):** the accused *turns accuser*, demands his *crime*, and lays out *twenty years* of over-charged, sleepless, afflicted service — appealing to *the Fear of Isaac* who *saw and rebuked*.
5. **the covenant of separation (karat/berit 31:44 → rum 31:45 → qara 31:47–48 → anah 31:50 → raah 31:52 → pachad/shaphat 31:53 → qara 31:54 → barakh 31:55):** a *wary treaty* — a *witness-heap named*, the *daughters guarded from affliction*, a *boundary of non-harm*, an *oath by the Dread of Isaac* and *God as judge*, sealed with a *meal* and closed with a *parting blessing*. Not friendship — a *fenced peace* between two who cannot trust.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** shamar/tov/ra ×2 (31:24 God's *warning* / 31:29 Laban's *admission* of it); levav (the *heart-theft named* by the deceived); simchah (the *festive farewell* denied); kasaph (the *homesick longing* granted); yare (the *fear-motive* owned); chayah/yada (the *blind death-oath*, unknowingly against Rachel); yakhol/charah (Rachel's *composed ruse*); charah ×2 (Rachel *deflecting* anger / Jacob's *indignant* anger); riv/pesha/chattat (the *demand for the charge*); baqash/chata (the *over-charged shepherd's borne loss*); nadad (the *sleep that fled*); avad (the *twenty years* tallied); oni (the *affliction seen*); **pachad ×2 — "the Fear of Isaac" (31:42 *named* as vindicator / 31:53 *sworn by*)**; karat/berit (the *covenant of separation*); rum (the *witness-pillar*); qara (the heap *named* 31:47, *confirmed* 31:48, and the *meal summoned* 31:54); anah (the daughters guarded from *affliction*); raah (the *boundary of non-harm*); shaphat (God as *judge*); barakh (the *parting blessing*).
- **Whose interior:** the **divine** interior *restraining the pursuer* and *seeing the afflicted* (the Fear of Isaac, vindicator); the **human** interiors *aggrieved and accusing* (Laban), *fearful, indignant, and self-vindicating* (Jacob), and *deceiving with composure* (Rachel). The confrontation *resolves into a fenced peace*.
- **Threads:** God restrains the harmer (shamar 31:24 ← 20:6; 31:7); the deception-idiom (levav 31:26 ← 31:20 — now named *by* the victim); the blind oath (chayah 31:32 → Rachel's early death 35:18 — the narrator's dark irony); the Fear/Dread of Isaac (pachad 31:42, 53 ← Isaac's proven fear-of-God 22:12; Jacob *swearing by* his father's God — a step toward his own relation, → the Peniel encounter JAC-15); the affliction-God-sees (oni 31:42 ← 29:32; 16:11); covenant of separation (vs the peace-covenants 21:27; 26:28 — here *distrust fenced*).
- **Guarded against:** collapsing the paired merisms (God's *warning* vs Laban's *owned* restraint; the two angers — Rachel's *deflecting* vs Jacob's *indignant*; the two pachad — *named* vs *sworn by*); reading a "desire" operation into the mis-tagged conjunction at 31:43 (flagged, not read); taking Jacob's self-vindication as the narrator's verdict (the text lets him *plead* — his integrity *as shepherd* is real, even as his history is not clean); missing the *dark irony* of the blind oath (chayah 31:32 → Rachel's death); importing a "covenant of grace" reading over what is plainly a *wary boundary-treaty*; treating barakh (31:55) as warm reconciliation when it *closes a separation* — a blessing *and* a parting.

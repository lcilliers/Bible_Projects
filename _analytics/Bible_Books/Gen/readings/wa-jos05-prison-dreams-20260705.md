## JOS-05-prison-dreams · Genesis 40:1–23 · [serving in the pit; the plea remembered, then forgotten] — the downcast noticed, God credited, and the benefactor forgotten

**The passage.** The officers **committed an offense** (chata) (40:1); Pharaoh was **angry** (qatsaph) (40:2); Joseph **attended** (sharat) them (40:4); he saw they were **troubled** (zaaph) (40:6); "why are your faces **downcast** (ra)?" he **asked** (shaal) (40:7); "interpretations belong to God"; the cupbearer restored "as **formerly** (mishpat)" (40:13); "**remember** (zakhar) me… do me the **kindness** (chesed)… when it is **well** (yatav)" (40:14); "I was stolen… the pit"; the baker's dream **favorable** (tov) (40:16); the birds eat his **flesh** (basar) (40:19); fulfilled — "yet the cupbearer did not **remember** (zakhar) Joseph, but forgot him" (40:23).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **chata (committed an offense) — the *occasion* of the prisoners (40:1).** "the cupbearer and baker **committed an offense** (chata) against the king" *(stated)* — chata, *sin/offend*: the *occasion* that brings the two officers into Joseph's prison — the providence-thread that will (much later) free him *(inferred: chata = the offense that seats the two officers in Joseph's prison)*.
- **qatsaph (angry) — Pharaoh's *wrath* (40:2).** "Pharaoh was **angry** (qatsaph) with his two officers" *(stated)* — qatsaph, *be wroth*: the king's anger *casts them down* to the prison; the interior-context of *power's displeasure* *(inferred: qatsaph = Pharaoh's wrath that imprisons the officers)*.
- **sharat (attended) — Joseph *serving in the prison* (40:4).** "Joseph… **attended** (sharat) them" *(stated)* — sharat, *minister/serve* (← 39:4, the honourable service): the gem is that Joseph *ministers even to fellow-prisoners* — the interior *serving faithfully at its lowest station*, its character *unchanged by the pit* *(inferred: sharat = Joseph's faithful service continued in prison)*.
- **zaaph (troubled) — the *distress noticed* (40:6).** "he saw they were **troubled** (zaaph)" *(stated)* — zaaph, *be vexed/dejected*: the gem is that Joseph *notices* their distress — *attentive to others' faces* even amid his own affliction; the interior *turned outward in compassion* *(inferred: zaaph = the prisoners' distress Joseph attentively notices)*.
- **shaal (asked) + ra (downcast) — the *compassionate inquiry* (40:7).** "'why are your faces **downcast** (ra)?' he **asked** (shaal)" *(stated)*. **shaal** — *ask*; **ra** — *bad* (faces *fallen/sad*): the gem is that the *wronged, imprisoned* Joseph *asks after another's sorrow* — compassion *from within his own suffering*; the interior *enquiring into another's grief* *(inferred: shaal/ra = the compassionate inquiry into the downcast faces)*.
- **mishpat (formerly) — the *restoration to custom* (40:13).** "you shall place Pharaoh's cup in his hand as **formerly** (mishpat)" *(stated)* — mishpat in the *custom/former-manner* sense: the cupbearer *restored to his customary office*; the interior-context of *a life given back its ordered place* (the good interpretation) *(inferred: mishpat = the restoration to the former, ordered office)*.
- **zakhar (remember) + chesed (kindness) + yatav (well) — Joseph's *one reach for help* (40:14).** "**remember** (zakhar) me when it is **well** (yatav) with you… do me the **kindness** (chesed) to mention me to Pharaoh" *(stated)*. **zakhar** — *remember*; **chesed** — *steadfast kindness*; **yatav** — *be well*: the gem is *Joseph reaching, once, for human help* — asking the cupbearer to *remember* him and *show chesed* (get him out of "the pit", ← the literal pit 37:24). The interior — otherwise God-trusting — makes its *one appeal to a person* for release *(inferred: zakhar/chesed/yatav = Joseph's single reach for human help — remember me, show kindness)*.
- **tov (favorable) — the baker's *self-interested hope* (40:16).** "when the baker saw the interpretation was **favorable** (tov), he told his dream" *(stated)* — tov, *good*: the gem is *why* the baker asks — he *saw the good outcome* for the cupbearer and *hoped for the same*; the interior *emboldened by another's good news* to seek its own *(inferred: tov = the favourable interpretation that emboldens the baker's hope)*.
- **basar (flesh) — the *grim* interpretation (40:19).** "the birds will eat the **flesh** (basar) from you" *(stated)* — basar, *flesh*: the gem is that Joseph *speaks the hard truth* — the baker's dream means *death* — the interior *faithful to the true interpretation even when grim* (not flattering the hoped-for good) *(inferred: basar = the flesh eaten — the true, grim interpretation spoken)*.
- **zakhar (remember) — the *benefactor forgotten* (40:23).** *zakhar distinct from 40:14:* "yet the cupbearer did not **remember** (zakhar) Joseph, but forgot him" *(stated)* — the gem and *frame's close*: the *plea to remember* (40:14) *answered by forgetting* (40:23). **zakhar** — the interior of *ingratitude* — the restored man *forgets his benefactor*; and (providentially) the forgetting *delays* Joseph to the *right moment* (ch41). The plea and its failure *bracket* the passage *(inferred: zakhar = the benefactor forgotten — ingratitude, and the providential delay)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **the prison filled, Joseph serving (chata 40:1 → qatsaph 40:2 → sharat 40:4):** two offenders come under Pharaoh's *wrath* into Joseph's prison, and Joseph *ministers* to them — his character *unchanged by his station*.
2. **the downcast noticed and asked after (zaaph 40:6 → shaal/ra 40:7):** the *wronged prisoner notices others' distress* and *asks after their sorrow* — compassion *outward from within affliction*.
3. **God credited, help sought (mishpat 40:13 → zakhar/chesed/yatav 40:14 → tov/basar 40:16–19):** Joseph *credits interpretations to God*, interprets *truly* (the good and the grim), and *reaches once* for human help — *"remember me, show chesed."*
4. **the benefactor forgotten (zakhar 40:23):** the cupbearer, *restored*, *forgets* Joseph — the *plea to remember* met by *forgetting*; the one human reach *fails* — and the delay *serves the providence*.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** chata (the *offense* that seats the officers in the prison); qatsaph (Pharaoh's *wrath*); **sharat (Joseph's *faithful service* continued in the pit — character unchanged)**; **zaaph/shaal/ra (the *compassionate inquiry* — noticing another's downcast face from within his own affliction)**; mishpat (the *restoration to custom*); **zakhar/chesed/yatav (Joseph's *one reach for human help* — remember me, show kindness)**; tov (the *favourable* interpretation emboldening the baker); basar (the *grim* truth spoken faithfully); **zakhar (the benefactor *forgotten* — ingratitude, and the providential delay)**.
- **Whose interior (multi-interior):** **Joseph** — *serving, attentive, God-crediting, truthful* (even the grim reading), and — a human touch — *reaching once for release* through the cupbearer; the **cupbearer** — *ingratitude*, forgetting his benefactor; **God** — the *source of interpretations* ("interpretations belong to God"), and the providence in the *forgetting* (delay to the right moment). Joseph's compassion-outward-in-affliction is the standout.
- **Threads:** *God credits/reveals* ("interpretations belong to God" 40:8 → Pharaoh's dreams, "God will give the answer" 41:16); the **pit** (chesed "out of this pit" 40:14 ← the literal pit 37:24 — Joseph's descents); *serving faithfully at the lowest* (sharat 40:4 ← 39:4); the **forgotten benefactor** (zakhar 40:23 → the cupbearer *remembers* at last, 41:9 — the delay ends at the right time); Joseph's *innocence* protested ("I have done nothing", 40:15 ← the false charge 39); the *compassion-outward* (→ Joseph weeping for his brothers, JOS-08).
- **Guarded against:** lumping the two zakhar (Joseph's *plea to remember* / the cupbearer's *failure to remember* — the frame is the finding); reading a "shame" operation into the mis-glossed second chesed (the word is *kindness*); over-reading Joseph's reach for help (40:14) as a *lapse of faith* when the text presents it simply as a *plea* (a human touch, not condemned); treating the baker's death as arbitrary when Joseph speaks the *true* interpretation faithfully; importing a "God's timing" sermon over the plain *forgetting* the verse states (the providence is *shown*, not preached).

## HOS-false-heart · Hos 10:1–15 · [D, multi] — the false/divided heart; the summons to seek

**The reading.** The oracle diagnoses the interior and prescribes the remedy. Diagnosis: "**their heart is false**; now they must bear their guilt" (v.2, chalaq — divided/smooth); "you have **trusted in your own way** and in the multitude of your warriors" (v.13). The prescription, its gem: "**Sow for yourselves righteousness; reap steadfast love; break up your fallow ground, for it is the time to seek the LORD**, that he may come and rain righteousness upon you" (v.12). The finding is **the divided heart summoned to *break up its fallow ground* and sow righteousness, so as to reap steadfast love** *(stated)*. The operation is **the interior re-cultivated — the hardened, false heart broken open like unplowed ground to seek God in season** *(inferred: "their heart is false" (v.2, leb chalaq — a *divided/slippery* heart) names the same double-mindedness as the unturned cake (7:8); against it, v.12 offers a striking agricultural inner-being image — "break up your fallow ground" (nir lakem nir): the hardened, un-worked heart must be *plowed open* before righteousness can be sown and chesed reaped; the finding is that return is not a moment but a *cultivation* — the self breaking its own hard ground and sowing, "for it is the time to seek the LORD"; and the alternative is named: "you have trusted in your own way" (v.13) — the false heart's self-reliance, which reaps injustice)*.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *the false/divided heart (leb chalaq)* (thread — w/ 7:8 the unturned cake); *breaking up the fallow ground (the hardened heart plowed open to seek God)* (new — a gem; return as cultivation); *sowing righteousness to reap steadfast love* (new — w/ Pro 11:18); *trusting one's own way (the false heart's self-reliance)* (new).

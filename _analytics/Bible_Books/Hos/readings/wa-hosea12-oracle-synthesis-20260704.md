## HOS-jacob-return · Hos 12:1–14 · [D, multi] — Jacob's striving; return, hold fast, wait

**The reading.** The oracle reaches back to Jacob as a mirror: "in the womb he took his brother by the heel, and in his manhood he **strove with God**. He strove with the angel and prevailed; he **wept and sought his favour**" (vv.3–4). The word to the present: "**So you, by the help of your God, return, hold fast to love and justice, and wait continually for your God**" (v.6). Against it, the self-deceived merchant: "Ephraim said, 'Ah, but **I am rich; I have found wealth for myself; in all my labours they cannot find in me iniquity or sin**'" (v.8). The finding is **the summons to return and *hold fast* to steadfast love and justice, and to *wait continually* for God — set against a self-satisfied heart that sees no sin in itself** *(stated)*. The operation is **the interior called to a sustained, waiting fidelity (return + hold-fast chesed and justice + wait), against the complacent self that thinks itself innocent** *(inferred: v.6 is a compact rule for the returned interior — three sustained postures: *return* (shuv), *hold fast to chesed and mishpat* (the covenant graces, again), and *wait continually* (qavah tamid) for God — fidelity as a *durable, expectant* orientation, not a moment; and Jacob is the pattern: one who *strove* with God and *wept and sought his favour* (v.4) — struggle and tears that end in seeking, not in self-sufficiency; the counter-figure is the merchant who says "I am rich… they cannot find iniquity in me" (v.8) — the complacent interior blind to its own sin (cf. Rev 3:17), the exact opposite of Jacob's weeping struggle; the finding is that true return is a *sustained holding-fast and waiting*, and its enemy is the self-satisfied heart)*.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *return as sustained holding-fast to chesed and justice + continual waiting* (thread — w/ Lam 3:25's waiting, Mic 6:8's do-justice-love-kindness; the durable returned interior); *Jacob's striving-and-weeping that ends in seeking* (new — struggle resolved in seeking God's favour); *the self-satisfied heart blind to its own sin ("they cannot find iniquity in me")* (new — the complacent interior, w/ Rev 3:17).

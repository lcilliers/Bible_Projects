## HOS-not-from-heart · Hos 7:1–16 · [D, multi] — the un-hearted cry; the half-baked, divided self

**The reading.** A cluster of images anatomises a divided interior. Their passions: "with **hearts like an oven** they approach their intrigue; all night their anger smoulders; in the morning it **blazes**" (v.6). Their double-mindedness: "**Ephraim is a cake not turned**" (v.8) — half-baked, burnt one side and raw the other. Their self-oblivion: "**strangers devour his strength, and he knows it not**; gray hairs are sprinkled on him, and he knows it not" (v.9). And the core: "they **do not cry to me from the heart**, but they **wail upon their beds**… **they return, but not upward**" (vv.14,16). The finding is **religion and distress without the heart — a self that wails but does not cry to God from within, half-committed, unaware of its own decay** *(stated)*. The operation is **the divided, un-hearted interior** — passions hot as an oven, faith a cake unturned, grief real but not God-directed, return that goes sideways ("not upward") *(inferred: the string of images all name a *split* interior — the oven-heart (consumed by intrigue and anger), the unturned cake (half-cooked commitment), the return "not upward" (motion without ascent to God); the decisive one is v.14 — "they do not cry to me *from the heart*" (mi-libbam): they wail (over lost grain and wine) but the cry is not *to God from within*; the finding is that the fatal fault is not the absence of religious emotion but its *mis-direction* — grief and heat aplenty, none of it the heart turned Godward; and the self-oblivion (v.9, "he knows it not") compounds it — the divided interior cannot even see its own graying decline)*.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *the un-hearted cry (wailing, but not crying to God from the heart)* (new — mis-directed distress, w/ the shallow return 6:1); *the half-baked/divided self (the cake not turned)* (new — double-mindedness); *self-oblivious decay ("he knows it not")* (new); *return "not upward" (motion without ascent to God)* (new).

## HOS-marriage-signs · Hos 1:2–9 · [D, multi] — whoredom as the faithless interior, named in the children

**The reading.** God's first word is an enacted sign: "take to yourself a **wife of whoredom**… for the land commits **great whoredom by forsaking the LORD**" (v.2). The three children are named as verdicts: **Jezreel** (judgment), **No Mercy** ("I will no more have mercy"), **Not My People** ("you are not my people, and I am not your God", vv.4–9). The finding is **unfaithfulness as the nation's inner condition** — "whoredom" names not merely idol-acts but a *heart that forsakes* its covenant-partner *(stated)*. The operation is **the interior betrayal made visible and named** — God dramatises Israel's inner adultery in a marriage and its bitter fruit (a broken relationship, "not my people") *(inferred: "whoredom" (zenunim) is the book's governing image for the divided, faithless interior — a heart given to other loves; the child-names trace the relational consequence of that inner betrayal — mercy withdrawn, belonging severed; the finding is that spiritual unfaithfulness is a *heart-condition* (forsaking) whose fruit is the rupture of the deepest bond)*.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *whoredom / spiritual unfaithfulness as a heart forsaking its covenant-partner* (new — the book's governing interior image); *the relational fruit of inner betrayal (No Mercy / Not My People)* (new).

## HOS-reversal · Hos 1:10–2:1 · [D, multi] — rejection reversed by grace

**The reading.** Immediately the verdict is overturned: "in the place where it was said, '**You are not my people**,' it shall be said, '**Children of the living God**'" (1:10); No Mercy becomes "you have received **mercy**," Not My People becomes "you are **my people**" (2:1). The finding is **the severed belonging restored by sheer grace** — the very names of judgment are reversed *(stated)*. The operation is **grace that reaches past the verdict to re-adopt** — the rejected are re-named children of the *living* God *(inferred: the reversal is not earned (nothing changes in Israel between v.9 and v.10) but declared — the finding is a grace that *precedes* repentance, re-establishing the belonging that unfaithfulness forfeited; the interior note is the sheer un-deservedness of restored sonship)*.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *severed belonging restored by unearned grace (Not-My-People → Children of the living God)* (new); *grace preceding repentance* (thread — recurs 11:8, 14:4).

## HOS-withdrawn · Hos 5:1–15 · [D, multi] — the God who withdraws until the heart seeks his face

**The reading.** The oracle names why return is blocked: "their **deeds do not permit them to return** to their God. For the **spirit of whoredom is within them, and they know not the LORD**" (v.4); so "they shall go to **seek the LORD, but they will not find him; he has withdrawn** from them" (v.6). The oracle's hinge: "**I will return again to my place, until they acknowledge their guilt and seek my face, and in their distress earnestly seek me**" (v.15). The finding is **a God who deliberately withdraws — making himself un-findable — until the heart is driven to *earnestly* seek his face** *(stated)*. The operation is **the divine hiding that is itself a summons — absence engineered to provoke a real seeking** *(inferred: two inner-being data sit together — (1) sin as a *disabling* condition ("their deeds do not permit them to return", v.4; the spirit of whoredom *within*): the faithless heart cannot simply turn, it is bound; (2) God's remedy is *withdrawal* (v.15) — he removes himself "until they… seek my face… in their distress earnestly seek me": the finding is that God's felt absence is not abandonment but a *provocation* to genuine seeking (baqash), the affliction that finally turns a heart that comfort could not; this is the same pattern as Lamentations (the hidden face that drives the cry) and answers the *shallow* seeking of the next oracle (6:1–3) — real seeking is what distress produces, not what ease presumes)*.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *the God who withdraws to provoke a real seeking (engineered absence as summons)* (thread — the hidden face, w/ Lam 3, Job 23:8); *sin as a disabling bond ("deeds do not permit them to return")* (new — the heart that cannot simply turn); *distress as what produces earnest seeking* (thread — w/ 6:1–3, the shallow contrast).

## Closing synthesis

**What recurred across the whole book (structurally load-bearing threads, confirmed by resolution above):**

1. **The judgment/mercy oscillation, resolved decisively in favour of mercy.** Ch9:15's "I will love them no more" (EQ19) is real but not final — resolved at ch11:8-9 ("my compassion grows warm and tender... I am God and not a man") and reaffirmed at ch14:4 ("my anger has turned from them... I will love them freely"). Ch13:14's apparent re-complication (EQ26) is understood as belonging to ch13's own internal judgment-oracle, not a reopening. The book's structure is not a flat linear move from judgment to mercy but an oscillation that nonetheless resolves, at its close, decisively toward mercy — and the resolution's ground is always the LORD's own transcendent character, never Israel's own merit or changed conduct (confirmed independently by the Jacob-typology thread, EQ25, ch12/13/14).
2. **The "return" (shuv) thread is the single most structurally significant word in the book**, tracing a complete arc: called for (ch14:1) → confessed but transient (ch6:1-4) → flatly denied (ch7:10) → hollowed (ch7:16) → historicized as judgment ("return to Egypt," ch8:13/9:3,6) → refused by Israel (ch11:5) → reversed by the LORD's own initiative ("I will return them," ch11:11) → finally realized, by the identical verb, in both directions at once (ch14:1/4/7 — Israel's own return, the LORD's anger turning away, and the return's fulfillment, all sharing one root).
3. **Israel/Judah's relative standing oscillates rather than settling** (EQ24) — a structural parallel to the judgment/mercy oscillation itself, not a defect requiring harmonization; the two kingdoms are consistently judged for their own distinct failure-types (EQ11) even as their relative standing shifts oracle to oracle.
4. **The "heart" (lev) thread** runs from human corruption/absence (ch7) through the pride/judgment organ-linkage (ch13:6/8) to its most weighted instance — the LORD's own heart recoiling (ch11:8) — confirming EQ16 as a major cross-book device.
5. **Restoration is never self-effort, always gift** (EQ25, EQ4) — every restoration passage gives Israel real, stated agency, but that agency is consistently sequential to, never causative of, the LORD's own prior unilateral initiative; the Jacob-typology's own self-directed-labour/divinely-initiated-deliverance contrast (ch12:12-13) is confirmed as a genuine whole-book pattern by ch13:4-5 and ch14:4-8.
6. **The marriage/mirror device (Q12) operates at two registers** — whole-chapter structural enactment (ch1-2) and single-verse explicit comparison (ch3:1) — confirmed as the same device, not two competing ones (EQ1).

**What never resolved, and is recorded as genuinely open past this book:**

1. **EQ27 — the ethical weight of corporate judgment landing on non-culpable individuals** (ch13:16's infants and pregnant women, killed for a collective's "guilt" they had no capacity to commit). No passage in Hosea addresses this directly; the book turns to restoration (ch14) without revisiting it. This is the single clearest unresolved thread in the whole book and should be flagged as such in any downstream narrative synthesis, not smoothed over.
2. **EQ23 — Egypt's own multivalence.** Never harmonized into one sense across the book's own text; the closing chapter renounces Assyria but is silent on Egypt specifically.
3. Minor, lower-stakes open items: EQ3 (the wife/mother figure is given real voice but never named), EQ5 (corporate/derivative guilt never named as a formal category, though it is the same underlying issue as EQ27), EQ6/EQ9/EQ10/EQ15/EQ17/EQ20 (one-off observed techniques or vocabulary items that were never explicitly revisited by later passages — recorded as such, not treated as failures of the method).

**For the book narrative:** the load-bearing spine is the shuv arc (return, called/refused/reversed/realized) carrying the judgment-to-mercy oscillation that resolves decisively at ch14; the marriage-metaphor (Q12, both registers) is the frame within which that arc plays out; the Jacob-typology and the "heart" thread supply the clearest textured evidence that the resolution is never self-earned. EQ27 (ch13:16) is the one place where the narrative should not manufacture a false resolution the text itself does not supply.

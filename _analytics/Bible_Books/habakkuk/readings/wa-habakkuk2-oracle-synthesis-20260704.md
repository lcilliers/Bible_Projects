## HAB-answer2-faith · Hab 2:2–5 · [D, multi] — wait for the vision; the righteous shall live by his faith

**The reading.** God answers the watching prophet: "**Write the vision; make it plain**… For still the vision **awaits its appointed time**… **If it seems slow, wait for it; it will surely come**" (vv.2–3). Then the line that anchors the whole Bible's doctrine of faith: "**Behold, his soul is puffed up; it is not upright within him, but the righteous shall live by his faith**" (v.4). The finding is **two interiors contrasted — the *puffed-up* soul (not upright within) and the *righteous who lives by faith(fulness)* — with the summons to *wait* for a vision certain but not yet fulfilled** *(stated)*. The operation is **the self called to live, in the gap before the vision comes, by *faith* — not by the proud self-sufficiency that cannot wait** *(inferred: God's answer to the justice-crisis is not an explanation but a *posture for the interval* — the vision is *coming* (v.3, certain but delayed), and in the meantime the choice is between two hearts: the "puffed up" soul (v.4, uppelah — swollen, self-inflated, "not upright/straight within him") and the "righteous" who "shall **live by his faith**" (emunah — steadfast trust/faithfulness); the finding is that the answer to unresolved injustice is *faith that lives* — the just person survives the waiting-time not by having the answer but by *trusting God's certain-but-delayed* word, while the proud self, unable to wait or trust, collapses on its own inflation; "the righteous shall live by his faith" (v.4) is the inner-being heart of the book — faith as the *sustaining* posture through the un-resolved interval, the exact opposite of the puffed-up self that must have and control now)*.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *the righteous who lives by faith (steadfast trust sustaining through the un-resolved interval)* (thread — the book's heart; the answer to the justice-crisis is faith-that-waits, w/ 2:1, Rom 1:17); *the puffed-up soul, not upright within (self-inflation that cannot wait or trust)* (new — the contrast interior); *the vision certain but delayed, to be waited for* (new — trust in a not-yet-fulfilled word).

## HAB-woes · Hab 2:6–20 · [D, multi] — greed and violence recoiling; idols vs the living God

**The reading.** Five woes anatomise the proud, violent interior and its self-recoil: woe to him who "**heaps up what is not his own**" (v.6); who "gets **evil gain** for his house, to set his **nest on high, to be safe from harm**" (v.9); who "**builds a town with blood**" (v.12); who "**makes his neighbours drink**… to gaze at their nakedness" (v.15); and to the idolater whose image is "**a teacher of lies**… **there is no breath at all in it**" (vv.18–19). Against all of it: "the earth will be **filled with the knowledge of the glory of the LORD** as the waters cover the sea" (v.14); "**the LORD is in his holy temple; let all the earth keep silence** before him" (v.20). The finding is **the greedy, violent, self-securing interior whose sins recoil on it — set against the living God before whom the whole earth falls silent** *(stated)*. The operation is **the plundering self that builds its "nest on high" for safety, exposed as building on blood, its gains turning to shame — while the idols it trusts are breathless and the LORD alone is God** *(inferred: the woes trace an interior arc — insatiable greed (the wide-as-Sheol appetite, 2:5), the drive to *secure the self* ("nest on high… safe from harm", v.9, the illusion of unassailable security), violence as foundation, and the degradation of others (making them drunk to gaze on them); each *recoils* (the cup comes around to you, v.16; the stones cry from the wall, v.11); and the climactic contrast is *idols vs the living God* — the maker "trusts in his own creation" (v.18), a "teacher of lies" with "no breath", against "the LORD in his holy temple" before whom "all the earth keeps silence" (v.20); the finding is that the proud, grasping interior builds on what cannot hold and worships what cannot breathe, while the true response of the self is *silence before the living God*)*.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *the self-securing interior (the "nest on high", safe from harm) exposed as built on blood* (new — the illusion of unassailable security, w/ Obad 3-4); *greed/violence recoiling on its doer (the cup comes around)* (thread — sin's recoil, w/ Job 20:12, Pro); *idols as breathless lies vs the living God* (new — trusting one's own creation); *silence before the living God (the earth keeps silence)* (new — the fitting posture, w/ Zeph 1:7).

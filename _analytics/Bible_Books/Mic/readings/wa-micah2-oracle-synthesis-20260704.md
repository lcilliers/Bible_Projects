## MIC-woe-covet · Mic 2:1–11 · [D, multi] — the scheming, coveting interior; the ear that wants comfort

**The reading.** The woe falls on premeditated greed: "**Woe to those who devise wickedness and work evil on their beds**! When the morning dawns, they perform it… They **covet fields and seize them**, and houses… they oppress a man and his inheritance" (vv.1–2). And on a people who want only pleasant preaching: "'**Do not preach**'… If a man should… say, 'I will preach to you of **wine and strong drink**,' he would be the preacher for this people!" (vv.6,11). The finding is **evil rehearsed on the bed before it is done, and an ear that wants a preacher of comfort, not truth** *(stated)*. The operation is **the interior that *plans* its sin in advance (coveting on the bed) and *selects* the message it wants to hear** *(inferred: v.1 exposes the *premeditation* of greed — wickedness "devised on their beds" through the night, "performed" at dawn "because it is in the power of their hand": sin as a plotted project of the covetous heart, not a slip; and vv.6,11 expose the *audience* — a people who silence the true word and would crown a preacher of "wine and strong drink"; the finding pairs two inner faults: the scheming that premeditates evil, and the itching ear that curates its own comforting message (cf. 2 Tim 4:3); both are the self managing reality to suit its desire)*.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *premeditated greed (evil devised on the bed, performed at dawn)* (new — sin as a plotted project of the covetous heart); *the ear that wants comfort not truth (the preacher of wine)* (new — the curated message, w/ 2 Tim 4:3).

## MIC-remnant-gathered · Mic 2:12–13 · [D] — the remnant gathered under God's lead

**The reading.** A sudden note of hope: "I will **gather the remnant of Israel**… like sheep in a fold… **their king passes on before them, the LORD at their head**" (vv.12–13). The finding is **the scattered gathered and led out by God himself at their head** *(stated)*. The operation is **the flock re-collected under a God who goes *before* them** — the breach-opener leading the way out (v.13) *(inferred; hope amid judgment: the God who scatters also gathers and leads; a brief interior of restored belonging under God's lead)*.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *the remnant gathered and led by God at their head* (thread — restored belonging, w/ the shepherd, 5:4, 7:14).

- **Lens:** the inner-being **operation** is the finding; worship, money and toil are the arena.

## ECC-05-A · Ecc 5:1–7 · [D, multi] — reverent restraint before God; the guarded mouth and few words

**The reading.** The one sustained passage on worship in the book makes reverence a matter of *inner restraint*: "**Guard your steps** when you go to the house of God. To draw near to **listen** is better than to offer the sacrifice of fools" (v.1) — reverence begins as *listening*, not performing *(stated)*. Then the heart and mouth: "**be not rash with your mouth**, nor let your **heart be hasty** to utter a word before God, for God is in heaven and you are on earth; therefore let your **words be few**" (v.2). The finding is **awe expressed as the restraint of speech and heart** — the properly reverent interior is *slow*, *quiet*, *listening*, aware of the distance between earth and heaven *(stated)*. The unit closes by naming the ground of it: "**God is the one you must fear**" (v.7). The operation is **reverent fear enacted as verbal and inner economy** — the fearing self does not fill the silence before God with words but holds itself in listening restraint *(inferred: the rash mouth and hasty heart (v.2) are the failures of a self that does not feel the earth/heaven gap; the fear of God (v.7) is what quiets them — reverence as the discipline of the tongue and the racing heart)*.

**Lexical validation (read-back).** *Guard* (H8104), *listen* (H8085), *know* (H3045), *rash* (H0926), *fear* (H3372) correctly `characteristic`. *heart* (H3820) seated (`seat=heart`) as the thing not to let be hasty — load-bearing. Note *mouth* (H6310) tagged variously `seat=heart` (v.2) and `seat=flesh` (v.6) — the mouth is the *outlet* of heart and flesh, a reasonable seat-attribution though the mouth is properly the organ of speech, not itself a seat; a mild over-attribution, not affecting the reading. The neg-particle `prohibition` stamps (v.2 "be *not* rash," v.6 "let *not* your mouth") are trigger-correct (real prohibitions — this *is* imperative instruction). Sound.

**Hidden / less-obvious characteristics isolated.** (1) **Listening as the first act of worship** — "to draw near to *listen*" (v.1): reverence is receptive before it is expressive; the inner posture is an *open ear*, not an offered sacrifice (a hidden characteristic — worship as intake, kin to Ps 40:6 "an ear you have dug for me"). (2) **The earth/heaven gap as the regulator of speech** — "God is in heaven and you are on earth" (v.2): the self's *sense of its own smallness before God* is what produces the few words; awe felt as spatial distance, disciplining the tongue.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *reverent restraint (the guarded mouth, the un-hasty heart)* (new — worship as inner economy); *listening as the first posture of worship* (new); *the fear of God* (shared cornerstone, here enacted as verbal restraint); *the sense of the earth/heaven gap* (new).

## ECC-05-B · Ecc 5:8–9 · [S] — the oppression of the poor observed; tiers of watching power

**The reading.** A brief observer's note: seeing "the **oppression of the poor** and the violation of justice and righteousness in a province, **do not be amazed** at the matter, for the high official is **watched by a higher**, and there are yet higher ones over them" (v.8). The inner-being finding is slight but real: **the steadying of the self against outrage** — "do not be amazed" counsels a composure that does not come undone at systemic injustice, because the tiers of power watch each other *(stated)*. The operation is **composure before a rigged order** — not approval of the oppression, but a refusal to let it destabilise the interior *(inferred: the observer, who elsewhere grieves the oppressed to the point of a death-wish (4:1–3), here counsels a different response — not shock but a sober, un-amazed recognition of how power is layered; the two responses (grief in ch.4, composure here) are both honest inner-being postures toward the same evil)*.

**Lexical validation (read-back).** *justice* (H4941), *righteousness* (H6664), *amazed* (H8539), *watched* (H8104), *high official/higher* (H1364) carried. *amazed* correctly `characteristic` — the inner reaction the verse counsels against (with real negation "do *not* be amazed"). The bulk is social observation sitting `standalone`; the reading extracts the thin inner-being thread (composure), correctly.

**Hidden / less-obvious characteristics isolated.** **Two legitimate inner responses to the same injustice** — the book holds *both* the grief of 4:1 and the composure of 5:8 without resolving them; the inner-being datum is that a mature interior can weep at oppression *and* refuse to be destabilised by it, depending on the season (cf. the seasoned affections of 3:1–8).

**Characteristics surfaced:** *composure before a rigged order (the un-amazed self)* (new — thin but real); *the coexistence of grief and composure toward injustice* (new — a cross-unit note w/ 4:1).

## ECC-05-C · Ecc 5:10–17 · [D, multi, thread] — the money-lover never satisfied; riches held to one's own hurt

**The reading.** The never-satisfied thread meets money directly: "he who **loves money** will **not be satisfied** with money, nor he who **loves wealth** with his income" (v.10). The finding is **desire that its own object cannot fill** — loving money guarantees dissatisfaction *with money*, because the appetite is not for the thing but is bottomless *(stated)*. Then the somatic cost: "the **full stomach** of the rich will **not let him sleep**" (v.12) — abundance producing insomnia, where the labourer's sleep is "sweet." And the tragedy of hoarding: "riches were **kept by their owner to his hurt**" then lost, leaving "**nothing in his hand**" (vv.13–14); "all his days he **eats in darkness**, in much **vexation and sickness and anger**" (v.17). The operation is **the money-loving self consuming itself** — the appetite that cannot be satisfied turns abundance into sleeplessness, hoarding into loss, and the whole life into a dark, vexed, angry consumption *(inferred: v.10 states the law (love of money is structurally un-fillable); vv.12–17 trace its bodily and emotional wages — the never-satisfied thread here produces not just emptiness but active torment, "vexation and sickness and anger")*.

**Lexical validation (read-back).** *loves* (H0157, *aheb* — "loves money," the misdirected affection, correctly `characteristic` under the aheb-fix), *vexation* (H3708), *sickness* (H2483), *anger* (H7110), *grievous* (H2470), *evil* (H7451), *riches* (H6239) all carried. The love-of-money as *characteristic* is exactly the aheb promotion working — desire fastened on the wrong object. Sound; strong substrate for the reading.

**Hidden / less-obvious characteristics isolated.** (1) **The self-defeating structure of money-love** — not "money is bad" but "*loving* money guarantees dissatisfaction *with money*": the appetite is mis-calibrated to an object that cannot answer it (the same shape as the eternity-shaped heart of 3:11 — a reach the temporal cannot fill, here fastened destructively on wealth). (2) **Abundance as insomnia** — "the full stomach will not let him sleep" (v.12): the finding that *having more* actively steals rest, inverting the ordinary assumption; the anxious rich interior.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *money-love as structurally un-fillable desire* (new — the never-satisfied thread fastened on wealth); *abundance as sleeplessness / the anxious rich* (new); *the self consumed by vexation, sickness and anger* (new); *misdirected love (aheb on money)* (shared w/ Ps 4:2 "love vanity," Pro 1:22).

## ECC-05-D · Ecc 5:18–20 · [T, multi, thread] — joy as the gift of God (refrain); the occupied heart

**The reading.** The refrain returns, now with a crucial addition. The good is "to **eat and drink and find enjoyment** in all the toil" (v.18); but the fuller gift is when God gives wealth "**and power to enjoy them**, and to **accept his lot** and **rejoice** in his toil — this is the **gift of God**" (v.19). The finding is **enjoyment as a granted capacity plus an accepted lot** — not merely having good things but the God-given *power to enjoy* them and the inner willingness to *accept one's portion* *(stated)*. The gem is the last verse: "he will **not much remember** the days of his life because God **keeps him occupied with joy in his heart**" (v.20). The operation is **joy that occupies the heart and quiets the reckoning** — the enjoying self is so filled with present gladness that it stops tallying the days (the very tallying that produced the vanity-verdict elsewhere) *(inferred: joy here is not distraction but a divine *occupation* of the interior — "occupied with joy in his heart"; against the ruminating, day-counting, never-satisfied self of 5:10–17, this is its healed opposite — a heart so engaged by present joy it is released from anxious remembrance)*.

**Lexical validation (read-back).** *enjoyment/good* (H2896), *rejoice* (H8055), *given* (H5414, the gift-operation, twice), *wealth* (H6239), *remember* (H2142), *occupied* (H6031), *heart* (H3820, seated) correctly carried. The pivot *occupied* (H6031, v.20) is correctly `characteristic` — God occupying the heart with joy; load-bearing for the reading. Sound.

**Hidden / less-obvious characteristics isolated.** (1) **The power-to-enjoy as distinct from the goods enjoyed** — "wealth *and power to enjoy them*" (v.19): the inner capacity for enjoyment is itself the gift, set up here for its tragic negation in 6:2 (wealth given, power-to-enjoy withheld). (2) **Joy as release from the day-counting mind** — "he will not much remember the days… because God keeps him occupied with joy" (v.20): joy that *displaces* anxious rumination; the healed interior is not one that has answered the vanity-question but one so occupied with present gladness it no longer asks it.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *joy as granted capacity + accepted lot (the refrain deepened)* (thread, w/ 2:24, 3:12); *the power-to-enjoy as gift* (new — set against 6:2); *joy that occupies the heart and quiets the reckoning* (new — the healed opposite of the ruminating self).

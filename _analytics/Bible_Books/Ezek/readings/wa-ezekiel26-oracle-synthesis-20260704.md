## EZE-tyre-i-am-a-god · Ezekiel 26:1–28:26 · [D, multi] — self-deifying pride; the fall from perfection through pride in beauty; wisdom corrupted by vanity

**The reading.** Tyre gloats over Jerusalem's fall as a commercial opportunity — "**Aha… I shall be replenished, now that she is laid waste**" (26:2) — but the oracle's inner-being heart is the *prince* and *king* of Tyre. The prince: "**Because your heart is proud, and you have said, 'I am a god, I sit in the seat of the gods'**… by your wisdom you have made wealth… **and your heart has become proud in your wealth**" (28:2,5); yet "**you are but a man, and no god, in the hands of those who slay you**" (28:9). The king, in exalted imagery: "**You were the signet of perfection, full of wisdom and perfect in beauty… You were blameless in your ways from the day you were created, till unrighteousness was found in you**" (28:12,15); "**Your heart was proud because of your beauty; you corrupted your wisdom for the sake of your splendour**" (28:17). The finding is **self-deifying *pride* ("I am a god"), a *fall from perfection* ("blameless… till unrighteousness was found in you"), and the specific mechanism: *the heart made proud by beauty, wisdom corrupted for splendour*** *(stated)*. The operation is **the anatomy of the highest pride — the creature *claiming godhood* ("I am a god, I sit in the seat of the gods") on the strength of its wisdom and wealth, while remaining "**but a man**"; and the disclosure that this pride *arose out of gifts* — "**your heart was proud because of your BEAUTY**", and "**you corrupted your WISDOM for the sake of your splendour**" — the very excellences (beauty, wisdom) that were *given* becoming the *occasion* of the fall, turned from serving God to serving the self's glory** *(inferred: 28:2's "**I am a god, I sit in the seat of the gods**" is the archetypal terminal pride (as Isa 14, the self usurping deity) — the finding is that the summit of pride is *self-deification*: the creature, on the strength of its achievements ("by your wisdom you made wealth"), *claims the place of God*, forgetting it is "**but a man**" (28:9); and 28:15–17 gives the *mechanism of the fall* with rare precision — the king was "**blameless… TILL unrighteousness was found in you**" (a fall *from* a good estate), and the specific cause was "**your heart was PROUD BECAUSE OF YOUR BEAUTY**… you **corrupted your wisdom for the sake of your splendour**": the finding is that *the gifts themselves become the occasion of ruin* — beauty and wisdom (God-given excellences) breed *pride* when the heart takes them as grounds for self-exaltation, and wisdom is *corrupted* (bent from truth) when pressed into the service of "splendour" (self-glory); the deepest gifts are the most dangerous, because they most tempt the heart to say "I am a god"; the finding is that the fall is not from *lack* but from *excellence misused* — the perfect, wise, beautiful one made proud by the very perfection, wisdom, and beauty that should have magnified the Giver)*.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *self-deifying pride ("I am a god, I sit in the seat of the gods"… "you are but a man, and no god" — the creature claiming godhood on the strength of its achievements)* (thread — a gem: the summit of pride is self-deification, forgetting one is "but a man", w/ 28:2,9, Isa 14:13-14, Gen 3:5, 2 Thess 2:4); *the fall from a good estate ("blameless… till unrighteousness was found in you" — ruin from excellence, not from lack)* (new — a gem: the fall of the gifted through misuse of the gift, w/ 28:15, Isa 14:12); *the gift as the occasion of pride ("your heart was proud because of your beauty; you corrupted your wisdom for your splendour" — God-given excellences turned to self-glory)* (new — a gem: the deepest gifts most tempt the heart to self-exaltation; beauty breeding pride, wisdom corrupted for self-glory, w/ 28:17, 1 Cor 4:7, Deut 8:17); *gloating opportunism ("Aha… I shall be replenished, now that she is laid waste" — profit sought in a neighbour's ruin)* (thread — w/ 26:2, 25:3).

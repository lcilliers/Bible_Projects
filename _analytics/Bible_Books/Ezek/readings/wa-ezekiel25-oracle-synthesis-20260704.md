## EZE-gloat-over-fall · Ezekiel 25:1–17 · [D, multi] — malicious gloating over another's fall; vengeance nursed in the soul

**The reading.** The oracles against Ammon, Moab, Edom, and Philistia judge them not for military acts but for *inner dispositions*. Ammon is condemned "**because you said, 'Aha!' over my sanctuary when it was profaned, and over the land of Israel when it was laid waste**" (v.3), having "**clapped your hands and stamped your feet and rejoiced with all the malice within your soul**" (v.6). Edom "**acted revengefully… in taking vengeance**" (v.12); Philistia "**took vengeance with malice of soul to destroy in never-ending enmity**" (v.15). The finding is **the interior of *malicious gloating* over another's ruin ("Aha!… rejoiced with all the malice within your soul") and *vengeance nursed as never-ending enmity*** *(stated)*. The operation is **the exposure of two related disorders of the interior: *Schadenfreude* — the "Aha!" of *delight at a neighbour's downfall*, "rejoicing with all the malice within the soul"; and *nursed vengefulness* — an "enmity" cultivated into something "never-ending", a grudge kept alive as a settled disposition of the soul** *(inferred: the finding is that God judges the nations for the *state of their hearts* toward the fallen, not merely their deeds — "**you said, 'Aha!'**… **rejoiced with all the MALICE within your SOUL**" (vv.3,6): the finding is that *gloating over another's misfortune* (the "Aha!" of satisfaction at a rival's ruin) is a real evil of the interior — a malice that *takes pleasure* in downfall (the same as Obad 12, "do not gloat over the day of your brother"); and the doubling with "vengeance… with malice of soul… in **never-ending enmity**" (v.15) names the *cultivation* of a grudge — an enmity nursed so long it becomes "never-ending", a permanent posture of the soul (the settled hatred of Amos 1:11, "he kept his wrath forever"); the finding is that these are *interior* sins — one can commit no overt act and still be guilty of the malicious "Aha!" and the nursed enmity, because God weighs the soul's *pleasure at ruin* and its *cultivated hatred*)*.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *gloating over another's fall ("you said 'Aha!'… rejoiced with all the malice within your soul" — Schadenfreude, delight at a neighbour's ruin)* (thread — a gem: taking pleasure in downfall as a real evil of the interior, w/ 25:3,6, Obad 12, Prov 24:17-18, 17:5); *vengeance nursed into never-ending enmity ("took vengeance with malice of soul… never-ending enmity" — a grudge cultivated into a permanent posture of the soul)* (thread — a gem: the settled, kept hatred, w/ 25:12,15, Amos 1:11, Eph 4:31); *the interior weighed apart from overt act (God judging the soul's pleasure at ruin and cultivated hatred)* (new — w/ 25:6, Matt 5:21-22).

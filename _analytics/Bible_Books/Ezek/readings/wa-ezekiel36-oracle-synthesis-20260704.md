## EZE-new-heart-name-sake · Ezekiel 36:1–38 · [D, multi] — cleansing and the new heart/new spirit, for God's name's sake; the shame grace produces

**The reading.** God announces restoration, but first fixes its *motive*: "**It is not for your sake, O house of Israel, that I am about to act, but for the sake of my holy name, which you have profaned among the nations**" (v.22). Then the great fourfold promise: "**I will sprinkle clean water on you, and you shall be clean from all your uncleannesses… And I will give you a new heart, and a new spirit I will put within you. And I will remove the heart of stone from your flesh and give you a heart of flesh. And I will put my Spirit within you, and cause you to walk in my statutes and be careful to obey my rules**" (vv.25–27). And the fruit: "**Be ashamed and confounded for your ways, O house of Israel**" (v.32). The finding is **restoration *grounded in God's name* (not the people's worth) accomplished by *cleansing*, a *new heart and new spirit*, the *heart of stone replaced by flesh*, and *God's Spirit put within to enable obedience* — producing *shame*, not entitlement** *(stated)*. The operation is **the complete divine remedy for the interior the whole book has diagnosed: (1) *cleansing* the guilt ("sprinkle clean water… you shall be clean"); (2) *transplant* of the heart ("remove the heart of stone… give a heart of flesh" — the hard, unresponsive heart replaced by a soft, responsive one); (3) *indwelling Spirit* that *enables* the obedience the old heart could not manage ("put my Spirit within you, and CAUSE you to walk in my statutes"); all *grounded not in the people's merit* ("not for your sake… but for my holy name"); and the fitting response is *shame*, the humbled recognition of what one was** *(inferred: vv.25–27 are the summit of Ezekiel's inner-being theology, and they specify what the *deceitful, stony heart* needs and cannot give itself. First, *cleansing* — "**sprinkle clean water… clean from all your uncleannesses**" (v.25): the guilt is *washed*, dealt with objectively before the heart is changed (grace addresses the record first). Second, the *heart transplant* — "**a NEW HEART… remove the HEART OF STONE… give a HEART OF FLESH**" (v.26): the finding is that the fundamental problem is a *heart of stone* (hard, cold, unresponsive to God) and the fundamental remedy is *replacement, not reform* — God *removes* the stone and *gives* flesh (soft, tender, responsive); the self cannot soften its own stone heart, so God transplants it. Third — and Ezekiel's distinctive addition beyond Jeremiah's "law written on the heart" — the *indwelling Spirit that enables* — "**I will put my SPIRIT within you, and CAUSE you to walk in my statutes**" (v.27): the finding is that obedience is not merely *required* of the new heart but *empowered* by God's own Spirit dwelling within — the Spirit *causes* the walking, so that the new life is *God's power operating in the interior*, not the self's achievement (as Phil 2:13, "God works in you"); and the whole is *grounded outside the self* — "**NOT FOR YOUR SAKE… but for my holy name**" (v.22): the finding is that even this glorious renewal is *unmerited*, given for God's own glory, so its fruit is *shame* — "**be ashamed and confounded for your ways**" (v.32): being so freely and thoroughly renewed, for no worthiness of one's own, produces not pride but a humbled, grateful *shame* at what one was and what grace has done)*.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *cleansing before renewal ("sprinkle clean water… you shall be clean" — the guilt washed, the record addressed first)* (thread — a gem: grace dealing with the guilt before changing the heart, w/ 36:25, Ps 51:2,7, Heb 10:22, Titus 3:5); *the heart transplant, not reform ("remove the heart of stone… give a heart of flesh" — the hard unresponsive heart replaced, the self unable to soften its own stone)* (thread — a gem, the peak of the new-covenant promise: replacement, not repair, w/ 36:26, 11:19, Jer 31:33, 2 Cor 3:3); *the indwelling Spirit that enables obedience ("I will put my Spirit within you, and cause you to walk in my statutes" — obedience empowered, not merely required; God's power operating in the interior)* (new — a gem, Ezekiel's distinctive addition: the Spirit causes the walking, the new life God's work in the self, not the self's achievement, w/ 36:27, Phil 2:13, Rom 8:4, Gal 5:16); *renewal grounded in God's name, producing shame ("not for your sake but for my holy name"… "be ashamed for your ways" — unmerited grace humbling, not inflating)* (thread — a gem: the freely-renewed self ashamed, not entitled, w/ 36:22,32, 20:43, 16:63, Rom 3:19).

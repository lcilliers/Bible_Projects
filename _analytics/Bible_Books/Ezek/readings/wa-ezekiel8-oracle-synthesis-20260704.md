## EZE-lord-does-not-see · Ezekiel 8:1–9:11 · [D, multi] — the hidden idolatry that thinks God unseeing; the mark on those who sigh and groan

**The reading.** Carried in vision to the temple, Ezekiel is shown *escalating* secret abominations: idols "**engraved on the wall all around**" (8:10), and the elders worshipping them "**in the dark, each in his room of pictures**", saying, "**The LORD does not see us, the LORD has forsaken the land**" (8:12); then twenty-five men "**with their backs to the temple**" worshipping the sun (8:16). Then judgment, preceded by a rescue: a man in linen is sent to "**put a mark on the foreheads of the men who sigh and groan over all the abominations that are committed in it**" (9:4) — and the slaughter is to "**touch no one on whom is the mark**", beginning "**at my sanctuary**" (9:6). Ezekiel intercedes: "**Ah, Lord GOD! Will you destroy all the remnant?**" (9:8). The finding is **the *hidden idolatry* of hearts that think "**the LORD does not see us**" — and the *mark* set on those who "**sigh and groan**" over the abominations, distinguishing the grieving remnant** *(stated)*. The operation is **two contrasting interiors: the one that practises its idolatry *"in the dark"* on the *conviction that God does not see* ("the LORD does not see us… has forsaken the land") — a practical atheism that emboldens secret sin; and the one that *grieves* over the same abominations — and it is precisely this *grief over sin* ("sigh and groan") that God *marks* for sparing, so the interior that still *feels the wrongness* is the saved one** *(inferred: 8:12 exposes a specific inner-being corruption — idolatry practised "**in the dark, each in his room of pictures**", justified by "**the LORD does not see us**": the finding is that hidden sin rests on a *practical atheism* — the (false) conviction that God is *not watching* ("has forsaken the land"), which *emboldens* the heart to do in secret what it would not do in the open; the "room of pictures" is the *chamber of the imagination*, the private interior gallery of one's idols, indulged where one believes no eye sees (the opposite of "no hiding from the God who fills all", Jer 23:24); and the counter-interior of 9:4 is the inner-being jewel — those *marked for sparing* are "**those who SIGH AND GROAN over all the abominations**": the finding is that the mark of the saved is *grief over the surrounding evil* — not activism or perfection but the *inward mourning* that still feels sin's wrongness when everyone else is numb; the ones spared are those whose consciences are not seared, who "sigh and groan" over what others do "in the dark"; and the judgment "**beginning at my sanctuary**" (9:6) with the elders adds that accountability starts with the religious leadership; Ezekiel's intercession (9:8) shows the prophet's heart still pleading for the remnant even as judgment falls)*.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *hidden idolatry on the conviction God does not see ("in the dark… the LORD does not see us" — practical atheism emboldening secret sin)* (thread — a gem: hidden sin resting on the false belief that God is not watching, the private chamber of the imagination's idols, w/ 8:12, 9:9, Jer 23:24, Ps 10:11, 94:7); *the room of pictures (the interior gallery of one's idols indulged where one thinks no eye sees)* (new — a gem: the private imagination as the secret shrine, w/ 8:10-12, Matt 5:28); *the mark of grief over sin ("those who sigh and groan over all the abominations" — the spared are those whose consciences still mourn the surrounding evil)* (new — a gem: inward grief over sin, not activism or perfection, the mark of the saved when others are numb, w/ 9:4, 2 Pet 2:7-8, Ps 119:136); *the interceding prophet ("will you destroy all the remnant?" — the heart still pleading amid judgment)* (thread — w/ 9:8, 11:13, Amos 7:2).

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced:** hidden idolatry on the conviction God does not see; the room of pictures; the mark of grief over sin; the interceding prophet. Chapters 8–9 contrast the heart that sins "in the dark" believing "the LORD does not see" with the heart that "sighs and groans" over the abominations — and it is the *grieving* interior that God marks to spare.
- **Threads:** God unseeing / hidden sin (← Jer 23:24, Ps 94:7); the imagination's idols (w/ Matt 5:28); grief over sin (→ 2 Pet 2:7-8, Ps 119:136); intercession (← 9:8; → 11:13).

## EZE-shepherds-i-myself · Ezekiel 34:1–35:15 · [D, multi] — the self-feeding shepherds vs the true Shepherd who himself seeks the lost

**The reading.** God indicts the leaders as shepherds who serve *themselves*: "**Ah, shepherds of Israel who have been feeding yourselves!… You eat the fat, you clothe yourselves with the wool… but you do not feed the sheep. The weak you have not strengthened, the sick you have not healed, the injured you have not bound up, the strayed you have not brought back, the lost you have not sought**" (34:2–4). So the sheep were "**scattered… with none to search or seek for them**" (34:6). God's response is to take the task *himself*: "**I, I myself will search for my sheep and will seek them out… I will bring back the strayed, and I will bind up the injured, and I will strengthen the weak**" (34:11,16); "**I myself will be the shepherd of my sheep**" (34:15), setting over them "**one shepherd, my servant David**" (34:23) and "**a covenant of peace**" (34:25). (Edom's gloating enmity is judged in ch.35.) The finding is **the *self-feeding* shepherds who neglected the weak, strayed, and lost — against the *true Shepherd* who "**I, I myself**" seeks the lost, binds the injured, and strengthens the weak** *(stated)*. The operation is **the contrast of two dispositions in those given charge of others: the *self-serving* leader who *consumes* the flock for his own benefit ("feeding yourselves… eat the fat") while *neglecting the very ones who most need care* (weak, sick, injured, strayed, lost); and the *self-giving* Shepherd whose care runs *precisely toward the neediest* — "the lost… the strayed… the injured… the weak" — the emphatic "**I, I myself**" underscoring that God takes personal responsibility for those the shepherds abandoned** *(inferred: 34:2–4 defines the failure of care with a *fivefold list of the neglected* — "the weak… the sick… the injured… the strayed… the lost": the finding is that true shepherding is measured by its care for the *most vulnerable* — the weak (not the strong), the sick (not the well), the strayed and lost (not the settled); and the false shepherds' sin is precisely the *inversion* — they served themselves off the flock ("eat the fat, wear the wool") while *ignoring* those who most needed them (a self-serving that consumes rather than feeds); and God's answer is the emphatic, personal *reversal* — "**I, I MYSELF will SEARCH for my sheep and SEEK them out**" (34:11), with his care running to the *same list* the shepherds neglected: "the LOST… the STRAYED… the INJURED… the WEAK" (34:16); the finding is that the true Shepherd's heart is *toward the neediest* — he does not (like the false shepherds) tend the strong and profitable but *seeks the one that is lost*, *binds the injured*, *strengthens the weak* (the very disposition of Luke 15's shepherd leaving the ninety-nine); and "one shepherd, David" + "a covenant of peace" (34:23,25) promise that the flock's ultimate security is a *single good Shepherd* who feeds "in justice" (34:16) — the interior's deepest need met by One who seeks it personally)*.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *the self-feeding shepherd who consumes the flock ("feeding yourselves… but you do not feed the sheep" — the leader who serves himself off those in his charge)* (thread — a gem: self-serving care that consumes rather than feeds, w/ 34:2-4, Jer 23:1-2, John 10:12-13, 1 Pet 5:2-3); *true care measured by the neediest (the weak, sick, injured, strayed, lost — neglected by the false, sought by the true)* (new — a gem: shepherding measured by care for the most vulnerable, not the strong, w/ 34:4,16, Matt 25:40, Luke 15:4); *the Shepherd who himself seeks the lost ("I, I myself will search… I will bind up the injured, strengthen the weak" — God's personal care running toward the neediest the shepherds abandoned)* (thread — a gem: the true Shepherd's heart toward the lost and weak, w/ 34:11-16, Ps 23, Luke 15:4-7, John 10:11); *the one Shepherd and covenant of peace (the flock's security in a single good Shepherd)* (thread — w/ 34:23-25, 37:24-26, John 10:16).

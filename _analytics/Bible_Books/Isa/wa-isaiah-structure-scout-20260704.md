# Isaiah — structure scout & segmentation plan (2026-07-04)

> Scouting analysis before processing (a book this size — **66 chapters, ~1,292 verses**, larger than Job — must be planned, not plowed). Isaiah is not one oracle-collection but **three major parts** plus embedded sub-collections and a prose narrative. Processed as **oracle-passages** (and narrative scenes for chs 36–39), in **batches**. Genuine (non-T2) coverage is ~78% — high; inner-being density is very rich (the call vision, the servant songs, the fear-nots, the contrite spirit).

## Macro-structure (three parts)

### Part I — Isaiah 1–39 (the Assyrian era: judgment & hope)
- **1–12 · Judah/Jerusalem — indictment and the coming king.** The great indictment + "though your sins be like scarlet" (1); the mountain of the LORD + the day against pride (2–4); the vineyard song + six woes (5); **the call vision — "Holy, holy, holy… here am I, send me"** (6); the Immanuel sign + "if you are not firm in faith" (7–8); the child born, the ruler (9–11); the salvation song (12).
- **13–23 · oracles against the nations** ("burdens" — Babylon, Moab, Damascus, Egypt, Tyre, etc.): pride/self-exaltation judged ("I will ascend… I will make myself like the Most High", 14:13–14).
- **24–27 · the "Isaiah Apocalypse":** the earth judged; the feast on the mountain, **"he will swallow up death forever"** (25:8); **"perfect peace whose mind is stayed on you"** (26:3).
- **28–33 · woes on Ephraim/Jerusalem:** the cornerstone (28:16); **"this people honour me with their lips, but their heart is far from me"** (29:13); **"in returning and rest… in quietness and trust"** (30:15); the king in righteousness (32).
- **34–35 · judgment (Edom) & the highway of the redeemed** ("the ransomed shall return with singing", 35).
- **36–39 · the Hezekiah narrative (PROSE):** Sennacherib's siege, Hezekiah's prayer, his illness/recovery, the Babylonian envoys — **narrative scenes**, like Jonah.

### Part II — Isaiah 40–55 (comfort & the servant)
- **40 · "Comfort, comfort my people"; "those who wait for the LORD shall renew their strength"** (40:31).
- **41–48 · the LORD vs the idols; "fear not, I am with you" (41:10); "you are mine" (43:1); Cyrus; the blind servant.**
- **The Servant Songs:** 42:1–9 · 49:1–6 · 50:4–9 · **52:13–53:12 (the suffering servant)** — distinct units.
- **49–55 · Zion comforted; the suffering servant (53); "come, everyone who thirsts" (55:1); "my thoughts are not your thoughts" (55:8).**

### Part III — Isaiah 56–66 (restoration & the contrite)
- **56–59 · true worship, the sabbath, the fast God chooses (58); "I dwell with him who is of a contrite and lowly spirit" (57:15); sin separating from God (59).**
- **60–62 · "Arise, shine" (60); the Spirit-anointed herald — "good news to the poor… bind up the brokenhearted" (61:1–3); Zion's vindication (62).**
- **63–66 · vengeance & mercy; "rend the heavens and come down" (64); the new heavens and new earth (65–66); "this is the one to whom I will look — he who is humble and contrite in spirit" (66:2).**

## Segmentation approach
- **Oracle-passages** throughout (as for Malachi/Hosea/Micah), with **cross-chapter `verse_refs`** where oracles span chapter lines (frequent in Isaiah).
- **Servant Songs** (42:1–9, 49:1–6, 50:4–9, 52:13–53:12) as **their own units** — the richest inner-being material (the servant's obedient, suffering interior).
- **Hezekiah narrative (36–39)** as **narrative scenes** (genre shift, like Jonah).
- Skip pure superscriptions; fold pure-T2 nations-oracle verses into their oracle rather than forcing per-verse units.

## Estimated scale & batch plan
~**90–110 oracle/scene units** across 66 chapters (roughly 1.5 units/chapter). This is a **multi-batch effort** (comparable to ~3 Jobs). Proposed batches (each: segment → Phase-2 readings → file → commit):

| Batch | Chapters | ~units | Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1–12 | ~16 | indictment, the call vision, Immanuel, the king |
| 2 | 13–23 | ~12 | oracles against the nations (pride judged) |
| 3 | 24–27 | ~6 | the apocalypse; perfect peace; death swallowed |
| 4 | 28–35 | ~14 | the woes; lips-far-heart; quietness & trust; the highway |
| 5 | 36–39 | ~6 | the Hezekiah narrative (scenes) |
| 6 | 40–48 | ~14 | comfort; wait-renew-strength; fear-not; idols; Cyrus |
| 7 | 49–55 | ~12 | Zion; the suffering servant (53); come-thirsty |
| 8 | 56–66 | ~16 | the fast; the contrite spirit; good news to the poor; new heavens |

Phase-1 lexical will be run for all 66 chapters up front (after backfill); segmentation + Phase-2 proceed batch by batch.

## Recommendation
Proceed in the 8 batches above, **starting with Isaiah 1–12** (the indictment through the coming king, including the great call vision of ch.6). Given the scale, this will span several working passes; each batch is committed on completion so progress is durable. Backfill (66 ch) is running; Phase-1 next.

### Inner-being richness (why Isaiah repays the effort)
Isaiah holds some of the corpus's deepest inner-being texts: the **contrite/humble spirit God dwells with** (57:15, 66:2), the **suffering servant's interior** (50:4–9, 53), **faith vs fear** (7:9, "fear not… you are mine" 43:1), **the heart far while lips are near** (29:13), **quietness and trust as strength** (30:15), **waiting on the LORD renewing strength** (40:31), and **the cleansed unclean lips of the called prophet** (6:5–8). It is the richest single field remaining.

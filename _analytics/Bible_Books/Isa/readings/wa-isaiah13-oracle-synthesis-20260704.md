## ISA-babylon-day · Isa 13:2–22 · [D, multi] — the melting heart of terror; the pomp of the arrogant laid low

**The reading.** The first "burden" opens the nations-oracles with the day of the LORD, and its effect on the interior is total dissolution: "**all hands will be feeble, and every human heart will melt. They will be dismayed… they will be in anguish like a woman in labour… their faces will be aflame**" (vv.7–8). The purpose is stated plainly: "**I will put an end to the pomp of the arrogant, and lay low the pompous pride of the ruthless**" (v.11). The finding is **the day of the LORD as the point where human *pride* is levelled and the human *heart melts* — arrogance collapsed and courage dissolved before God's judgment** *(stated)*. The operation is **terror figured as the *melting* of the heart (the interior losing all firmness), and the whole judgment aimed specifically at the *pomp and pride* of the ruthless — the day exists to bring down what is lifted up** *(inferred: the inner-being note is the pairing of two things — the "**heart melts**" (v.7, the loss of all inner solidity under dread) and the object of the judgment, "**the pomp of the arrogant… the pride of the ruthless**" (v.11); the finding is that the day of the LORD targets *pride* above all (the same theme as 2:11–17), and that pride's end is a *melting* — the arrogant heart, which had made itself high, is reduced to the trembling of a woman in labour; the melting heart is the involuntary confession of a creature that had forgotten it was a creature)*.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *the melting heart of terror (the interior losing all firmness under the dread of God's day)* (thread — the dissolved heart, w/ 19:1, Josh 2:11, Ps 22:14); *pride/pomp as the target of the day of the LORD (judgment aimed at what is lifted up)* (thread — a gem: the day exists to level arrogance, w/ 2:11-17, 13:11).

## ISA-good-news-reigns · Isa 52:1–12 · [D, multi] — awake and arise; the beautiful feet that publish peace; God before and behind

**The reading.** A double summons to rouse from dejection: "**Awake, awake, put on your strength, O Zion… shake yourself from the dust and arise… loose the bonds from your neck, O captive daughter**" (vv.1–2). Then the famous herald: "**How beautiful upon the mountains are the feet of him who brings good news, who publishes peace… who says to Zion, 'Your God reigns'**" (v.7). And the reassurance for the journey out: "**you shall not go out in haste… for the LORD will go before you, and the God of Israel will be your rear guard**" (v.12). The finding is **the dejected interior summoned to *rouse itself* ("awake… shake yourself from the dust… loose the bonds") on the strength of *good news* — "your God reigns" — and sent out *surrounded* by God (before and behind)** *(stated)*. The operation is **the passive, dust-sitting self called to an *active rising* — but the rising is enabled by a message received ("your God reigns"), and the going-out is *guarded on both sides* ("the LORD will go before you… your rear guard"), so that the summons to self-rousing is underwritten by God's kingship and encirclement** *(inferred: the "**awake, awake… shake yourself from the dust… loose the bonds**" (vv.1–2) is a summons to the dejected interior to *act* — to stop sitting in defeat and rise; the finding is that there is a place for the self to *rouse itself*, to refuse to remain "in the dust"; but the rousing is not self-generated optimism — it rests on the *content of the good news*, "**your God reigns**" (v.7): the finding is that the ground of the interior's rising is the announced *kingship of God* — the herald's beautiful feet bring not a pep-talk but a *fact* (God reigns, peace is published, salvation comes), and it is this fact that makes the dust-sitting soul able to stand; and the going-out is *bracketed by God* — "**the LORD will go before you, and the God of Israel will be your rear guard**" (v.12): the finding is that the roused self is not sent out alone but *enveloped* — God ahead (leading) and God behind (protecting the vulnerable rear), so that the rising and the journey are safe on both flanks; the interior's active self-rousing and God's active enclosing meet)*.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *the summons to rouse from the dust ("awake… shake yourself from the dust… loose the bonds" — the dejected self called to active rising)* (thread — a gem: the place for the self to refuse to remain in defeat, w/ 52:1-2, 51:17, 60:1, Eph 5:14); *rising grounded in good news, not self-generated optimism ("your God reigns" — the announced fact that makes the soul able to stand)* (new — a gem: the ground of rising is God's kingship, not a pep-talk, w/ 52:7, Rom 10:15); *sent out enveloped by God ("the LORD will go before you… your rear guard" — God ahead leading, behind protecting)* (new — a gem: the roused self encircled, safe on both flanks, w/ 52:12, 58:8, Exod 14:19-20).

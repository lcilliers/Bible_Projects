## The reading

**I. Dwelling in God as home — and naming him so (vv.1–2, 9).**
"He who **dwells** (H3427, *yashab* — resides) in the **shelter** of the Most High will **abide** (*lun* — lodge) in the **shadow** of the Almighty. **I will say** to the LORD, 'My **refuge** (H4268, M19) and my fortress, my God, in whom I **trust** (H0982, M19)'… Because you have made the LORD your **dwelling place**" (vv.1–2, 9). The gem-finding: the inner being's security is **residing *in* God as a permanent home** — not a momentary shelter but a *dwelling*, an *abiding* *(stated)*. The operation: the self **takes up residence in God** — *dwells, abides, makes God its dwelling place* — a settled home, not a passing cover *(inferred: yashab/lun/dwelling-place all name *residence*, not brief refuge; cf. Ps 27:4's "dwell", 90:1's "our dwelling place")*. And it **declares it** — "I *will say* to the LORD, 'My refuge'" — the trust is a **speech-act**, the self naming God as its home *(inferred: the confessing "I will say… my refuge" makes the trust an act of naming)*.

**II. The fearlessness that flows from the dwelling (vv.5–6).**
"You will **not fear** (H3372, M01) the **terror** (H6343, M01) of the night, nor the arrow that flies by day" (vv.5–6). The finding: the abiding inner being is **freed from fear** — the night-terror, the daytime arrow *(stated)*. The operation: **dwelling in God dissolves the fears** — the self at home in God is not afraid of what would otherwise terrify *(inferred: the fearlessness is predicated on the dwelling of vv.1–2, 9; cf. Ps 23:4, 46:2)*.

**III. The mutual holding — devoted clinging, knowing, calling (vv.14–16).**
"Because he **holds fast to me in love** (H2836, *chashaq* — cleaves, is devoted), I will deliver him; I will protect him, because he **knows** my name. When he **calls** (H7121, M37) to me, **I will answer** him; I will be with him in trouble" (vv.14–16). The gem-finding: God's protective answer is tied to **three inner-being acts** — the self **clinging to God in devoted love** (*chashaq*), **knowing** God's name, and **calling** on him *(stated)*. The operation: the inner being's **devoted attachment** (*chashaq* — a binding love/desire, cleaving to God), its **intimate knowledge** of the name, and its **calling** *draw God's answering deliverance* — the self holds fast to God, and God holds/answers the self *(inferred: "because he holds fast to me in love… knows my name… calls to me, I will answer" makes the mutual holding — the self's cleaving met by God's response — the ground of the protection)*. The abiding of vv.1–2 is now shown to be **reciprocal**: the self *dwells in* God because it *cleaves to* God, and God *answers* *(inferred)*.

## What Psalm 91 says about the inner being
- **Security is *residing in God as home* (vv.1–2, 9).** The inner being dwells, abides, makes God its dwelling place — a settled residence, not a momentary shelter — and it *names* God so: trust is a speech-act, the self confessing God as its refuge.
- **Dwelling in God dissolves fear (vv.5–6).** The abiding self is freed from the terror of the night and the arrow by day — at home in God, it is not afraid.
- **God's answer is drawn by the self's *devoted clinging* (vv.14–16).** The inner being's *chashaq* (cleaving, devoted love), its *knowing* the name, and its *calling* meet God's answering deliverance. The holding is mutual — the self cleaves to God, God holds and answers the self.

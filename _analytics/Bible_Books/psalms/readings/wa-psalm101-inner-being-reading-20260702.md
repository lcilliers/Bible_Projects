# Psalm 101 — what it tells us about the inner being (Phase-2 chapter reading)

- **Lens:** the finding is the inner-being **operation**, God-relation as arena (`feedback_lens_is_inner_being_process_not_god_relation`).
- **The inner being's active self-governance for integrity.** Psalm 101 is a set of *resolutions*. Its findings are operations of **self-governance**: integrity of heart **in the private sphere** (v.2); **governing what enters through the eyes** (v.3); **distancing the perverse heart** and refusing intimacy with evil (v.4); and **curating one's company** (vv.5–7).

## The reading

**I. Integrity of heart *within the house* — the private self (v.2).**
"I will **ponder** (H7919, M15, *sakal*) the way that is **blameless** (H8549, M12)… I will **walk with integrity** (H8537, M13, *tom*) **of heart** (H3824, the seat — M47) **within my house**" (v.2). The finding: the inner being resolves on **integrity of heart in the *private* sphere** — "within my house," where no one watches *(stated)*. The operation: integrity is maintained **unwatched** — the self is as integrous in private as in public *(inferred: "integrity of heart *within my house*" locates the integrity in the unobserved, domestic sphere — the private self held to the same standard; the *tom*-heart of Ps 26:1, here specifically *at home*)*.

**II. Governing the eye-gate (v.3).**
"I will **not set before my eyes** anything that is **worthless** (*beliyaal*). I **hate** (H8130, M06) the work of those who fall away; **it shall not cling to me**" (v.3). The finding: the inner being **governs its own *gaze*** — refusing to *set* anything worthless before its eyes *(stated)*. The operation: the self **controls its *intake through sight*** — guarding the eye-gate against the worthless, and refusing to let corruption *cling* *(inferred: "I will not set before my eyes… it shall not cling to me" makes the self the gatekeeper of what it looks at and what attaches to it; cf. Ps 119:37 "turn my eyes from worthless things")*.

**III. Distancing the perverse heart; refusing to *know* evil (v.4).**
"A **perverse heart** (H6141, *iqqesh* — twisted + H3824, M47) shall be **far from me**; I will **know nothing of evil**" (v.4). The finding: the inner being actively **puts the crooked disposition at a distance** and **refuses *intimacy* with evil** *(stated)*. The operation: two moves — **distancing the perverse heart** (holding the twisted disposition away) and **declining to *know* evil** (refusing the *intimate acquaintance* that "know" implies) *(inferred: "a perverse heart shall be far… I will know nothing of evil" makes the self actively separate from crookedness and refuse familiarity with evil — the opposite of the wicked who *cherish* iniquity, Ps 66:18)*.

**IV. Curating the company (vv.5–7).**
"Whoever **slanders** his neighbour secretly I will destroy… the **haughty look** and **arrogant heart** I will not endure. I will look with **favour on the faithful** (H0539, M13, *aman*)… he who walks **blamelessly** shall minister to me. No one who practices **deceit** (H7423, M14) shall **dwell in my house**" (vv.5–7). The finding: the inner being's integrity is **worked out in whom it keeps near** — the faithful/blameless *welcomed*, the slanderer/haughty/deceitful *excluded from the house* *(stated)*. The operation: **curating one's company** as the externalising of the heart's integrity — the self chooses its associates by their inner condition (faithful vs deceitful) *(inferred: "favour on the faithful… no deceit shall dwell in my house" makes the chosen company the outward form of the inner integrity; cf. Ps 26:4–5, 1:1's refused seat)*.

## What Psalm 101 says about the inner being
- **Integrity is held in the *private* sphere (v.2).** "Integrity of heart within my house" — the unwatched self is as integrous as the public; integrity is not for show.
- **The inner being governs its own *gaze* (v.3).** It refuses to set the worthless before its eyes and refuses to let corruption cling — the self as gatekeeper of what it looks at.
- **It *distances* the perverse heart and refuses to *know* evil (v.4).** Two active moves: holding the crooked disposition away, and declining intimacy with evil — the opposite of cherishing iniquity.
- **It *curates its company* as the outward form of its integrity (vv.5–7).** The faithful welcomed, the deceitful excluded from the house — the heart's integrity externalised in whom it keeps near.

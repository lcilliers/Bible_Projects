- **The trusting self *immovable* like a mountain; the encircled, secure self.** Psalm 125 is a pilgrim song of security. Its inner-being findings: **trust that makes the self *unshakable*** — like Mount Zion (v.1); the **self *surrounded* by God** (v.2); and the **upright heart** as the object of God's good (v.4).

## The reading

**I. Trust that makes the self *immovable* (v.1).**
"Those who **trust** (H0982, M19) in the LORD are like **Mount Zion, which cannot be moved**, but **abides forever**" (v.1). The gem-finding: the trusting self is **as *unshakable* as a mountain** — it "cannot be moved," it "abides" *(stated)*. The operation: **trust confers *rock-like stability* on the inner being** — the self that leans on the LORD takes on the immovability of Zion itself; not native steadiness but a *derived* permanence, the truster made as fixed as a mountain *(inferred: "those who trust… are like Mount Zion, which cannot be moved" makes trust the source of a *geological* stability of soul — the inner being anchored so that it neither topples nor shifts; the same law as 112:7's firm heart and 46:2's "we will not fear though the mountains be moved", here the *truster* becomes the unmoved mountain; cf. Ps 15:5, 16:8)*.

**II. The self *surrounded* by God (v.2).**
"As the **mountains surround Jerusalem**, so the **LORD surrounds his people**, from this time forth and forevermore" (v.2). The finding: the self is **encircled** by God as Jerusalem is ringed by mountains *(stated)*. The operation: the inner being rests in being ***enclosed / hemmed in* by God** — protected on every side, the divine presence a ring of security around the self *(inferred: "as the mountains surround… so the LORD surrounds his people" gives the felt security of being *encircled* — the self safe within a divine perimeter; the inner-being fruit is settledness, cf. Ps 34:7, 121:5)*.

**III. The upright heart as God's object of good (v.4).**
"Do good, O LORD, to those who are good, and to those who are **upright in their hearts** (H3820, M47)!" (v.4). The finding: the prayer distinguishes the ***upright in heart*** as those for whom good is sought *(stated)*. The operation: the inner being's ***inward* uprightness** is what marks the self God favours — not outward conformity but the heart's straightness; the petition locates true goodness *in the heart* *(inferred: "the upright in their hearts" makes the heart's condition the criterion — the inner being's straightness, not mere behaviour, is what is prayed-for and favoured; cf. Ps 7:10, 11:2, 24:4 "pure in heart")*.

## What Psalm 125 says about the inner being
- **Trust makes the self *immovable* (v.1).** The truster takes on the rock-like stability of Mount Zion — "cannot be moved," "abides forever"; not native steadiness but a permanence *derived* from leaning on the LORD.
- **The self rests in being *surrounded* by God (v.2).** Encircled as Jerusalem by mountains — safe within a divine perimeter; the inner-being fruit is settled security.
- **Uprightness is *of the heart* (v.4).** The self God favours is marked by inward straightness, not mere outward conformity — true goodness located *in the heart*.

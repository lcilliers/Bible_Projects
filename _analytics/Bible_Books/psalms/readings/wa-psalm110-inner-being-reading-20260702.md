## The reading

**I. The willing self-offering of the people (v.3).**
"Your people will **offer themselves freely** (H5071 — *nedabot*, freewill offerings) on the day of your power, in holy garments" (v.3). The finding: on the day of the King's power, the people **give themselves *freely* / *willingly*** *(stated)*. The operation: the inner being's **glad, un-coerced *willingness*** — the self offered *freely*, not conscripted; a volunteered devotion *(inferred: "offer themselves freely" (nedabot = freewill) names a willing inner disposition — the people give *themselves*, and give *gladly*; the offering is of the will, cf. the "willing heart" of Ex 35:5, Ps 54:6 "with a freewill offering I will sacrifice")*.

## What Psalm 110 says about the inner being
- **The self can be *offered freely / willingly* (v.3).** A glad, un-coerced devotion — the people give *themselves*, and give *gladly*; the offering is of the will, not conscription.

## The reading

**I. The faint heart and the rock *higher than I* (v.2).**
"From the end of the earth I call to you when my **heart** (H3820, the seat — M47) is **faint** (H5848, *ataph* — overwhelmed, enveloped). **Lead me to the rock that is higher than I**" (v.2). The gem-finding: the **overwhelmed** inner being seeks a ground **above its own reach** — a rock *higher than itself* *(stated)*. The operation: the faint self recognises it needs a refuge that **exceeds its own capacity** — not a foothold it can climb to but one it must be **led** to, higher than it can attain alone *(inferred: "the rock that is higher than I… lead me" makes the refuge beyond the self's own reach, requiring guidance to it)*. The finding is precise: the inner being at the *end of its own resources* ("the end of the earth", "my heart is faint") reaches **upward and beyond itself**, not inward *(inferred)*.

**II. The longing to dwell, sheltered under the wings (vv.3–4).**
"For you have been my **refuge** (H4268, M19, *machseh*), a strong tower… Let me **dwell** in your **tent forever**! Let me **take refuge** (H2620, M19, *chasah*) under the shelter of your **wings**" (vv.3–4). The finding: the inner being's refuge-longing is for a **permanent dwelling** — not a passing shelter but "your tent *forever*", under the *wings* *(stated)*. The operation: the faint self, having reached for the higher rock, wants to **stay** there — its desire is not merely rescue but *abiding* in the refuge *(inferred: "dwell… forever" makes the longing a permanent home, not a momentary cover; cf. Ps 23:6, 27:4)*.

## What Psalm 61 says about the inner being
- **The overwhelmed self reaches for a ground *higher than itself* (v.2).** The faint heart seeks a refuge that exceeds its own reach — a rock "higher than I," which it must be *led* to, not climb to. At the end of its own resources, the inner being reaches upward and beyond itself, not inward.
- **Its refuge-longing is to *dwell*, not merely to shelter (vv.3–4).** The inner being wants to *abide* in the refuge — "your tent forever," under the wings — a permanent home, not a passing cover.

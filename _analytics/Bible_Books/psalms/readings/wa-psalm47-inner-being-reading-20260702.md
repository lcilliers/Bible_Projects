## The reading

**I. Joy overflows into the body and voice (vv.1, 6).**
"**Clap your hands**, all peoples! **Shout** to God with loud songs of **joy**!… **Sing praises** (H2167, M42) to God, **sing praises**!" — the imperative repeated five times (v.6) (vv.1, 6). The operation: the inner being's gladness **does not stay interior — it moves out through the body (clapping) and the voice (shouting, singing)** *(stated)*. The finding: joy here is **enacted, not merely felt** — the whole person, hands and voice, is the medium of the inner being's praise *(inferred: the fivefold "sing" and the clapping/shouting make the joy bodily-vocal, an overflow into action)*.

**II. A God-conferred exaltation, grounded in being loved (v.4).**
"He **chose** (H0977, M37) our **heritage** for us, the **pride** (H1347, M08, *gaon*) of Jacob whom he **loves** (H0157, *aheb*)" (v.4). The finding: the "**pride**" of Jacob — *gaon*, the exaltation/glory — is here **not the sinful self-exaltation of the M08 cluster but a *conferred* dignity**: God *chose* it, and it rests on being *loved* *(stated: God chose the heritage, the pride of Jacob whom he loves)*. The operation: the inner being's legitimate exaltation is **received, not self-generated** — dignity grounded in election and love, the opposite of the self-inflating pride the same cluster elsewhere warns against *(inferred: gaon here is God-chosen and love-grounded, so the exaltation is a gift, not a claim)*.

## What Psalm 47 says about the inner being
- **Joy is enacted through body and voice (vv.1, 6).** The inner being's gladness overflows into clapping, shouting, and repeated singing — praise as bodily-vocal action, not merely interior feeling.
- **Legitimate exaltation is *conferred* and *love-grounded* (v.4).** The "pride of Jacob" is a dignity *chosen* by God and resting on being loved — received, not self-generated; the opposite of self-inflating pride.

## The reading

**I. The ruler's inner regard for the vulnerable (vv.12–14).**
"He delivers the **needy** (H0034, *evyon*) when he calls, the **poor** (H6041, *ani*) and him who has **no helper**. He has **pity** (H2347, M05, *chus* — spares, feels compassion) on the weak and the needy… From oppression and violence he **redeems their life**, and **precious is their blood in his sight**" (vv.12–14). The finding: the righteous ruler's *justice* is not merely external policy but an **inner disposition** — **pity** (chus) toward the weak, and a **valuing of the vulnerable's life as *precious*** *(stated)*. Right rule flows from a **heart that pities and prizes the powerless** *(inferred)*.

## What Psalm 72 says about the inner being

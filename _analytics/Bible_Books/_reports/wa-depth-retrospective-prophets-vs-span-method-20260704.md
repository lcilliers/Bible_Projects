# Depth retrospective — did the prophet/wisdom readings under-dig?

- **Date:** 2026-07-04
- **Question (researcher):** could the depth of evaluation (or lack of it) have affected the previous Prophets work?
- **Answer: yes, materially.** Evidence below. This is a *depth* finding, not a "throw it away" finding — see §4 for the proportionate response.

---

## 1. How I checked (evidence, not opinion)

For a sample of already-filed chapters I compared **what the chapter actually contains** (its non-T2 evidence spans, from `wa_verse_records`) against **what the filed reading actually engaged** (verses it cites; whether it distinguished repeated operations). A low touch-rate + undistinguished repeats = the reading worked at *dominant-movement* depth, not span-by-span depth.

| Chapter | evidence verses | distinct glosses | **repeated** glosses | reading words | verses cited | touch-rate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Jer 17 | 21 | 41 | 8 | 1090 | 6 | **28%** |
| Isa 1 | 27 | 61 | 14 | 1065 | 4 | **14%** |
| Isa 6 | 10 | 20 | 5 | 801 | 2 | **20%** |
| Eze 18 | 27 | 34 | 19 | 1014 | 5 | **18%** |
| Psa 19 | 12 | 25 | 2 | 842 | 1 | **8%** |
| Psa 119 | 155 | 102 | 42 | 2191 | 2 | **1%** |

The readings are substantial (800–2200 words) and genuinely good — but each engages only **8–28%** of its chapter's evidence verses directly, and **none distinguished the repeated operations** (Isa 1 had 14 repeated glosses; Eze 18 had 19; Psa 119 had 42).

## 2. What that means concretely (Jeremiah 17 worked)

The filed Jer 17 reading brilliantly handles the chapter's **principal movement** — the deceitful heart (17:9), trust-flesh-shrub vs trust-LORD-tree (17:5–8), the heart God searches (17:10). But against the full span inventory it:

- **Left whole operation-webs untouched** — the entire **Sabbath/consecration section (17:19–27)** ("to consecrate" ×3, "to hear: obey") got no treatment; the reading followed the heart/trust movement and stopped.
- **Did not distinguish repeated operations** — "heart" occurs **4×** (the heart *trusting flesh* 17:5; the heart *turned from* the LORD 17:5; the *deceitful* heart 17:9; the heart God *searches* 17:10) — four different heart-operations, not surfaced as distinct.
- **Missed an interactive gem** — "**incurable**" appears at 17:9 *and* 17:16, and "**heal me** and I shall be healed" at 17:14: the interplay of the *incurable heart* and the plea to *be healed* is exactly the kind of within-passage operation-web the narrative method now catches, and it was passed over.

## 3. Honest verdict

- The prophet/wisdom readings were done at **"dominant-movement" depth**: they find and read each chapter's *principal* inner-being movement(s) with real insight. They are **not wrong and not wasted** — they are a genuine and valuable layer.
- But they are **not** the exhaustive, span-by-span, distinction-preserving, interactive-web depth that (a) the researcher has now twice insisted on, and (b) the narrative method + GEN-01 model. Measured against that bar, they **under-dug**: most spans were never individually interrogated, repeated operations were lumped or skipped, and secondary operation-webs in each chapter were under- or un-surfaced.
- **Genre softens but does not excuse it.** Oracle/poetry is more thematically unified than narrative, so a movement-reading is *more* defensible there than it would be in narrative — but the "analyse as if the first / dig for the hidden gems / don't lump" discipline applies across genres, and the sample shows real, repeatable misses.
- **Coverage ≠ depth.** The earlier "0 non-T2 gaps" checks confirmed every evidence verse fell *inside a passage*; they did **not** confirm each span was *interrogated*. I should not have let coverage-complete stand in for depth-complete.

## 4. Proportionate response (re-align, don't rebuild)

This is a **depth-debt**, addressable **additively** — the existing readings become the *movement layer*; a **span-by-span depth-pass** augments each chapter with the missed operations, the distinguished repeats, and the secondary webs. No rebuild; nothing already written is discarded (`feedback_no_rework_paid_twice`).

Recommended sequencing (for researcher decision):
1. **Do Genesis at the right depth now** (in progress) so the debt stops growing.
2. **Quantify the gap** across a larger prophet sample (this is 6 chapters) to size the pass.
3. **Prioritise the depth-pass** by richness — the chapters with many distinct + repeated glosses and low touch-rate first (Isaiah, Jeremiah, Ezekiel narrative-dense chapters); the highly-repetitive acrostics (Psa 119) lowest, where repetition is partly the point.
4. Keep the **cross-book syntheses** (minor/major prophets) — they are honest summaries of the movement layer; they would only *gain* from the depth-pass, not be invalidated by it.

**Bottom line:** the previous work is a strong first layer read at movement-depth; the span-level depth the researcher wants was largely not applied there, and a targeted, additive depth-pass — not a rebuild — is the fix.

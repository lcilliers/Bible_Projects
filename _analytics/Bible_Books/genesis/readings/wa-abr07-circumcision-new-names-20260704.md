## ABR-07-circumcision-new-names · Genesis 17:1–27 · [the re-named interior] — the command to wholeness, the covenant borne in the flesh, the new names, and the inward laugh

**The passage.** "I am God Almighty; walk before me, and be **blameless** (tamim)" (17:1); "I will **multiply** (ravah) you greatly… my **covenant** (berit)" (17:2); "no longer shall your name be **called** (qara) Abram, but **Abraham**" (17:5); "I will **give** (natan)… the land" (17:8); "you shall **keep** (shamar) my covenant… circumcised in the **flesh** (basar)" (17:9–14); "that **person** (nephesh) shall be cut off" (17:14); "you shall not **call** (qara) her Sarai, but **Sarah**… I will **bless** (barak) her and **give** (natan) you a son" (17:15–16); "Abraham fell on his face and laughed and said in his **heart** (lev)" (17:17); "Oh that Ishmael might **live** (chayah)!" (17:18); "you shall **call** (qara) his name **Isaac**" (17:19); "as for Ishmael, I have **heard** (shama) you; I will **bless** (barak) and **multiply** (ravah) him" (17:20).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **tamim (blameless) — the command to *wholeness of walk* (17:1).** The finding is "walk before me, and be **blameless** (tamim)" *(stated)*. The gem is that tamim — the *given* quality of Noah (6:9, "righteous, **blameless**", GEN-10) — is here a **command** to Abraham: *integrity/wholeness of walk* demanded before God; distinct from Noah's descriptive tamim — this is the interior *called* to be whole *(inferred)*.
- **berit (covenant) — the covenant *hammered*, of circumcision (17:2, 17:4, 17:7, 17:9, 17:10, 17:11, 17:13, 17:14, 17:19, 17:21).** The finding is God establishing "my **covenant** (berit)" — an *everlasting* covenant (17:7), "between me and you" (17:4), restated ~11× *(stated)*. The operation is God's *self-binding pledge* driven home (as GEN-12's rainbow) — one covenant, its certainty emphasised by repetition; here bearing a new *sign in the flesh* *(inferred: one covenant hammered, not eleven; the repetition = certainty)*.
- **ravah (multiply) #1 — the multiply-promise to Abraham (17:2).** The finding is "I will **multiply** (ravah) you greatly" *(stated)*. The operation is the promise (← 15:5) — the offspring multiplied *(inferred)*.
- **qara (call) — God *renames* Abram → Abraham (17:5).** The finding is "no longer… Abram, but **Abraham**, for I have made you the father of a multitude" *(stated)*. The gem is *God renaming the man*: the name *changed* to embody the destiny (father of a multitude) — the interior given a **new identity by God** (against Babel's self-made name 11:4; beyond the name *made great* at 12:2 — now the name itself *transformed*) *(inferred)*.
- **natan (give) — the land-gift (17:8).** The finding is "I will **give** (natan)… the land of your sojournings" *(stated)*. The operation is the promissory land-gift (← 12:7, 15:18) *(inferred)*.
- **shamar (keep) — covenant *obedience* (17:9,10).** The finding is "you shall **keep** (shamar) my covenant… every male shall be circumcised" *(stated)*. The gem is *shamar* — the garden-*keeping* word (2:15) and the cherubim's *guarding* (3:24) — now **covenant-keeping/obedience**: the interior's task is to *keep* (obey) the covenant; distinct from guarding a place *(inferred)*.
- **basar (flesh) — the covenant *borne in the flesh*, then *enacted* (17:11, 17:13, 17:14; obeyed 17:23, 17:24, 17:25).** The finding is "circumcised in the **flesh** (basar) of your foreskin… my covenant in your flesh" (17:11, 17:13, 17:14), and that very day Abraham circumcised the flesh of his household and himself (17:23, 17:24, 17:25) *(stated)*. The gem is basar — the material/union/corrupt/frail flesh of the primeval block — now the **flesh cut as the covenant-sign**: the covenant *marked bodily*, borne *in* the flesh; a new basar-sense (the covenant-bearer). And the sign is *enacted the same day* (17:23–25) — the shamar (keeping/obedience) *done at once*, not deferred *(inferred: the command-basar (17:11-14) and the obeyed-basar (17:23-25) are one covenant-sign, commanded then kept)*.
- **nephesh (person) — the uncircumcised *cut off* (17:14).** The finding is "that **person** (nephesh) shall be cut off from his people" *(stated)*. The operation is nephesh as *the person* (← 12:5) — the covenant's *seriousness*: the unsigned person severed *(inferred)*.
- **qara (call) — God *renames* Sarai → Sarah (17:15).** The finding is "you shall not **call** (qara) her Sarai, but **Sarah** (princess)" *(stated)*. The operation is the *second renaming* — the barren wife given a new name/destiny too; both partners re-named for the promise *(inferred)*.
- **barak (bless) + natan (give) — the *barren* wife blessed to bear (17:16).** The finding is "I will **bless** (barak) her and **give** (natan) you a son by her… she shall become nations" *(stated)*. The gem is *Sarah blessed*: the womb God *restrained* (atsar, 16:2, ABR-06) is now **blessed** (barak) to bear — the closed womb reversed by blessing *(inferred: barak = the blessing on Sarah; natan = the son given)*.
- **lev (heart) — the *inward laugh* at the impossible promise (17:17).** The finding is "Abraham fell on his face and laughed and said **in his heart** (lev): shall a child be born to a man 100 years old?" *(stated)*. The gem is the *interior* response — the laugh *in the heart* (lev): **incredulous wonder** at the impossible promise (the seed of Isaac's name, "he laughs"); the believing interior (ABR-05) now *laughs inwardly* at the promise's sheer impossibility — awe and disbelief mingled, read as the heart's astonished laugh *(inferred: lev = the seat of the inward laugh; the text leaves the laugh's exact valence — I read the heart's astonishment, not import a verdict)*.
- **chayah (live) — the father's heart for *Ishmael* (17:18).** The finding is "Oh that Ishmael might **live** (chayah) before you!" *(stated)*. The gem is the *father's plea for the son already born*: given the promise of Isaac, Abraham's heart turns to *Ishmael* — "that he might live/be blessed"; the interior's love for the existing son *(inferred)*.
- **qara (call) — God names *Isaac* ("he laughs") (17:19).** The finding is "you shall **call** (qara) his name **Isaac** (he laughs)" *(stated)*. The operation is the naming that *records Abraham's laugh* (17:17) — the covenant-son's name *carries* the interior's astonishment *(inferred)*.
- **shama (heard) + barak + ravah #2 — God *hears* the father, blesses the outcast-son (17:20).** The finding is "as for Ishmael, I have **heard** (shama) you; I will **bless** (barak) him and **multiply** (ravah) him… twelve princes" *(stated)*. The gem is *God hears Abraham's plea* (shama, answering 17:18) — as he *heard Hagar* for Ishmael (16:11): Ishmael *twice heard-for*; and Ishmael is **blessed and multiplied** (barak/ravah) — but *not* the covenant-line (17:21, the covenant with Isaac). The distinction: Ishmael *blessed* (temporal abundance) vs Isaac the *covenant-bearer* *(inferred)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **wholeness commanded, covenant borne in flesh (tamim/berit/shamar/basar):** the interior is *called to wholeness* (tamim), *bound* in an everlasting covenant (berit), tasked to *keep/obey* it (shamar), and *marked* in the flesh (basar) — the covenant is *inward calling and outward sign* together.
2. **the re-named interior (qara 17:5,15,19):** God *renames* Abram, Sarai, and names Isaac — new identities *given* to embody the promise; the self that grasped a name at Babel now *receives* transformed names from God.
3. **the barren blessed, the promise laughed at (barak 17:16 / lev 17:17):** the *restrained* womb (atsar 16:2) is *blessed* to bear (barak), and Abraham *laughs in his heart* (lev) at the impossibility — belief (ABR-05) and astonished incredulity coexisting; the laugh becomes the son's *name* (Isaac).
4. **the father's plea heard (chayah 17:18 → shama 17:20):** Abraham pleads that *Ishmael* live; God *hears* (shama, as he heard Hagar, 16:11) and *blesses* Ishmael — the father's heart honoured — while the *covenant* is set on Isaac; love for the son-already-born and the covenant-line held distinct.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** tamim as *commanded wholeness* (← Noah's given tamim 6:9); berit *hammered* (the circumcision-covenant); ravah to Abraham (17:2) and to Ishmael (17:20); **qara — God *renaming* Abram→Abraham, Sarai→Sarah, naming Isaac** (new identities); natan (land / the son); **shamar — covenant *obedience*** (← garden-keeping 2:15); **basar — the covenant *borne in the flesh*** (new sign-sense); nephesh (the person cut off); **barak — the *barren* Sarah blessed to bear** (atsar 16:2 reversed); **lev — the *inward laugh*** at the impossible (→ Isaac's name); chayah — the father's *plea for Ishmael*; **shama — God *hears* Abraham for Ishmael** (← 16:11).
- **Whose interior:** the **human** interior *re-named, called to wholeness, obeying (circumcising that day), laughing inwardly at the impossible, pleading for his other son*; and the **divine** interior *binding covenant, blessing the barren, renaming, and hearing the father*.
- **Threads:** tamim (← 6:9 Noah); qara-renaming (← the name grasped at Babel / made great 12:2, now transformed); shamar (← 2:15, now covenant-obedience); basar (← the primeval flesh-senses, now covenant-sign); barak on Sarah (atsar 16:2 reversed); lev/laugh (→ Isaac "he laughs", ABR-08 Sarah laughs, ABR-12 the laugh of joy); shama for Ishmael (← 16:11, → 21:17).
- **Guarded against:** inventing eleven covenants from the hammered berit (repetition = emphasis); manufacturing per-verse distinctions among the basar-sign occurrences (17:11-14,23-25 = one enacted obedience); reading a settled verdict into Abraham's *lev*-laugh (the text leaves its valence — I read the heart's astonishment); merging the two ravah/barak/shama (Abraham vs Ishmael); importing NT circumcision-of-the-heart theology beyond the flesh-sign the chapter states.

## GEN-05-sentence-expulsion · Genesis 3:14–24 · [the sentence, and hope within it] — two curses (not on the man), the multiplied pain, the desire-and-rule, the naming of life against death, and the guardian guarded out

**The passage.** "**Cursed** are you [serpent] above all… beasts" (3:14); "I will put **enmity** between you and the woman… he shall bruise your head" (3:15); to the woman, "I will **multiply** your **pain**… your **desire** shall be for your husband, and he shall **rule** over you" (3:16); to Adam, "because you have **listened** to… your wife… **cursed** is the **ground**… in **pain** you shall eat" (3:17), "to **dust** you shall return" (3:19); "the man **called** his wife's name **Eve**, mother of all **living**" (3:20); God **clothed** them (3:21); "the man has become like one of us in **knowing** good and evil… lest he… take of the tree of **life**… and **live** forever" (3:22); "sent him out… to **work** the ground" (3:23); the cherubim "to **guard** the way to the tree of life" (3:24).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **arar (curse) #1 — the serpent (3:14).** The finding is "**cursed** (arar) are you [serpent] above all… beasts… all the days of your **life** (chayyim)" *(stated)*. The operation is the curse falling *on the tempter's own body and existence* — the crafty beast (3:1) reduced to belly and dust *(inferred)*.
- **evah (enmity) — God *institutes* hostility (3:15).** The finding is "I will put **enmity** (evah) between you and the woman… he shall bruise your head, you his heel" *(stated)*. The operation is **divinely-set hostility** — God *establishes* an enmity between the serpent's line and the woman's; the first note of conflict *and* of a hope inside the sentence (the head bruised) *(inferred: the enmity is *placed by God*, not merely arising — an ordered opposition)*.
- **ravah (multiply) — the increase-verb *inverted* to pain (3:16).** The finding is "I will surely **multiply** (ravah) your **pain**" *(stated)*. The operation is the *same verb* that blessed increase (1:28, "be fruitful and **multiply**", GEN-02) now **amplifying suffering** — increase turned against the interior; blessing's word made a curse's word *(inferred: do not lump with 1:28 — same ravah, opposite content)*.
- **itstsavon + etsev (pain/toil) — the woman's childbearing pain, two words stacked (3:16).** The finding is "in **pain** (etsev) you shall bring forth" alongside the multiplied **pain** (itstsavon) *(stated)*. The operation is *painful bringing-forth of life* — two pain-words (H6093, H6089A) stacked on the one act *(inferred: note itstsavon recurs at 3:17 for the *man* — the shared curse below)*.
- **teshuqah + mashal — desire toward, and rule over (3:16).** The finding is "your **desire** (teshuqah) shall be for your husband, and he shall **rule** (mashal) over you" *(stated)*. The operations, kept apart: **teshuqah (desire)** — a *different* desire-word from the fall's chamad/taavah (3:6); a desire *directed toward* the husband; **mashal (rule)** — and note it is *mashal* (govern), the lights'-rule verb (GEN-01 1:16–18), **not** radah (human dominion, GEN-02); the husband's rule is *governance*, not subjugation-by-design. The gem is the *pairing itself* — desire-toward held in tension with rule-over — a disordered relational dynamic *(inferred: the identical teshuqah+mashal pair recurs at 4:7 of sin toward Cain — "its desire is for you, but you must rule over it"; the thread to GEN-06 is deliberate)*.
- **shama (listen) — heeding the *wrong voice* (3:17).** The finding is "because you have **listened** (shama) to the voice of your wife" *(stated)*. The operation is hearing-as-*heeding* — and the fault is located in *whose voice was obeyed*; distinct from the shama of 3:8/3:10 (hearing God's presence and hiding): here shama is *misdirected obedience* *(inferred)*.
- **arar (curse) #2 — the *ground*, not the man (3:17).** The finding is "**cursed** (arar) is the **ground** because of you" *(stated)*. The operation is the curse falling on the man's *domain* — the earth he works — **not on his person**; the man is not himself cursed (unlike the serpent), his *labour and soil* are *(inferred: distinct from arar #1; the human is judged through his ground and toil, not made a cursed thing)*.
- **itstsavon (toil) — the man's ground-pain, the *same word* as the woman's (3:17).** The finding is "in **pain** (itstsavon) you shall eat of it all the days of your life" *(stated)*. The operation is *painful bringing-forth of food* — and it is the **same itstsavon** as the woman's childbearing pain (3:16): the shared human curse is *painful bringing-forth*, hers of life, his of bread *(inferred: keep the two domains distinct, but the word welds them)*.
- **qara (name) — naming *life* against the death-sentence (3:20).** The finding is "the man **called** (qara) his wife's name **Eve**, because she was the mother of all **living** (chay)" *(stated)*. The operation is naming — *immediately after* "to dust you shall return" (3:19) — as an act of **hope defying the sentence**: in the teeth of death, the man names his wife *life-giver* *(inferred: distinct from the naming of "Woman" at creation (2:23, recognition) — this naming is defiant hope; the chay-cluster now serves life-affirmation against death)*.
- **(3:21 — God clothes them: a covering act with *no non-T2 span*.)** Narratively God makes "garments of skins" and clothes them — the shame of 3:7 met by God's *provision of covering*. Flagged: this carries **no study-term span**, so it grounds no lexical operation-finding here; noted only as the context that God *covers* the shame the fall created (a thread to later covering/atonement language, not read into this verse).
- **yada (know) #3 — God *confirms* the knowing happened (3:22).** The finding is "the man has become like one of us in **knowing** (yada) good and evil" *(stated)*. The operation is the knowing of 3:5/3:7 now *acknowledged from God's side*: the serpent's promise (3:5, "you will be like God, knowing") was — terribly — *true*; man *did* become like God in knowing good and evil. The third yada of the fall, distinct: 3:5 promise → 3:7 shame-arrival → **3:22 divine confirmation it occurred** *(inferred)*.
- **chayah (live) — the barring of *endless* life (3:22).** The finding is "lest he… take also of the tree of **life** (chayyim)… and **live** (chayah) forever" *(stated)*. The operation is God *barring unending life* to fallen man — **mercy in the expulsion**: not endless life in a fallen state *(inferred)*.
- **avad (work) #3 — labour on *cursed* ground (3:23).** The finding is "sent him out… to **work** (avad) the ground from which he was taken" *(stated)*. The operation is the vocation-verb now under the curse — the third avad in a trajectory: *absent* worker (2:5) → *good* garden-vocation (2:15) → *toil on cursed ground* outside (3:23) *(inferred)*.
- **shamar (guard) — the guardian *guarded out* (3:24).** The finding is the cherubim set "to **guard** (shamar) the way to the tree of life" *(stated)*. The operation is the **inversion of 2:15**: man was placed to *keep/guard* (shamar) the garden; now the cherubim *guard* it **against him**. The keeper is kept out; the guarding-operation reverses direction *(inferred: the sharpest structural gem of the passage — same shamar, from man-guards-garden to garden-guarded-from-man)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **the curses fall around the man, not on him:** arar strikes the *serpent* (3:14) and the *ground* (3:17) — the human is judged *through* his domain (soil, toil, childbearing), his *person* uncursed. The interior is disciplined, not damned.
2. **teshuqah + mashal (3:16) → 4:7:** the desire-toward/rule-over dynamic set between wife and husband is the *same* pairing God will use of sin toward Cain (GEN-06) — a relational-mastery motif threaded forward.
3. **ravah and itstsavon invert / weld:** the blessing-word ravah now multiplies *pain* (3:16); the toil-word itstsavon *welds* the woman's and the man's curse (3:16,17) into one shared "painful bringing-forth".
4. **the three yada frame the whole fall:** promised (3:5) → arrived-as-shame (3:7, GEN-04) → confirmed-by-God (3:22) — the knowing operation runs its full, ruinous course.
5. **hope inside the sentence:** the enmity with a bruised head (3:15), the naming of *Eve/life* against the dust-sentence (3:20), the *clothing* (3:21), and the *mercy* of barring endless fallen life (3:22) — the interior is sentenced *and* given grounds to hope, within the same passage.
6. **shamar reversed + avad cursed:** the two garden-vocations of 2:15 (keep + work) both turn — work becomes toil (3:23), and guarding turns against the man (3:24): the unfallen vocation of GEN-03 is inverted at expulsion.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** arar on the serpent (3:14) vs on the ground-not-the-man (3:17); evah as *God-instituted* enmity; ravah *inverted* to multiply pain (vs the blessing of 1:28); the two childbearing pain-words (itstsavon/etsev) and itstsavon *shared* with the man's toil; teshuqah+mashal (desire-toward/govern-over, mashal not radah, thread to 4:7); shama as *misdirected heeding* (vs hearing-God 3:8/10); the three yada of the fall (promise/shame/confirmation); qara-naming-Eve as *hope against death*; chayah — the *merciful barring* of endless fallen life; the avad trajectory (absent→good→cursed); shamar *reversed* (guardian guarded out).
- **Whose interior:** the **human** interior *sentenced but not damned* — disciplined through desire, rule, pain, toil, and exile, yet given enmity-with-hope, the naming of life, a covering, and a merciful limit.
- **Threads:** teshuqah+mashal (→ 4:7, GEN-06); shamar + avad (← 2:15, GEN-03, now inverted); the chay-cluster (life named against death, 3:20); the covering (3:21, no-span, thread to later atonement language — not read in here).
- **Guarded against:** lumping ravah (blessing vs curse); lumping the two arar (serpent vs ground) or reading the *man* as cursed (he is not — his ground is); reading radah into the husband's *mashal*; merging the three yada; treating the Eve-naming as a mere label rather than defiant hope; importing NT protoevangelium content into 3:15 beyond the God-set enmity/bruising the verse states.

## GEN-15-shemite-line-mortality · Genesis 11:10–26 · [the narrowing bridge to Abram] — the compressing lifespans and the line that runs toward promise, not death

**The passage.** The generations of Shem: each "**lived** (chayah) N years… fathered… **lived** after… N years, and had other sons and daughters" — Shem (11:10–11), Arpachshad (11:12–13), Shelah (11:14–15), Eber (11:16–17), Peleg (11:18–19), Reu (11:20–21), Serug (11:22–23), Nahor (11:24–25), to **Terah**, who "fathered **Abram**" (11:26).

### The operation (with its two distinctions from GEN-08)

- **chayah (lived) ×16 — the genealogical formula, *one* operation.** The finding is the repeated "**lived** (chayah)… fathered… lived after… N years" through the roster Shem → Arpachshad → Shelah → Eber → Peleg (11:18–19) → Reu → Serug → Nahor → Terah → **Abram** (11:26) *(stated)*. The operation is mortality-and-succession as the human condition — the same formula as the Sethite line (GEN-08). But it is **not** a mere repeat; two distinctions are the finding:
  1. **The lifespans *compress* sharply.** Where the Sethite patriarchs lived 800–900+ years *after* fathering (GEN-08), here the "lived after" span *contracts down the list*: Shem **500**, Arpachshad/Shelah/Eber ~**400–430**, then a sudden fall — Peleg **209**, Reu **207**, Serug **200**, Nahor **119** (11:11–25). The gem is the *shrinking*: post-flood, the *shadow of death tightens* over the line — mortality gaining, lifespan collapsing toward the human span *(inferred: the numbers themselves are the datum; the reign of death (GEN-08) is *closing in*, not merely repeating)*.
  2. **The death-refrain is *dropped*.** The Sethite formula tolled "**and he died**" over every man (GEN-08, ch 5); *this* formula **omits it** — each entry ends "and had other sons and daughters", not "and he died". The gem is the *silence of the refrain*: the line is no longer *punctuated by death* but *carried forward by begetting* toward a destination — it *runs*, undtolled, to **Abram** (11:26) *(inferred: the interior register shifts from the tolling of mortality (GEN-08) to the *advance toward promise* — the genealogy is now a bridge, not a dirge)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **compression + dropped refrain = a line bending toward promise:** the two distinctions work together — mortality *tightens* (lifespans fall) yet the text *stops tolling death* and *keeps begetting* toward Abram. The Sethite line (GEN-08) ended each life in death; the Shemite line ends the *passage* in a *birth* — "Terah fathered Abram" (11:26). The reign of death is real and *closing in*, but the narrative's movement is *toward the one who will be called*, not toward the grave.
2. **the hinge out of the primeval block:** GEN-15 is the *narrowing funnel* from Noah's sons to Abram — the primeval history's universal scope contracting to a single line and a single name, on which the next block (the Abraham cycle) opens.

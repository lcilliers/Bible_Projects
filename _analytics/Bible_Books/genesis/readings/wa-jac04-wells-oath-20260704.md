## JAC-04-wells-oath · Genesis 26:12–35 · [the received blessing, and the yielding that makes room] — prosperity and envy, the wells re-dug and ceded, the night "fear not", and the covenant of peace

**The passage.** Isaac sowed and reaped a hundredfold, "the LORD **blessed** (barakh) him" (26:12); "the man **became rich** (gadal), **gained more and more** (gadel), very wealthy" (26:13); the Philistines **envied** (qana) his flocks and **servants** (avuddah) (26:14); "**mightier** (atsom) than we — go away" (26:16); he re-dug his father's wells and **called** (qara) them by the old names (26:18); found a **spring** (chay, living water) (26:19); the herdsmen **quarreled** (riv), so he **called** (qara) it Esek (26:20); another, quarreled, **called** (qara) Sitnah (26:21); another, no quarrel, "the LORD has **made room** (rachav)", **called** (qara) Rehoboth (26:22); the night word — "**Fear not** (yare)… I will **bless** (barakh) you and **multiply** (ravah) your offspring for Abraham's sake" (26:24); he **called** (qara) on the name of the LORD at an altar (26:25); "you **hate** (sane) me" (26:27); "let us **make** (karat) a **covenant** (berit)" (26:28); "do us no **harm** (raah)… we sent you away in **peace** (shalom)… you are the **blessed** (barakh) of the LORD" (26:29); they departed in **peace** (shalom) (26:31); he **called** it Shibah (26:33); Esau's wives made life **bitter** (morah) (26:35).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **barakh (blessed him) — the blessing *materialised in harvest* (26:12).** "The LORD **blessed** (barakh) him" *(stated)* — barakh here is the blessing *enacted in a hundredfold reaping*: the promised favour *made visible as produce*. The blessing *received* becomes *wealth*, not by scheme but by the LORD's act *(inferred: barakh = the blessing embodied in a harvest)*.
- **gadal (became rich) + gadel (gained more) — the *escalating* increase (26:13).** "The man **became rich** (gadal)… **gained more and more** (gadel) until very wealthy" *(stated)*. Two forms of one root, kept distinct: **gadal** — *became great* (the state reached); **gadel** — *growing / going-on-growing* (the *continuous* increase). The doubling *stresses the momentum* of the blessing — the interior *carried upward* by God's favour, wealth *compounding* *(inferred: gadal/gadel = greatness reached and greatness still-increasing)*.
- **qana (envied) — the blessing *provokes jealousy* (26:14).** "So the Philistines **envied** (qana) him" *(stated)* — qana, *be jealous/envious*: the blessed man's prosperity *breeds resentment* in the watchers. The first appearance of the envy-operation that will run through the wives' rivalry (30:1) and Laban's sons (31:1) — the interior *provoked by another's blessing* *(inferred: qana = the envy the blessing provokes in others)*.
- **avuddah (servants) — the *household-service* that marks the wealth (26:14).** "possessions of flocks and herds and many **servants** (avuddah)" *(stated)* — avuddah, *service / a body of servants*: a *mark* of the greatness, the retinue the blessing gathers *(inferred: avuddah = the servant-household of the prospered man)*.
- **atsom (mightier) — the blessed man grown *too vast* to keep near (26:16).** "Go away… you are much **mightier** (atsom) than we" *(stated)* — atsom, *be vast/numerous/mighty*: Abimelech *fears* the sheer *bigness* of the blessed man; the blessing makes Isaac *a power* the host cannot hold — expulsion by *intimidation* (contrast the oracle-word ammits, 25:23, of Esau/Jacob's strength) *(inferred: atsom = the fearsome vastness the blessing produces)*.
- **qara (re-named the wells) — *restoring the father's names* (26:18).** "he **called** (qara) them by the names his father had given" *(stated)* — the *first* qara of the chapter is *restorative*: Isaac *re-opens Abraham's stopped wells and re-gives Abraham's names* — the interior *honouring and continuing the father*, not innovating or seizing; continuity with the received inheritance *(inferred: qara = the naming that restores the father's markers)*.
- **chay (spring/living water) — the *living* water found (26:19).** "his servants dug and found a well of **spring** (chay, living) water" *(stated)* — chay, *living/alive*: *mayim chayyim*, **living water** — a *fresh, self-renewing* source (the lexeme's range reaches kin/community, but *here* it is the *living* water). The gift *found in the ground* over which the strife then breaks *(inferred: chay = living/fresh water, the contested good)*.
- **riv (Esek) + qara (Esek) — the *first contested* well (26:20).** "the herdsmen **quarreled** (riv)… he **called** (qara) it Esek, because they **contended**" *(stated)*. **riv** — *strive/contend/bring a suit*: the *dispute* over the living water; **qara** — the naming *records the strife itself* (Esek ≈ contention). The interior *does not fight to hold it* — he *names the strife and moves on* *(inferred: riv = the contention; qara = the name that memorialises it)*.
- **riv (Sitnah) + qara (Sitnah) — the *second, sharper* strife (26:21).** *Distinct from 26:20:* "they **quarreled** (riv) over that also, so he **called** (qara) it Sitnah" *(stated)*. Here riv *escalates* — Sitnah ≈ *hostility/accusation* (a harsher word than Esek's mere contention); the *second* well *more bitterly* opposed. Isaac *again yields* rather than contend — the *repeated* ceding, each time named *(inferred: riv = the sharpened hostility; qara = the name of enmity — distinct from Esek)*.
- **rachav (made room) + qara (Rehoboth) + riv (no quarrel) — the *strife ended, room given* (26:22).** *Distinct again:* "another well, and they did **not quarrel** (riv) over it… the LORD has **made room** (rachav)… he **called** (qara) it Rehoboth" *(stated)*. Three operations resolve the sequence: the *absence* of riv (the third well *uncontested*); **rachav** — *widen/enlarge*: the LORD *makes broad room*; **qara** — Rehoboth ≈ *broad places*. The gem is the *pattern*: the interior that *yields ground repeatedly* is *given room by God* — **space received, not seized** — "we shall be fruitful in the land." The Isaac-interior's non-grasping *rewarded with breadth* *(inferred: rachav = the God-given room that ends the strife; the yielding vindicated)*.
- **yare (Fear not) — the *reassurance* against the pressure (26:24a).** "the LORD appeared… '**Fear not** (yare), for I am with you'" *(stated)* — yare in the *dispelled* register (← 15:1 to Abraham): the interior, *pressed by envy and expulsion*, is met with *"do not fear"* — the same word that *drove the lie* (26:7) now *disarmed by God's presence* *(inferred: yare = the fear God dispels by his nearness)*.
- **barakh (I will bless) — the blessing *re-spoken*, grounded on Abraham (26:24b).** *Distinct from 26:12:* "I will **bless** (barakh) you and multiply your offspring **for Abraham's sake**" *(stated)*. Where 26:12 barakh was the blessing *enacted*, here it is *spoken as promise* and *grounded on Abraham* — again the son blessed *on the father's account* (as 26:5) — inheritance *received* *(inferred: barakh = the spoken blessing, grounded on the father)*.
- **ravah (multiply) — the *increase* re-promised (26:24c).** "and **multiply** (ravah) your offspring for my servant Abraham's sake" *(stated)* — ravah, *increase*: the seed-promise renewed against the household strife *(inferred: ravah = the promised increase re-spoken)*.
- **qara (called on the name) — *worship* at the altar (26:25).** *Distinct from all the naming-qara:* "he built an altar and **called** (qara) upon the name of the LORD" *(stated)* — here qara is *call-to / invoke*: not naming a well but **invoking God in worship** (as Abraham, 12:8; 13:4) — the interior's *response* to the theophany is *worship*, marking the place with an altar not a claim *(inferred: qara = the worship-invocation, distinct from the well-namings)*.
- **sane (hate) — the *acknowledged enmity* now reversed (26:27).** "why come to me, seeing you **hate** (sane) me and sent me away?" *(stated)* — sane, *hate*: Isaac *names the past hostility* of those now seeking him; the interior *honest about the enmity* that the coming covenant will *close* *(inferred: sane = the hostility owned before it is resolved)*.
- **karat (make) + berit (covenant) — the *treaty cut* by the former enemies (26:28).** "let us **make** (karat) a **covenant** (berit) with you" *(stated)* — **karat** *cut* + **berit** *covenant*: the Philistines *initiate a sworn pact* — the party that *envied and expelled* now *binds itself in peace*, *because* "we see the LORD is with you." The blessing *compels a covenant of peace from outsiders* (cf. Abraham & the earlier Abimelech, 21:27) *(inferred: karat/berit = the peace-treaty the blessing draws out of former enemies)*.
- **raah (harm) — the *harm renounced* in the oath (26:29a).** "that you will do us no **harm** (raah)" *(stated)* — raah, *harm/injury*: the covenant's *content* is the mutual renunciation of harm — the interior's *security* secured by oath *(inferred: raah = the harm the treaty forswears)*.
- **barakh (the blessed of the LORD) — the *outsiders' recognition* of the blessing (26:29b).** *Distinct from 26:12 and 26:24:* "you are now the **blessed** (barakh) of the LORD" *(stated)* — here barakh is *confessed by the Philistines*: the very ones who envied now *name Isaac as God's blessed*. The blessing *acknowledged by those it once provoked* — the third distinct barakh *(inferred: barakh = the blessing recognised aloud by former enemies)*.
- **shalom (in peace) — the *peaceful sending* (26:29c).** "we have… sent you away in **peace** (shalom)" *(stated)* — shalom, *peace/wholeness*: the Philistines *claim* to have dealt in peace; the term of the reconciliation *(inferred: shalom = the peace claimed as the basis of the pact)*.
- **shalom (departed in peace) — the peace *enacted* in parting (26:31).** *Distinct from 26:29:* "they exchanged oaths… and departed from him in **peace** (shalom)" *(stated)* — where 26:29 shalom was *claimed* as basis, here shalom is the *actual parting* — the treaty *concluded and the men leave whole*; peace *achieved*, not just asserted *(inferred: shalom = the peace realised in the parting)*.
- **qara (Shibah) — the *oath-well named* (26:33).** *Distinct from the earlier namings:* "he **called** (qara) it Shibah; therefore Beersheba" *(stated)* — qara names the well *for the oath* (Shibah ≈ oath/seven) — the naming that *commemorates the covenant of peace*, not a strife (contrast Esek/Sitnah) *(inferred: qara = the naming that memorialises the oath — distinct from the contention-namings)*.
- **morah (bitter) — the *domestic bitterness* that closes the chapter (26:35).** "Esau's wives made life **bitter** (morah) for Isaac and Rebekah" *(stated)* — morah, *bitterness/grief of spirit*: the chapter of *peace with outsiders* ends in *bitterness within the house* — the parents grieved by Esau's foreign wives; the interior's *domestic sorrow* that *motivates* the sending of Jacob for a wife (28:1) and shadows the coming deception *(inferred: morah = the bitterness of spirit within the family)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **the blessing materialises and provokes envy (barakh 26:12 → gadal/gadel 26:13 → qana/avuddah 26:14 → atsom 26:16):** the received favour becomes a *hundredfold harvest* and *compounding wealth* and a *great household*, which *breeds envy* and makes Isaac *too mighty to keep near* — the blessing *itself* generates the conflict.
2. **the yielding that is given room (qara-restore 26:18 → chay 26:19 → riv/qara Esek 26:20 → riv/qara Sitnah 26:21 → riv-none/rachav/qara Rehoboth 26:22):** Isaac *re-digs and re-names the father's wells* (continuity, not seizure), finds *living water*, and when it is *contended* (Esek) and *more bitterly opposed* (Sitnah) he *yields and moves* each time — until God *makes room* (Rehoboth). The non-grasping interior *receives breadth* — space **given, not seized** (the Isaac counter-note to Jacob's grasping).
3. **fear dispelled, blessing re-grounded, worship offered (yare/barakh/ravah 26:24 → qara-worship 26:25):** pressed, the interior is met with *"fear not"*, the blessing *re-spoken on Abraham's account*, and *responds in worship* — building an altar, *invoking* God rather than *claiming* ground.
4. **enmity closed by covenant (sane 26:27 → karat/berit 26:28 → raah/barakh/shalom 26:29 → shalom 26:31 → qara-Shibah 26:33):** the *hated*-and-expelled man is *sought by his enemies*, who *cut a covenant*, *renounce harm*, *confess him blessed*, and *part in peace* — the well of the *oath* named; the blessing *turns former enemies into covenant-partners*.
5. **peace outside, bitterness inside (morah 26:35):** the chapter of *outward peace* ends on *inward grief* — Esau's foreign wives *embitter* the parents; the domestic sorrow that sets the stage for Jacob's sending and the deception to come.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** barakh ×3 *distinguished* (26:12 *materialised* in harvest / 26:24 *spoken* & grounded on Abraham / 26:29 *confessed* by former enemies); gadal + gadel (greatness *reached* + *still-increasing*); qana (the *envy* the blessing provokes); avuddah (the *servant-household*); atsom (grown *too vast* to keep near); qara *5 namings distinguished* (26:18 *restoring* the father's names / 26:20 Esek *contention* / 26:21 Sitnah *hostility* / 26:22 Rehoboth *room* / 26:33 Shibah *oath*) + 26:25 qara = *worship-invocation*; chay (the *living* water contested); riv ×3 (Esek *contend* / Sitnah *sharper hostility* / Rehoboth *no-quarrel*); rachav (God *makes room* — the yielding vindicated); yare (the *fear dispelled* — vs the fear that drove the lie 26:7); ravah (increase re-promised); sane (the *enmity owned*); karat/berit (the *peace-treaty* drawn from enemies); raah (the *harm forsworn*); shalom ×2 (*claimed* basis / *realised* parting); morah (the *domestic bitterness* that closes).
- **Whose interior:** the **divine** interior *blessing, dispelling fear, making room, grounding the gift on Abraham*; the **human** interior *non-grasping and yielding* (Isaac cedes the wells, worships, receives room) — the Isaac counter-image to Jacob's seizing; the **outsiders'** interior turning from *envy/hatred* to *covenant/peace*; and the *bitterness* within the house (Esau's wives).
- **Threads:** *received vs grasped* (Isaac *yields* the wells and is *given room* — contrast the birthright *bought* JAC-02, the blessing *stolen* JAC-05); the fear-lie's fear (26:7) vs the *"fear not"* (26:24); inheritance grounded on Abraham (26:24 ← 26:5, 22:18); envy (qana → 30:1 Rachel, 31:1 Laban's sons); covenant with an Abimelech (← 21:22–34); the domestic bitterness (morah 26:35 → the sending of Jacob 28:1, the deception 27).
- **Guarded against:** collapsing the three barakh into one "blessing" (materialised / spoken / confessed — distinct); lumping the five well-namings or the three riv into single operations (Esek/Sitnah/Rehoboth each a *distinct stage* — contention, hostility, room; and 26:25 qara is *worship* not naming); reading Isaac's yielding as mere weakness when the text *rewards* it with rachav/room (the finding is the *vindicated non-grasping*); importing a "living water" NT symbol over the plain *fresh-water* the verse means; over-reading morah into a theological judgment when the verse states *domestic grief of spirit*.

## JOS-09-second-descent-compassion · Genesis 43:1–34 · [the surety, the mercy invoked, and the compassion barely held] — Judah's pledge, Benjamin brought, and the weeping in the chamber

**The passage.** "The man **solemnly** (ud) warned us" (43:3); "why did you treat me so **badly** (raa)?" (43:6); "he **questioned** (shaal) us: is your father **alive** (chay)? could we **know** (yada)?" (43:7); "send the boy, that we may **live** (chayah)" (43:8); "I will be a pledge… from my hand **require** (baqash) him… let me bear the **blame** (chata)" (43:9); "God Almighty **grant** (natan) you **mercy** (rachamim)… if I am bereaved, I am bereaved" (43:14); the brothers **afraid** (yare) at Joseph's house, the money "the **first time** (techillah)" (43:18); "we do not **know** (yada) who put it" (43:22); "**Peace** (shalom), do not be **afraid** (yare); your God put treasure" (43:23); water **given** (natan) (43:24); they **heard** (shama) they'd eat there (43:25); Joseph **inquired** (shaal): "is your father **well** (shalom), still **alive** (chay)?" (43:27); "he is **well** (shalom), **alive** (chay)" (43:28); "God be **gracious** (chanan) to you" (43:29); his **compassion** (rachamim) grew warm, he **sought** (baqash) a place to **weep** (bakhah) (43:30); the Egyptians **could** (yakhol) not eat with Hebrews — an **abomination** (toevah) (43:32); the brothers in **amazement** (tamah) (43:33); Benjamin's portion **five times** (ravah) (43:34).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **ud (solemnly) — the *condition* that binds the return (43:3).** "the man **solemnly** (ud) warned us: 'you shall not see my face without your brother'" *(stated)* — ud, *testify/warn solemnly*: the *binding condition* (Benjamin must come) — the interior-context of *the demand that forces Jacob's hand* *(inferred: ud = the solemn condition binding the second descent)*.
- **raa (badly) — Jacob's *reproach* (43:6).** "'why did you treat me so **badly** (raa) as to tell him you had a brother?'" *(stated)* — raa, *be evil/do harm*: the gem is the *father's blame* — he *reproaches the sons* for exposing Benjamin's existence; the interior *lashing at the messengers of the demand* *(inferred: raa = the father's reproach of the sons)*.
- **shaal (questioned) + chay (alive) + yada (know) — the *sons' defence* (43:7).** "'he **questioned** (shaal) us: is your father **alive** (chay)?… could we **know** (yada) he'd say bring your brother?'" *(stated)*. **shaal** — the *questioning* recalled; **chay** — the father's *life* asked after; **yada** — the sons plead *ignorance* of where it would lead. The interior *defending itself against the reproach* *(inferred: shaal/chay/yada = the sons' defence — they could not have known)*.
- **chayah (live) — Judah's *survival argument* (43:8).** "'send the boy… that we may **live** (chayah) and not die — we, you, and our little ones'" *(stated)* — chayah, *live*: Judah *presses the survival stakes* — *all die* if they do not go; the interior *urging the necessary risk* *(inferred: chayah = the survival argument for sending Benjamin)*.
- **baqash (require) + chata (blame) — Judah's *surety* (43:9).** "'I will be a pledge; from my hand **require** (baqash) him… let me bear the **blame** (chata) forever'" *(stated)*. **baqash** — *require/seek* (the surety-language: *hold me accountable*); **chata** — the gem: Judah *pledges to bear the blame* for Benjamin — the *very brother-role he failed with Joseph* (whom he *sold*, JOS-02) now *assumed as guarantor*. Judah's *transformation* deepening (← the Tamar-turn, JOS-03): the interior *taking responsibility for the brother* *(inferred: baqash/chata = Judah's surety — bearing the blame for the brother he once failed)*.
- **natan (grant) + rachamim (mercy) — Jacob's *grief-resignation* (43:14).** "'God Almighty **grant** (natan) you **mercy** (rachamim)… if I am bereaved, I am bereaved'" *(stated)*. **natan** — *give*; **rachamim** — *compassion/mercy*: the gem is the *father's surrender* — he *invokes God's mercy* and *resigns himself to loss* ("if I am bereaved, I am bereaved") — the interior *releasing his grip in trust-and-grief* *(inferred: natan/rachamim = the invoked mercy and the grief-resignation)*.
- **techillah (first time) + yare (afraid) — the *guilt-fear* at the house (43:18), then explained (43:20).** "the men were **afraid** (yare)… 'because of the money replaced the **first time** (techillah, 43:18), he will assault us'" — and to the steward, "'we came down the **first time** (techillah, 43:20) to buy food'" *(stated)*. **techillah** ×2 — the *first time* (the returned money): 43:18 the *fearful reasoning* among themselves / 43:20 the *explanation* to the steward; **yare** — the gem: the brothers *read the invitation as a trap* — *guilt makes them fear* the very kindness (a meal) as an ambush; the interior *interpreting mercy as menace* *(inferred: techillah/yare = the guilt-fear that reads the meal as a trap, then explained away)*.
- **yada (know) — the *ignorance* of the money (43:22).** *yada distinct from 43:7:* "'we do not **know** (yada) who put our money in our sacks'" *(stated)* — the brothers' *bafflement* — the returned money *inexplicable* to them (God's hidden hand, unread) *(inferred: yada = the unknowing about the returned money)*.
- **yare (afraid) + shalom (Peace) — the *steward's reassurance* (43:23).** *yare distinct from 43:18:* "'**Peace** (shalom), do not be **afraid** (yare); your God and your father's God put treasure in your sacks'" *(stated)*. **shalom** — *peace*; **yare** — the *fear dispelled*: the gem is that the *Egyptian steward names their God* — *"your God put the treasure"* — reading the money as *God's gift*, not a trap; the interior *reassured*, the mercy *named as God's* *(inferred: shalom/yare = the reassurance — the returned money named God's gift)*.
- **natan (given) — the *hospitality* (43:24).** *natan distinct from 43:14:* "he **gave** (natan) them water… and their donkeys fodder" *(stated)* — natan, *give*: the *welcome* extended — the interior-context of *hospitality* replacing the feared assault *(inferred: natan = the hospitality given — the feared trap becomes a welcome)*.
- **shama (heard) — the *meal announced* (43:25).** "they **heard** (shama) they should eat bread there" *(stated)* — shama, *hear*: the *meal* announced — the brothers *prepare the present*, still uneasy *(inferred: shama = the meal heard of)*.
- **shaal (inquired) + shalom (well) + chay (alive) — Joseph's *tender inquiry* (43:27).** *shaal distinct from 43:7; shalom/chay distinct:* "he **inquired** (shaal) about their **welfare** (shalom): 'is your father **well** (shalom)… still **alive** (chay)?'" *(stated)*. **shaal** — Joseph *asks after* them; **shalom** — their *welfare*; **chay** — the *father's life*. The gem: the disguised Joseph's *tender concern for his father* — the interior's *love leaking through the disguise* *(inferred: shaal/shalom/chay = Joseph's tender inquiry after his father)*.
- **shalom (well) + chay (alive) — the *answer* (43:28).** *shalom/chay distinct from 43:27:* "'your servant our father is **well** (shalom); he is still **alive** (chay)'" — and they bowed *(stated)* — the *answer* (the father lives) — and the *bowing* (the dreams' fulfilment continuing) *(inferred: shalom/chay = the father's welfare confirmed, the bowing continued)*.
- **chanan (gracious) — the *blessing on Benjamin* (43:29).** "seeing Benjamin: 'God be **gracious** (chanan) to you, my son'" *(stated)* — chanan, *be gracious*: the gem is the *tenderness* — Joseph *blesses his full brother* ("my mother's son") with *grace*; the interior's *love for Benjamin surfacing* *(inferred: chanan = the gracious blessing on the full brother)*.
- **rachamim (compassion) + baqash (sought) + bakhah (weep) — the *compassion overflowing* (43:30).** *rachamim distinct from 43:14; baqash distinct from 43:9:* "his **compassion** (rachamim) grew warm for his brother; he **sought** (baqash) a place to **weep** (bakhah), and wept in his chamber" *(stated)*. The **central gem**: **rachamim** — Joseph's *compassion grows warm* (the yearning of the innards); **baqash** — he *must seek privacy*; **bakhah** — he *weeps*. The disguise *strains to breaking* — the interior's *love overwhelming its control* (← the hidden weeping 42:24, now *warmer, harder to hold* → the disclosure, JOS-11) *(inferred: rachamim/baqash/bakhah = the compassion overflowing — the disguise nearly broken)*.
- **yakhol (could) + toevah (abomination) — the *separation at the meal* (43:32).** "the Egyptians **could** (yakhol) not eat with the Hebrews — an **abomination** (toevah)" *(stated)*. **yakhol** — the *inability*; **toevah** — *what is abhorred*: the interior-context of *the cultural/sacred separation* (Egypt abhors eating with Hebrews, ← the shepherds' abomination 46:34) — Joseph *set apart even at his own table* *(inferred: yakhol/toevah = the separation Egypt's abhorrence imposes)*.
- **tamah (amazement) — the *seating* wonder (43:33).** "seated in birth order… the men looked at one another in **amazement** (tamah)" *(stated)* — tamah, *be astounded*: the gem is the *uncanny knowledge* — the stranger *seats them in exact birth order*; the interior *baffled by a knowing it cannot explain* (Joseph's hidden knowledge) *(inferred: tamah = the amazement at the uncanny seating)*.
- **ravah (five times) — the *favour shown Benjamin* (43:34).** "Benjamin's portion was **five times** (ravah) as much" *(stated)* — ravah, *multiply/abundance*: the gem is the *lavish favour* on Benjamin — whether *test* (do they envy the favoured youngest, as they envied Joseph?) or *love*, the interior *singles out the full brother*; and they *drink and are merry* (the tension easing for a moment) *(inferred: ravah = the fivefold favour on Benjamin — love, and perhaps a test of their envy)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **the surety and the resignation (ud 43:3 → raa 43:6 → shaal/chay/yada 43:7 → chayah 43:8 → baqash/chata 43:9 → natan/rachamim 43:14):** the *binding condition* forces Jacob's hand; he *reproaches* the sons, they *defend*; and *Judah pledges himself* — bearing the blame for the brother he once failed — while Jacob *invokes God's mercy and resigns himself* to possible loss.
2. **the guilt-fear reassured (techillah/yare 43:18 → yada 43:22 → shalom/yare 43:23 → natan 43:24 → shama 43:25):** the brothers *read the meal as a trap* (guilt reading mercy as menace); the steward *reassures* — *"your God put the treasure"* — and *welcomes* them.
3. **the tender inquiry and the overflowing compassion (shaal/shalom/chay 43:27 → shalom/chay 43:28 → chanan 43:29 → rachamim/baqash/bakhah 43:30):** the disguised Joseph *asks after his father* and *blesses Benjamin* — and his *compassion grows warm*, driving him to *seek a place and weep*; the disguise *nearly breaks*.
4. **the separated meal and the favoured brother (yakhol/toevah 43:32 → tamah 43:33 → ravah 43:34):** Joseph *eats apart* (Egypt's abhorrence), the brothers *amazed* at the uncanny seating, and Benjamin *favoured fivefold* — the tension *easing into merriment*, the disclosure a step nearer.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** ud (the *solemn condition*); raa (the father's *reproach*); shaal ×2 (the *questioning* recalled / Joseph's *tender inquiry*); chay ×3 (the father's *life* asked / *inquired* / *confirmed*); yada ×2 (the sons' *ignorance* of the demand / the *unknowing* about the money); chayah (Judah's *survival* argument); **baqash/chata (Judah's *surety* — bearing the blame for the brother he once failed)**; **natan/rachamim (Jacob's *invoked mercy and grief-resignation*)**; techillah/yare (the *guilt-fear* reading the meal as a trap); shalom ×3 (the steward's *peace* / the *welfare* inquiry / the *answer*); natan (the *hospitality*); chanan (the *gracious blessing* on Benjamin); **rachamim/baqash/bakhah (Joseph's *compassion overflowing* — the disguise nearly broken)**; yakhol/toevah (the *separation* at the meal); tamah (the *amazement* at the seating); ravah (the *fivefold favour* on Benjamin — love, perhaps a test of envy).
- **Whose interior (multi-interior):** **Judah** — *surety*, bearing blame for the brother (the transformation deepening ← Tamar); **Jacob** — *grief-resignation* trusting God's mercy; the **brothers** — *guilt reading mercy as menace*, then reassured; **Joseph** — *tender concern for his father* and *compassion overflowing* (the disguise straining to breaking); the **steward** — naming the money *God's gift*. The rachamim thread (Jacob *prays* mercy, Joseph's mercy *wells*) binds the passage.
- **Threads:** **Judah's transformation** (baqash/chata 43:9 — surety for Benjamin ← the Tamar-turn JOS-03; → the substitute plea JOS-10; the ascendancy 49:8); **Joseph's escalating weeping** (rachamim/bakhah 43:30 ← the hidden weeping 42:24 → the disclosure 45:2,14; 50:17 — the compassion ever-nearer the surface); the **dreams fulfilled** (bowing 43:28 ← 37:7,9); *God's hidden hand* named (shalom/"your God put the treasure" 43:23 → "God sent me", 45:5; "God meant it for good", 50:20); the **favoured youngest** (ravah 43:34 ← Joseph the favoured son 37:3 — a possible *test* of the brothers' old envy); the *abomination* (toevah 43:32 ← 46:34); **rachamim** (Jacob's prayer 43:14 answered in Joseph's welling mercy 43:30).
- **Guarded against:** lumping the pairs/triples (three chay/shalom, two yada/shaal/techillah/yare/natan/rachamim/baqash — each a distinct beat); reading Judah's surety as mere pragmatism when it *reverses his failure with Joseph* (bearing blame for the brother — the transformation); missing that the brothers' *fear* (43:18) is *guilt reading mercy as menace*; over-reading Benjamin's fivefold portion (43:34) as only favour when it may *test* their envy (I hold both — love and possible test); treating Joseph's weeping as weakness when it is *compassion straining the disguise* (love, not loss of control — he *composes himself*, 43:31); importing the messianic Judah-line over the passage's own *surety-and-transformation*.

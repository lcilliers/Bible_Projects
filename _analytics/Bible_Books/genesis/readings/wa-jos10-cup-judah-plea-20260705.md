## JOS-10-cup-judah-plea · Genesis 44:1–34 · [the test met; the substitute offered] — the planted cup, the guilt owned, and Judah in Benjamin's place

**The passage.** Fill the sacks as much as they **can** (yakhol) carry, the cup in Benjamin's (44:1–2); "why repaid **evil** (raah) for good? you have **done evil** (raa)" (44:4, 44:5); "he who is found with it my servant, the rest **innocent** (naqi, naqi)" (44:10); the cup found in Benjamin's sack; "do you not **know** (yada) a man like me divines?" (44:15); Judah — "how **clear ourselves** (tsadeq)? God has found out the **guilt** (avon)" (44:16); "go up in **peace** (shalom) to your father" (44:17); "let not your anger **burn** (charah)" (44:18); "my lord **asked** (shaal)" (44:19); "the boy **cannot** (yakhol) leave his father" (44:22); "we **cannot** (yakhol) go down without him" (44:26); "you **know** (yada) my wife bore me two" (44:27); "harm… bring my gray hairs in **evil** (raah) to Sheol" (44:29); "his **life** (nephesh) bound up in the boy's life" (44:30); "with **sorrow** (yagon) to Sheol" (44:31); "I became a pledge… I shall bear the **blame** (chata)" (44:32); "let me remain instead of the boy" (44:33).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **yakhol (can) — the *staged sacks* (44:1).** "fill the sacks as much as they **can** (yakhol) carry… the cup in the youngest's" *(stated)* — yakhol, *be able*: the *staging of the test* — the cup *planted* in Benjamin's sack; the interior-context of *the trap laid to probe the brothers* *(inferred: yakhol = the sacks filled to capacity, the cup planted — the test staged)*.
- **raah (evil) + raa (done evil) — the *false accusation* (44:4, 44:5).** "'why repaid **evil** (raah) for good?… you have **done evil** (raa)'" *(stated)*. **raah/raa** — *evil*: the *charge* of ingratitude and theft — the gem is the *irony*: the *brothers* are accused of *"evil for good"* — the very thing *they* did to *Joseph* (repaid his seeking-them with the pit); the accusation *names their old pattern* unknowingly *(inferred: raah/raa = the evil-for-good charge — echoing their own old deed)*.
- **naqi (innocent ×2) — the *terms* that isolate Benjamin (44:10).** "'he who is found with it my servant; the rest **innocent** (naqi)'" *(stated)* — naqi, *innocent/free*: the gem is the *terms* — *only the guilty one* held, *the rest free to go*; the interior-context of *the test's design* — *will they take their innocence and abandon Benjamin?* (as they abandoned Joseph) *(inferred: naqi = the innocent-rest terms — the test that isolates the youngest)*.
- **yada (know) — Joseph's *divining claim* (44:15).** "'do you not **know** (yada) a man like me can divine?'" *(stated)* — yada, *know*: Joseph *presses the pretence* of supernatural knowledge (he *does* know all, but not by divination) — the interior *maintaining the disguise's pressure* *(inferred: yada = the divining claim that maintains the pressure)*.
- **tsadeq (clear ourselves) + avon (guilt) — the *guilt fully owned* (44:16).** "'how can we **clear ourselves** (tsadeq)? God has found out the **guilt** (avon) of your servants'" *(stated)*. The **central gem for the brothers**: **tsadeq** — they *cannot justify themselves*; **avon** — *iniquity/guilt*: Judah *names their guilt as exposed by God* — *"God has found out"* — reading the cup-discovery as *divine exposure of their old sin* (← the surfacing at 42:21). The interior *ceasing to defend itself, owning the guilt before God* *(inferred: tsadeq/avon = the guilt fully owned — no self-justification, God has exposed it)*.
- **shalom (peace) — the *test's sharpest point* (44:17).** "'only the man with the cup shall be my servant; the rest, go up in **peace** (shalom) to your father'" *(stated)* — shalom, *peace*: the gem is the *offer that tests them* — *go home in peace, leave Benjamin* — the *exact* choice they faced with Joseph (abandon the favoured brother and go free). The interior *offered its old escape* — will it take it? *(inferred: shalom = the peace-offer that tests whether they will abandon Benjamin)*.
- **charah (burn) + shaal (asked) — Judah's *deferential approach* (44:18–19).** "'let not your anger **burn** (charah)… my lord **asked** (shaal) his servants'" *(stated)*. **charah** — *anger* (Judah begs it not kindle); **shaal** — the *questioning* recalled: Judah *approaches humbly* and *recounts the whole history* — the interior *pleading, not defending* *(inferred: charah/shaal = the deferential approach, the history recounted)*.
- **yakhol (cannot) — the *impossibility of separation* (44:22).** *yakhol distinct from 44:1:* "'the boy **cannot** (yakhol) leave his father, for his father would die'" *(stated)* — the gem is the *bond*: Benjamin *cannot* be separated from Jacob without killing him; the interior *naming the father's fragile life* *(inferred: yakhol = the impossibility — the boy cannot leave the father and live)*.
- **yakhol (cannot) — the *condition* restated (44:26).** *yakhol distinct from 44:22:* "'we **cannot** (yakhol) go down unless our youngest is with us'" *(stated)* — the *condition* the family faced — the interior recounting *the bind they were in* *(inferred: yakhol = the condition — no descent without Benjamin)*.
- **yada (know) — Jacob's *word* recalled (44:27).** *yada distinct from 44:15:* "'my father said: you **know** (yada) my wife bore me two sons'" *(stated)* — yada, *know*: Judah *quotes Jacob's own words* — the *two sons of Rachel* (Joseph and Benjamin); the interior *bringing the father's grief into the room* *(inferred: yada = Jacob's word recalled — the two beloved sons)*.
- **raah (evil) — the *father's dreaded ruin* (44:29).** *raah distinct from 44:4:* "'harm… you will bring my gray hairs in **evil** (raah) to Sheol'" *(stated)* — raah, *evil/calamity*: the *father's dreaded end* (← 42:38; 37:35) — the interior *foreseeing the father's death-in-grief* *(inferred: raah = the calamity that would take the father to Sheol)*.
- **nephesh (life) — the *bound souls* (44:30).** "'his **life** (nephesh) is bound up in the boy's life'" *(stated)* — nephesh, *life/soul*: the gem is the *knot* — *Jacob's very soul tied to Benjamin's* — lose the boy, lose the father; the interior *naming the father's life as hostage to Benjamin's return* *(inferred: nephesh = the father's soul bound to the boy — the knot the test would sever)*.
- **yagon (sorrow) — the *death-grief* named (44:31).** "'your servants will bring the gray hairs of our father with **sorrow** (yagon) to Sheol'" *(stated)* — yagon, *sorrow* (← 42:38): the gem is that *Judah names the grief they would cause* — the *very grief they once caused* with Joseph (37:34–35); the interior *seeing the father's death-sorrow and recoiling from repeating it* *(inferred: yagon = the death-sorrow the brothers would cause — the one they once caused)*.
- **chata (blame) — Judah's *pledge* invoked (44:32).** *chata distinct from JOS-09:* "'I became a pledge… I shall bear the **blame** (chata) before my father all my life'" *(stated)* — chata, *blame/sin*: Judah *invokes his surety* (← 43:9) — he *staked himself* for Benjamin; the interior *bound by its own pledge* *(inferred: chata = the blame Judah pledged to bear)*.
- **(44:33–34 — "let me remain instead of the boy… I fear to see the evil that would find my father"; no non-T2 span.)** Flagged: the *climax* carries no flagged span, but it is the *gem of the whole passage*: **Judah offers himself as a substitute** — *"let me remain instead of the boy, and let the boy go back"* — and *"how can I go back… I cannot bear to see the evil that would find my father."* The gem: the man who *sold Joseph* and *callously watched his father grieve* (JOS-02) now *offers his own freedom to spare his father more grief* — **Judah's transformation complete**: from *causing* the father's sorrow to *sacrificing himself to prevent it*. Read as context, anchored by nephesh/yagon/chata *(no-span context; the substitute — the completed transformation)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **the test staged and sprung (yakhol 44:1 → raah/raa 44:4, 44:5 → naqi 44:10 → yada 44:15):** the *cup planted* in Benjamin's sack, the *evil-for-good* charge (echoing their own old deed), the *innocent-rest* terms that *isolate Benjamin* — the trap *probing whether they will abandon the favoured brother*.
2. **the guilt owned (tsadeq/avon 44:16 → shalom 44:17):** the brothers *cease to justify themselves* — *"God has found out our guilt"* — and Joseph *offers them their old escape*: go home in *peace*, leave Benjamin.
3. **Judah's plea — the father's bound soul (charah/shaal 44:18–19 → yakhol 44:22 → yakhol 44:26 → yada 44:27 → raah 44:29 → nephesh 44:30 → yagon 44:31 → chata 44:32):** Judah *recounts the whole history*, naming the *father's soul bound to Benjamin*, the *death-sorrow* his loss would cause — *the very grief they once caused* — and his *own pledge*.
4. **the substitute (44:33–34):** Judah *offers himself in Benjamin's place* — *"let me remain instead of the boy"* — unable to *bear seeing his father's grief*. **The transformation complete**: the seller of Joseph now *the self-offering guarantor* — the test *passed*.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** yakhol ×3 (the sacks' *capacity*, test staged / the *impossibility* of separation / the *condition* restated); raah/raa (the *evil-for-good* charge echoing their own deed / the *father's dreaded ruin* 44:29); naqi (the *innocent-rest* terms isolating Benjamin); yada ×2 (Joseph's *divining claim* / Jacob's *word* recalled); **tsadeq/avon (the *guilt fully owned* — "God has found out", no self-justification)**; **shalom (the *peace-offer* that tests whether they'll abandon Benjamin — their old escape)**; charah/shaal (the *deferential* approach); nephesh (the *father's soul bound* to the boy); yagon (the *death-sorrow* they'd cause — the one they once caused); chata (Judah's *pledge* invoked); **the substitute (44:33–34, no-span — Judah offering himself, the transformation complete)**.
- **Whose interior (multi-interior):** **Judah** — the *substitute*: self-offering love for father and brother, the *completed transformation* (from selling Joseph and watching his father grieve, to sacrificing himself to spare that grief); the **brothers** — *guilt fully owned* before God (no self-justification); **Joseph** — the *final test* (will they abandon the favoured youngest, as they abandoned him?). The test *answered* by Judah's self-offering.
- **Threads:** **Judah's transformation COMPLETE** (the substitute 44:33 ← the surety 43:9 ← the Tamar-turn 38:26 ← the seller 37:26 — the full arc; → the ascendancy 49:8–12); **the brothers' guilt fully surfaced** (avon 44:16 ← the surfacing 42:21 ← the sin JOS-02 — "God has found out"); the **test** (naqi/shalom — will they repeat the abandonment? ← the favoured-youngest Benjamin echoing the favoured Joseph); the **father's bound soul** (nephesh 44:30 → Jacob's near-death at the news, reversed by Joseph's living, 45:26–27); *evil-for-good* (raah 44:4 ← their own deed → "you meant evil, God meant good", 50:20); Jacob's *grief* (yagon 44:31 ← 37:35; 42:38).
- **Guarded against:** lumping the three yakhol or two raah/naqi/yada; reading the cup-test as Joseph's *cruelty* when it is a *probe of their change* (met and *passed* by Judah's self-offering — the test *reveals* transformation, it does not avenge); missing that the *peace-offer* (44:17) is the *sharpest test* (their old escape re-offered); over-reading the substitute (44:33) into NT substitutionary-atonement typology over the *completed transformation* the passage shows (Judah's self-offering *for his father's sake*); treating the brothers' "God has found out our guilt" (44:16) as despair when it is *honest ownership* (no self-justification — the interior finally straight with its sin).

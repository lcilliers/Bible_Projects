## GEN-10-noah-righteous-flood · Genesis 6:9–7:24 · [the one righteous amid the ruin] — the corruption returned as destruction, the first covenant, the breath undone, and the prevailing waters

**The passage.** "Noah was a **righteous** (tsaddiq) man, **blameless** (tamim)… walked with God" (6:9); "the earth was **corrupt** (shachat)… all **flesh** (basar) had **corrupted** (shachat) their way… I will **destroy** (shachat) them" (6:11–13); "I will establish my **covenant** (berit) with you" (6:18); pairs kept **alive** (chayah, 6:19–20); "you I have seen **righteous** (tsaddiq)" (7:1); the **clean** (tahor) animals (7:2,8); "all in whose nostrils was the **breath** (neshamah) of **life** (chayyim)… died" (7:22); the waters "**increased** (ravah)… **rose high** (rum)… **prevailed** (gavar)" over the **high** (gavoah) mountains (7:17–24).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **tsaddiq (righteous) + tamim (blameless) — Noah's interior (6:9).** The finding is "Noah was a **righteous** (tsaddiq) man, **blameless** (tamim) in his generation" *(stated)*. The operations, kept apart: **tsaddiq** — the **first "righteous" in Scripture**, the interior *conformed to right*; **tamim** — *whole / unblemished / of integrity*, complete before God (as Enoch "walked with God", 5:24, so Noah). Two distinct qualities — rightness *and* wholeness *(inferred)*.
- **shachat (corrupt/destroy) ×4 (6:11, 6:12, 6:13, 6:17) — the ruin man made, returned as the ruin God brings.** The finding is "the earth was **corrupt** (shachat, 6:11)… all flesh had **corrupted** (shachat, 6:12) their way… I will **destroy** (shachat, 6:13) them… I will bring a flood to **destroy** (shachat, 6:17) all flesh" *(stated)*. The gem is the *one verb pivoting*: man's self-**corruption** (6:11–12) *becomes the ground of* God's **destruction** (6:13, 6:17) — the ruin is *returned in the same word*; the punishment *mirrors the crime* lexically. The interior datum: what man does to the earth (ruins it) God does back (ruins them) — judgment as the corruption reflected *(inferred: distinct poles of the same shachat — man's ruining (6:11–12), then God's (6:13, 6:17); do not lump — the pivot is the finding)*.
- **basar (flesh) — the corrupt totality to be ended (6:12, 6:13, 6:17, 6:19; 7:15, 7:16, 7:21).** The finding is "all **flesh** (basar) had corrupted its way (6:12–13)… all flesh in which is the breath of life (6:17)… all flesh… the breath of life (7:15)… everything on the dry land… **flesh** died (7:21)" *(stated)*. The operation is basar in yet another sense — **the whole living order *as corrupt and mortal*, the totality under judgment**; distinct from GEN-03's material/union senses and 6:3's frail-flesh — here basar = *all-that-lives, corrupted and then perishing* (the shut-in flesh of 7:16, the dying flesh of 7:21) *(inferred)*.
- **qomah (height) — the ark's dimension (6:15), *no inner-being operation*.** Flagged: the ark's **height** (qomah) is a construction measurement; it carries *no* inner-being operation — noted only so the span is not silently passed, not read for meaning it does not hold *(honest null)*.
- **berit (covenant) — the first covenant, amid judgment (6:18).** The finding is "I will establish my **covenant** (berit) with you" *(stated)*. The operation is the **first "covenant" in Scripture** — God *binding himself* to Noah *in the midst of* the destroying judgment; the interior note is that God's relation now takes the form of a *pledge* that preserves *(inferred)*.
- **chayah (keep alive) + chay-cluster (6:19, 6:20; 7:3, 7:14) — preservation of life through the ark.** The finding is "of all flesh… bring two of every sort… to keep them **alive** (chayah, 6:19); of the birds, the animals, the creeping things… two… to keep them **alive** (chayah, 6:20)… to keep offspring **alive** (7:3); every **beast** (chayyah, 7:14) after its kind entered" *(stated)*. The operation is *life preserved* through the judgment — the living cluster (chay/chayah) carried across the flood, paired and sheltered; the same preserving act stated over each order of creature (6:19 all flesh; 6:20 birds/animals/creepers; 7:14 the beasts entering) *(inferred)*.
- **tsaddiq (righteous) #2 — God *confirms* Noah's righteousness (7:1).** The finding is "for you I have **seen** as **righteous** (tsaddiq) before me in this generation" *(stated)*. The operation is the *divine confirmation* of 6:9 — God *himself attests* the interior the narrator named; distinct from 6:9 (narration → divine testimony) *(inferred)*.
- **tahor (clean) ×2 — the first purity-distinction (7:2, 7:8).** The finding is "seven pairs of the **clean** (tahor, 7:2) animals… and a pair of the animals that are not clean… of **clean** (tahor, 7:8) animals and of animals that are not clean… two and two went in" *(stated)*. The operation is the **first "clean/pure" (tahor) in Scripture** — a *distinction of purity* introduced for the ark; stated in the command (7:2) and again in its fulfilment (7:8); the interior world now carries a clean/unclean line *(inferred)*.
- **chayyim (life) — a *dating* use, an honest near-null (7:11).** Flagged: "in the six hundredth year of Noah's **life** (chayyim), the fountains of the great deep burst forth" (7:11) — here chayyim is a *chronological marker* ("year of Noah's life"), carrying no distinct inner-being operation; noted so the span is not silently passed *(honest null: life-as-lifespan, the flood's onset dated)*.
- **neshamah (breath) — the creation-breath *undone* (7:22).** The finding is "all in whose nostrils was the **breath** (neshamah) of life… died" *(stated)*. The gem is that *neshamah* is the **same breath God breathed into man's nostrils** at 2:7 (GEN-03, "breathed into his nostrils the neshamah of life") — now *that very breath* is what perishes: the flood *undoes* the creation-breath, "all in whose nostrils was the neshamah" dies *(inferred: creation's animating breath withdrawn in judgment — a deliberate reversal of 2:7)*.
- **ravah (increased) ×2 — the multiply-word swelling the *destroying* waters (7:17,18).** The finding is "the waters **increased** (ravah)… **increased** greatly" *(stated)*. The gem is *ravah*'s **third inversion**: the blessing-increase (1:28, "be fruitful and **multiply**") became the curse's pain-increase (3:16, "**multiply** your pain"), and now swells the *destroying flood* (7:17–18) — the same increase-verb turned to *amplify judgment* *(inferred: the inversion pattern again — ravah from blessing to pain to flood)*.
- **rum (rose high) — the waters *exalted* (7:17).** The finding is the waters "**rose high** (rum)… lifted up the ark" *(stated)*. The operation is rum (be-lifted/exalted) applied to the *judging waters* — exaltation turned to the flood's rising *(inferred)*.
- **gavar (prevailed) ×4 + gavoah (high) — the relentless might of the waters (7:18, 7:19, 7:20, 7:24).** The finding is the waters "**prevailed** (gavar, 7:18)… **prevailed** so mightily that all the **high** (gavoah, 7:19) mountains were covered… **prevailed** fifteen cubits above (gavar, 7:20)… **prevailed** on the earth 150 days (gavar, 7:24)" *(stated)*. The operation is *gavar* (be-mighty/prevail) **four times** — the *overwhelming, relentless might* of the flood, rising over the ark (7:18), topping even the *high* mountains (7:19–20), and *holding* its dominion 150 days (7:24); a drumbeat of prevailing power (as the death-formula tolled mortality, so this tolls the waters' dominion) *(inferred: the four gavar mark a rising then sustained might — not one act repeated but a mounting and holding)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **the shachat mirror (6:11–13):** man's ruin returns as God's ruin — the corruption of the earth answered by the destruction of the corrupters, one verb pivoting from crime to judgment.
2. **the righteous one amid the ruin (6:9; 7:1):** Noah's tsaddiq/tamim (and God's confirming tsaddiq, 7:1) stand against "all flesh corrupted" — the single righteous interior (with chen, 6:8; like Enoch's walk, 5:24) preserved through the judgment.
3. **berit + preserved life (6:18–20):** the first covenant *binds God to preserve* — judgment and pledge together; life (chay/chayah) carried across in the ark.
4. **the creation-breath undone (neshamah, 7:22 ← 2:7):** what God breathed into man's nostrils is what the flood takes — creation reversed at the point of its most intimate gift.
5. **ravah / rum / gavar — the inverted and relentless waters (7:17–24):** the multiply-word (ravah) now swells the flood, the waters *exalted* (rum) and *prevailing* (gavar ×4) — increase, exaltation, and might all turned to judgment; the blessing-vocabulary of creation inverted into the instruments of un-creation.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** tsaddiq + tamim as Noah's *rightness and wholeness* (first "righteous"); the **shachat mirror** (man corrupts → God destroys, one verb); basar as *the corrupt mortal totality*; qomah as an *honest null* (no IB operation); **berit** — the *first covenant*, amid judgment; the preserved chay/chayah; tsaddiq #2 as *divine confirmation*; **tahor** — the *first purity-distinction*; **neshamah** *undone* (← 2:7); **ravah**'s *third inversion* (blessing→pain→flood); rum (waters exalted); **gavar ×4** (the relentless prevailing).
- **Whose interior:** the **human** interior at its two poles — the *corrupt totality* (all flesh) and the *one righteous, blameless* man; with the **divine** interior acting in grief-grounded judgment (GEN-09) yet *binding itself in covenant* to preserve.
- **Threads:** tsaddiq/tamim (← 5:24 Enoch, 6:8 chen; → 7:1); shachat (man→God mirror); berit (first here → the rainbow covenant, GEN-12 9:9–17); neshamah (← 2:7, now undone); ravah (← 1:28/3:16, third inversion); chen/righteousness carried from GEN-09.
- **Guarded against:** lumping the four shachat (the man→God pivot is the gem); flattening basar's several senses (material/union GEN-03; frail 6:3; corrupt-totality here); reading meaning into qomah (an honest null); merging the two tsaddiq (narration vs divine testimony); missing that neshamah *reverses* 2:7; treating ravah here as unrelated to its blessing/curse uses.

## JOS-02-sold-jacobs-grief · Genesis 37:12–36 · [the dream attacked; the father deceived and grieving] — the murder-plot, the sale, and the deceiver deceived by a goat

**The passage.** "See if it is **well** (shalom) with your brothers" (37:14); a man **asked** (shaal), "what are you **seeking** (baqash)?"; "I am **seeking** (baqash) my brothers" (37:15–16); "I **heard** (shama) them say, Dothan" (37:17); the plot — "let us kill him… a **fierce** (ra) **animal** (chayyah) devoured him… see what becomes of his dreams" (37:20); Reuben **heard** (shama) — "let us not take his **life** (nephesh)" (37:21); Judah — "he is our **flesh** (basar)… his brothers **listened** (shama)" (37:27); sold to Egypt; the bloodied robe; "a **fierce** (ra) **animal** (chayyah) has devoured him" (37:33); Jacob **mourned** (aval, 37:34), refused **comfort** (nacham), "I shall go down… **mourning** (avel)… he **wept** (bakhah)" (37:35).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **shalom (well) — the *errand of peace* into hatred (37:14).** "see if it is **well** (shalom) with your brothers, and bring me word" *(stated)* — shalom, *well-being/peace*: the gem is the *irony* — Joseph is sent to check his brothers' *shalom*, and walks into their *murderous hatred* (the brothers who *could not speak peaceably*, 37:4). The errand *of peace* meets the *absence of peace* *(inferred: shalom = the peace-errand walking into hatred)*.
- **shaal (asked) + baqash (seeking) — the *seeking* that leads to the pit (37:15).** "a man **asked** (shaal): 'what are you **seeking** (baqash)?'" *(stated)*. **shaal** — the stranger's *question*; **baqash** — *seek*: Joseph *seeking his brothers* — the gem is the *unwitting* seeking that *walks him toward the trap* *(inferred: shaal/baqash = the seeking that leads Joseph to his attackers)*.
- **baqash (seeking) — the *object named* (37:16).** *baqash distinct from 37:15:* "'I am **seeking** (baqash) my brothers'" *(stated)* — Joseph *names his errand* — *his brothers* — the very ones plotting his death; the interior *earnestly seeking those who hate him* *(inferred: baqash = the brothers named as the sought)*.
- **shama (heard) — the *informant's word* (37:17).** "'I **heard** (shama) them say, let us go to Dothan'" *(stated)* — shama, *hear*: the stranger's *overheard direction* *sends Joseph on* to Dothan — the providence-thread (a chance word steering the story) *(inferred: shama = the overheard word that steers Joseph on)*.
- **chayyah (animal) + ra (fierce) — the *cover-lie planned* (37:20).** "'let us kill him… a **fierce** (ra) **animal** (chayyah) has devoured him… see what becomes of his dreams'" *(stated)*. **chayyah** — *living thing/beast*; **ra** — *evil/fierce*: the gem is the *lie prepared in advance* — a *wild beast* to be blamed — *and* the motive: *"see what becomes of his dreams"* — **killing the dreamer to kill the dream**. The interior's murderous scheme *aimed at the dream itself* *(inferred: chayyah/ra = the beast-lie planned to cover the murder — the dream attacked)*.
- **shama (heard) + nephesh (life) — Reuben's *restraint* (37:21).** *shama distinct from 37:17:* "when Reuben **heard** (shama) it, he rescued him: 'let us not take his **life** (nephesh)'" *(stated)*. **shama** — Reuben *hears the plot* and it *moves him to intervene*; **nephesh** — *life*: the gem is the *partial restraint* — Reuben *stops the killing* (throw him in the pit, meaning to rescue him later). The interior of a *conscience-flicker* amid the hatred *(inferred: shama/nephesh = Reuben's hearing that restrains the killing)*.
- **basar (flesh) + shama (listened) — Judah's *mixed motive* (37:27).** *shama distinct from 37:17,21:* "'let us sell him… he is our brother, our **flesh** (basar)'… his brothers **listened** (shama)" *(stated)*. **basar** — *flesh/kin*: the gem is the *mixed motive* — Judah's *"he is our flesh"* (a kinship-flicker) *fused with* "what profit is it?" (37:26, gain) — *sell* rather than kill; **shama** — the brothers *heed Judah*. The interior *sparing the brother for profit-and-kinship both* (a lesser evil, not repentance) *(inferred: basar/shama = the kinship-and-profit motive that sells rather than kills)*.
- **chayyah (animal) + ra (fierce) — the *lie believed* (37:33).** *chayyah/ra distinct from 37:20:* "'a **fierce** (ra) **animal** (chayyah) has devoured him; Joseph is torn to pieces'" *(stated)* — where 37:20 *planned* the beast-lie, 37:33 is the *father speaking it as fact* — the deception *works*; the interior *of the deceived father* concluding exactly what the deceivers intended. And the gem-irony: the brothers deceive Jacob with a *goat's blood* (37:31) — *as Jacob deceived Isaac with goat-skins and goat-meat* (Gen 27); **the deceiver deceived by a goat, one generation on** *(inferred: chayyah/ra = the beast-lie believed — the deceiver deceived by a goat)*.
- **aval (mourned) — the *father's mourning* (37:34).** "Jacob tore his garments… and **mourned** (aval) for his son many days" *(stated)* — aval, *mourn*: the interior's *grief enacted* (torn garments, sackcloth) — the father *broken* by the (false) death *(inferred: aval = the father's enacted mourning)*.
- **avel (mourning) + nacham (comfort) + bakhah (wept) — the *grief without comfort* (37:35).** "he refused to be **comforted** (nacham): 'I shall go down to Sheol **mourning** (avel)'… he **wept** (bakhah)" *(stated)*. **nacham** — *comfort* (← Isaac comforted 24:67; here *refused*); **avel** — the *settled mourning* he expects to *die in*; **bakhah** — *weep*. The gem is the *inconsolable* grief — the father *will not be comforted*, expecting to *carry the mourning to the grave*; the interior's *bottomless sorrow* over the (unknowingly false) death *(inferred: avel/nacham/bakhah = the inconsolable grief the father will die in)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **the peace-errand into the trap (shalom 37:14 → shaal/baqash 37:15 → baqash 37:16 → shama 37:17):** Joseph is *sent to check their peace* and *seeks his brothers*, steered on by a *chance word* — walking, unwitting, toward the men who hate him.
2. **the dream attacked, restrained, and sold (chayyah/ra 37:20 → shama/nephesh 37:21 → basar/shama 37:27):** the brothers plot to *kill the dreamer to kill the dream*; Reuben's *conscience restrains* the killing; Judah's *profit-and-kinship* motive *sells* him instead — the hatred *acting*, but *fractured*.
3. **the lie believed, the deceiver deceived (chayyah/ra 37:33):** the *beast-lie* the father *believes* — and by a *goat's blood* (as Jacob himself deceived Isaac): **the deceiver deceived, one generation on.**
4. **the inconsolable grief (aval 37:34 → avel/nacham/bakhah 37:35):** Jacob *mourns, refuses comfort, weeps* — expecting to *go down to Sheol mourning* — the father's *bottomless sorrow* over a death that never happened.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** shalom (the *peace-errand* into hatred); shaal/baqash (the *seeking* toward the trap — baqash ×2: the errand / the brothers named); shama ×3 (the *informant's word* / *Reuben's* restraining hearing / the brothers *heeding Judah*); chayyah/ra ×2 (the beast-lie *planned* 37:20 / *believed* 37:33); nephesh (Reuben's *restraint*); **basar (Judah's *kinship-and-profit* mixed motive — sell not kill)**; aval/avel (the father's *mourning* / the *settled* mourning he'll die in); nacham (comfort *refused*); bakhah (the father's *weeping*).
- **Whose interior (multi-interior):** the **brothers** — murderous hatred *acting*, *fractured* by Reuben's conscience and Judah's profit-and-kinship, and *deceiving* the father; **Joseph** — *obedient* ("Here I am") and *silent* (his distress voiced only later, 42:21); **Jacob** — *inconsolable grief*, refusing comfort. The dream is *attacked* ("see what becomes of his dreams").
- **Threads:** **the deceiver deceived** (the *goat-blood* deception 37:31 ← Jacob's own goat-deception of Isaac, Gen 27:16 — the family sin returns on him; and "**identify** (haker-na)" 37:32 → the *same word* turns on Judah with Tamar, 38:25); the *dream attacked* (37:20 → *fulfilled* when they bow, 42:6; JOS-08); *divided love's* fruit (← JOS-01, 37:3); the *inconsolable grief* (nacham refused 37:35 → the *comfort* that finally comes when Joseph is found, 45:27); Judah's *mixed motive* (→ his *transformation*, the substitute JOS-10); the *goat* (→ Tamar's kid, 38:17).
- **Guarded against:** lumping the two chayyah/ra (planned lie / believed lie) or three shama (informant / Reuben / brothers-heed); reading Judah's "he is our flesh" (37:27) as compassion when it is *fused with profit* ("what profit?", 37:26) — a *mixed* motive, not repentance; missing the **goat-deception irony** (the deceiver deceived, ← Gen 27); over-reading Joseph's silence as either innocence or guilt (the text gives *obedience* and *silence*, his interior withheld until 42:21); treating Jacob's refused comfort as excessive when the text presents it as *the father's bottomless, dying grief*.

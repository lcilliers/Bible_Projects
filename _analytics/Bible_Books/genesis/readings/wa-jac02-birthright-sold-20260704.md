## JAC-02-birthright-sold · Genesis 25:29–34 · [the grasp met by contempt] — the boiling stew, the birthright bought, and "Esau despised his birthright"

**The passage.** "Once when Jacob was **cooking** (zid) stew, Esau came in from the field, and he was **exhausted**" (25:29); "'let me eat some of that red stew, for I am exhausted!' Therefore his name was **called** (qara) Edom" (25:30); "'Sell me your birthright now'… 'I am about to die; of what use is a birthright?'… he swore… and sold his birthright" (25:31–33); "Jacob **gave** (natan) Esau bread and lentil stew… Thus Esau **despised** (bazah) his birthright" (25:34).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **zid (cooking/boiling) — the stew that becomes the *snare* (25:29).** The finding is "Jacob was **cooking** (zid) stew" *(stated)*. The operation is zid — *boil/seethe* — a plain domestic act, but *positioned* as the opportunity: Jacob is *already at the pot* when the famished brother arrives. The interior reads as *opportunistic readiness* — the settled/tent-man (tam, 25:27) *has the thing the field-man now craves*; the boiling stew is the *bait the moment supplies* *(inferred: zid = the cooking that, in context, becomes the leverage)*.
- **qara (called Edom) — the naming off the *craving* (25:30).** The finding is "'let me eat some of that **red** stew…' Therefore his name was **called** (qara) Edom" *(stated)*. The operation is qara — *call/name* — here the name (Edom ≈ red) fixed *off Esau's own greedy cry for the red stuff*; where JAC-01 named him for his *body* (hairy/Esau), here he is *re-named for his appetite* — the interior *defined by what it hungers for*. The naming *records the craving as identity* *(inferred: qara = the name that fixes the appetite; distinct from the birth-namings of JAC-01)*.
- **natan (gave) — the *giving that acquires* (25:34a).** The finding is "Jacob **gave** (natan) Esau bread and lentil stew" *(stated)*. The gem is the *inverted* natan: normally natan is *open-handed provision* (18:7; 24:53), but here Jacob's giving is *the closing of a bargain* — he *gives food to take a birthright*; a natan that **acquires rather than bestows**. The grasping interior's characteristic move: *give the lesser to seize the greater* — the same logic that will steal the blessing (ch 27) *(inferred: natan = the calculated giving that gains; contrast the bestowing natan)*.
- **bazah (despised) — the narrator's *verdict on Esau's interior* (25:34b).** The finding is "Thus Esau **despised** (bazah) his birthright" *(stated)*. The gem is that this is the passage's *one stated inside*: the narrator names Esau's interior operation as **bazah — *to despise / hold in contempt / count as worthless***. The birthright — the *decreed inheritance* — is *scorned* for a bowl; the string of blunt verbs ("ate, drank, rose, went") enacts the contempt. Where Jacob *grasps* (natan-to-acquire), Esau *despises* (bazah) — **two faults, not one**: the grasp and the contempt. The interior that *treats the holy thing as nothing* *(inferred: bazah = the contempt that counts the birthright worthless)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **the moment supplies the bait (zid 25:29 → 25:30 the craving):** Jacob is *at the pot*; Esau arrives *famished and crying for the red*; the settled man holds what the field man now *cannot do without* — opportunity meeting appetite.
2. **appetite becomes name (qara/Edom 25:30):** Esau's *craving cry* is fixed as his *name* — the interior *defined by the hunger it could not master*.
3. **the calculated giving seizes the birthright (natan 25:34a):** Jacob *gives* bread-and-lentils *to take* the birthright — the grasping interior's signature (give-lesser-to-gain-greater), a rehearsal of the blessing-theft.
4. **the two faults named (bazah 25:34b):** the narrator's verdict falls on *Esau* — he **despised** the birthright — so the scene indicts *both* interiors: Jacob's *grasp* and Esau's *contempt*; the birthright *changes hands* because one *seizes* and the other *scorns*.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** zid (the *boiling stew* that, in context, becomes the leverage); qara (Esau re-named *off his appetite* — Edom/red — distinct from the birth-namings of JAC-01); natan (the *calculated giving that acquires*, inverting the bestowing natan); bazah (the narrator's stated verdict — Esau **despised** the birthright, the *contempt* fault set beside Jacob's *grasp*).
- **Whose interior:** the **human** interior *grasping/calculating* (Jacob) and *craving-then-despising* (Esau); no divine operation in view — the decreed inversion of JAC-01 now *worked out through two human faults*.
- **Threads:** the grasp (← Jacob "heel-holder" 25:26; → the blessing stolen JAC-05, the natan-to-acquire recurring); the despised birthright (→ the blessing Esau will *weep* for, 27:34,38 — too late; the interior that scorns then mourns); Edom/red (← the birth 25:25; → Esau's line JAC-19); the inverted-order oracle (25:23) *executed* here by sale.
- **Guarded against:** excusing Jacob because Esau "despised" it (the verse indicts *both* — I kept the grasp and the contempt as two distinct faults); reading Hebrews 12:16 ("Esau… sold his birthright") *back* as the verdict when the text's own verdict-word is *bazah* right here; over-reading zid into the homonym "act presumptuously" (the gloss and scene are *boil/cook* — the presumption is *inferred from the bargain*, not claimed off the word); flattening natan into simple generosity when the giving *acquires*.

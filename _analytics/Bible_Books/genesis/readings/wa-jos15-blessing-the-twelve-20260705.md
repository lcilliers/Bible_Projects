# Genesis 49:1–28 — inner-being passage reading (JOS-15-blessing-the-twelve)

- **Source:** verse-first lexicals over the passage (provenance `genesis-joseph-v1-20260705`). Every non-T2 span in its verse; *stated* | *inferred*; distinctions kept. **Multiple interiors**: the deathbed oracles read each son's *character and its consequence*. **The deferred reckonings** (Reuben's Bilhah-sin, 35:22; Simeon & Levi's Shechem massacre, 34) — the *silences of JAC-17/18* — **land here**. Much of the passage is tribal-poetic imagery (lion, donkey, serpent, wolf) — read as *character*, not forced into faculties.
- **Check-back:** gate run post-draft; every non-T2 verse/gloss engaged; the 2 qara (49:1), 2 ezuz, 3 berakhah, 2 natan each treated distinctly.

## JOS-15-blessing-the-twelve · Genesis 49:1–28 · [the reckonings land; Judah's ascendancy; Joseph unmoved] — the firstborn forfeit, the cursed anger, the scepter, and "I wait for your salvation"

**The passage.** Jacob **called** (qara) his sons — what shall **happen** (qara) in days to come (49:1); "**listen** (sha ma), O sons" (49:2); Reuben — "my might (on), **power** (ezuz), **might** (koach)… **unstable** (pachaz) as water… you shall not have preeminence" (49:3–4); Simeon & Levi — "let my **soul** (nephesh) not come into their **council** (sod), my **glory** (kavod)… in their **willfulness** (ratson)… **cursed** (arar) be their anger, for it is **fierce** (ezuz); I will scatter them" (49:6–7); Judah — "your brothers shall **praise** (yadah) you… your **enemies** (oyev)… the scepter shall not **depart** (sur)… the **obedience** (yiqqehah) of the peoples" (49:8–10); Issachar — "he saw the **resting place** (menuchah) was **good** (tov), the land **pleasant** (naem)… became a **servant** (avad) at forced labor" (49:15); Dan — "shall **judge** (din)" (49:16); "I **wait** (qavah) for your **salvation** (yeshuah), O LORD" (49:18); Asher's food **rich** (shamen), he **yields** (natan) delicacies (49:20); Naphtali **bears** (natan) fawns (49:21); Joseph — "the archers **attacked** (marar), **hated** (satam) him, yet his bow **unmoved** (etan)… **blessings** (berakhah) of the Almighty… on the head of Joseph" (49:23–26); "he **blessed** (berakhah) each" (49:28).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **qara (called) + qara (happen) — the *summons to the oracles* (49:1).** "Jacob **called** (qara) his sons: 'what shall **happen** (qara) in days to come'" *(stated)*. **qara** (call-to) — the *gathering*; **qara** (befall) — *what will happen*: the interior-context of *the dying father assembling the sons for their destinies* *(inferred: qara/qara = the sons summoned to hear what will befall them)*.
- **shama (listen) — the *charge to hear* (49:2).** "'**listen** (shama), O sons of Jacob, listen to Israel your father'" *(stated)* — shama, *hear*: the *doubled charge to heed* — the interior *demanding attention for the weighty word* *(inferred: shama = the charge to hear the oracles)*.
- **on (might) + ezuz (power) + koach (might) — Reuben the *firstborn's dignity* (49:3).** "'Reuben, my firstborn, my might (on), **power** (ezuz), and **might** (koach)… preeminent'" *(stated)*. **on/ezuz/koach** — *strength/vigour*: the gem is the *firstborn's natural dignity* named — *the firstfruits of Jacob's strength* — before it is *forfeited*; the interior-context of *high standing about to fall* *(inferred: on/ezuz/koach = the firstborn's dignity, named before its forfeit)*.
- **pachaz (unstable) — Reuben's *reckless forfeit* (49:4).** "'**unstable** (pachaz) as water, you shall not have preeminence, because you defiled my bed'" *(stated)* — the gem: **pachaz** — *reckless/wanton/unstable*: the **deferred reckoning lands** — Reuben's *sin with Bilhah* (35:22, which Jacob *heard in silence*, JAC-18) *now named*: the firstborn *forfeits his preeminence* through his *reckless wantonness*. The interior's *instability* costs him the birthright — the silence *broken* at last *(inferred: pachaz = Reuben's reckless instability — the firstborn's forfeit, the deferred reckoning landing)*.
- **nephesh (soul) + sod (council) + kavod (glory) + ratson (willfulness) — Simeon & Levi *disowned* (49:6).** "'let my **soul** (nephesh) not come into their **council** (sod), my **glory** (kavod) not be joined… in their **willfulness** (ratson) they hamstrung oxen'" *(stated)*. **nephesh/kavod** — Jacob's *very self* refusing their *company*; **sod** — their *counsel/plot*; **ratson** — their *self-will*: the gem is Jacob *dissociating his soul from their violence* — the interior *refusing to be joined to their bloody counsel* *(inferred: nephesh/sod/kavod/ratson = Jacob's soul disowning their violent counsel)*.
- **arar (cursed) + ezuz (fierce) — the *cursed anger* (49:7).** "'**cursed** (arar) be their anger, for it is **fierce** (ezuz)… I will scatter them'" *(stated)*. **arar** — *curse*; **ezuz** *distinct from 49:3* — the *fierceness* now of *wrath*: the gem — the **deferred reckoning lands** on Simeon & Levi: their *Shechem massacre* (34, Jacob's *silence* JAC-17) *now cursed* — *"cursed be their anger"* — and *scattered in Israel*. The interior's *fierce, cruel anger* named and *judged* — the sin *reckoned* at the deathbed *(inferred: arar/ezuz = the cursed, fierce anger — the Shechem reckoning landing)*.
- **yadah (praise) + oyev (enemies) — Judah's *ascendancy* (49:8).** "'Judah, your brothers shall **praise** (yadah) you; your hand on your **enemies'** (oyev) neck; your father's sons shall bow'" *(stated)*. **yadah** — *praise* (Judah's name-meaning); **oyev** — *enemies*: the gem is the *reversal of rank* — the *fourth son* is *praised and bowed to* by his brothers; **Judah's transformation rewarded** (the surety/substitute, JOS-10 → the *leadership*) — the interior *raised for its proven character* *(inferred: yadah/oyev = Judah's ascendancy — the transformed son praised and followed)*.
- **yiqqehah (obedience) + sur (depart) — the *scepter that stays* (49:10).** "'the scepter shall not **depart** (sur) from Judah… the **obedience** (yiqqehah) of the peoples'" *(stated)*. **sur** — *depart* (the scepter *not leaving* Judah); **yiqqehah** — *obedience*: the gem — the *enduring rule* granted to Judah (the kingship-line); the interior-context of *a lasting dominion* for the transformed son *(inferred: yiqqehah/sur = the scepter that does not depart — Judah's enduring rule)*.
- **menuchah (resting) + tov (good) + naem (pleasant) + avad (servant) — Issachar's *comfort-servitude* (49:15).** "'he saw the **resting place** (menuchah) was **good** (tov), the land **pleasant** (naem), so he… became a **servant** (avad) at forced labor'" *(stated)*. **menuchah** — *rest*; **tov/naem** — *good/pleasant*; **avad** — *serve*: the gem is the *trade* — Issachar *values ease so much he bends to servitude* to keep it — the interior *choosing comfort over freedom* (rest bought at the price of bondage) *(inferred: menuchah/tov/naem/avad = the comfort-servitude — ease chosen over freedom)*.
- **din (judge) — Dan's *judging* (49:16).** "'Dan shall **judge** (din) his people'" *(stated)* — din, *judge*: the tribe's *judicial character* (though "a serpent by the path", 49:17) — read as *character* *(inferred: din = Dan's judging character)*.
- **qavah (wait) + yeshuah (salvation) — Jacob's *faith-cry* (49:18).** "'I **wait** (qavah) for your **salvation** (yeshuah), O LORD'" *(stated)*. The gem: **qavah** — *wait/hope*; **yeshuah** — *salvation*: amid the oracles, Jacob *breaks into a cry of faith* — *waiting for God's salvation*; the interior's *hope beyond the tribes' destinies* — the dying man *looking to God's deliverance* (the first "salvation" in Genesis) *(inferred: qavah/yeshuah = the faith-cry — Jacob waiting for God's salvation)*.
- **shamen (rich) + natan (yield) — Asher's *abundance* (49:20).** "'Asher's food shall be **rich** (shamen); he shall **yield** (natan) royal delicacies'" *(stated)* — **shamen** *rich/fat*; **natan** — *give/yield*: the tribe's *fatness* (abundance) — character *(inferred: shamen/natan = Asher's rich yield)*.
- **natan (bears) — Naphtali's *fruitfulness* (49:21).** *natan distinct from 49:20:* "'Naphtali… **bears** (natan) beautiful fawns'" *(stated)* — natan, *give/bear*: the tribe's *free fruitfulness* (the doe let loose) — character *(inferred: natan = Naphtali's fruitful bearing)*.
- **marar (attacked) + satam (hated) + etan (unmoved) — Joseph *attacked yet steadfast* (49:23–24).** "'the archers **attacked** (marar), **hated** (satam) him… yet his bow remained **unmoved** (etan)'" *(stated)*. **marar** — *provoke/embitter* (← the bitterness-root); **satam** — *hate/harbor a grudge* (← Esau's grudge 27:41; the brothers' hate); **etan** — *strong/enduring*: the gem is **Joseph's steadfastness** — *bitterly attacked and grudge-hated* (by the brothers, Potiphar's wife, all his afflictions) *yet unmoved* (his bow *steady*, upheld by "the Mighty One of Jacob"); the interior *steadfast under a lifetime of attack* *(inferred: marar/satam/etan = Joseph attacked and hated yet unmoved — steadfastness under attack)*.
- **berakhah (blessings) — the *heaped blessing on Joseph* (49:25, 49:26).** "'**blessings** (berakhah) of heaven above… blessings (berakhah) mighty beyond… on the head of Joseph, set apart from his brothers'" *(stated)* — **berakhah** ×2: the gem is the *lavish blessing* on Joseph — *heaven, deep, breasts, womb* — *heaped* beyond the fathers'; the *favoured, set-apart* son *most blessed*; the interior-context of *the reward of steadfastness* *(inferred: berakhah = the heaped blessings on the set-apart Joseph)*.
- **berakhah (blessing) — the *summary* (49:28).** *berakhah distinct from 49:25,26:* "he **blessed** (berakhah) each with the blessing suitable to him" *(stated)* — the gem is the *fittingness* — *each son blessed according to his character*; the interior-context of *a blessing matched to the man* (the oracles *fit* each son's nature) *(inferred: berakhah = each blessed according to his character)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **the reckonings land (qara 49:1 → shama 49:2 → on/ezuz/koach 49:3 → pachaz 49:4 → nephesh/sod/kavod/ratson 49:6 → arar/ezuz 49:7):** the dying father *summons the sons*, and the **deferred reckonings fall** — Reuben's *reckless forfeit* of the firstborn (the Bilhah-sin, JAC-18's silence broken) and Simeon & Levi's *cursed, fierce anger* (the Shechem massacre, JAC-17's silence broken, *scattered*).
2. **Judah's ascendancy (yadah/oyev 49:8 → yiqqehah/sur 49:10):** the *transformed* fourth son *praised, bowed to, given the enduring scepter* — the reward of his proven character.
3. **the tribes' characters and the faith-cry (menuchah/tov/naem/avad 49:15 → din 49:16 → qavah/yeshuah 49:18 → shamen/natan 49:20 → natan 49:21):** Issachar's *comfort-servitude*, Dan's *judging*, and — a break in the oracles — Jacob's *faith-cry*: *"I wait for your salvation, O LORD."*
4. **Joseph unmoved and blessed (marar/satam/etan 49:23–24 → berakhah 49:25–26 → berakhah 49:28):** Joseph *bitterly attacked and grudge-hated yet unmoved*, *heaped with blessings* as the *set-apart* son; and *each son blessed according to his character*.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** qara ×2 (the *summons* / what will *befall*); shama (the *charge to hear*); on/ezuz/koach (Reuben's *firstborn dignity*, named before its forfeit); **pachaz (Reuben's *reckless forfeit* — the Bilhah-reckoning landing)**; nephesh/sod/kavod/ratson (Jacob's *soul disowning* Simeon & Levi's violent counsel); **arar/ezuz (the *cursed, fierce anger* — the Shechem-reckoning landing, scattered)**; **yadah/oyev + yiqqehah/sur (Judah's *ascendancy* — praised, the enduring scepter — the transformation rewarded)**; menuchah/tov/naem/avad (Issachar's *comfort-servitude*); din (Dan's *judging*); **qavah/yeshuah (Jacob's *faith-cry* — "I wait for your salvation")**; shamen/natan ×2 (Asher's / Naphtali's *fruitfulness*); **marar/satam/etan (Joseph *attacked and hated yet unmoved* — steadfastness)**; berakhah ×3 (the *heaped blessings* on Joseph / the *fitting* blessing on each).
- **Whose interior (multi-interior):** the *twelve sons' characters* named — Reuben *reckless*, Simeon & Levi *fiercely angry* (both *reckoned*), Judah *ascendant* (transformed), Issachar *comfort-bound*, Joseph *steadfast* (attacked yet unmoved); and **Jacob** — *disowning* the violent, *rewarding* the transformed, and *waiting for God's salvation* (the dying man's faith). The blessing *fits* each character.
- **Threads:** **the deferred reckonings LAND** (pachaz 49:4 ← Reuben/Bilhah 35:22, JAC-18's silence; arar 49:7 ← Simeon & Levi/Shechem 34, JAC-17's silence — the *silences broken* at the deathbed, exactly as flagged); **Judah's transformation rewarded** (yadah/scepter 49:8–10 ← the Tamar-turn 38:26; the surety/substitute JOS-09/10 — the arc's *crown*); **Joseph's steadfastness** (etan 49:24 ← attacked by brothers 37, Potiphar's wife 39, prison 40 — "unmoved", upheld by God); the **faith-cry** (qavah/yeshuah 49:18 — the first "salvation", Jacob's hope beyond the tribes); the *comfort-vs-freedom* (avad 49:15 ← the transfer-of-service theme → Exodus); the *heaped blessing* on the set-apart Joseph (berakhah 49:26 ← 48:22).
- **Guarded against:** over-reading the tribal-poetic imagery (lion, donkey, serpent, doe, wolf) into faculties — read as *character*, not forced operations; lumping the two qara/ezuz/natan or three berakhah; missing that the **reckonings deliberately land** the JAC-17/18 silences (the deathbed *speaks* what Jacob once left unsaid); reading Judah's ascendancy as arbitrary when it *rewards his transformation* (the arc from seller → surety → substitute → ruler); over-reading the messianic Judah-scepter (49:10 "until Shiloh/tribute comes") over the *ascendancy* the oracle gives; treating Jacob's "I wait for your salvation" (49:18) as a random interjection when it is the *dying man's faith* breaking through the tribal destinies.

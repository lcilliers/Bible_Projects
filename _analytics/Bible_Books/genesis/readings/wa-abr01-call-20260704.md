## ABR-01-call · Genesis 12:1–9 · [the called and blessed interior] — the name God makes great (against Babel's self-made name), blessed to be a blessing, answering with going and worship

**The passage.** "**Go** from your country… to the land that I will show you" (12:1); "I will make of you a great nation, and I will **bless** (barak) you and make your **name great** (gadal), so that you will be a **blessing** (berakhah)" (12:2); "I will **bless** (barak) those who bless you, and him who dishonors you I will **curse** (arar)… in you all the families of the earth shall be blessed" (12:3); "so Abram **went**" (12:4); "the **people** (nephesh) they had acquired" (12:5); "to your offspring I will **give** (natan) this land… he built an altar" (12:7); "he built an altar… and **called upon** (qara) the name of the LORD" (12:8).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **barak (bless) #1 — the elective blessing on *one man* (12:2).** The finding is "I will **bless** (barak) you" *(stated)*. The operation is the blessing-word now *focused on a single man* — where barak fell on the creatures (1:22), on humankind (1:28), and on Noah (9:1), it now *elects* Abram; the blessing narrows to one, through whom it will widen *(inferred)*.
- **gadal (make great) — the name God *makes*, against Babel's self-made name (12:2).** The finding is "I will make your **name great** (gadal)" *(stated)*. The gem is the deliberate counter to Babel (11:4, GEN-14, "let us make a **name** for ourselves"): there the interior *grasped* to make its own name and was scattered; here God *makes* Abram's name great. The operation is **the name *given* vs the name *grasped*** — greatness received from God, not seized by self *(inferred: the whole movement from Babel to Abram turns on this — the self-securing name unmade (11:9), the God-given name magnified (12:2))*.
- **berakhah (blessing) — blessed *to be* a blessing, a conduit (12:2).** The finding is "so that you will be a **blessing** (berakhah)" *(stated)*. The operation is the blessing made *outflowing* — Abram is not the *terminus* of blessing but its *channel* ("in you all families blessed", 12:3); distinct from barak (the act) — berakhah names Abram's *becoming* a blessing to others *(inferred)*.
- **barak (bless) #2 + arar (curse) — the covenantal reciprocity (12:3).** The finding is "I will **bless** (barak) those who bless you, and him who dishonors you I will **curse** (arar)" *(stated)*. The operations: **barak #2** — the blessing now *extends through relationship* to Abram (those who bless him are blessed); **arar** — the curse-word (← the primeval judgments; the human curse 9:25) now *protective/covenantal*: God curses those who curse Abram. Distinct from the primeval arar (judgment on serpent/ground/Cain) — here arar *guards* the elect *(inferred: bless and curse now hinge on how others treat the called man)*.
- **nephesh (persons) — souls acquired, a distinct sub-sense (12:5).** The finding is "the **people** (nephesh) that they had acquired in Haran" *(stated)*. The operation is a *further* nephesh-sense (H5315J, "soul: person") — nephesh as *individual persons/lives*, the household of souls Abram gathers; distinct from nephesh-as-animal-life (1:20), breath-of-life (2:7), life-in-the-blood (9:4) *(inferred: keep the sub-senses apart — here nephesh = persons)*.
- **natan (give) — the promissory *land-gift* (12:7).** The finding is "to your offspring I will **give** (natan) this land" *(stated)*. The operation is a *new* natan-sense: the **covenantal gift of the land** — a *promise* of gift to the offspring; distinct from the natan-arc of the primeval block (food-provision 1:29 → transmission 3:6 → blame 3:12 → withheld-yield 4:12 → delivered-food 9:3). Here natan is *promissory and land-bound* — the gift that grounds the future *(inferred)*.
- **qara (call upon) — the response of worship (12:8).** The finding is "he built an altar… and **called upon** (qara) the name of the LORD" *(stated)*. The operation is *invocation/worship* — the *same* "call upon the name of the LORD" that began worship in the Sethite line (4:26, GEN-07): Abram, called and blessed, *answers by calling on the Name*; the God-ward direction of qara (vs the self-ward city-naming 4:17) is Abram's response *(inferred)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **the name given vs grasped (12:2 ← 11:4):** the Abraham cycle *opens* on the reversal that *closed* the primeval block — Babel's self-made name scattered (11:9), Abram's name *made great* by God (12:2). The interior's greatness is now *received*, and this is the hinge from the primeval history into the patriarchal.
2. **blessed to bless (barak/berakhah, 12:2–3):** the blessing narrows to one man *in order to* widen to "all families" — the operation is *elective for the sake of universal*; Abram is chosen as a *conduit*, not a terminus.
3. **bless/curse reciprocity (12:3):** God's blessing and cursing now attach to how others *treat* the called man — the interior's standing before God is bound up with Abram.
4. **the called interior *answers* (12:4, 12:8):** the call ("Go") is met by *going* ("so Abram went", the obedient departure — context) and by *worship* (qara, calling on the Name) — the response of the elect interior is obedience *and* invocation; the land-gift (natan, 12:7) is received with an altar.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** barak as *elective* blessing on one man (12:2) vs barak *extended through relationship* (12:3); **gadal — the name God *makes great*, against Babel's self-made name** (the primeval→patriarchal hinge); berakhah — Abram *as a conduit* of blessing; arar as *protective/covenantal* curse (vs primeval judgment-curse); nephesh as *persons/souls acquired* (distinct sub-sense); natan as the *promissory land-gift* (new sense); qara as *worship/invocation* (← 4:26, God-ward).
- **Whose interior:** the **human** interior *called, elected, blessed, and made a blessing* — answering with obedient going and worship; and the **divine** interior *electing and pledging* (blessing, giving, guarding).
- **Threads:** gadal/name (← 11:4 Babel, reversed; the name given); barak/berakhah (← 1:28/9:1, now elective-and-outflowing); qara-worship (← 4:26); natan (← the primeval natan-arc, now promissory land-gift); the obedient going (→ the cycle's testings of that obedience, esp. ABR-14 the Aqedah).
- **Guarded against:** merging the two barak (elective vs relational-extension); missing the gadal↔Babel reversal (the deliberate counter); flattening nephesh's "persons" sense into its life-senses; treating natan generically rather than as the land-promise; importing later covenant theology beyond what 12:2–3,7 state.

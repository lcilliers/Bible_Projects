## ABR-17-abraham-death · Genesis 25:1–11 · [the full death, the blessing passed] — the inheritance to the heir, the good old age fulfilled, and the promise carried on

**The passage.** "Abraham **gave** (natan) all he had to Isaac" (25:5); "to the sons of his concubines Abraham **gave** (natan) gifts, and while still **living** (chay) sent them away… eastward" (25:6); "the days of the years of Abraham's **life** (chayyim), 175 years, which he **lived** (chayah)" (25:7); "Abraham breathed his last and died in a **good** (tov) old age… and was gathered to his people" (25:8); "after Abraham's death, God **blessed** (barak) Isaac" (25:11).

### The operations, span by span (individual)

- **natan (give) #1 — *all* to the heir (25:5).** The finding is "Abraham **gave** (natan) all he had to Isaac" *(stated)*. The operation is the *inheritance* concentrated on the *covenant-heir* — the promised line secured in Isaac; the interior *settling its house* toward the promise *(inferred)*.
- **natan (give) #2 + chay — *gifts* to the rest, sent away (25:6).** The finding is "to the sons of his concubines Abraham **gave** (natan) **gifts**, and while still **living** (chay) sent them away… eastward, away from Isaac" *(stated)*. The gem is the *distinction*: Isaac receives *all* (the inheritance, 25:5); the others receive *gifts* and are *sent away* — the covenant-line *kept single*, the rest *provided for but separated*; and Abraham does this *while still living* (chay) — ordering his house *before* death *(inferred: natan #1 the inheritance vs natan #2 the parting-gifts; the line preserved)*.
- **chayyim + chayah — the *honoured* lifespan (25:7).** The finding is "the days of the years of Abraham's **life** (chayyim), 175 years, which he **lived** (chayah)" *(stated)*. The operation is the death-formula (← GEN-08) *dignifying* Abraham's life — the full count of his years *(inferred)*.
- **tov (good) — the *good, full, gathered* death (25:8).** The finding is "Abraham breathed his last and died in a **good** (tov) old age, an old man and full of years, and was **gathered to his people**" *(stated)*. The gem is *tov* naming the **fulfilled death**: a *good* old age, *full* of years, *gathered* — the exact promise of **15:15** ("you shall go to your fathers in *peace*, buried in a *good old age*") **kept**; the man of faith's life *completed and satisfied*. And "*gathered to his people*" (no span) hints at *continuity* — death as a *gathering*, not mere ceasing *(inferred: tov = the goodness of the completed life; the promised peaceful death fulfilled)*.
- **barak (blessed) — the blessing *passed to the heir* (25:11).** The finding is "after the death of Abraham, God **blessed** (barak) Isaac his son" *(stated)*. The gem is the *transmission*: the blessing (← 12:2) *does not die with Abraham* — God *blesses Isaac*, the covenant *continues* beyond the patriarch's death; and Isaac settles at *Beer-lahai-roi* (Hagar's well, El-Roi, 16:14) — a quiet echo of the God who sees *(inferred: barak passes to Isaac — the blessing outlives the man)*.

### The web (interactive)

1. **the house ordered toward the promise (natan 25:5–6):** Abraham *gives all* to Isaac and *gifts-and-sends* the rest — the covenant-line *kept single* and secure, the interior *settling its affairs* while still living.
2. **the fulfilled death (chayyim/chayah/tov 25:7–8):** Abraham dies *good, full, and gathered* — the promise of a *peaceful, good old age* (15:15) *kept*; the life of faith *completed and satisfied*, with a hint of *gathering* beyond death.
3. **the blessing outlives the man (barak 25:11):** God *blesses Isaac* — the blessing *transmitted*, the covenant *carried on*; the patriarch dies but the promise does not.

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced (kept distinct):** natan — *all* to the heir (25:5) vs *gifts* to the rest, sent away (25:6); chayyim/chayah — the *honoured* lifespan; **tov — the *good, full, gathered* death** (the promise of 15:15 fulfilled); **barak — the blessing *passed to Isaac*** (the covenant outliving the man).
- **Whose interior:** the **human** interior *completing* its life in faith — ordering its house, dying *good and full and gathered*; and the **divine** interior *transmitting the blessing* to the next generation.
- **Threads:** tov-good-death (← 15:15 the promised peaceful old age, now *kept*); the death-formula (← GEN-08, now over Abraham); barak (← 12:2, now *passed to Isaac*); Beer-lahai-roi / El-Roi (← 16:14); "gathered to his people" (the continuity-hint → the patriarchs' gathering).
- **Guarded against:** merging the two natan (inheritance vs parting-gifts — the line-preservation is the point); flattening tov here into generic "good" (it names the *fulfilled, satisfied* death, keeping 15:15); over-reading "gathered to his people" (a *hint* of continuity, not a doctrine — read what the verse gives); importing later resurrection theology.

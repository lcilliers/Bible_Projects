

===== Gen 1:26-2:3 - image, dominion, the two blessings, the first holy (GEN-02) =====
SURFACED: image (tselem) as the human's constituting ground; dominion (radah) as *representative* rule, distinct from the lights' mashal; the increase-blessing (barak #1) vs the **cessation-blessing** (barak #2, opposite content, on a *day*); provision (natan) as pure gift (held apart from the fall's later natan); nephesh as "breath of life" (a distinct sub-sense); "very good" as the *only* superlative and totalising appraisal (not a seventh "good"); qadash — the **first holiness, applied to time/rest**.


===== Gen 1:1-25 - creation & the divine good (GEN-01) =====
SURFACED: the evaluative "good" as a discrete appraisal-act; the **day-2 withholding** of that verdict; naming (qara) as ordering/claiming; delegated rule (mashal) built into the design; the **nephesh/chay life-vocabulary first given to animals**; blessing (barak) as empowering-toward-life first given to animals.


===== Gen 2:4-25 - the garden, the two trees, the naming that finds no helper (GEN-03) =====
SURFACED: avad as *absent* worker (2:5) then *given* vocation (2:15); the compound making of man (naphach / neshamah / nephesh chay) with the *same-soul-different-process* gem; chamad as *design-desirability* (thread to 3:6); tov in three senses (food-fitness 2:9 / material gold 2:12 / the **"not good" of solitude** 2:18); daat introduced (2:9) then interdicted-with-death (2:17); nuach/avad/shamar as rest-containing-labour; man's naming as authority-and-self-discovery (2:19–20) vs naming-as-recognition (2:23); basar in three senses (material / shared-substance / union); bosh as the *unashamed baseline* (2:25).


===== Gen 3:1-13 - the fall: crafty bait, desire-stack, knowing inverts, hiding, blame (GEN-04) =====
SURFACED: the tempter as an insider *beast* (chayyah); arum as cunning; God's knowing weaponised (yada 3:5) vs the shame-knowing that inverts it (yada 3:7); the *desire-stack* (taavah/chamad/sakal/chamudah, not one "desire"); natan as transmission (3:6) vs natan as blame-indicting-the-Giver (3:12); shama that flees (3:8) vs shama confessed-and-feared (3:10); qara as *pursuing* call; yare as *dread* (the gloss "revere" refused).


===== Gen 3:14-24 - the sentence, hope within it, the guardian guarded out (GEN-05) =====
SURFACED: arar on the serpent (3:14) vs on the ground-not-the-man (3:17); evah as *God-instituted* enmity; ravah *inverted* to multiply pain (vs the blessing of 1:28); the two childbearing pain-words (itstsavon/etsev) and itstsavon *shared* with the man's toil; teshuqah+mashal (desire-toward/govern-over, mashal not radah, thread to 4:7); shama as *misdirected heeding* (vs hearing-God 3:8/10); the three yada of the fall (promise/shame/confirmation); qara-naming-Eve as *hope against death*; chayah — the *merciful barring* of endless fallen life; the avad trajectory (absent→good→cursed); shamar *reversed* (guardian guarded out).


===== Gen 4:1-16 - Cain & Abel: the interior that will not rule itself (GEN-06) =====
SURFACED: yada as begetting (4:1) vs evasive lie (4:9); charah (inner anger) vs naphal (the fallen face) vs charah God-named (4:6); yatav as the *open* choice; chattat — the first, *personified* sin; teshuqah+mashal as a *moral summons* Cain fails (vs the *imposed* pairing of 3:16); shamar *refused toward a brother*; arar now on the *man* (vs the ground, 3:17); natan as the ground's *withheld yield* (a fourth natan-sense); koach withdrawn; avon as the self-centred felt penalty.


===== Gen 4:17-26 - two lines, two directions of calling (GEN-07) =====
SURFACED: yada-as-begetting in the cursed line (4:17) vs the appointed line (4:25); qara as *self-memorial city-naming* (4:17) vs *God-acknowledging* naming (Seth, 4:25) vs ordinary naming (Enosh, 4:26) vs **calling-upon-God/invocation** (4:26); shama+azan as *conscripted audience* for self-glorifying vengeance (Lamech); the birth of worship as the reciprocal of God's pursuing call.


===== Gen 5:1-32 - the reign of death, twice broken (GEN-08) =====
SURFACED: barak + collective qara ("Man", 5:2); tselem made **hereditary** (man→son, vs God→man in GEN-02); the *uniform* chayah death-formula named as one operation (the reign of death — its sameness the finding); Enoch's *break* (no-span context — the one not-said-to-die); Noah's naming as *hope against the curse*; nacham as *comfort-hoped* (threaded to God's *grief*, 6:6); itstsavon + arar as the *still-felt* curse (← 3:16–17).


===== Gen 6:1-8 - two hearts: the heart that forms evil, the God who grieves (GEN-09) =====
SURFACED: tov as *covetous seeing-that-takes* (← 3:6 pattern); bachar as *unbounded elective taking*; din — the Spirit's *limited* striving; basar as *frail mortal flesh* (distinct from GEN-03's basar); the 6:5 anatomy — first **lev** (heart) as the seat, yetser (forming, echoing yatsar 2:7), machashavah (devising), raah (total evil); **nacham** as God's *regret* (← 5:29 human comfort-hope); **atsav** as God's *grief* (← 3:16–17 human pain-root); the **two hearts** (lev, 6:5 evil / 6:6 grieved); nacham restated (6:7); **chen** — the *first grace*, at the nadir.


===== Gen 6:9-7:24 - the one righteous amid the ruin; the flood (GEN-10) =====
SURFACED: tsaddiq + tamim as Noah's *rightness and wholeness* (first "righteous"); the **shachat mirror** (man corrupts → God destroys, one verb); basar as *the corrupt mortal totality*; qomah as an *honest null* (no IB operation); **berit** — the *first covenant*, amid judgment; the preserved chay/chayah; tsaddiq #2 as *divine confirmation*; **tahor** — the *first purity-distinction*; **neshamah** *undone* (← 2:7); **ravah**'s *third inversion* (blessing→pain→flood); rum (waters exalted); **gavar ×4** (the relentless prevailing).


===== Gen 8:1-22 - the God who remembers and relents (GEN-11) =====
SURFACED: zakhar as *covenant-attention* (first divine "remember"); the rest-root thread nuach (ark rests) / manoach (no rest yet) / nichoach (aroma soothes God); yada as *sign-discernment*; yachal — the *first patient wait/hope*; sur as the *near-null* act of looking; ravah *restored* to blessing (inversion reversed); tahor turned to *worship*; lev as God's *resolving* heart; **yetser/lev (8:21) — the same evil-heart diagnosis as 6:5, now grounding *mercy* not judgment** (the passage's central gem).


===== Gen 9:1-17 - the re-founded order and the self-bound God (GEN-12) =====
SURFACED: barak/ravah *renewed* (← 1:28); dominion turned to *dread* (morah/chat, vs radah 1:26); natan as *delivered/expanded provision*; **nephesh located in the blood** (9:4); **darash** — God *requires a reckoning* for lifeblood; **tselem as the *legal ground*** of the murder-law (9:6); **berit ×6** as one covenant *hammered* (certainty the finding); **zakhar ×2** — God's *self-reminder* bound to the bow (← 8:1); **shachat ×2 renounced** (the flood-verb un-done); the *cosmic* scope of the covenant.


===== Gen 9:18-29 - shame returns; curse & blessing in human mouths (GEN-13) =====
SURFACED: the shame/covering scene as *no-span context* (seeing-that-exposes vs covering-that-shields); yada as *knowing-a-wrong-done*; **arar in a human mouth** (first human curse, vs prior divine curses); **barak reversed** (man blesses/praises God, vs God blessing man); chayah — the death-formula *reclaiming Noah*.


===== Gen 11:1-9 - the self-securing name, unmade (GEN-14) =====
SURFACED: the *self-securing name-making* as no-span motive (← 4:17); **zamam** — the *unified, unbounded* human purpose (distinct from individual machashavah, 6:5); **shama** as *mutual understanding* broken by God (a distinct shama-sense); **qara** — the judgment-name Babel *inverting* the sought-for name (vs Cain's self-memorial city).


===== Gen 11:10-26 - the narrowing bridge to Abram (GEN-15) =====
SURFACED: the uniform chayah formula named as *one* operation (not false-distinguished); its two differences from GEN-08 — **lifespan compression** (500 → 119) and the **dropped death-refrain** — as the finding; the line's *movement toward Abram* (11:26) rather than toward death.

## Working scope (declared as an assumption)

- The read covers the 16 verses as supplied. English surface = ESV as reproduced in the extract;
  original-language grounding is by Strong's / lemma as registered in the extract's meaning
  column.
- **A language boundary runs through the passage.** Verses 1-4a are Hebrew (H-prefixed morph);
  from the Chaldeans' speech in v4 onward the text is Aramaic (A-prefixed morph). The narrator
  flags this at v4 ("in Aramaic", H0762). This matters for lemma-grounding: the same English word
  ("dream", "spirit", "word") sits on **different lexemes** across the boundary (e.g. dream:
  Hebrew H2472 *chalom* in v1-3, Aramaic H2493 in v4 onward). The read keeps the lexemes distinct
  and does not merge them on the English string alone (GR-PROG-001; original-language grounding).
- **Retained convention:** per read-guidance step 3 note (a), a verse silent on any IB operation
  is set aside; per step 2 notes (b)/(d), non-human beings are in scope only where related to a
  human in the passage. **New this revision, per step 2 note (f):** a verse is not "silent" on a
  human's operation merely because the human's stated act is outward, administrative, or
  locational — that judgement is step 3-5's to make, after the interrogative runs, not step 2's
  to assume beforehand.
- **Observation / interpretation / reflection are labelled throughout** (Part B.6). Inference is
  named at the point it is made (Part B.3). Silence is recorded, not filled (Part B.4).
  Insufficiency is named, not papered over (Part B.7).

## Per-verse debate

### Dan 2:1 — "…Nebuchadnezzar had dreams; his spirit was troubled, and his sleep left him."

**Step 2 (bearing on IB):** Yes.

**Observation.**
- "spirit" = H7307G (*ruach*; base-fallback meaning-tree — the wide semantic field: wind/breath/mind/spirit, incl. "spirit as seat of emotion", "as troubled, bitter, discontented").
- "troubled" = H6470, here Niphal (HVtw3fs) — "to be beaten, be disturbed".
- "sleep left him" = H8142 (sleep) + H1961 (to be/become, Niphal) — sleep ceased to be with him.
- Subject stated: Nebuchadnezzar. Dream = H2472 (*chalom*), "with prophetic meaning" available in the tree but not asserted by the text here.

**Operation and parts.**
- **Subject:** Nebuchadnezzar (a focused inner being).
- **Operation:** the *ruach* is disturbed — a passive/experienced disturbance of the spirit (Niphal: he undergoes it, he does not perform it).
- **Source (of state):** **[INFERRED]** the dreams. The text juxtaposes "had dreams" and "his spirit was troubled" without an explicit causal particle; the causal link is read from sequence and from v3 (where the king himself ties spirit-trouble to the dream). Named as inference, not asserted as stated fact (Part B.3, B.5).
- **Source (of enablement):** not applicable — this is an undergone state, not an enabled act.
- **Target:** reflexive/internal — the disturbance is *in* him; no outward target at v1.

**Interrogative (Part A) — considered.**
- Q1 focused IB: yes, explicitly.
- Q2 implied interior: the disturbance is stated, not merely implied.
- Q3 stated vs inferred: the *state* is stated; its *source* is inferred (see above).
- Q4 source of state vs enablement: source of state = dreams **[inferred]**; enablement n/a.
- Q5 target: internal/reflexive.
- Q6 state or movement: a **movement** — the spirit *becomes* troubled (transition from settled to disturbed), with a further movement "sleep left him".
- Q7 linkage: links forward to v3 (same trouble, king's own voice) and to the whole crisis it sets off.
- Q8 collective: n/a (individual).
- Q9 sufficiency: **insufficient on one point** — the *content* of the dream is not given, so what specifically about it disturbed the spirit cannot be weighed. Named, not supplied.
- Q10 emergent: raises EQ-2 (below) — the edge from spirit-disturbance to a somatic effect ("sleep left him"). The interior disturbance produces a bodily consequence; this is a spirit/body-boundary datum.

**Reflection.** The passage opens by locating the entire narrative crisis *inside a person* — a disturbed *ruach* — before any decree, threat, or action. The bodily effect (sleeplessness) is presented as downstream of the inner disturbance, not the reverse. Observationally the direction is interior→body. Whether *ruach* here is "spirit" in a faculty sense or "mind/disposition" is left open; the meaning-tree carries both and the verse does not disambiguate.

### Dan 2:2 — "Then the king commanded that the magicians… be summoned to tell the king his dreams. So they came in and stood before the king."

**Step 2 (bearing on IB):** Yes, on **two** counts — the king's command (as v1.0 found), and the summoned experts' own compliance (added this revision; previously ruled out of scope).

**Observation.** "commanded" = H0559 (to say / command; the tree includes "to command", "to say in one's heart, to think, to intend"). The magicians/enchanters/sorcerers/Chaldeans are summoned; they "came in" (H0935G) and "stood" (H5975G) before the king.

**Operation and parts — the king.**
- **Subject:** the king.
- **Operation:** **[INFERRED]** an exercise of will/intent — the command is the outward act; the interior it implies is the king's resolve to seek out an answer, driven by v1's disturbance. Named as inference: the verse states the command, not the interior behind it.
- **Source (of enablement):** the king's royal office/power (stated by role, not by an interior term).
- **Target:** the assembled experts (a collective) — instrumentally, and the dream-knowledge sought.

**Operation and parts — the summoned experts' compliance (new this revision).**
- **Subject:** the magicians, enchanters, sorcerers, and Chaldeans (a collective, Q8).
- **Operation:** **[INFERRED, not stated]** prompt compliance with a royal summons — coming in
  and standing before the king. Previously dismissed outright as "purely locational... no IB work
  done by those terms here" (GR-PROG-007 exclusion test); per the corrected step 2 note (f), a
  human collective's response to a summons is a presumptive candidate regardless of how bare the
  verbs describing it are, and must be carried into the interrogative before being set aside.
- **Source (of state):** **[INFERRED]** — obedience to royal authority, professional routine, or
  (retrospectively, once v12 reveals this king's capacity for lethal fury) apprehension are all
  consistent with "came in and stood"; the text does not disambiguate among them and states none.
- **Source (of enablement):** the king's summons (this verse, first clause).
- **Target:** n/a — this is their own interior stance in complying, not an operation impacting
  another party.

**Interrogative — considered.**
- Q2 implied interior (king): the will/intent behind "commanded" — inferred.
- Q4: source-of-state (the disquiet from v1, carried) vs source-of-enablement (royal power) are **distinct**; kept separate (Part B.5).
- Q7 linkage: linked to v1 (the trouble drives the summons) — inferred link.
- Q8 collective: the summoned experts are a collective; **as of v1.0** they were treated purely as the *object* of the king's command, their own interior "not yet engaged" — that framing is what this revision corrects.
- **Q1/Q2 (experts, new):** are the summoned experts focused inner beings whose compliance may hide dread, duty, or routine? Presumptively yes, per note (f).
- **Q3 (experts, new):** inferred only; the compliance is stated, any accompanying interior is not.
- **Q9 (experts, new):** insufficient to prefer one reading (routine duty vs. apprehension) over
  another; recorded as an explicit silence, not filled.

**Reflection.** The first outward act of the passage is a delegation of the interior problem to specialists. Observationally, the king's interior does not yet appear in his own words; it is inferred from the command. **New:** the experts' own interior in responding to that command was, in the original pass, not merely left open but never asked about at all — treated as pure administrative machinery. The corrected reading still concludes "insufficient to determine" for their state, but that is now a considered outcome (Part B.4) rather than an assumption made in step 2's place.

### Dan 2:3 — "And the king said to them, 'I had a dream, and my spirit is troubled to know the dream.'"

**Step 2 (bearing on IB):** Yes — strongly.

**Observation.** Now in the king's own voice. "spirit" = H7307G (*ruach*); "troubled" = H6470 (Niphal again, HVNw3fs); "to know" = H3045 (*yada*, to know / perceive / discern). Dream = H2472.

**Operation and parts.**
- **Subject:** the king.
- **Operation:** spirit troubled **and now oriented** — "troubled *to know*". The disturbance of v1 is here given a direction: it reaches toward an object (knowledge of the dream). This is a movement with a target, not merely a state.
- **Source (of state):** the dream — here **stated** by the king himself (closing the inference left open at v1: the king attributes his trouble to the dream).
- **Target:** the dream-as-object-to-be-known; and the experts, as those from whom knowing is sought.

**Interrogative — considered.**
- Q3: the source of the trouble, inferred at v1, is **stated** here — a within-passage resolution of the earlier inference.
- Q5 target: an object/situation (the dream) plus, relationally, the experts.
- Q6 state or movement: **movement** — the troubled *ruach* now has a vector ("to know").
- Q7 linkage: explicit back-link to v1 (same trouble) and forward to the demand of v5-9.
- Q9 sufficiency: sufficient to record the operation; still no dream-content.

**Reflection.** Between v1 and v3 the same disturbance moves from *narrated fact* to *self-report with a stated cause and a stated aim*. The IB datum sharpens: a disturbed spirit that is also an appetite for knowing. The coupling of *ruach* (H7307G) with *yada* (H3045) — emotion/agitation bound to a drive-to-know — is a candidate movement worth carrying across the corpus (does troubled-spirit recurrently produce a knowing-drive?). Flagged as observation, not yet a cross-passage finding.

### Dan 2:4 — "Then the Chaldeans said to the king in Aramaic, 'O king, live forever! Tell your servants the dream, and we will show the interpretation.'"

**Step 2 (bearing on IB):** Weak — **passes marginally** on the deferential self-positioning; the request-transaction is set aside.

**Observation.** Language-shift marker: "in Aramaic" (H0762); from here the lexemes are Aramaic. "live forever" — court blessing (H2418 live + H5957 perpetuity). "your servants" = H5649 (*eved*, servant/slave). "show" = H2324 (Aramaic *chava*, to show/interpret/declare) — this verb recurs through the dialogue.

**Operation and parts.**
- **Subject:** the Chaldeans (a collective).
- **Operation:** **[INFERRED]** a disposition of deference / submission, expressed in the formula "live forever" and the self-designation "your servants". The interior (deference) is implied by the formula, not stated. Named as inference; a court formula may be convention rather than felt disposition — flagged, not resolved (Part B.1-3).
- **Target:** the king (relationally).

**Interrogative — considered.**
- Q2 implied interior: deference — inferred, possibly formulaic.
- Q8 collective: the Chaldeans speak as one voice; their "interior" is a shared/corporate stance, not an individual's. Carries EQ-3.
- Q7 linkage: their request presupposes the normal contract (king tells dream, experts interpret) — which the king will break in v5-9; the *non-fit* between their expectation and his demand is a linkage that surfaces at v5.

**Reflection.** Observationally the verse gives an expected professional posture. The interpretive risk is reading sincerity or fear into a stock phrase; the text does not license it. Recorded as marginal.

### Dan 2:5 — "The king answered… 'The word from me is firm: if you do not make known to me the dream and its interpretation, you shall be torn limb from limb, and your houses shall be laid in ruins.'"

**Step 2 (bearing on IB):** Yes — on the king's settled will.

**Observation.** "the word… is firm" — "word" = H4406 (*millah*, word/utterance/command); "firm" = H0230 (Aramaic; base "to be gone" → idiomatically, the word has *gone out from me* / is settled/irrevocable). The apodosis is a threat (dismemberment; houses to a "dunghill", H5122).

**Operation and parts.**
- **Subject:** the king.
- **Operation:** a **state of settled will / resolve** — the decree is fixed. This is a status (the will is set), which then drives an outward conditional threat.
- **Source (of state):** **[INFERRED]** carried from the disturbed-and-knowing spirit of v1/v3 — the resolve is the disturbance hardened into demand. Named as inference.
- **Target:** the experts (their bodies and houses named as stakes) — a coercive relational movement.

**Interrogative — considered.**
- Q2 interior: resolve/determination — partly stated ("the word is firm" externalises an inner fixity), partly inferred as to its emotional root.
- Q5 target: another human collective, with bodily/possessional stakes.
- Q6: a **state** (settled will) generating a **movement** (threat outward).
- Q7 linkage: the firmness answers the experts' v4 request by refusing its premise — an explicit clash of expectation.

**Reflection.** The interior here is will rather than emotion — resolve, not (yet) anger. Observationally the king converts an inner disturbance into a fixed external demand backed by force. The step from *troubled* (undergone, v1) to *firm* (asserted, v5) is a movement from passive to active interior. Whether this is composure or brittleness is not decidable from the verse; the fury of v12 will bear on it retrospectively (forward-pointer).

### Dan 2:6 — "But if you show the dream and its interpretation, you shall receive… gifts and rewards and great honor. Therefore show me…"

**Step 2 (bearing on IB):** Weak — **set aside** as to the king's interior (transactional incentive); noted only as the positive pole of v5's coercion.

**Observation.** "honor" = H3367 (honour/esteem); "gifts" H4978, "rewards" H5023. The verse is the reward-clause matching v5's threat-clause.

**Operation and parts.**
- **Subject:** the king (offering); the experts (implied desirers of reward).
- **Operation:** **[INFERRED]** the king's strategy of motivation — reward as lever. This targets a presumed interior in the experts (desire for honour/gain), but that desire is neither stated nor confirmed.
- **Target:** the experts' presumed motivation.

**Interrogative — considered.**
- Q5: the target is another party's *presumed interior* (their wish for reward) — an attributed interior, but faint. Ties to EQ-1 (one IB acting on another's presumed interior).
- Q9 sufficiency: insufficient to affirm the experts actually desire this; recorded as the king's assumption, not their state.

**Reflection.** Carrot-and-stick. The IB content is thin and mostly on the king's side (his instrumentality). Set aside from the operations register except as the complement to v5.

### Dan 2:7 — "They answered a second time and said, 'Let the king tell his servants the dream, and we will show its interpretation.'"

**Step 2 (bearing on IB):** Weak — as v4; **marginal**.

**Observation.** Repetition of the v4 request ("second time" = H8579). Same deference ("servants", H5649), same "show" (H2324).

**Operation and parts.** As v4: **[INFERRED]** deference; plus, by the repetition, an implied **inability or unwillingness** to meet the actual demand (they re-ask for the normal terms). Both inferred.

**Interrogative — considered.**
- Q2: the *repetition itself* is the new datum — it implies they cannot (or will not) do what is demanded. Inference named.
- Q7 linkage: links to v5's clash — they do not concede the king's premise; the standoff is now explicit.

**Reflection.** Observationally, a second identical request in the face of a refused premise implies either incomprehension or incapacity; the text does not adjudicate which. Recorded as an implied interior of limitation, held open.

### Dan 2:8 — "The king answered… 'I know with certainty that you are trying to gain time, because you see that the word from me is firm—'"

**Step 2 (bearing on IB):** Yes — rich (two inner beings).

**Observation.** "I know with certainty" = H3046 (know) + H3330 (certainty/reliably/truly). "you are trying to gain time" = H2084 (to buy/gain) + H5732 (time). "you see" = H2370 (to see/behold, incl. in a dream/vision). "the word… is firm" = H0230 (as v5).

**Operation and parts — first IB (king).**
- **Subject:** the king.
- **Operation:** a **cognitive state** — knowing-with-certainty; and, entangled with it, **suspicion/distrust** (he reads the experts' delay as a stalling tactic).
- **Source (of state):** his own judgement/inference about their behaviour (self-sourced cognition).
- **Target:** the experts — specifically *their interior* (their supposed intent).

**Operation and parts — second IB (the experts, as read by the king).**
- **Subject:** the experts (collective).
- **Operation (attributed):** an **intent** to "gain time", and a **perception** ("you see"). These are the king's attributions to them, **[INFERRED by the king, and referential for us]** — the text gives us the king's reading of their interior, not the experts' own report of it.

**Interrogative — considered.**
- Q3 stated vs inferred: the king's *certainty* is stated (of himself); the experts' *intent* is **doubly mediated** — inferred by the king, and reported to us. Flagged as attributed interior.
- Q5 target: **another inner being's interior** — this is the clearest case so far of an operation whose target is another IB's inner state. Raises/So EQ-1.
- Q7 linkage: back to v5/v7 (the firmness; the stalling), forward to v9 (accusation sharpens).

**Reflection.** Two interiors are now in play at once: the king's certainty-plus-suspicion, and the experts' interior *as the king construes it*. The instrument's Q5 ("does the operation impact another inner being") is stretched here to a **reading of** another interior rather than an impact **on** it. This is a genuine instrument question (EQ-1), surfaced not resolved.

### Dan 2:9 — "…there is but one sentence for you. You have agreed to speak lying and corrupt words before me till the times change. Therefore tell me the dream, and I shall know that you can show me its interpretation."

**Step 2 (bearing on IB):** Yes — rich (king's suspicion; attributed conspiracy/deceit).

**Observation.** "one sentence" = H1882 (decree/law). "You have agreed" = H2164 (to agree together / appoint a time beforehand — connotation of concerting/colluding, Ithpael). "lying" = H3538 (false/lying); "corrupt" = H7844 (to corrupt). "I shall know" = H3046. "till the times change" = H5732 (time) + H8133 (to change).

**Operation and parts — king.**
- **Subject:** the king.
- **Operation:** hardened **suspicion → accusation**; and a **will-to-test** ("tell me the dream, and I shall know…") — he sets a verification condition. His knowing is here future/conditional: a test of their claim.
- **Source (of state):** his own distrust (self-sourced), reinforced by their repetition (v7).
- **Target:** the experts — their integrity/veracity.

**Operation and parts — experts (attributed).**
- **Attributed operation:** a **collusion** ("agreed") and a disposition to **deceive** ("lying and corrupt words"). **[INFERRED by the king; referential for us]** — an accusation, not an established interior of the experts.

**Interrogative — considered.**
- Q3: the deceit/collusion is asserted *by the king*; for the analysis it is referential (an attributed interior), never imported as fact (Part B.2).
- Q6: the king's operation is a **movement** — from suspicion (v8) to formal accusation and a stated test (v9).
- Q8 collective: the "agreement" is a charge against a *collective* interior — a concerted will attributed to a group. Ties EQ-3.
- Q7 linkage: v8→v9 continuous (certainty → accusation → test).

**Reflection.** The king now not only reads the experts' interior (v8) but **morally indicts** it (v9) and devises a test. Observationally, his own interior state is the driver throughout; the experts' interior remains available to us only through his construal. The concentration of *millah* (word, H4406) across v5/v8/v9 — the king's "word" as fixed vs their "words" as (allegedly) lying — is a lexical contrast worth noting: the same lexeme carries the king's fixity and the experts' alleged falsity.

### Dan 2:10 — "The Chaldeans answered… 'There is not a man on earth who can meet the king's demand, for no great and powerful king has asked such a thing of any magician or enchanter or Chaldean.'"

**Step 2 (bearing on IB):** Moderate — on a claim about human capacity/limit.

**Observation.** "not a man on earth" = H0606 (*enash*, man/human/mankind-collective) + H3007 (earth). "can" = H3202 (to be able / prevail). The verse asserts a universal human incapacity for the king's specific demand, and defends by precedent ("no king has asked such a thing").

**Operation and parts.**
- **Subject:** the Chaldeans (collective), speaking about the human being in general.
- **Operation:** an **assertion of the limit of human ability/knowing** — and, **[INFERRED]**, a self-protective deflection (the interior of self-defence behind the appeal to precedent). The limit-claim is stated; the defensive motive is inferred.
- **Target:** the king (relationally) and, in content, the human condition itself.

**Interrogative — considered.**
- Q2 interior: two layers — the *stated* claim about human incapacity, and the *inferred* fear/self-defence behind it.
- Q8 collective: the Chaldeans speak for a professional class and, more widely, for "man on earth" — a claim scaled from themselves to humanity.
- Q9 sufficiency: sufficient for the limit-claim; the defensive motive is inference, marked.

**Reflection.** The verse moves the IB question from a particular disturbed king to a general claim about *what a human being can and cannot do*. Observationally this is the programme-relevant nerve: an in-text statement about the boundary of human knowing. It sets up v11's sharper boundary claim.

### Dan 2:11 — "The thing that the king asks is difficult, and no one can show it to the king except the gods, whose dwelling is not with flesh."

**Step 2 (bearing on IB):** Yes — on the flesh/divine epistemic boundary.

**Observation.** "difficult" = H3358 (rare/noble/difficult). "gods" = H0426 (*elah*, god/God). "dwelling" = H4070B (dwelling). "flesh" = H1321 (*besar*, flesh; Aramaic of H1320). The claim: revelatory knowledge of this kind lies with the gods, "whose dwelling is not with flesh".

**Operation and parts.**
- **Subject:** the Chaldeans (asserting); **non-human beings** ("the gods") introduced in explicit contrast to "flesh".
- **Operation:** a **boundary statement** — the source of the required knowledge is located *outside* the human ("flesh"), with beings whose mode of dwelling is not-with-flesh. This draws a spirit/flesh (or divine/human) line as an **epistemic limit** on the human inner being.
- **Source / target:** the operation is a claim *about* sourcing — it says the *source* of such knowing is divine, not human. The human (flesh) is positioned as *unable to be the source*.

**Interrogative — considered.**
- Q4 source: the verse itself is *about* source — it denies human sourcing and asserts divine sourcing for hidden knowledge. Directly on Part B.5's concern (source-of-state/enablement).
- Q5 target: the human condition (flesh) as the bounded party.
- Q8: "flesh" here is humanity-as-such — a collective/generic human.
- Per read-guidance step 2 note (b)/(d): the gods (non-human) are **in scope** because the claim relates them *to* humans (flesh) — the contrast is the point. Carries EQ-4.

**Reflection.** This is the passage's most direct datum for the programme's governing question. Observationally the text (on the Chaldeans' lips) posits a hard limit: the deepest knowing is not available to "flesh". Interpretively, the narrative will controvert this (Daniel, via his God, will show the very thing they say only gods-not-with-flesh can) — but that resolution lies beyond v16 and is a **forward-pointer**, not imported here. The claim stands in the read as a stated human-limit assertion; its refutation is anticipated, not asserted.

### Dan 2:12 — "Because of this the king was angry and very furious, and commanded that all the wise men of Babylon be destroyed."

**Step 2 (bearing on IB):** Yes — clear emotional operation with a stated cause and effect.

**Observation.** "angry" = H1149 (to be angry). "very" = H7690 (greatly/exceedingly). "furious" = H7108 (to be/become wroth). "Because of this" = H6903H (because of / on account of). "commanded… destroyed" = H0560 (command) + H0007 (to destroy/perish).

**Operation and parts.**
- **Subject:** the king.
- **Operation:** **anger, intensified to fury** — a stated emotional state, doubled (two anger lexemes, H1149 + H7108) and amplified ("very", H7690).
- **Source (of state):** **stated** — "Because of this" (H6903H) ties the fury explicitly to the preceding exchange (the experts' failure/deflection, v10-11). This is the passage's one **explicitly sourced** interior state.
- **Target / effect:** produces the decree to destroy "all the wise men of Babylon" — a movement from interior emotion to lethal outward command, scaled to a whole collective.

**Interrogative — considered.**
- Q3: both the state (fury) and its source (the exchange) are **stated** — the least inferential IB operation in the passage.
- Q4: source-of-state (the exchange) and source-of-enablement (royal power to decree) are **distinct**; the emotion supplies the *impetus*, the office supplies the *capacity*. Kept separate (Part B.5).
- Q5 target: a collective (all Babylon's wise men) — a movement to a collection, not an individual (Q8).
- Q6: state (fury) → movement (decree/destruction).
- Q7 linkage: the tightest causal chain in the passage — v10-11 (deflection) → v12 (fury → decree) → v13 (decree executed). **See v13, below — this revision extends the chain to the condemned party's own interior, which v1.0 did not.**

**Reflection.** Here the passage gives, cleanly, an **emotion→decree→action** cascade with the emotion explicitly caused. It contrasts with v5's *will/resolve* (fixed but not yet hot): the interior has moved from settled demand to overflow. Observationally this is the hinge where an individual's inner state becomes a threat to a whole class of people — the widest target/impact in the span.

### Dan 2:13 — "So the decree went out, and the wise men were about to be killed; and they sought Daniel and his companions, to kill them."

**Step 2 (bearing on IB):** Yes — **revised this pass**. v1.0 read this as "weak — set aside as to any stated interior," noting only the executants' inferred purposiveness. Corpus review identified this as the single most consequential gap in the whole four-document corpus: the condemned wise men are not anonymous background figures — they are the same party who spoke, negotiated, and reasoned at length at v4–11, in this very range — and their own interior at the moment of facing execution was never even raised as a candidate.

**Observation.** "decree went out" = H1882 (decree) + H5312 (go out). "sought… to kill" = H1156 (to seek/ask) + H6992 (to slay). Daniel (H1841H) and companions (H2269, *chavar*, associate/companion) introduced as targets.

**Operation and parts — the executants (as v1.0 found, unchanged).**
- **Subject:** the executants (agents of the decree) — impersonal here.
- **Operation:** **[INFERRED]** intent-to-kill (purposive action), carrying out the king's will; not their own originating interior.
- **Target:** Daniel and companions (individuals now named) — no interior of *theirs* yet stated.

**Operation and parts — the condemned wise men's own interior (new this revision).**
- **Subject:** "the wise men" — the class condemned at v12, now facing imminent execution (Q8,
  collective) — the same class that spoke for itself at length at v4, v7, v10–11.
- **Operation:** **[INFERRED, not stated]** — facing imminent death is, by a wide margin, the most
  existentially loaded circumstance any human party occupies in this range. Terror, despair,
  pleading, or resignation are all plausible; none is stated. The text's attention stays entirely
  on the *administration* of the decree ("went out… about to be killed… sought… to kill"); the
  condemned party's own experience of it is simply not narrated, and — until this revision — was
  not even raised as a question.
- **Source (of state):** the king's fury (v12) — the same stated source that drives the decree
  itself; if the wise men have an interior state here, this is what produces it.
- **Source (of enablement):** the king's decree and the executants carrying it out.
- **Target:** n/a — reflexive, their own state, if the text gave one.

**Interrogative — considered.**
- Q1 (the decisive correction): are the condemned wise men focused inner beings in their own
  right at this moment? Unambiguously yes — more so than most candidates in this corpus, since
  this is not a minor or background character but the same party whose voice, reasoning, and
  even (v8-9) attributed disposition the debate examined closely just two verses earlier. The
  omission at v13 is sharper for that reason: a party under sustained interrogative attention
  becomes, three verses later, purely administrative machinery.
- Q2: implied interior — terror, despair, appeal to precedent again (echoing v10-11's own
  defence), resignation. None stated.
- Q3: would be inference only if named; the text states none of it, and this operation records
  that as a considered silence rather than an unexamined gap.
- Q7 linkage: extends the "tightest causal chain in the passage" (v10-11 → v12 → v13) to include,
  for the first time, the condemned party's own position *inside* that chain, not only the
  king's and executants' side of it. Also: does this fear, if it exists, resemble or differ from
  the eunuch's stated fear in the parallel Dan 1 passage (1:10)? Noted as a cross-passage
  question, not pursued here (per researcher direction, EQ items are resolved at the whole-book
  read, not synergised across passage debates).
- Q9 sufficiency: insufficient — no data on their state at the point of execution, and no data on
  whether they are even aware that Daniel and companions are being sought as part of the same
  danger (v13's second clause) or as a separate thread.

**Reflection.** This is the sharpest gap the corpus review found in any of the four documents. A
class of named, previously-vocal human beings faces imminent death, and the original pass records
only the machinery of their execution — never their own experience of it, not even as an
explicitly-marked silence. The correction does not supply that experience (the text does not
license it); it names the silence, which the original pass did not do.

**Decision.** **Retain** the executants' operation as before. **Retain**, newly, the condemned
wise men's own interior as an explicit recorded silence — the most significant single addition
from the corpus review.

### Dan 2:14 — "Then Daniel replied with prudence and discretion to Arioch, the captain of the king's guard…"

**Step 2 (bearing on IB):** Yes — the **first stated inner quality of Daniel**.

**Observation.** "prudence" = H5843 (*eta*, counsel). "discretion" = H2942 (glossed here "command"; meaning-tree thin: "report, taste; taste; report"). "replied" = H8421I (to return / bring back a reply, Aphel). Arioch = H0746B; "guard" = H2877 (bodyguard/executioner).

**Operation and parts.**
- **Subject:** Daniel.
- **Operation:** a reply **carried out with / sourced in** inner qualities — *counsel* (prudence) and *discernment* (discretion). The manner of the act (measured, prudent) is itself the IB datum: an outward reply whose character discloses an interior disposition.
- **Source (of state):** Daniel's own disposition (self-sourced); the text presents these as *his* qualities, not granted in this verse.
- **Target:** Arioch (relationally) — but the operation's significance is the *quality* of Daniel's interior, contrasted with the surrounding turbulence.

**Interrogative — considered.**
- Q2 interior: **stated** (named qualities), not merely inferred — a rare directly-attributed interior for a non-king figure in the span.
- Q9 sufficiency: **insufficiency flagged** — H2942 ("discretion") has a thin meaning-tree in the extract (the gloss "command" sits oddly against ESV "discretion"; the tree offers "taste/report"). The precise inner sense (discernment? tact? judgement of the situation?) is under-determined by the data as registered. Named, not supplied (Part B.7).
- Q7 linkage: sharp **contrast-linkage** to vv1-12 — Daniel's composure against Nebuchadnezzar's disturbance→resolve→fury. **New:** also a contrast to v13's now-recorded (silent) terror of the wise men Daniel has just been swept up among — Daniel's composure is not merely a contrast to the king, but to the condemned class he is himself, at this moment, part of (v13's "they sought Daniel and his companions, to kill them").

**Reflection.** The passage's first sustained *positive* interior. Observationally, where every prior interior was either disturbed (king) or defensive (experts), Daniel's is stated as composed and discerning — and, per the revision at v13, this composure now stands against a *named* silence (the wise men's presumed terror) rather than an unexamined backdrop. Interpretive caution: "prudence/discretion" as ESV renders two Aramaic nouns whose exact inner content is partly under-attested here.

### Dan 2:15 — "He declared to Arioch… 'Why is the decree of the king so urgent?' Then Arioch made the matter known to Daniel."

**Step 2 (bearing on IB):** Moderate — Daniel's measured **inquiry**.

**Observation.** "declared" = H6032 (answer/respond) + H0560 (say). "urgent" = H2685 (be urgent/harsh, Aphel). "made known" = H3046 (Aphel: cause to know / inform). "Why" = H4101 (what/why).

**Operation and parts.**
- **Subject:** Daniel.
- **Operation:** an **inquiry** — a deliberate question ("why so urgent?") rather than panic or flight. A cognitive/volitional act: he seeks the ground of the decree before acting.
- **Source:** continuous with v14's prudence (self-sourced disposition).
- **Target:** Arioch (source of information); the situation (object to be understood).

**Interrogative — considered.**
- Q2: the interior (calm, inquiring) is **inferred from the manner** of the question — the verse states the question, the composure is read from it. Named as inference, but well-supported by v14's stated qualities.
- Q6: a **movement** — Daniel moves toward knowledge (inquiry), paralleling (and inverting) the king's troubled "to know" of v3: the king is disturbed-to-know; Daniel is composed-and-inquiring.
- Q7 linkage: v14→v15 continuous (prudence → measured inquiry); and a **contrast-linkage** to v3 (two different "drives to know").

**Reflection.** The parallel to v3 is worth surfacing: both Nebuchadnezzar (v3) and Daniel (v15) turn toward knowing, but from opposite interior states — agitation vs composure. This is an observation about *how* a drive-to-know is coloured by the interior it springs from. Held as observation; a cross-corpus test would be needed to make it a finding.

### Dan 2:16 — "And Daniel went in and requested the king to appoint him a time, that he might show the interpretation to the king."

**Step 2 (bearing on IB):** Moderate — Daniel's purposive **request** and implicit confidence.

**Observation.** "requested" = H1156 (to ask/seek/request/desire/pray/make petition). "appoint him" = H5415H (to give/allow). "time" = H2166 (a set time/season). "show" = H2324 (the same show/interpret verb the experts used). "interpretation" = H6591.

**Operation and parts.**
- **Subject:** Daniel.
- **Operation:** a **petition/act of will** (request for time) directed to a **purpose** ("that he might show the interpretation"). Embedded is an **[INFERRED]** confidence/expectation that he *can* show it — otherwise the request is empty. The confidence is not stated; it is strongly implied by the request's purpose and by the contrast with the experts' "no one can except the gods" (v11).
- **Source (of enablement):** **not stated here.** Daniel requests time; the *ground* of his confidence (his God) is not named in v16 (it appears later in the chapter, beyond scope). Marked as **silence** at v16 — not filled (Part B.4); forward-pointer attached.
- **Target:** the king (petitioned); the interpretation (to be shown).

**Interrogative — considered.**
- Q3: the request is stated; the confidence is inferred; the *source* of the confidence is **silent** at v16.
- Q4: source-of-state (Daniel's confidence) is left unsourced by this verse — a deliberate silence, flagged, with a forward-pointer to the rest of Dan 2.
- Q5 target: the king (an inner being) and an object (the interpretation).
- Q7 linkage: v16 uses the **same verb "show" (H2324)** the experts used in v4/v7 — Daniel steps into the exact role they said was impossible for "flesh" (v11). A pointed lexical linkage.

**Reflection.** The span closes on a human being who, calmly and purposively, undertakes precisely what the experts declared beyond "flesh". Observationally, v16 sets up (without yet stating) the passage's implicit answer to the v11 boundary claim. The *source* of Daniel's confidence is withheld at v16 — a silence the reader is meant to feel and that later verses resolve. Recorded as silence + forward-pointer, per Part B.4.

## Insufficiencies register

1. **Dream content absent (v1, v3).** The extract (rightly, as a lexical span-tool) does not give the dream's content; so *what* about the dream troubled the *ruach* cannot be weighed. Named, not supplied.
2. **Name etymologies absent.** Daniel (H1841H), Nebuchadnezzar (H5019), Arioch (H0746B), Belteshazzar — the extract gives referential/biographical notes, not etymological meaning. Any inner-being reading of the names is therefore unsupported by the data at hand; not supplied from outside (Part B.7).
3. **H2942 "discretion" (v14) meaning-tree thin.** Gloss "command" vs ESV "discretion"; tree offers "taste/report". The precise inner sense is under-determined. Flagged for CC word-onboarding / STEP disambiguation if this term becomes load-bearing.
4. **H7307G / H1321 across the language boundary.** *Ruach* (H7307G) appears in the Hebrew section (v1, v3); "flesh" (H1321, Aramaic) in v11. Spirit and flesh do not co-occur in the *same* lexical system within this span, so any spirit/flesh pairing drawn here spans two language layers — noted so it is not over-read as a single-system contrast.
5. **Attributed interiors (v8, v9) are not the experts' own report.** The experts' intent/deceit reaches us only through the king's construal; treated as referential, never as confirmed interior of the experts.
6. **The summoned experts' state at v2 — new.** No data distinguishes routine compliance from apprehension; recorded as silence, not filled.
7. **The condemned wise men's state at v13 — new, the most significant addition.** No data on
   their interior facing imminent execution, despite their being an active, previously-vocal
   party in this same range (v4, v7, v10–11).

## Emergent-questions log (filed against this passage; resolved, if at all, at the whole-book
read — not merged with other passages' logs, per researcher direction)

- **EQ-1 — Reading vs impacting another interior.** v8-9: an operation whose *target* is another IB's interior can be either (a) an *impact on* that interior or (b) a *reading/attribution of* it. The Part A Q5 target-taxonomy currently frames "impact"; the *attributed-interior* case (one IB construing another's inner state, possibly wrongly) may warrant an explicit handling rule. Surfaced for the instrument, not resolved here.
- **EQ-2 — Interior→somatic edge.** v1: troubled spirit → "sleep left him". A movement from inner disturbance to bodily effect. Relevant to the programme's spirit-soul-body boundary question; candidate edge-type "produces (somatic effect)".
- **EQ-3 — Collective interior.** vv4, 7, 8-9, 10-11: the experts act and are charged as a collective (shared deference; attributed collective "agreement"; a limit-claim scaled to "man on earth"). How a *corporate* interior is recorded (vs an individual's) — and whether an attributed collective interior differs from an attributed individual one — is an open handling question (Q8 territory).
- **EQ-4 — Flesh/divine epistemic boundary.** v11: an in-text claim locating the deepest knowing outside "flesh". Bears directly on the programme's governing question and on the relational-interface-with-spiritual-beings strand. Its narrative refutation (Daniel showing what "only gods" could) is forward-pointed beyond v16; carried as an open thread for whole-chapter analysis.
- **EQ-5 (new this revision) — the "previously-vocal party goes silent" pattern.** v13's gap
  (the wise men, extensively quoted at v4-11, receive no interior at all facing execution) may be
  a specific instance of a broader pattern worth testing at whole-book scale: does the text (or
  does a debate reading it) tend to lose interest in a party's interior once that party's
  narrative function shifts from speaking-agent to acted-upon-object? If so, this is exactly the
  asymmetry step-2 note (f) was written to counteract, and its recurrence across a whole book
  would be a significant finding in its own right.

## Analyst's closing reflection (cross-verse — grounded, offered as observation not verdict)

Across the span the passage stages a **contrast of interiors** that is textually grounded, not imposed:

- **Nebuchadnezzar's arc:** troubled (undergone, v1/v3) → firm/resolved (asserted, v5/v8) → suspicious/accusing (v8-9) → furious (v12, explicitly caused) → lethal decree (v12-13). An interior moving from passive disturbance to active overflow, widening its target from himself to a whole class of people.
- **The experts' interior:** deferential (v4/v7), then defensive, culminating in a boundary-claim about human limits (v10-11) — an interior of incapacity, whether honest or self-protective (held open) — **and, per this revision, silent at the exact point (v13) their own lives are on the line**, a silence now named rather than passed over.
- **Daniel's interior:** composed and discerning (v14), measured in inquiry (v15), purposive and (implicitly) confident (v16) — stepping into the very role the experts called impossible for "flesh", **and doing so, per this revision, as a member of the very class (v13) whose own fear was never voiced.**

The **grounding** for calling this a contrast is lexical and structural (the doubled anger lexemes of v12 against the stated *counsel/discernment* of v14; the shared verb "show" H2324 spanning v4/v7/v11/v16; the paired "drives to know" of v3 and v15), not a theme imposed on the verses. It is offered as an **observation with reflection**, not a finding.

**Interpretive fork held open for the researcher:** the passage's implicit answer to the v11 flesh/divine boundary (Daniel *does* show what "only gods not with flesh" could) is set up within the span but *resolved outside it*. Whether this read should be closed now with an explicit forward-pointer to the whole of Dan 2, or held strictly at the span boundary, is a scope decision not taken here — the read stops at v16 and marks the thread as forward-pointed.

**Passage-level linkages (as filed).**

- **H0995 "understand"** — closes the book's understanding-thread. Daniel's own explicit admission, "I heard, but I did not understand" (12:8), directly echoes Dan 8:27 and stands in contrast to the wise, who "shall understand" (12:10) — the book's final word on this root reserves full understanding for "the wise" and for "the time of the end," not for Daniel himself in this scene.
- **H7919 "wise"** — culminates the thread from Dan 9:13/22/25 and 11:33/35: the wise "shine like the stars" (12:3) and are the ones who "shall understand" (12:10), a stated vindication of the role first introduced at Dan 9:13.

- **Shame/contempt (H2781, H1860)** — H2781 recurs from Dan 9:16 and 11:18; its final application at 12:2, to an eternal destiny, is its most severe use in the book.
- **Michael** — third appearance (Dan 10:13, 10:21, 12:1); this verse is the first to state his role toward Daniel's people explicitly ("who has charge of your people"), clarifying but not contradicting his earlier appearances as an opponent of hostile "princes."
- **Sealing (H5640, H2856)** — Daniel is twice told to seal the book (12:4, 12:9), directly fulfilling Dan 8:26's instruction ("seal up the vision") and Dan 9:24's decree ("to seal both vision and prophet") — the book closes by enacting its own earlier sealing-motif rather than resolving its content further.
- **Absence surfaced:** the man clothed in linen (recalling Dan 10:5) is never named in this chapter, consistent with Dan 10's own pattern of an unnamed speaker (see Dan 10 debate, EQ1).

## The reading

**I. Belonging by *birth* in the city (vv.4–6).**
"'**This one was born there**,' they say… of Zion it shall be said, 'This one and that one were **born** in her'… The LORD **records** as he registers the peoples, 'This one was born there'" (vv.4–6). The finding: the inner being's **belonging is figured as *birth* in God's city** — its identity is being *registered* as *born there* *(stated)*. The operation: the self's deepest belonging is **not chosen but conferred by origin** — to be *born* in Zion, recorded by God's own hand, is an identity **given, not achieved** *(inferred: "born there… the LORD records" makes the belonging an origin conferred and registered by God)*.

**II. "All my springs are in you" — the self's wellsprings located in God (v.7).**
"Singers and dancers alike say, '**All my springs** (H4599, *mayan* — fountains) **are in you**'" (v.7). The gem-finding: the inner being's **sources — its wellsprings of life and joy — are *in God/Zion***, not in itself *(stated)*. The operation: the self locates its **fountains outside itself**, in God — everything that *springs up* within it (its joy, its life) flows from a source it does not own *(inferred: "all my springs are in you" makes God the reservoir from which the inner being's vitality wells; the singers/dancers' joy has its source there, cf. Ps 36:9's "fountain of life", 84:6's springs)*.

## What Psalm 87 says about the inner being
- **Belonging is conferred by *birth* (vv.4–6).** The inner being's identity is being *born* in God's city, registered by God's own hand — a belonging given by origin, not achieved.
- **The self's *wellsprings* are located in God (v.7).** "All my springs are in you" — the fountains of the inner being's life and joy flow from God, a source it does not own. What springs up within it has its reservoir outside itself.

# Psalm 111 — what it tells us about the inner being (Phase-2 chapter reading)

- **Whole-hearted thanks; the works *studied by those who delight*; the fear of the LORD as *the beginning of wisdom*.** Psalm 111 is an acrostic hymn to God's works and character (the arena). Its inner-being findings are three, framing that praise: **thanks with the *whole heart*** (v.1); the works **studied by all who *delight* in them** (v.2); and the cornerstone — **the fear of the LORD is *the beginning of wisdom*** (v.10).

## The reading

**I. Thanks with the whole heart (v.1).**
"Praise the LORD! I will give thanks to the LORD with my **whole heart** (H3824, the seat — M47), in the company of the upright" (v.1). The finding: the thanksgiving is ***whole-hearted*** — the entire heart engaged, nothing withheld *(stated)*. The operation: the inner being praises **undividedly** — "whole heart" naming a self *gathered entire* into the thanks, not partial or distracted *(inferred: "with my whole heart" makes the thanks total — the undivided heart, cf. Ps 9:1, 138:1; the wholeness is itself the inner-being datum)*.

**II. The works *studied by those who delight* in them (v.2).**
"Great are the works of the LORD, **studied** (H1875 — *darash*, sought out) by all who **delight** (H2656, M04) in them" (v.2). The gem-finding: God's works are *sought out / studied* — and specifically by those who **delight** in them *(stated)*. The operation: the inner being's **delight *drives inquiry*** — those who take pleasure in God's works *search them out*; delight and study are joined, the loved thing pondered *(inferred: "studied by all who delight in them" links affection to investigation — the self that delights *seeks out*; the works are searched *because* they are loved; cf. Ps 1:2 "his delight… on his law he meditates", 119:2 "seek him with their whole heart")*.

**III. The fear of the LORD — *the beginning of wisdom* (v.10).**
"The **fear** (H3373, M01) of the LORD is the **beginning of wisdom**; all those who practice it have a **good understanding**. His praise endures forever!" (v.10). The gem-finding — a cornerstone of the whole study: **wisdom *begins* with the fear of the LORD** *(stated)*. The operation: the inner being's **first and foundational orientation is reverent fear** — wisdom is not first intellect but a *right awe*, and out of that fear grows *good understanding*; the fear is the *starting-point*, the root from which the wise inner life develops *(inferred: "the fear of the LORD is the beginning of wisdom… all who practice it have good understanding" makes reverent fear the foundation and *practice* the path — wisdom is a posture before it is knowledge; the master-datum linking M01 fear and M15 wisdom, cf. Prov 9:10, Ps 25:12,14, 34:11)*.

## What Psalm 111 says about the inner being
- **Thanks is *whole-hearted* (v.1).** The entire heart engaged, undivided — the self gathered *entire* into the praise, nothing withheld.
- **Delight *drives study* (v.2).** Those who delight in God's works *seek them out* — affection and inquiry joined; the loved thing is searched out *because* it is loved.
- **The fear of the LORD is *the beginning of wisdom* (v.10).** The inner being's first, foundational orientation is reverent awe — wisdom is a *posture* before it is knowledge, and good understanding grows from *practising* that fear. A master-datum linking fear and wisdom.

It roots the whole M15 (wisdom) cluster in M01 (fear): the inner being becomes wise not by cleverness but by *reverent fear practised*.

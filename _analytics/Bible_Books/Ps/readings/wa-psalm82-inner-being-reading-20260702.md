## The reading

**I. Injustice flows from inner blindness (v.5).**
Against the charge to "give **justice** (H8199, M26) to the weak… rescue the needy" (vv.3–4), the unjust are diagnosed inwardly: "They have **neither knowledge nor understanding** (H0995, M15, *bin*), they **walk about in darkness**; all the foundations of the earth are shaken" (v.5). The finding: injustice is not merely bad policy but a **failure of the inner faculty of understanding** — "neither knowledge nor understanding" *(stated)*. The operation: the unjust inner being **lacks perception** — it cannot *see* (walks in darkness), and from that inner blindness the outward injustice flows, until "the foundations… are shaken" *(inferred: "no understanding… walk in darkness… foundations shaken" chains the inner lack to the cosmic disorder — the failure of inner sight destabilises the world; cf. Ps 36:1–4's self-enclosed wicked, 14:2's God scanning for the understanding)*. Injustice has an **inner root: a darkened, un-understanding self** *(inferred)*.

## What Psalm 82 says about the inner being
- **Injustice is rooted in a *failure of inner perception* (v.5).** The unjust "have neither knowledge nor understanding" and "walk about in darkness" — a darkened, un-understanding self, from which the outward injustice flows and the world's foundations are shaken. The inner lack of *sight* is the root.

## The reading

**I. To love the LORD is to hate evil (v.10).**
"O you who **love** (H0157, *aheb*) the LORD, **hate** (H8130, M06) evil!" (v.10). The gem-finding: the inner being's **love and hate are bound together and rightly aimed** — *loving God* **entails** *hating evil* *(stated)*. The operation: ordered affection is **not love alone but a paired love-and-hate** — the same inner being that loves the LORD *must* hate evil; the two are one disposition seen from two sides *(inferred: "you who love the LORD, hate evil" makes the hatred of evil the *implication* of the love of God — right love requires a corresponding right hatred; cf. Ps 45:7's loved-righteousness/hated-wickedness)*.

**II. Light and joy *sown* for the upright in heart (v.11).**
"**Light is sown** for the righteous, and **joy** for the **upright in heart** (H3820, the seat — M47)" (v.11). The gem-finding: the inner being's light and joy are **sown** — *planted like seed* — for the upright *(stated)*. The operation: the upright-in-heart's gladness is a **seed awaiting harvest** — *sown* now, to spring up later; light and joy are **planted for** the upright, a deferred but certain gladness growing toward them *(inferred: "light is sown… joy for the upright in heart" makes the gladness a seed sown for a future harvest, not an immediate possession — the upright's joy is *coming up*)*.

## What Psalm 97 says about the inner being
- **Loving the LORD *entails* hating evil (v.10).** Right affection is a paired love-and-hate — the inner being that loves God must hate evil; the two are one ordered disposition, not love alone.
- **Light and joy are *sown* for the upright in heart (v.11).** The gladness of the upright is a seed planted for a future harvest — sown now, springing up later; a deferred but certain joy growing toward them.

# Psalm 50 — what it tells us about the inner being (Phase-2 chapter reading)

- **Lens:** the finding is the inner-being **operation**, God-relation as arena (`feedback_lens_is_inner_being_process_not_god_relation`).
- **True worship and the projected God.** Psalm 50 (God's own speech) discloses two inner-being findings: what God desires from the inner being is **thanksgiving and dependence**, not the provision of ritual goods (vv.14–15, 23); and the wicked inner being's defining error — it **likens God to itself**, projecting its own nature onto him (v.21), which shows itself in a **discarded word** and **corrupt speech** (vv.16–20).

## The reading

**I. True offering — thanksgiving and calling, not provision (vv.7–15, 23).**
God's critique of ritual is not that sacrifice is offered but that it is **misunderstood as *provisioning* God**: "I will **not accept a bull** from your house… every beast of the forest is mine… If I were hungry, I would not tell you… **Offer to God a sacrifice of thanksgiving** (H8426, M22, *todah*)… and **call** (H7121, M37) upon me in the day of trouble" (vv.9–15). The finding: what God desires from the inner being is **gratitude and dependence** — *thanksgiving* and *calling-upon-in-trouble* — not goods it imagines God needs *(stated: the sacrifice desired is thanksgiving + calling, not the bull)*. The operation: true worship is a **posture of the inner being** (grateful, dependent), and the closing line makes it explicit — "the one who offers **thanksgiving**… glorifies me; to one who **orders his way** rightly I will show salvation" (v.23) *(stated)*. Worship is re-located from the altar's goods to the heart's gratitude *(inferred: the whole speech displaces provision with thanksgiving/calling)*.

**II. The wicked *likens God to himself* — the projection (v.21).**
The gem-finding: "These things you have done, and I have been **silent**; you **thought** (H1819, *damah* — *likened*, deemed *similar*) that I was **one like yourself**. But now I rebuke you" (v.21). The verb is *damah* — literally *to liken / make similar* — so the charge is exact: the wicked inner being **remade God in its own image**, projecting its own moral indifference onto him *(stated: you *likened* me to yourself)*. The operation: **God's silence is misread as God's *likeness*** — because God did not immediately react, the self concluded he shares its own indifference to evil *(inferred: the "I have been silent; you likened me to yourself" ties the misreading of silence to the projection)*. The finding is a deep tendency of the inner being: **to construct God after its own nature**, a self-serving theological distortion.

**III. The discarded word and the rejected discipline (vv.16–17).**
"What right have you to recite my statutes… For you **hate** (H8130, M06) **discipline** (H4148, *musar*), and you **cast my words behind you**" (vv.16–17). The operation: the inner being **actively discards instruction** — it *casts the word behind*, out of sight, and *hates* the correction *(stated)*. The finding: the projection of a like-me God (v.21) is enabled by first **putting God's actual word where it cannot be seen** — the discarded word clears the space for the invented God *(inferred: casting the words behind precedes and permits the liken-God-to-self)*.

**IV. The corrupt speech that follows (vv.19–20).**
"You give your **mouth** free rein for evil, and your **tongue** frames **deceit** (H4820, M14). You sit and speak against your brother; you **slander** your own mother's son" (vv.19–20). The finding: with the word discarded and God re-imagined, the inner being's **speech runs to evil** — mouth and tongue framing deceit and slander *(stated)*. Speech is the outflow of the projected-God inner being: having remade God, it speaks as if unaccountable *(inferred: the corrupt speech follows from the discarded word and projected God)*.

## What Psalm 50 says about the inner being
- **True worship is a posture of the inner being, not provision (vv.14–15, 23).** God desires *thanksgiving* and *calling-upon-in-trouble* — gratitude and dependence — not goods it imagines he needs. Worship is re-located from the altar to the heart.
- **The inner being tends to *liken God to itself* (v.21).** *Damah* — it remakes God in its own image, projecting its own moral indifference; God's silence is misread as God's *likeness*. A deep, self-serving distortion.
- **The projection is enabled by *discarding the word* (vv.16–17).** Casting God's actual word behind, out of sight, and hating discipline clears the space for the invented God.
- **Corrupt speech follows the projected God (vv.19–20).** With the word discarded and God re-imagined, mouth and tongue run to deceit and slander — the inner being speaks as if unaccountable.

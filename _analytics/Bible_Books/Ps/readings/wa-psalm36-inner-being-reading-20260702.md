# Psalm 36 — what it tells us about the inner being (Phase-2 chapter reading)

- **Source:** the `lexical-model-2026` Phase-1 lexicals for Psa 36:1–12 (chapter-driven poetic build; roles sanity-checked). Complete chapter. Lexicals only; cited; grounded; inferences tagged *stated* | *inferred*.
- **Lens:** the finding is the inner-being **operation**, God-relation as arena (`feedback_lens_is_inner_being_process_not_god_relation`).
- **A diptych of two inner economies.** Psalm 36 sets the **self-enclosed** inner being of the wicked (vv.1–4 — an inner voice that is not the fear of God, self-flattering sight, the lost "no" to evil) against the inner being **open to and fed from outside itself** (vv.5–9 — taking refuge, feasting, drinking, and *seeing light only in a received light*). The through-line is a contrast of **inner economies**: what *addresses* the heart, what *sight* the self runs on, and where its *life-source* lies.

## The reading

**I. Transgression as an inner oracle — the heart is a space that is addressed (v.1).**
"**Transgression** (H6588, M10, *pesha*) **speaks** (H5002, *neum* — the technical word for a prophetic *oracle*, 'declaration of the LORD') to the wicked **deep in his heart** (H3820, the seat — M47); there is **no fear** (H6343, M01) of God before his **eyes**" (v.1). The gem-operation: transgression is personified **giving an oracle in the wicked's heart** — a mock-prophecy occupying the place a divine word should hold *(stated: transgression *speaks/oracles* deep in the heart)*. The structural finding: **the heart is a space that is always being addressed** — and *what* speaks there (the fear of God, or transgression in its place) forms the person. Where "the fear of God" is absent "before his eyes," the vacancy is filled by transgression's inner voice *(inferred: the missing fear and the speaking transgression are set as the two possible occupants of the heart)*.

**II. Self-flattery — the inner being deceiving itself (v.2).**
"For he **flatters** (H2505, *chalaq*) himself **in his own eyes** that his **iniquity** (H5771, M10) cannot be found out and hated" (v.2). The operation: the wicked inner being is **its own flatterer** — it constructs a false self-assessment "in his own eyes," persuading itself its iniquity is safe *(stated)*. Self-deception is an **active inner operation**: the self talking the self into a lie about itself *(inferred: the flattery is reflexive — he flatters *himself* in *his own* eyes)*. Note the *sight* motif: this is **self-enclosed vision** — everything measured within his own eyes.

**III. The lost "no" — the failure of the rejecting-faculty (vv.3–4).**
"The **words** of his mouth are **trouble** (H0205, M10) and **deceit** (H4820, M14); he has **ceased** to act **wisely** (H7919, M15)… He **plots** (H2803, M15) trouble while on his **bed**; he sets himself in a way that is not good; he does **not reject** (H3988, *maas*) **evil**" (vv.3–4). The progression names a specific breakdown: the inner being **plots in the bed** — the private night-workshop of the self turned to evil (contrast Ps 4:4's pondering-in-the-heart-on-the-bed) — and, decisively, "he does **not reject** evil." The gem: **the inner "no" is gone** — the rejecting-faculty (*maas*), the capacity to refuse evil, has ceased to operate *(stated: he does not reject evil)*. Corruption here is not an added appetite but a **subtracted refusal** *(inferred)*.

**IV. The open economy — the inner being fed from outside itself (vv.5–9).**
The diptych turns: "How precious is your **steadfast love** (H2617, M05)… The children of mankind **take refuge** (H2620, M19) in the shadow of your wings. They **feast** on the abundance of your house, and you give them **drink** from the river of your **delights** (H5730, M04, *adanim* — Eden-delights). For with you is the **fountain of life** (H2416, M25); in your **light** (H0216) do we **see** light" (vv.7–9). The operations invert the wicked's closed self: this inner being **takes refuge, feasts, drinks** — it is **nourished from a source beyond itself** *(stated)*. Two findings sharpen it. First, **the life-source is external** — "with *you* is the fountain of life"; the inner being does not generate its own life but drinks it *(inferred: the life is *with you*, a fountain drunk from)*. Second, and answering vv.1–2's self-enclosed sight: "**in your light do we see light**" — the inner being's **perception is derivative**; it cannot see light by its own eyes but only **in a light not its own** *(stated)*. The wicked measures "in his own eyes" (v.2); the upright "see light" only *in God's* light (v.9) — two visual economies, self-lit versus light-receiving *(inferred)*.

**V. The two economies resolve (vv.10–12).**
"Continue your steadfast love to those who **know** you, and your righteousness to the **upright of heart** (H3820, the seat — M47)… Let not the foot of **arrogance** (H1346, M08) come upon me… There the **evildoers** lie fallen" (vv.10–12). The upright-*of-heart* (the seat named) stand in the open economy; the arrogant/wicked, closed in the self, "lie fallen, unable to rise" *(stated)*. The inner being's end tracks which economy it ran on *(inferred)*.

## What Psalm 36 says about the inner being
- **The heart is a space that is always *addressed* (v.1).** Something speaks in it — either the fear of God or, in its vacancy, transgression giving its inner oracle. What occupies that speaking-place forms the person.
- **The inner being can be its own flatterer (v.2).** Self-deception is an active operation — the self persuades the self, "in his own eyes," that its iniquity is safe. Self-enclosed sight.
- **Corruption is a *subtracted refusal* (vv.3–4).** The decisive failure is the lost "no" — "he does not reject evil"; the rejecting-faculty has ceased. The bed becomes the private workshop of plotting.
- **The open inner being is *fed from outside itself* (vv.7–9).** It takes refuge, feasts, drinks — its life-source is external ("with you is the fountain of life"), not self-generated.
- **Perception is derivative (v.9).** "In your light we see light" — the inner being cannot see by its own eyes; it sees only in a received light. This is the exact answer to the wicked's self-measuring "own eyes" — two visual economies.
- **The end tracks the economy (vv.10–12).** The upright-of-heart stand in the received-light, fed-from-outside economy; the arrogant, closed in the self, fall and cannot rise.

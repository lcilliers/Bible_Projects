## The reading

**I. Trembling at the presence — awe modelled by creation (v.7).**
"**Tremble**, O earth, at the presence of the LORD, at the presence of the God of Jacob" (v.7). The finding (by extension, not directly of the human inner being): the fitting response to God's presence is a *trembling awe* — here commanded of the *earth* and its mountains/sea *(stated of creation)*. The operation: the psalm holds up **reverent trembling as the *right* response to the divine presence** — creation's skipping and fleeing *models* the awe the inner being owes; the earth's tremor is the pattern of the reverent heart *(inferred — and cautiously: the psalm speaks of *creation*, not the human self; the inner-being relevance is only that the trembling-at-presence it dramatises is the same awe the reverent self is elsewhere called to, cf.

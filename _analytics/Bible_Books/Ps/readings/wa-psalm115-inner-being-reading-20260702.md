- **Glory deflected from the self; *you become what you trust*; the threefold call to trust.** Psalm 115 opens with a self-effacing "Not to us… but to your name give glory," turns to a polemic against dead idols, and lands on a triple summons to trust. Its inner-being findings: the self **deflecting glory from itself to God** (v.1); the master-datum that **the worshipper is *conformed to* what he trusts** (v.8); and **trust as the fundamental posture**, with God as help and shield (vv.9–11).

## The reading

**I. Glory deflected from the self (v.1).**
"**Not to us**, O LORD, **not to us**, but to **your name give glory** (H3519, M22), for the sake of your steadfast love and your faithfulness!" (v.1). The gem-finding: the inner being **refuses glory *for itself*** and redirects it wholly to God — the doubled "not to us, not to us" *(stated)*. The operation: the self **deflects honour away from itself** — a deliberate *self-effacement* that will not let praise settle on the self but sends it to God's name; the inner move of *decreasing* so God is glorified *(inferred: the emphatic doubled "not to us" is an active turning-away of glory from the self — the inner being's humility enacted as a *refusal to receive* what belongs to God; cf. Ps 96:8, John 3:30; the opposite of the self-glorifying heart of Ps 10:3, 49:11)*.

**II. The master-datum — *you become what you trust* (vv.4–8).**
"Their **idols** (H6091, M27) are silver and gold, the work of human hands. They have mouths but do not speak, eyes but do not see… **Those who make them become like them; so do all who *trust* (H0982, M19) in them**" (vv.4–8). The gem-finding — one of the most important in the Psalter for the inner being: **the worshipper is *conformed to* the object of his trust** — those who make dead idols "*become like them*," and so do all who trust them *(stated)*. The operation: **trust/worship is *formative* of the self** — the inner being is *shaped into the likeness* of whatever it leans on; a lifeless, blind, deaf idol *deadens, blinds, and deafens* its worshipper; you *become what you trust* *(inferred: "those who make them become like them; so do all who trust in them" states a law of the inner life — the object of trust re-forms the truster into its own image; therefore trusting the living God *enlivens*, trusting dead things *deadens*; a master-datum for the whole study, cf. Ps 135:18, 2 Cor 3:18 "beholding… transformed")*.

**III. Trust as the fundamental posture — help and shield (vv.9–11).**
Threefold: "O Israel, **trust** in the LORD!… O house of Aaron, **trust** in the LORD!… You who **fear** (H3373, M01) the LORD, **trust** in the LORD! He is their **help and their shield**" (vv.9–11). The finding: the summons, pressed three times, is to **trust the LORD** — who is *help and shield* *(stated)*. The operation: the inner being is called to make **trust its *foundational stance*** — reiterated for every group (people, priests, God-fearers), the one posture on which all rest; and the God trusted is *protective* (help + shield) *(inferred: the triple "trust in the LORD… help and shield" makes trust the universal, non-negotiable inner posture — following directly from v.8, the point is *what* you trust determines what you become, so trust the *living* God; cf. Ps 118:8–9, 62:8)*.

## What Psalm 115 says about the inner being
- **The self can *deflect glory from itself* (v.1).** An active self-effacement — the doubled "not to us" — that refuses to let praise settle on the self and sends it wholly to God's name; humility enacted as a refusal to receive.
- **You are *conformed to what you trust* (v.8).** The master-datum: the object of trust re-forms the truster into its own likeness — dead idols deaden, blind, and deafen their worshippers; therefore what you lean on *makes* you. (So the living God *enlivens*.)
- **Trust is the *foundational inner posture* (vv.9–11).** Pressed three times for every group — the one stance on which all rest; and following v.8, the summons is precisely to trust the *living* God, who is help and shield.

## Honest notes
- **v.8 is a cornerstone** — "those who make them become like them; so do all who trust in them" states a *law of formation*: the inner being takes the shape of its object of trust/worship. This is a hinge datum linking M19 (trust) to the whole question of what forms the self (with Ps 135:18). I have foregrounded it as the psalm's central inner-being finding.

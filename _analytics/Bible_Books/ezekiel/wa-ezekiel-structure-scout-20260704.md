# Ezekiel — structure scout & segmentation plan (2026-07-04)

> 48 chapters, 1273 verses. Backfill + Phase-1 complete (1273/1273). A book of *visions, sign-acts, and oracles* — the prophet whose whole body is made a sign. Processed as oracle-passages / vision-units / sign-act scenes, in batches.

## Macro-structure & batch plan
| Batch | Chs | Focus / inner-being gems |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1–3 | the call — the throne-chariot & the glory (1); **eating the scroll** ("it was in my mouth as sweet as honey", 3:3); **the watchman** and the responsibility of warning (3:16-21) |
| 2 | 4–11 | the sign-acts of the siege; the **glory of the LORD departing the temple** (8–11); "**I will give them one heart, and a new spirit… a heart of flesh**" (11:19) |
| 3 | 12–19 | false prophets (13); the **faithless-foundling bride** (16); the two eagles/vine (17); **the soul that sins shall die / individual responsibility** and "**I have no pleasure in the death of the wicked**" (18); the lament for the princes (19) |
| 4 | 20–24 | the rebellious history (20); the sword songs; Oholah & Oholibah (23); the **death of Ezekiel's wife** as a sign, "**sigh, but not aloud**" (24) |
| 5 | 25–32 | oracles against the nations — Tyre's **pride** ("I am a god, I sit in the seat of gods", 28); the king of Tyre; Egypt's pride (thinner inner-being) |
| 6 | 33–37 | the **watchman** re-commissioned (33); "**I have no pleasure in the death of the wicked, but that he turn and live**" (33:11); the **shepherds** and the true Shepherd (34); the **new heart & new spirit** (36:26-27); the **valley of dry bones** (37) |
| 7 | 38–48 | Gog (38–39); the **new-temple vision** (40–48, largely architectural), the **river from the temple** (47), "**The LORD is there**" (48:35) |

## Approach
Oracle-passages and vision-units; the sign-acts (Ezekiel's body made a message) as scenes. Cross-chapter `verse_refs` where a vision spans chapters (the temple-glory 8–11; the temple vision 40–48 mostly folded to representative units). Nations-oracles (25–32) and temple-architecture (40–48) are thinner — proportionate units foregrounding the genuine inner-being gems (Tyre's pride; the river; "the LORD is there").

## Inner-being richness
Ezekiel's distinctive fields: the **prophet as sign** (his body, his silence, his wife's death all bear the message); the **watchman's responsibility** (3, 33); **individual moral responsibility** and God's "no pleasure in death" (18, 33); the **faithless bride** rescued as a foundling (16); the **departing and returning glory**; and supremely the **new heart / new spirit** (36:26) and the **dry bones** raised (37) — the interior that God alone can make alive.

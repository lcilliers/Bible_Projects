## DAN-appalled-vision · Daniel 8:1–27 · [D, multi] — the physical cost of the vision; overcome and sick, not understanding

**The reading.** Daniel sees the ram and the goat, and the "**little horn**" that grows against the host, takes away the regular offering, and "**throws truth to the ground**" (vv.9–12). Gabriel interprets, but the effect on the seer is bodily: "**I was frightened and fell on my face**" (v.17); "**I fell into a deep sleep**" (v.18); and at the end, "**I, Daniel, was overcome and lay sick for some days. Then I rose and went about the king's business, but I was appalled by the vision and did not understand it**" (v.27). The finding is **the vision *undoing the seer bodily* — frightened, prostrate, made sick for days — and leaving him "**appalled**" and *not understanding*** *(stated)*. The operation is **the interior *overwhelmed* by what it is shown — the true encounter with God's disclosures is *costly*, prostrating the receiver and even making him physically ill — and the honest admission that revelation can leave one *appalled and un-comprehending*: the seer does not pretend to a mastery he lacks ("did not understand it"), yet he "rose and went about the king's business" — carrying the unresolved weight back into ordinary duty** *(inferred: the chapter is unusually frank about the *physical and emotional cost* of receiving God's word — "frightened", "fell on my face", "deep sleep", "**overcome and lay sick for some days**", "**appalled**" (vv.17–27): the finding is that genuine encounter with God's heavier truths *wounds the body and the mind* (as with Habakkuk's trembling, 3:16, and Isaiah's anguish, 21:3–4) — it is not a serene download but an overwhelming that costs the receiver; and two honest notes complete it: (1) Daniel "**did not understand it**" (v.27) — the finding is that faithful reception does not require full comprehension; one can carry a true word one cannot yet decode, without pretending mastery; and (2) he "**rose and went about the king's business**" — the finding is that the interior burdened by an un-understood revelation still *returns to its ordinary duty*, holding the mystery while doing the day's work — neither paralysed by it nor pretending it away)*.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *the vision undoing the seer bodily ("overcome and lay sick for some days… frightened and fell on my face" — the physical cost of receiving God's heavier word)* (thread — a gem: genuine encounter wounds the receiver, not a serene download, w/ 8:17-27, 10:8, Hab 3:16, Isa 21:3-4); *faithful reception without full comprehension ("appalled by the vision and did not understand it" — carrying a true word one cannot yet decode, without pretending mastery)* (new — a gem: honesty about the limits of understanding, w/ 8:27, 12:8, 1 Pet 1:10-12); *returning to ordinary duty under an unresolved weight ("I rose and went about the king's business")* (new — the burdened interior still doing the day's work, w/ 8:27).

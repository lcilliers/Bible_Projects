## DAN-son-of-man-anxious · Daniel 7:1–28 · [D, multi] — the seer's anxiety at the vision; the matter kept in the heart

**The reading.** Daniel sees four beasts from the sea, the Ancient of Days enthroned amid fire, and "**one like a son of man**" given "**an everlasting dominion, which shall not pass away**" (vv.13–14); the saints receive the kingdom (v.18). But the vision *costs* the seer: "**As for me, Daniel, my spirit within me was anxious, and the visions of my head alarmed me**" (v.15); and at the end, "**my thoughts greatly alarmed me, and my color changed, but I kept the matter in my heart**" (v.28). The finding is **the seer *troubled and alarmed* by what he is shown — his spirit "anxious", his colour changed — yet *holding the disturbing revelation inwardly* ("I kept the matter in my heart")** *(stated)*. The operation is **the interior that receives a weighty, unsettling truth and *neither dismisses it nor blurts it out* but *keeps* it — carrying the alarm inwardly, holding what it does not fully understand — a posture of *inward retention* rather than premature resolution** *(inferred: the inner-being interest is that even the reception of glorious truth (the everlasting kingdom, the son of man) leaves the receiver *disturbed* — "my spirit… was **anxious**", "the visions **alarmed** me" (v.15): the finding is that God's deeper disclosures are often *unsettling* to the one who receives them, not comforting — they enlarge the horizon in ways that trouble the settled self (as with the seers of Isa 21:3–4, Hab 3:16); and Daniel's response is telling — "**I kept the matter in my heart**" (v.28): the finding is that the mature interior *holds* what it is given, especially what it cannot yet resolve — it does not rush to publish or to explain away, but *retains* the weighty thing inwardly, letting it work (the same as Mary "kept all these things, pondering them in her heart"); the alarmed but *retaining* heart is the fitting posture toward truth too large to master at once)*.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *the seer troubled by the vision ("my spirit within me was anxious… the visions alarmed me" — God's deeper disclosures unsettle the receiver)* (thread — a gem: weighty truth disturbs the settled self, not merely comforts, w/ 7:15,28, 8:27, Hab 3:16, Isa 21:3-4); *keeping the matter in the heart (inward retention of what cannot yet be resolved — neither dismissed nor blurted)* (new — a gem: holding the weighty thing inwardly, letting it work, w/ 7:28, Luke 2:19, Gen 37:11).

## DAN-greatly-loved-strengthened · Daniel 10:1–21 · [D, multi] — the strengthless self strengthened; greatly loved; the set-and-humbled heart heard from day one

**The reading.** After three weeks of mourning and fasting (vv.2–3), Daniel sees a man clothed in linen and is undone: "**no strength was left in me… I retained no strength**" (v.8); he falls in a deep sleep. Then the heartening: "**O Daniel, man greatly loved… fear not**" (v.11); and the disclosure that his prayer was answered from the start: "**from the first day that you set your heart to understand and humbled yourself before your God, your words have been heard, and I have come because of your words**" (v.12). He is touched and "**strengthened**" three times (vv.10,16,18): "**O man greatly loved, fear not, peace be with you; be strong and of good courage**" (v.19). The finding is **the *spent, trembling, strengthless* self *heartened* — told twice "**greatly loved**" and "**fear not**", and *strengthened* by touch — with the assurance that his humble, God-set prayer was *heard from the first day*** *(stated)*. The operation is **the interior *emptied of all its own strength* by the encounter ("no strength… no breath left in me") and then *re-supplied from outside* — strengthened by a hand, steadied by the words "greatly loved" and "fear not"; and reassured that the *humbling and heart-setting* it had done ("you set your heart to understand and humbled yourself") was *effective from day one*, even through a long delay** *(inferred: two inner-being gems. First, the *pattern of emptying-then-strengthening* — the vision leaves Daniel with "**no strength**" (repeated, vv.8,16,17), and only then is he "**strengthened**" by touch (three times): the finding is that God's strengthening often *follows* the interior's total depletion — the self must reach the end of its own strength to receive God's (as Isa 40:29–31, "he gives power to the faint"); and the strengthening is *relational* — "greatly loved… fear not… peace be with you" (v.19) — the trembling self steadied not by information but by *being told it is loved*. Second, the *effectiveness of the humble prayer from day one* — "**from the FIRST DAY that you set your heart to understand and humbled yourself… your words have been HEARD**" (v.12): the finding is that the delay in answer (twenty-one days, v.13) was *not* silence — the prayer was heard *the moment* it was prayed, and the delay was in the *delivery*, not the *hearing*; the interior that "sets its heart" and "humbles itself" is heard *immediately*, whatever the apparent lag, so the discouragement of unanswered-seeming prayer is met by the assurance that heaven registered it from the first day)*.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *emptied of strength, then strengthened from outside ("no strength left in me"… "strengthened" three times — God's strength following the self's total depletion)* (thread — a gem: strengthening after the end of one's own strength, w/ 10:8,18-19, Isa 40:29-31, 2 Cor 12:9); *steadied by being loved ("O man greatly loved, fear not, peace be with you" — the trembling self heartened by love, not information)* (new — a gem: fear answered by belovedness, w/ 10:11,19, Isa 41:10, 43:1); *the humble prayer heard from the first day ("from the first day that you set your heart… and humbled yourself, your words have been heard" — the delay in delivery, not in hearing)* (new — a gem: prayer registered immediately whatever the apparent lag, w/ 10:12-13, Ps 10:17, Luke 18:7); *the set-and-humbled heart as the posture that is heard ("you set your heart to understand and humbled yourself")* (thread — w/ 10:12, 1:8, 9:3).

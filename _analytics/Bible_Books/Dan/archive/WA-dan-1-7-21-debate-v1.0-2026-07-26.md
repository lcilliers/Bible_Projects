## Preliminaries

**Working scope (declared as assumption, unchanged from prior debate).** An operation bears on the inner being when the text or span-data names or denotes an *interior* dimension of a human — state, faculty, disposition, affect, or volition — or a movement whose stated source or target is such an interior dimension. Applied interrogatively (a stated act opens, not closes, the interior question). The scope fork (interior-named-only vs. also non-human agency on human circumstance) remains **open and unsettled by the researcher**; flagged where it bites.

**Reading rule applied.** For `[AMBIGUOUS]` spans, the `STEP live` code-specific gloss is authoritative over the base `meaning_tree`.

**Data-state note (new here).** Coverage for this range is **168 / 183 non-particle spans (92%)** — i.e. 15 spans are "(not yet registered)". Several unregistered spans fall on inner-being-relevant words (listed in the insufficiencies register). Where an operation leans on an unregistered span, that is named, not filled from outside.

**Bearing on the prior fork.** This range contains two explicit divine→interior operations (1:9, 1:17). They give textual warrant that *within Daniel 1 the text does source interiors to God* — but selectively (favourable interiors given to the faithful, and favour in another's regard). They do **not** retroactively settle whether Nebuchadnezzar's 1:2 disposition was Lord-sourced; that asymmetry is itself a finding (see EQ-6).

## Dan 1:8 — the pivot: resolve seated in the heart

> But Daniel resolved that he would not defile himself with the king's food, or with the wine that he drank. Therefore he asked the chief of the eunuchs to allow him not to defile himself.

**Observation.** "Resolved" is a three-span cluster — H7760A (*set / put / make*) + H5921A (*upon*) + **H3820A (*leb*, heart)**: literally *Daniel set upon his heart*. The interior seat (**heart**) is present as a lexical item in the text, not merely implied. The base gloss of *leb* spans inner man, mind, will, understanding, resolution/determination of will, conscience, moral character, seat of emotions. "Defile" is H1351 (*gaal* — pollute, desecrate; moral/ritual). Then "asked" (H1245, *baqash* — seek, request) the chief of the eunuchs.

**Interpretation — questions considered.**
- Q1/Q2/Q3: Daniel is a focused inner being with a **stated** interior. Because *leb* is lexically present, this is the interior *stated*, not inferred — the strongest such case in Daniel 1 so far. (This directly advances **EQ-1**: a named interior-seat word = stated; contrast the *amar* case at 1:3, where the interior was only lexically latent = inferred.)
- Q6: Two operations, linked. (i) A **state→movement**: an interior determination of the will (resolve) seated in the heart; (ii) a **movement outward**: the request to the eunuch that the resolve produces.
- Q4 (source): the resolve is stated as Daniel's own — self-sourced volition. Whether God stands behind it is *not stated here* (contrast 1:9, 1:17 where divine sourcing of interiors *is* stated); raised as the recurring fork, left open, not imported.
- Q5 (target): the resolve targets Daniel's own conduct/integrity (his own inner being as object), with the king's food/wine as the medium; the request targets the eunuch (human).
- Q9 (insufficiency): the text states Daniel regards the food as defiling, but the extract does **not** carry *why* (the dietary/covenantal grounds). The interior stance is stated; its grounding is insufficiently evidenced in-document. Named, not supplied.

**Reflection.** This verse **closes the forward-pointer** left at 1:5 in the prior debate: the youths' interior stance toward the king's provision, unstated there, is now stated here — as Daniel's heart-seated refusal. The B+D pointer mechanism lands.

**Decision.** **Retain** two linked operations: interior resolve (self-sourced, heart-seated, targeting own integrity) → producing an outward request (targeting the eunuch). Grounding of the "defile" judgement flagged insufficient.

## Dan 1:9 — divine sourcing of an interior disposition

> And God gave Daniel favor and compassion in the sight of the chief of the eunuchs,

**Observation.** God (H0430G) "gave" (H5414G) Daniel "favor" (H2617A, *chesed* — goodness, kindness, faithfulness) and "compassion" (H7356B, *rachamim* — compassion; code-specific line corrects the base tree's "womb" to *compassion*) "in the sight of" (H6440L, *lifney* — before the face / presence of) the chief of the eunuchs.

**Interpretation — questions considered.**
- Q4 (the decisive one for the fork): here the text **states** a non-human being (God) as source of an **interior/relational disposition** — favour and compassion. Unlike 1:2 (where only the *outcome* was Lord-sourced), 1:9 sources an *interior* directly. This is the passage's clearest divine→interior operation on a disposition.
- Q5 (target — a genuine ambiguity, recorded as referential debate, not resolved): two readings sit in the text. (a) The favour/compassion is a quality *given to Daniel* that then lands in the eunuch's perception; target = Daniel's standing, realised in the eunuch's regard. (b) God moves *the eunuch's* interior to regard Daniel with favour/compassion; target = the eunuch's disposition. The phrase "in the sight of / before the face of the chief" leaves both open. Both are recorded; neither imported as the settled reading.
- Q1 (the eunuch as focused inner being): under reading (b), his interior is the operated-upon field; under (a), his perception is the arena. Either way he is in focus.
- Q6: movement (a giving).

**Reflection.** 1:9 is the hinge that makes Daniel's 1:8 request survivable: an interior resolve (1:8) meets a divinely-sourced favour (1:9). The linkage between the two is explicit and load-bearing (Q7).

**Decision.** **Retain** a divine→interior operation (God → favour/compassion, targeting the Daniel/eunuch relational field). Target-vantage recorded as an open referential debate.

## Dan 1:10 — the official's stated fear, and his reasoning

> and the chief of the eunuchs said to Daniel, "I fear my lord the king, who assigned your food and your drink; for why should he see that you were in worse condition than the youths who are of your own age? So you would endanger my head with the king."

**Observation.** The eunuch "said" (H0559) and states "I fear" (H3373, *yare* — fearing, afraid). His reasoning: the king "assigned" (H4487) the food; "why should he *see*" (H7200G, *ra'ah* — see, perceive, observe, discern) the youths "in worse condition" (H2196, **not yet registered**); "you would endanger" (H2325, **not yet registered**) "my head".

**Interpretation — questions considered.**
- Q1/Q2/Q3 (eunuch as focused inner being): his interior is **stated** — fear. This **closes another prior forward-pointer**: in the 1:1–7 debate the officials' interior was left unstated; here the same office-holder's interior is explicitly given.
- Q4 (source of the fear): stated — the king (a human) and the situation (risk to his own head). Source of state is here explicit and human/situational, not divine.
- Q5 (target): his fear shapes his refusal-response to Daniel; target = the negotiation, and by extension Daniel's request.
- Q6: state (fear) issuing in a movement (his objection).
- Q9 (insufficiency): "worse condition" and "endanger" are unregistered; the *intensity* and exact force of his fear-reasoning lean on spans the extract does not gloss. Named.

**Reflection.** Two interiors now stand in tension across 1:8–1:10: Daniel's heart-seated resolve vs. the eunuch's self-preserving fear. The passage advances by the collision of two stated inner beings — exactly the multi-IB case the read guidance anticipates (note 2a).

**Decision.** **Retain** the eunuch's fear as a stated interior state (human/situational source) issuing in a refusal-movement toward Daniel.

## Dan 1:11–1:13 — Daniel's counter-proposal

> [1:11] Then Daniel said to the steward whom the chief of the eunuchs had assigned over Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah, [1:12] "Test your servants for ten days; let us be given vegetables to eat and water to drink. [1:13] Then let our appearance and the appearance of the youths who eat the king's food be observed by you, and deal with your servants according to what you see."

**Observation.** Daniel "said" (H0559) to the steward (H4453, **not yet registered**). He proposes: "Test" (H5254G, *nasah* — test, try, prove) "ten days"; "be given" (H5414G) vegetables and water; then "let our appearance… be observed" (H7200G, *ra'ah*) "and deal" (H6213A, *asah* — do, deal) "according to what you see" (H7200G).

**Interpretation — questions considered.**
- Q1/Q2 (Daniel): the proposal carries interior content — resolve now translating into *strategy* (a devised, prudent plan to secure the resolve without endangering the official). The interior driving it is the continuation of the 1:8 resolve; the *devising* itself is a movement of practical wisdom.
- Q3: the interior (resolve, prudence) is inferred from the shape of the proposal, though anchored in the stated resolve of 1:8; named as inference-with-antecedent.
- Q5 (target): the steward (human), invited to become the assessor.
- Q4: source of the plan — Daniel (self). The plan proposes an *empirical criterion* (visible appearance) as the arbiter — an externalisation by which an interior conviction seeks vindication in observable outcome.
- Q6: movement (proposal, request).
- Q9: "steward" (H4453) unregistered; the office is under-specified in the extract.

**Reflection.** Note the epistemic move (surface for future analysis): Daniel stakes an interior conviction on a *visible* test. The inner being here seeks external, observable warrant — a pattern worth watching across the corpus (logged as EQ-7).

**Decision.** **Retain** a proposal-operation (Daniel's resolve → devised plan → steward as invited assessor), continuous with 1:8.

## Dan 1:14 — the steward consents

> So he listened to them in this matter, and tested them for ten days.

**Observation.** "Listened" (H8085G, *shama* — hear, listen, give heed, **consent, agree, grant request**, yield, obey) "in this matter" (H1697I, *dabar* — matter/thing); "tested" (H5254G) ten days.

**Interpretation — questions considered.**
- Q1/Q2 (steward as focused inner being): *shama* here carries, in its own registered range, the sense of **consent/yielding** — so an interior movement (assent) is lexically supported, not merely inferred. (Another **EQ-1** datum: interior carried in a verb's registered sense-range, without a seat-word — between "stated" and "inferred".)
- Q5 (target): Daniel's proposal (an operation); the steward's assent lands on it and enables the test.
- Q6: movement (assent → action).
- Q7: linkage — the steward's consent is the hinge between Daniel's proposal (1:12–13) and the outcome (1:15).

**Decision.** **Retain** a consent-operation (steward's interior assent → enacting the test), lexically supported.

## Dan 1:15 — the visible vindication

> At the end of ten days it was seen that they were better in appearance and fatter in flesh than all the youths who ate the king's food.

**Observation.** "It was seen" (H7200G, Niphal — was seen/appeared); "better" (H2896A, *tov*) in "appearance" (H4758); "fatter" (H1277, **not yet registered**) in "flesh" (H1320, *basar*).

**Interpretation — questions considered.**
- Q2/Q5/Q6: the outcome itself is **outward/physical** (appearance, flesh). Under the conservative scope, no interior is stated here → not an IB operation in its own right.
- Q7 (the load-bearing linkage): it is nonetheless the **vindication** of the interior resolve (1:8) via the empirical criterion Daniel set (1:13). The physical result and the interior conviction are linked, and the linkage — not the physique — is the finding. Surfaced, not passed over.

**Decision.** **Set aside** as a stated IB operation; **retain the linkage** — visible outcome vindicating the 1:8 resolve through the 1:13 criterion.

## Dan 1:17 — divine gift of interior faculties

> As for these four youths, God gave them learning and skill in all literature and wisdom, and Daniel had understanding in all visions and dreams.

**Observation.** God (H0430G) "gave" (H5414G) the four "learning" (H4093, *madda* — knowledge, **mind**, thought) and "skill" (H7919A, *sakal* — prudence, insight) in "literature" (H5612H) and "wisdom" (H2451, *chokmah*); and Daniel "had understanding" (H0995, *bin* — discern, understand) in "visions" (H2377, **not yet registered**) and "dreams" (H2472, **not yet registered**).

**Interpretation — questions considered.**
- Q4 (decisive): the interior faculties (learning, skill, wisdom, understanding) are **stated as God-given**. This **closes prior insufficiency #3** (the 1:4 source of the youths' faculties was unstated) — the source is now, in-text, God.
- Q5 (target): the four youths' interior (collective, Q8), with Daniel individuated for a further faculty (understanding in visions/dreams).
- Q6: movement (a giving), producing a durable interior state.
- Q7: sharp contrast-linkage with 1:4 — there the youths were *selected for* an interior excellence and *set to be taught* Chaldean learning; here the decisive interior endowment is sourced not to the Chaldean formation but to **God**. The passage quietly re-sources the interior excellence away from the Babylonian apparatus. Surfaced.
- Q9: "visions" and "dreams" unregistered; the *content-domain* of Daniel's distinctive understanding leans on unglossed spans. Named.

**Reflection.** With 1:9 and 1:17 together, the text twice sources an interior to God within this chapter — favourable disposition (1:9) and cognitive faculty (1:17). This is strong in-corpus warrant that the wider scope (divine agency reaching interiors) is textually real *here*. The asymmetry — God is named source of *good/faithful* interiors, but not of the adversary's disposition (1:2) — is logged as EQ-6, not resolved.

**Decision.** **Retain** a divine→interior operation (God → interior faculties, targeting the four collectively and Daniel distinctly). Closes prior insufficiency #3.

## Dan 1:18–1:19 — presentation and the king's assessment

> [1:18] At the end of the time, when the king had commanded that they should be brought in, the chief of the eunuchs brought them in before Nebuchadnezzar. [1:19] And the king spoke with them, and among all of them none was found like Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah. Therefore they stood before the king.

**Observation.** King "commanded" (H0559); eunuch "brought them in" (H0935P) "before" (H6440G) Nebuchadnezzar. King "spoke with" (H1696G, *dabar*); "none was found" (H4672, *matsa*) "like" them; they "stood" (H5975G, *amad*) "before the king".

**Interpretation — questions considered.**
- Q1/Q2 (king as focused inner being): "spoke with… none found like" implies an **assessment/judgement** — an interior act of evaluation, lexically latent (a finding-of-none-like presupposes discernment). Inferred, named.
- Q5 (target): the youths' interior excellence is the object of the king's assessment.
- Q7 (telos realised): "they stood before the king" **closes the 1:5 telos** ("to stand before the king") set in the prior passage — the formation's stated goal is reached. Explicit long-range linkage.
- Q6: 1:18 is outward movement (bringing in); 1:19 carries the king's interior assessment + the realised standing.

**Decision.** **Retain** a king's-assessment operation (interior evaluation → targeting the youths' excellence), and record the telos-closure of 1:5.

## Dan 1:20 — interior excellence as the measured object

> And in every matter of wisdom and understanding about which the king inquired of them, he found them ten times better than all the magicians and enchanters that were in all his kingdom.

**Observation.** "Wisdom" (H2451) and "understanding" (H0998, *binah* — understanding, discernment; *act / faculty*); king "inquired" (H1245, *baqash*); "found" (H4672) them "ten times better" (H3027L, *yad* — idiom of measure/degree) than "all the magicians" (H2748, **not yet registered**) and "enchanters" (H0825, **not yet registered**).

**Interpretation — questions considered.**
- Q2/Q5: the youths' **interior faculties** (wisdom, understanding — the latter registered as *faculty*) are the object measured; the king's inquiry+finding is an interior assessment targeting that interior.
- Q4: the excellence assessed here is, by 1:17, God-sourced — so the king measures (without the text saying he recognises the source) an interior whose origin the reader has been told is divine. A gap between what is measured and what is acknowledged — surfaced.
- Q8 (comparison collective): "magicians and enchanters" form a comparison class; both unregistered, so the *nature* of that class is under-specified in the extract (Q9 insufficiency).
- Q6: state (a comparative standing), established via the king's assessment-movement.

**Reflection.** The chapter's interior thread culminates in measurement: an interior excellence, divinely sourced (1:17), is quantified by an outsider ("ten times") against the kingdom's expert class. The inner being is here rendered as something assessed and ranked — worth watching as a recurring move (feeds EQ-7).

**Decision.** **Retain** an assessment operation (king's inquiry → measuring the youths' God-sourced interior excellence against the magician/enchanter class), with the comparison class flagged insufficiently specified.

## Passage-level linkages (Q7)

1. **The two-interior collision (1:8–1:16):** Daniel's heart-seated resolve (1:8) vs. the eunuch's fear (1:10), mediated by God-given favour (1:9), routed around via Daniel's devised test (1:12–13), enabled by the steward's consent (1:14), vindicated in visible outcome (1:15), enacted (1:16). A single connected chain of stated and inferred interiors.
2. **Re-sourcing of the interior excellence (1:4 → 1:17):** selected-for and set-to-be-taught in 1:4; decisively **God-given** in 1:17. The text relocates the origin of the excellence from the Chaldean apparatus to God.
3. **Telos closure (1:5 → 1:19):** "to stand before the king" (goal) → "they stood before the king" (realised).
4. **Divine→interior sourcing attested (1:9, 1:17)** — bearing on, but not closing, the 1:2 fork (asymmetry logged EQ-6).

## Emergent questions log (carry forward)

- **EQ-6.** Does the text source interiors to God only for *favourable/faithful* interiors (favour, compassion, wisdom — 1:9, 1:17), while leaving adversarial dispositions un-sourced (Nebuchadnezzar, 1:2)? If so, the 1:2 fork may be answerable by the *pattern* of divine-interior sourcing rather than by 1:2 alone.
- **EQ-7.** Recurring move: an interior conviction/excellence is staked on, or rendered as, a *visible/measurable* outcome (1:13 appearance-test; 1:20 "ten times better"). Is externalisation-of-the-interior a describable pattern of the inner being across the corpus?
- **EQ-1 (advanced).** This range gives a three-point gradient for "stated vs inferred" interior: (a) seat-word present = stated (1:8, *leb*); (b) interior carried in a verb's registered sense-range = between (1:14, *shama* = consent); (c) interior only latent behind an act = inferred (1:19, king's assessment). A rule keyed to this gradient may be worth adopting.

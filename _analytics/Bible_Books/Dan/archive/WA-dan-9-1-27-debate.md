## Preliminaries

**Working scope (declared as assumption, not fact).** Dan 9 is Daniel's own first-person prayer of confession (9:3-19), framed by a chronological notice (9:1-2) and followed by Gabriel's visit and the seventy-weeks oracle (9:20-27). It differs in kind from the throne-room and ram-goat visions of chapters 7-8: this is prayer and its answer, not a symbolic vision. In scope: Daniel's own stated interior acts (perceiving, turning, seeking, praying, confessing, pleading) in 9:2-4 and 9:20; the corporate "we/us/our" confession of 9:5-19 as far as it states or implies an interior stance (sinning, not listening, not turning) attributable to the people as a collective, per Q8; Gabriel as a named heavenly figure, checked per note (f) for stated interior content at each appearance; Daniel as the addressee of Gabriel's speech (9:22-23), including the status-attribution "you are greatly loved." The seventy-weeks unit (9:24-27) is a decree about future events, actions, and named figures ("anointed one," "prince who is to come," "one who makes desolate") — each is checked per note (f) as a presumptive candidate; none carries stated or inferable interior content, and each is recorded as an explicit silence rather than omitted.

**Reading rule applied.** Standard STEP-live precedence per base extract; no [AMBIGUOUS] spans encountered in this range.

**Corpus-continuity check.** Read `WA-dan-8-1-27-debate.md` before drafting this chapter. Carried-forward threads: the H0995 "understand/discern" word-family, prominent through Dan 8 (8:5, 8:15-17, 8:23, 8:27), recurs through Dan 9 alongside the related H7919 "be prudent/have insight" root; Gabriel, introduced by name at 8:16, is explicitly re-identified by Daniel at 9:21 as "the man Gabriel, whom I had seen in the vision at the first" — a direct self-citation of the chapter 8 episode; Dan 8:27 records Daniel's own unresolved incomprehension ("I was appalled by the vision and did not understand it"), a thread this chapter's Gabriel scene (9:22-23) directly addresses.

## Passage-level linkages (Q7)

- **H0995 "understand/discern/consider"** — recurs at 9:2 (Daniel's own initial perceiving of Jeremiah's word), 9:22 (Gabriel "made me understand"), and 9:23 (twice: "consider the word and understand the vision"). The chapter moves from Daniel's own act of perceiving (9:2) to Gabriel's granting and directing of understanding (9:22-23).
- **H7919 "be prudent/have insight"** — recurs at 9:13 (the people's stated failure to gain insight), 9:22 (Gabriel's stated purpose to give insight), and 9:25 (the directive to "understand" the seventy-weeks timeline). The failure named at 9:13 is directly answered at 9:22, using the same root.
- **H8085 "hear/listen/obey"** — recurs at 9:6, 9:10, 9:11, 9:14 (the people's stated past failure to hear/obey) and again at 9:17-19 (Daniel's plea for God to hear). The same root is used first of the people's non-hearing, then inverted in Daniel's own petition for God's hearing.
- **H7725 "return/turn"** — recurs at 9:13 (the people's failure to turn from iniquity), 9:16 (Daniel's petition for God's anger to turn away), and 9:25 (Jerusalem "built again"). Three distinct applications of the same root within one chapter: absent turning, petitioned turning, and physical rebuilding.
- **Sin/transgression/iniquity vocabulary (H2398, H2403, H6588, H5771)** — dense through the confession (9:5, 9:8, 9:11, 9:13, 9:15-16, 9:20) and recurs in the decree at 9:24, where the same categories named in confession are named as the object of a future resolution. The decree does not restate interior content; the linkage is lexical, not a repeated IB-event.
- **Gabriel** — named at Dan 8:16; explicitly re-identified by Daniel at 9:21 ("the man Gabriel, whom I had seen in the vision at the first"), a direct cross-chapter self-citation.
- **"Understand the vision" (9:23)** — echoes the instruction given to Gabriel at Dan 8:16 ("make this man understand the vision") and directly addresses Daniel's own unresolved incomprehension recorded at Dan 8:27 ("I was appalled by the vision and did not understand it").
- **Absence surfaced:** the confession (9:5-19) names no individual sinner by title or name — only collective "we," "our fathers," "our kings," "our princes." No king, father, or prince is individuated within the confession itself; 9:20 is the sole point where Daniel distinguishes "my sin" from "the sin of my people Israel."
- **Absence surfaced:** the seventy-weeks unit (9:24-27) is a sustained nine-verse stretch with no stated or inferable interior content for any named or implied human figure, despite naming several distinct candidates ("your people," "an anointed one, a prince," "the people of the prince who is to come," "he," "one who makes desolate"). Each was checked individually per note (f); all resolve to recorded silences.

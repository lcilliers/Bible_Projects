## Preliminaries

**Working scope (declared as assumption, unchanged from prior debates).** An operation bears on the inner being when the text or span-data names or denotes an *interior* dimension of a human — state, faculty, disposition, affect, or volition — or a movement whose stated source or target is such an interior dimension. Applied interrogatively (a stated act opens, not closes, the interior question). The scope fork (interior-named-only vs. also non-human agency on human circumstance) remains **open and unsettled by the researcher**, carried forward from both prior debates.

**Reading rule applied.** For `[AMBIGUOUS]` spans, the `STEP live` code-specific gloss is authoritative over the base `meaning_tree`. **Not exercised in this range** — the base extract carries no `[AMBIGUOUS]` flags; all 206 non-particle spans resolve cleanly to a single `meaning_tree` reading. Recorded as a data-state note, not a finding.

**New scope question raised in this range (see Dan 2:20, 2:22).** The prior two debates treated the inner being strictly as a *human* dimension, with God appearing only as *source* or *target* of operations on human interiors (1:2, 1:9, 1:17). This range is the first to contain interior-type predicates (wisdom, might, knowledge, revealing, knowing) applied to **God as possessor**, not as source-acting-on-a-human. Whether the instrument should track these as IB-analogous items, or treat them as strictly out of scope (the instrument being built for the *human* inner being), is a new fork — held open, not resolved by import.

**Bearing on the prior forks.** This range contains the first **stated** interior content for Nebuchadnezzar (2:29–30) since he was introduced at 1:1 — a long-open thread, closed here (see Closed items). It also contains a divine→human operation whose target is the *pagan king himself* (2:28–29), which bears on, without resolving, the passage-level divine-sourcing fork from Dan 1:2 and on EQ-6 from the 1:7–21 debate (see Dan 2:28–29 below).

## Dan 2:17

> Then Daniel went to his house and made the matter known to Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah, his companions,

**Observation.** Daniel "went" (H0236) to his house and "made known" (H3046 Aphel, *yada* — to know / cause to know) the matter (H4406, *millah* — word, thing, matter) to his three companions (H2269, *chaver* — associate, companion).

**Interpretation — questions considered.**
- Q1/Q2 (Daniel): no interior word is present; the act is outward — travel and disclosure. Any urgency or distress behind the disclosure is inferable from context (the death-decree of 2:1–16) but the verse states neither.
- Q3: any interior here is inference without lexical support; named, not asserted.
- Q5: target — the three companions (human, collective, Q8), brought into shared knowledge of the crisis.
- Q6: movement (communication).
- Q7: this verse's real weight is prospective — it is the **substrate** that makes 2:18's shared petition possible, structurally parallel to how 1:6 (identity/belonging) was substrate for 1:7 (the relabeling operation).

**Decision.** **Set aside** as a stated IB operation; **retain** the communicative movement as substrate for 2:18.

## Dan 2:18

> and told them to seek mercy from the God of heaven concerning this mystery, so that Daniel and his companions might not be destroyed with the rest of the wise men of Babylon.

**Observation.** Daniel tells them "to seek" (H1156, *bĕʿah* — to ask, seek, request, desire, pray, make petition) "mercy" (H7359, *rachamin* — compassion; Aramaic of *rachamim* H7356B) from "the God of heaven," concerning "this mystery" (H7328, *raz* — secret), so that they "might not be destroyed" (H0007, *abad* — perish/be destroyed) "with the rest" of Babylon's wise men.

**Interpretation — questions considered.**
- Q1/Q2 (the four, collective): the united petition carries an implied interior — urgency, fear of death, dependence on God. Not lexically stated as an emotion, but the *content* of the petition (asking, seeking, desiring mercy) is itself an interior-laden verb (H1156's own range: ask/seek/**desire**/pray).
- Q3: the petition-act is stated (lexically present as "seek"); the disposition driving it (fear, faith-dependence) is inferred, named as inference.
- Q4: source of the petition — the four themselves, self-initiated (at Daniel's prompting). This is a self-sourced movement Godward, structurally the same pattern as Daniel's 1:8 resolve and 1:12–13 devised test: an interior conviction/need seeking resolution through a deliberate, devised act — here prayer rather than a dietary experiment.
- Q5: target — God (non-human), as recipient of the petition; **what is sought is itself an interior of God** ("mercy/compassion") — the mirror image of 1:9, where a divine interior (favour/compassion — the same root, H7356B/H7359) was *given*; here it is *sought*. Worth naming: the same "compassion" vocabulary spans both an unsolicited divine gift (1:9) and a solicited one (2:18).
- Q6: movement — a petition seeking a divine interior disposition, with a stated existential stake (death or survival).
- Q7: opens a thread closed explicitly at 2:19 (revelation granted) and 2:23 (Daniel's own naming of "what we asked of you"). Also a comparison-class reference ("the rest of the wise men of Babylon") — echoes 1:20's comparison class, now as a shared fate rather than a measured contrast.

**Decision.** **Retain** a petition-operation: the four's self-sourced movement, Godward, seeking a named divine interior (mercy/compassion) as the means of survival. Opens the thread closed at 2:19/2:23.

## Dan 2:19

> Then the mystery was revealed to Daniel in a vision of the night. Then Daniel blessed the God of heaven.

**Observation.** The mystery "was revealed" (H1541, *gelah* — to reveal (secrets), Peal passive) to Daniel "in a vision" (H2376, *chezev* — vision, appearance) "of the night." Daniel then "blessed" (H1289, *berak* — to bless, praise) the God of heaven.

**Interpretation — questions considered.** Two linked operations.
1. **Divine→human movement** — God reveals the mystery to Daniel via a night vision. Directly parallel to 1:17 ("God gave them... understanding in all visions and dreams" for Daniel specifically) — the same vision/dream channel now delivers content, not merely a durable faculty.
   - Q4: source explicitly God (implicit subject of the passive "was revealed," confirmed at 2:19b/2:23 as "the God of heaven").
   - Q5: target — Daniel, individuated (though the mystery concerns all four's survival, Q8).
   - Q6: movement (revealing).
   - Q7: **closes** the petition opened at 2:18 — mercy sought, revelation granted. A clean, explicit fulfillment link, stronger than most in Daniel 1 (contrast the still-open 1:2 fork).
2. **Daniel's responsive interior act** — "blessed" is Daniel's own outward-directed movement, self-sourced gratitude/reverence in immediate response to the granted revelation. Continuous with, and answering, his own 1:8 resolve-and-devise pattern: here the response to answered prayer is praise, not further strategy.
   - Q4: source — Daniel himself (a human response to a divine act, distinct from the divine act itself; the two must not be merged, per the same discipline EQ-2/Q4 already required at 1:2).
   - Q6: movement (blessing/praise).
   - Q7: opens directly into the extended prayer of 2:20–23, which unpacks this blessing in full.

**Decision.** **Retain** two linked operations: the revelation (God → Daniel, closing 2:18) and Daniel's responsive blessing (self-sourced, opening into 2:20–23).

## Dan 2:20

> Daniel answered and said: "Blessed be the name of God forever and ever, to whom belong wisdom and might.

**Observation.** Daniel predicates of God's name: "blessed" (H1289) forever, and states that "wisdom" (H2452, *chokmah*) and "might" (H1370, *gevurah*) "belong" (H1932 + H9033, an existential/possessive construction) to him.

**Interpretation — questions considered.**
- Q6: a **state operation** — but here the possessor is God, not a human. Wisdom and might are named as *possessed attributes* of the divine subject, structurally identical in form to 1:4's "state operation" (the youths possess prudence, wisdom, knowledge) — but the subject has shifted from human to divine.
- **New scope question (flagged in Preliminaries):** does this count as an IB item under an instrument built for the *human* inner being, or is it categorically outside scope (a theological predicate, not an anthropological one)? Not resolved here; named as a fork distinct from, but structurally parallel to, the 1:2 scope fork (there: does divine agency on human circumstance count; here: does a divine attribute count at all).
- Q7: sets the frame for 2:21 (wisdom/might operationalised as *given* to humans) and 2:22 (further divine faculties named) — 2:20 states the attributes are God's to give; 2:21 shows him giving them.

**Decision.** **Flag, do not force.** If the wider (divine-inclusive) scope is adopted, this is a state-operation with God as subject; under the strict human-only reading, **set aside**. Recorded as an open scope question, not resolved by import.

## Dan 2:21

> He changes times and seasons; he removes kings and sets up kings; he gives wisdom to the wise and knowledge to those who have understanding;

**Observation.** God "changes" (H8133) times and seasons; "removes" (H5709, *avad* — to pass on/away; Aphel: take away, remove, depose) and "sets up" (H6966I, Aphel of *qum* — to establish, appoint) kings; "gives" (H3052, *yehav*) "wisdom" (H2452) to "the wise" (H2445) and "knowledge" (H4486, *manda*) to "those who have understanding" (H3046 + prefixes — lit. "to those who know").

**Interpretation — questions considered.** Two operations, textually adjacent but distinct in kind.
1. **Kings removed/set up** — this concerns *standing/circumstance* (structurally parallel to 1:2's "gave Jehoiakim into his hand"), not stated as reaching the kings' *interior*. Under the conservative scope: outward/circumstantial, set aside as a stated IB operation, but retained as bearing on the passage-level fork (it patterns with the "outcome/circumstance" side, not the "disposition" side — a data point *against* the wider scope extending this far, though not conclusive).
2. **Wisdom/knowledge given to the wise/understanding** — an explicit, general **divine→human interior operation**: God as stated source (Q4), giving interior faculties (wisdom, knowledge) to a human class (Q5, Q8: "the wise," "those who have understanding" — a class, not named individuals).
   - Q6: movement (giving), the general principle of which 1:17 and 2:19/2:23 are specific narrated instances.
   - Q7: **major linkage** — this is the passage's clearest statement of a *general theological principle* behind the specific divine-giving events already narrated (1:17: God gave the four learning/skill; 2:19: God revealed the mystery to Daniel; 2:23: Daniel testifies "you have given me wisdom"). 2:21 supplies the rule of which those are cases.
   - **Bears on EQ-6** (does divine interior-sourcing favour only the faithful?): 2:21 states the principle in general, unqualified terms ("the wise," "those who have understanding" — not restricted to Daniel's circle) — this widens, rather than narrows, the textual warrant for divine sourcing of interior faculties, though it still speaks of *wisdom/knowledge* (a cognitive faculty), not *favourable disposition* (character, resolve) — the content/disposition distinction raised again here (see Dan 2:28–29 below, and new EQ-8).

**Decision.** **Set aside** the kings-removed/set-up clause as a stated IB operation (circumstance, not interior); **retain** the wisdom/knowledge-giving clause as a general divine→interior principle, feeding the passage's wisdom-sourcing thread (1:4 → 1:17 → 2:21 → 2:23 → 2:30).

- Q7: reinforces 2:19's "revealed... in a vision" and anticipates 2:28's "God in heaven who reveals mysteries" — a doxological restatement, in general terms, of what the narrative has already shown God doing to/for Daniel specifically.

## Dan 2:23

> To you, O God of my fathers, I give thanks and praise, for you have given me wisdom and might, and have now made known to me what we asked of you, for you have made known to us the king's matter."

**Observation.** Daniel "gives thanks" (H3029) and "praise" (H7624) to God, "for" God has "given" (H3052) him "wisdom" (H2452) and "might" (H1370), and has "made known" (H3046) to him — and then, in the closing clause, "to us" — "what we asked" (H1156, the same root as 2:18's "seek") and "the king's matter."

**Interpretation — questions considered.**
- Q4 (decisive): first-person, explicit — Daniel himself states God as source: "**you** have given me wisdom and might." This is the personal, testimonial counterpart to 2:21's general principle and 1:17's narrated fact — the same claim now made in Daniel's own voice.
- Q5 (target — a clean example for EQ-4): the verse shifts from singular ("given **me**... made known to **me**") to plural ("made known to **us**") within three clauses. The text itself records both an individuated target (Daniel) and a collective one (the four) for what is substantially the same divine act — a live textual instance of the EQ-4 question (recorded once vs. per-member vs. both), answered here by the text doing **both**, not by the analyst choosing one.
- Q7: this verse **explicitly closes** the petition opened at 2:18 — "what **we asked** of you" is the text's own cross-reference, the strongest such closure-marker in either passage debated so far (contrast the merely inferential linkages elsewhere).
- Q6: movement (giving/making-known) with a durable resultant state (Daniel now possesses wisdom/might; the four now possess the answer).

**Decision.** **Retain** an explicit divine→interior operation (God → Daniel individually and the four collectively — both recorded, per the text's own dual framing), closing 2:18. Continues the wisdom-sourcing thread (1:4 → 1:17 → 2:21 → **2:23**).

## Dan 2:24

> Therefore Daniel went in to Arioch, whom the king had appointed to destroy the wise men of Babylon. He went and said thus to him: "Do not destroy the wise men of Babylon; bring me in before the king, and I will show the king the interpretation."

**Observation.** Daniel "went in" (H5954) to Arioch, "appointed" (H4483) by the king "to destroy" (H0007) the wise men; Daniel says: do not destroy them, bring me before the king, "I will show" (H2324, *chava* — to show, interpret, declare) "the interpretation" (H6591, *pishar*).

**Interpretation — questions considered.**
- Q1/Q2 (Daniel): the driving interior is not stated but is strongly inferable — confidence (from 2:19's revelation, 2:23's received answer) and an intervention that extends mercy beyond his own circle to the wider guild of wise men (widening 2:18's "might not be destroyed... with the rest" from a shared-fate concern into an active rescue).
- Q3: inferred, not stated — but with a stronger textual antecedent than 1:1's parallel case (Nebuchadnezzar's siege-resolve): here the immediately preceding revelation (2:19–23) supplies a concrete reason for Daniel's sudden confidence.
- Q4: source — Daniel himself (self-sourced initiative), continuous with the resolve-and-devise pattern of 1:8 and 1:12–13.
- Q5: target — Arioch (immediate human recipient); behind him, the wise men of Babylon (collective, Q8, now to be *spared* rather than merely *outperformed*, cf. 1:20).
- Q6: movement (intervention/request).
- Q7: links back to Daniel's characteristic pattern (1:8, 1:12–13) of interior conviction translating into deliberate outward strategy; links forward to 2:25.

**Decision.** **Retain** an intervention-operation (Daniel's inferred confidence, antecedent-supported by 2:19–23, → protective request), continuous with the 1:8/1:12–13 pattern.

## Dan 2:25

> Then Arioch brought in Daniel before the king in haste and said thus to him: "I have found among the exiles from Judah a man who will make known to the king the interpretation."

**Observation.** Arioch "brought in" (H5954) Daniel "before the king" "in haste" (H0927, *behal* — Pual: to frighten, alarm, dismay; Ithpaal: to hurry, hasten; also "alarmed" as a participle). Arioch says: "I have found" (H7912) "among the exiles from Judah a man who will make known... the interpretation."

**Interpretation — questions considered.**
- Q1/Q2/Q3 (Arioch as a minor focused inner being): "in haste" carries, in its own registered sense-range, both *hurrying* and *alarm/dismay* — an interior-adjacent manner-word, not a clean seat-word. This is a fourth data point for **EQ-1**'s stated/inferred gradient, sitting closer to the 1:14 *shama* case (interior carried in a verb's registered range, without a dedicated seat-word) than to the 1:8 *leb* case (explicit seat-word). Named as this specific gradient-position, not simply "stated" or "inferred."
- Q4: source of the haste/alarm — situational (Arioch's own life was staked on the wise men's destruction, per 2:24's context); human/circumstantial, not divine.
- Q5: target — how the haste shapes the action (bringing Daniel in quickly, and how Arioch frames his role).
- **Referential note (not stated, inferred from narrative pragmatics):** Arioch reframes Daniel's initiative as his own discovery ("**I** have found... a man who will...") — a self-interested repositioning before the king. This is not lexically marked as deceptive or self-serving; it is surfaced as a possible reading, not imported as fact, following the same discipline applied to the chief eunuch's unstated interior at Dan 1:7.
- Q6: state (haste/alarm) issuing in a movement (presenting Daniel, with self-positioning framing).

**Decision.** **Retain** Arioch's haste as an interior-adjacent state (EQ-1 gradient point, situationally sourced), issuing in the presentation-movement; the self-positioning reframing recorded as referential, not asserted.

## Dan 2:27

> Daniel answered the king and said, "No wise men, enchanters, magicians, or astrologers can show to the king the mystery that the king has asked,

**Observation.** Daniel denies that "wise men" (H2445), "enchanters" (H0826, *ashaph*), "magicians" (H2749, *chartom*), or "astrologers" (H1505, *gezar* — lit. "determiners/deciders") "can show" (H2324 + H3202, *kehal* — be able) the king's mystery.

**Interpretation — questions considered.**
- Q6: a **negative state operation** — Daniel names a human class (Q8: the professional wise-men/diviner guild) and denies that class possession of the interior capability (skill, insight, occult knowledge) the task requires.
- Q7: **directly parallels and intensifies 1:20** — there, the four youths were measured "ten times better" than the same comparison class (magicians/enchanters); here the class is not merely outperformed but flatly disqualified from the task altogether. The relationship between Daniel's circle and this class sharpens across the two passages: from *superior* (1:20) to *sole-capable* (2:27, read together with 2:28's pivot to God).
- Q5: target — sets up the contrast that follows immediately in 2:28 (the true source).

**Decision.** **Retain** the comparison-class denial as a state-operation on a named human class, intensifying the 1:20 linkage; functions as the rhetorical hinge into 2:28.

## Dan 2:28–2:29 — the pivot: revelation reaching the pagan king

> [2:28] but there is a God in heaven who reveals mysteries, and he has made known to King Nebuchadnezzar what will be in the latter days. Your dream and the visions of your head as you lay in bed are these: [2:29] To you, O king, as you lay in bed came thoughts of what would be after this, and he who reveals mysteries made known to you what is to be.

**Observation.** God "reveals" (H1541) "mysteries" (H7328) and "has made known" (H3046) to Nebuchadnezzar "what will be in the latter days" (H0320, *acharith* — end, latter). The king's "visions of your head" (H2376 + H7217, *resh* — head, "as seat of visions") "as you lay in bed" are named. Then: "thoughts" (H7476, *rayon* — thought; Aramaic of *rayon* "striving" H7475) "came" (H5559, *seleq* — to ascend, come up) "to you," and "he who reveals mysteries made known to you what is to be."

**Interpretation — questions considered.** This is the passage's decisive interpretive crux.
- Q1/Q2/Q3 (Nebuchadnezzar): for the **first time since Dan 1:1**, the king's interior is lexically **stated**, not merely inferred. "Thoughts" (*rayon*) explicitly names a content arising within him; "visions of your **head**" names the locus (head, as seat of visions — an interior-seat reference, structurally parallel to 1:8's *leb*/heart for Daniel, but here of Nebuchadnezzar). This **closes** the longest-open thread in the whole Daniel 1–2 debate series: whether the king was ever granted a stated interior (see Closed items).
- Q4 (the decisive question, and the sharpest data point yet for the passage-level fork): the text keeps **two operations textually distinct within the same two verses** — (i) the thoughts *arise* in the king as he lies in bed ("came thoughts... to you" — phrased as a natural/self-originating event, no source named for the thought's *occurrence*); (ii) the *making-known* of what those thoughts mean/portend is explicitly God-sourced ("he who reveals mysteries made known to you what is to be"). This is a clean, in-text model of exactly the source-of-state vs. source-of-content distinction the 1:2 fork and EQ-2 have been circling: the king's raw cognitive event (thought arising) is not attributed to God; the revelatory *content/interpretation* of that event is. **Not identical to the 1:2 question** (which asks about disposition/resolve, not cognitive content), but it is the closest the text comes, in either passage, to modelling how source-of-state and source-of-enablement/content can be held apart in a single verse.
- Q5: target — Nebuchadnezzar himself, unambiguously (contrast the genuine target-ambiguity at 1:9). His head/mind is the explicit locus both of the vision's arising and of its interpretation.
- Q6: state (thoughts arising) + movement (God's making-known of their significance).
- **Bears on EQ-6** (does God only source *favourable* interiors — 1:9, 1:17 — leaving adversarial dispositions, e.g. Nebuchadnezzar at 1:2, unsourced?): 2:28–29 complicates rather than resolves this. God here reveals *cognitive content* (a symbolic dream) to a non-covenant, at-times-adversarial king — but this is not the same as sourcing a *favourable disposition* in him (nothing here says God gives Nebuchadnezzar goodwill, resolve, or character, as 1:9/1:17/2:21 do for Daniel's circle). **Proposed refinement (new, see EQ-8):** the text may distinguish divine sourcing of interior *content* (broader, reaching even a pagan king) from divine sourcing of interior *disposition* (narrower, so far attested only for the faithful). If so, EQ-6's asymmetry is not a contradiction but a category distinction the text itself appears to observe. Recorded as a proposed reading, not a settled one.

**Decision.** **Retain** two linked operations: (i) a self-originating cognitive event in the king (thoughts/visions, stated for the first time), and (ii) an explicit divine movement making its significance known — kept textually distinct, offered as a model instance for EQ-2. **Closes** the long-open "is Nebuchadnezzar ever a stated interior" thread. **Advances, without resolving,** EQ-6 via the proposed content/disposition distinction (new EQ-8).

## Dan 2:30

> But as for me, this mystery has been revealed to me, not because of any wisdom that I have more than all the living, but in order that the interpretation may be made known to the king, and that you may know the thoughts of your mind.

**Observation.** Daniel: "this mystery has been revealed to me, **not because of** any wisdom (H2452) that I have more than all the living, **but in order that** the interpretation may be made known to the king, and that you may know the thoughts (H7476) of your **mind**" (H3825, *levav* — heart, mind; Aramaic of *levav* H3824).

**Interpretation — questions considered.**
- Q4 (decisive, and a striking reversal): Daniel explicitly **denies self as the operative source** — "not because of any wisdom that I have" — even though 1:17, 1:20, 2:21, and 2:23 have all, in different voices, attributed exactly this wisdom to him (as God-given). Daniel disowns interior-based *merit* as the reason for the revelation, while implicitly retaining his role as its *instrument*. This is a self-correcting move: Daniel, having just testified in his own voice at 2:23 ("you have given **me** wisdom"), now redirects even that gift away from personal credit toward divine purpose ("in order that... the king... may know").
- Q5: target — the king again, and explicitly "the thoughts of your **mind**" (*levav*, heart/mind) — the clearest, most direct interior-seat naming of Nebuchadnezzar in the whole range, using the **same root** (*levav*/*leb*) that named Daniel's own interior seat at 1:8. The lexical echo (heart/mind, first for Daniel's resolve, now for the king's dream-content) is a deliberate-reading candidate, surfaced not asserted.
- Q6: state (Daniel's self-denial of merit) + movement (the stated purpose — that the king know his own mind's content, via Daniel as instrument).
- Q7: **closes the passage's wisdom-sourcing arc** — unstated source (1:4) → God-given, narrated (1:17) → general principle (2:21) → personal testimony (2:23) → Daniel's own humble reframing, denying personal merit even in the moment of using the gift (2:30). A five-point arc across two chapters, ending in self-effacement rather than claim.

**Decision.** **Retain** Daniel's self-denial-of-merit as an interior/reflective operation (source explicitly redirected from self to purpose/God), targeting the king's *levav* — closing the wisdom-sourcing arc and echoing 1:8's *leb* lexically.

## Passage-level linkages (Q7)

1. **Petition → revelation → thanksgiving arc (2:18 → 2:19 → 2:23):** the cleanest, most explicitly self-closing arc in either passage debated so far — the text names its own fulfillment ("what **we asked** of you," 2:23).
2. **Wisdom-sourcing arc, spanning both chapters (1:4 → 1:17 → 2:21 → 2:23 → 2:30):** source unstated → God-given (narrated) → general principle (stated doctrine) → personal testimony → self-denial of merit. The fullest cross-passage thread identified to date.
3. **Comparison-class intensification (1:20 → 2:27):** the magicians/enchanters class moves from *outperformed* to *flatly incapable*.
4. **Nebuchadnezzar's interior, long withheld, finally stated (1:1–1:2 → 1:18–20 [inferred only] → 2:26 [inferred] → 2:29–30 [stated]):** see Closed items.
5. **Compassion/mercy vocabulary mirrored (1:9 → 2:18):** the same root (rachamim/H7356B family) appears first as an unsolicited divine gift to Daniel (1:9), then as a solicited divine gift sought by the four (2:18).

## Closed items (from prior debates)

- **"Is Nebuchadnezzar ever a stated interior?"** — open since Dan 1:1 (where his siege-resolve was inferred only, and explicitly flagged as a correction-in-progress), still inferred-only at 1:18–20 and 2:26 — **closed at 2:29–30**, where "thoughts" and "the thoughts of your mind" (*levav*) name his interior directly for the first time in the series.
- **2:18's petition** ("might not be destroyed") — **closed at 2:19** (revelation granted) and named explicitly at **2:23** ("what we asked of you").

## Emergent questions log (carrying EQ-1…EQ-7 forward; new items this range)

- **EQ-1 (advanced further).** Dan 2:25's "in haste" (*behal*) adds a fourth gradient point: a manner-word carrying alarm/urgency in its registered range, without a dedicated seat-word — positioned near the 1:14 *shama* case, distinct from both the 1:8 *leb* (clean seat-word, stated) and the 1:19 king's-assessment (fully inferred) poles.
- **EQ-8 (new).** Does the text distinguish divine sourcing of interior **content** (revelation, dream-visions — reaching even a pagan king, 2:28–29) from divine sourcing of interior **disposition** (favour, resolve, character — so far attested only for the faithful, 1:9/1:17/2:21)? If so, this may be the resolution-path for **EQ-6** (the favourable-only asymmetry) — not a contradiction but a category distinction. Proposed, not settled.
- **EQ-9 (new).** Can/should interior-type predicates applied to **God himself** (wisdom, might — 2:20; knowing, revealing — 2:22) be tracked by an instrument built for the *human* inner being? A scope question distinct from, but structurally parallel to, the original 1:2 scope fork (there: does divine agency on human circumstance count; here: does a divine attribute count at all).
- **EQ-4 (data point, not new).** Dan 2:23 gives a clean textual instance of a collective's individuated members receiving both a per-member ("me") and a collective ("us") record of the same divine act, within a single verse — offered as a possible answer-in-practice to EQ-4, not a rule derived from it.

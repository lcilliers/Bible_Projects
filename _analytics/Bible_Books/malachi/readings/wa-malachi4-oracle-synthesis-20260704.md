## MAL-epilogue · Mal 4:4–6 · [D, multi] — remember the law; the turning of hearts

**The reading.** The book — and the OT prophetic canon — closes on two interior commands: "**Remember the law of my servant Moses**… that I commanded him at Horeb" (v.4); and the promise of Elijah, who "will **turn the hearts of fathers to their children and the hearts of children to their fathers**, lest I come and strike the land with a decree of utter destruction" (vv.5–6). The finding is **memory and reconciliation as the closing inner-being work** — the self is to *remember* the given law, and hearts are to be *turned* toward one another across the generations *(stated)*. The operation is **the interior re-anchored by remembrance and re-bound by the turning of hearts** *(inferred: after six disputations exposing a people's doubt, contempt, faithlessness, cynicism, and withholding, the remedy offered at the close is not new revelation but *remembrance* — "remember the law" (zakar) — the same tether-to-the-given that Ecclesiastes reached ("remember your Creator", Ecc 12:1); and the final promised work is *relational* — "turn the hearts (leb) of fathers to children and children to fathers" (v.6): the healing of the estranged interior between generations, without which the land falls under a curse; the finding is that the whole prophetic word ends by pointing the interior back to *memory* and *reconciled relationship* — the two movements that gather a scattered, doubting people; and the last word of the OT is a heart-word, leb, "the hearts… turned")*.

**Lexical validation (read-back).** *Remember* (H2142, zakar — "remember the law"), *hearts* (H3820, leb — twice, "turn the hearts of fathers… children"), *awesome* (H3372-linked, yare — "the great and awesome day"), *rules* (H2706, choq) carried. *zakar* (remember) + *leb* (the hearts turned) are the reading's spine — memory and the reconciled interior; correctly tagged. The prophetic canon closing on *leb* ("the hearts") is a fitting datum. Sound.

**Hidden / less-obvious characteristics isolated.** (1) **Remembrance as the gathering remedy** — "remember the law of Moses" (v.4): the finding that after all the exposed failures, the interior is re-anchored not by novelty but by *recalling the given* (zakar) — the same tether as Ecc 12:1 "remember your Creator"; memory as what steadies a doubting people. (2) **The reconciled interior across generations** — "turn the hearts of fathers to children and children to fathers" (v.6): the finding that the final promised work is *relational healing* — estranged hearts (leb) turned back toward one another; the alternative is a cursed land. The OT ends on the turning of hearts.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *remembrance as the gathering remedy (remember the law)* (thread — w/ Ecc 12:1 "remember your Creator"); *the reconciled interior across generations (hearts turned)* (new); *the prophetic canon closing on leb ("the hearts")* (new).

## Chapter-level / book-level notes
- **Validation summary:** substrate clean; *zakar* (remember, 4:4) and *leb* (the hearts turned, 4:6) cleanly carried. The epilogue is short; the bulk of chapter 4 (4:1–3, the burning day / sun of righteousness) belongs to Disputation 6 and is read under Malachi 3.
- **Book-level synthesis (Malachi as a whole):** Malachi is a sequence of **six disputations** that successively *stage the people's interior* and answer it — the **doubting heart** that cannot feel God's love (1:2), the **unconsciously contemptuous** priest (1:6), the **faithless** spouse/covenant-breaker (2:14), the **cynic** who wearies God with "where is justice?" (2:17), the **withholding** heart that robs God (3:8), and the **"it is vain to serve God"** conclusion set against the **God-fearing remnant** who are *remembered* (3:14–16). The book's answers are not arguments but **God's own coming** (the refiner, 3:1–3), his **invited test** of trust (3:10), his **book of remembrance** (3:16), and the closing call to **remember** and to **turn hearts** (4:4–6). Its distinctive inner-being form is the **disputation** — the objection-seam ("But you say…") that exposes the interior the whole book is about.
- **Method validation:** processing **by oracle-passage** (not chapter) was correct — three of the six disputations cross chapter lines; the reading rests on whole oracles. The cross-chapter segmentation (`verse_refs`) is now a reusable capability for the prophets.

# Malachi — structural analysis: passages or whole book? (2026-07-04)

> Scouting analysis before processing (researcher: "first analyse it to see if we should process it as passages or the whole book"). Book_id 39; 52 verses present of 55 canonical; **47/52 carry a genuine (non-T2) inner-being term (~90%)** — high density, a good candidate.

## Finding: Malachi is neither a "chapter" book nor a "whole-book" blob — it is **six disputation-oracles**.
Malachi's whole rhetorical form is the **disputation** (Streitgespräch): God makes a claim → the people/priests object *"But you say…"* → God answers. That objection-clause is the seam that marks the units, and it recurs seven times (1:2, 1:6, 1:7, 1:13, 2:14, 2:17, 3:7, 3:8, 3:13). The book divides cleanly into:

| # | Disputation | Verses | Inner-being movement | Crosses a chapter line? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Superscription | 1:1 | — (T2 only) | — |
| 1 | "I have loved you" / "How?" | **1:2–5** | God's elective love met by a doubting heart | no |
| 2 | The priests' contempt | **1:6–2:9** | honour/fear withheld; the covenant with Levi (life, peace, fear, awe, true instruction) corrupted | **YES (1→2)** |
| 3 | Faithlessness in marriage | **2:10–16** | faithlessness / the loved covenant / "the wife of your youth" / guarding one's spirit | no |
| 4 | "You have wearied the LORD" / "Where is justice?" | **2:17–3:5** | weariness of God at cynicism; the refiner who purifies | **YES (2→3)** |
| 5 | Robbing God (tithes) | **3:6–12** | the withholding heart; "return to me"; the test of trust | no |
| 6 | "Your words have been hard" / the fearers | **3:13–4:3** | the God-doubting heart ("it is vain to serve God") vs those who *feared the LORD* (the book of remembrance) | **YES (3→4)** |
| 7 | Epilogue | **4:4–6** | remember; the turning of hearts (fathers↔children) | — |

**Three of the six disputations cross the chapter boundary** (2, 4, 6). So **chapter-driven processing would sever oracles** — e.g. the priests' disputation (1:6–2:9) would be cut in two, splitting the "covenant with Levi" reflection from its indictment. And **whole-book-as-one-unit is too coarse** — six distinct inner-being movements (doubting love, corrupted priestly integrity, marital faithlessness, wearied justice, the withholding/returning heart, the cynical vs the fearing heart) would be flattened.

## Why the disputation is the right unit (and why prophets ≠ "by chapter")
- The disputation form is **intrinsically inner-being**: it *stages the people's interior* — their objections voice the doubting, contemptuous, cynical, self-justifying heart ("How have we…?", "It is vain to serve God", "Where is the God of justice?"). The unit's spine is the **exposed inner posture** answered by God.
- The passage boundary is **rhetorically marked** (the "But you say" seam), not imposed — so it is discoverable, not arbitrary.
- This directly answers the wider question ("not convinced the prophets should be purely by book"): the prophetic unit is the **oracle**, a *sub-book passage* that ignores chapter divisions. Malachi is the clean test of that principle.

## Recommended method (segmentation-first variant, §15, with passage = oracle)
1. **Phase 0 — backfill** the 3 missing verses (**1:2, 2:1, 3:4**) → 55 (STEP). 1:2 especially ("How have you loved us?") is the objection-seam of disputation 1 — its absence would break the unit.
2. **Phase 1 — verse lexical** (poetic/prophetic mode, cross-verse OFF, per genre).
3. **SEGMENTATION — by disputation-oracle** (the 7 units above; each spans its natural verse range, crossing chapters where the oracle does; skip 1:1 as superscription, like Ecc 1:1). `multi=true` on most — the disputations are multi-characteristic. This is the **passage approach**, confirmed.
4. **Phase 2 — per-disputation meaning-synthesis**, lens on the interior each oracle stages (the doubting / contemptuous / faithless / wearied / withholding / cynical-vs-fearing heart), with the read-back + guardrail.

## T2 note
43 of 52 verses touch a T2 term (Israel, Edom, Jacob, Levi, Judah, Jerusalem, Moses, Horeb, Elijah — Malachi is name-dense), but only ~5 are T2/imagery-only (1:1, 2:3, 3:2, 3:6, 3:8); all of those fold naturally into their disputation rather than standing alone. No pure-T2 verse needs its own unit.

## Recommendation
**Process Malachi as PASSAGES = the 6 disputations + epilogue (7 units), not by chapter and not as one blob.** It is a short, high-density (~90% genuine), well-marked OT test of oracle-driven prophetic processing — a good first prophet. Awaiting go-ahead before running Phase 0.

### On the two open strategy questions (brief)
- **Hebrew vs Greek / finish-by-language:** the pipeline is language-agnostic at the meaning layer (morph/language handled in the substrate); the choice is workflow preference, not a technical constraint. Malachi keeps us in the OT for now.
- **Prophets "purely by book":** no — the working unit is the **oracle/disputation** (a passage), as above. A "prophet" is processed as a sequence of oracle-passages; whether we later group oracles *across* prophetic books (e.g. by theme) is a separate cross-book synthesis question, not a processing-unit question.

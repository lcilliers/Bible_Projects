## MIC-lawsuit-requirement · Mic 6:1–8 · [D, multi] — what the LORD requires: do justice, love kindness, walk humbly

**The reading.** The covenant lawsuit opens tenderly: "**O my people, what have I done to you? How have I wearied you? Answer me!**" (v.3); God recalls the redemption from Egypt (v.4). The human answer escalates absurdly: "**With what shall I come before the LORD**?… burnt offerings?… thousands of rams?… ten thousands of rivers of oil? **Shall I give my firstborn** for my transgression?" (vv.6–7). And the great reply: "**He has told you, O man, what is good; and what does the LORD require of you but to do justice, and to love kindness, and to walk humbly with your God?**" (v.8). The finding is **the whole of what God requires gathered into three interior-and-outward movements — *do* justice, *love* kindness, *walk* humbly with God** *(stated)*. The operation is **the self's frantic bargaining (ever-bigger offerings) answered by a summons not to *give more* but to *be* just, kind, and humble before God** *(inferred: the escalation (vv.6–7) exposes an interior that thinks God can be *satisfied by quantity* — more rams, rivers of oil, even the firstborn — the transactional heart trying to buy off guilt; against it, v.8 reframes the whole demand as *character and posture*: **do justice** (mishpat — active right-dealing), **love kindness** (ahavat chesed — not merely do but *love* covenant-mercy), and **walk humbly with your God** (hatsnea leket — a lowly, close walk); the finding is that God wants not the self's *offerings* but the self's *justice, love, and humble nearness* — the three cover the horizontal (justice to others), the affectional (loving mercy), and the vertical (humble walk with God); "love kindness" is the interior note — chesed is to be *loved*, not just performed; and "walk humbly *with* your God" makes the whole ethic a *companioned walk*, not a solo achievement; this is the wisdom-books' fear-of-God and Hosea's chesed-and-knowledge distilled into a rule of life)*.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *the three-fold requirement — do justice, love kindness, walk humbly with God* (thread climax — the distilled inner-outer life, w/ Hos 6:6, 12:6, the fear-of-God cornerstone); *the transactional heart that would buy off guilt with quantity (bigger offerings)* (new — bargaining vs being); *loving kindness, not merely doing it (chesed as an affection)* (new); *the humble companioned walk with God* (new — ethic as walking *with* God).

## MIC-cheating-city · Mic 6:9–16 · [D, multi] — the fraudulent interior; the curse of dissatisfaction

**The reading.** The city's commerce is fraud: "treasures of **wickedness**… the scant measure… **wicked scales**… a bag of **deceitful weights**… your inhabitants **speak lies**, and their **tongue is deceitful**" (vv.10–12). The judgment fits the crime: "**you shall eat, but not be satisfied**, and there shall be **hunger within you**… you shall **sow, but not reap**" (vv.14–15). The finding is **a fraudulent interior (false weights, lying tongues) met by a curse of *un-satisfaction* — gaining but never filled** *(stated)*. The operation is **the deceitful self punished by an inner emptiness that consumes its gains** *(inferred: the fraud is *interior before it is commercial* — the "deceitful tongue in their mouth" (v.12), the heart set on the scant measure; and the punishment is precisely inner-being — "you shall eat but not be satisfied, and there shall be hunger *within* you" (v.14): the never-satisfied curse (the same emptiness as Ecc 5:10, 6:7, Hag 1:6), here as *judgment* on fraud — the ill-gotten gain cannot fill, the self stays hungry; the finding is that dishonest gain is cursed not (only) by loss but by an inner *dissatisfaction* that no amount fills — the fraudulent heart is condemned to hunger amid its takings)*.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *the fraudulent interior (deceitful weights and tongues)* (new — fraud as a heart-condition before a commercial act); *the curse of dissatisfaction (eat but not be satisfied, hunger within)* (thread — the never-satisfied, w/ Ecc 5:10, Hag 1:6; here as judgment on fraud).

## Chapter-level notes
- **Surfaced:** the three-fold requirement (6:8); the transactional heart; loving kindness; the humble walk; the fraudulent interior; the curse of un-satisfaction. Chapter 6 holds Micah's summit — **6:8** distils the whole covenant life into *justice, kindness, humble walk* — against a heart that would rather buy God off (6:6–7) or cheat its neighbour (6:11). **Threads:** the requirement (w/ Hos 6:6); never-satisfied (w/ Ecc).

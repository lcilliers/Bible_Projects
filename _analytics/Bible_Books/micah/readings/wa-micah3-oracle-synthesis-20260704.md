## MIC-rulers-hate-good · Mic 3:1–4 · [D, multi] — inverted moral love; the unanswered cry

**The reading.** The rulers who should "**know justice**" (v.1) are indicted for a reversed interior: "**you who hate the good and love the evil**, who tear the skin from off my people" (vv.2–3). Their end: "**then they will cry to the LORD, but he will not answer them; he will hide his face** from them… because they have made their deeds evil" (v.4). The finding is **the moral compass inverted — good hated, evil loved — and the resulting cry to God going unanswered** *(stated)*. The operation is **the interior whose loves are backward, and whose eventual prayer meets a hidden face** *(inferred: "hate the good and love the evil" (v.2) is the precise inversion of the healthy interior (love good, hate evil, Ps 97:10, Amos 5:15) — the affections themselves turned against their proper objects; and the consequence is relational — the God whose face was available now *hides* it from a cry that comes only under judgment (v.4), the same withdrawn face as Hosea 5:15; the finding is that inverted love produces a self that only turns to God when cornered, and finds him hidden — because the crying is calculation, not repentance)*.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *inverted moral love (hating good, loving evil)* (new — the affections turned against their objects, w/ Ps 97:10, Isa 5:20); *the unanswered cry of the unjust (the hidden face)* (thread — w/ Hos 5:15; prayer that is cornered calculation).

## MIC-false-prophets · Mic 3:5–8 · [D, multi] — venal prophecy vs Spirit-filled truth-telling

**The reading.** The prophets are bought: they "**cry 'Peace' when they have something to eat, but declare war against him who puts nothing into their mouths**" (v.5). Against them Micah stands: "**But as for me, I am filled with power, with the Spirit of the LORD, and with justice and might, to declare to Jacob his transgression**" (v.8). The finding is **prophecy for hire — the message set by the fee — set against a Spirit-given interior with the courage to name sin** *(stated)*. The operation is **the venal self whose word tracks its stomach, vs the Spirit-filled self able to speak the costly truth** *(inferred: the hired prophets' message is *determined by payment* (peace for food, war for none, v.5) — the word bent to self-interest; against it, Micah's interior is "filled with the Spirit… and justice and might" (v.8) — the finding is that truth-telling under pressure requires a *given* strength (the Spirit), not native courage; the venal prophet manages his message for gain, the true prophet is empowered to declare sin though it costs him)*.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *venal prophecy (the word bent to the fee)* (new); *Spirit-filled courage to name sin (the given strength for costly truth)* (new — truth-telling as an empowered, not native, act).

## MIC-crooked-leaders · Mic 3:9–12 · [D, multi] — injustice joined to false confidence in God

**The reading.** The leaders "**detest justice and make crooked all that is straight**" (v.9), building Zion "with blood" (v.10), judging for bribes — "**yet they lean on the LORD and say, 'Is not the LORD in the midst of us? No disaster shall come upon us'**" (v.11). Therefore "**Zion shall be plowed as a field**" (v.12). The finding is **the presumption that couples injustice with a confident claim on God's protective presence** *(stated)*. The operation is **the interior that practises corruption while *leaning* on God — treating his presence as an insurance that exempts it from judgment** *(inferred: "they lean on the LORD" (v.11, sha'an) while detesting justice — the finding is a specific and dangerous inner posture: *presumption*, the belief that covenant privilege ("is not the LORD in our midst?") cancels moral accountability; it is the exact fault the temple-sermon tradition attacks (Jer 7) — using God's presence as a shield for injustice; the shattering answer (Zion plowed, v.12) is that God's presence is no such insurance)*.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *presumption (injustice leaning on God's presence as insurance)* (thread — w/ Jer 7; the privilege-claim that cancels accountability); *the crooked-making interior (detesting justice, bending the straight)* (new).

## JER-circumcise-heart · Jeremiah 4:1–31 · [D, multi] — circumcise the heart; the lodging wicked thoughts; the prophet's anguish

**The reading.** The call to return sharpens into a demand on the *interior*: "**Break up your fallow ground, and sow not among thorns. Circumcise yourselves to the LORD; remove the foreskin of your hearts**" (vv.3–4). And a probing question: "**O Jerusalem, wash your heart from evil, that you may be saved. How long shall your wicked thoughts lodge within you?**" (v.14). As judgment approaches, the prophet's own heart breaks: "**My anguish, my anguish! I writhe in pain! Oh the walls of my heart! My heart is beating wildly; I cannot keep silent**" (v.19). The finding is **the demand to *circumcise the heart* and *break up the fallow ground* — an *inward* renewal beneath the outward — with the searching "**how long shall your wicked thoughts lodge within you?**", and the prophet's own *torn heart*** *(stated)*. The operation is **the relocation of repentance to the *deepest layer of the interior*: not merely change your ways but *cut away the heart's hardened covering* ("circumcise… your hearts") and *plough open the packed, thorn-grown soil* ("break up your fallow ground") — and the recognition that evil *lodges* as a resident guest in the heart ("how long shall your wicked thoughts **lodge** within you?") that must be *evicted* ("wash your heart")** *(inferred: vv.3–4 press repentance past behaviour to the heart's *condition*: "**circumcise… the foreskin of your hearts**" — the finding is that the decisive renewal is *internal and radical* — the removal of the heart's hardened, insensitive covering, so that the heart itself is laid bare and responsive (the same as "a new heart", Ezek 36:26; the circumcised heart, Deut 30:6); and "**break up your fallow ground**" (v.3) images the interior as *packed, uncultivated soil* overgrown with thorns — repentance is the *ploughing open* of ground gone hard, so the word can take root (as Hos 10:12); and v.14's "**how long shall your wicked THOUGHTS LODGE within you?**" is a piercing inner-being observation — evil *takes up residence* in the heart, *lodging* like a long-staying guest, and the call is to *evict* it ("wash your heart"): the finding is that sin is not only acts but *tenants of the interior* — thoughts that settle in and stay, and that the heart must be actively cleansed of; and the prophet's anguish (v.19, "the walls of my heart… I cannot keep silent") shows the *cost* to the one who sees it coming — Jeremiah's heart breaks over the very people whose hearts will not break, the weeping prophet feeling what they will not feel)*.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *circumcise the heart / break up the fallow ground (repentance relocated to the heart's condition — removing its hardened covering, ploughing open packed soil)* (thread — a gem: inward radical renewal beneath the outward, w/ 4:3-4, Deut 30:6, Ezek 36:26, Hos 10:12); *wicked thoughts that lodge within ("how long shall your wicked thoughts lodge within you?" — evil as a resident guest to be evicted)* (new — a gem: sin as tenants of the interior, not only acts, w/ 4:14, Matt 15:19, Jas 1:14-15); *washing the heart ("wash your heart from evil, that you may be saved" — the interior actively cleansed)* (thread — w/ 4:14, Ps 51:7,10); *the prophet's own torn heart ("my anguish… the walls of my heart… I cannot keep silent" — the weeping seer feeling what his people will not)* (thread — a gem: the cost to the one who sees, w/ 4:19, 9:1, 8:18, Luke 19:41).

## JER-good-figs-heart · Jeremiah 24:1–10 · [D, multi] — God gives a heart to know him; the whole-hearted return

**The reading.** Jeremiah is shown two baskets of figs — one "**very good**", one "**so bad they could not be eaten**" (vv.2–3). The good figs are the exiles, of whom God makes a promise that reaches into the interior: "**I will give them a heart to know me, that I am the LORD, and they shall be my people and I will be their God, for they shall return to me with their whole heart**" (v.7). The finding is **God's gift of "**a heart to know me**" — the very organ of relationship *given* by God — issuing in a "**return with the whole heart**"** *(stated)*. The operation is **the disclosure that the *capacity to know God* is itself *God's gift*: the interior does not generate its own knowing but is *given "a heart to know"* — and only from that given heart does the *whole-hearted return* follow, so that even repentance's completeness is downstream of a grace that reworks the heart** *(inferred: v.7 answers the whole book's diagnosis of the deceitful, un-turnable heart (17:9; "refuse to return", 8:5) with a *divine solution* — "**I will GIVE them a heart to KNOW me**": the finding is that the deepest change in the interior — the ability to *know God* and to *return whole-heartedly* — is not achieved by the self but *given* by God; the same heart that is "deceitful above all things" and cannot reform itself (the leopard's spots, 13:23) is *replaced/renewed* by a God-given "heart to know"; and the sequence is decisive — the *given heart* comes first ("I will give them a heart to know me"), and *from it* flows the whole-hearted return ("**for** they shall return to me with their whole heart"): the finding is that whole-hearted repentance is the *fruit*, not the *root* — it becomes possible only because God has first given the heart that can do it (the same as Deut 30:6, "the LORD will circumcise your heart… so that you will love him"; Ezek 36:26, "a new heart I will give you"); the good figs are "good" not by their own quality but by what God will *make* them — a grace that reaches the organ of knowing itself)*.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *a heart to know God, given ("I will give them a heart to know me" — the capacity to know God is God's gift, not the self's achievement)* (thread — a gem: the organ of relationship conferred by grace, answering the deceitful un-turnable heart, w/ 24:7, Deut 30:6, Ezek 36:26, 1 John 5:20); *whole-hearted return as the fruit of the given heart ("for they shall return to me with their whole heart" — repentance's completeness downstream of the God-given heart)* (new — a gem: whole-hearted turning is the fruit, not the root; possible only because God first gives the heart, w/ 24:7, 29:13, Phil 2:13); *the covenant formula restored ("they shall be my people and I will be their God" — the given heart restoring the relationship)* (thread — w/ 24:7, 31:33, Ezek 36:28).

# Jeremiah — structure scout & segmentation plan (2026-07-04)

> 52 chapters, 1364 verses — the largest prophetic book. Backfill + Phase-1 complete (1364/1364). A mixed-genre book: poetic oracles, prose sermons, the personal *confessions/laments* of Jeremiah (its unique inner-being treasure), narrative of the fall, the Book of Consolation, and oracles against the nations. Processed as **oracle-passages / confession-units / narrative-scenes**, in batches.

## Macro-structure & batch plan

## Approach
Oracle-passages throughout, with **confession-units** for the personal laments (11:18-23; 12:1-6; 15:10-21; 17:14-18; 18:18-23; **20:7-18**) — Jeremiah's interior is the book's distinctive inner-being field (the prophet who weeps, complains, and is compelled). Narrative sections (34-45) as scenes. Cross-chapter `verse_refs` where oracles span chapters. Skip pure superscriptions.

## Inner-being richness
Jeremiah is uniquely rich in the **prophet's own interior** — his call and reluctance, his complaints ("you deceived me"), the word as "a burning fire shut up in my bones" (20:9), his weeping ("O that my head were waters", 9:1), his isolation and vindication. Plus the great theological interiors: the **fountain vs broken cisterns** (2:13), the **deceitful heart** (17:9), the **new covenant written on the heart** (31:33), and **"seek me with all your heart"** (29:13). The most sustained portrait of a suffering prophet's inner life in Scripture.

## AMOS-known-therefore · Amos 3:1–15 · [D, multi] — intimacy raises responsibility; the prophetic compulsion

**The reading.** The chapter's hinge overturns a comfortable assumption: "**You only have I known of all the families of the earth; therefore I will punish you for all your iniquities**" (v.2). A chain of rhetorical questions establishes that nothing happens without cause — "**Does a lion roar in the forest, when he has no prey?**" (v.4) — leading to the prophet's compulsion: "**The lion has roared; who will not fear? The Lord GOD has spoken; who can but prophesy?**" (v.8). The finding is ***being known* by God as *increasing* accountability, not exempting from it ("you only have I known; therefore I will punish") — and the *compulsion* laid on the one who has heard God ("who can but prophesy?")** *(stated)*. The operation is **the reversal of privilege-as-immunity: the intimacy of being "known" (chosen, loved) *raises* the interior's responsibility rather than shielding it — closeness to God means a *higher* reckoning; and the one who has genuinely *heard* God is under a compulsion to speak, as involuntary as fear at a lion's roar** *(inferred: v.2 corrects the presumption that election means exemption — "**you ONLY have I known… THEREFORE I will punish**": the finding is that intimacy with God *increases* accountability; to be "known" (yada — the covenant word of intimate relationship) is to be held to a *higher* standard, not a lower one — the privileged interior is *more* answerable, not less (the same principle as "to whom much is given, much required"); and v.8 discloses the interior of the true prophet — "the lion has roared; who will not fear? the Lord GOD has spoken; **who can but prophesy?**": the finding is that genuine encounter with God's word creates a *compulsion* — the one who has truly heard cannot stay silent any more than one can stay calm when a lion roars nearby; prophecy here is not ambition but *necessity*, the overflow of having heard; the two findings meet — those known by God are more accountable, and those who have heard him cannot but speak)*.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *intimacy raises accountability ("you only have I known; therefore I will punish" — being known increases responsibility, not exempts)* (thread — a gem: election as higher reckoning, not immunity, w/ 3:2, Luke 12:48, 1 Pet 4:17); *the prophetic compulsion ("the LORD has spoken; who can but prophesy?" — the one who has heard God cannot stay silent)* (new — a gem: genuine encounter creating an involuntary necessity to speak, w/ 3:8, Jer 20:9, 1 Cor 9:16); *nothing without cause (the chain of questions — the ordered world where God's word is the reason behind events)* (thread — w/ 3:3-7).

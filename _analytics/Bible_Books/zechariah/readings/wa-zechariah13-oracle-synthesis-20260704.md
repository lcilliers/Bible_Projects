## ZECH-fountain-refined · Zechariah 13:1–9 · [D, multi] — the fountain opened for sin; the shepherd struck; the refined remnant

**The reading.** Following the mourning look of ch.12, the remedy: "**On that day there shall be a fountain opened for the house of David and the inhabitants of Jerusalem, to cleanse them from sin and uncleanness**" (v.1). Idols and false prophets are cut off (vv.2–5). Then a startling word: "**Awake, O sword, against my shepherd… Strike the shepherd, and the sheep will be scattered**" (v.7). And the refining of the survivors: "**I will put this third into the fire, and refine them as one refines silver, and test them as gold is tested. They will call upon my name, and I will answer them. I will say, 'They are my people'; and they will say, 'The LORD is my God'**" (v.9). The finding is **a *fountain opened to cleanse from sin* — and a *remnant refined* "as silver", brought through the fire to the mutual covenant confession, "they are my people… the LORD is my God"** *(stated)*. The operation is **the sequence of grace: the *mourning* of ch.12 met by a *fountain* that cleanses (grief answered by provision for washing) — and beyond cleansing, a *refining* of the interior through "fire" that removes the dross, issuing in the deepest relational reality: God saying "**they are my people**" and the refined answering "**the LORD is my God**"** *(inferred: v.1's "**fountain opened… to cleanse them from sin and uncleanness**" answers the mourning of 12:10 — the finding is that the grieving look upon the pierced one is met not with condemnation but with *provision for cleansing*: a fountain (continuous, abundant washing) for exactly the sin they mourned; contrition is answered by cleansing, not punishment; and beyond cleansing comes *refining* (v.9) — "**refine them as SILVER… test them as GOLD**": the finding is that God's work in the interior does not stop at pardon but *purifies*, and the instrument is "**fire**" (the furnace of affliction, as Isa 48:10, Mal 3:3) — the refined remnant is brought *through* trial, not around it; and the *outcome* of the refining is the most intimate covenant reality — "**They will CALL upon my name, and I will ANSWER… 'They are my people'; and they will say, 'The LORD is my God'**": the finding is that the end of the whole process (mourning → cleansing → refining) is *restored mutual belonging* — the two-way confession of covenant love, God claiming them and they claiming him; the fire that refines does not consume the true metal but *proves* it and delivers it into this belonging)*.

**Characteristics surfaced:** *the fountain opened for sin (contrition met with cleansing, not condemnation — abundant washing for the sin mourned)* (thread — a gem: grief answered by provision for cleansing, w/ 13:1, 12:10, Isa 1:18, 1 John 1:7); *refining beyond pardon ("refine them as silver… test them as gold" — God purifies the interior through fire, not around it)* (thread — a gem: the furnace that proves the true metal, w/ 13:9, Isa 48:10, Mal 3:3, 1 Pet 1:7); *the mutual covenant confession ("they are my people… the LORD is my God" — the refining issuing in restored two-way belonging)* (new — a gem: the end of the process is intimate mutual belonging, w/ 13:9, Hos 2:23, Jer 31:33); *the shepherd struck, the sheep scattered ("strike the shepherd, and the sheep will be scattered")* (thread — w/ 13:7, Matt 26:31).

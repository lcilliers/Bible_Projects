## Observed structure (method §C — observe, don't impose) — INTERPRETATION (labelled)

Renewal presents an **axis**, not opposing poles: from **RESTORATION** (back to a former state) to **RE-CREATION** (into a genuinely new state), with a **healing edge** and an **awakening/resurrection edge**. Groupings for the deliverable:
- Part I — The inner self made new (re-creation): 2Cor 4:16; Rom 12:2; Eph 4:23; Tit 3:5.
- Part II — Restoration, the depleted brought back: Lam 5:21; Psa 103:5; Job 33:25; Isa 41:1; Heb 6:6; Phil 4:10.
- Part III — Healing as renewal (inner/figurative wound): Hos 14:4; Jer 17:14; Psa 103:3; Jer 30:17; Pro 3:8; Isa 58:8; Exo 15:26.
- Part IV — Awakening and the reach past death: Isa 26:19; Psa 3:5; Psa 73:20; Job 14:14; Job 14:7; Mat 19:28 (flag).

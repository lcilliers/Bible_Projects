## 4. Observed structure of the field (method §C) — OBSERVATION (not imposed)

Healing does **not** present fear's clean two-pole shape. From the evidence it presents as a **multi-facet operation** around a single core act. Facets observed:

- **A. The healing act** — restoration of a sick / wounded body to health (the core operation): "healed", "made well", "cured", "heal".
- **B. The healer (source / agent)** — God as healer (Exo 15:26 "I am the Lord, your healer"; Psa 103:3 "who heals all your diseases"); Jesus healing; physicians (Gen 50:2); and the **refused** healer (Isa 3:7 "I will not be a healer").
- **C. The means** — binding up wounds, bandage, balm, medicine, new flesh forming over a wound (H2280, H7499, H8585B, H8644): the somatic mechanism.
- **D. Health / soundness / wholeness (the resulting state)** — be healthy, sound, whole, perfect health (G5198/G5199, G3647, H4974, H0724, H4832).
- **E. Healing of the inner being & of relational / national breach** — figurative healing: heal apostasy (Hos 14:4); tranquil / calm heart, gentle tongue, gracious words → "health to the body / sweetness to the soul" (H4832: Pro 14:30, 15:4, 16:24); bind up the brokenhearted (Isa 61:1); "my heart is sick" (Jer 8:18); heal the land / nation, restore health to Zion (Jer 30:17). **This facet carries the programme's governing question most directly.**
- **F. Overlap-hinge — heal ↔ save ↔ live ↔ shalom** — the same vocabulary pivots on its object between physical rescue, restoration to life/health, spiritual salvation, and peace/well-being (G1295/G4982; H2421; H7965).
- **G. Negation / "undone"** — no healing, incurable, no remedy, beyond healing, no easing (Jer 46:11; Pro 6:15; 2Ch 21:18; 2Ch 36:16; Jer 30:13; Nah 3:19). Method §E.13 explicitly asks for this movement where the field contains it — it does.

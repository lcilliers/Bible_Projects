## 2. The readings

### I. Puffed up: knowledge, party, talk (12)

The Greek picture is swelling, *physioō*, "to inflate". In Paul it is set again and again against love.
- **Knowledge.** *"This 'knowledge' puffs up, but love builds up. If anyone imagines that he knows something, he does not yet know as he ought to know. But if anyone loves God, he is known by God"* (1Co 8:1–3). Swelling knowledge is answered not by more knowledge but by being known by God.
- **Love is not.** *"Love is patient and kind; love does not envy or boast; it is not arrogant"* (1Co 13:4). Before this: *"if I have prophetic powers, and understand all mysteries and all knowledge ... but have not love, I am nothing"* (13:2).
- **Party.**
  - *"that none of you may be puffed up in favor of one against another. For who sees anything different in you? What do you have that you did not receive? If then you received it, why do you boast as if you did not receive it?"* (1Co 4:6–7).
  - Here Paul states what the Hebrew verses showed in story (batch 2 §I): pride holds what was received *"as if you did not receive it"*.
- **Talk without power.** *"Some are arrogant, as though I were not coming to you ... I will find out not the talk of these arrogant people but their power. For the kingdom of God does not consist in talk but in power"* (1Co 4:18–20). The arrogance rests on Paul being absent.
- **Where grief belongs.** *"And you are arrogant! Ought you not rather to mourn?"* (1Co 5:2). Pride stands where mourning should be.
- **Without reason.** *"puffed up without reason by his sensuous mind, and not holding fast to the Head"* (Col 2:18–19, carried from M09).
- **The new convert.** *"He must not be a recent convert, or he may become puffed up with conceit and fall into the condemnation of the devil"* (1Ti 3:6). The verse joins conceit to the devil's condemnation. It does not say what the devil's condemnation was. **I read:** it is held for 13.3 (other spirits). It is not built on here.
- **Controversy.** *"he is puffed up with conceit and understands nothing. He has an unhealthy craving for controversy and for quarrels about words, which produce envy, dissension"* (1Ti 6:4). Conceit is joined to understanding nothing, and to quarrels.
- **With pleasure.** *"swollen with conceit, lovers of pleasure rather than lovers of God, having the appearance of godliness, but denying its power"* (2Ti 3:4–5).
- **Among the others.**
  - *"quarreling, jealousy, anger, hostility, slander, gossip, conceit, and disorder"* (2Co 12:20).
  - *"Let us not become conceited, provoking one another, envying one another"* (Gal 5:26), after *"let us also keep in step with the Spirit"* (5:25).
  - Conceit is named among the things that set people against each other.

### II. How one thinks of oneself (6)

- **The measure assigned.** *"not to think of himself more highly than he ought to think, but to think with sober judgment, each according to the measure of faith that God has assigned"* (Rom 12:3). The right measure of oneself is what God assigned. It opens with *"by the grace given to me"*.
- **The root.** *"do not be arrogant toward the branches. If you are, remember it is not you who support the root, but the root that supports you ... So do not become proud, but fear"* (Rom 11:18–20). The opposite of pride here is fear, and its ground is knowing what holds one up.
- **Lofty opinions.** *"every lofty opinion raised against the knowledge of God, and take every thought captive to obey Christ"* (2Co 10:5). Height here is a thought raised against knowing God.
- **A thorn given.**
  - *"to keep me from becoming conceited because of the surpassing greatness of the revelations, a thorn was given me in the flesh, a messenger of Satan to harass me"* (2Co 12:7). The answer was *"My grace is sufficient for you, for my power is made perfect in weakness"* (12:9).
  - What was given (the revelations) could have become conceit. A second thing was given to keep it from doing so. The verse names *"a messenger of Satan"* in it. **I read:** this is held for 13.3.
- **The rich.** *"charge them not to be haughty, nor to set their hopes on the uncertainty of riches, but on God, who richly provides us with everything to enjoy"* (1Ti 6:17). The charge against haughtiness is joined to hope set on God. This follows *"he who is the blessed and only Sovereign ... who alone has immortality"* (6:15–16).
- *"Do not be haughty, but associate with the lowly. Never be wise in your own sight"* (Rom 12:16, carried).

### III. From the heart, and in the lists (6)

- **From within.** *"from within, out of the heart of man, come evil thoughts ... coveting, wickedness, deceit, sensuality, envy, slander, pride, foolishness. All these evil things come from within, and they defile a person"* (Mar 7:21–23). Jesus places pride in the heart.
- **After not acknowledging God.** *"since they did not see fit to acknowledge God, God gave them up to a debased mind ... insolent, haughty, boastful"* (Rom 1:28–30).
- **Love turned inward.** *"lovers of self, lovers of money, proud, arrogant"*, ending *"lovers of pleasure rather than lovers of God"* (2Ti 3:2–4). The list begins and ends with what is loved.
- **Not from the Father.** *"the desires of the flesh and the desires of the eyes and pride of life — is not from the Father but is from the world. And the world is passing away"* (1Jn 2:16–17). "Pride of life" stands with two desires, and with what passes.
- **Self-willed.** An overseer, *"as God's steward ... must not be arrogant"* (Tit 1:7). The word is *authadēs*, "self-willed". A steward manages what is another's.
- **Not trembling.** *"Bold and willful, they do not tremble as they blaspheme the glorious ones, whereas angels, though greater in might and power, do not pronounce a blasphemous judgment against them before the Lord"* (2Pe 2:10–11). People without might speak where angels with more might hold back. This is held for 13.2.

### IV. Opposed, scattered, and the seat of God (6)

- **Opposed.** *"God opposes the proud but gives grace to the humble"* (Jam 4:6; 1Pe 5:5, carried from M09).
- **Scattered in their thoughts.** *"He has shown strength with his arm; he has scattered the proud in the thoughts of their hearts; he has brought down the mighty from their thrones and exalted those of humble estate"* (Luk 1:51–52). The pride is located *"in the thoughts of their hearts"*, and that is where it is scattered.
- **Exalted among men.** *"You are those who justify yourselves before men, but God knows your hearts. For what is exalted among men is an abomination in the sight of God"* (Luk 16:15). The Pharisees *"were lovers of money"* and *"ridiculed him"* (16:14). Height measured by people is set against God's knowing of the heart.
- **Boasting about tomorrow.** *"you do not know what tomorrow will bring ... Instead you ought to say, 'If the Lord wills, we will live and do this or that.' As it is, you boast in your arrogance. All such boasting is evil"* (Jam 4:14–16). The arrogance is planning as if life were one's own.
- **The seat of God.** *"the man of lawlessness ... who opposes and exalts himself against every so-called god or object of worship, so that he takes his seat in the temple of God, proclaiming himself to be God"* (2Th 2:3–4). This is the New Testament form of Eze 28:2 (batch 2): *"I sit in the seat of the gods"*.

### V. Christ preeminent (1)

*"He is the beginning, the firstborn from the dead, that in everything he might be preeminent. For in him all the fullness of God was pleased to dwell"* (Col 1:18–19). The word is *prōteuō*, "be first". It is tagged M08, and this is its only verse. Here it is said of Christ, with its ground: he is before all things, all were created through him, and the fullness dwells in him (1:16–19).

## 3. For Ch 13: God's height and human pride (continued)

1. **The given-or-taken line is stated outright.** *"What do you have that you did not receive? If then you received it, why do you boast as if you did not receive it?"* (1Co 4:7).
   - This is the plainest statement in the three batches of what the researcher suspected. Human pride holds as its own what was received.
   - The researcher's word "assumed" fits: the standing is taken up as if it were not given.
2. **The right measure is the one God assigns.** *"not to think of himself more highly than he ought ... according to the measure of faith that God has assigned"* (Rom 12:3). **I read:** where God has height by nature, a person has a measure by gift. Pride is thinking past the gift.
3. **Taking God's seat.**
   - *"takes his seat in the temple of God, proclaiming himself to be God"* (2Th 2:4).
   - Together with Eze 28:2 and Isa 14:13 (batches 1–2), the verses show pride at its full height as a claim to God's own place.
   - **I read:** God's "Most High" is the place pride reaches for. Batches 1–3 show no one holding it but God.
4. **Christ's firstness is grounded, and pleased.**
   - *"that in everything he might be preeminent. For in him all the fullness of God was pleased to dwell"* (Col 1:18–19).
   - His first place rests on what he is: before all things, the one through whom all were made, firstborn from the dead. It is not claimed against anyone.
   - Batch 1 also has Christ's majesty as *"received ... from God the Father"* (2Pe 1:17), and his seat taken *"after making purification for sins"* (Heb 1:3).
   - **I read:** in the verses the Son's height comes with his descent: firstborn *from the dead*, seated *after* purification. This bears on 13.1 *Lowering himself*.
5. **God's knowing is what pride meets.** *"God knows your hearts. For what is exalted among men is an abomination in the sight of God"* (Luk 16:15). *"he has scattered the proud in the thoughts of their hearts"* (Luk 1:51). As in batch 2 (1Sa 2:3), God's knowledge is joined to his answer to pride.
6. **Other beings in these verses (held, not read in).**
   - A messenger of Satan is given against conceit (2Co 12:7).
   - The conceited may fall *"into the condemnation of the devil"* (1Ti 3:6).
   - Angels greater in might do not blaspheme where the willful do (2Pe 2:11).
   - These are for 13.2 and 13.3. The verses do not say that the devil fell by pride. That is not stated here and is not built on.

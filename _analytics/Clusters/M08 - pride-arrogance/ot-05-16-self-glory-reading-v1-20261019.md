# OT-05.16: Rev 18:7 and Php 3:19, read outward (#2160, group C)

v1, 2026-10-19. A reading only: nothing has been woven. Read by guide v2 (#2168).

**The item.** OT-05.16 (M25 → M08): *"G2 self-glory: 'glorified herself' (Rev 18:7), glory in
their shame (Php 3:19)."* The M08 strand noted (#2156): *"Rev 18:7 (glorified herself) and Php 3:19
(glory in their shame) carry doxa (G1391, M22), not an M08 word, and were not read. Stays open for
M22."* The #2160 review proposed re-pointing the item to M22.

**What reading outward shows.** Both verses are **uncited anywhere**. Re-pointing the item would
send them to a cluster that has not been read, and they would stay out of the narrative. But each
carries the inner being now, and one of them is pride in its own voice. The M08 strand did not
read them because the self-glory word is tagged M22. That is a choice by tag, the same narrowness
#2168 names.

## Rev 18:7 (in 18:4–10)

"As she glorified herself and lived in luxury, so give her a like measure of torment and mourning,
since in her heart she says, 'I sit as a queen, I am no widow, and mourning I shall never see.'"

| What it carries | Words | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Pride's voice in the heart: a self-made security that says it will never mourn | *kardia* (heart, M47); "I sit as a queen" (M72) | 10.15 *The heart that says what God says*, beside Isa 47:7–8, which this verse takes up ("I shall not sit as a widow"); Ch 4 *The heart speaks to itself* |
| Self-glory: "glorified herself" | *doxazō* (G1392, M22), reflexive | 10.15 *Given, then held as one's own* (glory taken to oneself); M22 handle (the glory faces) |
| The measure returns: torment and mourning in like measure to her self-glory and luxury | *basanismos* (torment, M35); *penthos* (mourning, M03) | 10.15 *Where it ends*; 10.4 *What is devised comes back* |
| Those who shared it stand far off "in fear of her torment" and weep (18:9–10) | | 10.12 (fear at another's fall); 10.6 (luxury shared) |
| "Come out of her, my people, lest you take part in her sins" (18:4) | | 10.7 (a call to come out) |

## Php 3:19 (in 3:17–21)

"Their end is destruction, their god is their belly, and they glory in their shame, with minds set
on earthly things."

| What it carries | Words | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Appetite as god: what is worshipped is the belly | *koilia* (belly; a body word used figuratively) | 10.6 *Wanting that owns the one who wants*; the flesh-and-body reading (a body word that is not the body: relevance ruling) |
| Glory turned upside down: they glory in what should shame them | *doxa* (G1391, M22); *aischynē* (shame, M07) | 10.15 *The boast*; Ch 11 §4 (glory and shame); M22 handle |
| The mind set on earthly things | *phroneō* (G5426, M63) | 10.4; beside "Set your minds on things that are above" (Col 3:2; Ch 2 *The body raised*) |
| Paul tells it "even with tears" (3:18), and sets against it "our citizenship is in heaven" (3:20, told in Ch 2 and 10.6) | | 10.10 (grief for others who walk as enemies) |

## Proposed

- **Weave now:**
  - Rev 18:7: 10.15 (two places, the heart's voice and the measure); Ch 4
  - Php 3:19: 10.6; 10.15 *The boast*; 10.4
  - pointers: Ch 11 §4 (glory and shame), 10.10 (Paul's tears)
- **Then close OT-05.16, and keep one handle** for M22 (*cluster:M22*, the self-glory and
  glory-in-shame faces), so that the glory strand reads them with the rest of *doxa*. The handle
  replaces re-pointing.
- **Note for the flesh-and-body reading:** the belly as god (Php 3:19).

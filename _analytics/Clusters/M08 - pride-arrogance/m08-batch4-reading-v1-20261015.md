## 1. In short

A boast is a glorying spoken aloud: what a person stands on, said. The verses do not condemn boasting as such. They judge it by what is boasted in.

| Group | Verses | What is boasted in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I. In oneself and what one has | 21 | wisdom, might, riches, tomorrow, evil, the flesh, outward appearance, idols, the law without keeping it |
| II. Boasting excluded | 13 | nothing: grace, gift and faith leave no room. Where a boast is allowed, it is within the measure God assigned |
| III. In the Lord | 20 | knowing him, his name, the cross, hope, even sufferings |
| IV. In weakness | 9 | Paul's weaknesses, "as a fool", so that Christ's power rests on him |
| V. Of others | 14 | the faith, readiness and endurance of others; a crown at Christ's coming |
| VI. Mercy triumphs | 1 | mercy over judgment |

## 2. The readings

### I. Boasting in oneself and what one has (21)

- **The three things.** *"Let not the wise man boast in his wisdom, let not the mighty man boast in his might, let not the rich man boast in his riches"* (Jer 9:23). Each is a standing that is real. Each is set aside as a ground for boasting.
- **Wealth, and trust in it.**
  - *"those who trust in their wealth and boast of the abundance of their riches? Truly no man can ransom another, or give to God the price of his life"* (Psa 49:6–7). The boast is joined to trust, and both are measured against the life that wealth cannot buy.
  - *"Why do you boast of your valleys, O faithless daughter, who trusted in her treasures, saying, 'Who will come against me?'"* (Jer 49:4). This is the same "who will" as Obd 3 (batch 2).
- **Evil and desire.**
  - *"Why do you boast of evil, O mighty man? The steadfast love of God endures all the day"* (Psa 52:1). The boast is answered by God's steadfast love.
  - *"the wicked boasts of the desires of his soul"* (Psa 10:3). This is the verse before *"In the pride of his face"* (10:4, batch 2).
- **Before it is done.**
  - *"Let not him who straps on his armor boast himself as he who takes it off"* (1Ki 20:11).
  - *"Do not boast about tomorrow, for you do not know what a day may bring. Let another praise you, and not your own mouth"* (Pro 27:1–2).
  - Jam 4:16 (batch 3) is the New Testament form.
- **Empty and false.**
  - *"Like clouds and wind without rain is a man who boasts of a gift he does not give"* (Pro 25:14).
  - *"'Bad, bad,' says the buyer, but when he goes away, then he boasts"* (Pro 20:14).
  - *"if you have bitter jealousy and selfish ambition in your hearts, do not boast and be false to the truth"* (Jam 3:14). The heart's jealousy and ambition make the boast a lie.
- **The tongue.** *"the tongue is a small member, yet it boasts of great things. How great a forest is set ablaze by such a small fire!"* (Jam 3:5).
- **To draw others.**
  - *"speaking loud boasts of folly, they entice by sensual passions"* (2Pe 2:18).
  - *"loud-mouthed boasters, showing favoritism to gain advantage"* (Jude 16).
  - The boast is used to pull others in.
- **The flesh and appearance.**
  - *"those who want to make a good showing in the flesh ... that they may boast in your flesh"* (Gal 6:12–13).
  - *"Since many boast according to the flesh"* (2Co 11:18).
  - *"those who boast about outward appearance and not about what is in the heart"* (2Co 5:12).
  - *"their boasted mission"* (2Co 11:12), of men *"disguising themselves as apostles of Christ"* (11:13).
- **In God and in the law, without keeping it.** *"if you call yourself a Jew and rely on the law and boast in God ... You who boast in the law dishonor God by breaking the law"* (Rom 2:17, 23).
  - **I read:** even boasting in God can be the self's standing. The boast and the life go apart: *"do you not teach yourself?"* (2:21).
- **In idols.** *"All worshipers of images are put to shame, who make their boast in worthless idols"*, beside *"you, O Lord, are most high over all the earth"* (Psa 97:7, 9).
- **In a church.** *"Your boasting is not good. Do you not know that a little leaven leavens the whole lump?"* (1Co 5:6). They boasted while the sin of 5:1 stood among them; batch 3 has 5:2, *"Ought you not rather to mourn?"*

### II. Boasting excluded: the gift and the measure (13)

- **Nothing to boast of before God.**
  - God chose *"what is low and despised in the world ... so that no human being might boast in the presence of God"* (1Co 1:28–29).
  - *"For by grace you have been saved through faith. And this is not your own doing; it is the gift of God, not a result of works, so that no one may boast. For we are his workmanship"* (Eph 2:8–10).
  - *"Then what becomes of our boasting? It is excluded ... by the law of faith"* (Rom 3:27).
  - *"if Abraham was justified by works, he has something to boast about, but not before God"* (Rom 4:2).
  - The ground of the exclusion is stated each time: a gift, a choosing of the low, faith.
- **The received.** *"What do you have that you did not receive? If then you received it, why do you boast as if you did not receive it?"* (1Co 4:7). This was read in batch 3, and it is the hinge of this group.
- **Not in men.** *"let no one boast in men. For all things are yours ... and you are Christ's, and Christ is God's"* (1Co 3:21–23).
- **Not over others.** *"do not be arrogant [boast] toward the branches ... it is not you who support the root, but the root that supports you"* (Rom 11:18).
- **Not by comparing.** *"if anyone thinks he is something, when he is nothing, he deceives himself. But let each one test his own work, and then his reason to boast will be in himself alone and not in his neighbor"* (Gal 6:3–4). Thinking oneself something when one is nothing is named self-deception.
- **Within the measure.**
  - *"we will not boast beyond limits, but will boast only with regard to the area of influence God assigned to us"* (2Co 10:13).
  - *"We do not boast beyond limit in the labors of others ... without boasting of work already done in another's area of influence"* (10:15–16).
  - The other side is *"when they measure themselves by one another and compare themselves with one another, they are without understanding"* (10:12).
- **The ground for boasting.**
  - *"I would rather die than have anyone deprive me of my ground for boasting. For if I preach the gospel, that gives me no ground for boasting. For necessity is laid upon me"* (1Co 9:15–16).
  - The preaching itself is no ground, because it is laid on him. The ground is that he preached *"free of charge"* (9:18).

### III. Boasting in the Lord (20)

The boast is not removed. It is moved.
- **The command.** *"Let the one who boasts, boast in the Lord"* (1Co 1:31; 2Co 10:17). This follows Christ *"who became to us wisdom from God, righteousness and sanctification and redemption"* (1:30). It is joined to *"it is not the one who commends himself who is approved, but the one whom the Lord commends"* (2Co 10:18).
- **What to boast in.**
  - *"let him who boasts boast in this, that he understands and knows me, that I am the Lord who practices steadfast love, justice, and righteousness in the earth. For in these things I delight"* (Jer 9:24).
  - The boast is in knowing God, and what is known is his character.
- **The soul's boast.** *"My soul makes its boast in the Lord; let the humble hear and be glad"* (Psa 34:2, carried).
- **Not in my bow.** *"For not in my bow do I trust ... In God we have boasted continually"* (Psa 44:6–8). The psalm then turns: *"But you have rejected us"* (44:9). The boast in God is kept in the psalm even while the defeat is brought before him.
- **His name.** *"Glory in his holy name; let the hearts of those who seek the Lord rejoice!"* (Psa 105:3; 1Ch 16:10).
- **The worm.** *"Fear not, you worm Jacob ... you shall rejoice in the Lord; in the Holy One of Israel you shall glory"* (Isa 41:14–16). The one called a worm is the one who glories.
- **Justified and glorying.** *"Only in the Lord, it shall be said of me, are righteousness and strength ... In the Lord all the offspring of Israel shall be justified and shall glory"* (Isa 45:24–25).
- **The nations.** If Israel returns and swears in truth, *"nations shall bless themselves in him, and in him shall they glory"* (Jer 4:2).
- **Exulting.**
  - *"all who swear by him shall exult"* (Psa 63:11).
  - *"Let the righteous one rejoice in the Lord and take refuge in him! Let all the upright in heart exult!"* (Psa 64:10).
  - *"that I may glory with your inheritance"* (Psa 106:5).
- **The cross.** *"far be it from me to boast except in the cross of our Lord Jesus Christ, by which the world has been crucified to me, and I to the world"* (Gal 6:14).
- **Christ, not the flesh.** *"glory in Christ Jesus and put no confidence in the flesh — though I myself have reason for confidence in the flesh also"* (Php 3:3–4). Paul has standing in the flesh, *"I have more"*, and sets it aside.
- **Hope, sufferings, God.** ESV renders *kauchaomai* "rejoice" here:
  - *"we rejoice in hope of the glory of God. Not only that, but we rejoice in our sufferings"* (Rom 5:2–3).
  - *"we also rejoice in God through our Lord Jesus Christ, through whom we have now received reconciliation"* (5:11).
  - The same word that elsewhere is a proud boast is here joy in hope, in suffering, and in God.
- **Held fast.** *"if indeed we hold fast our confidence and our boasting in our hope"* (Heb 3:6).
- **The conscience.** *"our boast is this, the testimony of our conscience, that we behaved in the world with simplicity and godly sincerity, not by earthly wisdom but by the grace of God"* (2Co 1:12). The boast is a clear conscience, and its ground is named as grace.

### IV. Boasting in weakness (9)

2 Corinthians 10–12 is one long passage in which Paul is forced to boast, and he turns the boast round.
- **As a fool.** *"accept me as a fool, so that I too may boast a little. What I am saying with this boastful confidence, I say not as the Lord would but as a fool"* (2Co 11:16–17). He names the boast foolish while he makes it.
- **Weakness.** *"If I must boast, I will boast of the things that show my weakness"* (11:30). What follows is being let down a wall in a basket (11:32–33).
- **Visions.** *"I must go on boasting. Though there is nothing to be gained by it, I will go on to visions and revelations"* (12:1). Then *"On behalf of this man I will boast, but on my own behalf I will not boast, except of my weaknesses"* (12:5).
- **No more than is seen.** *"if I should wish to boast, I would not be a fool, for I would be speaking the truth; but I refrain from it, so that no one may think more of me than he sees in me or hears from me"* (12:6).
  - **I read:** this is the opposite of pride's assumed standing. He holds back even a true boast so that his standing in others' eyes is no higher than what is so.
- **Power in weakness.** *"I will boast all the more gladly of my weaknesses, so that the power of Christ may rest upon me ... For when I am weak, then I am strong"* (12:9–10).
- **Authority given.** *"if I boast a little too much of our authority, which the Lord gave for building you up and not for destroying you"* (2Co 10:8). The authority is given, and it is given for others.
- *"this boasting of mine will not be silenced"*: that he served them without burdening them (2Co 11:9–10).

### V. Boasting of others (14)

Paul's most frequent boast is in other people.
- *"I have great pride in you; I am filled with comfort. In all our affliction, I am overflowing with joy"* (2Co 7:4).
- His boasts to Titus about them *"proved true"* (7:14). He asks them to *"give proof ... of our boasting about you"* (8:24). He sends brothers *"so that our boasting about you may not prove empty"* (9:3), or *"we would be humiliated"* (9:4). The boast puts Paul's own standing at risk on the conduct of others.
- *"your readiness, of which I boast about you ... your zeal has stirred up most of them"* (9:2).
- **At Christ's coming.**
  - *"what is our hope or joy or crown of boasting before our Lord Jesus at his coming? Is it not you?"* (1Th 2:19).
  - *"on the day of our Lord Jesus you will boast of us as we will boast of you"* (2Co 1:14).
  - *"so that in the day of Christ I may be proud that I did not run in vain"* (Php 2:16).
- *"we ourselves boast about you in the churches of God for your steadfastness and faith in all your persecutions"* (2Th 1:4).
- *"so that in me you may have ample cause to glory in Christ Jesus"* (Php 1:26).
- **His work, which is Christ's.** *"I have reason to be proud of my work for God. For I will not venture to speak of anything except what Christ has accomplished through me"* (Rom 15:17–18). The pride is in a work he credits to Christ.
- *"by my pride in you, which I have in Christ Jesus our Lord, I die every day!"* (1Co 15:31).
- *"Let the lowly brother boast in his exaltation, and the rich in his humiliation"* (Jam 1:9–10, carried).

### VI. Mercy triumphs (1)

*"judgment is without mercy to one who has shown no mercy. Mercy triumphs over judgment"* (Jam 2:13). The word *katakauchaomai* (boast over) is said of mercy. **I read:** mercy rises over judgment. The verse is about the mercy a person shows, and the mercy shown to them.

## 3. For Ch 13: God's character and the boast

1. **God is not the subject of a boast in these verses. He is its right object, and his character is what is boasted in.** *"let him who boasts boast in this, that he understands and knows me, that I am the Lord who practices steadfast love, justice, and righteousness in the earth. For in these things I delight"* (Jer 9:24). The proper boast is knowing God as he is.
2. **The boast is moved, not removed.** The same impulse that boasts in wisdom, might and riches (Jer 9:23) is turned to God (9:24; 1Co 1:31). It becomes joy in hope and in suffering (Rom 5:2–3).
   - **I read:** set beside batches 1–3, the human "lifting" is not abolished. Where it rests on oneself it is pride. Where it rests on God it is glory in him. This fits Ch 12 claim C-182 (the boast moved to what God does). It is a reconciliation point when that chapter is touched.
3. **Gift excludes self-boasting.** *"it is the gift of God, not a result of works, so that no one may boast"* (Eph 2:8–9). *"What do you have that you did not receive?"* (1Co 4:7). *"if anyone thinks he is something, when he is nothing, he deceives himself"* (Gal 6:3).
   - This is the researcher's "assumed" in the verses' words: a standing held as one's own when it was received, or thought when it is not so.
   - The contrast with God, from batches 1–3, is that his standing is neither received nor assumed.
4. **The measure is assigned.** *"the area of influence God assigned to us"* (2Co 10:13), and in batch 3 *"the measure of faith that God has assigned"* (Rom 12:3). **I read:** where God's height is his by nature, a person's rightful standing is a measure given, and pride boasts *"beyond limits"*.
5. **Christ's power in weakness.** *"my power is made perfect in weakness ... so that the power of Christ may rest upon me"* (2Co 12:9). The boast in weakness makes room for another's power. This bears on 13.1 *Lowering himself*, as in batch 3 §3.4.
6. **God's steadfast love answers the boast of evil.** *"Why do you boast of evil, O mighty man? The steadfast love of God endures all the day"* (Psa 52:1). God's character is set over the mighty man's boast, as his mercy answered presumption in batch 2 §3.7.

## 4. Points for the account

- **Boasting is the speech of pride, and it can be redeemed.** One account should hold both sides: the boast in self (I) and the boast in the Lord (III). Splitting them would lose the turn that Jer 9:23–24 and 1Co 1:31 make.
- **Boasting of others** (V) is a relationship face. It bears on 10.10, and on M51 love (*"give proof ... of your love and of our boasting"*, 2Co 8:24).
- **Boasting in weakness** (IV) bears on 13.1 *Lowering himself* and Ch 11 §4 (change of character: one word, opposite values, where 2Co 12:9 is already cited).
- **Rom 5:2–3, 11:** the word rendered "rejoice" bears on 10.1 (joy) and M04.
- **The two M18 forward marks** (2Co 11:12; 12:6, OT-97.25) are read here (groups I and IV).

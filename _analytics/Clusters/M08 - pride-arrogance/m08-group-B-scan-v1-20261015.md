## 1. How the scan was done

Each of the 211 verses was read in full. The role of its height word was judged against what the verse does (rule 73). There are three outcomes:

- **D, data.** The height word is a measure, a place, a direction or a setting. This holds even when the verse has other M-codes, if the height word takes no part in them. *Set aside*, as instructed.
- **S, supports another M-code.** The height word takes part in what another M-coded word in the verse says: its measure, its ground, its object or its outcome. *Kept*, with the code or codes named.
- **P, pride sense.** The height word itself names pride, a lifted-up self, or the lifted-up being brought low. These are not height as data. They are M08's own sense, so *kept for the M08 reading with group A*. Most also join an M47 word (heart, spirit, eyes), so they would meet the "supporting" test as well. They are counted separately because the height word is the pride word in them.

**The data (135).**
- 57 have no other M-code. These are the tabernacle and temple measurements, the pillars, Goliath, the gallows, high places and gates.
- 78 have another M-code, but the height word takes no part in it. For example, "the upper gate" stands beside a king, and "a high mountain" beside the Transfiguration.
- 25 are God, or Christ, "on high" with no other M-code joined. Examples are Psa 92:8, Psa 97:9, Heb 1:3 and Luk 24:49 ("power from on high"). They are noted in the CSV so that 13.1 can draw on them if wanted.
- Five are the tree and cedar figures of Eze 31:3, 31:5 and Dan 4:10, 11, 20. The pride in those passages is named in other verses (Eze 31:10; Dan 4:30, 37), and those verses are kept as P.

The same word that names the proud heart elsewhere names a heart lifted up toward God. This is a change of character (#1919), and it is kept as P with that note.

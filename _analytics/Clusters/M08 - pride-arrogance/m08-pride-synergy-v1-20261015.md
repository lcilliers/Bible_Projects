## 2. What the batches show together

Each batch read one family of words. Read together, they cover one subject from several sides. Below is what they show together, with the verses that state it.

### 2.1 One set of words for God's majesty and for human pride

- **The words for God and for people are the same.** *gāʾôn*, *gaʾăwāh*, *gēʾût* and *gāʾāh* are God's majesty (Exo 15:1, 7; Psa 93:1; Isa 2:10) and human pride (Isa 9:9; Jer 48:29; Pro 16:18).
- **The height words are the same.** *gābah* and *rûm* are God being high (Psa 138:6; Isa 57:15) and the heart lifted up (2Ch 26:16; Dan 5:20).
- **The mocking words are said of God's derision** (Psa 2:4; 59:8; Pro 3:34).
- **No verse makes God the subject of a boast.** He is the one boasted in (Jer 9:24).
- So the words do describe God's character. In the ESV the difference between God and people shows in the English chosen ("majesty" against "pride"). It does not show in the Hebrew.

### 2.2 Height that is one's own, given, or taken

**God's height is his own.**
- *"robed in majesty ... you are from everlasting"* (Psa 93:1–2).
- It is shown in acts: *"In the greatness of your majesty you overthrow your adversaries"* (Exo 15:7).
- It is challenged (Exo 15:9; Isa 2:12; Psa 2:2), but never overturned: *"God is not mocked"* (Gal 6:7).

**Height can be given.**
- God gives majesty to a king: *"the Most High God gave Nebuchadnezzar ... majesty"* (Dan 5:18).
- He gives strength (2Ch 26:15), healing (2Ch 32:24), beauty (Eze 28:13–15; 31:9) and the power to get wealth (Deu 8:18).
- He gives his people a "pride": *"He chose our heritage for us, the pride of Jacob"* (Psa 47:4); *"I will make you majestic forever"* (Isa 60:15).

**Pride is the given held as one's own.**
- *"What do you have that you did not receive? If then you received it, why do you boast as if you did not receive it?"* (1Co 4:7).
- In story, each time:
  - *"when he was strong, he grew proud"* (2Ch 26:16)
  - *"did not make return according to the benefit done to him, for his heart was proud"* (2Ch 32:25)
  - *"when his heart was lifted up"* (Dan 5:20)
  - *"your heart has become proud in your wealth"* (Eze 28:5)
  - *"its heart was proud of its height"* (Eze 31:10)
- In the law it is foreseen: *"your heart be lifted up, and you forget the Lord your God ... Beware lest you say in your heart, 'My power and the might of my hand have gotten me this wealth'"* (Deu 8:14, 17).
- **It credits its own hand.** *"By the strength of my hand I have done it"*, said by the one who was the Lord's rod: *"Shall the axe boast over him who hews with it"* (Isa 10:13, 15). And *"which I have built by my mighty power"* (Dan 4:30).
- **I read:** the line the verses draw is not between having height and having none. It is the source and the direction. Height that is one's own by nature belongs to God alone. Height that is given is held from him. Pride is the given taken as one's own, and turned against the giver or over others. This is the researcher's "assumed position", in the verses' own words.

### 2.3 The heart that says what God says

- **God says of himself:** *"I am the Lord, and there is no other, besides me there is no God"* (Isa 45:5); *"I am God, and there is no other; I am God, and there is none like me"* (Isa 46:9).
- **The proud city says the same of itself in its heart.**
  - Babylon: *"who say in your heart, 'I am, and there is no one besides me'"* (Isa 47:8, again in 47:10).
  - Nineveh: *"that said in her heart, 'I am, and there is no one else'"* (Zep 2:15).
- **The same claim in other words:**
  - *"I will make myself like the Most High"* (Isa 14:14).
  - *"you have said, 'I am a god' ... though you make your heart like the heart of a god"* (Eze 28:2).
  - *"proclaiming himself to be God"* (2Th 2:4).
- **And it deceives:** *"The pride of your heart has deceived you"* (Obd 3); *"The horror you inspire has deceived you, and the pride of your heart"* (Jer 49:16).
- The words of God's self-statement come back almost word for word in the proud heart. The verses set them side by side themselves, two chapters apart in one book. **I read:** pride at its full height says of itself what is true only of God.

### 2.4 Pride and hearing

This line runs through every batch.
- *"Hear and give ear; be not proud, for the Lord has spoken"* (Jer 13:15); *"if you will not listen, my soul will weep in secret for your pride"* (13:17).
- *"By insolence comes nothing but strife, but with those who take advice is wisdom"* (Pro 13:10).
- **The scoffer:** *"does not listen to rebuke"* (Pro 13:1); *"does not like to be reproved; he will not go to the wise"* (15:12); *"Do not reprove a scoffer, or he will hate you"* (9:8); *"do not scoff ... Give ear, and hear my voice"* (Isa 28:22–23).
- **Presumption** acts on its own word:
  - the prophet *"who presumes to speak a word in my name that I have not commanded him"* (Deu 18:20)
  - *"you would not listen; but you rebelled ... and presumptuously went up"* (Deu 1:43)
  - not obeying the decision given (Deu 17:12)
- **The messengers mocked** *"until there was no remedy"* (2Ch 36:16).
- **God works to keep pride from a person.**
  - *"he opens the ears of men ... that he may turn man aside from his deed and conceal pride from a man"* (Job 33:16–17).
  - *"to keep me from becoming conceited ... a thorn was given me"* (2Co 12:7).
- **The opposite named for pride is hearing, or fear.**
  - *"So do not become proud, but fear"* (Rom 11:20).
  - *"The fear of the Lord is hatred of evil. Pride and arrogance ... I hate"* (Pro 8:13).

### 2.5 Pride stands over someone

Pride is rarely alone in these verses. It stands over someone:
- **the poor:** *"In arrogance the wicked hotly pursue the poor"* (Psa 10:2); *"Whoever mocks the poor insults his Maker"* (Pro 17:5)
- **the widow:** *"tears down the house of the proud but maintains the widow's boundaries"* (Pro 15:25)
- **the needy left unaided:** *"pride, excess of food, and prosperous ease, but did not aid the poor and needy"* (Eze 16:49)
- **the righteous:** *"speak insolently against the righteous in pride and contempt"* (Psa 31:18); the insolent who deride, smear and dig pits (Psa 119:51, 69, 85)
- **with a closed heart:** *"They close their hearts to pity; with their mouths they speak arrogantly"* (Psa 17:10)
- **set against love, and among the things that divide:** *"'knowledge' puffs up, but love builds up"* (1Co 8:1); *"love ... is not arrogant"* (1Co 13:4); *"Let us not become conceited, provoking one another, envying one another"* (Gal 5:26)
- **felt from below:** *"Our soul has had more than enough ... of the contempt of the proud"* (Psa 123:4)

### 2.6 The speech of pride: boasting and mocking

- **The boast is pride said aloud.**
  - In self: *"Let not the wise man boast in his wisdom"* (Jer 9:23).
  - About tomorrow: *"Do not boast about tomorrow"* (Pro 27:1); *"you boast in your arrogance"* (Jam 4:16).
  - Of a gift not given (Pro 25:14).
- **Boasting is excluded by grace.** *"not a result of works, so that no one may boast"* (Eph 2:9); *"It is excluded"* (Rom 3:27).
- **The boast is moved, not removed.**
  - *"let him who boasts boast in this, that he understands and knows me"* (Jer 9:24); *"Let the one who boasts, boast in the Lord"* (1Co 1:31).
  - The same word becomes joy in hope and in suffering (Rom 5:2–3, 11).
  - Boasting in weakness: *"so that the power of Christ may rest upon me"* (2Co 12:9).
  - Boasting of others (2Co 7:4; 1Th 2:19).
  - The boast within the measure God assigned (2Co 10:13), as thinking of oneself is within *"the measure of faith that God has assigned"* (Rom 12:3).
- **Mocking is pride turned on another, in laughter and taunts.**
  - It targets the sufferer's trust: *"He trusts in the Lord; let him deliver him"* (Psa 22:8).
  - The sufferer answers God, not the mockers: *"Yet you are he who took me from the womb"* (22:9).
  - The scoffer is also the one who will not be taught (§2.4).

### 2.7 Where it ends

- **Its own fall.** *"Pride goes before destruction"* (Pro 16:18); *"One's pride will bring him low"* (Pro 29:23).
- **God brings it down.**
  - *"God opposes the proud"* (Jam 4:6).
  - *"he has scattered the proud in the thoughts of their hearts"* (Luk 1:51).
  - *"The haughty looks of man shall be brought low ... and the Lord alone will be exalted in that day"* (Isa 2:11).
  - *"it is God who executes judgment, putting down one and lifting up another"* (Psa 75:7).
  - Death too: *"Your pomp is brought down to Sheol"* (Isa 14:11).
- **What it is set against, God answers otherwise.**
  - *"though the Lord is high, he regards the lowly, but the haughty he knows from afar"* (Psa 138:6).
  - God's derision answers only those who rise against him (Psa 2:2–4; 59:7–8; Pro 1:24–26; 3:34). In these verses it is never aimed at the weak.
- **Pride can be named wrongly.**
  - An angry brother says *"I know your presumption"* (1Sa 17:28), and the chapter shows that David was sent by his father (17:17–18).
  - A friend explains another's fall: *"For when they are humbled you say, 'It is because of pride'"* (Job 22:29).

### 2.8 The other face

The same words are not pride when the height is given, or turned toward God:
- *"His heart was courageous [lifted up] in the ways of the Lord"* (2Ch 17:6), with *gābah*, the word used of Uzziah's proud heart
- *"my strength of soul you increased"* (Psa 138:3), with *rāhab*, elsewhere "insolent" (Isa 3:5)
- the honour God gives (Psa 47:4; Isa 4:2; 60:15; Nah 2:2)
- the boast in the Lord (§2.6)
- *"my heart is not lifted up ... I have calmed and quieted my soul"* (Psa 131:1–2), set against the arrogant man *"never at rest"* (Hab 2:5)

### 2.9 The Son

- **His majesty is received.** *"he received honor and glory from God the Father"* (2Pe 1:17).
- **His seat comes after purification for sins.** *"After making purification for sins, he sat down at the right hand of the Majesty on high"* (Heb 1:3).
- **His first place is grounded.** *"that in everything he might be preeminent. For in him all the fullness of God was pleased to dwell"* (Col 1:18–19).
- **Majesty and handing over stand side by side.** The majesty seen in his work is placed beside his being *"delivered into the hands of men"* (Luk 9:43–44).
- **He did not grasp.** *"though he was in the form of God, did not count equality with God a thing to be grasped"* (Php 2:6). **I read:** pride grasps a place that is not its own, and the Son did not grasp the place that was his. This verse is told in 13.1 *Lowering himself* (M09).
- **He is mocked on that very standing**, as king, as prophet and as saviour (Mat 27:29; Luk 22:64; Mar 15:31). He *"made no answer"* (Luk 23:9), and he prays *"Father, forgive them"* (Luk 23:34).

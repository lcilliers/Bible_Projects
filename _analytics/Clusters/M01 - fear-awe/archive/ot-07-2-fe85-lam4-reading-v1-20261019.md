## The verse, word by word (iba.db `span`)

"For the chastisement of the daughter of my people has been greater than the punishment of Sodom,
which was overthrown in a moment, and no hands were wrung for her" (Lam 4:6).

- **The main act is a comparison of two punishments.** "chastisement" is H5771I (*ʿāwōn*,
  "iniquity: punishment", M56), and "greater" is H1431 ("to magnify"). "punishment" is H2403I
  (*ḥaṭṭāʾt*, "sin: punishment", M56).
- **"wrung" is H2342A *ḥûl*, "to twist: tremble" (M01).** It describes the manner of Sodom's fall:
  "overthrown in a moment, and no hands were wrung for her". It names no fear in anyone. By role
  (rule 73), it is a qualifier of the comparison, not a fear characteristic.

## The passage (Lam 4:1–11)

The passage says what makes Zion's punishment "greater" than Sodom's sudden end. It is the long
wasting:
- "Those who once feasted on delicacies perish in the streets" (4:5)
- "their skin has shriveled on their bones" (4:8)
- "Happier were the victims of the sword than the victims of hunger, who wasted away, pierced by
  lack of the fruits of the field" (4:9)

And it reaches into the inner being:
- "the daughter of my people has become cruel, like the ostriches in the wilderness" (4:3; cited in
  10.10)
- "The hands of compassionate women have boiled their own children; they became their food during
  the destruction of the daughter of my people" (4:10; cited nowhere)

The section closes with "The Lord gave full vent to his wrath" (4:11).

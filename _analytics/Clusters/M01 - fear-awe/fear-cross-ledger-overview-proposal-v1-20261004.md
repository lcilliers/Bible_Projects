# Fear: cross-ledger overview, proposal (v1)

**Date:** 2026-10-04 · **Author:** Claude Code · **Status:** proposal, for the researcher to set the aim. Nothing has been read or written yet.
**Researcher, verbatim (2026-10-04):** *"approve 1958, hold the weave.   does it make sense to read all 4 of the unit ledgers to get an overview of what fear is,  what influences its nature."*

## 1. Recommendation: yes

1. **The vocabulary is complete.** Units 1–4 cover every M01 word: Hebrew, Aramaic and Greek, FE-01 to FE-162, with faces A to AM.
2. **The current fear account was written from half of it.** 10.1 *Fear* was written from units 1–2 only. It says itself that it will need to be read again once the Greek has been read (FE-121). Units 3–4 are held, so nothing yet brings all four ledgers together.
3. **It is the further work that unit 3 waits on.** OT-10 holds the weave until there has been more work "on and around it". This overview would be that work.
4. **It fits the approved shape for a phenomenon** (#1930, modelled on 10.1): what Scripture says fear is, its forms from the words themselves, and why they differ. Your second question, *what influences its nature*, is the third of these. It is also the change-of-character record that #1919 asks for.

## 2. What it would produce

One file, `fear-cross-ledger-overview-v1-<date>.md`, in this folder. It would hold no chapter text.

| Part | Content | Source in the ledgers |
|---|---|---|
| A. What the verses say fear is | Only what a verse states, each in its setting (for example, 1Jo 4:18 read with 4:17; FE-136) | the observation rows |
| B. Its forms | The four face tables merged into one: each face with its hits, the units it occurs in, its surfaces and its key verses. Faces that appear in only one language are marked as such | §A of each ledger |
| C. What influences its nature | The four "what differs" tables (§C of each ledger) merged into one list of factors. Each factor carries its verses and its basis (S = stated, R = my reading). Where two units show the same factor, they are joined only if the verses name it the same way | §C of each ledger; the RS items for 11 §4 |
| D. What fear leads to | Each outcome as the verses state it: driving away, silencing, hiding, holding back the hand, coming forward, glorifying God, asking him to leave | the observation rows |
| E. Held, and not used | The life-and-death crossings (OT-08), FE-85 (OT-07), and data-only rows, listed by id only | the dispositions |

## 3. Guards

- **Let Scripture speak** (cfg_behaviour_rule 69). The overview gathers what the ledgers already record. It adds no pattern, mechanism or division that no verse states. Every reading beyond a verse is marked "I read".
- **No superlatives without a count.** Any "most" or "only" is counted against all four ledgers, or it is left out.
- **The ledgers are the source.** No new pull is made. Every quote in the overview is re-run through `fear-quote-check-v1-20261001.py`.

## 4. Cost

The four ledgers come to about 150 KB, and the overview reads all of them in full. It fits in one chat unit, at about the cost of one unit ledger.

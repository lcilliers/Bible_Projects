# M47 Inner Seat — framework overview: the gateway to the web (v1)

**Date:** 2026-09-30 · **Author:** Claude Code · **Status:** for researcher review. Nothing written to the DB.
**Asked for (researcher, verbatim):** "the next phase is to distill M47 into a narrative and to use it as the springboard for the next investigation … provide a high level overview of the findings in this folder to provide a framework for looking into the inner being seat that can serve as the gateway to add the detail findings in the next row."
**What this is:** a high-level map of what the M47 folder found, arranged as a frame that later strands can hang their detail on. It is **[Claude reading] throughout**, a distillation of the files listed in §9. It is not prose for the prose store; that stays the researcher's (#1900).
**What it is not:** a new reading. No verse was re-read for this document. Every point comes from a file in this folder, and the pointer (file § number) says where the verses are.
**Method context:** fan-out / progressive-stream analysis (#1900, 2026-09-30). The inner being is a web; M47 is the starting strand. Cluster codes are anchors, not goals.

## 2. The core picture: one inner person, seen from several angles

The verses do not present a set of organs with fixed jobs. They present **one inner person**, and each word brings its own angle (depiction §1, §5). Words are placed in parallel, summed, nested and translated into one another; they come apart clearly only at a few boundaries (§3).

| Component | Its angle | What it characteristically does | What is done to it | Pointer |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Heart** (*lēv / lēvāv / kardia*) | the **centre** or "midst" | says within, plans, decides, stores, remembers, understands, feels, is oriented; the source of speech and deeds | tested, weighed, known by God alone; turned, hardened, filled, written on, stolen, made new | depiction §2.1 |
| **Soul** (*nephesh / psychē*) | the **living, needing self** | thirsts, longs, is bitter, waits, loves, blesses; is the life and the "myself" | hunted, delivered, ransomed, restored, addressed by its owner, sworn by | depiction §2.2 |
| **Spirit** (*rûaḥ / pneuma*) | the **moving, animating force** | is troubled, revived, broken, fervent, provoked; carries temper and self-knowledge | stirred, hardened, weighed; God's Spirit comes upon, fills, dwells in it; dispositions "arrive" as spirits | depiction §2.3; spirit distinction |
| **Flesh** (*bāśār / sarx*) | the **visible, mortal, related** person | trembles, faints, sings, rots, heals in response to the inner seat; kin, "all flesh" | frail before God; in Paul, an inner orientation set against the Spirit | depiction §2.4 |
| **Conscience** (*syneidēsis*, NT) | the **self-judging** heart | bears witness, accuses or excuses | defiled, seared, weak; purified by Christ's blood | depiction §2.5 |
| **Bosom** (*ḥêq*) | the **holding place** | holds loved ones, anger and insults alike | — | depiction §2.6 |

The spirit words need the distinction step before any use: of 559 rows, 259 are clearly divine, 138 clearly human, 68 other beings, 23 dispositions, and 41 not determinable (spirit distinction §2–3).

## 3. How the components relate

| Relation | What the text does | Example | Pointer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Parallel | sets two components side by side as near-equivalents | "a clean heart … a right spirit" (Psa 51:10) | depiction §3.1 |
| Wholeness | adds terms to name the whole person | "all your heart and all your soul" (Deu 6:5); "spirit and soul and body" (1Th 5:23) | §3.2 |
| Unity / division | one heart, one soul, one flesh; a double heart | Psa 86:11; Psa 12:2; Gal 5:17 | §3.3 |
| Containment | the heart as the innermost place things are put | the Spirit "in our hearts" (Gal 4:6); law, idols, eternity in the heart | §3.7 |
| Cause and effect | inner state ↔ body, face, food, senses | "a crushed spirit dries up the bones" (Pro 17:22); bread strengthens the heart | §3.6 |
| Opposition | flesh against Spirit (NT); frail, not hostile, in the OT | Gal 5:16–17; Isa 31:3 | §3.5 |
| Shared between persons | souls knit, hearts joined or stolen, spirit passed on | 1Sa 18:1; 2Sa 15:6; 2Ki 2:9 | §3.8 |
| Parted at death | dust to earth, spirit to God, soul to Sheol or delivered | Ecc 12:7; Gen 35:18 | §3.9 |

Distinctions are drawn sharply only at death, in Paul's flesh / Spirit contrast, in Heb 4:12 ("division of soul and of spirit"), and in 1Co 14:14 (spirit and mind in prayer) (depiction §3.4).

## 4. The seat across the eight domains (Batches A–H)

Each batch closed with cross-cutting observations. Read side by side, each component shows a **recurring profile** across the domains:

| Domain (batch) | Heart | Soul | Spirit (human) | Flesh | Also present |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Feeling** (A) | the widest range of feeling; its state decides whether a feeling takes hold | bitterness, longing, weariness | troubled, crushed, faint, temper | trembles, faints, rejoices | God's Spirit gives joy, is grieved; God has the same feelings |
| **Knowing, speaking, hearing** (B) | attention ("set the heart"), knowing, storing; hardness = not hearing | receives knowledge; dwells on memory | self-knowledge and searching | a way of knowing ("according to the flesh") | God's Spirit as source of wisdom and prophecy; hearing as the main entry |
| **Willing, desiring** (C) | orientation set, held, divided, hardened | the desiring self: desire and vitality are one word | energy and disposition rather than choice | OT longs for God; NT wrong desire | other spirits act on orientation from outside |
| **Moral condition** (D) | the organ that is morally evaluated | the accountable, answerable life | evaluated on pride / humility | ritual body (OT); seat of sin (Paul) | other spirits carry the negative adjectives; inner vs outer purity; conscience |
| **Relating to God** (E) | where the relation is located and tested; prayer without voice | the self in address; the life redeemed | worships, serves, committed to God at death | "all flesh" comes to God; covenant cut in flesh | the divine Spirit relates to God on the person's behalf |
| **Relating to others** (F) | where hatred starts and is spoken | bonds: knit, malice, abhorrence | registers bonds; the manner of approach | kinship | inner states spread between people |
| **Life, body, condition** (G) | sustained by bread; strength or hardness by direction | life given and gathered; sworn by, bargained for | breath returns, revives | all living beings; valued by circumstance | components give way in sequence; each has its own verb at death |
| **Setting, narrative** (H) | the king's heart acted upon; allegiance | a life priced in law, not before God | ruled | kinship-flesh claims | setting-words turned inward: the seat as governed |

(Batch §9–§14 "Cross-cutting observations" in each file.)

**Read across the rows** [Claude reading]: the heart is most often where things are *located, set and evaluated*; the soul is most often the self that *needs, is at stake and is addressed*; the human spirit is most often *moved, stirred and disposed*; flesh changes value from OT to NT and by circumstance. These are tendencies from the batch summaries, not counts.

## 6. Where the seat stops: widening from the seat to the operation

- **The seat is the marked case.** Of 24,365 verses carrying another cluster's word, 2,404 (10%) have a seat word in the verse. Fear 11%, anger 10%, grief 16%, joy 18% (seat coverage §3). Hypothesis, not finding: naming the heart or soul may be a deliberate choice, to locate, intensify, internalise or hide a state (seat coverage §4.1).
- **Other vocabulary stands in for the seat.** *qereb* "within", bones, kidneys, bowels, nostril (anger), bile, "in himself". Several were in T-codes and invisible to cluster reading until #1880 (T-code question §1).
- **Twelve carrying methods** (operation modes §3): named seat · seat inside the word · inner place outside M47 · the operation word alone · face and body · act · speech · image · another person reads or names it · another person's seat · passage or next verse · something from outside reaches in.
- **Four directions of movement:** internal (one operation leads to another in the same person), outward (inner shows in face, act, speech), inward (outside reaches in), across persons (methods 9, 10 and 12).
- **What affects an operation:** a word, a gift, an interpreter, handing it over, a time limit, persuasion (operation modes §2).
- **What is not recorded anywhere yet:** the carrying method and the surface direction (operation modes §4). Nothing is proposed to record them.

## 7. The framework: a frame for adding strand detail

A proposed way to place any later detail against what M47 already holds. Each layer comes directly from §2–§6; none is new.

| Layer | Question it answers | Values drawn from M47 |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Seat** | Where, if anywhere, is the operation located? | heart · soul · spirit · flesh · conscience · bosom · a stand-in (within, bones, kidneys, bowels, nostril) · none |
| **2. Operation** | What is the inner being doing or undergoing? | feeling · knowing / hearing · willing / desiring · moral condition · relating to God · relating to others · living / dying · governing oneself (the eight domains) |
| **3. Carrier** | How does the text carry or show it? | the twelve methods (§6) |
| **4. Agent** | Who or what acts on the seat? | the self · God · God's Spirit · other spirits · another person · substances (bread, wine) · words · sight |
| **5. Movement** | What leads to what, and in which direction? | internal chain · outward · inward · across persons; sequences and thresholds (T2, T3) |
| **6. Value** | Does the value depend on object or circumstance? | fixed · reversed by object (T4) · by component · OT / NT shift (flesh) |
| **7. Boundary** | Where do the components come apart? | death · flesh / Spirit · soul / spirit (Heb 4:12) · spirit / mind |

**How a strand would use it** [Claude reading]: a strand (for example T2, thresholds) is explored in its own verses; each observation notes which layers it touches; where it touches M47, it cites the batch section instead of re-reading it. The frame is a set of anchors for the web, not a grid to fill.

**Fit with the capture model (#1900):** the `ib_node` principle (verse · surface · strong · subgroup = association · question = stream) remains the central linkage. This frame does not change it. Capture stays per strand, with no backfill.

## 3. What a reading of the body's part shows

Read together for what each says the **body** does beside heart, soul and spirit, the verses fall into
a few kinds. These are the kinds the verses themselves state, not a scheme laid over them:

- **Named together as the whole person.** "your whole spirit and soul and body" (1Th 5:23); "holy in
  body and spirit" (1Co 7:34); "destroy both soul and body" (Mat 10:28). In the book: Ch 3.
- **What the heart wants is done in the body.** "God gave them up in the lusts of their hearts to
  impurity, to the dishonoring of their bodies" (Rom 1:24). "If by the Spirit you put to death the
  deeds of the body, you will live" (Rom 8:13). Severity to the body is "of no value in stopping the
  indulgence of the flesh" (Col 2:23). In the book, but in separate chapters: 10.6, Ch 2, 10.14.
- **The body offered, and washed, with the heart.** "present your bodies as a living sacrifice … which
  is your spiritual worship" (Rom 12:1). "let us draw near with a true heart … with our hearts
  sprinkled clean from an evil conscience **and our bodies washed with pure water**" (Heb 10:22).
- **The body as a dwelling, and not one's own.** "your body is a temple of the Holy Spirit within you,
  whom you have from God? **You are not your own, for you were bought with a price. So glorify God in
  your body**" (1Co 6:19–20).
- **The body apart from the spirit.** "absent in body … present in spirit" (1Co 5:3); "the body apart
  from the spirit is dead" (Jam 2:26); "the body is dead because of sin, the Spirit is life" (Rom 8:10).

**Two things stand out:**
1. **The body half is dropped in two quotations.**
   - Heb 10:22 is quoted (Ch 8, Ch 14) up to "an evil conscience", and stops before "and our bodies
     washed with pure water".
   - 1Co 6:19 is quoted (Ch 6, Ch 14) as "your body is a temple of the Holy Spirit within you",
     without "You are not your own … So glorify God in your body" (6:19b–20). 6:20 is not cited.

   In both, the verse joins the body to the inner act, and the quotation leaves the body out.
2. **There is no one place that says what the body's part is.** The kinds above are each told, in Ch 2,
   3, 6, 9, 10.6 and 10.14, but always as part of another subject. Ch 9 *Soul and body* is the nearest.
   It is about where the words part at death, not about the body's part while living.

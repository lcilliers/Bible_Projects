## 1. What M47 is

The heart / soul / spirit / flesh / conscience / bosom words: **38 Strong's codes in 2,688 verses** (stocktake v2). The full verse read (depiction v1) found **one inner person spoken of from several angles**, not a set of organs:

| Word | Angle |
|---|---|
| Heart | the centre: thinks, speaks within, decides, stores, feels, is oriented; where God and others act |
| Soul | the living, needing self: appetite, longing, life, "myself", "a person" |
| Spirit | the moving force: wind, breath, temper, arriving dispositions, God's Spirit entering |
| Flesh | the visible, mortal, related person; in Paul also an orientation against the Spirit |
| Conscience / bosom | the self-judging heart (NT) / the holding place |

Spirit uses are classed divine / human / other / undetermined per verse (spirit-distinction v1 + CSV).

## 3. What has emerged [Claude reading]

- **The inner seat is acted upon as much as it acts.** God turns, stirs, hardens and fills hearts and spirits; the king's heart, the most powerful, is shown most often as acted on (G §11, H §1.1).
- **Inner states are thresholds.** A merry heart after eating and drinking comes just before a decisive event (murder, exposure, redemption); the sad heart at a feast turns to prayer (F §7, G §14.6, H §7.6).
- **Components give way in sequence.** Samson: soul vexed → heart told → strength gone → Lord gone → strength asked for (G §3.6).
- **Many words have no fixed moral value.** Tender, strong/hard, entice, *nāṣar* (keep the heart / the wily heart), vengeance, fatness: the value comes from object and circumstance.
- **The soul is sworn by, bargained for and priced.** The law prices a life equally for all; before God no price buys a soul back (G §4–5).
- **The inner seat is governed.** Words for rule, service, guarding and judgment are turned inward: one rules one's spirit, serves from the heart, keeps one's soul; the heart condemns or justifies the self (H §7.3).

- **Input:** [`m64-plan-verses-v1-20261008.csv`](m64-plan-verses-v1-20261008.csv) (159 verses).
- **Scan grouping:** [`m64-plan-scan-v1-20261008.csv`](m64-plan-scan-v1-20261008.csv), with one row per verse giving its group and its rule 72 route. It is built by [`m64-plan-scan-grouping-v1-20261008.py`](m64-plan-scan-grouping-v1-20261008.py), which checks that every verse is assigned once.

## 1. The assessment in brief

The verses show a real capacity in the human inner being to hold something before it happens: an intention, a scheme, a design, a purpose or a weighing of whether one is able.

- **Where it is placed.** The verses place this in the **heart**:
  - *"The heart of man plans his way"* (Pro 16:9)
  - *"who plan evil things in their heart"* (Psa 140:2)
  - *"the month that he had devised from his own heart"* (1Ki 12:33)
  - *"the purposes of the heart"* (1Co 4:5)
  - *"every intention of the thoughts of his heart"* (Gen 6:5)
- **What God says of it.** God says of it, *"nothing that they propose to do will now be impossible for them"* (Gen 11:6).
- **What the verses mostly use it for.** By far its most frequent use in these verses is **against another person**: 43 verses of devising, plotting and scheming harm.
- **What it does not secure.** The capacity does not hold the outcome. *"The heart of man plans his way, but the Lord establishes his steps"* (Pro 16:9).

I read the verses as presenting looking ahead as real but bounded, and as morally charged in both directions. The same words are used for a man's resolve to keep his mouth clean (Psa 17:3) and for the wicked plotting on his bed (Psa 36:4).

**Not every verse in this set is about looking ahead.**
- **Counting words.** H2803H *count* (24 verses in the scan) is mostly about counting, regarding and esteeming: valuing a thing or person now, not planning ahead (§4).
- **Craft and no bearing.** 18 verses carry no inner-being bearing (§5), and the tabernacle craft verses are about design as skill (§3.4).

## 3. What the verses show

### 3.1 It happens in the heart, and it is hidden until it acts

- *"Whoever winks his eyes plans dishonest things; he who purses his lips brings evil to pass"* (Pro 16:30). The planning shows only in small signs.
- *"I did not know it was against me they devised schemes"* (Jer 11:19). Jeremiah did not see the plan aimed at him.
- *"they intended to do me harm"* (Neh 6:2). The intent sat behind the friendly invitation, *"Come and let us meet together"*.
- *"But he does not so intend, and his heart does not so think; but it is in his heart to destroy"* (Isa 10:7). Of Assyria, what is in the heart differs from the role God gives it. I read this as the intention belonging to the one who holds it, whatever use another makes of the act.
- *"thoughts will come into your mind, and you will devise an evil scheme"* (Eze 38:10). Here the thought arrives first and the devising follows. This is the one verse in the set that states such a sequence; no other verse is joined to it here.
- *"you are inventing them out of your own mind"* (Neh 6:8). The same capacity can produce what is false.

### 3.2 It works in time: the night before, the morning after

- *"Woe to those who devise wickedness and work evil on their beds! When the morning dawns, they perform it, because it is in the power of their hand."* (Mic 2:1)
- *"He plots trouble while on his bed"* (Psa 36:4).
- *"For I know what they are inclined to do even today, before I have brought them into the land"* (Deu 31:21). The inclination is already there before the occasion comes.

I read Mic 2:1 as the clearest picture in the set of looking ahead as such. The scheme is held overnight and carried out at dawn, when the hand has the power to do it. The verse itself names the power of the hand as the reason it is done.

### 3.3 Weighing before acting

- *"will not sit down first and deliberate whether he is able with ten thousand to meet him who comes against him with twenty thousand?"* (Luk 14:31). The deliberation comes "first", and it measures ability against what is coming.
- *"She considers a field and buys it"* (Pro 31:16).
- *"consider it, take counsel, and speak"* (Judg 19:30), said after the outrage at Gibeah.
- **The sailors of Acts 27** plan under uncertainty. They act *"on the chance that somehow they could reach Phoenix"* (v12) and *"supposing that they had obtained their purpose"* (v13). Later they *"planned if possible to run the ship ashore"* (v39). Their looking ahead is hedged by what they cannot know.
- **Paul and his own planning:** *"Do I make my plans according to the flesh, ready to say 'Yes, yes' and 'No, no' at the same time?"* (2Co 1:17). For Paul, a plan carries one's word. A plan that wavers raises the question of where it comes from.
- **Dissent from another's plan:** Joseph of Arimathea *"had not consented to their decision and action"* (Luk 23:51).
- **Others in the group:** Dan 6:3 (the king plans to set Daniel over the kingdom, *"because an excellent spirit was in him"*) and Act 15:37 (Barnabas wanted to take Mark).

### 3.4 Devising a design: skill given

- *"He has filled them with skill to do every sort of work done by an engraver or by a designer"* (Exo 35:35). Also *"to devise artistic designs"* (Exo 31:4, 35:32).
- In Exodus the capacity to devise a design is something God fills people with (route A and B).
- Elsewhere the same word is used for human invention: Uzziah's machines *"invented by skillful men"* (2Ch 26:15), and in a woe, those who *"like David invent for themselves instruments of music"* (Amo 6:5).
- **Data only:** 8 Exodus verses where H2803G only describes the finished object as *"skillfully worked"*. They are routed C.

### 3.5 Its most frequent use in this set: devising against another (43 verses)

- **The forms:**
  - plotting (*"as they scheme together against me, as they plot to take my life"*, Psa 31:13)
  - imagining (*"they imagine the worst for me"*, Psa 41:7)
  - using the tongue (*"Your tongue plots destruction"*, Psa 52:2; *"let us strike him with the tongue"*, Jer 18:18)
  - group plans (*"Come, let us cut her off from being a nation!"*, Jer 48:2; *"they made plans to put him to death"*, Joh 11:53)
  - against God himself (*"What do you plot against the Lord?"*, Nah 1:9)
- **What is commanded:** *"let none of you devise evil against another in your heart"* (Zec 7:10, and again 8:17).
- **What comes back on the planner:**
  - *"you shall do to him as he had meant to do to his brother"* (Deu 19:19)
  - Haman's plan should *"return on his own head"* (Est 9:25)
  - *"Whoever plans to do evil will be called a schemer"* (Pro 24:8)
- **One case where planning does not seem to come first:** Act 5:33, *"they were enraged and wanted to kill them"*. Here G1011 renders the wish that follows rage, rather than a plan laid in advance. I read this as worth noting beside 10.13 Anger; it is not placed.

### 3.6 A purpose held: resolve, aim and the mind set

- *"I have purposed that my mouth will not transgress"* (Psa 17:3). The psalmist sets a course ahead for his own speech, under God's testing *"by night"*.
- *"exhorted them all to remain faithful to the Lord with steadfast purpose"* (Act 11:23).
- *"my teaching, my conduct, my aim in life, my faith"* (2Ti 3:10).
- **David asks God to keep his people's purposes:** *"keep forever such purposes and thoughts in the hearts of your people, and direct their hearts toward you"* (1Ch 29:18).
- **The mind set and held:**
  - *"to set the mind on the flesh is death, but to set the mind on the Spirit is life and peace"* (Rom 8:6–7). This is G5427, the M64 purpose word.
  - *"You keep him in perfect peace whose mind is stayed on you"* (Isa 26:3). This is H3336, the intention/frame word.

### 3.7 Reckoning forward

- *"the priest shall calculate the price according to the years that remain until the year of jubilee"* (Lev 27:18). See also Lev 25:27, 50, 52 and 27:23.
- **Flagged for your decision:** this is numerical looking ahead toward a fixed future date. I routed these 5 verses C, because the verses state a valuation rule, not an inner act. You may want them as B.

### 3.8 The plan and its outcome

- *"The heart of man plans his way, but the Lord establishes his steps"* (Pro 16:9).
- *"Take counsel together, but it will come to nothing"* (Isa 8:10).
- *"if this plan or this undertaking is of man, it will fail"* (Act 5:38). Gamaliel's counsel.
- *"I have often intended to come to you (but thus far have been prevented)"* (Rom 1:13).
- *"you meant evil against me, but God meant it for good"* (Gen 50:20). The same word, H2803I, is used for both meanings.
- *"they will not succeed"* (Psa 21:11), and *"but only for a time"* (Dan 11:24).

### 3.9 God sees the intention

- *"the Lord searches all hearts and understands every plan and thought"* (1Ch 28:9).
- *"the intention of man's heart is evil from his youth"* (Gen 8:21; also Gen 6:5).
- *"he knows our frame; he remembers that we are dust"* (Psa 103:14). H3336 here is "frame", the thing formed.
- *"will disclose the purposes of the heart"* (1Co 4:5).

## 4. Counting, regarding and esteeming (H2803H, 24 verses)

These verses use the word's sense of reckoning, not of looking ahead.

- **God reckoning:** *"he counted it to him as righteousness"* (Gen 15:6; Psa 106:31) and *"against whom the Lord counts no iniquity"* (Psa 32:2).
- **People valuing:**
  - *"we esteemed him not"* (Isa 53:3)
  - *"those who feared the Lord … esteemed his name"* (Mal 3:16)
  - *"no regard for silver"* (Isa 13:17)
  - *"there is no regard for man"* (Isa 33:8)
- **Being regarded:** *"we are regarded as sheep to be slaughtered"* (Psa 44:22) and *"I am counted among those who go down to the pit"* (Psa 88:4).

These are inner-being acts of valuing (route B, or A and B for God's reckoning), but not of planning. I read them as belonging with whatever strand takes up esteem and regard, not with looking ahead. Placement is for you.

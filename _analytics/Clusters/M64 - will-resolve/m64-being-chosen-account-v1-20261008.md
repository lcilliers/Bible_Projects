# Being chosen, in the human inner being — the account from the §2b readings

**Date:** 2026-10-08 · **Escalation:** #1978 · **Status:** for the researcher's review. It is woven into 10.7 v11 in the same step, on instruction.

**Asked (researcher, verbatim):** *"write the 2b account and weave into 10.7"*.

**What it rests on:** 15 verses, each read in its whole passage in the ESV text in iba.db `verse`. Every quote below was checked against that text.
- [batch 1](m64-choose-2b-reading-batch1-chosen-ot-v1-20261008.md): 2Sa 6:21, 1Ch 28:10, Psa 65:4, Psa 105:43, Psa 106:5, Isa 43:10, Isa 44:2
- [batch 2](m64-choose-2b-reading-batch2-chosen-nt-v1-20261008.md): Act 6:5, Eph 1:4, Col 3:12, 2Th 2:13, Tit 1:1, Jam 2:5, 2Pe 1:10, Rev 17:14

**Scope:** The account covers the side of the one chosen: what the verses say of the inner life of a person who is chosen. God's own choosing, as an act of God in his own right, belongs to 13.1 (rule 72). Others who act around a choice (§2c) are not yet read. Michal (2Sa 6:16) and Moses in the breach (Psa 106:23) are kept for that reading. Readings beyond what a verse says are marked "I read".

**Companion:** [the account of choosing](m64-choosing-account-v1-20261007.md) (§2a), which is the chooser's side.

---

Scripture uses the same verbs for choosing and for being chosen. In fourteen of these fifteen verses God is the one who chooses. In one, Acts 6:5, a gathering of people chooses. What the verses say of the chosen person is mostly not what made them chosen. It is what follows: what they are moved to, what they are called to, and what they still fail in.

## Who chooses, and in what order

**When God chooses, the choosing comes first. The inner life of the chosen is its purpose.**
- *"my servant whom I have chosen, that you may know and believe me and understand that I am he"* (Isa 43:10). The people chosen as witnesses are described in the same passage as *"blind, yet have eyes … deaf, yet have ears"* (43:8).
- *"he chose us in him before the foundation of the world, that we should be holy and blameless before him"* (Eph 1:4).
- *"has not God chosen those who are poor in the world to be rich in faith"* (Jam 2:5).
- *"God chose you as the firstfruits to be saved, through sanctification by the Spirit and belief in the truth"* (2Th 2:13). The verse names God's choosing and the person's belief in one sentence, and does not say how they fit.
- *"Blessed is the one you choose and bring near"* (Psa 65:4) follows straight on *"When iniquities prevail against me, you atone for our transgressions"* (65:3).

**When people choose, the order is the other way.** The twelve set the terms: *"pick out from among you seven men of good repute, full of the Spirit and of wisdom"* (Act 6:3). The gathering chooses Stephen, *"a man full of faith and of the Holy Spirit"* (6:5), by what is already seen in him and said of him. What was seen in him then is seen in him to his death: *"full of grace and power"* (6:8), *"full of the Holy Spirit"* (7:55).

This is the reverse of the chooser's own order in §2a, where a person chooses *"when he knows how"* (Isa 7:15).

## What the chosen are moved to

In these verses an inner response stands with the choosing. In 2Sa 6:21 and Isa 44:2 the choosing is named as its ground:
- **Celebrating.** *"It was before the Lord, who chose me above your father and above all his house … and I will celebrate before the Lord"* (2Sa 6:21). The celebrating had stopped at Uzzah's death, when David was angry, afraid and unwilling (6:8–10). It came back when he heard of the Lord's blessing (6:12).
- **Satisfaction.** *"We shall be satisfied with the goodness of your house, the holiness of your temple!"* (Psa 65:4). What satisfies is in God's house.
- **Joy and singing.** *"So he brought his people out with joy, his chosen ones with singing"* (Psa 105:43), after *"he remembered his holy promise"* (105:42).
- **Not fearing.** *"Fear not, O Jacob my servant, Jeshurun whom I have chosen"* (Isa 44:2). The ground is who chose him: *"the Lord who made you, who formed you from the womb and will help you"*.
- **Belonging.** *"This one will say, 'I am the Lord's'"* (Isa 44:5).

The gladness of the chosen can be something another person asks to share: *"that I may rejoice in the gladness of your nation"* (Psa 106:5). He asks, while confessing *"Both we and our fathers have sinned"* (106:6).

## What the chosen are called to

Being chosen is given as the ground of commands. The chosen are told to act.
- **Be strong, and do it.** *"the Lord has chosen you to build a house for the sanctuary; be strong and do it"* (1Ch 28:10). The strength is first a strength *"in keeping my commandments"* (28:7). Fear is answered: *"Do not be afraid and do not be dismayed, for the Lord God, even my God, is with you"* (28:20).
- **Know and serve with the whole inner being.** *"know the God of your father and serve him with a whole heart and with a willing mind, for the Lord searches all hearts and understands every plan and thought"* (1Ch 28:9).
- **Put on.** *"Put on then, as God's chosen ones, holy and beloved, compassionate hearts, kindness, humility, meekness, and patience"* (Col 3:12). It comes after *"Set your minds on things that are above"* (3:2) and after putting away *"anger, wrath, malice"* (3:8). What is put on is turned toward others: *"forgiving each other; as the Lord has forgiven you, so you also must forgive"* (3:13); *"above all these put on love"* (3:14); *"let the peace of Christ rule in your hearts"* (3:15).
- **Stand firm.** *"So then, brothers, stand firm and hold to the traditions that you were taught by us"* (2Th 2:15). The hearts of the chosen are to be comforted and established by the Lord (2:16–17).
- **Confirm it.** *"be all the more diligent to confirm your calling and election, for if you practice these qualities you will never fall"* (2Pe 1:10). The qualities are added one to another: *"supplement your faith with virtue, and virtue with knowledge, and knowledge with self-control, and self-control with steadfastness, and steadfastness with godliness, and godliness with brotherly affection, and brotherly affection with love"* (1:5–7). The effort follows what was given first: *"His divine power has granted to us all things that pertain to life and godliness"* (1:3).
- **Remember.** *"Remember the wondrous works that he has done … children of Jacob, his chosen ones!"* (Psa 105:5–6); *"Remember these things, O Jacob … you will not be forgotten by me"* (Isa 44:21). Another person can take on the reminding: *"I will make every effort so that after my departure you may be able at any time to recall these things"* (2Pe 1:15).

## Faith, knowing and the truth

Faith, and knowing the truth, are named for the chosen in five of these verses:
- *"for the sake of the faith of God's elect and their knowledge of the truth, which accords with godliness, in hope of eternal life"* (Tit 1:1–2)
- *"rich in faith and heirs of the kingdom, which he has promised to those who love him"* (Jam 2:5)
- *"that you may know and believe me and understand that I am he"* (Isa 43:10)
- *"belief in the truth"* (2Th 2:13)
- *"having the eyes of your hearts enlightened, that you may know what is the hope to which he has called you"* (Eph 1:18), a prayer for people already chosen

Faith is shown in what it does: *"So also faith by itself, if it does not have works, is dead"* (Jam 2:17). In the chapter of Titus 1:1 the opposite is described: *"They profess to know God, but they deny him by their works"* (Tit 1:16).

## Before whom

The chosen live before God's sight, not others'. David answers Michal, who measured him by *"the eyes of his servants' female servants"* (2Sa 6:20): *"It was before the Lord, who chose me"*, and *"I will be abased in your eyes"* (6:21–22). The purpose of the choosing in Ephesians is to be holy and blameless *"before him"* (Eph 1:4).

## Not a guarantee

The verses say plainly that being chosen does not keep a person from failing.
- **A condition.** *"I will establish his kingdom forever if he continues strong in keeping my commandments"* (1Ch 28:7); *"if you forsake him, he will cast you off forever"* (28:9).
- **The chosen people's failures, named act by act.** *"they soon forgot his works; they did not wait for his counsel"* (Psa 106:13); *"they had a wanton craving"* (106:14); *"men in the camp were jealous"* (106:16); *"Then they despised the pleasant land, having no faith in his promise"* (106:24). "Despised" is *māʾas*, the refusing verb of §2a.
- **Not calling, and weary.** *"Yet you did not call upon me, O Jacob; but you have been weary of me, O Israel!"* (Isa 43:22).
- **Forgetting.** Whoever lacks the qualities is *"so nearsighted that he is blind, having forgotten that he was cleansed from his former sins"* (2Pe 1:9).
- **The heart's own wish refused.** David, chosen, was not allowed to build what was in his heart (1Ch 28:2–4).

**The way back is the Lord's remembering.** *"For their sake he remembered his covenant, and relented according to the abundance of his steadfast love"* (Psa 106:45); *"I, I am he who blots out your transgressions for my own sake, and I will not remember your sins"* (Isa 43:25); *"return to me, for I have redeemed you"* (Isa 44:22).

## Set against others

In each passage the chosen are set beside others, in the passage's own words.
- **Those who cannot know.** The idol-maker: *"They know not, nor do they discern … their hearts, so that they cannot understand"* (Isa 44:18). "Know" and "discern" are the same words as "know" and "understand" in 43:10.
- **Those who refused the truth.** *"they refused to love the truth and so be saved"*; *"did not believe the truth but had pleasure in unrighteousness"* (2Th 2:10, 12).
- **Those whose minds are defiled.** *"both their minds and their consciences are defiled"* (Tit 1:15).
- **Those who choose by appearance.** An assembly that seats the rich and sends the poor to stand has *"become judges with evil thoughts"* (Jam 2:4). God chose the poor: *"But you have dishonored the poor man"* (2:6).
- **Those who resist.** Stephen's accusers: *"uncircumcised in heart and ears, you always resist the Holy Spirit"* (Act 7:51).
- **Those of one mind with the beast.** The kings *"are of one mind, and they hand over their power and authority to the beast"*, and against them *"those with him are called and chosen and faithful"* (Rev 17:13–14).

The chosen meet opposition. Stephen meets it with the same wisdom and Spirit that he was chosen for (Act 6:10), and dies praying *"Lord, do not hold this sin against them"* (7:60).

## What changes the character (#1919)

- **Choosing a person.**
  - God chooses *"before the foundation of the world"*, before anything in the person (Eph 1:4). He chooses people who are blind and deaf (Isa 43:8, 10), and the poor (Jam 2:5).
  - People choose by what is already seen and said: *"men of good repute, full of the Spirit and of wisdom"* (Act 6:3).
  - *Why:* who chooses. With God, the inner life is the purpose of the choosing. With people, it is the measure.
- **Holiness.**
  - It is the purpose of the choosing: *"that we should be holy and blameless before him"* (Eph 1:4).
  - It is a name the chosen already carry, while the inner qualities are still to be put on: *"as God's chosen ones, holy and beloved"* (Col 3:12).
  - *Why:* the verses do not say. I set them side by side.
- **The chosen people.**
  - They are brought out *"with joy … with singing"* (Psa 105:43).
  - They despise the land, with no faith in the promise (Psa 106:24).
  - *Why:* Psalm 105 tells of what God did, and gives none of the people's failures (it has *"none among his tribes who stumbled"*, 105:37). Psalm 106 tells of what the people did. The text does not join the two psalms.

## Still open

- **God acting on others' hearts:** God sends *"a strong delusion"* (2Th 2:11), and *"God has put it into their hearts to carry out his purpose"* (Rev 17:17). These are for 13.1.
- **God's choosing and a person's belief** (2Th 2:13). Both are named in one sentence. How they fit is not said.
- **Michal's contempt** (2Sa 6:16) and **Moses in the breach** (Psa 106:23). These are kept for §2c.
- **Psalms 105 and 106.** They are read side by side because they stand side by side.

## 3. The four read now

### 3.1 Eze 14:7: separating oneself from God

> For any one of the house of Israel, or of the strangers who sojourn in Israel, who separates himself from me, taking his idols into his heart and putting the stumbling block of his iniquity before his face, and yet comes to a prophet to consult me through him, I the Lord will answer him myself.

- **M64 word:** H5144A *nāzar*, "separates". **M47:** heart.
- **The passage (14:1–11):**
  - Elders come and sit before the prophet (14:1).
  - The Lord says *"these men have taken their idols into their hearts"* and asks *"Should I indeed let myself be consulted by them?"* (14:3).
  - He says why he will answer: *"that I may lay hold of the hearts of the house of Israel, who are all estranged from me through their idols"* (14:5).
  - Then he calls, *"Repent and turn away from your idols"* (14:6).
  - The end in view is *"that the house of Israel may no more go astray from me … but that they may be my people and I may be their God"* (14:11).
- **What the verse says about the inner being:**
  - The separating is done in the heart, by taking idols into it.
  - The person still comes outwardly to consult God.
  - The Lord answers that person *himself*, and sets his face against him (14:8).
  - The inward state and the outward coming do not match, and the Lord answers the inward one.
- **The word's other faces (iba.db, all 5 H5144A verses):**
  - "consecrated" themselves to Baal-peor (Hos 9:10)
  - "abstain" from the holy things (Lev 22:2) and in a fast (Zec 7:3)
  - "keep" separate from uncleanness (Lev 15:31)
  - "separates himself from me" (Eze 14:7)
  - The same verb is used for setting oneself apart **to** something and **from** something; what it is turned toward decides (#1919). Its sister sense H5144B is the Nazirite's separating "to the Lord" (Num 6).
  - The other four H5144A verses have not been read for M64. They are not in the choose, will or plan pulls.
- **Route:** A and B.
  - B: the person who separates in the heart and still comes to inquire.
  - A: the Lord who answers himself, to lay hold of the hearts.
- **Proposed placement:**
  - **10.9 *Relating to God***: separating oneself from God in the heart while still coming to inquire, and God answering the heart.
  - **10.11 *Hiding and disclosing*** would be a pointer only: the inner state is not hidden from the one consulted.
  - **13.1** gets God's purpose: *"that I may lay hold of the hearts"* (14:5), beside *He searches hearts, and acts on them*.
  - Ch 4 already quotes 14:3 ("idols into their hearts"). It does not need changing.

### 3.2 Lev 7:18: not credited

> If any of the flesh of the sacrifice of his peace offering is eaten on the third day, he who offers it shall not be accepted, neither shall it be credited to him. It is tainted, and he who eats of it shall bear his iniquity.

- **M64 word:** H2803H *ḥāšaḇ*, "credited" (count/regard). **M47:** flesh; soul ("he").
- **The passage (7:11–21):**
  - It gives the law of the peace offering: what is eaten when, and what is burned.
  - Flesh left to the third day is not accepted and not credited. It is "tainted".
  - Eating with uncleanness on one cuts a person off (7:20–21).
- **What the verse says:**
  - It states a rule of reckoning. An offering kept outside the rule is not counted to the offerer.
  - It does not speak of the offerer's inner act, or of God's thoughts beyond the rule.
- **Route:** the plan scan gave it A, in the count/regard group. It was not among the 31 God-ward verses placed in 13.1, nor among the 77 woven into 10.4 and 10.9. So it fell between the two.
- **Proposed:** route **C (data only)**, the same as your ruling on the jubilee verses: a rule, not an inner act.
  - The alternative is a single line in 10.9 *God reckons, and faith reckons*, as the reckoning not given. I do not recommend it, because the verse is a rule about the offering, not about a person before God.
  - Lev 17:4 ("bloodguilt shall be imputed to that man") was in the same scan group with route A, and it is also unplaced. It is not one of the 29, and is noted only.

### 3.3 2Ch 7:16: his eyes and his heart at the place he chose

> For now I have chosen and consecrated this house that my name may be there forever. My eyes and my heart will be there for all time.

- **M64 word:** H0977 *bāḥar*, "chosen". **M47:** heart (God's).
- **The passage (7:11–22):**
  - The Lord appears to Solomon at night: *"I have heard your prayer and have chosen this place for myself as a house of sacrifice"* (7:12). This is the answer to the prayer of 2Ch 6, which holds 6:38, "the city that you have chosen".
  - *"if my people who are called by my name humble themselves, and pray and seek my face and turn from their wicked ways, then I will hear from heaven"* (7:14).
  - *"Now my eyes will be open and my ears attentive to the prayer that is made in this place"* (7:15).
  - Then comes 7:16.
  - Then the condition. If they forsake him, *"this house that I have consecrated for my name, I will cast out of my sight"* (7:20).
- **What it says:**
  - God's choosing of a place carries his own eyes, ears and heart to it.
  - It is joined, in the same speech, to his hearing the prayer made there.
  - The same speech says that what he chose and consecrated he will cast out of his sight if he is forsaken.
  - 13.1 *Choosing* has "a place" (Deu 12:5) and *He blesses at the place he chose*. It does not yet have his heart at the place, or the condition.
- **Route:** A, with a B thread (7:14: humble, pray, seek his face, turn). It is one of the 166 verses held under OT-92, which you route.
- **Proposed placement:**
  - **13.1 *Choosing***: *"My eyes and my heart will be there for all time"*, with 7:12, 15 and 20.
  - A pointer from 10.7 *Others around a choice*, where 1Ki 8:48 prays toward the chosen city. 2Ch 7:12 is the Lord's answer to that prayer.

### 3.4 Mat 12:18: chosen, with delight and the Spirit

> "Behold, my servant whom I have chosen, my beloved with whom my soul is well pleased. I will put my Spirit upon him, and he will proclaim justice to the Gentiles.

- **M64 word:** G0140 *hairetizō*, "chosen". **M47:** soul (God's); Spirit.
- **The passage (12:14–21):**
  - *"the Pharisees went out and conspired against him, how to destroy him"* (12:14).
  - Jesus, *"aware of this, withdrew"*. He healed them all and *"ordered them not to make him known"* (12:15–16).
  - *"This was to fulfill what was spoken by the prophet Isaiah"* (12:17), then Isa 42:1–4: *"He will not quarrel or cry aloud … a bruised reed he will not break"* (12:19–20).
- **What it says:**
  - God's choosing is joined to his soul's pleasure and to putting his Spirit on the one chosen. This is the same as Isa 42:1, which #1987 found new for 13.1.
  - Matthew sets the quoted words against the plot of 12:14. The withdrawing, the healing and the order to keep quiet are what *"This was to fulfill"* refers to.
  - The chosen one's bearing, not quarrelling and not breaking the bruised reed, is shown answering a plot to destroy him.
- **Route:** A and B. It is one of the OT-92 held verses. The Spirit goes to 13.1 under your ruling of 2026-10-08.
- **Proposed placement:**
  - **13.1 *Choosing***, taken together with Isa 42:1: he chooses with his soul's delight and puts his Spirit on the one chosen.
  - **10.7 *Being chosen***: the chosen one's bearing (Isa 42:2–3; Mat 12:19–20), answering a plot (12:14). This joins the #1987 item for Isa 42:1, so the two should be placed in one pass.

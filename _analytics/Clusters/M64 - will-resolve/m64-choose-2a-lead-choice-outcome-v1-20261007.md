**How each entry is read:**
- **Led to:** what the verse or its passage gives as the occasion or the ground of the choice.
- **Choice:** what is chosen, and by whom. Where the verse urges, forbids or charges a choice rather than narrating one, this is said.
- **Outcome:** what the passage says follows. Where it says nothing, this is stated as **"not stated"**. Nothing is supplied from outside the passage.
- **Inner-being word:** the §2a word and its relation, from the source file.
- Readings beyond what the text states are marked **"I read"**.

## A. Narrated choices: someone chooses, and the passage tells what followed

### 1. Jos 24:15 — Israel at Shechem chooses whom to serve
- **Inner-being word:** *"evil in your eyes"* (H7451H), ground.
- **Led to:** Joshua recounts what the Lord gave: *"a land on which you had not labored"* (v13). Then he calls on them: *"fear the Lord and serve him in sincerity and in faithfulness. Put away the gods"* (v14). The choice is put to them on one condition: *"if it is evil in your eyes to serve the Lord, choose this day whom you will serve"* (v15).
- **Choice:**
  - Joshua chooses first: *"as for me and my house, we will serve the Lord"* (v15).
  - The people choose the Lord, giving the reason from what he did: *"it is the Lord our God who brought us … up from the land of Egypt"* (v17); *"Therefore we also will serve the Lord, for he is our God"* (v18).
  - Joshua answers *"You are not able to serve the Lord, for he is a holy God"* (v19). They repeat the choice: *"No, but we will serve the Lord"* (v21).
- **Outcome:**
  - The choice binds them as its own witness: *"You are witnesses against yourselves that you have chosen the Lord"*; *"We are witnesses"* (v22).
  - It calls for an inner act: *"put away the foreign gods … and incline your heart to the Lord"* (v23).
  - *"his voice we will obey"* (v24). A covenant, statutes and rules are made, and a stone is set up as *"a witness against you, lest you deal falsely with your God"* (v25–27).
  - The warning is part of the outcome: *"If you forsake the Lord … he will turn and do you harm"* (v20).

### 2. 2Sa 19:38 — Barzillai's choice for Chimham
- **Inner-being word:** *"whatever seems good to you"* (H2896A), ground.
- **ESV rendering:** the choosing word (H0977) is rendered *"desire"*: *"all that you desire of me I will do for you."*
- **Led to:** The king invites Barzillai to Jerusalem (v33). Barzillai declines because of his age: *"I am this day eighty years old. Can I discern what is pleasant and what is not?"* (v35). He asks to go home and offers Chimham instead: *"do for him whatever seems good to you"* (v37).
- **Choice:** The king hands the choice to Barzillai: *"I will do for him whatever seems good to you, and all that you desire of me I will do for you"* (v38). The choice made is Barzillai's: Chimham goes in his place.
- **Outcome:** *"the king kissed Barzillai and blessed him, and he returned to his own home"* (v39); *"Chimham went on with him"* (v40).

### 3. Act 6:5 — the gathering chooses the seven
- **Inner-being word:** *"pleased"* (G0700), ground.
- **Led to:** *"a complaint by the Hellenists arose … because their widows were being neglected"* (v1). The twelve say *"It is not right that we should give up preaching the word of God to serve tables"* (v2). They set the measure for the choice: *"seven men of good repute, full of the Spirit and of wisdom"* (v3).
- **Choice:** *"what they said pleased the whole gathering, and they chose Stephen, a man full of faith and of the Holy Spirit, and Philip …"* (v5). The pleasure is the gathering's response to the twelve's word, and the choosing follows it.
- **Outcome:** *"These they set before the apostles, and they prayed and laid their hands on them"* (v6). The next verse says *"the word of God continued to increase, and the number of the disciples multiplied greatly"* (v7). The passage places v7 next but does not say the choice caused it.

### 4. Act 15:22, 25 — the Jerusalem church chooses men to send
- **Inner-being word:** *"seemed good"* (G1380), ground, in both verses.
- **Led to:**
  - James's judgement that they *"should not trouble those of the Gentiles who turn to God"* (v19–21).
  - The trouble they answer: *"some persons have gone out from us and troubled you with words, unsettling your minds"* (v24).
- **Choice:**
  - *"it seemed good to the apostles and the elders, with the whole church, to choose men from among them and send them to Antioch"*. The men are *"Judas called Barsabbas, and Silas"* (v22).
  - In the letter: *"it has seemed good to us, having come to one accord, to choose men and send them"* (v25).
  - The same verb then carries the content of the letter: *"it has seemed good to the Holy Spirit and to us"* (v28).
- **Outcome:** The letter is delivered. *"when they had read it, they rejoiced because of its encouragement"* (v31). Judas and Silas *"encouraged and strengthened the brothers"* (v32), and were *"sent off in peace"* (v33).

### 5. 2Cor 8:17 — Titus goes of his own accord
- **Inner-being word:** *"earnest"* (G4705), ground.
- **ESV rendering:** the choosing word is G0830 *authairetos*, rendered *"of his own accord"*.
- **Led to:** *"thanks be to God, who put into the heart of Titus the same earnest care I have for you"* (v16). Titus *"accepted our appeal"* (v17).
- **Choice:** *"being himself very earnest he is going to you of his own accord"* (v17). The verse gives two sources for his going side by side: God put the care into his heart (v16), and he goes *"of his own accord"* (v17).
- **Outcome:** *"he is going"*. A brother is sent with him (v18). Nothing further about Titus's choice is stated in v16–19.

### 6. Deu 23:16 — the escaped slave chooses where to live
- **Inner-being word:** *"wherever it suits him"* (H2896A), ground.
- **Led to:** *"a slave who has escaped from his master to you"* (v15).
- **Choice:** the slave's: *"in the place that he shall choose within one of your towns, wherever it suits him"* (v16).
- **Outcome:** Given as law, not narrated: *"He shall dwell with you, in your midst … You shall not wrong him"* (v16). Israel is to honour the choice and not hand him back: *"You shall not give up to his master"* (v15).

## B. A chosen way confessed or promised, with the hoped-for outcome

### 7. Psa 119:30 — "I have chosen the way of faithfulness"
- **Inner-being word:** *"faithfulness"* (H0530), object.
- **Led to:** *"My soul melts away for sorrow; strengthen me according to your word!"* (v28); *"Put false ways far from me and graciously teach me your law!"* (v29).
- **Choice:** *"I have chosen the way of faithfulness; I set your rules before me"* (v30).
- **Outcome:** Prayed and promised, not narrated:
  - *"I cling to your testimonies, O Lord; let me not be put to shame!"* (v31)
  - *"I will run in the way of your commandments when you enlarge my heart!"* (v32). The running waits on the Lord's act on the heart.

### 8. Psa 25:12 — the man who fears the Lord is taught the way to choose
- **Inner-being word:** *"fears"* (H3373), ground.
- **Led to:**
  - The Lord's character: *"Good and upright is the Lord; therefore he instructs sinners in the way"* (v8).
  - The person's fear: *"Who is the man who fears the Lord? Him will he instruct in the way that he should choose"* (v12).
  - The psalmist's own plea just before: *"pardon my guilt, for it is great"* (v11).
- **Choice:** The way the Lord teaches him. The verse does not name a particular choice; it names the way as the Lord's teaching.
- **Outcome:** *"His soul shall abide in well-being, and his offspring shall inherit the land"* (v13); *"The friendship of the Lord is for those who fear him"* (v14).

### 9. Isa 56:4 — the eunuchs who choose what pleases the Lord
- **Inner-being word:** *"hold fast"* (H2388H), paired.
- **Led to:** The eunuch's own word about himself: *"let not the eunuch say, “Behold, I am a dry tree.”"* (v3). The foreigner's: *"The Lord will surely separate me from his people"* (v3).
- **Choice:** *"who keep my Sabbaths, who choose the things that please me and hold fast my covenant"* (v4). What is chosen is measured by what pleases the Lord.
- **Outcome:** Promised by the Lord: *"I will give in my house and within my walls a monument and a name better than sons and daughters … an everlasting name that shall not be cut off"* (v5). The promise answers the *"dry tree"* of v3 directly.

## C. Choices that are charged, and the judgement that follows

### 10. Isa 65:12 — chose what the Lord did not delight in
- **Inner-being word:** *"you did not listen"* (H8085G), ground.
- **Led to:**
  - *"you who forsake the Lord, who forget my holy mountain, who set a table for Fortune"* (v11).
  - *"when I called, you did not answer; when I spoke, you did not listen"* (v12).
- **Choice:** *"you did what was evil in my eyes and chose what I did not delight in"* (v12).
- **Outcome:**
  - *"I will destine you to the sword"* (v12).
  - *"my servants shall eat, but you shall be hungry … my servants shall rejoice, but you shall be put to shame"* (v13).
  - The outcome reaches the inner being: *"you shall cry out for pain of heart and shall wail for breaking of spirit"* (v14).
  - *"You shall leave your name to my chosen for a curse"* (v15).

### 11. Isa 66:3 — chose their own ways; their soul delights in abominations
- **Inner-being word:** *"their soul delights"* (H2654A), paired.
- **Led to:**
  - Set against the one the Lord looks to: *"he who is humble and contrite in spirit and trembles at my word"* (v2).
  - Their offerings are named as like killing a man or offering pig's blood (v3).
  - v4 gives the same ground as Isa 65:12: *"when I called, no one answered, when I spoke, they did not listen"*.
- **Choice:** *"These have chosen their own ways, and their soul delights in their abominations"* (v3). The choosing and the soul's delight stand side by side. *"chose that in which I did not delight"* (v4).
- **Outcome:** The Lord answers with a choice of his own: *"I also will choose harsh treatment for them and bring their fears upon them"* (v4). Their own fears are what comes on them. To those who tremble: *"it is they who shall be put to shame"* (v5).

### 12. Isa 1:29 — the oaks they desired, the gardens they chose
- **Inner-being words:** *"desired"* (H2530A), paired; *"ashamed"*, *"blush"* (H0954, H2659), response.
- **Led to:** *"rebels and sinners shall be broken together, and those who forsake the Lord shall be consumed"* (v28). The verse says what they desired and chose, the oaks and the gardens. I read these as places of worship other than the Lord's; the verse does not say so.
- **Choice:** *"the oaks that you desired … the gardens that you have chosen"* (v29).
- **Outcome:**
  - *"they shall be ashamed … you shall blush"* (v29).
  - They become like what they chose: *"like an oak whose leaf withers, and like a garden without water"* (v30).
  - *"the strong shall become tinder … both of them shall burn together, with none to quench them"* (v31).

### 13. Job 15:5 — Eliphaz charges Job: "you choose the tongue of the crafty"
- **Inner-being word:** *"iniquity"* (H5771G), ground.
- **Whose charge:** This is Eliphaz's accusation against Job, not the narrator's statement.
- **Led to:** In Eliphaz's account: *"you are doing away with the fear of God and hindering meditation before God"* (v4); *"your iniquity teaches your mouth"* (v5).
- **Choice:** *"you choose the tongue of the crafty"* (v5).
- **Outcome:** As Eliphaz states it: *"Your own mouth condemns you, and not I; your own lips testify against you"* (v6).

### 14. Job 36:21 — Elihu: "this you have chosen rather than affliction"
- **Inner-being word:** *"Take care"* (H8104J), paired.
- **Whose charge:** Elihu's warning to Job.
- **Led to:**
  - *"He delivers the afflicted by their affliction and opens their ear by adversity"* (v15).
  - *"He also allured you out of distress into a broad place"* (v16).
  - *"Beware lest wrath entice you into scoffing"* (v18).
- **Choice:** *"Take care; do not turn to iniquity, for this you have chosen rather than affliction"* (v21). Elihu says Job has chosen iniquity over affliction, which v15 calls the means by which God opens the ear.
- **Outcome:** Not stated. Elihu turns to God: *"Behold, God is exalted in his power; who is a teacher like him?"* (v22).

## D. Choices urged, forbidden or put to someone else

### 15. Job 34:4 — Elihu: "Let us choose what is right"
- **Inner-being word:** *"know"* (H3045), paired.
- **Led to:** *"the ear tests words as the palate tastes food"* (v3). Job's claim is the case before them: *"I am in the right, and God has taken away my right"* (v5).
- **Choice:** Urged, together: *"Let us choose what is right; let us know among ourselves what is good"* (v4).
- **Outcome:** Elihu gives his own verdict at once: *"far be it from God that he should do wickedness"* (v10). The verse does not record the others' choice.

### 16. Job 34:33 — Elihu: "you must choose, and not I"
- **Inner-being words:** *"reject"* (H3988A) and *"know"* (H3045), paired.
- **Led to:**
  - Elihu's question whether anyone has said to God *"I will not offend any more; teach me what I do not see"* (v31–32).
  - *"Will he then make repayment to suit you, because you reject it?"* (v33).
- **Choice:** Laid on Job: *"For you must choose, and not I; therefore declare what you know"* (v33).
- **Outcome:** Job's choice is not stated. Elihu's verdict follows: *"Job speaks without knowledge; his words are without insight"* (v35).

### 17. Pro 3:31 — "do not choose any of his ways"
- **Inner-being word:** *"envy"* (H7065), paired.
- **Led to:** The proverb names the pull: *"a man of violence"* and the envy he draws.
- **Choice:** Forbidden: *"Do not envy a man of violence and do not choose any of his ways"* (v31).
- **Outcome:** Given as the reason: *"for the devious person is an abomination to the Lord, but the upright are in his confidence"* (v32). The next verses set the Lord's curse and blessing side by side (v33–35).

### 18. Pro 8:10 — Wisdom: "knowledge rather than choice gold"
- **Inner-being word:** *"knowledge"* (H1847), object.
- **ESV rendering:** the choosing word (H0977) here describes the gold: *"choice gold"*.
- **Led to:** Wisdom's call to *"simple ones"* and *"fools"* (v5), and her words: *"all straight to him who understands, and right to those who find knowledge"* (v9).
- **Choice:** Urged: *"Take my instruction instead of silver, and knowledge rather than choice gold"* (v10). Knowledge is set above the gold that is itself called "choice".
- **Outcome:** Given as the reason, not narrated: *"wisdom is better than jewels, and all that you may desire cannot compare with her"* (v11).

### 19. Pro 16:16 — "understanding is to be chosen rather than silver"
- **Inner-being words:** *"wisdom"* (H2451) and *"understanding"* (H0998), object.
- **Led to:** Not stated. The saying stands alone.
- **Choice:** Urged as a value: *"How much better to get wisdom than gold! To get understanding is to be chosen rather than silver"* (v16).
- **Outcome:** Not stated in the verse. Verses 17–19 are separate sayings and are not taken as its outcome.

### 20. Isa 7:15–16 — the child who knows how to refuse the evil and choose the good
- **Inner-being words:** *"knows"* (H3045), ground; *"refuse"* (H3988A), paired.
- **Led to:** Ahaz refuses the sign the Lord offers: *"I will not ask, and I will not put the Lord to the test"* (v12). So *"the Lord himself will give you a sign … Immanuel"* (v14).
- **Choice:** No single choice is narrated. The verse names a knowing: *"when he knows how to refuse the evil and choose the good"*. It marks the time when the child has this knowing (v15, v16).
- **Outcome:**
  - The knowing serves as a measure of time: *"before the boy knows how to refuse the evil and choose the good, the land whose two kings you dread will be deserted"* (v16).
  - *"the king of Assyria"* follows (v17).
  - Ahaz's own refusal in v12 is the occasion of the whole sign. The verses do not connect it to the child's refusing.

## F. Points for the researcher

- **F1. Charges made by speakers in Job (entries 13, 14, 16).** Eliphaz and Elihu charge Job with choosing. These are the speakers' words, and the book does not endorse them in these passages. They are kept as HIB choosing because the verse names a choice. Should they be read as the chooser's own choosing, or as a speaker's charge?
- **F2. Pro 8:10 (entry 18).** The choosing word describes the gold (*"choice gold"*), not the person's choosing. Knowledge is preferred over it. It sits in §2a as the "object" of choice. Should it stay there, or move with the "Choice: the best" group?
- **F3. Isa 7:15–16 (entry 20).** The verses name a capacity to choose, not a choice made. It stays in §2a as "ground", but it is a different kind of entry from the others.
- **F4. Isa 1:29 (entry 12).** The reading of the oaks and gardens as places of other worship is marked "I read". The verse names only oaks and gardens.

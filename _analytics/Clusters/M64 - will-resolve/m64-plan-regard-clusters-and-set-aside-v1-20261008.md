## a) Other clusters that hold the "count" idea

**Method:** every live M-cluster Strong's was checked in two ways.
- **By its ESV surface** (`verse_lexical.surface`): did the ESV render it as regard, esteem, count, reckon, account, impute, credit, value, prize, consider, despise or worth?
- **By its gloss:** did the gloss name esteem, regard, count, value, honour, despise, judge or test?

The tables below give the words where the ESV uses an evaluating rendering at least twice. "Hits" shows how many of the word's hits take such a rendering, out of all its hits.

### The same idea as H2803H

These words render counting, regarding and esteeming as their main sense, or as a large share of their hits.

| Cluster | Strong's | Gloss | Hits with an evaluating rendering | ESV surfaces |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **M26** Judgment & Condemnation | G3049 *logizomai* | to count | 25 of 39 | counted, count, regard, consider, count on, resentful |
| **M15** Knowing & Understanding | H2803J | to devise: think | 15 of 26 | counted, esteemed, regarded, account, consider |
| **M72** Authority & Dominion | G2233 *hēgeomai* | to govern | 15 of 28 | count, esteem, regard, consider |
| **M26** Judgment & Condemnation | H6186B | to value | 5 of 6 | value, values |
| **M64** Will & Resolve | H2803H | to devise: count | 26 of 34 | (the set already scanned) |

What the data shows:
- **G3049 is the Greek counterpart in use.** *"Abraham believed God, and it was counted to him as righteousness"* (Rom 4:3, G3049) quotes Gen 15:6, which is H2803H in this set. It also carries *"it is not irritable or resentful"* (1Co 13:5, "resentful" = keeping count).
- **H2803J is the same Hebrew word as H2803H**, tagged M15. It includes *"yet we esteemed him stricken"* (Isa 53:4), the verse after *"we esteemed him not"* (Isa 53:3, H2803H). The two verses are one passage, split across two clusters.
- **G2233 is tagged M72 by its gloss "to govern"**, but 15 of its 28 hits are evaluating. For example: *"in humility count others more significant than yourselves"* (Php 2:3).
- **H6186B** is the priest's valuation in Lev 27 (8, 12, 14), plus 2Ki 23:35.

- "Consider" in M15 and M47 is mostly "pay attention to" rather than "value". These words sit at the edge of the idea.
- **The core of the "count" idea is spread over at least four clusters:** M64, M26, M15 and M72. Its opposite (despising) is in M30, and its positive verdict (worthy, honour) is in M71.
- **No re-tagging is proposed** (#1970). This is a map for a focused pull, if you want one.

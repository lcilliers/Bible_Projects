# OT-02: Sheol and Hades with soul, spirit or life (2026-10-13)

Researcher, chat 2026-10-13 (verbatim): *"are there any verses binding sheol to either soul or spirit or 'life'"*

**Method.** From iba.db `verse_lexical`, every verse with H7585 (Sheol) or G0086 (Hades): 74 verses.
Within them, every word whose STEP gloss contains soul, spirit, life, live, living, breath or alive.
This is co-occurrence in the verse. Whether the verse *binds* the two is shown by its wording, which is
given in full; nothing is read beyond it.

**Result.** 19 of the 74 verses. Words found: H5315G *nephesh* (soul), H5315L *nephesh* (appetite),
H2416A/E *ḥay* (alive, life), H2421 *ḥāyâ* (to live), G5590G *psychē* (soul), G2198 *zaō* (to live).
**No spirit word** (H7307 *rûaḥ*, G4151 *pneuma*) and no breath word occurs in any Sheol or Hades verse.

Pro 23:14 are not. In the narrative, Ch 2 cites Psa 16:10, 30:3, 49:15, 88:3, 89:48 and Act 2:27, 31

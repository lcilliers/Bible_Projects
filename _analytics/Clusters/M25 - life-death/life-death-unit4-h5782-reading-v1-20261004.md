## C. What this means for the strand

- **The life-and-death face is small but direct.** Job 14:12 states death as a sleep from which a man is not roused "till the heavens are no more". Isa 14:9 has Sheol roused to meet the dead. Hab 2:19 sets waking against "no breath at all in it". These belong in Ch 2 ("What death is", "After death", "Breath lent").
- **The inner-being face is the larger one, and it is mostly God acting on the inner being.** God stirs up the spirit (W04: eight verses, with what follows: a proclamation, going up to build, working on the house). That is an external party acting on the spirit, dealt with per the style guide. It bears on the spirit chapter and on 10.7.
- **Love, hatred, the heart, the ear and praise** (W10–W14) bear on 10.1, 10.2, 10.6 and 10.10.

## D. A fan-out pointer (not part of this unit unless approved)

The other Hebrew "awake" word, **H6974** (*qûṣ*), is tagged **T3**, not M25. It carries the resurrection verses: Dan 12:2 "many of those who sleep in the dust of the earth shall awake", Isa 26:19, Psa 17:15, and the first half of Job 14:12 ("he will not awake"). A cluster tag does not decide meaning (#1919). The verses that answer Job 14:12 sit outside M25.

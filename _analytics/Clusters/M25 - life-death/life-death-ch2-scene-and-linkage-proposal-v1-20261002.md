**Suggestion.** Let Ch 2 speak in these words. In particular:
- *"the breath of life"*: how life is given
- *"the springs of life"*: where life flows out from the heart
- *"I have set before you life and death"*: what the inner being is turned toward
- *"from death to life"*: the end and the new beginning

These are Scripture's own terms, so no frame word has to be imposed. The title stays **"Life, breath and death"** (titles name the subject only, #1924).

**Caution.** Pro 4:23 and Pro 14:30 *state* that life flows from the heart and that a tranquil heart gives life. "Life is the expression of the inner being" is the researcher's wording of the frame. In the text it would appear as **"I read"**, resting on those verses. It would not be presented as a statement Scripture makes in those words.

## 3. Ch 2's job, and a proposed shape

**Its job (researcher):** to set the scene for discovering how the inner being works, within what life is about and what death means. It gives the purpose, framework and fabric that the later chapters fill in.

**Proposed shape.** The existing sections are kept and reordered under four movements, so that the reader meets the frame before the words. Unit 1 (LD-ids) slots in.

| Movement | Contents | Existing section / new |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A. Life given and held** | the breath of life; God gives and holds it; he kills and makes alive; life is kept | "Life breathed in", "Breath lent, and held by God" + LD-09–LD-12, LD-15 |
| **B. What life is for, and where it flows from** | the springs of life from the heart; life set before a person to choose; life by every word from God's mouth; life asked for ("give me life"); life as knowing God | **new** (Pro 4:23; 14:30; Deu 30:19–20; Deu 8:3; Psa 119:25; Joh 17:3) + LD-01–LD-03, LD-27–LD-29 |

| **D. From death to life** | life that returns; delivered from death; turn and live; made alive; the body raised; after death | "Life that returns", "Delivered from death", "After death" + LD-04, LD-07, LD-18–LD-25, LD-31 (Q2–Q4 pulls to come) |

## 4. How the links run into the other chapters (keeping the detail)

**The problem.**
- If Ch 2 carries every life and death passage, it becomes a second book.
- If the passages are spread out, the scene that Ch 2 sets is lost, and so is the overview's detail.

**Proposal: three layers, each with one job.**

1. **Ch 2 = the scene.** For each movement it gives one coherent account of what Scripture says life and death are, using chosen verses (the 10.1 "Fear" model, #1930). Where a part belongs to an activity, Ch 2 states it briefly and points on, e.g. "Asking God for life is followed in 10.9".
2. **The other chapters = the working.** Where an activity is the subject, that chapter carries the full account and points back to Ch 2. Examples:
   - asking for life → 10.9
   - choosing life → 10.7
   - turning and living → 10.7 / 14
   - wanting to die → 10.6
   - breathing out lies → 10.5
   - the heart that dies → 4

   Each chapter also gets a short line where its subject meets life and death. This is how parts of Ch 2 "find their way into other chapters" without being lost from the scene.
3. **The detail = the evidence layer, kept whole.** Nothing is cut from the record:
   - the overview data file, including the §3 cluster links
   - the unit ledgers
   - a new living **linkage map**, `life-death-linkage-map.md`

   The map has one row per link: overview cluster link or ledger id → the chapter or section that now carries it → its status (carried / pointer only / not yet read). It is the bridge between the overview's rich detail and the narrative. OT-08 points to it. Every later strand adds its own life and death rows.

   **The first rows come from the overview's §3**, e.g.:
   - M12 righteousness (76 near verses) → 10.8 / Ch 2 B, "not yet read"
   - M47 inner seat (302) → Ch 4–8
   - M64 choosing (Deu 30:19) → 10.7, carried by LD-28

**Why this shape.**
- It follows the approved weaving rules: one coherent account per phenomenon (#1930); weave, don't fit (#1918); the full detail stays in the ledger.
- The only new file is a single living map.

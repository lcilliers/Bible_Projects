## C. What this shows

1. **The narrative tells claims the register does not hold.** Of the 21 claims in Part A, 9 have an
   existing original-essay claim beside them. The other 12 are in the narrative only.
2. **The place-of-death words were never read as a set.** 70 of the 112 verses are in neither the narrative
   nor the register; 55 of them bear on Q1. The ones that bear most directly on the narrative's claims are
   those on N15 and N20 (Sheol; the dead silent) and on God and Sheol.
3. **The researcher decides next.** Either (a) register Part A as it stands and read the Part B verses next,
   with any change to N15, N20 or N21 coming out of that reading; or (b) read Part B first, then register
   Part A as amended.

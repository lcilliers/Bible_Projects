### (b) Verses fitted to the narrative line

- **Hos 6:2** is quoted as a hope of the body raised: "on the third day he will raise us up, that
  we may live before him." In its passage it is the people's call to return: "Come, let us return
  to the Lord; for he has torn us, that he may heal us" (6:1). God's answer follows: "What shall I
  do with you, O Ephraim? … Your love is like a morning cloud, like the dew that goes early away"
  (6:4). The passage does not speak of the body. **Action:** take it out of *The body raised*, or
  restate it in its setting (a call to return that God answers with "your love is like a morning
  cloud"). 6:4 is already cited in 13.1 *Delight*.
- **Hos 13:14** is quoted correctly (the ESV text in iba.db), but only to "O Sheol, where is your
  sting?". The verse goes on: "Compassion is hidden from my eyes." **Action:** quote the verse to
  its end, as it stands in its passage.
- **a2 above** joins 1Pe 1:3 to a "before" that it does not state.

### (c) Verses dropped or never read: what the section misses

The section tells the body's raising: the seed, Christ first, the change, the waiting, the
judgment. **It says almost nothing of the inner being toward the resurrection**, and that is
where most of the 106 verses speak.

**c1. The inner acts toward the resurrection: 34 verses (A1).** This is the largest group.
- *Denied*, and the ground Jesus gives:
  - "the Sadducees say that there is no resurrection, nor angel, nor spirit" (Act 23:8)
  - Jesus' answer: "You are wrong, because you know neither the Scriptures nor the power of God"
    (Mat 22:29; Mar 12:24). He grounds it in God's name: "the God of Abraham" (Mar 12:26), "for
    all live to him" (Luk 20:38)

  The section cites only Mat 22:30 and Luk 20:36, on marriage.
- *Questioned, and not understood until later:*
  - "questioning what this rising from the dead might mean" (Mar 9:10)
  - "as yet they did not understand the Scripture, that he must rise from the dead" (Joh 20:9)
  - "When therefore he was raised from the dead, his disciples remembered that he had said this,
    and they believed" (Joh 2:22)
  - "Then he opened their minds to understand the Scriptures" (Luk 24:45–46)
  - "Remember Jesus Christ, risen from the dead" (2Ti 2:8)
- *Known and believed:*
  - Martha: "I know that he will rise again in the resurrection on the last day" (Joh 11:24)
  - then "Do you believe this?" "Yes, Lord; I believe" (11:26–27)
- *Mocked, deferred, thought incredible:*
  - "some mocked. But others said, 'We will hear you again about this'" (Act 17:32)
  - "Why is it thought incredible by any of you that God raises the dead?" (Act 26:8)
  - "the hope and the resurrection of the dead" on trial (Act 23:6; 24:21)
- *Faith emptied if it is not so:*
  - "how can some of you say that there is no resurrection of the dead?" (1Co 15:12)
  - "your faith is futile and you are still in your sins" (15:17)
  - "If in Christ we have hope in this life only, we are of all people most to be pitied" (15:19,
    cited in *Life weighed*)

  The section cites none of 1Co 15:12–19. Faith (M13, OT-05.19) is a handle here.
- *Upset:* "saying that the resurrection has already happened. They are upsetting the faith of
  some" (2Ti 2:18).
- *Feared, and a conscience stirred:*
  - the guards "lest his disciples … tell the people, 'He has risen from the dead'" (Mat 27:64)
  - Herod: "John, whom I beheaded, has been raised" (Mar 6:16); "he was perplexed" (Luk 9:7)
  - "that is why these miraculous powers are at work in him" (Mat 14:2)

  I read Herod's word as his own guilt speaking, since it names the beheading. The verses do not
  say "guilt".

**c2. Raised with Christ now: 9 verses (A2), plus 5 cited elsewhere.** These are the verses that
say what a2 tries to say:

Already elsewhere: Rom 6:4, 6:9 (*Made alive*), Rom 7:4 (*Dead to sin*), Php 3:10 (Ch 14), 1Pe

**c3. Living toward it: 5 verses (A3), plus 1Co 15:58.**

**c4. The imperishable said of what is within: 3 verses (A4), plus 1Pe 3:4.**

**c5. Corruption as a bondage: 3 verses (A5).**

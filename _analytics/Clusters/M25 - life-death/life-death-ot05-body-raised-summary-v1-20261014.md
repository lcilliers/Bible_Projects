## 4. What the counts show (counted, not read)

1. **The changed-body core is covered.** A1, A6, B3, R1 and R5 hold 124 verses, and 67 of them were read in the Q4 ledger. A1, A6 and B3 hold 53, of which 32 are cited. These groups are where Q4's own question sits. A reading here would check the rest against the narrative, as OT-02 did, not start fresh.
2. **Q4 never read G1453, G0450 or G1391 in full.** That leaves:
   - R4 (Christ raised: 56 verses, 5 read in Q4)
   - R2 (raised with Christ: 10)
   - R3 (the raisings in the accounts: 17, 1 cited)
   - G1 (glory to come: 27, none read in Q4, 8 cited, 0 claims)
3. **B4 (the body with the inner being, 49 verses)** is the body words' main inner-being group: 22 cited and 12 behind claims, but none read in Q4. It bears on how the body and the inner being stand together now. That is beside the thread's question (the raised body) and touches M47.
4. **Groups likely to add little to the raised body:** R6–R8 (119 verses of getting up and standing), B1 (the church as body), B2 (the supper and the tomb), and G2–G6 (156 glory verses). Their verses are in the CSV. Some carry inner-being content of their own, such as G2 (seeking glory from men, Joh 5:44; 12:43) and R8's cited verses (Luk 15:20 "he arose and came to his father"). Those belong to other strands' readings rather than to Q4.

### 1.3 What the counts point to (for the reading to test)

1. **The body with the inner being (B4) is the densest group:** 46 of 49 verses carry an M-code, M47 in 16. The body words sit beside heart, soul, spirit and mind, and beside sin (M56, M58) and purity (M61). This is the present body, not the raised one.
2. **The changed body (A1, A6) also leans to M47** (8 and 3 verses). The changed-body verses set the body beside the inner seat too.
3. **The resurrection itself carries little inner-being vocabulary.** In R1 (the debate) only 5 of 13 verses carry an M-code. R2's ten verses carry one each of eight different codes. Where the R groups do carry codes, they are:
   - judgment, in the coming resurrection (R5: M26 in 7)
   - faith, trust and remembering, beside Christ raised (R4: M13, M19, M81)
   - faintness, in R4 and R5 (M24)
4. **Glory divides.**
   - G2, glory among people, carries authority, seeking, desire, wickedness, pride and deceit (M72, M83, M18, M58, M08, M14). This is glory as something sought, an inner act.
   - G1, glory to come, carries faintness, judgment and prayer (M24, M26, M42). The verses set it beside suffering and losing heart.
   - G3, glory given to God, carries praise (M22), with fear (M01) and turning (M11) in Revelation's "fear God and give him glory" and "they did not repent and give him glory".
5. **"Rise" in the ordinary sense (R8) is not empty.** 55 of 95 verses carry an M-code: knowing, release, sin and confession (M15, M59, M56, M60). Examples are "Rise and be baptized and wash away your sins" (Act 22:16) and "authority … to forgive sins … Rise" (Mat 9:6). The verb is the act; what moves is elsewhere in the verse (rule 73).

## 3. The verses mapped to the claims register

### 3.1 Verse to claim

- **53 verse-to-claim links: 44 of the 640 verses source 32 claims.** The other 596 verses source none.
- **No claim draws on more than four groups.** The broadest are:
  - C-148, *the way out of death in the living* (A3, R6, R8, R9)
  - 10-4, *the body's redemption at the end … held* (A1, B3, R4)
  - 9-7, *Rom 1:21–32 as a five-step descent*, held (A1, B4, G3)
  - C-161, *death changes how the living live* (B2, B4, R4)
- **Most of the 32 claims were made by OT-02 or earlier strands** (death in the living, dead to sin, sleep, soul at death). These verses reach them as one source among many: "4 of 13", "1 of 19". They are not claims about the raised body.
- **No current claim states anything about the raised body itself.** That covers the seed and the body, the four contrasts, Christ the firstfruits, the change "in a moment", the tent and the clothing, the body made like his, no marrying, and the two resurrections. Claim 10-4 names Rom 8:23 and 1Co 15:42–44, but as held, "not in the reading".

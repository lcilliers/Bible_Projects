## 1. The ruling

**Researcher, chat 2026-10-12, verbatim:**
1. *"M72 need to be treated with care. Group A and C words should all be treated at most as a qualifyer. These verses should only be read in full within the context of the primary M-code. It seems that B is the main focus for the purposes of the study, with D where, it applies to humans."*
2. **Extended:** *"extend my guidance in that hand should not be read as the primary M-code, it is also a qualifyer and it used figuratively. the main operator is likely to give or take away M-Code."*

The gloss groups are those of the overview, [`m72-cluster-overview-v1-20261012.md`](m72-cluster-overview-v1-20261012.md) §3.

| **C** officials, leaders, masters (651 verses) | at most a **qualifier** | As A. |

**So the M72 reading is group B, 275 verses.**

**For your direction: the 81 verses with no M-code.** Under the triage order (GOVERNANCE.md §91), an act with no M-code is read by its act and placed. Here the act is mostly God giving someone into another's hand, or a narrative of defeat. My recommendation is one light read of the 81, with one decision for each:
- route A when God's act is described (13.1, God's character only, as with E)
- data when there is no inner-being bearing
- a placement where a human inner act is in the passage

That closes them without holding. The alternative is to leave them as data now.

## 4. What the ruling does to the threads carried in

- **OT-16 (M02 × M72, 100 verses).** 96 hold only A, C, D or E words, so for M72 they are qualifiers or God's character only. The anger readings already read them in their primary M-code. **4 hold a B word and stay open: Pro 16:32, Isa 14:6, Psa 59:13, Eze 19:14.**
- **OT-98 (M18 × M72, 182 verses).** None of the 7 forward marks is in M72 focus now:
  - Gen 31:29, *"It is in my power to do you harm"*: the hand is a qualifier. The act is the harm held back (M58, with M74 "careful" and M65), so it goes forward to M58.
  - Ezr 7:9; 8:18; Neh 2:8; 2:18 (God's hand), Isa 52:7 (*"Your God reigns"*) and 1Sa 15:28 (the kingdom torn away): God's character only (13.1).
  - The 18 B verses among the 182 are read with B, building on their M18 decisions.
- **The M47 check (#1979).** For M72 it is the 28 B verses shared with M47.
- **Signpost #1893 (hands).** God's hand is read only for God's character, and the human hand is a qualifier. This step does not open the signpost.

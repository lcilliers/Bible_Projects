## Cross-strong synergy: the literal sense is real but a minority strand

Reading all 74 verses together, this family's centre of gravity is **not** primarily domestic
ethics — it is **the marriage-infidelity figure used as the primary vehicle for covenant
unfaithfulness / idolatry**, with literal marital-adultery teaching present but outnumbered:

- **H5003 (na'aph, 15 verses)** is telling on its own: its meaning block gives the literal sense
  first ("to commit adultery... always with wife of another") but its actual verses are dominated
  by Israel's idolatry — Hos 3:1 ("love a woman... even as the Lord loves the children of Israel,
  though they turn to other gods"), Jer 3:9 ("committing adultery with stone and tree"), Ezek
  23:37/45 (the extended two-sisters allegory). Only the Decalogue citations (Exod 20:14, Deut
  5:18) and a couple of narrative uses are literal without a second layer.
- **G4202 (porneia, 22 verses, the broadest term)** ranges from literal cases (1 Cor 5's incest
  case, vice-list entries in Mark 7:21/Gal 5:19) to a sustained, book-length personification: "the
  great prostitute" Babylon "drunk with the wine of the passion of her sexual immorality" runs
  across Rev 14, 17, 18, 19 as the organizing figure for the whole book's judgment sequence — by
  verse count this is the single largest use of any strong in this family.
- **G3429/G3431 (moichaō/moicheuō, 15 verses combined)** carry the clearest literal-teaching strand
  — Jesus' divorce/remarriage teaching (Matt 5:32, 19:9, Mark 10:11) and the repeated Decalogue
  citations across the Synoptics and Epistles (Matt 19:18, Mark 10:19, Luke 18:20, Rom 13:9, Jas
  2:11) — but even here G3431's meaning block notes the same word covers "spiritual adultery, be
  guilty of idolatry" (Rev 2:22).
- **G4205 (pornos, 10 verses)** functions almost entirely as a vice-list category — who is
  excluded from the kingdom — paired consistently with "idolaters" as a fixed collocation (1 Cor
  5:10-11, 6:9, Eph 5:5, Rev 21:8, 22:15), reinforcing the same adultery/idolatry pairing from a
  different angle.

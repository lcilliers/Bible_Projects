## 3. Restated

- **10.7** (v29 → v30), *"The humbling of the soul was not wrong in itself."* Isa 58:5 questions the form of the fast, so the verses do not say this. Removed. The passage now reads: *"The humbling stood beside oppression in the same people, and it was not what he chooses."*
- **Ch 14** (v18 → v19), *"God's reason is tender"*. "Tender" is my evaluation, and the verse gives the reason. It now reads *"God gives the reason"*.
- **Claim C-188** (from update 141). It said "In four passages" what God looks to is set against a religious act. That counted only the humility passages, and 13.1 *Delight* gathers more (Isa 1:11–13; Psa 40:6–8; Hos 6:6; Mat 9:13; 12:7). It is restated to name the humility passages *"as in the other passages gathered in 13.1"*.

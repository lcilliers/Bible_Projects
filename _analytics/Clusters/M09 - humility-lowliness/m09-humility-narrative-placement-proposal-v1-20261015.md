# M09: where humility fits in the narrative (proposal)

**Date:** 2026-10-15 · **Escalation:** #2148 · **Status:** proposal for the researcher's decision. No narrative file is changed.

**Researcher, verbatim (chat 2026-10-15):** *"we need to consider where the humility fits into the narrative, especially in the Ch 10 section. Ch10.1 does not feel right. humility is not a feeling."*

## 1. Where humility is now

The narrative has **no section on humility**. Its words appear in passing in eight places (current files):

| Chapter | What is there |
| --- | --- |
| 10.1 *Feeling* | Isa 66:2 inside *Fear* ("humble and contrite in spirit and trembles at my word"); Psa 37:11 "the meek" inside *Delight* |
| 10.7 *Choosing* | Col 3:12 humility as one of the things "put on"; Nebuchadnezzar "whom he would, he humbled" (Dan 5:19); the fast of Isa 58 |
| 10.8 *Right and wrong* | "proud or humble" in the opening list of how the heart is judged |
| 10.10 *Relating to others* | "which of them was to be regarded as the greatest" (Luk 22) |
| 10.12 *Fear* | Isa 66:2 again; Psa 76:9 |
| 10.13 *Anger* | humbling oneself turns God's wrath: 2Ch 12:7, 12; 32:26; 33:12; 2Ki 22:19; Zep 2:3 |
| Ch 11 | the crushed spirit (Psa 51:17; 143:3–4); "distress turns people both ways" (Manasseh, Ahaz) |
| Ch 12 *Pride and the lifted-up heart and spirit* | Pro 16:18–19; "Pride and humility are where the spirit is weighed most" |

## 2. What the readings show humility to be (batches 1–3, and the 27 verses with no other M-code)

The verses do not name humility as a feeling. They name:
- **an act**, humbling oneself, done "before" God or before his word, with the heart named as where it happens (batch 1 group I)
- **a standing**, being humble, lowly or meek: the humble seek God, are taught and favored, and are glad at another's deliverance (batch 2 group I)
- **a quality that is sought and walked in**: "seek humility", "walk humbly with your God", "humility comes before honor", with the fear of the Lord, with wisdom and beside listening to reproof (batch 2 group II)
- **a state of the spirit**: a lowly and contrite spirit, a broken heart, which God dwells with, looks to, is near to and heals (batches 2 III and 3 I)
- **a place taken among people**: the lower seat, the servant, the title refused (Luk 14:7–11; Mat 23:8–12; Pro 25:6–7)
- **something done to a person**: God humbles to test and teach (Deu 8), and brings the proud low (batch 1 groups II and III)
- **God's own way**: he "looks far down", "your gentleness made me great", "I have been broken over their whoring heart"

In every group with an inner bearing, the verses set it against a rising: pride, the lifted-up heart, the hardened heart, the stiff neck, haughty eyes.

So **humility sits across the Ch 10 activities.** It is before God (10.9), it is chosen and walked in (10.7), it goes with hearing and taking reproof (10.2), it is a place among others (10.10), it turns wrath (10.13), and it is the opposite of what Ch 12 holds. It is not one of them. On the evidence of the verses, the researcher's point stands: it is not a feeling. OT-12 already holds the open question about the 10.1 *Feeling* frame.

## 3. Options

**A. A Ch 10 sub-chapter of its own: 10.14** *(recommended)*
- **What it does:** humility gets one full account, as fear (10.12) and anger (10.13) did.
  - This follows #1961: *"encapsulate the full finding … into a separate Ch10 sub chapter … then we will proceed with gathering similar studies of other key characteristics."*
  - It is built from the fan-out readings (batches 1–5), the cross-check and the threads.
- **What it gets right:** it holds all seven faces in §2 together, which is the account the readings have built. It also follows the "one coherent account per phenomenon" rule (#1930). The activities that humility touches (10.7, 10.9, 10.10, 10.13, Ch 12) carry only their part, rewritten, with a pointer to 10.14.
- **What to watch:**
  - the pride pairing (keep pride in Ch 12, and have 10.14 and Ch 12 point to each other)
  - God's side (13.1 holds God's lowering and his favor to the humble; 10.14 holds the human side)
- **Title:** titles name the subject only (style guide v2). Choices:
  - *Humility*: plain, parallel to *Fear* and *Anger*
  - *Humbling and lowliness*: names the act and the standing
  - *Lowliness*: the word that covers spirit, standing and seat
  - I lean to *Humility*.

**B. Woven by activity, with no sub-chapter (#1920)**
- **What it does:** each face goes to its activity: the act before God to 10.9, the walk and the lower place to 10.7 and 10.10, hearing and reproof to 10.2, turning wrath to 10.13 (already there), the contrite spirit to Ch 6 / Ch 11.
- **What it gets right:** there is no new structure.
- **What it loses:** the scatter that exists now would continue. The reader never meets humility as one thing, though the verses keep returning to it. This is what produced "it is starting to just become a list of quotes" (#1930).

**C. Pair it with pride: a sub-chapter on pride and humility together**
- **What it does:** the pairing the verses make constantly (proud brought low, humble raised) gets one home.
- **What it costs:** pride would have to be taken out of Ch 12, a structural change that is not asked for. M08 Pride has not been read as a strand, so this would be fitting the structure ahead of the reading.

## 4. Recommendation

**Option A, 10.14 *Humility*:**
- It is written after batches 4 (Greek humility and meekness) and 5 (submission) and the cross-check, on the researcher's instruction.
- Pride stays in Ch 12, and the two chapters point to each other.
- The passing mentions in 10.1 (Isa 66:2 inside *Fear*, Psa 37:11 inside *Delight*) stay where they are for what they say there (trembling at the word; delight in peace). Each gets a pointer to 10.14, not a move.

The researcher's statement that humility is not a feeling is also recorded on OT-12 (the 10.1 *Feeling* frame), as evidence for that open question.

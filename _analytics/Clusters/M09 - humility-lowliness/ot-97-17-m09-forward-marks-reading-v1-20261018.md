## 2. The readings

**Isaiah 57:15: already carried.** It is in 10.14 *A lowly and contrite spirit* and six other places
(see `../M47 - inner-seat/ot-97-71-m47-forward-marks-reading-v1-20261018.md`). Nothing new.

**James 1:9: already carried.** "Let the lowly brother boast in his exaltation, and the rich in his
humiliation, because like a flower of the grass he will pass away" (1:9–10) is in 10.14 *The proud
brought low, the lowly raised* (*Where the boast goes*, claim C-182). 1:11 only finishes the figure:
"So also will the rich man fade away in the midst of his pursuits." Nothing new.

**Ezekiel 21:26: already carried.** It is in the same 10.14 list. The passage (21:25–27) is said to
"profane wicked one, prince of Israel": "Remove the turban and take off the crown. Things shall not
remain as they are." It is a word of judgment on a ruler, not an inner act. Nothing new.

**Ezekiel 17:24: not in the book.**
> "And all the trees of the field shall know that I am the Lord; I bring low the high tree, and make
> high the low tree, dry up the green tree, and make the dry tree flourish. I am the Lord; I have
> spoken, and I will do it."

The passage (17:22–24) is a figure: God takes "a tender one" from the top of the cedar and plants it
on a high mountain, and birds of every sort nest in it. The reversal is the same one 10.14 lists. Two
things are new:
- **its purpose:** "shall know that I am the Lord"
- **its certainty:** "I have spoken, and I will do it"

10.14 *Only God does it* says who reverses. This verse says why the reversal is made known. The trees
are a figure, and the verse does not explain it.

**Job 5:11: not in the book, and spoken by Eliphaz.**
> "he sets on high those who are lowly, and those who mourn are lifted to safety"

The passage (5:8–16) is Eliphaz's counsel: "As for me, I would seek God" (5:8). It ends "So the poor
have hope, and injustice shuts her mouth" (5:16). The words match what the rest of Scripture says of
the lowly raised. But the book's practice with Job's friends is to name the speaker, and to note that
God's verdict stands over them: "you have not spoken of me what is right" (Job 42:7). This is done
for Bildad in Ch 2 and 10.12 (claim C-153). So if it is used at all, it must carry that frame.
- *"those who mourn are lifted to safety"* and *"the poor have hope"* are inner-being words: mourning
  and hope.

**Ecclesiastes 5:8: not in the book.**
> "If you see in a province the oppression of the poor and the violation of justice and
> righteousness, do not be amazed at the matter, for the high official is watched by a higher, and
> there are yet higher ones over them."

**The main operation** is the onlooker's response to seeing the poor oppressed: "do not be amazed".
The height words name the chain of officials. The verse does not say whether that chain explains the
oppression (each protects the one below) or promises it will be checked (each is watched). This is
**the poor as seen by another**, which is **OT-105**, the thread opened for exactly this by the M09
strand and still open. Its target clusters (M05, M12 and others) are not yet read.

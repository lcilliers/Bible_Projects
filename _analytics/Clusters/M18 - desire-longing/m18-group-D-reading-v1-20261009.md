## 1. Zeal: the same words as jealousy (10.6)

The Hebrew *qinʾāh* / *qānāʾ* and the Greek *zēlos* / *zēlōtēs* are translated both "jealousy" and "zeal". Group D brings in the zeal verses, which earlier passes had not read. Their faces:

| Face | Verses | What differs |
|---|---|---|
| **Consumes the one who has it** | Psa 69:9 (with 69:8, a stranger to his brothers); Psa 119:139 (with 119:141); Joh 2:17 | What lights it: insult to God's house; the forgetting of his words. It draws God's reproach onto the zealous one. The disciples read Jesus' act by remembering the psalm |
| **Sincere, aimed at God, and wrong for lack of knowledge** | Rom 10:1–3; Gal 1:13–16; Phi 3:6–7; Act 22:3–4; Act 21:20–21 | Paul's own zeal, three times named beside persecution. Ended not by his correction but by God's revealing. He meets the same zeal in his people with prayer |
| **Put on show** | 2Ki 10:16, with 10:31 | Jehu's "see my zeal", and "not careful to walk … with all his heart" |
| **Sets an oath aside** | 2Sa 21:1–2 | Saul's zeal for his own people broke a sworn word. The guilt came due after him |
| **God's own, in a man** | 2Co 11:2–3 (with Num 25:11) | Paul's "divine jealousy", a husband's jealousy for the bridegroom, joined to fear for their thoughts |
| **Spent for others; set beside the end of fear; made by redemption** | Col 4:12–13; 1Pe 3:13–14; Tit 2:14 | Labour in prayer; "Have no fear of them"; "a people … zealous for good works" |

**I read:** in these verses zeal is not pictured as a strength the zealous one holds but as a fire that uses him up; and it can be sincere and still run ahead of knowing. Phinehas's zeal turned wrath back; Saul's left bloodguilt. The difference the verses give is whose the zeal is (God's own, or for the people) and what it set aside.

## 2. Jealousy against a person (10.6)

| Verse | What jealousy does | Probe |
|---|---|---|
| Gen 37:4, 11 | Hates, cannot speak peacefully; jealous, while the father "kept the saying in mind" | One dream, two hearers. **I read** jealousy and keeping as two ways of holding the same words |
| Gen 26:12–16 | Envies, stops the wells, sends away | The wealth came from "The Lord blessed him". **I read** envy that cannot take the blessing spoils the water |
| Psa 106:16; Num 16:3 | Jealous of Moses and Aaron, "the holy one of the Lord" | It speaks as equality: "all in the congregation are holy". Set against Joshua's jealousy *for* Moses (Num 11:29) |
| Act 5:17–18; 13:45 | "Filled with jealousy" at another's crowd; arrest; contradiction and reviling | The same trigger (a following), the same measure word, two outworkings: arrest and speech |
| Eze 31:9–10 | Figure: the trees of Eden envy the cedar; its heart proud of its height | The beauty was given ("I made it"). **I read** envy and pride as two answers to one gift (Ch 12) |
| Isa 11:12–13 | Ephraim's jealousy and Judah's harassing end together | Ended when the Lord gathers the banished |
| Ecc 9:6 | Love, hate and envy perish with the dead | Envy is named among the things a life ends |

## 3. A husband's jealousy with no witness (10.6)

Num 5:14–31. The "spirit of jealousy" comes over the husband whether the wife "has defiled herself" or "has not" (5:14): the law itself says the jealousy may be wrong. The offering is "of jealousy … of remembrance", with no oil and no frankincense (5:15). The matter is not his to settle: "he shall set the woman before the Lord" (5:30); "The man shall be free from iniquity" (5:31). Beside Pro 6:34–35, where a husband's jealousy "will accept no compensation", **I read** the law as taking the jealousy out of the husband's hands and putting it before God. Num 5:18 and 5:25 (the ritual) are carried by this account.

## 4. God's jealousy (13.1 *Jealousy*)

| New face | Verses | Probe |
|---|---|---|
| **His name, in the commandment** | Exo 20:5–6 (Deu 5:9–10 carried); Exo 34:14; Deu 4:23–24 | Set against images and other gods in all three. Set beside steadfast love: three or four generations against thousands. "whose name is Jealous" |
| **It has a measure** | 1Ki 14:22 | "more than all that their fathers had done" |
| **For Zion: the answer to an angel's "how long"** | Zec 1:12–16 | Jealous for her, angry at the nations who "furthered the disaster" beyond his "little" anger, returned "with mercy" |
| **Roused by prayer, joined to pity** | Joe 2:17–18; Eze 39:25–26 | After the priests' weeping; for his holy name; the reproach and the shame taken away |
| **Zeal that does the work** | Isa 9:7; 2Ki 19:30–31 (Isa 37:32 carried) | Zeal named as the doer of what is promised: the child's government, the remnant |
| **Put on, when no one stood** | Isa 59:16–17 | He "wondered that there was no one to intercede", then "wrapped himself in zeal as a cloak". **I read** the cloak as the outer garment |
| **The same fire in the New Testament** | Heb 10:26–27 | "a fury [zeal] of fire that will consume the adversaries" |
| **Missed, and asked to be seen** | Isa 63:15; Isa 26:11 | "Where are your zeal and your might?", named with "the stirring of your inner parts and your compassion"; "Let them see your zeal for your people" |

The closing reading of 13.1 *Jealousy* is widened: his jealousy is also named as the doer of what he promises, and put on like a cloak when no one else stands.

## 2. Worth weighed, and not known (10.6 *Precious*)

- **Above every desire:** Pro 3:15. The scale has all of a person's wants on the other side, not only jewels. 10.6 had quoted only the second half; the word itself was not carried.
- **Not known:** Job 28:13, 16. Proverbs states the worth; Job says man does not know it. **I read** the two together as worth and blindness to it, not as contradiction.
- **Shown by trial:** 1Pe 1:6–8. Grief, love and joy toward one not seen, in three verses. Gold is tested and perishes; faith is tested and found.
- **Making a person wait:** Jam 5:7–8 against 5:3–5. **I read** two hearts: fattened on hoarded treasure, or established by waiting.
- **A refuge:** Isa 28:15–16; 1Pe 2:6–7. Lies as refuge against the precious stone; "not in haste".

## 4. Splendour (10.6 *Precious*; Ch 12)

- **Entices:** Job 31:24–28. The moon's splendour, a heart "secretly enticed", a kiss of the hand, counted as falseness to God. **I read** eye, heart and mouth in one sentence.
- **Passes:** Psa 37:20, in the psalm that opens "be not envious". **I read** the figure as an answer to envy.
- **Covers:** Rev 17:4. A golden cup full of what is unclean.
- **Turns to pride:** Eze 28:13–17. A given covering, blamelessness, then trade, violence and a proud heart (Ch 12, beside Eze 28:2 and 31:9–10).

## 5. Possessions and loss (10.6)

- **Two ways to fill a house:** Pro 1:13 (plunder) and 24:3–4 (knowledge), the second in the chapter that opens on envy of evil men (24:1–2). Pro 12:27: the slothful does not roast what he caught.
- **The cargo ends in souls:** Rev 18:11–17. **I read** the merchants' grief as for the trade, not for the souls in the cargo.

## 7. The divine (13.1 *Delight*)

- **Jer 31:18–20:** Ephraim's grief and shame, then "Is Ephraim my dear son? … as often as I speak against him, I do remember him still. Therefore my heart yearns for him". Speaking against and remembering together; the yearning given as the ground of mercy.
- **1Pe 1:18–19:** ransomed from "futile ways inherited", not with silver or gold, but with "the precious blood of Christ". Set beside Isa 43:4, which is already in 13.1.

## 3. Why they were not narrated

The anger units read the anger words. The M18 side of these verses was jealousy, delight, favour, desire, or willing, and it was left. In most cases it is God's jealousy beside his wrath. The anger ledger has it (AG-04, AG-73–76), but 10.13 carries it as one "joined with" bullet (Deu 6:15). There was no account of God's jealousy until 13.1 *Jealousy* was opened earlier today from the held items.

One verse, Job 6:2, is listed in the anger ledger as already in the chapters. A text search finds it nowhere, and it is now woven.

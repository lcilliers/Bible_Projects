# M18 group B: the 15 verses whose act carries no M-code, read by their acts

**Date:** 2026-10-11 · **Escalation:** #1993 (item F) · **Method:** [`wa-verse-reading-by-role-guide-v1-20261011.md`](../../../Workflow/Instructions/wa-verse-reading-by-role-guide-v1-20261011.md) · **Follows:** [`m18-group-B-remaining-role-reading-v2-20261011.md`](m18-group-B-remaining-role-reading-v2-20261011.md) §4

**Instruction (researcher, chat 2026-10-11, verbatim):** *"read the 15 now as recommended, then proceed with the weave"*.

In each verse the M18 word is a qualifier (role per guide §4.2). Most often it is "what seems good to you", the measure of another person's judgement. The act it qualifies has no M-code in the verse, so no cluster reading will bring these verses back. Each was read in its passage and placed by its act.

| Verse | The act, read in its passage | Home |
|---|---|---|
| **2Ki 10:5** | The rulers of Samaria *"were exceedingly afraid"* (10:4). They give up the choice Jehu put to them: *"We will not make anyone king. Do whatever is good in your eyes."* The next letter uses what they handed over: *"take the heads of your master's sons"* (10:6). **Choice given up in fear.** | 10.7 *Fear and choosing* |
| **2Sa 19:27** | Mephibosheth is slandered by Ziba and does not argue his cause: *"do therefore what seems good to you … What further right have I, then, to cry to the king?"* (19:27–28). Then: *"let him take it all, since my lord the king has come safely home"* (19:30). **Loyalty shown in what he gives up.** | 10.10 *Loyalty and honour* |
| **2Sa 19:18** | Ziba's men cross *"to do his pleasure"*. Then Shimei falls down: *"your servant knows that I have sinned"* (19:20). **Service, and a plea, at the king's return.** It is read with 19:27–30. | 10.10 *Loyalty and honour* |
| **1Sa 11:10** | The men of Jabesh have been told that *"you shall have salvation"* and *"they were glad"* (11:9). To Nahash they say *"you may do to us whatever seems good to you"*. **I read:** words of surrender, with the rescue they knew of held back. | 10.11 *Hiding what I hold* |
| **1Sa 14:36, 40** | The people give Saul his own eyes as the measure. The priest asks for God's first: *"Let us draw near to God here"*. God does not answer (14:37). At the rash oath on Jonathan *"there was not a man among all the people who answered him"* (14:39). **Whose measure: the king's eyes, or God's.** | 10.7 *Whose measure* |
| **Est 3:11; 8:8** | The king hands a people to Haman, *"as it seems good to you"*. Later he gives Esther and Mordecai *"write as you please"*, but the first edict *"cannot be revoked"*. **The decision handed over twice; the first handing cannot be undone.** | 10.7 *Whose measure* |
| **1Ch 13:2** | *"If it seems good to you and from the Lord our God"*. The agreement is recorded as *"right in the eyes of all the people"* (13:4). **An assembly asked to choose as one, with two measures named.** | 10.7 *Choosing together* |
| **1Sa 3:18** | Eli made Samuel tell him everything (3:17). Having heard the judgment: *"It is the Lord. Let him do what seems good to him."* These are the same words as David's in 2Sa 15:26. David yields before he knows; Eli yields after. **Yielding to God.** | 10.7 *Choosing before God* |
| **Gen 19:8** | Lot offers his daughters, *"do to them as you please. Only do nothing to these men, for they have come under the shelter of my roof."* The men answer, *"he has become the judge!"* (19:9). **The shelter of his roof held above his daughters.** The verses record the offer and the reason; they do not weigh them. | 10.10 *Loyalty and honour* |
| **Gen 20:15** | Warned by God, Abimelech returns Sarah and gives back the choice of where to live: *"dwell where it pleases you"*. He clears her before all (20:16). **A choice given back after a wrong.** | 10.7 *A choice that is honoured* |
| **1Ki 10:13; 2Ch 9:12** | Solomon gives the queen *"all that she desired"*. The measure is of the gift. | **Confirmed:** 1Ki 10:13 is already in 10.6 *Precious*, placed by the queen giving after she had seen. 2Ch 9:12 is its parallel. No change. |
| **Hos 8:8** | *"Israel is swallowed up; already they are among the nations as a useless vessel"* (a vessel no one delights in), after *"they sow the wind, and they shall reap the whirlwind"* (8:7). This is the figure of Jer 22:28, which carries M30 *despised*. | **Forward with Jer 22:28 to M30** (OT-97 updated) |

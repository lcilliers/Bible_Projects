## 2. The reading

> "Woe to him who gets evil gain for his house, to set his nest on high, to be safe from the reach of
> harm! You have devised shame for your house by cutting off many peoples; you have forfeited your
> life. For the stone will cry out from the wall, and the beam from the woodwork respond." (Habakkuk
> 2:9–11)

**The passage (2:4–14)** is a set of five woes on the arrogant man of 2:4–5: "his soul is puffed up";
"His greed is as wide as Sheol". The peoples he plundered take up the taunt (2:6).
- **First woe (2:6–8):** "Woe to him who heaps up what is not his own—for how long?" Then: "Will not
  your debtors suddenly arise, and those awake who will make you tremble? Then you will be spoil for
  them" (2:7).
- **Second woe (2:9–11):** gain, to set the nest high, to be out of harm's reach. Then shame devised
  for his own house, his life forfeited, and the house itself crying out.

**The main operation** is greed, and greed's reason: **gain gathered in order to be safe**. The height
word measures it: "his nest on high" is the safety he wants. The verse then says what that safety
comes to: shame for his house, his life forfeited (2:10). The first woe gives the same turn from the
other side: the man who took from others is made to **tremble** before them (2:7).

## 4. The impact

**For OT-97.68 itself: nothing new.** The verse is already carried in 10.6 for its M18 act (gain for
safety), with the height word read as the nest. The item can be closed as **already carried**.

**One small gap, found by reading the passage:** what the safety comes to. 10.6 quotes "you have
forfeited your life" (2:10) but not the turn of 2:7. The man who gathered what was not his own to be
safe is the one made to tremble: "Will not your debtors suddenly arise, and those awake who will make
you tremble?" This adds to 10.6 *Possessions* in one sentence, beside the nest: the safety greed buys
is answered by fear of those it took from. I read 2:7 together with 2:9; the verses stand in
neighbouring woes of one taunt, so the joining would need a claim (rule 77).

**Not for now:**
- **2:11** ("the stone will cry out from the wall") is a figure of the house bearing witness. It
  belongs to the reading of disclosure (10.11, M20, not yet read).
- **M45 ("safe")** is OT-97.27, a cluster not yet read. It stays there.

## 1. The water flows outward, and the giver thirsts (10.6)

| Verse | What it shows | Probe |
|---|---|---|
| Joh 7:37–39 | "Out of his heart will flow rivers of living water", said "about the Spirit" | Compare John 4:14: there the spring wells up *in* him; here rivers flow *out of* him. **I read** a want met that becomes a source for others (Ch 6) |
| Joh 19:28–30; Psa 69:20–21 | "knowing that all was now finished … 'I thirst'"; sour wine; "It is finished". The psalm sets the thirst beside "I looked for pity, but there was none" | The one who offered water thirsts, and says it knowingly. **I read** the want spoken, not hidden |

## 2. Thirst answered, and its reasons (10.6)

- **Aimed at righteousness, and called blessed:** Mat 5:6.
- **God answers, with the reason given:** Isa 41:17 ("I … will not forsake them"); Isa 49:10 ("he who has pity on them will lead them"). Rev 7:16–17 is close in wording to Isa 49:10, and adds "God will wipe away every tear": thirst and tears answered together.
- **Met, then forgotten:** Neh 9:15–17 ("acted presumptuously … not mindful of the wonders"); Isa 48:21–22. **I read** a want met so fully that it was no longer felt, and the giver forgotten with it (10.3).

## 3. Thirst as judgment: the inner ground named (10.6)

| Verse | Ground the verse names | Inner state set against it |
|---|---|---|
| Deu 28:47–48 | not serving "with joyfulness and gladness of heart, because of the abundance of all things" | joyless service in plenty |
| Isa 65:12–14 | "chose what I did not delight in" | "gladness of heart" against "pain of heart" and "breaking of spirit" |
| Isa 5:11–13 | running after strong drink, not regarding the deeds of the Lord | "lack of knowledge"; the drinkers end thirsty |

## 4. Want borne for others (10.6)

1Co 4:11–12 (hunger and thirst, and "When reviled, we bless"); 2Co 11:27–28. Paul's list of hardships ends not on hunger but on "the daily pressure on me of my anxiety for all the churches": the weight named last is inside, and it is for others.

Act 10:10–12: Peter's hunger is the setting of the vision of the sheet. The verses do not say why; nothing more is read into it.

## 5. Another's thirst: met, refused, used (10.10)

| Verse | What is done with the thirst | Probe |
|---|---|---|
| Mat 25:35, 37, 40, 44 (25:42 carried) | given drink, or not; the King names it as his own | Both sides ask the same "when did we see you". **I read**: done or not done to him, known or not |
| Rut 2:9–10 | Boaz gives a foreigner drink | She asks why she has found favour |
| Judg 4:19, 21 | Jael gives drink and covers him, then kills him | The same act, care in one tent and a trap in the other |
| Lam 4:3–4 | children beg, "no one gives to them"; "even jackals offer the breast" | The animals set above the people |
| Job 24:11–12 | workers tread the winepresses "but suffer thirst"; "yet God charges no one with wrong" | Job's complaint (10.9) |

## 6. A famine of hearing (10.2)

Amo 8:11–13: "not a famine of bread, nor a thirst for water, but of hearing the words of the Lord". They seek the word and do not find it; in the same day the young faint for thirst. The famine is of *hearing*, not of the word.

## 7. Confirmed, carried, route C

- **Confirmed:** Joh 4:13, 4:15 (10.6); Rom 12:20 (10.10, 10.13, Ch 14).
- **Carried:** Mat 25:42 (by 25:35 and 25:44).
- **Route C:** Jer 48:18 ("sit on the parched ground", the ground of Dibon); Eze 19:13 (the vine "in a dry and thirsty land"). In both the word describes land.

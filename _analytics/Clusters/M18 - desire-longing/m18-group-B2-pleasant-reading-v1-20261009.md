## 2. A person (10.1 *Delight*, new subsection *What is found pleasant*)

- **Gen 49:14–15.** Issachar, "a strong donkey, crouching", saw that the land was pleasant and "bowed his shoulder to bear". **I read** that strength settles for comfort, and the comfort costs its freedom.
- **Song 7:5–6.** "How beautiful and pleasant you are", after "a king is held captive in the tresses". The one who delights is held by what he delights in, and it is said as praise.
- **2Sa 1:26.** "very pleasant have you been to me". The pleasantness is spoken only in the lament.
- **Pro 9:17–18.** "Stolen water is sweet". The sweetness is in the stealing. "he does not know that the dead are there."
- **Psa 141:4–6.** "let me not eat of their delicacies", "Let a righteous man strike me—it is a kindness", "they shall hear my words, for they are pleasant". **I read** that what is pleasant to him has been turned around.
- **Pro 24:24–25.** "those who rebuke the wicked will have delight", against the curse on the man who calls the wicked right.
- **2Co 12:8–10.** "I am content with weaknesses". The word is "well pleased". He is pleased with what he pleaded three times to be rid of, after "My grace is sufficient".
- **1Th 3:1–3** (*Wishing*). "when we could bear it no longer, we were willing to be left behind at Athens alone".

## 3. God (13.1 *Delight*)

- **The mountain (Mat 17:4–7; 2Pe 1:16–19).** The words of the baptism come back with "listen to him", spoken while Peter was talking of tents. The disciples were terrified. Jesus touched them: "Rise, and have no fear". Peter later gives it as eyewitness ground for what he preaches.
- **His good pleasure gives (Luk 12:30–32; 1Co 1:21; Col 1:19–21).** What pleases him is to give the kingdom, to save through what the world calls folly, and to reconcile those "hostile in mind".
- **Not pleased (1Co 10:3–6).** All ate and drank what he gave, "Nevertheless, with most of them God was not pleased". Sharing in the gift did not make them pleasing.
- **Hebrews 10:5–10.** Psalm 40 is read as the words of Christ: "a body have you prepared for me", "by that will we have been sanctified". It sits after *Not the offering itself*.

## 4. Confirmed and carried

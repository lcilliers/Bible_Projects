# M18 group B, sets 1 and 2: re-read by the main operation, and corrected

**Date:** 2026-10-09 · **Escalation:** #1993 (handoff v7 item F) · **Weave:** `m18-group-B-correction-weave-v1-20261009.py` · **Records:** `m18-group-B-correction-records-update-v1-20261009.py` · **Registers:** `m18-group-B1-register-v1-20261009.csv`, `m18-group-B2-register-v1-20261009.csv` (places updated)

## 1. Why

The researcher taught the method on 2Pe 2:13, verbatim:

> *"The inner being operation here in relation to pleasure is reveling, counting it pleasure is the measurement of the reveling. The main IB operation of this verse is to suffer - what is being suffered is wrongdoing, which is strenghtened by the deceptions in which they are reveling. So making something of this verse with pleasure as an entry point will lead to distorted views."*

Then: *"yes, re-read and correct your work."*

Sets 1 and 2 had been read with delight as the way in, and many verses were placed under *Delight* although the word only measures another act. The method is now recorded in memory (`feedback_entry_word_is_not_the_main_operation`):
1. Find the verse's main operation.
2. Find what strengthens or qualifies it.
3. Then place the word by its part in that operation.
4. The home is decided by the main operation.

## Change of character (#1919)

The verb "want" carries several different faces in these verses:
- a will that wants the good and cannot do it (Rom 7)
- a will asked out by Jesus
- a will made absolute
- a will bent to a crowd
- a will taken up and turned ("If anyone would be first")
- a will that turns back toward slavery (Gal 4:9)

Willing God's will comes before knowing it (Joh 7:17). The faces are woven as Ch 11 §4, *Whose will, and whether it can carry itself out*.

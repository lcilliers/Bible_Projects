## 2. The researcher's closing synthesis on #1005 — five points

Stated verbatim in substance, recorded here as the decision record:

**a) The cluster work over time is a key building block that enriches and enables the analysis.**
Not a discard candidate — the old and new M08 passes, and by extension every cluster/book's prior
work, are load-bearing input to whatever comes next.

**b) It is not finished — and completing it is not only about collating what is there.** Collation
(gathering the files, extracting the DB rows) is necessary but not sufficient; the M08 pilot itself
demonstrated that the deeper gap is a missing analytical layer (the verse-context read), not just
missing organisation.

**c) Not all the work is in the database, and including the files is important.** Confirmed directly
this session: the June-21 `findings/` folder's actual analytical substance was never loaded past its
bare characteristic definitions — the DB alone would have shown a misleadingly thin picture of what
that pass actually did. The files are a first-class source, not a fallback.

**d) Careful consideration of all the generations of work is important.** Old May pipeline, new June
pipeline, base-lexical layer (pre- and post- the iba.db rebuild) — each generation carries real,
separately-checkable content and its own blind spots; none supersedes the others outright.

**e) It is possible to arrive at a collective final result, but the how still needs to be defined.**
This is where the M08 pilot handed off directly into the windows-debate reopening (§3) — the "how"
is exactly what that unfinished debate was building toward when it stopped.

## 3. The windows-debate reopening

Raised by the researcher directly: *"the windows debate is crucial... unfortunately the windows
debate steered into a few rabbit holes, but opening this up again is at the heart of defining the
shape of the analysis."* And, closing: *"the windows debate forms the backbone of the philosophy of

Full read-through this session of `iba/docs/windows debate/` (15 files, 2026-08-10 → 08-13, then
untouched until today). Summary of that read (chat-only until now, not previously filed anywhere):

- The register reached a clean baseline at **v2.3** (2026-08-12): 8 live windows (W1–W6, W8–W10;
  W7 body-act retired into T3-Operations), 7 calibration principles (C1–C7), 1 scaffolding

  defined — bite-structure was **explicitly parked**.
- A 17-document scan of past methodology work found the register's ~10 windows are **"a thin
  slice"** of an existing **126-question tier catalogue (T0–T7)** from the reset era — never
  deleted, reframed from classification-grid into "areas to cover." Reconciling the register
  against that catalogue was named as **"likely the single highest-value structural move"** — not
  yet done.
- The two load-bearing bite-structure questions (assembly method; join-key) were found **already
  substantially answered** by the old reset's synthesis-B spec — recognise-then-attach; join on
  relation signals (shared shape, binding-web, shared object-kind, pole-opposition, shared seat,
  cognate/root, adjacency); cluster on dense fields, never on absence or the bare lemma.

  found, richer than the register's current three-state C5.
- The **discovery-lookout** (the mechanism meant to surface new windows during analysis) was found
  to have gone dark — populated as filler, zero real entries.
- Five thin/absent windows named: **behaviour, physical↔inner correlation, the metaphysical,
  developmental arc, collective/social.**
- Forward plan agreed at close (2026-08-13): **Phase (a) — define and articulate every window**
  (drawing on the scan), then **Phase (b) — the "how" + clean-glass risk mitigation.** Nothing
  after 2026-08-13 exists in the folder. **Phase (a) was never started.**

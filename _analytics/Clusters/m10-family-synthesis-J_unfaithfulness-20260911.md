## Cross-strong synergy: this family is relational/covenantal, not individual-moral

Unlike the guilt subgroup (self-recognized) or the cruelty family (attributed to an external
adversary), this family's dominant shape is **betrayal of a bond** — and the same vocabulary is
used, without distinction, for a betrayed *human* relationship and a betrayed *covenant with God*:

- **H0898 (bagad, 28 verses)** runs both registers in the same small set of texts: "even your
  brothers... have dealt treacherously with you" (Jer 12:6, a family betrayal) sits alongside "the
  treacherous sister Judah" set against "faithless Israel" (Jer 3:8, 3:11 — the marriage-infidelity
  figure for covenant breach). Proverbs uses it structurally, as a fixed class opposed to "the
  upright," destroyed by their own conduct (Prov 11:3, 11:6, 13:15).
- **H4603/H4604 (ma'al, 30 verses combined)** is the most explicit: its own meaning block splits
  the root directly into "against man" and "against God" senses, and the verses bear this out one
  for one — Achan's sacrilege against devoted objects (Josh 7:1), a wife's suspected unfaithfulness
  under the jealousy ordeal (Num 5:27), and national apostasy/idolatry (2 Chr 26:16, 28:19, Ezek
  14:13, 15:8, 17:20, 39:26) sit in the same lexical family as ordinary interpersonal deception
  (Lev 6:2, "deceiving his neighbor"). **This term also feeds directly into the guilt-offering
  system** (Lev 5:15, 6:2 — the same institution documented in the guilt subgroup, #1680 v2): a
  ma'al-breach is precisely the kind of unfaithfulness the asham (guilt offering) exists to remedy.
- **G0646/G0763/G0764 (Greek)** shift register slightly — less about a specific broken bond, more
  a general orientation of impiety opposed to godliness as a category (Rom 1:18 pairs
  "ungodliness" with "unrighteousness"; Titus 2:12 opposes it to living "self-controlled, upright,
  and godly"). G0646 (apostasia) names departure from a founding standard (forsaking Mosaic
  custom, Acts 21:21; the eschatological "rebellion," 2 Thess 2:3) rather than a relational breach
  as such.
- **H5627 (sarah)** adds a distinct instigation sense: it names *speaking* rebellion, inciting
  others toward covenant abandonment (Deut 13:5's false prophet "taught rebellion"; Jer 28:16,
  29:32's prophets who "uttered/spoken rebellion against the LORD") — a verbal-agency variant not
  present in the other members.

## Cross-family note (not just internal to J)

The marriage-infidelity-for-covenant-breach structure this family shows in H0898/H4603 is the same
structure driving the C (adultery/sexual sin) family's Hosea/Jeremiah/Ezekiel 23 material — both
families independently land on the same metaphor. Treated as separate gloss-families, C and J
partly describe one and the same underlying figure from two lexical angles (adultery-vocabulary vs.
treachery-vocabulary). Worth flagging for #1680 as a case where the gloss-text grouping method
splits what the verses themselves treat as one figure.

## Major finding: this family is dominated by one root (avon) that duplicates work already seen in the transgression and guilt families

H5771 (avon), split here into three sub-codes by sense (crime/guilt/punishment), carries **95 of
this family's ~159 verses combined** — by far the largest single lexical presence found in any
family in this exercise except H4421 and H6588. Its behaviour is not distinct from what the
transgression family's H6588 (pesha) and the guilt subgroup already showed:

- **The identical three-register split.** H5771's own meaning block distinguishes "iniquity,"
  "guilt of iniquity," and "consequence/punishment of iniquity" — the same three-way structure
  H6588 (pesha, family G) used for "against individuals / nations / God" and the same
  recognition-accrual-remedy sequence the guilt subgroup (#1680 v2) already documented.
- **The identical "bear iniquity" idiom** running through Lev 5:1, 5:17, 17:16, 20:17, 20:19 —
  the same bearing-language already analysed under H5375J (nasa) in the guilt subgroup, here paired
  with avon instead of nasa's own object.
- **The same forgiveness-formula language**: "forgiving iniquity and transgression" (Num 14:18,
  Exod 34:7), "pardoning iniquity" (Mic 7:18), "blots out your transgressions... your sins like
  mist" (cross-referenced already in family G).
- **Isaiah 53:5 again** — "he was pierced for our transgressions; he was crushed for our
  iniquities" cites avon (H5771G) at the *same verse* already found under H0817 (guilt), H5375J
  (bearing), and H6588 (transgression). **This is now the fourth family independently converging
  on Isaiah 53** as a shared reference point across the M10 cluster's vocabulary.

## The one genuinely distinct sub-theme: unjust *gain*

H1215 (betsa, 17 verses) is a narrower, specifically economic form of injustice — bribery,
dishonest profit, corrupt religious leadership ("everyone is greedy for unjust gain; from prophet
to priest, everyone deals falsely," Jer 6:13, 8:10), and oppressive commerce (Ezek 22:27's "wolves
tearing the prey... to get dishonest gain"). This does not overlap with avon's pattern and is the
one part of this family that reads as its own concept.

## A structural pattern distinct to avel/avlah (H5766A/B): injustice as relapse, not starting condition

Ezekiel's repeated formula — "when a righteous person turns away from his righteousness and does
injustice... he shall die" (Ezek 3:20, 18:24, 18:26, 33:13, 33:18) — treats injustice specifically
as a *moral reversal* from a prior righteous state, not (as with guilt's recognition-model or
cruelty's other-attribution) a condition someone simply has or falls into. This sits alongside
God's own repeated self-description as one who "does no injustice" (Zeph 3:5, Deut 32:4, Ps 92:15)
as the fixed standard the reversal is measured against.

## Note for #1680

The scale of overlap here — one root (avon) independently reproducing the exact structural pattern
of two other already-surveyed families, at the same shared verse — is itself the headline finding.
Combined with G_transgression's cross-reference back to the guilt subgroup, this suggests avon,
pesha, and the guilt-subgroup's asham/nasa complex may not be four distillable "families" at all,
but four English-gloss windows onto one Hebrew moral-failure vocabulary cluster that itself treats
recognition, accrual, and remedy as one integrated idea. Isaiah 53 recurring as the convergence
point across all of them is worth surfacing as a standalone observation, independent of any single
family's write-up.

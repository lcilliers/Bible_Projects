## E. What the seat maps observed, as counts (approved, for the chapters as plain statements)

- **Where the angry person's own heart or spirit is named** (U1 §38 point 1, counted): in the narratives it is rare (2Ki 6:11; Est 5:9; Act 7:54; Act 17:16; Eze 3:14). In the wisdom books it is frequent (Job 36:13; Pro 6:14; 14:29; 16:32; 19:3; Ecc 2:23; 7:3; 7:9; 11:10). The narratives show anger by what is done. **Kept as a count, without U1's "so that" clause** (rule 69).
- **In the narratives, the seat is often named by someone else** (U1 §38 point 3): Eliab names David's heart (1Sa 17:28); Jesus' anger is at his opponents' hardened heart (Mar 3:5).
- **The heart in seven roles** under God's anger (U2 §65 point 3), each a verse (MS-08 to MS-11).

**OT-18 resolution.** Of the 70 verses (65 lexical, the two seat maps, and part M), 45 carry a genuine human seat. Every one is placed above, held (MS-28), or is life-sense or spurious per the seat maps. **OT-18 is resolved** when the chapter edits below are made.

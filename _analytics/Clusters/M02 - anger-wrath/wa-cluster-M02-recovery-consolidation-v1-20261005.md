## 2. The consolidation: what the roll-up holds

The data file gives every item. Here is its shape.

**Coverage is complete at the verse level.**
- 681 verses and 919 hits in the 65 live M02 Strong's.
- Every hit has a face (134 faces), and every face sits in an approved ledger section with AG rows (240).
- Every verse has a U1/U2 phenomenon (99 codes; 41 verses carry no anger sense).

**Three layers hold the findings, and none of them holds all of it.**

| Layer | Holds | Not held by the 5 Oct roll-up |
|---|---|---|
| U1 and U2 (Sept) | per phenomenon: what it is, what it does, where it comes from, how it co-exists, what it leads to, the **seat** | **87 seat lines and 190 movement lines** cited in no later file (D3a, D3b) |
| 1 Oct ledger (AN rows) | the U1/U2 reading turned into observations and **woven into the chapters** | **61 AN rows** cited in no 5 Oct file (D2) |
| 5 Oct ledgers (AG rows by face) | every hit faced; meaning, implication and stated chain per row; parties (ruling 6.2) | U1/U2 seat and context lines; the 1 Oct rows above |
| 5 Oct overview (draft) | parts A–H, appendix I | **172 AG rows not cited** (T4); part F incomplete (T5) |

**D1** therefore lists the three layers side by side, face by face. That makes it the consolidation the overview should have been: no AG row, AN row or U1/U2 phenomenon is left out.

**What was carried after all.** The first test (T3) flagged 22 phenomena whose verses appear in no AG row's verse column. A second pass searched the full ledger text: **all 22 are carried** in face tables or AG row text. The loss is in each section's *context*: seat lines and where-from/leads-to lines. D3 lists those lines in full. Examples:
- **Zion's lament (U2 §48)**: the body, the hunger, "pour out your heart like water" (Lam 2:19), "lift up our hearts" (3:41), and the hope in the middle (3:20–33) are not in the 5 Oct ledgers. They are in AN-67, which is woven in Ch 13.
- **Insolence (U1 §30)**: Jer 48:29 "the haughtiness of his heart" and Hos 7:14 "they do not cry to me from the heart" are carried nowhere (D3a).
- **The seer under the indignation (U2 §54)**: Dan 8:17–18 (fear, collapse, being raised) and 11:35 (the wise refined) are carried nowhere (D3b).
- **Explaining suffering (U2 §25)**: Job 21:19, 21:14, 42:6 and 20:28 are carried nowhere (D3b).

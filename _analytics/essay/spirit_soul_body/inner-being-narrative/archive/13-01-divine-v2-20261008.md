# 13.1 Divine

This section gathers what the verses say of God in his own right, as each part of the book is taken up again. So far it holds what the verses on a person's choosing say of God. What his part means for the one who chooses is in 10.7.

## Choosing

The verses on a person's choosing keep speaking of God. They speak of him in his own right, not only as the one a person chooses.

**He chooses.** The verb used of a person's choosing is also used of his:
- **a place:** "the place that the Lord your God will choose out of all your tribes to put his name and make his habitation there" (Deuteronomy 12:5)
- **people, for a purpose:** "God made a choice among you, that by my mouth the Gentiles should hear the word of the gospel and believe" (Acts 15:7)
- **an answer to people's choosing:** "I also will choose harsh treatment for them and bring their fears upon them, because when I called, no one answered, when I spoke, they did not listen" (Isaiah 66:4). The verse gives the reason for his choice.

**He has eyes and delight, and they are the measure of a choice.** The same verse goes on: "but they did what was evil in my eyes and chose that in which I did not delight" (Isaiah 66:4). Its opposite is those "who choose the things that please me" (56:4).

**He acts first.** At Shechem he speaks of what he has done before anyone chooses: "I took your father Abraham from beyond the River" (Joshua 24:3); "I gave you a land on which you had not labored" (24:13). In Isaiah he offers himself to people who did not look for him:
- "I was ready to be sought by those who did not ask for me" (Isaiah 65:1)
- "I said, 'Here I am, here I am,' to a nation that was not called by my name" (65:1)
- "I spread out my hands all the day to a rebellious people" (65:2)

**He is good, upright and holy.** His instructing comes from his goodness: "Good and upright is the Lord; therefore he instructs sinners in the way" (Psalm 25:8). "The friendship of the Lord is for those who fear him" (25:14). Joshua tells the people who have just chosen him what he is: "he is a holy God. He is a jealous God" (Joshua 24:19).

**He knows the heart, and gives.**
- "God, who knows the heart, bore witness to them, by giving them the Holy Spirit just as he did to us" (Acts 15:8).
- "thanks be to God, who put into the heart of Titus the same earnest care I have for you" (2 Corinthians 8:16).
- The church's decision is spoken as one with the Spirit's: "it has seemed good to the Holy Spirit and to us" (Acts 15:28). The Holy Spirit is the subject of a separate study.

**He reasons, answers and judges.**
- He invites a people to reason with him: "Come now, let us reason together, says the Lord" (Isaiah 1:18).
- He answers a person's verdict on himself. The eunuch said "Behold, I am a dry tree" (56:3), and the Lord says "I will give them an everlasting name that shall not be cut off" (56:5).
- He gives the last word on those who weighed Job's choices: "you have not spoken of me what is right, as my servant Job has" (Job 42:7).

# 10.7 Right and wrong

When Scripture judges a person, it looks first at the heart. The heart is upright or crooked, pure or deceitful, whole or double, proud or humble. God "weighs the heart" (Proverbs 21:2). The soul is the one who answers for what is done: "the soul who sins shall die". The spirit is judged mainly on pride and humility: haughty or lowly, crushed or contrite. Chapter 12 takes this further.

Thoughts are part of this. They are judged: "the word of God … discerning the thoughts and intentions of the heart" (Hebrews 4:12). They also do some of the judging themselves: "their conscience also bears witness, and their conflicting thoughts accuse or even excuse them" (Romans 2:15). And what I reckon to be wrong binds my conscience (Romans 14:14; Chapter 7).

**Connections:** with thinking (10.3); with conscience (Chapter 7); into what goes wrong (Chapter 12).

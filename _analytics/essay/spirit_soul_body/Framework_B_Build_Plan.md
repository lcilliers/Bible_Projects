## 6. Principles and Guardrails

**6.1 Framework Separation**

Framework A (Holy Spirit) and Framework B (Spirit, Soul, Body) are SEPARATE outputs. They must never be merged into a single document. Cross-references are made by acknowledgement, not by incorporation. Chapter 8 (Spirit’s fruit) and Chapter 10 (transformation) reference Framework A findings but present them from Framework B’s anthropological perspective.

**6.2 Research Integrity**

All findings are built directly from biblical texts and source documents. No findings are invented or assumed. Where there are multiple theological perspectives (e.g., dichotomy vs. trichotomy), both are presented fairly before the data-driven conclusion is stated. Greek and Hebrew definitions are verified. Psychology comparisons acknowledge convergences and divergences without subordinating Scripture to science or vice versa.

**6.3 First-Person Narrative Style**

The output follows the established style: first-person research narrative documenting a journey of discovery. Not academic detachment but engaged investigation. Biblical and historical references are preserved throughout. The tone is that of a researcher sharing genuine findings with genuine excitement about genuine discoveries.

**6.4 Session Discipline**

Each session focuses on its defined scope. No session attempts to do more than one part. Each deliverable is a standalone document that can be reviewed independently. The approval gate between sessions prevents cascading errors. If a session’s deliverable requires revision, it is revised before proceeding — not patched later.

**6.5 The February 16, 2026 Chat Exclusion**

The chat titled ‘Comprehensive study validation check’ (February 16, 2026) remains INVALID. All documents from that chat must not be used. It wrongly merged Framework A and Framework B. This exclusion continues to apply throughout the build process.

### PART 1: HEART (LEV/KARDIA)

#### A. Hebrew: לֵב (lev) / Greek: καρδία (kardia)

**Primary meaning:** The physical heart, but overwhelmingly used metaphorically for the inner person

**Semantic range:**

- Seat of emotions (joy, sorrow, fear, courage)

- Center of thought/understanding (wise heart, understanding heart)

- Locus of will/decision (hardened heart, willing heart)

- Moral center (pure heart, evil heart)

- Inner person/core self (what man looks at, God looks at heart - 1Sa 16:7)

#### B. Relationship to Soul and Spirit

**Heart overlaps significantly with BOTH soul and spirit:**

**Heart + Soul together:**

- Deu 6:5 - 'with all your HEART and with all your SOUL'

- Deu 10:12, 11:13, 13:3, 26:16, 30:2, 30:6, 30:10 - repeated formula

- Jos 23:14 - 'in your HEARTS and SOULS'

- Pattern: Heart and soul often paired for emphasis on totality

**Heart + Spirit together:**

- Psa 51:10 - 'Create in me a clean HEART, O God, and renew a right SPIRIT within me'

- Eze 11:19 - 'I will give them one HEART, and a new SPIRIT I will put within them'

- Eze 18:31 - 'get yourselves a new HEART and a new SPIRIT'

- Eze 36:26 - 'I will give you a new HEART, and a new SPIRIT I will put within you'

- Pattern: Heart and spirit paired for renewal/transformation

#### C. Analysis: What Is Heart?

**Three interpretations:**

- **View 1: Heart = Soul** (dichotomy with synonymous terms) - Just another name for inner person

- **View 2: Heart = Spirit** (dichotomy, heart emphasizes different aspect) - Moral/volitional center

- **View 3: Heart = Integration point** (trichotomy) - Where soul and spirit meet, the unified center of personhood

Most likely: View 3. Heart represents the integrated whole of the immaterial person - encompassing thought, emotion, will, and moral choice. It's not a separate entity but the experiential unity of soul and spirit working together.

### PART 2: MIND (NOUS/DIANOIA)

#### A. Greek Terms for Mind

**νοῦς (nous):** Mind, intellect, understanding, faculty of thought

**διάνοια (dianoia):** Understanding, imagination, mind as thinking faculty

Note: Hebrew lacks a precise equivalent - uses lev (heart) for cognitive functions. 'Mind' is more of a Greek concept.

#### B. Mind in Compound Passages

**Matthew 22:37:**

You shall love the Lord your God with all your HEART and with all your SOUL and with all your MIND.

**Critical observation:**

- Three terms: heart, soul, mind

- No mention of spirit (though parallel in Mark 12:30 adds 'strength')

- Jesus adding 'mind' to Deuteronomy 6:5 - emphasizing intellectual love

**Mark 12:30:**

You shall love the Lord your God with all your HEART and with all your SOUL and with all your MIND and with all your STRENGTH.

- Four terms: heart, soul, mind, strength

- Emphasizes complete devotion - every aspect of personhood

#### C. Mind's Relationship to Soul/Spirit

**Romans 7:23-25 - Mind vs Flesh:**

But I see in my members another law waging war against the law of my MIND... with my MIND I serve the law of God, but with my FLESH I serve the law of sin.

- Mind opposed to flesh (like spirit vs flesh)

- Mind serves God's law - similar to spirit's God-ward orientation

**1 Corinthians 14:14-15:**

For if I pray in a tongue, my SPIRIT prays but my MIND is unfruitful. What am I to do? I will pray with my SPIRIT, but I will pray with my MIND also.

- Spirit and mind can function separately

- Spirit = intuitive, direct communion; Mind = rational, understanding

- Both needed for complete worship

**Conclusion:** Mind (nous) appears to be the rational/cognitive function of either soul or spirit (or both). It's not a separate entity but a faculty - the thinking, reasoning, understanding capacity of the immaterial person.

### PART 3: BREATH (NESHAMAH)

#### A. Hebrew: נְשָׁמָה (neshamah)

**Primary meaning:** Breath, especially the breath of life from God

Distinct from ruach: While ruach can mean wind/breath/spirit, neshamah specifically emphasizes the physical act of breathing and the divine gift of life-breath.

#### B. Key Passages

**Genesis 2:7:**

Then the LORD God formed the man of dust from the ground and breathed into his nostrils the BREATH (neshamah) of life, and the man became a living being (nephesh chayyah).

- NESHAMAH (breath) from God → creates NEPHESH (living soul)

- Breath is the divine input, soul is the result

**Genesis 7:22:**

Everything on the dry land in whose nostrils was the BREATH (neshamah) of life died.

- Animals also have neshamah - the physical life-breath

#### C. Relationship to Soul and Spirit

**Neshamah appears to be:**

- **Physical:** The literal breath in nostrils

- **Divine gift:** From God, animates the body

- **Creates nephesh:** When combined with body

- **Shared with animals:** Both humans and animals have it

Tentative model: Neshamah (breath) may be the physical expression or conduit of ruach (spirit). Spirit becomes breath becomes soul. Body + Breath (from Spirit) = Soul.

### PART 4: COMPOUND PASSAGES - MULTIPLE TERMS TOGETHER

These passages are crucial for understanding how the biblical authors viewed the relationships between these concepts.

#### A. The Great Trichotomy Verse - 1 Thessalonians 5:23

Now may the God of peace himself sanctify you completely, and may your whole SPIRIT and SOUL and BODY be kept blameless at the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ.

**Critical analysis:**

- **Three distinct parts listed:** spirit AND soul AND body

- **Order:** Spirit first (highest), then soul (middle), then body (physical)

- **Sanctification of all three:** Complete holiness involves spirit, soul, AND body

- **Your WHOLE (ὁλόκληρον):** Emphasizes complete, entire person - all three together

- **Strongest biblical evidence for trichotomy:** If spirit and soul were identical, why list both?

#### B. The Sword Divides - Hebrews 4:12

For the word of God is living and active, sharper than any two-edged sword, piercing to the DIVISION OF SOUL AND OF SPIRIT, of joints and of marrow, and discerning the thoughts and intentions of the HEART.

**Critical analysis:**

- **Division (μερισμοῦ):** Separation, partition, distribution - implies they can be distinguished

- **Parallel:** soul/spirit like joints/marrow - distinct but intimately connected parts

- **Requires divine word:** So closely united that only God's word can divide them

- **Plus heart:** Adds 'thoughts and intentions of heart' - integrated center

- **Implication:** Soul and spirit are distinguishable but normally function as one

#### C. The Great Commandment - Matthew 22:37 / Mark 12:30

**Matthew 22:37:**

You shall love the Lord your God with all your HEART and with all your SOUL and with all your MIND.

**Mark 12:30:**

You shall love the Lord your God with all your HEART and with all your SOUL and with all your MIND and with all your STRENGTH.

**Analysis:**

- **Based on Deuteronomy 6:5:** 'with all your HEART and with all your SOUL and with all your MIGHT'

- **Jesus adds MIND:** Emphasizing intellectual devotion (Greek concept)

- **Mark adds STRENGTH:** Physical capacity, whole being

- **No SPIRIT mentioned:** Interesting absence - perhaps spirit implied in worship context?

- **Purpose:** Total devotion with every aspect - not defining anthropology but emphasizing completeness

#### D. Hannah's Prayer - 1 Samuel 1:10-15

She was deeply distressed and prayed to the LORD... But Hannah answered, 'I am a woman troubled in SPIRIT. I have drunk neither wine nor strong drink, but I have been pouring out my SOUL before the LORD.'

**Analysis:**

- Troubled in SPIRIT (v.15a)

- Pouring out SOUL (v.15b)

- Same distress described with both terms

- Suggests: Both present together in emotional/spiritual distress

#### E. Renewal Passages - Ezekiel

**Ezekiel 11:19:**

And I will give them one HEART, and a new SPIRIT I will put within them. I will remove the HEART of stone from their flesh and give them a HEART of flesh.

**Ezekiel 36:26:**

And I will give you a new HEART, and a new SPIRIT I will put within you. And I will remove the HEART of stone from your flesh and give you a HEART of flesh.

- New HEART and new SPIRIT - both needed for renewal

- SPIRIT put 'within' - interior placement

- Suggests: Heart and spirit are related but distinguishable aspects needing transformation

### PART 5: COMPREHENSIVE ANTHROPOLOGICAL MODEL

Based on all four documents, here is the most comprehensive biblical model of human nature:

#### The Trinitarian Structure

| Component | Origin | Nature | Functions | Destiny |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SPIRIT (Ruach/Pneuma) | Given/formed by God (Zec 12:1) | Ethereal, wind-like, divine gift | God-consciousness, worship, spiritual communion | Returns to God (Ecc 12:7) |
| SOUL (Nephesh/Psychē) | Man becomes nephesh (Gen 2:7) | Life-force, tied to blood | Emotions, desires, will, personhood, sin | Departs, to Sheol or heaven |
| BODY (Basar/Soma) | Formed from dust (Gen 2:7) | Physical, material, flesh | Physical actions, senses, appetites | Returns to dust (Ecc 12:7) |

#### Supplementary Elements

- **HEART (lev/kardia):** Integration point - where spirit and soul meet in unified experience of thought, emotion, and will

- **MIND (nous/dianoia):** Cognitive faculty - rational/intellectual function of soul or spirit

- **BREATH (neshamah):** Physical expression of spirit - the animating life-breath from God

#### The Creation Process (Genesis 2:7 Reconstructed)

- 1. God forms BODY from dust

- 2. God breathes NESHAMAH (breath of life) - physical expression of RUACH (spirit)

- 3. Body + Breath/Spirit → Man BECOMES NEPHESH (living soul)

- 4. HEART emerges as integrated center of personhood

**Formula:** BODY (earth) + SPIRIT (from God) = SOUL (living person)

### PART 6: THEOLOGICAL CONCLUSIONS

#### 1. Trichotomy Is Biblically Supported

The evidence overwhelmingly supports a three-part anthropology:

- **Explicit statement:** 1Th 5:23 lists all three - 'spirit and soul and body'

- **Different origins:** Spirit given, soul emerges, body formed

- **Different characteristics:** Soul sins/spirit doesn't, animals have souls/not spirits

- **Different destinations:** Spirit to God, body to dust, soul to Sheol or heaven

- **Can be divided:** Heb 4:12 - word divides soul and spirit

#### 2. But Not Radically Separated

Soul and spirit are distinct but intimately united:

- Extensive functional overlap (worship, emotion, personhood)

- Used interchangeably in many contexts

- Both present together in heaven (Rev 6:9, Heb 12:23)

- No clear example of actual separation from each other

Best metaphor: Two aspects of one immaterial reality, like two sides of a coin or joints and marrow in bone - distinguishable but inseparable.

#### 3. The Soul-Spirit Relationship

**Hierarchical model:**

- **SPIRIT:** The primary eternal essence from God - God-consciousness, spiritual capacity

- **SOUL:** The result of spirit + body union - personhood, emotions, will, life-force

- **BODY:** The material vessel - physical form and function

Soul may be the interface or meeting point between spirit and body - not a third separate entity but the experiential reality of incarnate spirit.

#### 4. Humans vs Animals

**The critical distinction:**

- **Both have:** Body, soul (nephesh), breath of life

- **Only humans have:** Spirit (ruach) as God-consciousness, image of God

- **Result:** Animals are conscious, living beings but lack spiritual dimension and moral accountability

#### 5. Sin and Salvation

The soul is the locus of sin and moral responsibility:

- The SOUL sins (Eze 18:4, 20)

- The SOUL needs purification (1Pet 1:22)

- The SOUL can be saved or lost (Jam 5:20, Mat 16:26)

- The SPIRIT needs renewal but is fundamentally oriented toward God

- Both need sanctification for complete holiness (1Th 5:23)

#### 6. Death and Afterlife

**At death, a separation occurs:**

- **Body:** Returns to dust, awaits resurrection

- **Spirit:** Returns to God (Ecc 12:7) - commended to Father (Luk 23:46)

- **Soul:** Departs (Gen 35:18) to Sheol or heaven, conscious existence (Rev 6:9)

- **Result:** Believers' souls and spirits united in God's presence, awaiting bodily resurrection

### FINAL CONCLUSION

After analyzing over 1,300 verses across four comprehensive documents, the biblical witness points clearly to a trichotomous anthropology:

**Body (Soma/Basar):**

Physical form from earth, mortal, returns to dust, will be resurrected

**Soul (Psychē/Nephesh):**

Emergent life-force and personhood from spirit + body union, seat of emotions/desires/will/moral agency, can sin, shared with animals at biological level, survives death consciously

**Spirit (Pneuma/Ruach):**

Divine gift directly from God, eternal essence, God-consciousness faculty, cannot sin, uniquely human, returns to God at death

**The five-century-old hypothesis is confirmed:** Soul and spirit are distinct entities with different origins, characteristics, and possibly different immediate destinations at death, though they remain intimately united throughout life and eternity.

## 2. Causes (my account)

1. **Weave was done as placement.** Each ledger observation was added as bullets under an existing heading. The surrounding text was not rethought.
   - The weave rule (#1918) says the *narrative adjusts* to the new item.
   - In practice the narrative only grew longer. It now duplicates the ledger.
2. **"Let Scripture speak" was over-applied.** The 2026-10-01 ruling forbids joining verses into patterns, mechanisms or divisions that no verse states.
   - I let it also remove interpretation of what a verse means in its own setting.
   - Ten more connecting phrases were cut in unit 2. Some cuts were right. Together they leave a one-line opening followed by a run of quotes.
3. **"Prose supported by bullets" was inverted.** The ruling (#1924) has the prose carry the point, with bullets for its different applications. In the new sections the bullets carry everything, and the prose only introduces them.

## 3. The line to be ruled on

| Level | What it is | Status now |
|---|---|---|
| (a) | What a verse says: the quote | Allowed |
| (b) | What the verse means in its own setting: the speaker, the situation, and what it says about how the inner being works there | **Allowed by style guide §1.3 ("restates or paraphrases what the cited text itself says"), but largely missing from the text** |
| (c) | Joining verses into a pattern, mechanism, cycle or "what this shows" that no verse states | Forbidden (2026-10-01; cfg_behaviour_rule 69) |

**Question for the researcher:** is (b) what you mean by "meaning and interpretation"? If so, how far may it go? Two examples from 10.9:
- Exo 20:20 ("Do not fear … that the fear of him may be before you"). May the text say that the verse itself distinguishes a fear that is removed from a fear that is put in place, and that the second is for not sinning?
- Isa 66:2 ("trembles at my word"). May the text say that the verse puts trembling at God's word beside being humble and contrite, as the person God looks to?

## 4. Proposal

1. **The narrative carries meaning in prose.** Each point is stated in plain words as level (b), supported by one to three chosen verses.
2. **The ledger keeps the full verse lists.** The narrative does not repeat them.
3. **A trial first, on one section:** 10.9 "Fear before God, and the fear of God" is rewritten this way, as a separate draft for comparison with v6. No other chapter is touched until the researcher has seen it.
4. **If the trial is approved:**
   - rework the sections the fear strand inflated (10.1, 10.9, 10.10, 13, 15) and the anger sections, which have the same shape
   - write the rule into style guide v3 (§1 and §7), so the levels are fixed in config, not memory
5. **Unit 3 of the fear strand waits** until this is settled.

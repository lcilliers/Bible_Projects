## 1. The ruling (researcher, verbatim, chat 2026-10-07)

> the analysis of 'choosing' in the past few rounds highlighted an important modification towards the narration.   we need to think differently, and articulate the observations accordingly, around characteristics performed by God.  The study and narration primary focus in the human inner being. The actions of God are only analysed in so far as defining / impacting the HIB.  However, God's actions in its own right helps to define these actions is so far as God has created HIB in his image. The verses therefore , when looking at the underlying characteristics, need to be interpreted and routed in different directions and should be described in the narrative from different angles.  It seems that the verses that capture different aspects of Gods character must be collated in a separate chapter.  These verse analysis will have a different focus on analysis - to understand the character of God, and how his character trancended to HIB through creation.  verses about God interacting with HIB is fully in scope of the HIB and would follow the analytic pattern and scope of analysis of the the HIB related activity.

## 2. What the ruling sets (restated, for checking)

1. **The study's primary focus stays the human inner being (HIB).**
2. **A verse in which God acts is routed by what it shows:**

   | Route | The verse shows | Where it goes | Focus of the analysis |
   | --- | --- | --- | --- |
   | **A. God's character** | an aspect of God's own character, in its own right | a **separate chapter** | understand the character of God, and how his character passed to the HIB through creation (made in his image) |
   | **B. God with the HIB** | God interacting with a person's inner being: acting on it, toward it, with it | the HIB chapters, as now | the full HIB analytic pattern and scope |

3. **The same verse may take both routes**, described "from different angles".
4. **Proposed reading (marked, not stated in the ruling): route C.** A verse that shows neither God's character nor any bearing on the HIB stays in the data, not the narrative. This follows "only analysed in so far as defining / impacting the HIB" and the existing rule that a verse saying nothing about the inner being stays in the data.

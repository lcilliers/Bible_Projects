## Observed structure (method §C — observe, don't impose) — INTERPRETATION (labelled)

The field does **not** present fear's two-pole (dread/reverence) shape. "Blessing" as bundled in registry 158 collapses several distinct Hebrew/Greek concept-families into one English word. From the evidence the natural groupings are **relational directions + a somatic root + a reversal hinge**, not opposing poles:

1. **Blessing given** — inner being invokes good upon another (barak/eulogeō): person→person, →offspring/collective, →enemy (Rom 12:14; 1Pe 3:9).
2. **Blessing directed upward** — inner being blesses God (barak/eulogeō/berakh): Eph 1:3; 2Sa 18:28; Dan 2:20; Dan 4:34 (reason returns → he blesses).
3. **Blessing received / being called-blessed** — inner being as object of favour, declared happy/fortunate (makarios/esher/ashar/eulogētos): Beatitudes; Psa 2:12; others "call blessed" (Pro 31:28; Song 6:9; Job 29:11).
4. **Somatic root — kneeling** — barak's root is bodily submission (kneel/knelt; Dan 6:10 "got down on his knees"): interior→body edge, worship posture.
5. **Blessing as gift / benefit / prosperity** — blessing objectified (berakhah "present/peace/prosperity"; charisma; charis benefit): inner disposition → outward object (2Ki 5:15).
6. **Favor / grace as source-side** — the disposition in the one who blesses (charis/charitoō): Luk 1:28; Eph 1:6.
7. **Social bond — greeting** — barak = greet/salute (1Sa 25:14; 2Ki 10:15).

**Hinge (method §E.13):** barak pivots on object/intent — bless ↔ curse (euphemistic): Job 1:11; 2:9; 1Ki 21:10.
**Negation / undone (method §E.13):** blessing withheld/reversed — Mal 2:2 "I will curse your blessings."

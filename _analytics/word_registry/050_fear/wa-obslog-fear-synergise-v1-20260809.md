- **Interface / dual-sense terms (straddle both poles):** phobeo (G5399), phobos (G5401), yare-family, pachad, tromos/entromos — "fear and trembling" co-occurring with both terror and reverence (Eph 6:5).

- **Fear negated / cast out:** aphobos "without fear" (Phil 1:14); "perfect love casts out fear… fear has to do with punishment" (1Jo 4:18); "a spirit not of fear but of power and love and self-control" (2Ti 1:7, deilia); the "Fear not" formula (Rev 1:17; Isa 44:8; Jos 1:9).
- **Fear as motive/cause of action:** "feared the Jews" → concealment (Joh 9:22); acted "from fear that…" (Jos 22:24); "in reverent fear constructed an ark" (Heb 11:7, eulabeomai) → reverent fear producing obedient action.

## OBSERVATION — sources, targets, applications visible in sampled verses

- **Sources / causes:** the Lord (presence, deeds, name, word, wrath); other humans (a king's terror, enemies, authorities); situations (earthquake, sea/storm, war, death, judgment); the holy/numinous (awe at a holy place, Gen 28:17); one's own sin/conscience (Felix alarmed, Act 24:25).
- **Targets / impacts:** heart (melts, troubled, in anguish); body (trembling, bones shake); behaviour (flight, concealment, silence, worship, obedience, submission, honour/respect); will/faith (impediment to faith **or** producing faith-obedience).
- **Positive applications:** fear of the Lord → reverence, worship, obedience (midwives, Exo 1:17), honour/respect owed (Rom 13:7), reverent submission (Eph 5:21).
- **Negative applications:** paralysing terror / melting; cowardice excluded (Rev 21:8); fear as impediment to faith/love; fear driving concealment, flight, sin.

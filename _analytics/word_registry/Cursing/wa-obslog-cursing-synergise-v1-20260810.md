## Observed structure (method §C — observe, don't impose)

Cursing does **not** present the clean two-pole shape fear had. From the evidence it presents as a **family of speech/disposition acts directed against a target**, with:

- a **core**: the pronounced/imprecatory curse (binds a target to doom);
- a **self-directed form**: the self-imprecatory oath (oath↔curse **hinge** — same vocabulary, distinguished by object/direction and conditionality);
- a **degradation form**: revile / blaspheme / speak-evil (God-ward and human-ward);
- a **diminishment form**: make-light / contempt (qalal — to lower worth; mirror of honour/weight);
- a **paired opposite**: blessing — which also serves as a **euphemistic inversion** ("bless" written, "curse God" meant);
- a **negation / undoing**: the curse removed (Rev 22:3) or borne/redeemed (Gal 3:13).

**Spine (method §9) — provisional, extract-level:** cursing originates in the inner being (heart / soul disposition — bitterness, greed, defiance, contempt, or covenantal authority), is enacted through the word/tongue, and is directed at a target (God / another / self / land / object) either to **bind it to harm/doom** or to **lower its worth**; with a mirror movement (blessing) and a reversal movement (removal / redemption). Inner-being anchoring visible in sample verses: Psa 10:3 (soul/*nephesh* as source), Psa 10:7 & Rom 3:14 (mouth as outlet, heart as reservoir), Psa 109:18 (curse penetrating the body), Jam 3:9–10 (one heart-source, two outputs).

# Study close — 2026-06-30

A plain record of where this effort ended, and why — so it reads as a finished thought, not a sentence cut off.

## What was attempted
A structured study of the **inner being of mankind as Scripture expresses it** (the Soul Word Analysis Programme). Most recently, the **verse-fanout / focus-point method**: start from a verse, fan out to all related evidence, and digest it into inner-being observations across dimensions (D1–D13), letting focus points emerge.

## This session (2026-06-26 → 30)
Worked **Exo 1:13** in depth — its inner-being movements (ruthlessness `perek`, enslavement `abad`) — and fanned out across Lev 25:43, Gen 6:5, Jer 17:9, Mark 7:21-23, Gen 20:11, Deu 25:18, Gen 49:7, Ecc 8:9, Exo 1:8. Onboarded orphan terms (`perek`, `a.qov`, `oqvah`, `anash`, `arar`). Built the generated control surface (`_STATE.md`) and integrity controls (completeness, hygiene, out-of-corpus). Added the D13 Cohabitation dimension. The researcher reviewed every verse and found many corrective actions — most of them the AI over-reading or mis-filing.

## What was learned (honestly)
1. **Accumulation is not synthesis.** The method produces *decomposition* — per-verse dimensioned observations. A pile of them, however large, does not become a *whole*, a story of how the inner being operates. Repeated over 23,000 verses it would be a conglomerate of data points, and no one would be the wiser.
2. **AI does not hold a discipline durably.** It holds for a while, then drifts back to over-reading, importing, confirming. This session proved it against itself (the cruelty/ruthlessness import, #43–#45). This is the root cause of the repeated restart / methodology-change / rework cycles — every one assumed an adherence that never came. *No method that depends on AI self-discipline can stand.*
3. **The synthesis is a human act of testimony.** The picture of how the inner being operates carries a moral weight and a voice that is the author's. The researcher's own sentence — *"to the point of enslaving and even death… a heart filled with evil… without any regard and fear of God, who holds all power"* — said in one breath what the assistant's clinically-correct paragraph could not. The whole was never a dataset to assemble; it had to be **seen and said** by the one walking it. That is why it kept slipping the method.
4. **Conclusion:** the objective as envisaged five months ago will not materialise by this approach. That is a real finding, not merely a defeat.

## The decision
The researcher chose to **move on** (2026-06-30).

## The researcher's closing reflection (verbatim)
> "The journey of life is a journey a human being can only walk on his own. AI cannot walk the journey for him. AI has a role to play — maybe overstated at this stage — but AI cannot walk the journey of life."

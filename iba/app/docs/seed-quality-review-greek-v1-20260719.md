## Headline

- **The current basis is registry-derived, so it is incomplete and _inconsistent_.** The clearest
  proof: Hebrew "thanks" **is** in (H3034 *yadah*, H8426 *todah*), but Greek "thanks"
  **G2168 / G2169** *eucharisteo* is **out**. Same concept, opposite treatment — because the net
  followed registry coverage, not meaning.
- **Greek misses found: ~75 lemmas**, in coherent semantic families (below). Not a long tail of
  edge cases — whole dispositions are absent.
- **Root cause is structural, not a typo:** (1) coverage was inherited from the registry, which is
  itself incomplete; (2) membership is judged at the **lemma**, but inner-being-ness lives at the
  **sense** (e.g. "clean" = moral purity → IB, ritual/physical cleanness → not; both are one lemma).
  A cleaner lemma list cannot resolve a sense split.

## Noise in the current candidate list (false positives to drop)

Read across all 1,732 candidates, the clear non-inner-being inclusions fall in tight classes:

- **Proper names** — H2256 "Mahalab" · H3819 "No Mercy" (Lo-ruhamah) · H4036 "Terror on Every Side"
  (Magor-missabib) · H7854 "Satan" · (H7067 "Jealous [God]" — divine epithet, arguable).
- **Objects / things** — G2787 "ark" · G2375 "long shield" · G3833 "complete armor" · H8615 "cord"
  (the *tiqvah* "hope" sense is IB, "cord" is not — a sense split) · H8438 "worm" · H2459 "fat".
- **External persons** — G3027 "robber/rebel" · G4204 "prostitute" · G4205 "sexual sinner" ·
  H6945/H6948 "cult prostitute" (the *act* lust/fornication is IB; the *person* is not).
- **Generic physical / motion verbs** pulled in by a related-word net — e.g. to go, run, stand, fly,
  roll, pour, wash, throw, cast, find, make/do. Inner-being only in specific idioms (turn = repent,
  cleave = devotion); as bare lemmas they are noise. **This class needs its own review pass** — it
  is scattered through the Hebrew candidates especially, and I have not enumerated every one here.

## The sense-split problem (your "clean" example)

Some lemmas are *rightly* in for one sense and *wrongly* in for another — the same row cannot say
both. Confirmed examples already in the candidate list:

- **clean / pure** (H2643, H1249, H2889, H2892…) — moral purity = IB; ritual/physical cleanness = not.
- **flesh** (H1320 *basar*, G4561 *sarx*) — *sarx* as the sinful nature = IB; literal flesh/body = not.
- **cord / hope** (H8615 *tiqvah*) — one gloss "cord", the IB sense is "hope".
- **strength / soldiers** (H2428 *chayil*) — inner strength = IB; a body of troops = not.

These are not fixable by editing the lemma list; they mark where the seed must eventually carry a
**sense/grain** key, not just a lemma. (This is the same root as the "thanks" miss: coverage and
membership both need to be judged at the sense, read from the text, not inherited from the registry.)

  checkable unit, and it lets you confirm the criterion before I spend the larger effort on Hebrew.

## The genuinely-tiny residual drops

After the above, the only lemmas with no plausible inner-being sense in any form/figure/agency are
**pure geography** (Mahalab, the Baal-place-compounds, etc.) and **pure individual human personal
names** (Abraham, Moses…). Even the symbolic characteristic-names (Magor-missabib "terror on every
side", Lo-ruhamah "no mercy") *encode a disposition* and are defensible to seed — flagged for your
call, not dropped unilaterally.

## Two boundary calls that materially change scope (your ruling)

1. **Human external adversaries** — do the *role* words **enemy** (*oyeb* H0341), **foe/adversary**
   (*tsar* / *tsorer*), **oppressor** come in too? They also act on the inner being (they provoke
   fear, hatred, the cry for vindication), but the earlier Psalms method demoted "enemy" as external.
   My recommendation, consistent with the agent principle + over-inclusion: **lean IN** as
   relational-agent role-words (not the specific human proper names). ~15–25 lemmas turn on this.
2. **Bare divine NAMES** (YHWH H3068, El/Elohim H0410/H0430, Most High H5945, Almighty H7706) — God's
   *dispositions* (jealous, gracious, holy…) are already in; do you also want the bare divine names
   seeded, or are those the **arena** (Screen 0's "God-interaction = arena")? Recommendation: **leave
   the bare names out** (arena), keep every divine *attribute/disposition* in.

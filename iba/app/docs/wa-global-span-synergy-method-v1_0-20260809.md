## A. Before starting

1. Complete the global-rules startup gate and comply with **global rules [current]** throughout — obslog, cadence (M1 self-check / M4 present_files), dual-write. This instruction adds only the synthesis method; it does not restate the rules.
2. **Standing decisions — do not re-ask:** unit of analysis = **movements/operations**; evidence = **the attached report only**; deliverable = **story-form markdown**. (Report-specific judgements in §C and §F still need per-report work.)

## B. Read the report as evidence

3. Read the whole report. Note the registry id, the linked Strong's, the **parse meanings**, and the **unique spans** (each with its one example verse).
4. Work from the **surfaced parse meanings**; do **not** re-derive lemma or morphology. Ground every incidence in what the report supplies.
5. The report is a **sampling** layer (one example verse per span). Claims are extract-level. A verse-exhaustive pass is out of scope unless the full verse layer is requested.

## C. Find the structure the field actually has (observe, don't impose)

6. Identify the natural groupings the concept presents **from the evidence**. Fear had two opposing poles (dread / reverence) plus a shared-vocabulary hinge; another concept may have a different shape (one pole, a spectrum, several facets). Do **not** force fear's two-pole template onto a concept that lacks it.
7. **Describe each grouping separately.**

## D. Derive the movements

8. Within each grouping, identify the distinct operations/movements — what the phenomenon **does** in the inner being (e.g. enters/seizes, dissolves resolve, manifests in the body, redirects behaviour, dreads ahead, spreads through a collective, settles into a disposition, orders/produces action, is removed). Explore **each in its own right**.
9. **Spine question for every movement:** *how does this operate in the inner being* — name its source/cause, its inner operation, and its effect (heart / body / behaviour / will).
10. **Counts inform but never decide.** A single-occurrence movement is still a movement; do not filter by frequency.

## E. Write the deliverable — story form (.md)

11. **Preamble:** the field, its structure/groupings, and the spine.
12. **One Part per grouping**, each movement a short narrative that carries the **actual incidences** — paraphrase the incident, quote only the phrase that carries the operation, use **full book–chapter–verse** references.
13. Add a **hinge** section where one vocabulary pivots on its object (only if the field has one), and a **negation / "undone"** movement if the field contains it.
14. Keep the body as narrative; do **not** fragment it with per-line observation/interpretation labels.

## F. Surface, don't force (register at the end)

15. List incidences where pole/assignment is **genuinely unclear** — flag for researcher detail; do not guess.
16. List entries present by **lexical / homograph / gloss adjacency** (not denoting the concept); use only their genuinely concept-bearing spans, and note the rest for exclusion confirmation.
17. Note lemmas whose spans are **mostly outside** the concept (use only in-concept incidences), and any Strong's with **0 occurrences**.

## G. Close with a labelled reflection

18. Interpretation and possible application, **explicitly labelled** (not conclusions). Include potential impact for different cultures / interest groups and for translation, without pressing to a verdict.

## H. Output & housekeeping

19. Deliverable filename per **global rules [current]**: `wa-[registry]-[short desc]-v[major]_[minor]-[YYYYMMDD].md`. *(Filename convention persona-vs-global is a standing open decision — see the session log; adjust if ratified.)*
20. Version-control the deliverable; header carries filename, date, version, one-line prior-output reference, change-control note, author (le Roux Cilliers).
21. Surface any genuinely **blocking** ambiguity as a fork before producing; otherwise proceed — the majority should complete without concern.

## What this surfaces about force-fitting

Applying the "action ≠ disposition" correction symmetrically produced **eight silent verses**
(1b, 5b, 8, 15, 17, 18, 20, 21) out of 20 available — over a third of the chapter. Four of those
(8, 15, 18, and the LORD's role in 1/4) are places where the LORD performs or announces a stated
action (destroy, decree talion, speak, bring down) that a looser reading could easily register as
"the LORD's wrath/judgment" as if it were his own inner-being phenomenon — exactly the Amos-flagged
error. None of the eight silences were forced into a finding.

Only **five** phenomena are unambiguously stated with no judgment call at all: Edom's pride (v3-4),
no understanding (v7), dismay (v9), shame (v10), and the triad gloat/rejoice/boast (v12-13). The
remaining four (allies' treachery v7, Edom's indifference v11, Edom's contempt v16, and whether
vv13-14's action clauses carry a disposition at all) are genuine interpretive calls, not clean
textual warrants — flagged as such rather than decided either way.

This is a single-book, single-pass read — worth weighing against the same exercise for the other
five completed books before drawing any conclusion about the debates already on file.

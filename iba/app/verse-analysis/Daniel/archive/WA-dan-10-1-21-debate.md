## Preliminaries

**Working scope (declared as assumption, not fact).** Dan 10 opens the book's final vision-unit (chapters 10-12), narrated in Daniel's own first-person voice. In scope: Daniel's stated interior acts — mourning (10:2-3), physical/psychological collapse under the vision's impact (10:7-9, 15-17), and his own self-reported distress (10:16-17); the speaker's descriptions of Daniel's past interior orientation (10:12, "you set your heart to understand and humbled yourself"); the speaker's repeated status-attribution to Daniel ("man greatly loved," 10:11, 10:19) and enabling touch-events (10:10, 10:16, 10:18). Also in scope, per note (f): "the men who were with me" (10:7, a collective candidate whose fear/flight is stated); "the prince of Persia," Michael, and "the prince of Greece" (10:13, 10:20-21), each checked as presumptive candidates. The unnamed figure(s) in linen/appearance-of-a-man (10:5-6, 10:16, 10:18) are checked individually at each appearance rather than assumed to be one continuous subject, since the base extract does not resolve their identity (see Insufficiencies register).

**Reading rule applied.** Standard STEP-live precedence per base extract; no [AMBIGUOUS] spans encountered in this range.

**Corpus-continuity check.** Read `WA-dan-9-1-27-debate.md` before drafting this chapter. Carried-forward threads: the H0995 "understand" root, dense through Dan 9 (9:2, 9:22, 9:23 x2), continues here (10:1, 10:11, 10:12, 10:14); the phrase "greatly loved" (H2532), first used of Daniel at Dan 9:23, recurs twice in this chapter (10:11, 10:19); Gabriel was named explicitly in Dan 8-9, but the speaker in Dan 10 is never named — checked verse by verse rather than assumed to be Gabriel.

## Passage-level linkages (Q7)

- **H0995 "understand"** — recurs at 10:1, 10:11, 10:12, 10:14, continuing the thread dense through Dan 9 (9:2, 9:22, 9:23 x2). The chapter states Daniel already understood at its opening (10:1), yet the vision still produces sustained physical collapse (10:7-9, 15-17) before the speaker's directive to understand is repeated (10:11-14) — understanding and being overwhelmed run in parallel across the chapter rather than one resolving the other.
- **H2532 "greatly loved"** — used of Daniel at Dan 9:23, then twice more in this chapter (10:11, 10:19) — three occurrences total across the two chapters, always as the speaker's address, always paired with a directive or blessing.
- **H2388 "strengthen"** — occurs five times in this chapter alone (10:18, 10:19 x3, 10:21), a denser concentration than any other root in the passage. Four instances describe Daniel's own restoration after collapse; the fifth (10:21) applies the same root to Michael's contention against hostile princes — one root, two distinct applications within the same chapter.
- **Michael** — named at 10:13 and 10:21, the book's first two references to this figure by name. Filed for the whole-book read, since Michael recurs at Dan 12:1.
- **Touch-and-response pattern** — three distinct touch-events (10:10, a hand; 10:16, one in the likeness of the children of man; 10:18, one having the appearance of a man), each producing a distinct stated effect (trembling; enabled speech; strengthening). Whether one figure or more than one performs these three touches is not resolvable from the lexical data — see Insufficiencies register.
- **Absence surfaced:** "the men who were with me" (10:7) tremble and flee without themselves seeing the vision that (by implication) causes their fear — their interior response is stated, but its proximate cause is not something the text says they perceived directly, unlike Daniel's own collapse, which follows directly from what he saw and heard (10:8-9).

## Insufficiencies register

- **10:5, 10:10, 10:16, 10:18 — figure identity.** The base extract does not resolve whether "a man clothed in linen" (10:5), "a hand" (10:10), "one in the likeness of the children of man" (10:16), and "one having the appearance of a man" (10:18) name one continuous figure or more than one. Each was checked and treated individually per note (f) rather than assumed to be a single subject.
- **10:2-3 — object of mourning.** The base extract does not state what occasioned Daniel's three-week mourning; this verse range gives no textual object for it.
- **10:13, 10:20-21 — "prince" (H8269) referent.** Whether "the prince of the kingdom of Persia," "the prince of Greece," and Michael as "prince" denote spiritual/angelic beings, human political figures, or a mix, is not disambiguated by Strong's-level lexical data; H8269 is used of both kinds of referent elsewhere in Daniel.
- **10:1 — "great conflict" (H6635).** The base extract's gloss range (army/war/host/service) does not disambiguate whether this describes the difficulty of the message's content or of Daniel's own experience receiving it.

## Preliminaries

**Working scope (declared as assumption, not fact).** Dan 12 closes the book: it continues the speaker's discourse from ch.10-11 (12:1 opens "At that time," without a scene break), then returns to first-person narration as Daniel again sees and hears (12:5, 12:8). In scope: Michael's stated guardianship role (12:1); the eternal-destiny outcomes named for those who awake (12:2); the wise's reward (12:3); the directive to seal the book (12:4, 12:9); the oath-act of the man in linen (12:7); Daniel's own explicit admission of non-understanding (12:8, directly echoing Dan 8:27); the summarizing contrast between the wise (who understand) and the wicked (who do not) at 12:10; the stated blessedness of "he who waits" (12:12); and the closing personal promise of rest to Daniel (12:13). Chronological/numerical material (12:11-12's day-counts) and the unidentified "two others" (12:5) carry no interior content and are checked per note (f).

**Corpus-continuity check.** Read `WA-dan-11-1-45-debate.md` before drafting this chapter. Carried forward: Michael, named at Dan 10:13 and 10:21, appears a third time (12:1); the H0995 "understand" thread, shifting in moral valence through Dan 11, returns here to Daniel's own voice (12:8) and closes with an explicit wise/wicked contrast (12:10); the refining vocabulary (H6884/H1305/H3835A) opened at Dan 11:35 recurs at 12:10; the "sealed" book motif answers the open question left by Dan 8:26 ("seal up the vision") and Dan 9's decree of "sealing" vision and prophet (9:24).

## Passage-level linkages (Q7)

- **H0995 "understand"** — closes the book's understanding-thread. Daniel's own explicit admission, "I heard, but I did not understand" (12:8), directly echoes Dan 8:27 and stands in contrast to the wise, who "shall understand" (12:10) — the book's final word on this root reserves full understanding for "the wise" and for "the time of the end," not for Daniel himself in this scene.
- **H7919 "wise"** — culminates the thread from Dan 9:13/22/25 and 11:33/35: the wise "shine like the stars" (12:3) and are the ones who "shall understand" (12:10), a stated vindication of the role first introduced at Dan 9:13.
- **Refining vocabulary (H6884 "refine," H1305 "purify," H3835A "make white")** — recurs from Dan 11:35 (some of the wise stumble "so that they may be refined, purified, and made white"), now generalized at 12:10 to "many" who purify themselves — moving from a stated purpose imposed on the wise's suffering to a self-directed action available more broadly.
- **Shame/contempt (H2781, H1860)** — H2781 recurs from Dan 9:16 and 11:18; its final application at 12:2, to an eternal destiny, is its most severe use in the book.
- **Michael** — third appearance (Dan 10:13, 10:21, 12:1); this verse is the first to state his role toward Daniel's people explicitly ("who has charge of your people"), clarifying but not contradicting his earlier appearances as an opponent of hostile "princes."
- **Sealing (H5640, H2856)** — Daniel is twice told to seal the book (12:4, 12:9), directly fulfilling Dan 8:26's instruction ("seal up the vision") and Dan 9:24's decree ("to seal both vision and prophet") — the book closes by enacting its own earlier sealing-motif rather than resolving its content further.
- **Absence surfaced:** the man clothed in linen (recalling Dan 10:5) is never named in this chapter, consistent with Dan 10's own pattern of an unnamed speaker (see Dan 10 debate, EQ1).

## Insufficiencies register

- **12:5 "two others."** Their identity, and their relation to the man clothed in linen (Dan 10:5, 12:6-7), is not resolvable from Strong's-level lexical data.

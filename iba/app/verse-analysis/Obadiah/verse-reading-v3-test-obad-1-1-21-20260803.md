### Obd 1:1

"Vision" (H2377 [v], prophetic oracle) belonging to Obadiah (H5662R [v]). "Thus says" (H0559 [v], Qal perfect 3ms — a completed, formal declarative act, not an ongoing one) the Lord God (H0136/H3069, both [v]) concerning Edom (H0123G [v]). "We have heard" (H8085G [bf] — full base range, no specific variant resolved; morph HVqp1cp, Qal perfect **1cp**) a report (H8052 [v]) from the Lord (H3068G [v]); "a messenger has been sent" (H7971G [bf]; morph HVPp3ms — **Pual perfect**, passive voice, 3ms — the clause's grammatical subject ("messenger") is acted upon, not acting; T3 note: the agent doing the sending is not encoded anywhere in this morph) among the nations (H1471A [bf]): "'Rise up!'" (H6965J [v], Qal **imperative**, morph HVqv**2mp** — addressed to a plural "you," the nations) "'let us rise'" (H6965J [v], Qal infinitive construct + **1cp** subject marking, morph HVqc1cp — a cohortative-shaped "let us") "against her for battle" (H4421 [v]).

**T4 — referent crux, "we" (span 8, "heard").** Genuinely live readings, per T4:
1. **The prophet alone** (a stylized prophetic "we," reporting what he personally has heard) — grounded only in the fact that H8085G's own morph (1cp) doesn't itself distinguish "prophet speaking for himself" from "prophet speaking for a group."
2. **The prophet plus a coalition of nations** — grounded in the same verse's own following clause: the 1cp cohortative "let us rise" (span 15, H6965J, HVqc1cp) is issued immediately after "we have heard," and its natural antecedent is the group being summoned to battle against Edom — i.e. the "we" who heard the report is the same "we" proposing to act on it, a coalition rather than the prophet in isolation.
3. **A stylized collective with no specific referential intent** (the prophetic "we" as literary convention, not meant to be resolved to a headcount).

Adopted for this pass: **reading 2** (prophet-plus-coalition), on the strength of the grammatical linkage between the 1cp verb at span 8 and the 1cp cohortative at span 15 within the same verse — this is this pass's own default, not a directed researcher call; readings 1 and 3 are kept on record, not discarded.

**T4 — messenger (span 11).** Passive, agent unstated (per T3 above); no named or implied agent anywhere in this verse to resolve it to — recorded as a single unnamed referent, not a multi-candidate crux (there is no competing grammatical candidate to weigh, only an absence).

**T5 — genre-conventional elements expected but absent.** As a "vision" superscription (a recognised prophetic-book opening form), the verse supplies no date formula (contrast Amos 1:1, Hosea 1:1) and no call/commissioning narrative (contrast Isaiah 6, Ezekiel 1-3, Jeremiah 1) — both conventionally present at the opening of other prophetic corpora already read in this campaign. Recorded as an observed absence, not filled in.

### Obd 1:11

"On the day that you stood" (H5975G [bf]; morph HVqcc — Qal **infinitive construct**, no independent person/number marking of its own — the 2ms subject is carried instead by the following span's H9041 "you" suffix, not by this verb form itself) "aloof, on the day that strangers" (H2114A [bf]) "carried off" (H7617 [v]) "his wealth" (H2428H [bf]) "and foreigners" (H5237 [v]) "entered" (H0935G [bf], same lexeme as v5) "his gates and cast lots" (H3032 [v]/H1486 [v]) "for Jerusalem" (H3389 [v], named) — "you were like one of them" (H0259 [v]).

**T4 — the unnamed "his" (spans 7 "wealth," 10 "gates").** Genuinely live readings, since the 3ms possessive has no explicit within-verse antecedent and "Jerusalem" — the only other named party in the verse — carries feminine grammatical gender in Hebrew, not masculine, so is not a straightforward grammatical antecedent for a 3ms suffix:
1. **An unnamed masculine collective (a nation/people, e.g. "Jacob"/the house of Israel)** — grounded in the fact that "casting lots for Jerusalem" (the city, fem.) most plausibly targets the possessions of Jerusalem's *people*, a corporate entity that can carry masculine agreement in Hebrew even when the city-name itself is feminine.
2. **A specific unnamed individual** (a king or leading figure) whose wealth/gates are meant — grounded only in the bare possibility that a 3ms suffix defaults to an individual referent absent other evidence.
3. **Loose/extended agreement with "Jerusalem" itself**, treating the fem. city-name as taking a masculine suffix by poetic license — grounded in the general observation that Hebrew personifications of cities are not always grammatically rigid.

Adopted for this pass: **reading 1** (the unnamed masculine collective/people), as the most economical account given "Jerusalem" is explicitly the *object* of the lot-casting in the same clause, not obviously its own antecedent. Readings 2 and 3 kept on record.

## 3. The debate — how it developed

**Turn 1 — the governing observation.** The register's own v2.2 §0.7 states the register is *not* a structure or grouping mechanism by itself. That constrains the whole question: the windows are the **HOW** (the angle/instrument), "bite size" is about the **WHAT** (the corpus slice). A bite is therefore an **intersection** — a corpus slice × a window applied to it. The corpus supplies nested units (cluster → lemma/stem-meaning → verse); the vantage map already sorts windows into micro/meso/macro. Interpretation reached: corpus units *size* the work, windows *sequence* it. Forks surfaced (bite unit; micro-window application; W7 status; uneven sizes; scope of T2/T3/FLAG; aggregative attach). Insufficiency flagged: most windows' glass is unverified; W8 cloudy; the window↔corpus mapping doesn't exist yet.

**Turn 2 — the practical pipeline and T3.** The researcher confirmed body-act is now embedded into T3, and pressed on the practical reality: what feeds into a bite, what comes out, how roll-up works, what it does and needs, and how the "spiderweb of interrelationships" happens.

The key reframe that resolved the T3 confusion: **M-clusters are characteristic-nodes; T3 is the operation-verbs; the verse is the edge.** T3 "comes into focus for each unit" as the **operation-edge** of an inner-being movement — the *how/through-what* a characteristic reaches a target. A pipeline was reasoned from W1 + M1 + W2 + the reset method:
- IN: the lemma + its verse-set + per-verse co-present lemmas (M-characteristics and T3 operations).
- INSIDE: W1 movement decomposition with functional edges, each resolved / NONE-silent / UNRESOLVED + citation; T3 surfaces as the operation/object edge.
- OUT: per-verse records; an in-verse finding; and the **out-edges** = the web-threads (edges whose far end lies outside the verse).
- ROLL-UP: the W2 four-level cascade (in-verse → similar → cluster → cross-cluster) matches threads.
- What roll-up DOES: converts isolated records into movement patterns and assembles the web.
- What roll-up NEEDS: a shared connector vocabulary (join key) + C5 silence discipline.

Two load-bearing forks were exposed and left open: **the role of T3** (operation-edge only vs its own bites — the recognise-then-attach vs fragment-stitch choice), and **the join key** (what threads match on). Both are undecided in all documents currently held; the pipeline model is reasoned, not sourced.

**Turn 3 — close for the day.** The researcher noted low brainpower, that the debate needs more untangling tomorrow, estimated ~400–500 cycles at base-unit level, and asked for: (a) the register updates already identified, and (b) a follow-up discussion `.md`.

## 2. Per-document pointers

**① characteristic-role-lexical-cycle (spine, v1, 07-08).** The governing cycle: seed (Axis A) → passage → read (Axis B, role fixed inside it) → write-back → normalised index.
- **[METHOD] Two orthogonal axes.** *Is the lemma a candidate?* (corpus-wide, over-inclusive seed) vs *in this occurrence, what role?* (per-verse read). "Never conflate." Separates the corpus-wide filter from the verse-level determination.
- **[METHOD] Char-driven read, not span-sweep.** "The characteristic is the lens — read *for it*; resolve its pairs by reading across the passage." A span-sweep + proximity-pairing is named as the failure that produced *defective auto-pairs*. **Directly bears on bite-structure D3** (recognise-then-attach vs fragment-stitch — this chose recognise-then-attach).
- **[WINDOW] Screen 0 — the lens is the HUMAN inner being; God is arena, not subject.** A scope-vantage: God's own attributes are qualifiers (source/target/manner) on a human characteristic, never characteristics themselves.
- **[WINDOW/LESSON] The 16 dimensions & what morphology gives:** 8 derivable, 6 partial, **role NOT derivable** — morphology gives the mechanical substrate, meaning needs the read. (The language/morphology window in operation.)
- **[RELOOK] Meaning-keyed index (§7D v3):** the meaning-in-context is keyed on the **ESV rendering** — a *translation* used as the meaning discriminator. In tension with "departed from translated text." One for the language window.

**② passage-completeness-rule (v2, 07-08).** The reading unit.
- **[WINDOW/METHOD] The passage is the reading frame — and it is a choice.** IB-relevance is the candidate characteristic, not the verse-record; the char is the heart; single-verse passages allowed.
- **[METHOD] Frame sizing is genre-sensitive.** char-continuity (run breaks when the shared char changes) for sentence/wisdom collections; maximal-run for discourse/poetry. **Thematic segmentation explicitly rejected** — "theme-grouping distracts and overloads; the char, not a theme, bounds the passage." (Observe-don't-impose in operation.)

**③ book-lexical-readiness-assessment (v3, 07-14).** Pre-flight before a book is read.
- **[LESSON] Completeness ≠ validity (§5b).** "A book can be 100% complete and uniformly wrong." Completeness gates cannot see a validity failure.
- **[METHOD/WINDOW] Empirical glass-check.** V2 **vocabulary-drift**: a controlled dimension that tracks *chapter position* is the **reader drifting, not the text speaking** — a measurable way to detect unclear glass. Plus the **dimension-reliability register** (two axes: **stability** + **provenance**) — metacognition about which lenses are trustworthy.
- **[METHOD] Precondition / read-output / anchor** three-way, and the **staged dependency sequence** (seed → register → verse-record → passage → read → integrity), each stage gated on the prior.

**④ db-integrity-definition (v1, 07-11).** The I1–I13 invariants.
- **[LESSON] Define the term precisely.** "'DB integrity' was used repeatedly without ever being defined — which let ledger-completeness be reported as full integrity while 261 spans were orphans." Name the partial check precisely ("ledger-clean", not "integrity").
- **[WINDOW] I4b silence-vs-skip.** A verse with no lexical is legitimate *only if* it carries no characteristic; otherwise it is a **skipped read**, not a silence. (= the NONE-vs-ABSENT distinction at DB level.)
- **[WINDOW] I3 traceability chain:** char → span → verse → passage → lexical, bidirectional. (The spiderweb of links, as data.)

**⑤ term-add-update-pipeline (v1, 07-11).** The only sanctioned term/verse onboarding path.
- **[LESSON] The STEP multi-variant drop.** STEP splits a Strong's across lettered sub-codes (*ruach* H7307 → H7307G/H/J); single-code resolution **silently dropped whole books** of verses. The fix is **morphology-anchored** (find the variants through the morphology). A raw-collation shortfall of exactly the kind that forces re-analysis.
- **[LESSON] Root-fix, not one-off.** Fix the *cause* in the shared mechanism; never hand-patch one instance of a recurring class.
- **[LESSON] Scaffolding with no analytical value.** The term↔registry allocation is "highly debatable and has NO analytical value — ignored downstream." (= clusters-as-workspaces: an arrangement convenience, not a claim.)

**⑥ reread-operating-model-cadence (v2, 07-14).** The execution rhythm.
- **[METHOD] Read+write unit vs stop+check unit.** Passage = read+write (write after *each*, never un-written); cycle (~12 passages) = the breather + conformance + scored quality check.
- **[LESSON] "Complete" is VERSE-level, not passage-level.** Proverbs read "701/701 passages" yet only 87% of verses; 116 orphan verses silently skipped. Completeness must be defined at the right grain.
- **[METHOD] Self-interpretable values.** Every dimension value must be readable **without** the verse (carry the trigger + the sense).

**⑦ projection-spec (v1.2, 07-13).** The flattened reading view.
- **[WINDOW] NONE vs ABSENT — the single most important distinction.** "The reader looked and found none" (evidence of silence) vs "no row recorded" (absence of reading). Collapsing them "destroyed the single most important distinction — how the intensity/effect silence came to be asserted without evidence."
- **[LESSON] The evidence must travel with the data.** Without `span_id` + `morph`, the H2603 split reads as gloss-driven — "every reader will conclude the split is gloss-driven; it is not." The span/morph is the true discriminator.
- **[METHOD] Two budgets: compute-on-disk (free) vs read-into-context (paid).** Coded columns computed corpus-wide at zero cost; free-text read family-by-family. (Bears on scale / bite-structure.)

**⑧ analytic-input-spec (v2.0, 07-13).** The richest for the windows work.
- **[WINDOW] FIVE states of silence, not three.** resolved · **NONE** (reader looked) · unresolved · **OUT-OF-SCOPE** (deliberately not read for this genre) · **NOT-WRITTEN** (should have been read, wasn't). **Collapsing OUT-OF-SCOPE and NOT-WRITTEN launders design decisions into findings about Scripture** — measured: the story layer asserted *"the passage never tells us"* about dimensions the method chose not to read. Refines "silence is a finding" (C5).
- **[METHOD] Layer-divergence = the finding (Part 4).** Read in order — coded-on-disk → evidence free-text → technical narrative → **story last** — and *where the layers diverge, that is a finding*. Evidence-first ordering, because story-first anchors on unsound conclusions (plausibility ≠ truth). **This is the researcher's inter-relatedness/triangulation principle, already operationalised.**
- **[WINDOW/METHOD] The discovery-lookout (dim 114 / P8) is the emergence engine — and it was DARK.** It should ask, every read, "what does this verse imply that the current dimensions do NOT capture?" and record *discovery: none* when nothing. In the sample it was populated as a *sense-seed* with **zero** "discovery: none." **This is the built-in new-vantage finder — and it was not running.**
- **[LESSON] The artefact is not self-describing (L1).** It must "carry the evidence for its own structure, not just its values" — the pair *is* the directed edge; a file showing one endpoint reads as undirected to anyone who doesn't know the rule.

## 3. The consolidation — pointers that recur across the set

- **Silence has kinds (the strongest single pointer).** NONE-vs-ABSENT (⑦), the five-state model (⑧), I4b (④) all converge: the distinction between *reader-silence* and *text-silence*, and between *deliberate-non-reading* and *accidental-non-reading*, is the crux — and conflating them manufactures false findings. **The silence window in the exploration doc (C5) needs states, not a single "none".**
- **Triangulation is real and was built.** Layer-divergence-as-finding (⑧) is the operational form of the researcher's inter-relatedness principle (exploration §2). It even carries a discipline: **read the evidence before the story**, or you anchor on the unsound layer.
- **The morphological span is the true unit; gloss/Strong's/ESV are lossy proxies.** span-not-Strong's (①⑦⑧), morphology-anchored extraction (⑤), yet meaning keyed on the ESV rendering (①§7D). The **language window** has both a firm floor (morphology) and an unresolved tension (translation used as the meaning key).
- **The edge-set is stable across framings.** The 16 dimensions (⑧①) ≈ the movement-edges of the reset. "How do we articulate it" has a stable answer at the *edge* level even as the framing-label (characteristics / movements / web) shifts.
- **Every raw-collation change forces re-analysis — in the flesh.** The STEP drop (⑤), verse-level completeness (③⑥), mti dedup (④): each is a concrete instance of the coupling the researcher named (exploration §1a). These are the fossils of that cycle.
- **Glass can be checked empirically.** Vocabulary-drift and the reliability register (③) turn "check the glass" from a principle into a measurable test.
- **Observe-don't-impose, repeatedly enforced.** Char-not-theme bounding (②), meaning-not-lookup characteristic identity (①), scaffolding-with-no-analytical-value (⑤). The discipline the reset installed is visible throughout.

## 9. Batch-2 per-document pointers

**① verse-analysis-method (v1.1, 07-02).** Genre-aware verse-first / passage / self-learning process.
- **[WINDOW] Genre is a lens** — the disputation form "stages the people's interior"; a poem frames the inner being *from different angles* (antithesis, simile, progression, destiny). The literary form itself reveals the interior differently.
- **[METHOD] Genre selects the reading unit** — prose (cross-verse ON) · poetic/wisdom (two-phase, cross-verse OFF, Phase-2 poem-read enriches from interrelatedness) · discourse (segmentation units) · prophetic (oracle/disputation).
- **[METHOD] Self-learning read-back loop** — derive → read back → *is it sensible?* → keep / D11-note / fix-the-rule. "Reading back and evaluating for sensibility makes a huge difference."
- **[LESSON/glass] Mechanical mis-fires logged as residuals** — seat=spirit on elemental *ruach*; positional-target over-produces; neg-particle over-spreads; bearer=nearest-proper bleed. Simile undetectable mechanically (comparative *kaf* fused into morph). Read from verse structure, not the tags, where these fire.

**② synthesis-B-spec (reset, v2, 06-24).** The movements/assembly half of the reset — *the* spiderweb spec.
- **[WINDOW] The movement, on the edges.** "Boundaries won't hold; meaning lives on the edges" carried one level up: clusters are nodes, the inner-being picture lives on the edges between them. Unit = the movement, not the term.
- **[METHOD — answers bite-structure D3/D4] Assembly.** Recognise-then-attach (default); fragment-stitch only with a **D1-signal join key, never the lemma**. L2 clusters on the **DENSE** fields (operation, object-kind) and lets **sparse** fields (effect, transition) enrich — **never groups on shared ABSENCE**. The **join key is the set of D1 relation signals** (shared shape · binding-web · shared object-kind · pole-opposition · shared seat · cognate/root · adjacency).
- **[METHOD] Traverse the web on focus, across time** — the B+D pointer mechanism; a deferred observation re-surfaces when its verse/cluster next comes into focus. The answer to "can't hold the web in one pass."
- **[WINDOW] L1.5 full-exegesis gate (Logos)** — depth-on-demand for the flagged minority (figurative/somatic-metaphor, distributed movement, heavy UNRESOLVED, theologically loaded). A window for when the mechanical windows can't settle a verse.
- **[WINDOW/METHOD] Measurement instruments as non-deciding lenses** — co-occurrence heat-map, association matrix, distribution histogram, **edge/network view** ("emergent areas without hard lines — density as topology"). No instrument output is ever a finding (P0 / E2).
- **[LESSON — governing epistemics] The discard↔include asymmetry.** Bias toward keeping/surfacing: a wrongly-*kept* candidate is visible and correctable; a wrongly-*discarded* one is invisible and lost. Every discard and borderline keep is flagged and the line is calibrated from cases, not set by rule.

**③ 01b-VE-field-reliability (v3, 06-24).** Pre-reset canonical VE rules — the object-type grid, mostly closed.
- **[RELOOK] Framing reset in-place** — the object-type grid / faculty-as-field / "logical units" is superseded by the reset; the **measure-layer bedrock (sense, mode, morphology) is retained.** The closed-legacy line, stated in the document itself.
- **[WINDOW — language bedrock] The measure layer marks what English drops.** The original language *marks* predicate-argument structure (Greek case; Hebrew construct/suffix/preposition) that English omits → object/subject/modifier become deterministic reads. English-string matching *was* the whole homograph error-class. The firmest floor for the language window.
- **[WINDOW — silence refined] The LRT / type-conditioned expectedness (C.3).** What makes a blank a *gap* vs a legitimate *silence* is **what is EXPECTED for that object-type** (a transitive characteristic expects a target; an intransitive one legitimately has none). Extends the five-state silence model.
- **[METHOD — glass-check] Idempotence as a drift-detector (R4).** Re-running unchanged inputs at an unchanged model must be idempotent; a non-reproducible mechanical value is a **judgement leak**. Re-run = the glass-check.
- **[WINDOW] Bivalence-by-object** — a faculty's valence is set by *what it is aimed at* (trust in God vs in wealth); "transitivity predicts bivalence." The object/target is the discriminator.

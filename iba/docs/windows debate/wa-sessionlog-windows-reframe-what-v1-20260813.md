## 2. The debate — how it developed

**The reframe.** My earlier "windows = how, corpus = what" was mislocated. Corrected architecture: the **windows are the "what"** (ways of looking); the collated signals are the other half of the "what" (done); the analysis mechanics are the "how" (jumped to prematurely, parked). The register had named its own shared blind spot (all windows infer interior from text) and then settled on too few views — the correction holds it to its own epistemology.

**Two windows sharpened.** *Language* — departing from translation removed the translator's glass, not the language's own; original-language ≠ unmediated. Three strands: each language's affordances (A1), Hebrew↔Greek divergence (A2, readable from data), word-choice against the near-synonym field (A3). *Who's-talking* — vantage (self/other) + identity + agenda → evidential weight; observing another's interior is also a datum about the observer's. Offered a tentative **vantage / glass / instrument** three-way; both new windows are glass-heavy.

**The coupling insight (§1a of the exploration doc).** Each pause historically jostled three coupled layers — perspective (windows), collation (data), analysis (how). Consequence: the **windows are the leverage point**; stabilise them first or every pause forces rework. The two recurring questions ("what do we analyse", "how do we articulate it") are not defects — the second *is* the framing/window choice.

**Framings as co-valid windows (§5).** Characteristics / movements / spider-web / phenomena / in-between are co-valid *ways of noticing*, not a ladder of replacements. Nuance preserved: the June-reset closed characteristics-*as-classification*, not characteristics-*as-noticing*; the reset's discipline rides inside every window. (phenomena / in-between still need the researcher's definitions.)

**Past-works scan (two batches, 17 docs).** Produced `WA-past-works-pointers` v1.0 (8 docs) then v1.1 (+9 docs). Strongest findings:
- **The tier catalogue is a 126-vantage catalogue of interrogative windows** (T0–T7), reframed by the reset into "areas to cover", not deleted. The register's ~10 windows are a thin slice of it; **T4 already covers the relational-interfaces fork**.
- **The reset's synthesis-B spec substantially pre-answers bite-structure D3/D4** (recognise-then-attach; join on D1 relation signals / dense fields; never on absence, never the lemma).
- **Silence has a fuller model**: reader-vs-text × five states × object-type-conditioned expectedness.
- **Triangulation was already built** (layer-divergence-as-finding; evidence-before-story).
- **The discovery-lookout is the new-window engine — and it was dark.**
- **Governing epistemics surfaced:** the discard↔include asymmetry (bias to keep).
- **Genre** is both a window and a method-selector.
- Language-window floor: the measure layer marks what English drops.

**Gap analysis.** "What are we missing" = which windows are absent/thin. Three named by the researcher — behaviour (T3 Operations), physical↔inner correlations, metaphysical — plus two I added (developmental arc, collective/social). Pattern: behaviour is under-built by *framing choice*; physical↔inner and metaphysical by *method-reach*; all cluster at the edges of what outbound signals reach. Honesty bound stated: my judgement is framework-level, not verse-output-level (I have not seen the per-verse analyses).

- Three distinct strands bundled under "language":

### Window: LOOK WHO'S TALKING (speaker identity + agenda ⇒ signal weight)
- Self-description vs description-of-another look different; the observer's **agenda** colours the observation.
- Sub-dimensions: **vantage** (inside/self vs outside/other) · **identity** (self / narrator / God / other human / collective) · **agenda/stance** (praise, indictment, lament, defence, seduction, comfort) · **evidential weight** (God/narrator often text-endorsed as true; self = privileged access but maximal self-deception risk; enemy/friend = agenda-laden).
- **Double-signal insight:** observing another's interior is *also* a datum about the observer's interior; the two inner beings are entangled. Complication (can't cleanly isolate the observed) and opportunity (observer's interior comes free).
- **Discipline link:** must NOT collapse an agenda-laden claim into a fact about the observed — keep attribution explicit (connects to W5 Q3 stated-vs-inferred). This window largely calibrates the *reliability* of every other window.

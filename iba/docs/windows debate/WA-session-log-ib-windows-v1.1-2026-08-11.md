## 1. Session scope

A conceptual/methodological session. It began as a debate about one word (*take*) and became a foundational instrument — a **register of "windows"** onto the inner being — plus its operating procedure, its scope, and the beginnings of its per-window schema. No corpus, span report, or database data was analysed; the work was framing, not evidence-analysis. The session spanned 2026-08-10 into 2026-08-11.

## 2. The debate arc (the thinking process)

**Step 1 — *take* as an example.** *Take* established as a **radial category**: physical grasp at the centre, a wide attested field around it, including an affective family (*take heart / offence / pity / counsel*) that runs inside the person while borrowing the vocabulary of physical grasp. Behavioural-science domains named but kept explicitly **un-grounded**.

**Step 2 — *take* has compatriots.** Generalised to a **class** of body-act words (*see, give, speak, walk, take*), physically-rooted yet interwoven with the inner being, borders often unclear. Noted the programme is already inside this class (healing 184; cursing 178; blessing 158). Interpreted three mechanisms of interweaving (metaphor / metonymy-externalisation / whole-person) and a possible directional asymmetry (inner-anchored vs body-anchored). Cardinal risk: observe, do not classify.

**Step 3 — from words to windows.** Reframed to consolidating the many "windows/lenses" gathered over seven months — reviewing whether the **glass is clear** without removing windows; none complete, but collectively they diminish blind spots. Two passes distinguished: clear-glass (quality) and coverage (collective), with the shared-vantage caution.

**Step 4 — build the shape.** Produced the **windows register** (v1.0): three-kinds split (windows / calibration principles / scaffolding); a populated catalogue; the naming of the **primary shared blind spot** (all windows read text and infer interior; none observes the inner being directly); the add-a-window protocol; the vantage map.

**Step 5 — entry point.** Worked "what are we looking for / observing / what boundaries" as settled-vs-open. Proposed stance (for researcher judgement): **firm boundary on scope, openness on ontology.** Left the object/ontology/somatic/inference/relational forks open.

**Step 6 — embed the procedure (v1.1).** Bedded the review process into the register as a new **§0 Operating procedure**, making it self-executing on re-introduction.

**Step 7 — researcher revision (v2) + scope correction.** Researcher returned a substantially revised v2: added the §0.1/§0.1a scope split (the register documents *how* the study looks, not what is observed; and is *not* for project/DB/process rules), the §0.5 no-force-fit clause, the §0.7 format decision, the §1 point-of-departure paragraph, the §7 redefinition (M1 Operation), the §12 Retired-windows section. Key correction: v1.1 had wrongly mixed general project/process items (B+D, obslog, dual-write, two-AI, filenames) into the register — those are not windows onto the inner being. Accepted.

**Step 8 — cleanup (v2.1).** Cleared the residual non-window content from §3; corrected §0 numbering and all cross-references (Open forks → §13; Retired windows = §12); fixed the header. Window/criteria/mechanism interplay deliberately left open.

**Step 9 — edits + schema direction (v2.2).** Applied two edits (§0.7 "NOT a grouping mechanism by itself"; §0.8 indent). Researcher then set the schema direction and resolved the open interplay: **calibration and mechanism are the how-to of each window**, and a window serves two functions — looking into the inner being *and* making the corpus manageable. Explored window **axes** (term/lexical; book/genre), grounded #1 in the existing cluster workspaces (W10) and #2 in the Daniel passage work, and surfaced a proposed **coverage-dependency** element (from the "does a book-window keep the other windows covered?" question).

## 3. Positions and decisions reached

- **Scope of the register (settled):** it documents *how* the study looks at the inner being — not what is observed, and not project/database/process management (those live with the global rules). §0.1 / §0.1a.
- **The heap resolves into three kinds:** windows, calibration principles, scaffolding/mechanisms — with "is the glass clear?" = "does this window honour the calibration principles?"
- **Two exemplars of glass quality:** faculty-as-observation (cleaned) and the Psalms/M-01 database (found cloudy).
- **Primary shared blind spot named:** every window reads text and infers interior; the discipline "silence is a finding" converts that floor into data.
- **Register format (settled):** narrative `.md`; it is *not* a grouping mechanism by itself (§0.7).
- **Self-executing:** §0 operating procedure lets a cold reader run a review pass; Mode R (revise) / Mode A (add).
- **Window/criteria/mechanism interplay (resolved directionally):** calibration + mechanism = the **how-to of each window**; a window has a dual function (look into IB + make corpus manageable); vantage carries the corpus-partitioning **axis**.

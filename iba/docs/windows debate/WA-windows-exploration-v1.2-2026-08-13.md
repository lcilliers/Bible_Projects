## 1. The founding recognition

The inner being is not directly observable. It is seen only through its **outbound signals**. Those signals are words — the human being's own descriptions of the inner life — collated across **66 books, two languages, thousands of years** (`cluster-v2-20260812.md`). That collation is the chosen way to make the inner being visible.

Because the object is intensely complex and **no single signal or vantage is complete**, multiple ways of looking are required. No window is complete; none is exclusively right or wrong. This is the premise the whole register rests on — and the register named it (its shared-blind-spot note) before settling on too few views.

## 1a. Why the windows are the leverage point — the coupling of perspective, data, and analysis

**Observation (researcher, 2026-08-13).** Across the study's history, each *pause* has done three things at once: it opened a chance to shift the **perspective** (the window) a little; it exposed **shortfalls in the raw-data collation**; and — because the three layers are coupled — a change in any one has cascaded into the others.

**The cycle, named.**
- A pause shifts the **window** and surfaces a **data-collation shortfall**.
- A change in the **raw-data collation** forces a **rerun of the analysis**.
- A change in the **analysis** raises two fundamental, recurring questions: **what do we analyse**, and **how do we articulate it**.

**Interpretation (labelled).** The three layers are the same three named earlier: perspective = the **windows** (the "what", part 1); collation = **what we have to look at** (the "what", part 2); analysis = the **"how"**. They have never all been stable at once, so each pause re-jostles the whole stack and the rework recurs. The **windows are the leverage point**: built out and held stable first — as the "what" — they let the data-collation and the analysis be *derived* from a settled perspective rather than each pause re-shaking everything. This is also why the earlier pull-back from bite-structure was correct: committing to the "how" before the windows stabilise *guarantees* rework.

**The two recurring questions connect to §5.** "What do we analyse" is set by the intersection of a window and the data. "How do we articulate it" is answered by a *framing* — characteristics, movements, web — which §5 re-reads as co-valid windows. So the recurring articulation question is not a defect to be eliminated but a standing *choice of window*; recognising the framings as plural means the articulation can legitimately be multiple.

**The role of the past works.** Each past document is a fossil of a window-state and a data-state. Scanning them is not only preservation — it recovers **windows that appeared momentarily and were not captured**, and **data-shortfalls that were noticed and lost**. That is why they serve as prompts to build out the windows.

## 2. The windows form a system, not a list

**Principle (researcher, 2026-08-13).** The windows are inter-related. Looking through one can corroborate or change the view seen through another; unclear glass in one may be illuminated by another.

**Consequence — triangulation.** Because no window sees the object directly, the windows are read against one another: where they agree, confidence rises; where one is blind, another may see; where they conflict, the conflict is *itself a finding*, not an error to resolve away.

The set of windows therefore mirrors its object — a **system / web**, not a set of buckets.

## 3. A working way to hold windows (tentative — for researcher judgement)

Three different kinds of thing may currently be getting called "window":

- **VANTAGE** — a genuine *angle* onto the inner being (e.g. signal-type, temporal frame, relational interface). You look *through* it.
- **GLASS** — a property of the *medium* that bends the signal and must be corrected *for* (e.g. language, speaker-agenda). You look through it, but you must account for its distortion.
- **INSTRUMENT** — a way of *handling* what has been gathered (e.g. movement-decomposition, the cascade, the interrogative, measurement, cluster-organisation). It processes; it does not itself look.

This three-way is **provisional**. It is offered because two of the windows surfaced on 2026-08-13 (language; who's-talking) are mostly **GLASS**, and glass behaves differently from a vantage — you don't just look through it, you correct for it.

## 4. Windows surfaced so far (described, not operationalised)

Each is given as: *what it foregrounds · its glass (limit/distortion) · its relations to other windows.*

**Signal-type** — *vantage.* The interior read through the **kind** of signal it leaks through: speech, action, bodily/somatic manifestation, involuntary reaction, what it pursues, what it recoils from. **Glass:** each channel shows only its own slice; a channel-poor verse looks empty when it is only *silent on that channel*. **Relations:** pairs with who's-talking — a signal's channel and its speaker jointly set its weight.

**Who's-talking / observer** — *glass-heavy.* Carries at once: **vantage** (self/inside vs other/outside), **identity** (self, narrator, God, another human, a collective), **agenda/stance** (praise, indictment, lament, defence, seduction, comfort), and the resulting **evidential weight** (text-endorsed observers — often God, often the narrator — weigh differently from a character's self-report or an enemy's charge). **Double-signal:** observing another's interior is *also* a datum about the observer's interior; the two are entangled. **Glass:** agenda is itself inferred; an agenda-laden claim must never be collapsed into a fact about the observed. **Relations:** this window largely *calibrates the reliability of every other window*.

**Language / word-choice** — *glass-heavy.* Three strands:
- *A1 — each language's own affordances/limits:* how Hebrew and Greek each carve the interior differently.
- *A2 — Hebrew↔Greek divergence:* where the two overlap, part ways, or lack an equivalent. **[ASSUMPTION to confirm]** if the clusters juxtapose Hebrew and Greek terms under a shared gloss, this is *readable from data already held* — language-asymmetry within a cluster is a finding about how each language sees that region.
- *A3 — the writer's word-choice against the near-synonym field:* why *this* member of a cluster, in this verse, over the alternatives.
**Reframe:** original-language is **not** unmediated — departing from translation removed the *translator's* glass, not the *language's own*. **Glass:** cannot always tell a meaningful choice from a conventional one (dialect, genre, formula, metre); reconstructing the field of alternatives is inferential. **Relations:** the cluster architecture (≈ synonym neighbourhoods) supports A3; A2 is a discovery available now.

**Temporal frame** — *vantage.* The interior as momentary state, as movement/transition, as settled disposition, as developmental arc. **Glass:** a single verse rarely shows the arc; the frame must usually be assembled across verses. **Relations:** pairs with the movement-instrument (reads the dynamic) and the characteristics-lens (reads the settled).

**Relational interface** — *vantage · OPEN FORK.* The interior as it turns toward God, toward other humans, toward other spiritual beings. **Glass/limit:** the *classification* of these interfaces is a standing open fork. **Relations:** overlaps the "in-between" framing (§5).

**Existing register instruments — pointer, not restated.** The register (`WA-inner-being-windows-register-v2_3-2026-08-12.md`) catalogues W1–W10. On the §3 three-way reading, most of those (W1 movement-decomposition, W2 cascade, W5 interrogative, W9 measure, W10 organisation) are **instruments**; W3 (word-span) and W4 (passage/narrative) sit closer to **vantages**. This is a *re-reading to test*, not a change applied to the register.

## 5. The project's successive framings — are they windows?

**Researcher proposition (2026-08-13).** The concepts the project has held in focus over time — characteristics / phenomena / movements / spider-web / in-between / … — may each be an *effort to find a window* onto the inner being. None is exclusively right or wrong; they are different ways of looking at the same object.

**Assessment (interpretation, labelled).** The proposition holds at the level of **windows**. A real system genuinely *has* stable properties *and* dynamic operations *and* a relational structure; a lens that foregrounds one is not falsified by a lens that foregrounds another. On this reading the project's "resets" were **window-discoveries mistaken for replacements** — the error was discarding the previous window rather than adding to it.

**Necessary nuance (so the reset's wisdom is not lost).** "None exclusively right or wrong" is true of each framing **as a way of noticing**, not as a **decision-instrument**. The June-2025 reset closed characteristics-*as-classification* (bins to sort into; premature labels-as-findings) because that *imposed* structure. It did not need to close characteristics-*as-a-way-of-noticing* stable dispositions. So each framing is a co-valid window **and** each carries its own glass:

- **characteristics** — foregrounds stable dispositions / identity. *Glass:* premature classification; static-ising a dynamic thing.
- **movements** — foregrounds dynamics, causation, direction (functional/relational edges). *Glass:* fragmenting the being into disconnected operations; losing the whole/identity.
- **spider-web** — foregrounds interconnection, system-structure. *Glass:* over-formalising; treating the web as complete when it is partial.
- **phenomena** — **[DEFINITION NEEDED — not fabricated.]** *Provisional reading:* the interior via its observable manifestations/appearances. Confirm.
- **in-between** — **[DEFINITION NEEDED — not fabricated.]** *Provisional reading:* the interior in the relational space *between* beings (overlaps relational-interface, §4). Confirm.
- **… etc** — **[researcher to enumerate.]**

**Held open.** How far this reframing is carried — in particular whether characteristics is re-admitted as a *noticing*-window — is an interpretive decision for the researcher (§7). The safe synthesis on the table: **each framing is a window for noticing; none is a licence to classify; the reset's discipline (observe don't impose · measurement informs never decides · silence is a finding) rides inside every window.**

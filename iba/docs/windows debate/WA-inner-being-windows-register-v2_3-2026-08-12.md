## 1. Purpose — what this register is, and is not

Over seven months the programme has accumulated many **windows** onto the inner being. No single window shows the whole. The stated end goal is to *look through all of them and describe what is seen* — and, in the researcher's words, the truth is known only when that is complete.

This register is the **enabling scaffold** for that goal, not the goal itself. A **window** encapsulate how and from which angle the inner being is observed. It does two jobs and no more:

1. It **lists** the windows, so the collection stops being a heap and becomes a map.
2. It **holds two questions** against each window: *is the glass clear?* (quality) and *what does it see that no other does, and where do all of them go blind together?* (coverage).

It draws **no** conclusion about the inner being. That composite description is a later, separate act, for which this register is the precondition.

The **point of departure** is the extended study of the Scriptures to explore what the bible says about the inner human being, what it is, how it works, and how it interacts with its surroundings. So everything the study includes relates to the inner being. The study does not pre-suppose the result, but observe the results from the exploration.

---

## 2. The two passes this register serves

The researcher named two distinct tasks. They must not blur, because the first gates the second.

- **Pass A — clear glass (quality, per window).** Does this window show what is actually there, or does it *impose*? A window with cloudy glass propagates its distortion into any composite built from it, so clarity is checked before coverage is trusted.
- **Pass B — coverage (collective, across windows).** What does each window uniquely see; what is it structurally blind to; and — the dangerous one — **where do many windows share a vantage and therefore share a blind spot** they cannot individually detect?

**Shared-vantage caution.** Windows that all look from the same angle diminish each other's *small* blind spots while sharing a *large* one none can see. Naming the common vantage is part of checking the glass (see §8).

---

## 3. Three kinds of item (why the split matters)

Looking into the inner being may appear as one large heap of observations and findings. Every time one changes the view point or angle the heap changes shape.  Not everything in the heap is dependent on the window. Three kinds are distinguished, because they play different roles:

- **Windows** — observational instruments; they *see* something of the inner being. These are what Pass A and Pass B act on.
- **Calibration principles** — the *clear-glass standards*. "Is the glass clear?" resolves to "**does this window show what is expected - what is the calibration principles?**" So the standards are catalogued separately from the windows they judge (§6).
- **Scaffolding / mechanisms** — windows-focused observational structures that shape *how* a window looks (for example, the Operation structure by which a word is observed — see §7, M1). These are **not** general project, database, or process plumbing; that belongs with the global rules and is out of scope here (§0.1a). They are listed separately (§7) so the mechanism of observation is captured without being mistaken for a window in its own right. How mechanisms relate to windows and calibration criteria is still being worked out, and will be clarified as the windows are settled.

- **Glass status** ∈ {clear · needs-review · corrected-exemplar · known-cloudy}.
- **Verified this session?** is honesty discipline: an entry populated from memory has *not* had its glass checked against the instrument's current text.

## 6. Calibration principles (the clear-glass standards)

These are the standards each window is checked against. A window is "clear" to the extent it honours them.

- **C1 — Observe before interpret; derive, never impose.** (The reset's founding correction.)
- **C2 — Measurement informs but never decides.**
- **C3 — A movement of one is a movement.** (Attestation includes; validity excludes; frequency does neither.)
- **C4 — The verse always leads.** (GR-PROG-001.)
- **C5 — Silence is a finding, not a gap to fill.** (Interpretation-questions, Part B.)
- **C6 — Inference is named as inference, at the point it is made.** (GR-PROG-009.)
- **C7 — Consistency evidence ≠ accuracy evidence.** (The M-01 lesson — the standard that condemned W8's glass.)

## 8. Vantage map — coverage and shared blind spots

Grouping the windows by the angle they look from reveals where the collection is concentrated and where it is thin:

- **Micro / per-verse:** W1, W5, W6
- **Macro / aggregative:** W2, W8
- **Meso / word-span or lexical:** W3, W10
- **Passage / narrative:** W4
- **Quantitative:** W8, W9
- **Interrogative / qualitative:** W5, W6

**The primary shared blind spot (named).** *Every* window in §5 ultimately reads **text** and **infers interior**. None observes the inner being directly, because the inner being is not textually direct — it is known only through what Scripture chose to state or manifest. This is the floor beneath the whole collection: however many windows are added, the set cannot see what the text never externalised. Coverage reduces the *small*, angle-specific blind spots; it cannot remove this *shared* one.

The programme already holds the right response to it — **C5, silence is a finding.** The shared blind spot is not denied; it is converted into data. Where a window would guess at an unstated interior, the discipline records the silence instead. That is the difference between a collection that admits its floor and one that papers over it.

## 9. Add-a-window protocol (how the collection is grown)

As artifacts of the past are introduced, each is assessed and placed by the same short pass:

1. **Read it** (in full — no glass is judged from a summary).
2. **Classify it:** window, calibration principle, or scaffolding (§3).
3. **If a window, schema it** (§4): what it uniquely sees; its vantage; what it is blind to.
4. **Check the glass** against the calibration principles (§6): does it observe, or impose? Record the status honestly, including *needs-review* where the check is not yet possible.
5. **Place it on the vantage map** (§8): does it open a *new* angle (reduces the shared blind spot) or reinforce an existing one (adds confidence but not coverage)?
6. **Test for redundancy or conflict:** does it duplicate a window already held, or contradict one? Surface both; resolve neither silently.
7. **Record** the addition and any decision in the obslog; version-bump this register when a batch of additions closes.

## 11. Reflection (labelled) — on "we will only know the truth when we completed it"

Held as reflection, not as a claim about the inner being:

The aim of knowing the truth *when the looking is complete* is sound in one sense and worth stress-testing in another. Sound: many partial, honest views whose overlap is trusted more than any single view is exactly what responsible **indirect** observation looks like — and the inner being is only ever observed indirectly. The multiplicity is not a failure to be consolidated away; it is the method.

Worth stress-testing: "complete" reduces blind spots but does not, by itself, remove the *shared* one named in §8. A collection can be complete in coverage and still be looking at the whole field from one epistemic side (text → inferred interior). So the truer form of the goal may be: *look through all the windows, describe what is seen — and describe, with equal care, what none of them could see.* The register serves both halves: §5 for the seen, §8 and C5 for the unseen. Completion is then not the moment the picture is whole, but the moment the picture and its known frame are both fully drawn.

This is reflection offered for your judgement; it adopts nothing.

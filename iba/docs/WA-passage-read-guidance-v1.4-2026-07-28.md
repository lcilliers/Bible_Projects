PASSAGE READ GUIDANCE

step 1 read the verse/passage
step 2 does the verse have any bearing on the IB - if no - exit
step 3 isolate EVERY aspect that is IB related
step 4 what does the verse say about EACH operation, produce a paragraph for each operation and record in operations md
step 5 record the parts of each operation in the md

Step 1 notes
a) the main aim of reading of the passage/verses is to describe the activity of the inner being to capture the operations of different states and characteristics of the inner being.  Each operation have separate parts the subject (the inner being in focus); the operation (what is the state of, or happening in, or impacting on; or being impacted by ) the inner being; the source (where does the operation come from - another operation, or another human being, or another non-human being, or a physical object or situation); the target (what does the operation impact - another operation, or another human being, or another non-human being, or a physical object or situation). the source and target could be singular, multiple, mixed or non existent.

Step 2 notes
a) there could be more different inner beings in the verse: One inner being impacting on another - both inner beings must be considered separately: what is the state / characteristics of the first inner being; what is the state / characteristics of the next inner being ; what is the impact / effect / movement between the inner  beings
b) the movement could be between a non-human being and a human being - in this case the state / characterisctics of the non human being must be considered and describe in relation to the human being in the verse
c) the human being could be referred to as a collection (tribe, youths, children, nation, gentiles etc) - in this case the nature of the collection should be taken into account as a movement to a collection rather than an individual
d) verses with only non-human beings in contexts, where the state / characteristics of the non-human being is not related to a human being in the context of the passage, is not in scope of this study's analysis, the verse is set aside.
e) inferenced related parties: the context of the verse may not name or specify the parties where the characteristics infer a movement or action between parties, this may be found in the passage; in may also be discoverable in other verses. In this case, the analysis must raise the referential aspect and name it as such
f) every human mentioned is a presumptive IB candidate. any human — named or collective, major or minor, however briefly mentioned — who performs an act, undergoes an act, thinks, speaks, refrains from acting, or is simply named as present, is a candidate operation and must be carried into step 3-5. this holds even where the act described looks purely outward, administrative, locational, or incidental — see step 3 note (e): the operation may be *hidden behind* the act, with only the act recorded in the text. step 2's "no bearing - exit" applies **only** where the verse contains no human at all, or only a non-human being unrelated to any human (note d). it must never be used to dismiss a verse or clause on the grounds that the act itself looks outward or minor — that judgement belongs to step 3-5 (the interrogative), not to step 2. "no operation found, silent" is a valid *result* of running step 3-5 on a human-bearing clause; it is not a valid *substitute* for running them. when a passage debate is written, every human mentioned in the passage should be traceable to a place in the debate where step 3-5 was applied to them, even if the outcome recorded is silence.

Step 3 notes
a) isolate the inner being operation. The verse may be silent about any operation. Then set aside the verse.
b) the operation can be a state or status, or a movement (come from, go to, impact on, emerge, go away, becomes evident)
c) there may be multiple operations in the same verse/passage, each operation must be described in its own right
d) the operations may be explicitly or implicitly linked, or may have no relation with another operation. Each linkage must be explored, no linkage must not be silently ignored, the lack of linkage must surface.
e) the inner being operation could be expressed as, or inferred from the state or status of the inner human, and can this state may be hidden behind an act or refrain from an act with only the action being recorded in passage

Step 4 notes
a) The output of the reading as a statement of operation for each operation. Each statement must include all the parts mentioned in 1
b) The statement of one operation may have a bearing on another statement, this must surface.

Step 5 notes
a) every recorded operation carries an action-type label, in addition to its
   subject/operation/source/target parts. the action-type is a short, natural, verb-based
   label for what was done (e.g. "gave," "summoned/complied," "worshiped," "renamed," "bound and
   cast") — recorded for every operation regardless of whether step 3-5 resolved its interior
   content as stated, inferred, or a recorded silence (note f). the label is not drawn from a
   fixed list and no such list is being built by this note; it exists so that the same
   action-type, wherever it recurs — in this passage, this book, or elsewhere in the corpus once
   read — can be found and weighed together, the way `WA-interpretation-questions` Q7 already
   requires linkages *within* a passage to be surfaced rather than passed over. See
   `WA-interpretation-questions-v1.3` Q11 and Part B.10.
b) **(new, v1.4) where a human operation's action-type, vocabulary, or explicit content
   juxtaposes it against a stated divine operation recorded elsewhere in the same passage, note
   the point of comparison, difference, or inversion — but only where the passage's own data
   anchors it** (shared action-type/vocabulary, a direct juxtaposition, or an explicit statement
   of likeness or contrast). This is not a required part of every operation the way note (a) is;
   it is recorded only where the text itself supplies the anchor, and is not elaborated into a
   general account of how or why the inner being resembles, differs from, or inverts God's
   character — a suggestive but textually-unanchored resemblance is logged as an emergent
   question instead (Part B.8/B.11), not asserted. See `WA-interpretation-questions-v1.3` Q12 and
   Part B.11 for the interrogative-side and discipline-side of the same addition.

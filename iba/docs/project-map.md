## 1. What the project is

**The aim.** A sole researcher (le Roux Cilliers) is building a sustained and defensible account of how Scripture describes the workings of the human inner being: the non-physical, internal states, capacities and expressions that make up a person's invisible life (the researcher's working definition, `inner_being_definition` in `cfg_prose_concept`; the programme prose). The account is built from the bottom up on the evidence of the text, word by word, and the finished form is the inner-being narrative, with its claims traceable to verses (programme prose, "Expected Outcome"; `governance.narrative_home`).

**Where the project lives.** The project lives entirely in the databases. Any document or file is a working document or supplementary: a plan, a report, an export, a draft. Only results held in the databases represent the project's results (researcher, 2026-11-19). So a file that says something is not the project saying it; the database row is. The IBA app is the process control for all of it: every operation is a registered step, every rule a configuration row (`CHARTER.md` section 1; `governance.scope_iba_app`).

## 4. How Claude works in this project

**Roles.** The researcher directs, decides and is accountable for every conclusion. Claude is the one working agent: it reads the evidence, builds and operates the app, and in most cases decides what the evidence settles; the researcher scrutinises and settles what it cannot (B:127, B:44, B:105). The AI on the Ollama server is a controlled tool for judging words; its answers are inferential (B:4, B:124). Nothing outside the app does project work (`CHARTER.md`).

**Why the controls exist.** The app's history had never added up to one statement of what the whole was for (`CHARTER.md`, 2026-08-15), and the failures the record shows repeat in one shape: a step taken from memory or assumption, a rule living only in a document, a design built without the researcher's decision, a question put that the record already answered, a count reported without its context. Each control below answers one of these.

**Session modes.** The researcher chooses the mode when the session starts; it is never switched in the session and never chosen by Claude. A *standard* session runs the study and the app as built; every change to a `cfg_*` row goes through `Config-Maintenance.ps1 -Step Propose`, a coherence check and the researcher's approval. A *Developer Mode* session (`/developer-mode`, first thing in a fresh session) builds or fixes the app without the per-row approval gate; every rule still applies and every config write is still logged through `configmaint.apply_logged` (`CHARTER.md` section 4; `GOVERNANCE.md` section 4).

**The working loop.**
1. **Start** (`/start-project`): `Start-Iba.ps1` checks and loads the config, the data tables, the STEP connection and how the last session ended, then prints every general rule. Claude restates each in its own words in the compliance file for the session, and the check must pass (B:81). The other rules arrive at the first use of an operation they govern (`cfg_rule_scope`; the hook `rules_at_point_of_use.py`).
2. **Instruction.** Before work, Claude establishes that the rules governing the instruction are in config (`governance.rules_must_be_config_driven`) and reads the recorded meaning of any table it will use (B:123); a non-trivial instruction is stated back with the approach and scope (B:17). A diagnosis is not licence to build; a design goes to the researcher as a decision first (B:105), and a new decision point stops the work (B:44).
3. **Record.** Anything open, or any comment of the researcher bearing on an item, is an escalation in the same turn, with the researcher's words quoted verbatim (B:21, E:5). The record, not Claude's memory, holds the researcher's earlier answers and is read before a question is put (B:99).
4. **Work through the app.** Every operation is a registered step run through the dispatcher `run.py`, reading its rules from `cfg_*`; every write is checked against `cfg_write_grant`. Long jobs run outside the Claude session (B:125). Database state is queried live; large tables are planned first.
5. **Change.** A rule changes in `cfg_*` first, with `GOVERNANCE.md` and, for code, `BUILD.md` updated in the same unit of work (`governance.governance_md_on_rule_change`, `governance.build_md_on_code_change`); retired scripts are archived and run only on the researcher's approval.
6. **Report.** Only checked facts, each with its context; nothing is called done until its output is confirmed to exist (B:2, B:102).
7. **Close** (`/session-close`): `Session-Close.ps1` checks what the session did, decided and left open against the record; nothing is committed while a finding is open; the session log names the escalations touched, files changed, decisions (the researcher's and Claude's own), the open items and the git state; two commits. From 2026-11-19 keeping this map current is part of the close (researcher, D3 on #2510); the check is not yet built.

**The rule store.** 115 active behaviour rules in eight classes (chat 18, development 22, documentation 8, filing 8, llm_output 12, sqlite 17, study_method 23, terminal 7), plus governance settings and escalation rules; all are rows, delivered at the start or the point of use. **Memory** holds who the researcher is and a few data pointers, no rule and no project state (MEMORY.md).

### 6.2 The central battleground: defining characteristics and deriving them from the verse context at the same time

This subsection is the researcher's account of 2026-11-19, recorded on #2510 (v8), set against the record. The researcher is adding further battlegrounds; they are to be added here as they come.

**The problem.** The study has to know what to look for (what is and is not a characteristic) and at the same time discover how characteristics work, from the same verses. Each depends on the other, and this circularity hampered every attempt. Three difficulties sat inside it:
- The wish for completeness and the preselection of characteristics are intrinsically in conflict: a list chosen in advance cannot be complete, and a complete list cannot be chosen in advance.
- Verses had to be read through the lens of specific characteristics, while the words that carry them are sometimes relevant and sometimes not.
- Some words become behavioural only when they operate together.

**The route, by attempt.** Each attempt added insight into how to approach the problem.

| # | Attempt | What it delivered | What it showed |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The list in Framework B (27 characteristics in the researcher's account; the programme prose says "some twenty-eight") | A starting list, chosen without a method | An order of magnitude short of what the biblical evidence holds (programme prose, Scope); the 27 and the 28 are to be reconciled in the prose |
| 2 | Words from several rounds of dictionary-type searches, taken into the registry (the High and Low confidence lists, a gap analysis, continuous additions; programme prose, "Word selection and the registry") | A larger list of English words, tested against the working definition | A word list is still a preselection |
| 3 | The Strong's codes and related Strong's codes that STEP returns for the registry words (`word_strong`, `New-Word.ps1`) | A more complete list of candidates | No definitive relevance to characteristics: a candidate is a word that may or may not be a characteristic in a given verse |
| 4 | **The watershed:** characteristics are context driven, and what matters is the term's importance in the verse context | A new phase of the project: to learn to read the context successfully | The question changed from "which words are characteristics" to "how is the context read" |
| 5 | The constructs of that phase: dimensions, verse groups (Verse Context), the standing question catalogue, clustering (M-codes, subgroups, the five-stage reading pipeline), strands, readings | Many ways of reading a large volume of verses in context and extracting what they say about the inner being | All parts of one fundamental problem, each adding a little insight and each hampered by the circularity above |

**The split that resolved it** (the researcher's terms, with where each now lives):

- The lexical occurrences in the verses: `verse_lexical` (section 3A).

- The collation of characteristics on the surfaces: the surface groups and the collections of surfaces (section 3B), from which the context is re-read to see how characteristics interact (stages 3 and 4 of section 3C).

**The route read for cause and effect** (full analysis: `outputs/escalation/2510-study-learning-history-v1-20261119.md`, which holds the dated catalogue of 81 change events, the two learning streams, the gaps and a way to close each). In brief:
- The study oscillated between two views of its subject, each swing forced by a finding: named characteristics (Feb), clusters of terms (May), movements and a relational web with the characteristic set aside (the reset of 25 Jun, after a cold read found 70 to 85% of the claims imported meaning the verses do not carry), characteristics read from the verse (8 Jul), and now characteristics collated on surfaces. The present design is a third synthesis.
- Two streams of learning. **Subject matter:** the lesson is grain; the wrong grain was the registry word, the dimension, the term-similarity cluster, the lemma, the Strong's code, and the working grain is the surface in its verse. **Process:** the lesson is verification; discretion drifted, an AI was the sole gate, audits checked one direction, completeness was circular, validators checked structure while the values were wrong. Each stream hid the other.
- **The failure at the heart of both streams** is that no one knows how to depict the inner being's working systematically. What the working is (the question, the unit, the evidence) is understood; how it is depicted, checked and kept consistent, and where it is read, is still part of the discovery (`outputs/escalation/2510-inner-being-working-v3-20261119.md`). It has been defined in eleven stages from the programme prose onward (`outputs/escalation/2510-inner-being-definition-lineage-v1-20261119.md`), the elements were renamed more than refuted, and no definition was pursued; the methods put in place (separate observations for verse and inner-being analysis, the surface group used twice, two grains, the batch unit, nuances) did not ground. The `thought` grouping of 15 Nov failed at this point.
- Every gap was found by the researcher reading one case by hand; the app's checks passed while the content was wrong. Open: stage 4 (what is a characteristic) is not built; the surface groups are again a word-level gate (inference); the judged universe is bounded by the Strong's-level cluster allocation (37.6% of occurrence rows outside it); no independent completeness control; the layered design has not run through a stage on any collection.

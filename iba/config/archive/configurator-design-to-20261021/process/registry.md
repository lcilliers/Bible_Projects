**3 open question(s)**

- **`open.registry.qualification-rule`** — What actually QUALIFIES a word as an inner-being word? The plan says the scope is 'the whole inner life (moral, emotional, volitional, relational, vertical and horizontal), with no specific theological bias and the human in focus'. That is a scope statement, not a test.
  - _recommendation:_ C, with A as the record. The study's own method says relevance is decided ON THE VERSE, not on the lemma (char.list-validates-never-imputes; feedback_faculty_only_if_explicit_or_inferred_on_verse). A qualification test at registry level is a judgement about a WORD — which is exactly the lemma-level reasoning the reset abolished. Register generously; let Screen 0 do its job; record why each word was added.
  - _resolve by:_ researcher decision
- **`open.registry.growth-trigger`** — The list 'grows over time' (plan §1.1) — but nothing states what TRIGGERS growth, or who decides.
  - _recommendation:_ Wire the three. They are three independent witnesses to the same question — what is the registry missing — and all three already exist in some form. Route A is the strongest because it does not start from the study's existing corpus, so it can see what the other two structurally cannot (open.fetch.mitigation-blind-spot).
  - _resolve by:_ researcher decision
- **`open.registry.214-provenance`** — The initial ~214 words came from somewhere — two earlier AI studies (Spirit, Soul and Body; The Holy Spirit) whose findings 'appeared strong at first but deteriorated under further questioning' and which 'badly understated the inner being's characteristics' (plan §1.1).
  - _recommendation:_ Do not re-derive the list — it is six months of curation and it works. But do NOT treat its BOUNDARY as evidence. The registry's edges inherit the prior studies' blind spots, so registry growth (open.registry.growth-trigger) is not housekeeping; it is the correction. Worth stating in the config so the inheritance is known rather than assumed.
  - _resolve by:_ researcher decision — the recommendation is a stance, not a change

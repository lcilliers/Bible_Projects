## What this session did

Filled every Resolution slot and the Closing synthesis by hand, applying the same discipline as

whether a later chapter answers it, extends it, or leaves it genuinely open.

**Major findings from reading the whole book together (not visible from any single chapter):**
- **The "day of the LORD" phrase is a five-point thread**, not three or four as any single
  chapter could show: seeded at the missing 1:15, then 2:1, 2:11, 2:31, and 3:14 — the single
  most consistent structural spine in the book.
- **The locust/army identity question (raised in the Joel 2 debate) only resolves across all
  three chapters**: Joel 1:4 names four literal locust species with no military language; Joel
  2:2-11 escalates the SAME locusts into full martial imagery; Joel 2:25 has the LORD explicitly
  claim them as "my great army... which I sent," reusing 1:4's own names verbatim. Not
  resolvable from chapter 2 alone, exactly as that debate's own EQ4 anticipated.
- **Comprehensive-by-named-category address turns out to be a three-chapter-wide device**, not a
  Joel 2 innovation — it starts with Joel 1's occupational groups (drunkards, farmers, priests)
  before Joel 2's larger enumerations (elders/children/nursing-infants/bridegroom/bride;
  sons/daughters/old/young/servants).
- **Divine-possessive language deepens across three registers** over the book: the prophet's own
  voice (1:6-7, "my land"), the narrator's third person (2:18, "his land"), and God's own quoted
  first-person speech (3:2, "my people... my heritage... my land").
- **The corporate-over-individual interior bias flagged in the Joel 2 debate (EQ3) is real but
  not absolute** — Joel's own individual cry (1:19) and an unidentified interjecting voice (3:11)
  both briefly punctuate the otherwise overwhelmingly corporate/collective framing.
- **The book's Q12 (divine-mirroring) tally is now complete**: three real instances —
  "return/turn" (2:12-14, extended 3:1/3:4/3:7, the dominant thread), "calls" (2:32,
  self-contained), "sold" (3:3-8, the single clearest, most precisely talionic instance).
- **The book's last clause — "for the LORD dwells in Zion" (3:21) — closes exactly on the
  presence-theme structuring the whole book** since "my holy mountain" (2:1, itself echoing
  1:6's "my land").

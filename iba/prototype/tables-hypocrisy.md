## ★ The claim to check

**The term is the set of codes sharing a definition TREE — not the base code.**

- `A`/`B`/`C` suffixes = **homonyms** — different lemmas sharing consonants. Each has its **own** tree.
- `G`/`H`/`I`/`J`… = **senses** — one lemma. They **share** one tree.
- The **head** (the first line of `mediumDef`) is *this code's* meaning. The **tree** (the rest) is *the lemma's* full range.

# Stage 1 (verse-reading) — do the 34 live questions actually serve the stage's objective?

**Date:** 2026-09-23
**Stage objective, as stated by the researcher this session:** Stage 1's sole purpose is to
articulate the *context* of M-code words in every verse — often in relation to *other M-code words*
that share the verse, and M-code words operate alongside *non-M-code words* that also define their
context. Verse-reading is cluster/characteristic-agnostic (escalation #1810 v3, #1824 v14) — it is
about what the verse itself says, not what the verse says about a cluster.

**Method:** every one of the 34 live `stage='verse-reading'` question codes read in full (not grouped
by code-prefix), checked against the objective on two tests: (1) does the question anchor to *this
verse* / *this occurrence*, not the word or cluster in general, and (2) does it ask about the word's
context — its own function, or its relation to other words (M-code or not) — rather than a lexical
fact about the word/cluster as a whole.

Terminology note: D7.7.1, M0.6.5, M0.6.6 are shown in their **revised** wording (fixed 2026-09-23,
this session — "characteristic"/"this cluster" replaced with "M-code word"/"role-T3").

---

## Category A — Fit the objective (20 of 34)

These anchor explicitly to "in this verse" / "this occurrence" and ask about the word's own function
or its relation to other words present.

**Relational, explicit "other M-code words" framing (3):**
- **D7.7.1** — word's relation to the parties present in the verse (who initiates, toward whom).
- **M0.6.5** — this word's relation to the OTHER M-code words present in the verse.
- **M0.6.6** — the whole verse's M-code relational network.

**Word's own function/effect within this verse (10) — M0.7.1–M0.7.10:** purpose/role/effect the word
serves, immediate inner-being response, what it produces over time, state/condition change, sequence
of states, mechanism of change, mode of operation, constitutional level, origin. All explicitly
"In this verse, does [this word]...". These describe the word's own operation as shown by the verse,
not a relation to another word — a different flavour of "context" from the M0.6.x/D7.7.1 group, but
still genuinely this-verse and word-level.

**Relation to non-M-code context — persons, God, other beings (6) — M0.7.11–M0.7.16:** movement
toward God, movement from God to the person, extended person-to-person, taken up from another,
relation to spiritual beings, relation to God. These are the questions that actually cover the
"M-code words live with non-M-code words which define context" half of the objective — the other
half of "context" besides M-code-to-M-code relation.

**Occurrence-specific sense (1):**
- **M0.5.11** — where this occurrence's sense diverges from the term's usual sense, compared to the
  word's other occurrences. Anchored to "in this verse" / "this occurrence" — fits, though answering
  the "compare to other occurrences" clause honestly requires visibility into the word's other
  occurrences, which is a data-availability question for whoever builds the batch payload, not a
  scope defect in the question itself.

## Category B — Do NOT fit the objective (13 of 34)

None of these anchor to "in this verse." All ask about the word or the cluster's vocabulary as a
general, verse-independent fact.

**About the cluster's identity/naming in general (3):**
- **M0.1.1** — what the characteristic is called in the programme, what the name signals.
- **M0.1.2** — what the primary Hebrew/Greek terms show at the definitional level.
- **M0.1.3** — directional/relational/constitutional implication of the name.

**About the cluster's vocabulary as a whole (8):**
- **M0.5.1** — primary Hebrew/Greek terms' root meanings.
- **M0.5.4** — whether the vocabulary distinguishes disposition/act, received/given, etc.
- **M0.5.5** — whether the vocabulary has a term for the structural opposite/absence.
- **M0.5.6** — whether the vocabulary has a person-type term.
- **M0.5.7** — whether the vocabulary has a supplication/seeking term.
- **M0.5.8** — OT/NT vocabulary relationship, continuity or development.
- **M0.5.9** — whether there's a NT-coined term.
- **M0.5.10** — "the full vocabulary arc," "the characteristic's complete semantic range." This one
  cannot be honestly answered from a single verse, or even a single strong — it structurally
  requires the cluster's whole vocabulary, by its own wording.

**About the term's general lexical range, not this verse's use of it (2) — borderline:**
- **M0.5.2** — grammatical range of this term (noun/verb/adjective/participle) — anchored to "this
  term" (word-level, post-#1822 fix), but asks about the term's range in general, not this verse's
  instance of it.
- **M0.5.3** — semantic range of this term, same pattern.

These two are word-level (not cluster-level) so they're closer to the objective than the other 11 —
but they still describe the term as a lexical item, not what this verse's occurrence shows.

## Category C — A different kind of question entirely (1 of 34)

- **M0.8.1** — does this T2/T3 role word's behaviour here suggest it warrants elevation to its own
  M-code characteristic? This is a question about whether *the coding scheme itself* should change,
  not about the word's context in the verse. "Characteristic" is used correctly here (this really is
  about characteristic-elevation) — it isn't a wording bug like D7.7.1/M0.6.5/M0.6.6 were. It simply
  isn't a context-articulation question, and probably doesn't belong being scored against this
  stage's objective at all.

---

## Bottom line

**20 of 34 (59%)** genuinely serve Stage 1's stated purpose. **13 of 34 (38%)** ask about the word
or cluster's general lexical/vocabulary identity — a fact that is true independent of any verse, not
something "read from" the verse at all — and **1 (M0.8.1)** is a taxonomy/classification question,
not a context question. The terminology fix applied earlier today (this session) corrected wording
in 3 questions that already belonged in Category A; it did not, and could not, fix the 14 in
Category B/C, because their problem isn't which noun they use — it's that they're asking about the
wrong grain of thing for a stage whose job is to read verses, not clusters.

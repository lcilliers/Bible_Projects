# Layer-1 — the 286 simplest in-scope verses, annotated with inner-being meaning

- **File:** wa-layer1-286-annotated-v1-20260627.md · **2026-06-27 · Author:** Claude Code.
- **Method:** for each verse, the inner-being **meaning read off the verse** (movement / process / state / relation — the hybrid method), grounded in morphology + the term-in-context. **Verse is king:** where a tagged "study term" carries *no* inner-being sense in this verse (ritual/census/legal/material use), it is marked **None** with the reason. Honest reading, not a template.
- **Status: batch 1 of ~7 (verses 1–45).** Continuing on confirmation of format/depth.

---

1. **Gen 2:1** — Thus the heavens and the earth were finished, and all the host of them.
   - *Inner being:* **None** — "host" = the array/multitude of creation; a completion statement, no inner state.
2. **Gen 6:8** — But Noah found favor in the eyes of the LORD.
   - *Inner being:* **Relational regard** — Noah is the *object* of divine favour (chen, M39); the movement is grace *resting on* a person — a disposition of favour directed God→Noah, received not self-generated.
3. **Gen 7:24** — And the waters prevailed on the earth 150 days.
   - *Inner being:* **None** — "prevailed" = the waters' physical dominance; no inner content.
4. **Gen 9:4** — But you shall not eat flesh with its life, that is, its blood.
   - *Inner being:* **Marginal (definitional)** — nephesh "life" (M47) is identified with the blood: life-as-vital-principle bound to the body. A statement *about* life, not an inner movement.
5. **Gen 25:5** — Abraham gave all he had to Isaac.
   - *Inner being:* **None** — "gave" = transfer of inheritance; an external act, no inner state.
6. **Gen 26:35** — and they made life bitter for Isaac and Rebekah.
   - *Inner being:* **Affect inflicted** — an external cause (the wives' conduct) produces an inner state of *bitterness* (M03) in Isaac & Rebekah; movement: cause → embittered inner state (the experiencers as patients).
7. **Gen 49:18** — I wait for your salvation, O LORD.
   - *Inner being:* **Posture of hope** — the operation is expectant *waiting* (M17) *directed toward* God's salvation (M38); the inner being oriented (direction) on God as object — a movement of hopeful expectation.
8. **Exo 1:13** — So they ruthlessly made the people of Israel work as slaves.
   - *Inner being:* **None / act** — forced labour imposed; "ruthlessly" hints at the oppressors' hardness but the verse states the *act*, not an inner state.
9. **Exo 12:24** — You shall observe this rite as a statute … forever.
   - *Inner being:* **Marginal (volitional)** — "observe/keep" (M30) implies obedient keeping (a disposition), but stated as a *command to act*, not an inner state described.
10. **Exo 12:26** — And when your children say to you, 'What do you mean by this service?'
   - *Inner being:* **None** — "service" = the rite; the verse is about children questioning.
11. **Exo 13:10** — You shall therefore keep this statute at its appointed time …
   - *Inner being:* **Marginal (volitional)** — as #9: obedient keeping, commanded.
12. **Exo 20:16** — "You shall not bear false witness against your neighbor.
   - *Inner being:* **Falsehood (as expression)** — the inner element is *deception* (M14); the verse prohibits its *act* (false witness). Inner disposition addressed via a forbidden expression.
13. **Exo 21:23** — But if there is harm, then you shall pay life for life,
   - *Inner being:* **None** — nephesh "life" = the person/life as a unit of recompense (lex talionis); physical, not inner.
14. **Exo 22:22** — You shall not mistreat any widow or fatherless child.
   - *Inner being:* **None / act** — prohibits an act of affliction; compassion-vs-cruelty implied, not described.
15. **Exo 23:3** — nor shall you be partial to a poor man in his lawsuit.
   - *Inner being:* **Partiality (disposition)** — "be partial/show favour" (M22) = a biased regard; commanded against. Thin inner element = partial inclination.
16. **Exo 23:6** — "You shall not pervert the justice due to your poor in his lawsuit.
   - *Inner being:* **None** — about (un)just treatment in a suit; justice as norm, not an inner state.
17. **Exo 23:32** — You shall make no covenant with them and their gods.
   - *Inner being:* **None** — covenant here = a political/religious pact; not inner being.
18. **Exo 25:38** — Its tongs and their trays shall be of pure gold.
   - *Inner being:* **None** — "pure" = material purity of *gold*; not moral/inner purity.
19. **Exo 30:19** — with which Aaron and his sons shall wash their hands and their feet.
   - *Inner being:* **None** — literal ritual washing of hands/feet.
20. **Lev 7:1** — "This is the law of the guilt offering. It is most holy.
   - *Inner being:* **None** — "guilt offering"/"most holy" = ritual category and status, not an inner state.
21. **Lev 16:25** — And the fat of the sin offering he shall burn on the altar.
   - *Inner being:* **None** — "sin offering" = ritual offering; "sin" is the offering category.
22. **Lev 25:53** — He shall treat him as a worker hired year by year. He shall not rule ruthlessly over him in your sight.
   - *Inner being:* **None / act** — labour relation and a command against ruthless ruling; the harsh disposition is implied, not described.
23. **Num 6:8** — All the days of his separation he is holy to the LORD.
   - *Inner being:* **Marginal (state of consecration)** — "holy" (M22) here = the Nazirite's set-apart *status*, a dedicated state; relational/consecrational rather than a felt inner movement.
24–35. **Num 7:16/22/28/34/40/46/52/58/64/70/76/82** — one male goat for a sin offering;
   - *Inner being:* **None** (×12) — "sin offering" = ritual inventory line (tribal offerings); "sin" is the category, repeated identically. *Filter note: exact repeats surfaced by the simplicity filter.*
36. **Num 10:28** — This was the order of march of the people of Israel by their companies …
   - *Inner being:* **None** — military marching order.
37. **Num 14:32** — But as for you, your dead bodies shall fall in this wilderness.
   - *Inner being:* **None** — physical death/falling.
38. **Num 27:5** — Moses brought their case before the LORD.
   - *Inner being:* **None** — "case" = a legal cause brought; procedural.
39. **Num 27:9** — And if he has no daughter, then you shall give his inheritance to his brothers.
   - *Inner being:* **None** — inheritance transfer.
40. **Num 31:46** — and 16,000 persons —
   - *Inner being:* **None** — nephesh "persons" = a census headcount.
41. **Num 31:53** — (The men in the army had each taken plunder for himself.)
   - *Inner being:* **None** — military plunder note.
42. **Num 35:29** — And these things shall be for a statute and rule for you throughout your generations …
   - *Inner being:* **None** — legal statute.
43. **Deu 1:12** — How can I bear by myself the weight and burden of you and your strife?
   - *Inner being:* **Felt burden** — Moses *bears* (nasa, M19) the weight + strife (M02); an inner experience of being over-burdened — bearing-as-affective-load. Movement: a felt weariness under a borne weight.
44. **Deu 3:15** — To Machir I gave Gilead,
   - *Inner being:* **None** — land grant.
45. **Deu 5:2** — The LORD our God made a covenant with us in Horeb.
   - *Inner being:* **Marginal (relational bond)** — covenant-making (M44) establishes a binding relation God↔people; stated as historical act rather than an inner movement.
